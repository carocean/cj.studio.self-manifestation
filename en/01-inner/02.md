# Meta-Theory

Pool water — odor like rot-mouse,  
smell as Information;  
thus things sense one another through Information.

From And-Gate goes Not-Gate;  
Know information of Or-Gate, Or-Not-Gate, and And-Gate — not go;  
alone goes Not-Gate —  
this is Principle.

From And-Gate arrive at Not-Gate —  
this is Execution.

Information, Principle, Execution —  
basis of Logic-Realm;  
call them Three Elements.

Logic of Creation —  
Meta-Information, Meta-Principle, Meta-Execution Three Elements;  
Three prepared, myriad things arise.

What is thing?  
Fusion of Three Elements.  
Lower Three transformed by upper Three,  
upper again transformed by upper-upper;  
thus endless, no final reach.  
From realm born of me seek Meta Three Elements of world —  
like one inside prison seeking outside.

Know Information obtain Principle arrive at Execution.  
How does Information obtain Principle?  
Know it so, not know why so.  
How is Principle executed?  
Know Information, yet not clear.

Meta-Principle — instruction bestowed by Host-World,  
pre-set bias;  
thus this Principle not known in This-World.

Meta-Execution — executed by Host-World, making-so;  
thus not clear in This-World.

Matching of Information-Principle-Execution —  
This-World can know.  
Why matching — not know.

Science refined to its depth —  
stops at matching of Information-Principle-Execution.  
From Information-Element seek Principle,  
seek Execution corresponding to Principle-Element;  
then say “I understand,”  
yet closed inside axiom;  
wisdom of beings within realm ends here.

What is axiom?  
Commonly said — principle of shared recognition;  
thus recognition itself is boundary.

Therefore Information-of-I remains within the Recognition-Realm  
and pursuing Meta-Principle within This-World  
as though to see world’s Features —  
nor can it overstep the Features born of its striking-with-the-external,  
walled within the bounds of its own material nature.

Thus what is pursued  
stops at axiom and cannot go beyond.  
Without surpassing axiom, no Meta-Principle.  
Without stepping outside one’s Information-Realm, no knowing world.

Yet Information-Realm itself has its Meta;  
thus pursuing self up to axiom  
still cannot reach Meta of one’s own realm —  
how much harder to reach Meta of world?

Meta-Information, Meta-Principle —  
within Logic-Realm, source of arising;  
Meta-Execution —  
outside Logic-Realm, origin of driving.

Viewed within world:  
Information moves, Principle still.

Viewed outside world:  
Principle moves, Information still.

Information is still — by necessity of Principle.  
Thus Principle extends, and Information is born.

Simplest world —  
Three Elements present, Principle not evolve.

Most subtle world —  
Principle continues to evolve,  
Execution updates without stop;  
such world —  
ever harder to find the Meta Three.
