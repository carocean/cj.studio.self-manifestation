# Motion-Stillness Theory

Things that have image —  
in motion without cease.

What is meant by “having image”?  
Measured relative to living beings.

All things evolve.  
Inner and outer transform.  
Within this, wisdom is born.

Change is a state.  
This state turns into that state.  
Small systems within small systems within small systems  
each bears its own state.

In the shifting of states,  
lower realms change faster than higher realms.  
Within the same realm,  
smaller things change faster than larger ones.  
The smaller they are,  
the faster they change.

If it were not so,  
higher images could not form,  
and lower images would fall into confusion.  
This is the necessity of logic.

Living within a system and recognizing higher and lower,  
to determine the change of state,  
one must abstract and record it.

Living beings record it as time.  
Thus time is the logic of living beings.  
Applied to a given system, it functions as a reference  
for distinguishing change of state.  
It is not an independent thing.

That which does not change—  
that is, what is static,  
what is absolutely at rest—

has no change of state,  
therefore no reference,  
therefore no time,  
therefore no logic to analyze,  
therefore no mass or energy,  
therefore it is “Non.”

All things, because there is “Non,”  
have gaps through which movement may pass.  
Thus through Non, Being comes to be.

A body without interval—  
even if intelligence were within it—  
would not know “Being.”

Where there is no interval,  
things have no space for moving or stopping.  
State ceases to transform.  
Thus it returns to Non.

Non is everywhere in the world.

The Non of this world  
exists within the Being of the Host-World.

When the Opening-World with two or three materials  
determines the nature of its objects,  
“Being” is born in the Pangu-World.

What the Opening-World calls “Being”,  
the living beings of Pangu may recognize only one or two.  
What different worlds perceive  
may not be the same.

Where the Pangu-World holds absolute Non  
is where the logic of its opening leaves a vacancy.  
If law is placed there,  
Being may arise from Non.

Thus Being and Non complete one another  
and are known by reference.

From the perspective of the Host-World:  
Being is the logic of Being;  
Non is the logic of Non.

Logical entities transform into one another.  
Within a world they resonate through Information.  
Information is the trace of logical motion—  
its messenger.

The forces of all things  
are Information.  
What are called “all things”  
are the sum of Information.

Objects may be extremely minute  
or without bound.  
Yet at the place where they are hosted,  
at the point where emergence begins,  
between Being and Non,  
at the pivot where the Host triggers logic—  
there lies the source of the world,  
the seat of Meta-Principle,  
the hinge by which a world closes.

Is it also so  
for the Opening-World and its Host-World,  
and the Host of the Host of the Host?

Within the cosmos,  
the real and the void depend on one another.

My body is born within it,  
confined by the law of logic,  
by the law of nature:

To eat—no more than three days unfed.  
To leap—not beyond gravity.  
To call—not beyond a thousand meters.  
To labor a lifetime—

alas, living being.
