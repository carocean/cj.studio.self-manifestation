# Non-Ending-in-One Theory

Necessity is the state formed by converging forces;  
it is the result of many causes.

Tens converge into one.  
Hundreds converge into one.  
Millions converge into one.  
Even the infinite converges into one.

That One is necessity—  
relative to their convergence.

To know it as so,  
to know its ground as ground,  
to follow the vine and obtain the melon—  
this is one law of all appearances.  
Thus Newton rejoiced,  
believing the world no more than this.

Alas—  
God would laugh,  
even to a snort.

A ball proceeds straight;  
observed or unobserved, it proceeds straight.  
But a man walks three steps  
and turns left, not right.  
Another watches him  
and cannot determine his direction.

He may turn left,  
or right,  
or leap,  
or crouch,  
or incline,  
or go straight.

The succession of his states  
may extend without bound;  
yet once enacted,  
it too converges into one.

I do not know what to call this.  
I name it Contingency.

Contingency pervades the real.

Neurons converge into one—  
this is physical necessity.  
Internal formation within a Sedimentation-Field¹ converges into one—  
this too belongs to physical necessity.

But when intention arises of itself,  
selects its biased current and lets it issue forth,  
steps above the physical,  
turns within the Information-Realm according to its likes and aversions,  
and again acts upon the physical—

then it does not converge into one;  
thus Contingency spreads across the domain of physics.

I often marvel:

Sedimentation-fields arise in the world—  
some within a skull,  
some within galaxies,  
some within obscure corners.

Born upon the physical,  
yet returning to it through their Information-Realm,  
they govern necessity  
and turn the so into the not-so.

Strange indeed.

Thus—

When it is so as so,  
do I know it converges into one?

When it is so and yet not so,  
do I know it may not converge into one?

It is so—for I know the ground of sedimentation.

All things transform in succession.  
The Information-Realm grows within them  
and returns upon things,  
so that what emerges need not converge into one  
but incline toward what it favors.

Thus Contingency arises—  
its thrust enters the world,  
and it orders the not-yet.

Thing and Information erode one another  
and do not converge into one.

I flare as though glimpsing reality—  
what do I truly know?  
I once believed I had known the One.

Note

¹ Sedimentation-Field — see On Self-Manifestation · Inner Chapter 6 · the Sedimentation-Field Theory.
