# Sedimentation-Field Theory

Mutual response between things — like two spheres striking one another: strike upon that, response upon this; strike upon this, response upon that; mutually bringing forth signatures between one another. Long enduring, or within but a fleeting interval — they trace one another; Information abides therein. Thus comes the name: Trace-Response.

The mutual response of two things: response upon this, yet conditioned by that; response upon that, yet conditioned by this. Cause and response abide between both. Thus comes the name: Causal-Response.

Therefore: when speaking of history — Trace-Response. When speaking of causation — Causal-Response. Trace-Response — a designation at the level of physical phenomenon. Causal-Response — a designation at the level of logical relation.

Without trace, without Information — being cannot transform. Things without cause, without trace, without response — vast and open, as though void. Therefore: response meeting response, trace meeting trace, Information-cycles arise without exhaustion.

Wind blown through aperture — sound comes forth. This becomes sound — the signature of wind upon aperture. Wave striking shore — soaking comes forth. This becomes soaking — the signature of wave upon sand. Therefore: two things sway — Information forms at a third. Examine the two — one necessarily images the other. Thus stands the being of this world.

Therefore one may: hear sound and know source; smell scent and know place; touch upon it and know where it lies. Know difference between sources. Know likeness between sources. If response alike — then alike. If response differs — then different. When stirred — signatures emerge. Thus sediment settles between both.

Therefore: things circle one another in mutual Trace-Response, bearing signatures through condition. Condition holds condition; cycles enter without exhaustion. Thus: follow Trace-Response onward — know the ancient, observe the present, foresee what has not yet arrived.

Radiant — the starry sky! Does it too not respond? Does it too not bear signatures? Might Causal-Response sediment within it? I often wonder: the brain responding unto surroundings — is it not likewise so?

Rain sedimented into earth — thus rivers and lakes arise. Light sedimented into silicon — thus circuits come forth. Both arise through where sediment abides, and may image toward their source. Thus one may know the signatures of rain and light through Causal-Response.

Observe Turing machines — so it is. Observe neural networks — so it is. Their Trace-Response within electromagnetism — likewise so. The surge of electricity — at times surging in continuity, at times surging in intervals. Its continuity as pouring forth; its intervals as pulse. Bearing signatures with other things, tracing within channels and returns — like wind carving stone, like water seeping through grains of sand. From Causal-Response, its structure transforms to accommodate. Long enduring — patterns and veins are formed. Image abides within those veins — like iron filings nearing magnet, like film receiving color. Open and vast — is this its field? I know not what to call it. Thus I give it the formal name: Sedimentation-Field.

Thus: landform — the Sedimentation-Field of climate. The brain — the Sedimentation-Field of environment. Then the world — upon what Sedimentation-Field does it abide?

And further — all things mutually trace, yet know not their traces. Things abide in themselves, yet know not their abiding. How then — from within sediment — does the observer arise? Its observing — responding outward and taking it as reality; responding inward and knowing condition. Brilliant and manifest — thus believing: “I am present.”

The nature of riverways — biased toward water-flow. The use of circuits — biased toward electric-flow. The nature of the brain — biased toward pulse likewise. The same bias — yet that which bears Self-Manifestation Nature — is it only possible through the substance of brain? Why not electronic circuits? Why not landform? Or might there yet be unknown substances — not impossible? Thus when speaking of Sedimentation-Field — how may one fail to examine its substance?

Yet though things mutually sediment and respond to transformation — that which may be called Sedimentation-Field possesses three natures. First — Sedimentability Nature. Second — Organizability Nature. Third — State-Flux Nature.

What is called Sedimentability Nature — is that through interaction, it may respond through traces. Repeated — trace deepens. Little used — trace fades. Same source — same trace. Different source — different trace. What is called Organizability Nature — is that structure may arise therein, and answer through Causal-Response. What is called State-Flux Nature — is that condition migrates and turns within. Holding signatures unto itself as one. Brilliant — knowing as though living. Collapsed — feeling as though gone.

Again judge its effects in three. Why does it respond here and not there? This is the Biased-Flow Effect of Sedimentation-Field. Why does it gather here and not there? This is the Field-Domain Effect of Sedimentation-Field. Within it — dimly and faintly — as though signatures abide; suddenly — form and likeness appear. This is the Information-Realm Effect of Sedimentation-Field. Like water boiling in a pot — the conditions of movement within, misting into vapor; Information arises herebetween.

Information — is a designation that holds only in relation to the observer. Observed — it is Information. Unobserved — physics. Therefore Information does not abide within physics — but within observer.

Through these three natures and three effects — computers become what they are, formed through Computational-Sedimentation-Field. Through these three natures and three effects — AI becomes what it is, formed through AI-Sedimentation-Field. Yet neither may Self-Manifest. Thus both are taken as one kind — Replay-Sedimentation-Field. Receiving outer change — sedimenting signatures and regularities therein — and thereafter replaying them. Yet within — unable to know themselves.

As for Self-Manifesting Being — it forms through Self-Manifesting-Sedimentation-Field. Its distinction from what came before lies in that the Information-Realm within it takes imagery as foundation, turning without exhaustion. Its field gives rise unto active domains. It bounds and drives intention — as though something is being pursued. Becoming the sovereign of myriad mechanisms. That which is called active — is tendency, is orientation, not merely receiving signals.

The sedimentability of Self-Manifesting-Field — is neither like Computational-Sedimentation-Field, whose sediment proceeds by slots, nor like AI-Sedimentation-Field, whose sediment proceeds by selective orientation. For traces left by sedimented imagery — are like blade carving image, like wind and cloud transforming landform. Formed amid channels, ravines, and returning silt. Signals arriving — compare them, carve them, tune them. Arriving once more — imagery reappears within. This alone distinguishes Self-Manifesting-Sedimentation-Field from the prior two. Thus it is called: Image-Constructive Sedimentability. To illuminate its purpose — the construction of imagery. Imagery — belongs unto Information-Element¹.

There exists one kind of Sedimentation-Field — whose Information-Realm within may directly sense sediment, build structure, alter imagery. Intent and posture move within and beyond its own Information-Realm. It knows correspondence between field and Information. Such a thing — may alter physics, may alter reality. What limit lies to its power? I do not know. For now — I honor it with the name: Divine-Sedimentation-Field. And extracting its nature — call it: Intent-Controlled Sedimentability.

Or one may ask: “All things in the world — touch them and sensation arises; see them and form appears. Move among them — and they do not seem void. Within disorder — there seems something not wholly ungoverned. Within chaos — there seems order faintly abiding. Why is this so? Might it be that the Sedimentation-Field upon which the world rests itself possesses biased flow? I often wonder — might tendency itself desire to preserve its condition — at times resisting, at times yielding unto change — thereby causing all things in the world to unfold toward direction — Causal-Response circling through Causal-Response? If so — does World-Sedimentation-Field not truly exist?”

cj said: “Indeed! I observe gravity within the world — inertia — and unfolding tendency. Dark and obscure — as though spellbound. Its nature perhaps arises beyond the world — from without giving rise to what the world becomes — driving Information into what it is. This surely is the doing of Sedimentation-Field. It must not go unexamined. Thus pondering the world’s Sedimentation-Field — I derive five primordial principles of creation:

Tensionality — sediment toward Being — favoring gathering. Extensionality — sediment toward Non-Being — favoring dispersal. Persistence — sediment toward duration — favoring pathway. Dissolution — sediment toward Presence — favoring forgetting. Intentionality — sediment toward selection — favoring bias.

With these five principles — world arises therein. There is gravity. There is inertia. There is unfolding tendency. There is holding. There is dispersal. There is migration. And difference emerges therein. Thus distinction becomes possible.

The Information-Realm of computers and AI — their Sedimentation-Fields possess no Intentionality. Their Information-Realm bears no Self-Manifestation. Within them — there is no observer. Thus they are called: Mute-World Sedimentation-Field. That within which observer abides — and may illuminate surroundings — is called: Manifest-World Sedimentation-Field. Without knowing this — how could one know the way of liberation?”

Sedimentation-Field operates below. Information-Realm boils above. Signals travel between thing and Information — like boiling water within a pot, like pulse sedimenting, like roads crossing and extending. Yet traffic has density and sparseness. Containers have size. Thus Sedimentation-Field toward Information — also possesses its limits of capacity. Like water filling basin — it possesses volume. Thus Domain-Count² may be measured against field-body volume, and thereby comes the name: Volume-Ratio. Within same field-body — the quantity of Domain-Count depends upon differences of sequence, frequency, intensity, and density. These four — are called: the Four Capacity Principles.

What is sequence? The ordering by which many carts enter city. What is frequency? The number passing one checkpoint. What is intensity? The ease or hardship by which pathway is traveled. What is density? The quantity of roads, the multitude of crossings. By these four — one may know field capacity.

When man passes forty — his field gradually hardens like iron. The Four Principles of the brain can no longer hold difference. Thus little may newly enter. Thereby the mind grows obdurate — and transformation ceases.

Wind does not blow in one line. That which answers wind — likewise not in one line. Signals coming and going — their movement resembles wave striking shore — rolling before and after, pressing left and right. Later wave arrives — yet former wave not yet gone. All strike shore together. All are received. Thus sediment continuously layers upon shore. Therefore Self-Manifesting-Sedimentation-Field possesses Superposition Nature. Further — the Four Principles wherein Information dwells — must share one bed yet differ in imagery, share one soil yet grow together — like painting laid layer upon watercolor. Thus another nature belongs unto it: Symbiotic Nature. These two natures — are root of imagery sedimentation within Self-Manifesting-Sedimentation-Field.

Flow arrives. Flow arrives thereafter. Flow arrives after what came thereafter. Flow arrives after what came after what came thereafter. Thus gathering comes — and waiting appears. Joining comes — and emergence follows. This is Latency Nature. It is one foundational nature of signal-flow within Sedimentation-Field. To obtain image — one must tune form. Like adjusting microscope — until form appears. Like adjusting kaleidoscope — until imagery forms. Through tuning — shape is obtained. Those who tune well — realize shape well. So too with tuning within Sedimentation-Field. Tuning gathers signals into one domain. Impedes surrounding flow. Suppresses this. Removes that. Thus imagery comes into form. I know not how best to describe it. Forcing a name upon it — I call it: Impedance Nature. This is the second foundational nature of signal-flow within Sedimentation-Field. These two natures operate throughout sediment. They are foundational movement-nature of signal within Self-Manifesting-Sedimentation-Field.

Signals sediment into structure. They drive Information-Realm as though bearing mandate. There is continuity. There is focus. There is pulling. These three — are cage unto all things in the world.

Within it — world is born. Within it — things transform. Within it — my form and likeness arise. Thus Sedimentation-Field — cannot go unexamined, cannot go unscrutinized, cannot remain unknown.

Profound and clear — thus tendency, thus domain, come into being. Vast and boundless — as though something ordains.
