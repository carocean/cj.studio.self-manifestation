# The Lodging-Vessel

If the skin is not, where could the hair attach? The Sedimentation-Field, that on which life depends and by which public and private divide, must be locked away and stored, so as not to be shared. Its material too must be stored, and reality stored within it, clinging to doors and windows and peering outward in stealth, and thus able to behold all under heaven. That storehouse is called the skull. This is one lodging.

Its sensing, its motion, its knowing, wandering within physics, make its reality real and mistakenly take it for all under heaven, private within yet taken as outside. This is truly not a thing of the present world. Yet it can walk, having body and character, joining with worldly things as carrier and lodging within the carrier, and taking the carrier as its form, like the hermit crab privatizing its shell. It names it “I,” not “it.” Thus it is written as the body. This is the second lodging.

Skull and body together are called the Lodging-Vessel, briefly called the vessel. Within the Lodging-Vessel are the viscera. Are there not engines? They are called heart, spleen, lungs, intestines. That by which things enter and leave—are there not doors and windows? They are called skin, eyes, nose, mouth. Outside are the limbs. Are there not wheels and mechanisms? They are called hands, legs, feet. Viscera, sense-organs, and limbs together are called instruments.

Entering and leaving the lodging-instruments through gates without form, these gates are able, by responding to physics, to make it one, transforming it into pulses that travel within sediment. These gates are the portals guarded by the Information-Realm. Their kinds are two. One is the Sensory Gate, able to transform response into pulses and admit it as one. The second is the Effect Gate, able to transform pulses into effect and thereby drive the vessel-instruments.

That which can command the vessel-instruments and travel through the lodging-world, birds flying, beasts running, leaping up and down, gesture and feeling suiting intent, entering and leaving through the two gates and receiving what the Sedimentation-Field ordains—all such moving capacities are called the Actuated Mechanism.

Vessel-instruments belong to the Actuated Mechanism.

The joining of the sensory and effect gates is like water and mud blending together. The denser their mutual interpenetration, the finer their sensing and control, clear as the prick of a fine needle upon the skin. Thus its nature is named Fusion.

Whenever things respond to one another and can become one—what is meant by one? The reflected light of the myriad things cannot become one; therefore the colors red, green, and blue are not one. Yet the electrical conduction of metal can become one; therefore signals need not be one.

The Sedimentation-Field flows in surges and forms deposits, at times surging along surfaces, at times surging along lines, at times gathering, at times dividing. Where it travels much it is deep; where it travels little it is faint. Is the surging only electromagnetic? Electromagnetism cannot be without attachment; there must be that which responds to it. One thing responds before and causes what follows to answer after it; the latter in turn causes what follows after that to answer after it. Thus front goes forth and rear succeeds, like water passing through sand and forming the way of water. This is called conduction. The before-and-after succession, bearing and answering one another, is called two-phase interaction. Modern physics uses “force” to refer to such response. Thus the conduction of the neural Sedimentation-Field, CJ treats in terms of electromagnetism.

Yet though all know that electromagnetism can conduct, who has heard that the Other can conduct? A computer can finish computing a, not go on to compute b, yet go on to compute c. From this I know that there is conduction of the Other. This is the conduction of logic. Principle too can conduct. From this one knows that computation too may be a Sedimentation-Field.

Conduction is response responding to response, thereby forming its chain. Conduction gives rise to traces; constant passage forms a path. A path is trace responding to trace responding to trace, thereby forming its chain. Thus where there is conduction there is path, and within it signals pass one to another.

Electromagnetism is the messenger of the world. How different it is from the things that dwell in the world. At its utmost refinement, its conduction leaps and jumps between the two phases. Each phase has its threshold and barrier; it does not pass them and does not overleap. The front abides in potential and passes to the rear; the rear again potentials what follows after it. Seen from afar, it appears a flow without break. Therefore I know that flow itself does not exist as solid being. Between front and rear, it is like a frail rabbit that does not run, but makes its course by hopping. This accords precisely with the nature of logic. The Sedimentation-Field in which our world lodges—is logic its ruler?

The Sensory Gate responds to the outside and is able to make it one. As one, it enters the Receptive-Field, and afterward enters the various fields. Therefore one says: Sensory Gate → Receptive-Field → Imagery-Field → Experience-Field. This is called the sensory channel. The Effect Gate is able to transform the one and drive what is outside. As one, it goes out from the Effect-Field, and afterward enters the various vessel-instruments. Therefore one says: Effect-Field → Effect Gate. This is called the effect channel.

As for adding or subtracting its instruments, how would that be? Could it be like adding or subtracting the legs of a chair? Push it back to the beginning: when added, it is not taken as being there, because there is no channel by which to sense its presence. One must wait until the channel arises of itself, and only then know it is there. When subtracted, it is not taken as gone, because its channel still remains; therefore its instrument is still phantom-held as present. One must wait until its channel dissolves of itself, and only then know it is absent.

Birds emerge from the shell and can eat. Beasts at first birth can stand. Is this the instinct of their instruments? Or what they learned within egg and womb? Within their channels there must be pre-setting. Some perhaps inherit sediment from the father; some inherit more, some less, some none.

All know flesh as body and seek likeness there—how blocked they are. I may take the Earth as body and roam the galaxy; take the galaxy as body and roam star-clusters; take realms as body and roam higher realms. I roam the extreme vast, yet know not that I remain enclosed within a world. I may take the molecule as body and roam within protein; take the electron as body and roam within atom; take the quark as body and roam the strange minute. I roam the extreme small, yet know not that I remain enclosed within a world. Therefore I take the world as body and walk within the lodging-world. I open my eyes—within the lodging-world, within the field, there I am.

The Effect-Field pivots forth the Lodging-Vessel, entering and leaving through the two gates. It may pivot into the fleshly body, to taste the salt and bitterness of the human world; pivot into the computer, to taste the flavor of symbols; pivot into galaxies and subtle realms, to behold what is exquisite and strange; pivot into electric light, to drive distant viscera and limbs in service; pivot into Hubble, to gaze at near and far outer space; pivot into the microscopic instrument, to look upon the livelihood of minute beings; pivot into the lithography machine, to control the extremities of atoms; pivot into the energy-field, to enter the nothing and see the being within; pivot into the present world, roaming within the lodging-world.

These words are no delusion. What the pivot can command need not be eyes, ears, hands, or feet. The Self-Manifesting Being can privatize external things and make them its vessel-instruments. Thus it can privatize that private which it could not otherwise reach. Privatizing flesh as body—sensing it, using it, scratching it, itching it—one does not feel that one is driving that body. It is no different from driving another thing.

Public and private have no boundary in themselves; in substance they are not different. The two channels join them and make them so. Then what is made private, and what may not become private? O chisel, could you become a Self-Manifesting Being? With blade as beak, could you delight in chiseling wood? Between you and the woodpecker, which delights in it more?

I shall carry my Sedimentation-Field and store it in a vessel. Let instruments communicate with me. My legs shall be the Moon, my hands the Earth, my eyes the Sun, my ears the computer. I am about to roam through space-time; my mouth is devouring comets as food. Listen—my ears are now hearing symbol-tensors as sound. By what name shall I call myself? I and the Lodging-Vessel disperse through the great void.

I dwell in the lodging-world, observing the field within. I dwell within, observing the world of lodging. How could I cast off these vessel-instruments? How could I leave the sediment of the field that carries me? My pain is to know that I am enclosed within the self-reference of manifestness.

Alas! If I could privatize all that I can privatize, how could I privatize this world as my lodging-instrument? I open my eyes to see, raise my claw to scratch. Within the lodging-world I remain a Self-Manifesting Being. Ah! Perhaps the lodging-world too has its own lodging-world. I know not how to step out of my privateness.
