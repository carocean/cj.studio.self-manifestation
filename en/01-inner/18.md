# Observation of Observation

A play within a play—  
outside the stage—one observes.  
That which is outside—by it, the play is known.

Zhuang Zhou dreams he is a butterfly;  
the butterfly dreams it is Zhou—  
back and forth, unknown how many layers.  
Seeking “I,” no place of belonging, no place of return.

Observing my being in seeing,  
observing my being in hearing,  
observing my being in tasting,  
observing my being in thinking,  
observing my emotion turning within—  
this is observing—observing at the place where I observe.  
To know that what I now enact  
is observed at the place of observing—  
this—Observation of Observation.

Observation of Observation—  
self that moves back observes the self upon the stage.  
As watching a play—I act;  
as watching a dream—I perform.  
When one would seize it, the “I” has already moved behind the seizing,  
entering the scene of observing myself seize myself.  
Thus self moved back becomes Observation of Observation—  
and cannot be seized.

To observe at the place of observing is easy:  
once the Observation-Domain opens, it comes of itself.  
But Observation of Observation is not easy.  
At times one seeks it in stillness and does not obtain it;  
at times it comes unbidden.  
Often, amid deep thought and a hundred considerations,  
it suddenly stands far from me—  
seeing my unfolding actions before me.  
If one would hold it, effort does not cease—  
and it falls again to the front stage.  
Observation of the rear stage is swift;  
Observation of Observation is laborious.  
A slight slackening—and it returns.  
Its return—at times to the unfolding steps,  
at times to what is desired.

The rear stage observing the front stage—  
choosing among front-stage states and entering them—  
this is the use of Observation of Observation.  
The deeper stage observing the rear stage observing the front stage—  
choosing among those observations and entering them—  
this is the use of Observation of Observation of Observation.  
Thus the use of Observation of Observation:  
it departs from reality for a moment,  
examines what I belong to,  
determines what I enact,  
and returns again to reality.  
This is stepping out of desire—to know myself.

Front stage, rear stage, deeper stage—  
there may be an end to the later selves,  
where their force is exhausted.  
The later selves recede in succession;  
the earlier self returns swiftly.  
Thus it is seen: the later self exhausts its sharpness.

To know myself and to verify myself are not the same.  
Verification—tracing where I am,  
falling upon the sense that mind is here, body is here,  
and only then knowing “this is I”—  
this is sensing my being.  
To know myself—observing what I am enacting,  
seeing myself upon the stage—  
thus action reveals knowing.  
This is the distinction.

Why can the manifestation-circuit make manifestness?  
It mistakes the outside as having “being.”  
But who makes this “mistake”?  
The convergence-impedance of tuning under Intent’s command.  
By this, the “mistaking” is made—thus “being” arises.  
“Being” must have its source;  
it is driven by that which gives rise to it.  
That source is: the convergence-impedance of tuning under Intent’s command.  
This convergence occupies a single position within Intent;  
thus “being” belongs to the current unique position.  
Thus this unique position gathers the present into one—  
and from this, “I” emerges.

Therefore, being and I arise together;  
I and being come forth together.

If position is not one, then “being” is not one.  
If each being returns to its own one,  
then multiple I arise among them.  
With multiple I, Intent and tuning contend for use,  
and action cannot be unified.  
Yet though there be many I, each still holds a single position.

Thus one substitutes another I for one’s private self,  
and steps back to observe one’s own private self.  
That which cannot be stepped back from  
cannot reveal the relation between I and my privateness.

The manifestation-circuits gather in the Observation-Domain;  
Imagery moves within.  
Gaze serves convergence and roams beneath.  
I move within the Imagery-Realm;  
thus reality has its belonging.

If one constantly tunes the subtle  
and lets Gaze rest upon the Observation-Domain,  
over time a field of Observation of Observation arises.  
Fine strands of tuning gather and enter this field;  
Intent pulses pass along tuning lines and excite it.  
Passing through the Imagery-Field,  
reaching the Experience-Field,  
biased-flow goes to the Intent-Field,  
appointing the Experience-Target,  
issuing through the Boundary-Gate into the Intent-domain.  
Thus the circuit of Observation of Observation is formed  
and enters the Observation-Domain.  
Since convergence of tuning gathers upon this circuit,  
Imagery of “I am here” arises,  
gradually sedimenting within its Imagery-Field,  
becoming clear upon reuse.

At this point, within the Observation-Domain—“I am acting.”  
Within it, the Observation of Observation circuit is again gathered by Gaze,  
producing “I am here.”  
Thus the Observation-Domain aligns into one—  
forming “I observe myself acting.”  
Here, the condition of Observation of Observation appears.

Gaze rests upon the Observation-Domain—  
not taking the sensory field as input,  
but proceeding in parallel and then seeking alignment.  
This is the way of nature—simple, profound in use.

By this way, I am born;  
by this way, observing is born;  
by this way, Observation of Observation is born.  
This is the child of natural transformation—  
simple as a single stroke,  
yet giving rise to the thriving of reality,  
coloring within it,  
and granting me the power to observe myself.  
How exquisite this work.
