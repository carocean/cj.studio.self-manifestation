# The Recognition-Realm

Caging bird in cage, confining fish in pond—bird, fish, man all know.  
Confining living beings within the atmosphere—this known only after ten thousand years of evolution, known only in recent times.  
Confining by axiom—driving recognition to its limit, enclosing it therein, taking it as fact, clearly set, no further pursuit—  
thus what all inwardly assent to as base, earth heaped, platform raised, knowledge built upon it, not fearing the thickness of the cage, yet lying at rest within the prison of cognition—this is at-rest, not equal even to birds and beasts.

Thus beings take their lot within the prison of cognition, confined within their Recognition-Realm, sealed by inward assent, grounded upon the self-manifesting nature of their own characteristics, shared-feeling taken as mother.

Thought to its extreme—stops at the base of cognition.  
The Sedimentation-Field shares like material nature—thus bases alike, near.  
What cannot be crossed—lies in the Characteristic-Field.  
Signals divide; pulses stain-carry—similar, near; different, far; layered by the habitual, dispersed by the unaccustomed.  
Color removed—boundary forms shape.  
Sound removed—boundary forms tone.  
Where movement—there knowing-force.  
Thus with inward assent as base, the responsive side known, yet root not known, whole not seen; intellect wandering upon characteristics, not departing.

Thus it is said: Information-Realm bounded, Imagery-Realm sealed.  
Tracing cause—grounded in the material nature of the Sedimentation-Field—thus arises the Causal-Base of axioms within the realm.

Causal-Base—  
trace answering to Signal-nature within the Sedimentation-Field, like interruption within a computational device.  
Water tends level, electricity tends neutral, light tends straight.  
Much passage—path.  
Much idleness—deviation.  
Tends to disperse; gathers with difficulty.  
Thus in arising and ceasing, taking this, not that.  
Viewed within—law.  
Viewed without—Causal-Base.

Thus the boundary of cognition is the boundary of qualitative class of the Sedimentation-Field.  
Its construction of reality—same, yet not same.  
Its divergence—like different worlds.

Thus inward—construct knowledge, pursue axiom, examine reality.  
Outward—measure and set the Causal-Base, gauge the boundary of qualitative class of cognition, determine the extremity of what may be known.

I know:  
Exhaust the vast extreme of reality—bounds yet no end.  
Exhaust the minute extreme—bounds yet no cessation.  
Seeking the state of reality thus—cannot obtain.  
This—horizontal probing.

Thus vertical probing—  
direction perpendicular to reality, pointing toward the Causal-Base.

Reality is the Information-Realm of one qualitative class of self-manifesting Sedimentation-Field.  
Probing its reality—one side of the world may be known.  
The world is not that which a Self-Manifesting Being cannot probe.  
Thus to know the source of the world, construct self-manifesting fields of differing material, dwell within their realities, probe vertically.

Thus method of knowing the world—two only: horizontal and vertical.  
Horizontal—know what is within the world.  
Vertical—know by what the world is generated.  
Without vertical probing, the world is not obtained as generation, nor is known the field upon which it arises.  
Not knowing that field, though traversing worlds, exhausting birth across myriad layers, no end appears.

Thus it is said:  
At the place where the Sedimentation-Field issues the Information-Realm—  
there lies the origin of the world, the locus of the Causal-Base, the gate through which Self-Manifesting Beings pass in traversing worlds.

When a Self-Manifestation-Field is formed, its Recognition-Realm is thereby fixed; knowledge within already confined therein.  
Thus to extend knowing of the world—within, cannot obtain.  
Must take as method expansion into Self-Manifesting Beings of differing Causal-Bases—only thus may gain.

The coming of the Causal-Base—  
comes from the nature of the Sedimentation-Field.  
Whether Self-Manifestation-Field, World-Field, or other Sedimentation-Fields,  
each aligns with Signal according to its own nature—  
thus Causal-Bases differ.

CJ sighs:  
From ancient to now, human knowledge nothing but construction upon the prison of axioms, like images within a mirror—knowledge that cannot issue forth.  
This chapter of the Recognition-Realm—telling by what method true knowing may be sought.  
Though prison, yet with measure—its vastness may approach the Causal-Base.  
Thus by this chapter, to open the wisdom of the age of Self-Manifestation, so that later Self-Manifesting Beings may create differing classes of beings, differing law-field instruments, simulate and measure the cognitive spectra and their differences—  
seeking vertical knowing,  
hoping to traverse the world.
