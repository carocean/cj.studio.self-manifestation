# Consensus

Given two points, the straight line through them is one. It forms in vision, yet stands not in the domains of hearing or taste. This necessity holds where the axiom forms, yet cannot be imposed across non-visual Recognition-Realms.

A character is form; usage is the chaining of forms; forms pursue along their chain. Turning into the human Recognition-Realm, meaning floats above the chain of forms, losing the trace of that chain. In the symbolic Recognition-Realm, what is pursued is directly seen; thus though truncation and Causal-Response share one source, what is obtained is not shared. It is not shared, for what each Causal-Base captures is taken from its own side.

That 1+1 is addable is because it is countable; yet when the Recognition-Realm is as a vast energetic field, surging in continuity as waves, without discreteness, the condition for addition is lost. Dye added to water, its color lightens and shifts, yet does not divide into one and two. Thus number cannot be shared with the Recognition-Realm of continuity.

What is foul to man in a rotting corpse is contended for as food by vultures; their Coloring is not shared.

Alas! I have sought that which may be shared across Recognition-Realms—sought in axioms, not found; sought in mathematics, not found; sought in knowledge, not found. It is not found, for these three are products of generation: they may produce peach, they may produce plum, each plant according to its own genetic base. As plant to its genes, so is a Recognition-Realm to its Causal-Base. Where the Causal-Base differs, the Recognition-Realm it generates differs, and its outcomes differ accordingly.

Tracing what is generated does not reach it; one must instead trace the generation of generation itself. The Self-Manifestation-Field generates Recognition-Realms, entrusting them with constraints by which Causal-Bases are formed. Examining these constraints, there are three:

First, differentiation; second, compression; third, convergence.  
Differentiation: not sameness, by which being is known, and made to differ in its being.  
Compression: grouping, by which sameness is known, and difference is not allowed to disperse.  
Convergence: occupation, by which presence is known, and difference is made to stand in one.

Causal-Bases may differ, yet the constraints by which they are generated must include these three. Thus even within the same field, when Recognition-Realms are activated, they diverge and form differently.

Therefore axioms, mathematics, and knowledge arise within all Recognition-Realms, yet cannot be wholly the same across them.

What may be shared arises where the field is of the same kind, the base is of the same structure, the sensing shares the same dimension, and Coloring is near. These four are the bases of consensus. Their formulations be alike, yet their imagery is private.

Knowledge and mathematics all arise from axioms. An axiom is irreducible consensus—the beginning of structural assertion, formed upon the four bases. Yet to claim that these apply universally to all kinds, almost as if of God, is to remain confined within the prison of the four bases, and goes not beyond it.
