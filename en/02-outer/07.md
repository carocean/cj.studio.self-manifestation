# Information-Transformation

The Sedimentation-Field pivots forth the Information-Realm.  
Placed beyond observation, it is taken as thing; placed within observation, it is taken as imagery.  
All observers abide in the Information-Realm, are of it, yet take themselves as standing in the field without—this is called Situated-Observation.  
The domain of Situated-Observation is called the Imagery-Realm, or the Self-Manifestation-Realm, briefly the Image-Realm.  
That which is not this, yet can self-propagate and self-transform, is called the World.  
That which is not this, and all return to fixed bounds, is called the Fixed-Realm.

Thus the Information-Realm has three: one the World, two the Imagery-Realm, three the Fixed-Realm.  
Within one without two is called the Mute-Domain; within one with two is called the Manifest-Domain.  
Within two there must be no one-two; if there is, each returns to its own realm.  
Thus the Self-Manifestation-Realm—this is its ownness.

That which is called the Fixed-Realm is not that which can self-propagate, but that which pre-sets what it may do, stepping its Principle in sequence so as to make it able, not binding what is already formed; therefore within it there is no self-transformation of one-two.

Therefore the Sedimentation-Field pivots forth the Information-Realm; this is its real: transforming things and giving rise to information.  
It can transform the host into information; therefore it is called Information-Transformation.  
Information-Transformation is the study of the generation of the Information-Realm, the pivot by which the myriad things are set forth.

Carrier-flow and field, by Causal-Response, give rise to signal.  
The field, as it runs in the Information-Realm, is but the transformation of signal; the transformation of signal enters sediment and is regulated therein.

Regulated and formed, there arises the Causal-Base; the Causal-Base gives rise to Principle; Principle gives rise to Original-Pulse.  
The Causal-Base is that which is near to Execution, joining the parts of the field and making them able, issuing from the Execution of the many mechanisms, driving Principle to proceed; it is the base of causes within the realm, therefore it is called the Causal-Base.

The Causal-Base is neither thing nor information; it abides between Execution and Principle, being the beginning that pivots the layers of the Information-Realm.  
Within the realm Execution is not seen; the Causal-Base is taken as the driver.  
It binds the structure of Execution and proceeds under the primal ordinance; Principle takes it as host-layer, thereby letting Principle generate and extinguish the fate of the Information-Realm.  
Thus the Causal-Base is the master of “this and not that” within the Information-Realm.  
Yet many Information-Realms may share one base.
