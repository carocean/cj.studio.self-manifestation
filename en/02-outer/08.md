# Object-Transformation

To know object, first know information.

Information: cause and Causal-Response. The chain of Causal-Response unfolds; therein—Original-Having. Discernment halts at Original-Having, sees not what is prior; tracing Causal-Response to Original-Having, stopping where tracing ends—thus object, Original-Having its beginning.

That which is placed beyond observation: object. Its without: the World; its within: imagery. Imagery—object.

The coming of signal: Causal-Response. Carried, flowed—by electromagnetism, by ions, by constraint—its surge traces upon sediment as Trace-Response. Thus object-real not object-real, but Trace-Response manifest.

Within the world, Information-Transformation makes object: transforming Causal-Response, forming object; thus information transforming into object—Response-Transformation. This is one of Object-Transformation.

In observation—presence; inner imagery private, yet as outer existence—inner domain objectified in place of the outer: External-Transformation. Latent body raised as present; from self, object; from object, self—In-Situ Transformation. External-Transformation is the base of In-Situ Transformation; thus In-Situ Transformation and External-Transformation together make the second of Object-Transformation.

As for the Self-Manifesting entity: beyond observed—object named; yet this is the existence of itself, and inquiry within the world. Thus it has: the capacity to objectify the world, and the function of privately manifesting its own domain.
