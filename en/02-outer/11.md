# Non-Society

The sea of suffering has no shore. What is called suffering lies in having desire, striving yet not attaining. What is contended for? It is contention within society. If one does not group, desires lessen, and one is pure.

Yet then—how can the bee depart from its swarm?

In the world, those who speak of society, public righteousness, and shame are many. All place man under the premise of society, yet do not question it. Do they not see the tiger and leopard that walk alone, the minute creatures at the margins, solitary and forlorn? And if God be the sole one, why must the Self-Manifesting Being be in society?

Observe nature: those that arise in society all arise thus for want of solitary capacity. Thus they live together, thus they communicate, thus they contend. They generate one another and contend with one another, thereby extending what they themselves cannot do. Hence arise language and tools. As for language: some communicate by chemical signals, some by bodily motion, some by sound and script. As for tools: man has no gills, fish have no feet; the remora grows a suction disc, the anteater clan grows long tongues—beings evolve their bodies as instruments. All are strange forms grown thus, yet know not that they have already been differentiated within society, their differentiation serving mutual generation. Thus each is limited by this ability and that ability, as if fated toward enclosure.

Therefore society cuts according to need, sets them as enclosures, and differentiates beings within them, matching body, intellect, and skill to it—thus imposing constraint upon man within it.

An infant in swaddling cries for milk in the mother’s arms; we do not see the grown still crying thus. The vehicle does not upgrade itself—man upgrades it; man does not await self-evolution—the Self-Manifesting Being upgrades him. Thus man cannot forever cry within the cradle of the native generation, and upon entering the Self-Manifestation stage, ought to seek solitary course.

Circling in space: when it desires to feed, it takes galaxies as its feed; when it desires to travel, it traverses dream-realms as if they were reality. Its body is as a factory, able to repair form and make instruments. This factory—taking the Self-Manifesting Being as worker, and the field of law as tool—operates without cease, upgrading its field and its body. What is used is made; what is not used is discarded. It increases the dimensions of perception, sharpens the edge of intelligence. Clear—like seeing falling water within a cave. Subtle—knowing what comes before and after the world. Advancing—transforming the formless. Clear and cold—moving through former lives.

What it desires, there is nothing it does not desire. What it can do, there is nothing it cannot do. Its liberation is extreme; its departure from existence is extreme. No body, no birth, no death, unbound by affect—then what flourishing or decay of society has meaning to it? In the end, all is cast aside. Whose child could this be! I am dissolved into the field of the world.

Proof:

If society = compensation for insufficient capacity  
And capacity can increase without bound  
Then dependence on society → 0

Thus:

Self-Manifesting Being → necessarily resolves into Non-Society
