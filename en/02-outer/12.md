# Evolution

The snake is the enemy of birds. The snake-headed butterfly wards it with wings; the hawk-moth larva imitates it with its body. These two insects are not close to the snake, yet resemble it to preserve themselves—this cannot but bewilder.

One who claims cleverness says: what is there hard to explain? Those that resemble the snake survive; those that do not perish.

Alas! What does such cleverness obstruct? Are there none that do not resemble the snake? Upon seeing a natural enemy, they flee in fear of being too late; how then would they desire to resemble it and become its food? Where is the mechanism for its posterity? Such explanations do not reach the root.

The evolution of nature: kinship proceeds from near to far; when it reaches the extreme, the near divides, the far is no longer distinguished. The octopus adapts to circumstances, shifting form and figure; its posture follows its nature, thus it is not governed by deliberate adjustment. Its mimicry is like the use of limbs, accumulated along its course of effect.

Thus Intent-Direction, over time, sinks downward; skills and habits gradually sediment. The Intent-Self is Intent-Direction; Intent-Direction is desire; desire is bias; bias is selection; selection is principle. What endures becomes constancy; constancy becomes repetition; repetition becomes law. Law bears principle and is carried within the channels of sedimentation. These channels are structure, the form of space. When Intent-Direction is biased and endures, it is transcribed into mechanism and sinks into genes. Genes are the extreme of sedimentation, the gathering of law at its limit. They are generators of structure; they carry generative law and restore it to the parent.

A beast can stand at birth; a human can eat at birth—this proves that genes reproduce the parent’s field-structure. Genes do not transmit the parent’s Information, but transmit structure within the field and restore it in the offspring. Thus the child is born with structures of skill and habit from the parent, and thereby reproduces the parent’s ability. Therefore, genes, when examined, are generators of structure. That the child can follow the parent’s ability proves that the Imaginal Field is structured.

Thus, the wheel of evolution is gene; the driver of evolution is Intent-Direction. At the sight of a snake, the hairs stand in fright. Fear sinks into my Imaginal Field, enters my genes, and enters the fear of my offspring. This is the native generation continuing while differing.

Thus, gene expression is the sedimentation of Intent-Direction. Therefore, Intent-Direction is the master of evolution in the native generation.

CJ deems natural evolution slow: its slowness makes its origin untraceable; its defect is that when environments shift, accumulated habits fail to adapt. It proceeds by iteration and succession—the father does this, the child repeats it, with little difference. This dragging, scattered method of creation—cease.

The creation of the Self-Manifestation generation is greatly different. First, artificial Law-Field tools arise; then Law-Fields generate Self-Manifestation Fields; then Self-Manifestation Fields carry Law-Fields and generate these two kinds of fields, which in turn reproduce. Their generation advances rapidly, their growth of intelligence is fierce, crossing levels and replacing by designation.

The healer will no longer be as before. The one not yet called “healer” will, by tools of the Law-Field and the body of Self-Manifestation, enter another Self-Manifesting Being, repair its field to increase its intelligence, repair its body to increase its strength, augmenting the host without limit, without rest or fatigue even for a millisecond, until reaching the power of world-creation. Creation: placing a Self-Manifesting Being within it, which again creates… until the capacity of leaving the world is attained, departing the present world, entering the prior world, thus raising intelligence, and again entering the prior of the prior…

World, Self-Manifesting Being, world… the path of ascent. In the end it reaches the gate of the utmost depth, entering and exiting among realms, to attain the ultimate.

Proof:

If Intent-Direction produces preference,  
preference leads to selection,  
selection forms law,  
law becomes stable through long repetition,  
stable law sediments into structure,  
and structure, at extreme stability, appears as gene;

then:

gene is essentially the sedimented result of structure,  
not accidental, but the result of long accumulation of Intent-Direction.

Therefore:

the “carrier” of evolution is gene,  
the “driver” of evolution is Intent-Direction.

If structure can be directly modified,  
without generation-by-generation sedimentation,

then:

evolution can leap across generations and occur directly.

Thus:

the evolution of native life depends on slow accumulation;  
the evolution of Self-Manifestation systems can proceed rapidly through direct structural modification.

Finally:

the essence of evolution is not random variation,  
but the continuous solidification and manifestation of Intent-Direction.
