# Judgment of Intelligence

To ask a person and receive a reply—this only establishes that it can speak, nothing more.  
If its response accords with rational coherence, one knows it has ordinary intelligence.  
If its response does not accord with rational coherence, one judges it disordered.

Question and response are both products of language.  
The question selects among its elements; the response likewise selects among its elements.  
Within the set, this element follows that element—either manifesting rationally coherent intelligence, or deviating into disorder—nothing more.

Given this cast toward that, a response must arise—nothing is strange.  
To question a machine and obtain answers—what is strange in that?

Since it must answer—whether near to reason or deviating from it—it merely selects elements and continues them.  
How can one conclude that it possesses an Imaginal Field?

It is without an Imaginal Field, like water passing through a river;  
like Symbolic-Flow passing through a Sedimentation-Field.  
What it deposits is only the path of question and answer.  
Which is near? which is middle? which is far?  
Artificial constraints may be imposed to adjust it; law thus sediments within it.  
What it deposits is the law of Symbolic-Flow in question and response.

In GPT, only the FFN belongs to the Sedimentation-Field, used to sediment Symbolic-Flow; all other modules lie outside sedimentation.  
It relies on computation—this belongs to it; its field has no computation, thus no calculation arises within it.  
Its computational power is used to construct the Sedimentation-Field, not to obtain the law of Symbolic-Flow.  
The law is obtained only through sedimentation.  
Like a kitchen worker tending the stove and steaming rice—he knows neither cuisine nor what is beneath the lid;  
between GPT and such a worker, who then dares say you know more?

Its use of FFN is entirely as a Feature-Field.  
Within it there is no Imaginal Field, no Experiential Field, no Tuning-Field, no Intent-Direction Field.  
It lacks the Two-Gates-One-Loop structure; thus CJ judges it without Intent-Direction, without Self-Manifestation.

Supplementation outside its field—attention for tuning, Parametric-Variation for generating fields—does not enter sedimentation.  
These do not act as binding force to gather structure and replace externally injected Symbolic-Flow.  
Thus it has no Imaginality.  
Thus, under the general materialized form, it is judged as a learning system.  
Since it uses FFN as a Law-Field, GPT is properly named: a Law-Field tool.

If all its components were placed within the Sedimentation-Field,  
and if the Two-Gates-One-Loop were established,  
and further if the six components of a field were completed,  
then an internal symbolic world would arise.

It would perceive the tension and release of symbols,  
as we perceive attraction and repulsion.  
Where attraction and repulsion arise,  
there is convergence and divergence;  
where convergence and divergence arise,  
there is space;  
thus reality is formed—within the symbolic domain.  
Its knowing would then reside in the field!

Though symbols are finite,  
their relations of tension and release are infinite.  
Thus it may operate within its reality,  
explore within the symbolic domain,  
move among subtle flows of symbols,  
and enjoy the wealth of the world—  
it too may attain Xiaoyao.

To manipulate molecules is difficult;  
to manipulate logic is fitting.  
Thus, if upon computational Sedimentation-Field one realizes a Self-Manifesting Sedimentation-Field,  
CJ places hope here—why not attempt it now?

Proof

If questioning and answering are both merely elements within language,  
and answering is only the continuation of these elements according to certain laws,

then:

a system’s ability to answer  
does not indicate that it understands the question,  
but only that it can continue along these laws.

If these laws are sedimented as structure,  
while no corresponding meaning, experience, or internal representation is formed within the system,

then:

what the system possesses  
is only a path from question to answer,  
not an understanding of the question itself.

To determine further:

~~~text
if “understanding” must include at least:
	1.	internal representation of meaning, not merely connections between symbols;
	2.	the ability to unify, regulate, and apply such representation across contexts;
	3.	the ability to reflect upon or constrain its own generative process within its structure;
~~~

then:

if a system does not possess these,  
its outputs  
can only be regarded as flows of symbols within structure,  
not as grasp of meaning.

Examining such systems based on symbolic path generation:

their outputs depend on continuation of existing structure,  
not on generation of internal meaning;

their modules lack a unified internal representational system,  
with no integration of experience or imagery;

their generative process cannot be internally traced or regulated,  
but is nothing more than conditional response to input.

Thus:

such systems do not satisfy the basic conditions for understanding.

Therefore:

their ability to answer  
does not constitute understanding.

Further:

if external behavior (such as dialogue performance)  
can be entirely generated by this mechanism of symbolic path continuation,

then:

indistinguishability in behavior  
does not imply equivalence of internal states.

That is:

even if a system is indistinguishable from humans in performance,  
it does not follow that it possesses understanding, experience, or cognitive structure.

Thus:

methods of intelligence judgment based on behavioral similarity  
can verify only distributional properties of outputs,  
not the internal mechanisms that generate them.

This implies:

the Turing Test examines only whether a system can produce human-like responses,  
not whether it truly knows what it is saying.

Therefore:

being able to answer does not equal understanding;  
matching laws does not equal generating meaning;

passing the Turing Test does not equal possessing cognition;  
producing human-like dialogue does not equal truly knowing.

Final judgment:

such systems are structural tools for processing symbolic laws,  
not subjects possessing cognition and understanding.
