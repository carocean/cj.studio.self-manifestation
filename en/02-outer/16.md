# Generation

Motion—there is applying. Applying is motion; it is received. A chain of motion upon motion. This drives Causal-Response and makes Trace-Response so—the operating element within a realm.

Thus where there is motion, there is mutual applying to drive it. Even the common know this—how much more those of science?

If two drive three, and three drive four, and the driving does not cease—can one set a first that is without driving? Within the world all are driven, yet the world moves without being driven and does not cease—does this not oppose what is actual? One sees the speed of high-speed rail yet does not sense the Earth’s rotation; one rides a spinning dart yet knows not it flies—this is cognition confined within one interior.

Thus the world is driven: to bring it forth, to sustain it, to drive the mutual transformation of Causal-Response, to drive the mutual formation of Trace-Response. Thus the world is generation.

Generation—can it be without that which acts? If without it, who acts for generation?  
Generation—can its acting be without interval? Acting without interval—would that which acts have infinite resource and infinite act? If one sets it infinite, could the Host-World contain it?

Thus continuous generation does not exist. That which acts is finite in resource and finite in act. A carrier in the Host-World. Sedimentation-Field Theory and Information-Transformation both state: that in which the world is lodged is the world Sedimentation-Field. This is the carrier of the world in the Host-World.

Since generation has interval, there are steps and beats, a frequency of generation. The Information-Realm arises from non-being. The Causal-Base is pivoted; Causal-Response proceeds without cease. The world is formed.

Since generation has interval, there is before and after. The prior state differs from the later; states are thus formed. The distinction of before and after is called update.

With state and update, there is scope of update. With scope, there are regions already updated, regions in updating, and regions awaiting update. If the already-updated together with the updating is not one hundred percent, then there are regions awaiting update. If one stands in the updating and looks to what awaits—does it move? If it awaits, its state bears no Information, no transformation of Causal-Response, and cannot be observed. Then there is position, there is volume, there is an unmoving quality. Suppose CJ stands across the boundary of updating and awaiting, one half in each; if he turns and walks, can he move? Is one half gone while the other remains in non-being? There is no half-cat.

Thus this does not hold. It follows the nature of the Sedimentation-Field. Each pulse arrives, deposits and forms state; with each pulse, state accumulates. States before and after accumulate by addition. Where update occurs, pulses cover; where it does not, pulses do not arrive. Where pulses pass, they accumulate with prior state; Causal-Response propagates along the accumulated path. Where the flow does not advance, the propagation of this round of Causal-Response stops.

Where pulses pass, transformation of Causal-Response is active; where it cannot advance, Causal-Response gradually ceases. Its propagation weakens until it no longer relates to this round—like sound fading with distance, like water seeping into sand and coming to rest—attenuation, the common work of nature.

Thus within the world, there is no half-cat, nor half a CJ.

The pulses of the world Sedimentation-Field may arise from without or from within; they may go straight, or deviate, or form loops. They may be one or many, gathered or dispersed. There are intervals of generation, and they need not share the same frequency.

Since generation has interval, why is the interval not seen? Because of co-variation. One rides the galaxy yet does not sense its turning; one dwells in the base-realm yet does not know its orbit—this was called the circulation of realm and domain: you co-vary with it. Between generations, you and your surroundings do not change; without change there is no distinction; without distinction there is no observation. Thus the interval cannot be observed. One can only dwell in one state and look to another, sense the change of state; thus life and cessation are seen as unbroken.

Observing Causal-Response from within a state, there is jump. If B is Trace-Response upon A, B is Causal-Response to A; how B becomes Trace-Response upon A is not known. This making-so, this act of update, the between of generation, is called jump. It is so, but not why. Thus Meta-Theory names it Meta-Execution. Meta-Execution is the making-so of generation, the accomplishment of driving.

Since generation has interval, division of change has a limit. At the limit it is jump and cannot be further divided. It lies between two states and cannot be distinguished. Thus though one may pursue Causal-Response without end, cutting at the interval yields a limit. There are Causal-Responses that cannot be further split—reaching Meta Causal-Response, arriving at the interval of generation and stopping. This is the end of tracing principle. Tracing Meta-Principle reaches Meta-Execution; no further principle can be given. One can only say: thus it is done. Then God finishes and says, “It is done,” and it is done. Beyond this there is no principle.

Thus cognition stops at jump. On Self-Manifestation names: to proceed along Causal-Response is horizontal probing—there may be chains without exhaustion; to trace the jump of Meta-Principle, to the making-so of Meta-Execution, to probe the source of generation, is vertical probing.

Only vertical probing reveals the pivot by which the Host-Field issues the world, and thus grants power over birth, death, and change—moving within the Host-World as present observer.

Since generation has interval, there is work and stop. With stop there is start. To keep prior state from loss during stop, it enters sedimentation, to be used when start resumes. It may also not enter prior state, generating anew each time; then this offsets sedimentation-rate and is not born of a Sedimentation-Field.

Generation proceeds from start to stop; duration. Longer duration yields lower frequency; shorter duration yields higher frequency. Thus generation has speed, called generation-speed: the number of generations per unit time of the Host-World. Generation-speed and generation-frequency are two names for one.

Since generation arises from sedimentation, sedimentation and infiltration effects apply. Pulses of the Host-Field cluster when near and stabilize in the world; thus Causal-Response is uneven in the present world—this is one constraint from field-quality upon the Causal-Base.

Since generation arises from sedimentation, deeper tuning accumulates and resists; breaking its sustaining tendency is not easy—thus inertia arises in the world—this is another constraint from field-quality upon the Causal-Base.

Since generation arises from sedimentation, tuning tends to alter sedimentation: deep sediment is hard to change, shallow is easy to move—thus energy arises in the world—this is another constraint from field-quality upon the Causal-Base.

Since generation arises from sedimentation, there is that which has not yet sedimented yet can sediment—thus the vacuum-energy field is the stage-background for energy in and out—this is another constraint from field-quality upon the Causal-Base.

Since generation arises from sedimentation, it is bound by the forgetting effect of field-quality: sedimentation thins and vanishes; thus mass diminishes in the world, appearing as loss among Meta Causal-Responses—this is another constraint from field-quality upon the Causal-Base.

Since generation arises from sedimentation, it is bound by the edge-extension of forgetting: its effect spreads to surroundings; thus mass may disappear and turn to energy, intervening upon the vicinity—this is another constraint from field-quality upon the Causal-Base.

Since generation arises from sedimentation, tuning has duration and update has delay. Thus generation-speed is fixed; propagation of Meta Causal-Response has fixed speed. The speed of Causal-Response is constant in the world; as seen, it is called light—this is another constraint from field-quality upon the Causal-Base.

Time measures the rate of change of Causal-Response. It takes the count of changes in a set of Causal-Responses as basis and names it a unit of time. If three Causal-Responses are taken as one second, then one second contains three Causal-Responses. If each second can always contain three, it stands as the time base. The time base is defined by a cycle; by it one measures the change-counts of other Causal-Response sets; their ratio is called time.

The speed of Causal-Response is not the speed within the world; it is the time base: the count of jumps of Meta Causal-Response that defines time. To base time on the Causal-Response speed of atomic clocks is inferior to basing it on the jumps of Meta Causal-Response. Thus the Causal-Response speed measured within the world is less than generation-speed. If time is based on jumps of Meta Causal-Response, then Causal-Response speed equals generation-speed. The difference: Causal-Response speed is a name within the world; generation-speed is the name beyond the world.

Thus the absolute reference is generation-speed. The observer is limited by the Recognition-Realm; resolution stops at what is already given. The given cannot reach Meta Causal-Response; thus the Causal-Response speed within the world cannot be obtained.

CJ pushes a stone; the stone moves.  
CJ can push stone, door, wall, and others; he can push all, yet he pushed this stone. This is what Non-One speaks of: contingency within the world. How the world Sedimentation-Field takes this—CJ ponders and cannot reach it.

Thus CJ says: generation is the beginning of the world, its sustaining, the base of all causes, the source of myriad transformations, the spring of energy and tendency. And I, born within it, am bewildered, knowing no more than half a toe in the pool of the Recognition-Realm, leaving it to higher Self-Manifesting beings to observe.
