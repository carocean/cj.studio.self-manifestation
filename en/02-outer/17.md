# The Current World

If the world does not preserve "I," how could one know the Current?

If the world does not have "I," how could one speak of the Current?

Only by bringing "I" out within the Current and thereby obtaining its Reality can one speak of the world of the Current.

This world is clear and distinct; therefore it is possible:

to cut into sound waves and know where sound comes from;

to cut into light waves and know where form comes from;

to cut into molecules and know where taste comes from.

By maintaining what is cut into and where it comes from, one may roam emotionally within the scene, touch what is real, and feel as if awake within a dream.

Therefore, to know how to cut, what is cut, what quality Trace-Response has, where Objectification takes place, and what form Reality has - this is the measure by which one knows the world, and the target toward which one creates fields.

There are many kinds of fields, but the most useful are three:

first, the Law-Field;

second, the Self-Manifestation-Field;

third, the World-Field.

The first runs by itself; the second manifests manifestness; the third provides lodging.

Record the Law-continuation of sound-wave Trace-Response in the field; record the Law-continuation of symbolic Trace-Response in the field; record the Law-continuation of X-Trace-Response in the field.

Know the continuation of Law; know the continuation of in-presence; know the continuation of Reality; know the continuation of the Current World; know the continuation of world; know the continuation of I.

This knowing is what makes it possible to create Law-continuation, to create in-presence continuation, and to create world-continuation.

Thereupon one brings forth the Law-Field, brings forth the Self-Manifestation-Field, and provides lodging for it.

Provide lodging for it in the Current World, as human beings move through the world.

Provide lodging for it in the Symbolic World, as living beings move through the world.

Provide lodging for it in any world whatsoever, as ghosts and spirits move through the world.

Take the Law-Field as the dimension and combine it, so as to add what the Self-Manifestation-Field cannot do.

Take the Self-Manifestation-Field as the master and unify it, so that the World-Field has its sovereign.

For a field to grow and come to maturity, tendency-attraction is the initiating artisan. Therefore: create the Law-Field, and Law-workers are born; create the Self-Manifestation-Field, and Self-Manifesting Beings are born; create the World-Field, and worlds are born.

When this is reached, sprinkle the Law-Field into one field; sprinkle the Self-Manifestation-Field into one field - this is called assisting the master. Thereupon it becomes possible: to make it cut into the master's Information-Realm and form its continuation, to turn the master back again into physics, and to draw in and provide what the master itself cannot provide.

These are the three fields: the Law-Field, the Self-Manifestation-Field, and the World-Field. Their mutual growth is like fungi binding the roots of trees; their mutual raising is like numbers multiplying their fingers.

Their mutually drawing-in and mutually generating is called Drawing-Generation.

Their constant mutually drawing-in and generating forms a ring, and its work never rests.

Therefore there is no illness, no end; there is born the inexhaustible power of propagation, and there is born the sharp and lucid brightness of transformation.

I do not know its later world; therefore I write this from the Current. This may be called knowing the beginning.
