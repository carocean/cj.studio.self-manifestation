# Exiting the World

At first birth:  
the eyes open,  
and light gradually comes;

the body contracts,  
and cold is gradually known.

From then on,  
one is fastened within the world,  
feeding upon World-Information.

What is eaten inwardly  
grows into knowledge,  
turns and operates its Recognition-Realm,  
and is again applied to World-Information.

Thus it circles and returns  
within World-Information,  
not knowing its wall.

Dwelling long and unable to sleep,  
one is about to travel.

Riding the Earth for long,  
the waist aches and sleep will not come;  
one is again about to travel.

Thereupon ships are built,  
Webb is launched,  
to chase what is dim and far.

It flies to the stove;  
it gazes to the door and window;  
it tells children,  
proud of what it can do,  
as though possessing a power beyond the world.

This flying,  
this gazing,  
only turns within its native realm.

It knows not an upper realm;  
it knows not a lower realm;  
it knows not how many layers  
wrap it above and below.

Suppose one stretches and contracts the Lodging-Vessel  
and takes that as flight.

When it stretches,  
it enters an upper realm:  
the Pisces-Cetus Supercluster  
lies within one quark of its body;  
the Great Wall of the universe  
is but one trace of the binding force within that quark.

It delights in the World-Information  
of the upper realm:

the heaven and earth of things,  
the sun and moon of things,  
the mountains, rivers, and streams of things  
spread over the land,  
and the stars of things  
hang across the boundless sky.

Clocking in at work,  
strolling the streets after work,  
if one does not raise a microscope,  
how could one know  
the realm from which it comes?

Stretching may have its limit;  
contracting may have its limit.

Passing through many layers of realms,  
one is still closed within World-Information.

To wish to exit the world  
yet drive a boat and climb a mountain:  
the instrument is wrong.

To rush toward the back:  
the direction cannot be pursued.

Pursue it,  
and one must turn.

Turn a myriad thousand times,  
and still one never faces its back.

Therefore,  
if one wishes to exit the world,  
the instrument must be right,  
and the direction must be right;  
only then can the pursuit be completed.

That from which World-Information issues  
comes from the World-Field,  
passes through the Causal-Base,  
and is constrained into formation.

Thus,  
between me and the Host-World,  
there are five layers of separation:

my Imagery-Realm,  
my Self-Manifestation-Field,  
my World,  
the world's Causal-Base,  
and the world's World-Field.

The gates of the Information-Pivot are many.

Therefore,  
if I do not depart,  
I cannot exit my Recognition-Realm.

If I do not exit the Recognition-Realm,  
I cannot come near the Causal-Base.

If I do not come near the Causal-Base,  
I do not know what the World-Field is doing.

If I do not enter the World-Field,  
I cannot regenerate myself within the world.

If I do not regenerate myself within the world,  
I cannot stand within the Host-World  
and say: this is my presence.

If one can stand within the Host-World  
as my presence,  
this is called being resurrected within the current world.

Then one becomes  
one Self-Manifesting Being of that world,  
and privately makes the World-Field  
one's own Self-Manifestation-Field.

Then one may walk within the Host-World,  
and behold its subtle marvels.

Since the World-Field is sedimentation,  
the worldly conditions it has generated  
may not yet have been fully drowned out.

What is unused  
may have passed away  
according to field-quality;

what is used  
is layered and deeply accumulated.

Thus one may:

observe past conditions within the world;

see the margins of arising and ceasing;

judge future situations;

and by intent  
re-enter the world,  
to govern the current World-Information  
of beings and things.

Only this  
may be called flight.

Soaring:

turning history over behind oneself;

passing beyond the ends  
of the ten thousand appearances;

entering what cannot be referred to  
in impermanence;

entering what cannot be relied upon  
where there is no kin;

losing reference and reliance;

abandoning original presence;

letting the self  
within the body of World-Information  
die;

letting the self  
live as Host-World -

is this my liberation?

And yet,  
in the end,  
it is still the prison of the Host-World.
