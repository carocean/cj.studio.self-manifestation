# The Observer Group Master Diagram

A group form does not prove a new theory; it organizes several already-established Forms so that their basis of aggregation, membership boundary, relational structure, propagation paths, and integral reading obtain a unified norm.

Why these Forms must compose one group: the twelve Forms share one chain of succession — the General Form of Objectification yields the coexistence of multiple candidates, and from there the problem of the current and the observer arises; once the General Form has forced out the three duties, their formalization (the Mathematical Form, the Mathematical Form of Individuation), their individuation (the General Form of Individuation), their limits (the Limit Form), their field-layer source (the Construction Form), their cross-time conditions (the Pool-Split Transition Form), their identification bounds (the Current Inversion Form), their operational protocols (the Adjudication Form, the Single-Dual Discrimination Form, the Realization Form), and their strengthened domain (the Strengthened Form of Current Generation) each bear one link, each consuming and preconditioning the others — any one published independently breaks the chain: the formal layer loses its ground of necessity, the operational layer loses the source of its criteria, the source layer loses the name of its object. Hence one group.

Basis of aggregation: one object (the holding of the current and the individuation of the observer), one chain of succession (Objectification → the observer), one methodological discipline (forcing-out argumentation, the three-valued regime, the two-axis transmission system).
