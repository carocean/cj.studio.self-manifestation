# General Form of Mis-Externalization

This chapter attempts a general proposition:

To admit that structures within the system are generated by Objectification, and converged upon and maintained as the current by the observer (the maintained current being a single convergence form whose constituents are jointly one: duty I of the General Form of the Observer), is to admit that in any system where the attribution demand holds, there necessarily exists a set of structures by which the current structure s is judged to "come from outside."

That is to say: after a structure s has been generated, admitted, and maintained as the current, the system must assign s a source attribution.

This chapter proves: so-called "externality" is not an input property, but the attribution result the system generates for structure s under the condition that the source cannot be uniquely determined.

Here "the external" denotes only the source-attribution label of structure s, not an independent existence beyond reality.

Our aim is:

without repeating the structures of Objectification, the Observation-Domain, and the observer, to derive the conditions under which the source attribution of structure s forms, and to give the general form of Mis-Externalization.

The result first.

The minimal Mis-Externalization structure, general form:

Externalization(s) = (E, X, Δₛ, Ʃₛ, Λₛ, Kₛ)

satisfying:

Ʃₛ ⊆ P, with Ʃₛ delimited by E(s)  
Λₛ : (Ʃₛ, X) → L, with Λₛ a single-valued and totally defined mapping  
L = {lᵢₙ, lₒᵤₜ}  
if l\* = Λₛ(Ʃₛ, X), then Kₛ(s, l*) = l*

where P is the space of all paths capable of generating structure s.  
Notational convention: X is the bias value actually in place at the moment; 𝒳 is the bias space (its type) to which it belongs. The minimal structure is listed with X, because the holding of Mis-Externalization requires a concrete bias in place — a bias space alone does not suffice to complete the compression; 𝒳 as the type background, see Appendix Sections 4 and 5.

Where there is a current structure s, the source is not uniquely adjudicable, and the system must nonetheless form a source attribution, any system capable of forming "s is of external origin" necessarily possesses at once: the appearance entry, the source gap, the source interpretation set, the attribution mapping, the attribution bias, and the attribution convergence structure.

Therefore, externality is not an input property, but the attribution result — pressed out within indeterminacy — that the system generates for structure s.

One sentence:

The observer gives "which one is there now"; Mis-Externalization gives "this s — where it is taken to be from."

Abstract

The Objectification General Form has said: the system can form stable structures internally. The Observation-Domain General Form has said: structures within the pool must pass the double boundary of qualification and reachability before entering the competition of appearing. The Observer General Form the Observer Group has said: under sustained continuation, multiple-candidate coexistence, the determinacy gap, and finite tuning capacity, the maintained current is a single convergence form whose composite constituents must be jointly one on the system's side (duty I of the General Form of the Observer, a proven necessary direction); as for "at most one maintained occupancy per pool, and exactly one maintained as the current under convergence-reachability," that rests on the pool-level current single-value principle (a constitutive commitment, unproven, open to empirical breach), not on a proven theorem; what this chapter actually requires is the former (the single convergence form), the latter being cited only where cross-pool counting or an assertion of existence is needed. The question now advances: once a structure s has become the current, why does the system further pass judgment on the source of s — and why can that judgment be wrong?

The "current structure s" of this chapter denotes the occupied imagery whose convergence is complete and which is maintained (for the formal definition of occupancy see the Observer Mathematical Form; its bridge to CurrentFact is open, see General Form of the Observer H3). Source attribution is defined on the appearing layer only: contents at the boundary fringe, and the unmanifested within the boundary, receive no source attribution (Form Chapter 22, the downstream-interface section).

This chapter proposes: the source attribution of structure s is no reading-off of the true source, but the closed result the system's internal structures generate for s when the source cannot be uniquely determined. So long as the system has internal generation, path irreversibility, and information compression, a source gap regarding s forms unavoidably; and so long as, under that source gap, the system must still deliver a source judgment on s, there necessarily exist an attribution mapping and an attribution bias by which the interpretation set is compressed into a single source label, and a convergence structure by which that label becomes the current stable attribution of s. The uniqueness and stability of attribution are not posited as further axioms: the attribution label attaches to the maintained single convergence form, and its maintenance shares one mechanism with the maintenance of occupancy, inherited from the Observer Group.

The core conclusion: generation says "how s comes about"; the observer says "who is there now"; Mis-Externalization says "where s is taken to be from."

Introduction

What the system faces is not only "whether there is structure," nor merely "what is there now," but also: "the current structure s — where is it from?"

A structure s can be internally generated, yet judged to be of external origin; a pattern can be formed by historical backflow, yet attributed to input; a current occupancy can be completed wholly within the system, yet still be interpreted by the system as coming from outside.

Note: "outside" here names the direction of source attribution for structure s, not a world beyond reality.

This raises the fundamental question:

Why does the system judge an internally generated structure s to be of external source?

This chapter proposes: the source is not read off directly; it is constructed for structure s under conditions of indeterminacy.

The stream of argument

We proceed by sustained questioning, step by step; the stream of argument forms accordingly, and is best read from the top.

1.

This chapter does not discuss how structures are generated — that is given by the Objectification General Form; nor what may enter the competition of appearing — that is given by the Observation-Domain General Form; nor which structure becomes the current — that is given by the Observer General Form.

This chapter discusses only: after some structure s has been maintained as the current, how the system assigns s its source attribution.

2.

"Of external origin" is not a property of structure s.

Structure s is merely generated and occupied within the system; it carries no property of "where it is from." Hence "externality" is not information attached to input, but an attribution judgment the system lays upon structure s.

3.

Can the system, then, simply not judge sources?

Not every system needs to. But whenever a system must distinguish input from inner generation, must form the source stratification within its reality structure, or must respond differently to different sources, it must assign the current structure s a source attribution. All conclusions of this chapter take this condition as premise; systems not satisfying it, see breach counter-example 4.

Given that the attribution demand holds, how are the uniqueness and stability of attribution founded? This version makes it explicit: they are not posited as new constraints, but obtained by two inheritances.

First, the inheritance of uniqueness. The attribution label attaches to the maintained occupant s. By duty I of the General Form of the Observer (the constituents of a composite current must be jointly one on the system's side — a proven necessary direction) together with the integration running of the Observer Mathematical Form (full-member bidirectional coverage, no independent partition): the maintained current is a single convergence form whose constituents are not independent occupants standing side by side; hence the vehicle carrying a maintained attribution is this one form and not several. This does not depend on the commitment of "at most one occupancy per pool"; where the latter is separately needed (as in cross-pool counting), it rests on the pool-level current single-value principle (a constitutive commitment, unproven). Ask further: can this one vehicle carry two attributions at once? It cannot, and the reason lies at the form layer: the label dimension is a constituent dimension of the convergence form; the maintained form is a single balance, and the assignment on the two-valued dimension is one component of that balance — two mutually negating maintained assignments would mean the dimension has not completed convergence and the form has not reached balance, in direct contradiction with "s is a maintained occupancy." The two-valued mutually negating structure of the label space (lᵢₙ ≔ ¬lₒᵤₜ; in detail, the Dissolution Form of Mis-Externalization) closes this contradiction at the logical layer as well. "At one and the same moment, exactly one maintained attribution for structure s" is thereby a consequence, not a posit.

Second, the inheritance of stability. The attribution convergence Kₛ is the component of the tuning-convergence K along the label dimension: l*, as a constituent verdict of the converged imagery's impedance form, is built and maintained together with s. The maintenance of s's occupancy depends on sustained resource allocation pressing past dispersal (the completion-retention condition of the Observer Mathematical Form, Definition 47; its converse being the decay condition, Definition 48); the stability interval τ of l* therefore falls within the maintenance interval of s — lose the maintenance, and the attribution disperses together with the occupancy. "The stable sense of being-outside" is thereby dynamically founded, with no separate stability postulate.

4.

Ask next: can the source attribution of structure s be uniquely derived from current conditions?

If it can, source judgment is mere deterministic mapping, and no problem of Mis-Externalization exists.

But whenever the system has internal generation, historical backflow, path compression, or information loss, one situation arises unavoidably: the current conditions do not suffice to determine uniquely the generative path of structure s.

Let Cₛ be the current-condition information set regarding structure s. On this basis, define the source interpretation set:

Ʃₛ ⊆ P

where P is the set of paths capable of generating structure s, and Ʃₛ is delimited by E(s).

Definition 1: the source gap Δₛ (defined on Ʃₛ)

Δₛ ≠ ∅ ⟺ ∃ p₁, p₂ ∈ Ʃₛ, p₁ ≠ p₂, and ∀ f ∈ F, f(p₁, Cₛ) = f(p₂, Cₛ)

where F is the set of all determination functions realizable within the system, f : P × Cₛ → D.

5.

Once the source gap Δₛ exists, and the system must still assign structure s a source attribution, multiple possible source interpretations necessarily appear.

Definition 2: the source interpretation set Ʃₛ

Ʃₛ is the set of source paths to which structure s may correspond.

6.

Yet the system must still deliver a unique attribution (its uniqueness founded in Section 3).

Therefore there must exist a structure by which the multiple source interpretations for structure s are compressed into a single source label.

Definition 3: the attribution mapping Λₛ

Λₛ : (Ʃₛ, X) → L

Λₛ is a single-valued and totally defined mapping, and: for structure s, given Ʃₛ and X, there exists a unique l\* ∈ L such that Λₛ(Ʃₛ, X) = l\*.

7.

But if the attribution mapping were wholly decided by the current conditions Cₛ, source judgment would fall back once more to deterministic mapping — contradicting the source gap.

Hence:

Lemma 1: Δₛ ≠ ∅ ∧ Λₛ(Ʃₛ, X) = l\* ⟹ Λₛ cannot be a function of Cₛ alone.

8.

From this follows:

Definition 4: the attribution bias X

X is the internal biasing structure which, under the source gap Δₛ, makes the attribution mapping Λₛ for structure s able to complete its compression.

Therefore: Λₛ = Λₛ(Ʃₛ, X).

9.

Ask next: can the source attribution of structure s be a merely instantaneous judgment?

It cannot.

Definition 5: the attribution convergence Kₛ

Kₛ : (s, l*) → l*

satisfying: there exists a time interval τ such that if Λₛ(Ʃₛ, X) = l*, then ∀ t ∈ τ, Kₛ(s, l*) = l\*.

Its basis of realization has been given in Section 3: Kₛ is the component of the tuning-convergence K along the label dimension; τ falls within the occupancy-maintenance interval of s; the maintenance mechanism is shared (resource allocation pressing past dispersal).

10.

Definition 6: the appearance entry E

E is the structure by which structure s enters the source-adjudication domain, and it delimits the domain of Ʃₛ — that is, Ʃₛ is determined by E(s).

The working range of E coincides with the Observation-Domain: only contents of the appearing layer enter the source-adjudication domain. Contents at the boundary fringe, and the unmanifested within the boundary, are neither occupancies nor objects of maintained attribution, and are judged neither inner nor outer (Form Chapter 22, the downstream-interface section).

11.

By this point the general form of Mis-Externalization has been forced out step by step.

Given a current structure s, a source gap Δₛ, and the attribution demand, the system must possess:

the appearance entry E  
the source gap Δₛ  
the source interpretation set Ʃₛ  
the attribution mapping Λₛ  
the attribution bias X  
the attribution convergence Kₛ

With any one item lacking, "s is of external origin" cannot form.

12.

The general form of Mis-Externalization can now be formally written:

Externalization(s) = (E, X, Δₛ, Ʃₛ, Λₛ, Kₛ)

satisfying:

Ʃₛ ⊆ P and delimited by E(s)  
Λₛ : (Ʃₛ, X) → L  
if l\* = Λₛ(Ʃₛ, X), then Kₛ(s, l*) = l*

13.

The general theorem can now be given:

Given a current structure s, a source gap Δₛ, and the attribution demand, any system capable of forming and maintaining "s is of external origin" necessarily possesses E, X, Δₛ, Ʃₛ, Λₛ, Kₛ at once.

This structure set is the minimal complete set: no proper subset of it satisfies the source-attribution condition for structure s.

Proof:

without E, Ʃₛ does not stand;  
without Δₛ, the condition of source non-adjudicability does not stand, and there is no Mis-Externalization in this chapter's sense;  
without Ʃₛ, there is no multiplicity of interpretations, and no attribution compression stands;  
without Λₛ, there is no source adjudication;  
without X, Λₛ degenerates into a mapping dependent on Cₛ alone — contradicting Δₛ ≠ ∅;  
without Kₛ, the attribution of structure s cannot be maintained over τ, and no stable externality stands.

Since if l\* = Λₛ(Ʃₛ, X) then Kₛ(s, l*) = l*: without Λₛ, Kₛ has no object; and were Kₛ prior to Λₛ, the attribution would be presupposed, and no source adjudication would stand.

Hence Λₛ-before, Kₛ-after is the necessary structural order. ∎

14\. Corollaries

Corollary 1 (attribution manipulability, testable).  
The attribution outcome is decided jointly by Ʃₛ and X, not by the true source. Hence any intervention that widens the source gap Δₛ (adding compatible paths, lowering the resolution of the determination functions) or manipulates the attribution bias X (rewriting inner/outer priors, temporal expectations, self-prediction) should shift the attribution verdict systematically — and, with sufficient manipulation, flip it: internally generated structure judged external, or external input judged internal. Attribution illusions are thereby no anomaly, but the normal output of the Mis-Externalization structure under controlled parameters; the direction and magnitude of attribution shift should vary with the gradient of manipulation upon Δₛ and X, with the flip occurring where the adjudication boundary is crossed.

Corollary 2 (attribution and occupancy co-disperse, testable).  
By the stability inheritance of Section 3: τ falls within the maintenance interval of s. Hence where occupancy dissolves, the maintained current attribution necessarily dissolves with it — in the manifestation-vacancy regime (Observer Mathematical Form, Theorems 7 and 11; the vacancy criterion is not r ≤ η but the non-existence of any realizable candidate–integration–maintenance path), no residue of "the sense of being-outside" remains; where occupancy alternates sequentially (multi-stable switching), the current attribution alternates segment by segment with the occupancy, with no residue across segments.  
Two objects must be distinguished, lest the corollary be misread: what it concerns is the maintained current attribution, which begins and ends with the occupancy; attribution may at the same time sediment along Trace-Response, forming an attribution record — and the record may, through historical backflow, be regenerated within a later occupancy as a new current attribution ("I remember it came from outside"). Regeneration is not residue: it occurs within the new occupancy, and should bear the marks of backflow — driftable, re-adjudicable, decaying with sedimentary depth. The corollary's measurable forms are therefore two: first, the real-time temporal structure of "the sense of being-outside" is strictly synchronous with the temporal structure of occupancy; second, cross-segment attribution regeneration should exhibit backflow marks distinguishable from real-time attribution.

15\. The breach argument

Counter-example 1: the fully traceable system.  
If Δₛ = ∅, the source of structure s is uniquely determinable from Cₛ; Ʃₛ constitutes no multi-interpretation set; Λₛ degenerates into deterministic mapping; no Mis-Externalization stands.  
A distinction must be kept: under adjudicable conditions the system may still misjudge the source — but that is an execution error, the determination function misapplied or noise-disturbed, not structural Mis-Externalization in this chapter's sense. The nature of Mis-Externalization is: where the source is non-adjudicable, an attribution is constructed nonetheless; it is not a misjudgment, but the necessary output at the place where adjudication has nothing to go on — and note that this is non-adjudicability at the epistemic layer (the system's available determination functions cannot tell the paths apart), not a fact of the ontic layer (the true generative path still exists; the system simply has no access to it). The criteria of the two error kinds separate: execution error vanishes as adjudication conditions improve; structural Mis-Externalization moves only as the gap and the bias change.

Counter-example 2: the system without internal generation.  
If the system has no internal generation, all paths in Ʃₛ point to external input; no inner–outer mixture of sources exists; no attribution compression stands; no Mis-Externalization stands.

Counter-example 3: the random-attribution system.  
If Λₛ is unstable or random-valued on Ʃₛ, no unique l\* exists, or Kₛ cannot maintain over any τ; the system shows only attribution fluctuation, and no externality forms.

Counter-example 4: the system without attribution demand.  
If the system draws no distinction as to the source of structure s, Λₛ does not exist, L does not stand, "the external" as an attribution label is absent altogether, and no Mis-Externalization stands. This counter-example at the same time marks out the scope of this chapter's premise (Section 3).

Counter-example 5: the instantaneous-attribution system.  
If Kₛ does not exist or cannot maintain Λₛ's output over τ, the attribution of structure s forms no stable structure — a merely instantaneous judgment, constituting no externality.

Therefore: Mis-Externalization is no special error, but the necessary result under the structural conditions Δₛ ≠ ∅, Ʃₛ not a singleton, Λₛ required to compress, and Kₛ maintainable.

16\. Closing

Compressed at last into three sentences:

Objectification answers "what is generated";  
the observer answers "what is there now";  
Mis-Externalization answers "where it is taken to be from."

Pressed into the plainest lines:

Without Objectification, nothing can exist;  
without the observer, nothing appears here;  
without Mis-Externalization, there is no "outside" to speak of.

Appendix: The General Form of Mis-Externalization · Formalization

This appendix gives the minimal formal system of the Mis-Externalization general form, so that the structures, propositions, and theorem of the main text close completely at the formal layer.

1\. Basic objects and types

Let:

s ∈ S be the current structure. Anchoring: s ∈ Cur(t), the converged imagery maintained in the occupied state at the moment (for the formal definition of occupancy see the Observer Mathematical Form; Form Chapter 22, Appendix 1, Section 3), with \|Cur(t)\| ≤ 1 per pool (resting on the pool-level current single-value principle, a constitutive commitment; what this chapter actually requires is that Cur(t) be a single convergence form, which duty I of the General Form of the Observer gives).  
P be the universe of all possible generative paths.  
Cₛ be the current-condition information set relevant to structure s.

Define:  
Pₛ ≔ { p ∈ P ∣ p can generate s }  
E : S → ℘(P), E(s) ⊆ Pₛ, denoting the set of generative paths admitted after structure s has been brought into the source-adjudication domain; the working range of the source-adjudication domain is the appearing layer (Form Chapter 22).

Define the source interpretation set:  
Ʃₛ ⊆ E(s), with Ʃₛ ≠ ∅.

2\. Source non-adjudicability (the source gap)

Let Fₛ be the set of determination functions realizable by the system under the current conditions Cₛ, ∀ f ∈ Fₛ, f : P × Cₛ → D.

Given Ʃₛ, define the source-gap object:

Δₛ(Ʃₛ) ≔ { (p₁, p₂) ∈ Ʃₛ × Ʃₛ ∣ p₁ ≠ p₂ ∧ ∀ f ∈ Fₛ, f(p₁, Cₛ) = f(p₂, Cₛ) }

and require: Δₛ(Ʃₛ) ≠ ∅.

3\. The source-attribution labels

L = {lᵢₙ, lₒᵤₜ}, where lᵢₙ denotes "of inner origin" and lₒᵤₜ "of external origin."

4\. The attribution mapping

Let 𝒳 be the system's internal bias space.

Define Λₛ : ℘(P) × 𝒳 → L, and require:

1\. Single-valuedness: ∀ A ⊆ P, ∀ X ∈ 𝒳, there exists a unique l ∈ L with Λₛ(A, X) = l.  
2\. Total definedness: Λₛ is totally defined on ℘(P) × 𝒳.  
3\. Non-degeneracy: when Δₛ(Ʃₛ) ≠ ∅, there exists no function g : Cₛ → L such that ∀ Ʃₛ ⊆ E(s), ∀ X ∈ 𝒳, Λₛ(Ʃₛ, X) = g(Cₛ). That is: where the source gap exists, the attribution mapping cannot depend on Cₛ alone.

5\. The attribution bias

X ∈ 𝒳, satisfying: there exists Ʃₛ ⊆ E(s) such that if Δₛ(Ʃₛ) ≠ ∅, then there exist X₁, X₂ ∈ 𝒳 with Λₛ(Ʃₛ, X₁) ≠ Λₛ(Ʃₛ, X₂). That is: the bias space has substantive influence upon the attribution outcome.

6\. The attribution convergence

Let T be the time domain. Define Kₛ : S × L → L, satisfying: there exists τ ⊆ T such that if l\* = Λₛ(Ʃₛ, X), then ∀ t ∈ τ, Kₛ(s, l*) = l*.

Dynamical founding: Kₛ is the component of the tuning-convergence K along the label dimension; τ ⊆ the occupancy-maintenance interval of s; the maintenance condition shares the Observer Mathematical Form's resource–dispersal inequality (the label version of r·x \> η·z; see Definitions 47 and 48). Whence:

\|{ maintained attributions }\| ≤ 1 (inherited from "the current is a single convergence form": the carrier being one, the assignment is one; cross-pool counting rests separately on the commitment of the pool-level current single-value principle);  
s ∉ Cur(t) ⟹ no maintained attribution of that s (attribution and occupancy co-disperse; main text, Corollary 2).

7\. The structural order constraint

If l\* = Λₛ(Ʃₛ, X), then Kₛ(s, l*) = l*; and there exists no Kₛ prior to Λₛ that makes some l\* lawful for structure s. That is: the attribution mapping precedes the attribution convergence.

8\. Definition of the Mis-Externalization structure

Externalization(s) ⟺ there exist E, 𝒳, Ʃₛ, Λₛ, Kₛ such that the following hold at once:

1\. Ʃₛ ⊆ E(s) and Ʃₛ ≠ ∅  
2\. Δₛ(Ʃₛ) ≠ ∅  
3\. Λₛ : ℘(P) × 𝒳 → L is a single-valued, totally defined mapping  
4\. there exists X ∈ 𝒳 with Λₛ(Ʃₛ, X) = l\*  
5\. there exists τ ⊆ T such that ∀ t ∈ τ, Kₛ(s, l*) = l*

9\. The condition for externality

Out(s) ⟺ Externalization(s) ∧ there exist Ʃₛ ⊆ E(s), X ∈ 𝒳, such that Λₛ(Ʃₛ, X) = lₒᵤₜ.

That is: given the Mis-Externalization structure, structure s is attributed as of external origin.

Notational remark: this chapter's Out(s) is the external-verdict predicate upon structure s, and belongs to a different object from Form Chapter 22, Appendix 1's Out(t) (the outside-the-boundary set within a pool) — the former acts on the attribution of a single appearing structure, the latter is a partition band over sets of structures; the two have different domains of action, each notation is retained, and this is noted here.

10\. Minimal completeness

Let ℰₛ = {E, X, Δₛ, Ʃₛ, Λₛ, Kₛ} (X ∈ 𝒳, in accord with the main-text tuple).  
Then: Externalization(s) requires the existence of ℰₛ; and ∀ ℰ′ ⊂ ℰₛ, ¬Externalization(s). That is, ℰₛ is the minimal complete set of the Mis-Externalization structure.

11\. Non-Mis-Externalization conditions

If any of the following holds, then ¬Externalization(s):

1\. Ʃₛ = {p}  
2\. Δₛ(Ʃₛ) = ∅  
3\. Λₛ does not exist or is not single-valued  
4\. there exists g : Cₛ → L such that Λₛ(Ʃₛ, X) = g(Cₛ)  
5\. Kₛ does not exist, or no stable τ exists  
6\. L does not stand

12\. System classification

By Δₛ and Λₛ, three system classes are defined:

1\. Determinate systems: Δₛ(Ʃₛ) = ∅  
2\. Unclosed systems: Δₛ(Ʃₛ) ≠ ∅ ∧ Λₛ does not exist  
3\. Mis-Externalizing systems: Δₛ(Ʃₛ) ≠ ∅ ∧ Λₛ exists ∧ Kₛ stable

13\. Formal closure conclusion

When the following conditions hold:

Ʃₛ ⊆ E(s), Ʃₛ ≠ ∅  
Δₛ(Ʃₛ) ≠ ∅  
Λₛ : ℘(P) × 𝒳 → L is a single-valued total function  
Kₛ : S × L → L and stable over τ

then Externalization(s) holds, and Out(s) is one of Λₛ's values on L.
