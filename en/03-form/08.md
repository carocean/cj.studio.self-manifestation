# Limit Form of Mis-Externalization

This chapter attempts a general proposition:

The mechanism generating the attribution result "of external origin" is irreducible — any mechanism capable of forming and maintaining "s is of external origin" necessarily realizes every functional item of the Mis-Externalization structure; the six most common substitute proposals — direct reading, deterministic mapping, stochastic process, parallel attribution, structure-carried externality, and the static set — each fails to realize it, through the lack of at least one function or through direct conflict with the premises.

The division of labor with the General Form of Mis-Externalization (Form Chapter 7 v2.1):

Form Chapter 7 supplies two things: a construction (the six-element structure forming "s is of external origin," Externalization(s) = (E, X, Δₛ, Ʃₛ, Λₛ, Kₛ)) and universal necessity (any system capable of forming and maintaining source attribution must contain that minimal complete set — its general theorem). This chapter takes up the latter, bringing universal necessity down onto the six most common substitute proposals and checking them one by one: each class's mode of failure is exactly the mode of some necessary function's absence, or a direct conflict with the premises of the self-manifest structure. Completeness is borne by the universal theorem; this chapter's exclusions supply its instance verification.

The logical source of the conclusion must be declared in advance, lest it be misread: the universal conclusion "'the outside' can only be generated by the Mis-Externalization structure" (see Section 16) is not derived from the exclusion of the six mechanism classes — the space of mechanisms is not enumerable, and non-exhaustive exclusion yields no universal conclusion. Its source is the functional necessity of Form Chapter 7's general theorem: a universal quantification over every system capable of forming and maintaining source attribution. This chapter's six exclusions are diagnostic instances of that universal theorem.

One sentence:

The Observer Limit Form proves that the generation of the current has no other way; this chapter proves that "the outside" has no other source — it is not read off, not computed out, not drawn, not carried along; it is constructed.

1\. Basic Setup

Let the system's current structure at moment t be sₜ ∈ S.

Anchoring: sₜ is the converged imagery maintained in the occupied state at that moment; that its constituents are jointly one is given unconditionally by duty I of the General Form of the Observer, while its existence and uniqueness rest on the pool-level current single-value principle (a constitutive commitment, unproven, open to empirical breach). What this chapter actually requires is the former (that sₜ is a single convergence form), the latter being cited only where an assertion of existence is needed. Source attribution is defined on the appearing layer only (Form Chapter 22, downstream-interface section). This chapter's working domain is the exactly-one regime (the "exactly one" resting on that commitment) with the attribution demand holding (for the condition and boundary of the attribution demand, see Form Chapter 7, Section 3 and counter-example 4).

Let its source interpretation set be Ʃₜ ⊆ P, satisfying:

\|Ʃₜ\| ≥ 2, with the source gap holding: Δₜ(Ʃₜ) ≠ ∅ (Form Chapter 7, Definition 1 — distinct paths exist that are indistinguishable under all determination functions available to the system).

And: Ʃₜ is generated or delimited by E(sₜ), not taken as an external input variable.

Let the source-attribution outcome be:

lₜ ∈ L = {lᵢₙ, lₒᵤₜ}

This outcome is not presupposed as the output of an existing function, but treated as a result awaiting generation.

The uniqueness and stability of attribution are not posited as axioms: by Form Chapter 7 v2.1, Section 3, the maintained attribution is exactly one (the carrier layer: the maintained current is a single convergence form whose constituents are not independent occupants standing side by side, so the carrier is this one form — duty I of the General Form of the Observer together with the integration running of the Observer Mathematical Form; the form layer: the label dimension is a constituent dimension of the convergence form, and a single balance admits at most one maintained assignment on the two-valued dimension; the logical layer: lᵢₙ ≔ ¬lₒᵤₜ, two-valued mutual negation), and the stability interval falls within the occupancy-maintenance interval of sₜ. This chapter cites that inheritance directly, denoting the maintained attribution l\*ₜ.

2\. Problem Definition

Within the source interpretation set Ʃₜ, multiple source paths are each compatible with the current structure sₜ.

The system forms, at that moment, a source attribution for sₜ, and the maintained attribution is exactly one (inherited per Section 1: the conjunction of the carrier, form, and logical layers, not resting on the commitment alone).

There exists, therefore, a process generating l\*ₜ for sₜ (its complete dependencies, see the functional form of Section 10).

This process does not concern whether the structure sₜ exists; it concerns: among multiple compatible sources, which is attributed as the source-character of sₜ.

What this chapter must determine is: to which class the mechanism realizing this generative process belongs — and to which classes it does not.

3\. Exclusion Path One: Direct Reading of the External

Suppose the system could directly read the real external domain Ω — that is, there exists f : Ω → L with l\*ₜ = f(Ω).

This path conflicts doubly with the premises of the self-manifest structure.

First, the channel does not exist. Within the self-manifest structure, what the system directly obtains is only the current structure sₜ and its internal conditions — there is no real external domain Ω independent of structure.

Second, the type-layer impossibility. The determination functions realizable by the system are defined over objects available to the system: f ∈ Fₛ, f : P × Cₛ → D (Form Chapter 7, Appendix Section 2) — the domain being path representations and current conditions. But Ω is by definition the real external domain independent of all structure — not an object available to the system; f(Ω) is undefined at the level of type. Direct reading is not a failed attempt; it is a function that cannot be written down.

Third, the translation layer as auxiliary support. Any influence of Ω, to enter the system, must first pass candidate-translation into within-pool structure; what translation yields is already an internal structure of the same rank as sₜ — itself becoming an object of source attribution, not "the external itself." Note here the correct scope of the Observation-Domain theorem: the consequent of Form Chapter 22, Theorem 1 acts upon the set of within-pool structures (outside-boundary structures cannot cross the boundary as content; they can pass only through boundary change); Ω is not in the within-pool structure set, so the theorem does not bear on Ω directly, but on the admission of its translated products — what is "read" can only ever be a translated product already within the boundary, never Ω itself.

Therefore: ¬∃ f : Ω → L such that l\*ₜ = f(Ω).

The direct-reading class is excluded. Its location: not a functional lack, but the conjunction of the type-layer impossibility (principal ground) and the necessity of translation (auxiliary ground) — this class is not a failed mechanism, but a channel that does not exist.

4\. Exclusion Path Two: Deterministic Mapping

Let the current explicit conditions be Γₜ. If there exists f : Γₜ → L with l\*ₜ = f(Γₜ), then source attribution is wholly decided by Γₜ.

But by Section 1, the source gap holds: there exist distinct paths indistinguishable under all determination functions available to the system. Hence no single-valued distinguishing function is definable under Γₜ:

¬∃ f : Γₜ → L such that l\*ₜ = f(Γₜ)

The deterministic-mapping class is excluded. Its missing-item location: X lacks independence — were attribution single-valuedly given by Γₜ, every bias would reduce to an unfolding of the current explicit conditions; whereas the definition of the attribution bias requires exactly that it supply, under the source gap, a ground of compression beyond Γₜ (Form Chapter 7, Definition 4; Appendix Section 4, non-degeneracy). X is therefore functionally absent, in direct conflict with the source-gap premise (answering to the X item of Form Chapter 7's general theorem).

5\. Exclusion Path Three: Stochastic or Unstable Process

Suppose l\*ₜ \~ P(L) — the source attribution given by random drawing.

A stochastic process has no attribution stability: there exists no Δt \> 0 such that ∀ τ ∈ \[t, t+Δt\], l\_τ = l\*ₜ. Each moment's fresh draw may replace the label; the so-called "outside" degenerates into label fluctuation, constituting no maintained attribution.

Further: every case in which the label switches between the two values without a stable interval — whether the switching arises from drawing, from noise, or from competitive oscillation — is gathered into this class: no stable τ, no maintained attribution.

The stochastic-or-unstable class is excluded. Its missing-item location: the attribution convergence Kₛ is absent — neither drawing nor oscillation contains the structure that solidifies the label and maintains it within the occupancy interval (answering to the Kₛ item of Form Chapter 7's general theorem; for Kₛ's dynamical founding, see Form Chapter 7, Section 3, second inheritance).

6\. Exclusion Path Four: Parallel Attribution

Suppose one and the same structure sₜ holds lₜ = lᵢₙ and lₜ = lₒᵤₜ at once.

This case is excluded at two layers independently — either layer alone suffices; and neither layer invokes any "unique-attribution constraint" — in this version no such constraint exists to invoke: uniqueness is an inherited consequence, not a posit.

First, the form layer (dynamical exclusion). By Form Chapter 7, Section 3: the label dimension is a constituent dimension of the convergence form, and the maintained form is a single balance. Two mutually negating maintained assignments would mean the label dimension has not completed convergence and the form has not reached balance — in direct contradiction with sₜ being a maintained occupancy (occupancy is the maintained completion-state; candidate completion must be sustained through the completion window, Observer Mathematical Form, Definition 45).

Second, the logical layer. By the two-valued mutual-negation structure lᵢₙ ≔ ¬lₒᵤₜ (Dissolution Form of Mis-Externalization, Section 4), lₜ = lᵢₙ ∧ lₜ = lₒᵤₜ is lₜ = ¬lₜ — a logical contradiction, excluded without invoking any structural condition.

As for the label alternating between the two values: that is not parallelism but oscillation, already handled by Exclusion Path Three.

The parallel-attribution class is excluded. Its location: not a mechanism's lack, but the conjunction of dynamical impossibility and logical contradiction — this class is not a failed mechanism, but a state that does not exist.

7\. Exclusion Path Five: Structure-Carried Externality

Suppose "of external origin" were an intrinsic property of structure sₜ — that is, there exists g : S → L with g(sₜ) = l\*ₜ.

But a structure contains only its existential content, not source-assignment information; source attribution is an added verdict upon the structure, not a property of it (Form Chapter 7, Section 2).

Further, from the order constraint: if the label existed prior to the verdict, the attribution would be presupposed — Kₛ prior to Λₛ — violating the necessary order of Form Chapter 7, Section 7 (Appendix Section 7): the attribution mapping precedes the attribution convergence; there exists no Kₛ prior to Λₛ that makes some l\* lawful for the structure.

Therefore: ¬∃ g : S → L such that g(sₜ) = l\*ₜ.

The structure-carried class is excluded. Its missing-item location: the attribution mapping Λₛ and the process of adjudication are absent — a label that were intrinsic would need no adjudication; with no adjudication, Λₛ is missing from the minimal complete set, and the order constraint Λₛ ≺ Kₛ is violated (answering to the Λₛ item and the order constraint of Form Chapter 7's general theorem).

8\. Exclusion Path Six: Static Existence of the Source Set

Suppose reliance solely on the static existence of the source interpretation set Ʃₜ — that is, multiple compatible source paths exist in Ʃₜ.

But static existence guarantees only "interpretable"; it supplies no "attribution compression": the coexistence of multiple interpretations itself produces no label, still less a maintained label.

Therefore: the existence of Ʃₜ ⊬ a maintained source attribution.

The static-set class is excluded. Its missing-item location: Λₛ (no compression) and X (no bias) are absent — the interpretation set is the material of adjudication, not the adjudication itself (answering to the Λₛ and X items of Form Chapter 7's general theorem).

9\. The Necessary Existence of the Attribution Mechanism

First, the strictly obtainable part. By Sections 3 through 8, the generation of source attribution belongs to none of the six excluded mechanism classes; meanwhile, within this chapter's working domain, attribution is in fact formed (the exactly-one regime has a maintained occupant — the "exactly one" resting on the commitment of the pool-level current single-value principle — the attribution demand holds, and by Form Chapter 7's construction, an attribution can be formed and maintained). Therefore:

there must exist a class of mechanisms Ψₜ realizing source attribution, whose output satisfies:

Ψₜ(sₜ) = l\*ₜ

with Ψₜ belonging to none of the excluded types above. (The signature here is indicative: Ψₜ's complete dependencies — Ʃₜ, X, and the constraints of E and Δₜ — see the functional form of Section 10.)

Up to this point, the exclusion argument yields only "another class of mechanism exists" — not "it can only be the Mis-Externalization structure": the six classes do not exhaust the mechanism space, and this step must not be overstepped.

The universal conclusion comes by the other road. Form Chapter 7's general theorem quantifies over all mechanisms: any system capable of forming and maintaining "s is of external origin" necessarily possesses E, X, Δₛ, Ʃₛ, Λₛ, Kₛ at once, satisfying Λₛ ≺ Kₛ. And the definition of "the Mis-Externalization structure" is exactly this functional group. Hence every realization of Ψₜ is a Mis-Externalization structure — not because the other classes have each been named and excluded, but because the functional group is necessary to the task itself.

The two roads meet here and check one another: the universal theorem asserts that any realization must contain the six-element minimal complete set; the six exclusions display, one by one, that each excluded class lacks at least one of its items, or conflicts directly with the premises (the locations at the end of each path). The exclusions bear no burden of completeness; they supply the universal theorem with six independent instance verifications.

10\. Structural Definition and Form

Define this class of mappings as the Mis-Externalization mechanism. The structures its realization requires must include:

E: generating and delimiting Ʃₜ  
Δ: securing that no definable distinguishing function exists on Ʃₜ  
X: supplying the attribution bias  
Λ: compressing the multiple source interpretations into a single source label  
K: stabilizing that label into a sustained attribution

Ψₜ is not to be understood as the functional composition of E, Δ, X, Λ, K — X is a bias value in place and Δₜ a condition object, neither a mapping; written as a composition chain, the types do not go through. Their working relation is: E delimits the source interpretation set; Δₜ gives the condition of source non-adjudicability; X takes part in the attribution compression; Λ generates the source label; K maintains that label. Ψₜ's output can accordingly be written in functional form:

Ψₜ(sₜ) = Kₛ(sₜ, Λₛ(Ʃₜ, X)), where Ʃₜ ⊆ E(sₜ) and Δₜ(Ʃₜ) ≠ ∅

satisfying: there exists Δt \> 0 such that ∀ τ ∈ \[t, t+Δt\], l\_τ = l\*ₜ.

This condition is no added axiom, but the explicit form of the maintained attribution (inherited per Section 1), holding as an output property of Ψₜ, not the attribute of any single substructure; its basis of realization is the shared mechanism of Kₛ and occupancy maintenance (Form Chapter 7, Section 3, second inheritance).

11\. Consistency with the General Form, and Constructibility

In the General Form of Mis-Externalization, the minimal complete structure for a single structure s has been defined:

Externalization(s) = (E, X, Δₛ, Ʃₛ, Λₛ, Kₛ)

where: Ʃₛ ⊆ P and internally generated by E(s); Λₛ : (Ʃₛ, X) → L; if l\* = Λₛ(Ʃₛ, X), then Kₛ(s, l*) = l*.

Now, in the Limit Form, taking s = sₜ, Ʃₛ = Ʃₜ, l\* = l\*ₜ, the structures Ψₜ requires correspond one-to-one with the six-element structure of Externalization(sₜ).

Moreover, since Externalization(sₜ) has been constructively given in the General Form, and each substructure is defined within S, Ψₜ is constructibly existent within the system.

Therefore: Ψₜ ≅ Externalization(sₜ), and Externalization(sₜ) is the necessary-and-sufficient structural form of Ψₜ.

This section is the meeting point of Section 9's two roads: universal necessity gives "any realization must contain the six-element structure"; this section gives "that structure is constructed, and is necessary and sufficient."

12\. Structural Necessity (the Complete Breach)

This section gives, item by item, the necessity of the six-element structure. Note its relation to Sections 3 through 8: this section is the universal argument (the lack of any item destroys the holding conditions of the attribution task); Sections 3 through 8 are its instances (each excluded mechanism class corresponds to the lack of at least one item here, or to conflict with the premises — see the locations at the end of each path).

If E is lacking, Ʃₜ cannot be generated, and no source-adjudication domain exists.

If Δ is lacking, a definable distinguishing function exists, and the system degenerates into deterministic mapping (Path Two).

If X is lacking, the attribution compression loses its independent ground of compression under the source gap; Λₛ no longer satisfies the non-degeneracy requirement (Form Chapter 7, Appendix Section 4), and no Mis-Externalization structure is constituted; deterministic mapping (bias wholly reduced to Γₜ, Path Two) and the stochastic process (no stable source of bias, Path Three) are merely its typical degenerate instances, not exhaustive of its forms.

If Λ is lacking, the multiple source interpretations cannot be compressed into a unique label, and no Mis-Externalization structure is constituted; the static set (Path Six) and the presupposition of structure-carriage (Path Five) are its typical appearances, not exhaustive of its forms.

If K is lacking, the attribution outcome cannot be maintained, and no stable "outside" forms (Path Three).

Hence the lack of any structure destroys at least one of: multi-source coexistence; the maintained source attribution (in its inherited form); temporal coherence; the non-reading generation of "the outside."

13\. The Limit Constraint

If the system cannot directly read the real external domain Ω (Section 3: the type-layer impossibility as principal ground, the necessity of translation as auxiliary), and:

\|Ʃₜ\| ≥ 2 ∧ Δₜ(Ʃₜ) ≠ ∅  
the maintained attribution is exactly one (inherited per Section 1: the conjunction of the carrier, form, and logical layers, not resting on the commitment alone)  
¬∃ f : Γₜ → L

then the result "of external origin" cannot come from: external reading, decision by current conditions, stochastic or unstable process, parallel holding, structure-carriage, or the static interpretation set.

Therefore, under the limit conditions above, "the outside" can only be generated by the Mis-Externalization structure (universal ground: Form Chapter 7's general theorem; instance verification: Sections 3 through 8), and that structure can only be realized as Externalization(sₜ) (Section 11, necessity and sufficiency).

14\. Main Theorem

When the following conditions hold:

sₜ is a maintained occupancy (that it is a single convergence form given by duty I of the General Form of the Observer; its existence resting on the commitment of the pool-level current single-value principle), and the attribution demand holds (Form Chapter 7, Section 3)  
\|Ʃₜ\| ≥ 2 ∧ Δₜ(Ʃₜ) ≠ ∅  
¬∃ f : Γₜ → L, ¬∃ f : Ω → L (the latter by Section 3: the type-domain of the determination functions does not contain Ω; the necessity of translation as auxiliary)

then:

(a) Existence: there necessarily exists a mechanism Ψₜ realizing the source attribution, with Ψₜ(sₜ) = l\*ₜ (the complete functional form, Section 10; the attribution is in fact formed and maintained — the construction per Form Chapter 7).

(b) Universal necessity (the load-bearing clause): every realization of Ψₜ possesses (E, X, Δₛ, Ʃₛ, Λₛ, Kₛ) at once, satisfying Λₛ ≺ Kₛ — that is, every realization is a Mis-Externalization structure, and Ψₜ ≅ Externalization(sₜ) (Form Chapter 7's general theorem, quantifying over all mechanisms, with no reliance on enumeration).

(c) Instance exclusion (corollary): in particular, Ψₜ belongs to none of the direct-reading, deterministic-mapping, stochastic-or-unstable, parallel-attribution, structure-carried, or static-set classes — the six classes each lack at least one item of (b), or conflict directly with the premises (the locations of Sections 3 through 8).

15\. Minimal Expression

sₜ —Ψₜ→ l\*ₜ (Ψₜ containing within it Ʃₜ, X, and the constraints of E and Δₜ)

with Ψₜ ≅ Externalization(sₜ).

If l\*ₜ = lₒᵤₜ, structure sₜ is judged "of external origin."

16\. Conclusion

Where the source gap, the attribution demand, and the attribution inheritance jointly hold, and direct reading and deterministic mapping are excluded:

"of external origin" is no presupposed relation, but a result generated by the Mis-Externalization structure — its warrant being the universal theorem of functional necessity, not an enumeration of mechanism types; the failures of the six substitute proposals are six instances of that theorem.

If a system cannot directly obtain the external, yet still forms the result "reality is outside," that result is necessarily Mis-Externalization, and its unique structural form is Externalization(s).

Form Chapter 7 proves that "the outside" can be constructed; this chapter proves that "the outside" cannot be read — it has no other source; it can only be constructed.
