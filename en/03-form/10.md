# General Form of Reality

This chapter attempts a general proposition:

Under the conditions of structure generation in existence, unique occupancy (this "unique" rests on the pool-level current single-value principle, a constitutive commitment; what this chapter actually requires, however, is that "the current is a single convergence form," which duty I of the General Form of the Observer gives unconditionally — see the load-bearing note of Section 4), and source not uniquely adjudicable, what is called "reality" is not a collection of self-standing objects, but the system's source-attribution closure over the current structure-family.

That is to say: reality is not a set of inputs, but the attribution closure over the current structure-family.

In the Mis-Externalization Forms we have proven: the "externality" of a single structure s arises from attribution compression under source non-adjudicability (Form Chapter 7); that attribution has no other source and can only be constructed (Form Chapter 8); and it has no ontic status (Form Chapter 9). This chapter extends the mechanism from the single structure to the structure-family H, and proves: when the system performs source attribution upon the whole current structure-family, and the resulting assignment is maintained over a positive time interval and the interpretation set satisfies minimality, the result constitutes "reality" (Section 10; how a minimal interpretation set is obtained is not yet proven in this chapter and is listed as matter to be proven (d)).

And this chapter will prove a stronger conclusion v1.0 could not give: the unitariness of reality is not a choice the system is forced to make, but the only possible form — the maintained current is a single convergence form whose constituents are jointly one rather than independent occupants standing side by side (duty I of the General Form of the Observer: the constituents of a composite current must be jointly one on the system's side; for its running form see the integration-running definitions of the Observer Mathematical Form), so the members of the structure-family cannot each hold an independent source label; if family-level attribution exists at all, it is necessarily single — "single" meaning one unified label assignment (values within the assignment may differ; made precise in Section 4).

Note of junction: the object occupying reality is the convergence-state. What is attributed as "outside" is no collection of objects independent of appearing, but exactly the converged imagery maintained as occupancy, with its constituent structures (for the formal definition of occupancy see the Observer Mathematical Form); attribution is defined on the appearing layer only (Form Chapter 22).

One sentence:

Mis-Externalization gives "where this s is taken to be from"; the General Form of Reality gives "how this whole current array comes to hold one unified source assignment" — and what the assignment marks as outer (H-outer) constitutes the object-face of present reality.

Abstract

The General Form of Mis-Externalization has explained: under source non-adjudicability, the system delivers a source attribution to the single structure s, and may judge it "of external origin." That attribution is no source-reading (Form Chapter 8) but a result of structural compression; its uniqueness and stability are inherited from the observer's occupancy (Form Chapter 7, Section 3).

This chapter takes the further step: the system's current is no bare single point, but a composed whole — the converged imagery maintained as occupancy is a structure-family holding together at once (object, background, and relational constituents jointly maintained within one convergence form). As soon as the system performs source attribution upon this structure-family, the attribution result no longer presents itself as local judgment, but as whole structure — that is, "reality."

The source of the wholeness must be stated precisely: the family's "joint presence" is secured by the singleness of the occupancy form (duty I of the General Form of the Observer: the constituents of a composite current are jointly one, hence the form is one; this is an unconditional necessary direction and does not rest on the at-most-one-per-pool commitment); the "singleness" of the family attribution is secured by the label's form-level standing (Form Chapter 7: the label dimension is a constituent dimension of the convergence form — one form, one maintained assignment) — but precisely: the content of that one assignment is a label assignment covering the whole family (the single label-map composed of each constituent's inner / outer value), with no requirement that all members take the same value. Hence the option "attribute member by member, then piece together" does not exist structurally — the members are not independent occupants; they have no individually maintained labels; their values are components of one and the same assignment.

The core conclusion: reality is not a collection of objects, but the maintained, minimal closure the current structure-family forms, under source non-adjudicability, through the attribution mechanism — the positive-time maintenance of the assignment and the minimality of the interpretation set are conditions of its standing, and this chapter does not prove that both are necessarily obtained (Section 10); its unitariness is an inherited consequence, not an added constraint.

Introduction

In the Mis-Externalization Forms we have explained: a structure s can be generated within the system and yet be judged of external origin. "Externality," then, is no property of the structure itself, but a source attribution the system lays upon it.

Yet a question remains: if only individual structures are judged external, do those attributions still hang upon an independently existing reality?

If the answer is "yes," reality is independent of the attribution mechanism, and local Mis-Externalization is only an added deviation atop it; if "no," it must be explained how the system, with no ready-made reality presupposed, forms a unified presence over a set of structures holding together at once.

So what this chapter truly asks is not "what objects does reality contain?" but: "when the system faces a set of structures holding together at once, how does whole attribution come to stand — is it a forced unification, or the only possible form?"

⁂

The stream of argument

We proceed by sustained questioning, step by step; the stream of argument forms accordingly, and is best read from the top.

1.

The system's "current" is no bare single point, but a composed whole.

Definition 1: the current structure-family H

H ⊆ S, H ≠ ∅. H is the constituent structure-family of the current occupied imagery c\*ₜ: the converged imagery maintained as occupancy is a composite form, and its constituents (object structures, background structures, relational structures) are jointly maintained as parts of that single form.

Anchoring and boundary: "holding together at once" means being co-maintained as constituents of a single convergence form — not multiple independent occupancies side by side — the joint oneness of the constituents is given by duty I of the General Form of the Observer (an unconditional necessary direction); as for "no several currents standing side by side within one pool," that rests on the pool-level current single-value principle (a constitutive commitment, unproven, open to empirical breach), not on a proven theorem. H is non-empty on the exactly-one regime (a maintained occupancy exists; the "exactly one" rests on that commitment); this chapter's attribution has as its working range the appearing layer (Form Chapter 22): boundary-fringe contents and the unmanifested within the boundary do not enter H.

Whence the first question: facing all the structures in H, could the system deliver a source for each separately, and form no whole attribution?

If it could, so-called reality would be no more than the juxtaposition of many individual source judgments; if it could not, whole attribution is structural. The answer comes at Section 4 — and it is stronger than "it could not."

2.

For the structure-family H, define the source interpretation structure.

Let P be the set of all possible generative paths, E(H) the source-interpretation domain of H, with E(H) ⊆ P.

Define: Ʃ\_H ⊆ E(H) ⊆ P. Ʃ\_H is the set of source paths capable of generating the structure-family H, with Ʃ\_H ∈ ℘(P).

And require: for every s ∈ H there exists at least one p ∈ Ʃ\_H generatively linked with s.

That is: if H has no interpretable source set at all, source attribution is not even in question; only when H has entered the interpretation domain does the problem of reality truly arise.

The question then advances: can the system read off, from Ʃ\_H, the unique source of the structure-family H? If it can, reality is merely a reading; if it cannot, reality must pass through compression.

3.

Definition 2: the source gap Δ\_H (a relational structure)

Δ\_H ⊆ Ʃ\_H × Ʃ\_H, with:

Δ\_H ≠ ∅ ⟺ ∃ (p₁, p₂) ∈ Δ\_H, p₁ ≠ p₂, and ∀ f ∈ F, f(p₁, C\_H) = f(p₂, C\_H)

where C\_H is the current-condition information set regarding the family H, and F the set of determination functions available to the system.

That is: at least two distinct paths are indistinguishable under the system's currently available conditions. Reading fails here (direct reading at the family level is as impossible as at the single-structure level — Form Chapter 8, Exclusion Paths One and Two).

4.

Once Δ\_H ≠ ∅, the source of the family H is not uniquely determinable. This is the section where v1.0 and this version diverge most; why the old argument is no longer used must be said first, and the new argument then given.

v1.0 argued here that "the system cannot refuse attribution": were the question of whole source unanswerable, "the current" could not stand as a whole, and reality could not stand. That argument carries two faults. First, the whole presence of "the current" is maintained by tuning occupancy (the candidate-running and integration-running definitions of the Observer Mathematical Form) and does not depend on source attribution — occupancy precedes attribution and is independent of it; attribution is the label-dimension component of the occupied imagery (Form Chapter 7, Section 3), not a condition of occupancy's standing. Second, the "source-attribution constraint" was in that argument effectively used as a premise — against the working direction of the Mis-Externalization Forms v2.0, which dismantle smuggled premises.

This version argues at two layers, with a stronger conclusion.

First, the existence of attribution is a conditioned inheritance. A system in which the attribution demand holds (distinguishing input from inner generation; forming source stratification; responding differently by source) must deliver a source attribution to the current — this is exactly the condition of Form Chapter 7, Section 3, needing no separate stipulation at the family level: attribution to the current is attribution to the current occupied imagery, and the current occupied imagery just is the form H composes. For systems without the attribution demand, see breach counter-example 6. This layer gives only the existence of attribution; the standing of Reality(H) further requires that the resulting assignment be maintained over a positive time interval and that the interpretation set satisfy (1.9) — both are listed as conditions of the proposition in Section 10, and neither is derived from the attribution demand.

Second, the unitariness of attribution is an unconditional inheritance — the core of this version. By duty I of the General Form of the Observer (the constituents of a composite current must be jointly one — a proven necessary direction) together with the integration running of the Observer Mathematical Form (full-member bidirectional coverage, no independent partition): the maintained current is a single convergence form. The members of H are not independent occupants (they are constituents of a single form), and therefore cannot individually hold maintained source labels — the option "attribute member by member, then piece into reality" is not forbidden; it does not exist structurally. The label dimension is a constituent dimension of the convergence form; the form is one, the maintained assignment is one, and that assignment natively covers the whole constituent family.

Hence: if family-level attribution exists at all, it is necessarily single — the precise sense of "single" being: at most one maintained label assignment, not at most one value. That one assignment is the value-map from the constituents of H to {lᵢₙ, lₒᵤₜ}, standing as a whole as the single maintained assignment of the form-level label dimension; within the assignment, different constituents may take different values. The mixed presence-format of the everyday — perceptual constituents judged outer and inner speech judged inner, coexisting within one imagery — is exactly the normal shape of a single assignment, not two attributions side by side; it corroborates this section's conclusion rather than countering it.

The unitariness of reality is thereby no compromise the system is forced into, but the only possible form — v1.0 asked "can the system refuse whole attribution"; this version answers: between whole attribution and item-by-item attribution there is no choice at all; only whole attribution (one assignment) is structurally possible.

5.

Definition 3: the attribution mapping Λ\_H

Λ\_H : ℘(P) × 𝒳 × Rel → L\_H, where Rel = {Δ \| Δ ⊆ P × P}

L\_H is the label-assignment space over H: the totality of value-maps from H to L = {lᵢₙ, lₒᵤₜ}. The single-structure two-valued space L (Form Chapter 7) supplies the pointwise values of an assignment; the family-level output is one whole assignment.

And require: for every (Ʃ, X, Δ), Λ\_H yields a unique value — a single-valued, totally defined mapping. Its single-valuedness is no new posit: it is the mapping-level expression of "the maintained assignment is exactly one" (Section 4, second layer).

Hence: Λ\_H(Ʃ\_H, X, Δ\_H) = l*, with l* ∈ L\_H. And write:

H-outer ≔ { s ∈ H : l\*(s) = lₒᵤₜ }

The phrase "the whole array taken as outside" must be read precisely: what is assigned as outer is H-outer; the object-face of reality is H-outer, and its structure-face is the closure of the whole maintained assignment.

Definition 4: the attribution bias X

X ∈ 𝒳: the internal biasing structure which, under Δ\_H ≠ ∅, makes Λ\_H able to complete its compression (a value in place; 𝒳 the bias space, notation per Form Chapter 7). This bias is the internal leaning of attribution compression; it is not the same object as the Continuation Bias B of Form Chapter 20, and this chapter does not substitute B for X.

If the source gap holds and the attribution can nonetheless be pressed to a single value, the completion of compression cannot come wholly from current conditions themselves — else the source were determinable and no gap would remain (the family version of Form Chapter 7, Lemma 1). Λ\_H is therefore no passive reading of Ʃ\_H, but a compressive decision upon Ʃ\_H with X taking part.

6.

Definition 5: the attribution convergence K\_H

K\_H : ℘(S) × L\_H → L\_H

Even with l\* given, if it appears only instantaneously, no reality is constituted. If reality is to stand, the attribution must not only be delivered, but must serve the current continuously. There must exist a positive time interval τ \> 0 such that:

if Λ\_H(Ʃ\_H, X, Δ\_H) = l*, then ∀ t ∈ \[t₀, t₀ + τ), K\_H(H, l*) = l\*

Its dynamical founding is the same as Form Chapter 7, Section 3, second inheritance: K\_H is the component of the tuning-convergence along the family's label dimension; τ falls within the occupancy-maintenance interval — the "sustained sense of presence" of reality shares one mechanism with the maintenance of occupancy; lose the maintenance, and reality's attribution disperses together with the occupancy.

7.

Definition 6: the appearance entry E

E(H) delimits the source-interpretation domain of the family H. Its work is not to deliver attribution directly, but to delimit first: which source paths are qualified to enter the interpretation of H.

Without E(H), Ʃ\_H has no bounds; without bounds on Ʃ\_H, Δ\_H cannot stand either; and if Δ\_H does not hold, the problem of reality has not yet truly arisen. E's working range coincides with the Observation-Domain (Form Chapter 22): only contents of the appearing layer enter the source-adjudication domain.

8.

By this point the family-level attribution mechanism has been forced out step by step. The system must possess:

E, Ʃ\_H, Δ\_H, Λ\_H, X, K\_H

With any one item missing, no reality can form upon H. For:

without E, the interpretation domain does not stand;  
without Ʃ\_H, the source set does not stand;  
without Δ\_H, there is no source gap, and attribution degenerates into reading (excluded by Form Chapter 8);  
without Λ\_H, there is no unified attribution;  
without X, compression under the gap loses its independent ground, and Λ\_H fails non-degeneracy (Form Chapter 7, Appendix Section 4);  
without K\_H, the attribution cannot be maintained.

That is: so-called reality is no object layer added atop these structures; it is exactly the closure result of this set of structures upon the current whole.

9.

The General Form of Reality (formal definition):

Reality(H) ⟺ ∃ E, X, Ʃ\_H, Δ\_H, Λ\_H, K\_H such that the following hold at once:

Ʃ\_H ⊆ E(H) ⊆ P, with Ʃ\_H ∈ ℘(P);  
Δ\_H ⊆ Ʃ\_H × Ʃ\_H, with Δ\_H ≠ ∅;  
Λ\_H : ℘(P) × 𝒳 × Rel → L\_H (the assignment space, Section 5);  
∃ l\* ∈ L\_H such that Λ\_H(Ʃ\_H, X, Δ\_H) = l\*;  
∃ τ \> 0 such that ∀ t ∈ \[t₀, t₀ + τ), K\_H(H, l*) = l*;

and moreover:

Ʃ\_H is not further reducible under the conditions required to maintain the current attribution result;  
the attribution forms the minimal closure upon H.

Notational remark: "closure" names the conjunction of the conditions listed (compression completed; maintained; interpretation set not further reducible), not some composite operator — the types of K\_H and Λ\_H do not compose (K\_H's input contains ℘(S) while Λ\_H's output is only an element of L\_H); the correct functional relation is l*(H) = Λ\_H(Ʃ\_H, X, Δ\_H), with K\_H(H, l*) = l\* holding on τ (the notational lesson of Form Chapter 8, Section 10).

Whence it can be read off directly: reality is not a collection of objects, but the maintained, minimal, unified attribution closure completed upon the whole current structure-family.

10.

Core proposition:

Where there exist a current structure-family H (the constituent family of a maintained occupancy; for the formal definition of occupancy see the Observer Mathematical Form, its bridge to CurrentFact being open), a source gap Δ\_H, and the attribution demand (Form Chapter 7, Section 3), and where the resulting assignment is maintained over a positive time interval and the interpretation set Ʃ\_H satisfies (1.9), Reality(H) holds. If attribution exists, its family-level unitariness is still argued by Step 3 (the unconditional part).

Proof:

Step 1: Δ\_H ≠ ∅ — at least two distinct source paths are indistinguishable over H; the source is not uniquely determinable.

Step 2: source not uniquely determinable — attribution cannot be completed by reading (the family versions of Form Chapter 8, Exclusion Paths One and Two apply).

Step 3 (unitariness, unconditional): by duty I of the General Form of the Observer together with the integration running of the Observer Mathematical Form, the maintained current is a single convergence form; the members of H are not independent occupants and have no individually maintained labels; hence attribution, if it exists, is necessarily a single assignment covering the whole family (at most one maintained label-map; values within the assignment may differ).

Step 4 (existence, conditioned): with the attribution demand holding, the system must form a source attribution to the current; by Form Chapter 7's construction and Form Chapter 8's universal necessity, that attribution is generated by the Mis-Externalization structure — at the family level: there exists a single-valued, totally defined Λ\_H that completes the compression with X taking part, yielding l\*.

Step 5: were l\* not maintainable over some positive interval, what is obtained is only an attribution flash, constituting no reality; by this proposition's positive-time maintenance condition, the resulting assignment l\* is maintained by K\_H over the declared interval. The mechanism shared by K\_H and occupancy maintenance, described in Section 6, places this maintenance within the occupancy-maintenance interval; this chapter does not separately prove that this condition necessarily holds.

Step 6: by this proposition's minimality condition, Ʃ\_H satisfies (1.9) (Appendix 1). This chapter has not yet proven that the system necessarily obtains such a minimal interpretation set from an arbitrary interpretation set; the existence of a minimal interpretation set, the manner of obtaining it and its relation to the actual attribution mechanism are listed as matter to be proven (d).

Therefore: under the conditions above, the system's attribution upon H constitutes a maintained, minimal, single attribution-closure structure. That closure structure is reality. ∎

11\. Corollaries

Corollary 1 (the unitariness of reality, testable).  
Within one pool there exist no two parallel "realities": two mutually distinct label assignments simultaneously maintained would mean the form-level label dimension holds two maintained assignments — excluded by Step 3. Note the precise target of unitariness: what is excluded is two assignments side by side, not mixed values within an assignment — the mixed presence-format (some constituents judged outer, some inner) is the normal content of a single assignment. The observable correspondence: even in multi-stable competition, the presence-format within any maintained interval is single — switching is the sequential replacement of assignments, not the coexistence of two realities (answering to the attribution face of the sequential-alternation case in the counterexample family of the Observer Group).

Corollary 2 (reality and occupancy co-disperse; cross-segment regeneration, testable).  
By Section 6: the maintenance interval of reality's attribution falls within the occupancy-maintenance interval. Hence in the manifestation-vacancy regime (Observer Mathematical Form, Theorems 7 and 11; the vacancy criterion is not r ≤ η but the non-existence of any realizable candidate–integration–maintenance path) no residue of "the world's presence" remains; where occupancy alternates sequentially, the sense of present reality alternates segment by segment. The cross-segment sense of "the same world" is no cross-segment survival of a single attribution, but the regeneration, through backflow, of sedimented attribution records (Form Chapter 7, Corollary 2): in each new segment of occupancy, the attribution is generated anew, continuous with prior records because of the backflow. Its measurable form: the cross-segment sense of world should bear backflow marks — driftable, re-adjudicable, varying with sedimentary depth (formal joints at Appendix 2, cross-time stability, and Appendix 3, the Collapse Form).

12\. The breach argument

Counter-example 1: Δ\_H = ∅. Source unique — attribution degenerates into reading; no reality structure stands (missing item: Δ\_H; answering to the family version of Form Chapter 8, Exclusion Path Two).

Counter-example 2: Ʃ\_H a singleton. No plural interpretation — no source competition; no attribution closure stands (missing item: the multi-interpretation condition).

Counter-example 3: no Λ\_H. No source adjudication — whole attribution cannot come about; no reality (missing item: Λ\_H).

Counter-example 4: no X. Under Δ\_H ≠ ∅, with no internal bias, compression loses its independent ground and Λ\_H fails non-degeneracy; no reality structure is constituted — deterministic mapping and stochastic mapping are merely typical degenerate instances of it, not exhaustive of its forms (missing item: X; wording in step with Form Chapter 8, Section 12).

Counter-example 5: no K\_H. Attribution cannot stabilize; merely instantaneous judgment; no reality (missing item: K\_H).

Counter-example 6: no attribution demand. If the system requires no source verdict on the current at all, the concept of reality is absent altogether — this counter-example marks out the scope of the core proposition's existence part (Section 10, Step 4; the unitariness part is untouched: where there is no attribution, there is no multiple attribution either).

Counter-example 7: reality larger than the current appearing? The intuitive rejoinder: "my present reality contains the room behind me and the world beyond the city, and they are not appearing." Handling: present reality = the maintained assignment-closure upon H, and its range indeed does not exceed the appearing layer; the room and the city are not in presence — they constitute, in the form of sedimented attribution records, a regenerable reserve (Corollary 2), and their "belonging to reality" is dispositional (re-enterable at any moment through backflow), not a currently maintained attribution. To mistake the reserve for presence is to take reality for a self-standing collection of objects — exactly the reading the general proposition denies. The intuition is therefore no counter-example, but a residue of the object-collection reading; the continuity it points to is taken up by Appendix 2's cross-time stability and the backflow regeneration.

Therefore: reality is the attribution closure that stands under the structural conditions Δ\_H ≠ ∅, Ʃ\_H non-singleton, Λ\_H compressible under the gap, K\_H maintaining the resulting assignment over a positive time interval, and Ʃ\_H satisfying (1.9); how a minimal interpretation set is obtained is not proven in this chapter (matter to be proven (d)).

13\. Closing

Compressed at last into three sentences:

Objectification answers "what is generated";  
the observer answers "what is there now";  
the General Form of Reality answers "how this whole array receives one unified source assignment."

Pressed into the plainest lines:

Without Objectification, nothing can be generated;  
without the observer, nothing appears here;  
without attribution, there is no reality to speak of.

## Appendix 1: The General Form of Reality (formalized)

Let S be the structure space, P the path space, T the time domain.

(1.1) The current structure-family  
H(t) is the constituent structure-family of the converged imagery c\*ₜ maintained as occupancy at moment t (for the formal definition of occupancy see the Observer Mathematical Form; the joint oneness of the constituents is given by duty I of the General Form of the Observer), H(t) ⊆ S, and H(t) ≠ ∅ on the exactly-one regime (the "exactly one" resting on the pool-level current single-value principle, a constitutive commitment). v1.0 gave H(t) = C(t) by a choice function C : T → ℘(S); this version anchors the content of C to the observer mechanism (for its definitional standing, see Appendix 8).

(1.2) The source set  
Ʃ(H) ⊆ P. Existence: ∀ H ⊆ S, if H ≠ ∅, then ∃ Ʃ(H) ⊆ P such that ∀ s ∈ H, ∃ p ∈ Ʃ(H) with (p, s) ∈ R.

(1.3) The linkage relation  
R ⊆ P × S, satisfying ∀ s ∈ H, ∃ p ∈ Ʃ(H) with (p, s) ∈ R.

(1.4) The source gap  
Δ(H) ⊆ Ʃ(H) × Ʃ(H), and Δ(H) ≠ ∅ ⟺ ∃ p₁ ≠ p₂ ∈ Ʃ(H) satisfying the conjunction of two conditions:  
first, joint sufficiency: ∀ s ∈ H, (p₁, s) ∈ R and (p₂, s) ∈ R;  
second, adjudicative indistinguishability: ∀ f ∈ F, f(p₁, C\_H) = f(p₂, C\_H).  
Unification note: the v1.0 appendix defined the gap by joint sufficiency alone, inconsistent with the main text's adjudicative-indistinguishability definition; this version unifies by the conjunction (joint sufficiency securing that both are candidate sources of H; indistinguishability securing that the system has no way to pick).

(1.4′) Structural constraint  
The adjudicative-indistinguishability relation (equality of all function values) is an equivalence relation on Ʃ(H) (the all-values-equal relation being reflexive, symmetric, transitive); Δ(H) is contained in its off-diagonal part.

(1.5) The attribution mapping  
Λ : ℘(P) × 𝒳 × Rel → L\_H, Rel = {Δ \| Δ ⊆ P × P}; L\_H = (H → L) is the label-assignment space, L = {lᵢₙ, lₒᵤₜ} the single-structure two-valued space (Form Chapter 7).  
Λ is a single-valued, totally defined mapping: ∀ (Ʃ, X, Δ), ∃ unique l ∈ L\_H. Its single-valuedness is founded by inheritance (main text, Section 4, second layer), not newly posited.  
Define: l*(H) = Λ(Ʃ(H), X, Δ(H)); H-outer = { s ∈ H : l*(s) = lₒᵤₜ }.

(1.6) The convergence mechanism  
K : ℘(S) × L\_H → L\_H. K is the component of the tuning-convergence along the family's label dimension (main text, Section 6).

(1.7) Stability (with time anchor)  
Stability(H, l; t₀) = sup { τ \> 0 \| ∀ t ∈ \[t₀, t₀+τ), K(H(t), l) = l }  
Convention: when this set is empty, Stability(H, l; t₀) ≔ 0; hence Stability(H, l; t₀) \> 0 means that the label has a positive candidate maintainable duration. Maintainability is a property at the candidate layer; it is not the same as the label being selected by Λ and actually maintained.

(1.8) Optimal attribution (a realization-layer clause, going with (1.8′); not part of (1.11))  
l\*(H(t₀)) ∈ argmax\_{l ∈ L\_H} Stability(H, l; t₀), satisfying ∃ l ∈ L\_H with Stability(H, l; t₀) = sup\_{l′ ∈ L\_H} Stability(H, l′; t₀). To secure the definability of argmax, stipulate: L\_H is finite (with H finite, \|L\_H\| = 2^\|H\|), or Stability attains its supremum on L\_H.

(1.8′) The coherence constraint  
Λ(Ʃ(H), X, Δ(H)) ∈ argmax\_{l ∈ L\_H} Stability(H, l; t₀).  
Standing note: this clause is a realization-layer hypothesis, not a logical necessity — it states the realization manner of the bias X (attribution compression leans toward the label most stably maintainable). Its proposed motivation is maintenance cost: if the more stable were cheaper to maintain, compression would lean toward the most stable. But "the more stable is cheaper to maintain" is itself a condition still to be set up, and it does not follow from Field Chapter 11: per Field Chapter 11 v1.2, familiar is a name for the deep, being more familiar is compared by Sedimentation-Degree δ, and Familiar-Route qualification rests on δ \> 0 and a frequent-travel record within the declared window; "low-cost continuation" requires a further condition that the continuation cost 𝒵 decreases (𝒵 cited from Field Chapter 4 v1.2), which that chapter does not set up, and its passages on the stability of reality are interpretive only. Familiar-route sedimentation therefore provides no ground for this clause. Logically, Reality(H) does not need (1.8)(1.8′)(1.10) (see (1.11)); these three characterize attribution's optimizing realization in the dynamics. Listed as matter awaiting formalization (a).

(1.9) Minimal closure  
Ʃ(H) is minimal: there exists no Ʃ′ ⊂ Ʃ(H) such that ① ∀ s ∈ H, ∃ p ∈ Ʃ′ with (p, s) ∈ R; ② Δ(H) ∩ (Ʃ′ × Ʃ′) ≠ ∅; ③ Ʃ′ still yields the same reality-attribution result as Ʃ(H).
