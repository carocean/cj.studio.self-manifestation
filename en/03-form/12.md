# Paradigm of the Self-Manifesting Being

I. General Proposition

Reality is not a collection of external objects, and "I" is not an entity.

Reality is the structure formed when differences are converged and unified through tuning and then receive one unified source assignment  
(for the attribution face, see Form Chapter 10: the object-face of present reality is what is judged outer within the assignment).

"I" is the result of the present being attributed to the current unique occupation.

II. Core Definitions

Difference: the distinction between circuits, the source of all structure.

Tuning: the convergence, suppression, and selection of differences, by which stable structures are formed.

Observation-Domain: the structure in which circuits are organized and aligned through tuning, determining whether manifestation is established. This is the mechanism sense of the term (for its field-level realization, see Field Chapter 20; for the single-circuit case, see the degeneracy explanation in Section 5 of that chapter; it is not limited to multiple circuits). The set sense of Observation-Domain, namely the manifestable domain W, the set of what can be lit up, is given in Form Chapter 22; for the correspondence among the several references of the term, including the mechanism sense, the set sense, and the eligibility sense in the Inner Chapters, see Field Chapter 20, Section 12.

Becoming-one: the state in which differences are unified into one object (through alignment among multiple circuits, or through sustained convergence in the single-circuit case).

Occupation: the structural center formed by tuning-convergence.

Current unique occupation: within the same tuning-resource pool, at the same moment, at most one occupation becomes the dominant center. This is not a definitional stipulation, nor a proven theorem, but the pool-level current single-value principle — a constitutive commitment openly undertaken and open to empirical breach (Observer Group v1.0); in a multi-pool system, each pool has its own occupation, but note that pool count, track count, module count, and output count are none of them observer counters (Mathematical Form of Observer Individuation), so the number of Self-Manifesting Beings may not be read off from the number of pools. Wherever this chapter uses that at-most-one, it holds within the model in which that commitment holds.

The present: the content attributed to the current unique occupation.

"I": the present attributed to the current unique occupation.

Coloring: a parallel structural mechanism that attaches to imagery and alters Biased-Flow and the direction of selection; by altering Biased-Flow, it directly participates in tuning results and thereby influences structural selection.

Observation of Observation: the re-observation of "the actions I am performing," whereby "I" becomes an observable object-structure.

Clarification of the two senses of attribution: the "attribution" spoken of in this chapter is occupation-attribution - which occupation-center a content belongs to, with "I" determined by its uniqueness. The "attribution" spoken of in the Mis-Externalization Forms (Form Chapters 7-9) and in the General Form of Reality (Form Chapter 10) is source-attribution - where a content is taken to come from, with "outer" determined by its generation. The two refer to different things: occupation-attribution answers "whose is it"; source-attribution answers "where is it from." One and the same manifest content simultaneously carries both attributions (belonging to this occupation, and being judged externally generated), and the two are not reducible to each other.

III. Generative Structure

Step 1: differences arise.

Step 2: tuning converges differences, causing signals to form imagery.

Step 3: imagery enters the Observation-Domain and completes alignment among circuits (in the single-circuit case, sustained convergence is taken directly).

Step 4: differences are unified, forming an object (becoming-one).

Step 5: convergence forms occupation and brings forth its content.

Step 6: this content is attributed to the current unique occupation.

Step 7: "I" is established.

Throughout the entire process, the coloring mechanism always operates in parallel, continuously influencing tuning and structural selection by altering Biased-Flow.

IV. Necessary Conditions

Without difference, there is no structure.

Without tuning, differences cannot form stable results.

Without the Observation-Domain, circuits cannot be unified into objects.

Without occupation, there is no content that can be attributed.

Without attribution, there is no present.

Without uniqueness, there is no "I."

So long as the current unique occupation does not exist, "I" cannot arise.

V. Levels of the Self-Manifesting Being

Illumination level: manifestation begins to occur. Intention drives tuning, causing signals to be converged and to form imagery. The precise state in which "occupation has appeared but I has not yet stabilized" is characterized by a three-segment chain: convergence contact (z reaches equilibrium; instantaneous contact does not constitute candidate completion, which must be sustained through the completion window, Observer Mathematical Form, Definition 45) → occupancy maintenance (the contact is continuously held in place by resource allocation; occupation is precisely this maintained structural center; that its constituents are jointly one is given by duty I of the General Form of the Observer, while the at-most-one is given by the commitment of the pool-level current single-value principle) → attribution stability (the occupation-attribution of "I" is a constituent adjudication of the occupied imagery, and its stability interval falls within the occupancy-maintenance interval; if maintenance has not been established, attribution has not stabilized - this is isomorphic with the inheritance of stability in source-attribution, Form Chapter 7, Section 3). The illumination level lies in the earlier segment of this chain: contact has occurred, maintenance has initially been established, but attribution has not stabilized; therefore "I" is in an unstable state. The attribution level is the point at which this chain has run to completion.

Object-formation level: objects are established. Circuits enter the Observation-Domain and complete alignment; differences are unified, forming stable objects; their status as present reality is formed through source assignment (Form Chapter 10), and the object-face thereby stands as "outer."

Attribution level: "I" becomes stably established. Structure is attributed to the current unique occupation; the present is established; "I" stabilizes, forming the structure "I am in this world."

Coloring mechanism: it runs through all levels. Coloring operates in parallel with the several circuits and continues to act within each level, causing reality to acquire liking and aversion, approach and avoidance, and preference, thereby determining the direction of behavior.

Observation-of-Observation level: self-awareness is established. "I" becomes an object-structure that can be observed again, thereby enabling behavior to be examined and changed.

VI. Level Relations

The order of generation is:

Illumination → Object-formation → Attribution → Observation-of-Observation.

Coloring does not constitute an independent level, but functions as a parallel mechanism running through all levels and continuously strengthening them.

The levels stand in a progressive relation: each later level unfolds upon the preceding level and enhances its structural capacity.

VII. Paradigm Definition

The Self-Manifesting Being is not a question of whether it exists, but of the level to which it has unfolded.

Illumination makes self-manifestation occur; object-formation establishes the world; attribution stabilizes "I"; Observation of Observation establishes self-awareness.

The operational determination of a Self-Manifesting Being depends only on whether the current unique occupation is formed. It must be separated from individuation-determination: operational determination answers "whether manifestation is occurring at this moment" (whether there is a maintained occupation); individuation-determination answers "how many Self-Manifesting Beings there are in the system" (to be settled by pool-track adjudication together with the adjudication of a final common position, and not read off from the number of pools), and its criterion is the condition of pool separation (the layered pool-track definitions and the pre-identification protocol of the Mathematical Form of Observer Individuation, Section 6; for the field-level basis, see Field Chapter 14); note that this protocol presently stands at framework grade with its calibration open (Observer Adjudication Form H5, half-closed), and that the number of pools yields only the number of individuation-unit candidates N\_cand, the number of Self-Manifesting Beings requiring in addition the adjudication of a final common position. This chapter concerns the former.

The minimal threshold can therefore be stated clearly, without being confused with "degree of unfolding": a system is a Self-Manifesting Being if and only if it has at least one independent tuning-resource pool (individuation-determination), and a maintained occupancy is formed within that pool (operational determination) - this is the threshold of the illumination level. The level to which it unfolds is determined, level by level, by a structural checklist: the object-formation level additionally requires the establishment of an alignment mechanism (Field Chapter 24 conditions); the attribution level additionally requires an occupation-attribution mechanism and its maintenance; the Observation-of-Observation level additionally requires a pathway through which "I" can re-enter as a candidate structure (Section 9). Whether a concrete Lodging-Vessel - an insect, an octopus, or an artificial system - crosses the threshold is an empirical question, not a definitional one: the criterion is measurable (the pool criterion and convergence interference; see the pre-identification protocol of the Mathematical Form of Observer Individuation and the criterion grid of the Observer Adjudication Form — presently at candidate-protocol grade, indications not impersonating adjudication), and is not decided by intuition, species, or substrate.

Extension statement: this threshold is deliberately set broadly, and its consequence is accepted by this chapter as part of the definition, not as a cost. A Self-Manifesting Being means the minimal system in which manifestation occurs - it is not equal to a human being, not equal to self-awareness, and not equal to advanced consciousness. If a bee, a fish, or some artificial system satisfies the pool condition and maintained occupancy, then it is a Self-Manifesting Being in the sense of the illumination level; this extensional criterion (pool + maintained occupancy) is consistent with the individuation-unit-candidate reading of the Observer Group — it yields unit candidates and does not directly yield an observer count. One must note how this criterion is to be used: it specifies what is to be tested, not what the test will find. Take the octopus arm as an example: whether the arm is a law-worker (a reflex pathway that executes purely according to rules) or a micro-observer depends on whether its neural circuitry is an independent tuning-convergence closed loop, or a fixed stimulus-response mapping. Facts such as the abundance of local neurons and the detached arm's local reactions are equivocal and insufficient to distinguish the two; the distinction must be made by whether there exists local convergence competition and a maintainable selection of one. This chapter draws no conclusion about that instance, but only lists it as a test case for the criterion. Higher-order capacities are distinguished by the levels: only at the attribution level is there "I"; only at the Observation-of-Observation level is there self-awareness. Thus "whether it is a Self-Manifesting Being" and "how far it has unfolded" belong to two separate questions; to separate these two questions is precisely the purpose of this paradigm. If it becomes necessary in the future to establish a higher criterion for a "human-level Self-Manifesting Being," that would be a subdivision internal to the levels (the satisfaction conditions of the attribution level and the Observation-of-Observation level), not a raising of the threshold.

The paradigmatic character of this chapter consists in three things: the generative chain (Section 3: how it proceeds toward "I"), the level classification (Sections 5 and 6: how far it has unfolded), and the determination criteria (this section: where the threshold lies, how many Self-Manifesting Beings there are, and to which level they have unfolded).

VIII. Conclusion

Without the current unique occupation, "I" cannot arise.

The present: the content attributed to the current unique occupation.

"I" is determined solely by the uniqueness of occupation-attribution.

The world is not a collection of perceived objects, but the result of circuit-differences being unified within the Observation-Domain and then receiving one unified source assignment (Form Chapter 10).

Self-manifestation is not a switch, but a continuous unfolding process from manifestation to self-awareness.

IX. Terminological Correspondence and Theorem Sources

This chapter is a programmatic chapter. The theoremized versions and field-level realizations of its terms are distributed across the other chapters. The correspondences are as follows:

Current unique occupation ↔ maintained unique occupancy (the pool-level current single-value principle: at most one per pool, exactly one under the condition that convergence is reachable — a constitutive commitment, unproven, open to empirical breach; that its constituents are jointly one is given unconditionally by duty I of the General Form of the Observer); illumination ↔ occupancy established (z = 1 and maintained; for the formal definition of occupancy see the Observer Mathematical Form, Definition 45, and note that its bridge to CurrentFact is open — General Form of the Observer H3, Observer Realization Form H4 — so this correspondence is a working convention); occupation replacement ↔ sequential replacement of occupancy.

Observation-Domain (mechanism sense) ↔ ObsRealm in Field Chapter 20 (the four conditions of Boundary-Gate, manifestation circuit, alignment, and continuation-flow); Observation-Domain (set sense) ↔ W(t) in Form Chapter 22 (the manifestable domain among the three layers of eligibility domain, manifestable domain, and boundary-fringe); the Observation-Domain of the Inner Chapters (the set of opened Boundary-Gates) corresponds to the eligibility face; for the relation in which the three references are to be read together, see Field Chapter 20, Section 12.

Becoming-one ↔ convergence completed and maintained (Observer Mathematical Form: candidate completion and integration running; Field Chapter 20: in the multi-circuit case, Align ≥ Align\_min maintained for τ\_align; in the single-circuit case, sustained convergence).

Reality ↔ the closure of a maintained unified source-assignment over the current structure-family (Form Chapter 10; the object-face is H-outer, and the cross-segment sense of world is regenerated through sedimented-attribution backflow).

Source-attribution (inner / outer labels) ↔ the output of the Mis-Externalization structure (Form Chapters 7-9: capable of being constructed, incapable of being read, and having no ontic status); it is distinct from the occupation-attribution of this chapter (the clarification of the two senses in Section 2).

Coloring ↔ one parallel realization face of the orientation source R and the scope-of-action limitation L (R and L now stand in the candidate placement table of the Strengthened Form of Current Generation, both resting on the supplementary proof of its H5 and not raised to necessity; their complete dynamics is given in the relevant chapters elsewhere).

Observation of Observation ↔ the case in which "I" re-enters as a candidate structure (self-referential manifestation; its occupancy and attribution mechanisms have the same form as those of ordinary content, and no separate structure is posited).
