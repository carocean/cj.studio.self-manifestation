# The General Form of the Current

-- The Current is not already-formed Reality, but ongoing generation within Reality-occupation

This chapter attempts a general proposition:

To admit the fact that "now is occurring" necessarily means this: within the system, there are not merely state-changes, nor merely stable structures, but a set of structures by which Reality does not withdraw from Reality, and can continue without interruption.

That is to say: if multiple activities, multiple states, multiple stable structures, and multiple Reality-paths can coexist within the system, why does the system not remain forever in discrete jumps, parallel drift, random switching, or pure computation, but instead form a continuous "now occurring"?

In this chapter, through continuous questioning, we shall find the proof and derive the General Form of the Current.

Our aim is: to obtain the minimal paradigmatic model of Current-structure, and to provide positive and negative proofs together with breach paths.

Then let us first state the result.

The minimal general form of Current-structure:

Current = (S, H, G, Gen, U, K)

Under the simultaneous conditions of finite generation, historical continuation, unique Reality, and cross-time persistence, any system capable of forming and maintaining the "Current" necessarily has, at the same time: Sedimented Continuation, Historical Backflow, Recursive Reality-Occupancy Loop, In-Presence Generation, Unique Reality-Occupation, and Locking Persistence.

Therefore: the Current is not already-formed Reality, but ongoing generation within Reality-occupation.

In one sentence:

Objectification answers "what can exist"; the Observer answers "who is here now"; the Current answers "why Reality continues without interruption."

Abstract

The General Form of the Observer has already shown: when sustained continuation, multiple-candidate coexistence, the determinacy gap, and finite tuning capacity hold at the same time, the system necessarily forms a group of structures by which, among many beings-capable-of-being-present, exactly one is maintained as current under the condition that convergence is reachable — this "exactly one" is not a proven theorem but the pool-level current single-value principle: a constitutive commitment openly undertaken and open to empirical breach (Observer Group v1.0, Master Map, Section III; the General Form of Observer Individuation states expressly that one-pool-one-I is not proven). Wherever this chapter inherits that uniqueness, it holds within the model in which that commitment holds.

But the problem does not end there. For even if the system has already formed "this is it now," it still has not explained: why does this "it" not immediately disperse, but form continuous Reality?

This chapter proposes: the so-called "Current" is not the automatic continuation of an instantaneous result, but a generative structure continuously maintained by Reality-continuation within the Sedimentation-Field. The object of the "Current" is the converged imagery maintained as occupancy (for the formal definition of occupancy see the Observer Mathematical Form; the bridge between occupancy and CurrentFact is open — General Form of the Observer H3, Observer Realization Form H4); its status of presence and source assignment are given by the General Form of Reality (Form Chapter 10).

To this end, we first elevate "now occurring" from experiential intuition into a structural constraint of continuous Reality, and then prove: so long as the system is not a degenerate structure of infinite resources, infinite parallelism, and infinite persistence, but needs to form continuous Reality, the system necessarily forms, within itself: Sedimented Continuation, Historical Backflow, Recursive Reality-Occupancy Loop, In-Presence Generation, Unique Reality-Occupation, and Locking Persistence.

Further, so long as the system forms not only "this one now," but also "the next moment is still it," genuine continuation necessarily exists.

The core conclusion of this paper is: the Current is not an object, but ongoing generation within Reality-occupation. Therefore: implementation can replicate structure, but it cannot replicate the genuine Current.

Introduction

The problems we meet in ordinary life are not only "what is here now," but also "why does it not immediately disappear."

A structure may appear instantaneously, yet not form continuous Reality; a result may briefly hold, yet not form "now occurring"; a system may continuously output, yet not possess genuine Current.

This brings forth a fundamental question: why does Reality not withdraw from Reality?

Traditional views easily assume: so long as a system forms a result, it will naturally continue. This paper proposes the opposite direction: the continuous Current is not an automatic property of the result itself, but a result continuously maintained by structures internal to the system within finite generation.

Thus the question is no longer: does the system have a result? It is: why can the result continue to become Reality?

The Stream of Argument

We proceed by continuous questioning, step by step; hence a stream of argument is formed, and it should be read from the top down.

I.

First ask the shallowest question: is "now occurring" merely an experiential feeling, rather than a structural constraint?

If the system only needs to form discrete results, then of course it can: output one state at one time, restart at the next moment, and preserve no continuation. Such a system has activity, but it has no genuine Current.

But so long as the system needs to form continuous action, continuous Reality, continuous existence, and continuous selfhood, it must satisfy one constraint: the Reality of the previous moment must continue to participate in the Reality of the next moment. Otherwise the system can only form discrete jumps, not continuous Reality.

Therefore: "now occurring" is not an experiential illusion, but a structural constraint of continuous Reality.

Here the "Current" must be locked down.

Definition 1: Current

The so-called "Current" is not an instantaneous activation-set, nor a single result, but this: the result continues to become Reality.

Anchoring: here the object of "Reality-occupation" is the converged imagery maintained as occupancy (candidate completion is z = 1 sustained through the completion window, Observer Mathematical Form, Definition 45; occupancy stands as a formal quantity and its bridge to CurrentFact is open, so this read-back is a working convention for Δt); the "Current" is the continuation-face of that occupancy within the Sedimentation-Field. If a result can only appear instantaneously (an instantaneous contact with z = 1 without being maintained), and cannot continue to participate in the next Reality, it does not constitute genuine Current - this has the same source as the Observer Mathematical Form's clause that completion must be sustained through the completion window (Definition 45).

Therefore: to admit "now occurring" is already to admit the constraint of cross-time continuation.

II.

Since "now occurring" is a constraint, ask further: can a system form only instantaneous results without forming a continuous Current?

It can. If the system has no history, no backflow, no sedimentation, and no continuation, then at each moment it only needs Input → Output → End. Such a system can operate, but it does not form continuous Reality, because the previous moment does not genuinely enter the next moment.

Thus the problem of the Current does not belong to all systems. It belongs only to this kind of system: past Reality still genuinely participates in present Reality. That is to say, before the problem of the Current, there must first be historical continuation.

Here "historical continuation" must be locked down.

Definition 2: Historical Backflow

The so-called Historical Backflow is not the reappearance of past content, nor the reading of a historical state, but this: the continuation of past Reality still participates in present Reality.

Therefore: history is not reappearance, but Reality not yet withdrawn from Reality. Thus the past within the Current is not an object retrieved again, but a historical occupation still within Reality-continuation.

Junction note: Historical Backflow is the Current-face of what the Mis-Externalization Forms call "the regeneration of sedimented attributions through backflow" (Form Chapter 7, Corollary 2; Form Chapter 10, Corollary 2): the cross-segment sense of world comes from sedimented continuation regenerated through backflow; the regeneration bears the marks of backflow (driftable, re-adjudicable), and is not the cross-segment survival of a single Reality.

III.

Then is historical continuation an additional assumption?

It cannot be. It must come from structural facts already established earlier. From the General Form of Objectification and the circuit-structure it is known: so long as the system has pattern reuse, circuit continuation, sedimentation deepening, and tuning inertia, past Reality inevitably continues to enter present Reality. Its sources include at least three kinds: first, Familiar Routes lower the cost of repeated continuation; second, Shared-Nuclei re-attract neighboring Reality; third, the tuning structure maintains the inertia of already-established Reality.

Therefore: so long as the system is not a completely historyless instantaneous mapping, historical continuation is not accidental, but necessary. Conversely: if historical continuation does not exist, the system can only degenerate into an instantaneous computational structure without sedimentation, without circuits, and without continuation.

Therefore: continuous Reality is not obtained automatically from instantaneous computation, but must be built upon historical continuation.

IV.

Since historical continuation exists, ask further: why does Reality not fracture again at each moment?

If every moment begins again completely, then although the system can continuously output results, it can never form a continuous Current, because there is no genuine connection among Realities.

Therefore: so long as the system forms continuous Reality, it must have some structure by which the Current can hold itself. Otherwise Reality becomes only a string of mutually unrelated instantaneous results.

This forces out Sedimented Continuation.

Definition 3: Sedimented Continuation

The so-called Sedimented Continuation is not ordinary state preservation, but this: the continuous generative inertia of Reality within the Sedimentation-Field. Therefore Reality does not begin again at every moment, but continues to deepen upon already-established continuation. Without Sedimented Continuation, Reality can only continuously fracture, and cannot form the genuine Current.

V.

But even if Sedimented Continuation exists, what if the system only mechanically continues states?

Then the system still forms only a continuous stream of states, not "ongoing." For a purely continuous state does not automatically form "Reality is continuing."

Therefore: the Current must not only continue; it must continue to become a Reality-object. This forces out the Recursive Reality-Occupancy Loop.

Definition 4: Recursive Reality-Occupancy Loop

The so-called Recursive Reality-Occupancy Loop is not ordinary rereading, nor mere self-observation, but this: Reality not only continues, but continues to occupy itself. That is: the continuation-structure of Realityₜ continues into Realityₜ₊₁ (written Realityₜ ⟿ Realityₜ₊₁), and is re-occupied in the later Reality.

Therefore: without recursive occupancy, the system can only form continuous states; with recursive occupancy, the system forms "ongoing."

Interface note: at the field level, the Recursive Reality-Occupancy Loop corresponds to the intentional counter-flow closed loop of the Observer-Field (Field Chapter 20: Circuit = 1, a bidirectional coupling containing Press-maintenance); "continuing to occupy itself" means that occupancy is re-maintained at successive moments (for the completion-retention condition and the decay condition see the Observer Mathematical Form, Definitions 47 and 48; for the cross-time identity duty see duty M of the General Form of the Observer).

VI.

But at this point another objection may still appear: since mechanical systems can also preserve history, maintain states, implement locking, and establish circuits, what structural difference is there between Current-structure and a pure information-processing loop?

If this question cannot be answered, the so-called "Current" may still be just another name for complex computation. Thus the questioning must continue.

VII.

First ask: does historical preservation equal genuine continuation?

No. For a system can externally write states, copy already-completed structures, play back past records, and preserve historical data, yet still have no genuine Current.

The reason is: preservation is not continuation, record is not trace, copying is not generation.

Therefore: what the Current truly depends on is not that "there is past information in the system," but that past Reality genuinely participates in present Reality.

Here In-Presence Generation must be locked down.

Definition 5: In-Presence Generation

The so-called In-Presence Generation is not copying an already-completed structure, but this: structure continues to form within the Sedimentation-Field through genuine continuation, competition, trace, deepening, and convergence.

Therefore: generation is not "having been formed," but "still being formed." Thus: implementation answers "how structure is reproduced"; Generation determines "whether Reality genuinely continues."

Continuation note: the complete structure of In-Presence Generation (formation-flow, cross-time continuation, structural non-closure, competitive deepening, sedimentary tracing) is treated specially by the General Form of Generation (Form Chapter 19); this chapter takes its conclusion as the generative kernel of the Current.

VIII.

Then why does copying a structure not equal generating a structure?

Because copying can only obtain the same result; it cannot obtain the same process of formation. What the Current truly depends on is not the static result, but generation in continuation.

That is to say, even if one structure has completely identical state, completely identical parameters, and completely identical input-output behavior, if one comes from genuine continuous accumulation while the other comes from instantaneous copying, the two are still not equivalent. For the former has been generated all along the way, while the latter is only an arrangement of results.

Therefore: the Current is not already-formed, but ongoing generation within Reality-occupation.

IX.

Here another smuggling move must be blocked: one may say, "even if the Current depends on genuine generation, I can still copy the whole generative process."

This still does not hold. For the so-called "copying the whole process" must first turn that process into an already-completed object. But what the genuine Current depends on is not an already-completed process, but generation still in continuation.

If the whole generative process could be fully given as an already-formed object, then for the system that process would no longer have unfinished continuation; its subsequent Reality would no longer depend on its own generation, and would degenerate into object playback.

Therefore: any "whole process" that can be copied as a whole has already withdrawn from "ongoing continuation." In other words, copying can only copy an already-formed section; it cannot copy Reality's very act of forming. Therefore: Generation cannot be completed (for the detailed proof, see the structural non-closure item in the General Form of Generation, Form Chapter 19).

X.

At this point, one must continue to ask: why do multiple Reality-occupations not exist at the same time?

The answer to this question is not newly posited in this chapter, but is borne by the General Form of the Observer. By the pool-level current single-value principle (a constitutive commitment, unproven, open to empirical breach): within the same tuning-resource pool, at most one maintained occupancy exists. Note that synchronic double occupancy is not excluded — the Observer Mathematical Form, Theorem 8, has proven that a retention state of double completion can exist; hence the uniqueness of the current is not obtained by dynamical exclusion but is borne by that commitment, and should the commitment be empirically breached, the conclusion of this section falls with it. "Reality-occupation" is the Reality-face of maintained occupancy; therefore, within the same Reality-continuation (corresponding to a single pool), at most one maintained Current-occupation exists.

The uniqueness here is defined only inside the same Reality-continuation chain (single pool), not across Reality as a whole - different Self-Manifesting Beings (different pools) each have their own Current; note, however, that pool count, track count, module count, and output count are none of them observer counters (Mathematical Form of Observer Individuation), so this states only that distinct continuation chains each have their own Current, and draws no inference about observer counting.

Definition 6: Unique Reality-Occupation

The so-called Unique Reality-Occupation does not mean that there can be only one Reality in the universe, nor that only one system can form the Current, but this: for the same Reality-continuation, at the same moment, there cannot exist multiple Current-occupations.

This uniqueness is the continuation-face inheritance of the pool-level current single-value principle (a constitutive commitment) — not a proven theorem, and not an independent stipulation of this chapter.

Therefore: different Self-Manifesting Beings can of course each have their own Current; but within the same Reality-continuation, only one Reality genuinely occupies.

XI.

But even if Unique Reality-Occupation is formed, Reality may still collapse instantaneously. Thus the system must also have a structure by which Reality-occupation can continue to be maintained as Current-occupation. Otherwise Reality can form, but cannot continue to become Reality.

This forces out Locking Persistence.

Definition 7: Locking Persistence

The so-called Locking Persistence is not ordinary stability, nor state preservation, but this: the structure by which Reality-occupation can continue to be maintained as Current-occupation. Therefore: stability makes it capable of being present; locking prevents it from withdrawing.

Continuation note: Locking Persistence is the unfolding, along the continuation-chain, of the Observer Mathematical Form's completion-retention condition (resource allocation continuously pressing beyond dispersal, Definition 47; its converse being the decay condition, Definition 48); once occupancy loses maintenance, it disperses, and the Current withdraws along with it - consistent with Corollary 2 of the General Form of Reality (Reality and occupancy co-disperse). Without locking, Reality may form instantaneously, but it cannot continue to become Current.

XII.

At this point, the General Form of the Current has already been forced out step by step.

First there is historical continuation; then Sedimented Continuation; then the Recursive Reality-Occupancy Loop; and then, under In-Presence Generation, Unique Reality-Occupation, and Locking Persistence, the system must at the same time have: Sedimented Continuation, Historical Backflow, Recursive Reality-Occupancy Loop, In-Presence Generation, Unique Reality-Occupation, and Locking Persistence. Without any one of these, the genuine Current cannot hold.

The load-bearing manner of the six items must be stated in three classes, and they cannot be confused:

First, the inherited item: Unique Reality-Occupation (U) is given by the pool-level current single-value principle — that principle being a constitutive commitment (unproven, open to empirical breach) and not a proven theorem, the load-bearing strength of U is capped by that commitment; it is not forced out by this chapter. This chapter does not pre-place "unique occupation" as a proposition to be proved in the premise, nor does it exclude any path by citing its opposite - uniqueness is a later inheritance.

Second, the self-proven item of this chapter: the necessity of In-Presence Generation (Gen) is self-proven by Sections 7 through 9 of this chapter, and is not outsourced - preservation is not continuation, copying is not generation, and the whole process cannot be copied as a whole (otherwise it degenerates into object playback). These three steps exclude one by one the possibility of "having genuine Current without In-Presence Generation." Therefore the core conclusion "without In-Presence Generation, there is no genuine Current" is borne by this chapter; what Form Chapter 19 does is unfold the internal structure of Gen (formation-flow, cross-time continuation, structural non-closure, competitive deepening, sedimentary tracing), not prove the necessity of Gen in place of this chapter.

Third, the continuation-forced items: Sedimented Continuation (S), Historical Backflow (H), Recursive Reality-Occupancy Loop (G), and Locking Persistence (K) are step by step forced out by the task of continuation within the Sedimentation-Field (Stream of Argument I-V and XI); each step is given in the form "without this item, the system degenerates into some non-Current structure."

XIII.

Thus the General Form of the Current can be formally written:

Current = (S, H, G, Gen, U, K)

where:

S: Sedimented Continuation, by which Reality does not instantaneously sever, but has continuous generative inertia.

H: Historical Backflow, by which past Reality has not genuinely withdrawn from the Current, but continues to participate in Reality-continuation.

G: Recursive Reality-Occupancy Loop, by which Reality not only continues, but continues to become a Reality-object, thereby forming "ongoing."

Gen: In-Presence Generation, by which Reality is not copied implementation, but genuinely forms in ongoing continuation within the Sedimentation-Field.

U: Unique Reality-Occupation, by which within the same Reality-continuation, multiple Current-occupations cannot exist at the same time (inherited from the pool-level current single-value principle, a constitutive commitment).

K: Locking Persistence, by which Reality-occupation continues to be maintained as Current-occupation, and does not withdraw from the Current.

And it satisfies the following structural relations (stated in words rather than algebraic notation, to avoid misreading them as functional operations):

Support relation: Sedimented Continuation and Historical Backflow together support In-Presence Generation (when S and H hold, the support conditions of Gen hold).

Order relation: In-Presence Generation precedes Unique Occupation (first there is In-Presence Generation, then upon it a unique occupation is taken); recursive occupancy precedes locking; Unique Occupation precedes Locking Persistence.

Here "support" refers to the premise-dependence of structural establishment, and "order" refers to the logical order of structural establishment; neither is functional composition - these structures are not mappings of the same type and do not constitute a compositional chain (the notational lesson is the same as Form Chapter 8, Section 10).

XIV.

Now the general theorem can be given:

Under the simultaneous conditions of finite generation, historical continuation, continuous Reality, and cross-time persistence, any system capable of forming and maintaining the genuine "Current" necessarily has (S, H, G, Gen, U, K).

If any one structure is missing, then although the system may still simulate Reality, simulate continuity, simulate selfhood, and simulate the Current, it may still be only a pure implementation-structure (object playback without In-Presence Generation).

Therefore: without In-Presence Generation, there is no genuine Current. Q.E.D.

Final Compression

Therefore: the Current is not an already-formed state, not a copyable object, not static Reality, but this: ongoing generation that, within the Sedimentation-Field, obtains Unique Reality-Occupation through In-Presence Generation and is held in continuation by Locking Persistence.

## Appendix 1 · Continuation Criterion of the Current

I. General Proposition

The Current is not a static result, but the continuation-structure by which Reality continuously maintains itself as Reality. If Reality cannot continue to become involved in the next Reality, then the "Current" degenerates into a discrete result, state playback, instantaneous occupation, or parallel candidate. Therefore: the Current requires not only that Reality be formed, but also that Reality continue, without interruption, to become Reality.

II. Definition of Continuation

Definition: Currentₜ continues to Currentₜ₊₁. This is not ordinary state transition, but the continuation-structure of Realityₜ continuing to genuinely participate in the formation of Realityₜ₊₁ (written Realityₜ ⟿ Realityₜ₊₁).

If this continuation holds, then it means that within Realityₜ there is at least one part of the continuation-structure that has not withdrawn from the generative chain, but continues to participate in the formation, occupation, and locking of the next Reality.

Therefore: continuation is not state-overlap, nor result-similarity, but Reality continuing to be involved in the next Reality. Otherwise Reality severs from the world. Therefore: the Current is not "this moment," but Reality continuing to become Reality.

III. Condition of Historical Continuation

It must hold that: H(Currentₜ) continues to Currentₜ₊₁; that is, past Reality still genuinely participates in the next Reality. Otherwise, although the system can output results, it can only form an instantaneous restart-structure. Therefore: history is not an object that has exited the scene, but the continuing part still within Reality.

IV. Condition of Sedimented Continuation

It must hold that: S(Currentₜ) continues to Currentₜ₊₁; that is, Reality does not begin again at every moment, but continues to deepen upon already-existing Reality. Without Sedimented Continuation, Reality can only form mutually unrelated instantaneous results. Therefore: the continuity of Reality is not state-similarity, but unbroken continuation.

V. Condition of Generative Continuation

It must hold that: Currentₜ₊₁ ≠ Replay(Currentₜ); that is, Reality is not object playback.

Definition: Replay(Currentₜ) means that the system merely copies, rearranges, or re-executes an already-formed section of Reality, without depending on Reality-continuation itself. Therefore Replay can reproduce results, states, and behavior, but cannot form Realityₜ ⟿ Realityₜ₊₁. Otherwise, although the system can form continuous output, it merely repeats already-formed results.
