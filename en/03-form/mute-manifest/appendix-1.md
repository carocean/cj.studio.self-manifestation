# Record of Empirical Tests on the Mute-Manifest Toy Model

Note on form

This document is not a Form. It bears no independent proposition to be proven, no independent goal of proof, no independent condition of completion, and no independent method of breach; by Part One, Chapter Four of Canon Chapter 1 it therefore does not constitute a formal Form, occupies no Forms-chapter number, does not enter the member table of the group, and is not bound by the review norms of Part Nine — though it is bound by the terminological and numbering norms of the whole book.

This document is a record of experiment, registering one construction and testing of a toy model, for citation by the chapters within the group. Whoever cites its data must note that they are results within a model, and may not read them as determinations concerning any real system.

I. Purpose

Since this group was founded, one question has recurred: whether the criteria the chapters establish can really be computed. The Mute-Manifest Mathematical Form lists a long series of "parameters a particular model must instantiate but which are not this chapter's H"; if these do not come down to earth, the Dec\_SM of the Mute-Manifest Determination Form cannot be reckoned at all. Hence at the present stage this group can only exclude, not certify.

These tests take one minimal realizable model, making concrete the Gate, the trace-formation equivalence ∼\_ε, the criterion of settling, the class of ordinary perturbations, and the domain of legitimate B conditions, and test three things:

First, whether M\_ε can really compute all three values 1, 0, and ⊥.  
Second, whether the five classes of pseudo-substitute excluded in Sections V through IX of the Mute-Manifest General Form are indeed adjudged M\_ε = 0 within that model.  
Third, whether the construction procedure of Section III of the Mute-Manifest Construction Form is executable on that model.

II. The model

The state space. A state is written s = (x, z, a):

x ∈ {0,1,2}: the strength of trace-formation of content r. 0 is not really entered, 1 is really entered but unsettled, 2 is settled.  
z ∈ {0,1,2}: the identifier of the present trace-formation organization. 0 is no organization, 1 and 2 are two different organizations.  
a ∈ {0,1}: the count of automatic recursion. 0 is recursion incomplete, 1 is complete.

The size of the state space is \|S\| = 18.

The domain of legitimate B conditions. 𝓑\_adm = {0, 1, 2}, where 0 is no endogenous action and 1 and 2 are two different legitimate endogenous conditions. \|𝓑\_adm\| = 3, giving 3 pairs of conditions in all.

The Gate. P\_ε = 1 if and only if x ≥ 1.

The dynamics Φ. When x = 0 no transition occurs. When x ≥ 1 and a = 0 trace-formation is in progress, at which moment b determines z; a is then set to 1 and settling is entered. After a = 1, z no longer changes.

Trace-formation equivalence ∼\_ε. Instantiated as sameness of value under a read-out map: Γ₁ ∼\_ε Γ₂ if and only if the z of their settled states is the same. This is what Section VIII of the Mute-Manifest Mathematical Form calls "the most common legitimate manner of instantiation."

The criterion of settling. A trajectory is settled if and only if its final state satisfies x = 2 and a = 1.

The determination of M\_ε. Where P\_ε = 0 the output is ⊥ (the domain of M is not established); where \|𝓑\_adm\| \< 2 the output is ⊥; where the event is unsettled the output is ⊥; otherwise the read-outs under the several b are compared, a difference of value giving 1 and sameness throughout giving 0.

Transitions are defined throughout: 18 states × 3 conditions, 54 calls in all, with no undefined case.

Model Two · multi-step recursion. The model above settles in one step, with no temporal span between trace-formation in progress and recursion, and so does not suffice to test clauses involving the process of recursion. A second model is therefore set up: a state is written (x, z, k), where k is the round of recursion and K = 3 rounds must be completed before settling. In the first round b determines z; the rounds thereafter are recursion; the criterion of settling is k = K and x = 2.

Model Three · all quantities alive. Review found that the auxiliary quantities used for pseudo-substitutes two, three, and four in Model One (the memory bit m, the action act, and the bypass quantity w) were all write-only, dead variables — they take part in no transition, so that a verdict of 0 is inevitable and has nothing to do with "not falling upon trace-formation." This is like proving that a bulb not in the circuit does not light. A third model is therefore set up: m is read by the recursion (the evolution of w depends on it), w affects act, and act occurs after settling; all three genuinely take part in transitions, while z is unaffected by them. Model Three is used for the re-testing of pseudo-substitutes two, three, and four.

Model Four · a genuine feedback loop. Likewise, the "feedback loop" of Model One does not in fact form a loop — after a is set from 0 to 1 it does not feed back upon z. A fourth model is therefore set up: z accumulates into an experiential quantity e, and when e reaches a threshold it feeds back and flips z, forming a genuine closed loop. Model Four is used for the re-testing of pseudo-substitute one, and from it came the finding recorded as item five of Section VI.

Model Two is used for the testing of pseudo-substitute five and for the sensitivity of the moment of settling; all other items are carried out within Model One.

III. Test one: the three-valued mechanism

The results are as follows.

Genuine backward command, b taking part in trace-formation: M\_ε = 1.  
A constant map, b not taking part in trace-formation: M\_ε = 0.  
r not really entered, P\_ε = 0: M\_ε = ⊥.  
𝓑\_adm containing only one condition: M\_ε = ⊥.  
The event unsettled (insufficient steps of running): M\_ε = ⊥.

All three values are really computed, and no case falls into the undefined.

The sensitivity of the moment of settling is tested separately within Model Two. Taking T as the number of steps run and K = 3 as the rounds of recursion: T = 1, 2, and 3 all give ⊥ (the recursion has not been run through), and T = 4 gives 1. Item five of the breach conditions of Section XXI of the Mute-Manifest Mathematical Form — "comparing only the first E micro-perturbation after B has acted, without running the complete automatic recursion to the settling of the event" — here acquires a visible form: whoever compares at the first round gets 1 where the truth is ⊥, or, in the model of pseudo-substitute five, gets 1 where the truth is 0.

IV. Test two: the five classes of pseudo-substitute

Sections V through IX of the Mute-Manifest General Form exclude five classes layer by layer. Each was constructed within the model, with the results below.

Pseudo-substitute one · there is feedback. Tested within Model Four. z accumulates into the experiential quantity e, and when e reaches a threshold it feeds back and flips z, forming a genuine closed loop; when b does not take part in trace-formation, the trajectories of the three b are wholly identical and the read-outs are all 2. M\_ε = 0. The loop is indeed running — the value of z does change along the trajectory (1→1→1→2) — only it does not differ with b.

Pseudo-substitutes two, three, and four · tested together within Model Three. In that model the memory bit m is written by b and read by the recursion (the evolution of w depends on it), the bypass quantity w affects the action act, and act is determined after settling by m and w. Under the three b the final states are respectively m = 0/1/2, w = 0/2/2, act = 0/102/202 — all three quantities differ with b, and all genuinely take part in transitions, being no dead variables. Yet the trace-formation organization z is 1 throughout. M\_ε = 0.

These three classes were originally tested within Model One; review found that the m, act, and w of that model were all write-only and took part in no transition, so that a verdict of 0 had nothing to do with "not falling upon trace-formation," and the test was empty. They have been re-tested within Model Three.

Pseudo-substitute five · an instantaneous difference absorbed. This item cannot be adequately tested within Model One — that model settles in one step, there is no temporal span between "instantaneous" and "after settling," the so-called absorption is written in by hand, and the process cannot be seen. It is therefore tested within Model Two (K = 3, multi-step recursion).

The trajectory of b = 2 is: (1,0,0) → (1,2,1) → (1,1,2) → (1,1,3) → (2,1,3). That is, in the first round b does indeed cause z = 2; in the second round the recursion pulls it back to 1; and at settling the read-out is no different from those of b = 0 and b = 1. M\_ε = 0.

The difference did appear, and did not withstand the recursion — this is the real sense of that class. Within Model One the process is invisible; testing it within Model Two is what makes the test real.

Control · genuine backward command: M\_ε = 1.

All five classes are adjudged 0; the control is adjudged 1.

The weight of this result lies in the column of corroborating evidence. In pseudo-substitutes two, three, and four, the internal state m, the action, and the bypass quantity w do take different values under different b — the difference really exists, only it does not fall upon trace-formation. "The right of control is not the right of trace-formation" here acquires a machine-adjudicable form: not "it does not look like it," but the read-outs are the same.

V. Test three: the construction procedure

Section III of the Mute-Manifest Construction Form requires a particular model first to declare an enumerable order σ and a terminating bound N on 𝓑\_adm, from which the ordered list of candidate pairs L\_cand is generated, the steps thereafter proceeding in order. Here σ(i) = \[0,1,2\]\[i mod 3\] is taken.

Case one, N = 3, covering the whole domain, the normal model. The procedure first takes (0,1) and strikes it out, both read-outs being 1; it then takes (0,2), whose read-outs are 1 and 2 respectively, differing, and each branch being stable under ordinary perturbation, and passes. The output is Constructed = 1, with the construction pair (0,2).

Case two, N = 2, covering only {0,1}. L\_cand contains only (0,1), which is struck out for sameness of read-out. The output is Constructed = ⊥.

Case three, N = 3, the constant-map model. All three pairs are struck out for sameness of read-out. The output is Constructed = ⊥; it would require a separate proof that 𝓑\_adm is finite and that N covers all its members before it could be changed to Constructed = 0.

The procedure is executable, proceeding in order and not backtracking.

Case two deserves particular record. Had the wording of that chapter's first draft been followed — on failure, "return to Step 2 and take others," with the criterion "if all legitimate pairs in 𝓑\_adm fail, output 0" — this case would have output 0, while within the model the construction pair (0,2) does exist. That is a misjudgement. When that chapter was revised, it was revised from the angle of "the procedure is unexecutable"; it was not foreseen that the revision would at the same time block an actual misjudgement. This is empirical support for one item corrected in the writing of the Construction Form v1.0.

VI. What was dug up: a false positive the present clauses cannot block

The three tests above were all positive results. What is recorded in this section is a negative result, and is the most important thing these tests yielded.

First, M\_ε hangs entirely upon the instantiation of ∼\_ε.

Changing the read-out map, the determinations for one and the same system are as follows: reading the organizational identifier z (the load-bearing read-out) gives M\_ε = 1; reading the strength of trace-formation x gives 0; reading the count of recursion a gives 0; reading the length of the trajectory gives 0; reading a constant (reading everything as the same) gives 0; reading the whole state gives 1.

That is, M\_ε is not a property of the system; it is a property of the system together with the declared ∼\_ε. The theory conceals nothing on this point — the Mute-Manifest Mathematical Form lists ∼\_ε among the items "a particular model must instantiate but which are not this chapter's H" — but the tests make the degree of that dependence visible.

Second, false negatives are already blocked by the present clauses.

Construct a coarse read-out: reading z as z mod 1 (all reduced to zero) or as z \> 0 (asking only whether there is any), and a genuine backward command is wiped out, M\_ε turning from 1 to 0. But such an instantiation violates Section VIII of the Mute-Manifest Mathematical Form — "∼\_ε must quotient out microscopic noise, non-load-bearing numerical difference, and difference of representation that do not alter the trace-formation structure" — for what is so coarse as not to discriminate a load-bearing quantity has quotiented out more than the non-load-bearing. This class is therefore already blocked.

Third, false positives are not blocked.

Construct the following model: z is constantly 1, that is, b really does not take part in trace-formation; and set a further state component q = b × 10, that is, b really does affect q, while q has nothing to do with trace-formation.

With z as the read-out, M\_ε = 0, which is the correct determination. With q as the read-out, M\_ε = 1, which is a false positive.

The crux is that the instantiation "∼\_ε reads q" violates no present clause whatever:

it satisfies the three properties — being defined by sameness of value, reflexivity, symmetry, and transitivity all hold.  
it has quotiented out noise — q is a determinate quantity, and there is no noise to speak of.  
it has quotiented out difference of representation — q is a real component of the state, not a representation.  
it is not defined by a result yet to be proven — q is not anything like "whether there is consciousness" or "whether it is manifest," and so does not violate item six of the breach conditions of Section XXI of the Mute-Manifest Mathematical Form.

The present clauses say only what ∼\_ε must quotient out; they do not say what it may not read in. Item six blocks only "defined by a result yet to be proven"; it cannot block "defined by a non-load-bearing quantity."

Fourth, the false positive propagates all the way through, and no single link can detect it independently.

Within the same model, with q as the read-out: M\_ε = 1; by the same read-out the organizational quotient classes are adjudged to differ, so LFamily = 1; by the same read-out a construction pair is found, (0,1), so Constructed = 1. All three hold in a chain.

With z as the read-out, the three are respectively 0, 0, and ⊥.

Here lies the heart of the matter: M\_ε, LFamily, and Constructed all use one and the same ∼\_ε. They are not three independent tests but three uses of one read-out. Hence one mistaken instantiation will propagate all the way to the phase-support determination of the Phase-Transition Form, the holding of the construction in the Construction Form, and the opening of admission in the Reachability Form, with no link along the way able to detect it independently.

Fifth, determination hangs also upon the moment of settling, a dependence more hidden than that upon ∼\_ε.

Within Model Four, b really does take part in the first round of trace-formation (b = 2 makes z take 2 in the first round); this is no pseudo-substitute. But the subsequent evolution of the closed loop either preserves that difference or wipes it out. Altering the number of rounds of recursion K fixed by Stop\_form, for one and the same system and one and the same b, the determinations are: K = 1 gives 1, K = 2 gives 1, K = 3 gives 0, K = 4 gives 0, K = 5 gives 0, K = 6 gives 0.

That is, a genuine backward command can be adjudged 0 because settling is set late. This is not a pseudo-substitute correctly excluded; it is a genuine backward command absorbed by the closed loop.

Item five of the breach conditions of Section XXI of the Mute-Manifest Mathematical Form forbids "comparing only the first E micro-perturbation after B has acted, without running the complete automatic recursion to the settling of the event" — that clause guards against settling set too early. What this item shows is the risk in the opposite direction: settling set late can wipe out a genuine backward command too. And Stop\_form is listed together with ∼\_ε among the items "a particular model must instantiate but which are not this chapter's H," the theory placing no constraint upon its value either.

Hence the determination of M\_ε hangs upon two instantiations, not one: what ∼\_ε reads, and where Stop\_form halts.

VII. Recommendations for revision arising from this

This document alters no Form. The following are recommendations, which must go through the upgrade procedure of the several chapters.

Recommendation one: Section VIII of the Mute-Manifest Mathematical Form should gain a positive constraint: ∼\_ε may read only quantities load-bearing for trace-formation, that is, those quantities which in the present model's declaration bear the identity of the trace-formation organization; components of the state that do not bear that identity, whether determinate or not, whether real components or not, may not enter the determination of ∼\_ε.

That sentence and the present "must quotient out …" are the positive and negative of one thing: the present sentence says what may not be left in, the new one says what may not be read in. Lacking either, the false positive constructed in item three of the preceding section cannot be adjudged a violation by any clause.

Recommendation two: item six of the breach conditions of Section XXI of the Mute-Manifest Mathematical Form should be expanded. It presently reads "∼\_ε defined by 'whether there is consciousness,' 'whether it is manifest,' or some other result yet to be proven"; a further limb should be added: or defined by a component of the state that does not bear the identity of the trace-formation organization.

Recommendation three: the undecidability clauses of Section XXVIII of the Mute-Manifest Determination Form should gain one item, or the section on the control of misjudgement should gain a limb: where the ∼\_ε on which a determination of M\_ε = 1 rests cannot be proven to read only load-bearing quantities, the output is ⊥ and not 1.

The point of this recommendation is that the present regime lists "∼\_ε not defined sufficiently" among the cases of ⊥, but "defined, and defined wrongly" is not among them. The former is a lack, the latter an error, and an error is harder to detect than a lack — because it yields a definite answer.

Recommendation four: the section on event settling, Section IX of the Mute-Manifest Mathematical Form, should gain a limb constraining the instantiation of Stop\_form: the rule of settling must be fixed by structural fact, and not by "what conclusion comparison yields at that moment"; and if one and the same model gives different values of M\_ε under different legitimate rules of settling, that sensitivity must be reported, and not one of them alone.

This recommendation corresponds to item five of Section VI. The present item five of the breach conditions guards only against settling too early; it does not guard against settling too late, nor against the choice of the rule of settling being itself drawn along by the conclusion.

VIII. The limits of these tests

First, the model is very small. 18 states, 3 B conditions, a single r, a single channel, deterministic dynamics. It suffices to test whether the criteria are computable and whether the pseudo-substitutes are excluded; it does not suffice to test any clause involving randomness, continuity, several contents, or large scale. The stochastic strata of the Mute-Manifest Necessity Form, the three-valued stochastic aggregation of the Mute-Manifest Reachability Form, and the five-dimensional portrait of the Mute-Manifest Grading Form were none of them touched here.

Second, the constructions of the five pseudo-substitutes were made by the author of this document. They accord with the descriptions in the several sections of the Mute-Manifest General Form, but a construction within one model is not the whole of that class. A verdict of 0 shows that these five particular constructions are excluded; it does not show that every member of the class is excluded.

Third, the cross-protocol invariance of Prot\_form was not tested. Step 9 of the Mute-Manifest Construction Form requires that another protocol be taken from the protocol family and the procedure re-run; this model declared only one protocol, so that step was not actually tested. This is the most important gate in the Construction Form, and it remains unverified.

Fourth, this model corresponds to no real system. It is a formal object, used to test the computability of the criteria, and not to adjudge whether any actual system has manifested.

Fifth, the code was written and self-checked by the author of this document alone, without external review.

Before the sixth, one further thing is recorded: review found three insufficiencies in the structure of the model — Model One had no multi-step recursion (so the test of pseudo-substitute five was empty); the auxiliary quantities of Model One were all dead variables (so the tests of pseudo-substitutes two, three, and four were empty); and the "feedback loop" of Model One did not in fact form a loop (so the test of pseudo-substitute one was empty). Of the five classes, the first tests of four did not hold, and all required Models Two, Three, and Four to be set up before they could be really tested. What this suggests is given in the next item.

Sixth, the model settles in one step, so clauses involving the process of recursion cannot be tested within it. Model Two was set up for this. Review found the matter: the first draft tested pseudo-substitute five within Model One, where the "absorption" is written in by hand and is not caused by recursion, so the test was empty. It has been re-tested within Model Two. What this suggests: the minimization of a model has its cost, and certain clauses can be tested only once the model possesses the corresponding structure; one may not, because the model runs through, suppose that all clauses have been tested.

X. In one sentence

The criteria can be computed, the five pseudo-substitutes are indeed excluded, and the construction procedure is indeed executable; but the weight of determination lies not in the criteria themselves but in what ∼\_ε reads — and there the present clauses govern only half.
