# General Form of the Observer

This Form ventures one master proposition:

Under conditions in which several candidates can be active at once, advance at once, and even complete at once, how does a structure family composed of one or more constituents become, and remain, the current of this very moment? In this Form we shall find, under sustained questioning, where the proof must go, and derive the general form of the observer.

Then let us first give the result. (What follows is the reading organization set back at the head of the text after the whole was completed; it only previews the result and serves as no premise of the derivation to come.) The necessary-duty formula of the observer's current layer:

Within the finite tuning-field working domain Φ\_cap of On Self-Manifestation:  
CurrentFact(H, I\_t) ⇒ FormDuty(H) ∧ SameDuty(H, I\_t) ∧ \[Composite(H) ⇒ IntegrateDuty(H)\]  
where:

F: the duty of member constitution. The activity or completion of candidates cannot of itself yield the membership range of H; the constitution of H must hold non-arbitrarily on the system's side.

I: the duty of jointly-being-one. Only when H is a composite structure, the several constituents fixed by F must not be a merely external roster, but must jointly become one current on the system's side; if H is indivisible at the working resolution, the duty is trivially satisfied.

M: the duty of cross-time identity bearing. If the earlier and later H within I\_t are counted as one maintained event, their identity must have an object-side ground, and must not be merged merely by an external observer according to similarity. This Form does not prejudge in what causal, structural, or processual form that ground must be realized.

Φ\_cap: finite tuning capacity. It is only the working domain of the present field model of On Self-Manifestation — not a fourth duty, and not a universal necessary condition, proven here, for all possible observers.

In one sentence:

The observer's current layer is not a completed or winning candidate; its members must hold non-arbitrarily on the system's side, its composite constituents must jointly be one, and its cross-time identity must have an object-side ground.

Abstract

The General Form of Objectification has shown that stable structures can form, be reused, and coexist. The observer problem arises from there: the system already holds several candidates, several stable structures, and several completable processes — what makes one structure family among them the current now manifesting? Candidate activity does not suffice to answer. Candidate completion does not suffice either. The completion layer only yields which candidates have reached their own completion conditions; it cannot yield which completers belong to the same current, or which, though completed, remain outside the current. From this the duty of member constitution is forced out. Constitution still does not suffice. Even if the system explicitly registers certain members as belonging to H, if the members merely stand in external juxtaposition, there is still no fact that makes them jointly one current. From this the duty of jointly-being-one under the composite case is forced out.

Constitution and integration still do not suffice. A whole can form in an instant and dissolve at once; similar wholes can appear in succession with no object-side continuation between them. If a maintained current is to form, there must further be sustained bearing across a non-zero time window. From this the object-side duty of cross-time identity bearing is forced out.

The argument thus forces out at least three necessary duties. Finite capacity only sets the boundary within which the three operate; non-explicit orienting sources, orienting action, and re-constraint can hold only under the strengthened domain; pool tracks and pool-level current single-valuedness belong to the tasks of observer individuation and mathematization; candidate dynamics, adjudication protocols, split-brain cases, and experimental breach are handled by the other Forms of the Observer Group respectively.

1\. Version Position and Task Boundary

This Form is the General Form of the Observer after the splitting of the Observer Group. The old General Form of the Observer v3.0 bore at once candidate dynamics, the pool graph, pool tracks, occupancy types, current equivalence, the determinacy gap, experimental estimation, the single-value principle, split-brain transitions, and an eight-item functional decomposition — more than one general form can carry as a single proof task. Those contents have gone respectively into the Observer Mathematical Form, the Observer Limit Form, the Observer Adjudication Form, the Observer Single-Dual Discrimination Form, the Observer Pool-Split Transition Form, the Current Inversion Form, and the Strengthened Form of Current Generation. This Form answers only:

Under conditions in which several candidates can exist at once, what duties, at minimum, must be borne for a maintained current to hold? This Form does not answer:

1\. How many mutually independent observer instances the system contains  
2\. Whether one pool track forms at most one current  
3\. How pools, pool tracks, occupancy, current equivalence, and counting are formalized  
4\. Whether the explicit state suffices to determine the outcome of the current  
5\. Under what parameters candidates are reachable, coexist, decay, or switch  
6\. How constitution, integration, maintenance, and occupancy are adjudicated from observation  
7\. How one composite current is distinguished from two independent currents  
8\. How a pool track splits, merges, or forms two currents after a split brain  
9\. Whether C, B, T, P, R, D, K, L constitute an independent minimal decomposition of current generation.

The object of proof in this Form is the necessary duties of the observer's current layer — not the minimal structure of a complete observer; it does not treat observer individuation, and it does not prove the sufficiency of Lighting.

2\. Working Domain and Inherited Boundaries

The General Form of Objectification has shown that once stable structures form, they can be reused, unfolded, and exist together with other structures. Hence, at the entry point of this Form, the system may hold several candidate structures active at once. Candidates may:

1\. advance each on its own  
2\. compete with one another  
3\. jointly form composite results  
4\. under certain conditions reach candidate-layer completion at the same time  
5\. reach completion without entering the current.

This Form takes no particular candidate dynamical equation as a premise of the general form. Whether double completion of candidates is realizable by some set of parameters is for the Observer Limit Form to prove; this Form uses only the weaker logical fact: the activity or completion predicates of candidates contain, in themselves, no information about "which current they belong to."

This Form adopts a non-zero maintenance window:

I\_t = \[t − Δ\_maint, t\], Δ\_maint \> 0.

What this Form discusses is the structure family H maintained as one and the same current within that window. A mathematical instant with no temporal width whatsoever lies outside the domain of this Form.

Finite tuning capacity Φ\_cap is inherited as a field-layer working condition. It limits the resources that can be devoted at once to constitution, integration, and maintenance; but this Form does not write it in advance as an observer function.

The problem is thereby fixed:

Candidates are many, and completion need not be unique. What facts make an H composed of one or more constituents become, and remain, the current of this moment?

3\. Prior Distinctions

First exclude several explanations that cannot bear the holding of the current. The current is not the globally unique activation state.

A system can hold at once sensory processing, memory unfolding, bodily regulation, action preparation, and local reentry. Even if some activities have not entered manifestation, they can go on running. Defining the current as the sole activity of the whole system would exclude all normal parallel processes; calling every activity the current would degrade "the current" into the sum of activity.

The current is not execution right.

Mutual-exclusion locks, schedulers, thermostats, and ordinary winner-take-all circuits can all output exclusively. Exclusive execution only says which line has obtained the qualification to write, control, or act; it does not say whether that line constitutes a current now manifesting. Conversely, when there is no external action, the current does not thereby necessarily vanish.

The current is not broadcasting.

That a content is read by more modules only says its range of availability has widened. Broadcasting can be a downstream consequence of the current, and can also be realized by an automatic system without any current. Reading range cannot substitute for the constitutive relation of the current.

The current is not report.

Report is output. If manifestation is first defined by report and then proven by report, the argument is circular; if it is not so defined, report can only be a possible consequence and cannot bear the holding of the current.

The current is not candidate completion.

Completion only says a candidate has reached the completion conditions it set for itself. It does not say whether that candidate enters the current, nor whether several completed candidates jointly form one current, severally form several currents, or all remain outside the current.

Therefore the observer problem cannot be closed at the layer of activation, execution, broadcasting, report, or candidate completion.

4\. The Flow of the Argument

The system contains several candidates.

Suppose first that a candidate, merely by being active, belongs to the current. Then all background activity, local regulation, candidate unfolding, and irrelevant processes enter the current automatically. The current is no different from the sum of the system's activity. This explanation cannot say why some activities are now manifesting while others, active at the same time, have not entered manifestation. So activity cannot automatically yield the current.

Suppose next that a candidate, merely by completing, belongs to the current. Now let several candidates reach their own completion conditions in the same period. The completion layer yields only a completion set:

C\_t^\* = {c : Comp\_t(c) = 1}.

Can this set directly become the current?

It cannot.

For one and the same C\_t^\*, at least the following distinct cases exist:

1\. all completers jointly become one current  
2\. part of them become the current, the remaining completers stay in the background  
3\. the completers are divided into different structure families  
4\. there are completers, yet no maintained current forms at all.  
The completion set can be exactly the same across the four cases. Knowing only who has completed, one cannot know who belongs to the current of this moment. Then may a describer take an arbitrary subset of the completion set and call it the current?

Still no.

If nothing inside the system determines why precisely this subset is taken, any subset can equally be named the current. "This moment it is this family" is then only external description, not a fact in the system. So, for H to become the current, there must exist in the system a relation that determines which candidates, completers, or structural constituents jointly enter H, and which simultaneous actors do not.

Here the first item is forced out.

Note it provisionally as F.

F is not presupposed as a selector, a gate, a list, an attractor, or a separate module. It bears only one minimal role:

to make the constitution of H hold inside the system.

F answers:

who constitutes the current of this moment?

Now F is in place. The members of H are fixed. Is the task closed? No.

Construct a roster system.

The system explicitly registers inside itself:

A, B, C belong to H; D, E do not.

The membership boundary is perfectly clear. But A, B, C each vary on their own, are perturbed on their own, recover on their own, with no relation of joint updating, joint constraint, joint reorganization, or joint continuation among them. They are merely listed under one name.

This system has member attribution, yet still does not answer: why are A, B, C one current, rather than three mutually independent processes?

If an external roster sufficed to make one current, then one could equally divide A and B into one current and list C separately — or merge C with D. Nothing inside the system distinguishes these divisions. So constitution answers only "who is in it"; it cannot alone answer "why they are jointly one."

For H to become one current, there must further exist, among the constituents fixed by F, a unifying relation within the system that makes joint presence different from external juxtaposition.

This relation demands no fixed realization. It can appear as joint updating, mutual constraint, joint perturbation and recovery, joint reorganization, joint reentry, or other equivalent relations able to make the members jointly hold.

Here the second item is forced out.

Note it provisionally as I.

I answers:

why do the members fixed by F jointly become one current? For an H containing a single constituent indivisible at the working resolution of this Form, I takes the trivial reflexive shape and adds no independent structural requirement; for a composite H, I bears the non-trivial duty of jointly-being-one. If the so-called single constituent is only the describer's joint naming of mutually unrelated degrees of freedom, it is not truly single-constituent at that resolution, and the roster counterexample still holds. The unified type covering the single-constituent and composite cases is for the Observer Mathematical Form to establish.

Now F and I are in place. A structure family has, at some moment, been constituted and become jointly one current. Is the task closed? Still no.

Construct an instantaneous system.

At moment τ, H forms completely: the membership boundary is fixed, and the internal unifying relation genuinely holds. But that relation vanishes the very moment it forms. At moment τ + δ there appears an H′ identical in content, yet between H and H′ there is no object-side succession whatsoever.

This system can generate similar instantaneous wholes without end, yet cannot form a current maintained through a non-zero time window. Similarity cannot substitute for succession. If between the earlier and the later whole there is no actual object-side succession, to call them one and the same current is only a classification made by an external observer on the basis of similarity. So constitution and integration answer only how a current holds at some moment; they cannot alone answer how it remains the same one within I\_t.

For H to become a maintained current, there must exist object-side cross-time identity bearing, so that the already constituted and integrated H is not counted as the same between earlier and later states merely through external similarity. Here the third item is forced out.

Note it provisionally as M.

M answers:

why does a current already in place not dissolve upon forming, but keep object-side identity through a non-zero time window?

M does not require the contents to stay unchanged item by item. Objects may be replaced, the background may vary, intensity may rise and fall, internal relations may reorganize; so long as the cross-time identity of H has object-side bearing rather than being merged merely by external similarity, H can continue as one and the same current. What M requires is: for the judgment of identity between the earlier and later H within I\_t, an object-side ground exists. If several instantaneous wholes can only be grouped as one by external similarity, M does not hold. This Form does not further assert that the ground must be a process running moment by moment, covering the whole window continuously, and describable in terms of "interruption" — those realization conditions go to the Observer Mathematical Form and the Adjudication Form.

Now F, I, M are in place. Is the task closed?

Continue examining structures that might be added.

Is finite capacity a fourth function?

No.

Φ\_cap limits the tuning resources available to candidate unfolding, constitution, integration, and maintenance, affecting competition, switching, complexity, and the strength of maintenance. But capacity itself does not determine the members of H, does not make the members one, and does not sustain its continuation.

Hence capacity is the operating boundary of F, I, M — not a function alongside the three.

Is a non-explicit orienting source unconditionally required? No.

Suppose a system's complete explicit state Γ uniquely determines F, I, M. So long as the three hold, a maintained current can still hold. Only by the additional stipulation:

there exists a pair of histories with the same Γ yet different currents,  
can a source of difference outside Γ and inside the boundary be forced out. Hence the non-explicit orienting source belongs to the strengthened domain of current generation, not to the unconditional minimal structure of every observer current. Must orienting action be listed independently?

This Form has not forced it out.

F already expresses that candidate differences are actually converted into differences of current membership. To split difference registration, source reading, amplification, direction-taking, threshold conversion, and scope limitation into independent functions, one must construct a countermodel in which F holds completely, those actions are missing, and the task still fails.

This Form has not obtained that independent gap. Whether it holds is left to the Strengthened Form of Current Generation to re-derive.

Is re-constraint required?

From the holding of a current within one maintenance window it cannot be derived unconditionally. M can persist through a time window free of strong shocks without proving that the current reversely restricts all subsequent candidates. If the task is strengthened to: maintaining robustly under the continual entry of new candidates and competition for resources, re-constraint may be forced out, and may merge into the two-directional shape of M. But that strengthened premise does not belong to the minimal facts of this Form. Are pools and pool tracks required?

They are not a fourth function of this Form.

Pools and pool tracks answer at what tuning-carrier boundary the actual support of F, I, M is located, and how different currents form mutually independent observer instances. That is the task of the Observer Mathematical Form and the General Form of Observer Individuation.

This Form proves at the functional layer what a current minimally needs; it does not prove in advance that it must be carried by one particular pool track. Is the pool-level current single-value principle derivable from the three? It is not.

F, I, M can make one current hold, yet cannot exclude another mutually independent F–I–M structure within the same pre-identified carrier boundary. To claim at most one current per pool, a separate individuation proposition must be erected and left open to empirical breach.

By here, this Form has forced out three necessary duties. Lacking F: the completion layer holds no fact of current membership. Lacking I: the members are only a roster and cannot jointly become one current. Lacking M: the whole holds only instantaneously and cannot become a maintained current. Capacity, source, orientation, re-constraint, pool tracks, and the single-value principle of the old draft can none of them be added unconditionally under the present premises. Whether a yet undiscovered fourth independent necessary duty exists is entered as H5; until that question is settled, this Form does not claim the three to be exhaustive.

5\. Formal Naming of the Necessary Duties

Reviewing the argument, the three functional duties were forced out by three systems that keep the preceding items in place while the task still fails.

First:

the duty of member constitution, F.

Constitution makes certain candidates, completers, or structural constituents jointly compose H, and keeps the other simultaneous actors from belonging to H automatically through activity or completion.

Second:

the duty of jointly-being-one, I.

Integration makes the members fixed by F jointly become one current through a unifying relation within the system, rather than an external roster. Third:

the duty of cross-time identity bearing, M.

Cross-time identity bearing gives the identity of the earlier and later H within I\_t an object-side ground, rather than letting several instantaneous wholes be grouped as one merely by external similarity.

Finite capacity Φ\_cap is the working-domain boundary in the present field model of On Self-Manifestation.

Hence the general formula of the necessary duties of the observer's current layer:  
ObserverCurrentDuties^{Φ\_cap} = (F, I, M).

F, I, M are the coordinates of three duties — not a temporal pipeline, and no promise of three independent structural entities. One relation may bear several duties at once, and several structures may jointly bear one.

6\. Necessity and Non-Redundancy Review

Remove F.

The system may still hold several active candidates and several completers, yet no fact within the system determines who constitutes H. The current can only degrade into all activity, all completers, or a subset arbitrarily taken by a describer. Hence the duty of member constitution cannot be lacked.

Remove I.

The system may explicitly register the members of H, yet holds only an external roster. The members can be arbitrarily split and recombined, and nothing inside the system determines who is jointly one current. Hence the duty of jointly-being-one under the composite case cannot be lacked. Remove M.

The system may at every instant form a whole with a clear boundary and internal unity, yet cannot give the earlier and later wholes object-side succession. What results is a sequence of instantaneous wholes, not a maintained current. Hence the duty of cross-time identity bearing cannot be lacked.

Can F and I be merged?

What this Form has proven is that the functional duties differ — not that every realization must contain two mutually independent, unmergeable structures. The roster system proves: bearing member attribution alone without bearing jointly-being-one, the task cannot close. Hence any qualified realization must bear both "who is in it" and "why they are jointly one." The two may be borne separately by two relations, or jointly by one stronger internal constitutive relation. Whether a unified form exists, and whether after unification the general form should still keep two functional coordinates, is entered as H1; this Form does not feign a completed proof of structural unmergeability.

Can I and M be merged?

The instantaneous whole proves: jointly-being-one can hold at some moment while cross-time succession does not. Hence saying "it is one" does not suffice to answer "why it is maintained."

Can F and M be merged?

A system can long preserve a member roster without internal unity; it can also instantaneously form a clear membership boundary without persisting. Member constitution and cross-time sustainment answer different gaps. Do the three duties demand three modules?

They do not.

This Form claims only that the three duties must be borne, not that three structural entities severally exist. One physical closed loop may bear several duties at once, and several structures may jointly bear one; how the parts are divided is not prescribed by this Form.

Should the other candidate items of the old draft enter this Form? The present argument does not support it.

Capacity is demoted to a boundary; source, orientation, and re-constraint are conditionalized; pool tracks, single-valuedness, dynamics, and adjudication are transferred to other Forms. Hence the three duties — member constitution, jointly-being-one under the composite case, cross-time identity bearing — are each forced out by the corresponding failing system. Whether F and I are structurally separable still awaits H1; whether the three exhaust all necessary duties still awaits H5. This Form therefore claims only "the necessary duties so far forced out," not that the complete set of necessary duties is closed.

7\. The Necessary-Duty Formula, Symbols, and Read-Back

Let:

I\_t = \[t − Δ\_maint, t\], Δ\_maint \> 0; C(I\_t) be the domain of candidates and current-eligible structural constituents within I\_t; H be the current structure family examined within I\_t; Composite(H) mean that H contains two or more distinguishable constituents at the working resolution of this Form; Φ\_cap be the finite tuning-field working domain of On Self-Manifestation.

The task-side predicate CurrentFact(H, I\_t) registers one pure task fact only: H is, within I\_t, the current now manifesting and being maintained. This registration contains nothing of the mode of member constitution, the shape of constituent relations, or the ground of cross-time identity — whether those three must hold, and in what minimal shape, is forced out independently by the countermodels of the main text; the antecedent pre-buries no consequent (the v4.2 non-circular contraction).

The necessary-duty formula obtained by this Form:

Within the working domain Φ\_cap,  
CurrentFact(H, I\_t) ⇒ FormDuty(H) ∧ SameDuty(H, I\_t) ∧ \[Composite(H) ⇒ IntegrateDuty(H)\].

Read back as follows.

FormDuty(H): the membership range of H must hold non-arbitrarily on the system's side; candidate activity or completion does not automatically entail belonging to H.

IntegrateDuty(H): only when Composite(H) holds, the several constituents of H must jointly become one current on the system's side, rather than an external roster. Under a single-constituent H the duty is trivially satisfied.

SameDuty(H, I\_t): if the earlier and later H within I\_t are counted as one maintained event, their cross-time identity must have an object-side ground and cannot be merged by external similarity alone. This Form does not prejudge the unique realization of that ground. This Form proves only the above direction of necessity; it does not prove that the three duties suffice to produce feeling, first-person manifestation, Lighting, or a complete observer instance, nor that the three exhaust all necessary duties.

8\. Theorem of the Necessary Duties of the Observer's Current Layer

Let the system stand in the multi-candidate, finite-tuning-capacity working domain Φ\_cap of On Self-Manifestation, and let CurrentFact(H, I\_t) hold. Then:

1\. the duty of member constitution FormDuty(H) must be borne  
2\. the duty of cross-time identity bearing SameDuty(H, I\_t) must be borne  
3\. if Composite(H) holds, the duty of jointly-being-one IntegrateDuty(H) must be borne; if H is indivisible at the working resolution, the duty is trivially satisfied. Proof.

If the duty of member constitution is not borne, candidate activity and completion cannot determine the members of H; one and the same candidate state admits different current attributions, and the constitution of H is left to external arbitrary choice.

If Composite(H) holds and the duty of jointly-being-one is not borne, then even with the members of H registered, they remain an external roster; nothing inside the system makes these members jointly one current.

If the duty of cross-time identity bearing is not borne, then even where the membership range and jointly-being-one of H hold at some moment, the earlier and later wholes within I\_t can only be grouped as one by external similarity and obtain no object-side cross-time identity.

Hence the three duties are severally forced out by three systems that keep the preceding items while the task still fails. F, I, M are only the coordinates of these duties; this theorem does not declare that three independent structural entities necessarily exist, nor that the three exhaust all necessary duties. QED. Declaration of the theorem's strength:

This theorem is a necessity theorem at the level of task duties. It does not assert:

1\. that the three are realized by three separate modules  
2\. that F and I are structurally separable in all realizations  
3\. that cross-time identity bearing must be one particular causal process  
4\. that the three suffice for Lighting, feeling, or first-person manifestation  
5\. that an H necessarily equals a complete observer instance  
6\. that one pool track holds at most one H  
7\. that finite capacity is a universal necessary condition for all possible observers  
8\. that the three already constitute a closed, complete functional core.

9\. Breach Arguments

The breach system lacking constitution:

The system holds several candidates and completers, but no relation within the system determines which jointly constitute H. If such a system can still form a non-arbitrary current, the necessity of the duty of member constitution is breached. The breach system lacking integration:

The system explicitly registers the members of H, but the members bear no internal relation that makes them jointly hold. If such a roster can still constitute one current, the necessity of the duty of jointly-being-one under the composite case is breached.

The breach system lacking continuation bearing:

The system continually produces similar instantaneous wholes, but adjacent wholes have no object-side succession whatsoever. If such a system can still constitute one and the same maintained current within a non-zero time window without any object-side identity bearing, the necessity of the duty of cross-time identity bearing is breached. Ruling on merging the duty coordinates:

If it is proven that one relation can bear several duties at once, that is no breach of necessity; only proving that some duty generates no independent task gap at all breaches the necessity of that duty. Non-breach cases:

1\. discovering realizations of F, I, M different from the old draft  
2\. one physical process bearing all three  
3\. one current containing several objects  
4\. several candidates completing at once  
5\. the system holding several observer instances  
6\. the pool track not being the unique carrier  
7\. finite capacity failing to entail single-valuedness  
8\. non-explicit sources existing only in some systems.

None of the above breaches the necessary duties this Form has proven.

10\. Conditional Consequents

1\. Finite capacity

Φ\_cap constrains the constitution domain, the scale of integration, and the resources of maintenance. It can produce competition, switching, decay, and reachability bounds, but cannot substitute for F, I, M.

2\. Non-explicit orienting source

If the further condition is added:

there exist two histories ω1, ω2 within a complete boundary whose pre-registered explicit projections are the same while the current outcomes differ,  
then the difference of currents must have a source outside Γ and inside the boundary, or a strictly proven internal random transition.

That conclusion goes to the Strengthened Form of Current Generation or the Current Inversion Form; it does not enter the unconditional necessary duties of this Form.

3\. Orienting action

If source differences must be converted into differences of current membership, the realization must contain a conversion relation sensitive to candidate differences. Whether it is independent of F must be proven separately.

4\. Re-constraint

If the current must maintain robustly under the continual impact of new candidates, the current already in place may need to reversely restrict subsequent candidates and resource allocation. When that strengthened premise holds, re-constraint may stand as the two-directional shape of M; this Form makes no unconditional assertion.

5\. Pool tracks and individuation

To count how many mutually independent observer instances a system holds, it must further be determined whether the actual support of F, I, M runs along one or several independent tuning-carrier tracks. That question is treated by the General Form of Observer Individuation.

Closing

Candidates may be many, and completions may run in parallel.

But completion only says a candidate has reached its own conditions; it does not say who belongs to the current of this moment.

So the duty of member constitution must be borne. Members can be explicitly listed.

But a roster only says who is in it, not why they are jointly one. So the duty of jointly-being-one under the composite case must be borne. A whole can form in an instant.

But instantaneous holding is not being maintained, and similarity is not succession. So cross-time identity must have object-side bearing. This Form argues within the finite tuning-field working domain of On Self-Manifestation. Pressed into one sentence:

The observer's current layer is not the winning candidate; its members must be non-arbitrarily constituted, its composite constituents must jointly be one, and its cross-time identity must have object-side bearing.
