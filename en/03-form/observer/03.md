# Observer Mathematical Form

Master Proposition

The General Form of the Observer has proven: within the finite tuning-field working domain Φ\_cap of On Self-Manifestation,  
CurrentFact(H, I\_t) ⇒ FormDuty(H) ∧ SameDuty(H, I\_t) ∧ \[Composite(H) ⇒ IntegrateDuty(H)\].

This Form does not re-prove that necessity. This Form establishes:

1\. the formal types of candidates, structure families, working time windows, and the current fact;

2\. the unified form of the three duties Form, Integrate, Same and of their bearing relation;

3\. the unified type of F and I borne separately, borne jointly, and borne jointly by several relations;

4\. the formal family of object-side cross-time identity bearing;

5\. the running system of candidate advancement, competition, completion, retention, decay, and double completion;

6\. the running read-back of Mem, KCore, actual support, and reserve support onto the three duties;

7\. running coupling and conditional re-constraint under finite capacity;

8\. the necessary direction of the current fact, the model-relative solving boundary, and the downstream interfaces.

This Form does not establish complete observer individuation; does not establish pool-level current single-valuedness; does not count observers;

does not define pool, pool track, occupancy, or report as the current; does not establish real host realization; does not write structures unproven by the General Form as universal theory. Part One: Theoretical Mathematics

I. Working Domain

Definition 1. Time domain

T is the time domain.

t ∈ T.

Definition 2. Non-zero maintenance window

Δ\_maint ∈ R\_{\>0}.

I\_t := \[t − Δ\_maint, t\].

τ ∈ I\_t.

Definition 3. Domain of non-zero time windows

I\_+ := {\[t − Δ, t\] : t ∈ T, Δ \> 0}.

I\_t ∈ I\_+.

A mathematical instant with no temporal width whatsoever does not belong to the domain of CurrentFact in this Form.

Definition 4. Domain of non-empty relation families. For any relation domain Y, define:

P\_+(Y) := P(Y) \\ {∅}.

Definition 5. Finite-capacity working domain

Φ\_cap is the finite tuning-field working domain of On Self-Manifestation. Φ\_cap only limits the resources available to candidate unfolding, constitution, integration, and maintenance; it does not serve as a fourth duty.

Definition 6. Candidate domain

C(I\_t) is the domain of candidates and structural constituents active, advancing, competing, completing, or current-eligible within I\_t.

Definition 7. Domain of structure families

H is the total domain of cross-window structure families examinable as the current. For any I\_t ∈ I\_+, write:

H(I\_t) ⊆ H for the subdomain of structure families lawfully covering I\_t. H ∈ H(I\_t).

H may consist of one or several constituents.

Definition 8. Composite predicate

Composite : H(I\_t) → B.

Composite(H) = 1 means H contains, at the working resolution of this Form, two or more distinguishable constituents. Composite(H) = 0 means H is indivisible at that resolution.

II. Candidate Completion and Structure Families

Definition 9. Candidate activity predicate. Act\_t(c) ∈ B.

Definition 10. Candidate completion predicate

Comp\_t(c) ∈ B.

Definition 11. Completion set

C\_t^\* := {c ∈ C(I\_t) : Comp\_t(c) = 1}.

Definition 12. Formal membership domain

Mem\_t : H(I\_t) → P\_+(C(I\_t)).

Mem\_t(H) denotes the set of candidates or structural constituents actually constituting the structure family H within I\_t.

Mem\_t(H) is no externally arbitrary roster.

One and the same C\_t^\* may correspond to different lawful H; nor is one H required to equal C\_t^\*.

Theorem 1 (activity is not the current). Act\_t(c) = 1 does not entail:

c ∈ Mem\_t(H).

Nor does it entail:

CurrentFact(H, I\_t) = 1.

QED.

Theorem 2 (completion is not the current). Comp\_t(c) = 1 does not entail:

c ∈ Mem\_t(H).

C\_t^\* being determined does not entail:

H being determined.

Several candidates completing at once does not entail:

one current, several currents, or any complete observer instance. QED.

III. The Task-Side Predicate

Definition 13. The current fact

CurrentFact : H × I\_+ → B.

CurrentFact(H, I\_t) = 1 means H genuinely holds within I\_t as one and the same maintained current. CurrentFact is a task-side fact; this Form does not define it as the conjunction of the three duties.

Definition 14. The duty of member constitution

FormDuty : H(I\_t) → B.

FormDuty(H) = 1 means the membership range of H holds non-arbitrarily on the system's side.

Definition 15. The duty of jointly-being-one

IntegrateDuty : H(I\_t) → B.

When Composite(H) = 1, IntegrateDuty(H) = 1 means the several distinguishable constituents of H jointly become one current on the system's side, rather than an external roster.

When Composite(H) = 0, IntegrateDuty(H) takes the trivial reflexive shape.

Definition 16. The duty of cross-time identity bearing

SameDuty : H × I\_+ → B.

SameDuty(H, I\_t) = 1 means that when the earlier and later H within I\_t are counted as one maintained event, their identity has an object-side ground.

IV. The Unified Type of Duty Bearing

Definition 17. Licensed running relation domain

R(I\_t) is the domain of licensed in-system relations able to bear, within I\_t, the roles of constitution, integration, continuation-identity, or conditional re-constraint. R ∈ R(I\_t).

R may be a single relation, a composite relation, a closed loop, a process, or another role-equivalent structure.

Definition 18. Relation family

X ∈ P\_+(R(I\_t)).

Definition 19. Duty coordinate set

D := {F, I, M}.

Definition 20. The bearing relation

Bear ⊆ P\_+(R(I\_t)) × D × H(I\_t) × I\_+.

Bear(X, F, H, I\_t) = 1 means the relation family X actually bears, within I\_t, the member-constitution duty of H.

Bear(X, I, H, I\_t) = 1 means the relation family X actually bears, within I\_t, the jointly-being-one duty of the composite H. Bear(X, M, H, I\_t) = 1 means the relation family X actually bears, within I\_t, the cross-time identity duty of H.

Definition 21. Read-back of duty holding

FormDuty(H) = 1 if and only if there exists X\_F ∈ P\_+(R(I\_t)) such that:

Bear(X\_F, F, H, I\_t) = 1.

IntegrateDuty(H) = 1 if and only if:

Composite(H) = 0;

or there exists X\_I ∈ P\_+(R(I\_t)) such that:

Bear(X\_I, I, H, I\_t) = 1.

SameDuty(H, I\_t) = 1 if and only if there exists X\_M ∈ P\_+(R(I\_t)) such that:

Bear(X\_M, M, H, I\_t) = 1.

Definition 22. Many-to-many bearing

It is permitted that:

1\. one relation family bears several of F, I, M at once;

2\. different relation families bear different duties severally;

3\. several relation families jointly bear one duty;

4\. one relation bears different roles in different working windows. Three independent modules must not be derived directly from the duty coordinates.

V. The Unified Form of F and I

Definition 23. The separately-borne type

Separate\_FI(H, I\_t) = 1 if and only if there exist X\_F ∈ P\_+(R(I\_t)) and X\_I ∈ P\_+(R(I\_t)) such that:

Bear(X\_F, F, H, I\_t) = 1;

Bear(X\_I, I, H, I\_t) = 1;

with X\_F and X\_I not required to be the same.

Definition 24. The jointly-borne type

Joint\_FI(H, I\_t) = 1 if and only if there exists one and the same relation family X\_FI ∈ P\_+(R(I\_t)) such that: Bear(X\_FI, F, H, I\_t) = 1;

Bear(X\_FI, I, H, I\_t) = 1.

Definition 25. Lawful F–I realization type

Valid\_FI(H, I\_t) = 1 if and only if:

Separate\_FI(H, I\_t) = 1;

or:

Joint\_FI(H, I\_t) = 1.

Theorem 3 (F–I functional distinction). Joint\_FI holding does not cancel the functional coordinates F and I. Proof.

One relation can bear at once "who is in it" and "why they are jointly one," but the roster counterexample has proven the two task gaps to be different. Hence mergeability of structure is not deletability of duty. QED.

VI. The Form of Cross-Time Identity Bearing

Definition 26. Structure-slice domain

H\_slice is the domain of single-moment structure slices.

Definition 27. Cross-window structure family

For any H ∈ H(I\_t), a slice mapping is stipulated to exist: Path\_H : I\_t → H\_slice.

Write:

H\_τ := Path\_H(τ).

Thus H is the cross-window structure family covering I\_t, and H\_τ is its structure slice at moment τ.

Definition 28. Licensed succession relation family

Succ(I\_t) ⊆ R(I\_t).

Licensed forms may include:

continuous dynamical succession;

discrete state-update succession;

structural replacement with the load-bearing relation persisting; joint reentry;

closed-loop maintenance;

other role-equivalent forms able to give the earlier and later H an object-side ground.

Definition 29. Fixed covering partition

Π(I\_t) = {t\_0 \< t\_1 \< ... \< t\_n} is a covering partition of I\_t fixed before the run, satisfying: t\_0 = t − Δ\_maint; t\_n = t.

Definition 30. Local succession

Succ\_R(H\_{t\_k}, H\_{t\_{k+1}}) = 1 means R ∈ Succ(I\_t) actually bears the object-side succession between adjacent structure slices.

Definition 31. Full-window succession

CoverSucc(X\_M, H, I\_t) = 1 if and only if:

X\_M ∈ P\_+(Succ(I\_t));

and for any k ∈ {0, ..., n−1} there exists R ∈ X\_M such that:

Succ\_R(H\_{t\_k}, H\_{t\_{k+1}}) = 1.

Definition 32. Read-back of Same bearing

Bear(X\_M, M, H, I\_t) = 1 requires:

CoverSucc(X\_M, H, I\_t) = 1;

and the identity of the earlier and later H not being given merely by external similarity, temporal proximity, or repeated generation.

This Form prescribes no unique succession algorithm, nor does it write sameness of state values as SameDuty.

Definition chain:

lawful running relations → Bear → FormDuty, IntegrateDuty, SameDuty.

Solving chain:

candidates and running relations → Sol\_H → Sol\_F^all, Sol\_I^all, Sol\_M^all → DutyClosed.

CurrentFact supplies only the necessary direction proven by the General Form; DutyClosed supplies only the running solving result and does not unconditionally define CurrentFact in reverse.

Theorem 4 (similarity is not identity). For any adjacent slices:

H\_{t\_k} ≈ H\_{t\_{k+1}} does not entail:

SameDuty(H, I\_t) = 1.

Proof.

The similarity relation gives only an external or descriptive-layer correspondence, not object-side succession.

QED.

VII. Separation of Directions

Definition 33. Necessary direction

NecessityDir(H, I\_t) denotes:

CurrentFact(H, I\_t) ⇒ FormDuty(H) ∧ SameDuty(H, I\_t) ∧ \[Composite(H) ⇒ IntegrateDuty(H)\].

By the duty–universal-solution equivalence theorem, further: CurrentFact(H, I\_t) ⇒ DutyClosed(H, I\_t).

Definition 34. Operational direction

OperationalDir(H, I\_t) denotes:

candidate state → Admit → Build → Sol\_H → Sol\_F^all, Sol\_I^all, Sol\_M^all → DutyClosed.

The operational direction only obtains duty closure; it does not unconditionally derive CurrentFact.

Theorem 5 (necessary duties of the observer's current layer). Within the working domain Φ\_cap:

CurrentFact(H, I\_t) = 1 entails:

FormDuty(H) = 1;

SameDuty(H, I\_t) = 1;

if Composite(H) = 1, then IntegrateDuty(H) = 1.

That is:

CurrentFact(H, I\_t) ⇒ FormDuty(H) ∧ SameDuty(H, I\_t) ∧ \[Composite(H) ⇒ IntegrateDuty(H)\].

QED.

Theorem 6 (non-sufficiency). FormDuty(H) = 1;

SameDuty(H, I\_t) = 1;

Composite(H) ⇒ IntegrateDuty(H) = 1;

do not unconditionally entail:

CurrentFact(H, I\_t) = 1.

QED.

Definition 35. Model-specific sufficient condition

For a specific model M, one may separately posit:

H\_cur(M, H, I\_t) = 1, meaning that model has independently proven: the conditions registered within the present working domain suffice to read back CurrentFact in the positive direction.

Only when that condition holds is the model-relative formula permitted: H\_cur(M, H, I\_t) ∧ FormDuty(H) ∧ SameDuty(H, I\_t) ∧ \[Composite(H) ⇒ IntegrateDuty(H)\] ⇒ CurrentFact(H, I\_t).

That formula is no universal observer theorem.

VIII. Degenerate Models

Definition 36. The F-lacking model

Deg\_F(H, I\_t) = 1 means candidates may be active, complete, or juxtaposed, but no X\_F exists such that:

Bear(X\_F, F, H, I\_t) = 1.

Definition 37. The I-lacking model

Deg\_I(H, I\_t) = 1 means FormDuty(H) = 1, Composite(H) = 1, but no X\_I exists such that:

Bear(X\_I, I, H, I\_t) = 1.

Definition 38. The M-lacking model

Deg\_M(H, I\_t) = 1 means H may at some instant be constituted and jointly one, but no object-side succession relation family covering I\_t exists. Theorem 7 (degenerate failure). Deg\_F(H, I\_t) = 1 entails:

CurrentFact(H, I\_t) = 0.

Deg\_I(H, I\_t) = 1 entails:

CurrentFact(H, I\_t) = 0.

Deg\_M(H, I\_t) = 1 entails:

CurrentFact(H, I\_t) = 0.

QED.

Part Two: Running Mathematics

IX. Candidate Convergence Dynamics

Definition 39. Candidate advancement degree

For any c ∈ C(I\_t): z\_c(τ) ∈ \[0, 1\].

Definition 40. Candidate tuning resource

x\_c(τ) ≥ 0.

Candidate resources and duty running resources are jointly constrained by Cap\_Φ; the two no longer use mutually independent total upper bounds.

Definition 41. Candidate overlap

Ovl(c, c′) ∈ \[0, 1\].

Definition 42. Candidate dynamics

For any c: F\_c(τ) = r\_c(τ) x\_c(τ) − χ Σ\_{c′≠c} Ovl(c, c′) z\_{c′}(τ) − η\_c(τ) z\_c(τ) + ξ\_c(τ).

Where:

r\_c ≥ 0;

χ ≥ 0;

η\_c ≥ 0;

ξ\_c is a permitted perturbation term.

Definition 43. Interval-projected dynamics

ż\_c(τ) = Π\_\[0,1\](F\_c(τ); z\_c(τ)).

Where:

Π\_\[0,1\](F; z) = max(F, 0), if z = 0;

Π\_\[0,1\](F; z) = F, if 0 \< z \< 1;

Π\_\[0,1\](F; z) = min(F, 0), if z = 1.

X. Candidate Completion, Reachability, and Decay

Definition 44. Completion window

Δ\_comp \> 0.

I\_t^comp := \[t − Δ\_comp, t\].

Definition 45. Candidate completion

Comp\_t(c) = 1 if and only if:

z\_c(τ) = 1 holds for every τ ∈ I\_t^comp.

Definition 46. Single-candidate simplified model

If:

Cap\_Φ(τ) ≥ 1;

the remaining duty-task resources are already deducted or provisionally taken as zero; x\_c = 1;

Ovl = 0;

ξ\_c = 0;

r\_c = r; η\_c = η; then:

ż\_c = r − η z\_c.

Its solution is:

z\_c(τ) = r/η + \[z\_c(0) − r/η\] e^{−ητ}.

If r \> η, then from z\_c(0) \< 1 the value reaches 1 in finite time, with hitting time:

τ\_hit = (1/η) ln\[(r/η − z\_c(0)) / (r/η − 1)\].

If r = η, the value reaches 1 only asymptotically.

If r \< η, the equilibrium value stays below 1.

Definition 47. Completion-retention condition

For candidate c to retain completion at z\_c = 1 requires at least: r\_c x\_c + ξ\_c ≥ η\_c + χ Σ\_{c′≠c} Ovl(c, c′) z\_{c′}.

Definition 48. Decay condition

If:

r\_c x\_c + ξ\_c \< η\_c z\_c + χ Σ\_{c′≠c} Ovl(c, c′) z\_{c′}, then:

ż\_c \< 0.

Theorem 8 (existence of the double-completion retention state). There exist parameters and resource allocations under which two candidates that have entered the completion boundary jointly retain completion over a non-zero period. Proof.

Take a period in which the residual capacity available to candidates is at least 1, and let the remaining duty resources be already deducted or provisionally zero. Then take:

z\_i = z\_j = 1;

x\_i = x\_j = 1/2;

r\_i = r\_j = 4;

η\_i = η\_j = 1;

χ = 1;

Ovl(i, j) = Ovl(j, i) = 1;

ξ\_i = ξ\_j = 0.

Then:

F\_i = 0;

F\_j = 0.

Hence the double-completion retention state exists. This theorem does not prove that the state is reachable from every incomplete initial state. QED.

Corollary 1. N\_comp(t) := \|C\_t^\*\| cannot unconditionally be read back as the number of CurrentFacts, of observer units, or of observers.

XI. Candidate State, Admission, Build, and Constitution Running

The three running specializations:

FormRun(X\_F, H, I\_t) = 1 ⇔ Bear(X\_F, F, H, I\_t) = 1.

IntegrateRun(X\_I, H, I\_t) = 1 ⇔ Bear(X\_I, I, H, I\_t) = 1.

SameRun(X\_M, H, I\_t) = 1 ⇔ Bear(X\_M, M, H, I\_t) = 1.

Definition 49. Candidate running state

CandState\_t(c) = (z\_c\|\_{I\_t}, x\_c\|\_{I\_t}, Comp\_t(c), Act\_t(c)).

Definition 50. Candidate–relation effective coupling

EffectiveCouple(c, R, I\_t) = 1 if and only if:

1\. R ∈ R(I\_t);

2\. candidate c and R stand in actual coupling within I\_t;

3\. licensed intervention on that coupling can change the admission, exclusion, or membership outcome of c.

Mere statistical correlation or an arbitrarily weak positive coupling does not suffice for EffectiveCouple.

Definition 51. Candidate eligibility relation

Eligible ⊆ C(I\_t) × P\_+(R(I\_t)) × I\_+.

Eligible(c, X\_F, I\_t) = 1 means CandState\_t(c), the actual resource state, and the candidate preconditions declared by the model jointly permit c to enter the constitution process. This Form neither prescribes that only completed candidates may be eligible, nor that completed candidates are necessarily eligible.

Definition 52. Candidate admission relation

Admit(c, X\_F, I\_t) = 1 if and only if:

1\. Eligible(c, X\_F, I\_t) = 1;

2\. there exists R ∈ X\_F such that

EffectiveCouple(c, R, I\_t) = 1;

3\. candidate activity or completion does not of itself yield Admit;

4\. with in-system preconditions unchanged, external roster rewriting does not change the Admit outcome.

Definition 53. Admission set

AdmitSet\_t(X\_F) = {c ∈ C(I\_t) : Admit(c, X\_F, I\_t) = 1}.

AdmitSet\_t(X\_F) is allowed to be empty; an empty admission outcome means only that this relation family has formed no structure within this working window.

Definition 54. The structure-composition relation

Build ⊆ P\_+(R(I\_t)) × P(C(I\_t)) × H(I\_t) × I\_+.

Build(X\_F, AdmitSet\_t(X\_F), H, I\_t) = 1 means the relation family X\_F composes the admission set into the structure family H, requiring:

AdmitSet\_t(X\_F) ≠ ∅;

Mem\_t(H) = AdmitSet\_t(X\_F).

Definition 55. Lawful structure-family solution set

Sol\_H(I\_t) = {H ∈ H(I\_t) : ∃X\_F ∈ P\_+(R(I\_t)), Build(X\_F, AdmitSet\_t(X\_F), H, I\_t) = 1}.

Definition 56. The constitution running relation

FormRun(X\_F, H, I\_t) = 1 if and only if:

1\. X\_F ∈ P\_+(R(I\_t));

2\. AdmitSet\_t(X\_F) ≠ ∅;

3\. Build(X\_F, AdmitSet\_t(X\_F), H, I\_t) = 1;

4\. Mem\_t(H) = AdmitSet\_t(X\_F);

5\. the difference between entering and not entering H genuinely holds within the system;

6\. no external arbitrary roster can rewrite H while in-system conditions are unchanged.

The running chain from candidates to the structure family: CandState\_t(c) → Eligible → EffectiveCouple → Admit → AdmitSet\_t(X\_F) → Build → Mem\_t(H) → H ∈ Sol\_H(I\_t) → FormRun → FormDuty(H).

It must not be prescribed in reverse that every F be realized by one and the same constitution algorithm.

XII. The Integration Relation and Integration Running

Definition 57. The integration relation

IntRel ⊆ P\_+(R(I\_t)) × H(I\_t) × I\_+.

IntRel(X\_I, H, I\_t) = 1 means the relation family X\_I establishes, within I\_t, one common running relation over the composite structure family H.

Definition 58. Member-to-whole projection

MemberToWhole(c, X\_I, H, I\_t) = 1 means c ∈ Mem\_t(H), and the state changes, running changes, or perturbation responses of c can enter the whole-level relation defined by IntRel.

Definition 59. Whole-to-member projection

WholeToMember(X\_I, c, H, I\_t) = 1 means the whole-level relation defined by IntRel can reversely constrain the subsequent running of member c.

Definition 60. Full-member bidirectional coverage

BiCover(X\_I, H, I\_t) = 1 if and only if:

for every c ∈ Mem\_t(H), MemberToWhole(c, X\_I, H, I\_t) = 1;

WholeToMember(X\_I, c, H, I\_t) = 1.

Definition 61. The subdomain closure relation

SubIntRel(X\_I, A, I\_t) = 1 means the non-empty member subset A forms, within I\_t, a local common-running closure by a sub-relation of X\_I.

SubIntRel makes no reference to the whole-level IntegrateDuty of H.

Definition 62. Cross-partition binding

CrossBind(X\_I, A, B, H, I\_t) = 1 means that under the non-trivial partition Mem\_t(H) = A ∪ B, IntRel still establishes a common governing relation across A and B.

Definition 63. Independent partition

IndependentPartition(X\_I, H, I\_t) = 1 if and only if there exist:

Mem\_t(H) = A ∪ B, A ≠ ∅;

B ≠ ∅;

A ∩ B = ∅;

SubIntRel(X\_I, A, I\_t) = 1;

SubIntRel(X\_I, B, I\_t) = 1;

CrossBind(X\_I, A, B, H, I\_t) = 0.

Definition 64. No independent partition

NoIndependentPartition(X\_I, H, I\_t) = 1 if and only if:

IndependentPartition(X\_I, H, I\_t) = 0.

Definition 65. Integration running

IntegrateRun(X\_I, H, I\_t) = 1 if and only if:

1\. Composite(H) = 1;

2\. X\_I ∈ P\_+(R(I\_t));

3\. IntRel(X\_I, H, I\_t) = 1;

4\. BiCover(X\_I, H, I\_t) = 1;

5\. NoIndependentPartition(X\_I, H, I\_t) = 1;

6\. the relation covers the claimed period of the current's holding. Joint updating, mutual constraint, joint perturbation and recovery, joint reorganization, joint reentry, and other role-equivalent unifying relations may all serve as licensed realization types of IntRel.

If Composite(H) = 0, IntegrateDuty(H) takes the trivial reflexive shape.

Theorem 9 (juxtaposition is not integration). Same roster, same activity, same completion, same output, or simultaneous retention — none unconditionally entails:

IntRel(X\_I, H, I\_t) = 1, and hence none unconditionally entails: IntegrateRun(X\_I, H, I\_t) = 1.

QED.

XIII. The Continuation-Identity Relation and Its Running

Definition 66. The continuation-identity relation

ContRel ⊆ P\_+(R(I\_t)) × H(I\_t) × I\_+.

ContRel(X\_M, H, I\_t) = 1 means the relation family X\_M establishes, within I\_t, an object-side continuation-identity relation over the cross-window structure family H.

Definition 67. Interval succession

Succ\_R(H\|\_\[t\_k, t\_{k+1}\]) = 1 means R ∈ Succ(I\_t) actually bears the object-side succession of H throughout the closed interval \[t\_k, t\_{k+1}\], not merely comparing its two endpoints.

Definition 68. Full-window succession

CoverSucc(X\_M, H, I\_t) = 1 if and only if:

1\. X\_M ∈ P\_+(Succ(I\_t));

2\. for every pair of adjacent subwindows \[t\_k, t\_{k+1}\] of the fixed covering partition Π(I\_t), there exists R ∈ X\_M such that:

Succ\_R(H\|\_\[t\_k, t\_{k+1}\]) = 1.

Definition 69. Continuation-identity running

SameRun(X\_M, H, I\_t) = 1 if and only if:

1\. ContRel(X\_M, H, I\_t) = 1;

2\. CoverSucc(X\_M, H, I\_t) = 1;

3\. the identity of the earlier and later structure family is not given merely by external similarity, temporal proximity, or repeated generation. The contents, members, intensity, and internal relations of H are allowed to change within lawful continuation-identity.

It is not prescribed that ContRel or Succ\_R be realized by a unique update algorithm.

XIV. The KCore Realization Subsystem

KCore is not the sole form of the universal duty-bearing system. This section only defines one class of optional running realization subsystems composed of K\_t(H) and its role subdomains.

Any relation family that lawfully satisfies Bear without passing through KCore still enters the universal duty solution sets.

Definition 70. Admissible running mechanism domain

W\_t(H) ⊆ R(I\_t).

Definition 71. Actual running core

K\_t(H) ⊆ W\_t(H).

Definition 72. Role mapping

Role\_K : W\_t(H) × D × H(I\_t) × I\_+ → B.

Definition 73. Role subdomains

K\_F(H, I\_t) = {K ∈ K\_t(H) : Role\_K(K, F, H, I\_t) = 1}.

K\_I(H, I\_t) = {K ∈ K\_t(H) : Role\_K(K, I, H, I\_t) = 1}.

K\_M(H, I\_t) = {K ∈ K\_t(H) : Role\_K(K, M, H, I\_t) = 1}.

XV. Actual Support and Reserve Support

Definition 74. Subtask labels

Task := {cfg, int, same, back}.

Definition 75. Minimal sufficient support families of a subtask

For q ∈ Task: SK\_q(H, I\_t) is the set of all minimal sufficient running-support relation families of task q. Any minimal sufficient support, if it exists, must belong to SK\_q(H, I\_t).

Definition 76. Actual support

Given an actual history ω: SuppK\_q^ω(H, I\_t) is the set of relations genuinely participating in the running of task q in history ω. If task q is actually engaged in history ω, it is required that:

1\. there exists A ∈ SK\_q(H, I\_t) such that:

A ⊆ SuppK\_q^ω(H, I\_t);

2\. every relation in SuppK\_q^ω has actual causal participation in history ω;

3\. SuppK\_q^ω may contain relations redundant relative to some minimal sufficient set.

If task q is not engaged in history ω, it is permitted that: SuppK\_q^ω(H, I\_t) = ∅.

The task back is required to be engaged only when H\_back(H, I\_t) = 1.

Definition 77. Total actual support

SuppK^ω(H, I\_t) = ⋃\_{q∈Task} SuppK\_q^ω(H, I\_t).

Definition 78. Possible support

PossK\_q(H, I\_t) = ⋃\_{A∈SK\_q(H, I\_t)} A.

Definition 79. Reserve support
