Introduction

The General Form of the Observer proved the necessity of the three duties (F, I, M) under the current fact; the Observer Mathematical Form wrote the duties into a closed formal system. But a formal system can have two ancestries: grown out of the structure of the object itself, or laid upon the object as an extraneous form fitting only at the input-output layer — the latter is functional simulation, and however good the fit, its primitive items are rootless within the theory. There is only one way to discriminate the two ancestries: to demand a construction procedure item by item. That is what this Form does.
