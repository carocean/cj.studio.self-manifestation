# Observer Adjudication Form

This Form ventures one master proposition: for a candidate system, which candidate operational protocols can be established for selected targets among the three duty bearings, institutional events, and individuation structure; what indication readings can the protocols produce; and what are their boundaries of application and known sources of misjudgment. Opening declaration: the present version completes only the candidate-protocol layer of the Adjudication Form; the proxy-validity layer and the formal-verdict layer are not completed.

In this Form we shall find, under sustained questioning, where the proof must go, and derive the general form of observer adjudication.

Then let us first give the result. (What follows is the reading organization set back at the head of the text after the whole was completed; it only previews the result and serves as no premise of the derivation to come.)

The master adjudication formula (preview):

ProxyResult = (object, protocol, reading ∈ {＋, −, ⊥ + reason code}, boundary of application and known sources of misjudgment) — ＋/− are positive and negative candidate-proxy indications, not the target predicate's 1/0; the theoretical Verdict is to be erected separately after the proxy-validity upgrade.

The four-layer verdict regime: Layer 1 · candidate duty indications (＋/− readings, carrying the misjudgment-source list) — issuable; Layer 2 · institutional-event theoretical model statements — reportable within the model only, no operational adjudicative evidence (ObsRealm has no measurement definition and no proxy; institutional-event evidence opens only after its grade-two-through-four upgrades are completed); Layer 3 · the CurrentFact verdict — must not be issued (positive sufficiency is model-relative, requiring H\_cur); Layer 4 · the Lighting verdict — must not be issued (the bridge unclosed). The strength delivered by this Form: one set of candidate operational protocols plus the list of known misjudgment sources; a protocol's promotion to adjudicative grade requires the five-piece validity kit (the promotion credentials in the grade transmission table); before promotion, verdicts are issued only at Layers 1 and 2 in the evidential voice.

In one sentence: what this Form delivers is candidate protocols and a misjudgment-source list, not a validated adjudicator — it speaks at the evidence layer; duty bearing and institutional events may receive evidence, while the current and Lighting receive no verdicts; ⊥ carries a reason code, and − carries three preconditions.

Abstract

The General Form of the Observer erected the three duties, the Observer Mathematical Form the formal system, the General Form of Observer Individuation FinalCommonPos, and the Observer Construction Form gave field-layer constructions, conditional constructions, or candidate correspondences for part of the theoretical predicates (grades cited per the interface table) — all four declare the operational layer assigned to the Adjudication Form. This Form delivers: the three-valued indication regime (＋/−/⊥, four reason-code classes, the three preconditions for a reportable −); the four-layer verdict regime; the criterion grid separating duty × candidate proxy × field quantity × intervention and reading (the two-way validity of each proxy unproven — this version truthfully lowers them to candidate protocols, each proxy annotated with known misjudgment sources); the standard form of the deletion test with its six preconditions; the single-value test procedure and ruling table (the verdict lowered to institutional-configuration candidate grade, with the three-grade breach regime); the misjudgment-source list (presently known sources, overlap and additions permitted). Discipline throughout: theoretical predicates are not defined by proxies; adjudication does not overstep the necessary direction; downstream citations must carry this Form's grade (candidate-protocol grade).

1\. Task Demarcation and Inheritance

Four upstream inheritances: the three duties and their failing systems (the General Form); Bear, the solution sets, DutyClosed, and the direction-separation theorem (the Mathematical Form — above all Theorem 11: DutyClosed = 0 ⇒ CurrentFact = 0, while DutyClosed = 1 ⇏ CurrentFact = 1); the five conditions of FinalCommonPos with the five false common positions and five degenerate successions (the General Form of Individuation); the field-quantity channels of the discriminant proxies — Gate, Pass, the three Align components, the measurability of ReCont (the Construction Form).  
The task of this Form: to lower the above theoretical criteria into candidate operational protocols, and to list the known misjudgment sources of each protocol; adjudicative validity and misjudgment bounds are not delivered. This Form re-proves no upstream conclusion; failure of an operational proxy breaches this Form, not the upstream.

2\. The Three-Valued Regime and the Adjudication Record

Proxy readings take three values: ＋ (indication present), − (indication absent), ⊥. ⊥ carries a reason code of four classes (unified at group level): ⊥\_measure, ⊥\_conflict, ⊥\_transition, ⊥\_unclassified; they may not be encoded as 0 or 1, and may not be merged into either side of any statistic. The three preconditions for a reportable −: intervention sufficient, measurement sensitive, time window valid — lacking any one, the reading lowers to ⊥\_measure. Declaration: the three preconditions guarantee only that the experiment has not manifestly failed; − is a negative proxy indication, and its negative force requires a separate proof of negative validity.  
The four elements of the adjudication record: the object; the protocol (intervention points, reading points, sampling scheme, pre-registered thresholds); the result (three values plus reason code); the boundary of application and known misjudgment sources (quantitative confidence composition not erected — this version does not claim confidence bounds; the promotion credential is the computation or calibration of per-proxy misjudgment probabilities). Lacking any of the four elements, the record is invalid. Issuing layers of the record: Layers 1 and 2 of the four-layer verdict regime only.  
The separation requirement (master discipline): an intervention point may not double as a result reading — wherever one and the same variable both receives the intervention and reads the result, the adjudication record is invalid (inheriting the corollary principle of the Mathematical Form and the same clause of the law-holding adjudication form).

3\. The Criterion Grid (duty × proxy × field quantity × separation of intervention and reading)

F · member-constitution bearing (candidate protocol; output: supporting/opposing evidence):  
Proxy 1 (intervention variability): licensed intervention upon the admission pathway of candidate c (the Boundary-Gate parameter side), the reading being change in Mem\_t(H) — variability holding gives supporting evidence for the bearing of F; it does not suffice alone to prove the bearing (an intervention changing the outcome is not the whole content of non-arbitrary system-side constitution), hence it is used together with Proxy 2 and issues in the evidential voice only.  
Proxy 2 (pure-referential invariance): external registrations divide into five classes (pure referential labels, normal inputs, configuration writes, training history, feedback records — the demarcation inheriting the F criterion of the Construction Form); the invariance test applies only to pure referential labels and takes causal isolation as precondition; rewrites of the other four classes are input or mechanism interventions and do not enter this test. The old wording "rewriting all external registrations" is withdrawn (inoperable and self-destroying of its precondition).  
Field-quantity channel: the three Gate preconditions (R\_{f\_B}, MatchTheta, AnchorCapacity) are intervention points, the Pass outcome the reading — intervention point and reading naturally separated.  
Misjudgment defense: change of candidate activity or completion may not be recorded as change of membership (downstream constraint 1 of the Limit Form).

I · jointly-being-one bearing (non-trivial only when Composite(H) = 1; a two-layer evidence regime — coupling is not jointly-being-one: a shared controller can pass all three proxies with no common position, hence only both layers complete give supporting evidence for the bearing of I):  
Layer A · integration-dynamics evidence: Proxy 1 (joint perturbation) — perturb one member and read the coordination of the rest and of the whole; the common-drive control procedure: build in parallel a common-input explanatory model, and only the residual of coordination beyond the common-drive prediction counts as integration evidence (input severing is one control means among others, not the whole of valid separation). Proxy 2 (partition test, restricted reading) — for each pre-registered bipartition, test cross-group propagation and governance; its probative force is limited to excluding a completely independent stitching, and does not positively prove FCP. Proxy 3 (joint recovery) — severally independent recovery yields opposing evidence.  
Layer B · governance and constitutivity candidate indications: the deletion test (six preconditions) and the governance test — the outputs are governance indications and constitutivity candidate indications, not called complete common-position evidence (complete FCP evidence is erected separately by the merged conditions of the individuation cluster); with Layer A complete and Layer B lacking, at most integration-dynamics indications are entered on record.  
Field-quantity channels: Sync (temporal synchrony), Itr (overlap, measurable), π\_tune (reading candidate for tuning-resource allocation weight and parameter-update contribution).  
Misjudgment defenses: juxtaposed facts may not be recorded as jointly-being-one (Limit Form constraint 2); a single-constituent H may not be asked for non-trivial integration evidence (asking is itself a false negative).

M · cross-time identity bearing (candidate protocol, five-part regime — sampling gives Part 1 only: no break seen proves no succession):  
Part 1 · break exclusion: sampling at τ\_s ≤ τ\_reg excludes complete breaks up to the declared bound. Precondition (H1a): the existence of τ\_reg requires the succession process to satisfy a regularity precondition (finite propagation rate, minimal retention time, or rupture leaving traces); this existence is a theoretical precondition and not a mere calibration matter — on hosts permitting arbitrarily fast rupture-and-recovery, τ\_reg does not exist and this proxy does not open. Part 2 · causal-succession evidence: a test of causal continuation between the structures of adjacent subwindows (no similarity comparison). Part 3 · chain continuity: the subwindow-by-subwindow evidence can be strung to cover Π(I\_t). Part 4 · replacement exclusion: the control signatures of rupture-and-rebuild (trace-leaving, rebuilding cost) not present. Part 5 · the ReCont model-estimation candidate: ReCont has no measurement definition, and estimates are uniformly called model estimates (the claim of direct reading where measurable is withdrawn). Opening condition: hosts on which τ\_reg is proven to exist and is calibrated (H1b); in this version ReCont produces model-estimation candidates only.  
Reading discipline: change of content, membership, or intensity is no rupture — M permits continuation-identity through lawful change; the object of reading is the succession relation, not state similarity.  
Misjudgment defense: sameness of state may not be recorded as identity (Limit Form constraint 3); similarity merging yields ⊥, not 1.

Candidate completion Comp (candidate proxy):  
Proxy: behavioral saturation (re-walking cost fallen to baseline, return after perturbation) — saturation is a candidate indication of the holding of 𝒞\_c, not its proof (the holding-domain regime of the Construction Form); this proxy issues supporting evidence only; known misjudgment sources: plateau false saturation, resource-cap false saturation.  
Misjudgment defense: completion may not be recorded as current membership (Limit Form constraint 1); background completion is a lawful case.

4\. The Constitutive Deletion Test (inheriting Individuation H3)

The operationalization of the fourth FinalCommonPos condition (the undeletable constitutive role). The four kinds of deletion are inequivalent; this Form erects the standard form:  
Standard form · reversible functional masking: recoverably, repeatably, and with controllable dosage, mask the succession function of the candidate common position, the reading being the structural indications of jointly-being-one vanishing under masking and returning upon recovery. Force declaration: the equivalence of the theoretical operator Rem and this operational form is unclosed (the equivalence H split-tracked on record), hence the output of this test is supporting evidence of constitutivity, not an adjudication of Constitutive = 1. The six preconditions (lacking any, the evidence lowers to ⊥): scope specificity (masking the succession function only, not touching the local closures); local-closure retention control (independent verification that the several locals remain each complete during masking); recovery isomorphy (the post-recovery structure of the same type as the prior baseline); dosage effect (a pre-registered, repeatable, and interpretable dose-effect relation, thresholds and non-monotonicity permitted); sham-masking control (a null operation produces no like indication); bypass control (the vanishing of the indication not masked by compensation through a bypass channel).  
The standing of the three non-standard forms: physical deletion — single-shot evidence, unrepeatable, auxiliary only; causal blocking — it must be proven that the blocking has not collaterally damaged the local closures (collateral damage yields false positives); model deletion — simulation evidence, supporting only, never adjudicating.  
The equivalence of the four forms is unproven (H2); the adjudication record must declare the form used.

5\. The Single-Value Test Procedure and Ruling Table

(Inheriting the General Form and the General Form of Individuation: the principle itself resides there, the test here.)  
Procedure: 1. pool-track pre-identification (protocol per the Mathematical Form of Individuation, precondition H5); 2. within the track, seek H₁, H₂ each passing the three-duty candidate protocols (evidence grade); 3. the four tests: separately perturbable, separately recoverable, mutually non-integrating, no common continuation chain; 4. the common-upper-position exclusion procedure: upon the candidate common-position set of H₁ ∪ H₂, execute the deletion test of Section 4 and the governance test — where all four pass yet the common upper position is unexcluded, the case may be the two sides of one composite structure (the shared-controller counterexample family), and the verdict may not be promoted.  
Ruling table (three-grade verdict regime): Grade 1 · no candidate counterexample formed — any item 0; Grade 2 · candidate counterexample — all four indications ＋, pre-identification up to standard, and within the present search domain no common-position supporting indication obtained (carrying the search-completeness status; an incomplete relation graph yields ⊥\_measure and does not enter Grade 2); the verdict is "same-track dual independent institutional-configuration candidate" (neither "dual currents" nor "common position excluded" is claimed); Grade 3 · confirmed breach — requiring dual positive CurrentFact verdicts; Layer 3 being unopened, this grade is presently unreachable. Any item ⊥ — ⊥ with its code.  
Discipline: the accumulation of Grade 1 is not the principle proven; the attainment of Grade 2 is not the breach — the constitutive-commitment standing of the principle and the closure of Layer 3 are both unchanged by test outcomes.

7\. Adjudication Limits

1\. Positive sufficiency of CurrentFact: unadjudicable (Layer 3 closed, the H3 domain of the Mathematical Form). The strongest verdicts of this Form are the evidential statements of Layers 1 and 2 — "duty-bearing evidence on record, institutional-event evidence on record"; the closure of Layers 3 and 4 is an institution of the whole Form (the four-layer regime of Section 2), not an isolated case of this section.  
2\. The identifiability bound of the explicit state and the boundary of ontic randomness: assigned to the Current Inversion Form; this Form does not adjudicate.  
3\. Observer counting: this Form provides candidate supporting or opposing indications for part of the conditions of FinalCommonPos only, and does not adjudicate FCP as holding or not holding; counting and partition go to the Mathematical Form of Individuation.

8\. Breach Modes

1\. Construct a system in which both F proxies read 1 while the member-constitution duty is in fact unborne (or conversely) — the F proxies fail; 2. an instance of the common-input defense failing — where coordination persisting after input severing is still not integration; 3. the τ\_reg-type regularity bound systematically uncalibratable on some host family — the M proxies fail; 4. the standard form of the deletion test producing systematic false positives or false negatives under reversible masking; 5. all four ruling-table items 1 while the system is proven by independent criteria to be in fact one current — the single-value test procedure fails. These breach the protocols of this Form; none breaches the upstream theoretical criteria.

Gap Declarations

Observer Adjudication – H1a　The existence of τ\_reg  
Status: the existence of τ\_reg rests on a regularity precondition of the succession process (finite propagation rate, minimal retention time, rupture-leaving-traces class); on hosts where the precondition fails (arbitrarily fast rupture-and-recovery permitted), τ\_reg does not exist and the M proxies do not open. The earlier version's omission of existence, docketing calibration only, was corrected by Volume 6 and is on record.  
What is needed: the theoretical characterization of the regularity precondition and a classification of hosts.  
What it does not affect: the protocol structure within the domain where the precondition holds.  
Location: Section 3, M Part 1.

Observer Adjudication – H1b　The calibration of τ\_reg  
Status: the calibration method within the existence domain is not erected.  
What is needed: a calibration protocol from the Realization Form or the empirical side.  
What it does not affect: the ⊥ discipline.  
Location: Section 3, the M proxies.

Observer Adjudication – H2　The equivalence of the four deletion forms  
Status: reversible functional masking is erected as the standard form; the equivalence conditions of physical deletion, causal blocking, and model deletion with the standard form are unproven.  
What is needed: cross-form controlled experiments or a theoretical equivalence proof.  
What it does not affect: the structure of the standard form as candidate operational protocol. What it affects: its adjudicative force upon Rem/Constitutive (no adjudicative force before equivalence is proven).  
Location: Section 4.

Observer Adjudication – H3　The Lighting bridge (inherited)  
Status: inheriting the one-directional reading of g1-H3 and the CurrentFact correspondence of the Construction Form; independent phenomenal adjudication unopened.  
What is needed: the unified ruling of the Observer Group Master Map on the constitutive standing of the bridge.  
What it does not affect: the structure of the candidate protocols and the reporting of indications. What it affects: their promotion to duty-bearing evidence or formal verdicts.  
Location: Section 7.

Observer Adjudication – H4　The role specificity of the proxies  
Status: the F, I, M candidate proxies each erect protocols, but where one physical process bears several duties, the role assignment of proxy readings requires counterfactual separation; the separation protocol is not detailed.

What it does not affect: the candidate-protocol structure and the reporting of indications. What it affects: promotion to duty-bearing evidence and role-level assignment.
