# Mathematical Form of Observer Individuation

This Form ventures one master proposition: the individuation structure already forced out by the General Form of Observer Individuation — joint attribution, the genuine final common position, constitutive private-exhaustion gathering-into-one, one position per I — can be written into a closed formal system: the complete type of FinalCommonPos, the minimal observer unit, the same-unit relation, and unit counting, with partition and counting proven well-defined under declared conditions.

In this Form we shall find, under sustained questioning, where the proof must go, and derive the mathematical form of observer individuation.

Then let us first give the result. (What follows is the reading organization set back at the head of the text after the whole was completed; it only previews the result and serves as no premise of the derivation to come.)

The master individuation formula (preview):

Unit(J, P\_G, I\_t) ⟺ FCP(P\_G, J, I\_t) ∧ J maximal　(formal candidate definition, unconditional)  
The set of Units constitutes a non-overlapping system of minimal units, and N\_obs(t) = \|{Unit}\| is well-defined — this layer rests on the NoShare and finality clauses (conditional layer; the standing of the two clauses in Section 4).

In one sentence: a final common position satisfying the five conditions, paired with its maximal local set, is the formal candidate of the minimal observer unit; these candidates constitute a countable system of units conditional on the two clauses NoShare and finality — counting observers is conditional counting.

Abstract

This Form formalizes the full output of the General Form of Observer Individuation: the set of local convergences and the being-here — position types; the five relations attribution, succession, governance, private-exhaustion, constitutivity; the complete type of FinalCommonPos (the five conditions formalized one by one, the five false common positions and five degenerate successions excluded by the relation definitions); the minimal observer unit and maximality; the same-unit relation and its equivalence conditions; unit counting and its well-definedness conditions; the pool-track mapping interface and the pool-track pre-identification protocol (delivered to Adjudication H5). The mathematical assets of the old General Form of the Observer v3.0 (the pool graph, admissible pool tracks, current equivalence) migrate into this Form after reworking (the asset-migration record in the version section). Honesty clause: the partition and counting theorems are conditional theorems, the standing of their conditions (NoShare, finality) explicitly declared; cross-time continuation is given a slot only, its full account going to the Observer Pool-Split Transition Form.

1\. The Type Layer

I\_t is the non-zero working time window (inherited from the Mathematical Form). L\_t = {L₁, …, L\_n} is the set of local manifestation convergences within the window; each L\_i generates a local being-here In\_i and a local attribution P\_i. 𝒫 is the position domain: the domain of structural positions within the system able to bear attribution and private-exhaustion — membership in 𝒫 is given by facts of bearing relations, not by naming (set names, labels, encapsulating boundaries, routing nodes, and record repositories do not enter 𝒫 by reference). H\_i is the current structure family relevant to L\_i (type inherited from the Mathematical Form).

2\. The Relation Layer (five relations)

Attribution. Anchor(In, P, I\_t): the final return of the being-here In lands on the position P — "final" meaning: at this resolution layer no more direct position bears the same return.  
Succession. Inherit(P\_G, P\_i, I\_t): P\_G causally receives the return of the being-here on the P\_i side. Both clauses are necessary: first, causal succession — the attribution event on the P\_i side has causal succession on the P\_G side; second, reverse governance — P\_G reversely governs the side it succeeds to. The second clause is the genuine load-bearer of exclusion: routing and forwarding are also causal transmission, and the causal clause alone cannot exclude them; their being false common positions fails on the absence of reverse governance. By the conjunction of the two clauses, the five false types — set, naming, encapsulation, routing, record — none satisfies succession.  
Governance. Governs(P\_G, H, I\_t): P\_G reversely constrains the running of the current family H (the whole-to-member direction non-zero).  
Private-exhaustion. Approp(P\_G, H, I\_t): H is gathered into one as the current of P\_G (the formal slot of the Inner-Chapter chain "the position, in turn, privately exhausts the current and gathers it into one").  
Constitutivity. Constitutive(P\_G, J, I\_t): defined by the removal operator Rem — under the counterfactual Rem(the succession and private-exhaustion bearing of P\_G), the joint structural composition of J no longer holds. The joint structural composition := the conjunction of three predicates already independently defined in this type system — ∃P: the several In\_i are jointly attributed to P through succession chains ∧ Governs(P, ∪H\_i) ∧ Approp(P, ∪H\_i) (that is, the structural remainder of FCP short of this condition and condition five). The intervention semantics of Rem (the counterfactual clause of the theoretical operator): fix the system boundary, the non-target update laws, the external inputs, and the several local closures; sever only the Anchor, Inherit, Governs, Approp bearing relations of P\_G toward J; judge within a pre-registered short counterfactual window whether the joint structural composition fails — the long-run takeover by substitute positions and system restructuring do not enter this judgment (erected separately as problems of succession and transition). Non-circularity declaration: the criterion uses none of the to-be-defined target words "one I" or "unit"; the correspondence of the joint structural composition to jointly-being-one-I is this Form's direction of conclusion, no defining constituent. Reversible functional masking is the candidate operational proxy of Rem, equivalence unproven (the split-track order); by the definition of Constitutive, the five degenerate types — upper observer, broadcast receiver, logger, arbiter, post-hoc narrator — none satisfies it (their removal leaves the several local closures each complete).

3\. The Complete Type of FinalCommonPos

FCP(P\_G, J, I\_t) ⟺  
(1) ∀L\_i ∈ J: Anchor(In\_i, P\_G, I\_t), holding directly or through a succession chain Inherit(P\_G, P\_i);  
(2) P\_G ∈ 𝒫 (the genuine-bearer clause — the five false types excluded by the conjunction of the 𝒫 qualification definition and the two Inherit clauses (causal succession plus reverse governance) — the routing type failing specifically on reverse governance);  
(3) Governs(P\_G, ∪H\_i) ∧ Approp(P\_G, ∪H\_i);  
(4) Constitutive(P\_G, J, I\_t);  
(5) Atomic(P\_G): no bearing decomposition of P\_G exists. The decomposition relation Decompose(P\_G; P′, P″), formal definition: (a) the part relation: PartOf(P′, P\_G) ∧ PartOf(P″, P\_G) — PartOf being the structural constitution relation on 𝒫, with three axioms: non-externality (PartOf(P, Q) ⇒ all bearing relations of P are realized through the structure of Q; external collaborators do not satisfy it), antisymmetry, proper parthood (P ≠ Q); its transitivity and finer axioms entered as H1a. (b) bearing preservation: there exists a partition J′ ⊔ J″ = J (mutually exclusive, jointly exhaustive, both non-empty) such that Resp(P\_G, J) = Resp(P′, J′) ⊔ Resp(P″, J″) — Resp(·,·) being the four-tuple of attribution, succession, governance, and private-exhaustion bearing of a position toward a local set, ⊔ the disjoint union: responsibility exclusivity (no bearing doubly booked), complete coverage (nothing omitted), no surplus (nothing in the union beyond the bearings of P\_G). (c) independence: between P′ and P″ no Inherit chain and no common governing position. The binary-sufficiency lemma (to be proven, H1a): any finite bearing decomposition is obtainable by iterated binary decomposition — before the lemma is proven, the negation domain of Atomic is restricted to binary decompositions. Atomic(P\_G) :⟺ ¬∃(P′, P″) Decompose(P\_G; P′, P″) — what is negated is the decomposability of P\_G itself, not the existence of any independent double-bearing scheme. The formal face of the one-position-per-I clause: what can bear decomposition is no minimal one position.  
Quantifier declaration: P\_G is existentially quantified over 𝒫; J ⊆ L\_t is non-empty. This is the complete type demanded by H2 of the General Form of Observer Individuation, its five conditions corresponding clause by clause to that Form's five.

4\. Unit, Same-Unit, and Counting

The minimal observer unit: Unit(J, P\_G, I\_t) ⟺ FCP(P\_G, J, I\_t) ∧ J maximal (no J′ ⊋ J and P′ exist with FCP(P′, J′, I\_t) — maximality forestalls the double counting of "any subset also counts as an observer").  
Clause two (finality, inherited from the constitutive layer of the Inner Chapters and the General Form of Individuation): the final attribution of each In is unique — Anchor is functional at the final layer. Standing declaration: this is an inherited constitutive clause, no theorem of this Form (the Inner Chapters' "being-here is attributed to the sole position of the current"); its breach is an upstream breach.  
Clause one (NoShare): no local belongs to the J of two different units. Standing: a condition, no axiom — the complete treatment of overlap cases entered as H1.  
The same-unit relation: the domain of SameUnit is the coverage domain Dom(I\_t) := ⋃{J : ∃P\_G, FCP(P\_G, J, I\_t)}; for L\_i, L\_j ∈ Dom(I\_t): SameUnit(L\_i, L\_j) ⟺ ∃(P\_G, J): FCP ∧ L\_i, L\_j ∈ J. Locals outside the domain do not enter this relation; reflexivity is claimed within the domain only.  
Theorem 1 (equivalence, conditional theorem): under NoShare and finality, SameUnit is an equivalence relation on the covered locals. Proof: reflexivity and symmetry by definition; transitivity — let L\_i ∼ L\_j (via U₁), L\_j ∼ L\_k (via U₂); then L\_j ∈ J₁ ∩ J₂, NoShare forces U₁ = U₂, transitivity holds. QED.  
Theorem 2 (partition, conditional theorem): under the same conditions, the J's of the several units partition all covered locals. QED by Theorem 1.  
Counting: N\_cand(t) := the number of unit candidates (unconditional); under NoShare and finality, separately erect N\_obs(t) := N\_cand(t) (the well-defined observer number, a conditional quantity) — downstream citations must carry the preconditions.  
Theorem 3 (well-definedness, conditional theorem): under NoShare and finality, N\_obs is well-defined (the number of partition classes). QED.  
Theorem 4 (unit-position bijection, conditional theorem, under the finality clause): each unit has exactly one final position. Proof: existence by FCP; uniqueness — suppose U = (J, P) and (J, P′) both hold with P ≠ P′; then the beings-here of J finally attribute both to P and to P′, contradicting finality (functionality). QED.

5\. The Non-Counter Theorem Group (the formalization of the corollaries of the General Form of Individuation)

Proposition 1 (independence from the local count, formal proof pending): to be proven that a model pair exists with the same number of local convergences and different N\_obs (one FCP governing many locals; many FCPs each governing one) — the local count is no counter.  
Proposition 2 (independence from the pool-track count, formal proof pending): to be proven that a model pair exists with the same number of pool tracks and different N\_obs: one track carrying two complete closures with no common position (one track, many units); several tracks jointly realizing one FCP (many tracks, one unit). Pool count and track count are no counters. The model pairs of Propositions 1 and 2 must be given explicitly with objects, relations, and the satisfaction of the five conditions, checked compatible with NoShare and finality; the formal construction goes to H4 (raised to a proof task; before proof they are not called theorems).  
Theorem 7 (zero contribution of the observer, under the bystander condition): let O read the several units, Constitutive(O, ·) = 0, and let the bystander condition hold — the addition or removal of O has no causal influence on the existing Anchor, Inherit, Governs, Approp, Constitutive, or maximality. Then N\_obs is unchanged. Proof: under the bystander condition the holding of the existing FCPs is unchanged; O itself fails condition four, becomes no position, merges no units. QED. When the bystander condition fails (O rewrites the relational structure), the conclusion lowers to: O itself adds no unit; changes to the existing units are booked separately.  
Theorem 7 is proven; the constructions of Propositions 1 and 2 follow the intent of the countermodel group of the General Form of Individuation, the formal details being the proof task of H4.

6\. The Pool-Track Mapping Interface and the Pre-Identification Protocol

Pool-track assets (migrated from the v3.0 source draft, re-erected in layers after reworking — resolving the definitional circle): Layer 1 · instantaneous pool components: the connected components of the per-window capacity-sharing coupling graph (adjudicable window by window, no cross-time involvement); Layer 2 · candidate tracks: obtained by cross-window trajectory matching — the matching criteria restricted to pool-layer relations proper: component-membership overlap and pool-layer succession (SuccPool-class capacity-sharing continuity or physical continuity of field state); Inherit, Approp, SuccUnit and other unit-level relations are forbidden in matching (against grade backflow); splitting and merging are outcome classes of matching, not prior exclusions of the track definition; Layer 3 · admissible pool tracks (stable tracks) as the a-posteriori class of candidate tracks; Tube(q) the track tube.  
The mapping condition (inheriting Section 9 of the General Form of Individuation): a pool track T may serve as a realization read-back of the final position P\_G if and only if the structure on T bears all five conditions of FCP — pool-track identity does not automatically confer individuation identity.  
The pre-identification protocol (delivered to Adjudication H5): Step 1, identification of instantaneous pool components (window by window, the Cap bookkeeping channel); Step 2, cross-window trajectory matching (per the Layer-2 criteria; splitting and merging are matching outputs, no preconditions); Step 3, independence verification — the separation-advantage statistic S := the time integral of \[Sep − (Coact + Infilt + Reentry)\] over the verification window. The three-valued mapping: S ≥ θ₊ adjudges different tracks; S ≤ θ₋ (θ₋ \< 0, with the preconditions of capacity sharing and pool-layer succession holding — same-track is not derived from non-separation alone) adjudges same track; θ₋ \< S \< θ₊ yields ⊥\_measure; thresholds and fluctuation tolerances are host-calibration items (calibration docketed jointly, with Adjudication H1); the relation to τ\_split is a citation of the same sustained-reading semantics, not one and the same constant. On uncalibrated hosts Step 3 yields ⊥ only. All three steps passed, pool-track pre-identification is up to standard; any ⊥, and the single-value test procedure yields ⊥. Protocol standing declaration: what this protocol delivers is the framework and the three-step adjudication sequence; its operational closure rests on calibration — Adjudication H5 is accordingly recorded half-closed, not closed.

7\. Cross-Time Continuation (slot)

SuccUnit(U\_t, U\_{t+δ}): the cross-window continuation of units, its slot defined by the succession chain of P\_G (the cross-window prolongation of Inherit and Approp); the full account of continuation, forking, merging, and vanishing goes to the Observer Pool-Split Transition Form (inheriting H4 of the General Form of Individuation; this Form does not prove by proxy). SuccUnit and pool-track succession SuccPool are kept apart and may not be conflated (inheriting the separation discipline of the v3.0 assets).

8\. Breach Modes

1\. Construct a system in which all five FCP conditions pass while by independent criteria it is not one I (or conversely) — the complete type is breached; 2. an instance in which NoShare and finality hold while the transitivity of SameUnit fails — Theorem 1 is breached; 3. one minimal position (Atomic holding) proven to constitute at once two genuinely distinguishable I's — condition five and the upstream one-position-per-I breached together; 4. a pool-track pair passing all three protocol steps, upon which the single-value test systematically misjudges — the protocol is breached. Breach of the upstream constitutive clause (finality) goes to the upstream.
