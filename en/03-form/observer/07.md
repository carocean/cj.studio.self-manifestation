# Observer Pool-Split Transition Form

This Form ventures one master proposition: the four basic cross-time variants of the minimal observer unit — continuation, forking, merging, vanishing — each possess statable structural conditions and field-layer mechanism conditions, time windows, and stability requirements; the path dependence of transition (the forking and merging thresholds not coinciding) is advanced in propositional form, its proof task declared on record.

In this Form we shall find, under sustained questioning, where the proof must go, and derive the general form of observer-unit transition.

Then let us first give the result. (What follows is the reading organization set back at the head of the text after the whole was completed; it only previews the result and serves as no premise of the derivation to come.)

The master transition formula (preview):

Trans ∈ {SuccUnit, SplitUnit, MergeUnit, VanishUnit}  
Forking: two structurally necessary conditions (each side severally persisting ∧ no complete FCP on the union) plus the field-layer mechanism condition (cross-boundary separation advantage sustained)  
Merging: one structurally necessary condition (complete FCP holding on the union) plus the field-layer mechanism condition (cross-boundary mutual-seep advantage sustained)  
Within the transition band, adjudication yields ⊥ only (adjudication layer); path dependence stands as a proposition (the H3 proof task).

In one sentence: how one I becomes two, how two return to one, how one is no more — four basic variants, each with structural conditions and candidate mechanism conditions, each with its time window; and a to-be-proven proposition that the way in may bear upon the way out.

Abstract

The Mathematical Form of Observer Individuation erected same-window units and counting, leaving the SuccUnit slot and the dynamical conditions of pre-identification protocol Step 2; the split-brain interface of the General Form of Observer Individuation declared "the anatomical severing itself makes no many I's," and the necessary conditions, time windows, stability, and hysteresis of transition fall to this Form. This Form delivers: the relational framework of the four basic variants (the succession mapping partially formalized, the remaining clauses H4a); the structural conditions of forking and merging (definitional corollaries) and the field-layer candidate mechanism conditions (the four rates; in-model necessity unproven); the severance-insufficiency theorem; the transition-band adjudication clause (defined directly by stability-window failure); the path-dependence proposition (mechanism-supported, unproven). Honesty clause: the time-window constants await calibration; identity of regeneration across an empty window is not answered by proxy (H4c).

2\. The Formal Definitions of the Four Variants

Let I\_t, I\_{t+δ} be adjacent windows, with the former-window unit U = (J, P\_G).  
Continuation SuccUnit(U, U′): in the latter window there exists U′ = (J′, P\_G′), with P\_G′ the successor of P\_G's succession chain (the cross-window causal extension of Inherit and Approp), and J′ the succession image of J.  
Forking SplitUnit(U; U₁, U₂): in the latter window there exist U₁ = (J₁, P₁), U₂ = (J₂, P₂), J₁ ∩ J₂ = ∅, the locals of both being succession images of J; P₁, P₂ each severally inherit from the succession chain of P\_G (the provenance clause — excluding the confusion "old unit vanishes plus two new units arise unrelated"); and ¬∃P″ such that FCP(P″, J₁ ∪ J₂).  
Merging MergeUnit(U₁, U₂; U): the symmetric reverse, with the genuine-common-position clause added (Section 4).  
The succession-mapping clause (shared by forking and merging): the minimal constraints on local succession Succ\_L and position succession Succ\_P — each old local has at most one successor side (no cross-side twinning); old locals without successor are permitted (with-vanishing), as is the genesis of new locals (with-genesis), and where these occur the variant verdict must be recorded in composite form (pure forking / with-vanishing / with-genesis distinguished); the succession-image parts of both sides are non-empty. The complete formalization of the succession mapping (functionality, coverage, the permissibility of shared intermediate nodes) is entered under the H4 extension.  
Vanishing VanishUnit(U): in the latter window there exists no succeeding unit whatsoever (a purely relational definition). Mechanism note: the dissipation of P\_G's constitutive bearing with no several inheritor is its typical mechanism and adjudicative evidence, entering not the definition — dissipation in place while succession continues through a new position is no vanishing (adjudged as SuccUnit or MergeUnit).  
Variant declaration: the four are the basic variants (one-to-one succession, one-to-many, many-to-one, no succession); the composite case many-to-many decomposes into concurrent basic variants, the complete handling of concurrency entered under H4 — this Form claims no exhaustiveness.

3\. The Necessary Dynamical Conditions of Forking

Definitional Corollary 1 (the structurally necessary conditions of forking): for SplitUnit to hold, necessarily:  
(a) each side severally persists: after the several inheritance, P₁, P₂ each satisfy FCP on their side (several Re-Continuation and constitutivity holding) — lacking this, the case is vanishing or mutilation, not forking;  
(b) no complete FCP on the union: no P″ whatsoever (including the old P\_G or one newly formed) satisfies FCP on J₁ ∪ J₂; old governance channels may partially remain, provided only that they cannot support any FCP on the union.  
Proof: (a) is given directly by U₁, U₂ being units in the forking definition; (b) is given directly by the final clause of the definition — both are structural necessities carried by the definition; the substance of this corollary is the declaration of their irreducibility: cut (a) and forking is confounded with vanishing; cut (b) and it is confounded with internal differentiation. QED.  
The field-layer mechanism condition (an in-model condition, not universally necessary): within the dynamical model of Field 14, (b) can be realized by a cross-boundary separation advantage — Sep sustainedly overpowering Coact + Infilt + Reentry for at least τ\_split, making cross-group governance unmaintainable. Standing: a candidate mechanism condition — the exhaustiveness of the four rates is unproven, and in-model necessity is likewise unproven (the derivation from the Field-14 equations to the FCP conditions not given); any other mechanism, in the model or beyond it, that achieves (b) contradicts this corollary in nothing.

4\. The Necessary Dynamical Conditions of Merging

Definitional Corollary 2 (the structurally necessary condition of merging): for MergeUnit to hold, necessarily: complete FCP holds on the union — P″ must pass all five conditions, the genuine-bearer clause and the constitutivity clause (the Rem counterfactual) among them: shared output, shared records, joint encapsulation, routing bridges — none constitutes merging (the exclusion of false common positions applies unchanged in the cross-time case; constitutivity is not listed separately, avoiding double counting).  
Proof: given directly by the definition of merging; the substance lies in its irreducibility — cut any condition and a false common position is admitted. QED.  
The field-layer mechanism condition (an in-model condition, not universally necessary): within the Field-14 dynamical model, the formation of the union FCP can be realized by a cross-boundary mutual-seep advantage — Coact + Infilt + Reentry sustainedly overpowering Sep for at least τ\_merge, supplying the dynamical basis for the causal clause of Inherit. Standing: a candidate mechanism condition, as noted at Definitional Corollary 1.  
The merging-trap declaration: two systems establishing high-bandwidth communication, sharing all data, synchronizing all states — none of this automatically makes a merge: communication is routing-type false commonality, data sharing record-type false commonality; what makes a merge is solely the formation of a genuine final common position.

5\. The Severance-Insufficiency Theorem and the Split-Brain Read-Back

Theorem 1 (severance insufficient): anatomical or architectural severance (the physical removal of cross-side Infilt and Coact channels) is insufficient for many I's.  
Proof (logical form): severance at most furthers the field-layer mechanism condition of forking (the furthering of separation advantage); yet SplitUnit further requires the several inheritance of the provenance clause, the several FCP persistence of both sides, and the absence of complete FCP on the union — none of the three is entailed by severance: severance gives no inheritance mapping, secures no persistence of the sides, and excludes not the formation of a new common position. Hence severance is insufficient. QED. (Residual channels maintaining the union FCP, one side failing to be a unit, and the like are instance witnesses of the insufficiency, not an exhaustion the proof requires.)  
The split-brain read-back (inheriting Section 10 of the General Form of Individuation, dynamically completed): the adjudication of many I's after a split brain must check all three conditions; output conflict, report separation, behavioral inconsistency are no criteria (each an execution-layer phenomenon); after severance the system may long remain in the transition band — adjudication yields ⊥, neither many I's nor a confirmation of one I. The test protocol for residual governance channels is entered as H2.

6\. Stability Window, Transition Band, and Hysteresis

The stability window: the adjudication of any variant requires its defining conditions to persist ≥ τ\_stab; instantaneous satisfaction does not count.  
The transition-band adjudication clause: the transition band := the period within the stability window τ\_stab during which the relevant structural criteria (the holding or not of the union FCP, the holding or not of each side's FCP) flip or cannot be held — by this definition, the protocol precondition of window-level verdicts (criteria holding throughout the window) is directly unsatisfiable, and the sole output is ⊥\_transition. Four-rate equipoise and half-persisting governance are lowered to candidate indicators of the transition band (equipoise does not necessarily cause flipping — deep sediment, strong anchoring, and external stabilization can all keep FCP stable under equipoise), and do not enter the definition. Level declaration: the object-level instantaneous count N\_obs(t) is definable moment by moment — flipping is instability of the count, not undefinedness; the window-level verdict Verdict(I\_t), its stability-window precondition unsatisfiable, yields ⊥ only; ⊥ is an adjudication-layer conclusion, no object-level assertion. QED.  
Proposition (path dependence · hysteresis, to be proven): the forking threshold and the merging threshold do not coincide; transition exhibits hysteresis. Field-layer support: after forking, each side's positive feedback strengthens itself (Field 4); after merging, the joint governance sediments into a common core (Field 14) — the two yield a retention tendency of configurations. Declaration: a retention tendency does not directly entail non-coinciding thresholds and a hysteresis loop; the proof requires control parameters, state equations, and bistable-interval analysis — the proof task of H3 (upgraded from a calibration item). This Form advances it in propositional form and calls it no theorem.  
Theorem 2 (vanishing is not track-death): VanishUnit does not entail the vanishing of the pool track — track persisting while the unit dies is a lawful case (constitutive bearing dissipating, Re-Continuation collapsing while the carrier structure persists; the unit-level correspondent of Field 20 case four, the read-back slot of deep-sleep-class states). The reverse case (track dying while succession continues along another track and the unit may continue) is not within this theorem: its holding requires proof of three things — cross-track migration of the succession chain, the identity of the original final position with the new realization, and the satisfaction of the SuccUnit conditions; Proposition 2 of the Mathematical Form of Individuation is a same-window statement and licenses no cross-time citation. The reverse case is entered into the H4 extension tasks.

7\. Breach Modes

1\. A definitional counterexample: an instance satisfying the forking definition while lacking (a) or (b) — Definitional Corollary 1 breached (merging likewise); 2. an in-model counterexample: an instance within the Field-14 model where the four rates hold no advantage yet forking/merging succeeds — the candidate standing of the mechanism condition negated, or its necessity proven (the latter a gain); 3. a proof that severance entails, beyond furthering the mechanism condition, also the provenance inheritance, the persistence of the sides, or the union exclusion — Theorem 1 breached; 4. an instance where the criteria hold throughout the stability window yet the protocol still cannot issue a valid verdict — the transition-band adjudication clause breached; 5. path dependence negated by counterexample — the proposition negated (under the propositional regime, no theorem breach).
