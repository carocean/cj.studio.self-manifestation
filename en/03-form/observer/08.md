# Observer Single-Dual Discrimination Form

This Form ventures one master proposition: for a system presenting two distinguishable structures, a three-layer candidate discrimination framework can be advanced — structure-family boundary, pool-track boundary, common-position candidate boundary — each layer with its candidate criteria, procedures, and known misjudgment sources, its open preconditions declared on record; before the proxy validity of each layer is proven, no claim of "already discriminable" is made; verdicts on the number of currents and the number of observers are not issued in this Form.

In this Form we shall find, under sustained questioning, where the proof must go, and derive the general form of observer single-dual discrimination.

Then let us first give the result. (What follows is the reading organization set back at the head of the text after the whole was completed; it only previews the result and serves as no premise of the derivation to come.)

The master discrimination formula (preview):

The three-layer framework: family-boundary candidate indications → track boundary (same-track supporting indications / different-track supporting indications / ⊥) → position-boundary candidate indications  
Output: the layer-by-layer indication bundle plus precondition status plus the misjudgment-source list (high-strength configuration labels are not automatically composed — the four principal configurations are a target classification, not the present output).  
Forbidden verdicts: the number of currents, the number of observers, and "the single-value principle confirmed breached" — the three belong to Layers 3 and 4 of the four-layer verdict regime or Grade 3 of the three-grade breach regime, presently unreachable for this Form.

In one sentence: seeing two, this Form advances a three-layer candidate discrimination framework — family boundary, track boundary, position boundary each gathering indications; indications do not impersonate adjudication, and how many currents, how many I's, is not for this Form to answer.

Abstract

The former version adjudicated the family boundary by the integration proxies; the deep review adjudged this untenable: two independent structure families can be governed by a higher genuine common position so that the cross-boundary proxies all pass — the many-families-one-I case makes that axis systematically misjudge; and the verdicts "dual currents," "one I," "two I's" overstepped the adjudicable bounds of the upstream. This reconstructed draft re-erects on three layers: the family boundary gathers structure-family candidate indications (the integration proxies retreating to the recording position); the track boundary invokes the candidate pre-identification protocol to gather carrying indications; the position boundary generates candidate positions and gathers governance and constitutivity supporting indications (exhaustiveness explicitly declared). Throughout, verdicts are lowered to institutional-configuration and evidence grade, ⊥ carries its reason code, and the time-window compatibility inequality stands as the open precondition of coexistence adjudication.

1\. Task and Form Kind

The form kind re-adjudged per the Canon adjudication tree: the direction "discrimination form of the inversion-identification group" holds; completion-degree declaration — Layer 1's family boundary awaits the upstream Fam identity, Layer 2 awaits calibration, Layer 3's search and proxy efficacy are unclosed; this Form presently stands as a discrimination-procedure candidate, not an accomplished discrimination form. The former version's three-names-one-thing claim (single-dual discrimination / discrimination / diagnosis) is withdrawn: the diagnosis form is another kind of task (fault attribution), and this Form does not bear that name. Naming awaits Ruling Point Three; this draft uses the working name. Upstream grade declaration: this Form consumes the Adjudication Form (candidate-protocol grade), the Mathematical Form of Individuation (conditional-framework grade), and the Pool-Split Transition Form (structural-condition grade plus model-domain conditions) — all citations carry grade and preconditions, and no verdict oversteps the upstream strength.

2\. The Objects of Discrimination and the Configuration Space

The presenting case: two distinguishable structures within the window with d(A,B) \> θ\_sep. Each of the three layers is three-valued, combining into a configuration space. The four principal configurations are a target classification (only after each layer attains its corresponding validity may they be composed into configuration verdicts; the present output is the layer-by-layer indication bundle): one composite family (settled at Layer 1); two families · same track · no common-position candidate — the same-track dual independent institutional-configuration candidate (corresponding to Grade 2 of the Adjudication Form's three-grade ruling); two families · different tracks · common-position candidate evidence on record — the many-tracks-one-position candidate configuration; two families · different tracks · each side its own position candidate — the dual-unit candidate configuration. Any layer's ⊥ yields that layer's reason code; within the transition band of the Transition Form, ⊥\_transition. The configuration space is a combinatorial space, not an ontological classification, and no exhaustiveness claim is set.

3\. Layer One · The Family Boundary (the structure family's own criteria)

The criteria (the integration proxies not used — in the many-families-one-I case the integration proxies pass across the boundary and cannot adjudge the family boundary):  
First, constitution-domain separateness: A and B each have their own member-constitution domain — the gate-passing attribution (the Boundary-Gate attribution domain of Pass) separate, no shared member;  
Second, convergence-process separateness (a candidate operational indication, carrying preconditions: the compression precondition H8 of z, the dynamics assumption groups H6/H7, the intervention domain of InterventionSensitive undefined — this indication takes no part in determinative verdicts): A and B each carry the signature of their own candidate convergence process;  
Third, closure separateness (one candidate indication among others, bearing no necessity-and-sufficiency — a family may hold several closures, and two families may share a large closure): the internal closures of the respective circuit subdomains are discernible.  
The standing of the three indications: the positive adjudication of the family boundary (one family / two families) requires the formal identity criterion of the same-family relation — that criterion belongs to the H/Fam layer of the Observer Mathematical Form and is not yet erected (H5 newly docketed); until it is, this layer only gathers family-boundary candidate indications and issues no one-family/two-families verdict. All three indications present constitute two-family candidate indications; any one lacking yields only "two-family indications unproven," and one composite family may not be adjudged (two families may share gates, members, or closure interfaces — sharing proves no same family); evidence insufficient — ⊥\_measure.  
The place of the integration proxies: cross-boundary integration-dynamics evidence (Layer A of the Adjudication Form's I) is in this layer recorded, not ruled upon — its full passage can coexist with a two-family adjudication (precisely the many-families-one-I case), handled at Layer 3.

4\. Layer Two · The Track Boundary

The pre-identification protocol of the Mathematical Form of Individuation is invoked (the layered circle-resolving version: instantaneous pool components → cross-window trajectory matching → stable-track posterior) — that protocol being framework grade (calibration unclosed, statistic validity unproven), this layer's output is lowered to: same-track supporting indications / different-track supporting indications / ⊥ (carrying thresholds and precondition status; uncalibrated hosts yield ⊥\_measure only); before proxy validity is completed, no same-track or different-track verdict is issued. This layer adjudges no attribution; it gathers carrying indications only.

5\. Layer Three · The Position Boundary

The generation rule of the candidate common-position set (heuristic candidate-generation declaration): with the relation graph registered within the window as the search domain, ascend from the positions of A and B along the composite paths of Anchor, Inherit, Governs, Approp (loop detection, termination depth, and maximal domain pre-registered); the totality of positions reached is the candidate set; the complete generation rule of the FCP relational closure is not erected (H2), hence this rule is called heuristic. Exhaustiveness declaration: where relation registration is incomplete, the candidate set is non-exhaustive — this layer then yields ⊥\_measure only (incomplete data belongs to the measurement class; ⊥\_unclassified is reserved for data sufficient yet no type corresponding).  
Per-candidate testing: the standard form of the deletion test (six preconditions) plus the governance test — passers are recorded as common-position supporting indications. The three-output coexistence regime: common-position supporting indications on record / no common-position supporting indication presently obtained (all causes of candidate failure — protocol inapplicable, preconditions unmet, proxy insensitive — fall here, and none converts into negative evidence of no common position) / each-side-own-position supporting indications on record; the three may coexist, and the lack of the former does not automatically establish the latter.

6\. Procedural Discipline

The flow is a default order (Layer 1 → 2 → 3), no definitional dependency: the layers may gather indications in parallel and combine in the report; a track-boundary protocol genuinely requiring two-family objects is invoked conditionally. Inter-layer conflict (Layer 1 adjudging two families while Layer 3 sees a strong common-position candidate) is no contradiction — it is precisely the many-families-one-position configuration, recorded truthfully side by side. Intra-layer evidence conflict: one re-examination at most; persisting conflict lowers to ⊥\_conflict with the evidence pair recorded. The necessary time-window compatibility condition: τ\_proxy ≤ τ\_sub \< T\_alt/2 — a necessary compatibility condition, not a completed defense; simultaneity still requires independent same-window causal evidence; systems without a stable T\_alt uniformly yield ⊥\_measure. The four ⊥ reason-code classes are uniform throughout.

7\. Misjudgment Sources (presently known; additions permitted)

High-position search omission (incomplete relation registration leaving the candidate set short — defense: the honest use of ⊥\_measure; ⊥\_unclassified reserved for data sufficient yet no type corresponding); common-input confusion (the perturbation-separateness test of Layer 1's second criterion one defense, the common-drive control procedure another); alternating presentation (time-shared alternation misjudged as coexistence — the time-window compatibility inequality the defense); similarity merging; the arbiter false common position (the six-precondition deletion test the defense); report-separation misjudgment (output conflict is no criterion of any layer).

8\. Breach Modes

1\. Construct a system with all three Layer-1 criteria present that is proven by independent criteria to be one family (or conversely) — the family-boundary criteria fail; 2. an instance of a genuine common position missed within the reachable domain of the generation rule — the search rule fails; 3. the time-window compatibility inequality satisfied while alternation misjudgment still occurs systematically — the defense fails; 4. stable configurations of theoretical significance recurring outside the four principal configurations — the combinatorial reporting regime of the configuration space is disqualified (an extension rather than a breach, yet it must be recorded).

9\. Interfaces

The Observer Adjudication Form — the docking of Grade 2 of the three-grade ruling (family-separateness supporting indications plus same-track supporting indications plus no common-position supporting indication obtained within the present search domain constitute the configuration face of its Grade-2 verdict); the deletion test and six preconditions drawn upon. The Mathematical Form of Observer Individuation — the pre-identification protocol and the FCP condition framework drawn upon; this Form's position-boundary output supplies evidence for its FCP testing. The Observer Pool-Split Transition Form — the transition band's ⊥\_transition; configurations amid ongoing forking or merging undetermined. The General Form of the Observer and the General Form of Observer Individuation — interface name and substance (Ruling Three). The grade transmission table — all verdicts of this Form stand at the evidence grade of Layers 1 and 2, registered on record.
