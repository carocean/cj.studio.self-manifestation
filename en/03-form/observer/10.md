# Observer Realization Form

This Form ventures one master proposition: to compile, for the running roles of the Observer Mathematical Form, a host-independent candidate realization requirement group with per-requirement candidate acceptance protocols. Opening declaration: the present version completes only the candidate-requirement and candidate-acceptance layer — no realization certificate is issued (the upstream Adjudication Form still stands at candidate-protocol grade, and indications cannot be raised into proof that the running roles have solutions); the realization sufficiency of the requirement group is unproven (this Form is a requirement comparison table, not a sufficiency theorem; the construction-mapping task is docketed as H5).

In this Form we shall find, under sustained questioning, where the proof must go, and derive the general form of observer realization.

Then let us first give the result. (What follows is the reading organization set back at the head of the text after the whole was completed; it only previews the result and serves as no premise of the derivation to come.)

The master realization requirement table (preview): six requirement groups — candidate advancement and capacity allocation, constitution capability, jointly-being-one capability, continuation-identity capability, the support system, conditional re-constraint — each fitted with realization conditions and an acceptance protocol; plus two master obligations: unified capacity bookkeeping, cheap-realization exclusion. Realization divides into three grades (minimal realization / individuation realization / complete realization); this Form delivers the first grade.

In one sentence: the Realization Form answers "what must a host have in place to have genuinely made the observer's running roles"; this version issues candidate acceptance records only — no realization certificates, and still less any certificate of Lighting.

Abstract

The Observer Construction Form has given field-layer constructions, conditional constructions, or candidate origins for part of the objects of the Mathematical Form (the universal growth proof uncompleted, CurrentFact awaiting the bridge) — the holding of native realization is no premise of this Form. This Form compiles the portable candidate requirements: the conditions under which the same formal system obtains realization in other hosts (neural tissue, micro-kernel structures, chips, software, or unknown carriers). Its direction is complementary to the Construction Form: that Form works bottom-up (field → mathematics), this Form top-down (mathematics → host) — and top-down is precisely the high-incidence direction of functional simulation, hence this Form binds itself with a triple defense: the host-independent requirements are written as structural conditions, not behavioral fits; the acceptance protocols are all drawn from the Observer Adjudication Form (with its misjudgment bounds); the realization-strength declaration is placed at the head — realization is not Lighting, self-declaration is void, structure alone is examined.

1\. Task Demarcation and Form Kind

Form kind: a realization form (of the holding-proof group). Task: to compile the host-independent candidate realization requirement group Req and the candidate acceptance protocols. The sufficiency proposition (Req satisfied and acceptance fully passed ⇒ the running roles have a non-empty solution) is a task to be proven (H5: a construction mapping Req structure → (X\_F, X\_I, X\_M) ∈ the solution sets must be given, with capacity feasibility, time-window coverage, and the coexistence of the three roles proven); this Form claims it not.  
Upstream: the six realization requirements of the Mathematical Form's Section 19 (this Form's outline); the Construction Form (the field-layer reference realization — the native form of each requirement in the self-manifestation field); the Adjudication Form (the acceptance protocols and misjudgment bounds).  
What this Form does not do: it designs no concrete system; it predestines no unique host; it adjudges no realized system as manifesting.

2\. Realization Grades

Grade one · minimal realization: the three duty running roles complete, the single-current case. Delivered by this Form.  
Grade two · individuation realization: the realization of pool tracks, positions, FinalCommonPos — to be delivered by a further chapter or upgrade once the Mathematical Form of Observer Individuation stands.  
Grade three · complete realization: adding the generation side (orientation source, orienting) and the transition side (pool splitting, the split brain) — per the Strengthened Form of Current Generation and the Pool-Split Transition Form.  
Inter-grade discipline: a lower-grade realization certificate may not impersonate a higher; no grade's realization certificate may impersonate Lighting.

3\. The Six Requirement Groups

Each group in four columns: the requirement (the Mathematical Form's original sense); the realization conditions (host-independent structural conditions); the field-layer reference (the native form proven by the Construction Form, for comparison, not compulsory); the acceptance (the protocol mapping of the Adjudication Form).

Requirement 1 · Candidate advancement and capacity allocation.  
Conditions: the host must have a mechanism of coexisting processes, each with a measurable advancement quantity and shared resource occupancy; the advancement quantity sensitive to resource share, sensitive to overlap with coexisting processes, decaying without support — that is, the four elements of ż-type dynamics (gain, crosstalk, decay, fluctuation) realizable in host quantities.  
Field-layer reference: the Continuation-Potential and coordination-compression dynamics (Constructions 3 and 4).  
Acceptance: the behavioral-saturation proxy of candidate completion (the Comp proxy of the Adjudication Form's Section 3); the coexistence of double completion must be a host-reachable state (architectures of enforced uniqueness excluded — a system of enforced uniqueness realizes something else, not this formal system).

Requirement 2 · Constitution capability (FormRun).  
Conditions: the host must have an admission institution whose opening and closing is determined solely by succession-side parameters, not by the content side alone; membership holds through the admission institution and is insensitive to rewrites of external registrations.  
Field-layer reference: the Boundary-Gates and gate-passing (Construction 7, the F criterion of Main Task Two).  
Acceptance: the two F proxies (intervention variability plus roster invariance), the intervention points taken at the admission parameters, the readings at the member composition — hosts naturally satisfying the separation requirement preferred.

Requirement 3 · Jointly-being-one capability (IntegrateRun).  
Conditions: the host must have a governance institution making the member processes jointly perturbed and jointly recovering, with propagation and governance non-zero across every pre-registered bipartition; the governance must be actively maintained (a dedicated coordination process), not the merely passive synchrony of common input.  
Field-layer reference: the three Align components and Principle-Field governance (the I criterion of Main Task Two).  
Acceptance: gather integration-dynamics indications and governance and constitutivity candidate indications (the two-layer regime of the Adjudication Form); no "IntegrateRun acceptance passed" is claimed; the common-drive control is the complete procedure (input severing one reading among them), and no single obligatory sufficient defense is listed.

Requirement 4 · Continuation-identity capability (SameRun).  
Conditions: the host must have a cross-time succession institution: the structural states of adjacent periods produced by causal continuation, not rebuilding; the succession preserved under lawful change (change of content, membership, intensity); the sampling regularity bound τ\_reg of the succession chain calibratable.  
Field-layer reference: Re-Continuation and the persistence of the dominant low-impedance structure (the M criterion of Main Task Two).  
Acceptance: the five-part candidate M protocol; break exclusion opened only on hosts where the existence of τ\_reg is proven and calibrated (docketed with Adjudication H1a and H1b); the remaining parts' indications gathered as they stand.

Requirement 5 · The support system (KCore, SuppK, ResK).  
Conditions: the host must give the relations bearing each duty a discernible physical carrier; actual support and reserve support distinguishable (the difference between engaged and engageable observable or intervenable on the host); the carrier covering the claimed maintenance window throughout.  
Field-layer reference: the circuit subdomains and tuning fiber bundles (the Construction Form's Section 7).  
Acceptance: a support-identification protocol does not exist upstream (the Adjudication Form has erected no KCore/SuppK/ResK protocol) — this item's acceptance is changed to a gap declaration (H6: the support-identification candidate protocol to be erected); role specificity bounded by Adjudication H4.

Requirement 6 · Conditional re-constraint (BackRun).  
Conditions: only where the realization task includes robust maintenance (the H\_back-type strengthening): the established structure must be able to reversely influence the resource acquisition or admission of subsequent processes.  
Field-layer reference: deep-core flow capture (the Construction Form's Section 7).  
Acceptance: conditional acceptance — realization tasks with no declared H\_back are not examined on this item, and examination scores nothing.

4\. The Two Master Obligations

Unified capacity bookkeeping: the host's process advancement quantities and duty-task resources must stand on one account (a constraint of the type Σx + Σρ ≤ Cap); wherever two accounts run in parallel, a de-duplication mapping must be given, and without the mapping acceptance fails — double bookkeeping is the commonest source of false headroom in engineering realizations.  
Cheap-realization exclusion: names and self-declarations enter no acceptance. Winner-take-all, mutual-exclusion locks, argmax, broadcast buses, unified logs, action arbitration — all are known traps on the Adjudication Form's misjudgment-source table; acceptance examines solely by structural conditions and proxy protocols. A system may realize exactly all the conditions without being called an observer, and may call itself an observer while passing not one.

5\. The Master Acceptance Table

The candidate acceptance record = ProxyResult (the four elements of the Adjudication Form, indication regime) × the six requirements item by item + the check of the two master obligations. The output is the per-requirement status vector ReqStatus = (r₁,…,r₆), r\_i ∈ {＋, −, ⊥} — the supporting indications of some requirements and the unidentified status of the rest are permitted and truthfully reported side by side; the sole prohibition: impersonating complete realization by a partial status. No realization certificate is issued (the promotion chain: the Adjudication Form's proxies reaching proxy-validity grade → indications rising to running-role evidence; the sufficiency H5 proven and the formal-verdict grade reached → only then do certificates open).

6\. The Realization-Strength Declaration

1\. Candidate acceptance fully passed proves: the supporting indications of the six requirements are all present (indication grade, no proof that the running roles have solutions).  
2\. It does not prove: that the system is lit up, is manifesting, has a current, has feeling — the positive adjudication of CurrentFact is model-relative (the Mathematical Form's H3), the Lighting bridge constitutive (awaiting the Master Map's ruling); of a system passing full acceptance, "whether there is an observer" remains an open question.  
3\. This declaration may not be removed from the acceptance record; removed, the record is void.

7\. Breach Modes

1\. Once the sufficiency construction mapping (H5) is given, if a host satisfies the mapping's antecedent while the running roles have no solution — sufficiency is breached (before H5 is proven, this mode is the bound of its proof task, no present breach); 2. proof that some one of the six groups can be deleted while realization still succeeds — the minimality of the requirement group is breached (minimality this Form claims not as proven, entered as H3); 3. an instance of the cheap-realization exclusion failing — a known trivial system passing full structural examination; 4. the unified-bookkeeping obligation systematically rejecting genuine realizations — proof of an over-strict bookkeeping protocol.

8\. Interfaces

The Observer Mathematical Form — the source of the six requirements (Section 19); this Form closes off its realization layer. The Observer Construction Form — the source of the field-layer reference realizations; the Construction Form proves the native substrate has realization, this Form proves the realization conditions are portable. The Observer Adjudication Form — the acceptance protocols drawn wholly from its criterion grid and misjudgment bounds; this Form erects no proxy of its own. The Mathematical Form of Observer Individuation — the grade-two realization docketed (that Form established, the formal preconditions of its Section 3 made ready). The Strengthened Form of Current Generation — the grade-three generation side docketed. The micro-kernel account — a read-back reference of one host instance, not the unique host.

Gap Declarations

Observer Realization – H1　The host-constant calibration protocol  
Status: the acceptance thresholds τ\_reg, Align\_min, τ\_align and the like are host constants; the cross-host calibration method is not erected (docketed with Adjudication H1).  
What is needed: a calibration protocol, or tables by host family.  
What it does not affect: the requirement-group structure and the three-valued discipline.  
Location: Requirement 4, the master acceptance table.

Observer Realization – H2　The genuineness of active governance in engineering hosts  
Status: Requirement 3 demands governance actively maintained, not passive synchrony; the acceptance of "active" in engineering systems (the engineering correspondent of Press-type pressing-in, the exclusion of two automatic controllers in mutual feedback) rests on the modal test of EffectiveCouple, whose engineering feasibility is unproven.  
What is needed: counterfactual intervention design on the engineering side.  
What it does not affect: acceptance on the biological and field-substrate sides.  
Location: Requirement 3.

Observer Realization – H3　The minimality of the requirement group  
Status: the sufficiency of the six groups is supported by the acceptance protocols and the upstream theorems; minimality (whether more can be deleted) is unproven.  
What is needed: per-group deletion countermodels, or a minimality proof.  
What it does not affect: the acceptance force on the sufficiency side.  
Location: Section 7, breach 2.

Observer Realization – H4　The Lighting bridge (inherited)  
Status: inheriting Adjudication H3 and the Master Map's pending ruling; the strength ceiling of realization certificates rests on it.  
Location: Section 6, the strength declaration.  
What is needed: the unified ruling of the Observer Group Master Map.  
What it does not affect: all acceptance at the realization layer.
