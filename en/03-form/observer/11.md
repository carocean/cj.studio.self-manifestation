# Strengthened Form of Current Generation

This Form ventures one master proposition: under strengthened premises, to re-examine the additional functions of current generation — under Premise A (the gap), there must exist a within-boundary source of difference unregistered in Γ, or an internal random transition; the candidate-specific orienting mechanism is its candidate explanation, whose necessity requires supplementary premises and countermodels to stand (H5). Under Premise B strengthened into the competition-model premise (B′), robust maintenance requires some protective mechanism; the reverse re-constraint of the established current is one of four protective classes, whose necessity requires the exclusion of the other classes to stand (H6). The ruling on the definitional standing of the two premises takes the proving of the two conditional theorems as its activation precondition.

In this Form we shall find, under sustained questioning, where the proof must go, and derive the strengthened form of current generation.

Abstract

The General Form of the Observer housed source, orienting, and re-constraint under conditional consequents without an in-text forcing review; this Form supplies that review. The review proceeds under two explicitly declared strengthened premises: Premise A, explicit-state insufficiency (same Γ, possibly different currents; the protocol relativity inherited from the Current Inversion Form); Premise B, maintenance as a repeatable robust phenomenon. Forcing review one obtains: under A there must be a non-explicit candidate-specific orienting mechanism (source and orienting tested unmergeable as one item); forcing review two obtains: under B there must be synchronic re-constraint. Both reviews keep the identification bound of the Inversion Form (existence assertions not overstepping the identifiable bound) and the discipline of the randomness open slot. Section 5 prepares the consequence table of the two premises and two ruling directions, the ruling being the author's. Section 6's final table of the eight words retires the account of g1's strengthening task.

2\. Declaration of the Strengthened Premises

3\. Forcing Review One: The Orienting Mechanism (under Premise A)

The three duties are in place. Under A, ask again: is the task closed? Not closed — a history pair with same Γ and different currents exists, and the three duties say only that there is constitution, being-one, and continuation-identity; they say not why what was constituted this time is this family. By boundary closure, the causal source of the difference lies within the boundary and outside Γ.  
The proven part: from boundary closure and the existence of the history pair, the causal source of the difference lies within the boundary, outside Γ — either an unregistered difference state (candidate-specific or global), or a duly ruled internal random transition.  
The candidate explanation and its debt (re-erected per Volume 9 item 35): the "non-explicit candidate-specific orienting mechanism" is the strongest candidate explanation, but its necessity is unproven — a global hidden variable can produce different outcomes through the non-linearity of the existing candidate dynamics, in which case the candidate specificity comes from the original dynamics, not from an independent orienting mechanism. The supplementary proof task (H5), three pieces: a countermodel proving task failure where there is only global difference and no candidate-specific relation; a proof that the existing candidate dynamics cannot convert global difference into difference of outcome; the declaration that the randomness slot bears no difference (all three present, necessity stands).  
The splitting of source and orienting (re-erected per Volume 9 item 36): SourceState (the standing of within-boundary, outside-Γ difference) and OrientCouple (the specific transmission of difference to candidate outcomes) are two functional coordinates — bearable by one and the same structure, or borne apart (the same regime as the treatment of F and I: functional coordinates separable, structural realization mergeable); the former version's proof "one failure, one function, no split" is withdrawn (its second-world argument was a terminological compression absorbing the definitional character of orienting into the source, no unsplittability proof).  
Adherence to the inversion constraints: this conclusion is existence-grade — asserting that the mechanism must exist, not that the mechanism is identifiable from Γ and observation (Inversion Theorem 2: structural inversion reaches at most the solution set); the randomness open slot is not engaged (the Inversion corollary: the internal-randomness option may not serve as a proven source before the author's ruling).

4\. Forcing Review Two: Re-Constraint (under Premise B)

Premise-strengthening declaration (Volume 9 item 37): the proof of review two in fact uses premises beyond B — now explicitly strengthened into B′ (the competition-model premise): shared capacity in one pool, zero-sum, new candidates continually entering and reachable, no static resource reservation, no architectural isolation, no external protection.  
The proven part (within B′): wholly undefended, maintenance degrades arbitrarily under perturbation, contradicting robustness — hence under B′ there must be some protective mechanism.  
The four protective classes and the forcing debt (Volume 9 item 38): static pre-allocation, architectural isolation, dynamic resource guarding, the established current's reverse re-constraint — all four can accomplish protection; the necessity of BackRun (the fourth class, whose protective state varies with the actual holding of the current H) requires first proving that the first three classes cannot accomplish the task (H6); this Form has not proven it, hence within B′ BackRun is a candidate protective mechanism, and its elevation to duty is not issued. The junction with the Mathematical Form's conditional BackRun clause remains in its conditional state.
