# The General Form of Law-Action

What follows attempts one general proposition:

To grant that a Law already stands in the field is not to grant that it is acting now. If an already standing Law is in fact exercising itself at this moment, that cannot consist merely in its qualification being complete, merely in something happening in the field, merely in its carrier being touched, or merely in its realization being started. Its current facts must already contain one exercise that is real, that is attributable to the Law itself, and that is carried through according to the duty semantics this relation instance already has.

"One" is used here to press two layers of constraint, together with the identity of their witnesses, into a single sentence. That identity is not given by this text; it is a result of the argument, forced out in Section 5 by the splicing world. The exact layered statement is given below under "The result first."

The General Form of Law has already shown that for a relation to be a Law it cannot be merely one instance of correspondence, one current response, or one trace left behind; it must already have formed, and must really and stably bear what comes after. The Necessity Form of Law supplies the converse, and the two directions together close qualification in both directions.

The argument continues from there.

The question of qualification carries no quantifier over current events. A Law can stand fully at time t while the field at t contains no actual Continuation relevant to it at all. When may one say only that "this Law is in the field," and when may one say that "this Law is acting now"? That is the irreducible task this text takes on.

The scope must be drawn in advance, so that nothing oversteps it. This text proves one thing, sets one list of questions aside, and declines to rule on another.

It proves: the minimal necessary structure of the current facts of an already standing Law when it is exercising itself in a current relation instance.

It does not ask: why it starts; what sets it going; why it stops; how it sediments; how it strengthens; how it grows shallower; how it competes; how it causes another Law to change; how it forms successor states; how it migrates; how it dies; how it recovers. These belong, respectively, to the later texts The General Form of Law-Dynamics, The General Form of Law Interaction, and the Law-identity texts.

It does not rule on: whether the result of its exercise is achieved, whether it prevails over other Laws, or whether it is free of interference. The result side does not enter the necessary qualification of this text; the reason is given in Section 6 of the Line of Argument. This text does not thereby claim that results are unimportant, only that they do not belong to the question "is it acting?"

Nor does this text judge anew whether L is a Law, or redefine Rel(L), I, Type, Read, Cov, Ante, or Trig. It builds no second set of current formal objects and establishes no new form-text; every formal base it needs is taken from the current edition of The Mathematical Form of Law.

The degree to which this text answers the question must also be stated in advance. Only the necessary direction is proved. The converse — whether the necessary structure, once complete, suffices to establish that the Law is acting — belongs to The Necessity Form of Law-Action. The finding that "no third ontological constraint needs to be added" says only that the recursion has stopped in the necessary direction; "no further constraint has been found so far" is not "no further constraint exists." The Status of the whole text's conclusion is Conditional; its conditions follow the inherited items, and this text, by standing, closes none of them.

In this chapter, through continuous questioning, we shall discover where the proof must go and derive the General Form of Law-Action.

The aim:

to obtain the minimal necessary structure, no part of which can be deleted, of an already standing Law that is exercising itself in a current relation instance; to give the proof that no part can be deleted, its boundaries, and its breach modes; and to draw its lines against qualification, against results, and against the sources of dynamics.

The result first.

Fix a Sedimentation-Field F, a current time t, an already standing Law L and its declared applicability-cover domain I, with Law\_{F,t}(L, I) = 1 as the entry condition; fix one current relation instance ξ\_t at time t. When the declared working domain, the non-degeneracy conditions, and the inherited conditions hold:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;LawAct\_{F,t}(L; ξ\_t) ⇒ CurLawBear\_{F,t}(L; ξ\_t)

where

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;CurLawBear\_{F,t}(L; ξ\_t)  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;\= CurrentLawOccurrence\_{F,t}(L; ξ\_t) ∧ DutyConformance\_{F,t}(L; ξ\_t)

CurrentLawOccurrence is the first layer: in this relation instance an actual Continuation does occur, and that occurrence is attributable to a legal current realization of L's own. Within this layer there are two checks — that something is in fact currently occurring, and that the occurrence belongs to L. They are two constraints on the same current event within one layer, not two mutually independent ontological items.

DutyConformance is the second layer: under the same current relation instance ξ\_t, some legal realization of L achieves duty discharge according to the existing semantics of Type(d), Read(d), Cov(L), Ante(d), and Trig(i), rather than merely reaching start-up, transmission, or co-location. Whether this realization is the one the first layer recognizes is not asked at this layer; identity is required separately, by the binding discipline.

The two layers are further subject to one binding discipline: they must be witnessed jointly by one and the same legal current realization. The binding discipline is not a third ontological layer; it acquires its standing in Section 5 of the Line of Argument.

In full:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;C0 ∧ C2 ∧ H1 ∧ H2 ∧ H3  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;⇒ \[ LawAct\_{F,t}(L; ξ\_t) ⇒ CurLawBear\_{F,t}(L; ξ\_t) \]

These are only the conditions inherited by the ontological necessary direction — that is, the upstream conditions under which the entry condition Law\_{F,t}(L, I) = 1 can hold.

If it is further required that this structure be read back formally by the objects of the current edition of The Mathematical Form of Law, the Read group, the Rep group, and the corresponding three-valued conditions are inherited in addition. That layer decides only whether the structure can be computed, not whether it holds ontologically; the two layers are declared separately in Section 3 of the Appendix.

The above is a result yet to be proved. It is not a premise of any round of questioning below.

In One Sentence

Standing is not acting, acting is not being attributed to it, and being attributed to it is not having discharged; each of the three questions blocks one kind of failure, and one and the same witness ties the three blocked facts into a single instance.

Abstract

The General Form of Law has already shown that for a Law to stand in a Sedimentation-Field, at least one formed relational organization must already be really and stably bearing later Continuation. The Necessity Form of Law has already shown that this structural qualification, once complete, suffices for being a Law. Together the two directions close the question of qualification.

The Principle Group-Form has already shown that for an already standing Law to become a Principle relative to another Law, it must form a counter-hold on the same established duty through which it acquired Law qualification; and the second layer of CurAnti, the core object of Principle-action, expressly requires that the target Law be currently exercising itself.

Yet the object "currently exercising itself" has never been characterized independently on the Law side. The Principle Group-Form obtains only a local read-out of it, AHold, on the projection of a particular joint Principle evaluation. A field whose qualification is complete need not have anything happening in it; what happens need not be done by this Law; and what this Law does need not be done in full. Until now these three matters had not been separated.

This text takes up that open point.

The questioning displays failure worlds one after another. Qualification complete while the Law is currently at rest: this forces out the check of actual occurrence. A same-type occurrence currently present but borne by a bypass: this forces out the check of same-Law attribution. The two checks together make the first layer, CurrentLawOccurrence. A current chain that reaches only start-up, transmission, or co-location while the duty is not discharged: this forces out the second layer, DutyConformance. Two layers each independently evidenced, yet still assemblable from different witnesses: this forces out the binding discipline of one and the same realization.

Finally the result side is tested. An ordinary world of two competing Laws suffices to show that L's legal current realization can have completed its own duty while another Law, acting at the same time, pulls the total output away from L's result alone: acting does not imply that the result is achieved. The counter-hold world of The Principle Group-Form serves as a cross-check within the system: if "not being counter-held" were written into the qualification for acting, then whenever a Principle is counter-holding, the target Law could not be acting, and the second layer of CurAnti could never hold. Every result-side item is therefore struck out.

The core conclusion: qualification says that "this Law is in the field"; this text says what "this Law is doing now"; the result says whether "it got done." These are three questions, and none may answer for another.

Introduction

Once a Law stands, the field has one more thing in it.

But for a thing to be there and for that thing to be moving at this moment are not the same.

A well-formed Law can stay motionless for an entire moment. The inflow did not reach it, or reached it while the path was pre-empted by other conditions. Its carrier is intact, its capacity to re-walk is intact, and it could move at any time. This time it did not.

Conversely, something in the field really did move, and the read-out really is exactly what this Law requires — but what moved was something else. In a shared-core region two Laws share nodes, and one and the same read-out can be produced by different structures; an external controller can also write that value in directly.

One step further back: it really is the Law's own realization that moves, yet it moves only halfway. It reads the antecedent in, passes data downstream, or merely lets a chain run through its own carrier, while the consequent of the relation takes no value at all.

Not moving, something else moving, moving halfway: in everyday language all three are described as "this Law is having an effect" or "is not having an effect," and they are lumped together without distinction.

Hence a fundamental question:

on what grounds can one say that an already standing Law is acting now?

This text proposes that acting now is neither an extension of qualification nor a read-back from results, but an independent structure on the side of current facts. It has two layers of constraint, neither of which can be deleted, and the two layers must fall on one and the same instance.

The Line of Argument

We proceed by sustained questioning, one step at a time; the argument therefore forms a line, and is to be read from the top down.

1

This text does not discuss how a relation becomes a Law; that is given by The General Form of Law and The Necessity Form of Law. It does not discuss the formal representation and solution of Laws; that is given by The Mathematical Form of Law. It does not discuss how one Law becomes a Principle relative to another; that is given by Zone One of The Principle Group-Form.

This text discusses only this: whether an already standing Law, in a current relation instance already declared, is actually exercising itself.

The starting points are the following, and nothing else.

The entry condition is Law\_{F,t}(L, I) = 1. The Status of that closure is Conditional, with conditions C0 ∧ C2 ∧ H1 ∧ H2 ∧ H3. This text inherits that Status; it neither proves it again nor upgrades it.

Definition 1 (current relation instance).

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;ξ\_t = ( i, ν\_t, D\_{i, ν\_t} )

where i ∈ I\_cov(L, I), ν\_t ⊨ Cov(L), and D\_{i, ν\_t} = Trig(i) ∩ { d \| ν\_t ⊨ Ante(d) }.

ξ\_t is not a new field quantity and not a new ontological predicate. It is only a current evaluation instance declared in advance, and all three of its components are taken from existing objects of the current edition of The Mathematical Form of Law.

Working protocol: if several legal instances exist at the same time, each is adjudicated separately. This text defines no aggregate value across instances and does not claim that such an aggregate exists. This is a convention about the unit of evaluation, not a gap awaiting verification.

Definition 2 (Law-Action). LawAct\_{F,t}(L; ξ\_t) states that L is in a state of current action in the current relation instance ξ\_t.

This definition fixes only the target predicate. Its content is obtained by the recursion of this text. This section presupposes neither how many layers it has nor what those layers are, and does not assert in advance that attribution or discharge is among them.

Confusions that must be excluded: LawAct is not LawQualification — the latter is qualification, the former a current state. It is not DynamicRole — the latter adjudicates whether a carrier really enters successor generation; it has existential polarity, and it is not a current event at time t. It is not ΩSupport — the latter says "it could walk again," not "it is walking now." It is not Dom. It is not OutcomeSuccess. It is not OrigCont — the latter belongs to the dynamical-function language of The General Form of Law-Dynamics; this text never uses it, never defines LawAct in reverse through it, and later texts may not back-fill it into the content of this text.

There are three non-degeneracy conditions: Law\_{F,t}(L, I) = 1; I\_cov(L, I) ≠ ∅; ξ\_t is declared and D\_{i, ν\_t} ≠ ∅. If any one fails, this text does not apply; it is not breached. The three conditions only delimit the facts and the working domain through which the question is entered. They contain none of the necessary structures below, no restatement of them, and no conclusion that can be derived only from them.

The adjudicability of the evaluation mode is not listed among the non-degeneracy conditions. MixedMode affects whether Type-sensitive quantifiers can be evaluated, and belongs to the layer of formal read-back. Formal non-adjudicability is not ontological inapplicability; its treatment is given in Section 2 of the Appendix.

Inherited items are cited in their original status, neither redefined nor upgraded: from The General Form of Law, the nine components and 𝒲\_S(F, t, L, I) = { R \| Established\_{F,t}(R) ∧ Govern\_{F,t}(L; R, I) }; from The Mathematical Form of Law, the three-layer chain and the realization objects; from The Determination Form of Law, PR1–PR4; from The Principle Group-Form, AHold and CurAnti. The full list is in Section 1 of the Appendix.

The definitions below fix only objects, conditions, and exclusions. The standing of each as a necessary constraint can be forced out only by the questioning that follows it; no definition confers it.

So the first question: on the strength of the items above alone, can "L is acting now" already be established?

2

Take a Law that already stands and let it be entirely motionless at this moment.

There exists a carrier organization R ∈ 𝒲\_S(F, t, L, I) with Established\_{F,t}(R) = 1 and Govern\_{F,t}(L; R, I) = 1; that is, both DynamicRole and ΩSupport have been acquired. Law\_{F,t}(L, I) = 1 holds beyond doubt.

Suppose ν\_t does fall within Cov(L), i ∈ I\_cov(L, I), and D\_{i, ν\_t} ≠ ∅. The relation instance is legal and the duty domain is non-empty.

But at this moment there is no actual Continuation in the field relevant to L. The hypothetical chains of the structural layer are intact; the re-walk chains under re-entry evaluation are intact; the historical stock R ∩ Estab\_t is undamaged; the realization set of the role is non-empty in the relevant context. That "if it walked, it could walk" holds in every respect.

This time it did not walk.

In field dynamics this is the normal case: the actual path of this inflow was pre-empted by other immediate conditions, or the triggering path was not activated within this window, while L's carrier organization suffered no damage at all.

Nothing fails on the side of qualification.

Where, then, is the failure? In the quantificational content of the qualification predicates.

Established asks whether the carrier has formed. DynamicRole asks whether the carrier really enters the successor generation of Rel(L). ΩSupport asks whether every claimed range of application has stable re-walk support. None of the three contains the quantifier "what actually happened at time t."

ΩSupport is especially easy to misread as currency. What it says is that, when the re-entry conditions arrive, the formed structure can re-walk stably. It is a qualification to re-walk, not a re-walk event. In the world above, ΩSupport holds fully and there is no current event; that is precisely a counterexample for this distinction.

Hence:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;LawQualification ⇏ LawAct

To exclude this world there must be a first check on the side of current facts: in this relation instance a corresponding actual event, or family of events, does occur in the field, rather than merely being able to occur.

Call this check actual current occurrence (ActualCurrentEvent).

It checks whether, on the (i, ν\_t) fixed by ξ\_t, an actual Continuation event exists.

It sits on the current layer of the three-layer chain of The Mathematical Form of Law, as against the other two layers. The structural layer asks "can it walk, structurally?"; the re-entry layer asks "can it re-walk under re-entry conditions?"; the current layer asks "did it walk this time?" The distinction among the three layers is inherited from The Mathematical Form of Law; this text does not rewrite it.

The historical stock is not within the three-layer chain and must be kept apart. R ∩ Estab\_t is what remains of formed structure; it is not a chain. Its being intact shows only that the carrier is still there, not that any chain has been walked this time. This check stands against it as well.

It excludes: hypothetical chains of the structural layer only; re-walk chains under re-entry evaluation only; historical sedimentation only; qualification for role realization only; potential to re-walk only; no actual Continuation at present.

It does not check: whom this occurrence belongs to — attribution is the business of the next check; how far this occurrence gets — discharge belongs to Section 4; whether the current event is unique; whether the result changes in any way.

This section does not establish this check as an ontological item on its own. Whether it can be borne separately from the next check depends on the outcome of the next section.

So the second question: if something is indeed occurring now, is it this Law that is acting?

3

Let something in the field really move, and let the read-out really be right — but what moved is something else.

Suppose that F contains, besides L, another already standing Law L′, or an external control structure E acting directly on the field. Either shares some nodes with L, or is physically co-located with L's carrier organization on the same Shared Core.

At time t, i arrives. There is an actual Continuation at present; the value its read-out takes under ν\_t matches exactly the relational content Rel(L) requires of the corresponding duty. Stronger still: because of co-location, the components of this actual chain have a non-empty intersection with one of L's carriers.

The check of Section 2 passes item by item. It is not a structural chain, not a re-walk chain, not historical stock, not a qualification for realization; there is an actual Continuation at present.

But the bearer of this chain is not a legal current realization of L's own. What generates the current read-out is a realization of L′, or a direct write by E. The components that intersect the chain are co-located structures merely passed through along the way; they do not take part in the capacity of bearing L's duty. L's own legal current realization is not invoked at all in this instance.

In the field this is the normal case, not an exception. The same read-out can be produced by different structures, above all in shared-core regions.

Consider a more covert form. Suppose L has two legal carriers R₁ and R₂, both within 𝒲\_S. In this instance the actual event falls on a stretch of structure of R₁, while the realization witness bearing the duty is taken from R₂'s record of historical re-walks. Item by item: there is an event — true; the event intersects L's carriers — true; L has a legal realization — also true. Yet no single realization actually bore this event. This is splicing across alternative Law witnesses.

Where, then, is the failure? In the difference between "intersecting" and "bearing."

A positional relation can arise from three causes unrelated to L: co-location, shared nodes, and same-type read-outs. The task asks whose realization this occurrence belongs to, and a positional relation cannot answer that.

To exclude this world, a second check must be added on the same current event: this occurrence truly belongs to the legal realization that makes L this Law, and attribution is given by bearing, not by same type, co-location, or sharing.

Call this check same-Law attribution (Same-Law Attribution).

It checks whether the current occurrence recognized in Section 2 is the product of some legal current realization of L's own. Attribution is a relation of bearing, not a positional relation, not a relation of same type.

It excludes: an external bypass — E generating the current read-out directly; another Law producing a same-type result; an external controller generating the same read-out directly; an independent structure of the same type; splicing across alternative Law witnesses; ascribing the occurrence to L merely because of shared nodes, a Shared Core, or physical co-location.

It does not require: that L be the sole producer of the current event — under redundancy, cooperation, or multiple carriers, other structures may take part at the same time; that L's realization fully determine the current read-out; that the current event necessarily disappear if L's realization is deleted — this follows the same reasoning as The General Form of Law's treatment of DynamicRole, where under redundant bearing a simple deletion does not change the result; that attribution be exclusive — one event may belong to L and to other Laws at once, each on the strength of its own bearing witness.

It does not check: again whether the occurrence really happened — that is the first check; nor, in advance, whether the duty is discharged — that belongs to the next section. A world in which attribution holds while the duty is not discharged is exactly what the next section constructs.

The first layer can now be assembled.

Definition 3 (current Law occurrence). CurrentLawOccurrence\_{F,t}(L; ξ\_t) holds if and only if, on ξ\_t, both checks are passed together: there is an actual current occurrence; and that occurrence is attributable to some legal current realization of L's own.

The two checks fall on the same current event. They are two constraints within one layer, not two mutually independent ontological items. The second takes as its object the event recognized by the first. Setting the second out as a conjunct parallel to the first would leave it, whenever there is no event, with nothing to check, so that it could only degenerate into counterfactual attribution or re-walk attribution — and those are exactly the two things Sections 2 and 3 exclude. The separate necessity of each check is proved in Section 4 of the Appendix.

The scope of identity in "attributable to L." In this text, "attributable to L" means only attributable to the current witness family — that is, under the fixed F, t, L, I, the family of legal carriers and their realizations by virtue of which Law\_{F,t}(L, I) = 1 holds. It is a current adjudication; it takes on neither token identity across time nor any adjudication of lineage.

This text therefore does not answer whether a realization of the same type as L is L's; whether a realization copied from L still counts as L's; or whether a carrier that has migrated keeps the same token. The distinctions among copying, migration, same type, and identity belong to the Law-identity texts.

All this text needs is whether, in this one instance at present, the realization bearing this occurrence belongs to the current witness family described above. That adjudication depends on none of the unresolved identity questions just listed, and so does not pre-empt the Law-identity texts. Conversely, should those texts later give a criterion for tokens, the attribution check of this text is refined by that criterion, and the direction of its conclusion does not change.

So the third question: if it really is this Law that is moving, has the duty already been borne?

4

Let the Law's own realization really move, but only halfway.

Suppose one legal carrier of L and its current realization are indeed invoked in this instance. An actual Continuation is present; the components of the chain pass through its carrier; attribution is clear: this stretch really is L's own realization moving — not a bypass, not the same type, not splicing. Definition 3 passes item by item.

Now look at how far the chain gets.

It may complete only the preparatory segment: the state quantities indicated by Anchor(d) are read in and a pre-buffer is set, while Read(d) has not yet taken any value.

It may be only transmission: in this instance a stretch of the carrier is used as a passage to send upstream read-outs elsewhere, and the consequent of L's relation is not discharged there.

It may be only co-located passage: the chain runs through the carrier without completing any duty action on it.

Consider next a failure at the level of type. Suppose Type(d) declares EvalMode(d) = measure, and its relational content requires a read-out to meet a threshold under a conditional measure. In this instance the current chain is non-empty and its attribution is clear, but the set of evaluation points actually obtained has measure zero. Under measure semantics this does not constitute discharge; if the same fact were read under set semantics, it would be judged a pass. Mode drift is precisely the point at which acting can be forged.

Suppose further that D\_{i, ν\_t} contains several duties d₁ and d₂, and that Type requires, for this instance, discharge item by item. This time only d₁ is discharged, and the chain for d₂ stops at the preparatory segment. If a separate uniform rule "it suffices that one duty is discharged" were set up, this instance would be judged acting — but that rule is not the semantics of Rel(L); it has been added from outside.

Where, then, is the failure? In the difference between "attribution" and "discharge."

Attribution shows only that this stretch of action is being done by L's realization; it does not show what it has completed. Whether the duty has been borne must be evaluated by the duty semantics this relation instance already has, and those semantics are already written entirely into Type(d), Read(d), Cov(L), Ante(d), and Trig(i). Neither of the two preceding sections made any use of them.

To exclude this world, a second layer of constraint must be added: the current occurrence already attributed to L must truly enter duty bearing according to the existing duty semantics of this relation instance.

Definition 4 (duty discharge). DutyConformance\_{F,t}(L; ξ\_t) holds if and only if there exists some legal realization of L that, on D\_{i, ν\_t}, discharges the duties according to the existing semantics of Type(d) for each d, with Ante(d), Cov(L), and Trig(i) not relaxed.

This definition does not require in advance that this realization be the very one Definition 3 recognizes. The two layers are evidenced independently: the first asks "is this current occurrence its own?", the second asks "has its duty been carried out according to the semantics of this instance?" Whether the two certificates must come from one and the same realization is the question the next section answers. Binding them here in advance would make the binding discipline something planted in the definition rather than a result of the argument.

Nor does this definition restrict the realization to one obtained in the current context. Definition 3 says "some legal current realization"; this definition says only "some legal realization." The difference in wording is deliberate, not a slip. The duty domain remains confined to D\_{i, ν\_t} of this instance, but the witness of discharge may be taken from re-walk records under ReplayCtx.

This relaxation serves two purposes, both redeemed later. The splicing world of Section 5 is precisely one that uses re-walk records as its certificate of discharge; had the witness been pinned to the current context here, that world could not be constructed, and the binding discipline could not be forced out. The counterexample "delete CurrentLawOccurrence" in Section 4 of the Appendix depends on it too; without it, the deletion tests of the two layers could not be made symmetrical.

The relaxation does not weaken necessity. If LawAct holds, the current realization bearing this exercise has itself already discharged the duty, and it is the very witness this definition requires. The relaxation only enlarges the domain of witnesses, and existence is easier to satisfy on a larger domain; so the necessary direction still holds. Conversely, the enlarged domain of witnesses is exactly where the splicing channel lies, so it must be taken back — and it is taken back in Section 5, not in this definition.

Semantic discipline, stated item by item:

Duties are quantified throughout by D\_{i, ν\_t}; no separate pool of duties is set up.

Each d is evaluated according to the successor-description type declared by its Type(d) and by EvalMode(d); deterministic, existential, measure, mixed, and window are each evaluated by their existing upstream semantics, none of which this text rewrites.

No uniform rule is invented. The three formulations "it suffices that one duty exists," "all duties occur simultaneously," and "a majority of duties pass" are all prohibited. The duty quantifier of this instance is given by Rel(L) and Type, not stipulated by this text.

EvalMode may not drift. The corresponding three-valued treatment belongs to the layer of formal read-back; see Section 2 of the Appendix.

If Type itself requires window or measure, only the evaluation window and measure required by the original Type are observed; this text adds no uniform requirement of duration.

It excludes: start-up only — the chain is activated while Read(d) takes no value; preparation only — Anchor(d) is read in while the duty action does not occur; transmission only — the carrier is used as a passage and the consequent of the relation is not discharged there; co-location only — the chain runs through the carrier without completing the duty on it; mere passage through the carrier; failure to enter real duty bearing; completion only of partial steps insufficient to constitute a current legal relation instance.

It does not require: that discharge be perfect, noise-free, or optimal — it suffices that Read(d) takes its value by the semantics of the original Type; that discharge bring about any external result — the result side is struck out in Section 6; that discharge be completed by a single component — distributed bearing, redundant carriers, cyclic structures, and minimal sufficient groups are all admissible; any additional duration — if Type declares no window, this text supplies none; that the quantifier over D\_{i, ν\_t} take any uniform shape — the quantifier is given by Type, and this text only prohibits rewriting it.

So the fourth question: if both layers hold, is that one instance of acting?

5

Let both layers hold, but each on its own.

Suppose L has two legal carriers R₁, R₂ ∈ 𝒲\_S(F, t, L, I). In this instance:

on R₁ there is indeed a current actual Continuation, and the occurrence is attributable to a legal current realization on R₁ — Definition 3 holds on the witness of R₁;

there is a further witness, taken from R₂'s re-walk records under ReplayCtx, on which the Type semantics is fully discharged — Definition 4 holds on that witness.

Written down separately, each layer is true. This world can be constructed precisely because Definition 4, taken literally, is evidenced independently: it asks only "has some legal realization of L discharged according to Type?", and does not say that this must be the one Definition 3 recognizes.

Yet no actual exercise of the duty has occurred. On R₁ something moved and its attribution is clear, but nothing was discharged. What was discharged on R₂ was a re-walk, not a current occurrence. There is no relation of bearing between the two.

Set the two certificates side by side and they read like one instance of acting; in fact they are two unrelated matters.

Forms of the same kind include: an event at present, with evidence of discharge taken from a re-walk; an event attributed to one realization of L, with evidence of discharge taken from another realization; the event recognized by Definition 3 lying on one realization, and the duty domain evaluated by Definition 4 lying on another.

Where, then, is the failure? Not in the content of the two layers — neither layer's list of exclusions has been violated. It lies in the absence of any constraint between the two certificates: they can come from different carriers, different contexts, different temporal layers.

The task asks whether "this one instance is acting." One instance means one and the same exercise.

To exclude this world, what must be added is not a third ontological constraint but a binding requirement on the witnesses of the two layers.

Definition 5 (binding discipline). Definitions 3 and 4 must be witnessed jointly by one and the same legal current realization: there exist R ∈ 𝒲\_S(F, t, L, I) and a current realization Π on that R such that the witnesses of both layers are all obtained on the same (R, Π, RoleCtx(i, ν\_t)).

Admissible: the following are all legal forms internal to one and the same realization and do not constitute splicing — distributed bearing within it; redundant carriers, that is, several parallel passages within the same realization bearing jointly; cyclic structures; minimal sufficient groups, that is, a minimal sufficient group formed by several components within the same realization; several constituent parts within one legal realization bearing jointly.

Prohibited: splicing across alternative witnesses; splicing across replay and current; splicing across different independent Laws; splicing across an external bypass and an internal witness; assembling one instance of acting from parts of the facts taken from different realizations.

Its standing: the binding discipline is not a third ontological layer. It adds no new requirement on current facts — in the world above, the facts of both layers already exist. What it stipulates is the identity between the witnesses of the two layers; it is a rule for binding certificates. Wherever the structure is cited, read back, summarized, or inherited downstream, it may not be written as a third conjunct of CurLawBear, nor may it be deleted while the conjunction of the two layers keeps its original strength.

Nor is it something planted in a definition. Definition 4 deliberately leaves the realization unspecified, so that the two layers are evidenced independently; the requirement of identity is a result of the argument, forced out by the splicing world above, not something written into a definition first and read out of it afterwards.

So the fifth question: with two layers and the binding, must a condition from the result side still be added on the side of current facts?

6

Let it really be acting, but let it not win.

This question must be answered. Intuitively, "acting" is all too easily read as "having had an effect."

Consider first an ordinary world of two competing Laws, with no Principle involved.

Suppose F contains another already standing Law M, with M ≠ L. Each stands independently, each has legal carriers and current realizations, and in this instance they share one set of result variables or resources.

The relation between M and L must be fixed as one that is not a Principle relation; otherwise this counterexample would be absorbed by The Principle Group-Form. M and L undergo ordinary superposition or competition on the shared result variables or resources, but M's Law-forming duty does not take L's current bearing of its holding-tendency as its object. M's relational content is not directed at "restricting L's current exercise": no duty in Rel(M) has L's holding-tendency as its antecedent or as its recipient. Hence M does not satisfy CounterHoldRole, and Principle(M; L, ·) does not hold. The two simply go their own ways and happen to act on the same quantities.

In constructing this counterexample, the opening move requires particular care.

One may not first lay out both layers as complete and then say "therefore L is acting." That would infer LawAct from CurLawBear — exactly the direction only the next text is to prove. Were this section to open that way, its conclusion of striking out would rest on an implication not yet proved.

So the order is reversed: take a legal field state in which L is indeed in the state LawAct. That such a field state exists is a premise of this construction; it is not derived from any conclusion of this text.

On this field state, by the necessary direction of this text, L must have CurLawBear. That both layers are complete is a conclusion here, not a premise.

At the same time let M also be acting in this instance, its action contributing together with L's to the shared result variables. The two superpose, and the total output equals neither the result of L acting alone nor the result of M acting alone. The terminal L pushes toward is displaced.

One symbol must be fixed here first. In this section, OutcomeSuccess(L) denotes "the total output of this instance coincides with the terminal L would push toward if acting alone without interference." It is a comparison symbol set up only to state the conclusion of striking out, and it is used only in this section. This text does not define how L's interference-free terminal is computed, does not claim that the comparison is adjudicable in every field state, and introduces OutcomeSuccess into no definition, theorem, or interface. To strike out a candidate item one need not formalize it first; one need only show that it can come apart from LawAct.

So, on one and the same field state, we have together:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;LawAct\_{F,t}(L; ξ\_t) holds　and　OutcomeSuccess(L) does not hold

and thereby, independently:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;LawAct ⇏ OutcomeSuccess

This step uses only the necessary direction of this text, the fact that "two Laws can be acting in the field at the same time," and the existence of a field state in which L is in the state LawAct. It does not use the sufficient direction, and it depends on no clause of The Principle Group-Form.

Next, the counter-hold world serves as a cross-check within the system.

Suppose CounterHoldRole holds for P against L in some relational context j, so that Principle(P; L, j) holds. Again take a field state in which L is in the state LawAct; at the same time P's realization discharges its established counter-hold on a dynamical degree of freedom L actually holds, and the final result is not fully realized in L's interference-free direction.

If achieving the result were written into the necessary qualification for acting, one would here have to adjudicate LawAct(L) = 0.

That adjudication conflicts directly with The Principle Group-Form. According to Zone Two of The Principle Group-Form, the content of CurAnti has at least three layers: the same d\* actually occurs in the current actual Continuation; its current legal realization actually enters A's current bearing of its holding-tendency; and there it discharges its established counter-hold on a dynamical degree of freedom A actually holds. The second layer expressly requires that the target Law A be currently exercising itself. Definition 3.28 of The Principle Group-Form states likewise: AHold\_A(j, η) = 1 if and only if A, in π\_A^j(η), actually enters current duty bearing on the strength of its own Law witness.

Hence: if LawAct had "not being counter-held" or "the result being achieved" as a necessary condition, then whenever P is counter-holding A, A could not be acting; AHold\_A = 0; the second layer of CurAnti could never hold; and Act(P; A, j) could never hold. The whole of Zone Two would cancel itself.

Taking the two paths together: the conclusion of striking out is borne independently by the world of two competing Laws, and the consistency check against The Principle Group-Form serves as a cross-check. Even if the relevant clauses of The Principle Group-Form are later upgraded or turned pending, the conclusion of this section still stands.

Definition 6 (current bearing).

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;CurLawBear\_{F,t}(L; ξ\_t)  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;\= CurrentLawOccurrence\_{F,t}(L; ξ\_t) ∧ DutyConformance\_{F,t}(L; ξ\_t)

Each layer takes its value by its definition in the section above: the first asks whether this current occurrence is L's; the second asks whether some legal realization of L has achieved duty discharge in this instance according to the existing semantics. The witnesses of the two layers are constrained by Definition 5 and must fall on one and the same realization.

The binding discipline is not a third conjunct of this definition. The two differ in standing, and both read-back and downstream citation must preserve the distinction. To write the second layer as "the discharge of the very one the first layer recognizes" would plant the binding in the definition and leave Definition 5 with nothing to prove; this text does not adopt that formulation.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;LawAct\_{F,t}(L; ξ\_t) ⇒ CurLawBear\_{F,t}(L; ξ\_t)

The converse — whether complete CurLawBear suffices to establish LawAct — is not asked here; it belongs to The Necessity Form of Law-Action.

Pressed into its plainest statement:

A Law stands: the field already has something in it. A Law acts: that something is moving at this moment.

Between the two statements lie three gates: did it really move, was it this Law that moved, did it move all the way? The first two together make "a current occurrence that is its own"; the third asks whether that occurrence carried the duty through.

Even with all three passed, one more question must be asked: do these two certificates belong to one and the same instance?

As for whether it won once it moved — that is not asked. Two Laws can be acting in the field at the same time, and the total output belongs to neither alone; and a counter-hold presses precisely on something that is acting — against something that is not acting there is nothing to counter.

3\. The General Theorem

Necessary-Direction Theorem of Law-Action. Under the working domain listed in Section 1, the three non-degeneracy conditions listed in Section 1 of the Line of Argument, and the inherited conditions:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;LawAct\_{F,t}(L; ξ\_t) ⇒ CurLawBear\_{F,t}(L; ξ\_t)

and the witnesses of the two layers are constrained by Definition 5.

Proof. Suppose LawAct\_{F,t}(L; ξ\_t) holds.

By Section 2 of the Line of Argument: if there were no actual current occurrence in this instance, then what is true is at most that the structural layer can walk, that the re-entry layer can re-walk, or the historical stock — none of which is current exercise — contradicting LawAct. Hence the first check is passed.

By Section 3 of the Line of Argument: if the current occurrence were not borne by a legal current realization of L's own, then what is exercising is another Law, an external controller, or an independent structure of the same type, and L itself is not exercising in this instance, contradicting LawAct. Hence the second check is passed. Both checks are passed on the one current event of this instance; by Definition 3, CurrentLawOccurrence holds.

By Section 4 of the Line of Argument: if no legal realization of L discharged the duties under this ξ\_t according to the existing semantics of Type(d) on D\_{i, ν\_t}, then the relational duties of L were not exercised at all in this instance — at most they were started, transmitted, or passed through — contradicting LawAct. Hence there exists at least one legal realization for which DutyConformance holds.

All that is obtained here is "there exists such a realization." Whether it is the one recognized in the second step is not asserted at this step; asserting that it is would complete the work of Section 5 here in advance.

By Section 5 of the Line of Argument: what LawAct asserts is one exercise, not two matters each true on its own. If the two certificates obtained in the preceding three steps belonged to different carriers, different realizations, or different contexts, then the field would contain the two facts "there is a current occurrence that is its own" and "there is a realization of it that has discharged the duty," while no single exercise had both — contradicting the singleness asserted by LawAct. Hence

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;∃ ( R, Π ): CurrentLawOccurrence and DutyConformance hold together on (R, Π, RoleCtx(i, ν\_t)).

The first three steps give that each layer holds; the fourth gives that both share one witness. Their conjunction is CurLawBear together with its binding discipline. ∎

Status. Conditional. The conditions are declared in two layers, which are not merged.

Conditions of the ontological theorem. What the general theorem itself inherits are only the upstream conditions needed for the entry condition Law\_{F,t}(L, I) = 1 to hold: C0 ∧ C2 ∧ H1 ∧ H2 ∧ H3. Wherever these five hold and the entry condition and the three non-degeneracy conditions are satisfied, the ontological necessary direction holds — irrespective of whether it can be read out by existing formal objects.

Conditions of formal read-back. The concrete evaluation of each read-back candidate in Section 2 of the Appendix further inherits the antecedents of the Read group and the Rep group of The Mathematical Form of Law, and is subject to that text's three-valued closure. This layer decides only "can it be computed?", not "does it hold ontologically?"

Neither layer may stand in for the other. When formal read-back is recorded as ⊥, the ontological necessary direction does not thereby become pending; it is merely non-adjudicable in that instance. Conversely, a complete Read group and Rep group cannot make up for C0, C2, or H1–H3 missing on the ontological side.

Wherever this theorem is cited, the antecedents corresponding to the layer used must be inherited: a citation of the ontological necessary direction only inherits the conditions of the ontological theorem; a citation that further invokes formal read-back inherits, in addition, the conditions of formal read-back. The conclusion may not be inherited alone.

Corollary 3 (the Law at rest). There exists t such that Law\_{F,t}(L, I) = 1 while LawAct\_{F,t}(L; ξ\_t) does not hold.

The side that can be checked is the negative one. Suppose that, for every d ∈ D\_{i, ν\_t} and every R ∈ 𝒲\_S(F, t, L, I), the chains of the current layer are all empty. Under the existing read-back antecedents, this suffices to construct a negative instance: "the Law already stands and is not currently acting."

The converse does not hold: a non-empty chain on the current layer is far from sufficient to establish LawAct positively, because neither attribution nor discharge has yet been answered. The formal layer of The Principle Group-Form has likewise pointed out that a non-empty current chain shows only that the duty is currently being generated; it does not imply entry into the target's bearing, still less that action holds.

4\. Minimality of the Necessary Structure

What this section proves is that the necessary constraints cannot be deleted, not that they are complete. This text has not proved the sufficient direction, and therefore uses no name such as "complete set"; whether the two layers and the two checks together suffice to establish LawAct belongs to The Necessity Form of Law-Action.

Neither layer can be deleted.

Deleting DutyConformance. Take the world of Section 4 of the Line of Argument: in this instance there is an actual current occurrence, and it is attributable to a legal current realization of L's own — CurrentLawOccurrence holds in full, with no counterfactual or re-walk certificate needed. But the chain reaches only preparation, transmission, or co-location, and Read(d) takes no value. Keeping the first layer and deleting the second would count L as acting as soon as its realization is merely touched. Hence DutyConformance cannot be deleted.

Deleting CurrentLawOccurrence. Because Definition 4 does not specify the realization, the second layer can be evidenced independently, and so a symmetrical counterexample is available here.

Take the branch of R₂ in the world of Section 5 of the Line of Argument and remove the branch of R₁: L has a legal realization whose re-walk records under ReplayCtx fully discharge the duties of D\_{i, ν\_t} according to the Type semantics — DutyConformance holds by Definition 4. Yet there is no actual current occurrence in this instance: for every d and every R, the chains of the current layer are all empty.

Keeping the second layer and deleting the first would count a Law as acting now as long as "it discharged the last time it walked," reading the qualification to re-walk directly as current exercise. Hence CurrentLawOccurrence cannot be deleted.

The failure forms of the two layers are thus visibly symmetrical: deleting the first layer leaves "taking a re-walk for the present"; deleting the second leaves "counting a touch as a job done." Neither is "acting at this moment," and neither covers the other.

Each of the two checks within the first layer is necessary.

Removing actual current occurrence and keeping only same-Law attribution. Attribution needs an object. With no current event to attribute, the criterion of attribution can only degenerate into one of two things: counterfactual attribution ("if there were an event, it would belong to L") or re-walk attribution ("the last event belonged to L"). Both hold in the world of Section 2 of the Line of Argument, where L is plainly not acting. Hence the check of actual current occurrence cannot be removed.

Removing same-Law attribution and keeping only actual current occurrence. Take the world of Section 3 of the Line of Argument: there is an actual Continuation at present, the read-out matches the relational content Rel(L) requires, and the components of the chain intersect one of L's carriers through co-location. The first check passes while the bearer is another Law or an external controller. Without checking attribution, L could be adjudicated as acting while itself entirely idle. Hence the check of same-Law attribution cannot be removed.

The failure forms of the two checks are of different types. The first fails as "there is no such thing"; the second as "there is such a thing, but it is not its own." Merging them into one check would lose one of the questions. Merged into "there is a current occurrence conforming to Rel(L)," they would be satisfied exactly by the same-type bypass world of Section 3, and the question of attribution would disappear.

Note that the two kinds of non-deletability are of different types. Between the two layers, non-deletability is that of independent conjuncts: delete either layer and the other can still be evidenced independently, with a counterexample for each. The two checks within the first layer do not stand in that relation: the second takes as its object the event recognized by the first, and once the first is removed the second can only degenerate into counterfactual or re-walk attribution; it does not go on standing independently in its original sense. The former is the necessity of parallel items; the latter is that of two successive, interdependent constraints within one layer. That is exactly why this text merges the two checks into one layer rather than writing them alongside discharge as three ontological items.

The binding discipline cannot be deleted. Take the world of Section 5 of the Line of Argument: both layers hold, but are borne by different witnesses. Keeping both layers and deleting the binding would let one instance of acting be assembled from two unrelated matters. The consequence of deleting it is of a different type from that of deleting a layer: deleting either layer leaves a real lack of one kind; deleting the binding leaves a channel for forgery. That is exactly why it is listed as a rule for binding certificates rather than as a third ontological layer. Writing it inside either layer would make that layer bear the other's obligation of identity, and would break the item-by-item checking structure above.

No redundancy. Section 6 of the Line of Argument struck out every candidate from the result side and the source side, each either by an independent counterexample from the world of two competing Laws or because it belongs to another question. Any proposed additional item that does not open a new constraint of the task is only a repetition, combination, or realization of existing items, relations, or realization details, and does not belong to CurLawBear.

Non-deletability is minimal necessity of function and structure; it prescribes no unique physical realization. One and the same CurLawBear may be realized in many forms: distributed bearing, redundant carriers, cyclic structures, minimal sufficient groups.

5\. A Condition Sufficient to Negate LawAct

The nature of this section must be made clear first.

This text proves only the necessary direction LawAct ⇒ CurLawBear. All that can legitimately be derived from it is its contrapositive:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;¬CurLawBear\_{F,t}(L; ξ\_t) ⇒ ¬LawAct\_{F,t}(L; ξ\_t)

What this section gives is exactly the sufficient condition for negation obtained from that contrapositive: satisfying it is enough to adjudicate that current action does not hold.

This section does not claim that the condition exhausts every case of ¬LawAct. To assert that "whatever fails this condition must be acting," one would first need

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;CurLawBear ⇒ LawAct

and that direction is not proved in this text; it belongs to The Necessity Form of Law-Action. Until it is completed, this section may be used in one direction only: from the condition holding one may infer not acting; from the condition failing one may not infer acting.

Sufficient condition for negation. The adjudication must quantify over all legal current realizations. The failure of one witness is not enough to negate a whole Law — another legal witness may still be bearing.

If

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;¬∃ ( R, Π ): R ∈ 𝒲\_S(F, t, L, I), Π is a legal current realization on that R,  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;such that CurrentLawOccurrence and DutyConformance hold together on the same (R, Π, RoleCtx(i, ν\_t))

then

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;¬LawAct\_{F,t}(L; ξ\_t)

Unfolded: if there is no legal current realization such that, in this instance, there is an actual current occurrence, that occurrence is attributable to this realization, and this realization has discharged the duties on D\_{i, ν\_t} according to the original Type semantics, then current action does not hold.

The converse may not be read off. The negation of the formula above — that such an (R, Π) exists — shows only that CurLawBear holds, not that LawAct holds. Any downstream reading of this section as ¬LawAct ⟺ ¬CurLawBear exceeds its authority by using a direction the second text has not yet proved.

The adjudicability of this condition itself. The formula above is a sufficient condition for negation at the ontological layer: whenever it holds, ¬LawAct can be adjudicated. But to check whether it holds in a concrete field state, one must go through formal read-back, and is therefore subject to the three-valued treatment of Section 2.

One point here is easily passed over: a universal negation is not simply the complement of the conjunction of truth values taken witness by witness. If, for some (R, Π), the formal read-back of one of the two layers is recorded as ⊥ — for example, because MixedMode(L, i) ∈ {1, ⊥} makes discharge non-adjudicable — then that witness is neither excluded nor confirmed; and the judgment "there is no such witness" is then itself non-adjudicable.

Rule of treatment: in checking, a non-⊥ read-out must be obtained for every legal current realization. If on every one of them one of the two layers is false, the condition holds and ¬LawAct is adjudicated. If on some one of them both layers are true, the condition does not hold and this section gives no conclusion. If any one of them has a read-out of ⊥ while none of the others is confirmed to have both layers true, the whole judgment is recorded as ⊥: only non-adjudicability may be reported; ¬LawAct may not be adjudicated on that basis, and the non-adjudicable witness may not be treated as excluded.

The ontological side is unaffected: whether the two layers hold ontologically has nothing to do with whether they can be read out by existing formal quantifiers. The question of adjudicability falls only on the side of checking.

The following formulations are all prohibited, because each infers a global negation from the failure of a single witness:

that the chain of the current layer is empty for some R, therefore ¬LawAct;

that ActBear\_R(d) = ∅ for some R, therefore ¬LawAct;

that some one realization has not discharged the duty, therefore ¬LawAct.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Law\_{F,t}(L, I) ≠ 1; I\_cov(L, I) = ∅; D\_{i, ν\_t} = ∅.

6\. The Interface with The Principle Group-Form

AHold\_A(j, η) of Definition 3.28 in The Principle Group-Form is the projection, in a particular joint Principle evaluation (j, η), of the relevant part of the target Law A's current bearing of its holding-tendency.

The direction that can be expressed. If AHold\_A(j, η) = 1, then, on the Law-duty involved in π\_A^j(η), A satisfies the structure of current bearing that CurLawBear requires in this text: an occurrence is indeed current, it is attributable to a Law witness of A's own, and it enters current duty bearing. &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;LawAct(A) ⟺ AHold\_A(j, η)

as a global equivalence. A Law may be exercising other duties while not entering at all the part involved in relational context j; in that case LawAct(A) holds while AHold\_A(j, η) cannot be obtained on that projection. LawAct does not automatically imply any given AHold.

Items forced out by this text. Conditional items. C0, C2, H1, H2, H3 (The General Form of Law); the Read group and the Rep group (The Mathematical Form of Law). They are cited under their original numbering and are not restated as proved.

Breach. With the working domain, entry condition, non-degeneracy conditions, and inherited conditions declared by this text all preserved, exhibit a system in which L really is in a state of current action at this moment while the negation condition of Section 5 holds. There are four concrete forms: exhibit an instance of acting in which there is no actual current occurrence at all in this instance; exhibit an instance of acting in which the current occurrence is borne by a structure other than L, with no legal realization of L taking part; exhibit an instance of acting in which the duty has not been discharged according to the original Type semantics; prove that the two checks can in fact be merged, or the two layers merged, or the binding discipline deleted, without any splicing channel remaining. A breach of Section 5 of the Appendix must respect the one-directionality declared in that section: it must exhibit a system that satisfies that sufficient condition for negation and in which L is nevertheless acting. A counterexample of the form "does not satisfy the condition and yet is not acting" is not a breach, because that section never claims that the condition is exhaustive.

A breach must identify exactly which general proposition, layer, check, or binding it breaches. It may not substitute a different object, a different task, or a different quantifier, and it may not replace a universal negation with the failure of a single witness.

Boundary cases. The following are not breaches: a non-degeneracy condition fails — this text does not apply; MixedMode(L, i) ∈ {1, ⊥} makes the formal read-back of discharge ⊥ — formal non-adjudicability, not a failure of an ontological conclusion; the check of the sufficient condition for negation in Section 5 is recorded as ⊥ — by that section's rule of adjudicability only non-adjudicability may be reported, which constitutes neither ¬LawAct nor a breach of this text; another form of realizing the same CurLawBear is exhibited — this belongs to the list of admissible forms in Definition 5, and what is claimed as necessary is the difference of function and structure, not the outward form of realization; L is acting while the result is not achieved — this is an item struck out in Section 6 of the Line of Argument and the expected conclusion of this text; L is exercising other duties without entering some Principle relation — this is the asymmetry of interface directions, see Section 6; a counterexample built from facts at t′ ≠ t or i′ ≠ i — this falls outside the scope of this text; a different adjudication on another instance at the same t — the normal result of adjudicating instance by instance, since this text defines no aggregate value; an inherited condition fails — the conclusion of this text becomes pending accordingly, the normal propagation of a conditionalized conclusion.

Working Protocol and Undefined Aggregation

What is set out in this section is not a gap but this text's convention on the unit of evaluation. It is already closed within this text and enters no list of items awaiting verification.

Unit of evaluation. By Definition 1, this text takes

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;ξ\_t = ( i, ν\_t, D\_{i, ν\_t} )

as a single current evaluation instance declared in advance. Every adjudication — the two layers of constraint, the binding discipline, the general theorem, the negation condition — is completed within that single instance.

Several instances. If several legal instances exist at the same time, each is adjudicated separately. This text gives each its own value of LawAct and does not merge them.

Undefined aggregation. Whether a meaningful aggregate read-out across instances exists — for example, "is L, taken as a whole, acting at time t?" — this text neither defines such a read-out nor claims that one exists.

If a later text needs aggregation, it must define its own rule of aggregation and bear the corresponding obligation of proof: state at what level of quantifier the aggregate takes its value, state its relation to the values taken instance by instance, and give its own breach modes. No aggregate value may be obtained from this text, and the single-instance conclusions of this text may not be read directly as conclusions about a moment.

A different adjudication on different instances at the same t is the normal result of adjudicating instance by instance and does not constitute a breach of this text.
