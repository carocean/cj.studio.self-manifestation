# The Necessity Form of Law-Action

Section One

Purpose

This text proves the sufficient direction of Law-Action: once the two layers of current bearing of an already standing Law are complete, it is acting at this moment.

The necessary direction has been proved in The General Form of Law-Action, which handed the sufficient direction down to this text. The two directions together give current action on the side of Law an ontological closure in both directions. That is the place of this text within The Law-Dynamics Group-Form.

Formally: fix a Sedimentation-Field F, a current time t, an already standing Law L and its applicability-cover domain I, and one current relation instance ξ\_t at time t, with Law\_{F,t}(L, I) = 1 as the entry condition. What is proved is

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;CurLawBear\_{F,t}(L; ξ\_t) ⇒ LawAct\_{F,t}(L; ξ\_t)

where

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;CurLawBear = CurrentLawOccurrence ∧ DutyConformance

and the two layers are witnessed jointly by one and the same legal current realization.

The content of the two layers: CurrentLawOccurrence says that in this instance an actual occurrence does take place, and that it is attributable to a legal current realization of L's own; DutyConformance says that the relational duties of L are discharged according to the original semantics of Type, Read, Cov, Ante, and Trig.

The route of proof is: freeze the canonical source material and atomize it into a finite semantic domain; partition the atoms by the duty each primarily bears in the canonical source; force out from them the three items of current realization; prove that within that domain there is neither a fourth condition of realization nor an independent disqualifying condition; then prove that the object-layer structure of Chapter 1 realizes all three items. The sufficient direction follows.

The necessary direction is not proved again here. That the necessary constraints cannot be deleted is not completeness, and non-deletability does not imply sufficiency; the minimality result of the general form serves here only as a consistency back-test and does not enter the antecedent.

### 4.3 The Semantic Partition

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;U\_LAct = U\_R ⊔ U\_D ⊔ U\_O

U\_R (CurrentRealization): the semantic atoms that directly determine that the Law is acting at this moment.

The three zones are mutually exclusive and together exhaust the fifteen atoms. Candidates formed by conjunction are handled separately in Sections Eight and Nine, by a check of canonical warrant.

### 4.4 The Criteria of Classification

(a) The temporal-phase criterion. Does the atom state how something comes to be, when it starts, whether it is doing it at this moment, to what degree it is doing it, whether it persists afterwards, whether it ends, or what it generates once it is done?

(b) The deletion criterion. Negate the atom while the others are unchanged: if L still has, in this instance, an actual duty bearing that is attributable to it and discharged by the original semantics, then the atom is not a qualification for current action.

The bearing relied on here is the object-layer CurLawBear of the sealed Chapter 1, not the R1, R2, R3 this text has yet to force out. The two are not interchangeable: the former is sealed; the latter is derived from the canonical source by the reduction of Section Six.

The direction of (b) is one-way: it licenses exclusion, not inclusion. Where negating an atom leaves the bearing in place, the atom may be adjudicated not a qualification for current action. Conversely, where negating an atom removes the bearing, this shows only that (b) does not exclude it; it does not prove that the atom is a qualification for current action. Inclusion in U\_R rests positively on (a); in each such place (b) serves only as corroboration. Wherever an atom is said below to be "not removable," this means that (b) does not exclude it; the ground for its zone is (a).

(c) The criterion of canonical self-attestation. Does a sentence elsewhere in the same canonical source expressly allow the atom to be absent while Execution still takes place?

(d) The two-sided criterion. The special check on apparent vetoes: with every atom of current realization holding, can the atom by itself make LawAct = 0? If its occurrence necessarily makes some atom of current realization fail, it is not independent, and it goes to its own class, not to U\_D.

### 4.5 Instantiation of the Target Index

What each canonical sentence states is general semantics. The "being executed" of M2 and M3 says that an already formed relational content is being executed, without specifying whose relational content it is.

Once the fixed working domain is entered, this semantics is instantiated to the target when it is used to adjudicate LawAct\_{F,t}(L; ξ\_t):

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;U4(L)　≔　the relational content of the fixed target L is being executed

In the same way U13 is instantiated as U13(L), meaning the distinctive relational duty of L, that is, its holding.

Instantiation settles whom the question is about. The argument of the target predicate is L, and the canonical semantics taken up in order to adjudicate it is instantiated, in its reference, to L throughout; facts about the execution of other Laws are not substituted into this adjudication. It adds no atom and bears no independent truth value: U4(L) and U13(L) are the forms in which U4 and U13 are taken up under the fixed target.

Instantiation fixes the reference only; it excludes nothing. The exclusion of external bypasses, same-type results of other Laws, shared nodes, physical co-location, and splicing across alternative witnesses is borne at the object layer by the same-Law attribution of Chapter 1. Instantiation asks whom the statement is about; same-Law attribution answers whether this occurrence is really its own. To let the former stand in for the latter would be to replace "this was really done by L" with "this is said of L."

Section Six

The Three Items of Current Realization

### 6.1 Reduction

What U3 names is exactly the matter U1 states; the naming and what is named belong to one matter. U7 states running at present and refers, with U1 and U3, to this occasion of actually walking. The three merge into one item: in this instance an actual occurrence does take place.

U4 and U5 add a further layer. They do not merely say that there is movement; they say that this already formed relational content has been executed, and that the existing relation makes the current fact actually so. Together they require a relation of bearing between the current occurrence and that relational content, not merely simultaneity in time or co-location in space.

This layer divides into two further questions, with different semantic sources.

First: whose is the relational content that is executed? The answer must be obtained in two steps: first instantiate U4 to the fixed target as U4(L) by 4.5, then take it together with U13(L). The order may not be reversed, and the step of instantiation may not be skipped; otherwise "some relational content is being executed" and "L has its holding" are two facts each of which can be true without any bearing on the other.

Second: has that relational content been truly executed in full? Being executed differs from being started or passed through; making-so differs from not-yet-so. This question is borne jointly by the sense of completeness in U4(L) and the sense of already-so in U5, and the content discharged is specified by U13(L).

Hence three items.

### 6.2 R1　Current Actuality

In this ξ\_t there is an actual current occurrence.

Canonical source: U1 + U3 + U7; U5 reinforces actuality here, excluding the case in which the relation is there but the fact is not yet so.

It is not being able to walk, having walked, being able to re-walk, structural integrity, or the existence of a potential.

Canonical self-attestation: U11, Law—repetition, stands in M6 beside walking at this moment without being equal to it; being able to re-walk is a qualification, not an event.

Counterpart in Chapter 1: actual current occurrence.

### 6.3 R2　Law Attribution

The current occurrence belongs to a legal current realization of this L.

Canonical source: U4(L) + U13(L), both instantiated to the fixed target by 4.5.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;U4(L) — the relational content of the fixed target L is being executed;  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;U13(L) — that relational content as the distinctive duty of the Law, that is, L's holding.

Taken together they give: what is currently executed is precisely the holding of this L.

U5 does not bear this item. Making-so says that the fact is indeed so on account of the relation; it strengthens actuality and completeness, and does not answer which Law brought it about. To derive attribution of identity from U5 alone would let any relation that brings about the same fact be ascribed to L — the same-type bypass that the same-Law attribution of Chapter 1 excludes.

What U4(L) + U13(L) gives is a locking of reference: the execution in question is attributed to L. That this occurrence is really borne by a legal realization of L is a finding of fact, made at the object layer by the same-Law attribution of Chapter 1. The two do not stand in for each other.

R2 is a relation of bearing, not a relation of exclusivity, and not a counterfactual relation. The canonical "being executed" requires only that this holding is indeed executed by its executor; it does not require that no one else is moving at the same time, nor that the fact would necessarily not be so if the executor were deleted. The three "does not require" clauses of Chapter 1's same-Law attribution hold here unchanged.

Excluded: other Laws; external bypasses; same-type results; shared nodes; physical co-location; splicing across alternative witnesses.

At the object layer, R1 and R2 together make CurrentLawOccurrence.

### 6.4 R3　Duty Actualization

The relational duties of L are actually discharged in this ξ\_t according to the original Type / Read / Cov / Ante / Trig semantics.

Canonical source: the sense of completeness in U4(L) + the sense of already-so in U5 + the distinctive relational duty that U13(L) specifies.

R3 is Type-faithful actual duty bearing: a legal realization actually bears and discharges the duty.

It is not a result value that happens to satisfy the requirement. The canonical "being executed" says that this holding is executed, not that an identical read-out appeared somewhere else; making-so says that this holding makes the current fact so, not that the current fact happens to be so.

Excluded: start-up only; preparation only; transmission only; passage through the carrier only; co-location only; an external result that happens to coincide with Read.

Counterpart in Chapter 1: DutyConformance.

### 6.5 Nothing Remains in U\_R

U1 → R1; U3 → R1; U7 → R1; U4, once instantiated as U4(L), supplies the sense of execution in R2 and the sense of completeness in R3; U5 → the reinforcement of actuality in R1 and the sense of already-so in R3.

U13 goes to U\_O; once instantiated as U13(L), it supplies content to R2 and R3. That supply does not change its zone: U13 itself states the content of the duty, not whether that content is being discharged at this moment.

U\_R contains no fourth independent content. This conclusion is confined to the frozen canonical domain M1–M7, and is supplemented by the check of conjunctions in Section Eight.

Section Seven

The Standing of Binding

R1, R2, and R3 must belong to one and the same action. At the object layer, Definition 5 of Chapter 1 continues to apply: there exists one and the same

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;( R, Π, RoleCtx(i, ν\_t) )

witnessing them jointly.

The canonical ground lies in U3 and U5. What M1's "this is Execution" refers to is that one actual transit, a single occasion; what M3's making-so refers to is this holding making this current fact so, subject and result being two ends of one chain. The canonical source does not warrant separately the three matters of there being one movement, there being one executor, and there being one discharge.

remains a conjunction of two items; Binding is a discipline of one and the same witness, is not recorded as R4, and is written into no conjunction.

Section Eight

The Exhaustion of R4

### 8.1 Method

Exhaustion is given by the classification of a finite canonical domain. U\_LAct has been frozen at fifteen atoms; they have been sorted item by item, each to exactly one zone; and U\_R has been proved to reduce to three items with nothing remaining.

Any additional condition of current action that is proposed, if its semantics has a ground in M1–M7 and bears the semantics of current action, is carried either by a single atom or by an irreducible conjunction of atoms. The two levels are checked separately: 8.2 checks single-atom candidates, and 8.3 checks candidates formed by conjunction, by canonical warrant.

Two further cases do not fall on an atomic carrier, and the manner of ruling on each is declared together here. First: where the canonical source has a relevant sentence that bears no semantics of current action but expressly allows the item to be absent while Execution still takes place, the item is excluded directly by the criterion of canonical self-attestation of 4.4 (c), without passing through the partition. Second: where M1–M7 contain no relevant sentence at all, the item lies outside the domain; this text takes on no ruling about it, and confirms only that it is not in U\_R and is therefore not R4.

### 8.2 Single-Atom Candidates

First. NetChange: requires that this exercise leave a non-zero net change in the field. It belongs to Consequence on the result side, with its canonical ground in the second half of U15, the ceaseless transformation. The actions of two Laws can cancel on shared variables so that the net change is zero, while each holding is still executed by its own; U4 and U5 do not fail on that account.

Second. OutcomeSuccess: belongs to Consequence on the result side. Chapter 1 has already struck it out independently through the world of two competing Laws. On the canonical side, U2, arriving at Not-Gate, was established as an item of its own in Section Four and goes to Completion, and M1's Execution is borne by U1 and U3.

Third. Dom: belongs to Relation, with its canonical ground in the inter-Law relation of breaking the hold in the first half of U15. Dominance is a comparison between two Laws, not whether this Law is executing its own holding at this moment.

Fourth. NoCounteraction: belongs to Relation. The special check of (d) in Section Five has proved it not independent: writing it into the qualification for current action would make the second layer of CurAnti in The Principle Group-Form never hold.

Fifth. Causal necessity and but-for causation: require that the matter would not happen without L. Excluded by R2. R2 is a relation of bearing, not a counterfactual relation, and the canonical "being executed" asks only whether this holding is executed by its executor. Under redundant bearing the but-for test necessarily fails, while the exercise really does take place.

Sixth. Sole causation: requires that L be the sole producer. Excluded by R2, which does not require exclusivity.

Seventh. ActivationSource: belongs to Source, with its canonical ground in U6, origin of driving, and U8, start. M4 places the origin of driving outside Execution; the canonical source itself does not count it as part of Execution.

Eighth. Autonomy: Chapter 1 has already declared that autonomy is a property orthogonal to the causal trajectories of ℛ/𝒰. M1–M7 say nothing at all to the effect that Execution must be autonomous; by the second case of 8.1 it lies outside the domain: no ruling is taken on it, and it is only confirmed that it is not in U\_R and is therefore not R4.

Ninth. Extra Duration: belongs to Persistence, with its canonical ground in U10, duration. If Type itself requires window or measure, only the evaluation window required by the original Type is observed.

Tenth. Current-Ω: belongs to Qualification, with its canonical ground in U11, Law—repetition. ΩSupport already covers re-walking on the side of qualification.

Eleventh. FutureTrace: belongs to Consequence, with its canonical ground in the second half of U15. What is generated after the exercise is not whether the Law is acting at this moment.

Twelfth. Recognition: there is no corresponding atom for recognition — the part of M2 on knowing and not being clear bears no semantics of current action and, by 4.1, did not enter this domain. But that sentence expressly allows Execution to go unknown in This-World and still be Execution: "Know it so, not know why so … Know Information, yet not clear." By the first case of 8.1, it is therefore excluded directly by the criterion of canonical self-attestation of 4.4 (c), without passing through the partition.

The rulings on the sixteen items thus fall into three types: items five, six, fifteen, and sixteen are absorbed by R1–R3; items one, two, three, four, seven, nine, ten, eleven, thirteen, and fourteen each have a registered atom as carrier and are classified by that atom's zone; item eight lies outside the domain, and item twelve is excluded by canonical self-attestation.

Fourteenth. Final arrival: belongs to Completion. U2 was established as an item of its own in Section Four and goes to this class.

Fifteenth. Irreplaceability: requires that L cannot be replaced by another structure. Excluded by R2; it is a realization-side variant of but-for causation, and Chapter 1 expressly allows redundancy, cooperation, and multiple carriers.

8.3 The Check of Canonical Warrant for Candidates Formed by Conjunction

Let

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;X = ⋀\_k U\_k

be an irreducible conjunction of atoms within the frozen domain — remove any member, and X is no longer the condition originally claimed.

The criterion is canonical warrant: whether an item of content is a condition of current realization is decided by whether the canonical source assigns that duty to it. A conjunction is not in itself a new canonical atom. The fifteen atoms are already all the carriers in M1–M7 of the semantics of current action, and writing two registered atoms together does not produce an additional canonical sentence. For a conjunction to be raised to a condition, the canonical source must expressly bear that combination, too, as a semantic fact.

That A is compatible with R and B is compatible with R does not imply that A ∧ B is compatible with R. So joint compatibility is not inferred here from the separate compatibility of the components, nor are all subsets of the ten items of U\_O verified one by one.

On that basis, three classes.

Class one: X contains a member of U\_R. The content of current realization carried by that member is already borne by one of R1, R2, R3. What X adds relative to the three items consists only of its U\_O members; for these to raise X, the canonical source would have to assign them expressly a new duty of current realization — there would have to be a sentence stating that this atom together with that atom determines, as a further matter, whether something is being executed at this moment. M1–M7 contain no such warrant: the primary duty each sentence assigns to each atom has been registered item by item in Section Five, and neither any single sentence nor any relation across sentences sets up the combination of some group of U\_O members as that on which current realization depends.

Class two: X comes entirely from U\_O. Each member has already been registered, by its primary canonical semantics, as one of Qualification, Source, Propensity, Degree, Persistence, Completion, Formation, Recognition, Relation, Consequence, InstanceForm. For X to be raised, there would have to be canonical ground showing that the combination of these non-realizing semantics itself directly determines the current truth value of LawAct. M1–M7 contain no such joint warrant: M6 explains the difference among the three; M7 states holding and the pursuit of breaking the hold; M4 places the origin of driving outside Execution; M5 lists the phases of start, work, stop, and duration. Each sentence assigns its atoms duties of qualification, relation, source, or phase, and none designates the combination of any group of them as that on which Execution depends.

Class three: X is claimed to give rise to a new content that none of its members has. By definition that content lies within no U\_k; since the fifteen atoms are all the carriers in M1–M7 of the semantics of current action, that content lies outside the semantics of current action of M1–M7 as well, and is outside the domain. Content outside the domain is not ruled on: it is neither admitted as R4 nor denied to exist.

The three classes exhaust all candidates formed by conjunction. No irreducible conjunction of atoms within the frozen canonical domain has canonical warrant to bear a new duty of current realization independent of R1, R2, and R3.

### 8.4 Theorem One

Theorem One. Within the frozen canonical domain M1–M7, there is no fourth condition of current-action realization, independent of R1, R2, and R3, borne by its semantic units or by an irreducible conjunction warranted by the canonical source.

Proof. U\_LAct has been frozen at fifteen atoms; they have been sorted item by item, each to exactly one zone; and U\_R has been proved to reduce to three items with nothing remaining.

Suppose a candidate condition X is proposed as a fourth item.

If the semantics of X has a ground in M1–M7 and bears the semantics of current action, its carrier is either a single atom or an irreducible conjunction.

If its carrier is a single atom: by the sorting of Section Five, that atom is either in U\_R, in which case X is already absorbed by R1–R3, or in U\_O, in which case X does not belong to the qualification for current action. The sorting is exhaustive and no atom straddles zones or is left over. 8.2 verified this classification item by item for sixteen common candidates, without exception.

If its carrier is an irreducible conjunction: the three classes of 8.3 are exhaustive. In class one, X contains a member of U\_R, which is already absorbed by R1–R3, and its other members have no canonical warrant conferring a new duty of current realization. In class two, X comes entirely from U\_O; the frozen canonical source does not warrant that conjunction to bear new semantics of CurrentRealization, so it cannot be raised to R4 by the operation of conjunction. In class three, X is claimed to give rise to new semantics, which is not within the fifteen atoms and lies outside the domain.

If the semantics of X has a ground in M1–M7 but bears no semantics of current action, then by the first case of 8.1 it is either excluded by the canonical self-attestation of 4.4 (c), or confirmed not to state whether something is being done at this moment; neither makes X an R4.

If the semantics of X has no ground at all in M1–M7, then X is not within the frozen canonical domain.

Hence within M1–M7 there is no independent R4, either among the units or among the irreducible conjunctions warranted by the canonical source. ∎

The conclusion is confined to the frozen M1–M7. Theorem One does not assert that human language cannot contain other concepts of action, does not assert that there is still no R4 after the canonical source is upgraded, and does not assert that there is no R4 in any theoretical tradition that has not entered this canonical domain. Should the canonical domain be upgraded with new sentences bearing the semantics of current action, Theorem One is re-examined in step.

Section Nine

The Check for Independent Counter-Realization

### 9.1 Setting

Theorem One deals with whether a condition of realization is still missing. Here the other direction is dealt with: with all the conditions of realization complete, is there still something else that can cancel it?

Suppose R1 = R2 = R3 = 1 and Binding = 1, and ask whether there exists an independent D ∈ U\_D such that LawAct = 0.

The criterion is that of 4.4 (d): if the occurrence of some factor necessarily makes some atom of current realization fail, it is not independent, and it goes to its own class rather than to U\_D. Only something that can still veto on its own while all three items hold is an independent disqualifier.

### 9.2 Class-by-Class Tests

First. Cancelled by another Law. The action of another Law cancels L's action on shared variables. L's holding is still executed by L's realization; the three items are unchanged. The cancellation takes place on the side of results, not on the side of bearing.

Second. Net change zero. A zero net change is the sum of two non-zero bearings, not the absence of bearing.

Third. Not dominant. Dom belongs to Relation. Not dominating and not acting are two different matters.

Fourth. Full compensation. The compensating party and the compensated party are each acting, and each holding is executed by its own.

Fifth. Redundant bearing. Several components of L bear jointly, or bear in parallel with other structures; Definition 5 of Chapter 1 expressly allows redundant carriers and minimal sufficient groups.

Sixth. Replaceable. That some other structure could produce the same read-out is a variant of but-for causation. R2 is a relation of bearing and does not fail because a replacement exists.

Seventh. External start-up. It belongs to Source. M4 has already placed the origin of driving outside Execution.

Eighth. Not autonomous. M1–M7 contain no relevant sentence at all; by the second case of 8.1 it lies outside the domain.

Ninth. Currently weak. It belongs to Degree. Acting weakly is still acting.

Tenth. No new sedimentation left behind. It belongs to Consequence.

Eleventh. No Recognition. There is no corresponding atom for recognition; M2 expressly says that Execution can go unknown in This-World, and by 4.4 (c) it is excluded by canonical self-attestation.

Twelfth. Stopping immediately at the next moment. It belongs to Completion. Acting at this moment and stopping at the next are compatible; if the stop has already occurred at this moment, then there is no actual running at this moment, and R1 fails with it. It takes effect by way of R1 dropping out; it is not an independent disqualifier.

Thirteenth. No final arrival. It belongs to Completion.

Of the preceding thirteen classes, all except the twelfth leave the truth value of each of R1, R2, and R3 unchanged.

9.3 The Check of Canonical Warrant on the Side of Counter-Realization

Let

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;D = ⋀\_k U\_k

be an irreducible conjunction claimed to be able to make LawAct = 0 while the three items and Binding all hold.

For D to be an independent condition of counter-realization, two things must hold together: first, R1 = R2 = R3 = Binding = 1 still holds; second, the canonical source expressly assigns to that combination the duty D ⇒ LawAct = 0. The second is where the load falls: the power to cancel is conferred by the canonical source, not produced automatically by writing several conditions together. Here too, that no member cancels on its own is not used to infer that the conjunction does not cancel.

Checking the frozen canonical source gives four findings.

First. No single atom bears such a cancellation. Section Five sorted the fifteen atoms item by item, and every apparent veto passed the special check of (d); none fell into U\_D. 9.2 further tested thirteen classes of common disqualifying candidates, class by class.

Second. No sentence declares the combination of registered atoms to be a condition that cancels Execution. Where the canonical source speaks of Execution failing to come about, what it states is not working, not being executed, not making-so — that is, the absence of the respective U\_R atoms — not that some combination of U\_O holds.

Third. Wherever a combination really can cancel, it takes effect by making some atom of current realization drop out. If a conjunction cancels Execution by way of facts such as stopping, not being executed, or not making-so, then either there is no actual running at this moment, or what is executed is not L's holding, or the duty is not carried through. In each case the corresponding one of the three items no longer holds, contrary to the assumption. By criterion (d), this is not independence. Class twelve of 9.2 is an instance of this case.

Fourth. Wherever the cancelling semantics is not within the fifteen atoms, it lies outside the domain. Since the fifteen atoms are all the carriers in M1–M7 of the semantics of current action, that semantics is not within the semantics of current action of M1–M7. As with class three of 8.3.

With the four findings checked, all candidates formed by conjunction are exhausted. No irreducible conjunction of atoms within the frozen canonical domain has canonical warrant to bear the duty of cancelling LawAct independently while the three items and Binding hold.

### 9.4 Theorem Two

Theorem Two. Within the frozen canonical domain M1–M7, U\_D = ∅: among its semantic units and the irreducible conjunctions warranted by the canonical source there is no item that can by itself make LawAct = 0 while R1, R2, R3, and Binding all hold.

Proof. Section Five sorted the fifteen atoms item by item, and every apparent veto passed the special check of (d); none fell into U\_D.

Section 9.2 tested thirteen classes of common disqualifying candidates, class by class: twelve of them leave the truth value of each of the three items unchanged and are therefore not disqualifiers; the remaining one, when it really takes effect, appears as R1 dropping out and is therefore not independent.

Section 9.3 checked the irreducible conjunctions against four findings: no single atom bears such a cancellation; the canonical source has no sentence declaring the combination of registered atoms a condition that cancels Execution; if D comes entirely from U\_O, the frozen canonical source does not warrant that conjunction to bear independent semantics of cancellation, so it cannot be raised to IndependentDisactualization by the operation of conjunction; wherever a combination really can cancel, it takes effect by making some atom of current realization drop out, and by (d) is not independent; and wherever the cancelling semantics is not within the fifteen atoms, it lies outside the domain.

Hence there is no unit, and no conjunction warranted by the canonical source, that can still veto on its own while the three items hold. ∎

Theorem Two does not assert that nothing can make L not acting. What it asserts is that whatever really can make L not acting takes effect by making one of R1, R2, or R3 drop out, not by some independent cancelling predicate over and above the three items holding.

The conclusion is confined to the frozen M1–M7.

By (i), the witnesses of R1 and R2 are the witnesses of CurrentLawOccurrence; by (ii), the witness of R3 is the witness of DutyConformance. The Boundary of Formal Read-Back, and Interfaces

### 15.1 The Boundary of Formal Read-Back

Formal objects serve only as an executable read-back afterwards and bear none of the weight of ontological proof.

The ontological side: Theorems Three and Four use throughout the object-layer definitions of Chapter 1 and the canonical classification of this text.

The formal side: when an adjudication is executed on a concrete field state, the formal read-out of CurLawBear is obtained by the read-back interface and three-valued treatment of Section 2 of the Appendix of Chapter 1. When MixedMode(L, i) = 0, it is adjudicable; when MixedMode ∈ {1, ⊥}, the corresponding read-back is recorded as ⊥, only non-adjudicability may be reported, and LawAct may not on that basis be adjudicated as holding or failing.

Three-valued non-adjudicability is a matter of the formal layer and does not affect Theorem Four at the ontological layer: ontologically, CurLawBear = 1 gives LawAct = 1, irrespective of whether it can be computed.

### 15.4 Breach

The breach modes are as the specification of the necessity form prescribes: construct a counterexample in which the condition group holds and the result does not occur.

Breach one (the canonical domain). Exhibit an atom in the canonical M1–M7 that bears the semantics of current action and is not included in the total set of Section Four, or show an error in the sorting of Section Five.

Breach two (R4). Exhibit a candidate condition whose semantics has a ground in M1–M7 — some atom, or an irreducible conjunction warranted by the canonical source — and which can neither be absorbed by R1–R3 nor be placed in any of the eleven classes of U\_O. If it is a conjunction, it must be shown that the canonical source does have a sentence bearing that combination's additional semantics of current realization.

Breach three (counter-realization). Exhibit a factor — a single atom or an irreducible conjunction — that makes LawAct = 0 on its own while the three items and Binding all hold, and whose way of taking effect does not appear as any of the three items dropping out. If it is a conjunction, it must be shown that the canonical source does have a sentence declaring that combination a condition that cancels Execution.

Breach four (structural realization). Exhibit CurLawBear = 1 while one of R1, R2, R3 does not hold, or while the three items do not come from one and the same action.

Not breaches: concepts of action outside the canonical domain, on which this text has declared that it takes no ruling; instances whose formal read-back is recorded as ⊥, which are formal non-adjudicability; a breach of Chapter 1, which would make the working domain fail and belongs to the propagation of conditions.

### 15.5 The Boundary with Later Texts

This text settles only one thing: when the two layers of current bearing already suffice to be called LawAct.
