# The General Form of Law-Dynamics

What follows attempts one general proposition:

To admit the fact that "a Law really moved during this period" necessarily means this: there exists in the field a trajectory segment that is legal, whose target is fixed, whose qualification is preserved, and whose evaluation protocol was frozen in advance; and within that segment the Law-side facts of this Law really undergo a readable shift.

That is to say: the dynamics of a Law is not the difference between read-outs at two moments, but something within a segment. It first requires that the segment be real, and then requires that something really happen within it. Lacking either, what is called dynamics is either two matters set side by side or time spinning idly.

The division of labour between this text and its neighbours:

The General Form of Law-Action deals with current action at a single moment — for an already standing Law to be acting at this moment, it must minimally have an actual current occurrence attributable to itself, discharged according to the original duty semantics, the two layers coming from one and the same realization; The Necessity Form of Law-Action supplies the converse, and the two directions together close current action ontologically in both directions.

The Grading Form of Law deals with degree at a single moment — the comparison of familiarity goes by the Sedimentation-Degree δ̄ alone (the familiarity-order domain J\_δ); the Degree profile must be read, within a frozen comparison protocol, as four coordinates from the real load-bearing path (the profile domain J\_D), and what is obtained is a partial order, not a ranking.

What those three texts settle all concerns a single moment. This text deals with what spans moments: when may one say only that "this Law existed during this period," and when may one say that "this Law had dynamics during this period"?

The division of labour with earlier and later texts: how a Law comes to stand belongs to The General Form of Law and The Necessity Form of Law; where a Law comes from, how it migrates, and how it is rebuilt belong to The Source Form of Law; whether it is still the same Law after its carriers have been replaced belongs to the Law-identity texts; whether some dynamical state can be reached from a given initial state belongs to The Reachability Form of Law; how two Laws interact belongs to the Law Interaction texts. As for who in fact contributed the dynamics of a given change, its semantics is registered in the Appendix and its formalization is handed down to The Mathematical Form of Law-Dynamics — contribution is not a qualification for dynamics, as Section 10 proves.

Only the destinations are marked here; the specific matters each text takes up, and the substantive distinctions among them, are given in the section on interfaces.

The result first.

The general form of the minimal structure of Law-dynamics

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;LawDyn^η\_F(L; I, Γ\_dyn) = 1  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;⟺　LegalLawSegment^η\_F(L; I, Γ\_dyn) = 1  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;∧　DynActual^η\_F(L; Γ\_dyn) = 1

where

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;DynActual = ActDyn ∨₃ DegDyn

The first item is a legal Law-dynamics segment; the second is dynamical actuality, which is in turn composed, by three-valued disjunction, of two projections, the side of action and the side of degree.

The above is a result yet to be proved. It is not a premise of any round of questioning below. The starting points of the sections below are only the entry condition, the frozen upstream, and what has already been forced out; the name of each item appears only after its failure world has been displayed.

In One Sentence

That read-outs differ at two moments does not mean that a road was walked in between; that the road was walked does not mean that anything happened along it.

Abstract

The General Form of Law-Action and The Necessity Form of Law-Action have already shown whether a Law is acting at this moment, and The Grading Form of Law how familiar it has become and what its Degree profile is. All three are read-outs at a single moment.

Put the read-outs of two moments side by side, and there seems to be change. What this text asks is: on what grounds does such "change" count as the dynamics of this Law?

The questioning displays failure worlds one after another. Two endpoints come from two independent experiments; the read-outs really differ, and no road connects them — this forces out one and the same legal trajectory segment. The trajectory is legal, but midway the Law has died once and come to stand again — this forces out preservation of qualification within the segment and a fixed target. After the result is seen, the sampling resolution is changed, so that an interruption is read as persistence — this forces out an evaluation protocol frozen in advance. The three together make the first item.

The segment is legal, the qualification is there, time has passed, and yet the Law is at rest throughout and its Degree profile is Degree-equal before and after — if this counted as dynamics, any standing Law would have dynamics over any period. This forces out the second item: within the segment there must really be Law-side actual dynamics.

This actual dynamics is read in two places, and in both it must be read step by step along the segment, not reduced to the difference between first and last. The side of action: read the rest, Start, Continue, and Stop of current action, pair by adjacent pair along T\_η. The side of degree: compare the Rise, Fall, Degree equality, and Migration of the Degree profile, pair by adjacent pair along the same T\_η. Reducing to first and last has a counterexample on each side — on the side of action, an interruption with the Law acting at both ends; on the side of degree, a rise followed by a fall with first and last Degree-equal — and both are wrongly adjudicated as nothing happening.

The two cannot be reduced to each other: a world at rest while the Degree profile changes shows the side of action insufficient; a world of continued exercise with no change of profile shows the side of degree insufficient. So the composition takes a disjunction.

Both sides must also be three-valued: the formal read-out of current action can be non-adjudicable, and so can a read-out of degree, and a point that cannot be adjudicated may not be deleted from the frozen set of samples because it cannot be read. So where one side determinately has dynamics and the other has a step that cannot be read, the unknown may not wash away the dynamics already determined; where one side determinately has none and the other cannot be read, the unknown may not be written as no dynamics either; where neither side can be read, the result is undecided.

The three questions that follow force out no new item: binding to one segment is a discipline of well-formedness, not a third conjunct; the replacement of a qualification witness has already been assigned upstream to stability and to identity; dynamical contribution is another question, and zero contribution does not imply zero dynamics.

The core conclusion: qualification says that this Law is in the field; current action says what it is doing at this moment; the Degree profile says what its degree on the load-bearing path is; and dynamics says what, over a stretch of road, actually happened to it — four questions, none of which may answer for another.

The Antecedent Discrimination

Three words must be fixed before they bear load, or the failure worlds below will be read awry.

Dynamics. What this text calls dynamics is that, within a legal trajectory segment, the Law-side facts of this Law really undergo a shift. It is not a force in the field, not a potential, not a weight, and not a causal variable.

This must be stated firmly, because the five objects this text newly introduces — LegalLawSegment, ActDyn, DegDyn, DynActual, LawDyn — are all readings and adjudications of existing field states; none is a new field quantity. They do not enter the dynamical equations of the field, take no part in the allocation of flow, and act on no quantity. To write LawDyn as something in the field that can drive other quantities is to read an adjudication as a cause.

Should it later really be necessary to place some quantity related to the conclusions of this text in the dynamical equations, it must be established separately by The Mathematical Form of Law-Dynamics, and the problem of double counting must be dealt with — the four Degree coordinates and the read-outs on the side of action are already determined by the institutions of the Field Chapters and the preceding three texts, and if the adjudications of this text were in turn made to drive their inputs, one and the same dynamics would be counted twice.

Change. What this text calls change is not the difference between the read-outs at two endpoints. Section 1 excludes that reading with the world of juxtaposition; Sections 5 and 6 further exclude the reading "compare only the two ends of T\_η" with worlds of skipped steps. Wherever change is spoken of, it means a shift between some pair of adjacent evaluation points within the frozen T\_η.

Continue. What this text calls Continue is a conclusion relative to the protocol; it asserts only that the Law is acting at both ends of the corresponding adjacent step. It does not assert that there is no interruption between those two points at a finer scale. Section 5 has a paragraph devoted to this.

One further distinction, placed here because it runs through the whole text: the dynamics of this Law and the dynamics provided by this Law are two different matters. The former asks what happened to it; the latter asks to whose account a given occurrence should be credited. Section 10 proves, with the world of external start-up, that the latter being zero does not imply the former being zero; it is marked here in advance, so that "dynamics" in each of the first ten sections is not read as "what it caused."

Introduction

Once a Law stands, time goes on.

After a while we look back and say that this Law "changed" or "did not change." The sentence sounds natural; in fact it makes nothing clear.

What changed? Did it go from not moving to moving, or was it moving all along without stopping? Did its Degree profile change, or did other roads in the field win more flow so that it seems to have receded? Or did nothing at all happen to it, except that the structure bearing it was exchanged for another batch?

More important still: on what grounds is this "its" change? Take the read-outs at two moments, subtract, and call the difference change — yet the two read-outs may well come from two unrelated experiments, with no road at all connecting them. That is not change; it is juxtaposition.

Conversely, identical read-outs need not mean no dynamics. A Law may be exercising its duties throughout the period, the read-out at both ends being "acting," the difference between the endpoints zero — did nothing happen to it during this period?

Hence a fundamental question:

on what grounds can one say that an already standing Law has dynamics during this period?

This text proposes that dynamics is not the difference between endpoints but something within a segment. It first requires the segment, and then requires that within the segment there really be readable Law-side actual dynamics. Neither may be missing, and neither may stand in for the other.

The Line of Argument

We proceed by sustained questioning, one step at a time; the argument therefore forms a line, and is to be read from the top down.

1

Ask first at the shallowest level: take the read-outs of one Law at two moments; the read-outs differ — is that its dynamics?

If it is, then the following world also has dynamics.

Under one and the same field specification, run two independent experiments. The first runs to some moment and reads the Law at rest; the second starts from another initial state, runs through again to some moment, and reads it acting. The two read-outs really differ, and each experiment is legal in itself.

Yet there is nothing between the two read-outs. No trajectory runs from the first read-out to the second; no state update carries the former into the latter. The so-called "from rest to acting" is two unrelated photographs laid side by side, with the claim that a change happened in between.

That is not change; it is juxtaposition. If juxtaposition counted as dynamics, then any two read-outs on any two independent trajectories could be used to claim that some Law has dynamics — dynamics could be manufactured by selection.

Hence: real Law-dynamics is necessarily not the difference between the read-outs at two endpoints.

The "segment" in question must be fixed here first.

Definition 1: legal trajectory segment

Let Spec\_F be the field specification, Init the initial state, and Prot the protocol of state update. Take

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;τ ∈ Traj(Spec\_F, Init, Prot)

a legal field trajectory, and take its restriction to an interval

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;η = τ\|\_{\[t₀, t₁\]}

A legal trajectory segment is not a pairing of two moments but a stretch of one and the same legal trajectory: every state update within it follows the existing protocol, and each earlier state is really carried into the later.

Hence: dynamics is not "a read-out here and a read-out there," but "what happened within this segment."

2

Since dynamics must fall within one and the same segment, ask again: with a legal trajectory segment, is that enough?

It is not. A legal τ does not guarantee that what is discussed within the segment remains one and the same Law.

Consider this world. τ is legal, and every update within the segment follows the protocol. But shortly after t₀, the carrier of this Law collapses completely, and neither Established nor Govern holds any longer — by The General Form of Law, Law\_{F,s}(L, I) = 0 at that point; it is no longer a Law. Later a new structure accumulates in the field and, at time t₁, again satisfies the conditions of Law-formation.

The read-outs at both ends are there, and a legal trajectory between them is there too. But what this segment records is the death of one Law and a further standing, not the dynamics of one and the same Law. To count it as dynamics is to read "died and was born again" as "changed."

Consider a more covert world. Within the segment Law holds throughout, but the fixed applicability-cover domain I is swapped midway — the first half read with I₁, the second with I₂. The read-outs at the two ends are each legal, but they are not two answers to the same question.

There is one more kind: rewriting by hand. Someone alters the sediment directly midway through the segment and then treats the read-outs before and after the alteration as evolution. The trajectory looks continuous on the surface, but in fact the state update of some step does not come from the protocol.

The three worlds fail at the same point: the legality of the segment ensures only that "there is a road," not that "it is this Law all along the road."

Hence: real Law-dynamics must further satisfy that within the segment the target is fixed and qualification is preserved.

"It is this Law all along" must be stated exactly, so as not to take in advance, by wording, the answer that belongs to later texts. What is fixed here is the target reference L, I declared in advance by Γ\_dyn, together with its upstream semantics; what is required is that this reference not be swapped within the segment, and that what it refers to satisfy the conditions of Law-formation throughout the segment. Whether, after migration and replacement of carriers, what is referred to is still the same Law in the ontological sense, this text does not prove — that is the task of the Law-identity texts (at present only Chapter 8, The Distinction Form of Law-Identity Pairings, is sealed, and no closed criterion of Law identity has yet been established). Wherever "it is this Law all along the road" is said below, it means the former and does not touch the latter.

3

But even with the target fixed and qualification preserved within the segment, the following can still happen.

Read at one resolution and obtain an unwelcome result; read again at another resolution; repeat until the desired result is read.

The failure lies here: the conclusion read out is decided not by what happened in the segment but by the choice of reading. For one and the same trajectory segment, as long as the reading may be changed after the fact, both persistence and interruption can be read out.

This must be stated exactly, so as not to prohibit legitimate coarse reading along with it. A protocol with only two evaluation points, if frozen in advance, yields legitimate conclusions — they hold only relative to that resolution, and assert nothing about what happened between the two points at a finer scale. Coarse reading is not wrong; changing the reading after the fact is.

Hence: real Law-dynamics must further satisfy that the protocol of temporal evaluation is frozen in advance.

The three failures — no road to walk, the road not being this Law, the reading adjustable after the fact — exclude one and the same thing: letting someone, after seeing the result, manufacture a stretch of dynamics that does not exist by selecting endpoints, selecting objects, or selecting readings. The three together make the first item.

Definition 2: legal Law-dynamics segment

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;LegalLawSegment^η\_F(L; I, Γ\_dyn) = 1

if and only if the following five items hold together:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;First: there exists τ ∈ Traj(Spec\_F, Init, Prot) with η = τ\|\_{\[t₀, t₁\]};  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Second: L and I, as the target reference, are fixed within the segment and not swapped midway;  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Third: ∀ s ∈ \[t₀, t₁\]: Law\_{F,s}(L, I) = 1;  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Fourth: every state update within the segment follows Prot, with no rewriting from outside the segment;  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Fifth: the protocol of temporal evaluation T\_η is frozen before the segment begins, and may not be changed during or after the segment.

Γ\_dyn is this text's protocol of dynamical evaluation; it declares at least how τ is taken, the bounds of \[t₀, t₁\], the set of sampling points of T\_η, and the Γ\_D inherited. It is an auxiliary symbol, not a field quantity.

T\_η must satisfy the condition of well-formedness:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;T\_η = ( t₀ \< t₁ \< ⋯ \< t\_n ),　n ≥ 1

that is, strictly ordered, with at least two evaluation points.

This may not be omitted. Were T\_η allowed to contain a single point, there would be no adjacent step at all within the segment; and the zero grades of Definitions 4 and 5 are both conditioned on "every adjacent step is at rest" or "every adjacent step is determinately Degree-equal." A universal proposition over the empty set is vacuously true, so a single-point protocol would read out ActDyn = 0 and DegDyn = 0, and hence adjudicate that the whole segment has no dynamics. One point is not enough to adjudicate anything across time, yet it would give a definite conclusion through an empty quantifier — writing "nothing read" as "read out as nothing." So two points is the minimum requirement of well-formedness for a judgment across time.

The number of points is otherwise unrestricted: any finite number of points will do, provided it is frozen in advance.

A line must be drawn here against The Boundary Form of Law. What the third item requires is that qualification not exit within the segment. If within the segment Law really undergoes

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Law : 1 → 0

that is not a Stop in this text; it is the death of the Law, and belongs to The Boundary Form of Law.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;LawAct Stop ≠ Law death

The former is a Law that still stands ceasing its current exercise; the latter is its ceasing to be a Law. Both read-outs can be written as some quantity going from one to zero, but what changes lies at different layers. Were the two conflated, the task of The Boundary Form of Law would be swallowed by this text, and the working domain of this text would be quietly extended to the layer of qualification.

4

With a legal Law-dynamics segment, is that enough?

It is not.

Consider this world. The segment is entirely legal: one and the same trajectory, the target fixed, qualification preserved throughout, every update following the protocol, the protocol frozen in advance. At every evaluation point within the segment one reads

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;LawAct\_{F,s}(L; ξ\_s) = 0

This Law is at rest throughout, and exercises itself not once. At the same time its Degree profile is completely readable at every point within the segment, and every adjacent step is adjudicated Degree-equal — by The Grading Form of Law,

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;∀ k: G^Γ\_{D,t\_k}(L) ∼\_D G^Γ\_{D,t\_{k+1}}(L)

Its Degree profile never moved from beginning to end. What must be said here is that every step is Degree-equal, not that first and last are Degree-equal; for the difference see Section 6.

Within this segment, nothing happened to this Law.

Yet the segment is legal, and time really did pass. If this counted as dynamics, any standing Law would have dynamics over any legal period — a Law left untouched for a thousand years would count as moving throughout those thousand years, provided they formed a legal trajectory. The concept of dynamics would then spin idly: it would no longer distinguish anything.

The failure is plain: what a legal segment guarantees is only that "this segment is real," not that "something happened within it."

Hence: real Law-dynamics must further satisfy that within the segment there really is Law-side actual dynamics.

This forces out the second item.

Definition 3: dynamical actuality

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;DynActual^η\_F(L; Γ\_dyn)

Dynamical actuality is not time flowing past, nor the legality of the segment, but this: within this segment, the Law-side facts of this Law really undergo an actual shift.

Where it is read is forced out by the next two sections.

5

Dynamical actuality must be read somewhere. Take first the most direct place: current action.

Along one and the same segment η, at every evaluation point of T\_η, read

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A\_s = LawAct\_{F,s}(L; ξ\_s)

By The General Form of Law-Action and The Necessity Form of Law-Action, the value of A\_s is decided by CurLawBear in both directions and is not adjudicated again here.

Between two adjacent evaluation points, the read-outs can go four ways:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;0 → 0　rest  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;0 → 1　Start  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;1 → 1　Continue  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;1 → 0　Stop

Start and Stop are plain shifts. Continue does not look like one — the two ends are the same, and the difference between the endpoints is zero. But to say of a Law that has been exercising its duties throughout a period that nothing happened to it in that period is plainly wrong. So Continue is likewise a kind of actual dynamics, and the kind most easily missed by differencing endpoints.

But Continue requires particular care.

Consider this world. T\_η is frozen in advance at three evaluation points t₀ \< t₁ \< t₂, at which one reads

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A\_{t₀} = 1,　A\_{t₁} = 0,　A\_{t₂} = 1

The Law really did stop once within the segment, and this stop falls exactly on a frozen evaluation point, not hidden in a gap between samples.

Yet if only the two endpoints of T\_η, t₀ and t₂, are compared, one reads A\_{t₀} = A\_{t₂} = 1, and records the whole segment as continuously acting.

The failure is not that the sampling was too coarse — t₁ was within the protocol all along; it was skipped. The "Continue" read out is false within the read-out sequence of this frozen protocol itself.

Hence: dynamics of action cannot be adjudicated from the two endpoints of T\_η; every pair of adjacent evaluation points within T\_η must be read.

This differs from what Section 3 prohibits, and the two must be kept apart. Section 3 prohibits changing T\_η after the fact; this prohibits not using up the frozen T\_η. The former is changing the reading; the latter is reading only half.

One more point requires care. By The Necessity Form of Law-Action, when adjudication is executed on a concrete field state, the formal read-out of CurLawBear can be ⊥ — when MixedMode falls on one or ⊥, only non-adjudicability may be reported, and current action may not be forced to hold or to fail. So there may be points on T\_η at which A\_s cannot be read.

Such a point may not then be deleted from T\_η. T\_η was frozen before the segment, in Section 3; deleting a point after seeing that its read-out is ⊥ is exactly the after-the-fact adjustment Section 3 prohibits — there, changing the resolution to accommodate a conclusion; here, deleting a point to manufacture adjudicability; in both cases altering the set of samples after the result is out.

Hence: dynamics of action must take three values; a point that cannot be read stays in T\_η, and its unreadability is passed on as it is.

Definition 4: dynamics of action

For each pair of adjacent evaluation points (A\_{t\_k}, A\_{t\_{k+1}}) of T\_η = { t₀, t₁, …, t\_n }, if both can be adjudicated, the step falls under one of the four ways: 00 rest, 01 Start, 11 Continue, 10 Stop. If either is ⊥, the step is a non-adjudicable step.

Aggregation over the segment:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;ActDyn^η = 1　if at least one adjudicable adjacent step is 01, 10, or 11;  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;ActDyn^η = 0　if every adjacent step can be adjudicated and every one is 00;  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;ActDyn^η = ⊥　if there is no step already adjudicated as dynamic, and at least one necessary adjacent step cannot be adjudicated.

The order of the three grades is: what is already determined takes precedence. Once a Start, Continue, or Stop has really occurred at one step, the dynamics of action of the segment has been obtained, and does not return to undecided because some point elsewhere cannot be read.

The standing of Continue must be declared: it is a conclusion relative to the protocol.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Continue in ActDyn asserts only that the Law is acting at both ends of the corresponding adjacent step.

It does not assert that there is no interruption between those two points at any finer scale. What reading step by step guarantees is that the frozen T\_η is used up, not that physical detail is exhausted — any finite set of evaluation points has gaps it cannot read.

T\_η is frozen first, and the conclusion holds relative to it; reading again with a finer T′\_η may give a different conclusion, which is another evaluation, not an overturning of this one.

To overclaim it as "physically there is no interruption at any finer scale" is to read a conclusion relative to a protocol as an unconditional physical assertion. This overclaiming is the mirror image of the after-the-fact adjustment excluded in Section 3: there, changing the protocol to accommodate a conclusion; here, raising the conclusion above the protocol.

6

With dynamics of action, is that enough?

It is not.

Consider this world. The segment is entirely legal. Throughout,

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A\_s = 0

This Law exercises itself not once, the side of action is entirely still, and ActDyn = 0.

But its Degree profile changes within the segment. Other paths in the field win more flow, and the flow share of the path that bears this Law falls as a result, the other three dimensions unchanged — by The Grading Form of Law,

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;G^Γ\_{D,t₀}(L) ≻\_D G^Γ\_{D,t₁}(L)

Its Degree profile has fallen. This is not growing shallower: growing shallower means a fall of Sedimentation-Degree, and here δ̄ is unchanged (equally familiar on the familiarity-order domain J\_δ); what falls is the one dimension of flow share.

Is this the dynamics of this Law? It is. Its Degree profile really changed, and changed along this legal trajectory segment. If only the side of action were recognized, this change would have nowhere to go: the side of action is zero throughout, and yet something really happened.

The opposite world is equally useful. Throughout,

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A\_s = 1

This Law is continuously acting, ActDyn = 1; while on the side of Degree every adjacent evaluation step is determinately Degree-equal, DegDyn = 0. If only the side of degree were recognized, a Law exercising its duties throughout the segment would be adjudicated as having no dynamics.

Each of the two worlds deletes one side, and each leaves behind a real lack of one kind.

Hence: dynamical actuality must be read in two places, and the two cannot be reduced to each other.

But before the second place is given, it must first be asked: may the side of degree be read by comparing only the two ends?

It may not. Consider this world.

The segment is entirely legal, A\_s = 0 throughout, and nothing happens on the side of action. Meanwhile Degree follows this course within the segment:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;G\_{t₀} ≺\_D G\_{t₁} ≻\_D G\_{t₂},　and　G\_{t₂} ∼\_D G\_{t₀}

Its profile rises plainly midway, and then falls back to its former degree.

Comparing only first and last gives G\_{t₀} ∼\_D G\_{t₂}, adjudicating no change on the side of degree; combined with ActDyn = 0, the whole segment is adjudicated as having no dynamics.

Yet within this segment it plainly rose and fell. To adjudicate it as having no dynamics is to let differencing first and last undercut exactly the sentence Section 4 set up — "whether anything happened within this segment."

The reasoning here is the same as in Section 5: t₁ was within the frozen T\_η all along; it was skipped; the "no change" read out is false within the read-out sequence of this protocol itself.

So the side of degree, like the side of action, must read every pair of adjacent evaluation points within T\_η, and may not be reduced to the difference between first and last. What The Grading Form of Law provides is an institution for comparing two profiles under one and the same frozen protocol; what spans time it expressly hands down to this text. Covertly reducing what spans time to first and last would do only two points of the task handed down.

Its relativity must likewise be declared: what reading step by step guarantees is that the frozen T\_η is used up, not that the physical evolution of Degree is exhausted. If between two adjacent points there is a rise followed by a fall, this evaluation cannot read it; that is a limit of resolution, not an overturning of this conclusion.

Definition 5: dynamics of degree

For each pair of adjacent evaluation points of T\_η, compare the Degree profiles on the two sides under one and the same Γ\_D; by the adjudication of relations in Section Fifteen of The Grading Form of Law, the step falls under one of five cases:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Rise　G\_{t\_{k+1}} ≻\_D G\_{t\_k}  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Fall　G\_{t\_k} ≻\_D G\_{t\_{k+1}}  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Degree equality　G\_{t\_k} ∼\_D G\_{t\_{k+1}}  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Migration　G\_{t\_k} ∥\_D G\_{t\_{k+1}}  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;non-adjudicable　⊥\_D

Aggregation over the segment:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;DegDyn^η = 1　if at least one adjacent step is a Rise, a Fall, or a Migration;  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;DegDyn^η = 0　if every adjacent step is determinately Degree-equal;  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;DegDyn^η = ⊥　if there is no step already determined as a change, and at least one adjacent step is ⊥\_D.

So the trajectory above — "rising then falling, first and last Degree-equal" — has a Rise at its first step and a Fall at its second, and DegDyn = 1. A zero difference between first and last and something happening within the segment no longer conflict.

The item Migration must be made explicit: ∥\_D is not undecided.

By The Grading Form of Law, it means that a common complete domain of comparison exists, the necessary read-outs have been obtained, the comparisons in both directions have been completed, and the profiles on the two sides are each determinately unable to cover the other. That is a determined result of comparison, a different matter from ⊥\_D, where read-outs are incomplete.

Its source is not limited to one kind. It can be a crossing within a single Degree record — one set of read-outs rising on some of the four dimensions and falling on others; it can be a crossing between different evaluation units — this side dominant on one unit, that side on another; it can also be crossing advantages among members of the front within one unit. The crossing across several duties in The Grading Form of Law is of the second kind.

What is meant in every case is incomparability at the level of the profile, and it may not be narrowed to the single pattern of rising and falling within one four-dimensional vector. This text inherits its original sense and compresses nothing further.

Since it is determined, and the two sides are not Degree-equal, it is a kind of actual dynamics of degree.

Here the objects of The Grading Form of Law are inherited throughout: the four coordinates, the directions of order, ⪰\_D, Front, G^Γ\_D, Γ\_D, the three-valued determination of direction, the distinction between ∥\_D and ⊥\_D. No scalar strength, no Grade, no LawStrength is created, and no weighted overall score is set — all of these are prohibited by Section Twelve of The Grading Form of Law, and that prohibition is inherited along with them, not relaxed because the comparison spans time. The rule of projection is likewise inherited: one may not compare across time on only some of the four dimensions and then raise the result into a conclusion about dynamics of degree.

The two items are out. Ask again: how are they composed?

If they are taken as a conjunction, each of the two worlds of Section 6 is adjudicated as having no dynamics: the one at rest while its profile falls, because ActDyn = 0; the one continuously acting with no change of Degree, because DegDyn = 0. Both contradict what is seen.

So a disjunction is taken.

But a disjunction is not yet enough. Consider this world.

Within the segment A\_s = 0 throughout and every step can be adjudicated; the side of action is determinately at rest, ActDyn = 0. At some adjacent step, however, some evaluation unit of the Degree profile cannot be read — by The Grading Form of Law, the Front of that unit is recorded as ⊥, the step falls under ⊥\_D, and so DegDyn = ⊥.

If ⊥ is treated as 0, the composition gives 0, adjudicating that this segment has no dynamics. Yet whether the side of degree changed was never read at all; writing the unknown as nothing is pronouncing on what is not known.

If ⊥ is treated as 1, the composition gives 1, adjudicating that this segment has dynamics. That is worse: dynamics manufactured out of the unknown.

Look at the other side. At some adjudicable step within the segment there really is a Start or a Stop, ActDyn = 1, while a step elsewhere cannot be adjudicated. Here dynamics is already determined to exist — the shift at that step is a fact read out, and does not vanish because something elsewhere cannot be read. If the whole were recorded as undecided because of ⊥, an adjudication already obtained would be washed away by the unknown.

A world in which neither side can be adjudicated must likewise have somewhere to go: there is a step on the side of action that cannot be read, a step on the side of degree that cannot be read, and no step at which a shift has really been adjudicated. Here one can adjudicate neither that there is dynamics nor that there is none.

What the four worlds force out is one and the same thing: the composition must be three-valued, and the rules of the three values may not be chosen at will.

Definition 6: three-valued composition

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;DynActual^η = ActDyn^η ∨₃ DegDyn^η

where both sides take three values alike:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;ActDyn^η ∈ { 0, 1, ⊥ },　DegDyn^η ∈ { 0, 1, ⊥ }

Rules of composition:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;1 ∨₃ x = 1　(if either side determinately has it, it determinately has it)  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;0 ∨₃ 0 = 0　(if both sides determinately lack it, it determinately lacks it)  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;0 ∨₃ ⊥ = ⊥　(if one side determinately lacks it and the other is unread, undecided)  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;⊥ ∨₃ ⊥ = ⊥　(if both sides are unread, undecided)

With both sides three-valued, the rules become, if anything, tidier: ⊥ is passed on only when neither side takes one.

\[Translator's gloss: ∨₃ is a disjunction and is commutative; the four rules above are stated up to the order of the two sides. So x ∨₃ 1 = 1, and ⊥ ∨₃ 0 = ⊥, which together with the four rules cover all nine combinations of the two three-valued sides.\]

One corollary must be recorded: if the read-out sequence is 1, 1, ⊥, then the earlier step has clearly been a Continue, ActDyn = 1, and it is not washed back into undecidedness because the later step cannot be adjudicated. This is the first rule at work within the segment.

The three values may not be lowered to two. The two routes of lowering them — reading ⊥ as 0, or deleting unreadable points from T\_η — are prohibited by the first rule and by Section 5 respectively.

Consider first a world that looks as if it needs one.

Someone takes a Start from some segment of trajectory τ₁, and a fall of degree from some segment of trajectory τ₂, sets them side by side, and declares them one and the same Law-dynamics.

That of course cannot count. But does it need a new conjunct to block it?

It does not. Definitions 4 and 5 are written as

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;ActDyn^η　and　DegDyn^η

with one and the same η as superscript. ActDyn reads the state of action at all adjacent evaluation points on T\_η; DegDyn likewise reads the relation of Degree profiles, under one and the same Γ\_D, at all adjacent evaluation points on T\_η. Both take one and the same η and one and the same frozen evaluation protocol as parameters.

The thing assembled across trajectories has its two components under different η; it is not a value of any DynActual^η at all — it is not adjudicated false, but lies outside the range of values of the definition.

Hence: binding to one segment is a discipline of well-formedness of DynActual, not a third ontological conjunct.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;LawDyn = LegalSeg ∧ DynActual ∧ ProjectionBind

Writing this extra item looks more careful on the surface; in fact it passes off a requirement of well-formedness as a current fact, and makes DynActual look as though it could take a value apart from η.

Consider a world that looks even more as if it needs one.

The segment is entirely legal and Law holds throughout. Every adjacent step on T\_η can be adjudicated: on the side of action every step is at rest, so ActDyn = 0; on the side of degree every step is determinately Degree-equal, that is,

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;∀ k: G^Γ\_{D,t\_k}(L) ∼\_D G^Γ\_{D,t\_{k+1}}(L)

so DegDyn = 0. By Definition 6, DynActual = 0, and this segment has no dynamics.

It must be written here that every adjacent step is Degree-equal, not that first and last are — a world Degree-equal at first and last but rising and falling midway has DegDyn = 1, and cannot be used to show what this section is to show.

But one thing really did happen within the segment: the witness bearing the Law-forming qualification of this Law was exchanged, from w₁ to w₂. The old one exited, the new one took over, and so qualification was preserved throughout.

That such a world can be constructed must be shown first, or the argument of this section is empty. Degree is read on the load-bearing path; when the witness changes, the path may change with it, and Degree need not stay still. But by The Grading Form of Law, the profile of a whole Law consists of the non-dominated fronts of its evaluation units, and a front is the non-dominated set of read-outs. If w₁ and w₂ have the same Degree read-outs on every unit, the front is unchanged; or, if the read-outs of both are dominated by the same member of the front, then by the theorem that the front preserves order in that text, deleting the dominated changes no covering adjudication, and the front is likewise unchanged. In both cases the bearer changes while the profile is Degree-equal step by step.

One further sentence is needed: even if, in some world, the replacement of a witness does carry Degree with it, that is no reason to set up a third projection — the change is then already read by DegDyn, and there is still no third place to fill. Neither route is open.

Something at the underlying level really did change. Should a third projection be set up for it?

It should not. Look at whose business this is.

The persistence, replacement, failure, and return of a witness is the question of whether an established qualification can be maintained within the domain of permitted change — by Canon Chapter 1, Part Four, Chapter Fifteen, that is exactly the task of The Stability Form of Law, and that text expressly distinguishes the persistence of a fixed object from persistence at the existence layer, and replacement from the persistence of the original object. w₁ being exchanged for w₂ without loss of qualification is exactly what it calls persistence at the existence layer together with replacement of the object.

Whether the witness is still "the same one" is a question of identity, and belongs to the Law-identity texts.

Both places are already secured upstream. Setting up a projection here would swallow the tasks of the stability form and of the Law-identity texts, and swallow them covertly — for it really is a change at the underlying level.

So it is frozen:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;the replacement of a qualification witness does not, in itself, imply LawDyn = 1.

LawDyn takes three values, so this sentence must be stated exactly: the replacement in itself neither makes LawDyn take one nor, by occurring, changes a value already adjudicated by the two projections. In the world above, both projections are determinately zero, so LawDyn is zero, and the replacement does not alter it.

This does not say that the replacement is unimportant, nor that it is not a change. It says that it is not the kind of change asked about here: what is asked is what happened to this Law in its Law-side current facts, and the replacement of a witness takes place precisely on the premise that the current facts do not change — the side of action unchanged, the side of degree unchanged, the bearer changed. The business of bearers already has texts of its own upstream.

A last question: does LawDyn require that this Law itself provided a dynamical contribution?

Consider this world.

At some adjacent step within the segment there is a Start: the A at the two ends of that step goes from 0 to 1, and this Law passes from rest into exercise. What brought about this Start was entirely external — upstream input arrived, other structures changed the tuning, competing flow receded, and so the road of this Law was opened. This Law itself provided no generative contribution to this transition.

If contribution were required, one would have to adjudicate LawDyn = 0 here: this Law had no dynamics during this period.

Yet it plainly went from not moving to moving. To say of a Law that has just begun exercising its duties that it "has no dynamics" is to move the word dynamics away from its object — onto "what made it move."

That is another question.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;the dynamics of L　and　the dynamics provided by L

The two differ. The former asks what happened to this Law; the latter asks to whose account a given occurrence should be credited. A Law can be started externally, altered by tuning, weakened by competition, changed by another Law, without any endogenous contribution of its own — and still something really and truly happened to it.

Hence:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;DynContrib\_L = 0　does not imply　LawDyn\_L = 0

Contribution is not a qualification for dynamics. Its semantics is registered in the Appendix, and its formalization is handed down to The Mathematical Form of Law-Dynamics.

if and only if

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;∧　DynActual^η\_F(L; Γ\_dyn) = 1

Three-valued propagation. Within the working domain where LegalLawSegment^η = 1:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;DynActual^η = 1　⇒　LawDyn^η = 1  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;DynActual^η = 0　⇒　LawDyn^η = 0

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;LegalLawSegment^η = 0

then that candidate segment has not acquired this text's qualification for legal dynamical evaluation. It may not then be recorded as LawDyn^η = 0.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;an illegal object of evaluation　≠　LawDyn = 0 on a legal object

The former does not enter this text's evaluation at all — it lacks one and the same trajectory, or its qualification is not preserved within the segment, or its protocol can be adjusted after the fact, or its T\_η is not well-formed; in short, it is not an object on which dynamics can be adjudicated. Downstream, the two are handled differently: the former requires supplying a segment, supplying a protocol, or changing the object; the latter is a conclusion already obtained. Recording them as one leaves the reader no way of knowing what needs supplying.

This distinction is not a third ontological condition and does not change the principal formula. The principal formula remains a conjunction of two items; what is supplied here are the rules by which the two grades other than one are passed on from the principal formula, and their line against the layer of qualification.

Pressed into its plainest statement:

First there must be a real stretch of road, it must be this Law all along the road, and the reading may not be changed after the fact; then something must really have happened to it along this road — either its exercise Starts, Continues, or Stops, or its Degree profile Rises, Falls, or Migrates. Without the first half, the so-called change is two photographs laid side by side; without the second half, the so-called dynamics is only time passing.

Deletion Item by Item

Deleting LegalLawSegment.

The symmetrical form "keep the other item and delete this one" cannot be used here. Both projections of DynActual take η and its T\_η as parameters: ActDyn reads the A\_s of each adjacent step within T\_η, and DegDyn compares the profiles of each adjacent step within T\_η. That is exactly why the first item cannot be deleted. Were it cancelled, the criterion of DynActual could only fall elsewhere: on two read-outs on two unrelated trajectories, the world of juxtaposition of Section 1; or on a set of samples that can be changed after the fact, the world of chosen readings of Section 3. Neither is "what happened within this segment."

So the first item cannot be deleted, and its non-deletability is not of the same type as that of the second: the latter is the necessity of a parallel conjunct, the former the non-cancellability of a precondition for taking a value. Deleting DynActual. Keep LegalLawSegment: the segment is entirely legal, the target fixed, qualification preserved, the protocol frozen. The second world of Section 6: the side of action has a determined Continue, while every adjacent evaluation step on the side of Degree is determinately Degree-equal. Deleting DegDyn and keeping ActDyn. If only the side of action were recognized, this Fall of the profile would have nowhere to go. So it cannot be deleted.

Substituting the difference between first and last for reading step by step. The General Theorem

The Law-dynamics theorem

With Γ\_dyn frozen, and the sealed conclusions of the preceding three texts and their conditional items inherited as holding:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;LawDyn^η\_F(L; I, Γ\_dyn) = 1  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;⟺　LegalLawSegment^η\_F(L; I, Γ\_dyn) = 1　∧　DynActual^η\_F(L; Γ\_dyn) = 1

Necessary direction. Suppose LawDyn = 1. By Sections 1 through 3, if there were no single legal trajectory segment, or the target and qualification were not preserved within the segment, or the evaluation protocol could be adjusted after the fact, the so-called dynamics would be juxtaposition, or death and birth, or manufactured by sampling, contradicting LawDyn = 1; so LegalLawSegment = 1. By Section 4, if there were no Law-side shift of fact within the segment, what would remain would be only time passing, contradicting LawDyn = 1; so DynActual = 1. ∎

Sufficient direction. Suppose both items are one.

The scope of what is claimed must be made clear first, and not overstated.

The domain of dynamics declared by this text consists of the two projections that the preceding three texts have already sealed and that this text adopts: the side of action and the side of degree. Within this text, dynamical actuality is defined as, and only as, the three-valued disjunction of these two declared projections — this is Definition 6. It is not a universal induction over all possible current facts of Law, but a working domain this text marks out for itself.

What the sufficient direction proves, then, is:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;LegalLawSegment = 1 and DynActual = 1 already satisfy every qualification of what this text calls LawDyn.

Proof. The qualification of LawDyn is given by Definition 7, and its content is exactly the conjunction of the two items. The first holds by assumption. The second holds by assumption: the three-valued disjunction of the two projections takes one, that is, at least one side determinately takes one — either at some adjudicable adjacent step within the segment there really is one of Start, Continue, and Stop, or at some adjacent step the Degree really has one of Rise, Fall, and Migration. With both items in place, by Definition 7, LawDyn = 1. ∎

Two side issues must be explained along with this, lest they be taken for a third ground of negation.

First, binding to one segment is not a condition of fact, so zero cannot be adjudicated on the ground of "not bound" — both projections take η as parameter, and what is assembled across trajectories lies outside the range of values of DynActual^η altogether; it is not adjudicated false (Section 8).

Second, zero contribution does not imply zero dynamics (Section 10).

Restriction of the conclusion. The sufficient direction claims exhaustion only within the domain of dynamics declared by this text.

It does not claim that Law-side current facts are of only two kinds, nor that there is no other change in the field. What it claims is that within the domain of dynamics this text declares, the qualification of LawDyn is exactly those two items, with no third item hidden inside.

Should a third kind of Law-side fact later be brought into the domain of dynamics, that would not be a breach of this theorem but an enlargement of the domain of dynamics — a new projection would have to be declared separately, its order and three-valued treatment given, its irreducibility to the existing two projections proved, and the minimality checks of this text gone through again. This text would then be upgraded.

T\_η: the set of evaluation points, T\_η = ( t₀ \< t₁ \< ⋯ \< t\_n ), n ≥ 1, strictly ordered with at least two points. Frozen before the segment begins; no point may be added, deleted, or changed during or after the segment. Every "step by step" in this text means running through every pair of adjacent points within T\_η.

L, I: the target reference and its applicability-cover domain, fixed within the segment. What is fixed is the reference declared in advance by Γ\_dyn and its upstream semantics; ontological identity after the replacement of carriers across time is not among what this text proves.

Γ\_dyn: the protocol of dynamical evaluation. An auxiliary symbol, not a field quantity, bearing no independent truth value. It declares at least how τ is taken, the bounds of \[t₀, t₁\], T\_η, and the Γ\_D inherited.

Γ\_D: the protocol of Degree comparison, inherited from The Grading Form of Law, its original sense unchanged.

ξ\_s: the current relation instance at time s, inherited from Definition 1 of The General Form of Law-Action, individuated instance by instance under that text's working protocol.

A\_s: the read-out of current action at point s, A\_s = LawAct\_{F,s}(L; ξ\_s), with range { 1, 0, ⊥ }. ⊥ comes from the three-valued boundary of formal read-back set by The Necessity Form of Law-Action; a point may not be deleted from T\_η because its read-out is ⊥.

G^Γ\_{D,t}(L): the Degree profile of the whole Law at time t, inherited from The Grading Form of Law.

LegalLawSegment^η\_F(L; I, Γ\_dyn): a predicate of the layer of qualification, with range { 1, 0 }. Its content is the conjunction of the five items of Definition 2. Taking zero means that the candidate segment has not acquired this text's qualification for evaluation, not zero dynamics on a legal segment.

ActDyn^η(L): dynamics of action, with range { 1, 0, ⊥ }. Aggregated in three grades, by Definition 4, from the four ways of the adjacent steps within T\_η. Existential polarity comes first: once one of Start, Continue, and Stop has really occurred at a step, it takes one.

DegDyn^η(L): dynamics of degree, with range { 1, 0, ⊥ }. Aggregated in three grades, by Definition 5, from the five relations of the adjacent steps within T\_η under one and the same Γ\_D. Existential polarity comes first, as above.

DynActual^η\_F(L; Γ\_dyn): dynamical actuality, with range { 1, 0, ⊥ }, equal to ActDyn ∨₃ DegDyn. Both projections take this one η and this one T\_η as parameters; what is assembled across segments lies outside its range of values.

∨₃: three-valued disjunction: 1 ∨₃ x = 1; 0 ∨₃ 0 = 0; 0 ∨₃ ⊥ = ⊥; ⊥ ∨₃ ⊥ = ⊥. \[Commutative; see the translator's gloss under Definition 6.\]

LawDyn^η\_F(L; I, Γ\_dyn): Law-dynamics, with range { 1, 0, ⊥ }. The condition for taking one is given by Definition 7; taking zero and taking ⊥ follow the three-valued propagation after Definition 7, and are passed on only within the working domain where LegalLawSegment = 1.

The relation between the two items must be read exactly. The general formula is a conjunction, not a causal chain, not a temporal sequence, and not a composition of functions — the first item does not produce the second; it is the precondition for the second to take a value. Between the two projections is a disjunction, not a conjunction, and neither comes first.

The order of the sections is the order of proof; it asserts nothing about the actual order in which things happen in the field.

Appendix: Semantic Boundaries and Handings-Down

This appendix builds no formal system. The main proof is borne by the failure worlds of the Line of Argument; here only the semantic boundaries of several concepts are registered, and where their formalization goes is indicated. None of the working concepts below is an ontological item, and none enters the general formula.

1\. The semantic boundary of Stop

Somewhere within the segment,

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;CurLawBear\_t = 1,　CurLawBear\_{t'} = 0

is recorded as one StopFact.

It must be declared that it is not inferred from the failure of a single witness. By The General Form of Law-Action, current action allows redundant realizations: when one realization exits while another still bears, current action does not stop on that account.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Stop ≠ single witness failure

The correct semantic direction is: on the next beat, every legal current-bearing candidate that could jointly satisfy CurrentLawOccurrence and DutyConformance loses at least one necessary load-bearing point. The working concept is written Cut⁻, the cut set of loss of bearing.

The machine definition of Cut⁻, how the candidate set is taken, the adjudication of covering, and WithdrawContrib are all handed down to The Mathematical Form of Law-Dynamics; only the semantic boundary is written here.

2\. The semantic boundary of DegreeFall

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;G^Γ\_t(L) ≻\_D G^Γ\_{t'}(L)

is recorded as one DegreeFallFact.

It is not the disappearance of a witness, not a fall of all four coordinates, and not necessarily a degeneration of this Law's own structure. The world of Section 6 is an example: increased flow on competing paths makes FlowShare fall, while the other bearing quantities of this Law itself do not change at all.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;DegreeFallFact ≠ RetreatContribution

The attribution of the dynamics of a Fall of the profile must be analyzed separately; the working concept is written LostAdv^Γ, the loss of the old front's advantage and its subsequent coverage. Its mechanization is handed down to The Mathematical Form of Law-Dynamics.

3\. The semantics of dynamical contribution

The basic structure of a contribution of the positive generative type is: a Law-bearing source, plus ActArg^μ, plus GenReach, plus SameLawDynBind. Its sense is: only when the legal, established load-bearing structure of this Law really enters the parameters and chains of successor generation can it be said to have provided a real dynamical contribution to that successor fact.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;DynContrib ≠ sole cause

Joint contribution by several Laws, several paths, and redundancy is allowed.

Its formalization is handed down to The Mathematical Form of Law-Dynamics.

4\. A boundary of Start

When a Start occurs, LawAct\_t = 0. So the source of contribution cannot be required to have CurLawBear\_t already at that moment — a standing Law at rest still has a legal Established and Govern load-bearing structure; if it enters the generation of the next beat, it can form an endogenous contribution to the Start.

So the bearer required on the side of contribution is written EstablishedLawBearer, more general than CurLawBear.

This belongs to the layer of explaining contribution and does not enter the qualification of LawDyn.

5\. The place of OrigCont, Press, and ReCont

The three are concrete realizations of the dynamics of Continuation, not universal necessary qualifications of LawDyn.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;One may not write LawDyn ⇒ OrigCont  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;One may not write Stop ⟺ ¬OrigCont

The reason is type. The type of OrigCont(f, p, t) is flow times position times time; the object of LawDyn is Law times legal trajectory segment. There is at present no proved universal bridge between them.

If in a concrete instance a correspondence "Law-bearing realization → (f, p)" can legitimately be established, the three can serve as the underlying realization in that instance. This is an interface, not an H, and not an ontological antecedent.

Breach

K-LD1 Breach by illegal history. If LawDyn can still be established across trajectories, across specifications, across protocols, or through rewriting by hand from outside the segment, the first item fails.

K-LD2 Breach by empty dynamics. If every adjacent step within the segment can be adjudicated, every step on the side of action is at rest, every step on the side of degree is Degree-equal, and LawDyn can still be established, the second item fails.

K-LD2′ Breach by first-and-last substituted for step-by-step. If some adjacent step within T\_η really has one of Start, Continue, and Stop, or one of Rise, Fall, and Migration, and yet LawDyn = 0 is adjudicated merely because the read-outs at the two ends of T\_η show no difference, then the step-by-step reading of Sections 5 and 6 fails.

This breach targets only steps skipped within the frozen T\_η. What rests on finer changes outside T\_η, between two adjacent evaluation points, is not a breach — that is a limit of resolution; see K-LD4.

K-LD3 Breach of three values. If one of ActDyn and DegDyn is ⊥ and the other 0, and yet LawDyn = 0 is adjudicated, the rule of three-valued composition fails. If some point's read-out is ⊥ and that point is deleted from T\_η so as to make the read-out two-valued, then the frozen protocol of Section 3 and the discipline of keeping points of Section 5 both fail.

K-LD4 Breach of resolution. If Continue or Degree equality is overclaimed as continuity or no change at an infinitely fine physical scale, the declaration of relativity to the protocol in Sections 5 and 6 fails.

The converse likewise: to judge the conclusions of this text invalid because any finite T\_η necessarily has gaps it cannot read is to demand a conclusion relative to a protocol as though it were an unconditional assertion; it is not a breach.

K-LD5 Breach by contamination of the Law boundary. If the exit of Law's qualification within the segment is counted as an ordinary Stop, the division of labour between Section 2 and The Boundary Form of Law fails.

K-LD6 Breach by contamination of stability and identity. If the replacement of a qualification witness is itself set up as a third projection of LawDyn, the freeze of Section 9 fails.

K-LD7 Breach by contamination of contribution. If DynContrib = 0 is used to negate LawDyn, the distinction of Section 10 fails.

K-LD8 Breach by conflating the layer of qualification. If a candidate segment with LegalLawSegment = 0 is recorded as LawDyn = 0 — that is, an object that has not acquired the qualification for evaluation is classed together with a qualified object adjudicated as having no dynamics — then the three-valued propagation of Definition 7 and the line of the layer of qualification fail.

Not breaches: changes outside the segment — not within η, not in the scope; a Degree read-out of ⊥ with the whole recorded as ⊥ — the normal result of three-valued composition; a breach upstream — which would make the working domain fail, and belongs to the propagation of conditions; exhibiting another form of realization of the same LawDyn — what is claimed as necessary is structural difference, not outward form of realization.

The Division of Labour with Later Texts

The Mathematical Form of Law-Dynamics takes up: the formal read-out of LegalLawSegment; the point-by-point adjudication of ActDyn; the computation across time of profiles for DegDyn; DynContrib; Cut⁻; WithdrawContrib; LostAdv; RetreatContrib; and, where necessary, an interface for reading out OrigCont.

The Reachability Form of Law takes up reachability. What is asked here is what happened along a given stretch of road; what it asks is whether, from a given initial state, there exists a legal path to some dynamical state.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;ActualDynamics ≠ Reachability

The Law-identity texts take up identity. The two symbols L and I are fixed here, but whether, after migration and replacement of carriers, it is still the same Law is not proved here.

The Source Form of Law takes up source.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;DynContrib ≠ LawSource

Dynamical contribution says to whose account a given successor fact should be credited; source says from where this Law was formed, copied, migrated, rebuilt, projected, or recovered.

The Stability Form of Law takes up the persistence, replacement, failure, and return of qualification within the domain of permitted change. This is exactly what Section 9 yields.

The Boundary Form of Law takes up the crossing of the truth value of Law between zero and one. This is exactly what Section 2 yields.

The Law Interaction texts take up the relations between Laws. If another Law takes part in the Start, Stop, Rise, Fall, or change of direction of this Law, at most the name InteractionContribution is registered; no formal theory of interaction between Laws is set up.
