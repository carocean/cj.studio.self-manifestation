# The Source Form of Law

Section One

Purpose, Form-Kind, and the Single Task

### 1.1 The Single Task

Fix a Law candidate-qualification object frozen in advance

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;χ\_L = ( L, I, Rel(L), Γ\_judge )

and a source protocol Π\_src.

If, within the declared window of history, this candidate crosses for the first time from formal Law = 0 to formal Law = 1, then the question is: which stretch of actual history bore this first Law-formation; and where the source is not unique, how far at most can theory determine it?

What is studied is only the source of first Law-formation.

### 1.2 Form-Kind

The source form, an auxiliary form, set up as an independent text.

By Canon Chapter 1, Part Four, Chapter Four, the purpose of the source form is to prove the generative source of a structure or label — by what process it is pressed out, rather than by what it is given; its entry conditions are that the structure or label is already established, while its source is still presumed given or external; its standard composition is declaration of the object; exclusion of the presumed source; characterization of the generative process; source theorem; declaration of where the source cannot be uniquely determined; interfaces; its completion conditions are that the source theorem is proven and where the source cannot be uniquely determined is listed explicitly.

Accordingly: Section Three is the declaration of the object; Section Four, the exclusion of the presumed sources; Sections Five through Eight, the characterization of the generative process; Section Nine sets up the core of source; Section Ten gives the source theorems; Section Eleven declares where the source cannot be uniquely determined; Sections Twelve and Thirteen give the modes of generation, conditions, and state; Section Fourteen is breach; Section Fifteen, interfaces.

### 1.3 What Is Not in This Text

Copying, migration, recovery, replacement, and reappearance across carriers are none of them in this text.

Requalification after 1 → 0 → 1 is not in this text either. Two questions must be separated here. If the question is framed as "the original Law is recovered," a criterion of identity is needed first; and Chapter 8 has proved that the two bases of pairing may not pass for each other, while no closed property of Law identity has yet been established, so there is nowhere to begin. If the question is only the renewed qualification of one and the same frozen candidate, a separate task of the source of re-formation can be set up, but this text may not be borrowed to answer it — the Genesis of this text guards only the first time within the window; renewed qualification is not within its sense.

### 1.4 Four Boundaries

Four statements must be pinned down first, and may not be used in reverse anywhere in the text.

First

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;SameCandidateQualification　≠　SameLawIdentity

That in a replay one and the same frozen candidate acquires Law = 1 again does not mean that "the same Law appears again" has been proved. What is the same is the candidate's qualification, not the Law's identity.

Second

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;LawQualification　≠　LawGenesisMode

The formal Law judge rules on whether it is a Law now, not on where it came from. The procedure of adjudicating qualification contains no item of source.

Third

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Law = 1　⇏　FieldBorn

After a direct external edit, Law qualification can still be acquired so long as the formal adjudication holds; and this does not mean that the qualification was generated by sedimentation within the field.

Fourth

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;CounterfactualNecessity　⇏　SourceBearing

That some condition is such that "without it there is no Law-formation" does not automatically show that it is the source of this Law's formation. Background, host, and global running conditions can all have this property without bearing formation.

### 1.5 In One Sentence

Source is not inferred backward from the final state, nor from "delete it and it fails." It requires a stretch of history that actually happened, which both really lies on the chain of formation and bears under legal counterfactuals.

Section Two

The Law Group-Form and The Determination Form of Law (sealed) — formal Law qualification. The Law = 0 and Law = 1 of this text are both obtained by their procedure of adjudication; no simplified version is made.

The Mathematical Form of Law-Dynamics (sealed) — the formal discipline of running segments, actual execution, and the attribution of contribution. The chain of formation of Section Seven is written according to its two indices and its institution of evaluation segments.

Section Three

Declaration of the Object

### 3.1 The Candidate-Qualification Object

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;χ\_L = ( L, I, Rel(L), Γ\_judge )

L is the candidate Law, I its domain of application, Rel(L) its relational duty, Γ\_judge the formal protocol of qualification adjudication.

All four are frozen in advance.

### 3.2 The Source Protocol

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Π\_src = ( χ\_L, X\_0, H\_obs, B^Π, Ops^Π, Dyn^Π, Judge^Π, T\_Π, Gran^Π )

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;X\_0　　　the declared initial state  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;H\_obs　　the ordered history actually observed  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;B^Π　　　the frozen background  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Ops^Π　　the permitted operations of deletion, masking, Mask, suppression, and the like, frozen item by item, with the three things they must have given in 6.1  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Dyn^Π　　the legal Sedimentation-Field dynamics  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Judge^Π　the formal Law judge  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;T\_Π　　　the set of evaluation points, finite, on which alone Judge^Π is evaluated  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Gran^Π　 the granularity of the division of history

All of the above are frozen in advance.

The item T\_Π cannot be omitted. Firstness hangs on it: that τ is the first time is relative to that set of points. Were it not frozen in advance, then by adding or deleting a point after seeing the result one could move where "the first time" lies — add an earlier hit and the first time is not at τ; delete an inconvenient point and what was not the first time becomes the first time.

### 3.3 Adjustment after the Fact Prohibited

After seeing the result, one may not change the granularity of the division of history; may not change the domain of source candidates; may not change what can be deleted; may not change the Law judge; may not add or delete points in the set of evaluation points T\_Π; may not rename a favourable result as the source.

Whatever has had any item adjusted after the result is not among what this text recognizes. The reason is the same as the upstream freezing of the evaluation protocol: if the reading is chosen after seeing the result, the conclusion is given by the one who chooses.

3.4 The Empty-History Control and the Completeness of the Source Domain

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;H\_∅

is the empty-history control: no historical event is kept, while the other declared items of Π\_src are as before.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;SourceDomainIncomplete^Π( χ\_L, τ ) ∈ { 1, 0, ⊥ }

Its sense is: whether the domain of historical candidates declared by the current protocol is insufficient to bear this first Law-formation.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;1　if SuffGenesis^Π( H\_∅ ) = 1, that is, first Law-formation is still obtained with no historical event kept  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;0　if SuffGenesis^Π( H\_∅ ) = 0  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;⊥　if SuffGenesis^Π( H\_∅ ) = ⊥, or H\_∅ does not enter its domain of evaluation

This quantity is a state of adjudication of the source protocol, not the source itself. Where it takes 1, what is declared is that the domain drawn has a leak, not that the source does not exist; for the difference between the two see Theorem Three.

### 3.5 The Event of First Law-Formation

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Genesis^Π\_τ( χ\_L ) ∈ { 1, 0, ⊥ }

Evaluation is carried out only on the frozen set of evaluation points T\_Π, with no universal quantification over continuous time.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Genesis^Π\_τ = 1　　if Judge\_τ = 1 and ∀ t ∈ T\_Π, t \< τ : Judge\_t = 0

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Genesis^Π\_τ = 0　　if Judge\_τ = 0, or ∃ t ∈ T\_Π, t \< τ : Judge\_t = 1

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Genesis^Π\_τ = ⊥　　in the remaining cases

The remaining cases are two: the current Judge\_τ = ⊥; the current is 1 while there is at least one earlier ⊥ and no earlier 1.

The priority of values must also be stated exactly: an earlier 1 suffices to negate firstness, so even if some other earlier point is ⊥, the value is still 0.

The third value cannot be omitted. If the formal qualification at some earlier evaluation point cannot be adjudicated, one cannot say that all before were 0 — non-adjudicable and adjudicated zero are two things, and pressing them into one value takes the former for the latter.

The Law = 1 in this must be obtained along the existing sealed system. "The structure already looks very like a Law," "the groove is already very deep," or "there is already a bearer" may not stand in for formal qualification.

1 → 0 → 1 may not be called the first Law-formation studied by this text. If the question is only the renewed qualification of one and the same frozen candidate, a separate task of the source of re-formation can be set up; only if it is further called "the recovery of the original Law" must the criterion of identity first be settled. See 1.3.

Section Four

Exclusion of the Presumed Sources

This section lists eight failure worlds, excluding one by one eight presumed ways of writing the source. Each world is constructed under the frozen protocol.

4.1 W-S1　Inference Backward from the Final State

Construct two different histories

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;H\_A ≠ H\_B

both of which give

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Law\_τ = 1

Hence

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Law\_τ = 1　⇏　𝔖^Π\_src

that is, the same value of final-state qualification is not sufficient to determine the family of source solutions. No undefined bare Source is used here; for its formal shape see Theorem One.

### 4.2 W-S2　Temporal Precedence

Let h ≺ τ, that is, h occurs before the first hit.

Precedence is not sufficient to infer bearing:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;h ≺ τ　⇏　PossibleSourceCarrier^Π( h ) = 1

where h is an occurrence frozen according to Gran^Π. Many things happen within the window before τ, most of which have nothing to do with this Law-formation.

### 4.3 W-S3　Synchrony or Correlation

Let Corr( h, Genesis ) hold — h co-occurs statistically with the first Law-formation, changes in step with it, or accurately predicts Genesis.

Correlation is not sufficient to infer bearing:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Corr( h, Genesis )　⇏　PossibleSourceCarrier^Π( h ) = 1

Statistical co-occurrence, synchronous change, or accuracy of prediction may not stand in for source. One of the cases the Canon prohibits for the source form is precisely substituting correlation for cause.

4.4 W-S4　The Formal Witness of the Final State

Let R\_τ ∈ 𝒮( F\_τ, L, I ).

It bears only the formal witness of qualification in the final state, and may not be written directly as the historical source.

R\_τ is a formal witness that qualification holds in the final state: it answers by what the final-state qualification obtains formal certification, not how that qualification formed in history.

4.5 W-S5　The Physical Bearing of the Final State

Let q\_τ = PathMap( L, R\_τ ).

It is the bearing of the final state, not the history of its formation. Chapter 8 has already put bearing and identity in separate layers, and the same holds here: where the final state falls does not say how it grew.

### 4.6 W-S6　Illegal Deletion

If the H′ obtained by deleting some event e has

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Replayable^Π( H′ ) = 0

this shows only that the counterfactual itself is illegal, and one cannot infer

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;NecessarySourceCarrier^Π( e ) = 1

A deletion that cannot be executed does not prove the necessity of a source. This is the form at the level of operations of the fourth item of 1.4; its form at the level of minimality is given in 8.1 and 8.3.

### 4.7 W-S7　Background Enabling

The power supply, the host, the global running conditions — without them the whole system cannot work at all.

This shows only that the condition has counterfactual necessity for this Genesis, and counterfactual necessity by itself is not sufficient to make it a source.

One may not distinguish the two by "whether it acts only on this one Law." A local gate, some particular sensor, some input channel can entirely enable only one candidate and still not be its source of formation.

Whether it is a source must still be proved separately: that it actually enters the generating chain, and bears within the protocol-relative minimal sufficient history. So the difference between background enabling and the source of formation lies not in the breadth of the range of action, but in whether both qualifications — the actual generating chain and counterfactual bearing — are obtained together. This agrees with the fourth item of 1.4.

4.8 W-S8　External Installation Passing for Generation within the Field

Directly modifying weights, grooves, sedimentation values, formal witnesses, or final-state structure, so that the formal judge finally returns PASS.

Law = 1 can indeed be obtained in this case, but FieldBorn = 1 may not be written on that basis.

What is excluded is this one step of passing off, not external installation itself. External installation can be the actual source of a first Law-formation — it is, after all, a real occurrence of qualification, and its chain of occurrence can be examined — but it is not generation within the field. Formal qualification and the mode of occurrence must be kept apart: qualification is adjudicated by Judge^Π, the mode by the branch of the generating chain. Section Nine accordingly sets the two kinds of chain side by side.

With these eight excluded, the source must rest on something else. The following four sections characterize its generative process.

Section Five

The Representation of History

### 5.1 History Is an Ordered Finite Object

The actual history must be represented as an ordered object

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;H\_obs = ( e\_1, e\_2, …, e\_n ),　\| H\_obs \| = n \< ∞

and may not be represented as an unordered set. What is represented as a set this text does not recognize as history.

Finiteness is a condition of the protocol, not a technical convenience.

And the finiteness of occurrences does not exhaust it. The operations permitted by 6.1 include deletion, Mask, and suppression; if Mask or suppression can take continuous strengths, then even with a single occurrence there can be

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;H\_1 ≻\_Π H\_2 ≻\_Π H\_3 ≻\_Π …

each sufficient and none minimal. So what is needed is not only the finiteness of the history but the finiteness of the domain of reduction.

Write the frozen domain of reduction

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;ℛ\_Π( H\_obs ) = { H′ \| H′ ⪯\_Π H\_obs }

This text v1.0 requires

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;\| H\_obs \| = n \< ∞　and　\| ℛ\_Π( H\_obs ) \| \< ∞

that is: the occurrences of the history are finite, and the adjudicable schemes of reduction permitted by Π\_src form a finite discrete domain. Mask of continuous strength, suppression of unbounded levels, or other operations that can produce infinite strictly descending chains must first be frozen discretely before they can enter this text.

Whether this item can be checked hangs on the item-by-item freezing of Ops^Π in 6.1: the operations being listed item by item, and their scope of action being confined to occurrences already in H, while occurrences are finite, the schemes of reduction produced by operation sequences of finite length are countable and finite too. If Ops^Π is not frozen item by item, or one of its items takes a continuous parameter, the finiteness of ℛ\_Π cannot be verified.

With the two established, the protocol itself produces no infinite strictly descending chain. If the family of solutions is then empty, the reason is not the lack of minimal elements. Continuous or unbounded histories must first be divided by Gran^Π into finitely many event occurrences.

### 5.2 What Must at Least Be Kept

Every historical occurrence must at least keep its temporal order, its count, its interval of duration, the content that actually occurred, and the object it acted on. These are the common fields.

Beyond these, each branch has its own requirements:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;for the branch of formation within the field　execution, trace, sedimentation, and the connections among the three must also be kept  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;for the branch of external installation　　　 the external editing event, the target of editing, the states before and after editing, and the corresponding readings of the judge must also be kept

Fields that do not exist may not be fabricated to satisfy the format.

The two branches share one and the same ordered framework of history, without forcing the latter to disguise itself as a history of sedimentation. Were the fields of the branch within the field made obligatory for all history, external installation events could not even enter as legal history, while 7.3 recognizes them as a legal generating branch — the two places would collide.

### 5.3 Two Prohibitions

One stimulus and a hundred repetitions may not be written as one and the same element — count is an actual variable of sedimentation, and erasing count erases the weight of formation.

e\_1 → e\_2 and e\_2 → e\_1 may not be taken as one and the same source candidate — change the order and the actual chain of execution changes, and what is sedimented changes with it.

### 5.4 Granularity of Division

The division of history is frozen in advance by Gran^Π. Once the granularity is set, the atoms of events are set; thereafter the granularity may not be changed to obtain some conclusion.

Section Six

Reduction of History and Legal Replay

### 6.1 Protocol-Relative Reduction

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;H′ ⪯\_Π H

holds only if both its positive and its negative sides are present.

The positive side: there exists a finite sequence of operations

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;ω = ( ω\_1, …, ω\_k ),　ω\_i ∈ Ops^Π

applied to H to obtain H′. Every item of Ops^Π must be frozen in advance, and must satisfy three things: first, it acts only on occurrences already in H, not beyond them; second, its effect is to remove that occurrence, or to weaken its action without changing its position in time; third, whether it is carried out, and its result, can be read out by the implementation environment.

What does not meet these three does not enter Ops^Π. Wherever the permitted operations are not frozen item by item, the reductions they produce may not be used to adjudicate minimality — minimality hangs on quantification over all reductions, and if the domain of quantification is not fixed, the quantification has nothing to act on.

The negative side: the H′ obtained must satisfy

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;X\_0 not changed  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;χ\_L not changed  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;the legal dynamics Dyn^Π not changed  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;the Law judge not changed  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;no new event added  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;the original order of the remaining events not changed  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;the final state not covertly supplied

The one clause of the positive side and the seven of the negative side: if any is lacking, the reduction is not legal.

Neither side may be lacking. With the negative side only, what violates none of the seven is not necessarily a reduction — an alteration that is neither a deletion nor a Mask can violate none of them; with the positive side only, the operation may be legal while what it changes spills outside the history.

Strict reduction is written

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;H′ ≺\_Π H

6.2 Minimality Is Relative to This Partial Order

Every "minimal" hereafter is relative to ⪯\_Π. Ordinary set inclusion may not stand in for it — set inclusion ignores order, and order is exactly the component of history established in 5.3.

### 6.3 Legal Replay

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Replayable^Π( H ) ∈ { 1, 0, ⊥ }

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;1　the replay conforms to the frozen protocol  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;0　it can be carried out only by changing the dynamics, changing the judge, changing the initial state, or adding undeclared operations  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;⊥　its legality cannot at present be determined

### 6.4 The Difference from Genesis

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Replayable^Π = 0　　is not　　Genesis = 0

The former says that this counterfactual itself is illegal; the latter, that a legal counterfactual has a failed result.

Mix the two, and every deletion inconvenient to carry out can be read as the necessity of a source. This is exactly what 4.6 excluded.

Section Seven

Sufficiency for Source and the Actual Chain of Formation

### 7.1 Sufficiency for Source

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;SuffGenesis^Π( H\_s ; χ\_L ) ∈ { 1, 0, ⊥ }

enters evaluation only when Replayable^Π( H\_s ) = 1.

Its holding is not as weak as "finally Law = 1"; a legal first occurrence of qualification must appear again: there exists τ\_s such that

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Judge\_t( χ\_L ) = 0　　for every t ∈ T\_Π with t \< τ\_s  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Judge\_{τ\_s}( χ\_L ) = 1

The three values are as follows:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;1　in the legal replay there is indeed this first 0 → 1  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;0　the legal replay is complete and there is definitely no such first 0 → 1  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;⊥　the legal replay holds, but the readings of Law are insufficient to adjudicate its firstness

When Replayable = 0, SuffGenesis is not set to 0, but regarded as not entering the domain of evaluation. Otherwise illegal counterfactuals would be mixed into failed counterfactuals — the former is that this counterfactual cannot be done, the latter that it was done and did not succeed. The ⊥ and 0 of the two layers may not stand in for each other.

### 7.2 Restriction of Wording

One may not write "the replay reproduces the same Law."

One may only write: the replay makes one and the same frozen candidate acquire formal Law qualification again.

The difference between the two is the first item of 1.4. What the replay obtains is the renewed holding of the candidate's qualification; whether it is the same Law on the two occasions this text does not adjudicate.

### 7.3 The Actual Generating Chain

Source cannot have counterfactual sufficiency alone. It further requires

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;ActualGenesisLink^Π( H\_s ; χ\_L, τ )

It requires that the stretch of history really enter this final-state qualification along the generating chain that actually occurred. This chain has two legal branches:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;ActualGenesisLink　=　FieldFormationLink　∨₃　ExternalInstallLink

First, formation within the field:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;FieldFormationLink^Π  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;　Execution → Trace → Sedimentation → Formation → R ∈ 𝒮 → Law = 1

Second, external installation:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;ExternalInstallLink^Π  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;　an explicitly declared external editing event really enters the chain of change of qualification, turning the formal judge from 0 to 1

The reason for setting the two branches side by side is in 4.8: external installation is a real occurrence of qualification, and its chain of occurrence can be examined, so it may not be left without any source to speak of merely because it is not generation within the field. Were only the first branch recognized, whatever first became a Law by external editing would, by this text, never have a source — while what 1.1 asks includes exactly such cases.

Each of the two branches takes its value by the same rule. For any generating-chain candidate G:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;G^Π( H\_s ; χ\_L, τ ) = 1　when the actual events that branch requires, and their connections entering the change of qualification, all hold on formal reading

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;G^Π = 0　when the interfaces that branch requires are all readable, and at least one necessary connection definitely does not hold

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;G^Π = ⊥　when an interface or connection necessary for adjudicating that branch is unreadable, unauditable, or insufficiently evidenced

The interfaces the two branches require are listed separately:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;FieldFormationLink　　execution, trace, sedimentation, formation, the formal witness, Law qualification  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;ExternalInstallLink　 the declared editing occurrence, the target state, the states before and after editing, the formal judge before and after, and the connection of that edit entering the change of qualification

∨₃ is three-valued disjunction: if both branches are 0, it is 0; if one branch is 1, it is 1; if there is no 1 and there is ⊥, it is ⊥.

### 7.4 What This Chain Excludes

Pure correlation; synchronous accompaniment; proxies of the final state; auxiliaries on a bypass; events without actual contribution to sedimentation.

All of these can appear important counterfactually, and yet are not really on the chain.

7.5 Counterfactuals Alone May Not Stand in for It

"Delete it and the result would differ" may not stand in alone for the actual chain of formation.

That deleting it changes the result can have many causes — it is an enabling condition, it covaries with the real source, its deletion disturbs something elsewhere along with it. None of these shows that it is on the chain.

Section Eight

Minimal Sufficient History

### 8.1 Definition

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;MinSuff^Π( H\_s ; χ\_L ) ∈ { 1, 0, ⊥ }

First the candidate itself is dealt with:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;MinSuff = 0　if Replayable( H\_s ) = 1 and SuffGenesis( H\_s ) = 0

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;MinSuff = ⊥　if Replayable( H\_s ) ∈ { 0, ⊥ }, or Replayable( H\_s ) = 1 and SuffGenesis( H\_s ) = ⊥

A Replayable = 0 of the candidate itself goes to ⊥, not to 0. That it cannot be legally replayed under the protocol does not prove that it is not a minimal sufficient source — it has not even been legally tested for sufficiency.

This is the same as at the layer of reductions, and must be adjudicated the same way:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;cannot be replayed　≠　replayed and not sufficient

One and the same Replayable = 0 may not be adjudicated ⊥ on reductions and 0 on the candidate itself. Assigning one semantics two epistemic strengths in two places is inconsistency.

Only after the candidate itself has

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Replayable( H\_s ) = 1,　SuffGenesis( H\_s ; χ\_L ) = 1

are its strict reductions examined. Then quantify over every H′ ≺\_Π H\_s:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;MinSuff = 0　if there exists H′ ≺\_Π H\_s with Replayable( H′ ) = 1 and SuffGenesis( H′ ) = 1

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;　　that is, a legal proper reduction that is still sufficient has been found. This is a determinate counterexample, and takes priority over every ⊥.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;MinSuff = 1　if there is no counterexample as above, and for every H′ ≺\_Π H\_s  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;　　　　　　　Replayable( H′ ) = 1　and　SuffGenesis( H′ ) = 0

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;　　that is, every strict reduction has really undergone a legal test and failed.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;MinSuff = ⊥　if there is no counterexample as above, and there exists at least one H′ with  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;　　　　　　　Replayable( H′ ) = 0, or Replayable( H′ ) = ⊥,  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;　　　　　　　or Replayable( H′ ) = 1 and SuffGenesis( H′ ) = ⊥

The item Replayable( H′ ) = 0 among these must go to ⊥ and may not go to 1. This is the load-bearing point of this section: were it allowed to go to 1, then "no reduction can be legally executed" would on the contrary adjudicate the history minimal, which is exactly the "it cannot be deleted, so it is necessary" that 8.5 prohibits. That it cannot be deleted gives no minimality; only what can really be deleted and really fails to form a Law supports minimality.

8.2 Quantifying over All Strict Reductions, without First Calling Them Load-Bearing

The quantification above is over every H′ ≺\_Π H\_s, and may not be written "over the load-bearing strict reductions."

It is precisely by the results of reduction that one adjudicates what bears load; to call something load-bearing beforehand is circular. Bearing load is what is obtained after the quantification, not what is posited before it.

8.3 A Determinate Counterexample Takes Priority over Non-Adjudicable

The priority of values must be fixed definitively: once a legal proper reduction that is still sufficient is found, it is known not to be minimal, and the value is 0; it does not return ⊥ because other reductions cannot be executed or cannot be adjudicated.

Conversely, if there is no such counterexample, then wherever there is one place that cannot be executed or cannot be adjudicated, the value is ⊥, and may not be adjudicated 1 outright.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;cannot be deleted　≠　deleted and fails

The former is a limitation of operation, the latter the bearing of a source. In this section they lie at ⊥ and 0 respectively, and may not be exchanged.

8.4 Three Values May Not Be Pressed into Two

The value ⊥ cannot be omitted. Omit it, and "could not be deleted" would be read as "must not be deleted," while the two are far apart: the former is a limitation of operation, the latter the bearing of a source.

### 8.5 Specially Prohibited

Writing "it cannot be deleted, so it is necessary" is prohibited.

A deletion that cannot be executed does not prove the necessity of a source. This comes from the same principle as 4.6 and 6.4, but at the layer of minimality it is the easiest to commit: whenever one place of reduction cannot be executed, to record it as what minimal sufficiency hangs on — that is taking ⊥ for 1.

Section Nine

The Core of Source and the Discipline of the Double Chain

### 9.1 The Core Definition

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;LawGenesisSource^Π( H\_s ; χ\_L, τ ) = 1

if and only if all three hold:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Genesis^Π\_τ( χ\_L ) = 1  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;ActualGenesisLink^Π( H\_s ; χ\_L, τ ) = 1  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;MinSuff^Π( H\_s ; χ\_L ) = 1

Each of the three guards one thing: firstness is guarded by Genesis; this real chain of occurrence by ActualGenesisLink; a history neither onlooking nor redundant by MinSuff.

Every quantity is three-valued; the master table is as follows:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Genesis^Π\_τ( χ\_L ) ∈ { 1, 0, ⊥ }　　　　　3.5  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Replayable^Π( H ) ∈ { 1, 0, ⊥ }　　　　　6.3  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;SuffGenesis^Π( H ; χ\_L ) ∈ { 1, 0, ⊥ }　　7.1  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;ActualGenesisLink^Π ∈ { 1, 0, ⊥ }　　　　7.3  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;MinSuff^Π( H ; χ\_L ) ∈ { 1, 0, ⊥ }　　　 8.1

If any of the three items is 0, LawGenesisSource is 0; if there is no 0 and there is ⊥, it is ⊥.

### 9.2 The Double Chain

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;LawGenesisSource　=　ActualGenesis　∩　CounterfactualBearing

What actually took part in formation but does not bear counterfactually is not enough — it is on the chain, but deleting it does no harm, so it is not what this Law-formation hangs on.

What is counterfactually sufficient but is not the chain that actually occurred this time is not enough either — delete it and there is no Law-formation, yet this Law-formation did not pass through it, so it is at most another possible road.

Only the intersection of the two is the source. This is the most important discipline of this text.

### 9.3 The Form of Failure on Each Side

Actual generation only, without counterfactual bearing: see MinSuff = 0 in 8.1 — there exists a legal strict reduction that is still sufficient, so the stretch of history contains something deletable.

Counterfactual bearing only, without actual generation: see 7.4 and 7.5 — enabling conditions, what covaries, and what disturbs along with it can all make the result of deletion differ without being on the chain.

9.4 The Mode of Generation Is a Reading of the Chain

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;FieldBorn^Π( χ\_L, τ ) = 1　when ActualGenesisLink holds through the branch FieldFormationLink  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;ExternalInstall^Π( χ\_L, τ ) = 1　when it holds through the branch ExternalInstallLink

The two are not isolated labels outside the source, but the reading of which branch the chain took. So a first Law-formation can have a source without being generation within the field — its source is that one declared external editing event.

The two do not presuppose mutual exclusion: where both branches hold, both are 1; see 12.1.

Section Ten

Source Theorems

Theorem One　Insufficiency of the Final State for Source

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Law\_τ = 1　⇏　𝔖^Π\_src

that is: there is no function that determines the family of source solutions from the final-state value of qualification alone.

Proof. Take the failure world of 4.1, and complete it to the layer of the family of solutions.

What 4.1 gives is two different histories H\_A ≠ H\_B, both giving Law\_τ = 1. Now it is further required that the frozen final-state candidate data of the two at τ also be the same — by 3.2 that data is read from the fields the protocol specifies, and two different histories can fall on the same readings.

The actual generating chains of the two differ. By 7.3 the generating chain is fixed by what actually occurred, not by the final state; so ActualGenesisLink recognizes, in each of the two worlds, the stretch of its own. Through the legal reductions of 6.1 and the replays of 6.3, certified one by one by the three items of 9.1, what is obtained is

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;𝔖^Π\_src( W\_A )　≠　𝔖^Π\_src( W\_B )

This step is one that 4.1 did not take: that subsection went only as far as the difference of two histories, while what is needed here is the difference of two families of solutions.

Suppose a function determined the family of source solutions from Law\_τ = 1 alone. On these two worlds its inputs are the same — both Law\_τ = 1, and the final-state candidate data the same too — while its outputs differ, so it is not a function. Hence there is no such function. ∎

This proof does not rely on the uniqueness of the source. Section Eleven has permitted the family of solutions to be plural, so "one and the same final state should correspond to only one source" may not serve as a ground — that is exactly what the permitted plurality of solutions denies. What is proved is only this: the final state gives qualification, not source.

Theorem Two　The Double Chain of Source

If LawGenesisSource^Π( H\_s ; χ\_L, τ ) = 1, then

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Genesis^Π\_τ( χ\_L ) = 1  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;ActualGenesisLink^Π( H\_s ) = 1  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;MinSuff^Π( H\_s ) = 1

Not one of the three can be deleted.

Proof. The three items are given directly by 9.1. That none can be deleted has, for each, a form of failure: delete Genesis, and what is asked is not a first Law-formation, and the object of this text does not stand; delete ActualGenesisLink, and all those listed in 7.4 can be selected, though they are not on the chain; delete MinSuff, and H\_s can contain arbitrarily much unrelated history, and "source" cannot be narrowed down — every longer history containing the real source could call itself the source. ∎

Theorem Three　Leakage of the Source Domain

If

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;SuffGenesis^Π( H\_∅ ) = 1

then

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;SourceDomainIncomplete^Π = 1

and under the current protocol no complete conclusion of source may be output.

Proof. By 3.4, H\_∅ is the empty-history control, and when SuffGenesis^Π( H\_∅ ) = 1 that quantity takes 1. The reason is this: if first Law-formation is still obtained with no historical event kept, then the domain of historical candidates declared by the current protocol is not the complete bearing domain of the source — the bearer lies outside that domain.

Where it lies cannot be decided hastily. The omission may lie in the frozen background B^Π; enough structure may already be hidden in the initial state X\_0; there may be in the spontaneous evolution of Dyn^Π a load-bearing process not divided into historical events; Gran^Π may be too coarse and have merged the bearer into another atom; or the domain of source candidates may itself have left out events. All five are possible, and this theorem does not adjudicate which.

In this case one may not write Source = ∅. An empty source and "the source is not within the domain drawn" are two different things: the former says that nothing bears; the latter, that the bearer was drawn outside the examination.

Nor is a separate overall source variable made up to take this case. The formal objects of this text are only LawGenesisSource and 𝔖^Π\_src; an undefined quantity may not suddenly be introduced here.

So the source adjudication of the current protocol is unclosed: the existing 𝔖^Π\_src may not be output as a complete family of source solutions. The division among the five X\_0, H^Π, B^Π, Dyn^Π, and Gran^Π must be re-examined, and a new source protocol set up. Under the original protocol one may not go on claiming that the source has been determined. ∎

Theorem Four　Preservation of Plural Solutions

If

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;\| 𝔖^Π\_src \| \> 1

then the output of source must keep the whole certified family of solutions, and may not raise one item of it to the unique source.

Proof. By the definition of 11.1, every item of 𝔖^Π\_src satisfies the two certifications ActualGenesisLink and MinSuff. If one item were taken from it and reported as the unique source, what is reported would be indistinguishable from the other items under the criteria of this text — the ground for taking this and not that lies not within the criteria but in the intention of the one choosing.

One of the cases the Canon prohibits for the source form is precisely reporting only one source where several resolutions exist. ∎

Section Eleven

Where the Source Cannot Be Uniquely Determined

### 11.1 The Family of Source Solutions

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;𝔖^Π\_src( χ\_L, τ ) = { H\_s \| LawGenesisSource^Π( H\_s ; χ\_L, τ ) = 1 }

Its three items are not spelled out again here, lest the two definitions drift apart later. The three items are in 9.1, the item Genesis among them — without that item, taking the definition by itself, formal source candidates could appear even where there is no first event.

One may not presuppose \| 𝔖^Π\_src \| = 1.

H\_A ≠ H\_B may both belong to the family.

### 11.2 Two Kinds of Bearing

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;PossibleSourceCarrier^Π( e ) = 1　　when　∃ H\_s ∈ 𝔖^Π\_src : e ∈ H\_s

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;NecessarySourceCarrier^Π( e ) = 1　if and only if  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;　　　𝔖^Π\_src ≠ ∅　∧　∀ H\_s ∈ 𝔖^Π\_src : e ∈ H\_s

The clause of non-emptiness cannot be omitted. A universal statement over an empty family is true in classical logic; without this clause, when not a single source solution has been found, everyone would on the contrary be a necessary bearer.

And for the empty family the value is not 0 but

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;𝔖^Π\_src = ∅　⇒　NecessarySourceCarrier^Π( e ) = ⊥

The reason is that an empty family has at least three origins: the current protocol has obtained no certified source; the source domain has left out items; none of the existing candidates has got past ⊥. The three may not all be pressed into "is not a necessary source."

"There is definitely no source" is not written. Since Genesis = 1 already holds, and what this text studies is the actual history of change of qualification, that phrase easily leads to a causeless genesis, while this text has neither proved such a world legal nor denied it — what it claims goes only as far as no certified source having been obtained under the existing protocol.

One further origin, the case of there being no minimal element, has been blocked by the finiteness of the frozen domain of reduction established in 5.1.

Here e is an event occurrence frozen according to Gran^Π, not a type of event. The two must be kept apart: an event of the same type repeated a hundred times is a hundred occurrences, not one type; counted by type, "necessary bearing" would be confused between type and token — that an event of some type appears in every solution of the family is not the same as some one occurrence of it being required by every solution.

11.3 Two Statements That May Not Be Used in Reverse

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;SourceExistence　⇏　UniqueSource  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;PossibleSourceCarrier　⇏　NecessarySourceCarrier

A unique source may be declared only when \| 𝔖^Π\_src \| = 1.

11.4 What Cannot Be Uniquely Determined, Listed Explicitly

By the completion conditions of the source form, where the source cannot be uniquely determined must be listed explicitly. This text lists the following.

First, the cardinality of the family of solutions. \| 𝔖^Π\_src \| = 1 is not claimed, nor are conditions given under which it is one.

Second, relativity to the protocol. 𝔖^Π\_src hangs on the nine frozen items of Π\_src, the set of evaluation points T\_Π among them; with another protocol, the family of solutions obtained need not be the same. It is not claimed that the source is consistent under any protocol.

Third, the distribution of ⊥. Whatever takes ⊥ is undetermined as to whether it belongs to the family of solutions. The range in which that value appears is determined jointly by four things: the legality of replay, the adjudicability of formal qualification, the auditability of the actual generating chain, and the granularity of the protocol. Its boundary is not given.

Fourth, the line between background and history. What Theorem Three shows is that where this line is drawn wrongly, the source cannot be determined; what the correct way of drawing it is, no criterion is given here.

Section Twelve

Modes of Generation

### 12.1 The Two Modes

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;FieldBorn^Π( χ\_L, τ ) = 1　　when there exists a FieldFormationLink that holds  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;ExternalInstall^Π( χ\_L, τ ) = 1　when there exists an ExternalInstallLink that holds, that is, a chain of external editing declared in advance and auditable, not belonging to the dynamics of sedimentary formation within the field

Wording of the kind "mainly by" is not used. Whether something is main has no criterion to rest on — how much counts as main, the largest contribution, a necessary role, or a sufficient role: none is fixed. The value of each is the reading of its corresponding branch; see 9.4.

The two are exhaustive within the ActualGenesisLink defined by this text v1.0 — that chain being fixed by the three-valued disjunction of the two branches, when it is 1 at least one branch is 1. But this text does not claim on that basis that the two exhaust all possible Law genesis modes: exhaustion within a formal definition is not ontological exhaustion.

The two do not presuppose mutual exclusion. A genesis can entirely contain both ordinary sedimentation and an external edit of some load-bearing state midway, both actual chains taking part; then both items are 1. This text does not further rule on "which branch is the main one" — unless FieldBorn ⊕ ExternalInstall is proved separately, the two may not be written as an either-or mode.

Neither "declared" nor "auditable" can be omitted. An undeclared external edit is not in this class but falls under K-SRC2 — if the two shared one name, a legal external source and a smuggled-in external variable would be covered by one word.

### 12.2 Neither Changes Qualification

Formal Law qualification does not differ by mode. The correct relation is:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;FieldBorn = 1　⇒　Law = 1　(at the target endpoint)

while

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Law = 1　⇏　FieldBorn = 1

ExternalInstall can also finally obtain Law = 1 formally.

### 12.3 Prohibited Wording

One may not write "an installed Law is not a real Law."

This text distinguishes only how it acquired qualification, not whether it is a Law. Qualification is adjudicated by Judge^Π, mode by the source protocol, and neither stands in for the other.

Section Thirteen

Conditional Items, State, and What Is Not Claimed

### 13.1 The H Group

Three items.

H-SRC1　The interface of Law qualification. The upstream formal Law judge and its antecedents remain valid. Should its antecedents be withdrawn, Genesis cannot be adjudicated.

H-SRC2　The actual generating chain can be audited. Each branch has its own requirements: for FieldFormationLink, the relations among execution, trace, sedimentation, formation, and the final-state witness can be read; for ExternalInstallLink, the declared external editing event, the state edited, the formal judge before and after editing, and the chain by which it enters the change of qualification can all be read. This is what 7.3 needs; if either branch lacks it, that branch cannot be audited.

H-SRC3　The protocol of reduction of history can be executed. The operations of deletion, masking, and replay used by ⪯\_Π have legal and explicit semantics of implementation. This is what Sections Six and Eight need.

Not to be added: SameLaw; unique source; NoFork; "every source can be fully replayed." None of the four is an antecedent here; written in, they would look like commitments about identity or uniqueness.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Conditional

conditionalized on H-SRC1, H-SRC2, H-SRC3.

### 13.3 Instantiation State

No new harness is set up, so no PASS is recorded.

Section Fourteen

Breach

By Part Four, Chapter Four of the Canon Chapter, the breach method of the source form is: with all declared source conditions holding, the target structure still fails to be generated; or prove that the generative chain depends on undeclared external conditions; or show by intervention that the designated process does not bear the generative role claimed. And one further clause: only where the Form claims a necessary or unique source does the target structure's still arising in the absence of that process constitute a breach.

Accordingly five are listed.

K-SRC1　A source certificate in conflict with formal adjudication. If the system outputs LawGenesisSource^Π( H\_s ) = 1 or MinSuff^Π( H\_s ) = 1, while after actually replaying H\_s under the same frozen protocol the formal Law judge does not obtain a legal first 0 → 1, then that source certificate fails, and the implementation or formal mapping of this text is breached.

　　This item must be written so in order to be executable. Were it written "all source conditions hold and legal replay cannot make it a Law," it would not be a breach: the definition of MinSuff = 1 already contains SuffGenesis = 1, so that case is impossible within the definition; what is exposed is an inconsistency between certificate and adjudication, not a counterexample to the theory.

K-SRC2　Dependence on the undeclared. The so-called actual chain of formation depends on an undeclared external input, edit, or background bearing.

K-SRC3　Intervention proving that it does not bear. Intervention confirms that the declared H\_s does not bear the claimed chain of formation.

K-SRC4　A counterexample to necessary bearing. If the main text claims NecessarySourceCarrier = 1 for some historical atom, then finding a legal source solution not containing that atom breaches that claim.

K-SRC5　A counterexample to unique source. If the main text claims a unique source, then finding a second certified source solution breaches that claim.

Not breaches: the source having several solutions — 11.3 has declared that this text does not claim uniqueness; some reduction not executable, giving ⊥ — 8.1 has declared that value, not a failure of a conclusion; an incomplete division of the source domain, giving SourceDomainIncomplete^Π = 1 — Theorem Three is set up for exactly this, and its output is the correct output for that case, the existing 𝔖^Π\_src then not to be output as a complete family of source solutions; one of the three antecedents not holding — the conditionalization declared in 13.2, not a failure of a conclusion.

Section Fifteen

Interfaces

### 15.1 Upward

The Law Group-Form and The Determination Form of Law (sealed) — formal Law qualification. The antecedents of each propagate with it.

The Mathematical Form of Law-Dynamics (sealed) — the formal discipline of running segments, actual execution, and the attribution of contribution. The chain of formation of 7.3 rests on it.

The Construction Form of the Law-Dynamics Operating Substrate (sealed) — the running of a real Sedimentation-Field and the interface of formal adjudication.

The Distinction Form of Law-Identity Pairings (sealed) — neither the correspondence of candidate declarations nor the correspondence of roots may pass for SameLaw. The first item of 1.4 rests on it.

### 15.2 Downward

The double chain of LawGenesisSource — downstream texts may use it as the ground that "a source must combine actual formation and counterfactual bearing." Citations must inherit together the three antecedents of 13.1 and the Conditional Status of 13.2.

The preservation of plural solutions — Theorem Four propagates with the conclusions. Downstream texts may not take one item from the family of solutions and report it as the unique source.

Readings of ⊥ and of the unclosed — MinSuff = ⊥ and SourceDomainIncomplete = 1 are both declared values, not values of failure; neither may be read as the source not existing. Nor may downstream texts read ⊥ as 0.

The difference between mode and qualification — 12.2 propagates with the conclusions. Downstream texts may not infer from ExternalInstall that qualification does not hold.

The difference between source and identity — the four statements of 1.4 propagate with the conclusions. Downstream texts may not obtain from this text any assertion about whether two occurrences are the same.

### 15.3 Boundaries between Texts

The General Form of Law Interaction — how one Law changes another. There this text can be used to distinguish "current action" from "the historical source of formation."

The Mathematical Form of Law Interaction — takes up the binding of source and contribution.

The Stability Form of Law Ecology — takes up newborn events in an environment of several Laws, and may not take source as identity.

The Law-identity texts — copying, migration, recovery, replacement, reappearance across carriers, and re-formation after 1 → 0 → 1. These all first require a closed property of Law identity, which has not yet been established; see 1.3.
