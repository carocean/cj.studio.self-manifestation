# The General Form of Law Interaction

Section One

General Proposition, Working Domain, and Boundaries of Inheritance

### 1.1 General Proposition

If it is acknowledged that a direct inter-Law interaction really occurred between one standing Law and some dynamical event of another standing Law, then there must be some minimal structure by which this acknowledgement holds.

The objects are three: the standing Law reference L\_S in the source role, the standing Law reference L\_T in the target role, and the LawDyn event e\_T of the target.

The fact of standing that must be explained or maintained: a direct inter-Law interaction occurred from L\_S to this dynamical event of L\_T.

The logical strength claimed by this form reaches only the necessary direction.

### 1.2 Question-Driven

In this chapter, through continuous questioning, we shall discover where the proof must go and derive the General Form of Law Interaction.

The path of proof is not laid out in advance. Each next step can be generated only by what the previous round left unclosed.

1.3 A Preview of the General Formula to Be Established

The shape of what this text sets out to establish is previewed as follows:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;LawInteraction^η\_F( L\_S → L\_T ; e\_T )　⇒　LawInteractionBear^η\_F( L\_S, L\_T ; e\_T )

What the super- and subscripts refer to: F is the Sedimentation-Field under examination, η a legal running trajectory on it. Both are inherited from upstream and not defined separately here; the interaction declared is an event-level relation falling on that field and that trajectory.

This is an object to be proved, not an established one. The preview only lets the reader know where the text is heading, and may not serve as a premise in any round below — whatever a given layer has not been forced out by its failure world may not be drawn on before that round.

### 1.4 Form-Kind and Version

The general form, a principal form, set up as an independent text, first edition.

By Canon Chapter 1, Part Three, Chapter One, the purpose of the general form is to prove the minimal structure by which an object stands, in the shape O ⇒ N; its entry conditions are that the object O is already clearly delimited; "O stands" can be stated as a reviewable fact; … the minimal structure has not yet received an established unconditional conclusion.

The entry conditions of this text are checked accordingly: the Law qualification of the two ends and the LawDyn event of the target are all given by upstream, and are reviewable facts; while "what this interaction must minimally have" has been given by none of the upstream texts.

### 1.5 Working Domain

This text studies only pairwise, event-level, direct LawDyn interaction.

The domain of target events is fixed as

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Θ\_LD = { Start, Continue, Stop, Rise, Fall, Migration }

Not one of the six is added, not one removed. It may not be expanded to "any change on the side of Law"; Continue above all may not be removed, for the reason given in Section Ten.

Not studied: multi-level causal ancestry; interaction graphs; long-term feedback loops; ecological stability; networks of several Laws; SameLaw; the first source of a Law; the sufficient definition of Principle.

### 1.6 Four Boundaries

Four statements must be pinned down first, and may not be used in reverse anywhere in the text.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;LawInteraction　≠　Coexistence

Coexistence, synchrony, and co-location do not constitute interaction.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;LawInteraction　≠　Principle

Principle is an established qualification of relation between Law and Law; its standing does not by itself imply that an inter-Law interaction is currently occurring. Qualification and current execution are two layers, already separated by the Principle Chapter: an established Principle and what of it is currently being executed do not stand in for each other.

The current exercise of an established Principle, if it further satisfies the conditions of event-level direct interaction established by this text, can become a special instance of LawInteraction; while ordinary LawInteraction does not require CounterHold.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;LawInteraction　≠　LawSource

Source asks about first Law-formation; interaction asks whether another standing Law enters this Law's dynamical event. The two ask different things; see Chapter 9.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;LawInteraction　≠　LawIdentity

This text does not adjudicate whether two occurrences are the same. No closed criterion of Law identity has yet been established; see Chapter 8.

### 1.7 In One Sentence

Interaction is not two Laws both being there, nor two Laws being side by side; it requires that on the source side there really issue an item of duty-bearing as a Law, and that this item really enter the generation of that event of the target.

Section Two

Upstream Input and the Condition of Non-Degeneracy

### 2.1 Upstream Inheritance

The Law Group-Form and The Determination Form of Law (sealed) — formal Law qualification.

The General Form of Law-Action and The Necessity Form of Law-Action (sealed) — Rel(L), the semantics of duty, and its difference from current action.

The General Form of Law-Dynamics (sealed) — the six classes of LawDyn event and their legal adjudication.

The Mathematical Form of Law-Dynamics (sealed) — the formal discipline of running segments, actual execution, and the attribution of contribution; the institution of three-valued non-adjudicability.

The Construction Form of the Law-Dynamics Operating Substrate (sealed) — the separation of layers between formal witness and physical bearing; the actual forms of DynamicRole and legal realization.

The Distinction Form of Law-Identity Pairings (sealed) — neither the correspondence of declarations nor the correspondence of roots may pass for SameLaw.

The Source Form of Law (sealed) — the difference between source and interaction; the third item of 1.6 rests on it.

Each item is inherited in its existing definition; this text makes no second set.

### 2.2 The Semantic Anchor of the Object

This text takes LawInteraction as the reference to an object-level fact to be analysed. Its semantic anchors are two.

First, the relational semantics already in the canonical source: mutual disturbance, mutual pursuit, making-to-execute. These already have their referents in the canonical source, and its corpus suffices to show that there exist, between established holdings of momentum, directed dynamical relations that can be referred to.

"Actual entry between Laws" is not taken as an anchor. That phrase is already close to one of the items that must be forced out below; were it to enter the semantic anchor, one item of N would be buried in O beforehand, and the entry to the object would become circular again. On the basis of the senses of the canonical source, this text declares LawInteraction an object-level fact to be analysed, and does not define it by "actually entering the generation of the target event."

Second, the domain of LawDyn events Θ\_LD already frozen by this group-form. What is declared is a direct dynamical interaction from the source role to the target role, falling on an event within that domain.

The object-level fact declared is this one sentence only: a direct dynamical interaction from the source role to the target role has occurred.

This entry is not defined by the items this text has yet to force out. Whatever items the recursion below forces out, whether of the ontological layer or of the discipline of binding, may not in turn be used to define this entry fact.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;LawInteraction is an object to be analysed, not an abbreviation of N

This clause cannot be omitted. The recursion of this text advances by failure worlds, and every round must first be able to recognize whether interaction holds in that world; if that recognition covertly used the two layers forced out only later, what is forced out would merely be the object into which the criterion had already been stuffed, and the recursion would be circular. So the entry must be established first, independently of N.

### 2.3 The Condition of Non-Degeneracy

Fix two different declared Law references:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Ref\_S ≠ Ref\_T

where Ref\_S is the Law reference declared on the source-role side and Ref\_T that on the target-role side. The formula means only that the two do not use one and the same declared reference.

One may not write SameLaw( L\_S, L\_T ) = 0. No closed criterion of Law identity has yet been established, and it may not be asserted here on that basis that the two are not the same. What is relied on is only that the two references differ, and different references do not imply different referents — Chapter 8 has established this line.

Throughout the text, wherever "another Law" is said for short, it means only another frozen Law-role reference, carrying no assertion of identity SameLaw = 0. In natural language the phrase easily carries a sense of identity, so it is declared here at the same time.

Section Three

Both Ends Are Laws: Is That Enough?

Both ends are already standing Laws: is that enough to imply interaction?

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Law( L\_S ) = 1 ∧ Law( L\_T ) = 1　⇒　LawInteraction ?

Construct failure world one: each independent.

In one and the same field there are two Laws, L\_S and L\_T. Each stands in an independent running chain, each has its own family of bearing, each passes formal adjudication of qualification. The two even Rise at the same time — their Degrees rise in step, covarying in their readings.

And between the two there is no cross-end actual relation at all: the bearing of L\_S does not enter any running of L\_T, and the generation of L\_T's events draws on no contribution of L\_S.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Law( L\_S ) = 1 ∧ Law( L\_T ) = 1, and interaction does not hold

Qualification is a property of each end itself: it says that this one really is a standing Law. Interaction is a relation between the two ends: it says that this one entered some event of that one. The former is said once for each end, the latter once for a pair.

The point about Rising at the same time must be stated especially exactly: the covariation of the two readings can come from their being driven by one and the same external condition, with no traffic between them. Covariation is the similarity of the shape of two curves, not one entering the other.

So what is obtained is only one sentence:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;coexistence of qualification is not enough

What, then, does real interaction still lack? No name can yet be given here; what is obtained is only a gap: what is lacking is a cross-end actual relation. The question then advances — even if there really is physical entry, is that enough?

Section Four

With Actual Physical Entry, Still Not Enough

So one item is added: on the source side there really is an actual occurrence, and that occurrence really enters the physical mechanism where the target lies — not coexistence, not covariation, but something really passing over.

And that occurrence is only a physical side effect of the bearer of L\_S: its heat changes the impedance of the nearby medium, its occupancy squeezes the bandwidth of a shared channel, its current causes an offset on a shared ground.

None of these has anything to do with Rel(L\_S), with the DynamicRole of L\_S, or with its legal realization. The bearer happens to bear L\_S, while this occurrence is not issued by it as L\_S's duty-bearing.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;PhysicalContributionFromBearer( L\_S )　⇏　LawInteraction

If interaction were still adjudicated to hold in this world, what interacts would no longer be the Law. Wherever two Laws share a stretch of medium, their bearers necessarily have all manner of physical influence on one another; were all this adjudicated inter-Law interaction, interaction would become a matter between carriers, while the Law established upstream is precisely not the carrier.

This is failure world two: a side effect of the carrier.

Only now can the first layer be named:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;SourceLawContribution

What it answers is: whether the actual contribution under examination really belongs to L\_S's duty-bearing as a Law.

What this layer asks is only "who issued it." It does not ask whether the contribution arrives, nor whether it enters the generation of the target — that is the business of another round below; were it anticipated here, the world of Section Six, "the first layer holds and the contribution does not arrive," could not be constructed, and the step that forces out the second layer would destroy itself.

What this layer must at least examine internally is set out in the next section.

Section Five

The First Layer: SourceLawContribution

### 5.1 Three Examinations

This layer contains three examinations, and may not be split into three ontological layers — the three together answer one and the same question, namely whether the contribution really belongs to L\_S's duty-bearing.

First, qualification at the source point.

At the source point r\_s from which the actual contribution issues,

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Law\_{F, r\_s}( L\_S, I\_S ) = 1

Qualification is adjudicated at the source point.

It is not required that the source Law maintain Law = 1 throughout the stretch of propagation; for the reason see Section Eleven.

Second, the actual occurrence of contribution.

There must exist a real occurrence of contribution

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;c\_S

It is something that actually happened, not a potential capability, not static wiring, not statistical correlation.

Capability, wiring, and correlation can all hold when nothing has actually happened, while what this layer seeks is what has happened.

Third, attribution on the side of Law.

c\_S must be readable back along three things: the legal realization of L\_S; DynamicRole; Rel(L\_S).

A physical side effect may not be credited to L\_S because the physical root is the same, the bearer is shared, the location is shared, or the material structure is the same. All four are facts of the layer of carriers, while attribution asks about the layer of duty.

### 5.2 Among the Three Examinations

The three follow one another in the order of examination, and are not three independent ontological layers: first the qualification at the source point is examined, before there can be talk of bearing as a Law; then the actual occurrence, before there is an object to attribute; last the read-back on the side of Law, which fixes to whom it is attributed.

This is the order of proof and adjudication, not the temporal order of generation. It is not said that in reality qualification must be acquired first, then there be an occurrence, then an attribution — the three are three aspects of one and the same thing, and here they are only examined in three passes.

If one is lacking, this layer does not hold, while the three together establish only one layer.

Section Six

The First Layer Is Still Not Enough

With the first layer established, ask again whether it suffices. Let SourceLawContribution( L\_S, c\_S ) = 1 — the source point has qualification, the contribution really happened, and it really belongs to L\_S's duty-bearing.

And c\_S is bypassed: it takes another channel and does not reach L\_T's running; or it is blocked: a gate on the way stops it; or it is dissipated: its quantity decays before arriving to below what enters generation; or it simply does not enter any legal LawDyn event of L\_T.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;SourceLawContribution　⇏　LawInteraction

If a contribution really belonging to L\_S really issues and does not enter any event of the target, then this interaction did not occur. Issuing and arriving are two things. This is failure world three: the contribution does not arrive.

Only now can the second layer be named:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;TargetDynGeneration

What it answers is: whether the same c\_S actually entered the generating mechanism of some legal LawDyn event of L\_T.

Section Seven

The Second Layer: TargetDynGeneration

### 7.1 What Is Required

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;e\_T ∈ Θ\_LD

and c\_S really enters the actual generating chain of e\_T.

### 7.2 What Is Not Required

This layer is not "the target ends up different." So one may not require:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;OutcomeSuccess = 1  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;ButForNecessity = 1  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;NetDifference ≠ 0

None of the three is necessary, for the following reasons.

A redundant contribution is real interaction: two contributions arrive together, and with one removed the target is as before — by the but-for criterion, neither is necessary, so two contributions that really entered generation would both be adjudicated non-interaction. Yet they did enter.

A compensated contribution is real interaction, and this clause must be tightened by one layer: the declared target event e\_T must actually hold.

If e\_T still actually holds while another contribution partially, or on the side of outcome, compensates c\_S, the interaction is not cancelled — c\_S pushes by two, another item offsets one, the target still Rises by one, and its interaction holds; or again the target is a Continue to begin with, and two opposite contributions jointly maintain an actual continuation whose reading does not change, and its interaction holds too.

But if the compensation is complete, so that the declared event did not occur at all — what was declared was a Rise and it ended level, and level is not within Θ\_LD — then it may not still be called an interaction with that Rise. That Rise never happened, and there is nothing to interact with.

This line may not be crossed. This text having locked the target into the six classes of Θ\_LD, "actually entering some internal mechanism" may not bypass whether the declared event holds.

Continue is real interaction: one Law actually maintains another Law's Continue, the target's reading unchanged while the event really occurs. By the criterion of outcome, no change means no interaction — yet Continue is one of the six classes of event.

### 7.3 What Direct Means

Direct is not the same as a single physical step of direct connection.

On the way it may pass through: channels; gates; media; non-Law transforming nodes. None of these changes c\_S's identity as a contribution on the side of Law.

So long as c\_S's identity as a contribution on the side of Law has not been taken over by another Law, and the actual generating chain is continuous and traceable, it still belongs to one and the same direct inter-Law interaction.

### 7.4 Takeover Splits the Segment

If on the way another Law L\_M has acquired a new contribution on the side of Law, c\_M, then it must be split into two segments:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;L\_S → L\_M　　and　　L\_M → L\_T

and L\_S → L\_T may not be inferred automatically from this.

Hence

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;LawInteraction does not presuppose transitivity

The two segments each hold, and do not compose into a third. Whether they compose must be proved separately, and this text does not prove it.

Section Eight

The Two Layers Holding Separately Are Still Not Enough

With the two layers established, ask again whether their holding separately suffices. Let the first layer hold on some contribution:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;SourceLawContribution( L\_S, c\_a ) = 1

Let the second layer hold on another contribution:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;TargetDynGeneration( c\_b ; L\_T, e\_T ) = 1

while

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;c\_a ≠ c\_b

c\_a really belongs to L\_S's duty-bearing but does not reach e\_T; c\_b really enters the generation of e\_T but does not belong to L\_S — it comes either from another Law, or is even a side effect of a carrier.

Each of the two layers is true, while "an item of L\_S's contribution on the side of Law entered this event of L\_T" does not hold.

What is lacking is not yet another layer of content, but that the two layers must fall on one and the same contribution. Were each of the two layers allowed to take what it needs, any pair of "somewhere there is a legal contribution" and "somewhere there is an entry into generation" could be pieced together into an interaction. This is failure world four: pieced-together evidence.

From this the discipline of binding is forced out:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;SameInteractionBind

The binding key is

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;β\_int = ( L\_S, r\_s, c\_S, Z\_ST, L\_T, e\_T )

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;L\_S　　the source Law  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;r\_s　　the source point from which the contribution issues  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;c\_S　　the actual occurrence of contribution  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Z\_ST　the actual generating sub-segment from the source point to the target event  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;L\_T　　the target Law  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;e\_T　　the target's LawDyn event

What it requires is: the c\_S recognized by the first layer is the very c\_S that the second layer has actually entering e\_T; the generating sub-segment between them is one and the same; the event entered is one and the same.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;It is not a third ontological layer

This point must be fixed definitively.

SameInteractionBind adds no new content; it requires only that the two existing layers be witnessed jointly by one and the same actual occurrence of contribution, one and the same generating sub-segment, and one and the same target event. Delete it, and the two layers can each hold on a different thing and each be true; set it up, and what is added is the sameness of witness, not another layer of the object.

It is of the same kind as the Binding of The General Form of Law-Action: there too no third layer is set up; it requires only that each item fall on one and the same action.

Section Nine

The Recursion Stops

With the three established, one must ask once more: can a failure world be constructed that keeps all three true and still forces out a new independent necessary difference?

Each of the following candidates was examined and forced out no third ontological layer:

coexistence; simultaneity; co-location; physical side effects of the carrier; a common cause; redundant causes; contributions from several sources; compensation of outcome; zero net difference; the source Law afterwards leaving qualification; residue after death; Continue; Migration; takeover by several Laws; pieced-together evidence.

These are either already excluded by the first four rounds, or fall within the two layers, or are stopped by the discipline of binding.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The recursion stops

A third layer may not be added for the sake of "completeness." What a general form establishes is the minimal structure; whatever is added without a failure world forcing it is not necessary, and the minimal then ceases to be minimal.

Section Ten

Why Each Item Does Not Enter the Necessary Structure

None of the following items enters the necessary structure of this text, each for its own reason.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;LawAct\_{t\_T}( L\_S ) = 1　and　WholeSegmentLawAct( L\_S ) = 1

That the source Law is still acting when the target event occurs is not necessary; that it keeps acting throughout the stretch of propagation is not necessary either. Once c\_S has been legally issued and has entered generation, whether L\_S is still acting afterwards does not change its attribution; see 11.1.

But one may not claim, without a time index, that LawAct( L\_S ) = 1 is not necessary. At the source point r\_s, what the first layer seeks — an actual occurrence of contribution, belonging to the legal realization of L\_S, DynamicRole, and Rel(L\_S) — is already close to the two-layer condition of the upstream CurLawBear; the upstream The Necessity Form of Law-Action having established the two-way LawAct ⟺ CurLawBear, whether LawAct\_{r\_s}( L\_S ) = 1 follows further from SourceLawContribution at r\_s is a consequence of inheritance, not something this text may cancel.

So what this text cancels is: LawAct does not serve as a newly added independent necessary layer, and continuous action at the target time and over the whole stretch of propagation is not required; the derived relation at the source point is not among what is cancelled.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;LawDyn( L\_S ) = 1

Whether the source side itself has a dynamical event has nothing to do with whether its contribution enters the target's event. What this text asks about is the event on the target side.

What is cancelled is setting up the source's own occurrence of a LawDyn event as an independent necessary layer. Upstream takes LawDyn as a read-out at the level of the segment, not an unindexed state quantity, so if a downstream text formalizes this item, it must supply its segment index, and may not carry over the unindexed writing here.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;OutcomeSuccess = 1

See 7.2. Whether the target ends up different is not what interaction hangs on.

What is cancelled is success of outcome after the event, not whether e\_T itself occurs — the latter is explicitly required by the second layer; see 7.1 and the tightening clause of 7.2. "The outcome does not matter" may not be misread as "the event may not exist."

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;ButForNecessity = 1

See 7.2. Redundant contributions would be wrongly killed by this criterion — two arrive together, with one removed the target is as before, so both are adjudicated unnecessary, while both actually entered generation.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;NetDifference ≠ 0

It is not a uniform necessary condition of this text. A Continue can really hold when the net difference in the target's reading is zero, while the source's contribution still actually enters the mechanism of its continued execution — with net difference as a uniform condition, all such events would be excluded.

For the events Rise and Fall, if a difference in the corresponding direction is required by the definition of the event itself, it belongs to the upstream qualification of e\_T as an event, not to an additional condition of inter-Law interaction.

"The compensated contribution, with net change zero, did once enter" is not invoked here as a ground: if the net change really is zero while what is declared is a Rise, that Rise most likely did not occur, and it may not then still be called an interaction; see 7.2.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;UniqueCause = 1

Contributions from several sources are the ordinary case. Were uniqueness required, whatever has a second contribution would not be an interaction.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;InteractionSign ∈ { +, − }

Interaction may not be pressed into the two values positive and negative. What Migration shows is that the dimensions of Degree are mutually incomparable, and it cannot be assigned to increase or decrease; compress it, and that class of event has nowhere to go.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;WholeSegmentLawHold( L\_S ) = 1

Qualification is adjudicated at the source point; continuous holding over the whole stretch of propagation is not required; for the reason see 11.1.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;SpatialSeparation( L\_S, L\_T ) = 1

The two Law-role references are not required to be separated in space. One and the same stretch of medium can appear at once in the legal realizations of both references.

A shared carrier is neither sufficient to imply SameLaw = 1 nor sufficient to cancel this text's working distinction of the source role and the target role. Whether the two occurrences are one or two in the sense of a closed Law identity, this text does not adjudicate — that criterion has not yet been established; see 2.3.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;DifferentPhysicalRoot = 1

Likewise. Sameness or difference of physical root is only a fact of the layer of carriers: it implies neither SameLaw = 1 nor SameLaw = 0. It cannot stand in for the not-yet-established criterion of Law identity.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Transitivity = 1

See 7.4. Two segments each holding do not compose into a third.

Section Eleven

Attribution in Time

### 11.1 Anchoring at the Source Point

The qualification of SourceLawContribution is adjudicated at r\_s.

If c\_S was legally issued while L\_S was still a Law, and reached the target along a continuous actual generating chain, then L\_S's leaving qualification afterwards does not erase in reverse the attribution of the existing contribution.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Attribution is anchored at the source point

The reason is that what attribution records is the fact at the time of issue, and a fact that has happened does not become not to have happened through later changes of state.

11.2 Residue after Death Gives No Indefinite Attribution

But the other side must be fixed definitively at the same time.

After L\_S leaves qualification, an independent effect newly produced by the residual state of its former bearer, if it is not a successor in the continuous generating chain of the original c\_S, may not continue to be attributed to L\_S.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Residue after death gives no indefinite attribution

The difference between the two: the former is a contribution already issued, on its way; the latter a newly arising effect. The source point of the former lies within qualification; that of the latter outside it.

Without this clause, once a Law stood, every stir of its bearer thereafter could be attributed to its name forever; and that is no longer interaction but posthumous recognition.

Section Twelve

The General Formula and Minimality

### 12.1 The General Formula

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;LawInteraction^η\_F( L\_S → L\_T ; e\_T )  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;　⇒　LawInteractionBear^η\_F( L\_S, L\_T ; e\_T )

where

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;LawInteractionBear　≡　Bind\_INT( SourceLawContribution, TargetDynGeneration )

Here Bind\_INT is the binding operator: it takes the two layers as terms, and the conjunction it forms must be borne by one and the same witness, that is, SameInteractionBind = 1. The operator introduces no third term; it only prescribes that its two terms must fall on one and the same witness; what it rests on is the binding key β\_int established in Section Eight.

Expanded:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;LawInteractionBear = 1  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;　⟺　SourceLawContribution = 1 ∧ TargetDynGeneration = 1 ∧ SameInteractionBind = 1

The right-hand side has three conditions of standing, but only two ontological layers: SameInteractionBind only prescribes the sameness of the witness of the two layers, and adds no third kind of ontological content; see the end of Section Eight.

The binding must enter the definition of N, and may not be placed outside it. Were N formed only by the conjunction of the two layers, N would still hold even with the Binding zero and the two layers each one — the general formula would then promise not to contain the duty of binding that the general theorem proves, and the listing in Section Fourteen of a zero Binding as a breach would have nothing to attach to. Entered into its definition, the two places agree.

### 12.2 The General Theorem

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Theorem　Let LawInteraction^η\_F( L\_S → L\_T ; e\_T ) = 1; then  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;　　　　　SourceLawContribution = 1, TargetDynGeneration = 1, SameInteractionBind = 1

Proof. Reading back round by round.

First. Suppose the actual contribution entering the target side were only a side effect of the bearer, not belonging to L\_S's duty-bearing on the side of Law. Then what holds is at most a physical influence of the bearer on the target, not the declared interaction of Law with Law — while what the antecedent declares is precisely the latter; the two conflict. Hence

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;SourceLawContribution = 1

This step takes the failure world of Section Four.

Second. Suppose that same contribution did not actually enter the generation of the LawDyn event e\_T that L\_T declares. Then the source side only issued a contribution and did not form this interaction with the target — issuing and arriving are two things; see Section Six; while what the antecedent declares is that this interaction has already occurred; the two conflict. Hence

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;TargetDynGeneration = 1

This step takes the failure world of Section Six.

Third. Suppose the two layers cite not one and the same occurrence of contribution, or not one and the same generating sub-segment, or not one and the same target event. Then what exists is two facts, each true separately, and not "this contribution of L\_S entered this e\_T" — which conflicts with the singularity of one direct interaction. Hence

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;SameInteractionBind = 1

This step takes the failure world of Section Eight.

With the three steps established,

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;LawInteraction^η\_F　⇒　LawInteractionBear^η\_F

the two layers being constrained by the discipline of binding. ∎

### 12.3 Not to Be Written as an Equivalence

This text does not establish LawInteraction ⟺ LawInteractionBear. The ⟺ in 12.1 is only the definitional expansion within LawInteractionBear, not the two-way between O and N. The two are of different levels and not to be mixed.

Whether the three together suffice to imply interaction is the business of The Necessity Form of Law Interaction; if that text proves them insufficient, it forces out a new item, which this text may not bury beforehand.

### 12.4 None of the Three Can Be Deleted

Delete SourceLawContribution: then the bearer's non-Law side effects would also be recorded as inter-Law interaction. This is the failure world of Section Four.

Delete TargetDynGeneration: then a legal contribution of the source Law would be recorded as interaction even if it entered no event of the target. This is the failure world of Section Six.

Delete SameInteractionBind: then evidence could be pieced together from different occurrences of contribution. This is the failure world of Section Eight.

The three failures do not stand in for one another: the first errs in attribution, the second in not arriving, the third in the witness not being the same.

### 12.5 Stopping Here

With all three kept, no new independent necessary difference was forced out, so by the discipline of recursion of the general form the adding of items stops; see Section Nine.

Section Fourteen

Breach

By Part Three, Chapter One of the Canon Chapter, the breach method of the general form is: exhibit a system in which O still stands while … some component of N … does not stand.

The necessary direction of this text is breached if and only if, in a legal world keeping the working domain and the H conditions, LawInteraction = 1 while one of the following appears.

The recognition of its antecedent must follow the semantic anchor of 2.2: on the basis of the senses of mutual disturbance, mutual pursuit, and making-to-execute already in the canonical source, together with the event domain frozen by this group-form, it must be recognized that in that world there really is a direct dynamical interaction from the source role to the target role. This recognition may not be made by any item this text forces out — made by it, what is struck is a tautology, not the conclusion of this text.

Each of the three must name its ground:

K-INT1　SourceLawContribution = 0. That is, interaction holds, while the contribution entering generation does not belong to L\_S's duty-bearing — its source point has no qualification, or there is no actual occurrence of contribution, or it cannot be read back along L\_S's legal realization, DynamicRole, and Rel(L\_S). If any one of the three is lacking, this item holds; see 5.1.

K-INT2　TargetDynGeneration = 0. That is, interaction holds, while no contribution of L\_S enters any legal LawDyn event of L\_T — either it does not arrive, or the declared e\_T did not actually hold; see 7.1 and 7.2.

K-INT3　SameInteractionBind = 0. That is, interaction holds, while what the two layers recognize is not one and the same occurrence of contribution, not one and the same generating sub-segment, not one and the same target event — that is, the six items of the binding key β\_int cannot be satisfied jointly by one set of witnesses; see Section Eight.

Not breaches: the three together holding while LawInteraction = 0 — that is a question of the sufficient direction, belonging to The Necessity Form of Law Interaction, and may not be written as a breach of this text; the reading unchanged within a Continue — declared in 7.2; two Laws sharing a carrier — declared in Section Ten; two segments not composing into a third — declared in 7.4; one of the H conditions not holding — the conditionalization declared in 13.3, not a failure of a conclusion.
