# The Mathematical Form of Law Interaction

Section One

Purpose

### 1.1 What Problem This System Solves

A LawDyn event has occurred, and there is more than one Law in the field. The task is to judge whether one of those Laws really took part in bringing this event about.

The difficulty: when two Laws share a stretch of medium, their bearers always have all manner of physical influence on each other — heat changes impedance, occupancy squeezes bandwidth, current causes an offset on a shared ground. These influences really reach the target, yet have nothing to do with the duty of that Law. Judging by "both Laws are present" or "the target changed at the time" would count such influences as inter-Law interaction too.

This system gives an executable way of judging: its input is a field and trajectory already run, two Law-role references, and one target event; its output is

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Read^M\_INT ∈ { 1, 0, ⊥ }

1 means there is indeed a contribution belonging to the source Law's duty that actually entered the generation of this event; 0 means everything was read and there is none; ⊥ means the material is insufficient to adjudicate — this value is not written as 0; not being able to read and reading without its holding are two different things.

The judging runs in two checks and one binding: the first checks whether this contribution was issued by that Law's duty; the second, whether it really entered the generating mechanism of this event; then it is checked whether what the two steps recognize is one and the same contribution, one and the same stretch of execution, one and the same event.

### 1.2 The Structure Formalized

The three steps above are not newly established by this text; they are the necessary structure proved by Chapter 10:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;S\_IntBear = LawInteractionBear

from the general formula of Section Twelve of that text. This text establishes a formal system for it:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;S\_IntBear ⟼ M\_IntBear

The form-kind is the mathematical form, a principal form, set up as an independent text, first edition. The form-kind is determined by Canon Chapter 1, Part Three, Chapter Two. Its entry conditions are that the structure S is already established by a proof principal form …; the components of S already have stable terminology. S\_IntBear was proved by the sealed Chapter 10, and both layers and the discipline of binding have stable terms.

### 1.3 Boundaries

What Chapter 10 proved is LawInteraction ⇒ LawInteractionBear; its converse has not yet been proved, and belongs to Chapter 12, The Necessity Form of Law Interaction.

So the structural input of this system takes only Bear, and read-back stops at

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Read^M\_INT = 1　⇒　LawInteractionBear = 1

1 does not imply that this interaction holds; it implies only that the structure necessary for interaction holds.

The trajectory η relied on is a legal actual trajectory of execution that has already occurred, so all enumeration is confined within the actual support that has already occurred; see 5.3. This system does not ask "could it have happened," only "in this one that has already happened, did that Law enter."

Section Two

Core Thesis

### 2.1 The Central Content

What this system is to express exactly is one criterion: to separate a contribution belonging to a Law's duty from the physical influence of a bearer; and to make every adjudication fall on one and the same contribution, one and the same stretch of execution, one and the same event.

The separation turns on whether the contribution can be read all the way along the duty: from the formal witness at the source point, to the realization of this duty, to the declared duty, to that Law's relational organization. Whatever reads through along this chain belongs to the contribution of duty; whatever runs only along the bearer's physical pathway does not.

Falling on one and the same thing turns on four sorts of joint witnessing: the three items of the first layer must rest on one and the same record; the event fact and the mechanism entry of the second layer must belong to one and the same event; along the path, one and the same line of provenance must run from the source point all the way to the target; what the two layers recognize must project back onto one and the same key of the object layer. If any of the four is lacking, each place is true while together they make no single interaction — that is pieced-together evidence.

Whatever cannot be read returns the third value, and does not pass for having been read and not holding.

### 3.2 The Sequence of Definitions

Numbered item by item below. Each first says what it does, then gives the form. For every item containing a relation, its properties are declared item by item in Section Seven.

D1　The space of occurrences of contribution

Collects every registrable occurrence of contribution on the source side.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;𝒞^occ\_INT

A purely formal auxiliary object.

D2　The record of an occurrence of contribution

Sets up, on the machine side, a record for one occurrence of contribution of the object layer.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;ĉ\_S = \[ L\_S, R\_S, ρ\_S, d\_S, r\_s, π^INT\_contrib( ζ\_{r\_s} ) \] ∈ 𝒞^occ\_INT

The fields:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;R\_S　the candidate formal witness on the source side, to be checked. Whether it belongs to 𝒮( F\_{r\_s}, L\_S, I\_S ) is adjudicated by D4  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;ρ\_S　the candidate realization, to be checked. Whether it legally belongs to RoleReal^{RoleCtx}\_{R\_S}( d\_S ) is adjudicated by D6  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;d\_S　the duty this contribution declares. Whether it belongs to Rel( L\_S ) is adjudicated by D6  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;r\_s　the source point from which the contribution actually issues  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;π^INT\_contrib　a purely formal projection reading this contribution of duty out of the actual witness of execution

This item only registers the fields of candidates, and does not assert that they hold. Were the fields written as membership relations, every record that can be constructed would already satisfy the core conditions of D4 and D6 beforehand, and the negative cases of those two items could never enter the candidate set — the representation of candidates would quietly become a representation of successes. So each membership relation is left to be adjudicated by the corresponding read-out.

R\_S and ρ\_S belong to two levels, following the existing hierarchy of The Law Group-Form: first the level of the formal witness, then under it the concrete realization of this duty. The two are not merged under one name.

ρ\_S enters only the certificate on the machine side, and can be listed in β^M\_int and W\_S; it does not enter the object-level β\_int.

ĉ\_S is a record on the machine side; the occurrence of the object layer is still written c\_S. The two are linked by a projection:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;π\_c( ĉ\_S ) = c\_S

By the first class of 3.1, the object-level c\_S is only invoked and not redefined; what this item defines is its representation on the machine side.

D3　Equal projected values are not the same occurrence

Separates "the readings are the same" from "it is the same one."

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Value( c\_a ) = Value( c\_b )　⇏　c\_a = c\_b

Two contributions with the same projected value are not necessarily the same occurrence. The identity of contributions across time does not belong to this system, so the record has no field of the kind id\_c.

D4　Read-out of qualification at the source point

Judges whether the source side has formal Law qualification at the point from which the contribution issues.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;SrcLawQual^M( ĉ\_S ) ∈ { 1, 0, ⊥ }

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;1　R\_S ∈ 𝒮( F\_{r\_s}, L\_S, I\_S )  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;0　the solution set on F\_{r\_s} has been obtained completely, and R\_S is not in it  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;⊥　the field state at r\_s cannot be read, or the solution set could not be obtained at that point

Qualification is adjudicated at the real source point of issue r\_s. This item adjudicates only the level of the formal solution; whether the concrete realization ρ\_S of this duty is legal is adjudicated by D6.

D5　Read-out of the existence of the contribution

Judges whether the actual witness of execution really contains this occurrence of contribution.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;ContribOcc^M( ĉ\_S ) ∈ { 1, 0, ⊥ }

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;1　the witness of execution at r\_s is completely readable, and π^INT\_contrib obtains that occurrence on it  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;0　that witness is completely readable, and there is no such occurrence on it  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;⊥　that witness cannot be read, or π^INT\_contrib is undefined on it

D6　Read-out of attribution to duty

Judges whether this contribution really belongs to L\_S's duty-bearing.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;SrcDutyAttrib^M( ĉ\_S ) ∈ { 1, 0, ⊥ }

Read-back runs along this chain:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;R\_S　→　ρ\_S　→　d\_S　→　Rel( L\_S )

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;1　ρ\_S ∈ RoleReal^{RoleCtx}\_{R\_S}( d\_S ), d\_S ∈ Rel( L\_S ), and DynamicRole^M confirms that R\_S has legal dynamical role-bearing in that domain of duty  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;0　every link of the chain is readable, and the read-back fails — for example, it runs only along the bearer's physical pathway and not along the pathway of duty  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;⊥　some link of the chain cannot be read, or the duty obtained by read-back is not unique

DynamicRole^M is responsible only for the legality of the duty role on the side of Law; it belongs to the dynamical-role branch of Law qualification. The correspondence of this occurrence with ρ\_S is confirmed jointly by the witness of execution of D5 and the occurrence–duty binding of this item.

This item does not infer LawAct from DynamicRole^M alone: the legality of the role on the side of qualification and whether it is currently acting belong to two layers, the latter belonging to Chapters 1 and 2.

The physical side effects of the bearer do not count toward this item; the separation of the two follows the separation of layers between formal witness and physical bearing inherited in Section Six.

D7　The reader of the first layer

Combines the three items above to give the reading of the first layer.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;ReadSrcLawContrib^M( ĉ\_S ) = Bind^M\_SRC( SrcLawQual^M, ContribOcc^M, SrcDutyAttrib^M ) ∈ { 1, 0, ⊥ }

where Bind^M\_SRC first checks whether the three items fall on one and the same set of keys, then takes its value by zero-absorbing conjunction:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Bind^M\_SRC = 0　one of the three items is 0, or the keys the three rest on are not the same  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Bind^M\_SRC = 1　all three items are 1 and the keys are the same  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Bind^M\_SRC = ⊥　the keys are the same, there is no 0, and at least one item is ⊥; or the sameness of the keys itself cannot be adjudicated

Sameness of keys means that the three items all rest on one and the same set ( L\_S, R\_S, ρ\_S, d\_S, r\_s, ĉ\_S ). Bind^M\_SRC is a well-formed binding internal to the reader of the first layer; for its difference from the binding of the object layer see 3.3.

D8　Actual support of execution and the relation of restricted segment

Points out, on the machine side, the stretch of execution already occurred that this interaction involves.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Ẑ\_{e\_T} = ( ζ\_a, …, ζ\_b )

is the representation, on the record of execution, of the actual event sub-segment Z\_{e\_T} of the upstream target event e\_T, with π\_Z( Ẑ\_{e\_T} ) = Z\_{e\_T}.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Ẑ\_ST = ( ζ\_p, …, ζ\_q ),　Ẑ\_ST ⪯ Ẑ\_{e\_T}

Ẑ\_ST is a contiguous ordered restricted segment of the record of execution already occurred, taken from η. ⪯ is the relation of contiguous ordered restricted segment; for its properties see 7.2.

The actual generating sub-segment of the object layer is still written Z\_ST; the two are linked by a projection:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;π\_Z( Ẑ\_ST ) = Z\_ST

As in D2: the object-level Z\_ST is only invoked; what this item defines is its representation on the machine side.

D9　The formal witness path

Takes out, within Z\_ST, a path for examination.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;P\_ST = ( ζ\_{r\_0}, …, ζ\_{r\_m} ),　r\_0 \< … \< r\_m,　P\_ST ⪯ Ẑ\_ST

Each node is an item of the record of execution; write z\_i ≔ ζ\_{r\_i}. This notation is only for convenience of writing: wherever an index is needed, r\_i is written; wherever an item of the record is needed, z\_i or ζ\_{r\_i}; the two are not mixed.

A purely formal auxiliary object.

D10　Items of actual execution and continuity of provenance

First the type of the items that flow along the path is established, then the joining of boundaries of two adjacent steps is fixed, and last the chain of provenance running through the whole path is established — each edge joining on its own is not enough to adjudicate succession; one and the same line of provenance must be passed all the way down.

First, establish the general type of items of actual execution:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;𝒪^act\_INT

Its members, written o, are the items actually going into and out of each step in the record of execution — they can be ordinary results of transformation, quantities in a medium, outputs of gates, and need not be records of contribution on the side of Law.

The path may pass on the way through channels, gates, media, and non-Law transforming nodes, and the items at these nodes are o; were they all required to be records of Law contribution, the direct paths permitted by Chapter 10 would on the contrary be excluded. Whether another Law takes over on the way is examined separately by D11.

The actual inputs and outputs of each step are given by the provenance reading fixed by Γ\_path:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Out^M( z\_i ) ⊆ 𝒪^act\_INT　　the actual output items of that step  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Arg^M( z\_{i+1} ) ⊆ 𝒪^act\_INT　the actual input items of the original generating mechanism of the next step

Read-out of the joining of boundaries:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;InputLink^M( z\_i, z\_{i+1} ) ∈ { 1, 0, ⊥ }

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;1　there exists o\_i ∈ Out^M( z\_i ) ∩ Arg^M( z\_{i+1} )  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;0　both sets are completely read, and their intersection is empty  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;⊥　one of the two sets cannot be read, or the identity of the items cannot be adjudicated

This item governs only whether the inputs and outputs of two adjacent steps join, not whether what joins is one and the same line of provenance. Its conjunction edge by edge alone is not enough to adjudicate succession: each edge can be witnessed separately by unrelated items.

Let the first edge be witnessed by a, a ∈ Out^M( z\_0 ) ∩ Arg^M( z\_1 ); while z\_1 has another, unrelated input x, whose output is b, what a produces being c; let the second edge be witnessed by b, b ∈ Out^M( z\_1 ) ∩ Arg^M( z\_2 ). Each edge is 1, while a's line of provenance is broken at z\_1. This is pieced-together evidence within the path.

So a further item must be set up, checking whether within each step there really is a relation of generation between input and output:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;ProvStep^M( z\_i ; o^in\_i, o^out\_i ) ∈ { 1, 0, ⊥ }

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;1　the actual witness of execution of that step shows that o^out\_i is the related successor that o^in\_i actually generated or was transformed into in this step  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;0　that witness is completely readable, and there is definitely no such relation of generation on it  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;⊥　the identity of generation within that step cannot be adjudicated

Then the anchoring of the two ends:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;AnchorItem^M( ĉ\_S, o\_0 ) ∈ { 1, 0, ⊥ }　whether o\_0 is the actual output item corresponding to ĉ\_S  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;TargetInput^M( o\_m, w\_{e\_T} ) ∈ { 1, 0, ⊥ }　whether o\_m enters the witness of mechanism of the target event

The chain of provenance:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;ProvChain^M( ĉ\_S, P\_ST, w\_{e\_T} ) ∈ { 1, 0, ⊥ }

takes the value 1 if and only if there exists a string of items of actual execution

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;( o\_0, o\_1, …, o\_m )

such that all the following hold:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;AnchorItem^M( ĉ\_S, o\_0 ) = 1  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;for each internal step　o\_{i-1} ∈ Arg^M( z\_i ),　o\_i ∈ Out^M( z\_i ),　ProvStep^M( z\_i ; o\_{i-1}, o\_i ) = 1  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;TargetInput^M( o\_m, w\_{e\_T} ) = 1

If all such strings have been checked and none holds, it takes 0; if no string holds and some item in it cannot be adjudicated, it takes ⊥.

Hence

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;StepwiseInputContinuity( ĉ\_S, P\_ST, w\_{e\_T} ) ≔ ProvChain^M( ĉ\_S, P\_ST, w\_{e\_T} )

What is tracked is the continuity of one and the same line of provenance: from that item on the source side, passed through the actual generation of each step all the way to the witness of the target's mechanism — not each edge happening to have some item joining.

What is tracked here is the continuity of provenance, not equality of value: the value of the contribution can change on the way, while where it came from is unbroken.

D11　An intermediate source on the side of Law

Judges whether another Law acquires a new source anchor on the way along the path.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;IntermediateLawSource^M( P\_ST, w\_{e\_T} ) ∈ { 1, 0, ⊥ }

where w\_{e\_T} is the witness of the target's mechanism used by the certificate D12 is currently examining; it is passed in as a parameter, and not existentially quantified anew within this item.

It takes the value 1 if and only if there exist, within the path, an index r\_i and a machine record constructed according to D2,

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;ĉ\_M = \[ L\_M, R\_M, ρ\_M, d\_M, r\_i, π^INT\_contrib( ζ\_{r\_i} ) \] ∈ 𝒞^occ\_INT

such that

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;ReadSrcLawContrib^M( ĉ\_M ) = 1

and the suffix leading to that target witness is really taken over by ĉ\_M. Write that suffix as

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;P^{(i:m)}\_ST = ( ζ\_{r\_i}, …, ζ\_{r\_m} )

Takeover is

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;ProvChain^M( ĉ\_M, P^{(i:m)}\_ST, w\_{e\_T} ) = 1

where w\_{e\_T} is the one passed in. Then the direct path L\_S → L\_T originally leading to that witness is cut off.

No separate witness is taken here. Were an existential quantifier allowed to arise anew within this item, then even if some Law on the way had suffix provenance only toward another target witness, it would cut off the present direct path leading to this witness — that is another kind of pieced-together evidence: the ground of the cutting and what is cut are not one and the same event.

Two things must be kept apart: a Law acquiring qualification for contribution within the path is only ReadSrcLawContrib^M( ĉ\_M ) = 1; whether it really took over the suffix leading to the target is adjudicated separately by the formula above. Where the former holds and the latter does not, that Law merely has some activity on the way, which does not constitute cutting off.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;LawAct( L\_M ) = 1　⇏　IntermediateLawSource^M( P\_ST, w\_{e\_T} ) = 1

A Law on the way having some activity is not the same as its acquiring a new source anchor. This item is a read-out internal to the path, not a new LawDyn event.

D12　The direct generating path

Judges whether a path already occurred really carries ĉ\_S into the generation of the target event.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;DirectGenPath^M\_INT( ĉ\_S, P\_ST, w\_{e\_T} ) ∈ { 1, 0, ⊥ }

The value 1 requires all five of the following at once. Each of the five has its own signature and values.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;ActualPath( P\_ST ) ∈ { 1, 0, ⊥ }  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;　1　each r\_i is an actual index of η, and each ζ\_{r\_i} is taken from η's record of execution  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;　0　one of the items is not within η — that is, it is taken from a possible path, a replayed path, or a counterfactual path  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;　⊥　whether some item is within η cannot be adjudicated

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;SourceAnchor( P\_ST, ĉ\_S ) ∈ { 1, 0, ⊥ }  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;　1　r\_0 = r\_s, that is, the first item z\_0 = ζ\_{r\_s} is exactly the step of the source point registered by ĉ\_S  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;　0　r\_0 is readable and not equal to r\_s  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;　⊥　r\_0 or r\_s cannot be read

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;StepwiseInputContinuity( ĉ\_S, P\_ST, w\_{e\_T} ) ∈ { 1, 0, ⊥ }　see D10, that is, ProvChain^M: there exists a string of items of actual execution which, from the item anchored by ĉ\_S, passes through the actual generation of each step all the way to the witness of the target's mechanism

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;TargetMechanismEntry( ĉ\_S, P\_ST, w\_{e\_T} ) ∈ { 1, 0, ⊥ }　see D14, whose P\_ST and w\_{e\_T} are those used by this item

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;IntermediateLawSource^M( P\_ST, w\_{e\_T} ) ∈ { 1, 0, ⊥ }　see D11, here required to take 0; its w\_{e\_T} is the same target-mechanism witness used by this item

The five combine by zero-absorbing conjunction: if any item gives a determinate value contrary to what is required, DirectGenPath^M\_INT = 0; if all five are as required, it is 1; if there is no contrary determinate value and there is ⊥, it is ⊥.

These five are the formal unfolding of the upstream "direct" and of TargetDynGeneration.

D15　The reader of the second layer

Combines the event certificate and the mechanism entry to give the reading of the second layer.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;ReadTargetDynGen^M( ĉ\_S, P\_ST, e\_T, w\_{e\_T} ) = Bind^M\_T( TargetEventCert^M( e\_T ), TargetMechEntry^M\_INT( ĉ\_S, P\_ST, w\_{e\_T} ) ) ∈ { 1, 0, ⊥ }

D16　The machine binding key

Records, on the machine side, all the key positions this one examination rests on. The six principal positions are all filled by machine quantities; object keys are not carried in as ready-made fields:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;β^M\_int = ( L\_S, r\_s, ĉ\_S, Ẑ\_ST, L\_T, e\_T ; R\_S, ρ\_S, d\_S, E\_k, w\_{e\_T}, P\_ST )

Migration　the target must first satisfy incomparability of profiles, which belongs to the side of event fact; ĉ\_S entering one of Cross^+\_k or Cross^-\_k suffices. Set

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;MigPlus^M,　MigMinus^M  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;MigEnter^M = MigPlus^M ∨₃ MigMinus^M

The two stand side by side, and are not merged into one sign: what Migration refers to is incomparability of profiles, and there is no distinction of increase and decrease to speak of.

4.5 Branching, Merging, and Indistinguishable Mixing

Branching　When ĉ\_S branches toward P\_1 and P\_2, it suffices that at least one actual direct path reaches the target witness to obtain a witness of target generation. It is not required that every branch arrive.

Merging with the identity of inputs preserved　Let Arg^M( y ) = { o\_A, o\_B }, the two entering one merging step y together, whose output is o′. If the actual witness of that step can give separately

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;ProvStep^M( y ; o\_A, o′ ) = 1　and　ProvStep^M( y ; o\_B, o′ ) = 1

then both lines of provenance can continue, and each ProvChain^M can take 1. No necessity in the but-for sense is required here, nor any numerical apportionment.

Merging with the identity of inputs lost　If it is known only that o′ exists, and it cannot be adjudicated which of o\_A and o\_B it inherits, then ProvStep^M( y ; · , o′ ) = ⊥, the chain of provenance takes ⊥ with it, and so

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;DirectGenPath^M\_INT = ⊥

This is a value of not being able to read, belonging to a different layer from zero on the side of the object. In this case this system makes no numerical decomposition, and has no quantity of the kind of a proportion of contribution.

### 5.3 The Range of Enumeration

All enumeration is confined within the frozen finite actual support. That domain is defined as

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;FiniteActualSupport( η, e\_T, Γ\_INT ) = { ζ\_r ∈ η \| r ∈ \[ p\_0, q\_0 \] }

where \[ p\_0, q\_0 \] is a finite interval of indices frozen in advance by Γ\_path, containing all the indices of the evaluation segment E\_k in which e\_T lies, and extended forward to the depth of tracing fixed by Γ\_path.

That interval is finite, and η's record on it is finite too, so the ĉ\_S and P\_ST that can be enumerated on it are finitely many.

The same holds for the enumeration of chains of provenance. Hence

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;FiniteActualSupport finite　⇒　P\_ST finite　⇒　candidate chains of provenance finite

So each of the seven steps of 5.2 terminates.

### 5.4 Propagation of Three Values

Every read-out is three-valued. Propagation follows the zero-absorbing rule: a determinate 0 takes priority over ⊥; if there is no 0 and there is ⊥, it is ⊥; only if all three items are 1 is it 1.

The third value records that the machine side cannot read, which belongs to a different layer from zero on the side of the object; for the distinction in read-back see 8.3.

### 5.5 Aggregation of Several Certificates

What is aggregated here is witness of the existential type: one certificate that holds suffices. So take

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Agg\_INT = ∨₃

The rule is:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Agg\_INT = 1　at least one complete certificate takes 1  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Agg\_INT = 0　the candidate domain has been solved completely, and all legal candidates are 0  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Agg\_INT = ⊥　there is no 1, and at least one candidate is undecided, or the enumeration of candidates is itself incomplete

### 5.6 Two Cases of an Empty Candidate Set

An empty candidate set has two causes: one, that after complete enumeration there really is no candidate; two, that the ability of the reader or the enumeration was insufficient and failed to enumerate them. The two must be adjudicated separately.

And checking only "the enumeration by protocol is complete" is not enough. A protocol that from the start looks at only half of the paths can also enumerate its own candidate domain cleanly, so that this item takes 1, while the real witness lies exactly in the other half — reading back an object-level 0 on that basis would be wrong. So completeness must be in two parts.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;EnumComplete\_INT ∈ { 1, 0 }

The value 1 requires that 𝒲\_INT has been exhausted according to Γ\_INT, and that every candidate in it has obtained a determinate value. This item governs whether the machine side has enumerated cleanly.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;WitnessCover\_INT ∈ { 1, 0 }

The value 1 requires: every legal combination that exists at the object layer — with SourceLawContribution, TargetDynGeneration, and SameInteractionBind all present — has at least one corresponding W\_INT in 𝒲\_INT. This item governs whether the candidate domain fixed by the protocol has left out a real witness.

It is not an internal state the solver can compute by itself, but a bridge of complete coverage between the formal and the object, registered as G-INT3; see 13.2.

The two combine into

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;ReadComplete\_INT = EnumComplete\_INT ∧ WitnessCover\_INT

So the condition of read-back is

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Read^M\_INT = 0 ∧ ReadComplete\_INT = 1　⇒　LawInteractionBear = 0

The case ReadComplete\_INT = 0 does not enter read-back. It then means only "it cannot at present be proved that the domain of read-out is complete," not that LawInteractionBear = 0.

Section Ten

This section proves two things: that this formal system really realizes the structure it serves; and that within the readable domain, reading and structure correspond to each other.

Theorem One　M\_IntBear ⊨ S\_IntBear

When the upstream conditions, the frozen protocol Γ\_INT, and the corresponding readability conditions of G-INT1 and G-INT2 hold, the following five items hold. This theorem does not need G-INT3, which is needed only by Theorem Two.

First, SolveIntBear can be executed within the frozen finite actual support.

Second, the propagation of three values is legal.

Third, Read^M\_INT = 1 can be read back item by item to the necessary structure of Chapter 10.

Fourth, the auxiliary objects newly added by this text are all purely formal, adding no ontological condition and no field quantity.

Fifth, none of the auxiliary objects enters the underlying laws of running.

Proof.

First, by 5.2 and 5.3: what each of the seven steps enumerates lies within FiniteActualSupport, which is frozen according to Γ\_INT and finite; the ĉ\_S, P\_ST, and candidate chains of provenance ( o\_0, …, o\_m ) on it are all finite and enumerable, so the procedure terminates.

Section Eleven

Breach

Any one of the following eight holding breaches this system. The first four concern read-out and read-back; the last four, levels and types.

K-MI1　Omission of correspondence. Some load-bearing item of Chapter 10 has no correspondent in M.

K-MI2　Wrong attribution. ReadSrcLawContrib^M = 1, while on read-back what was read is found to be in fact a physical side effect of the bearer.

K-MI3　A pieced-together target certificate. Different witnesses of event and of mechanism are pieced into one and the same target certificate, and Bind^M\_T fails.

K-MI4　A false path. A possible path, a replayed path, or a counterfactual path passes for an actual path, and ActualPath fails.

K-MI5　Directness wrongly signed. An intermediate boundary of a new source on the side of Law determinately exists, while DirectGenPath^M\_INT = 1 is still signed out.

K-MI6　⊥ written as 0. Provenance cannot be adjudicated, and 0 is returned.

K-MI7　Signing 1 on correlation. A key witness cannot be adjudicated, and 1 is returned on correlation, simultaneity, or change at the endpoints.

K-MI8　Read-back across levels. Read^M\_INT = 1 is read directly as LawInteraction = 1.

Not breaches: counterexamples to the necessity of the general form, which belong to Chapter 10; the sufficient direction not proved, see 1.3 and 8.5; G not closed and ⊥ returned, which is a value declared by 5.4, not a failure of a conclusion.

Section Twelve

A Check of Six Classes of False Positive

The event holding is not the same as the source taking part. This has its own form in each of the six classes of event, and must be checked class by class.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Start　　both ends start at the same time, while the source did not enter the target's mechanism of Start  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Continue　the target is continuing, while the source took no part in the actual Continuation  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Stop　　a redundant bearer is knocked out, while the actual Stop has another source  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Rise　　the target Rises, while the source did not enter GainAdv^Γ\_k  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Fall　　the target Falls, while the source did not enter LostAdv^Γ\_k  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Migration　the profiles are incomparable, while the source did not enter Cross^+ or Cross^-

The six cases together show one thing:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;EventFact = 1　⇏　TargetDynGeneration = 1

So D15 uses Bind^M\_T to bind the event certificate and the mechanism entry together, and D14 checks entry separately: whatever takes only the event certificate is a false positive.
