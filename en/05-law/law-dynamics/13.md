# The Stability Form of Law Ecology

Section One

Purpose, Form-Kind, and the Single Task

### 1.1 What Problem This Text Solves

A group of Laws works together to form an organization: some Laws each hold their place, and between some Laws there is repeated interaction, event by event. In the later evolution of the field this organization meets disturbances — members leave, bearers are replaced, degrees rise and fall, interactions are interrupted.

What is asked is: is this organization still there? If for a while it is not, can it come back according to the rules? And does "still there" speak of that original organization itself, or of there still being one organization of the kind? The answers to these two questions can differ, and must be signed separately.

### 1.2 The Single Task

Fix a template of a multi-Law organization realized jointly by formal Law and formal LawInteraction facts; fix the domain of permitted variation, the evaluation protocol, and the dynamic stability protocol; and ask how, along the legal successor trajectories, the organization's fixed realization and its set of realizations are respectively preserved, exit, are replaced, and return, and under what strength of quantifier stability can be legally signed out.

1.3 Two Axes: Stability May Not Be Written Bare

Any stability signed must state two things at once: which layer of object is stable, and which level of trajectories is covered.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;object layer　x ∈ { fix, set }  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;trajectory quantifier layer　Λ ∈ { single, family, guaranteed }

Writing "ecological stability" bare is prohibited by this text. Wherever stability is spoken of, x and Λ must be specified.

### 1.4 Form-Kind and Version

The stability form, an auxiliary form, set up as an independent text, first edition.

By Canon Chapter 1, Part Four, Chapter Fifteen, the purpose of the stability form is to prove whether an already-established qualification, structure, object, or solution can be preserved within a declared domain of permitted variation; whether, if it exits, it can return under lawful dynamics; and to distinguish the quantifier levels of preservation by the same bearer, preservation by some bearer, stability of a declared trajectory family, and guaranteed stability over all lawful trajectories. The Stability Form proves the preservation of an established qualification under variation, not the first existence, first reachability, or first occurrence of the object.

### 1.5 What This Text Is Not

It is not a general form: it does not prove "what an ecology must minimally have in order to stand."

It is not a necessity form: it does not prove "an ecology is necessarily stable."

It is not a reachability form: it does not prove "there exists a path of recovery." That there exists a path of recovery and stability are two things; guaranteed stability over all legal trajectories is further still a different thing.

It is not an identity form: it does not answer whether the bearer after return is the original Law.

### 2.2 No New Ontology

This text adds no ontological entity called Eco.

Nor does it add: PersistentInteraction, EcoForce, EcoField, EcoTrigger, SameEco.

The items set up below — EcoQual, EcoCert, node-role, interaction-role, JointEcoBind — are all objects of organization and certificate at the layer of stability review. All of them can finally be unfolded back into

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Law　+　LawInteraction　+　a role template and evaluation protocol frozen in advance

So this text has not used the authority of an auxiliary form to write a separate "general form of ecology."

This clause must be placed at the front. Otherwise the reader will ask: what an ecology is has not yet been proved, so why stabilize it first? The answer: this text claims no new ontology of ecology; what it stabilizes is a certificate of qualification of a multi-Law organization constituted by already established upstream facts.

Section Three

The Template of Ecological Qualification and Current Qualification

### 3.1 The Template

Freeze

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Σ\_Eco = ( 𝒩\_Eco, ℰ\_Eco, Γ\_qual )

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;𝒩\_Eco　the set of node-roles. Each ν is a place of ecological duty, and must be borne by a formal Law-role realization  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;ℰ\_Eco　the set of interaction-roles. Each ε prescribes between which two node-roles what relational duty must be fulfilled by formal event-level LawInteraction occurrences  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Γ\_qual　the protocol for how current qualification is read

Γ\_qual governs only the reading of current qualification, not stability. It freezes the rules of node-window evaluation, interaction-window evaluation, the selection and aggregation of events, joint-witness binding, and three-valued read-out.

The stability protocol does not enter Σ\_Eco. "What the ecology is," "how it is permitted to vary," and "what counts as its being stable" are three things.

3.2 An Interaction-Role Is Not a Persistent Edge

This is the highest-risk line of the whole text.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;InteractionRole　≠　LawInteractionOccurrence

still less may one write

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;InteractionRole　=　PersistentInteraction

The upstream LawInteraction is a formal interaction occurrence on some one LawDyn event. So the long-standing relations of an ecology can only be fulfilled repeatedly, under a frozen window protocol, by many formal occurrences.

A long-standing edge is not a new entity, but

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;a role qualification read repeatedly out of the history of events

This is why this text must evaluate by windows rather than by an instantaneous static graph.

### 3.3 Current Window Qualification

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;EcoCert = NodeWindowQual ∧₃ EdgeWindowQual ∧₃ JointEcoBind

∧₃ is the zero-absorbing three-valued conjunction: if all three items are 1 it is 1; if any item is determinately 0 it is 0; if there is no 0 and at least one item is ⊥ it is ⊥.

The three values are established at the entry to qualification, not only from Section Seven onward. So what has EcoCert = ⊥ does not enter ℰ\_k: to admit it would treat "cannot be read" as "read and holds." And whether ℰ\_k is really empty further requires the candidate domain to be completely adjudicable, as 7.2 demands.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;NodeWindowQual　every node-role obtains, in that window, formal Law bearing by the frozen protocol  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;EdgeWindowQual　every interaction-role is fulfilled, in that window, by formal LawInteraction occurrences by the frozen protocol of event evaluation  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;JointEcoBind　　the bearing of the nodes and the occurrences belong to one and the same role realization

Not one of the three can be deleted.

Delete JointEcoBind, and the nodes are taken from one set of certificates and the edges from another, which can piece together an ecology that does not really exist.

3.4 Two Items Do Not Enter Current Qualification

Coverage is not set up as a fourth item: it is the result of the two items Node and Edge being full, not a further condition.

JointViability is not set up either: if it means "can hold jointly now," it is already contained in the three items; if it means "can still hold jointly in future," that is a matter of the layer of stability, and putting it into current qualification would be circular.

Section Four

Structural Realization, Event Witnesses, and the Window-Level Solution Set

### 4.1 The Two Must Be Kept Apart

A structural realization of an ecology that can be compared across windows is written

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;E^str = μ\_N

that is, the bearing structure of the node-roles: who bears each place.

On the side of interaction-roles no separate component is set up. The roles at its two ends are fixed by ε in ℰ\_Eco, and the bearers at its two ends are uniquely determined by μ\_N, so its structural assignment can be derived from Σ\_Eco and μ\_N and is not recorded separately — recorded, it would be an empty slot with no degree of freedom.

While within some evaluation window H\_k, the event occurrences actually used to prove that each interaction-role is fulfilled are recorded separately as

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;W\_k

So

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;W\_0 ≠ W\_1

is ordinary.

A concrete interaction occurrence token may not be put into the fixed identity across windows — put in, that fixed layer would almost certainly die of itself in the next window, and what is stabilized would become an unrepeatable event.

4.2 The Static Certificate of Qualification

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;EcoCert( E^str, W\_k ; Σ\_Eco, H\_k )

### 4.3 The Window-Level Solution Set

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;ℰ\_k ≔ ℰ( F\|\_{H\_k}, Σ\_Eco ) = { E^str \| ∃ W\_k　EcoCert( E^str, W\_k ; Σ\_Eco, H\_k ) = 1 }

This solution set is the base of every reading of stability below.

It must be stated exactly: ℰ\_k ≠ ∅ says only that current qualification exists, and does not imply stability.

### 4.4 The Temporal Semantics of Events

An interaction-role may not require "LawInteraction at every micro-moment." Evaluation falls on observation windows frozen in advance

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;H\_0, H\_1, …, H\_n

and the reading of events for each edge-role is frozen by Γ\_qual.

A concrete application may require at least one within some window, or a stricter coverage; all such are parameters of the protocol, not written as constants of the theory.

The core discipline is only two: the rules of evaluation are frozen in advance; 0 ≠ ⊥. When the material is insufficient, "no interaction seen" may not be adjudicated 0 outright.

Section Five

The Stability Instance and the Domain of Permitted Variation

### 5.1 The Stability Instance

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Ξ\_Eco = ( F\|\_{H\_0}, Σ\_Eco, E^str\_0, Δ\_Eco, ℋ\_Eco )

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;E^str\_0 ∈ ℰ\_0　the fixed realization already standing in the initial window  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Δ\_Eco　　the domain of permitted variation  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;ℋ\_Eco　　the stability protocol, freezing at least the domain of observation, the time limit of recovery, the duration of continued verification, and the rules of dynamic read-out

Σ\_Eco and Δ\_Eco must be kept apart: what the ecology is, and how it is permitted to vary, are not one thing.

### 5.2 The Domain of Permitted Variation

The variations Δ\_Eco permits include: changes of field state; the rise and fall of Degree; the starting and stopping of LawAct; the exit of members; legal replacement; the joining of new Laws or additional interactions; the renewal of interaction occurrences; changes of non-Law physical paths.

5.3 Alterations Not in the Domain of Permitted Variation

Rewriting Σ\_Eco, rewriting Γ\_qual, rewriting Δ\_Eco itself, rewriting ℋ\_Eco — none of the four is a disturbance within the original stability instance.

Once any is changed,

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Ξ\_Eco　→　Ξ′\_Eco

and what is stabilized thereafter is no longer the original instance. Claiming that the original stability instance has returned after changing the template, the domain of permitted variation, the layer of identity, or the evaluation protocol constitutes a breach; see 13.2.

Section Six

The Fixed Layer and the Existence Layer

### 6.1 The Two Layers

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Q^fix\_Eco( k ) = 1　⟺　E^str\_0 ∈ ℰ\_k  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Q^set\_Eco( k ) = 1　⟺　ℰ\_k ≠ ∅

The former asks whether the original realization is still there; the latter, whether one of the kind still remains.

### 6.2 One Direction

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Q^fix = 1　⇒　Q^set = 1

The converse does not hold. Legal replacement is its counterexample: E^str\_0 ∉ ℰ\_k while ℰ\_k ≠ ∅ — the original has exited, and another legal one has taken its place.

### 6.3 Only Two Layers

The trajectory is not set up as a third layer of qualification between the two. It belongs to another axis; see Section Seven.

Section Seven

The Dynamic Domain of Adjudication and the Institution of Three Values

### 7.1 The Axis of Trajectories

Let γ be a legal underlying successor trajectory. Then

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Q^x\_{Eco,γ}( k ),　x ∈ { fix, set }

is recomputed window by window along that trajectory.

The object layer and the trajectory quantifier layer are two axes: the former asks what is stabilized, the latter how many legal futures are covered.

### 7.2 Three Values

Every reading takes

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Q^x( k ) ∈ { 1, 0, ⊥ }

The layer set above all must be stated exactly: Q^set = 0 may not come from "the search found nothing." Only if the candidate domain is completely adjudicable and every candidate determinately fails does it take 0; otherwise it takes ⊥.

Legal but non-adjudicable futures remain within the domain of definition, and are not deleted in advance.

### 7.3 The Dynamic Domain of Adjudication

Legal trajectories are not screened out by the coverage of observation windows or by present adjudicability. TrajLegal\_Eco collects all legal successor trajectories; adjudicable or not is set up as a separate layer.

For γ ∈ TrajLegal\_Eco and the given original realization E^str\_0:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;DynDomFix\_Eco( γ, E^str\_0, ℋ\_Eco ) = 1　if and only if

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;　One　the observation windows cover that trajectory completely  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;　Two　over the domain of observation, the window-level readings the task of the fixed layer needs can all be obtained  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;　Three　when Fail^fix\_γ is non-empty, its first actual failure can be determined; if a continuous domain is used and the infimum taken, it must be adjudicable whether it is attained, and, where the task of recovery needs it, an actual point of failure that can be legally timed must be obtained  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;　Four　all the protocol conditions the dynamic task of the fixed layer needs are satisfied

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;DynDomSet\_Eco( γ, ℋ\_Eco ) = 1　if and only if

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;　One　the observation windows cover that trajectory completely  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;　Two　over the domain of observation, the window-level readings the task of the existence layer needs can all be obtained  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;　Three　when Fail^set\_γ is non-empty, its first actual failure can be determined; if a continuous domain is used and the infimum taken, it must be adjudicable whether it is attained, and, where the task of recovery needs it, an actual point of failure that can be legally timed must be obtained  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;　Four　all the protocol conditions the dynamic task of the existence layer needs are satisfied

If the corresponding DynDom is not 1, then the adjudicated values of Keep, Recover, and Stable on that trajectory at that layer all take ⊥.

DynDom is the overall gate, not a side note: the adjudications of 8.1, 9.2, and 10.1 all take its being 1 as antecedent. The third clause is written as far as "an actual point of failure that can be legally timed" precisely to avoid DynDom having been adjudicated 1, only for 9.1 to find no k\_f with which to time.

When DynDom^x\_Eco( γ ) = 1, the corresponding dynamic task has entered the adjudicable domain, so the three Keep^x, Recover^x, and Stable^x take only 1 or 0.

Where a key fact the corresponding task needs is still non-adjudicable, DynDom^x\_Eco( γ ) = 1 may not be set; that trajectory remains in TrajLegal\_Eco ＼ TrajDec^x\_Eco, and the three dynamic values take ⊥ by the overall gate.

The position of the three values is thereby clear:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;DynDom ≠ 1　⇒　⊥  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;DynDom = 1　⇒　{ 0, 1 }

⊥ is given only by the overall gate, and no separate place for it is opened within each item. This institution is inherited as it stands from the sealed The Stability Form of Law.

### 7.4 The Adjudicable Trajectory Families

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;TrajDecFix\_Eco = { γ ∈ TrajLegal\_Eco \| DynDomFix\_Eco( γ, E^str\_0, ℋ\_Eco ) = 1 }  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;TrajDecSet\_Eco = { γ ∈ TrajLegal\_Eco \| DynDomSet\_Eco( γ, ℋ\_Eco ) = 1 }

Below, wherever TrajDec^x\_Eco is written, x = fix takes the former and x = set the latter.

### 7.5 The Rule of Recomputation

In every window EcoCert is read again by Γ\_qual, giving ℰ\_k, and the values of the two layers are then obtained by 6.1. How qualification is recomputed after variation is this.

Section Eight

Preservation, Actual Failure, and Boundaries

### 8.1 Preservation

DynDom^x\_Eco( γ ) is the overall gate: where it is not 1, Keep^x\_{Eco,γ} = ⊥, and it does not enter the adjudications below.

When DynDom^x\_Eco( γ ) = 1, Keep^x\_{Eco,γ} ∈ { 0, 1 }:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Keep^x\_{Eco,γ} = 1　⟺　every k in the domain of observation has Q^x( k ) = 1  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Keep^x\_{Eco,γ} = 0　⟺　there exists k with Q^x( k ) = 0

⊥ is given only by the overall gate. A trajectory whose coverage is incomplete while some window has already shown 0 has DynDom not 1; it does not enter TrajDec^x\_Eco, and Keep takes ⊥ by the overall gate, not 0 — seeing one failure is not the same as the question of preservation on this trajectory having become adjudicable.

8.2 Quantifiers May Not Be Covertly Exchanged

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;∀k ∃E\_k　supports only the layer set  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;∃E\_0 ∀k　is the layer fix

The two are not equivalent. To pass off the former as the latter is to say "every window has some realization" as "there is one realization running through the windows." This is a hard point of the review of a stability form.

8.3 The Failure Set and the First Actual Failure

This section too takes DynDom^x\_Eco( γ ) = 1 as antecedent.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Fail^x\_γ = { k \| Q^x\_γ( k ) = 0 }

Only a determinate 0 enters this set. ⊥ is not failure.

This must be read against the ⊥ of the two layers. The window-level reading Q^x( k ) is itself three-valued, and where it takes ⊥ that window cannot be read; while the second clause of 7.3 requires every window-level reading over the domain of observation to be obtainable, so wherever a window needed by the task takes ⊥, that trajectory does not satisfy DynDom^x\_Eco = 1, and so remains in TrajLegal\_Eco ＼ TrajDec^x\_Eco, its Keep, Recover, and Stable taking ⊥ by the overall gate.

So on a trajectory with DynDom = 1, every window needed obtains a determinate value, and there are no "undecided windows" outside Fail^x\_γ to be concerned about. What the statement "⊥ is not failure" guards against is counting a non-adjudicable window as failure, not permitting it to coexist with 0 on an adjudicated trajectory.

On a discrete domain of evaluation

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;k\_f^x = min Fail^x\_γ

If a continuous domain is used and inf Fail taken, it must be proved separately that the infimum is really attained before it may be called the actual point of failure. This too is what the third clause of 7.3 requires — what cannot be adjudicated may not enter TrajDec.

A mathematical boundary does not automatically give the truth value at that point: b = inf A or b = sup A gives only the boundary. Before failure, state, or responsibility is read off the boundary, attainment of the boundary must be proven separately.

Section Nine

Reappearance, Recovery, and Continued Verification

### 9.1 The Five Items of Recovery

For x ∈ { fix, set }, recovery must have all five items: actual failure, subsequent return, time limit, binding to the same stability task, continued verification.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Recover^x\_{Eco,γ} = 1　only if there exist k\_f^x \< k\_r with

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;　Q^x( k\_f^x ) = 0  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;　Q^x( k\_r ) = 1  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;　Dur( k\_f^x, k\_r ) ≤ T^x\_rec  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;　binding unchanged: Ξ\_Eco, Σ\_Eco, Δ\_Eco, ℋ\_Eco, γ all as before  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;　after the return, continued verification of at least h\_x is satisfied, and it is preserved by the protocol to the end of the domain of observation

### 9.2 Values

DynDom^x\_Eco( γ ) is the overall gate of this item.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;DynDom^x\_Eco( γ ) ≠ 1　⇒　Recover^x\_{Eco,γ} = ⊥

Only when it is 1 does the two-valued adjudication begin:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Recover^x = 1　there is already a first actual failure, and all five items of 9.1 hold

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Recover^x = 0　one of the following holds:  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;　　First　within the domain of observation no actual failure has in fact occurred  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;　　Second　an actual failure did occur, and within the time limit of recovery at least one of the five items determinately does not hold

The first clause for 0 does not mean the system is poor, only that no recovery has taken place — then if Keep^x = 1, Stable is still obtained by the branch Persistent; see 10.2.

If among the occurrence of failure, the return, the time limit, the binding of the task, or continued verification some key fact cannot be adjudicated, then that trajectory does not satisfy DynDom^x\_Eco = 1; its Recover does not take ⊥ separately within this item, but takes ⊥ directly by the overall gate.

With this item closed, the Stable of 10.1 is a total function.

9.3 The Time Limit Runs from the First Actual Failure

A later failure may not reset T^x\_rec. Timing always runs from the first actual failure.

### 9.4 Reappearance Is Not Recovery

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Recover^x　⇒　Reappear^x  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Reappear^x　⇏　Recover^x

A momentary 0 → 1 → 0 is only reappearance. What returns after the time limit is also only reappearance. What regains its 1 only by changing the template or the stability protocol is still less the recovery of the original stability task.

What only reappears, with no prior actual failure, does not constitute recovery.

Section Ten

Stability on a Single Trajectory

### 10.1 The Principal Criterion

The following adjudication also takes DynDom^x\_Eco( γ ) = 1 as antecedent: where it is not 1, Keep^x and Recover^x both take ⊥ by the overall gate, and Stable^x\_{Eco,γ} is ⊥ with them.

Where it is 1, Keep^x and Recover^x have both been fixed as 0 or 1, so Stable^x\_{Eco,γ} ∈ { 0, 1 }:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Stable^x\_{Eco,γ} = Keep^x\_{Eco,γ} ∨₃ Recover^x\_{Eco,γ}

Its sense is: under the frozen protocol, an ecological qualification, along some one legal trajectory, is either preserved throughout, or after actual failure returns by the legal conditions of recovery; obtaining either, stability may be signed.

∨₃ is three-valued disjunction: if one is 1 it is 1; if both are determinately 0 it is 0; if there is no 1 and there is ⊥ it is ⊥.

### 10.2 The Two Sources of Stability

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Persistent^x　≡　Keep^x = 1  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Resilient^x　≡　Fail^x ≠ ∅ ∧ Recover^x = 1

Stable can be obtained by either. The preservation, return, and resilience the title of this text contains are exhausted by these two.

No separate ontology of resilience is set up.

Section Eleven

Trajectory-Family Stability and Guaranteed Stability

### 11.1 Trajectory-Family Stability

Declared in advance:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;∅ ≠ 𝒢^x\_Eco ⊆ TrajDec^x\_Eco

The family must be non-empty, and must be confirmed to be a subset of the corresponding adjudicable trajectory family.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;FamilyStable^x\_Eco( 𝒢 ) = 1　⟺　∀ γ ∈ 𝒢, Stable^x\_{Eco,γ} = 1  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;FamilyStable^x\_Eco( 𝒢 ) = 0　⟺　∃ γ ∈ 𝒢, Stable^x\_{Eco,γ} = 0

Where the declared family is empty, or cannot be confirmed to be a subset of TrajDec^x\_Eco, FamilyStable^x\_Eco = ⊥.

One legal and adjudicable trajectory of instability within the family breaches it.

### 11.2 Guaranteed Stability

The GuaranteedStable^x\_Eco of this text is not taken as the truth value of a bare universal proposition detached from the institution of review, but as:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;the state of a certificate of guaranteed stability that can be formally signed out after the adjudication of the corresponding layer has been completed for all legal successor trajectories.

So its taking 1 and its taking 0 both have

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;TrajLegal\_Eco ≠ ∅  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;TrajLegal\_Eco = TrajDec^x\_Eco

as a common gate of qualification.

That is: if there are still legal but non-adjudicable successor trajectories, the review of the guarantee about "all legal trajectories" has not yet closed; even if a determinately unstable trajectory has already appeared, the certificate state defined by this text is still not signed 0 outright, but takes ⊥. What this institution expresses is whether the review of the guarantee has closed completely, not the independent truth value of a bare universal formula in ordinary three-valued logic.

Under this definition:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;GuaranteedStable^x\_Eco = 1　if and only if all three hold

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;　TrajLegal\_Eco ≠ ∅  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;　TrajLegal\_Eco = TrajDec^x\_Eco  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;　∀ γ ∈ TrajLegal\_Eco, Stable^x\_{Eco,γ} = 1

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;GuaranteedStable^x\_Eco = 0　if and only if all three hold

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;　TrajLegal\_Eco ≠ ∅  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;　TrajLegal\_Eco = TrajDec^x\_Eco  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;　∃ γ ∈ TrajLegal\_Eco, Stable^x\_{Eco,γ} = 0

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;otherwise　GuaranteedStable^x\_Eco = ⊥

Taking 0 and taking 1 share the gate of qualification of complete coverage. Any legal but non-adjudicable trajectory shows that the review of the guarantee has not yet closed, so the corresponding certificate state takes ⊥ for now.

FamilyStable answers only for a trajectory family declared in advance and already within the adjudicable domain; GuaranteedStable permits 1 or 0 to be formally signed out only after all legal successor trajectories have entered the adjudicable domain.

### 11.2.1 Two Things Must Be Kept Apart

The institution above is inherited from the sealed The Stability Form of Law, and this text inherits it as it stands, without changing it on its own.

It does not collide with the zero-absorbing principle of the whole text, because what is adjudicated is not in fact one and the same thing:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;First　the truth value of the universal — whether ∀ γ ∈ TrajLegal\_Eco, Stable^x\_γ = 1 is true  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Second　the qualification to certify — whether all legal trajectories have been adjudicated, and so there is qualification to sign out a complete certificate of guarantee

One determinately unstable trajectory suffices to negate the first, but not to give the second. What this predicate signs is the second.

These two are pressed into one predicate in the upstream institution. Whether it should be split into the two items GuaranteedStableTruth and GuaranteedStableCertifiable is a question for an upgrade of The Stability Form of Law, not something this text may decide alone — were this text to change it outright to "on seeing one determinately unstable trajectory, take 0," it would be inconsistent with the already sealed upstream institution.

So it is registered as a pending point at the level of the group:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;G-PEND1　whether the truth value of guaranteed stability and its certifiability should be separated

Should this point be decided as separation by an upstream upgrade, 11.2 of this text and K-ECO11 are revised accordingly, through the upgrade procedure. Until it is decided, this text runs under the present institution.

### 11.3 No Deletion in Advance

Legal but non-adjudicable trajectories may not be deleted from "all legal trajectories" before guaranteed stability is announced.

Nor may a trajectory's failure to cover the observation windows be used to manufacture, from what it lacks, a vacuous truth of the universal.

11.4 The Three Levels May Not Pass for One Another

Stability on a single trajectory is not family stability, and family stability is not guaranteed stability. The existence of a path of recovery is still less guaranteed stability.

Section Twelve

The Relation between the Fixed Layer and the Existence Layer, and High-Risk Boundaries

12.1 The Implication between the Two Layers

Let DynDomFix\_Eco( γ ) = DynDomSet\_Eco( γ ) = 1; then by 6.2

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Keep^fix = 1　⇒　Keep^set = 1

The antecedent must have both layers complete, and one may not stand in for the other: DynDomFix = 1 does not imply DynDomSet = 1. The 0 of the layer set requires the candidate domain to be completely adjudicable, see 7.2; the layer fix needs only to adjudicate whether the original one is within. So a trajectory can be adjudicable at the layer fix and non-adjudicable at the layer set — then Keep^fix = 1, while Keep^set takes ⊥ by the overall gate, not 1.

The window-level implication is not subject to this restriction: Q^fix( k ) = 1 ⇒ Q^set( k ) = 1 holds window by window, without waiting for the dynamic overall gate. This is what 12.2 uses.

### 12.2 Recovery Does Not Carry Over

One may not write

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Recover^fix　⇒　Recover^set

The reason: fix can fail and then return, while set may never have failed at all — what has not failed has nothing to recover from.

What is correct is

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Recover^fix = 1　⇒　Q^set( k\_r ) = 1

This formula falls on the window-level reading: the window of return k\_r has Q^fix( k\_r ) = 1, and the window-level implication stated at the end of 12.1 gives it. It does not assert what values Recover^set and Keep^set take — these two have determinate values only with DynDomSet = 1.

And

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Recover^set = 1　⇏　Recover^fix = 1

Its counterexample is legal replacement: all the realizations exit and afterwards another one takes the place; set returns while the original one never returns.

### 12.3 The Order of Failure

Let DynDomFix\_Eco( γ ) = DynDomSet\_Eco( γ ) = 1 on one and the same trajectory γ, with the first failures of both layers actually attained; then

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;k\_f^fix　≤　k\_f^set

The reason is Q^set( k ) = 0 ⇒ Q^fix( k ) = 0 — this implication too holds only if both layers of that window can be adjudicated, so the antecedent cannot be omitted. Its sense: the original fixed realization can fail first while the set of realizations is not yet empty; only when every legal realization afterwards exits does set collapse.

This is a theorem of boundaries, not raised into the definition of principal stability.

12.4 The Place of Feedback, Competition, and Mutual Aid

All three belong to the concrete content of the template Σ\_Eco, and none is a necessary condition of ecological stability in general.

So it is prohibited to write

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;EcoStable　⇒　Feedback  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Feedback　⇒　EcoStable

Likewise for competition and mutual aid. A template that needs a feedback loop writes the corresponding interaction-roles into that template; one that does not need it has none imposed.

### 12.5 Replacement Is Not Identity

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;RoleReplacement　≠　SameLaw

A new formal Law can legally take over the same node-role:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;L\_A ⤳ L\_B

This proves only that L\_B satisfies the qualification for bearing the same place, and does not imply SameLaw( L\_A, L\_B ) = 1.

Chapter 8 has proved that neither declared pairing nor physical-root pairing may pass for identity, while no closed criterion of Law identity has yet been established. This text does not supply one by the back door.

12.6 Replacement of Witness and Replacement of Object May Not Be Written Together

Under a fixed E^str\_0, the W\_k used in some window being changed is a replacement of internal witness; E^str\_0 has not moved, and Q^fix can still be 1.

While E^str\_0 exiting and another E^str\_1 taking its place is a replacement of object; Q^fix = 0 while Q^set may still be 1.

The two may not be written together: to call a change of witness a failure of the object is an error; to call a replacement of object a preservation of the original object is a worse one.

Section Thirteen

Group of Theorems and Breach

### 13.1 Propositions and Theorems

Proposition One　Recomputation of Ecological Qualification (an unfolding of definitions)

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;E^str ∈ ℰ\_k

if and only if there exists a set of event witnesses W\_k of that window such that

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;EcoCert( E^str, W\_k ; Σ\_Eco, H\_k ) = 1

that is,

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;E^str ∈ ℰ\_k  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;⟺  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;∃ W\_k \[  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;　　NodeWindowQual( E^str, W\_k )  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;　　∧₃ EdgeWindowQual( E^str, W\_k )  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;　　∧₃ JointEcoBind( E^str, W\_k )  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;　　\= 1  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;\]

Its sense: the object compared across windows is still E^str; within some evaluation window, the concrete interaction occurrences used to prove that this structural realization is currently qualified exist only as the event witnesses W\_k of that window, and do not enter the identity across windows.

The existential quantifier cannot be omitted. Remove it, and the three items become bare predicates without witnesses, and the rule established in 4.1, that structural realization and event witnesses may not be mixed, destroys itself here.

Proof. By 4.3,

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;ℰ\_k = { E^str \| ∃ W\_k　EcoCert( E^str, W\_k ; Σ\_Eco, H\_k ) = 1 }

So E^str ∈ ℰ\_k if and only if there exists W\_k with EcoCert = 1. And by 3.3,

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;EcoCert = NodeWindowQual ∧₃ EdgeWindowQual ∧₃ JointEcoBind

So the formula above holds. ∎

Theorem One　The Levels of the Stabilized Object

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Q^fix = 1　⇒　Q^set = 1, while the converse does not hold.

Proof. Forward: if E^str\_0 ∈ ℰ\_k then ℰ\_k is non-empty. That the converse does not hold is given by the counterexample of legal replacement in 6.2. ∎

Proposition Two　The Criterion of Stability on a Single Trajectory (an unfolding of definitions)

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;DynDom^x\_Eco( γ ) ≠ 1　⇒　Stable^x\_γ = ⊥  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;DynDom^x\_Eco( γ ) = 1　⇒　Stable^x\_γ = Keep^x\_γ ∨ Recover^x\_γ ∈ { 0, 1 }

Proof. The first formula is by the overall gate of 7.3. The second is by the definition of 10.1; then Keep and Recover have already been fixed as 0 or 1 by 8.1 and 9.2, so the three-valued disjunction ∨₃ reduces on them to ordinary disjunction. ∎

The ⊥ of Stable is given only by the overall gate, not obtained from the disjunction of two ⊥ — after the overall gate neither has ⊥ to begin with.

Proposition Three　Aggregation over a Trajectory Family (an unfolding of definitions)

For ∅ ≠ 𝒢^x\_Eco ⊆ TrajDec^x\_Eco,

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;FamilyStable^x( 𝒢 ) = 1　⟺　∀ γ ∈ 𝒢, Stable^x\_γ = 1  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;FamilyStable^x( 𝒢 ) = 0　⟺　∃ γ ∈ 𝒢, Stable^x\_γ = 0  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;otherwise ⊥

Proof. By the definition of 11.1. Where the family is empty or cannot be confirmed to be a subset of the adjudicable family, the premises of both clauses fail, so it takes ⊥. ∎

The four propositions of this section are unfoldings of definitions, and the two theorems are what is derived from them: the former show how the criteria combine, the latter prove the implication between the two layers and the order of failure.

Proposition Four　Aggregation of Guaranteed Stability (an unfolding of definitions)

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;GuaranteedStable^x = 1　⟺　TrajLegal\_Eco ≠ ∅ ∧ TrajLegal\_Eco = TrajDec^x\_Eco ∧ ∀ γ ∈ TrajLegal\_Eco, Stable^x\_γ = 1  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;GuaranteedStable^x = 0　⟺　TrajLegal\_Eco ≠ ∅ ∧ TrajLegal\_Eco = TrajDec^x\_Eco ∧ ∃ γ ∈ TrajLegal\_Eco, Stable^x\_γ = 0  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;otherwise ⊥

Proof. By the definition of 11.2. When the two sets are unequal, their difference is the legal but non-adjudicable trajectories; by 11.3 these may not be deleted in advance, so the premises of both taking 0 and taking 1 fail, and the value is ⊥. ∎

Theorem Two　The Order of the Boundaries of Failure

Let DynDomFix\_Eco( γ ) = DynDomSet\_Eco( γ ) = 1, with the first failures of both layers actually attained; then

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;k\_f^fix ≤ k\_f^set

Proof. Let DynDomFix\_Eco( γ ) = DynDomSet\_Eco( γ ) = 1. Then both layers obtain a determinate value in every window, so by the contrapositive of 6.2, Q^set( k ) = 0 implies Q^fix( k ) = 0, that is,

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Fail^set\_γ　⊆　Fail^fix\_γ

and the least elements of the two sets have this order.

The antecedent cannot be omitted: if in some window Q^set takes ⊥ while Q^fix takes 0, the inclusion of the two sets cannot be established. ∎

### 13.2 Breach

In each of the following cases, with the declared original qualification, domain of permitted variation, and condition group held, any one holding constitutes the corresponding breach.

K-ECO1　Keep^fix = 1 or Persistent^fix = 1 is claimed, while some window in the domain of observation has E^str\_0 ∉ ℰ\_k; or the fixed layer has actually failed without satisfying the five items of recovery of 9.1, while Stable^fix = 1 is still claimed.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"It once exited" may not be used to strike Stable^fix outright: by 10.1, Stable = Keep ∨₃ Recover, and the route Resilient permits 1 → 0 → 1. Exit strikes only the branch of preservation, not stability itself; to strike stability one must also prove that its recovery fails.

K-ECO2　Keep^set = 1 is claimed, while some window has determinately ℰ\_k = ∅; or the existence layer has actually collapsed without satisfying the five items of recovery of 9.1, while Stable^set = 1 is still claimed.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;As above: collapse strikes only the branch of preservation.

K-ECO3　Recovery is claimed without a prior actual failure.

K-ECO4　Recovery is claimed while the return occurred before the failure.

K-ECO5　Recovery is claimed beyond the declared time limit.

K-ECO6　Recovery is claimed where there is only momentary reappearance.

K-ECO7　Recovery is claimed without the declared continued verification being satisfied.

K-ECO8　The time limit of recovery is reset by a later failure.

K-ECO9　After changing the template, the domain of permitted variation, the evaluation protocol, or the stability protocol, the original stability instance is still said to have returned.

K-ECO10　FamilyStable is claimed while there is a legal and adjudicable unstable trajectory within the declared family.

K-ECO11a　With TrajLegal\_Eco = TrajDec^x\_Eco and a legal unstable trajectory existing, GuaranteedStable^x = 1 is still claimed.

K-ECO11b　With TrajLegal\_Eco ≠ TrajDec^x\_Eco, GuaranteedStable^x = 1 or 0 is claimed, and ⊥ is not taken.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;This clause must be read against 11.2 and 11.2.1: even if one unstable trajectory has been seen, if there are at the same time other legal but non-adjudicable ones, the review of the guarantee under the definition of this text has not yet closed, so the certificate state of GuaranteedStable is still ⊥.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;This is not the same as denying that the unstable trajectory already constitutes a counterexample to the bare universal proposition of stability — the two adjudicate different things; see 11.2.1.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;This clause hangs on the present upstream institution; whether it should change, see G-PEND1.

K-ECO11c　Legal but non-adjudicable trajectories are deleted in advance from TrajLegal\_Eco, and 1 or 0 obtained on that basis.

K-ECO12　Failure is claimed to have occurred at the point of a boundary, while that inf or sup has not been proved attained.

K-ECO13　Different bearers at different times are pieced together into one and the same fixed realization.

K-ECO14　The bearing of the nodes and the interaction occurrences come from different role mappings, and are pieced together into one EcoCert.

K-ECO15　The silence of events is adjudicated outright as the failure of an edge, without authorization by the protocol.

### 13.3 What Is Not Breach

The W\_k of some window changes while E^str\_0 has not moved — this is a replacement of internal witness; see 12.6.

Some window takes ⊥ rather than 0 — ⊥ does not enter the failure set; see 8.3.

The upstream H or closure of conditions has not been achieved — the conclusions of this text hold only within its closure; see 2.3.

The template has no loop of feedback — feedback is not a necessary condition of stability in general; see 12.4.
