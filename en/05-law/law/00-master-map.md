1.2　The Systemic Position of Law within *On Self-Manifestation*

*The Law Group-Form* does not treat an isolated problem of classifying regularities. The following five layers set out the upstream source of law, its internal position, and its downstream effect. These five are relations of different types, and may not be compressed into a single linear causal chain (see the declaration of relation-types at the end of this section).

\**Layer One　The Layer of Object-Transformation*\*

What *On Self-Manifestation* calls Object-Transformation generates not only "the object." Object-Transformation generates two classes of result together: the object bears the material expression, the law bears the pattern expression — that is, the pattern of the object's motion; and within the Sedimentation-Field this pattern is further exhibited as a relational organization already formed and able to bear Continuation.

One severe misreading is to be avoided here: the object is not material given in advance by the external world. The object is itself a material expression generated, compressed and organized by the system within Object-Transformation; the law is the pattern expression acquired within that same Object-Transformation. Hence one may not write "there are first objects outside, and the Sedimentation-Field afterwards learns their regularities," nor "there is first the object, and afterwards the law is affixed to it."

　　The joint generation of an object and its law is Object-Transformation.

Object-Transformation takes place within the Self-Manifestation-Field; here the constitution of its results is set out, and Layer Three then sets out the differing sedimentary offices of those generated results among the three fields.

\**Layer Two　The Layer of Law Formation*\*

Layer One sets out the constitutive relation among the results of Object-Transformation; the present layer sets out by what "law" — that pattern expression — has already acquired its present qualification to stand, on the side of the Sedimentation-Field, together with the structural order of Trace-Response — sedimentation — formation — entering the present — bearing what follows, to which that qualification corresponds. The two describe different question-levels of one and the same generative fact; they are not two generations of law.

As regards that present qualification to stand, the order of its structural formation shows itself thus:

　　external action gives rise to Trace-Response;  
　　Trace-Response enters sedimentation;  
　　repeated Trace-Response shapes a relational structure;  
　　the formed relation enters the present;  
　　the formed relation bears later Continuation;  
　　the Sedimentation-Field may thereby organize later Causal-Response by means of a pattern already made.

Law therefore stands at the pivotal bridging position between "leaving behind the action of the past" and "organizing later Causal-Response by means of a pattern already made." Trace-Response does not turn directly into Causal-Response; between them lie the several steps of sedimentation, formation, and entering the present.

Only once the Observer has arisen can the Causal-Response relations already occurring in the field be further discerned, distinguished and articulated.

\**Layer Three　The Layer of the Three Fields*\*

Within the Self-Manifestation-Field the three fields sediment different contents:

　　the Imagery-Field — sediments the imagery of the object, that is, the imagery organization of material expression within the Self-Manifestation-Field;  
　　the Experience-Field — sediments law, that is, the patterns already formed, the relations of continuation, and experiential structure;  
　　the Intent-Field — sediments Principle.

Principle is not another kind of law, but the structure of choosing, biasing, breaking and rewriting generated further upon laws already in place. The source-text relation is preserved as: law — holding, repetition; Principle — choosing, bias, breaking.

\**Layer Four　The Layer of Subsequent Generation and of Running*\*

At the level of generation: law is formed first, and Principle is generated upon law as its basis. Law is both the basis for the generation of Principle and the basis for the generation of Self-Manifestation.

At the level of running: Principle may drive laws already in place.

For example, Perceptual Experience is itself a law; the Principle of the Intent-Field drives that law to run, so that it tunes the present signal within the Imagery-Field, forms impedance, and thereby gives rise to the Imagery of the object. Here Principle neither gives rise to the object directly nor tunes the signal directly; what is driven is the law of Perceptual Experience.

This is only one concrete example of the running relation, and is not the general definition of the relation between law and Principle: one may not on this ground hold that every law is a perceptual law, or that every Principle aims at manifesting an object. How Principle selects some one law, how it drives it, what the driving interface is, how different Principles compete, and the full dynamical mechanism of a law once driven — none of these does this group prove.

\**Layer Five　The Boundary of Proof*\*

That "law is the basis for the generation of Principle and of Self-Manifestation," as said at Layer Four, signifies only a \**relation of precedence in the order of generative qualification*\*, and not a sufficient causal relation. One may not write "once law stands, Principle and Self-Manifestation are necessarily generated." The relation of joint generation set out above (Layer One), the relation of office within the fields (Layer Three) and the running relation (the running side of Layer Four) are each still to be understood by the declaration of relation-types at the end of this section, and may not be absorbed into this relation of precedence. What this group adopts is only the stricter formulation of precedence:

　　A field that cannot yet generate law, and cannot yet organize later Causal-Response by means of a pattern already made, has not yet acquired the prior qualification for the further generation of Principle and of Self-Manifestation.

Object-Transformation, the division of office among the three fields, Principle's driving of law, and the example of Perceptual Experience all come from the existing theory of *On Self-Manifestation*; this group map uses them only to set out the systemic position of *The Law Group-Form* within the book as a whole, expands none of them into a new proposition awaiting proof for this group, and does not thereby enlarge Q\_LawSuite. The range of what the eight texts themselves prove remains strictly confined within the Q\_LawSuite frozen at 1.4.

\**Declaration of relation-types*\*　The relations listed above fall into at least four classes, of different logical types, which may not be substituted for one another:

　A　Relation of joint generation: Object-Transformation → object + law.  
　B　Relation of prior qualification: law → the basis of the further generation of Principle; law → the basis of the further generation of Self-Manifestation.  
　C　Relation of office within the fields: Imagery-Field → the imagery of the object; Experience-Field → law; Intent-Field → Principle.  
　D　Relation of running: Principle → drives a law already in place. The example of Perceptual Experience is an instance of D.

One may not write a single chain "Object-Transformation → object → law → Principle → Self-Manifestation → perception": that would wrongly compress four different logical types into one and the same arrow of sufficient causation.

\**Engineering position*\*　This also sets out the position of the eight texts of *The Law Group-Form*: if one cannot yet say what counts as a law, how the qualification of law is to be formalized, how a Sedimentation-Field able to generate law is to be constructed, how it is to be adjudicated whether that qualification has been acquired, and how that qualification is preserved and where it is lost — then one does not yet have a Sedimentation-Field basis sufficient for going on to study the generation of Principle and of Self-Manifestation. Hence, for the engineering route of *On Self-Manifestation*, constructing a Sedimentation-Field that can really generate law is one of the prior tasks of going on to study the generation of Principle and of Self-Manifestation.

Among the members of an organizational group form there is only a relation of arrangement, and the group map serves only to organize; a theoretical group form, by contrast, requires that its members jointly attain a group-level conclusion attainable by no single text, and that the tests of minimality and completeness of office be completed. This group possesses both, and is therefore determined to be a theoretical group form; this group map is its group-level proof document, and not merely a table of contents.

### 6.4　The Discipline of Condition Routing

　\**Discipline One (no reverse back-flow)*\*　The formal bridges and adjudicative conditions downstream may not become, in reverse, antecedents of structural ontological theorems upstream. 　\**Discipline Two (local propagation along branches)*\*　Conditions propagate only along actual routes of dependence:

　　　H\_i propagates to T\_j if and only if the proof route of T\_j actually uses an output of G\_i protected by H\_i.
