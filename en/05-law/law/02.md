# The Mathematical Form of Law

## Section I　Purpose

Structural input: S = *The General Form of Law*. Formal output: M = the formal system of law of this text. This text is the mapping S ↦ M.

The structure given by S is:

　　Law\_F,t(L,I) ⇒ ∃R \[ Established\_F,t(R) ∧ Govern\_F,t(L;R,I) \],  
　　Govern\_F,t(L;R,I) ⇔ DynamicRole\_F,t(L;R,I) ∧ ΩSupport\_F,t(L;R,I).

Its components are: Rel(L), CurrentCarrier, CarrierRealizes, CarrierNonInstant, Established, DynamicRole, StableReplay, ΩSupport, Govern.

Upon the Sedimentation-Field of Field Chapter 1 (at v2.6) this text establishes, for the above components, formal criteria, static relations, a solving procedure, couplings and readback rules, and takes on the formalization of H3 of S (the objective truth value of the redundant dynamical office).

This text does not reprove ∃R(Established ∧ Govern) ⇒ Law; that sufficient direction has been established by *The Necessity Form of Law* (principal sufficient direction, Status = Established), and this text provides only the interface of representation and readback from the formal solution up to the structural layer (6.3). Neither does this text reprove the necessity of S, nor make instance adjudications (assigned to *The Determination Form of Law*).

## Section II　Core Theses

The structure of S falls into three layers: the structural bearing layer (a complete relational bearing structure already present in the field), the dynamical bearing layer (that structure really entering the generation of what follows), and the re-entry bearing layer (upon re-entry, the already formed structure goes on bearing and is supported by Ω). The three layers share the system of offices and the system of realizations, and use different evaluation contexts.

This text must express precisely:

　(1) the formal witness of Established: the presence in the current field of a complete bearing, and its non-instantaneity;  
　(2) the formation-state criterion of DynamicRole: not presupposing the present input, and distinguishing hot redundancy, structural cold standby, structural pre-emption, temporal pre-emption, pure correlation, and multi-level participation;  
　(3) the re-entry criterion of StableReplay: the bearing segment must be already formed and within the present R, while the access segment may be rebuilt instantaneously by the inflow;  
　(4) the stable-support criterion of ΩSupport: evaluating the existing Ω upon the bearing segment of the re-entry realization;  
　(5) the coupling of Govern: the witnesses of DynamicRole and of ΩSupport must belong to one and the same realization family of Rel(L); cross-realization chain-witnessing is not permitted.

The formal directions divide into forward readback M ⇒ S and reverse representation S ⇒ M; the two differ in strength.

### 3.2　Generation and Evaluation

\**Definition 3.5*\*〔aux〕（Local generation function）　For a generable quantity y, y = G\_y(a₁,…,a\_m), Arg(y) = {a₁,…,a\_m}. G\_y preserves the local hierarchy; no substitution-elimination is performed.

\**Definition 3.5′*\*〔aux〕（Local generation kernel）　For a generable quantity y, the realization gives a local generation kernel K\_y(· \| A\_y): A\_y is the set of inputs actually read at that generation step, and K\_y is the generation distribution of y under the values of A\_y. Deterministic generation is the special case K\_y = δ\_{G\_y(A\_y)}, where A\_y = Arg(y). K\_y is the formal interface of the generation rule: softmax is merely one realization's particular choice of K\_y.

\**Example 3.1*\*　Y = A ∨ Z, Z = ¬A ∧ B. Arg(Y) = {A,Z}. Substituting to obtain Y = A ∨ B miswrites Arg as {A,B}: B, though already pre-empted by Z when A = 1, would be judged to enter Y.

\**Definition 3.6*\*〔aux〕（Assignment environment）　ν is a set of mutually compatible values covering the local generation functions under consideration together with their upstream quantities; the present actual assignment is ν\_t, with ν\_t(a) = val\_t(a).

\**Definition 3.7*\*〔aux〕（Evaluation context）　Ctx = (B, c, σ, m): B ⊆ 𝔘\_t is the structural base available for use; c is the external condition (arrival of an inflow, the antecedent of an office, and so forth — structural variables not belonging to B); σ is a partial fixed assignment on quantities within B; m ∈ {inject, fixed} is the evaluation mode. Eval(Ctx) is the totality of ν satisfying: c holds; the quantities fixed by σ take their σ values; quantities in B unaffected by c and σ retain their values; affected quantities are recomputed by the local generation functions, structural variables outside B being unavailable; all local generation relations are mutually compatible; when m = inject and c contains the arrival of an inflow, the entry dynamics generates instantaneous structure upon B according to the entry rules of Field Chapter 1, and its solution forms part of ν; when m = fixed, c serves only as an external condition already holding (its truth value and the branch already selected being retained), no entry generation is performed, and instantaneous structure outside B may not be regenerated by c. Eval(Ctx) may contain several elements. We abbreviate Eval(B,c,σ,m) := Eval((B,c,σ,m)). inject is used only in 3.13 for the initial generation of ν; thereafter every context is fixed.

Background projection: for an assignment ν and (B,c), BaseFix\_{B,c}(ν) is the restriction of ν to those quantities already in B which "ought to be retained, being unaffected by c"; generated quantities affected by c are not among them. Wherever a complete assignment ν is written into the σ position, this always means BaseFix\_{B,c}(ν); the whole of ν may not be fixed. Otherwise Eval(Ctx) degenerates to a singleton, the minimal sufficient set of Definition 3.10 satisfies (i) even for C = ∅, and ActArg collapses to ∅.

\**Definition 3.7′*\*〔aux〕（Evaluation measure）　For a context Ctx, the evaluation measure is a probability space

　(Eval(Ctx), Σ\_Ctx, μ\_Ctx),

satisfying μ\_Ctx(Eval(Ctx)) = 1. μ\_Ctx is induced by composing the local generation kernels given by the realization according to the order of generation in the temporal unfolding, and agrees with the priority-continuation rule of Field Chapter 1 (equation 4) under that realization.

For an ordinary sequential generation node y\_j, the conditional kernel K\_{y\_j}(· \| A\_{y\_j}) is used. For a same-moment joint node Z\_τ of Definition 3.8, the realization must directly give the joint generation kernel K\_Z(· \| A\_Z); only where the realization has already declared a legitimate conditional decomposition or a conditional-independence structure may the joint kernel be composed from member kernels according to that decomposition. Member marginal kernels do not by themselves uniquely determine the joint kernel; if some joint node cannot be given a unique legitimate K\_Z, then μ\_Ctx is not unique, M3 is not satisfied, and that instance is recorded in M as undecidable (⊥); no coupling may be arbitrarily chosen as μ\_Ctx.

When Eval(Ctx) is finite or countably discrete,

　μ\_Ctx({ν}) = ∏\_j K\_{y\_j}(ν(y\_j) \| ν(A\_{y\_j}))

where the product runs over the ordinary nodes of the temporal unfolding and the joint nodes already declared decomposed, taking the corresponding factors, and

　Σ\_{ν∈Eval(Ctx)} μ\_Ctx({ν}) = 1.

In the general case μ is not defined by a sum of point probabilities, but by the probability space and kernel composition.

μ is induced from K, and is not inferred backwards from observed data; μ\_Ctx is uniquely induced by the K given by the realization together with Ctx, and is the generation measure of the evaluation context, not varying with Rel(L) or Type(d). Type(d) declares the Read functional, D, ε\_role, the satisfaction threshold, and other conditions of reading internal to the office; the outer set/measure interpretation of EvalMode(d) is handled by Definitions 3.14 and 3.16′. If Read/PreRealized itself requires full support, a probability threshold, or any stronger condition, that requirement belongs solely to the conditions for holding internal to the office and does not alter the outer structural quantifier; the two layers (K,Ctx) ↦ μ\_Ctx and Type(d) ↦ reading rule are kept apart. When EvalMode(L,i) = set, whether a legitimate evaluation is null-measure does not alter the set-semantics of the ordinary ∀/∃; only when EvalMode(L,i) = measure does μ\_Ctx take part in the outer a.e./positive-measure reading. The computability of μ\_Ctx is listed under M3.

\**Definition 3.8*\*〔aux〕（Temporally unfolded generation graph）　Nodes (y,τ); if u enters the generation of y, write (u,τ) ↝ (y,τ+Δ), Δ \> 0. Quantities mutually dependent at the same moment are merged into a joint node Z\_τ, whose legitimate values are the solutions of the simultaneous equations; a case with no solution does not belong to Eval, and where there are several solutions each solution belongs to Eval separately. The temporal unfolding of a physical loop A → B → A is acyclic, so no acyclicity assumption is imposed.

\**Definition 3.9*\*〔aux〕（Host support）　Nodes of the generation graph are divided by type into four kinds, and for each node z the set Hosts(z) ⊆ 𝔘\_t is the set of structural constituents bearing that node: (a) structural state nodes, that is, the value of a field quantity of some constituent u (δ(u), θ(u), κ(u), S(u), and so forth), Hosts = {u}; (b) hosted intermediate generated quantities, that is, intermediate quantities in the local dynamical equations of Field Chapter 1 belonging to some constituent u (such as the continuation tendency of u, or a gating output), Hosts = {u}; (c) joint nodes Z\_τ = (z₁,…,z\_n), Hosts = ⋃\_k Hosts(z\_k); (d) purely computational quantities without structural host, pure output readings, and Read(d), Hosts = ∅. Every node of the generation graph must be assigned to one and only one of these kinds within the characterization of generation functions under M3; there is no fifth kind. Hosts describes which constituents bear a node; it is not the node's set of causal ancestors: a node of kind (b), even if generated from the states of several upstream constituents, still has as its Hosts only the host u. For any set Z of generation-graph nodes, Comp(Z) = ⋃\_{z∈Z} Hosts(z); regarding a path π as its node set, Comp(π) is a special case of this definition.

\**Definition 3.9′*\*〔aux〕（Legitimate counterfactual variation family）　For a context Ctx, a generable quantity y and its local input set U\_y (deterministic generation takes U\_y = Arg(y); weighted-type and distribution-type generation take U\_y = A\_y), first define the legitimate counterfactual family

　AdmVar\_Ctx(U\_y) ⊆ ∏\_{a∈U\_y} Dom(a).

Its elements are those legitimate counterfactual joint values which that local generation relation permits to be compared, under preservation of the structural equations of the realization, the field constraints of Field Chapter 1, the simultaneity constraints, the shared-resource constraints, and the type constraints on each quantity. Joint nodes are handled as joint values, and are not split into mutually incompatible independent assignments; combinations of states that would forge, by direct assignment, what could only be acquired through upstream generation are excluded.

The counterfactual setting of values here serves only for formal comparison; it does not require the instance to possess an interface of physical manipulation. Whether a quantity enters generation is an ontological fact; whether that quantity can be directly manipulated is a matter of adjudicative capacity. The two must not be conflated.

For structural state quantities, AdmVar may contain their legitimate counterfactual values, and is not confined to the present values fixed by BaseFix within Eval(Ctx); for generated quantities, AdmVar is confined to values compatible with the generation equations once the structure is fixed. The type domain is only an outer bound:

　AdmVar\_Ctx(U\_y) ⊆ ∏\_{a∈U\_y} Dom(a),

generally without equality.

For

　C ⊆ {(a,ν(a)) \| a ∈ U\_y},

define

　Var\_C^Ctx(y;U\_y)  
　\= {v ∈ AdmVar\_Ctx(U\_y) \| v agrees with C on each input involved in C}.

At the instance layer a further manipulable subfamily may be declared,

　OperableDo\_Ctx(U\_y) ⊆ AdmVar\_Ctx(U\_y),

for the use of *The Determination Form of Law* alone. If OperableDo is insufficient to cover the comparisons required by some formal criterion, the adjudicative output is ⊥; ActArg or ActArg^μ may not on that ground be re-adjudicated as 0. The characterizability of AdmVar is listed under M6.

\**Definition 3.10*\*〔aux〕（Bearing arguments）　For Ctx and ν ∈ Eval(Ctx), u ∈ ActArg\_ν^Ctx(y) if and only if there exists

　C ⊆ {(a,ν(a)) \| a ∈ Arg(y)}

such that:

　(i) for every ν′ ∈ Var\_C^Ctx(y;Arg(y)), ν′(y) = ν(y);  
　(ii) (u,ν(u)) ∈ C;  
　(iii) after removing (u,ν(u)) the remaining conditions no longer satisfy (i).

This definition uses the legitimate counterfactual family of 3.9′; it does not take the present structural states fixed by BaseFix within Eval(Ctx) as the totality of counterfactuals.

\**Definition 3.10′*\*〔aux〕（μ-sufficient sets and distributional bearing arguments）　For weighted-type and distribution-type offices, Rel(L) declares, along with Type(d), a generative-office resolution ε\_role ∈ \[0,1) and a distributional divergence D (by default the total-variation distance); ε\_role and the satisfaction threshold of Read(d) are declared at separate layers.

The candidate set is A\_y, that is, the input set actually read by the local generation kernel K\_y; quantities related to y but not read by K\_y are not in the candidate set.

For ν ∈ Eval(Ctx) and

　C ⊆ {(a,ν(a)) \| a ∈ A\_y},

C is μ-sufficient if and only if, for any two complete legitimate joint assignments

　v,v′ ∈ Var\_C^Ctx(y;A\_y),

we have

　D(K\_y(· \| v), K\_y(· \| v′)) ≤ ε\_role.

The elements of Var\_C^Ctx(y;A\_y) are already complete joint assignments on A\_y agreeing with C; hence this definition does not again split them into do(C) plus a remainder. Formal counterfactual admissibility is determined entirely by the AdmVar of Definition 3.9′, and does not require the instance to possess a corresponding interface of physical manipulation. Sufficiency is judged by legitimate counterfactual comparison of the generation distribution of K\_y, not by observed conditional distributions.

If A\_y is finite, then μ-sufficiency is monotone under enlargement of C, and minimal μ-sufficient sets exist. In the general infinite-input case, if the existence or computability of minimal sets cannot be guaranteed, then M6 is not satisfied and the instance is recorded as ⊥.

Define

　u ∈ ActArg^μ\_ν(y)

if and only if there exists a minimal μ-sufficient set C with (u,ν(u)) ∈ C.

What this definition adjudicates is whether u belongs to at least one minimal μ-sufficient generative set under the present evaluation; it does not require u alone to be a but-for necessary condition. Hot-redundant bearers may belong to different minimal sufficient sets severally.

Under weighted-type and distribution-type offices, the Chain of 3.11 and all objects thereafter which invoke ActArg invoke ActArg^μ instead; deterministic-type and existential-type offices continue to use 3.10.

\**Theorem 3.10′.1*\*（Degeneracy consistency）　If G\_y is a deterministic function, K\_y = δ\_{G\_y(A\_y)}, A\_y = Arg(y), and 0 ≤ ε\_role \< 1, then

　ActArg^μ\_ν(y) = ActArg\_ν^Ctx(y).

*Proof.* The total-variation distance between point measures takes only the values 0 and 1. Hence C is μ-sufficient if and only if, for all variations of the remaining inputs within the same legitimate counterfactual family Var\_C^Ctx(y;Arg(y)), the value of y does not change; and this is precisely Definition 3.10(i). The two definitions use the same candidate input domain and the same Var, and their minimality conditions are likewise identical; hence they are equal. □

\**Erratum.*\*　3.10(i) of v1.1 read "for every ν′ ∈ Eval(Ctx)". Since BaseFix may fix structural states across all elements of Eval(Ctx), on that literal reading structural states might never enter ActArg, which is contrary to the office-semantics of DynamicRole. The present version uniformly changes the counterfactual quantification domain of deterministic ActArg to Var\_C^Ctx(y;Arg(y)) of Definition 3.9′. This is a substantive erratum to v1.1; it is not claimed that the literal ActArg of v1.1 and the ActArg of v1.2 agree item by item.

The present version deletes GenEffect; the main chain is kept as

　K\_y → μ\_Ctx → ActArg^μ

together with the deterministic degeneracy

　K\_y = δ\_G → ActArg^μ = ActArg.

Should *The Determination Form of Law* require an additional effect quantity, it must set up its own definition and proof of validity; none is pre-installed here.

\**Theorem 3.10′.2*\*　Under deterministic generation, replacing ActArg by ActArg^μ leaves Chain, FullChain, RolePath, RoleReal and DynamicRole^M each unchanged.  
*Proof.* Each object invokes the generative office only through ActArg; by Theorem 3.10′.1 they agree node by node. □

\**Example 3.2*\*（three cases）　Hot redundancy y = A ∨ B, with A = B = 1 in ν: {A} is minimal μ-sufficient, and so is {B}; hence A, B ∈ ActArg^μ. Structural cold standby Z = ¬A ∧ B, with A = 1 in ν: {A} is minimal μ-sufficient and B belongs to no minimal μ-sufficient set, so B ∉ ActArg^μ, even though B ∈ A\_Z is read. Temporal pre-emption: the generation kernel of y\_{t+Δ} reads only A\_t, so C ∉ A\_{y\_{t+Δ}} and C ∉ ActArg^μ. All three agree with the adjudications of v1.1; were A\_y itself taken as ActArg, the cold-standby B would enter Chain, and the exclusion of cold standby would have to be re-imposed at RolePath or at DynamicRole — which is to return to the present definition.

\**Definition 3.11*\*〔aux〕（Generation chain）　Chain\_ν^Ctx(y) is the totality of bearing nodes consisting of the target node y together with those obtained by tracing back level by level along the generation graph from ActArg\_ν^Ctx(y) (under weighted-type and distribution-type offices, ActArg^μ); upon meeting a joint node the tracing proceeds by the whole. y ∈ Chain\_ν^Ctx(y) always. HypChains(y \| Ctx) = {Chain\_ν^Ctx(y) \| ν ∈ Eval(Ctx), with the c of Ctx on the effective antecedent path of the chain}.

3.3　Candidate Law, Domain of Application, and Offices

\**Definition 3.12**〔aux〕　The candidate bearing organization R ⊆ Existing\_t may be a single, distributed, redundant, jointly acting, or composite structure. The domain of application I ⊆ 𝒫\_t, I ≠ ∅, is given before adjudication and may not be shrunk after the fact. I is the predeclared coverage domain of application (*The General Form of Law\*); that within it which truly bears the coverage obligation is the applicability-coverage guard domain I\_cov(L,I) (3.15′), likewise fixed before adjudication by the declaration of offices of Rel(L), and likewise not to be shrunk after the fact.

\**Definition 3.13*\*〔aux〕（Inflow evaluations）　For i ∈ I (the evaluation families are constructed over the whole of I; the quantification of criteria is separately confined to I\_cov, see 3.15′):

　Role evaluation family　RoleEval(i) = Eval(Existing\_t, arrival of i, ∅, inject);  
　Re-entry evaluation family　ReplayEval(i) = Eval(Estab\_t, arrival of i, ∅, inject).

For ν belonging to one of the two, Inst(i,ν) is the set of instantaneous structural constituents generated in ν by the entry dynamics upon the base, satisfying: (i) it comes only from the existing entry dynamics, and contains no structure fabricated in order to make Rel(L) hold; (ii) it contains no stable bearing structure. Where there are several solutions, different ν may correspond to different Inst(i,ν). Write RoleBase(i,ν) = Existing\_t ∪ Inst(i,ν), ReplayBase(i,ν) = Estab\_t ∪ Inst(i,ν); the realization domains X^Role\_{i,ν} = R ∪ Inst(i,ν) ⊆ RoleBase(i,ν), X^Replay\_{i,ν} = (R ∩ Estab\_t) ∪ Inst(i,ν) ⊆ ReplayBase(i,ν). Unformed constituents of R do not enter the re-entry realization domain.

\**Definition 3.14*\*〔S〕　Rel(L) is the index of relational content of the candidate law L, giving the office family 𝒪(Rel(L)) = {d\_α}. Each office d carries: an antecedent condition domain Ante(d) (under what circumstances the office applies); a relational antecedent Anchor(d) (the set of structural state quantities to which the relation is tied — that is, from what state the relation sets out); a successor description type Type(d); and a read function Read(d) (the relational consequent). The quantities in Anchor(d) must be state quantities of structural constituents; they may not be the inflow itself. Val(d) = {ν \| ν ⊨ Ante(d) and sufficient to evaluate Read(d)}. Rel(L) further gives an applicability constraint Cov(L), prescribing the field-state circumstances it claims to cover.

Besides the successor description type, Type(d) must declare EvalMode(d) ∈ {set, measure}. set indicates reading by the set of legitimate evaluations; measure indicates reading by the generation measure of Definition 3.7′. Probabilities, expectations, thresholds and other integral functionals of Read(d) still belong to the content of the office itself, and do not alter the outer quantifier polarities below.

For any i ∈ I\_cov(L,I) (for I\_cov see Definition 3.15′; the dependency chain is Rel → Trig → I\_cov → Adm/ReplayAdm → ModeSet, and this clause is to be read along that chain), define, for its joint office family over the role and re-entry evaluation domains, the outer mode set (conditions in I∖I\_cov trigger no office and take no part in mode determination):

　ModeSet(L,i)  
　\=  
　{ EvalMode(d) \|  
　　ν ∈ Adm(L,i) ∪ ReplayAdm(L,i),  
　　d ∈ D\_{i,ν} }.

A local diagnostic quantity may still be recorded:

　ModeConsistent(L,i,ν)  
　⇔ ∀d,d′ ∈ D\_{i,ν}: EvalMode(d) = EvalMode(d′).

But ModeConsistent serves only to locate mode conflicts internal to a single evaluation; it does not bear the office of defining EvalMode(L,i).

Define the three-valued closure of MixedMode(L,i):

　MixedMode(L,i) = 0, if \|ModeSet(L,i)\| = 1;  
　MixedMode(L,i) = 1, if \|ModeSet(L,i)\| \> 1;  
　MixedMode(L,i) = ⊥, if ModeSet(L,i) = ∅.

When MixedMode(L,i) = 0, define the unique element of ModeSet(L,i) as:

　EvalMode(L,i) = the unique element of ModeSet(L,i).

When MixedMode(L,i) = 1, the joint office family of that same i drifts between set and measure across different legitimate evaluations; the corresponding Type-sensitive quantifiers and the global predicates depending upon them are recorded in M as ⊥. No mode may be arbitrarily chosen, nor may mixed quantifiers be spliced together to continue evaluation.

When MixedMode(L,i) = ⊥, EvalMode(L,i) is not defined and all four Type-sensitive quantifiers are recorded as ⊥. If an upstream definition invoking these quantifiers has already been adjudicated 0 through the explicit failure of a non-emptiness qualification on Adm, ReplayAdm or D\_{i,ν}, then by the three-valued propagation rule of this text that 0 is the final failure; the vacuous truth of a universal over an empty domain may not be used to manufacture a 1.

*Note.* Ante and Anchor are different in content: the former determines when the office applies, the latter determines from which state the relation is borne. The same relation may have coarser or finer equivalent formulations of Ante; Anchor does not change with them.

\**Definition 3.15*\*〔aux〕　Trig(i) ⊆ 𝒪(Rel(L)) is the sub-family of offices that inflow i can trigger. If Rel(L) prescribes for i that something "remains unchanged," that outcome must be written explicitly as an office entering Trig(i).

\**Definition 3.15′*\*〔aux〕（Applicability-coverage guard domain）　I\_cov(L,I) ⊆ I is the totality of legitimate conditions in I which, by the predeclared range of offices of Rel(L), belong to the actual applicable coverage claimed by L:

　i ∈ I\_cov(L,I) if and only if Rel(L) claims an office for i, that is, Trig(i) ≠ ∅.

Four disciplines:

　(a) \**Prior to realization.*\*　I\_cov is fixed through Trig by the predeclared office structure of Rel(L), and must be determined before the adjudications of realization — Real, JointReal, ReplayWit, ΩWit, Bind and so forth; Ante and Cov are constraints at the evaluation level and do not determine membership in I\_cov (see (d)). It is strictly forbidden to define it by success or failure of realization: i ∈ I\_cov ⇔ Adm(L,i) ≠ ∅, ⇔ ReplayAdm(L,i) ≠ ∅, ⇔ the corresponding witness exists — all are illegitimate; otherwise one obtains "since that condition fails, declare it inapplicable," in violation of *The General Form of Law*'s "the domain may not be shrunk once a counterexample is seen." If a condition belonging to I\_cov has empty Adm or ReplayAdm, Mass = 0, or a failing office domain, that item is to be adjudicated as failing in the ordinary way, and may not on that account be moved out of I\_cov.  
　(b) \**Emptiness and undecidability kept apart.*\*　Trig(i) = ∅ makes i ∉ I\_cov only in the case "Rel(L) claims no office for i in the first place." If Trig(i) cannot be determined owing to missing office encoding, undecidability, or insufficient information under M4, then the membership of i is recorded as ⊥, and the corresponding formal items depending on I\_cov are recorded as ⊥ by three-valued propagation; silent exclusion is not permitted.  
　(c) \**Purely formal auxiliary.*\*　I\_cov is an 〔aux〕 object; it adds no ontological predicate at the S layer and is not read back. It merely renders explicit, at the M layer, the "applicability-coverage guard" of *The General Form of Law*.  
　(d) \**Declaration level and evaluation level kept apart.*\*　I\_cov adjudicates "whether this inflow condition is declared by L to bear at least one office," which is applicability at the declaration level; Cov(L) and Ante(d), by contrast, delimit — among conditions already within I\_cov — which evaluations and office instances legitimately apply, which is applicability at the evaluation level. Cov and Ante do not re-determine whether some i belongs to I\_cov; failure at the evaluation level may not retroactively modify the declaration-level guard domain.

　\**Monotonicity.*\*　Directly from this definition: if I ⊆ J, then I\_cov(L,I) ⊆ I\_cov(L,J). For membership is determined solely by Trig(i) ≠ ∅, independently of the size of I and of success or failure of realization. The single-R domain convexity of the downstream *Boundary Form of Law* depends on this property (its K-B5 is the breakdown point for it).

　I\_cov(L,I) ≠ ∅ is an explicit antecedent of each of the coverage-type criteria below; see 3.35, 3.39.

\**Definition 3.16*\*〔aux〕（Evaluation domains）

　Adm(L,i) = {ν ∈ RoleEval(i) \| ν ⊨ Cov(L)}; AdmPair(L,I) = {(i,ν) \| i ∈ I, ν ∈ Adm(L,i)};  
　ReplayAdm(L,i) = {ν ∈ ReplayEval(i) \| ν ⊨ Cov(L)}; ReplayPair(L,I) = {(i,ν) \| i ∈ I, ν ∈ ReplayAdm(L,i)}.

Adm(L,I) = ⋃\_i Adm(L,i) is notation only and bears no quantification of criteria. AdmPair(L,I) and ReplayPair(L,I) are constructed over the whole of I and are notations for object sets; the quantification of every structural predicate's criteria takes as its domain the I\_cov(L,I) of 3.15′ (the same-domain convention, 3.46). If some criterion or theorem takes (i,ν) ∈ AdmPair / ReplayPair as a universal, its i must likewise first be confined to I\_cov, written as (i,ν) ∈ AdmPair with i ∈ I\_cov.

\**Definition 3.16′*\*〔aux〕（Evaluation mode, applicability measure, and Type-sensitive quantifiers）　For i ∈ I, write (this definition gives the quantifier operators over the whole of I; when invoked by a structural predicate its i is confined to I\_cov, see 3.15′ and 3.46):

　RoleSeedCtx(i) = (Existing\_t, arrival of i, ∅, inject),  
　ReplaySeedCtx(i) = (Estab\_t, arrival of i, ∅, inject).

If the corresponding ordinary local kernels and joint kernels uniquely induce a generation measure by Definition 3.7′, write

　μ\_i^Role = μ\_{RoleSeedCtx(i)},  
　μ\_i^Replay = μ\_{ReplaySeedCtx(i)}.

Where the required sets are measurable and the measures computable, define

　RoleMass(L,i) = μ\_i^Role(Adm(L,i)),  
　ReplayMass(L,i) = μ\_i^Replay(ReplayAdm(L,i)).

When RoleMass(L,i) \> 0, define the conditional applicability measure

　μ̄^Role\_{L,i}(A) = μ\_i^Role(A ∩ Adm(L,i))/RoleMass(L,i).

When ReplayMass(L,i) \> 0, define the conditional applicability measure

　μ̄^Replay\_{L,i}(A) = μ\_i^Replay(A ∩ ReplayAdm(L,i))/ReplayMass(L,i).

If the measure itself cannot be uniquely given, if the required sets are not measurable, or if the relevant measure operations are not computable, then the formal items depending on that measure are recorded as ⊥; this is not measure zero. RoleMass = 0 or ReplayMass = 0 is a defined mathematical fact and must not be conflated with ⊥: in that case the conditional measure is undefined, and we stipulate that measure-mode quantifiers take their empty-domain values — ∀^{Role}\_{L,i}\[P\] = 1 (vacuously) and ∃^{Role}\_{L,i}\[P\] = 0, and likewise for Replay. A vacuous universal may not by itself establish any coverage criterion: every structural predicate whose content is a ∀^{Type} (CarrierRealizes, ReplayReady, StableReplay, ΩSupport, Bind) must additionally carry a non-emptiness antecedent for the corresponding domain.

To preserve the original quantifier polarity of each structural predicate, define two classes of Type-sensitive quantifier operator:

　∀^{Role}\_{L,i}\[P\],　∃^{Role}\_{L,i}\[P\],  
　∀^{Replay}\_{L,i}\[P\],∃^{Replay}\_{L,i}\[P\].

Their polarity is determined by the structural predicate that invokes them; Type determines only the interpretation of that polarity within the evaluation space:

　(a) set mode:  
　　　∀^{Role}\_{L,i}\[P\] = 1 ⇔ ∀ν ∈ Adm(L,i): P(ν);  
　　　∃^{Role}\_{L,i}\[P\] = 1 ⇔ ∃ν ∈ Adm(L,i): P(ν);  
　　　likewise for Replay, with ReplayAdm(L,i) as domain.

　(b) measure mode: only when the corresponding Mass \> 0 and the conditional measure is decidable,  
　　　∀^{Role}\_{L,i}\[P\] = 1 ⇔ μ̄^Role\_{L,i}({ν ∈ Adm(L,i) \| P(ν)}) = 1;  
　　　∃^{Role}\_{L,i}\[P\] = 1 ⇔ μ̄^Role\_{L,i}({ν ∈ Adm(L,i) \| P(ν)}) \> 0;  
　　　likewise for Replay.

Accordingly, measure mode only interprets "universal" as almost everywhere and "existential" as existence with positive measure; it does not change the existential polarity of DynamicRole into a universal one, nor the coverage polarity of CarrierRealizes, StableReplay, ΩSupport and Bind into an existential one. Should Read(d) itself require a probability threshold, an expectation, or any other integral functional, that is still evaluated within the realization qualification of Definition 3.21, and is not replaced by the present definition.

The four Type-sensitive quantifiers are defined only when \|ModeSet(L,i)\| = 1, that is when MixedMode(L,i) = 0, and they uniformly read the unique EvalMode(L,i):

　if EvalMode(L,i) = set, interpret by the ordinary ∀/∃ of (a);  
　if EvalMode(L,i) = measure, interpret by the a.e./positive-measure reading of (b);  
　if MixedMode(L,i) = 1, all four quantifiers are recorded as ⊥;  
　if MixedMode(L,i) = ⊥, that is ModeSet(L,i) = ∅, the four quantifiers are likewise recorded as ⊥; only when an upstream definition invoking them has already been adjudicated 0 by an explicit failure of non-emptiness qualification is a final 0 obtained by three-valued propagation.

Hence all legitimate role/re-entry evaluations of one and the same i jointly require a unique outer evaluation mode; not only is mixing within a single ν forbidden, but set/measure drift between different ν is likewise forbidden. Different i may still adopt different modes.

The four quantifier operators all have range {1,0,⊥}. ⊥ is taken only when MixedMode, the measure depended upon, measurability, or the operation is undecidable; where these are defined but the quantifier condition fails, the value is 0.

\**Definition 3.17*\*〔aux〕（Office domain）　D\_{i,ν} = Trig(i) ∩ {d \| ν ⊨ Ante(d)}. Throughout the text, quantification over offices uses D\_{i,ν}.

\**Definition 3.18*\*〔aux〕（Contexts）　For (i,ν) ∈ AdmPair: RoleCtx(i,ν) = (RoleBase(i,ν), arrival of i, BaseFix(ν), fixed). For (i,ν) ∈ ReplayPair: ReplayCtx(i,ν) = (ReplayBase(i,ν), arrival of i, BaseFix(ν), fixed). The (B,c) of BaseFix is given by the respective base and condition. Inst(i,ν) has already been selected by ν and has entered the base; in fixed mode it is not regenerated by c. Hence ν ∈ Eval(RoleCtx(i,ν)), and Eval(RoleCtx(i,ν)) retains all legitimate variation of the generated quantities affected by i, upon which domain ActArg is computed. For P ⊆ B and a context κ = (B,c,σ,fixed): the restricted context Restrict(κ,P) = κ\_P = (P, c, σ\|\_P, fixed); the truth value of c and the branch already selected are retained, while structural variables outside P (including Inst(i,ν) ∖ P) are unavailable and may not be regenerated by c. The restricted evaluation witnesses are ResEval(κ,P,ν) = {η ∈ Eval(κ\_P) \| η agrees with ν on the background quantities fixed by σ\|\_P and on the branch already selected by c}. A whole-field assignment ν does not in general belong to Eval(κ\_P) (after deleting variables outside P, the affected quantities must be recomputed); hence every P-restricted operation takes η ∈ ResEval(κ,P,ν) as its chain witness, and does not use ν directly.

\**Definition 3.19*\*〔aux〕（Three-layer chains）　StructChains(d,ν) = HypChains(Read(d) \| (Existing\_t, Ante(d), BaseFix(ν), fixed)); ActChain\_t(d) = Chain\_{ν\_t}^{(Existing\_t, present inflow, BaseFix(ν\_t), fixed)}(Read(d)) (where ν\_t ⊨ Ante(d) and the chain passes the antecedent path of d, otherwise ∅); ReplayChains(d \| i,ν) = Chain\_ν^{ReplayCtx(i,ν)}(Read(d)).

\**Definition 3.20*\*〔aux〕　StructBear\_R(d,ν) = R ∩ ⋃\_{χ∈StructChains(d,ν)} Comp(χ); ActBear\_R(d) = R ∩ Comp(ActChain\_t(d)); NonInstBear\_R(d,ν) = StructBear\_R(d,ν) ∩ Estab\_t. For Comp see Definition 3.9.

### 3.4　Realization

\**Definition 3.21*\*〔aux〕（Pre-realization）　For a context κ = (B,c,σ,fixed), ν ∈ Eval(κ), an office d, and P ⊆ B:

First define single-evaluation qualification. To avoid confusion with the evaluation measure μ, we write η for a restricted evaluation point. η ∈ ResEval(κ,P,ν) qualifies for d if and only if:

　(iii) Read(d) is defined under η;  
　(iv) Closed^κ(P,d,η):  
　　　Comp(Chain\_η^{κ\_P}(Read(d))) ⊆ P;  
　(v) Read(d) under η satisfies the relational content of d.

Write

　Good^κ(d,ν;P)  
　⊆ ResEval(κ,P,ν)

for the set of qualifying evaluations.

The realization evaluation witness family WitEval^κ(d,ν;P) is determined by Type(d) and Cov(L):

1\. Deterministic-type office:  
　W = ResEval(κ,P,ν), requiring Good = ResEval.

2\. Existential-type office:  
　W is some singleton {η} within Good; different η give different witnesses.

3\. Distribution-type and weighted-type offices:  
　W = ResEval(κ,P,ν), and no favourable subfamily may be taken;  
　it is required that W ∈ Σ\_{κ\_P} and μ\_{κ\_P}(W) \> 0;  
　the integral reading of Read(d) acts upon the conditional measure

　　μ\_{κ\_P}(· \| W)  
　　\= μ\_{κ\_P}(· ∩ W)/μ\_{κ\_P}(W).

　Define the regular evaluation set

　　Reg^κ(d,ν;P)  
　　\= {η ∈ W \| Read(d) is defined and Closed^κ(P,d,η)}.

　The default requirement is

　　μ\_{κ\_P}(Reg^κ(d,ν;P) \| W) = 1,

　that is, (iii) and (iv) hold μ-a.e.; if that requirement is explicitly declared by the internal Read/PreRealized conditions of Type(d), it may be strengthened to Reg = W. That strengthening alters only the realization qualification internal to the office; it does not alter the outer quantifier polarity of Definition 3.16′ nor its set/measure interpretation. The relational content (v) is then adjudicated by the value of the integral functional of Read(d) upon the conditional measure μ\_{κ\_P}(·\|W).

When μ\_{κ\_P}(W) = 0, (P,W) does not constitute a legitimate weighted-type or distribution-type witness.

PreRealized^κ(d,ν;P,W) = 1 if and only if:  
　(i) ν ∈ Val(d);  
　(ii) ResEval(κ,P,ν) ≠ ∅;  
　(vi) W is a legitimate WitEval^κ(d,ν;P) by the above rules, W ≠ ∅, and satisfies requirements (iii)–(v) corresponding to Type(d).

Once W is fixed it accompanies the realization witness throughout Bear, Realized, Real, ExtReal, JointReal and ReqSeg; no evaluation subfamily may be re-selected thereafter.

\**Definition 3.22*\*〔aux〕（Bearing segment and access segment）　For (P,W) with PreRealized^κ(d,ν;P,W) = 1, computation proceeds evaluation by evaluation; paths are not spliced across evaluations.

For deterministic-type and existential-type offices, set W° = W.  
For weighted-type and distribution-type offices, set

　W° = Reg^κ(d,ν;P),

so that μ\_{κ\_P}(W°\|W) = 1.

For each η ∈ W°:

　χ\_η = Chain\_η^{κ\_P}(Read(d)).

Write State(z) for the state quantity corresponding to a generation node z. Define AnchorNodes(d) = {z \| State(z) ∈ Anchor(d)}. If every state quantity in Anchor(d) has a corresponding node on χ\_η, then BearEntry\_η is the bearing frontier of those Anchor state quantities on χ\_η; otherwise BearEntry\_η = ∅.

Let ↝\*\_{χ\_η} be the reflexive transitive closure within χ\_η, and define

　BearNodes\_η  
　\= {z ∈ χ\_η \|  
　　 ∃e ∈ BearEntry\_η:  
　　 e ↝*\_{χ\_η} z ∧ z ↝*\_{χ\_η} Read(d)}.

Deterministic-type and existential-type offices take

　BearNodes^κ(P,W,d,ν)  
　\= ⋃\_{η∈W°} BearNodes\_η.

Weighted-type and distribution-type offices do not take an ordinary full union over W°, but take the essential bearing nodes. For each candidate node z, the event

　E\_z = {η ∈ W° \| z ∈ BearNodes\_η}

must belong to Σ\_{κ\_P}. If some required E\_z is not measurable, then the Bear of that realization witness is recorded in M as ⊥, and may not be adjudicated 0 or 1; if measurable, define

　BearNodes^κ(P,W,d,ν)  
　\= {z \| μ\_{κ\_P}(E\_z \| W) \> 0 }.

That is, only nodes taking part in the bearing with positive measure under the conditional generation measure enter Bear; anomalous nodes within null-measure evaluations may not re-enter the bearing structure at this layer.

Then

　Bear^κ(P,W,d,ν)  
　\= Comp(BearNodes^κ(P,W,d,ν)),

　Acc^κ(P,W,d,ν)  
　\= P ∖ Bear^κ(P,W,d,ν).

Bear is still determined by Anchor(d), by W, and by the bearing chains within each evaluation; paths are not spliced across evaluations, it does not depend on the linguistic formulation of Ante, and Ω is not evaluated within this definition.

The realization witness is still ρ = (P,W), with Supp(ρ) = P. Objects parameterized by ρ are defined as:

　BearNodes^κ(ρ,d,ν) = BearNodes^κ(P,W,d,ν),  
　Bear^κ(ρ,d,ν) = Bear^κ(P,W,d,ν),  
　Acc^κ(ρ,d,ν) = Acc^κ(P,W,d,ν).

When ρ = ρ\_d is taken from a joint realization and κ, ν are determined by context, we abbreviate as Bear(ρ\_d), Acc(ρ\_d).

\**Proposition 3.1*\*（Invariance of Bear under formulations of Ante）　Let Ante(d) and Ante′(d) be two equivalent formulations of the same office antecedent, inducing the same Val(d), ResEval and WitEval qualifications. For any realization witness ρ = (P,W), if the same W is a legitimate WitEval under both formulations, then Bear^κ\_{Ante}(ρ,d,ν) = Bear^κ\_{Ante′}(ρ,d,ν). This proposition does not assert that different W over the same P give the same Bear.  
*Proof.* With ρ fixed, Bear^κ(ρ,d,ν) depends only on Anchor(d), on W, and on Chain\_η^{κ\_P}(Read(d)) for each η ∈ W°; none of the three depends on the linguistic expression of Ante (c contains the fact that the antecedent holds, not its formulation). □

\**Example 3.2*\*　g → R\_Ω → Read(d), P = {g} ∪ R\_Ω, Anchor(d) the state quantity at the entrance of R\_Ω. The restricted chain is: gating state of g → state of R\_Ω → Read(d); BearEntry lies at the entrance of R\_Ω; Bear = R\_Ω, Acc = {g}. If Rel(L) sets Anchor(d) to be the gating state of g, then Bear ∋ g; in that case the relation takes an instantaneous gate as its antecedent and is not borne by R, and the later adjudication of 0 is the correct result.

\**Definition 3.23*\*〔aux〕（Complete realization）　Realized^κ(d,ν;P,W) = 1 if and only if PreRealized^κ(d,ν;P,W) = 1 and Bear^κ(P,W,d,ν) ≠ ∅. For B′ ⊆ B\_κ: Real^κ\_{B′}(d,ν) = {ρ = (P,W) \| P ⊆ B′, Realized^κ(d,ν;P,W) = 1}. The same P may correspond to several ρ; each ρ enters the subsequent criteria independently, and its Bear, Acc, ReqSeg and RolePath are computed with its own W. Abbreviation: PreRealized^κ(d,ν;P) = 1 ⇔ ∃W PreRealized^κ(d,ν;P,W) = 1; hence PreRealized^κ(d,ν;P) = 0 ⇔ ¬∃W \[PreRealized^κ(d,ν;P,W) = 1\].

The sequence of definitions ResEval → WitEval → PreRealized → Bear → Realized → Real is acyclic; the restricted evaluation witness W is carried from PreRealized onwards into Bear, Realized, Real and the subsequent ExtReal, JointReal and ReqSeg.

\**Definition 3.24*\*〔aux〕（Extended realization）　Write X^κ\_{i,ν} for X^Role\_{i,ν} when κ = RoleCtx(i,ν), and for X^Replay\_{i,ν} when κ = ReplayCtx(i,ν); in both cases X^κ\_{i,ν} ⊆ B\_κ, so Real^κ\_{X^κ\_{i,ν}} is defined. ExtReal^κ\_R(d) = {ρ ∈ Real^κ\_{X^κ\_{i,ν}}(d,ν) \| Bear^κ(ρ,d,ν) ⊆ R ∧ Supp(ρ) ∩ Inst(i,ν) ⊆ Acc^κ(ρ,d,ν)}.

\**Definition 3.25*\*〔aux〕（Joint realization）　For κ, an office sub-family D and ν, JointReal^κ\_{B′}(D,ν) is the set of Π = {ρ\_d}\_{d∈D} such that: each ρ\_d ∈ Real^κ\_{B′}(d,ν); all offices share one and the same complete evaluation environment ν; the shared structure, gating, states, weights and resources of the several Supp(ρ\_d) are compatible, and the several W\_d agree on shared quantities; and all realizations hold simultaneously within the same ν. Wherever a function needs only local quantities of d, it reads them internally by the projection ν↾d; ν↾d is not an element of Eval(κ) and may not serve as an evaluation-environment parameter. ExtJointReal^κ\_R(D) consists of those with each ρ\_d ∈ ExtReal^κ\_R(d). When D = ∅, JointReal takes the empty family {∅}; the criteria of this text take D ≠ ∅ as antecedent (see ReplayReady).

\**Definition 3.26*\*〔aux〕　Bear^κ(Π) = ⋃\_d Bear^κ(ρ\_d,d,ν), Acc^κ(Π) = ⋃\_d Acc^κ(ρ\_d,d,ν), Q(Π) = Acc(Π) ∪ Bear(Π); Supp(Π) = ⋃\_d Supp(ρ\_d); the superscript κ is omitted when determined by context. Q\_L^∪ = ⋃{Q(Π) \| Π a legitimate joint realization in R} (distinct from the Q\_L of S, which corresponds to the Bear(Π) of one specific Π).

### 3.5　Interfaces, Reach, and Ω

\**Definition 3.27*\*〔aux〕　E\_B(i) ⊆ B is the site upon which i can act directly on the base B.

\**Definition 3.28*\*〔aux〕（Reach）　Reach\_B(E,P) holds if and only if there exist e ∈ E and p ∈ P such that e can arrive at p along existing structural relations within B (Continuation between Structural Positions, Structural-Position-to-Groove-Line membership, Groove-Line-to-Flow-Hollow confluence, Groove-Line-to-Line-Bundle subordination, and so forth). The reach segment is not required to have Ω \> 0.

\**Definition 3.29*\*〔aux〕（Bearing continuation segments and the Ω witness）　For ρ = (P,W):

For deterministic-type and existential-type offices, W° = W;  
for weighted-type and distribution-type offices, W° = Reg^κ(d,ν;P).

For each η ∈ W°, define the single-evaluation bearing continuation segments

　ReqSeg^κ\_η(ρ,d,ν)  
　\= {x ⇝\_t y \|  
　　 x,y ∈ ⋃\_{z∈BearNodes\_η} Hosts(z),  
　　 and there is an adjacent generation edge within BearNodes\_η,  
　　 z\_x ↝ z\_y,  
　　 with Hosts(z\_x) ∋ x, Hosts(z\_y) ∋ y,  
　　 and x ⇝ y lying in the domain of definition of Ω}.

Deterministic-type and existential-type offices take

　ReqSeg^κ(ρ,d,ν)  
　\= ⋃\_{η∈W°} ReqSeg^κ\_η(ρ,d,ν).

Weighted-type and distribution-type offices take the essential bearing continuation segments. For each candidate continuation segment x ⇝ y, the event

　E\_{x⇝y}  
　\= {η ∈ W° \| (x⇝y) ∈ ReqSeg^κ\_η(ρ,d,ν)}

must belong to Σ\_{κ\_P}. If some required E\_{x⇝y} is not measurable, then the ReqSeg of that realization witness is recorded in M as ⊥, and may not be adjudicated 0 or 1; if measurable, define

　ReqSeg^κ(ρ,d,ν)  
　\= {x⇝y \| μ\_{κ\_P}(E\_{x⇝y}\|W) \> 0 }.

Hence anomalous continuation segments within null-measure evaluations do not enter the Ω criterion.

Define

　ΩOK^κ(ρ,d,ν) = 1

if and only if

　\[ReqSeg^κ(ρ,d,ν) ≠ ∅  
　 ∧ ∀(x⇝y) ∈ ReqSeg^κ(ρ,d,ν): Ω\_t(x→y) \> 0\]

or

　\[ReqSeg^κ(ρ,d,ν) = ∅  
　 ∧ StableType^κ(ρ,d,ν) = 1\].

The Acc segment still does not enter the evaluation of Ω.

### 3.6　Role Objects

\**Definition 3.30*\*〔aux〕（Bearing states）　For ρ and u ∈ Bear^κ(ρ,d,ν): RelState^κ\_ρ(u,d,ν) = {State(z) \| z ∈ BearNodes^κ(ρ,d,ν), u ∈ Hosts(z), State(z) defined}, that is, those state quantities belonging to u among the bearing nodes of that realization witness. Under the same (κ,d,ν), different ρ₁, ρ₂ (including the same P with different W) may have different BearNodes; hence RelState is parameterized by ρ.

\**Definition 3.31*\*〔aux〕（Whole-field chain）　For a context κ ∈ {RoleCtx(i,ν), ReplayCtx(i,ν)}: FullChain^κ(d) = Chain\_ν^κ(Read(d)), by whole-field evaluation, without P-restriction.

\**Definition 3.32*\*〔aux〕（Role path）　RolePath^κ\_ρ(d) is the set of paths π on the generation graph satisfying: (i) π begins at a state quantity in RelState^κ\_ρ(u,d,ν) for some u ∈ Bear^κ(ρ,d,ν); (ii) π ends at Read(d); (iii) every edge of π belongs to FullChain^κ(d); (iv) Comp(π) ⊆ Supp(ρ).

\**Definition 3.33*\*〔aux〕（Role-real realization）　RoleReal^κ\_R(d) = {ρ ∈ ExtReal^κ\_R(d) \| RolePath^κ\_ρ(d) ≠ ∅}, where κ is the RoleCtx or ReplayCtx of that (i,ν).

3.7　Formal Representation of the Structural Components

\**Definition 3.34*\*〔S〕（CurrentCarrier^M）　Organization block: B ⊆ Existing\_t is an organization block if and only if B is a single constituent of legitimate type, or the constituents of B are connected by the following existing relations: Structural-Position-to-Groove-Line membership; Groove-Line-to-Flow-Hollow confluence; Groove-Line-to-Line-Bundle subordination; Continuation ⇝\_t between Structural Positions; membership in the same joint generation block. The co-organization relation Org\_t(B\_j,B\_k): B\_j and B\_k belong to the same Line-Bundle or the same Flow-Hollow; or there exists a legitimate generation node z with Hosts(z) ∩ B\_j ≠ ∅ ∧ Hosts(z) ∩ B\_k ≠ ∅; or there exists a generation node z whose local generation chain simultaneously receives state quantities from B\_j and from B\_k (a common successor); or they are connected by a ⇝\_t chain. CurrentCarrier^M\_F,t(R) = 1 if and only if R ≠ ∅, R ⊆ Existing\_t, every constituent of R is of legitimate type with its field quantities defined, and R = ⋃\_{k∈K} B\_k with K ≠ ∅, each B\_k an organization block. No Org\_t relation is required between distinct B\_k. This criterion invokes neither Rel(L), nor offices, nor StructBear.

*Note.* S permits R to be a distributed structure: physically separated R₁, R₂ may severally bear different offices of the same Rel(L). CurrentCarrier knows nothing of the division of offices, and therefore may not exclude in advance by connectivity; whether several blocks jointly constitute the complete bearing of Rel(L) is adjudicated by CarrierRealizes^M. Org\_t is retained as a descriptive quantity of organization for the use of *The Determination Form of Law*, and is not a necessary condition of this criterion.

\**Three-valued evaluation convention (mode qualification).*\*　MixedMode(L,i) determines only whether the corresponding Type-sensitive quantifiers can be defined; it does not enter as an ordinary Boolean antecedent into the conjunctions of CarrierRealizes, CarrierNonInstant, DynamicRole, ReplayReady, StableReplay, ΩSupport or Bind. When MixedMode(L,i) = 0, the four quantifiers are evaluated by the unique EvalMode(L,i); when MixedMode(L,i) ∈ {1,⊥}, the corresponding quantifiers take the value ⊥. If some property has an explicit failing item independent of that quantifier already adjudicated 0, then the decisive 0 overrides ⊥; otherwise ⊥ continues to propagate into that property. In particular, DynamicRole retains existential polarity: if ∃^{Role}\_{L,i} = 1 for any i, then DynamicRole^M = 1; if none is 1 and at least one necessary candidate quantifier is ⊥, then DynamicRole^M = ⊥; only when all decidable candidates are 0 is it adjudicated 0.

\**Definition 3.35*\*〔S〕（CarrierRealizes^M）　CarrierRealizes^M\_F,t(L;R,I) = 1 if and only if I\_cov(L,I) ≠ ∅ and, for every i ∈ I\_cov(L,I): the role domain is non-empty — Adm(L,i) ≠ ∅ when EvalMode(L,i) = set, RoleMass(L,i) \> 0 when EvalMode(L,i) = measure — and

　∀^{Role}\_{L,i}\[  
　　D\_{i,ν} ≠ ∅  
　　∧ JointReal^{RoleCtx(i,ν)}\_R(D\_{i,ν},ν) ≠ ∅  
　\] = 1.

Non-emptiness of the role domain is an explicit antecedent: this criterion may not be obtained by a vacuous universal over an empty Adm(L,i). Non-emptiness of I\_cov is likewise an explicit antecedent: this criterion may not be obtained by a vacuous universal over an empty guard domain. Conditions in I∖I\_cov do not enter the universal of this criterion — by *The General Form of Law*, bare conditions not belonging to the applicable office of L bear no coverage obligation; but if a condition belonging to I\_cov has an empty role domain, it is adjudicated 0 in the ordinary way.

\**Definition 3.36*\*〔S〕（CarrierNonInstant^M）　CarrierNonInstant^M\_F,t(L;R,I) = 1 if and only if CarrierRealizes^M = 1 and, for every i ∈ I\_cov(L,I) (the same guard domain as in 3.35),

　∀^{Role}\_{L,i}\[  
　　∃Π ∈ JointReal^{RoleCtx(i,ν)}\_R(D\_{i,ν},ν): Q(Π) ⊆ Estab\_t  
　\] = 1.

\**Definition 3.37*\*〔S〕（EstWitness^M）　EstWitness^M\_F,t(L;R,I) = 1 if and only if CurrentCarrier^M ∧ CarrierRealizes^M ∧ CarrierNonInstant^M. If a necessary formal item is 0, then EstWitness^M = 0; if none is 0 and at least one is ⊥, then EstWitness^M = ⊥. The structural predicate of S is Established\_F,t(R); its H1 antecedent carries (L;R,I), and hence this witness carries those parameters too.

\**Definition 3.38*\*〔S〕（DynamicRole^M）　DynamicRole^M\_F,t(L;R,I) = 1 if and only if there exists i ∈ I\_cov(L,I) such that

　∃^{Role}\_{L,i}\[  
　　∃d ∈ D\_{i,ν}: RoleReal^{RoleCtx(i,ν)}\_R(d) ≠ ∅  
　\] = 1.

Here the formation-state existential polarity required by S is retained; Type determines only whether that "existence" is legitimate existence or positive-measure existence. Changing the domain of the existential quantifier to I\_cov serves only same-domain tidiness with the coverage-type criteria: for conditions in I∖I\_cov, Trig(i) = ∅ and hence D\_{i,ν} = ∅, so they could contribute no role witness in any case, and the change of domain does not change the truth value. This predicate may not be changed into a universal — it bears the qualification of reality, "whether R really enters the generation of Rel(L)," and does not require each applicable condition to obtain an independent dynamical witness of its own (the division of polarities of *The General Form of Law*). If all i are explicitly 0, then DynamicRole^M = 0; if no i is 1 and at least one required item is ⊥, then DynamicRole^M = ⊥.

\**Definition 3.39*\*〔aux〕（Re-entry readiness）　ReplayReady(L,I) = 1 if and only if I\_cov(L,I) ≠ ∅ and, for every i ∈ I\_cov(L,I) (the same guard domain as in 3.35): Trig(i) ≠ ∅ (automatic from the definition of I\_cov); the re-entry domain is non-empty — ReplayAdm(L,i) ≠ ∅ when EvalMode(L,i) = set, and additionally ReplayMass(L,i) \> 0 when EvalMode(L,i) = measure; and

　∀^{Replay}\_{L,i}\[D\_{i,ν} ≠ ∅\] = 1.

Non-emptiness of the re-entry domain is an explicit antecedent: in measure mode a null-measure ReplayAdm may not, through a vacuous universal, let ReplayReady and its downstream (StableReplay, ΩSupport, Bind) hold out of nothing. Non-emptiness of I\_cov is likewise an explicit antecedent. Conditions in I∖I\_cov take no part in this universal, and emptiness of Trig or of ReplayAdm for such a condition may not be used to adjudicate ReplayReady as 0; by contrast, if a condition belonging to I\_cov has empty ReplayAdm, ReplayMass = 0, or D\_{i,ν} = ∅, it is adjudicated 0 in the ordinary way and may not be removed from I\_cov.

\**Definition 3.40*\*〔aux〕（Re-entry witness）　Below κ = ReplayCtx(i,ν). ReplayWit\_R(L,i,ν,Π) holds if and only if (i,ν) ∈ ReplayPair(L,I), D\_{i,ν} ≠ ∅, Π ∈ ExtJointReal^κ\_R(D\_{i,ν}), and: (i) Bear^κ(Π) ⊆ R ∩ Estab\_t (automatic from X^Replay); (ii) Acc^κ(Π) ⊆ X^Replay\_{i,ν}; (iii) ∀d ∈ D\_{i,ν}: Realized^κ(d,ν;Supp(ρ\_d),W\_d) = 1, with ρ\_d = (Supp(ρ\_d),W\_d); (iv) ∀d: Bear^κ(ρ\_d) ∩ Inst(i,ν) = ∅.

\**Definition 3.41*\*〔aux〕（Ω witness）　ΩWit\_R(L,i,ν,Π) holds if and only if ReplayWit\_R(L,i,ν,Π) and ∀d ∈ D\_{i,ν}: Reach\_{X^Replay\_{i,ν}}(E\_{X^Replay\_{i,ν}}(i), Bear^κ(ρ\_d)) ∧ ΩOK^κ(ρ\_d,d,ν).

\**Definition 3.42*\*〔S〕（StableReplay^M）　StableReplay^M\_F,t(L;R,I) = 1 if and only if ReplayReady = 1 and, for every i ∈ I\_cov(L,I):

　∀^{Replay}\_{L,i}\[∃Π ReplayWit\_R(L,i,ν,Π)\] = 1.

\**Definition 3.43*\*〔S〕（ΩSupport^M）　ΩSupport^M\_F,t(L;R,I) = 1 if and only if ReplayReady = 1 and, for every i ∈ I\_cov(L,I):

　∀^{Replay}\_{L,i}\[∃Π ΩWit\_R(L,i,ν,Π)\] = 1.

\**Definition 3.44*\*〔aux〕（Realization family）　Scheme\_R(L,i,ν) = {Π ∈ ExtJointReal^{ReplayCtx(i,ν)}\_R(D\_{i,ν}) \| ∀d ∈ D\_{i,ν}: ρ\_d ∈ RoleReal^{ReplayCtx(i,ν)}\_R(d)}.

\**Definition 3.45*\*〔aux〕（Bind^M）　Bind^M\_F,t(L;R,I) = 1 if and only if ReplayReady = 1 and, for every i ∈ I\_cov(L,I):

　∀^{Replay}\_{L,i}\[  
　　∃Π(ΩWit\_R(L,i,ν,Π) ∧ Π ∈ Scheme\_R(L,i,ν))  
　\] = 1.

\**Definition 3.46*\*〔S〕（Govern^M）　Govern^M ⇔ DynamicRole^M ∧ ΩSupport^M ∧ Bind^M.

\**Same-domain convention.*\*　CarrierRealizes^M, CarrierNonInstant^M, ReplayReady, StableReplay^M, ΩSupport^M, Bind^M and DynamicRole^M all use one and the same I\_cov(L,I); the domain of application may not be redetermined severally at different stages. This corresponds to the "same coverage domain" and "H2 has the same domain at both ends" of *The General Form of Law*.

Three-valued propagation: if any conjunct is 0 the value is 0; if none is 0 and at least one is ⊥ the value is ⊥; only if all are 1 is the value 1.

\**Definition 3.47*\*〔aux〕（Solution set）　𝒮(F\_t,L,I) = {R ⊆ Existing\_t \| EstWitness^M = 1 ∧ Govern^M = 1}.

### 3.8　Declaration of Relational Properties

　⇝\_t: not assumed reflexive or transitive; not symmetric.  
　↝: irreflexive, not symmetric; antisymmetric on strictly time-advancing nodes; transitive closure borne by the generation chain.  
　ActArg\_ν^Ctx, ActArg^μ: the two sides are of different types; many-to-one; not monotone under enlargement of the condition set; the same ν may differ under different Ctx; ActArg^μ has no general monotonicity in ε\_role.  
　StructBear, ActBear, NonInstBear: the two sides are of different types; NonInstBear ⊆ StructBear; ActBear and NonInstBear have no general inclusion.  
　Reach\_B: reflexive and transitive at the element level; in general neither symmetric nor antisymmetric.  
　Org\_t: symmetric; defined between distinct blocks, reflexivity not at issue; not transitive.  
　π within RolePath: a finite chain of ↝. ↝\*\_χ: the reflexive transitive closure of ↝ within χ, reflexive and transitive; in general not symmetric.  
　Hosts: a map from nodes to sets of constituents; Comp: a map from node sets to sets of constituents, a path being a special case.  
　⊆: a partial order.

### 4.2　Theorems

\**Theorem 4.1*\*　Bind^M = 1 ⇒ ΩSupport^M = 1 ⇒ StableReplay^M = 1.

*Proof.* For any i and ν,

　\[∃Π(ΩWit ∧ Π ∈ Scheme)\] ⇒ \[∃Π ΩWit\] ⇒ \[∃Π ReplayWit\].

All three use at the outer layer the same coverage polarity ∀^{Replay}\_{L,i}. In set mode the ordinary universal preserves pointwise implication; in measure mode, if an event A ⊆ B and A holds almost everywhere, then B holds almost everywhere too. Hence at each i the Bind condition implies the ΩSupport condition, which implies the StableReplay condition; and all three share ReplayReady = 1. □

\**Theorem 4.2*\*　NonInstBear ⊆ StructBear; ActBear and NonInstBear have no general inclusion.  
*Proof.* The former from Definition 3.20. The latter: a structure now being generated may still be maintained by instantaneous input; an already formed structure may be presently idle. □

\**Theorem 4.3*\*　∀d ∈ D ∃ρ\_d ∈ Real^κ\_B(d,ν) does not entail JointReal^κ\_B(D,ν) ≠ ∅.  
*Proof.* The several ρ\_d may each be realized under mutually conflicting gatings, resource values or W\_d, with no common ν. □

\**Theorem 4.4*\*（CarrierRealizes does not entail CarrierNonInstant）　CarrierNonInstant^M ⇒ CarrierRealizes^M; the converse fails.  
*Proof.* The former from Definition 3.36. Counterexample: R = g ∪ R\_Ω, with g a necessary gate presently maintained by instantaneous input and R\_Ω ⊆ Estab\_t. Every legitimate Π has g ∈ Q(Π) while g ∉ Estab\_t; hence there is no Π with Q(Π) ⊆ Estab\_t, and Definition 3.36 fails. □

The following 4.5–4.10 are single-office, single-context lemmas; they do not yield global predicates. Notation of Section IV: realization witnesses are written ρ\_A, ρ\_B, ρ\_C, ρ\_d throughout, and physical support is written Supp(·) throughout; braces denote candidate supports only.

\**Lemma 4.5*\*（Structural cold standby）　g → A → y, g → \[sel\_B\] → B → y, sel\_B = ¬failure, with failure = 0 in ν, and κ any context containing this structure. Then ¬∃W PreRealized^κ(d,ν;{g,B},W) = 1.  
*Proof.* When sel\_B is a state quantity of B, every η in ResEval has sel\_B = 0 by σ\|\_P, B does not generate y, and 3.21(iii) fails; when sel\_B is a structural variable outside P it is unavailable, the gating of B is undefined within ResEval(κ,P,ν), and 3.21(ii) or (iii) is adjudicated 0. □

\**Lemma 4.6*\*（Structural pre-emption）　y = A ∨ Z, Z = ¬A ∧ C, with A = 1 in ν. Then ¬∃W PreRealized^κ(d,ν;{g,C},W) = 1.  
*Proof.* Z refers to the structural variable A outside P. □

\**Lemma 4.7*\*（Temporal pre-emption）　The local generation functions of A and C do not refer to one another, and A arriving first settles y\_{t+Δ}. Then there exists a legitimate W with ρ\_C = ({g,C},W) ∈ ExtReal^κ\_R(d), while ρ\_C ∉ RoleReal^κ\_R(d), where κ is a context containing A.  
*Proof.* Within the restricted evaluation η ∈ ResEval(κ,{g,C},ν) no variable records that A arrived first; y is regenerated from C, and taking W = {η} gives Realized^κ(d,ν;{g,C},W) = 1, Bear = {C} ⊆ R, with g only in Acc, so ρ\_C ∈ ExtReal — and this is exactly where the whole-field ν differs from the restricted η. Under κ the ActArg of y\_{t+Δ} contains only A\_t; the state of C is not in FullChain^κ, and 3.32(iii) fails. □

\**Lemma 4.8*\*（Shared-segment free ride）　The main path g → B₁ → A₂ → y arrives first, the standby path g → B₁ → B₂ → y is temporally pre-empted, κ is a context containing A₂, and ρ\_B = ({g,B₁,B₂},W\_B) is its legitimate realization witness. Then ρ\_B ∈ ExtReal^κ\_R(d) while ρ\_B ∉ RoleReal^κ\_R(d).  
*Proof.* The path B₁ → A₂ → y has A₂ ∈ Comp(π) and A₂ ∉ Supp(ρ\_B), violating 3.32(iv); the path B₁ → B₂ → y is not in FullChain^κ, violating (iii). □

\**Lemma 4.9*\*（Joint-node free ride）　Suppose y is generated by a joint node Z = joint(B,A′) with A′ ∉ Supp(ρ). Then every π through Z has A′ ∈ Comp(π), violating 3.32(iv).  
*Proof.* Hosts(Z) ⊇ Hosts(state of A′) ∋ A′. □

\**Lemma 4.10*\*（Hot redundancy, conditional lemma）　Suppose ρ\_A, ρ\_B ∈ ExtReal^κ\_R(d), with A ⊆ Bear^κ(ρ\_A,d,ν) and B ⊆ Bear^κ(ρ\_B,d,ν), A and B connected in parallel and simultaneously conducting, each of their bearing states belonging to some minimal sufficient set of Read(d) under κ, and suppose there exist paths π\_A, π\_B from the bearing states of A and of B respectively to Read(d), with edges in FullChain^κ(d) and Comp(π\_A) ⊆ Supp(ρ\_A), Comp(π\_B) ⊆ Supp(ρ\_B). Then ρ\_A, ρ\_B ∈ RoleReal^κ\_R(d).  
*Proof.* The stated paths satisfy 3.32(i)–(iv), so RolePath is non-empty; this connects directly with Definition 3.33. □

\**Theorem 4.11*\*（Pure correlation）　If for every (i,ν) ∈ AdmPair (with i ∈ I\_cov) and every d ∈ D\_{i,ν}, no state of R lies in the bearing chain of Read(d) under its office type — the Arg/ActArg chain for deterministic type, the A\_y/ActArg^μ chain for weighted and distribution types — then DynamicRole^M = 0.  
*Proof.* RelState is empty or not in FullChain, so RolePath = ∅. □

\**Theorem 4.12*\*（g ∪ R\_Ω）　Let I = {i}, Trig(i) = {d}, AdmPair(L,I) ≠ ∅ (and, when EvalMode(L,i) = measure, RoleMass(L,i) \> 0), ReplayReady holding, Anchor(d) the entrance state of R\_Ω, R\_Ω ⊆ R ∩ Estab\_t, and the necessary continuation segments of R\_Ω having Ω \> 0; and suppose that for every ν ∈ Adm(L,i) ∪ ReplayAdm(L,i) and the corresponding κ: ρ = ({g} ∪ R\_Ω, W) ∈ ExtReal^κ\_R(d), Bear^κ(ρ,d,ν) = R\_Ω, Acc = {g}, g ∈ Inst(i,ν), g ∉ Existing\_t; Reach\_{X^Replay\_{i,ν}}(E\_{X^Replay\_{i,ν}}(i), R\_Ω) holds; RolePath^κ\_ρ(d) ≠ ∅. If in measure mode all required measures are decidable, then EstWitness^M = 0, DynamicRole^M = 1, StableReplay^M = ΩSupport^M = Bind^M = 1, and Govern^M = 1.  
*Proof.* EstWitness: JointReal^{RoleCtx}\_R takes R as base, κ\_P is in fixed mode, and g ∈ Inst(i,ν) ∖ R is a structural variable outside and may not be regenerated by i; for any P ⊆ R, within ResEval either Read(d) is undefined or Closed fails, Good = ∅, ¬∃W PreRealized^{RoleCtx}(d,ν;P,W) = 1, and CarrierRealizes^M = 0. This theorem exhibits only the boundary where Est = 0 while Govern = 1; the realization qualification of P is given by the antecedent and is not reproved here. For each ν the strong antecedent directly yields the formation-state RoleReal; in set mode a legitimate existence is obtained, and in measure mode, since the strong antecedent holds evaluation by evaluation, a positive-measure existence is obtained; hence ∃^{Role}\_{L,i} = 1 and DynamicRole^M = 1. Likewise on the re-entry side: the antecedents of ExtReal, RolePath, Reach and ΩOK, holding evaluation by evaluation, give the ReplayWit, ΩWit and Scheme coverage under ∀^{Replay}\_{L,i}; hence StableReplay^M = ΩSupport^M = Bind^M = 1. Govern^M follows by Definition 3.46. □

\**Theorem 4.13*\*（One strong impulse）　Let a new Groove-Line A ⊆ R have entered the field, and suppose there exists i′ ∈ I\_cov(L,I) with

　∃^{Role}\_{L,i′}\[∃d′ ∈ D\_{i′,ν} ∃ρ\_A ∈ RoleReal^{RoleCtx(i′,ν)}\_R(d′): A ⊆ Supp(ρ\_A)\] = 1;

the necessary continuation segments of A have Ω = 0; and there exists i ∈ I\_cov(L,I) with

　∀^{Replay}\_{L,i}\[∃Π ΩWit\_R(L,i,ν,Π)\] = 0,

that value not being ⊥. Then DynamicRole^M = 1 and ΩSupport^M = 0.

*Proof.* The former by Definition 3.38; the latter because the coverage condition explicitly fails at some i, whence ΩSupport^M = 0 by Definition 3.43. □

\**Theorem 4.14*\*（Abandoned old Groove-Line）　Let R be already formed, with Ω \> 0 and reachable, and suppose that for every (i,ν) ∈ AdmPair (with i ∈ I\_cov) and every d ∈ D\_{i,ν}, no state of R enters the generation of Read(d). Then DynamicRole^M = 0; StableReplay^M and ΩSupport^M may be 1.  
*Proof.* Definitions 3.40 and 3.41 do not check role. RolePath is empty for every (i,ν). □

\**Theorem 4.15*\*（Dead end; Ω elsewhere）　Let i ∈ I\_cov(L,I). If

　∀^{Replay}\_{L,i}\[∃Π ΩWit\_R(L,i,ν,Π)\] = 0,

then ΩSupport^M = 0. The following two constructions suffice to make that quantifier 0, each interpreted according to mode:

　\**Dead end.*\*　Let ν ∈ ReplayAdm(L,i) and d ∈ D\_{i,ν} be such that, for every P ⊆ X^Replay\_{i,ν}, there exists under Restrict(ReplayCtx(i,ν),P) no generation chain from the antecedent to Read(d) satisfying Closed^κ. Then for every P, ¬∃W PreRealized^{ReplayCtx(i,ν)}(d,ν;P,W) = 1, and that (i,ν) has no ΩWit.  
　\**Ω elsewhere.*\*　Let ν ∈ ReplayAdm(L,i), and suppose there exists d ∈ D\_{i,ν} such that ∀ρ ∈ ExtReal^{ReplayCtx(i,ν)}\_R(d): ¬Reach\_{X^Replay\_{i,ν}}(E\_{X^Replay\_{i,ν}}(i), Bear^{ReplayCtx(i,ν)}(ρ,d,ν)). Then that (i,ν) has no ΩWit. That there are Ω segments elsewhere in R is merely background to the counterexample and does not enter the antecedent.

\**Mode interpretation.*\*　When EvalMode(L,i) = set, a single such ν already makes that quantifier 0 (a counterexample to the ordinary universal). When EvalMode(L,i) = measure, a single-point failure does not suffice: the failure event

　{ν ∈ ReplayAdm(L,i) \| ¬∃Π ΩWit\_R(L,i,ν,Π)}

must have positive measure (equivalently, μ̄^Replay\_{L,i}(∃Π ΩWit) \< 1) before the quantifier takes 0; null-measure dead-end evaluations do not make ΩSupport^M = 0 (the a.e. reading of 3.16′).

In both branches i must belong to I\_cov: conditions in I∖I\_cov bear no coverage obligation, and the absence of ΩWit there does not yield ΩSupport^M = 0. □

\**Theorem 4.16*\*（Bind and DynamicRole）　If Existing\_t = Estab\_t (Drive\_t^inst = ∅), and RoleCtx = ReplayCtx, and the relevant i satisfies \|ModeSet(L,i)\| = 1 so that Role and Replay share the unique EvalMode(L,i), then Bind^M = 1 ⇒ DynamicRole^M = 1.

*Proof.* Bind^M = 1 gives, at each i,

　∀^{Replay}\_{L,i}\[∃Π(ΩWit ∧ Π ∈ Scheme)\] = 1.

Pointwise,

　∃Π(ΩWit ∧ Π ∈ Scheme) ⇒ ∃d ∈ D\_{i,ν}: RoleReal^{ReplayCtx}\_R(d) ≠ ∅.

Hence in set mode there exists at least one legitimate evaluation possessing RoleReal; in measure mode the set of evaluations on which coverage holds has full measure, and since ReplayAdm is non-empty and the total mass of the conditional measure is 1, that RoleReal event has positive measure. From RoleCtx = ReplayCtx together with the shared unique EvalMode(L,i), we obtain at some i that ∃^{Role}\_{L,i}\[∃d RoleReal ≠ ∅\] = 1, whence DynamicRole^M = 1. □

*Note.* Where there is a present instantaneous drive the two contexts differ, and present instantaneous structure may under RoleCtx pre-empt a role path that holds under ReplayCtx, so the implication need not hold. Theorems 4.13 and 4.14 show that DynamicRole^M and ΩSupport^M each have independent failure worlds; (4.6) explicitly retains all three items, in correspondence with the Govern ⇔ DynamicRole ∧ ΩSupport of S.

\**Theorem 4.17*\*（The cross-realization chain-witnessing failure type of Bind）　Let R = A ∪ C ⊆ Existing\_t, ReplayReady(L,I) = 1, and:

　① there exists i\_A ∈ I\_cov(L,I) with

　　∃^{Role}\_{L,i\_A}\[∃d\_A ∈ D\_{i\_A,ν} ∃ρ\_A ∈ RoleReal^{RoleCtx(i\_A,ν)}\_R(d\_A)\] = 1;

　② for every i ∈ I\_cov(L,I),

　　∀^{Replay}\_{L,i}\[∃Π ΩWit\_R(L,i,ν,Π)\] = 1;

　③ there exists at least one i ∈ I\_cov(L,I) with

　　∀^{Replay}\_{L,i}\[∃Π(ΩWit ∧ Π ∈ Scheme)\] = 0,

that value not being ⊥.

Then DynamicRole^M = 1, ΩSupport^M = 1, Bind^M = 0, Govern^M = 0.  
*Proof.* ① gives DynamicRole^M = 1 by Definition 3.38; ② together with ReplayReady gives ΩSupport^M = 1; ③ gives Bind^M = 0; Govern^M = 0 by (4.6). □

*Note.* A witness in ② possessing ΩWit yet not entering Scheme may be constructed by the temporal pre-emption of Lemma 4.7: C is Realized within the restricted base, but under a ReplayCtx containing A it is not in FullChain. ③ separately excludes other in-family Ω witnesses. A must belong to R ⊆ Existing\_t; constituents of Inst(i,ν) can enter only Acc, and cannot serve as the bearing subject A.

\**Theorem 4.18*\*（Weak binding is insufficient）　If (4.5) is changed to ∃(i,ν), then there exists a world with Bind^M = 1 while some (i,ν) has no in-family Ω witness.  
*Proof.* I = {i₁,i₂} with I\_cov(L,I) = I (both belonging to the legitimate office domain claimed applicable by L), and A ⊆ R ∩ Estab\_t. At i₁ set matters as in ②③ of Theorem 4.17: ΩWit is satisfied by Π\_C while Π\_C ∉ Scheme, and there is no in-family substitute; at i₂, C bears normally and there exists Π\_C satisfying both ΩWit and family membership. After changing to ∃(i,ν) the condition is satisfied by i₂, while at i₁ there is no in-family Ω witness. □

\**Theorem 4.19*\*（A substitutive family is insufficient）　Under the antecedents of Theorem 4.17, if Scheme is changed to ExtJointReal^{ReplayCtx(i,ν)}\_R(D\_{i,ν}) itself, then the Π\_C of ② enters the family and Bind^M = 1.  
*Proof.* Π\_C ∈ ExtJointReal by Definition 3.41 (ΩWit ⇒ ReplayWit) and 3.40. □

\**Theorem 4.20*\*（Govern under hot redundancy）　Let A ∥ B be in parallel, with B possessing Ω and A not; let ReplayReady hold; and for every i ∈ I\_cov(L,I) let

　∀^{Replay}\_{L,i}\[there exists a complete joint realization Π\_B satisfying ΩWit and entering Scheme\] = 1;

and let there exist i ∈ I\_cov(L,I) with

　∃^{Role}\_{L,i}\[∃d ∈ D\_{i,ν}: there exists ρ\_A or ρ\_{B,d} ∈ RoleReal^{RoleCtx(i,ν)}\_R(d)\] = 1.

Then Govern^M = 1; and the formation-state witness of DynamicRole^M is not required to be the same realization witness as the Π\_B bearing Ω in each Replay evaluation, so the two may differ (witnesses with the same Supp but different W are likewise distinct witnesses).  
*Proof.* DynamicRole follows from the formation-state existential quantifier ∃^{Role} taking the value 1 together with Definition 3.38. ΩSupport follows from ReplayReady and the ∀^{Replay} ΩWit coverage at each i; Bind follows from the same Π\_B satisfying both ΩWit and family membership; Govern follows by (4.6). □

\**Proposition 4.21*\*（Maintenance-type office, conditional proposition）　Let κ = ReplayCtx(i,ν), let Rel(L) prescribe for i that y remains unchanged, let d ∈ D\_{i,ν}, and let Anchor(d) ∋ θ (a threshold or gating state in R blocking i); let ρ ∈ ExtReal^κ\_R(d) with θ ∈ RelState^κ\_ρ(u,d,ν) for some u ∈ Bear^κ(ρ,d,ν); let θ under κ belong to some minimal sufficient set for y\_{t+Δ} taking its unchanged value; and let the path π\_θ from θ to Read(d) within FullChain^κ(d) satisfy Comp(π\_θ) ⊆ Supp(ρ). Then RolePath^κ\_ρ(d) ≠ ∅.  
*Proof.* π\_θ satisfies 3.32(i)–(iv). □  
*Note.* If y\_{t+Δ} = y\_t is sufficiently determined by y\_t alone and θ belongs to no minimal sufficient set, then that maintenance is not borne by θ and RolePath may be empty; in that case Anchor(d) should be taken as the state of y itself, as determined by Rel(L).

## Section V　Running and Solving

### 5.1　Running

Given F\_t, L and I, the system runs by the following steps.

　1\. Take Existing\_t from F\_t; enumerate candidates R ⊆ Existing\_t.  
　2\. Adjudicate CurrentCarrier^M (3.34). On failure, R is eliminated.
