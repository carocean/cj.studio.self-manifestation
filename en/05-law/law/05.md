# The Construction Form of the Law Operating Substrate

## Section I　Purpose

*The Construction Form of the Minimal Sedimentation Field* has already constructed a runnable Sedimentation-Field F\_min satisfying C1–C7. This text treats the constructive problem that follows it: how to add, upon F\_min, the structures required for law-side discrimination, so as to obtain an operating substrate F\_LB sufficient to bear the generation, running and adjudication of law together with the testing of pseudo-law counterexamples. This text does not certify the minimality of the added structures.

Constructive goal:

　　∃Spec\_LB \[ Spec\_LB = Augment(Spec\_min, A\_LB) ∧ SedFieldSpec(Spec\_LB) ∧ LawBaseCapable(Spec\_LB) \]

where Spec\_LB is the fixed operating-substrate specification. Inst(Spec\_LB) = Traj(Spec\_LB, Init\_LB, Prot\_LB) is the set of legitimate instances / state trajectories produced by that specification from a common initial state through the permitted protocol Prot\_LB. Prot\_LB = {Mode\_exo, Mode\_endo, Freeze, Thaw, IdleStep, do(Mask), do(Avail)}; direct rewriting of g, d, rep or Ω does not belong to the permitted protocol, and if a structural-deletion intervention is to be made it must be declared separately and the closure of derived quantities preserved.

LawBaseCapable(Spec\_LB) ⇔ FormationCap ∧ RoleCap ∧ StabilityCap ∧ BoundaryCap:

　FormationCap　able to produce Deposited, Groove and Ω, and able to distinguish a single strong impulse from repeated recurrence (LB1, LB2, LB5, LB9);  
　RoleCap　able to perform do(Mask\_R) upon a designated R and compare with a control organization (LB3);  
　StabilityCap　able, by Freeze^inst, to halt sedimentation while the already formed structure goes on running (LB8);  
　BoundaryCap　able to operate Avail independently, and to construct a counterexample with Corr = 1 ∧ MediumReach = 1 ∧ Dyn = 0 (LB4, LB6, LB7).

LB1–LB9 are the verification suite for these four capacities; each is witnessed by an instance from Inst(Spec\_LB). This text does not require that the state at a single moment satisfy all nine items simultaneously; "the same F" means several legitimate instances of the same specification.

　　LawBaseCapable(Spec\_LB) ⇏ Law(L).

This text does not certify R ∈ 𝒮, and does not certify Law(L). In the end it certifies only this: that several law-side diagnostic predicates can be separately manipulated upon instances of the same specification.

3.3　Added by this text (the law operating substrate layer)

　p　pulse intensity: Sediment writes by α·p and β·p;  
　θ\_e　threshold; S\_e = \[g\_e ≥ θ\_e\]　the continuation structural state;

Note (θ and S are this text's engineering readings): the θ of this text is a qualification threshold on sediment quantity, used only in S\_e = \[g\_e ≥ θ\_e\]; the θ of Field Chapter 1 is an outflow threshold, used to decide outflow once the entry flow pressure reaches it (Equation 4, Theorem 17). No identity has been established between the two: this construction does not implement the flow-pressure–outflow-threshold mechanism, and a fixed θ does not implement "the deeper, the smaller the threshold" (Field Chapter 1 Equation 3a); the S of this text is only an engineering reading of whether g exceeds the threshold, and is not automatically the continuation structure S of Field Chapter 1, which is given by route status and other conditions (Definition 14). The variables and the code are unchanged.  
　\**the two clocks*\*　t\_run, the running step, incremented at each pulse; t\_sed, the sedimentation step, incremented only when the sedimentary law executes (Decay). At a normal pulse both increase together; IdleStep increases only t\_sed; Freeze-run increases only t\_run.  
　rep\_e　the recurrence-event moments r satisfying t\_sed − W ≤ r ≤ t\_sed (reckoned by t\_sed; a span of W with both ends included, so that on an integer clock up to W + 1 moments are held; the code deletes a record when t\_sed − r \> W); window expiry is bound to t\_sed, not to t\_run;  
　Γ\_t　the derived Groove-Line set: Groove(e) ⇔ S\_e ∧ \|rep\_e\| ≥ 2;  
　Ω\_e　Stable Recurrence Capacity: Ω\_e = min(1, \|rep\_e\|/2n\_Ω) if \|rep\_e\| ≥ n\_Ω ∧ S\_e, and 0 otherwise;

Note (Groove-Line and Groove, against Field Chapter 1 v2.7): the Groove-Line of Field Chapter 1 is the groove formed by Trace-Response, which has depth δ and Threshold θ from its formation (the generative chain; Definition 14); in this construction it corresponds to Deposited(e) (g\_e \> 0). The Groove(e) ⇔ S\_e ∧ \|rep\_e\| ≥ 2 of this text is an engineering qualification predicate that adds a threshold and windowed recurrence; it is not the Groove-Line of Field Chapter 1, nor is it a route (Route\_W ⇔ Ω\_W = 1); Γ\_t is the set on which this qualification predicate holds. "Groove-formation" and "striking a Groove-Line off" below refer to gaining and losing this qualification, not to the coming into being or passing away of the groove formed by Trace-Response itself: after Groove is struck off by window expiry, Deposited may still be 1 and the Groove-Line still has depth (Outer Chapter 3, "Gully-Lines and Ravine-Ends possess depth"); see the note to LB9.

Note (Ω\_e and Ω\_W, against Field Chapter 1 v2.7): the Ω\_W of Field Chapter 1 is a historical qualification predicate within a declared window W (taking 0 or 1), checking three facts: the recurrence record within W reaches the declared criterion; the connections of the groove-lines followed are maintained (Brk\_W = 0); recurrence events occur in every sub-interval (Definition 12). The Ω\_e of this text is an operationalized degree quantity of its own: Ω\_e \> 0 ⇔ \|rep\_e\| ≥ n\_Ω ∧ S\_e, so it checks only the first fact (the window being \[t\_sed − W, t\_sed\], W = 30, both ends included; criterion n\_Ω = 3), adding the threshold qualification S\_e; the second fact has no independent breakage mechanism in this construction (Avail being an exogenous control) and is not checked separately; the third fact is not checked. When Ω\_e \> 0, Ω\_e is non-decreasing in the number of recurrences within the window, saturating at 1. To compare the two, this note adopts a sub-interval convention: \[t\_sed − W, t\_sed\] is divided into thirds \[lo, lo + W/3\], (lo + W/3, lo + 2W/3\], (lo + 2W/3, t\_sed\] (lo = t\_sed − W); the criterion is n\_Ω; maintained connection means that the sedimentary connection of every edge followed persists within the window and is unobstructed; in this construction the witness checks at least both g \> 0 (Deposited) and Avail = 1, each holding after every Decay within the window. Avail = 1 says only that the medium is available; it does not guarantee that the sedimented groove remains, and cannot by itself be equated with the upstream Brk\_W = 0: e.g., after one pulse of p = 0.05 followed by 5 idle steps, Avail = 1 while g = 0 and Deposited = 0. Under this convention \[Ω\_e \> 0\] and Ω\_W = 1 do not imply each other. First, Ω\_W = 1 while Ω\_e = 0: 32 consecutive pulses of p = 0.05 along a1→m1→c; at the end t\_sed = 32, the window \[2, 32\] retains the moments 2 to 32, 31 recurrences in all (the first fact holds), after every Decay within the window both a1→m1 and m1→c have Avail = 1, and the minimum g of the two edges is about 0.0025 \> 0 (the second fact holds under this convention), and the three sub-intervals contain 11, 10, 10 recurrences (the third fact holds); yet g(m1→c) ≈ 0.08923 \< θ, S = 0, and Ω\_e = 0. Second, Ω\_e \> 0 while the third fact fails: three pulses of p = 2 followed by 20 IdleSteps give t\_sed = 23, window \[−7, 23\], rep moments 1, 2, 3, Ω\_e = 0.5, and the three sub-intervals contain 3, 0, 0 recurrences. The Ω\_e of this text does not replace Ω\_W; ΩSupport^pre ⇝ ΩSupport^M in Section VIII is likewise only a candidate interface (K13).

Note (repetition and deepening): at each passage Sediment increases g by β·p·(1 − g/g\_max); this is the deepening rule declared by this implementation — repeated passage deepens, saturating at g\_max — and how much a given passage deepens is given by this rule. Ω\_e counts recurrence and g measures depth; the two are kept apart (Field Chapter 1 Definition 12: Ω corresponds to recurrence, δ to depth).  
　\**exogenous control quantities*\*　Avail\_t(e), Mask\_t(e) ∈ {0,1}: not written by Sediment, not subject to Decay, altered only through a permitted intervention, and preserved across steps until the next intervention. They are of a kind distinct both from the instantaneous quantities (τ, κ) and from the persistent sedimentary quantities (d, g, rep, Ω).  
　Avail\_t(e)　availability; Continuation and MediumReach pass only through edges with Avail = 1; in normal running it is constantly 1;

　Freeze^inst　the sedimentation-rule switch; IdleStep　shallows only and expires windows only, producing no τ, no rep and no flow;

### 3.4　State Hierarchy

　basic sedimentary state　d, g　　(persistent; written by Sediment, decreased by Decay; the State\_persist of F\_min)  
　structural qualification quantities　S, Γ　　(derived from g, θ, rep)  
　historical window state　rep　　(persistent; appended by Sediment, expiring by t\_sed)  
　derived capacity quantity　Ω　　(derived from rep and S; not a new sedimentary memory)  
　exogenous control quantities　Avail, Mask, SED  
　clocks　t\_run, t\_sed  
　instantaneous quantities　τ, κ, q

The persistent sedimentary state remains only (d, g, rep), where rep is a window record; the low-configuration character of the State\_persist = (d, g) of F\_min is not destroyed by the added layer.

κ adds S and Mask to the inherited formula: κ\_e = κ₀ + w\_g·Mask\_e·g\_e·(1 if S\_e else s\_weak) + w\_d·d\_{terminal position of e}.

Note: this construction realizes the choice of continuation by softmax shares. Comparison condition: with the scores of the other candidates unchanged, the edge available (Avail = 1), Mask = 1, and the terminal d and the threshold parameters fixed, under the parameters of this text (w\_g \> 0, s\_weak \> 0), raising the g of that edge does not lower its softmax selection probability; if the entry has at least one other available candidate, it raises it strictly; if that edge is the only available out-edge, its selection probability is constantly 1. For two different edges, a common entry and a difference of depth alone do not determine the order of probabilities: e.g., with a deep branch g ≈ 4.2306 and a shallow branch g ≈ 1.1804, after do(Mask = 0) on the deep branch its selection probability is about 0.000455. This is an operational choice of this implementation (in the sense of share), distinct from the precedence criterion of Field Chapter 1 Law 5 (under C₌ the deeper satisfies the outflow condition no later than the shallower, and strictly first under C₌ and P₁₇); neither implies the other. The κ of Field Chapter 1 is a convergence condition derived from δ, θ and S; the present formula is composed only of g, S, d and the exogenous controls Mask and Avail, and introduces no independent variable.

The added layer preserves the order of the sedimentary chain of F\_min: Decay → Continuation → Trace → Sediment.

## Section IV　The LawBaseCapable Condition Group

Instances of the same specification must be able actually to accommodate and to distinguish:

　\**LB1*\*　Historical groove-formation: from the initial state Γ₀ = ∅, the Groove qualification is derived through repeated action (Γ is the set on which the qualification predicate holds, not the groove formed by Trace-Response; see the note in 3.3).  
　\**LB2*\*　Single strong impulse: a single strong action leaves a complete non-instantaneous bearing and really influences what follows, yet forms no Stable Recurrence Capacity.  
　\**LB3*\*　Real dynamical office: the states of the candidate organization R really enter subsequent generation, the effect being attributable to R and not elsewhere (local role intervention with a control).　That record ≠ governance has already been proved by C5 upstream and is not reproved here.  
　\**LB4*\*　Pure correlation: two quantities correlate at 1 under the macro-event co-occurrence protocol and are structurally reachable, yet the state of one does not enter the generation of the other.  
　\**LB5*\*　Stable recurrence: Ω is derived from the history of recurrence together with structural qualification, not stipulated; a sub-threshold weak trace does not acquire the Groove qualification, and Ω\_e = 0, even when its recurrence count is sufficient (the witness for the office of θ).  
　\**LB6*\*　Reachability interface: do(Avail = 0) switches Reach upon one and the same R from 1 to 0, while g, Ω and Γ are unchanged.  
　\**LB7*\*　Abandoned old Groove-Line: Established(R) = 1 ∧ ∃e ∈ R: Ω\_e \> 0 ∧ Reach(I,R) = 0.  
　\**LB8*\*　Freeze-run: sedimentation is switched off while the dynamics of holding-tendency goes on generating.  
　\**LB9*\*　Shallowing: with no action, the trace fades and a Groove-Line may be struck off.

5.4　The Measurement Protocol for Correlation

Macro-event: each external event M\_k consists of several exogenous pulses belonging to one and the same label. Corr\_M(a,b) = #{k \| a and b both occur in M\_k} / #{k \| a or b occurs in M\_k}. Long-window presence is not used.
