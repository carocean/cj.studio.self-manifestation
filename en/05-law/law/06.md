# The Determination Form of Law

## Section I　Purpose

This text is a Determination Form. Its use: to give the procedure and criteria for adjudicating, from an observation set Y, whether a property P holds or fails, either two-way valid or with explicitly declared bounds of misadjudication.

　　P := R ∈ 𝒮(F\_LB, L, I).

𝒮 is taken from *The Mathematical Form of Law* (4.7): R ∈ 𝒮 ⇔ EstWitness^M ∧ Govern^M. This text redefines no upstream predicate and undertakes no ontological proof of law; it only turns already sealed criteria into an executable adjudication protocol upon F\_LB, and runs it upon positive and negative instances.

\**Declaration of the adjudication domain*\*: the adjudication domain of this text consists of candidates upon a frozen snapshot that are single-office, single-entry (I = {Ante(L)}), and measure-type (weighted). The interface of the reference implementation is narrowed accordingly: the candidate data structure (\`duty\`) admits only single-office, measure-type candidates; at run time it further checks single entry I = {Ante}, outputting ⊥ where this fails; and the program receives a freezable F\_LB state and generates a Freeze^inst snapshot before adjudicating. Multi-office, set-type and mixed-type candidates do not belong to the domain of definition of this implementation, and their adjudication belongs to subsequent versions of this text.

\**I\_cov check*\*　From *The Mathematical Form of Law* onward, the quantification domain of the coverage-type criteria and of DynamicRole is the applicability-coverage guard domain I\_cov(L,I) = {i ∈ I \| Trig(i) ≠ ∅}. The adjudication domain of this text is the single entry I = {Ante(L)}, and the candidate L declares an office for that entry (Trig ≠ ∅); hence I\_cov(L,I) = I, and the adjudications of the five cases of this text are unaffected by that narrowing of the guard domain. Under a multi-entry adjudication domain, Trig must be checked entry by entry; that belongs to subsequent versions of this text.

\**Verification of entry conditions**: the definition of P is closed (*The Mathematical Form of Law*); Y has been delimited (Section III); the forward relation between P and Y has been given by texts already standing (Sections III and IV of *The Mathematical Form of Law\* + the interface object of Law Group-Form V + the protocols PR1–PR4 of this text, the failure conditions of the protocols entering the bounds of misadjudication).

## Section III　Declaration of the Property and the Observation Set

### 3.1　Adjudication Scenario

Adjudication proceeds upon a Freeze^inst snapshot, within which there are no pulses in transit.

\**Lemma 3.1 (collapse of the base)*\*　In a frozen snapshot, Existing\_t = Estab\_t = {e \| g\_e \> 0}.  
*Proof.* By the interface definition of 5.3 of Law Group-Form V, Existing\_t = {e \| g\_e \> 0} ∪ the edges presently carrying flow, and Drive^inst = the edges maintained by flow alone; the snapshot has no pulses in transit, so the set of flow-carrying edges is empty, whence Existing\_t = {g \> 0} = Estab\_t. □

By Lemma 3.1, RoleBase = ReplayBase, RoleCtx(i,ν) = ReplayCtx(i,ν) (the two formulas of 3.18 of *The Mathematical Form of Law* agree item by item upon the same base under the same condition), and AdmPair = ReplayPair; the antecedent of Theorem 4.16 of *The Mathematical Form of Law* holds in this scenario.

## Section IV　Instance Protocols

Bringing the objects of *The Mathematical Form of Law* down onto F\_LB requires four protocols. The protocols are declared items; they do not alter the upstream definitions, and the failure conditions of each enter the bounds of misadjudication of Section VII.

　\**PR1 (restricted evaluation kernel)*\*　The clause of κ\_P that "structure outside P is unavailable" is read as: each edge-selection kernel K\_y is restricted to the out-edges within P and renormalized; a position with no out-edge within P is a dead end. Dead-end evaluations contribute no satisfying mass to Read: the overall weighted-type readout counts dead-end mass as unsatisfied (not ⊥ — a dead end is a defined failure, ⊥ being reserved for the unmeasurable and the unsolvable). Where the dead-end mass is of full measure, CR is adjudicated 0.

　\**PR2 (the variation-family grid and its validity condition)*\*　Within AdmVar, the permitted domain of a structural state g is \[0, g\_max\]; the program takes the maximum of the total variation over the product of the endpoint grid {0, g\_max}. Define the validity condition PR2-valid(F, q, C) :⇔ sup\_{AdmVar} TV = max\_{endpoint grid} TV. Three declarations:

　(a) In the single-free-variable case PR2-valid is provable: κ is piecewise linear in g with an upward jump across the threshold, and is everywhere monotone non-decreasing; when only one logit varies, the target coordinate of the softmax is monotone and the remaining coordinates decrease together, TV(t₁,t₂) = \|p\_j(t₁) − p\_j(t₂)\|, and along a monotone path the maximum is attained at an endpoint of the domain. □  
　(b) In the multi-free-variable case PR2-valid is an unproved condition; K-J1 bears its breakdown.  
　(c) The negative direction of the grid is unconditionally reliable: finding on the grid a pair of distributions with TV \> ε\_role proves insufficiency, whether or not the sup is attained by the grid.

Among the five cases of this text, the set of free variables at every point where "sufficiency" is granted is empty (trivially exact), and every "insufficient" adjudication is given by a grid counterexample ((c), unconditionally reliable); hence the adjudications of the five cases do not depend upon PR2-valid.

　\**PR3 (bearing segment and the domain of Ω)*\*　The Ω of F\_LB is defined upon edges; ReqSeg is read as the Groove-Line edges within Bear themselves, and ΩOK ⇔ ∀e ∈ Bear: Ω\_e \> 0.

　\**PR4 (host convention and chain continuation)*\*　The present position and the edge-selection nodes are hostless pure flow quantities (Hosts = ∅); the Hosts of a structural-state node is that edge. Comp collects structural edges only, in accordance with 5.3 of Law Group-Form V.

Argument for chain continuation: each edge-selection kernel of F\_LB takes the present position as a required input — holding all edge states fixed and varying the position alters the whole family of distributions, so the position belongs to every μ-sufficient set of that kernel; and the position is in turn a determinate function of the preceding edge-selection. Hence FullChain continues automatically through "edge-selection → position → next edge-selection," and upon a multi-hop bearing path the only non-trivial ActArg^μ check is concentrated at the tail position of Anchor. The "sufficient set at Anchor + reachability within R" of judge is therefore complete for multi-hop bearing segments as well, and not only for the single hop.

## Section V　Construction of the Criteria

The adjudication procedure (level by level, outputting the first failing predicate; passing all levels adjudicates R ∈ 𝒮):

　　Y\_LB → CC → CR → CNI → Dyn → ΩSupport → Bind → P.

The domain of definition of the program is narrowed by the interface (the \`duty\` structure being single-office and measure-type) together with a run-time check of single entry (outputting ⊥ where I ≠ {Ante}); for candidates within the domain it first generates the frozen snapshot, then exhausts the existential quantifier over implementations: for each P ⊆ R containing Anchor it constructs the canonical witness Π\_P (bearer P, witness family W = ResEval(κ\_P) entire, by PR1) and checks it level by level; R passes if and only if there exists a P for which Π\_P passes all four levels. Failure is located at the deepest level attained among the several P. The levels:

　\**CC*\*　CurrentCarrier^M: R ≠ ∅ ∧ R ⊆ E ∧ ∀e ∈ R: g\_e \> 0. (3.34)

　\**CR*\*　CarrierRealizes^M (weighted type): Anchor ∈ P ∧ μ\_LB^{κ\_P}(Read \| Ante) ≥ π₀, the restricted measure being computed exactly by PR1. (3.35, 3.21)

　\**CNI*\*　CarrierNonInstant^M: given by a reduction lemma, the program asserting its antecedent.

\**Lemma 5.1 (CNI reduction)*\*　Frozen snapshot ∧ CC ∧ CR(Π\_P) ⇒ CarrierNonInstant^M holds for that (L,R,I).  
*Proof chain.* Under each (i,ν) of AdmPair, the Supp of each ρ\_d of Π\_P is ⊆ P (the restricted evaluation of PR1 uses only structure within P), hence Q(Π\_P) ⊆ P ⊆ R; CC gives ∀e ∈ R: g\_e \> 0, and Lemma 3.1 gives Estab = {g \> 0}, hence Q(Π\_P) ⊆ Estab\_t. Further, Inst(i,ν) contains only edges whose g was 0 before entry; during freezing the sedimentary law is switched off and g is not updated (Law Group-Form V, LB8: under Freeze, g is unchanged position by position, K4 bearing its breakdown), and the snapshot has no pulses in transit, so the g before the evaluation enters is the g of the snapshot; CC gives snapshot g \> 0 for every edge within P, whence g \> 0 before entry as well, so P ∩ Inst(i,ν) = ∅ and Bear ∩ Inst = ∅ (3.40 (iv)) holds automatically; the implementation witness is a non-instantaneous joint realization. The several ν share the same frozen structure, so ∀^{Role} holds pointwise. □ (3.36)

　\**Dyn*\*　DynamicRole^M: the Anchor state belongs to some minimal μ-sufficient set at the tail position of Anchor (all minimal sets being exhausted upon the PR2 grid by 3.10′), and from the head of Anchor, Read is reachable through edges within P having Deposited ∧ Mask = 1 (RolePath, 3.32; completeness for multiple hops being given by the chain-continuation argument of PR4). (3.38)

　\**ΩSupport*\*　Reach^inst(I, P) (reachability, along adjacencies with Avail = 1, to the head position of some edge in P with Avail = 1; adjudicated only as far as the head position, Law Group-Form V, K11) ∧ ΩOK upon the Bear of Π\_P (PR3). The world in which the bearing segment itself is sealed is intercepted at the earlier level by CR (the restricted kernel gives a dead end there, PR1). (3.43, 3.41)

　\**Bind*\*　Given by a collapse lemma; no unconditional pass is set up.

\**Lemma 5.2 (Bind collapse)*\*　Under a frozen snapshot, if Π\_P passes the ΩSupport level and passes the Dyn level, then the existential condition of Bind^M, ∃Π(ΩWit ∧ Π ∈ Scheme), is witnessed by that same Π\_P.  
*Proof.* Scheme\_R(L,i,ν) = {Π ∈ ExtJointReal^{ReplayCtx} \| ∀d: ρ\_d ∈ RoleReal^{ReplayCtx}(d)} (3.44), whose conditions lie entirely within ReplayCtx, with no cross-context requirement; and RoleReal^κ(d) = {ρ ∈ ExtReal^κ(d) \| RolePath^κ\_ρ(d) ≠ ∅} (3.33, with κ taken as ReplayCtx). That Π\_P passes the ΩSupport level is ΩWit(Π\_P); that Π\_P passes the Dyn level is RolePath^{ReplayCtx}\_{ρ\_P}(d) ≠ ∅ (Lemma 3.1 making RoleCtx = ReplayCtx, so that the RolePath test at the Dyn level is conducted in this very context); hence ρ\_P ∈ RoleReal^{ReplayCtx}(d) and Π\_P ∈ Scheme. The existential quantifier is satisfied by Π\_P. □

*Note.* Lemma 5.2 proves only the ⇐ direction (that the same witness suffices); the program's adjudication of Bind = 1 always passes through this witness, so there is no false positive. The completeness of an adjudication of 0 (that there exists no successful Π outside the canonical family) is given by Lemma 5.3.

\**Lemma 5.3 (complete reduction to canonical witnesses)*\*　Under freezing, single office D = {J}, weighted type and PR1, for any P ⊆ R: the totality of legitimate implementation witnesses is exactly {Π\_P = {(P, ResEval(κ\_P, ν))} \| Anchor ∈ P}; hence exhausting the P containing Anchor is exhausting the legitimate witnesses.  
*Proof.* (i) Reduction of components — under a single office Π = {ρ\_J} (3.25), ρ\_J = (P, W) (3.23), so the free components of Π are only P and W; Bear, Acc, Q, RolePath, ΩWit and Scheme membership are all derived properties of (P, W, ν) and not free components (3.23: "its Bear, Acc, ReqSeg and RolePath are all computed with its own W"). (ii) W is unique — under the weighted type W = ResEval(κ\_P, ν), no subfamily being taken (3.21), so given P the W is unique, ρ is unique, and Π is unique, = Π\_P. (iii) Anchor is necessary — when Anchor ∉ P the restricted evaluation has no Anchor state, BearNodes is empty from RelState(J) onward, Bear = ∅ and Realized = 0 (3.23 requiring Bear ≠ ∅); hence such P have no legitimate witness, and the program's skipping them does not impair completeness. □

Thus what "true is adjudicated true" requires — that if a successful Π exists in the formal system the program's exhaustion will meet it — is given by (i), (ii), (iii) together with the exhaustion.

Reference implementation ljudge.py (Appendix A): everything is exact computation — finite enumeration together with closed-form softmax, with no Monte Carlo and no step of approximation.

## Section VII　Bounds of Misadjudication

　\**7.1*\*　In the multi-free-variable case the granting of sufficiency depends upon PR2-valid, which this text has not proved to hold generally. If there exists a case with sup\_{AdmVar} TV \> ε\_role while the maximum over the endpoint grid is ≤ ε\_role, the program will wrongly grant μ-sufficiency. The five cases do not touch this bound (the set of free variables is empty at every point where sufficiency is granted). Breakdown K-J1.

　\**7.2*\*　The renormalization of the restricted kernel in PR1 is one reading of "outside P is unavailable," and its effect is that a weak trace is let through at CR (the unique path within P being forced). An alternative reading retains the original kernel and counts the competing mass as dead end; under that reading the restricted measure of the weak trace is 0.3745 \< π₀ (computed), CR reverses to 0, and the failure location moves earlier. The adjudication between the two readings belongs upstream. Breakdown K-J2.

　\**7.3*\*　π₀ and ε\_role are content of Rel(L); μ is an exact value with no measurement error, but when μ falls in a neighbourhood of π₀ the adjudication is sensitive to the declaration. In the present set of cases μ ∈ {0, 1}, and the bound is not touched.

　\**7.4*\*　The abandoned old Groove-Line fails at CR rather than as "Est = 1 with only Reach failing": the EstWitness of *The Mathematical Form of Law* contains CarrierRealizes(L;R,I), and if an edge of R itself is sealed the realization does not hold. The Est^inst of Law Group-Form V is Deposited plus connectivity, and is weaker than the formal EstWitness; the difference between the two tables is a difference of definitional level, not a contradiction. This item is the formal record of this text's revision to the diagnostic table of Law Group-Form V.

　\**7.5*\*　The adjudication of Bind holds by Lemma 5.2 upon a frozen snapshot; under non-frozen adjudication, or under existential-type or mixed-type offices, the collapse does not apply and an independent Bind criterion is required. The world of independent failure of Bind (4.17 of *The Mathematical Form of Law*) has not yet been constructed within the present F\_LB topology; this does not affect the same-witness sufficiency direction of Lemma 5.2, the completeness of the exhaustion over canonical witnesses being borne by Lemma 5.3. Breakdown K-J3.

　\**7.6*\*　The control of the do(Mask) intervention effect in the DynamicRole^inst of Law Group-Form V is not incorporated into this program; this program obtains ActArg^μ directly by the formal definition of 3.10′. The consistency of the two has not been verified. Breakdown K-J4.

　\**7.7*\*　Dynamic stability (the T\_obs, T\_rec and trajectory families of *The Stability Form of Law*) is not in this text; this text adjudicates only static qualification upon a frozen snapshot.

## Section VIII　The Adjudication Theorem

\**Theorem (two-way validity relative to the protocols and the antecedents of the lemmas)*\*　Let M1–M6 hold upon F\_LB, let PR1, PR3, PR4 be the protocols taken, and let PR2-valid hold at every point where the program grants sufficiency (which holds automatically when the set of free variables is empty or a singleton). Then for any frozen snapshot F satisfying the candidate specification of this text (with no pulses in transit) and any single-office, single-entry, weighted-type candidate (L, R, I) with I = {Ante(L)}:

　judge(F, L, R, I) outputs PASS ⇔ the CC, CR, CNI, Dyn, ΩSupport and Bind of *The Mathematical Form of Law* are all 1 under the instance-protocol reading, that is, R ∈ 𝒮;  
　judge outputs FAIL @ X ⇔ X is the failing criterion at the deepest level attained by the several implementations P.

*Proof.* \**True is adjudicated true**: the criteria of the several levels holding ⇒ a witness exists; by Lemma 5.3 the totality of legitimate witnesses is {Π\_P \| Anchor ∈ P ⊆ R}, which the program exhausts and must therefore meet; CNI follows by Lemma 5.1 and Bind by Lemma 5.2, along with CR, Dyn and ΩSupport. **False is adjudicated false*\*: the program's adjudication of PASS passes only through the level-by-level checking of some Π\_P, each level being an exact evaluation of the corresponding criterion upon finite objects — CC a finite conjunction, CR a restricted measure that is a finite sum over paths (PR1), the granting of sufficiency in Dyn equal under PR2-valid to the adjudication of the supremum over AdmVar with negation given unconditionally reliably by a grid counterexample, RolePath a finite-graph reachability (PR4, chain continuation), ΩSupport a finite check (PR3), and Bind an explicit witness by Lemma 5.2 — hence PASS ⇒ every criterion is 1. □

Strength: two-way validity relative to PR1, PR3, PR4, PR2-valid and the antecedents of Lemmas 3.1, 5.1, 5.2, 5.3 (freezing, no pulses in transit, weighted type, single office, single entry I = {Ante}). The legitimacy of the protocols and antecedents is borne by the bounds of misadjudication of Section VII; this is the execution of the Canon Chapter's Chapter Eleven requirement to "list explicitly where both cannot be had."

## Section IX　Breakdown

　\**K-J1*\*　There exists an instance kernel for which the sup of TV is not attained upon the PR2 endpoint grid, and the adjudication, going by the grid, gives an ActArg^μ contrary to the global sup.

　\**K-J2*\*　The upstream adjudicates that a restricted evaluation may not be renormalized, and under that adjudication some case reverses while this text has not forewarned it.

　\**K-J5*\*　An instance is constructed for which P holds while the program adjudicates otherwise, or for which P does not hold while the program adjudicates that it does, and which does not fall within the bounds declared at 7.1–7.6.

　\**K-J7*\*　A counterexample to Lemma 5.3: there exists a legitimate successful witness not belonging to the family {Π\_P} (for instance, the upstream permitting a subfamily W to be taken under the weighted type, or Π under multiple offices having free components not listed by this text).

            if z==0: out.append((tuple(route),prob,'dead')); return
            K={e:p/z for e,p in K.items()}   # 受限评价：P 外结构不可用，核在 P 内重归一
        if not K or hops>=maxhop: out.append((tuple(route),prob,'end')); return
        for e,p in K.items(): rec(e[1],route+[e[1]],prob*p,hops+1)
    rec(entry,[entry],1.0,0)
    return out

def mu_read(F,entry,target,edges_allowed=None):
    return sum(p for r,p,_ in eval_paths(F,entry,edges_allowed) if target in r)

# ---------- ActArg^μ at a choice node (weighted duty) ----------
def actargs_at(F,q,eps=0.01):
    """minimal μ-sufficient groups over per-edge state fixings at position q.
    AdmVar: each out-edge's g varies over its admissible domain. The implementation evaluates
    endpoint grids {0,g_max} (S recomputed): single-free-variable sufficiency is exact, while
    multi-variable positive sufficiency is conditional on PR2-valid (paper §4 PR2, §7.1);
    negative verdicts from grid counterexamples are unconditionally sound."""
    cand=[e for e in F.out[q] if F.Avail[e]]
    def dist(gvals):
        ks=[]
        for e in cand:
            g=gvals[e]; S=1.0 if g>=F.th[e] else 0.0
            k=F.k0+F.w_g*F.mask[e]*g*(1.0 if S else F.s_weak)+F.w_d*F.d[e[1]]
            ks.append(k/F.T)
        m=max(ks); ws=[math.exp(k-m) for k in ks]; z=sum(ws)
        return [w/z for w in ws]
    def tv(p,qq): return 0.5*sum(abs(a-b) for a,b in zip(p,qq))
    base={e:F.g[e] for e in cand}
    def sufficient(C):
        freevars=[e for e in cand if e not in C]
        if not freevars: return True
        grids=[[0.0,F.g_max] for _ in freevars]
        dists=[]
        for combo in itertools.product(*grids):
            gv=dict(base); gv.update({e:v for e,v in zip(freevars,combo)})
            for e in C: gv[e]=base[e]
            dists.append(dist(gv))
        return max(tv(a,b) for a in dists for b in dists)<=eps
    minimal=[]
    for r in range(len(cand)+1):
        for C in itertools.combinations(cand,r):
            if sufficient(set(C)) and not any(set(M)<=set(C) for M in minimal):
                minimal.append(C)
    return set(u for M in minimal for u in M), minimal

# ---------- duty / candidate law ----------
def duty(ante,anchor,read): return dict(ante=ante,anchor=anchor,read=read)

# ---------- the judging pipeline ----------
def _judge_P(F,L,P,R,pi0,eps):
    """evaluate CR/CNI/Dyn/ΩSupport on one implementation P ⊆ R (canonical witness Π_P: W=ResEval(κ_P), PR1).
    Returns (depth_reached, detail). Depth order: CR=1, CNI=2, Dyn=3, ΩSupport=4 (all-pass)."""
    ante,anchor,read=L['ante'],L['anchor'],L['read']; d={}
    m_res=mu_read(F,ante,read,edges_allowed=set(P))
    d['P']=[f"{e[0]}→{e[1]}" for e in sorted(P)]
    d['CR']=dict(anchor_in_P=anchor in P,mu_restricted=round(m_res,4),pi0=pi0)
    if not (anchor in P and m_res>=pi0): return 0,d
    # CNI by reduction lemma: frozen ⇒ Estab={g>0}; witnesses under PR1 have Q(Π)⊆P⊆R⊆Estab (CC);
    # Bear∩Inst=∅ automatic since P has no g=0 edge. Assert the lemma's premises:
    assert all(F.g[e]>0 for e in P), "CNI reduction premise violated"
    d['CNI']=dict(by_reduction_lemma=True,Q_subset_Estab=True)
    # Dyn on Π_P
    aa,minimal=actargs_at(F,anchor[0],eps)
    adj=defaultdict(list)
    for (i,j) in P:
        if F.g[(i,j)]>0 and F.mask[(i,j)]: adj[i].append(j)
    seen={anchor[1]}; st=[anchor[1]]
    while st:
        x=st.pop()
        for y2 in adj[x]:
            if y2 not in seen: seen.add(y2); st.append(y2)
    rolepath=(anchor in aa) and (read in seen or read==anchor[1])
    d['Dyn']=dict(ActArg_at_anchor_tail=[f"{e[0]}→{e[1]}" for e in sorted(aa)],minimal_groups=[[f"{e[0]}→{e[1]}" for e in M] for M in minimal],anchor_in_ActArg=anchor in aa,rolepath=rolepath)
    if not rolepath: return 2,d
    # ΩSupport on Π_P
    seenA=F._closure(ante,[e for e in F.E if F.Avail[e]])
    reach=any(F.Avail[e] and e[0] in seenA for e in P)
    bear=[e for e in P if e==anchor or (anchor[1]==e[0] or e[0] in seen)]
    omega_ok=all(F.Om[e]>0 for e in bear)
    d['ΩSupport']=dict(Reach=reach,Bear=[f"{e[0]}→{e[1]}" for e in bear],Omega={f"{e[0]}→{e[1]}":round(F.Om[e],2) for e in bear})
    if not reach: return 3,dict(d,fail='Reach')
    if not omega_ok: return 3,dict(d,fail='ΩOK')
    return 4,d

def judge(F,L,R,I,pi0=0.9,eps=0.01,verbose=True):
    """Decision domain: single-duty, single-entry I={Ante}, measure-type candidate;
    the input F_LB state is converted to a Freeze^inst snapshot before judging.
    Existential over implementations: R passes iff ∃P ⊆ R (anchor ∈ P) whose canonical witness Π_P
    passes CR∧CNI∧Dyn∧ΩSupport; Bind then follows by the collapse lemma (frozen: RoleCtx=ReplayCtx,
    same Π_P bears ΩWit and ρ_P ∈ RoleReal via its Dyn check). fail_at = deepest level reached over all P."""
    T={}
    # interface domain: duty structure admits single-duty measure-type candidates only;
    # runtime guard below checks single entry I={Ante}; F is frozen into a Freeze^inst snapshot before judging
    if list(I)!=[L['ante']]:
        T['fail_at']='⊥(判定域外: 本篇只判单职责、单入口 I={Ante} 之权重型候选)'
        if verbose: print(T['fail_at'])
        return None,T
    F=copy.deepcopy(F); F.Freeze()
    T['Y']={e:(round(F.g[e],2),int(F.S(e)),round(F.Om[e],2),F.Avail[e],F.mask[e]) for e in F.E if F.g[e]>0 or e in R}
    cc = len(R)>0 and all(e in F.E for e in R) and all(F.g[e]>0 for e in R)
    T['CC']=cc
    if not cc: return _out('CC',T,verbose)
    anchor=L['anchor']
    best=(-1,None)
    for r in range(1,len(R)+1):
        for Pt in itertools.combinations(R,r):
            if anchor not in Pt: continue
            depth,detail=_judge_P(F,L,set(Pt),R,pi0,eps)
            if depth>best[0]: best=(depth,detail)
            if depth==4: break
        if best[0]==4: break
    depth,detail=best
    for k in ('P','CR','CNI','Dyn','ΩSupport'):
        if detail and k in detail: T[k]=detail[k]
    if depth==4:
        T['Bind']=dict(by_collapse_lemma=True,value=True)
        T['verdict']='R ∈ 𝒮'
        return _out(None,T,verbose)
    fail=['CR','CNI','Dyn',('ΩSupport(Reach)' if detail and detail.get('fail')=='Reach' else 'ΩSupport(ΩOK)')][depth if depth<3 else 3]
    return _out(fail,T,verbose)

def _out(fail,T,verbose):
    T['fail_at']=fail
    if verbose:
        print(("PASS  R∈𝒮" if fail is None else f"FAIL @ {fail}"))
        for k,v in T.items():
            if k!='fail_at': print(f"  {k} = {v}")
    return (fail is None),T
