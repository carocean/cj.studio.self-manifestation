# The Necessity Form of Law

## Section I　Purpose

*The General Form of Law* proves, in the necessary direction:

　　Law\_F,t(L,I) ⇒ ∃R \[ Established\_F,t(R) ∧ Govern\_F,t(L;R,I) \]

and hands the sufficient direction explicitly to this text. This text proves the sufficient direction only:

　　∃R \[ Established\_F,t(R) ∧ Govern\_F,t(L;R,I) \] ⇒ Law\_F,t(L,I)

where Govern\_F,t(L;R,I) ⇔ DynamicRole\_F,t(L;R,I) ∧ ΩSupport\_F,t(L;R,I).

This text does not reprove the necessary direction, does not redefine Established, DynamicRole, ΩSupport or Govern, adds no field quantity, adds no ontological predicate, and adds no physical bridge awaiting verification; the only condition awaiting proof that it adds is the semantic carry-over condition SC\_Hold (Section VI; Section XII, gap six).

The proof of this text does not proceed by "the necessary items being exhausted, the converse therefore holds." Exhaustion of the necessary items (the CurrentMinimal of *The General Form of Law*) does not entail sufficiency; that conclusion serves only as a consistency retest and is not an antecedent of this text. The route of proof here is: fix the source-text corpus → atomize the source-text propositions → sort them by office → exhaust the present qualification for law-standing → show that the formal structure realizes that qualification (the second layer carrying the carry-over condition SC\_Hold) → Law.

## Section IV　Atomization and the Rules of Sorting

Source-text sentences are not sorted whole. Each is first divided into minimal propositional components, and each component is assigned, by its \**dominant office*\*, to one of the following ten classes:

　\**Formation*\*　　　　how a law is generated from nothing.  
　\**CurrentQualification*\*　by what a candidate relation already present in the field state now acquires the qualification of Law (the acquiring side).  
　\**Disqualification*\*　a component able, when all the acquiring conditions required by CurrentQualification hold, independently to void the present qualification of Law (the voiding side).  
　\**Degree*\*　　　　　how deep a law is, how well-worn, how readily first to emerge.  
　\**Consequence*\*　　what is generated after a law stands.  
　\**Relation*\*　　　　the relation of law to Principle and to Axiom.  
　\**CategoryOrigin*\*　by what mechanism law, Principle and Axiom arise (where they arise).  
　\**Recognition*\*　　whether a subject discerns, knows or names it.  
　\**Location*\*　　　　in what class of field structure law bears what position.  
　\**Application*\*　　illustration by instances in concrete systems.

Criteria of sorting (used for item-by-item adjudication, so as to avoid arbitrary assignment):

　(a) \**Temporal-phase criterion*\* — whether the component states "how it comes to be" (Formation), "whether it counts now" (CurrentQualification), "how it goes after coming to be" (Consequence / Degree), or "whether it is preserved in future" (Persist, already excluded by *The General Form of Law* as not a top-level necessary item).  
　(b) \**Removability criterion*\* — suppose the component negated, all others unchanged: if the candidate relation is still an organization already formed in the field, really and stably bearing Rel(L), then that component is not part of the present qualification.  
　(c) \**Source-text self-attestation criterion*\* — whether the source text elsewhere has a sentence explicitly permitting that component to be absent while a law still stands.  
　(d) \**Two-sided criterion*\* — if a component resembles a veto ("should such-and-such occur, it is not a law"), it must be specially examined as to whether it is an independent voiding condition: supposing all the acquiring conditions of CurrentQualification to hold, can that component by itself make Law = 0? If its occurrence necessarily makes some acquiring condition fail, then it is not independent, and it belongs to its own class rather than to Disqualification.

A component may belong by dominant office to one class and still be subject to the special examination of (d); the conclusions of that examination are recorded together in Section V.

This text bears the two classes CurrentQualification and Disqualification only — the former giving the acquiring conditions, the latter the voiding conditions; the components of the other eight classes do not enter the antecedent of the sufficient direction.

## Section V　Item-by-Item Sorting of the Source-Text Semantics

\**M1*\*　「一而遇，再而遇，继尔遇，久而意之，则调之成习，经验因是以沉积，若道成其多走，自而沉积为律。」

　M1.a　"Encounter once, encounter again, encounter again and again. Over time Intent takes it up. Tuning becomes habit" — repeated encounter issuing in habit: \**Formation*\* (temporal-phase criterion: it states how it comes to be).  
　M1.b　"Experience sediments" — what has become habit enters sedimentation: \**Formation*\*; its resultant state (already entered sedimentation) is used by M1.d.  
　M1.c　"Like a road made by many feet" — this clause contains two components: the process component "made by many feet" (the path being traversed repeatedly) is \**Formation**; the resultant component "the road is made" (a road has been worn, that is, the road made by many feet is there) is the present state thereby attained, co-referential with the "repetition" of M3.a and the "tendency" of M4.a-ii, and belongs to **CurrentQualification**. What the present qualification inherits is the formative result (the road already made), not the count of its formative history within this field. This division accords with *The General Form of Law\*'s "the actual number of repetitions is not an ontological antecedent"; the recurrence record within the window checked by Ω\_W at the formal layer is historical evidence that "the road is made" (its carry-over to the source text being given at SC\_Hold in Section VI), not formative history (Section VII (iii)).  
　M1.d　"it settles as Law" (「自而沉积为律」) — the result of the road's being made thereby settles of itself into law: \**CurrentQualification*\*. 「自而」, "of itself," marks that this "being law" comes from the sedimentation itself and awaits no conferral or stipulation added from outside; it gathers up the result of M1.b (already entered sedimentation) and the resultant component of M1.c (the road is made), and gives the point at which "is Law" comes to rest. 「自而」 says only that law-standing awaits nothing added from outside; it does not say what sort of sedimentation suffices for law-standing (see the note below).

　*Note.* M1.d gives the \**point of settlement*\* of law-standing; it is not by itself raised into a sufficient criterion. The full present content of the qualification of its "law" must be read together with the summary definitions of M3 and M4 — the "holding" of M4.a (ActualHold) does not appear in M1, so M1.d alone is insufficient. M1, M3 and M4 each contribute a part; together they make two layers, and none usurps the sufficiency of the others.

　*Note.* The processes of M1.a and M1.b, and the process component of M1.c (made by many feet), are the historical antecedents of M1.d, not the qualification itself. By the removability criterion: an R acquired by inheritance, transplantation or external writing-in, if it is already in fact in the sedimentary bearing state of the present field with the road already made (its formal conditions being given at the transplanted-structure case of Section XI), satisfies M1.d while the history of M1.a–c has not occurred in this field; and the transplantation clause of *The General Form of Law* expressly permits such an R to retain the qualification. Hence formative history belongs to Formation and not to CurrentQualification.

\**M2*\*　「流之于沉积，其好走常熟，渐而其深也，律由是逐澄其下。」

　M2.a　"Flow sinks into Sedimentation; what is often passed becomes well-worn" (「其好走常熟」) — where flow passes through sedimentation, its route is easy to travel and constantly well-worn, that is, signal readily proceeds along an already deep-sedimented route (Outer Chapter 3, 「因深者先得其流，故信号好走常熟，此所谓常熟之径」; main book: "Because what is deep first receives the flow, signals favor the paths long familiar. These are the familiar paths, the familiar domains."): \**Degree*\*. 「好走」 means easy to travel. This is a tendency along the slope formed by sedimentation (Inner Chapter 15, 「传导者，就势之流也，其偏流为顺势自成也」; main book: "Conduction is flow that follows tendency. Its biased flow forms of itself by following tendency"), and it is a different matter from the choosing and bias of Principle (M3.c, "Principle—choosing and bias"): the former forms of itself from sedimentation, the latter belongs to the choosing of Principle; this text neither assigns every "bias" to Principle nor reads a tendency along the slope as a choice.  
　M2.b　"Gradually it deepens" — depth increasing by degrees: \**Degree*\*.  
　M2.c　"Thus Law clears beneath" — law clarifying beneath by degrees: \**Degree*\* (degree of clarification).

　*Note.* The order of the sentences is one piece of evidence: M1.d has already "settled as Law" first, and the "gradually it deepens" of M2 comes after. Deepening thus continues after a law has come to stand, and the degree of depth is not fixed at the moment of law-standing; hence the degree of depth is not a threshold of coming to stand. But the order of the sentences does not by itself exclude a minimal depth threshold; the ground of Deep ∉ CurrentQualification(Law) is that M1–M8 give no threshold d\* of law-standing independent of sedimentary formation (Section VI, step two). Nor does this text claim that a law has no depth: sedimentary formation already contains a Sedimentation-Degree δ \> 0, and the recurrence checked by ΩSupport also produces a deepening action (*The Mathematical Form of the Field* v2.7, Definition 12 and Equation 2: recurrence produces Dτ \> 0, net deepening further requiring Dτ \> Eλ). Hence Deep ∈ Degree(Law) means that the degree of depth belongs to Degree, not that law-standing has nothing to do with depth. M6 states the office borne by "depth" (see below).

　M3.c　"Principle—choosing and bias" — \**Relation*\*.

　*Note.* M3.e may not be read as "a law must arise from a certain place in order to be a law." It answers \**why the three differ from one another*\*, not what the threshold of law-standing is; and N1, "Transformer Sedimentation-Field—Law; within it, no Principle," expressly permits a law to exist within a Sedimentation-Field that is not a complete Experience-Field (source-text self-attestation criterion). \**M4*\*　「言以蔽之：律者，持之势也，理者，破持之势也……」

　M4.a　"Law holds" — \**CurrentQualification*\*, and the source text's summary characterization of law. It divides into two components:  
　　M4.a-i　"holding" — really holding fast, bearing later continuation (not bystanding, not recording, not concomitant);  
　　M4.a-ii　"tendency" — not a single accidental effect, but an inclination formed by repeated holding (whether it further contains a sense of present re-traversability is taken up in Section VI).  
　M4.b　"Principle breaks the hold … thus they pursue one another. Causal-Response transforms without cease" — dominant office \**Relation*\* (the relation of law to Principle). Since it resembles a veto, it is specially examined by criterion (d) as to whether it is also a Disqualification:

　　Suppose SedimentedFormation ∧ StableHolding both hold at t while a hold-breaking action is present. Two cases are distinguished according to whether the holding-tendency has been severed at t (the two cases are logically exhaustive and do not rest upon the quotation). First, at t the hold-breaking has already in fact severed the holding-tendency — then R's bearing of Rel(L) no longer enters generation at t (ActualHold fails), or its recurrence no longer satisfies ReplayPotential, so StableHolding is 0 at t, the antecedent does not hold, and this case is therefore not one of "the acquiring conditions holding while X voids them." Second, at t the hold-breaking is in progress while the holding-tendency is not severed — then the law is still holding at t. Here the source text states expressly 「彼破之，则又持，二者之相追，致因应相化不息」 ("broken, it holds again. Thus they pursue one another. Causal-Response transforms without cease"): after breaking it holds again, and breaking and holding pursue one another without cease. The source text writes hold-breaking as a back-and-forth process pursuing the holding, not as a clause whose mere occurrence makes a law fail; whether and when it holds again after being broken belongs to the dynamic process (exit and recovery in *The Stability Form of Law*) and does not enter the present qualification. In neither case can M4.b, with the acquiring conditions holding, independently make Law = 0. Hence M4.b ∉ Disqualification.

　　\**Principle is not the veto upon law, but the adversary of law's holding-tendency: broken, it holds again, and they pursue one another without cease; the outcome at any moment is recorded in the value taken by StableHolding at that moment.*\*

　*Note.* The force of the word "tendency" lies here: if a thing has influenced what follows once only and has no recurrence, the source text calls it an encounter, a trace, and does not call it a tendency. M4.a-ii and the "repetition" of M3.a refer to one and the same thing — the tendency formed by recurrence, not a single accident — and hence the two are combined into one item of qualification (ReplayPotential) and not counted twice.

## Section VI　The Closure Theorem for the Present Qualification of Law

The five components of the class CurrentQualification: the resultant component of M1.c ("the road is made"), M1.d (the point of settlement of law-standing), M3.a (law is repetition), M4.a (law is the tendency that holds), M7.c (where the law is, is in sedimentation). Merging what is co-referential:

　\**First layer, sedimentary formation (SedimentedFormation)*\*: the relational organization bearing Rel(L) has acquired a present, in-field, non-instantaneous sedimentary existence — it is not one signal, not shaped instantaneously by the present input, not a stipulation outside the field, not an observational description. Source: the "sedimentation" of M1.d, the "enters sediment" of M7.c.

　\**Second layer, stable holding (StableHolding)*\*: that organization holds, with tendency, the later continuation involved in Rel(L). It divides into two components:  
　　\**Actual holding (ActualHold)*\*: the structure really enters later generation — not bystanding, not recording, not concomitant. Source: the "holding" of M4.a-i.  
　　\**Tendency (ReplayPotential)*\*: the tendency of that bearing is already formed — an inclination of holding formed by recurrence, not a single accident. Source: the "tendency" of M4.a-ii, the "repetition" of M3.a, the "road made by many feet" of M1.c. The source text does not declare whether this "tendency" further contains some present re-traversability (not necessarily in the strong sense "every re-entry would recur"); this text does not prejudge it.
