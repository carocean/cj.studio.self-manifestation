# The General Form of Probability

What follows attempts one general proposition:

What is ordinarily called probability, once it holds in a Sedimentation-Field, cannot be merely a numerical value written on some outcome, cannot be merely an observer's measure over a number of possible outcomes, and cannot be merely a frequency read out after long repetition; it must already have obtained the necessary qualification of having relative continuation-generation borne by already formed structures within the field.

Probability is ordinarily defined as: the measure of the magnitude of the possibility that some random event occurs.

This definition sets three things in front in advance: a "who" that does the measuring; a space of "all possible outcomes" laid out in advance; and a numerical value hung on each outcome.

On Self-Manifestation does not start from here.

At the layer of physical execution, every actual continuation settles into one actual occurrence. The so-called "several possible outcomes" can be objects of recognition, description or formal expression; but this text does not take a possibility space laid out in advance as the ontological starting point of Probability.

The main book of On Self-Manifestation has already connected continuation, Trace-Response, sedimentation and Sedimentation-Degree with later continuation; the subsequent Law system has further closed, as Law, the relational organization that can be formed, can govern what comes later, and has legal replay support. Hence what this text really needs to ask is: by virtue of what do the formal continuation dimensions of a system-side continuation-rate organization acknowledged as Probability ultimately obtain the qualification of being borne within the field.

Therefore this text neither infers Probability backwards from "all possible outcomes + a numerical measure", nor prescribes in advance the complete history of the occurrence of Probability. Source, first formation and historical attribution are left to The Source Form of Probability. This text asks only for the necessary constitution of current qualification.

The research starting point of this group form set down one sentence:

"Probability is Law."

What this sentence gives is a direction: probability is not outside the Sedimentation-Field, but must fall onto the bearing of the already formed Law system within the field. This text does not formalize the sentence directly as Probability = Law, but tests it in continuous questioning: once a Probability holds, in what relation does it actually stand to Law, and what does it at least need besides Law?

So what this text asks is:

If probability is not a measure of possibility but a continuation-rate organization generated in the Sedimentation-Field, then what must a Probability at least have once it holds in the field? Lacking what can it not be a Probability that already holds?

In this text, under unceasing questioning, we are to find where the proof must go, and derive the General Form of Probability.

Our objective is:

to obtain the currently derived minimal necessary structure for Probability to hold in the Sedimentation-Field, and to give the proof of minimality and the paths of breach.

This text proves only the necessary direction:

Probability ⇒ N\_P

The content of N\_P is not presupposed here; it is forced out round by round by the questioning below. The upstream conditions carried by this necessary direction are given in the condition ledger below.

The converse:

N\_P ⇒ Probability

is not proven by this text, nor may it be obtained covertly by any definition, naming or deletion test of this text. The sufficient direction belongs to The Necessity Form of Probability.

The condition ledger of this text:

H\_inherit = { H2, H3^red, SC\_Hold }

This text sets up no new unproven formal antecedent. But the actual proof path of the necessary direction of this text passes over two bridges of the upstream The General Form of Law that are not yet closed: H2 (the Ω complete-expression bridge) bears load universally; H3 (the DynamicRole redundant-causal-duty existence bridge) bears load only in cases of redundant, cooperative, standby or multiple carrying, written H3^red. Further, the sufficient direction of The Necessity Form of Law invoked in Section Four is, in v1.4 of that text, Conditional on SC\_Hold (the carry-over condition for the tendency that holds, awaiting proof); it is inherited along with the step "every dimension obtains formal Law-bearing" and entered in H\_inherit; SC\_Hold is a semantic carry-over condition declared upstream, not an H newly set up by this text. to denote that the candidate Probability organization 𝒫 holds at moment t in the Sedimentation-Field F, relative to the Continuation-Position C and the rate domain 𝔯\_P.

may it be said that the dimensions truly belong together to the common Continuation-Position declared by this Probability.

how many elements V\_P has;

∂Pr(q,t)/∂t ∝ ∂Depth(q,t)/∂t

Fourth, the main book, in the general expression of Law, has already pointed out: Law is not probability; probability can describe the outward distribution after many runs, but cannot serve as the generating mechanism; one settling is given by the minimization of Θ.

"Probability" in this passage means the outward distribution read out after many runs, that is, the layer this text calls empirical frequency and recognized rate. ## Section Three　Is One Stable Continuation Already a Probability?

First take the strongest single-path case.

Let there already be in F a stable continuation:

L\_1 : C → v\_1

It may already be sedimented, already have become a Law, and be able to replay stably; at C the successor is always v\_1.

Mathematically one may even write:

p(v\_1) = 1

Now ask:

Is this a Probability?

If it is said to be one, then between what does this "rate" hold?

Mathematically a one-point distribution can perfectly well assign the value 1 to v\_1. This text does not deny that this way of writing can hold; what this text asks is whether this 1 brings an object independent of L\_1.

In the world above there is only v\_1 at C. Without a second distinguishable continuation direction there is no relative-generation relation among dimensions to speak of. Writing p(v\_1) = 1 says nothing more than "at C, continuation stably goes to v\_1"; what it says is already wholly borne by L\_1 as a Law.

So a single continuation can be assigned the rate 1, but this is not enough to form the independent Probability organization this text studies; in qualification it is still wholly borne by an ordinary Law.

If it were still called a Probability, that Probability would differ in no content from L\_1: it would only be an ordinary Law under another name. If the Probability this text asks about has an object independent of ordinary Law, that object cannot hold on a single continuation dimension.

So, if Probability\_F,t(𝒫; C, 𝔯\_P) = 1, then:

\|V\_P\| ≥ 2

and the dimensions in V\_P are mutually distinguishable:

∀v\_i, v\_j ∈ V\_P, i ≠ j:  
&nbsp;&nbsp;Dist(v\_i, v\_j)

The boundary obtained in this round must be noted here.

This round proves only "multiple dimensions". It has not proven by what these dimensions are borne, nor that "two distinguishable things" suffice to be two dimensions of a probability. What the second dimension may be is at this point still wholly open.

## Section Four　Is One More Distinguishable Outcome Already a Dimension of Probability?

Keep:

\|V\_P\| ≥ 2

Keep v\_1 borne by the already formed Law L\_1.

Now let another so-called dimension u be only one of the following:

First, a chance occurrence. Once, an actual continuation at C went to u. This case can be further seen in three layers: in the first, the occurrence left no bearing structure behind; in the second, the occurrence left a trace, the trace even being preserved for a long time, but this retention does not enter the generation of later continuation at C; in the third, the retention once really took part in a later continuation to u, but on a later legal arrival at C there is no longer stable support.

Second, a reachable branch. Starting from C, u is structurally reachable, but no relational organization bearing C → u has ever formed in the field.

Third, an outcome temporarily opened by an external switch. Some external condition temporarily connects C to u; once the switch is withdrawn, this continuation no longer exists.

Fourth, a mathematical candidate label. An analyst writes u down in a sample space and gives it a number.

All four cases can make "two distinguishable things" hold.

Now ask:

In these worlds, is u a dimension of the system's own continuation rate?

Take them one by one.

A chance occurrence:

Occurrence without retention. One occurrence only says that this one actual continuation agreed with u. It left no structure able to bear what comes later; the next time C is reached, nothing in the field bears any share for u.

Retention that does not govern what comes later. The trace is in the field, but it is only recorded; when C is later reached, whether u is generated has nothing to do with this retention. The retention is related to u but does not take part in generating u.

Influence once, without replay. The retention once really took part in one later continuation, but that was only bearing on one occasion; on a later legal arrival at C there is no already formed structure able to support C → u.

None of the three layers can make u a dimension of the system's own continuation rate:

Occurrence(u) = 1 ⇏ u is a continuation dimension

A reachable branch: reachability only says that u is not structurally excluded. No organization bearing C → u has formed in the field; u has never entered later continuation.

Reachable(u) ⇏ u is a continuation dimension

An external switch: the continuation C → u does occur, but what bears it is a temporary condition outside the field; the field has formed no bearing structure of its own, and once the switch is withdrawn u no longer exists.

A mathematical label: it exists in the analyst's representation, not in the continuation of F.

In these four worlds u is in every case only a bare candidate. To say "at C there are two dimensions v\_1 and u, each taking a certain rate" is to say something whose u-share is borne by nothing in the field; it is attached to the system by the analyst or by an external condition, and is not a dimension of the system's own continuation rate.

Then what at least is needed for u to become a dimension of the system's own continuation rate?

Against the failures above, the requirements can be read off item by item:

First, the whole organization bearing C → u must already have become a current sedimentary carrying organization of F, not a response state maintained instantaneously only by current input or external conditions — an occurrence without retention, a reachable point, a mathematical label have no such organization at all; an external switch maintains this continuation instantaneously only while the switch is open.

Second, this organization must really enter the generation of the later relation that continuation at C goes to u: it cannot merely be recorded, related to u without taking part in generating u — this is exposed by the layer "retention that does not govern what comes later".

Third, this later relation must be supported by already formed stable replay structures within the field that are reachable from the entry C in this organization and actually bear C → u: it cannot hold only on one occasion, nor be maintained by the repeated appearance of conditions outside the field — the former is exposed by the layer "influence once, without replay", the latter by the external-switch world: even if the external switch happens to be open every time, the repeated appearance of C → u is not support by already formed structures within the field.

These three requirements correspond, in the sealed Law qualification system, to the three structural predicates

Established  
DynamicRole  
ΩSupport

## Section Five　If Every Dimension Is Borne by a Law, Is It Already a Probability?

Keep:

MultiLawDimension\_F,t(𝒫) = 1

Construct the following world:

L\_1 : C\_a → v\_1  
L\_2 : C\_b → v\_2

where:

C\_a ≠ C\_b

C\_a is one continuation question, for example approach or avoidance after some stimulus arrives; C\_b is another continuation question, for example the migration of sediment in another structure. The two dimensions each obtain formal Law-bearing, each replays stably, and they are mutually distinguishable.

The results of the previous section are fully kept here: \|V\_P\| ≥ 2, Dist(v\_1, v\_2), and each dimension has its own C\_i, L\_i, I\_i.

An analyst sets v\_1 and v\_2 side by side, counts how many times each is realized over some period, and writes down a ratio.

Now ask:

By virtue of what do these two Law-bearing dimensions belong to one and the same probability, rather than merely existing at the same time in F?

They do not answer the same continuation question at all. L\_1 answers "how continuation goes after C\_a", L\_2 answers "how continuation goes after C\_b". Between the two there is no relation of "how the successor of one and the same continuation is generated between the two dimensions". The ratio written down is only a ratio of the counts of two unrelated events, not a rate of any continuation between v\_1 and v\_2.

The "relative" that a rate requires can hold only between different successors of one and the same continuation question.

## Section Six　Being at One Continuation-Position, Do They Already Belong to One Rate Relation?

Keep:

MultiLawDimension\_F,t(𝒫) = 1  
SameContinuationPosition\_F,t(𝒫; C) = 1

Construct the following world.

At one and the same Continuation-Position C:

L\_1 bears C → v\_1 only under condition a  
L\_2 bears C → v\_2 only under condition b

Under condition a, C is always followed by v\_1; under condition b, C is always followed by v\_2.

Historically, F reached C 70 times under condition a and 30 times under condition b. An observer afterwards mixes the histories of the two conditions together and obtains:

v\_1 : v\_2 = 70 : 30

In this world:

every dimension obtains formal Law-bearing;

the Law-bearings of both dimensions fall on one and the same C.

Now ask:

By virtue of what do these two Law-bearing dimensions belong to one and the same legal rate relation, rather than the outcomes under two conditions being stitched together?

In the generation situation of condition a, C is followed only by v\_1; in the generation situation of condition b, C is followed only by v\_2. Inside each generation situation there is only a single continuation, back to the single-path case of Section Three. The number 70 : 30 is not the relative-generation relation between v\_1 and v\_2 in any one generation situation, but the ratio of how often two different conditions appeared, read afterwards by the observer as "the rate at C".

This is cross-condition stitching of evidence: taking the bearing of v\_1 from condition a and the bearing of v\_2 from condition b, and stitching them into a "rate" that exists in no single generation situation.

So it must be required that the bearings of the dimensions of V\_P lie in one and the same generation domain.

SameGenerationDomain\_F,t(𝒫; C, 𝔯\_P)  
:⇔  
&nbsp;&nbsp;∀(L\_i, v\_i, C\_i) ∈ 𝓛\_P:  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;the bearing of v\_i by L\_i holds in one and the same 𝔯\_P  
&nbsp;&nbsp;∧ NoCrossConditionStitch\_F,t(𝒫; 𝔯\_P)

where:

NoCrossConditionStitch\_F,t(𝒫; 𝔯\_P)  
:⇔  
&nbsp;&nbsp;𝔯\_P is not stitched together from different conditions  
&nbsp;&nbsp;to which the dimensions separately belong and which,  
&nbsp;&nbsp;as declared, should have been frozen apart

What is quantified here is a general element (L\_i, v\_i, C\_i) of 𝓛\_P; C\_i = C is not presupposed inside the definition. In the derivation of this section SameContinuationPosition = 1 is already kept from Section Five, so at this point all C\_i = C; but SameGenerationDomain itself bears only the duty "same domain, and no cross-condition stitching of evidence", and does not repeat the duty of a common Continuation-Position. Same position, same domain and same generation thus each bear a different task of exclusion.

A domain discipline is also forced out here.

If 𝔯\_P could be chosen after seeing the results, the same stretch of history could be cut as "conditions a and b merged into one domain", or as "only condition a", or the domain could be arbitrarily narrowed, changed or the sample re-chosen until the desired rate is obtained. Then "one and the same generation domain" would lose its binding force: any stitching could be renamed afterwards as "one and the same domain".

So 𝔯\_P must be declared in advance: before the bearings of the dimensions of V\_P are tested, 𝔯\_P is already fixed; one may not, after seeing the results, narrow the domain, change the domain or re-choose the sample.

Frozen(𝔯\_P) ≺ testing 𝒫

At the same time "freezing the rate domain" must be separated from "freezing everything".

What is frozen in advance are the qualification conditions: the Continuation-Position C, the declared generation/re-entry regime, and what disturbances and legal variations the regime permits. It is not pinning down every legal variable inside the regime one by one.

If the complete microstate of a deterministic system is wholly fixed, its successor is of course only one; but that is another, finer generation situation, not the originally declared 𝔯\_P. Within the declared 𝔯\_P, environmental disturbance, legal input and in-field states may vary according to the declared regime.

Therefore the difference between "legal variation permitted within the domain" and "cross-condition stitching" lies not in whether variation exists, but in whether these variations belong to one and the same generation regime declared in advance. If conditions a and b by their own prescription should be frozen apart, mixing them together is cross-condition stitching of evidence; if some variation already belongs to the legal variation within the declared regime, it does not constitute stitching.

If conditions a and b are re-declared in advance as legal variation within one and the same generation regime, and this regime truly has system-side actual legal generation/re-entry grounding, then the instance is no longer the counterexample of this section. In that case 70 : 30 is given by the appearance of a and b in that regime; whether it is borne by the declared organization is no longer a question of the same domain, but the question pursued later — "by whom relative generation is borne". This section excludes only: a and b, belonging as declared to different generation regimes, being stitched into one domain.

Thus, if Probability\_F,t(𝒫; C, 𝔯\_P) = 1, then:

SameGenerationDomain\_F,t(𝒫; C, 𝔯\_P) = 1

What remains unclosed:

Do the dimensions at the same position and in the same domain, with their Law-bearings, truly belong to the generation of one and the same continuation?

Section Seven　At the Same Position and in the Same Domain, Do They Already Belong to One Continuation Generation?

Keep:

MultiLawDimension\_F,t(𝒫) = 1  
SameContinuationPosition\_F,t(𝒫; C) = 1  
SameGenerationDomain\_F,t(𝒫; C, 𝔯\_P) = 1

Construct the following world.

At one and the same Continuation-Position C, in one and the same 𝔯\_P declared in advance, F has two mutually independent parallel generation processes:

Process one: borne by L\_1, C is followed by the generation of v\_1  
Process two: borne by L\_2, C is followed by the generation of v\_2

On every legal re-entry of C, the two processes each run at the same time: both v\_1 and v\_2 occur. They appear at the same position and in the same context, but they are not mutually exclusive successors of one and the same continuation event: the occurrence of process one does not exclude the occurrence of process two, nor the reverse.

In this world:

the two dimensions are distinguishable and each obtains formal Law-bearing;

they belong together to C;

they are together in 𝔯\_P.

If an analyst counts the two separately, some ratio can also be written down.

Now ask:

Do these two Law-bearing dimensions truly belong to one and the same continuation-generation relation?

No.

The "relative" that a rate requires can exist only among the successor directions of one and the same continuation-generation event. In the world above, v\_1 and v\_2 are not two different destinations of one continuation event, but the respective destinations of two continuations. They coexist, but are not mutually exclusive. The ratio written down is only the ratio of the respective outputs of two parallel processes, not the relative generation of any one continuation event between the two dimensions.

At the layer of physical execution, every continuation returns to one. The "multiple dimensions" of a probability can only be the several successor directions faced by one and the same continuation-generation relation; an actual continuation, if it falls into these dimensions, realizes only one of them. Two parallel processes each returning to one do not constitute multiple dimensions of one and the same continuation.

So it must be required that the dimensions of V\_P are the distinguishable successor directions corresponding to one and the same continuation-generation event.

SameGenerationRelation\_F,t(𝒫; C, 𝔯\_P)  
:⇔  
&nbsp;&nbsp;the dimensions of V\_P are the distinguishable successor directions  
&nbsp;&nbsp;corresponding to one and the same continuation-generation event  
&nbsp;&nbsp;at C in 𝔯\_P;  
&nbsp;&nbsp;an actual continuation, if it realizes a dimension in V\_P,  
&nbsp;&nbsp;realizes at most one of them

This prescribes only the mutually exclusive realization of the dimensions within V\_P as successors of one and the same continuation; it does not require every actual continuation to fall into V\_P, nor require V\_P to exhaust all actual successors at C, and therefore does not imply FullCoverage.

Nor does this predicate prescribe by whom the relative-generation relation among the dimensions is formed or borne. The latter is the question of Section Eight, which this section does not answer in advance.

"One and the same continuation-generation event" here means the continuation generation caused by one legal re-entry of C in 𝔯\_P; it is not a sub-situation subdivided by condition within 𝔯\_P. So in the world of Section Six, every legal re-entry of C (whether under condition a or b) realizes only one of v\_1, v\_2, and SameGenerationRelation can hold there; what fails in that world is the same domain, not the same generation.

The parallel-process world above violates exactly this: on every legal re-entry of C, v\_1 and v\_2 are realized simultaneously in one and the same continuation, violating "at most one dimension".

Thus, if Probability\_F,t(𝒫; C, 𝔯\_P) = 1, then:

SameGenerationRelation\_F,t(𝒫; C, 𝔯\_P) = 1

Only at this point may the binding conditions obtained in three consecutive rounds be named together:

CommonContinuationBind\_F,t(𝒫; C, 𝔯\_P)  
:⇔  
&nbsp;&nbsp;SameContinuationPosition\_F,t(𝒫; C)  
&nbsp;&nbsp;∧ SameGenerationDomain\_F,t(𝒫; C, 𝔯\_P)  
&nbsp;&nbsp;∧ SameGenerationRelation\_F,t(𝒫; C, 𝔯\_P)

Four declarations must be made about this naming.

First, CommonContinuationBind is not a new physical entity, not a "binding thing" existing besides the dimensions and their Law-bearings. It is a discipline of organizational binding: the dimensions and their bearings must come from one and the same Continuation-Position, one and the same generation domain declared in advance, and one and the same continuation-generation relation; evidence may not be stitched across tasks, domains or generation relations. This belongs to the same class as the discipline of "one and the same R" in the sealed Law qualification system: same witness, no stitching of evidence.

Second, "different dimensions" is already borne by \|V\_P\| ≥ 2 and Dist in MultiLawDimension, and is not put into the Bind again.

Third, CommonContinuationBind constrains the common belonging of dimension roles and generation relations; it does not bear Law identity. It requires SameContinuationPosition, SameGenerationDomain and SameGenerationRelation, but neither requires nor implies:

L\_i ≠ L\_j

or:

SameLaw(L\_i, L\_j) = 0

Whether different dimensions may be borne by different role occurrences of one and the same Law identity is a question of Law identity; this text does not cross that boundary.

Fourth, the three bindings each have their own failure world: in the world of Section Five MultiLawDimension is satisfied and SameContinuationPosition fails; in the world of Section Six same position and same generation are satisfied and SameGenerationDomain fails; in the world of Section Seven same position and same domain are satisfied and SameGenerationRelation fails. Hence same domain is not absorbed by same position and same generation, and same generation is not absorbed by same position and same domain.

The relation between same position and same generation must be stated truthfully: SameGenerationRelation takes "at C" as its position of evaluation; if the dimensions are successor directions of one and the same continuation-generation event at C, the Continuation-Position where they lie is already C, so it may in content imply part of the same-position requirement. This text still lists SameContinuationPosition as an independent component, because it is the responsible item that first gathers the C\_i into C in the recursion, and it directly constrains the Continuation-Position of every bearing registration in 𝓛\_P, whereas SameGenerationRelation constrains the belonging of the dimensions as successor directions. Whether there is a mergeable overlap of content between the two is a question of arranging components in the formalization of The Mathematical Form of Probability; it concerns only how the components inside CommonContinuationBind are written, and does not affect the necessity and current derived minimality of the three top-level items.

Thus, if Probability\_F,t(𝒫; C, 𝔯\_P) = 1, then:

CommonContinuationBind\_F,t(𝒫; C, 𝔯\_P) = 1

What remains unclosed:

The dimensions each have Law-bearing and are bound together in one and the same continuation generation. But how is the successor of this continuation generated among the dimensions, and who bears it?

## Section Eight　With the Dimensions Bound Together, Is the Rate Already Borne?

Keep:

MultiLawDimension\_F,t(𝒫) = 1  
CommonContinuationBind\_F,t(𝒫; C, 𝔯\_P) = 1

Construct the external-selector world.

Let the Law-bearing role family 𝓛\_P declared by the candidate Probability 𝒫 already bear the outcome execution of v\_1 and v\_2: if this continuation takes v\_1, L\_1 executes C → v\_1; if it takes v\_2, L\_2 executes C → v\_2.

There is in addition a mechanism M, lying outside this declared organization, which alone decides the relative-generation relation between the two dimensions:

M: takes v\_1 with 0.7, takes v\_2 with 0.3

On every legal re-entry of C: M first decides whether this time v\_1 or v\_2 is taken; then the corresponding Law-bearing executes.

The Law-bearings of v\_1 and v\_2 each truly hold: they have already formed by sedimentation, really enter later continuation, and replay stably. The two dimensions belong together to C, are together in 𝔯\_P, and are two distinguishable successor directions corresponding to one and the same continuation-generation event; in this counterexample, each actual continuation declared by this candidate organization realizes one of v\_1, v\_2.

By the definition of Section Seven, SameGenerationRelation holds here: that predicate prescribes only that the dimensions belong to one generation event, not by whom the relative generation among the dimensions is formed. M deciding first which dimension to take does not make v\_1, v\_2 leave the same generation event; what it changes is only "who forms the relative generation between them".

The long-run frequency can stabilize at:

v\_1 : v\_2 ≈ 70 : 30

In this world both MultiLawDimension and CommonContinuationBind hold.

Now ask:

Who bears the relative-generation relation 0.7 : 0.3 between v\_1 and v\_2?

Not the declared organization 𝓛\_P.

This relative-generation relation has, in every continuation, already been decided beforehand by M. By the construction of this counterexample, 0.7 : 0.3 is wholly given by M's selection rule; the declared duty of L\_1, L\_2 is only to bear the outcome execution of the corresponding dimension after it has been taken; it does not bear the formation of the relative-generation relation between the two dimensions.

Two kinds of bearing must be separated here:

Outcome bearing (OutcomeBearing): once a dimension is taken, the corresponding Law-bearing executes it.

Rate bearing (RateBearing): the formation of the relative-generation relation among the dimensions is borne by the organization.

In the external-selector world the declared organization has outcome bearing and no rate bearing:

OutcomeBearing ⇏ RateBearing

Its members are branch executors, not the Laws of a probability.

What this section excludes is: the declared organization bears only outcomes, while the relative-generation rate is wholly borne elsewhere.

If M itself were later proven to belong to the formal system-side rate-bearing structure of this Probability organization, the instance would no longer be the counterexample of this section; the candidate organization must re-declare M together with itself and be re-evaluated as a complete organization. This section excludes no organization merely because M exists; it excludes only the case "the declared organization is separated from the rate-bearer".

A point easily confused must also be noted.

In this world, if the only executor of v\_1 is removed, v\_1 can no longer be executed, and the observed frequency changes accordingly. But this change comes from a dimension losing its executor, not from that executor bearing the relative generation between v\_1 and v\_2. A change of outcome caused by removing the only executor can show only outcome bearing, not rate bearing.

If this world were adjudged a Probability, then "the dimensions of probability are borne by Laws" would be left with only "every outcome has a Law to execute it"; and the rate among the dimensions would fall wholly on M, outside the declared organization. The direction "probability is constituted by a Law organization" would be wholly hollowed out by the external selector.

So it must be required that the relative-generation relation among the dimensions be truly borne by this declared organization itself.

DomainRelativeRateBearing\_F,t(𝒫; C, 𝔯\_P)  
:⇔  
&nbsp;&nbsp;in a 𝔯\_P frozen in advance, non-empty, non-vacuous,  
&nbsp;&nbsp;having system-side actual legal generation/re-entry grounding,  
&nbsp;&nbsp;the legal load-bearing state of 𝓛\_P as an organization  
&nbsp;&nbsp;really enters the formation of the relative-generation relation  
&nbsp;&nbsp;Rel\_ρ(𝒫; C, 𝔯\_P) among the dimensions of V\_P;  
&nbsp;&nbsp;it cannot have only outcome execution  
&nbsp;&nbsp;while handing the relative-generation rate wholly to a mechanism outside the declared organization

It cannot be written as any of the following:

RateExists            — "a rate exists" says only that a numerical relation exists, not who bears it;  
StableFrequency       — stable frequency equally holds in the external-selector world;  
NumericRateRead       — that a value can be read out does not say who generates it;  
OutcomeBearing        — every dimension having a Law to execute it has been proven insufficient by the world of this section.

Thus, if Probability\_F,t(𝒫; C, 𝔯\_P) = 1, then:

DomainRelativeRateBearing\_F,t(𝒫; C, 𝔯\_P) = 1

What remains unclosed:

"In 𝔯\_P" and "borne by 𝓛\_P" are both still too wide. What kind of domain may the rate domain be? Must the bearers bear role by role, each on its own?

## Section Nine　In What Kind of Domain, and by Whom, Must the Rate Be Borne?

9.1 The rate domain must be frozen in advance

Section Six has already forced out, from cross-condition stitching, the advance declaration of 𝔯\_P. Rate bearing uses this discipline once more: if 𝔯\_P could be re-cut after the rate is seen, then for any externally chance frequency a domain could be "found", by choosing the domain afterwards, in which it seems to be borne by the declared organization.

So rate bearing is evaluated only in a 𝔯\_P frozen in advance, and the sample may not be re-cut after the results are seen:

Frozen(𝔯\_P)

9.2 The rate domain must be a system-side non-vacuous generation domain

Freezing a set of symbols in advance is not enough to make it a legal generation domain of Probability.

Construct the analyst-domain world: an analyst writes down a sample space at C and assigns a number each to v\_1, v\_2; but F itself has never actually legally re-entered C in the situations this "domain" describes; this "domain" exists only in the analyst's representation.

To say "the organization bears the relative-generation rate" on such a "domain" is only to write the analyst's sample space back into the system itself. Likewise, to call a set made up of hidden variables unknown to the observer a rate domain is to take ignorance on the observer's side for generation on the system's side.

So 𝔯\_P must at least be:

non-empty;

non-vacuous;

grounded in system-side actual legal generation/re-entry;

not made up solely of observer ignorance, after-the-fact sample division or purely counterfactual sets.

These requirements are the internal discipline of "in what domain rate bearing is evaluated"; they belong to the meaning of DomainRelativeRateBearing itself and are not set up as a separate top-level necessary item.

This text establishes only this structural requirement. What non-zero generation mass is, what generation measure can represent the domain, and when these requirements are instantiated by a generation kernel or other formal objects belong to The Mathematical Form of Probability; this text establishes no predicate of measure, mass or generation kernel.

9.3 The bearer is the organization, not role by role

Construct the redundant-bearing world.

At C, v\_1 has two registrations in 𝓛\_P: (L\_a, v\_1, C) and (L\_b, v\_1, C). The load-bearing states of the two take part redundantly in the formation of the relative-generation relation between v\_1 and v\_2: either one in place suffices to maintain the same rate relation.

Delete L\_a alone: L\_b compensates completely; the rate relation is unchanged.

Delete L\_b alone: L\_a compensates completely; the rate relation is unchanged.

Delete L\_a and L\_b together: the rate relation changes.

If it were required that:

∀(L\_i, v\_i, C\_i) ∈ 𝓛\_P: do(L\_i off) ⇒ Rel\_ρ changes

then in this world both L\_a and L\_b would be adjudged not to bear the rate, and the rate bearing of the declared organization would be adjudged 0; whereas in fact the rate relation is maintained precisely by L\_a and L\_b jointly, as joint deletion exposes.

So it may not be required that every member satisfy the but-for condition item by item. The bearer of the rate is the Probability organization:

it may be joint bearing: the roles together form the rate relation, no single one deciding it alone;

it may be distributed bearing: the rate relation is distributed in the carrying organizations of the roles;

it may be redundant bearing: several roles each suffice to maintain the same rate relation.

This agrees with the discipline in the sealed Law qualification system that DynamicRole takes existential polarity and allows redundant and cooperative bearing.

### 9.4 What this section does not do

This section fixes only the discipline of the domain of evaluation of rate bearing and the bearer. The operational criterion of rate bearing — what legal intervention, what comparison, what read-out can determine whether Rel\_ρ is borne by 𝓛\_P — belongs to The Mathematical Form of Probability and The Determination Form of Probability, and is not unfolded here.

### 10.2 Discipline of counter-review

The task of this section is not to prove:

N\_P ⇒ Probability

nor is it responsible for proving that Probability still holds when some candidate property X is missing or changed.

The latter kind of claim, whether about another world at the same moment or about the state of the same instance after legal evolution, needs "if the three items hold, Probability holds" or the cross-time persistence of Probability; the former belongs to The Necessity Form of Probability, the latter to The Stability Form of Probability. This text has the right to use neither.

So counter-review does only one thing: look back at every failure world and every unclosed gap actually appearing in Sections Three to Nine, to see whether any gap must depend on some candidate property X to be closed.

If no gap must depend on X to be closed, then this text has not obtained:

Probability ⇒ X

and X therefore does not enter the current necessary structure of this text.

If some failure world can be described by X but what makes Probability fail is actually the failure of one of the three items, then the excluding force involving X is already borne by that item and is not independent.

The strength obtained in this section stops here: "X has not been forced out by this text as a necessary duty". It is not a proof that "X is not a necessary duty". ### 12.2 Deletion tests

First, cancel the duty "a dimension must obtain formal Law-bearing".
