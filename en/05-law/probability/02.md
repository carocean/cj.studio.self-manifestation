# The Mathematical Form of Probability

## Section One　Purpose

To establish a formal system for the three necessary qualifications of probability, such that for any candidate Probability organization these three qualifications can be formally evaluated, yielding a definite reading: holds (1), fails (0), or insufficient material (⊥).

The three necessary qualifications are those obtained in The General Form of Probability v1.0:

N\_P  
:⇔  
&nbsp;&nbsp;MultiLawDimension\_F,t(𝒫)  
&nbsp;&nbsp;∧ CommonContinuationBind\_F,t(𝒫; C, 𝔯\_P)  
&nbsp;&nbsp;∧ DomainRelativeRateBearing\_F,t(𝒫; C, 𝔯\_P)

They determine respectively: whether every dimension obtains formal Law-bearing; whether the dimensions are at one Continuation-Position, in one frozen generation domain and in one generation relation; whether the relative-generation relation among the dimensions is borne by the organization.

The formal objective is:

S\_P ↦ M\_P

## Section Two　Core Thesis

Given, in the current Sedimentation-Field, a candidate Probability organization 𝒫, a Continuation-Position C, a rate domain 𝔯\_P frozen in advance, a declared set of continuation dimensions V\_P and a Law-bearing registration family 𝓛\_P: how are the formal truth values of the three necessary duties of The General Form of Probability to be obtained from actual formal Law witnesses, continuation-generation events, rate-domain evaluations, generation structures and their source chains, while strictly distinguishing structural failure 0 from insufficient formal material ⊥?

The structure of S\_P has three layers: the dimension-qualification layer (which directions are qualified to be probability dimensions), the common-binding layer (by virtue of what these dimensions belong to one continuation-rate question), and the rate-bearing layer (who bears the relative generation in this common continuation question). Each layer in turn cites objects already obtained by the earlier layer.

M\_P must express exactly:

(1) dimension qualification: every declared dimension must be borne by an upstream formal Law solution, and the dimensions must be mutually exclusive and separable under the declared resolution protocol;

(2) common binding: one Continuation-Position, one frozen generation regime, mutually exclusive successors of one generation relation;

(3) rate bearing: in a rate domain that is frozen, non-empty, non-vacuous and system-side grounded, the relative-generation relation can be formally represented and is borne by the actual generation contribution of the declared organization.

The following five pairs of objects must be distinguished formally:

OutcomeBearing ≠ RateBearing

StableFrequency ≠ Rel\_ρ

RateRead ≠ qualification

ρ(v\_i) = 0 ≠ v\_i ∉ V\_P

normalization within V\_P ≠ FullCoverage

The formal directions divide into positive read-back M ⇒ S and reverse representation S ⇒ M, the two differing in strength (Section Seven).

### 3.3 Dimension realization

Definition 3.3 \[S\]

For the Continuation-Position C, write Y\_C for the successor-signature space: the signature space sufficient, under the present declared resolution protocol, to judge the identity of a continuation successor. It is not necessarily equal to the complete field-state space. The actual successor signature of a continuation-generation event e is Succ(e) ∈ Y\_C. Hence D\_i ∩ D\_j = ∅ below means only that the declared dimensions are distinguishable under the resolution protocol used, and does not require the two successors to have no overlap in any physical coordinate. For every v\_i ∈ V\_P, the declared successor-recognition domain decidable in running is:

D\_i ⊆ Y\_C, D\_i ≠ ∅

Define:

Realize^M(e, v\_i) = 𝟏\_{D\_i}(Succ(e))

It is required, under the declared resolution protocol, that:

i ≠ j ⇒ D\_i ∩ D\_j = ∅

It is not required that:

⋃\_i D\_i = Y\_C

An event whose successor falls outside ⋃\_i D\_i belongs to no declared dimension; this is neither the failure of any dimension nor a new dimension.

### 3.4 BearDim

Definition 3.4 \[S\]

BearDim^M(L\_i, v\_i; C\_i, R\_i) = 1 if and only if:

(a) R\_i ∈ 𝒮(F, L\_i, I\_i);

(b) DimGenBind^M(L\_i, v\_i; C\_i, R\_i) = 1.

DimGenBind^M is a compressed name, unfolding as: there exist one and the same duty d ∈ O(Rel(L\_i)) and a legal realization Π of that duty in R\_i, such that

(b1) by The Mathematical Form of Law, RoleReal^M(d, R\_i, Π) = 1;

(b2) the actual duty-generation chain of Π enters successor generation at the Continuation-Position C\_i;

(b3) the successor direction borne by Π is recognized as v\_i by the realization domain D\_i (Definition 3.3).

This definition does not require that the read-out Read(d) of the duty itself fall within D\_i: a Law duty that really generates the dimension, but whose read-out description lies at another level, is not thereby excluded. What is required is that the generation chain of one and the same duty realization bear the successor direction corresponding to v\_i.

If (a) or any of (b1)–(b3) fails after complete evaluation, take 0; if the upstream formal Law solution, the duty realization or the generation chain is undecidable, take ⊥.

### 3.10 NoCrossConditionStitch

Definition 3.10 \[S\]

Every load-bearing record x (an event read-out, dimension witness or rate evaluation used for bearing load) must carry the rate-domain protocol identifier DomainProtocol(x) to which it belongs and its evidence time point EvidenceTime(x).

NoCrossConditionStitch^M = 1 if and only if every load-bearing record x satisfies:

DomainProtocol(x) = Γ\_r

FreezeTime(Γ\_r) = τ\_0 \< EvidenceTime(x)

If there exist two load-bearing records x, y with DomainProtocol(x) = Γ\_a, DomainProtocol(y) = Γ\_b, Γ\_a ≠ Γ\_b, and the current declared domain can be formed only by merging the two after the fact, then NoCrossConditionStitch^M = 0. Take ⊥ when the protocol identifier or evidence time point cannot be obtained.

Different microconditions a, b, if already registered at τ\_0 as legal variations of one and the same Γ\_r, have records whose DomainProtocol is in every case Γ\_r, and do not constitute stitching. This criterion judges one and the same frozen generation regime; it does not require the underlying variables to be literally equal.

### 3.13 SameGenerationRelation

Definition 3.13 \[S\]

For a legal continuation-generation event e at C, the underlying running system must provide its generation-relation identity:

GenSchema(e)

GenSchema is the field system's or operating substrate's reading of the actual generation root, generation schema or equivalent generation-relation identity; it is not a new ontological object. The declared instance registers in advance the common generation-relation identity g\_C, frozen together with Γ\_r.

SameGenSchema^M = 1 if and only if every legal continuation-generation realization e ∈ ℰ\_r used in evaluating the declared dimensions and the rate relation satisfies:

GenSchema(e) = g\_C

If there clearly exist two realizations belonging respectively to g\_a ≠ g\_b, and V\_P can be formed only by stitching together their successors, then SameGenSchema^M = 0. Take ⊥ when the generation-relation identity cannot be obtained.

MutualSuccessor^M = 1 if and only if:

(i) every D\_i is a legal distinguishable successor domain of one and the same g\_C at C;

(ii) for every legal continuation-generation realization e\_k ∈ ℰ\_r belonging to g\_C and used for qualification evaluation:

Σ\_{i=1}^{\|V\_P\|} Realize^M(e\_k, v\_i) ≤ 1

It is not written = 1: it is not required that every actual successor fall into V\_P, and FullCoverage is not implied. If one event realizes two dimensions at once, (ii) is 0; take ⊥ when the event identity or the attribution of successors is undecidable.

SameGenerationRelation^M = SameGenSchema^M ∧₃ MutualSuccessor^M

SameGenSchema is responsible for excluding the stitching of different generation processes; MutualSuccessor is responsible for the attribution of mutually exclusive successors within one generation relation. Neither may replace the other: two independent generation processes each realizing only one dimension satisfy the exclusivity inequality while SameGenSchema fails; one event within one generation relation realizing two dimensions at once has SameGenSchema holding while MutualSuccessor fails.

3.15 Rate-domain evaluation and the outer quantifier mode

Definition 3.15 \[Aux\]

Eval(𝔯\_P) is the family of legal evaluations over the rate domain: each evaluation ν is one legal generation configuration permitted by Γ\_r (input state, environmental disturbance, in-field state and so on).

DomainEvalMode(𝔯\_P) ∈ {set, measure}

It decides only how quantifiers over the whole rate domain are evaluated: the set mode uses ordinary universal/existential quantifiers; the measure mode uses, under the legal evaluation measure μ\_r, a.e. universal and positive-measure existential quantifiers. In the measure mode, a null-measure anomaly may not automatically breach universal coverage; nor may a single null-measure success point establish an existential conclusion.

μ\_r acts only on the outer evaluation quantifiers over Eval(𝔯\_P); it is a different object from the generation measure μ\_{C,ν} of the weight mode (Definition 3.18); unless the underlying system has a separate legal mapping, they may not be interchanged or inferred from one another.

### 3.20 Conditional rate and complete rate

Definition 3.20 \[Aux\]

If Σ\_j w\_j \> 0, define the conditional rate within V\_P:

ρ\_i^cond = w\_i / Σ\_j w\_j

Only if the underlying complete successor-generation mass M\_C = μ\_{C,ν}(Y\_C) \> 0 is obtainable is the complete rate defined:

ρ\_i^full = w\_i / M\_C

In that case Σ\_i ρ\_i^full ≤ 1.

Σ\_i ρ\_i^cond = 1 is only the result of conditioning within V\_P and is not read as FullCoverage. M\_C − Σ\_i w\_i is the residual mass outside V\_P and may not be automatically filled in as an "unknown dimension", a "stopping dimension" or any other probability dimension.

### 3.21 Bridge for the old Pr(q,t)

### 3.22 Domain of rate-generation factors

Definition 3.22 \[Aux\]

A\_ρ^gen(ν) is, under evaluation ν, the domain of actual generation factors in that evaluation's local generation mechanism that are qualified to enter the analysis of Rel\_ρ bearing. Its elements may be: input states, generation nodes, gates, masks, Avail, the states of Law-bearing realizations in that evaluation, and structural parameters directly related to rate formation.

Must be excluded: variables related only to outcome statistics; nodes that execute outcomes only after the generation decision is complete; observers' after-the-fact frequencies; correlates without a generation source chain.

a ∈ A\_ρ^gen(ν) does not imply that a bears the rate. A cold standby may enter the candidate domain, but does not bear in evaluations where it has not taken over.

3.26 Minimal sufficient rate-generation groups

Definition 3.26 \[Aux\]

For B ⊆ A\_ρ^gen(ν), if for all legal ν′, ν″ ∈ AdmVar\_ρ(ν), whenever ν′ and ν″ agree on B, one has:

d\_ρ( Rel\_ρ^M(ν′), Rel\_ρ^M(ν″) ) ≤ ε\_ρ

then B is called a sufficient rate-generation group.

MinSuff\_ρ(ν) is the family of all sufficient groups minimal in the sense of inclusion (inclusion-minimal). Minimum cardinality is not taken, and uniqueness is not required.

Hot redundancy is thereby allowed: MinSuff\_ρ = {{A}, {B}}; for a cold standby the family in that evaluation should be only {{A}}, B not being wrongly taken in as a bearer in that evaluation.

3.27 In-evaluation rate-generation binding

Definition 3.27 \[Aux\]

Belonging to some minimal sufficient group obtains only a candidate formal qualification for rate bearing; it does not by itself say that ProbabilityAct is currently occurring at real time t.

For a legal evaluation ν, take a legal generation realization e\_ν inside that evaluation. It may come from the current running evaluation, or from a legal re-entry or replay evaluation conforming to the qualification protocol; it is not required that the event be occurring at real time t.

For a Law witness W\_i, an actual in-evaluation generation contribution c\_i, an in-evaluation generation sub-segment Z\_i^ρ and a realization e\_ν, take the binding key:

β\_ρ = (W\_i, c\_i, Z\_i^ρ, e\_ν, C, 𝔯\_P, ν)

EvalRateBind^M(W\_i, ν, e\_ν) = 1 if and only if the following four hold together:

B1 Same source: c\_i comes from the legal Law realization corresponding to one and the same W\_i;

B2 Generation entry: c\_i actually enters the formation of Rel\_ρ^M(ν) in the realization of that evaluation;

B3 Same realization: c\_i, Z\_i^ρ, Rel\_ρ^M(ν) and the corresponding successor generation all belong to one and the same e\_ν;

B4 Same domain, same evaluation: all the above belong to one and the same C, 𝔯\_P, ν.

If any item fails after complete evaluation, take 0; if the legal in-evaluation realization or the source chain cannot be obtained, take ⊥.

EvalRateBind^M is only the compressed name of B1–B4 and can be wholly unfolded away; it is not a fourth ontological layer. It only guarantees same-witness closure in qualification evaluation and does not define ProbabilityAct; whether a current real event is realizing the action of Probability is judged separately by The Mathematical Form of Probability-Action.

### 3.28 RateBearWitness

Definition 3.28 \[S\]

RateBearWitness^M(ν) = 1 if and only if there exist B ∈ MinSuff\_ρ(ν), W\_i ∈ 𝓛\_P and an in-evaluation realization e\_ν such that:

B ∩ GenIn(W\_i, ν) ≠ ∅

and:

EvalRateBind^M(W\_i, ν, e\_ν) = 1

What is obtained here is the rate-bearing qualification in evaluation ν, not the same as current real ProbabilityAct.

Existential polarity is taken: it is not required that every minimal sufficient group contain generation factors of the declared organization. Hence another wholly external redundant mechanism is allowed to exist, provided that the realization within that evaluation is indeed borne by the declared organization. Conversely, if in that evaluation the mechanism of the declared organization is only a standby and the realization is wholly borne by an external mechanism, then RateBearWitness^M(ν) = 0.

Qualification aggregation over the whole rate domain:

where ∀^{mode} takes, according to DomainEvalMode, the set universal or the a.e. universal under measure.

## Section Four　Equations and Static Relations

### 4.1 Static relations

This is not a dynamics problem, and no differential equation is set. There are only four load-bearing relations:

(P-E1) Dimension realization

Realize^M(e, v\_i) = 𝟏\_{D\_i}(Succ(e))

(P-E2) Mutual exclusion within one generation

Σ\_i Realize^M(e, v\_i) ≤ 1

(P-E3) Projection in the weight branch

w\_i(ν) = μ\_{C,ν}(D\_i)

Rel\_ρ^M(ν) = \[w\_V(ν)\]\_∼ρ

The struct branch is not forced to write numerical equations.

(P-E4) Three-valued composition

Read\_{N\_P}^M = MultiLawDimension^M ∧₃ CommonContinuationBind^M ∧₃ DomainRelativeRateBearing^M

The following may not be written as qualification equations:

Σ\_i p\_i = 1

Probability = f(ρ)

Rel\_ρ = ρ̂\_N

RateBearing ⇔ ∂ρ/∂L\_i ≠ 0

The last formula would wrongly judge redundant bearing and fixed-proportion bearing as not bearing.

### 4.2 Formal properties

Proposition 4.1　SameGenerationRelation^M does not imply FullCoverage.

Proof: take V\_P = {v\_1, v\_2}, Y\_C = D\_1 ∪ D\_2 ∪ D\_out, the three pairwise disjoint and D\_out ≠ ∅. Let an event e satisfy Succ(e) ∈ D\_out; then Σ\_i Realize^M(e, v\_i) = 0 ≤ 1, (P-E2) holds, while ⋃\_i D\_i ≠ Y\_C. □

Proposition 4.2　∼\_ρ is an equivalence relation, and Rel\_ρ^M is well defined in the weight mode.

Proof: a = 1 gives reflexivity; w′ = a·w gives w = a⁻¹·w′, hence symmetry; w′ = a·w and w″ = b·w′ give w″ = (ab)·w, hence transitivity. The zero vector is equivalent only to itself, so \[0\]\_ρ is a class on its own. □

Proposition 4.3　When Σ\_j w\_j \> 0, Σ\_i ρ\_i^cond = 1; when M\_C \> 0, Σ\_i ρ\_i^full = Σ\_i w\_i / M\_C ≤ 1, with equality if and only if the residual mass outside V\_P is zero.

Proof: follows directly from Definition 3.20 and, since the D\_i are pairwise disjoint, Σ\_i w\_i = μ\_{C,ν}(⋃\_i D\_i) ≤ μ\_{C,ν}(Y\_C). □

Proposition 4.4　The current weight of a dimension being zero does not change its dimension-qualification reading.

Proof: DimQual^M(v\_i) depends only on 𝒲\_Dim(v\_i) (Definition 3.6) and does not contain w\_i. w\_i = 0 only makes Rel\_ρ^M take the corresponding class. □

Proposition 4.5　Membership in MinSuff\_ρ does not imply RateBearWitness^M.

Proof: take an internal mechanism I and an external mechanism M, each independently sufficient to maintain the same Rel\_ρ; then MinSuff\_ρ = {{I}, {M}}. If in evaluation ν the realization is borne by M and I is only a standby, then the contribution of I does not enter the generation chain of that evaluation's realization, B2 fails, and EvalRateBind^M = 0; M is not in GenIn(W\_i, ν), so RateBearWitness^M(ν) = 0. □

## Section Five　Running and Solving

### 5.1 Order of solving

The order of solving is fixed as:

MultiLawDimension^M → CommonContinuationBind^M → DomainRelativeRateBearing^M

Later items cite the dimensions and the common generation relation already obtained by earlier items. When an earlier item is clearly 0, downstream solving may stop and later items are not evaluated; by ∧₃, the total reading takes 0.

1\. Check the registration χ\_P (M1).

2\. For every v\_i, invoke The Mathematical Form of Law to obtain 𝒮(F, L\_i, I\_i), judge BearDim^M, and obtain 𝒲\_Dim(v\_i) (M2).

3\. Obtain Card^M, Dist^M, DimQual^M and RegValid^M, and thereby MultiLawDimension^M (M3).

4\. Check that all C\_i = C in 𝓛\_P, obtaining SameContinuationPosition^M.

5\. Read the freezing record and judge Frozen^M (M5).

6\. Generate the legal Eval(𝔯\_P) from Γ\_r and ℰ\_r, and fix DomainEvalMode (M6, M15).

7\. Read the DomainProtocol and EvidenceTime of each load-bearing record, judge NoCrossConditionStitch^M and InRateDomain^M, and obtain SameGenerationDomain^M.

8\. Judge SameGenSchema^M by generation-relation identity, judge (P-E2) by event identity to obtain MutualSuccessor^M, and compose SameGenerationRelation^M (M4).

9\. Sign CommonContinuationBind^M.

10\. Judge NonEmpty^M, NonVacuous^M and Grounded^M, obtaining RateDomainQual^M.

11\. Fix RelRepMode(ν) for each evaluation.

12\. Form Rel\_ρ^M(ν) and judge RelForm^M (M7, M8).

13\. Obtain A\_ρ^gen(ν) (M9).

14\. Obtain GenIn(W\_i, ν) (M10).

15\. Construct AdmVar\_ρ(ν) (M11).

16\. Check Sound\_Adm and Cover\_Adm (M11); when both are 1, use d\_ρ and ε\_ρ to obtain all of MinSuff\_ρ(ν) (M12, M13); otherwise MinSuff\_ρ(ν) is recorded ⊥.

17\. For the in-evaluation realization e\_ν of each evaluation, check β\_ρ and B1–B4 (M14).

18\. Obtain RateBearWitness^M(ν).

19\. Aggregate over the whole 𝔯\_P according to DomainEvalMode, obtaining RateBearWitness^M\_{𝔯\_P}.

20\. Sign DomainRelativeRateBearing^M and compose Read\_{N\_P}^M.

5.2 Three values and the guard on negative read-outs

1: clearly holds.

0: clearly fails after complete evaluation.

⊥: the current legal formal input is insufficient.

Conjunction follows ∧₃; quantifiers follow the three-valued quantifier rules of Section Three. Every negative read-out 0 must carry the grounds of solving completeness at the corresponding layer; lacking evidence, 0 is not written.

### 5.3 Solvability conditions

The following M1–M16 are conditions of formal representation and solving, not new necessary structures of Probability, and not H. If any Mi is missing, only the formal items depending on that condition take ⊥; N\_P or Probability is not automatically judged 0.

M1 The declared objects are completely identifiable: F, t, 𝒫, C, 𝔯\_P, V\_P, 𝓛\_P can all be effectively referred to.

M2 The upstream formal Law interface is usable: the formal Law solutions, duty decompositions, duty realizations RoleReal and their generation chains needed for every declared dimension are decidable.

M3 The dimension-realization rule is executable: for every D\_i, the attribution of successors can be effectively judged.

M4 The identity of generation events is traceable: event identities and their successor signatures, inputs and event attribution are decidable; the generation-relation identity GenSchema(e) can be read.

M5 Freezing and membership rules of the rate domain are decidable: Γ\_r, τ\_0, g\_C and version consistency are decidable; the DomainProtocol and EvidenceTime of load-bearing records can be obtained.

M6 The system-side grounding of the rate domain is decidable: the generation sources of rate-domain realizations are traceable, and NonVacuous^M and Grounded^M can be evaluated. When the grounding is not traceable, both take ⊥; when it is traceable and clearly has no system-side grounding, Grounded^M takes 0, which is structural failure and not a missing of this condition.

M7 The Rel\_ρ representation interface is usable: struct has at least an executable same-relation comparison; weight has at least legal generation weights or a measure.

M8 A bridge across representation modes is decidable: when mixed struct and weight evaluations must be compared, they can be legally projected to a common representation; otherwise the related comparison is ⊥.

M9 The domain of rate-generation factors is obtainable: A\_ρ^gen(ν) can be obtained from the real generation structure.

M10 The generation source chain is traceable: GenIn(W\_i, ν) is obtained from the real generation chain.

M11 The family of legal diagnostic variations can be faithfully characterized: AdmVar\_ρ(ν) has two-way fidelity to the currently frozen domain of legal duty-diagnostic variation.

Sound\_Adm = 1: every variation in it is permitted by the field system and keeps C, Γ\_r, V\_P, {D\_i}, g\_C and the frozen generation schema; it contains no variation violating the field system, the frozen protocol or the generation schema.

Cover\_Adm = 1: under the condition that C, Γ\_r, V\_P, {D\_i}, g\_C and the frozen generation schema are kept unchanged, every diagnostic variation that the field system permits and that can change the values, availability or legal realizations of the generation factors in A\_ρ^gen(ν), or the result of the formation of Rel\_ρ, is taken into AdmVar\_ρ(ν), or covered by a representative equivalent at the declared resolution. The domain of coverage is given by the field system's domain of legal variation, not inferred backwards from the final determination of rate bearing. Omitting such variations would make MinSuff\_ρ misjudge sufficiency: some group B would be judged sufficient only because the legal variations that could make it insufficient were not taken in.

When legality or coverage is undecidable, the results depending on MinSuff\_ρ are ⊥. When Sound\_Adm = 0 or Cover\_Adm = 0 is clear, the solving is a distortion of the formal protocol, is not permitted to sign 1 or 0, and MinSuff\_ρ and every item depending on it are recorded ⊥; it may not be written DomainRelativeRateBearing^M = 0, because this is not the failure of a structural fact.

M12 The comparison of relative-generation relations is decidable: d\_ρ or an equivalent criterion can be computed to the declared resolution.

M13 Minimal sufficient groups are obtainable: finite domains can be enumerated; in infinite domains, if the existence or obtainability of minimal groups cannot be guaranteed, the related quantities are ⊥.

M14 Same-witness binding within evaluations is decidable: the legal realization e\_ν of each evaluation can be obtained, and B1–B4 can be checked.

M15 The outer quantifier mode of the rate domain can be fixed: set/measure can be determined; in the measure mode the related sets are measurable or effectively approximable.

M16 Three-valuedness and the completeness of negative read-outs are executable: every 0 has complete solving grounds.

### 5.4 Solution

Read\_{N\_P}^M = 1 is a formal solution of M\_P. Read\_{N\_P}^M = 0 means that under complete evaluation the formal criterion of some duty fails. Read\_{N\_P}^M = ⊥ means insufficient formal material, and may not be read as the failure of N\_P, still less as Probability not holding.

## Section Seven　Read-Back Rules

### 7.1 Three layers of evidence

The first layer, mathematical formal facts: such as D\_i ∩ D\_j = ∅, w ∼\_ρ w′, B ∈ MinSuff\_ρ. Only the corresponding mathematical facts are read back.

The second layer, actual generation facts within the field: such as GenIn(W\_i, ν), EvalRateBind^M. They state that the generation factors of a legal Law witness actually enter the formation of one and the same relative generation.

The third layer, structural read-back to the General Form: see 7.2.

### 7.2 Positive read-back M ⇒ S

What is in square brackets is the condition carried by that arrow.

(7.1) DimQual^M(v\_i) = 1 ⇒ v\_i obtains formal Law-bearing, that is, there exist L\_i, I\_i, C\_i such that Law\_F,t(L\_i, I\_i) = 1 ∧ BearDim\_F,t(L\_i, v\_i; C\_i)  
\[H\_LawRead(W\_i)\]

H\_LawRead(W\_i) is: all the valid establishment or read-back antecedents carried, according to its actual source and the currently cited version, by the upstream Law qualification cited by that witness (a registration in 𝓛\_P, or an element of 𝒲\_Dim(v\_i) used for read-back). Specifically:

if W\_i comes from an immediate read-back of a current formal solution of The Mathematical Form of Law, it inherits the conditions actually carried by that read-back arrow, and Law is then obtained through the sufficient direction of The Necessity Form of Law, that step adding no condition;

if W\_i cites an already established structural fact of Law, it inherits only the current conditions of that fact itself.

The upstream internal list of read-back conditions is neither copied nor frozen; when upstream is upgraded, H\_LawRead(W\_i) is read according to its currently cited version. Each W\_i inherits one by one according to its own source, without mechanical merging across dimensions.

The read-back of the BearDim part is given by the following theorem, without setting up a separate condition.

Theorem 7.1　Read-back of dimension bearing

If BearDim^M(L\_i, v\_i; C\_i, R\_i) = 1, then the relational content borne by L\_i is exactly the successor relation that continuation at the Continuation-Position C\_i goes to v\_i, that is, the structural-layer BearDim\_F,t(L\_i, v\_i; C\_i) holds.

Proof: by Definition 3.4, there exist one and the same duty d ∈ O(Rel(L\_i)) and its legal realization Π in R\_i satisfying (b1)–(b3). That d is a duty of Rel(L\_i) is given directly by definition; (b1) gives that d is realized in R\_i; (b2) gives that the duty-generation chain of this realization enters successor generation at C\_i, so what d bears is continuation at C\_i; (b3) gives that the successor direction of this continuation is recognized by D\_i as v\_i under the declared resolution protocol. d belongs to Rel(L\_i), so the relational content borne by Rel(L\_i) contains the successor relation that continuation at C\_i goes to v\_i, which is the meaning of the structural-layer BearDim. The Law-qualification part of L\_i is obtained from (a) and H\_LawRead(W\_i). □

(7.2) MultiLawDimension^M = 1 ⇒ MultiLawDimension = 1  
\[the conjunction of H\_LawRead of the witnesses used for DimCover\]

The read-back of Card^M and Dist^M is synonymous with the S components by Definitions 3.5 and 3.7, and carries no separate condition.

(7.3) CommonContinuationBind^M = 1 ⇒ CommonContinuationBind = 1  
\[this arrow adds no condition; the Law qualifications of the 𝓛\_P registrations it quantifies over are each carried according to (7.1)\]

SameContinuationPosition^M, SameGenerationDomain^M and SameGenerationRelation^M are direct formalizations, as identities, protocols and event relations, of existing structural items of the General Form: C\_i = C; one frozen protocol; one generation-relation identity; mutually exclusive successors within one generation relation. They are exact representations of S, not reduced through formal tools absent on the structural side, and therefore no separate read-back bridge is set up. If it is later found that GenSchema identity is insufficient to guarantee the structural-side "one and the same continuation-generation relation", it is handled as a read-back conflict under 8.5(3).

Theorem 7.2　Reduction of rate bearing (closing H\_ρB)

Suppose M9–M14 hold, and the qualifications of the Law witnesses W\_i cited by RateBearWitness^M(ν) are valid according to their H\_LawRead(W\_i). If:

RateBearWitness^M(ν) = 1

then in evaluation ν the legal load-bearing state of the declared organization 𝓛\_P really enters the formation of Rel\_ρ(𝒫; C, 𝔯\_P).

Proof: by Definition 3.28, there exist B ∈ MinSuff\_ρ(ν), W\_i ∈ 𝓛\_P and an in-evaluation realization e\_ν such that B ∩ GenIn(W\_i, ν) ≠ ∅ and EvalRateBind^M(W\_i, ν, e\_ν) = 1. Take a ∈ B ∩ GenIn(W\_i, ν).

Step one, excluding mere correlation. a ∈ B ⊆ A\_ρ^gen(ν). By Definition 3.22, A\_ρ^gen has already excluded variables related only to outcome statistics, nodes that execute outcomes only after the generation decision is complete, observers' after-the-fact frequencies and correlates without a generation source chain. So a is an actual generation factor in that evaluation's generation mechanism, not a correlate or an outcome-execution variable.

Step two, excluding being merely read without bearing load. B is a minimal sufficient group in the sense of inclusion (Definition 3.26): among all the legal diagnostic variations of AdmVar\_ρ(ν), fixing B fixes Rel\_ρ^M at the declared resolution ε\_ρ, while no proper subset of B has this property. So a is not a quantity merely read without affecting Rel\_ρ, but belongs, within the family of legal diagnostic variations, to a minimal sufficient forming group of the relative-generation relation. M11 guarantees that AdmVar\_ρ(ν) has fidelity to the declared domain of legal duty-diagnostic variation: it contains no illegal variation violating the field system, the frozen protocol or the generation schema (Sound\_Adm), and at the same time omits none of the legal generation variations relevant to the load-bearing above, or at least contains their equivalent representatives at the declared resolution (Cover\_Adm). Therefore the sufficiency and inclusion-minimality of B hold relative to the real domain of legal generation variation, not relative to arbitrary mathematical assignments or a family of variations screened after the fact.

Step three, excluding a bearer outside the organization. a ∈ GenIn(W\_i, ν); by Definition 3.23 and M10, a is a generation factor realized, through the real generation source chain under ν, by the formal Law solution R\_i corresponding to W\_i, so a belongs to the declared Law-bearing organization, not to a selection mechanism outside the organization.

Step four, excluding having the capacity to bear but not bearing in this evaluation. B2 of EvalRateBind^M = 1 gives: the contribution c\_i of W\_i actually enters the formation of Rel\_ρ^M(ν) in that evaluation's realization e\_ν, rather than merely having standby capacity.

Step five, excluding stitching of evidence. B1–B4 give: the same source W\_i, generation entering the same formation chain of Rel\_ρ, the same realization e\_ν, the same C, 𝔯\_P, ν. So minimal sufficiency, source attribution and actual entry fall on one and the same bearing, not stitched from one minimal sufficient group, another source chain and a third event.

Combining the five steps: a Law-bearing realization of the declared organization, with its load-bearing state in a legal evaluation, as a member of a minimal sufficient forming group of the relative-generation relation, has actually entered the formation of Rel\_ρ in that evaluation. This is exactly what The General Form of Probability calls "the legal load-bearing state of 𝓛\_P really enters the formation of the relative-generation relation among the dimensions"; existential polarity agrees with the joint, distributed and redundant bearing allowed by the General Form.

By aggregation of rate-domain quantifiers: when RateBearWitness^M\_{𝔯\_P} = 1, it holds universally (or a.e.) over Eval(𝔯\_P) according to DomainEvalMode, so the organization bears Rel\_ρ over 𝔯\_P. □

Theorem 7.2 closes the rate-bearing reduction bridge H\_ρB (see 8.3).

(7.4) DomainRelativeRateBearing^M = 1 ⇒ DomainRelativeRateBearing = 1  
\[invokes H\_ρB closed by Theorem 7.2, not propagated downstream as an open condition; the W\_i cited by RateBearWitness each inherit H\_LawRead(W\_i)\]

(7.5) Read\_{N\_P}^M = 1 ⇒ N\_P = 1

### 8.5 Breach methods

(1) Failure of representation: there exists a legal instance satisfying S\_P that, with M1–M16 all satisfied, still cannot be represented by M\_P, and this is not mere insufficiency of input.

(2) Internal contradiction: under the same χ\_P, the same criterion yields both 1 and 0 by definition.

(3) Read-back conflict: the formal reading is 1 while the corresponding structure of the General Form is 0.

(4) Overstepping qualification conditions: turning normalization, full coverage, positive rate, stable frequency and the like, not required by the General Form, into qualification conditions.

(5) Misjudging an external selector: judging an external selector to be the rate bearing of the declared organization.

(6) Misjudging redundancy: judging the actual in-evaluation bearer in hot redundancy as not bearing, or judging a cold standby as the bearer in that evaluation.

(6′) Conflating qualification and action: reading the in-evaluation realization requirement of RateBearWitness as "an event must be occurring at real time t", thereby merging the judgment of ProbabilityAct into the qualification criterion.

(7) Correlates entering the chain: taking pure correlates into A\_ρ^gen or into the generation-bearing chain.

(8) Writing ⊥ as 0: recording insufficient formal material as failure.

(9) Dangling symbols: some load-bearing symbol finds no counterpart of storage, reading, determination or update in a future operating substrate.

(10) Omissions in the correspondence table: some object bears a duty of criterion, solving or read-back but is not listed in 3.0.

(11) Conflict of relation properties: usage in the body disagrees with the declaration of 3.33.

(12) Change of quantifier polarity: changing the existential polarity of RateBearWitness into universal, or the universal of rate-domain aggregation into existential; in the measure mode, establishing or breaching a conclusion by null-measure points.

(13) Counterexample to the reduction: there is RateBearWitness^M(ν) = 1, M9–M14 hold (including Sound\_Adm and Cover\_Adm of M11), and the qualifications of the cited Law witnesses are valid, while at the structural layer the declared organization does not bear the formation of Rel\_ρ in ν. This breaches Theorem 7.2, and H\_ρB must be reopened.

Not constituting a breach: M1–M16 not being satisfied so that the related items are ⊥; upstream read-back conditions not being closed; N\_P^M = 1 while Probability is not judged (the sufficient direction belongs separately to The Necessity Form of Probability).

### 8.6 Regression tests

The following tests serve only as regression and breach checks, not as general proofs.

Test 1　Positive case: both dimensions have formal Law witnesses; same C; same frozen rate domain; one and the same generation relation; the declared organization actually forms 70:30. Requires MLD^M = 1, CCB^M = 1, DRRB^M = 1, hence Read\_{N\_P}^M = 1.

Test 2　External selector: an external M decides 70:30, and 𝓛\_P only executes the selected outcome (so in every evaluation). The selection node of M is in no GenIn(W\_i, ν), and the execution nodes do not enter A\_ρ^gen. Requires MLD^M = 1, CCB^M = 1, DRRB^M = 0; a stable frequency does not change the reading of the third item.

Test 3　Cross-condition stitching: 70 times under condition a, 30 times under condition b, a and b not belonging to one frozen Γ\_r. Requires NoCrossConditionStitch^M = 0, SameGenerationDomain^M = 0, hence CCB^M = 0.

Test 4　Parallel processes: v\_1 and v\_2 come respectively from two different generation-relation identities (different GenSchema); every event realizes only the one dimension it belongs to, all exclusivity inequalities passing; the two share C and Γ\_r. Requires SameGenSchema^M = 0, hence SameGenerationRelation^M = 0.

Test 4b　Double realization in one generation: one event of the same GenSchema falls into D\_1 and D\_2 at once. Requires MutualSuccessor^M = 0, hence SameGenerationRelation^M = 0.

Test 5　Hot redundancy: either of A, B suffices to maintain the same relative-generation relation. Requires MinSuff\_ρ = {{A}, {B}}, and the declared organization to which the actual in-evaluation bearer belongs obtains RateBearWitness^M = 1.

Test 6　Partial coverage and a zero-rate dimension: (w\_1, w\_2, w\_out) = (7, 0, 3). Requires that v\_2 not leave V\_P because w\_2 = 0; Σ\_i ρ\_i^full = 0.7 \< 1; no failure on account of incomplete coverage.

Test 7　Insufficient source chain: there are outcome frequencies, Law witnesses, same position and same domain, but W\_i → c\_i → Z\_i^ρ cannot be obtained. Requires DRRB^M = ⊥, not 0 and not 1.

Test 8　Cold standby: A bears, B takes over only after A exits. Requires MinSuff\_ρ = {{A}} in that evaluation; A obtains rate bearing, B does not; B may belong to A\_ρ^gen but does not belong to the minimal sufficient groups of that evaluation.

Test 9　Pure correlate: Z is highly correlated with the rate but has no generation source chain. Requires Z ∉ A\_ρ^gen.

Test 10　Complete internal-external redundancy: the internal I and the external M are each independently sufficient to maintain the same Rel\_ρ. When the in-evaluation realization goes through I, RateBearWitness^M = 1; when it goes through M with I only a standby, RateBearWitness^M = 0 (Proposition 4.5). This case shows that qualification by minimal sufficient group and in-evaluation binding must both be present.

### 8.7 What this text does not do

It does not prove N\_P ⇒ Probability; that is handed to The Necessity Form of Probability.

It makes no determination of instances; that is handed to The Determination Form of Probability.

It does not study current action, source, dynamics, stability or reachability; these are handed to the corresponding form-texts.

It builds no separate formal Law system or physical generation system.

It sets no global completeness predicate ReadComplete\_ρ; completeness is shared by M9–M16 and the guards on negative read-outs at each layer.

It sets no abstract unified generation operator G\_C.
