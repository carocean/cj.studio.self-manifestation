# The Necessity Form of Probability

## Section One　Purpose

To prove: within the working domain of Probability qualification in the current Sedimentation-Field, a candidate organization, as soon as it has the following three structures together, is a Probability, and need not satisfy any fourth independent qualification condition.

The three structures are:

First, it has at least two distinguishable continuation dimensions, every dimension obtaining formal Law-bearing;

Second, these dimensions start from one and the same Continuation-Position, lie in one and the same generation domain frozen in advance, and are mutually exclusive successors of one and the same generation relation;

Third, the relative-generation relation among these dimensions is borne by the organization itself, not decided by a mechanism outside the organization.

In formulas:

N\_P ⇒ Probability\_F,t(𝒫; C, 𝔯\_P)

N\_P  
:⇔  
&nbsp;&nbsp;MultiLawDimension\_F,t(𝒫)  
&nbsp;&nbsp;∧ CommonContinuationBind\_F,t(𝒫; C, 𝔯\_P)  
&nbsp;&nbsp;∧ DomainRelativeRateBearing\_F,t(𝒫; C, 𝔯\_P)

"Need not satisfy any fourth independent qualification condition" means specifically: whether a numerical rate can be read out, whether the rate of every dimension is positive, whether the rates of the dimensions are normalized, whether these dimensions cover all successors, whether the system is ontologically random, whether the empirical frequency is stable — none of these affects the conclusion. A wholly deterministic system, or a system in which some dimension currently has rate zero, likewise obtains Probability qualification if the three structures all hold.

### 2.2 Route of proof

The proof does not take the route "the General Form has obtained currently derived minimality, so the three items all holding suffice for Probability". The CurrentDerivedMinimal of The General Form of Probability proves only that deleting any necessary duty lets in a non-probability world already excluded; it does not prove that the conjunction of the three items suffices for Probability. That result is used only in Section Eight as a consistency back-test, not as an antecedent of the proof.

The route of proof is:

fixed source-text corpus domain → semantic atomization → classification of duties → closure of current qualification → N\_P completely realizes the qualification → Probability

The corresponding three theorems are: first obtain, on the fixed corpus, all the duties of the current qualification of probability, and prove that there is no independent cancelling condition (Theorem One); then prove that the three structures of N\_P completely realize these duties (Theorem Two); finally obtain the theorem of the necessity of probability (Theorem Three).

## Section Four　Rules of Atomization and Classification

### 4.1 Classification

Sentences are not classified as whole sentences. Every sentence is first split into minimal propositional components, and each component is assigned to one of the following classes by its dominant duty.

CurrentQualification: by virtue of what a candidate organization in the current field now obtains Probability qualification (the obtaining side). By the question answered it is further divided into three classes:

U\_D　dimension qualification: what formal dimensions there are, and whether each dimension obtains Law-bearing;

U\_C　common continuation: whether these dimensions belong to one continuation question, one generation domain, one generation relation;

U\_R　rate bearing: who bears the relative-generation relation among these dimensions.

Disqualification: components that, when the conditions of obtaining of CurrentQualification all hold, can on their own make the current Probability qualification fail (the cancelling side).

The remaining classes are together called U\_O:

Genesis　how probability is generated, and by what it is driven;

Dynamics　how a probability organization changes;

Degree　the degree of bias and anisotropy;

Representation　numerical representation, recognition, read-out;

Observation　empirical frequency, outward distribution;

Execution　how a single physical execution settles;

Act　the current action or general action of probability;

Relation　the relation of probability to Law, Principle, Intention and so on;

Location　the field or level where probability lies.

### 4.2 Criteria of classification

(a) Criterion of question: see whether the component answers "what formal dimensions there are" (U\_D), "whether these dimensions belong to one continuation question" (U\_C), "who bears the rate among these dimensions" (U\_R), or another question (U\_O).

(b) Criterion of phase: see whether the component says "how it forms" (Genesis), "whether it now holds" (CurrentQualification), "how it changes" (Dynamics), or "as what it appears" (Representation, Observation).

(c) Criterion of deletion: suppose the component is negated, the other components unchanged. If the candidate organization still has Law-bearing of every dimension, common continuation of the dimensions and organizational bearing of relative generation, and no sentence in the fixed corpus denies that it is a probability when that component is negated, then the component does not belong to CurrentQualification. This criterion is used only to re-check components already assigned to U\_O by (a), not to produce duties of the obtaining side; duties of the obtaining side are read from the corpus only by (a).

(d) Review of the cancelling side: if a component looks formally like a negation ("if some situation appears, it is not a probability"), it must be specially reviewed: supposing the conditions of obtaining of CurrentQualification all hold, can the component on its own make Probability = 0? If, in order to take effect, it must make some condition of obtaining fail, then it is not an independent cancelling condition and is returned to its original class.

### 4.3 Review of irreducible conjunctions

After all single components have been classified, the irreducible conjunctions authorized by the fixed corpus must still be checked. If someone claims that a conjunction of two or more components

X = A\_1 ∧ … ∧ A\_n

bears a new current-Probability qualification duty or cancelling duty, then grounds explicitly authorizing that combination to bear the duty must be found in the fixed corpus. Such joint authorization can occur within one sentence, or be formed by causal, conditional, referential, summarizing or anaphoric relations across sentences.

Conjunctions are reviewed in three classes:

(i) conjunctions containing members of U\_D, U\_C, U\_R: the qualification members among them are realized by the three structures; the remaining members can form a fourth duty only if the corpus explicitly gives the combination new current-qualification semantics or cancelling semantics.

(ii) conjunctions made wholly of U\_O components: without authorization by the corpus, they cannot rise to qualification merely by being conjoined.

(iii) claims that the conjunction produces new current-qualification semantics present in none of the atoms before: if there is no authorization for these semantics in the fixed corpus, the claim has gone beyond the frozen source-text corpus domain and does not enter the proof.

## Section Six　Closure of Current Qualification

6.1 The three duties of the obtaining side

Merging the components of CurrentQualification that point to the same content gives three duties.

U\_D　dimension qualification: there are at least two distinguishable continuation directions (B8.b); these directions are all already sedimented paths, not possible paths (result component of B4.b); every direction is borne by a formal Law (B1, B4.d, B7.b, C1.a); what they rest on is regularity, not a momentary value (B9.b).

U\_C　common continuation: the directions start from the same starting point (B4.a), come from the same source (B8.a), lie within one generation regime that can accommodate different environmental disturbances inside it (B4.e), and compose one organization (B7.c). "Can accommodate" here is a permission, not a requirement: legal variation within the regime does not constitute stitching, but it is not required that every probability form multiple dimensions in dependence on environmental disturbance.

U\_R　rate bearing: probability itself is the rate among directions (B3.d); this rate is produced by "this thing" itself (B8.c); the bearer is the organization composed of the Laws of the dimensions (B7.c, C1.b).

6.2 Theorem One　Theorem of the closure of the current qualification of probability

On the fixed source-text corpus domain A1–A3, B1–B11, C1, all the semantic components directly affecting the current truth of Probability are exhausted: the obtaining side is exactly the three classes U\_D, U\_C, U\_R; the cancelling side is empty,

IndependentQualifier\_P = ∅

and there is in the fixed corpus no irreducible conjunction bearing a fourth qualification duty or cancelling duty.

Proof

### 9.3 Deterministic branching

Suppose x = a ⇒ v\_1, x = b ⇒ v\_2, and single executions are wholly deterministic. The key to judgment is: by whom the generation proportion of x and the generation relation to each dimension are borne.

If the proportion of x is decided by the external environment and the organization only executes the mapping, then DomainRelativeRateBearing = 0;

Determinism ⇏ ¬Probability

## Section Eleven　Breach

The case directly breaching the main theorem is:

N\_P = 1 ∧ Probability = 0

By the layer at which it occurs it divides into four classes. If any class appears, a review must be made:

Breach One　Omission in the source-text corpus domain: outside the fixed corpus there exist sentences bearing the current qualification of probability, or after revision of the corpus such sentences are added, and their components cannot be assigned to U\_D, U\_C, U\_R or U\_O.

Breach Two　Counterexample of a fourth qualification: there exists a single component or irreducible conjunction X with grounds in the corpus that can still on its own cancel the qualification when the conditions of obtaining all hold, and that can neither be assigned to the three structures nor belongs to a downstream task.

Breach Three　Failure of structural realization: N\_P = 1, but some content of at least one of U\_D, U\_C, U\_R is actually not realized, showing that the correspondence of Section Seven is mistaken.

Breach Four　Anti-sufficiency world: the three classes of duty have all actually been realized, and there is still an independent factor making Probability = 0, and this factor, in taking effect, does not appear as the exit of any of the three structures.

The following cases do not constitute a breach:

Read\_{N\_P}^M = ⊥: this is only insufficient formal material.

Some dimension having ρ(v\_i) = 0: a zero rate does not cause the dimension to exit.

The numerical rate being unreadable: this is a question of representation.

The empirical frequency deviating: this is a question on the outcome side.

The probability currently not acting: this belongs to the range of the action forms.
