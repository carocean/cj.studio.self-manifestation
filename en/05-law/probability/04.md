# The General Form of Probability-Action

What follows attempts one general proposition:

When a Probability that already holds is in fact acting at this moment, what structure must this current action at least have? In this text, under unceasing questioning, we are to find where the proof must go, and derive the General Form of Probability-Action.

Then let us first give the result. (What follows is a reading arrangement placed back at the head of the text after the whole text was completed, according to what it actually contains; it only announces the result and is not a premise of the subsequent derivation.)

Formula of the necessary structure of Probability-Action:

Within the working domain where Probability\_F,t(𝒫; C, 𝔯\_P) = 1:

ProbabilityAct\_F,t(𝒫; C, 𝔯\_P, ξ\_t)  
⇒  
∃(e\_t, R\_t, Z\_t) \[  
&nbsp;&nbsp;CurrentProbabilityOccurrence\_F,t(𝒫; e\_t, R\_t, Z\_t)  
&nbsp;&nbsp;∧ ProbabilityDutyConformance\_F,t(𝒫; e\_t, R\_t, Z\_t)  
\]

where:

R\_1 Current occurrence, that is, CurrentProbabilityOccurrence. There is currently in fact one actual continuation e\_t, and in the actual generation chain of e\_t there is a segment Z\_t actually borne by a legal current realization R\_t of 𝒫. It does not require 𝒫 to monopolize the whole continuation, nor 𝒫 to decide the final settling.

R\_2 The duty-conformance layer, that is, ProbabilityDutyConformance added on the same witness as R\_1: R\_2 := R\_1 ∧ ProbabilityDutyConformance. The content of ProbabilityDutyConformance is: in the same continuation e\_t, R\_t, as a realization of 𝒫 as a Law-bearing organization, with a legal current load-bearing state, actually enters the formation of the relative-generation relation among the probability dimensions on that occasion, and makes the current bearing on that occasion hold. It allows redundant bearing and legal variation of the rate value, and excludes the mere existence or read-out of the rate relation, structures outside the organization, and partial execution.

The two items share one and the same set of witnesses (e\_t, R\_t, Z\_t). R\_2 ⇒ R\_1, and the type of dependence is linear progression R\_1 ≺ R\_2: the second layer is a tightening on the same occurrence as the first layer, not an independent fact on another event.

In one sentence:

The action of probability is not the outcome falling into some dimension; at this moment there is in fact one legal real occurrence, and in that same occurrence it really bears, as a Law organization, the relative generation among the dimensions.

※※※

Abstract

The first three texts of the Probability Group-Form have established respectively the structural candidate, the formal system and the sufficient closure of probability qualification; of these, The Necessity Form of Probability closed the duty semantics of probability qualification on a frozen source-text corpus domain. This Form advances one step on that basis: it studies the current structure indispensable to an already holding Probability when it is in fact acting at moment t. Only the duty semantics of qualification is inherited here; the conditional necessary direction Probability ⇒ N\_P of The General Form of Probability is not invoked.

The necessary structure obtained contains two progressive current duties: in the current actual continuation there must be a generation sub-segment borne by a legal current realization of that probability; in the same continuation, that Law-bearing organization must actually realize its relative-generation duty.

This structure does not require final dimension realization, sole bearing, but-for necessity, a fixed rate value, ontological randomness, full coverage or stable empirical frequency.

Only the necessary direction is proven, carrying no H. The sufficient direction, formal read-back, dynamic change and determination of instances are handed respectively to subsequent form-texts.

## Section Three　Preliminary Distinctions

First exclude several explanations that cannot bear current action.

Action is not qualification. The frozen corpus speaks of qualification ("having become a probability Law" in B11.a) and action ("go by this" in B11.a, "to go returning to one" in B5.c) separately. If qualification holding were taken directly as action, a probability resting for a long time would also be acting, and the question of action would not exist.

Action is not the outcome. If action were defined in advance as "the final outcome falls into some probability dimension", then any mechanism sending the outcome into that dimension would be defined as the action of this probability. Whether the outcome is necessary must be decided by the argument and cannot be written in advance into the definition of action.

Action is not the existence or read-out of the rate relation. The rate relation can exist while no one passes through it at this moment, and can be read out while the generation is borne elsewhere.

Action is not frequency. Empirical frequency is the statistics of many outcomes; a single action is not defined by it.

"Current" here means occurring at real time t; it does not include what occurred historically or what may occur in future, nor legal re-entry or replay realizations in qualification evaluation. "Legal current realization" here means one realization at moment t of the Law-bearing organization of 𝒫, belonging to the bearing registration of 𝒫 and lying within the range permitted by 𝔯\_P.

Therefore the question of Probability-Action cannot close at the layer of qualification, outcome, rate relation or frequency.

## Section Four　Line of Argument

What is known at present is only: 𝒫 has obtained probability qualification, the Continuation-Position and rate domain are fixed, and there is the inheritance above.

First suppose that once qualification holds, this probability is acting.

Construct a resting case. Keep Probability = 1 and all entry conditions unchanged, but at moment t there is no continuation event in current reality at C. Likewise: it acted historically but not at moment t; it can act in future but has not yet occurred; there is only structural capacity; there is only legal re-entry or replay in qualification evaluation, not an event at real time t.

These cases keep all entry conditions. If they also counted as 𝒫 acting at moment t, then as long as qualification holds action would hold, and action would not differ from qualification, violating the non-degeneracy condition on the task side. Look at the action corpus again: the action spoken of in B5.c is making it possible "to go returning to one" in every direction, and what B11.a speaks of is "automatically goes to some direction dimension …… and continues", "go by this"; the actions of probability in the corpus all fall on "going". At moment t, at C, there is no continuation at all, and such action has nowhere to fall. As for historical, future and replayed continuations, none lies within the declared situation ξ\_t at moment t. So qualification cannot automatically give action.

For 𝒫 to act at this moment, there must at least be one actual continuation at real time t, written e\_t.

Now there is one current continuation. Does the task close? No.

Construct an external-bypass case. The current actual continuation exists, and the outcome even falls into some dimension of 𝒫: Realize(e\_t, v\_i) = 1. But the whole current generation bypasses 𝒫: a mechanism outside 𝒫 sends the successor directly into the realization domain of v\_i. Likewise: another structure of the same type as 𝒫 elsewhere produces an outcome of the same type; one first sees the outcome fall into v\_i and then attributes the event to 𝒫 on that basis.

These cases all have current continuation, and the outcome can fall on a dimension of 𝒫. But the landing point of the outcome says only that the successor position agrees with the realization domain; a structure of the same type is not 𝒫; attribution after the fact infers the source backwards from the outcome. Current continuation alone cannot show that there is currently an occurrence of 𝒫 itself.

So, for this continuation to belong to 𝒫, there must be in the actual generation chain of e\_t a current generation sub-segment Z\_t actually borne by a legal current realization R\_t of 𝒫. This attribution does not require 𝒫 to monopolize the whole continuation, nor 𝒫 to prevail finally, nor 𝒫 to decide the final settling.

At this point the first item is forced out.

Write it provisionally as R\_1.

R\_1 answers: is there now really an occurrence, this time, of this probability?

It does not answer what 𝒫 did in this occurrence.

Now there is R\_1. Does the task close? No.

Construct a single-dimension solo case. Some dimension v\_i of 𝒫 is borne by a formal Law L\_i; at moment t, L\_i is currently really acting, and continuation occurs along v\_i; in the generation chain of e\_t there is also a segment borne by a legal current realization of 𝒫. But the relative-generation relation among the dimensions is not currently borne by 𝒫: going in the direction v\_i is decided alone by conditions outside the organization, and neither the other dimensions of 𝒫 nor the load-bearing state of 𝒫 as an organization takes part in this generation; at moment t, there is also no other current continuation in which 𝒫 bears the relative generation among the dimensions.

This case has R\_1, but one can only say that one member of 𝒫 is going. By the inherited duty semantics, the duty peculiar to probability is that the Law-bearing organization bears the relative generation among the dimensions; this duty is not carried out in this continuation. If this case also counted as probability acting, Probability-Action would degenerate into the action of one of its Laws, and the two could not be distinguished.

So there must also be a current duty condition peculiar to probability: in this continuation given by R\_1, 𝒫 really realizes its relative-generation duty as a probability. Since R\_1 requires only that such a continuation exist, the single-dimension solo case must hold for every continuation belonging to 𝒫 at moment t, which has been set out above.

At this point only the direction of the duty is obtained, written provisionally as the candidate of R\_2. What counts as the current realization of this duty is not yet fixed.

First ask: is the existence of the rate relation, or its readability, enough?

The following four cases — idle rate structure, read-only rate, illegal side branch, partial execution — all keep R\_1 and follow the setting of the single-dimension solo case: at moment t, in no other current occurrence belonging to 𝒫 does 𝒫 bear the relative generation among the dimensions. Otherwise action could fall on that occurrence, and these cases would not be cases where action fails.

Construct an idle-rate-structure case. The relative-generation relation Rel\_ρ of 𝒫 currently exists, the organizational structure is complete, and once passed through it would generate successors according to Rel\_ρ; but the present actual generation did not pass through it, occurring instead by other paths. Construct further a read-only-rate case. Rel\_ρ is currently read, recorded or displayed, but the present generation is borne by another mechanism. Likewise: there is only historical rate bearing; only rate-bearing capacity in a standby state; only bearing in non-current replay; only bearing in another event, another Continuation-Position or another rate domain.

These cases keep R\_1 and all "have a rate relation". But in this continuation 𝒫 has not realized the relative-generation duty: the rate structure was not passed through, read-out is not generation, and the rest are all not in this current continuation.

RateExists ⇏ ProbabilityAct

RateRead ⇏ ProbabilityAct

So R\_2 must at least reach: in this continuation, the legal current load-bearing state of 𝒫 really enters the formation of the relative-generation relation among the dimensions on that occasion.

Then does "really entering the formation" further mean sole bearing, irreplaceability, the outcome changing upon deletion?

Construct a hot-redundancy case. Here ProbabilityAct = 1 is taken as an object fact fixed independently in the case, not inferred from structures already obtained. In this action known to hold, 𝒫 has two legal realizations R\_a and R\_b, both bearing the same relative-generation duty at the same time; deleting either one, the other still suffices to maintain the relative-generation bearing on this occasion, and the final outcome can also be unchanged.

If sole bearing, main bearing, but-for necessity or the outcome changing upon deletion were written into R\_2, this action known to hold would be judged not to satisfy R\_2, contradicting the necessary direction. So real bearing is not counterfactual irreplaceability: R\_2 allows joint bearing, distributed bearing and hot-redundant bearing.

Since the bearer may be non-unique, one must ask: whose bearing counts? If another path in the same physical system, not belonging to 𝒫, forms the relative-generation relation, can it count as the bearing of 𝒫?

Construct an illegal-side-branch case. Another path in the same physical system forms the relative-generation relation in this continuation, but it does not belong to the legal realizations of 𝒫, is not within the range of duty permitted by 𝔯\_P, and does not belong to the Law-bearing organization of 𝒫.

Relative generation is indeed formed, and the place of formation is in the same physical system. But physical co-location is not attribution of duty: the bearer here is a structure outside 𝒫. This belongs to the same class as the case where an external selector decides the relative generation among the dimensions; the difference is only whether that structure lies inside or outside the same physical system, a difference that plays no role in judging action. So the bearing required by R\_2 must come from the legal duty realization of 𝒫 itself.

The bearer has been limited to legal realizations of 𝒫; one must still ask: to what degree must this realization go to count as bearing?

Construct a partial-execution case. Some members of 𝒫 are currently active: the Laws of some dimensions are acting, some preparatory steps or transmissions are under way. But these activities together are still not enough to make the current bearing of the relative-generation duty on this occasion hold.

The bearer is a legal realization of 𝒫, but the duty has not been realized currently: local activity is not the realization of the duty. So R\_2 must reach "the current bearing of the relative-generation duty on this occasion holds". At the same time this does not require all members to be active: if some legal redundant realization alone suffices to make the current bearing hold, other members resting does not hinder R\_2. "Realization" here means neither the completion of the final outcome nor the final settling.

The duty has been limited to 𝒫 itself currently really bearing relative generation. Finally one must ask: if the rate value formed by the bearing differs from the past, is the identity of the duty thereby broken?

Construct a legal-rate-change case. Here likewise ProbabilityAct = 1 is taken as an object fact fixed independently in the case. At an earlier moment 𝒫 formed the relative generation between v\_1 and v\_2 at 7 : 3; in this current action known to hold, owing to a change in sedimentation, 𝒫 forms the relative generation between the two at 1 : 9, this change being produced by the legal duty realization of 𝒫 itself within the range permitted by 𝔯\_P.

If Rel\_ρ(t\_1) = Rel\_ρ(t\_0) were required, this action known to hold would be judged not to satisfy the duty requirement, contradicting the necessary direction. The duty of probability is to bear the relative-generation relation among the dimensions, not to keep some fixed rate value.

DutyIdentity ≠ RateValueIdentity

At this point the second item is forced out.

Write it provisionally as R\_2.

R\_2 answers: in this occurrence, is this probability carrying out its peculiar duty as a probability? R\_2 holds on the same witness as R\_1 and presupposes R\_1.

Now there is R\_1, and on the same witness as R\_1 duty conformance has been forced out, together making R\_2. Does the task close?

First test a wrong unfolding. If what is obtained were written as two mutually independent existential formulas:

(∃w: CurrentProbabilityOccurrence(w)) ∧ (∃w′: ProbabilityDutyConformance(w′))

then a stitching case could be constructed: w is a current continuation at C at moment t, in whose generation chain a segment is borne by a legal current realization of 𝒫, but 𝒫 does not bear relative generation in it; w′ is a legal replay in qualification evaluation, or a continuation in an instance at another Continuation-Position or in another rate domain, in which 𝒫 completed the relative-generation duty; at moment t at C there is no other continuation in which 𝒫 completes this duty. Both items of the formula above are true, but at moment t at C there is no continuation in which 𝒫 acts as a probability.

So the structure obtained cannot be unfolded thus. Duty conformance must be evaluated on the one current occurrence witness already obtained in the first round; this is exactly the way of the second round of recursion: what it always asks is "in this current occurrence belonging to 𝒫, is 𝒫 acting as a probability". Same witness is therefore not a separately set third necessary relation, but the second round's discipline of inheriting the witness of the first round, expressed by the common existential quantifier.

Continue testing structures that might be added. The following items all arise from one and the same question: is there yet another duty that can be placed neither in R\_1 nor in R\_2?

Is final dimension realization necessary?

No.

Construct a rerouting-after-action case. Take ProbabilityAct = 1 as an object fact fixed independently in the case. Write e\_t^P for the current action event in which this Probability-Action lies, and e\_t^final for the final outcome of the whole continuation including subsequent mechanisms. After the actual action sub-segment of e\_t^P is complete, a legal subsequent mechanism X not belonging to this action joins in and changes the final settling, so that e\_t^final falls into no dimension in V\_P; this change neither deletes nor rewrites the action that already held earlier. Thus ProbabilityAct = 1 while ¬∃v\_i ∈ V\_P: Realize(e\_t^final, v\_i) = 1. That action has occurred is not final prevailing. Conversely, the external-bypass case has already shown the outcome falling into v\_i while 𝒫 does not act. So final dimension realization is neither necessary nor sufficient. This agrees with the discipline of mutually exclusive successors Σ\_i Realize(e, v\_i) ≤ 1.

Are change of outcome, sole decision and but-for necessity necessary?

No.

The hot-redundancy case and the rerouting-after-action case show respectively: the outcome unchanged after one realization is deleted, the outcome rerouted after action — and action has held in both. They belong to outcome or causal strength.

Are the degree and representation of the rate necessary?

No.

Positive rate, every dimension currently having positive flow: qualification does not require a positive rate, and action requires only that 𝒫 bear the relative generation on this occasion, not that every dimension have positive flow at this moment; this belongs to degree. A readable numerical rate: the read-only-rate case has shown that read-out is not bearing; this belongs to representation. Constant rate value: excluded by the legal-rate-change case; this belongs to dynamics. Unequal rates of the dimensions: belongs to degree.

Are ontological randomness, full coverage and stable frequency necessary?

No.

Ontological randomness answers whether a single execution can be predicted; what action must judge is by whom the relative generation on this occasion is borne; the two are not the same question. Σ\_i Realize ≤ 1 already allows outcomes to fall outside the dimensions; full coverage belongs to the discipline of the range of coverage. Stable empirical frequency is the statistics of many outcomes; a single action does not depend on it.

Are recognition, source-drive driving, triggering by environmental disturbance, passing through the Law Ecology and breaking the holding of the Law Ecology necessary?

The existing argument does not support it.

Whether it is recognized does not change whether it is bearing relative generation at this moment; this belongs to representation. The last four come from specific cases or specific subclasses and belong to generation processes, triggering antecedents and relations. Whether they can rise from the corpus to general duties requires a further review of the frozen action corpus.

The direct action corpus is B5.c and B11.a; the range of cross-sentence review is A1–A3, B1–B11, C1.

Split one by one. In B5.c, the current "going" corresponds to R\_1; "in every direction" is the duty background of R\_2, that is, what probability faces is relative generation among several directions; "to go returning to one", "to be able to go as one" are the mode and consequence of execution; "while passing through many Law Ecologies" is relation and dynamics; "an important function …… in lower organisms" is function and example. In B11.a, "some environmental disturbance appears" is the triggering antecedent of this continuation, entering the R\_2 of a probability only when the specific duty of that probability needs it; "through reward and punishment received" is the generation process; "having become a probability Law" is qualification, already fixed by the entry condition; "automatically …… and continues" corresponds to R\_1; "goes to some direction dimension" corresponds to R\_2; "lower organisms go by this" is function and example.

Conjunctions within one sentence. Each of the two sentences writes the members above in one sentence, but neither authorizes the combination of these members to bear a new action duty; in B11.a, "If some environmental disturbance appears" is the antecedent of this continuation, and the sentence does not prescribe the appearance of environmental disturbance as a necessary condition of every Probability-Action.

## Section Five　Formal Naming of the Necessary Structure

Looking back on the argument, the two duties are each forced out by cases in which the earlier items are kept while the task still fails.

The first item:

the duty of current occurrence CurrentProbabilityOccurrence, written R\_1.

There is currently in fact one actual continuation e\_t, and in the actual generation chain of e\_t there is a current generation sub-segment Z\_t actually borne by a legal current realization R\_t of 𝒫. Monopolizing the whole continuation is not required, nor deciding the final settling.

The second item:

the duty of duty conformance ProbabilityDutyConformance, written R\_2.

In the same continuation e\_t as R\_1, R\_t, according to the duty semantics of probability qualification, really realizes the organization-level relative-generation duty peculiar to probability; that is, 𝒫 as a Law-bearing organization, with a legal current load-bearing state, actually enters the formation of the relative-generation relation among the probability dimensions on that occasion, and makes the current bearing on that occasion hold. It allows redundant bearing, joint bearing, distributed bearing, members not all active at once, and legal variation of the rate value; it excludes a single-dimension Law going solo, the rate structure merely existing, the rate relation merely being read out, only historical, standby or non-current bearing, an external selector, an illegal side branch in the same system, and partial execution.

Notation at the derivation layer:

R\_1(w) := CurrentProbabilityOccurrence(w)

R\_2(w) := R\_1(w) ∧ ProbabilityDutyConformance(w)

where w = (e\_t, R\_t, Z\_t). R\_2 is a tightening of duty on the same witness already obtained by R\_1, so R\_2 ⇒ R\_1.

Hence the necessary structure of Probability-Action is:

N\_PAct = ∃w \[ CurrentProbabilityOccurrence(w) ∧ ProbabilityDutyConformance(w) \]

The two items are not obtained side by side but progressively: the second item takes the current occurrence witness already obtained by the first item as its domain of load-bearing and continues to tighten on the same witness. The type of dependence is linear progression: R\_1 ≺ R\_2. Same witness is not a third necessary relation but the second round's discipline of inheriting the witness of the first round. The two items are coordinates of duty, not a pipeline in time, and they promise no two independent structural entities.

## Section Six　Review of Necessity and Non-Redundancy

Remove R\_1.

R\_2 presupposes R\_1; deleting R\_1 must delete with it the part of R\_2 that presupposes it. If only "𝒫 bears relative generation" is kept without requiring that there currently in fact be one actual continuation belonging to 𝒫, then having only qualification without a current event, historical bearing and bearing in non-current replay would all be judged as acting at this moment. So R\_1 is indispensable.

Remove R\_2.

If only "there is currently one continuation belonging to 𝒫" is kept, then the single-dimension solo, idle rate structure, read-only rate, illegal side branch and partial execution would all be judged as probability acting. So R\_2 is indispensable.

Weakening and strengthening.

Weakening R\_1 into "the outcome falls into some dimension", the external-bypass case makes it fail; strengthening it into "𝒫 monopolizes the generation of the whole continuation" or "𝒫 decides the final settling", the rerouting-after-action case shows it too strong.

Weakening R\_2 into "the rate relation exists", "the rate relation is readable" or "some members of 𝒫 are active", the idle-rate-structure, read-only-rate and partial-execution cases respectively make it fail; strengthening it into but-for necessity, sole bearing or constant rate value, the hot-redundancy case and the legal-rate-change case respectively show it too strong.

Can R\_1 and R\_2 be merged?

R\_2 cannot be merged into R\_1. The single-dimension solo case shows R\_1 ⇏ R\_2: there is currently in fact an occurrence of 𝒫, but 𝒫 does not bear the relative generation on this occasion.

In the reverse direction it must be acknowledged explicitly that R\_2 ⇒ R\_1: R\_2 is defined from the start on the same witness as R\_1. This is linear progressive dependence R\_1 ≺ R\_2, of the same type as in The General Form of Principle, where what the second round obtains presupposes what the first round obtains; there is no test that deletes R\_1 while independently keeping the current R\_2. The two must still be listed separately: R\_1 is forced out by the resting and external-bypass cases and answers "is this occasion an occurrence of this probability"; R\_2 is forced out by the single-dimension solo case and the tightenings after it, and answers "in this occurrence, is it acting as a probability". Deleting R\_1 together with the part of R\_2 that presupposes it, the class of resting and replay cases comes back in; deleting R\_2, the class of single-dimension solo cases comes back in.

Do the two duties require two modules?

No. Here it is claimed only that the two duties must be borne and witnessed by one and the same action, not that two structural entities exist separately.

## Section Nine　Breach Argument

Each of the following must keep the working domain, the entry conditions and the non-degeneracy condition on the task side.

Breach case lacking current occurrence: ProbabilityAct = 1, while at moment t at C there is no actual continuation, or in the generation chain of every current continuation there is no segment borne by a legal current realization of 𝒫. If such a case holds, the necessity of R\_1 is breached.

Breach case of common witness: ProbabilityAct = 1, while there is no set of witnesses satisfying both items at once; the two items can hold only on different witnesses, for example current occurrence on one continuation and duty conformance on a replay or on an instance at another Continuation-Position or in another rate domain. Breach case of a third duty: in the frozen action corpus a specific third independent necessary duty R\_3 is found such that ProbabilityAct = 1 ⇒ R\_3 = 1, while R\_3 cannot be reduced to R\_1, R\_2, items of the working domain, upstream inherited items or downstream tasks. Breach by merging: proving that R\_2 can be wholly absorbed by R\_1, or that, after deleting R\_1 and the part of R\_2 that presupposes it, the original task still closes.
