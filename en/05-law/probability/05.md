# The Mathematical Form of Probability-Action

## Section One　Purpose

To establish a formal system for the necessary structure of Probability-Action, such that the current action structure of any already holding Probability at moment t can be formally evaluated, yielding a definite reading: holds (1), fails (0), or insufficient material (⊥). Of these, a 0 obtained at the second layer (duty conformance) carries, in the strength of its structural-layer negation, the reverse-representation gap H\_PAct-Rep (7.2, 8.3).

The structure formalized comes from The General Form of Probability-Action v1.0:

ProbabilityAct\_F,t(𝒫; C, 𝔯\_P, ξ\_t) ⇒ N\_PAct

N\_PAct = ∃(e\_t, R\_t, Z\_t) \[ CurrentProbabilityOccurrence ∧ ProbabilityDutyConformance \]

where the two items are in linear progression R\_1 ≺ R\_2, R\_2 ⇒ R\_1, and are witnessed together by one and the same set of witnesses (e\_t, R\_t, Z\_t).

The formal objective is:

S\_PAct ↦ M\_PAct

that is: to give formal criteria of current occurrence and current duty conformance, evaluated on one and the same witness, keeping the progressive relation; and to give the enumeration of candidate witnesses, the coverage conditions needed for negative read-outs, the solving procedure, and the three-valued read-back rules.

## Section Two　Core Thesis

Given an already holding Probability 𝒫 with its Continuation-Position C, frozen rate domain 𝔯\_P and declared situation ξ\_t, how is the formal truth of "there exists a set of witnesses on which current occurrence and current duty conformance both hold" to be obtained from real event records, registrations of current realizations, actual generation source chains and upstream rate-bearing certificates, while strictly distinguishing structural failure 0 from insufficient formal material ⊥?

Four points must be expressed exactly:

(1) Current occurrence: in an actual continuation at C at real time t, a generation sub-segment is actually borne by a legal current realization of 𝒫;

(2) Current duty conformance: on the same witness, the realization of 𝒫, with its current load-bearing state, enters the formation of the relative-generation relation among the probability dimensions on that occasion, and makes the current bearing hold;

(3) Progression and common witness: the second item is evaluated on the same witness as the first item and may not be split into two independent existential formulas;

(4) Currentization: an upstream rate-bearing witness may come from current running, legal re-entry or replay; current action can use only witnesses of this one evaluation in current reality.

The following groups of objects must be distinguished formally:

registration of an organizational realization ≠ current occurrence

current occurrence ≠ current duty conformance

single-evaluation rate bearing RateBearWitness^M(ν\_t^cur) ≠ rate-domain aggregation RateBearWitness^M\_{𝔯\_P}

rate bearing in replay ≠ action in current reality

final landing in a dimension ≠ action

3.1 Solving instance and declared situation

Definition 3.1 \[S\]

χ\_PAct = (F, t, 𝒫, C, 𝔯\_P, ξ\_t, V\_P, 𝓛\_P)

Entry condition: Probability\_F,t(𝒫; C, 𝔯\_P) = 1. V\_P, 𝓛\_P, C, 𝔯\_P are taken from the already holding structure of probability qualification, given before solving, and not added to or deleted from afterwards. Probability qualification is not solved anew here.

ξ\_t is the current declared situation at moment t, including the current field state, the current input, and the context needed for the present declaration of action. Whether an actual continuation event exists in current reality is not presupposed by ξ\_t.

### 3.2 Events in current reality

Definition 3.2 \[S\]

CurrentEvent^M(e; t, C, ξ\_t) = 1 if and only if e is, in the event record of the operating substrate, a continuation event actually occurring at the Continuation-Position C at real time t, and belongs to the situation declared by ξ\_t.

When the event is registered in the event record as a re-entry or replay realization in qualification evaluation (those in ℰ\_r of Definition 3.8 of The Mathematical Form of Probability that are not current running), take 0. When the event record cannot be obtained, take ⊥.

3.3 The current organizational realization package

Definition 3.3 \[Reg/Aux\]

R\_t of the General Form is written at the formal layer as the current organizational realization package:

Π\_t^𝒫 = (𝒫, J\_t, {(W\_i, r\_i)}\_{i ∈ J\_t}, C, 𝔯\_P, υ\_t)

where W\_i ∈ 𝓛\_P, r\_i is one legal realization, in the current running instance, of the Law-bearing duty corresponding to W\_i, and υ\_t is the identifier of the current running instance.

J\_t is not required to cover all probability dimensions, nor to have at least two members currently active; \|J\_t\| = 1 is a legal case.

LegalPReal^M(Π\_t^𝒫) = 1 if and only if: the organizational identity and registration of 𝒫 are verifiable; every member in J\_t satisfies MemberReal^M = 1 (Definition 3.4); all members belong to one and the same current running instance υ\_t, under one and the same C and 𝔯\_P.

LegalPReal^M verifies only organizational identity, member registration, current instantiation and one and the same declared environment; it does not verify that the duty has been realized:

LegalPReal^M = 1 ⇏ CurrentProbabilityOccurrence^M = 1

LegalPReal^M = 1 ⇏ ProbabilityDutyConformance^M = 1

Correspondence between structural witnesses and formal witnesses: the structural witness of the General Form is written w^S = (e\_t, R\_t, Z\_t). Define its set of legal formal registrations

𝔉(w^S) := { (e\_t, Π\_t^𝒫, Z\_t) \| Π\_t^𝒫 is a legal formal registration of R\_t }

where "Π\_t^𝒫 is a legal formal registration of R\_t" means: LegalPReal^M(Π\_t^𝒫) = 1, and the member realizations r\_i registered by Π\_t^𝒫 are all component realizations of R\_t. W^S denotes the set of structural witnesses (e\_t, R\_t, Z\_t), and W^M the set of formal witnesses (e, Π, Z).

Conversely, for a formal witness w^M = (e\_t, Π, Z\_t), write R(Π) for the current realization constituted by the member realizations registered by Π, and write w^S(w^M) := (e\_t, R(Π), Z\_t); then w^M ∈ 𝔉(w^S(w^M)).

Any w^M ∈ 𝔉(w^S) is called a corresponding formal witness of w^S. 𝔉 is a set-valued formal correspondence and adds no ontological relation; no single-valuedness, injectivity, surjectivity or uniqueness is declared. One and the same R\_t can have several legal formal registrations, for example when the member realizations are registered at different granularities, or when different legal realizations are registered in hot redundancy. Below, w at the formal layer always means a formal witness w^M.

### 3.4 Attribution of member realizations

Definition 3.4 \[Aux\]

MemberReal^M(r\_i, Π\_t^𝒫; W\_i) = 1 if and only if W\_i ∈ 𝓛\_P, r\_i is a legal realization of W\_i in the instance of current reality (by the criterion of duty realization RoleReal of The Mathematical Form of Law), and is registered in Π\_t^𝒫 under one and the same (𝒫, C, 𝔯\_P, υ\_t). When the realization criterion is undecidable, take ⊥.

It denotes only the attribution of the realization; it does not denote that the realization is already active, nor that it bears the rate duty.

### 3.5 Actual generation sub-segment

Definition 3.5 \[S\]

ActualGenSeg^M(Z; e) = 1 if and only if Z is a sub-segment of the actual generation chain of the event e, read from the generation source chain of the operating substrate. When the source chain cannot be obtained, take ⊥.

3.6 Actual bearing of a generation sub-segment by the realization package

Definition 3.6 \[Aux\]

PRealSegBear^M(Π\_t^𝒫, Z; e) = 1 if and only if there exists i ∈ J\_t such that MemberReal^M(r\_i, Π\_t^𝒫; W\_i) = 1, and r\_i really bears Z in the actual generation source chain of e.

The same host, shared nodes, the same variable name, the same value, agreement of outcomes or statistical correlation may not stand in for the actual generation source chain. This discipline agrees with the requirement of The Mathematical Form of Probability on GenIn. When the source chain cannot be obtained, take ⊥.

### 3.7 Current occurrence

Definition 3.7 \[S\]

For a witness w = (e, Π, Z):

CurrentProbabilityOccurrence^M(w) = CurrentEvent^M(e; t, C, ξ\_t) ∧₃ LegalPReal^M(Π) ∧₃ ActualGenSeg^M(Z; e) ∧₃ PRealSegBear^M(Π, Z; e)

It does not require 𝒫 to monopolize the whole generation of e, nor 𝒫 to decide the final settling.

3.8 The current running evaluation in reality

Definition 3.8 \[Aux\]

ν\_t^cur is the one legal evaluation corresponding to the current running in reality at moment t: it is a member of Eval(𝔯\_P) of Definition 3.15 of The Mathematical Form of Probability, whose in-evaluation realization is running at real time t, not re-entry or replay. The operating substrate must be able to identify which evaluation is ν\_t^cur; when it cannot, the items depending on it take ⊥. If it has been ascertained that the configuration of the current running in reality does not belong to Eval(𝔯\_P), then this running is not within the rate domain of 𝒫, C5 fails, and the related certificate items take 0, not ⊥.

### 3.9 Upstream rate-bearing certificate

Definition 3.9 \[Aux\]

First define the realization source of a generation contribution:

SourceReal^M(c; a, r\_i, W\_i, ν) = 1 if and only if c is the actual generation contribution of the generation factor a in evaluation ν, and can be traced by the real generation source chain to the realization r\_i of W\_i. When the source chain cannot be obtained, take ⊥.

For RateBearWitness^M(ν) = 1 of Definition 3.28 of The Mathematical Form of Probability, its existential witness is unfolded explicitly into a certificate:

κ\_ρ = (B, a, W\_i, r\_i, c\_i, Z\_i^ρ, e\_ν, ν)

Cert\_ρ^M(κ\_ρ; ν) = 1 if and only if the following items hold together on one and the same certificate:

(a) B ∈ MinSuff\_ρ(ν);

(b) W\_i ∈ 𝓛\_P, a ∈ B ∩ GenIn(W\_i, ν);

(c) SourceReal^M(c\_i; a, r\_i, W\_i, ν) = 1, that is, the contribution c\_i in the certificate is exactly the actual contribution of the generation factor a in the intersection, coming from the realization r\_i;

(d) EvalRateBind^M(W\_i, ν, e\_ν) = 1, and the binding key obtaining this reading is exactly β\_ρ = (W\_i, c\_i, Z\_i^ρ, e\_ν, C, 𝔯\_P, ν).

(a)–(d) must be satisfied by one and the same set (B, a, W\_i, r\_i, c\_i, Z\_i^ρ, e\_ν, ν): the generation factor proving rate bearing, the contribution and realization proving realization attribution, and the sub-segment and event of in-evaluation binding may not, inside the certificate, be taken from different sources. If any item fails after complete evaluation, take 0; if undecidable, take ⊥.

Cert\_ρ^M is only the unfolding of the existential witness of upstream Definition 3.28, not a new rate-bearing predicate. Upstream allows MinSuff\_ρ to have several elements (hot redundancy), so here likewise neither sole bearing nor but-for necessity is required.

3.10 Placing a certificate onto the current common witness

Definition 3.10 \[Aux\]

For a certificate κ\_ρ and a witness w = (e, Π, Z):

CurrentRateRealize^M(κ\_ρ; w) = 1 if and only if the following five items hold together:

C1 Current evaluation in reality: ν = ν\_t^cur;

C2 Same event: e\_ν = e;

C3 Realization attribution: the realization r\_i in the certificate belongs to Π, that is, i ∈ J\_t and MemberReal^M(r\_i, Π; W\_i) = 1;

C4 Same sub-segment: Z\_i^ρ = Z;

C5 Same Continuation-Position and rate domain: the Continuation-Position of ν is C, and the rate domain is 𝔯\_P.

C2, C4, C5 are identities; C3 is realization attribution, the r\_i used being exactly the one locked in item (c) of the certificate; C1 is the identity of the current evaluation in reality. If any item fails after complete evaluation, take 0; if undecidable, take ⊥.

### 3.11 Current duty conformance

Definition 3.11 \[S\]

ProbabilityDutyConformance^M(w) = ∃₃κ\_ρ \[ Cert\_ρ^M(κ\_ρ; ν\_t^cur) ∧₃ CurrentRateRealize^M(κ\_ρ; w) \]

It is evaluated only on witnesses with CurrentProbabilityOccurrence^M(w) = 1; on other witnesses it is undefined. It invokes only certificates on the single evaluation ν\_t^cur, not the rate-domain aggregation RateBearWitness^M\_{𝔯\_P}. It corresponds one to one with the ProbabilityDutyConformance of the same name on the structural side, that is, the duty content added in the second round, not the whole R\_2 layer.

### 3.12 The R\_2 layer

Definition 3.12 \[S\]

R\_2^M(w) = CurrentProbabilityOccurrence^M(w) ∧₃ ProbabilityDutyConformance^M(w)

Hence R\_2^M(w) = 1 ⇒ CurrentProbabilityOccurrence^M(w) = 1. This is the representation at the formal layer of R\_1 ≺ R\_2 of the General Form. The formal layer sets up no separate relation of common witness; common witness is expressed by one and the same w.

3.13 Layered enumeration of candidate witnesses

Definition 3.13 \[Aux\]

Ê\_t(C, ξ\_t): the candidate set of events in current reality;

R̂\_𝒫(e): the candidate set of current organizational realization packages of 𝒫 in the event e;

Ẑ\_𝒫(e, Π): the candidate set of actually borne generation sub-segments under the event e and the realization package Π;

Ŵ\_PAct = { (e, Π, Z) \| e ∈ Ê\_t, Π ∈ R̂\_𝒫(e), Z ∈ Ẑ\_𝒫(e, Π) }.

The hatted sets are only enumeration domains and are not by nature equal to all legal witnesses.

3.14 Coverage certificates for negative read-outs

Definition 3.14 \[Aux\]

EventCover^M = 1: Ê\_t(C, ξ\_t) already contains all events in current reality at C at moment t.

PRealCover^M(e) = 1: R̂\_𝒫(e) already contains all legal current organizational realization packages of 𝒫 in the event e.

SegCover^M(e, Π) = 1: Ẑ\_𝒫(e, Π) already contains all generation sub-segments actually borne by Π in e.

RateCertCover^M(w) = 1: for a witness w already satisfying CurrentProbabilityOccurrence^M(w) = 1, all rate-bearing certificates on ν\_t^cur related to w have been enumerated.

Coverage certificates serve only the completeness of negative read-outs and are not ontological conditions. The layers are used in progressive order, with no unified gate requiring all layers to be complete in advance:

if EventCover^M = 1 and Ê\_t = ∅, 0 may be signed directly, without needing the rate-bearing certificate interface;

if, for some event, PRealCover^M and every SegCover^M are 1, and no witness among them makes CurrentProbabilityOccurrence^M = 1, that event ends at the first layer;

only witnesses with CurrentProbabilityOccurrence^M(w) = 1 enter the second layer, and RateCertCover^M(w) = 1 is required before duty failure can be signed for it.

When a coverage certificate is undecidable or 0, the corresponding negative read-out can only be recorded ⊥.

Only when preparing to sign 0 is the certificate of negative read-out completeness defined:

NegComplete^M\_PAct = 1 if and only if: EventCover^M = 1; for every event e in Ê\_t, PRealCover^M(e) = 1; for every relevant (e, Π), SegCover^M(e, Π) = 1; for every witness w with Q\_1(w) = 1, RateCertCover^M(w) = 1.

It is not a unified gate before solving: once a positive reading appears, 1 can be signed without it.

### 3.15 Witness-by-witness readings

Definition 3.15 \[Aux\]

Q\_1(w) = CurrentProbabilityOccurrence^M(w)

Q\_PAct(w) =  
&nbsp;&nbsp;0, Q\_1(w) = 0;  
&nbsp;&nbsp;⊥, Q\_1(w) = ⊥;  
&nbsp;&nbsp;ProbabilityDutyConformance^M(w), Q\_1(w) = 1.

This piecewise writing expresses progression: the second layer is evaluated only on witnesses where the first layer is 1. Of these, ProbabilityDutyConformance^M(w) = 0 requires RateCertCover^M(w) = 1 as a premise; otherwise ⊥ is recorded.

### 3.16 Formal total reading

Definition 3.16 \[S\]

Read\_{N\_PAct}^M =  
&nbsp;&nbsp;1, there exists w ∈ Ŵ\_PAct with Q\_PAct(w) = 1;  
&nbsp;&nbsp;0, there is no such w, and NegComplete^M\_PAct = 1, and Q\_PAct(w) = 0 for all w ∈ Ŵ\_PAct;  
&nbsp;&nbsp;⊥, otherwise.

This piecewise writing writes the completeness of negative read-outs into the reading itself: when the candidate domain is empty and EventCover^M has not been proven to be 1, the reading is ⊥, not 0.

3.17 Declaration of the properties of relations

MemberReal^M: a heterogeneous relation of attribution between realizations and organizational realization packages; no reflexivity, symmetry or transitivity is declared; it is neither a partial order nor an equivalence relation.

PRealSegBear^M, CurrentRateRealize^M, SourceReal^M: heterogeneous three-valued predicates; no reflexivity, symmetry or transitivity is declared.

Event identity e\_ν = e, sub-segment identity Z\_i^ρ = Z: identities, treated as equivalence relations (reflexive, symmetric, transitive).

Identity of Continuation-Positions and identity of rate-domain protocols: treated as equivalence relations.

𝔉: a set-valued correspondence from structural witnesses to the sets of their legal formal witnesses, 𝔉: W^S → Pow(W^M), where Pow(W^M) is the power set of the set W^M of formal witnesses (𝒫 is not used to denote the power set, so as not to confuse it with the candidate Probability organization 𝒫). Single-valuedness is not required; no injectivity, surjectivity or uniqueness is declared; no reflexivity, symmetry or transitivity is declared; it is neither a partial order nor an equivalence relation. Whether at least one formal registration exists for a legal structural witness, that is, 𝔉(w^S) ≠ ∅, is not guaranteed by the notation 𝔉 itself: for the first layer it is obtained by Theorem 7.2 under PA-M1–PA-M5; for the second layer, H\_PAct-Rep bears the load.

R(Π) and w^S(w^M): constituted directly by the member realizations registered by Π, single-valued for a given Π; no injectivity is declared (different formal registrations can correspond to the same structural realization). It is only the reverse notation of 𝔉 and adds no ontological relation.

Candidate sets, certificate packages and coverage certificates are set-valued or predicates, not binary relations.

## Section Four　Equations and Static Relations

### 4.1 Static relations

This is not a dynamics problem, and no differential equation is set. There are only three load-bearing relations:

(PA-E1) Progression

R\_2^M(w) = CurrentProbabilityOccurrence^M(w) ∧₃ ProbabilityDutyConformance^M(w)

(PA-E2) Witness-by-witness reading

Q\_PAct(w) takes values piecewise by Definition 3.15.

(PA-E3) Reading of the common witness

Read\_{N\_PAct}^M takes values piecewise by Definition 3.16.

The following may not be written as criteria:

(∃w: CurrentProbabilityOccurrence^M(w)) ∧₃ (∃w′: ProbabilityDutyConformance^M(w′))

RateBearWitness^M\_{𝔯\_P} = 1 as a condition of a single action

∃v\_i ∈ V\_P: Realize(e\_final, v\_i) = 1 as a condition of action

### 4.2 Formal properties

Proposition 4.1　R\_2^M(w) = 1 ⇒ CurrentProbabilityOccurrence^M(w) = 1; and ProbabilityDutyConformance^M is evaluated only on witnesses with CurrentProbabilityOccurrence^M(w) = 1.

Proof: by Definition 3.12, R\_2^M(w) is the three-valued conjunction of CurrentProbabilityOccurrence^M(w) and ProbabilityDutyConformance^M(w); the conjunction is 1 if and only if both items are 1. The second sentence follows from the limitation of the domain of evaluation in Definition 3.11. □

Proposition 4.2　A replay witness cannot obtain Read\_{N\_PAct}^M = 1.

Proof: if the event of w is a re-entry or replay realization in qualification evaluation, then by Definition 3.2 CurrentEvent^M = 0, so Q\_1(w) = 0 and Q\_PAct(w) = 0. If the event of w is an event in current reality while the evaluation ν of the certificate κ\_ρ is a replay evaluation, then C1 fails and CurrentRateRealize^M = 0. In neither case can Q\_PAct(w) = 1. □

Proposition 4.3　A single-dimension solo can obtain Q\_1(w) = 1 while Q\_PAct(w) = 0.

Proof: take a realization package Π with \|J\_t\| = 1 whose only member r\_i actually bears the sub-segment Z in an event e in current reality; then Q\_1(w) = 1. If all certificates on ν\_t^cur related to w have been enumerated (RateCertCover^M(w) = 1), and no certificate satisfies CurrentRateRealize^M(κ\_ρ; w) = 1 — the relative generation among the dimensions being decided by conditions outside the organization, GenIn containing no member of Π — then ProbabilityDutyConformance^M(w) = 0 and Q\_PAct(w) = 0. □

Proposition 4.4　Sub-segment identity C4 is not too strong.

Proof: Read\_{N\_PAct}^M takes the existential quantifier over w = (e, Π, Z). If the in-evaluation generation sub-segment Z\_i^ρ of some certificate κ\_ρ on ν\_t^cur satisfies C1–C3 and C5, and Z\_i^ρ itself is an actual generation sub-segment of e actually borne by a member of Π, then take Z := Z\_i^ρ; w = (e, Π, Z\_i^ρ) satisfies both Q\_1(w) = 1 and C4. So C4 does not force the rate-bearing sub-segment to equal a Z fixed in advance, but allows the witness sub-segment to be chosen according to the certificate; no relation such as Z\_i^ρ ⊆ Z need be set up. □

Proposition 4.5　Hot redundancy does not make the reading 0.

Proof: if MinSuff\_ρ(ν\_t^cur) = {{A}, {B}}, A and B both belonging in GenIn to members of Π, and the certificate of at least one of them satisfies CurrentRateRealize^M, then ProbabilityDutyConformance^M(w) = 1. Definitions 3.9 and 3.11 require neither a unique minimal sufficient group nor that the reading change upon deleting some member. □

Proposition 4.6　Read\_{N\_PAct}^M = 0 ⇒ NegComplete^M\_PAct = 1.

Proof: follows directly from the second case of Definition 3.16. When NegComplete^M\_PAct has not been proven to be 1, the reading can only be 1 or ⊥. □

## Section Five　Running and Solving

### 5.1 Order of solving

1\. Check the registration χ\_PAct and the entry condition (PA-M1).

2\. Read the events in current reality at C at moment t, form Ê\_t, and judge EventCover^M (PA-M2). If EventCover^M = 1 and Ê\_t = ∅, the reading is 0; end.

3\. For every e ∈ Ê\_t, read the current organizational realization packages of 𝒫, form R̂\_𝒫(e), and judge LegalPReal^M, MemberReal^M and PRealCover^M(e) (PA-M3, PA-M4).

4\. For every (e, Π), read the actually borne sub-segments from the generation source chain, form Ẑ\_𝒫(e, Π), and judge ActualGenSeg^M, PRealSegBear^M and SegCover^M(e, Π) (PA-M5).

5\. For every candidate witness w, obtain Q\_1(w).

6\. Identify ν\_t^cur (PA-M6).

7\. For every witness with Q\_1(w) = 1, read the upstream certificates κ\_ρ on ν\_t^cur (invoking the solving of Definitions 3.26–3.28 of The Mathematical Form of Probability, including its M9–M14), and judge RateCertCover^M(w) (PA-M7).

8\. For every certificate judge C1–C5, obtaining CurrentRateRealize^M and ProbabilityDutyConformance^M(w) (PA-M8).

9\. Obtain Q\_PAct(w).

10\. Compose Read\_{N\_PAct}^M by Definition 3.16; before signing 0, obtain NegComplete^M\_PAct.

As soon as any candidate witness obtains Q\_PAct(w) = 1, 1 may be signed and further enumeration may stop.

5.2 Three values and the guard on negative read-outs

1: there exists a common positive witness. One positive witness suffices to sign 1.

0: the candidate domain has been proven complete by coverage certificates, and there is no positive witness in it.

⊥: insufficient material, including event records, realization registrations, source chains, the identification of the current evaluation, upstream certificates or coverage certificates being unobtainable.

Every 0 must carry the coverage grounds of the corresponding layer; lacking evidence, 0 is not written.

### 5.3 Solvability conditions

The following are conditions of formal representation and solving, written PA-M1–PA-M9 to distinguish them from the solvability conditions M1–M16 of The Mathematical Form of Probability (when M9–M14 among them are cited below, they are always prefixed with "upstream" or "its"). They are not new necessary structures of Probability-Action, and not H. If any condition is missing, only the items depending on it take ⊥; N\_PAct or ProbabilityAct is not automatically judged 0.

PA-M1 The solving instance is identifiable: every item of χ\_PAct can be effectively referred to, and the entry condition has been given by upstream.

PA-M2 Events in current reality are readable: the operating substrate can give the event record at C at moment t and can distinguish current running in reality from re-entry and replay.

PA-M3 The current organizational realization package is readable: the organizational identity of 𝒫, member registrations and the identifier υ\_t of the current running instance can be obtained.

PA-M4 Member realizations are decidable: the duty-realization criterion of each r\_i (RoleReal of The Mathematical Form of Law) is decidable.

PA-M5 The generation source chain is traceable: the actual generation chain of an event and the bearing of sub-segments by each member realization can be obtained from the real source chain.

PA-M6 The current evaluation is identifiable: the operating substrate can identify which evaluation is ν\_t^cur.

PA-M7 Upstream certificates are obtainable: MinSuff\_ρ, GenIn and EvalRateBind of The Mathematical Form of Probability on ν\_t^cur can be obtained, its solvability conditions M9–M14 being satisfied; the realization source SourceReal^M of generation contributions is traceable; and the coverage of the related certificates can be given.

PA-M8 Identities are decidable: the identity of events, of sub-segments, of Continuation-Positions and of rate-domain protocols can be determined.

PA-M9 Coverage is decidable: EventCover, PRealCover, SegCover and RateCertCover can be determined.

### 5.4 Solution

Read\_{N\_PAct}^M = 1 is a formal solution, denoting that there exists a set of common witnesses on which current occurrence and current duty conformance both hold. Read\_{N\_PAct}^M = 0 denotes that under complete coverage there is no such formal witness; the strength of its structural-layer negation is distinguished in Section Seven: unconditional at the first layer, carrying H\_PAct-Rep at the second. Read\_{N\_PAct}^M = ⊥ denotes insufficient material, and may not be read as the failure of N\_PAct, still less as ProbabilityAct not holding.

### 7.1 Positive read-back

(7.1) w^M ∈ 𝔉(w^S) ∧ CurrentProbabilityOccurrence^M(w^M) = 1 ⇒ CurrentProbabilityOccurrence(w^S)

By Definitions 3.2–3.7: CurrentEvent^M gives the actual continuation in current reality, ActualGenSeg^M gives the actual generation sub-segment, and PRealSegBear^M gives, through the real source chain, that this sub-segment is actually borne by a legal current realization of 𝒫. This is a direct representation of structural components, and no separate read-back bridge is set up. Of these, the Law qualification of each W\_i inherits H\_LawRead(W\_i) according to its source (see 8.3).

Theorem 7.1　Read-back of current duty conformance

Suppose PA-M5–PA-M8 hold, and the upstream certificate κ\_ρ satisfies the conditions of application of Theorem 7.2 of The Mathematical Form of Probability (its M9–M14). If, on a formal witness w^M = (e\_t, Π\_t^𝒫, Z\_t) with CurrentProbabilityOccurrence^M(w^M) = 1, ProbabilityDutyConformance^M(w^M) = 1, then on the structural witness w^S(w^M) = (e\_t, R(Π\_t^𝒫), Z\_t), the legal current realization of 𝒫, with its current load-bearing state, actually enters the formation of the relative-generation relation among the probability dimensions on that occasion and makes the current bearing hold, that is, the ProbabilityDutyConformance(w^S(w^M)) of the General Form holds.

Proof: by Definition 3.11, there exists a certificate κ\_ρ such that Cert\_ρ^M(κ\_ρ; ν\_t^cur) = 1 and CurrentRateRealize^M(κ\_ρ; w) = 1.

Step one: by Cert\_ρ^M and Theorem 7.2 of The Mathematical Form of Probability (H\_ρB, closed in that text), in the evaluation ν\_t^cur the legal load-bearing state of the declared organization 𝓛\_P really enters the formation of Rel\_ρ, the bearers including the realization of W\_i, whose contribution c\_i actually enters the formation of relative generation in the generation sub-segment Z\_i^ρ of the in-evaluation realization e\_ν.

Step two: by C1, ν\_t^cur is the evaluation of the current running in reality, so this bearing occurs at real time t, not in replay.

Step three: by C2 and C4, the event and sub-segment where this bearing lies are exactly the e\_t and Z\_t of w.

Step four: by item (c) of the certificate, the contribution c\_i is exactly the actual contribution of the generation factor a in the intersection of the minimal sufficient group and GenIn, coming from the realization r\_i; and by C3, r\_i belongs to Π\_t^𝒫. So the bearer is a legal current realization of 𝒫, not a structure outside the organization; what proves rate bearing and what proves realization attribution are one and the same contribution and one and the same realization.

Step five: by C5, the bearing lies within one and the same C and 𝔯\_P.

Combining the five steps: on w, the legal current realization of 𝒫, with its current load-bearing state, actually enters the formation of the relative-generation relation on that occasion. The upstream bearing criterion rests on minimal sufficient groups and actual in-evaluation binding, already containing "the current bearing holds" rather than partial execution; it allows redundant bearing and does not require a constant rate value, agreeing with the ProbabilityDutyConformance of the General Form. □

(7.2) w^M ∈ 𝔉(w^S) ∧ CurrentProbabilityOccurrence^M(w^M) = 1 ∧ ProbabilityDutyConformance^M(w^M) = 1 ⇒ ProbabilityDutyConformance(w^S)  
\[Theorem 7.1; the upstream H\_ρB is closed and not propagated as an open condition; the W\_i cited each inherit H\_LawRead(W\_i)\]

(7.3) Read\_{N\_PAct}^M = 1 ⇒ N\_PAct = 1  
\[the conjunction of the conditions carried by the arrows (7.1) and (7.2)\]

By Definition 3.16, when Read\_{N\_PAct}^M = 1 there exists a w^M with Q\_PAct(w^M) = 1, that is, Q\_1(w^M) = 1 and ProbabilityDutyConformance^M(w^M) = 1; take w^S(w^M); by (7.1) and (7.2), both items hold on the same structural witness w^S(w^M).

### 7.2 Reverse representation

For negative read-back to hold, reverse representation must come first: when there exists at the structural layer a set of witnesses satisfying both items at once, the formal layer can obtain a corresponding positive witness. Otherwise "the formal candidates have been checked and there is no positive witness" does not imply "there is no such witness at the structural layer".

Theorem 7.2　Reverse representation of current occurrence

Suppose PA-M1–PA-M5 and PA-M9 hold. If at the structural layer CurrentProbabilityOccurrence(w^S) = 1, w^S = (e\_t, R\_t, Z\_t), then there exists w^M ∈ 𝔉(w^S) with CurrentProbabilityOccurrence^M(w^M) = 1; and when EventCover^M, PRealCover^M(e\_t) and the corresponding SegCover^M are 1, w^M ∈ Ŵ\_PAct.

Proof: e\_t is an actual continuation at C at moment t; by PA-M2, the event record gives CurrentEvent^M(e\_t) = 1; R\_t is a legal current realization of 𝒫; by PA-M3 and PA-M4, it has at least one legal formal registration Π\_t^𝒫 satisfying LegalPReal^M and the MemberReal^M of every member; take w^M = (e\_t, Π\_t^𝒫, Z\_t) ∈ 𝔉(w^S); Z\_t is a sub-segment of the actual generation chain of e\_t and actually borne by R\_t; by PA-M5, ActualGenSeg^M and PRealSegBear^M are 1. Every item of Definitions 3.2–3.7 is a direct representation of the corresponding structural component, so CurrentProbabilityOccurrence^M = 1. When the coverage certificates are 1, e\_t, Π\_t^𝒫 and Z\_t lie respectively in the three layers of candidate sets. □

Reverse representation of duty conformance

For the second layer, what is needed is: if at the structural layer ProbabilityDutyConformance(w^S) = 1 on the same witness, then there exists w^M ∈ 𝔉(w^S) with CurrentProbabilityOccurrence^M(w^M) = 1, and there exists on ν\_t^cur a certificate κ\_ρ satisfying Definition 3.9 that satisfies C1–C5 for this w^M, whence ProbabilityDutyConformance^M(w^M) = 1.

This direction cannot be obtained from upstream Theorem 7.2: upstream Theorem 7.2 proves "a member of a minimal sufficient group, through in-evaluation binding ⇒ the organization really bears relative generation", whereas what is needed here is its reverse direction, "the organization really bears relative generation ⇒ the generation factors through which the bearing passes belong to some minimal sufficient group and can form same-witness in-evaluation binding". The Mathematical Form of Probability has not proven this direction, and has declared that its reverse representation does not claim completeness. In hot redundancy and joint bearing, whether the factors really taking part in generation are always within some minimal sufficient group depends on the correspondence between "really entering the formation" at the structural layer and minimal sufficiency at the formal layer; this is a representational proposition still to be proven and cannot be replaced by coverage certificates: RateCertCover^M(w) = 1 says only that all certificates conforming to Definition 3.9 have been enumerated, not that every mode of duty conformance at the structural layer can be captured by this definition of certificates.

Hence the gap H\_PAct-Rep is set up (see 8.3): when PA-M1–PA-M9 and upstream M9–M14 hold, CurrentProbabilityOccurrence(w^S) = 1 ∧ ProbabilityDutyConformance(w^S) = 1 ⇒ ∃w^M ∈ 𝔉(w^S): CurrentProbabilityOccurrence^M(w^M) = 1 ∧ ProbabilityDutyConformance^M(w^M) = 1. It requires only that every real duty conformance have at least one legal formal representation; it requires neither that all formal registrations be able to represent it nor that the registration be unique.

### 7.3 Negative read-back

Negative read-outs divide into two kinds by the layer where the failure lies.

(7.4a) First-layer negative read-out: Read\_{N\_PAct}^M = 0, and every witness in the candidate domain has Q\_1(w) = 0 ⇒ N\_PAct = 0  
\[this arrow carries no H\]

Proof: suppose N\_PAct = 1 at the structural layer; then there exists w^S with CurrentProbabilityOccurrence(w^S) = 1. By Theorem 7.2 and NegComplete^M\_PAct = 1 (Proposition 4.6), there exists w^M ∈ 𝔉(w^S) in Ŵ\_PAct with Q\_1(w^M) = 1, contradicting "every witness has Q\_1 = 0". □

(7.4b) Second-layer negative read-out: Read\_{N\_PAct}^M = 0, and there exists a witness with Q\_1(w) = 1 ⇒ N\_PAct = 0  
\[H\_PAct-Rep\]

Proof: suppose N\_PAct = 1 at the structural layer, and take a structural witness w^S satisfying both items. By H\_PAct-Rep, there exists w^M ∈ 𝔉(w^S) with CurrentProbabilityOccurrence^M(w^M) = 1 and ProbabilityDutyConformance^M(w^M) = 1; by NegComplete^M\_PAct = 1, this w^M lies in Ŵ\_PAct (EventCover covers e\_t, PRealCover covers all legal current organizational realization packages of 𝒫 in e\_t, SegCover covers the corresponding sub-segments), so Q\_PAct(w^M) = 1, contradicting Read = 0. □

(7.5) N\_PAct = 0 ⇒ ProbabilityAct = 0

This is the contrapositive of the already proven necessary direction ProbabilityAct ⇒ N\_PAct of The General Form of Probability-Action, not the sufficient direction proven here. That necessary direction carries no H, so this arrow adds no condition; what is obtained through (7.4b) still carries H\_PAct-Rep.

### 7.4 Undecided

Read\_{N\_PAct}^M = ⊥ implies neither that action holds nor that action fails.

Before H\_PAct-Rep is closed, the strength of the structural-layer negation of a second-layer 0 reading is conditional: what the formal layer signs is "under complete coverage no duty-conformance certificate was obtained", and its structural meaning "the duty was not realized" carries H\_PAct-Rep. A first-layer 0 reading is not subject to this limitation.

### 8.5 Breach methods

(1) Failure of representation: there exists a legal instance with N\_PAct = 1 for which Read\_{N\_PAct}^M = 0 when all solvability conditions are satisfied. If the failure is at the first layer, Theorem 7.2 is breached; if at the second layer, H\_PAct-Rep is breached.

(2) Read-back conflict: Read\_{N\_PAct}^M = 1, while the corresponding common witness cannot be read back as the two items at the structural layer.

(3) Failure of currentization: some replay witness obtains Read = 1 when there is no event in current reality.

(4) Failure of progression: a single-dimension solo is judged 1.

(5) Too strong: hot redundancy is necessarily judged 0.

(6) Overreach: rerouting after action is judged 0 because the final outcome landed in no dimension.

(7) Negative read-out overstepping: 0 is signed when the candidate domain or source chain has not been covered.

(8) Quantifier splitting: the formal system allows the two items each to be true on different witnesses and reads out 1; or the certificate internally allows the generation factor proving rate bearing and the contribution proving realization attribution to be taken from different sources.

(9) Misuse of aggregation: the rate-domain aggregation RateBearWitness^M\_{𝔯\_P} stands in for a single-evaluation witness.

(10) Omissions in the correspondence table: some load-bearing symbol is not listed in 3.0.

(11) Conflict of relation properties: usage in the body disagrees with the declaration of 3.17.

(12) Internal contradiction: under the same χ\_PAct, the same criterion yields both 1 and 0 by definition.

Not constituting a breach: solvability conditions not being satisfied so that the result is ⊥; upstream read-back conditions not being closed; H\_PAct-Rep not yet being closed; Read = 1 while ProbabilityAct is not judged (the sufficient direction belongs separately to The Necessity Form of Probability-Action).

### 8.6 Regression tests

The following tests serve only as regression and breach checks, not as general proofs.

Test 1　Resting: no event in current reality at C at moment t. When EventCover^M = 1 the reading is 0; with insufficient coverage, ⊥.

Test 2　External bypass: the outcome falls into some probability dimension, but the realization package of 𝒫 bears no actual generation sub-segment. Q\_1 = 0.

Test 3　Single-dimension solo: \|J\_t\| = 1, Q\_1 = 1; the relative generation among the dimensions is decided outside the organization, the certificate coverage is complete, and there is no placing certificate. Q\_PAct = 0 (Proposition 4.3).

Test 4　Idle rate structure: Rel\_ρ exists, but on ν\_t^cur no certificate's event and sub-segment are the event and sub-segment of w. C2 or C4 fails, ProbabilityDutyConformance^M = 0.

Test 5　Read-only rate: Rel\_ρ is read out, but generation is borne by another mechanism. The reader is not in MinSuff\_ρ, and there is no certificate. ProbabilityDutyConformance^M = 0.

Test 6　Hot redundancy: MinSuff\_ρ = {{A}, {B}}, A and B both members of Π. ProbabilityDutyConformance^M = 1 (Proposition 4.5).

Test 7　Illegal side branch: another path in the same system forms relative generation but does not belong to 𝒫. Its contribution is not in GenIn(W\_i), or C3 fails. ProbabilityDutyConformance^M = 0.

Test 8　Partial execution: members are active while the current bearing has not been established. The minimal sufficient groups do not contain their generation factors, or in-evaluation binding fails. ProbabilityDutyConformance^M = 0.

Test 9　Legal rate change: 7 : 3 changes to 1 : 9, still borne on ν\_t^cur by members of Π. ProbabilityDutyConformance^M = 1.

Test 10　Rerouting after action: after the action sub-segment is complete, a downstream mechanism makes the final outcome fall outside V\_P. The reading does not change because the final outcome landed in no dimension.

Test 11　Replay passing as current: the certificate comes from a replay evaluation. C1 fails (Proposition 4.2).

Test 12　Stitching: one event in current reality satisfies Q\_1 but has no certificate; another replay evaluation has a certificate. By Definitions 3.15 and 3.16, the two cannot be composed on the same w, and the reading is not 1.

Test 12b　Stitching inside a certificate: a generation factor a ∈ B ∩ GenIn(W\_i, ν\_t^cur) proves rate bearing, while another contribution c′ coming from a member realization of Π is taken to serve as realization attribution. Item (c) of the certificate requires c\_i to be exactly the contribution of a; c′ does not satisfy it, and Cert\_ρ^M = 0.

Test 13　Insufficient source chain: the actual generation source chain cannot be read. The related items are ⊥.

Test 14　Insufficient coverage: the candidate domain has not been proven complete, and there is no positive witness. NegComplete^M\_PAct ≠ 1, the reading is ⊥.

### 8.7 What this text does not do

It does not prove probability qualification anew, does not prove ProbabilityAct ⇒ N\_PAct anew, and does not prove N\_PAct ⇒ ProbabilityAct.

It builds no second set of rate bearing, and does not let rate-domain aggregation stand in for single-evaluation witnesses.

It does not require all probability dimensions to be currently active, does not require at least two members to be active, does not require the final outcome to fall into some dimension, does not require 𝒫 to monopolize the whole continuation, does not require sole bearing or but-for necessity, and does not require a fixed rate value, positive rate, full coverage, stable empirical frequency or ontological randomness.

It does not take replay witnesses as action in current reality, does not stitch two independent existential formulas into a common witness, and sets up no separate relation of common witness.

It does not study cross-time change and makes no determination of instances.
