# The Necessity Form of Probability-Action

## Section One　Purpose

To prove: an already holding Probability, as soon as there exists at moment t a set of witnesses on which current occurrence and duty conformance both hold, is necessarily acting at this moment; no additional independent content of action is needed, and there is no independent condition able on its own to cancel this action.

That is: within the working domain where Probability\_F,t(𝒫; C, 𝔯\_P) = 1,

N\_PAct = 1 ⇒ ProbabilityAct\_F,t(𝒫; C, 𝔯\_P, ξ\_t) = 1

N\_PAct = ∃(e\_t, R\_t, Z\_t) \[ CurrentProbabilityOccurrence ∧ ProbabilityDutyConformance \]

Specifically: whether the final outcome falls into some probability dimension, whether this Probability is the sole or irreplaceable bearer, whether the rate value is the same as before, whether the system is ontologically random, whether this action is recognized or observed, whether there is currently environmental disturbance, whether this Probability has a history of formation by reward and punishment — none of these affects the conclusion.

## Section Two　Core Proposition

Within the working domain of an already holding Probability 𝒫, the current content of the action of probability spoken of by the frozen action corpus consists exactly of two items: at this moment there is in fact one real occurrence of this Probability; this Probability, as a Law-bearing organization, bears the relative generation among the dimensions. The two items are constrained by one discipline of same witness and must fall on one and the same occurrence and one and the same realization; this discipline is not a third item of content. In the frozen corpus there is no third item of action content, nor any condition able on its own to cancel action when both items hold. Therefore, once the necessary structure N\_PAct forced out by The General Form of Probability-Action holds, Probability-Action cannot fail to occur.

The proof does not take the route "ProbabilityAct ⇒ N\_PAct, so the converse also holds". The duty-removal minimality of the General Form proves only that neither item can be lacking; it does not prove that the two items together suffice for action. The structure of the General Form serves here only as the antecedent; sufficiency is obtained by an independent closure of the frozen action semantics.

## Section Five　The Theorem Group

5.1 Theorem One　Theorem of the closure of action semantics and binding

Every semantic component in the frozen action corpus belongs to one of the following three classes: conditions of obtaining current action; conditions of cancelling current action; components that do not decide the truth of current action (mode of execution, triggering antecedent, generation process, relation, dynamics, function and example, degree, representation and observation). Of these, the obtaining side is exactly two items of action content:

A\_1　Current occurrence: this already holding Probability is at this moment in fact occurring in a real continuation.

A\_2　Duty conformance under the identity of a probability: the legal current realization of this Probability is not merely active, but bears the duty peculiar to probability, namely the formation of the relative-generation relation among the probability dimensions.

The two items are constrained by the same-witness binding discipline:

B\_same: ∃w \[ A\_1(w) ∧ A\_2(w) \], not (∃w A\_1(w)) ∧ (∃w′ A\_2(w′)).

B\_same is the witness-binding discipline of A\_1 and A\_2, not a third item of action content. The obtaining side has no third independent duty: R\_3^independent = ∅.

Proof

First, atomization. B5.c, "it makes it possible, while passing through many Law Ecologies, to go returning to one in every direction, that is, to be able to go as one. This is an important function of probability in lower organisms.", splits into: the current "going", belonging to the obtaining side, giving A\_1; "in every direction", belonging to the obtaining side, giving the duty background of A\_2, namely that what probability faces is relative generation among several directions; "to go returning to one", "to be able to go as one", belonging to the mode of execution, characterized by L1; "while passing through many Law Ecologies", belonging to relation and dynamics; "an important function …… in lower organisms", belonging to function and example.

B11.a, "If some environmental disturbance appears, then, having become a probability Law through reward and punishment received, it automatically goes to some direction dimension …… and continues; lower organisms go by this.", splits into: "some environmental disturbance appears" is the triggering antecedent of this continuation; "through reward and punishment received" belongs to the generation process; "having become a probability Law" is qualification, already fixed by D\_PAct; "automatically …… and continues" belongs to the obtaining side, giving A\_1; "goes to some direction dimension" belongs to the obtaining side, giving A\_2, namely generating the successor in the direction of the probability; "lower organisms go by this" belongs to function and example.

Second, the source of B\_same. Both sentences write "going" and "every direction", "some direction dimension" onto one and the same continuation: B5.c speaks of "…… to go …… in every direction", B11.a of "goes to some direction dimension …… and continues". The action the corpus speaks of is the generation of directionality within one and the same continuation, not one continuation set side by side with another generation of directionality. So A\_1 and A\_2 must fall on the same witness. This requirement constrains the witness of the two items of content and adds no new action content, agreeing with the ruling of The General Form of Probability-Action that same witness is a discipline of inheritance and not a third structure.

Third, the classification is exhaustive, and there is no third condition of obtaining. For every component of B5.c and B11.a, and for the conjunctions within one sentence and the joint authorizations across sentences between them and the remaining sentences of the fixed corpus, the review of the action corpus in The General Form of Probability-Action has checked one by one: mode of execution, triggering antecedent, generation process, relation, dynamics, function and example, degree, representation and observation each have their place, and no component or combination is authorized to bear current action content beyond A\_1, A\_2. The remaining sentences of the fixed corpus do not directly describe action; the cross-sentence relations between them and B5.c, B11.a have also been checked by that review, and all serve only to state examples, antecedents, modes of formation, relations or modes of execution of action, not deciding the truth of current action. What is inherited here is that review's classification conclusion on the frozen corpus, not that text's necessary direction; this constitutes no inversion of the necessary direction.

Combining the three points above, Theorem One is proven. □

5.2 Theorem Two　Theorem that the cancelling conditions are empty

Within D\_PAct, when A\_1 and A\_2 are wholly obtained on one and the same witness satisfying B\_same, there is in the frozen action corpus no condition able on its own to make ProbabilityAct = 0:

IndependentCancel\_PAct = ∅

Proof

The candidates that might be taken as cancelling conditions are checked item by item. For each it is checked: supposing A\_1, A\_2 wholly hold on the same witness, can the holding or not holding of the candidate on its own make action fail?

The final outcome not falling into V\_P: by L2, the final successor of a continuation can fall outside the dimensions; by L1, the final settling is execution, not action semantics. Another legal mechanism changing the final settling after action is complete does not retroactively cancel action that has already occurred. Not a cancelling condition.

Non-sole bearing, not but-for necessary: A\_2 requires that the realization of this Probability actually bear relative generation, not that it be the sole or irreplaceable bearer; in hot redundancy several realizations bear at the same time, each of them bearing. Not a cancelling condition.

The rate value unchanged: continuing to bear an existing relative-generation relation is still bearing. Not a cancelling condition.

The rate value changing legally: the duty spoken of by A\_2 is to bear the relative generation among the dimensions, not to keep some rate value; the changed rate relation is still formed by the legal current realization of this Probability, and the duty is still being realized. Not a cancelling condition.

Not ontologically random: the action corpus speaks only of "going" and "direction" and does not require execution to be unpredictable; by L1, every execution returns to one anyway. Not a cancelling condition.

No current environmental disturbance: in B11.a environmental disturbance is the triggering antecedent of the example of action that sentence gives, not prescribed as a condition of every action; B5.c does not mention environmental disturbance. Not a universal cancelling condition.

Not "passing through many Law Ecologies": belongs to relation and dynamics, not to current action semantics. Not a cancelling condition.

No history of formation by reward and punishment: belongs to the generation process; by D\_PAct, qualification is already fixed, and its history of formation cannot be required again here. Not a cancelling condition.

Not recognized, read or observed: belongs to representation and observation; by L4 and the action semantics, real bearing when not read out is still bearing. Not a cancelling condition.

Not monopolizing the whole event: A\_1 requires only that there exist a sub-segment actually borne by the realization of this Probability, not that the whole continuation be generated by it. Not a cancelling condition.

No other candidates: The General Form of Probability-Action has already reviewed item by item the single components, the conjunctions within one sentence and the joint authorizations across sentences of the frozen action corpus B5.c, B11.a, and found no independent current action duty beyond current occurrence, conformance of the probability duty and their discipline of same witness; environmental disturbance, formation by reward and punishment, final settling, passing through the Law Ecology, degree, representation and observation have all been diverted. On this basis, the components have again been checked item by item above: when A\_1, A\_2 hold on one and the same witness satisfying B\_same, none of them can on its own cancel ProbabilityAct. So within the currently frozen corpus domain there is no remaining independent cancelling candidate. The review of the cancelling side in Section Five of The Necessity Form of Probability concerns probability qualification, not current action; its conclusion is not borrowed here.

So IndependentCancel\_PAct = ∅. □

5.3 Theorem Three　Theorem of structural realization

C\_PAct completely realizes A\_1, A\_2 and satisfies B\_same:

R\_1(w^S) ⇒ A\_1(w^S)

ProbabilityDutyConformance(w^S) ⇒ A\_2(w^S)

N\_PAct ⇒ ∃w \[ A\_1(w) ∧ A\_2(w) \], that is, the binding discipline B\_same is satisfied

Proof

R\_1(w^S) gives: at real time t there exists one actual continuation e\_t, in which a segment Z\_t is actually borne by a legal current realization R\_t of 𝒫. This is exactly what A\_1 says, "this already holding Probability is at this moment in fact occurring in a real continuation": the occurrence is in current reality, and what occurs is a realization of this Probability itself, not an external bypass, a structure of the same type or attribution after the fact.

ProbabilityDutyConformance(w^S) gives: in the same e\_t, R\_t, as a realization of 𝒫 as a Law-bearing organization, with a legal current load-bearing state, actually enters the formation of the relative-generation relation among the probability dimensions, and makes the current bearing hold. This is exactly what A\_2 says, "not merely active, but bears the duty peculiar to probability": it excludes a single-dimension Law going solo, the mere existence or read-out of the rate relation, structures outside the organization and partial execution.

N\_PAct takes one and the same existential quantifier over w^S; by L3, the two items hold on one and the same (e\_t, R\_t, Z\_t), so B\_same is satisfied.

A\_1, A\_2 have no remaining content. □

5.4 Theorem Four　Theorem of the necessity of Probability-Action

Within the working domain D\_PAct (Probability\_F,t(𝒫; C, 𝔯\_P) = 1), if there exists one and the same set of witnesses w^S = (e\_t, R\_t, Z\_t) with CurrentProbabilityOccurrence(w^S) = 1 and ProbabilityDutyConformance(w^S) = 1, then:

ProbabilityAct\_F,t(𝒫; C, 𝔯\_P, ξ\_t) = 1

That is: D\_PAct ⊨ ( N\_PAct ⇒ ProbabilityAct )

Proof

Within D\_PAct, suppose N\_PAct = 1 while ProbabilityAct = 0.

By Theorem One, the components of the frozen action corpus that decide the truth of current action are of only two classes, conditions of obtaining and conditions of cancelling; the remaining components do not decide the truth of action. So if ProbabilityAct = 0 held, there could be only two causes: some condition of obtaining was not realized, or some cancelling condition took effect.

The first is impossible. By Theorem One, the conditions of obtaining are exactly A\_1, A\_2, constrained by B\_same, with no third item; by Theorem Three, N\_PAct = 1 makes A\_1, A\_2 wholly realized on the same witness, and B\_same is satisfied.

The second is impossible. By Theorem Two, when A\_1, A\_2 are wholly obtained on the same witness, there is no independent cancelling condition.

Both causes are excluded, contradicting ProbabilityAct = 0. So ProbabilityAct = 1. □

What is obtained here is not "a structure similar to action". N\_PAct completely realizes all the action content and binding discipline obtained by independent analysis of the frozen action corpus; the proof does not depend on defining ProbabilityAct as N\_PAct, nor does it invert the necessary direction of The General Form of Probability-Action.

5.5 Theorem Five　Theorem of the minimality of N\_PAct as a sufficient structure

Within D\_PAct, and within the currently frozen action corpus and task domain, N\_PAct is a derivation-minimal sufficient structure: removing any part of it, the conclusion no longer holds; adding any candidate item is either too strong or without grounds in the corpus. The working domain D\_PAct takes no part in this test: outside it, the object is not a Probability and the question does not enter; this is not "the sufficient condition lacking one item".

Proof

Removing R\_1: only "a structure or capacity having the duty of probability" remains, and resting, historical realization, bearing in replay and cases having only potential for bearing would all mix in. R\_1 cannot be removed.

Removing ProbabilityDutyConformance: only R\_1 remains, and the Law of some one dimension of the probability may continue alone while the organization does not bear relative generation. ProbabilityDutyConformance cannot be removed.

Removing the discipline of same witness (splitting N\_PAct into two independent existential formulas): one occurrence in current reality without duty conformance and duty conformance in a replay or elsewhere can be stitched together into a true antecedent while action does not hold. The discipline of same witness cannot be removed.

Adding "final landing in a dimension": too strong; see the rerouting-after-action case of Section Six.

Adding "sole bearing" or "but-for necessary": too strong; see the hot-redundancy case of Section Six.

Adding "fixed rate value": too strong; see the legal-rate-change case of Section Six.

Adding "environmental disturbance", "ontological randomness", "being recognized or read": without grounds in the corpus; Theorem One has shown that they do not belong to the conditions of obtaining action, and Theorem Two that they are not cancelling conditions either.

So within D\_PAct:

C\_PAct^min = N\_PAct

"Minimal" here means derivation-minimal; it does not claim that no other logically equivalent expression exists, nor that it stays unchanged after a corpus upgrade. □

## Section Six　Boundary Cases and Structural Breakdown Conditions

### 6.1 Boundary back-tests

The following cases are all within D\_PAct, serving only as back-tests, not as general proofs. Those judged ProbabilityAct = 1 all satisfy N\_PAct and follow from Theorem Four; those judged ProbabilityAct = 0 all have N\_PAct failing and follow from the contrapositive of the necessary direction of The General Form of Probability-Action (carrying no H).

T1　Rerouting after action: N\_PAct holds; after the action sub-segment is complete, a downstream mechanism makes the final outcome fall outside V\_P. ProbabilityAct = 1.

T2　Hot redundancy: two legal realizations each suffice at the same time to bear relative generation; the realization of this Probability really takes part, but is not but-for necessary. N\_PAct holds, ProbabilityAct = 1.

T3　Legal rate change: the rate relation changes from 7 : 3 to 1 : 9, still formed by the legal current realization of this Probability. N\_PAct holds, ProbabilityAct = 1.

T4　Single-dimension solo: R\_1 holds, and ProbabilityDutyConformance fails on every occurrence belonging to 𝒫 at moment t. N\_PAct fails, ProbabilityAct = 0.

T5　General physical influence: members of this Probability change physical quantities such as heat, impedance or bandwidth, but do not bear the relative generation among the dimensions, and at moment t no other occurrence belonging to 𝒫 bears relative generation either. ProbabilityDutyConformance fails on every occurrence belonging to 𝒫, N\_PAct fails, ProbabilityAct = 0.

T6　Replay passing as current: duty conformance appears only in replay or history, and at moment t at C there is no occurrence in current reality belonging to 𝒫. R\_1 fails, ProbabilityAct = 0.

T7　Stitching: one real event satisfies R\_1 without duty conformance, another evaluation (a replay, or an instance at another Continuation-Position or in another rate domain) satisfies duty conformance, and at moment t at C there is no other witness satisfying both items at once. The two are not on the same witness, N\_PAct fails, ProbabilityAct = 0.

T8　Not recognized: there is no reading at all, but this Probability currently really bears relative generation. N\_PAct holds, ProbabilityAct = 1.

T9　Not ontologically random: N\_PAct is satisfied in a wholly deterministic system. ProbabilityAct = 1.

T10　No history of reward and punishment: the source of some Probability does not depend on reward and punishment, but the current structure and duty are both legal. N\_PAct holds, ProbabilityAct = 1; current action cannot be cancelled for lack of a history of reward and punishment.

### 6.2 Structural breakdown conditions

The appearance of any of the following breaches Theorem Four: while the working domain and the declared conditions are all kept, there exists a legal case satisfying Probability = 1, N\_PAct = 1, while ProbabilityAct = 0. By the layer at which it occurs it divides into:

Breach One　Semantic omission: there exists in the frozen action corpus an item of current action content not covered by A\_1, A\_2, that is, there exists a third independent current duty; or the corpus is revised and such sentences are added. Breaches Theorem One.

Breach Two　Cancelling condition: there exists an independent condition able to cancel action while R\_1 and duty conformance both hold. Breaches Theorem Two.

Breach Three　Failure of realization: the discipline of same witness is not enough to guarantee that A\_1 and A\_2 fall on one and the same occurrence, or R\_1, ProbabilityDutyConformance do not completely realize A\_1, A\_2. Breaches Theorem Three.

Breach Four　Wrong diversion: some factor assigned to generation process, dynamics, degree, representation, observation, execution, relation or outcome is proven by the frozen action corpus to be in fact current action semantics. Breaches Theorem One or Theorem Two.

Breach Five　Misjudgment: legal cases such as hot redundancy, legal rate change, rerouting after action, not recognized, not ontologically random are judged ProbabilityAct = 0.

Breach Six　Violation of the manner of proof: the proof directly inverts the necessary direction of the General Form; or ProbabilityAct is renamed as a hidden predicate such as "real action", "whole action" or "effective action" and then taken as a new antecedent; or the formal readings or certificates of The Mathematical Form of Probability-Action stand in for the object-level sufficiency proof.
