# The Determination Form of Probability

For a real candidate Probability organization frozen in advance, under a legal and operable determination protocol, invoking the three-valued formal system of The Mathematical Form of Probability and the upstream interfaces of Law and the field, to give its probability qualification verdict 1, 0 or ⊥, and to prove: when judged 1 it is certainly a Probability; when judged 0 it is certainly not a Probability (carrying the declared conditions); whenever correctness cannot be guaranteed, ⊥ is judged throughout, with the reason written out.

## Section One　Declaration of the Property and the Observation Set

### 1.1 Property

The property to be determined is the probability qualification of the candidate, Probability\_F,t(𝒫; C, 𝔯\_P). Its definition has been closed upstream:

N\_P := MultiLawDimension ∧ CommonContinuationBind ∧ DomainRelativeRateBearing

The sufficient direction (The Necessity Form of Probability): N\_P ⇒ Probability, Status = Established, carrying no H.

The necessary direction (The General Form of Probability): H2 ∧ H3^red ⇒ \[ Probability ⇒ N\_P \]; its contrapositive H2 ∧ H3^red ⇒ \[ ¬N\_P ⇒ ¬Probability \].

The three qualifications are neither increased nor decreased, and no fourth is added.

### 1.2 Candidate instance

Following Definition 3.1 of The Mathematical Form of Probability:

χ\_P = (F, t, 𝒫, C, 𝔯\_P, V\_P, 𝓛\_P)

These are all declared objects, not objects that have already obtained qualification. V\_P, 𝓛\_P, C, 𝔯\_P must be frozen before determination; during determination they may not, in order to pass a test, be added to or deleted from, narrowed, changed in domain, changed in position or re-declared.

1.3 Observation set: layers of determination evidence

What determination reads are legal operable interfaces, divided into four layers by the qualification item they serve:

Y\_det = (Y\_D, Y\_C, Y\_R, Y\_cov)

Y\_D, serving MultiLawDimension: registration of dimensions, realization domains D\_i, formal Law solutions 𝒮(F, L, I), duty realizations RoleReal, duty-generation chains, the binding of dimensions and Law-bearing.

Y\_C, serving CommonContinuationBind: identity of Continuation-Positions, the freezing protocol and version of the rate domain, DomainProtocol, EvidenceTime, GenSchema, identity of legal generation realizations, the relation of mutually exclusive successors.

Y\_R, serving DomainRelativeRateBearing: A\_ρ^gen, GenIn, AdmVar\_ρ, Sound\_Adm, Cover\_Adm, MinSuff\_ρ, EvalRateBind^M, RateBearWitness^M, and the interfaces of the set and measure outer quantifiers.

Y\_cov, serving the method of determination itself: search coverage at each layer, solving completeness, completeness of negative read-outs, decidability of outer quantifiers.

Y\_cov is not a fourth qualification of probability; it only decides whether the method of determination is entitled to sign 1 or 0.

Probability or N\_P itself may not be read as input; outcome sequences, stable empirical frequencies or numerical rate read-outs may not serve as criteria of probability (The Inversion Form of Probability, 3.3, has proven that these three classes of observation cannot universally identify probability qualification).

### 1.4 Forward relation

The forward relation between the property and the observations is given by established forms: the reading of Y\_D comes from The Mathematical Form of Law and Definitions 3.3–3.7 of The Mathematical Form of Probability; Y\_C from Definitions 3.8–3.14 of The Mathematical Form of Probability; Y\_R from Definitions 3.15–3.31 of The Mathematical Form of Probability; Y\_cov from the solvability conditions M1–M16 of Section Five of The Mathematical Form of Probability.

1.5 Boundary with The Inversion Form of Probability

The Inversion Form of Probability answers: given existing observations, what at most can be known. Here it is answered: to determine a real candidate, which legal operation interfaces should be invoked, and how a formal probability verdict is signed. The Inversion Form prohibits taking internal qualifications as external observations; the Law witnesses, GenIn, RateBearWitness and the like read here are legal operation interfaces, not the qualification to be determined itself; the Probability and N\_P to be determined still do not serve as input.

## Section Two　Construction of the Criterion

### 2.1 Three-layer short circuit

Following the solving order of The Mathematical Form of Probability:

D\_1 := MultiLawDimension^M

D\_2 := CommonContinuationBind^M

D\_3 := DomainRelativeRateBearing^M

Read\_{N\_P}^M takes values by three-valued short circuit:

D\_1 = 0 ⇒ Read = 0

D\_1 = 1, D\_2 = 0 ⇒ Read = 0

D\_1 = D\_2 = 1, D\_3 = 0 ⇒ Read = 0

D\_1 = D\_2 = D\_3 = 1 ⇒ Read = 1

If the layer that must currently be solved is ⊥, and there has been no legal decisive 0 before, then Read = ⊥. One may not pass over the ⊥ of an earlier layer to look for a 0 in a lower layer.

2.2 Search coverage of dimension witnesses

For every dimension v\_i, define:

DimWitnessCover^Π(v\_i) = 1: the currently frozen search domain covers every legal candidate qualified to be a structural-layer Law-bearing witness of that dimension, or covers it by an equivalent representative.

It is a coverage condition at the layer of the method of determination, not a new ontological condition of probability.

2.3 Path determination domain and complete determination domain

PathDom: used for the actual determination of a specific candidate. It requires only that the inputs, search coverage and negative read-out certificates of the determination path actually walked be sufficient. If the first layer has already legally judged 0, the solvability conditions of the last two layers are not required to be available.

FullDetDom: used to state that the determiner has complete deciding power within some working domain. It requires that all interfaces, search domains, identity readings, source chains, rate-bearing diagnostics, minimal sufficient groups and outer quantifiers that may be accessed in the three layers be sufficiently complete: it cites M1–M16 of The Mathematical Form of Probability as a whole, and adds the DimWitnessCover of each dimension.

PathDom and FullDetDom are both conditions at the layer of the method of determination, not conditions of probability qualification, and do not enter N\_P.

### 2.4 Determination domain of rate bearing

DetDom\_ρ(ν) is composed by function of the solvability conditions of The Mathematical Form of Probability:

GenFactorCover: the real domain of generation factors can be obtained (M9);

SourceCover: the generation source chain can be traced (M10);

Sound\_Adm = 1 ∧ Cover\_Adm = 1: the family of legal diagnostic variations is faithful and complete (M11);

MinSuffSolvable = 1: comparison is decidable, and minimal sufficient groups exist and are obtainable (M12, M13);

ActualRealizationCover = 1: legal in-evaluation realizations and B1–B4 can be checked (M14);

DomainQuantifierDecidable = 1: the set and measure outer quantifiers are decidable (M15);

and M16: every 0 has complete negative read-out grounds.

These are all conditions at the layer of the method of determination and do not enter the probability ontology.

### 2.5 Final determiner

Det\_P^Π(χ\_P) ∈ {1, 0, ⊥}

It is the final probability verdict, not the same as the formal reading Read\_{N\_P}^M:

Det\_P = 1: Read\_{N\_P}^M = 1, and the positive read-back conditions H\_LawRead(W\_i) of the Law witnesses used have been closed.

Det\_P = 0: Read\_{N\_P}^M = 0 and is a structural zero (StructuralZero, see 3.5), the negative representation of the path where the failure lies has been closed (Section Three), and H2 ∧ H3^red have been closed.

Det\_P = ⊥: all other cases, with the reason attached.

### 2.6 Reasons for ⊥

InputOpen: insufficient real input or identity interfaces.

DeclarationInvalid: the formal reading 0 comes only from the invalidity of the frozen declared instance (DeclarationZero); re-declaration is required.

DimWitnessCoverOpen: the search coverage of the Law-bearing of dimensions is not closed.

UpstreamLawOpen: upstream Law qualification, the positive read-back condition H\_LawRead or the negative-end condition bundle H^-\_M is not closed.

BindReadOpen: the identity of the common Continuation-Position, the rate domain or the generation relation is undecidable.

RateCoverOpen: coverage of generation factors, source chains, diagnostic variations, minimal sufficient groups or in-evaluation realizations is not closed.

RateRepOpen: the negative-representation condition of rate bearing H\_ρRep^- is not closed (2.7, 3.4).

DomainQuantifierOpen: the set or measure outer quantifier is undecidable.

OntologyBridgeOpen: N\_P = 0 has already been obtained, but H2, H3^red are not closed, and Probability = 0 cannot be formally signed.

These reasons may not be written together as a general "insufficient material".

### 2.7 Two negative-representation bridges

Negative verdicts require reverse representation: when some item holds at the structural layer, the formal layer can certainly obtain the corresponding positive reading within the determination domain. Upstream has proven only positive read-back. The two reverse bridges needed here are:

H\_DimRep^-: negative representation of dimension Law-bearing. Its content and status are given in 3.2.

H\_ρRep^-: negative representation of rate bearing. Its content and status are given in 3.4.

## Section Three　Proof of the Criterion's Validity

3.1 Judging true what is true, in the positive direction: if judged 1, then true

Theorem One　Theorem of the validity of positive verdicts

Det\_P = 1 ⇒ Probability = 1

Proof

By 2.5, Det\_P = 1 implies Read\_{N\_P}^M = 1, with the H\_LawRead of the witnesses used closed. By (7.5) of The Mathematical Form of Probability, Read\_{N\_P}^M = 1 ⇒ N\_P = 1, the condition being the H\_LawRead of the witnesses used, which is closed. By The Necessity Form of Probability, N\_P ⇒ Probability, carrying no H. Hence Probability = 1. □

Theorem One does not carry H2, H3^red.

3.2 Negative completeness of dimension qualification

Theorem Two　Theorem of the negative representation of dimension witnesses

Let DetDom\_Dim(v\_i) := DimWitnessCover^Π(v\_i) = 1 ∧ M2 ∧ M3 of The Mathematical Form of Probability hold: the upstream formal Law interface is usable, and the attribution of successors to the realization domain D\_i can be effectively judged. The identifiability M1 of the candidate instance serves as the unified admission for determination throughout the text and is not repeated in each local theorem. Then:

DetDom\_Dim(v\_i) ∧ DimQual(v\_i) = 1 ⇒ DimQual^M(v\_i) = 1　\[H^-\_M, inherited by witness\]

Contrapositive:

DetDom\_Dim(v\_i) ∧ DimQual^M(v\_i) = 0 ⇒ DimQual(v\_i) = 0　\[H^-\_M, inherited by witness\]

Proof

DimQual(v\_i) = 1, that is, at the structural layer there exist L, I, C\_i with Law(L, I) = 1 and BearDim(L, v\_i; C\_i). By DimWitnessCover, this Law-bearing or its equivalent representative is in the search domain. For it to obtain R ∈ 𝒮(F, L, I) at the formal layer, the non-emptiness of its formal solution set must be inferred from its structural-layer Law qualification: this is exactly the contrapositive of Theorem 5.1(ii) of The Boundary Form of Law, holding under that text's negative-end condition bundle of Definition 5.2, H^-\_M = C0 ∧ C2 ∧ H2 ∧ H3 ∧ H\_RepC ∧ H\_RepN ∧ H\_RepD ∧ H\_RepΩ ∧ H\_RepBind, inherited witness by witness. The existing upstream negative-end condition bundle is inherited directly here without enumerating it anew: H\_RepR, H\_RepReplay and H1 are not in it. The BearDim item is given by direct reading of duty realizations and generation chains (Definition 3.4 and M2 of The Mathematical Form of Probability). Hence R is in 𝒲\_Dim(v\_i), and DimQual^M(v\_i) = 1. The contrapositive gives the second formula. □

The content of H\_DimRep^- is exactly Theorem Two. Under DimWitnessCover it has been reduced to the negative-end condition bundle H^-\_M of the upstream The Boundary Form of Law; no new open condition is set up here, and its status follows H^-\_M: when H^-\_M closes, H\_DimRep^- closes.

Failure of declaration is not structural failure:

DeclarationFail ≠ StructuralFail

The failure of some registration W\_i in 𝓛\_P shows only that this registration in the current frozen declaration fails. If DimWitnessCover is not closed, one may not thereby assert that no other legal Law-bearing exists in reality; a missed registration can only lead to coverage not being closed and a reading of ⊥, and cannot automatically lead to a structural 0.

Theorem Three　Theorem of the negative read-back of MultiLawDimension

DetDom\_1 is taken according to the sub-item where the failure lies, requiring only the path actually walked (agreeing with PathDom of 2.3): a 0 obtained from Card^M = 0 requires only that V\_P be completely enumerable; a 0 obtained from DistAll^M = 0 requires only that the intersection of the corresponding realization domains be decidable; a 0 obtained from some DimQual^M(v\_i) = 0 requires only DetDom\_Dim(v\_i) = 1 for that dimension. It is not required that the DetDom\_Dim of all other dimensions hold. Then:

DetDom\_1 ∧ MultiLawDimension^M = 0 ⇒ MultiLawDimension = 0

Conditions: if the 0 comes from an item DimQual^M = 0 in DimCover^M, it carries the H^-\_M of the witness of that dimension; if it comes from Card^M or DistAll^M, it carries no condition. The theorem holds only for the structural zeros below; a declaration zero caused solely by RegValid^M = 0 does not apply.

Proof

MultiLawDimension^M = Card^M ∧₃ DistAll^M ∧₃ DimCover^M (Definition 3.7 of The Mathematical Form of Probability); when it is 0, at least one item is 0.

Card^M = 0: V\_P is completely enumerable and \|V\_P\| \< 2, so \|V\_P\| ≥ 2 of the structural-layer MultiLawDimension fails.

DistAll^M = 0: the intersection of the realization domains of some pair of dimensions is non-empty and decidable, so structural-layer distinguishability fails.

DimCover^M = 0: if it comes from some DimQual^M(v\_i) = 0, by Theorem Two DimQual(v\_i) = 0, and the dimension has no structural-layer Law-bearing. If it comes only from RegValid^M = 0, then by Definitions 3.6–3.7 of The Mathematical Form of Probability the formal reading is still 0, and that definition is not changed here; but this 0 shows only that the registration family of the frozen declaration does not conform to the definition; it is a declaration zero (see 3.5) and is not read back through this theorem as a structural 0.

The first three structural failures all make MultiLawDimension = 0. □

DetDom\_2 ∧ CommonContinuationBind^M = 0 ⇒ CommonContinuationBind = 0

3.4 Negative completeness of rate bearing

Theorem Five　Theorem of the negative representation of rate bearing

Let DetDom\_ρ(ν) hold. Then:

DetDom\_ρ(ν) ∧ RateBearing(ν) = 1 ⇒ RateBearWitness^M(ν) = 1　\[H\_ρRep^-\]

Contrapositive:

DetDom\_ρ(ν) ∧ RateBearWitness^M(ν) = 0 ⇒ RateBearing(ν) = 0　\[H\_ρRep^-\]

where H\_ρRep^- is: under DetDom\_ρ(ν), any generation factor of the declared organization that actually enters the formation of Rel\_ρ in evaluation ν belongs to some B ∈ MinSuff\_ρ(ν).

Proof

Suppose RateBearing(ν) = 1: the legal load-bearing state of the declared Law-bearing organization really enters the formation of Rel\_ρ in evaluation ν.

Step one: by GenFactorCover, the actual generation contribution through which the load-bearing passes comes from some generation factor a ∈ A\_ρ^gen(ν).

Step two: by SourceCover, there exists W\_i ∈ 𝓛\_P with a ∈ GenIn(W\_i, ν).

Step three: by Sound\_Adm, Cover\_Adm and MinSuffSolvable, MinSuff\_ρ(ν) exists and is obtainable relative to the real domain of legal variation. To obtain B ∈ MinSuff\_ρ(ν) with B ∩ GenIn(W\_i, ν) ≠ ∅, it is further needed that this factor a actually entering the formation belong to some minimal sufficient group. This is exactly the content of H\_ρRep^-. It cannot be inferred from the first two steps or from the coverage conditions: in joint and redundant bearing, a factor really taking part in generation may be unnecessary in every sufficient group containing it (its effect being wholly determined by other factors in the group), in which case it lies in no minimal sufficient group. Of this correspondence between "really entering the formation" at the structural layer and "member of a minimal sufficient group" at the formal layer, the upstream The Mathematical Form of Probability has proven only the direction from the latter to the former (its Theorem 7.2); what is needed here is the direction from the former to the latter.

Step four: under H\_ρRep^-, take a ∈ B ∈ MinSuff\_ρ(ν). Since RateBearing(ν) is real bearing in evaluation ν and not standby capacity, by ActualRealizationCover, B1–B4 can be checked and hold, and EvalRateBind^M(W\_i, ν, e\_ν) = 1.

Hence RateBearWitness^M(ν) = 1. □

Notes:

It is not required that all minimal sufficient groups contain factors of the declared organization, only that one exist intersecting its actual contribution; hence hot redundancy is allowed.

A cold standby does not actually enter the formation in that evaluation and cannot obtain the RateBearWitness of the current evaluation.

When an external mechanism bears independently, the declared organization is not judged to bear because it "has the capacity to bear".

Status of H\_ρRep^-: pending verification. It is the same class of reverse-representation proposition as H\_PAct-Rep of The Mathematical Form of Probability-Action, one on the qualification evaluation ν, the other on the current evaluation in reality ν\_t^cur; the two should be closed together in the reverse representation of The Mathematical Form of Probability.

Infinite minimal sufficient groups: if, in an infinite domain of generation factors, the existence or obtainability of minimal sufficient groups cannot be guaranteed, MinSuffSolvable ≠ 1, and the related determination is ⊥ (reason RateCoverOpen); it may not be written RateBearing = 0. This is only an exit from the complete determination domain, not a structural failure of probability.

Measure mode: when DomainEvalMode = measure, bearing over the whole rate domain uses the a.e. universal. A single null-measure failure point cannot breach bearing over the whole rate domain; a single null-measure success point cannot establish an existential conclusion; only a failure set of positive measure can make the measure-type universal fail. μ\_r is used only for the quantifiers of rate-domain evaluation and may not be mixed with the generation weights or measure μ\_{C,ν}.

Theorem Six　Theorem of the negative read-back of DomainRelativeRateBearing

DetDom\_3 is composed of D\_1 = D\_2 = 1 and the following items: the freezing, non-emptiness, non-vacuity and system-side grounding of the rate domain are decidable (M5, M6 of The Mathematical Form of Probability); the Rel\_ρ representation interface is usable (M7, M8); DetDom\_ρ(ν) holds for each evaluation and the outer quantifier is decidable. D\_1 = D\_2 = 1 is listed as a premise because rate bearing is entered only after the first two items are kept. Agreeing with PathDom of 2.3, the remaining premises are taken by failing sub-item: a 0 obtained from RateDomainQual^M requires only M5, M6; a 0 obtained from RelForm^M requires only M7, M8; a 0 obtained from rate-domain aggregation requires DetDom\_ρ(ν) of each relevant evaluation and the outer quantifier to be decidable. Then, when DomainRelativeRateBearing^M = 0:

if the 0 comes from a definite structural counter-witness of RateDomainQual^M (the freezing record shows modification after the fact, ℰ\_r is empty, there is no realizable system realization, the source of the realization does not belong to F, stitching across protocols), then DomainRelativeRateBearing = 0, carrying no condition;

if the 0 comes from a definite failure of RelForm^M with complete interfaces (the relative-generation relation cannot be formed on the generation side), then DomainRelativeRateBearing = 0, carrying no condition;

if the 0 comes from RateBearWitness^M\_{𝔯\_P} = 0 after complete rate-domain aggregation, that is, by the outer quantifier there are evaluations ν of non-zero measure (in set mode, at least one) with RateBearWitness^M(ν) = 0, then by Theorem Five RateBearing(ν) = 0 in these evaluations, structural-layer rate bearing fails over 𝔯\_P, and DomainRelativeRateBearing = 0, carrying H\_ρRep^-.

If only the source chain is unobtainable, AdmVar coverage is not closed, minimal sufficient groups are unobtainable, the measure quantifier is undecidable or the generation-side representation interface is insufficient, the reading is ⊥, not 0.

Proof: item-by-item correspondence by Definitions 3.29–3.31 of The Mathematical Form of Probability and Theorem Five. □

3.5 Complete representation of formal qualification

Structural zero and declaration zero

Read\_{N\_P}^M = 0 divides by source into two classes:

StructuralZero: the decisive 0 comes from a structural counter-witness at some layer, that is, the failures of Card, DistAll, DimQual in Theorem Three, the failures of the three bindings in Theorem Four, or the three failures in Theorem Six.

DeclarationZero: the decisive 0 comes only from the invalidity of the frozen declared instance, at present the 0 caused solely by RegValid^M = 0.

The formal reading is not changed; the two classes of zero are distinguished only at the determination layer. Only StructuralZero can be read back as N\_P = 0; DeclarationZero is not enough to read back a structural zero, and the final verdict is ⊥, reason DeclarationInvalid; re-declaration is required.

Theorem Seven　Theorem of the complete representation of formal qualification

(a) Positive (already established): Read\_{N\_P}^M = 1 ⇒ N\_P = 1　\[H\_LawRead of the witnesses used\]

(b) Negative: if Read\_{N\_P}^M = 0 is a StructuralZero, and the layer where the failure lies satisfies the corresponding determination domain (DetDom\_1, DetDom\_2 or DetDom\_3), then N\_P = 0. The conditions follow the failure path: at the first layer, from DimQual, it carries H^-\_M; at the third layer, from rate bearing, it carries H\_ρRep^-; otherwise it carries no condition.

(c) Under FullDetDom, with the declared instance conforming (no DeclarationZero appearing), and when H\_LawRead, H^-\_M and H\_ρRep^- are closed: Read\_{N\_P}^M = N\_P.

Proof

(a) is (7.5) of The Mathematical Form of Probability.

(b) By 2.1, Read = 0 comes from a decisive 0 at some layer, all layers before it being 1; by the premise it is a StructuralZero. When the failure is at the second layer, D\_1 = 1 already satisfies the premise of DetDom\_2; when the failure is at the third layer, D\_1 = D\_2 = 1 already satisfies the premise of DetDom\_3. By Theorems Three, Four and Six, that layer corresponds, under the corresponding determination domain, to a failure at the structural layer, carrying the conditions listed in each theorem.

(c) Under FullDetDom every layer is decidable and ⊥ does not appear. If N\_P = 1, the three structures all hold: by Theorem Two (through H^-\_M) the formal reading of the first layer is 1; by the contrapositive of Theorem Four (if the structure holds, no structural counter-witness appears), the second layer is not 0, and since FullDetDom guarantees that DetDom\_2 holds and ⊥ does not appear, it is 1; by Theorem Five (through H\_ρRep^-) the RateBearWitness^M of each evaluation is 1, and by the direct representation of RateDomainQual and RelForm, the third layer is 1; hence Read = 1. If N\_P = 0, by the contrapositive of (a) Read ≠ 1, and under FullDetDom Read ≠ ⊥, so Read = 0. □

3.6 Judging false what is false, in the negative direction: if judged 0, then false

Theorem Eight　Theorem of the validity of negative verdicts

Det\_P = 0 ⇒ Probability = 0　\[H2 ∧ H3^red; and, according to the failure path, H^-\_M or H\_ρRep^-\]

Proof

By 2.5, Det\_P = 0 implies that Read = 0 and is a StructuralZero, that the negative representation of the failure path has been closed, and that H2 ∧ H3^red have been closed. By Theorem Seven (b), N\_P = 0. By the contrapositive of the necessary direction of The General Form of Probability, under H2 ∧ H3^red, Probability = 0. □

If N\_P = 0 already holds while H2, H3^red are not closed, the final verdict is ⊥, reason OntologyBridgeOpen. If the H^-\_M or H\_ρRep^- needed by the failure path is not closed, the verdict is ⊥, reason UpstreamLawOpen or RateRepOpen respectively. If Read = 0 is only a DeclarationZero, the verdict is ⊥, reason DeclarationInvalid.

Meta-level note: by the contrapositive of the sufficient direction, ¬Probability ⇒ ¬N\_P can be obtained without carrying H, that is, a real non-probability necessarily has a structural failure. But this cannot replace the necessary-direction conditions the determiner needs to go from N\_P = 0 to Probability = 0.

## Section Four　Declaration of the Misjudgment Bound

No statistical misjudgment rate is introduced here. The discipline of determination is: where correctness cannot be guaranteed, fall back to ⊥.

Positive-verdict domain: by Theorem One, FalsePositive = 0, the condition being that the H\_LawRead of the witnesses used has been closed (this has already been written into the definition of Det\_P = 1).

Negative-verdict domain: by Theorem Eight, when H2 ∧ H3^red have been closed, and the H^-\_M or H\_ρRep^- needed by the failure path has been closed, FalseNegative = 0 (this has also been written into the definition of Det\_P = 0).

Where both cannot be had: under the current upstream state, H2, H3^red, H\_ρRep^- and the negative-end condition bundle H^-\_M of The Boundary Form of Law are not yet closed. Negative verdicts depending on them all fall back to ⊥, with OntologyBridgeOpen, RateRepOpen or UpstreamLawOpen attached. So the current determiner's range of coverage in the negative direction is smaller than in the positive direction: it does not misjudge, but there will be more ⊥. This is an explicitly declared misjudgment bound, not an error of the determiner.

The real boundary of determination is the decidable domain, not a statistical confidence interval.

## Section Five　Determination Theorem

### 5.1 Theorem of complete determination

Theorem Nine　Theorem of complete determination

Under FullDetDom, with the declared instance conforming, and when H\_LawRead, H^-\_M, H\_ρRep^-, H2, H3^red are all closed:

Probability = 1 ⇒ Det\_P = 1

Probability = 0 ⇒ Det\_P = 0

Combined with Theorems One and Eight:

Det\_P = Probability

holds on the two-valued qualification domain; outside the domain ⊥ is kept.

Proof

Probability = 1: by the necessary direction of The General Form of Probability (H2 ∧ H3^red), N\_P = 1; by Theorem Seven (c), Read = 1; the H\_LawRead of the witnesses used has been closed, so Det\_P = 1.

Probability = 0: by the contrapositive of the sufficient direction, N\_P = 0; by Theorem Seven (c), Read = 0; the declared instance conforms, so this 0 is a structural zero and not a declaration zero; the negative-representation conditions needed by the failure path and H2 ∧ H3^red have all been closed, so Det\_P = 0. □

It is not claimed that all physical systems fall into FullDetDom.

### 5.4 Breach methods

Wrong positive verdict: there exists Det\_P = 1 while Probability = 0.

Wrong negative verdict: with all the declared conditions of negative verdicts holding, there exists Det\_P = 0 while Probability = 1.

Breach of dimension-witness coverage: DimWitnessCover = 1 is declared, while in reality there exists a legal BearDim witness with DimQual^M = 0, and H^-\_M holds.

Breach of rate-bearing completeness: under DetDom\_ρ and H\_ρRep^-, real RateBearing = 1 while RateBearWitness^M = 0.

Overstepping from ⊥ to 0: 0 is signed while interfaces, source chains, minimal sufficient groups, the measure quantifier or search coverage are not closed.

Declaration standing in for reality: claiming that a structural witness does not exist in reality solely because of a missed or wrong registration.

Error of quantifier polarity: changing the existential polarity of RateBearWitness into universal, or the universal or a.e. aggregation over the rate domain into existential.

Overstepping qualification: writing normalization, positive rate, stable frequency or complete coverage of successors as conditions of probability qualification.

### 5.5 Regression tests

The following serve only as regression checks, not as general proofs.

Test 1　Positive case: two dimensions with Law-bearing, same position, same domain and same generation, the declared organization actually bearing relative generation, every layer decidable, H\_LawRead closed. Det\_P = 1.

Test 2　Single dimension: V\_P is completely enumerable, \|V\_P\| = 1. First layer 0, carrying no condition; when H2 ∧ H3^red are not closed, Det\_P = ⊥ (OntologyBridgeOpen); when closed, Det\_P = 0.

Test 3　Missed registration: 𝓛\_P misses the actual Law-bearing of some dimension, and DimWitnessCover is not closed. Det\_P = ⊥ (DimWitnessCoverOpen), not 0.

Test 3b　Invalid registration: some registration in 𝓛\_P has no corresponding witness, while DimQual^M of every dimension is 1. RegValid^M = 0, the formal reading is 0, a declaration zero. Det\_P = ⊥ (DeclarationInvalid); re-declaration is required.

Test 4　Stitching across protocols: the evidence comes from different DomainProtocol. Second layer 0, carrying no condition; the final verdict follows H2 ∧ H3^red.

Test 5　External selector: an external mechanism decides the whole rate, and the declared organization only executes outcomes. RateBearWitness^M = 0 in every evaluation; while H\_ρRep^- is not closed, Det\_P = ⊥ (RateRepOpen).

Test 6　Hot redundancy: two mechanisms each suffice to bear, and a factor of the declared organization is in some minimal sufficient group and actually bound. Third layer 1, Det\_P = 1.

Test 7　Cold standby: the declared organization is only a standby. It does not obtain RateBearWitness in that evaluation; a negative verdict follows H\_ρRep^-.

Test 8　Infinite factor domain: minimal sufficient groups unobtainable. Det\_P = ⊥ (RateCoverOpen).

Test 9　Null-measure failure in measure mode: RateBearWitness^M = 0 on a null-measure set of evaluations, 1 a.e. elsewhere. Third layer 1.

Test 10　Stable frequency: there is only a stable empirical frequency, without Y\_R interfaces. Det\_P = ⊥ (InputOpen); determination by frequency is not permitted.
