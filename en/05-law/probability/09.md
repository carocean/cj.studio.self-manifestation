# The Limit Form of Probability

On the already closed structure of probability qualification, to carry out a limit review along legal comparison directions declared in advance, settling two things: whether probability qualification is still kept when the numerical, statistical, coverage, random and representational properties usually taken as "probabilistic" retreat to their extremes; and where the boundary that probability qualification really cannot cross lies, and into what it degenerates after crossing.

The conclusion is: zero rate, isotropy, complete determinacy and an unreadable value are endpoints all compatible with probability qualification; incomplete coverage of the complete successors by the declared dimensions does not constitute a qualification boundary, while zero coverage over the whole rate domain cannot keep N\_P = 1; whether coverage can approach zero arbitrarily closely in real realizations is at present open; within the current conditionally closed domain (under H2 ∧ H3^red), the hard bound of probability qualification falls on the three qualification structures, and crossing it has only three basic degenerations. The limit invariant of probability is not the probability value but the structure of probability qualification.

## Section One　Basic Setup

### 1.1 Frozen upstream conclusions

N\_P := D ∧ B ∧ R, where D := MultiLawDimension, B := CommonContinuationBind, R := DomainRelativeRateBearing. The common binding is written B so as not to be confused with the Continuation-Position C.

The sufficient direction (The Necessity Form of Probability): N\_P ⇒ Probability, Status = Established, carrying no H.

The necessary direction (The General Form of Probability): H2 ∧ H3^red ⇒ \[ Probability ⇒ N\_P \].

Combined: H2 ∧ H3^red ⇒ \[ Probability ⇔ N\_P \]. The conditions of the two directions differ: the entry direction carries no H, the exit direction carries H2 ∧ H3^red. This asymmetry is not washed out below.

The General Form of Probability has handed down to the Limit Form the task of determining the qualification boundaries along pre-declared legal comparison directions, including the exit of dimension qualification, the failure of common binding and the failure of rate bearing; and it has prohibited automatically writing zero rate, RateRead = ⊥ and incomplete coverage as qualification boundaries.

1.2 The frozen qualification semantic domain

Write Σ\_P^cur for the semantic domain of current probability qualification frozen by the currently sealed On Self-Manifestation and Probability Group-Form. It is a limitation of the domain of proof, not an ontological predicate, a field quantity, a fourth qualification or an H.

What is called below "hard bound", "not further reducible", "exact qualification boundary" holds only relative to Σ\_P^cur. If a new independent duty appears later and satisfies the reopening condition of The General Form of Probability, upstream and this text are upgraded together under control.

### 1.3 Limit comparison protocol

Π\_Lim = (Σ\_P^cur, A, Ord\_A, E\_A, Fix\_A)

A: the axis of the present comparison. Every limit conclusion must declare in advance which axis is compared: rate value, zero rate of a single dimension, anisotropy, complete coverage, randomness and determinacy, numerical readability, number of dimensions, or some qualification-structure item. One may not move several axes at once and then attribute the result to one of them.

Ord\_A: the direction of degeneration of that axis, for example the rate value decreasing to zero, anisotropy decreasing to zero, coverage decreasing to zero.

E\_A: the declared endpoint or boundary (written E\_A so as not to be confused with the common binding B). Four kinds must be distinguished: the endpoint reachable; the endpoint only arbitrarily approachable; an open soft range; crossing the qualification boundary.

Fix\_A: the identities and protocols that must be fixed in comparison. The meaning of probability is kept in one and the same Σ\_P^cur; when the comparison axis is not the number of dimensions, V\_P is not added to or deleted from; when comparing within one candidate, the Continuation-Position C is not changed, nor the frozen rate domain 𝔯\_P; when the comparison family consists of different candidates, the protocol of the comparison family must be frozen in advance, the Continuation-Position and rate domain of each member being frozen before testing, and members may not be selected or domains re-cut after the results are seen; DomainProtocol is not crossed; the resolution protocol is not changed; unfavorable results are not deleted after the fact; different conditions and different generation events are not stitched into one limit sequence.

### 1.4 Limit direction and limit conditions

Limit direction: along Ord\_A, push the comparison axis toward its degenerate end.

Limit conditions: every member of the comparison family {χ\_α} satisfies Fix\_A and lies under the qualification semantics of Σ\_P^cur; the comparison family consists of legal candidates, legal instances or legal representations.

### 1.5 Comparison is not dynamics

{χ\_α} ≠ χ(t\_0) → χ(t\_1)

What is compared is a family of legal candidates or representations, not how one and the same system changes from one state to another. Here it is not stated how the rate decreases, how some dimension appears or disappears, or whether qualification is kept or recovered; these belong to The General Form of Probability-Dynamics, The Mathematical Form of Probability-Dynamics, The Source Form of Probability and The Stability Form of Probability.

LimitComparison ≠ ProbabilityDyn

### 1.6 The meaning of reachable

"Endpoint reachable" here means that the endpoint is compatible with N\_P = 1 in qualification semantics: none of the three structural definitions of N\_P excludes the endpoint, so a legal candidate at that endpoint with N\_P = 1 is certainly a probability. It does not assert that some specific physical realization already exists; the construction of realizations belongs to The Construction Form of the Probability Operating Substrate. "Endpoint only approachable" means that there exist legal candidates arbitrarily close to the endpoint with N\_P = 1, while the endpoint itself is incompatible with N\_P = 1; this is an existential conclusion and must have real qualification witnesses. "Open soft range" means that some non-endpoint range of degeneration does not constitute a qualification boundary (its values being compatible with N\_P = 1), but it has not been proven that there exist candidates with N\_P = 1 able to approach the endpoint all the way.

## Section Two　Inevitability Argument

Why must the limit review fall on the three items D, B, R, and not on rate value, frequency, coverage or randomness?

First, the side of soft limits. N\_P is the sufficient core of qualification currently closed by this group form. By the sufficient direction, any comparison family that keeps N\_P = 1 member by member keeps Probability = 1 member by member, wherever the comparison axis goes; this side carries no H.

Second, the side of the exhaustion of hard bounds. Only under H2 ∧ H3^red does the exit of qualification necessarily fall as N\_P = 0: then Probability ⇔ N\_P, so any comparison going from inside qualification to outside it must make at least one of D, B, R change from 1 to 0, and a peripheral property can become a qualification boundary only by breaking one of D, B, R. Without carrying H, one can assert only that keeping N\_P keeps qualification, not that the exit of qualification must pass through D, B, R.

Third, therefore, under H2 ∧ H3^red, any comparison axis has only two possibilities: its degenerate end is compatible with N\_P = 1, in which case it is a soft limit and qualification is kept; or its degenerate end forces some item of D, B, R to fail, in which case it crosses the hard bound, and the cause of crossing is that structural item, not the axis itself.

This bifurcation is unavoidable within Σ\_P^cur under H2 ∧ H3^red.

## Section Three　Core Definitions

### 3.1 Soft limit

A property not belonging to N\_P whose degenerate end (or every value approaching the degenerate end) is compatible with N\_P = 1. Divided into:

ReachableEndpoint: the endpoint itself is compatible with N\_P = 1.

ApproachableEndpoint: for every ε \> 0, there exists a legal candidate within ε of the endpoint with N\_P = 1, while the endpoint itself is incompatible with N\_P = 1. This is an existential conclusion. At present there is no proven instance.

OpenSoftRange (open soft range): the values within some non-endpoint range of degeneration are compatible with N\_P = 1 and do not constitute a qualification boundary; but whether there exist real candidates with N\_P = 1 approaching the endpoint all the way has not been proven.

### 3.2 Hard bound

The structural qualification boundary at which Probability exits from 1. Within Σ\_P^cur, and when H2 ∧ H3^red hold, the current hard bound is characterized by the failure of the three structures of N\_P. Without carrying this condition, only N\_P = 1 ⇒ Probability = 1 may be used, and the exit of qualification may not be signed unconditionally from N\_P = 0.

### 3.3 Auxiliary comparison quantities

The following are only auxiliary quantities for comparison, not ontological quantities of probability:

anisotropy (two-dimensional weight mode): A\_ρ := \|w\_1 − w\_2\| / (w\_1 + w\_2);

complete coverage: Coverage := Σ\_i ρ\_i^full, where ρ\_i^full = w\_i / M\_C, M\_C being the complete successor-generation mass (Definition 3.20 of The Mathematical Form of Probability). Coverage is defined only in weight mode and when M\_C \> 0; in struct mode this quantity does not exist, and no comparison along this axis is made.

3.4 Classes of degeneration beyond the boundary

The following are limit classification labels, not new ontological predicates:

Deg\_D: D = 0.

Deg\_B: D = 1, B = 0.

Deg\_R: D = B = 1, R = 0.

## Section Four　Structural Characterization

4.1 The qualification region and its boundary

Within Σ\_P^cur, and under H2 ∧ H3^red, the qualification region { Probability = 1 } and { N\_P = 1 } are one and the same set. So the entry into and exit from qualification are both characterized by the three structures of N\_P. Without carrying H, one can assert only { N\_P = 1 } ⊆ { Probability = 1 }.

4.2 Partition of the region beyond the boundary

{ N\_P = 0 } is partitioned exactly into the three classes Deg\_D, Deg\_B, Deg\_R, pairwise disjoint and covering everything:

N\_P = 0 if and only if at least one of D, B, R is 0. Classify by the first failing item: D = 0 is Deg\_D; D = 1 and B = 0 is Deg\_B; D = B = 1 and R = 0 is Deg\_R. The criteria of the three classes are mutually exclusive and exhaust every case in which at least one item is 0. This agrees with the three-layer order of solving of The Mathematical Form of Probability: a later item has meaning only after the earlier item holds.

4.3 Equivalence classes on the soft-limit axes

In weight mode, the relative-generation relation is a positive-scaling equivalence class \[w\]\_ρ (Definitions 3.17–3.19 of The Mathematical Form of Probability). Scaling the weights of the dimensions proportionally as a whole only changes the representative of one and the same relative-generation relation; \[w\]\_ρ is unchanged. So the absolute magnitude of the weights Σ\_i w\_i, absent an additionally frozen scale, is not a representation-invariant quantity and does not serve as a limit axis; what is meaningful is the ratio relative to the complete successor-generation mass, Coverage = Σ\_i w\_i / M\_C. It is the comparison quantity of the complete-coverage axis below.

## Section Five　Main Conclusion

5.1 Theorem One　Theorem of the preservation of soft limits

Under Π\_Lim:

(i) zero rate of a single dimension, zero anisotropy, complete determinacy and an unreadable value — the four endpoints are all ReachableEndpoint;

(ii) complete coverage: 0 \< Coverage \< 1 is an OpenSoftRange, and partial coverage does not constitute a qualification boundary;

(iii) Coverage = 0 over the whole rate domain (universally or a.e. according to DomainEvalMode) is incompatible with N\_P = 1.

Whether coverage can be approached to zero arbitrarily closely by real candidates with N\_P = 1 is not concluded here and is listed as an open question.

So none of the properties above is a hard bound of current probability qualification.

Proof

(i) Zero rate of a single dimension. Let V\_P = {v\_1, v\_2}, ρ = (1, 0). D requires \|V\_P\| ≥ 2, the dimensions distinguishable, and every dimension obtaining formal Law-bearing (the definition of MultiLawDimension in The General Form of Probability), and does not require the rates of the dimensions to be positive; B requires same position, same domain and same generation relation, and does not involve rate values; R requires the organization to bear the relative-generation relation, and any proportion outside the zero class is a legal relative-generation relation; (1, 0) is not the zero class. None of the three excludes ρ(v\_2) = 0. So this endpoint is compatible with N\_P = 1; a candidate at this endpoint with N\_P = 1 is, by the sufficient direction, a probability. The Necessity Form of Probability, 9.2, already has the same conclusion.

Zero anisotropy. Take w = (1, 1), A\_ρ = 0. None of D, B, R requires the rate values to be unequal; The General Form of Probability did not obtain Probability ⇒ Anisotropy. So compatible.

Complete determinacy. Let x = a ⇒ v\_1, x = b ⇒ v\_2, single executions being wholly deterministic. D and B are unrelated to whether execution is deterministic; R asks by whom the generation proportion of x and the relative generation to the dimensions are borne, not whether execution is predictable. When the proportion of x is formed by the organization itself, R can hold. So compatible. The Necessity Form of Probability, 9.3, already has the same conclusion. If the proportion of x is wholly decided by a mechanism outside the organization, what fails is R, not "being too deterministic".

Unreadable value. The Mathematical Form of Probability allows RelRepMode = struct: the relative-generation relation has only a structural representation R\_ν^struct, without legal numerical weights. R requires the relative-generation relation to be borne by the organization, not that the relation be readable numerically. So NumericRateRead = ⊥ is compatible with N\_P = 1.

(ii) Partial coverage. None of the three structures of N\_P requires the declared dimensions to cover all successors: D and B do not involve coverage; R requires the relative-generation relation to be borne by the organization, not that this relation exhaust all successors. The General Form of Probability did not obtain Probability ⇒ FullCoverage; The Necessity Form of Probability explicitly excludes "whether all successors are covered" from a fourth qualification; The Mathematical Form of Probability already allows Σ\_i ρ\_i^full \< 1, and prescribes that the residue outside the domain may not be filled in as an unknown dimension, a stopping dimension or any other probability dimension. So 0 \< Coverage \< 1 is compatible with N\_P = 1: a candidate in partial coverage with N\_P = 1 is certainly a probability, and Coverage \< 1 cannot imply ¬Probability.

Here only a statement of compatibility is made, in the same terms as the reachable endpoints of (i); it does not assert that for some specific coverage value there exists a real candidate with N\_P = 1.

Why it is not further asserted that the infimum of coverage is 0: that would require, for every ε \> 0, a witness with N\_P = 1 and 0 \< Coverage \< ε. All three items of N\_P contain content of real structure; R in particular requires the rate domain to have system-side actual generation grounding; generation weights and legal generation realizations must be given by the field system or the operating substrate (The Mathematical Form of Probability). One cannot, inside a mode without physical realization, directly prescribe that the dimensions have obtained Law-bearing and the rate is really borne, and thereupon sign N\_P = 1. So, before The Construction Form of the Probability Operating Substrate is completed, there may still logically exist some 0 \< c\* \< 1 such that all real candidates with N\_P = 1 have Coverage ≥ c\*; such a lower bound, even if it exists, is not a defining item of qualification, only a fact of the space of physical realizations. Whether it exists is handed to that text to construct: if, for every ε \> 0, a real legal realization can be constructed with N\_P = 1 and 0 \< Coverage \< ε, the coverage axis is upgraded to ApproachableEndpoint.

(iii) The endpoint of zero coverage. Suppose that in weight mode every evaluation has M\_C(ν) \> 0, and that, according to DomainEvalMode (all ν in set mode, a.e. in measure mode), Coverage(ν) = 0. Since the terms are non-negative, Σ\_i ρ\_i^full(ν) = 0 implies every ρ\_i^full(ν) = 0, that is, every w\_i(ν) = 0, so Rel\_ρ(ν) = \[0\]\_ρ. Then the relative generation over the whole qualification-evaluation domain is the zero class, exactly the case of 9.1 of The Necessity Form of Probability: no generation factor takes part in forming relative generation, R = 0. So zero coverage over the whole rate domain is incompatible with N\_P = 1.

Coverage = 0 at only one evaluation point is not enough to imply R = 0 and is not within this conclusion. □

Carrying no H: Theorem One invokes only the sufficient direction and upstream definitions.

5.2 Theorem Two　Theorem of the hard bound of current qualification

Within Σ\_P^cur:

Entry direction: N\_P = 1 ⇒ Probability = 1. Status = Established.

Exit direction: H2 ∧ H3^red ∧ N\_P = 0 ⇒ Probability = 0. Status = Conditional.

So under H2 ∧ H3^red, the qualification hard bound is characterized exactly by the three structures D, B, R of N\_P.

Proof

The entry direction is the sufficient direction of The Necessity Form of Probability. The exit direction is the contrapositive of the necessary direction of The General Form of Probability. Together, the qualification region coincides with the region where N\_P = 1 (4.1), and the entry into and exit from qualification are both characterized by the three structures of N\_P. □

"Exactly" here holds only within Σ\_P^cur (see Section Six).

5.3 Theorem Three　Theorem of degeneration beyond the boundary

Under H2 ∧ H3^red, every candidate crossing the hard bound is not a probability and belongs to exactly one of the following three classes.

Deg\_D　Collapse of the dimensional base (D = 0). Including at least three kinds:

single-dimension collapse: \|V\_P\| \< 2, degenerating into a single-dimension continuation Law or a single-path organization;

collapse of distinguishability: some two dimensions are indistinguishable under the current resolution protocol, the so-called different dimensions being only repeated or overlapping labels;

collapse of Law-bearing: some declared dimension has no formal Law-bearing, degenerating into a bare candidate direction or an externally added rate label.

What the three lose in common is "the multi-dimensional base, distinguishable and Law-bearing".

Deg\_B　Dissolution of common binding (D = 1, B = 0). Including different Continuation-Positions, different frozen generation domains, cross-condition stitching, different generation regimes, and different generation events stitched into one. After degeneration, several Law-bearings can each exist, but they no longer answer one and the same continuation question, do not belong to one and the same frozen rate domain, and are not mutually exclusive successors of one and the same occasion. The result is a stitched collection of Law-bearing processes, not one unified probability. The duty-removal review of The General Form of Probability has already shown: cancel B, and the cases of parallel processes and cross-condition stitching come back in.

Deg\_R　Failure of the bearing of relative generation (D = B = 1, R = 0). Every dimension obtains Law-bearing and is at one Continuation-Position, in one frozen rate domain and in one generation relation, but R does not hold. By the composition of R (Definitions 3.29–3.31 of The Mathematical Form of Probability, the three sources of failure in Theorem Six of The Determination Form of Probability), there are at least four typical subtypes:

ineligibility of the rate domain: the advance freezing, non-emptiness, non-vacuity or system-side grounding of the rate domain fails; whatever failures of this kind have already made the common binding B fail (for example stitching across protocols) are assigned by the first failing item to Deg\_B; only cases where B still holds while the rate-domain qualification of R fails are taken in here;

failure of relation formation: the relative-generation relation cannot be formed on the generation side;

zero-class degeneration: the relative generation over the whole qualification-evaluation domain is the zero class \[0\]\_ρ (The Necessity Form of Probability, 9.1);

externalized bearing: the relative generation among the dimensions is decided by a selection mechanism outside the organization, and the declared organization only executes the selected outcome; the result is an organization of "external selection — internal execution". The duty-removal review of The General Form of Probability has already shown: cancel R, and the case of the external selector comes back in.

What the four subtypes lose in common is "the relative-generation relation borne by this organization itself and formed in a legal rate domain". Externalized bearing is a typical subtype of Deg\_R, not a synonym for it.

Proof

By 4.2, candidates with N\_P = 0 belong to exactly one of the three classes. By the exit direction of Theorem Two, under H2 ∧ H3^red none of them is a probability. The degeneration results of each class are given by the definition of the corresponding qualification item and by the failure cases that The General Form of Probability shows come back in after that item's duty is removed. □

The three classes correspond one to one with the three structural items of N\_P. It is not claimed here that they are absolutely exhaustive in all possible future theories.

5.4 Subsidiary proposition　Resource limits

The following two are limitations of resources, not qualification boundaries:

ResourceLimit ≠ QualificationBoundary

(a) Conditional-rate mode. If Σ\_i ρ\_i^cond = 1, for any ε \> 0 write N\_{≥ε} := \|{ i: ρ\_i^cond ≥ ε }\|; then N\_{≥ε} ≤ ⌊1/ε⌋. Otherwise the sum of conditional rates would exceed 1.

This shows only that the number of dimensions having a uniform positive lower bound of rate has a resource upper bound. It does not show that \|V\_P\| itself cannot grow, since v\_i ∈ V\_P does not imply ρ(v\_i) \> 0.

(b) The sub-domain compatible with the main-book continuation rate. Only when the main-book Pr(q,t) can enter the formal rate representation through a legal projection π\_V: q ↦ v\_i may w\_i = Σ\_{π\_V(q) = v\_i} Pr(q,t) be taken. If in addition Σ\_q Pr(q,t) ≤ Λ(t), then Σ\_i w\_i ≤ Λ(t), whence N\_{≥ε} := \|{ i: w\_i ≥ ε }\| ≤ ⌊Λ(t)/ε⌋.

The Mathematical Form of Probability prescribes: the old Pr(q,t) can enter Rel\_ρ^M only through the legal bridge, Probability = Pr may not be written, and Σ Pr ≤ Λ may not be taken as a normalization axiom of probability. So (b) holds only within that compatible sub-domain and is not a boundary of all probability.

the current exit of qualification is characterized exactly by the first failing item among D, B, R (4.2, Theorem Three).

## Section Eight　Interpretation (Not Part of the Formal Structure)

The following only explains the conclusions above; it takes no part in the proof and bears no load.

Probability is usually understood as a number: a number between 0 and 1, summing to 1, appearing as a stable frequency in many repetitions. By the conclusions here, all of these can retreat to extremes while probability remains: the rate of one direction can be zero; the directions can be wholly equal; the system can be wholly deterministic; the rate can be not readable as a value at all; the declared directions can occupy only part of all successors. Probability does not thereby vanish.

Within the current conditionally closed domain (under H2 ∧ H3^red), there are only three places where probability vanishes: there are no distinguishable multiple directions borne by Laws; these directions no longer answer one and the same continuation question; the relative generation among the directions is no longer borne by this organization itself.

So the "limit" of probability lies not in its value but in its structure.

### 9.4 Breach methods

The breach of a Limit Form is: the limit conditions already hold, while the asserted limit property does not appear.

Wrong boundary at a soft end: some endpoint is claimed compatible with N\_P = 1, while it can be proven that some item of N\_P excludes that endpoint.

Wrong boundary of partial coverage: it can be proven that some item of the current qualification semantics implies Coverage = 1, or implies Coverage ≥ c\_0 (for some c\_0 \> 0).

Misjudgment of zero coverage: if it can be proven that N\_P = 1 is still possible when Coverage = 0 over the whole rate domain, then Theorem One (iii) is breached.

Counterexample inside the hard bound: under H2 ∧ H3^red, there exists Probability = 1 while N\_P = 0.

Counterexample outside the hard bound: there exists N\_P = 1 while Probability = 0. This at the same time directly breaches The Necessity Form of Probability.

Error in the classification of degeneration: some candidate is claimed to belong to some subtype of Deg\_R, while the declared organization in fact still bears relative generation in a legal rate domain; or the partition into three classes overlaps or omits.

Contamination of the comparison protocol: the limit family obtains its limit result only by changing C, changing 𝔯\_P, changing DomainProtocol, changing the resolution protocol, deleting dimensions after the fact or stitching across conditions.

Smuggling in dynamics: writing the comparison family as a real temporal evolution of one and the same system, and thereupon asserting causes of formation, preservation or recovery.

Overstepping into absolute uniqueness: writing the exact qualification boundary within Σ\_P^cur as an eternal and unique ontological boundary of future theory.

The following serve only as regression checks, not as general proofs.

Test 3　Wholly deterministic execution, the proportion formed by the organization itself. Qualification kept.

Test 5　Scaling the weights as a whole w → a·w (a \> 0). Only the representative changes; \[w\]\_ρ is unchanged; this does not constitute limit degeneration.

Test 6　Partial coverage Coverage = 1/2, N\_P = 1. Qualification kept; partial coverage is not a qualification boundary. When Coverage = 0 over the whole rate domain, it falls into the zero class, the zero-class degeneration subtype of Deg\_R. Whether coverage can approach zero arbitrarily closely is not determined.

Test 6b　Coverage = 0 at a single evaluation point, the other evaluations normal. Not enough to imply R = 0; in measure mode, if that point has null measure, the rate-domain aggregation is unaffected.

Test 7　\|V\_P\| = 1. Deg\_D, single-dimension collapse.

Test 8　The realization domains of two dimensions overlap. Deg\_D, collapse of distinguishability.

Test 9　Stitching across frozen protocols. Deg\_B.

Test 10　An external selector decides the whole rate. The externalized-bearing subtype of Deg\_R.

Test 11　The comparison family obtains its limit only by changing the Continuation-Position. Contamination of the protocol, not a legal limit.
