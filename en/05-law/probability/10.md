# The Source Form of Probability

A candidate Probability first obtains formal qualification within a declared history window: which stretch of actual history bears this first formation as Probability? If several stretches of history can each legally bear it, how far at most can the theory determine it?

The conclusion is: the source of a probability is not the final probability value, not a stable frequency, not the qualification item that closed last, nor any counterfactual necessary condition; it is a stretch of actual history that bore the real formation of the first qualification closure of this frozen candidate, and this history must pass the certification both of the actual generation chain and of legal counterfactual minimal sufficiency. The source may be non-unique; not having found a source is not having no source.

## Section One　Declaration of the Object

### 1.1 Form-kind and the single task

Source Form, auxiliary form, an independent text. By the specification of the Source Form in Part Four, Chapter Four of the Canon Chapter: to prove by what actual process an already established structure is generated, rather than by what it is given.

Only the source of the first holding of probability is studied here. Not studied: whether there is currently probability qualification, how it currently acts, how an already formed probability changes, the loss and recovery of qualification, whether it is reachable, how to construct the first runnable probability, holding again after 1 → 0 → 1, identity continuity across hosts through migration or copying.

### 1.2 Frozen upstream structure

N\_P := D ∧ B ∧ R, D := MultiLawDimension, B := CommonContinuationBind, R := DomainRelativeRateBearing. The common binding is written B so as not to be confused with the Continuation-Position C.

The sufficient direction: N\_P ⇒ Probability, Status = Established, carrying no H.

The necessary direction: H2 ∧ H3^red ⇒ \[ Probability ⇒ N\_P \]. Only under H2 ∧ H3^red, Probability ⇔ N\_P. The conditions of the two directions are asymmetric, and this is not washed out here.

Below, mainly the sufficient direction, carrying no H, is used.

### 1.3 Cross-time candidate

The upstream static candidate is χ\_P = (F, t, 𝒫, C, 𝔯\_P, V\_P, 𝓛\_P). The Source Form cannot freeze some single moment as the identity of the candidate, so a cross-time frozen declaration is written:

χ̄\_P = (F, 𝒫, C, 𝔯\_P, V\_P, 𝓛\_P)

The candidate instance at an evaluation point is χ\_P(t) = (χ̄\_P, t). This is only the manner of declaring a cross-time source protocol, not a new ontology of probability.

In one source protocol, F, 𝒫, C, 𝔯\_P, V\_P, 𝓛\_P are all frozen in advance. After history is deleted, V\_P may not be added to or deleted from, and C, 𝔯\_P, the set of Law-bearing candidates or the generation regime may not be changed, nor another candidate be re-declared.

### 1.4 Source protocol

Π^P\_src = (χ̄\_P, X\_0, H\_obs, Bg^Π, Ops^Π, Dyn^Π, Judge\_P^Π, Judge\_N^Π, T\_Π, Gran^Π)

χ̄\_P: the frozen cross-time candidate declaration.

X\_0: the declared initial state of the source window.

H\_obs: the ordered history actually observed.

Bg^Π: the frozen background (written Bg^Π so as not to be confused with the common binding B).

Ops^Π: the permitted operations of history reduction — deletion, masking, Mask, suppression and the like — frozen in advance.

Dyn^Π: the legal dynamics of the field system or operating substrate.

Judge\_P^Π: the formal three-valued determination of probability qualification, Judge\_P^Π(t) ∈ {1, 0, ⊥}.

Judge\_N^Π: the structural reading of the qualification core, Judge\_N^Π(t) = (D\_t, B\_t, R\_t), N\_{P,t} = D\_t ∧ B\_t ∧ R\_t.

T\_Π: the finite set of evaluation points frozen in advance. "First" is defined only relative to this set of points; evaluation points may not be added or deleted after the results are seen.

Gran^Π: the frozen granularity of division of historical occurrences; it may not be changed after the results are out.

1.5 The event of first formation as Probability

Genesis\_P^Π(χ̄\_P, τ) ∈ {1, 0, ⊥}

1: Judge\_P^Π(τ) = 1, and Judge\_P^Π(t) = 0 for every t \< τ in T\_Π.

0: Judge\_P^Π(τ) = 0; or there exists t \< τ with Judge\_P^Π(t) = 1. An earlier definite 1 suffices to deny "first", even if other earlier points are ⊥.

⊥: all other cases, for example the point τ undecidable, or the point τ being 1 while some key earlier evaluation point is ⊥ and there is no earlier definite 1.

### 1.6 Conditions of the first event

Probability = 0 is not inferred anew here from N\_P = 0. Genesis\_P is the entry fact of the study of source. If the Probability = 0 at some time points is taken from the Det\_P of The Determination Form of Probability, that specific negative-end certificate carries on its own H2 ∧ H3^red, and the H^-\_M or H\_ρRep^- corresponding to its failure path. These are conditions of upstream determination certificates, not H added here.

## Section Two　Exclusion of the Presumed Source

The following are objects easily taken as sources that cannot serve as sources. Each item is a non-implication at the layer of definition: the source is certified by PGenesisSource of Section Four, whose second and third items take history as argument, and the final state, values, correlation and counterfactual necessity cannot satisfy it on their own.

W-P1　Inferring backwards from the final-state probability: Probability\_τ = 1 ⇏ 𝔖^Π\_{P,src} ≠ ∅.

W-P2　Inferring backwards from the final-state qualification core: D\_τ = B\_τ = R\_τ = 1 ⇏ GenesisSource. A qualification certificate is not a source certificate.

W-P3　The item closing last passing as the whole source: LastClose\_τ = X ⇏ SourceOnly(X).

W-P4　Final probability value, anisotropy, stable frequency: FinalRate, Anisotropy and StableFrequency none imply source.

W-P5　Temporal precedence, synchrony, correlation, prediction: h precedes τ ⇏ SourceCarrier(h); Corr(h, Genesis\_P) ⇏ SourceCarrier(h).

W-P6　"Having received reward and punishment" automatically passing as source: RewardPunishmentOccurred ⇏ GenesisSource. Reward and punishment must really enter the actual generation chain and bear load in minimal sufficiency.

W-P7　Background enabling: CounterfactualNecessity ⇏ GenesisSource.

W-P8　Illegal deletion: the history after deleting h cannot be legally replayed (Replayable = 0) ⇏ NecessarySourceCarrier(h) = 1.

W-P9　External installation passing as formation within the field: ExternalInstall\_P ⇏ FieldBorn\_P. External installation can be a source, but is not thereby formation within the field.

Four distinctions are thereby formed:

LastClosure ≠ GenesisSource

StructuralNecessity ≠ GenesisSource

FinalProbabilityValue ≠ GenesisSource

CounterfactualNecessity ≠ GenesisSource

The grounds of each: LastClosure ≠ GenesisSource by Theorem Three; StructuralNecessity ≠ GenesisSource by Theorem Four, where "structural necessity" means the component items of the qualification core and, under H2 ∧ H3^red, also the necessary qualification items of probability, neither reading implying source; FinalProbabilityValue ≠ GenesisSource by a corollary of Theorem Two: the final rate value, stable frequency and anisotropy do not even constitute the complete final-state qualification core, and Theorem Two has proven that even the complete final-state qualification core does not imply source, so these weaker final-state quantities still less give a source certificate independently; CounterfactualNecessity ≠ GenesisSource by the requirement of the actual generation chain in 3.6: counterfactually necessary without actual formation and end binding on the same witness, ActualPGenesisLink is not 1.

## Section Three　Characterization of the Generative Process

### 3.1 Representation of history

Actual history is written as an ordered finite object:

H\_obs = (e\_1, e\_2, …, e\_n), n \< ∞

It may not be written as an unordered set. Every occurrence keeps at least its temporal order, number of times, interval of duration, actual content of occurrence and the object acted on.

What belongs to the process of formation within the field keeps in addition, according to the structures actually present, execution, trace, sedimentation, formation, and the connection with the bearing of D, B, R at the endpoint. What belongs to the process of external installation keeps external editing events, the objects edited, the states before and after editing, and the connection by which editing enters the change of the qualification structure. Fields that do not exist may not be fabricated for completeness of format.

### 3.2 Reduction domain

A protocol-relative reduction is written H′ ⪯\_Π H: there exists a finite sequence of operations permitted by Ops^Π that reduces H to H′. Each operation can only delete an existing event or weaken the effect of an existing event; it does not change its original temporal position; its execution and result can be reviewed.

Reduction may not change X\_0, χ̄\_P, Dyn^Π, the probability determination or the definition of the qualification core; may not add new events; may not change the order of the remaining events; may not covertly supply the final state; may not re-cut C, 𝔯\_P, V\_P after the fact.

Strict reduction is written H′ ≺\_Π H. It is required that ⪯\_Π constitute a frozen finite partial order on the declared reduction domain; this is an admission condition of the protocol, not automatically implied by the operational prescriptions above. The minimality below is relative to this partial order, not to ordinary set inclusion.

### 3.3 The reduction domain is finite

v1.0 requires \|H\_obs\| \< ∞ and \|ℛ\_Π(H\_obs)\| \< ∞, where ℛ\_Π(H\_obs) = { H′ \| H′ ⪯\_Π H\_obs }. Continuous Mask intensities, infinitely fine granularity or infinite descending chains, if no discretization has been frozen, do not enter the v1.0 determination domain of minimal sufficient sources. This is a limitation of the working domain of the protocol, not a restriction of the probability ontology.

3.3a The domain of candidate source histories

All candidate source histories H\_s are taken from the frozen reduction domain:

𝓗^Π\_src := ℛ\_Π(H\_obs) ∖ { H\_∅ }

so H\_s ∈ 𝓗^Π\_src ⇒ H\_s ⪯\_Π H\_obs. Source candidates can be obtained only from the actual history H\_obs by reductions permitted by the protocol; substitute processes not occurring in the actual history, supplemented processes or another possible history may not serve as source candidates of this first formation as Probability. H\_∅ serves only as the background control of 4.3 and does not enter the domain of source candidates. By 3.3, \|𝓗^Π\_src\| \< ∞.

### 3.4 Legal replay

Replayable\_P^Π(H) ∈ {1, 0, ⊥}

1: there exists a legal replay complying with the complete frozen protocol. It keeps the same X\_0, the same χ̄\_P, the same identity rules, the same legal dynamics, the same permitted inputs, the same determination and evaluation protocols; only the historical events not kept undergo the deletion or masking permitted by the protocol, and then the system evolves on its own according to law.

0: the so-called replay can be completed only by changing the candidate, changing the host rules, changing the dynamics, changing the determination, changing the qualification definition, adding undeclared events or covertly supplying the final state.

⊥: it currently cannot be settled whether the replay is legal.

Replayable = 0 ≠ counterfactual failure

Cannot delete ≠ deleted and fails

### 3.5 Sufficiency of a source

Evaluated only after Replayable\_P^Π(H) = 1:

Suff\_P^Π(H; χ̄\_P, τ) ∈ {1, 0, ⊥}

1: when legally replayed to the evaluation endpoint τ of the actual first event, N^{Replay(H)}\_{P,τ} = 1, that is, D\_τ = B\_τ = R\_τ = 1. By the sufficient direction, Probability = 1 at the endpoint.

0: the legal replay has been completed, and at the endpoint definitely N\_{P,τ} = 0.

⊥: the legal replay holds, but the structural reading at the endpoint is insufficient to judge N\_P.

When Replayable = 0, the 0 branch of Suff is not entered.

In a counterfactual replay it is not required that probability still first hold exactly at τ; it may hold earlier. What is studied here is the cause of source, not the cause of exact timing:

cause of source ≠ cause of exact timing

3.6 The actual generation chain of probability

A source cannot have only counterfactual sufficiency; it must also have an actual chain of formation, and what is formed must be one and the same as what the endpoint qualification uses.

Order-preserving sub-history: H\_g ⊑\_ord H\_s denotes that H\_g is obtained from H\_s by extracting some events in their original order. It is used only to mark the stretch of history actually passed through by a formation witness; it is not the reduction partial order ⪯\_Π (the sub-history of a formation witness is not necessarily a complete, replayable legal reduction history), nor ordinary set inclusion.

Formation witness: g = (H\_g, s\_g), where H\_g ⊑\_ord H\_s is an order-preserving sub-history and s\_g is the structure formed by these events.

Two paths of formation (taking values in {1, 0, ⊥} for each g):

FieldFormationLink\_P(g): 1, the events of H\_g, through execution, trace, sedimentation and formation, really formed s\_g within the field, the whole process reviewable; 0, the interfaces needed are completely readable, and there is definitely no such connection of formation; ⊥, the events or connections are in some place unreadable or unreviewable.

ExternalInstallLink\_P(g): 1, H\_g contains declared, reviewable external editing events that really generated s\_g; 0, the interfaces needed are completely readable, and there is definitely no such connection of installation; ⊥, there is some place unreadable or unreviewable.

Actual formation:

ActualFormation(g) := FieldFormationLink\_P(g) ∨₃ ExternalInstallLink\_P(g)

This disjunction exhausts the paths of formation only within the formal definitions of v1.0; it does not claim that all modes of probability formation are ontologically exhausted. Correlation, synchrony, prediction, onlooking, purely background enabling and final-state proxies satisfy neither path.

End binding (taking values in {1, 0, ⊥} for each g):

EndBind\_X(g), X ∈ {D, B, R}: 1, s\_g formally enters the bearing of the qualification item X of this frozen candidate at τ; 0, the binding interfaces are completely readable, and it definitely does not enter; ⊥, the binding relation is undecidable.

EndBind\_P(g) := EndBind\_D(g) ∨₃ EndBind\_B(g) ∨₃ EndBind\_R(g)

It is not required that the source change all three items at once, only that one and the same result of formation enter at least one item of the endpoint qualification core.

The actual generation chain of probability:

ActualPGenesisLink^Π(H\_s; χ̄\_P, τ) := ∃₃ g, H\_g ⊑\_ord H\_s: \[ ActualFormation(g) ∧₃ EndBind\_P(g) \]

Formation and binding must fall on one and the same witness g: one may not take the formation of event A on one side and the end binding of another event or of a pre-existing structure B on the other.

Three-valued rules: ∨₃ is any 1 gives 1, no 1 but some ⊥ gives ⊥, all 0 gives 0; ∧₃ is any 0 gives 0, no 0 but some ⊥ gives ⊥, all 1 gives 1; ∃₃ is any 1 gives 1, no 1 but some ⊥ gives ⊥, all 0 gives 0, and "all 0" requires that the formation witnesses in H\_s have been enumerated completely, otherwise ⊥.

### 3.7 Two actual paths of source

For a certified source history H\_s:

FieldBorn\_P^Π(H\_s) = 1: there exists g, H\_g ⊑\_ord H\_s, with FieldFormationLink\_P(g) = 1 and EndBind\_P(g) = 1.

ExternalInstall\_P^Π(H\_s) = 1: there exists g, H\_g ⊑\_ord H\_s, with ExternalInstallLink\_P(g) = 1 and EndBind\_P(g) = 1.

The two are not presupposed mutually exclusive, can both be 1, and are not written as an exclusive or.

ExternalInstall ⇏ ¬Source: external installation can be a real source.

ExternalInstall ⇏ FieldBorn.

3.8 The discipline of the Sedimentation-Field

The upstream corpus has already assigned "probability is generated in the Sedimentation-Field" to location and generation process. So external installation is not interpreted as probability being able to exist apart from the Sedimentation-Field; the correct interpretation is: the legal sedimentary structure of the current candidate probability can be obtained for the first time in the current source window through a history of external installation.

ExternalInstallSource ≠ non-Sedimentation-Field probability

### 3.9 Minimal sufficient source history

MinSuff\_P^Π(H\_s; χ̄\_P, τ) ∈ {1, 0, ⊥}

For any declared H\_s, the candidate itself is judged first:

First, Replayable\_P(H\_s) ∈ {0, ⊥}: MinSuff\_P(H\_s) = ⊥. That the candidate itself cannot be legally replayed cannot be interpreted as "not minimal".

Second, Replayable\_P(H\_s) = 1 and Suff\_P(H\_s) = 0: MinSuff\_P(H\_s) = 0. It is already clearly not sufficient and cannot be a minimal sufficient history.

Third, Replayable\_P(H\_s) = 1 and Suff\_P(H\_s) = ⊥: MinSuff\_P(H\_s) = ⊥.

Fourth, only when Replayable\_P(H\_s) = 1 and Suff\_P(H\_s) = 1 are all H′ ≺\_Π H\_s examined:

0: there exists H′ ≺\_Π H\_s with Replayable\_P(H′) = 1 and Suff\_P(H′) = 1. A definite counterexample takes precedence.

1: there is no such counterexample, and every strict reduction has Replayable\_P(H′) = 1 and Suff\_P(H′) = 0.

⊥: there is no definite sufficient strict reduction, but at least one key strict reduction has Replayable = 0 or ⊥, or Suff = ⊥ after legal replay.

cannot be replayed ≠ not sufficient after replay

Not: cannot be legally deleted ⇒ necessary source.

## Section Four　Source Theorems

### 4.1 The source core

PGenesisSource^Π(H\_s; χ̄\_P, τ) = Genesis\_P^Π ∧₃ ActualPGenesisLink^Π ∧₃ MinSuff\_P^Π

Each of the three guards one thing: Genesis\_P guards the first probability event; ActualPGenesisLink guards the actual chain of formation; MinSuff\_P guards legal counterfactual load-bearing and non-redundancy.

ProbabilityGenesisSource = ActualGenesis ∩ CounterfactualBearing

Having only the actual chain without counterfactual load-bearing is not enough; having only counterfactual necessity without an actual chain of formation is not enough either.

Three-valued propagation: 1 when all three items are 1; 0 when at least one item is definitely 0; ⊥ when there is no definite 0 and at least one item is ⊥. Insufficient material is not written as "not a source".

### 4.2 The family of source solutions

𝔖^Π\_{P,src} = { H\_s ∈ 𝓗^Π\_src \| PGenesisSource^Π(H\_s) = 1 }

The family of source solutions can have several members; uniqueness is not assumed by default. When the source has several solutions, the whole certifiable family of solutions is reported.

### 4.3 Empty-history control

H\_∅: no declared source event is kept, the other items of the protocol unchanged.

SourceDomainIncomplete\_P^Π ∈ {1, 0, ⊥}:

1: Replayable\_P(H\_∅) = 1 and Suff\_P(H\_∅) = 1. At least part of the process really bearing load has been left out in X\_0, Bg^Π, Dyn^Π, a too coarse granularity of history, or an undeclared domain of source candidates. Then Source\_P^Π = ⊥, not written Source\_P = 0.

0: Replayable\_P(H\_∅) = 1 and Suff\_P(H\_∅) = 0. The empty history is not sufficient.

⊥: the empty history cannot be legally replayed, or its Suff is undecidable.

The empty set does not enter 𝔖^Π\_{P,src}.

### 4.4 The source-determinable domain

SrcDetDom\_P^Π := GenesisResolved ∧ DomainExhaustive ∧ ReductionDomainFinite ∧ ReplayCoverage ∧ ActualLinkCoverage ∧ EmptyControlClosed ∧ GranFrozen

GenesisResolved: Genesis\_P has been judged 1.

DomainExhaustive: the frozen domain of candidate source histories 𝓗^Π\_src has been completely declared and exhausted, and no history outside the domain is taken as a source candidate of this actual first formation as Probability.

ReductionDomainFinite: the finiteness of 3.3 holds.

ReplayCoverage: for every candidate history H\_s in the domain of source candidates with ActualPGenesisLink = 1, its MinSuff\_P(H\_s) has been completely judged 0 or 1 according to 3.9; that is, for the candidate itself and the strict reductions necessary to obtain that MinSuff, Replayable and Suff have obtained readings sufficient to close the three-valued propagation, and there is no key replay gap still making MinSuff\_P(H\_s) = ⊥. For candidates with ActualPGenesisLink = 0, the source core already has a definite 0, and the irrelevant MinSuff branch is not required to be evaluated separately. When Replayable = 0, Suff does not enter the domain of evaluation and is not required to take 0 or 1 separately.

ActualLinkCoverage: the ActualPGenesisLink of every candidate has been judged 1 or 0.

EmptyControlClosed: SourceDomainIncomplete = 0. (EmptyControlResolved is written in addition: SourceDomainIncomplete ∈ {0, 1}, denoting that the empty history has been judged; what is used in the determinable domain is EmptyControlClosed.) Hence SourceDomainIncomplete = 1 ⇒ SrcDetDom = 0 ⇒ Source\_P = ⊥.

GranFrozen: the granularity has been frozen.

It is only the complete domain of the work of source determination, not the probability ontology, not a fourth qualification, not an H.

### 4.5 The overall state of source

Two things are distinguished: whether at least one certified source already exists; whether the current protocol already suffices to give an overall determination of source.

SourceExistence\_P^Π ∈ {1, 0, ⊥}: 1, 𝔖^Π\_{P,src} ≠ ∅; 0, SrcDetDom = 1 and 𝔖^Π\_{P,src} = ∅; ⊥, all other cases. It is the existence reading of the family of solutions, not a new ontology.

Source\_P^Π(χ̄\_P, τ) ∈ {1, 0, ⊥}:

1: SrcDetDom = 1, and 𝔖^Π\_{P,src} ≠ ∅.

0: SrcDetDom = 1, and 𝔖^Π\_{P,src} = ∅. SrcDetDom = 1 already contains SourceDomainIncomplete = 0, a complete domain of source candidates, and all key legal replays closed.

⊥: all other cases, including an incomplete domain of source candidates, the empty history already sufficient, key replays illegal or undecidable, the actual generation chain unreviewable, insufficient granularity, the source set not yet exhausted.

So one can have at the same time SourceExistence = 1 and Source = ⊥: it is already known that there is at least one real source, but the frozen background itself is also already sufficient, or the source domain is not yet exhausted, and it cannot yet be said that the current family of solutions gives a complete determination of the source of this first formation as Probability. Already having some source certificates is not the source domain being exhausted.

not having found a source ≠ having no source

4.6 Theorem One　Theorem of the certification of the source of probability

Let H\_s ∈ 𝓗^Π\_src. If Genesis\_P^Π(χ̄\_P, τ) = 1, ActualPGenesisLink^Π(H\_s) = 1 and MinSuff\_P^Π(H\_s) = 1, then PGenesisSource^Π(H\_s) = 1, H\_s ∈ 𝔖^Π\_{P,src}, and:

legally replaying H\_s to τ gives N\_{P,τ} = 1, hence Probability = 1 at the endpoint;

H\_s contains a real process of formation entering at least one item of the endpoint qualification core;

no legal strict reduction of H\_s is sufficient any longer.

Proof

By the three-valued conjunction of 4.1, the three items being 1 gives PGenesisSource = 1; and H\_s ∈ 𝓗^Π\_src, so H\_s ∈ 𝔖^Π\_{P,src}. The case of the empty history is handled separately by 4.3 and does not enter the family of solutions. By 3.9, MinSuff = 1 is obtained only in the fourth case, so Replayable(H\_s) = 1 and Suff(H\_s) = 1; by 3.5, the replay endpoint has N\_{P,τ} = 1, and by the sufficient direction Probability = 1. By ActualPGenesisLink = 1 and 3.6, there is real formation entering at least one item of the endpoint qualification core. By the definition of MinSuff = 1, every strict reduction has Suff = 0. □

Status = Established, carrying no H.

4.7 Theorem Two　Theorem of the insufficiency of the final state for source

Probability\_τ = 1 ⇏ 𝔖^Π\_{P,src} ≠ ∅; N\_{P,τ} = 1 ⇏ 𝔖^Π\_{P,src} ≠ ∅

Proof

The second item ActualPGenesisLink and the third item MinSuff\_P of PGenesisSource both take the history H\_s as argument and need evidence of the actual chain of formation and evidence of legal replay; the Probability reading and the qualification-core reading at the endpoint do not contain this evidence.

Construct a finite abstract protocol model (a formal model, not asserting that a physical instance already exists): in the initial state X\_0, D = B = R = 0; H\_obs = (h\_0), h\_0 being one historical event; the reduction domain ℛ\_Π = { H\_∅, (h\_0) }, 𝓗^Π\_src = { (h\_0) }; at τ, Judge\_P = 1, N\_{P,τ} = 1, and the earlier points in T\_Π are all 0, so Genesis\_P(τ) = 1. For the only possible formation witness g\_0 = ((h\_0), s\_0) in (h\_0), the interfaces needed are completely readable, while FieldFormationLink(g\_0) = 0 and ExternalInstallLink(g\_0) = 0, so ActualFormation(g\_0) = 0, ActualPGenesisLink((h\_0)) = 0 (the formation witnesses having been enumerated completely), and PGenesisSource((h\_0)) = 0.

Thus 𝔖^Π\_{P,src} = ∅, while Probability\_τ = 1 and N\_{P,τ} = 1. So the final-state qualification facts do not imply that the family of source solutions is non-empty. In this model, what really bears the endpoint qualification is not in the declared history; if the legal replay of the empty history is also sufficient at τ, then SourceDomainIncomplete = 1 and Source\_P = ⊥, not "there is no source" (regression tests N4, N6). □

Status = Established.

4.8 Theorem Three　Theorem of the insufficiency of the item closing last for source

If the qualification item closing last at τ is X, it cannot be inferred that the qualification-bearing profile of the source is Σ\_DBR = e\_X (1 only in the item X).

Proof

Construct a finite abstract protocol model. It is a formal model of the definitions above, assigning values to formal predicates, and does not assert that the corresponding physical instance already exists.

In the initial state X\_0, D = B = R = 0. H\_obs = (h\_D, h\_B, h\_R), occurring in that temporal order, R closing last. The reduction domain is all sub-sequences of H\_obs, forming a frozen finite partial order under deletion. The first event Genesis\_P(τ) = 1.

Values of legal replay: every sub-sequence can be legally replayed (Replayable = 1); Suff(H\_obs) = 1; every strict reduction of H\_obs has Suff = 0 (lacking any one item, at least one qualification is 0 at the endpoint).

Formation witnesses: g\_X = ((h\_X), s\_X), where (h\_X) ⊑\_ord H\_obs, X ∈ {D, B, R}; FieldFormationLink(g\_X) = 1; EndBind\_X(g\_X) = 1.

Thus ActualPGenesisLink(H\_obs) = 1, MinSuff(H\_obs) = 1, PGenesisSource(H\_obs) = 1. By 5.4, σ\_D = σ\_B = σ\_R = 1, Σ\_DBR = 111 ≠ e\_R = 001. So LastClose\_τ = R does not imply Σ\_DBR = e\_R. □

Status = Established, a conclusion at the layer of definition.

4.9 Theorem Four　Theorem of the insufficiency of components of the qualification core for source

For any X ∈ {D, B, R}, that X is a component of the current frozen qualification core N\_P = D ∧ B ∧ R does not imply that the history of formation of X belongs to the source of this first formation as Probability:

X is a component of N\_P ⇏ GenesisSourceHistory(X)

Proof

Source is obtained only in the history within the source window (3.3a). Construct a finite abstract protocol model (a formal model, not asserting that a physical instance already exists): in the initial state X\_0, D = B = 1, R = 0; H\_obs = (h\_R); the reduction domain ℛ\_Π = { H\_∅, (h\_R) }; Genesis\_P(τ) = 1; both can be legally replayed, Suff(H\_∅) = 0, Suff((h\_R)) = 1; formation witness g\_R = ((h\_R), s\_R), FieldFormationLink(g\_R) = 1, EndBind\_R(g\_R) = 1. Thus MinSuff((h\_R)) = 1, 𝔖^Π\_{P,src} = { (h\_R) } (regression test P1).

In this model, D and B are still components of N\_P at the endpoint, but no history in the domain of source candidates contains events of the formation of D or B; their histories of formation are in no H\_s. So that X is a component of N\_P does not imply that the history of formation of X is this source. □

Status = Established, carrying no H.

Conditional corollary: under H2 ∧ H3^red, D, B, R at the same time obtain the interpretation of necessary qualification items of probability; even so, NecessaryQualifier\_P(X) ⇏ GenesisSourceHistory(X), the proof being as above. Without carrying this condition, D, B, R are not called necessary qualification items of probability.

### 4.10 Event-level source bearing

For a historical atom h, the following predicates all take values in {1, 0, ⊥}. "The source family is complete" is written for: SrcDetDom = 1, and 𝔖^Π\_{P,src} has been completely enumerated.

PossibleSourceCarrier\_P^Π(h): 1, at least one H\_s ∈ 𝔖 with h ∈ H\_s has been found (positive proof of existence, not requiring the source family to be exhausted); 0, the source family is complete and no H\_s in it contains h; ⊥, all other cases.

NecessarySourceCarrier\_P^Π(h): 1, the source family is complete and non-empty, and every H\_s contains h; 0, there already exists an H\_s ∈ 𝔖 not containing h (a definite counterexample takes precedence, not requiring the source family to be complete); ⊥, there is not yet such a counterexample, and the source family is not completely closed, or the source family is empty. When the source family is empty, no h is judged a necessary source by vacuous universality over the empty set.

PossibleSourceCarrier and NecessarySourceCarrier are mirror images of each other: Possible is judged 1 by one source containing h and 0 after exhaustion; Necessary is judged 0 by one source not containing h and 1 after exhaustion.

RedundantSourceCarrier\_P^Π(h): 1, an H\_A ∈ 𝔖 containing h has been found, and an H\_B ∈ 𝔖 not containing h has also been found; h is a redundant but real source carrier (positive proof of existence); 0, the source family is complete, and h belongs to no H\_s, or h belongs to every H\_s; ⊥, all other cases.

NonSourceCarrier\_P^Π(h): 1, the source family is complete, and h belongs to no H\_s; 0, some H\_s containing h has been found; ⊥, all other cases.

## Section Five　Where the Source Cannot Be Uniquely Determined

5.1 Theorem Five　Theorem of the non-uniqueness of source

Source\_P ⇏ UniqueSource

Proof

MinSuff\_P is minimality relative to the partial order ⪯\_Π, and minimal elements of a partially ordered set need not be unique; nor does the definition of PGenesisSource contain a clause of uniqueness. Construct a finite abstract protocol model (a formal model, not asserting that a physical instance already exists):

Under the frozen granularity Gran^Π there are two historical events h\_A, h\_B, H\_obs = (h\_A, h\_B). The reduction domain ℛ\_Π = { H\_∅, (h\_A), (h\_B), (h\_A, h\_B) }, the partial order being given by the deletion relation ⪯\_Π permitted by the protocol. Write H\_A := (h\_A), H\_B := (h\_B). Genesis\_P(τ) = 1.

Every element can be legally replayed; Suff(H\_∅) = 0, Suff(H\_A) = Suff(H\_B) = 1, Suff((h\_A, h\_B)) = 1. H\_A and H\_B each have a formation witness g\_A, g\_B with ActualFormation = 1 and EndBind\_P = 1.

Thus: the only strict reduction of H\_A is H\_∅, Suff(H\_∅) = 0, so MinSuff(H\_A) = 1; likewise MinSuff(H\_B) = 1; (h\_A, h\_B) has the strict reduction H\_A still sufficient, so MinSuff((h\_A, h\_B)) = 0. The empty history is not sufficient, SourceDomainIncomplete = 0. Further, freeze and completely declare the domain of candidate source histories of this model: 𝓗^Π\_src = { H\_A, H\_B, (h\_A, h\_B) }. The granularity has been frozen; the reduction domain is finite; Genesis has been judged 1; the ActualPGenesisLink of the three non-empty candidate histories has been judged 1 or 0; for all candidates among them with ActualPGenesisLink = 1, MinSuff has been closed to 0 or 1; the empty history is not sufficient. So SrcDetDom\_P^Π = 1. Thus 𝔖^Π\_{P,src} = { H\_A, H\_B }, and by 4.5, Source\_P = 1; the domain of source candidates has three non-empty candidate histories, while the family of source solutions has only two certified sources, the domain of source candidates ≠ the family of source solutions; both are real sources; h\_A is not in H\_B, and h\_B is not in H\_A, so neither is a necessary source.

Therefore Source\_P = 1 while \|𝔖^Π\_{P,src}\| = 2: Source\_P = 1 ⇏ UniqueSource. The weaker SourceExistence\_P = 1 ⇏ UniqueSource follows. □

Status = Established.

5.2 Points of indeterminacy that must be declared

At the following places, the source cannot be uniquely determined here, and this must be declared explicitly:

The family of source solutions can have several members; when the source has several solutions, the whole family of solutions is reported, not only one of them.

Necessary source at the event level: a positive verdict NecessarySourceCarrier = 1 requires the source family to be complete and non-empty; a negative verdict NecessarySourceCarrier = 0 can be obtained directly from one certified source solution not containing that historical atom. While the source family is not exhausted and there is not yet such a counterexample, NecessarySourceCarrier remains ⊥.

When the source-determinable domain SrcDetDom is not closed, neither non-source nor overall source 0 can be signed; it can only be ⊥.

When the empty history is already sufficient, the source domain is incomplete, and the overall source is ⊥.

When the reduction domain is infinite or the granularity is not frozen, the v1.0 determination domain of minimal sufficient sources is not entered.

5.3 Classification of source: three orthogonal axes

Environmental reward and punishment, source drive, evolution and external installation may not be written as four mutually exclusive types of source at the same level. Classification proceeds along three orthogonal axes.

Axis One　Provenance of the source: FieldBorn\_P, ExternalInstall\_P (3.7), the two not presupposed mutually exclusive.

Axis Two　Labels of the mechanism of formation: attached according to the certified actual source chain, such as RewardSedimentSource, SourceDriveSource, EvolutionarySource. RewardSedimentSource: can hold only when the minimal sufficient actual source history really contains trial → environmental reward and punishment → sedimentation → formation of Law-continuation, and enters the bearing of the endpoint probability qualification.

SourceDriveSource: can hold only when the history driven by source drive really formed the structure used at the endpoint and enters the minimal sufficient source set.

EvolutionarySource: the research-starting corpus B10 supports only the relation between the process of evolution and probability dimensions and the structure of Intention; only when the actual history of evolution is taken into the current source protocol and certified as the bearing of a minimal sufficient source may this label be attached. One may not write Evolution ⇒ ProbabilityGenesis.

Axis Three　Qualification-bearing profile: Σ\_DBR (5.4).
