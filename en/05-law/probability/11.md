# The General Form of Probability-Dynamics

What follows attempts one general proposition:

If a declared Probability candidate does have Probability-Dynamics in a stretch of real cross-time running, what structure must these dynamics at least have? In this text, under unceasing questioning, we are to find where the proof must go, and derive the General Form of Probability-Dynamics.

Then let us first give the result. (What follows is a reading arrangement placed back at the head of the text after the whole text was completed, according to what it actually contains; it only announces the result and is not a premise of the subsequent derivation.)

The dynamics of probability is not setting two probability states side by side for comparison. There must first be a stretch of path walked continuously by one and the same declared Probability candidate along a real legal trajectory, the manner of reading frozen in advance; then, on this stretch of path, something must really happen to this Probability itself: either its action shifts, or the relative-generation relation it bears shifts, or the formal organization constituting its qualification shifts. At least one of the three, not all at once. These judgments are all relative to the evaluation granularity frozen in advance.

H2 ∧ H3^red ∧ SC\_Hold ⇒ \[ ProbabilityDyn^η ⇒ N\_PDyn \]

The specific composition of N\_PDyn is forced out round by round below; see Section Five.

In one sentence:

Merely time passing, outcomes differing, empirical frequencies differing, environments differing, or the underlying realization being replaced, does not thereby become Probability-Dynamics; Probability-Dynamics is that one and the same Probability, on a stretch of real and legal path, really shifts in its own action, rate relation or qualification organization.

※※※

Abstract

The General Form of Probability and The Necessity Form of Probability have given the current qualification of probability N\_P = D ∧ B ∧ R; The General Form of Probability-Action and The Necessity Form of Probability-Action have closed the current action of probability; The Source Form of Probability has given the source of first formation as Probability. This Form advances one step: it studies what structure a declared Probability candidate must at least have if it has dynamics in real cross-time running.

The necessary structure obtained consists of two parts: a legal Probability-Dynamics segment, requiring the same real trajectory, a fixed probability anchor, legal state updates, an evaluation protocol frozen in advance and a well-formed set of evaluation points; and the dynamic actuality of the probability itself on this segment, which is the disjunction of three projections: action shift, rate-relation shift, and formal structural shift of the qualification organization. At least one of the three, not all at once.

Only the necessary direction is proven, Status = Conditional, the condition being H2 ∧ H3^red ∧ SC\_Hold; this condition bears load only at one place, "the three projections exhaust the dynamics on the side of qualification". The judgments of dynamics in this text are relative to the evaluation granularity declared by Γ\_PDyn. Back-and-forth within a sub-segment finer than that granularity, and wholly vanishing at that granularity, is not guaranteed by the theorem of this text to be detected; that the real process has dynamics at a finer granularity does not automatically imply that the judgment of dynamics under this protocol holds. The sufficient direction, formalization, reachability and stability are handed respectively to subsequent form-texts.

## Section Three　Preliminary Distinctions

Dynamics is not time passing. If mere passing of time counted as dynamics, a probability wholly at rest, every item unchanged, would also be "moving".

Dynamics is not outcomes differing. That one continuation falls into v\_i and another into v\_j are only the outcomes of two executions; every execution returns to one, and differing outcomes do not show that the probability itself has changed.

Dynamics is not a change of empirical frequency. Frequency is the statistics of many outcomes; The Inversion Form of Probability has proven that frequency cannot universally identify probability qualification, and its change likewise cannot proxy for the shift of the probability itself.

Dynamics is not a change of numerical rate. The relative-generation relation of probability can have only a structural representation without legal values (struct mode of The Mathematical Form of Probability), and a change of value can also be only a change of representative (positive-scaling equivalence class).

"Real" here means actually generated by the legal dynamics of the field system or operating substrate, not recorded, edited or stitched after the fact.

## Section Four　Line of Argument

What is known at present is only: there is a theory in which both probability qualification and action have been closed, there is the dynamics corpus, and there is the object anchor above.

First ask: is merely arranging probability states at several moments in temporal order already Probability-Dynamics?

Construct a temporal juxtaposition case. 𝒫\_A at t\_0, 𝒫\_B at t\_1 and 𝒫\_C at t\_2 each have legal probability qualification, their rate values and outward behavior even being wholly the same. Arrange them in a row by time.

This row has temporal order, but it cannot say "one and the same probability goes from one state to another": they are different candidates. The "direction dimensions are growing" and "the probability … is growing smaller" spoken of by the corpus all speak of the change of one and the same thing.

So temporal arrangement is not dynamics. For dynamics, there must at least be one and the same declared Probability candidate tracked continuously: F, 𝒫, C, 𝔯\_P and the reference to the dynamics protocol fixed over the whole segment. This requirement is written provisionally as FixedProbabilityAnchor. "Same" here is only the same declared candidate (SameDeclaredCandidate), not the same probability identity across a failure of qualification: if qualification undergoes 1 → 0 → 1, it is not thereby automatically asserted that the later 1 is the recovery of the earlier probability entity.

Now there is a fixed probability anchor. Does the task close? No.

Construct a state-setting case. The same candidate is in state S\_0 at t\_0; by t\_1 a recorder, external editor or manual operation directly rewrites its internal state as S\_1: directly rewriting ProbabilityAct, rewriting Rel\_ρ, adding or deleting probability dimensions by hand, or directly writing the truth values of D, B, R.

Both ends are really existing states, and the object reference has not been changed, but there is no real field dynamics or legal running process connecting S\_0 and S\_1. State editing is not dynamics.

Construct further a trajectory-splicing case. The t\_0 state of the same candidate is taken from one stretch of real running, the t\_1 state from another stretch of real running; each is legal field dynamics, but they are not joined to each other.

Both cases keep the fixed anchor, and neither is the dynamics of this probability: in the former there is no legal update between the states, in the latter the states are not on the same trajectory. So two further items are needed, answering two questions respectively. Is it one and the same path: all evaluation points and their adjacent sub-segments belong to one and the same actual continuous running history, not stitched across running instances, branches or different histories, written SameActualTrajectory. Was this path walked by the rules: the state updates in this actual history obey the field dynamics or running operations permitted by Γ\_PDyn, and may not directly rewrite the evaluated objects, written AdmissibleUpdate. The trajectory-splicing case violates the former, the state-setting case the latter.

Now there are a fixed anchor, the same real trajectory and legal updates. Does the task close? No.

Construct a field-dynamics case. On the same real trajectory, real changes occur around the same candidate: other Laws change, unrelated regions sediment, environmental input changes, other paths run. But this probability itself is at rest over the whole segment (ProbabilityAct is 0 at every evaluation point), the relative-generation relation it bears is unchanged, and its qualification organization is unchanged.

The whole field has dynamics, but nothing has happened to this probability itself. If this also counted as Probability-Dynamics, then as long as the field moved, every probability would be "moving", and Probability-Dynamics could not be distinguished from field dynamics.

So, besides a legal trajectory, there must also be a dynamic actuality belonging to this probability itself, written provisionally as DynActual\_P. How many forms it has inside is not yet settled at this point.

Then ask again: "something happening" to this probability itself — what things can it be? The three branches below all keep everything obtained above, each constructing one case, each closing a different aspect of one and the same question, and finally merging.

Branch One. The working domain of ProbabilityAct is Probability = 1 (the entry condition of The General Form of Probability-Action), so this branch reads action only when both adjacent evaluation points obtain Probability = 1; if the adjacent points straddle the entry into or exit from probability qualification, the value is not filled in with "ProbabilityAct = 0" from outside the working domain; if that qualification straddles an object-level witness of a change of the formal qualification organization, it is borne by QualStructDyn of Branch Three below; if the necessary accompanying change of the formal organization of D, B, R is inferred backwards only from the straddling of the truth of Probability, H2 ∧ H3^red ∧ SC\_Hold must be inherited. Keeping qualification, rate relation and qualification organization all unchanged, let only the action state shift: ProbabilityAct from 0 to 1 (from rest to exercise), or from 1 to 1 (continuing exercise), or from 1 to 0 (stopping exercise). If such a case were also judged "no Probability-Dynamics", a probability going from rest into exercise, continuing to exercise and stopping exercise would have no place in dynamics. So there must be a cross-time shift of action, written provisionally as ActDyn\_P, which reads the shift of ProbabilityAct between adjacent evaluation points. No degree or strength of action, not yet established, is introduced here. The so-called "continuing" of 1 → 1 means only that under the frozen evaluation protocol both adjacent ends obtain ProbabilityAct = 1; it does not assert that there was never an interruption at any finer time scale between the two evaluation points. Continuing exercise counts as an action shift because in this segment the probability is indeed exercising, something that is happening; 0 → 0 (continuing rest) does not count, since then nothing happens to the action of the probability. 0 → 1, 1 → 1 and 1 → 0 are the endpoint signatures of action dynamics at frozen evaluation points; the endpoint combination itself does not stand in for the actual fact of exercise in the adjacent real sub-segment: if both ends are 1, while within this sub-segment, at the frozen granularity, there is no witness of actual exercise at all, only two static endpoints, it is not continuing exercise.

Branch Two. Keeping the action state and qualification organization unchanged, let only the relative-generation relation borne by this probability organization change across time (at least one end of this step having probability qualification; see the discipline of qualification contact below): Rel\_ρ(t\_k) shifts to Rel\_ρ(t\_{k+1}). What the corpus says, "the probability of being continued in each direction is growing smaller", and the shift of the rate relation as Sedimentation-Degree changes (A3.c, taken only in direction), are exactly of this class. Even without the start or stop of action, the relative-generation relation borne by this probability has changed. It is not called "the Continuation Bias has changed" here: per Form Chapter 20 v1.2, Continuation Bias B denotes specifically the dependence of later continuation on the difference of Sedimentation-Degree (adjudicated by a levelling intervention), which is not the same thing as the shift of Rel\_ρ. So there must be a cross-time shift of the rate relation, written provisionally as RateDyn\_P. What it reads is the relative-generation relation itself, not its numerical representation:

RateDyn\_P ≠ NumericRateShift

The relative-generation relation of probability, in struct mode without legal numerical weights, is still a formal rate relation; the change of empirical frequency cannot proxy for it either.

Branch Three. Keep ActDyn = 0, RateDyn = 0, at least one end of this step having probability qualification (see the discipline of qualification contact below). Let V\_P(t\_0) = {v\_1, v\_2}; then a new path obtains formal Law-bearing through legal sedimentation and becomes a new probability dimension, V\_P(t\_1) = {v\_1, v\_2, v\_3}. What the corpus says, "the direction dimensions are growing" (B6.a), is exactly of this class. Here MultiLawDimension can be 1 at both ends, and Probability can also be 1 at both ends; reading only the truth of qualification would miss this real structural shift. So there must be a shift of the formal structure of probability dimensions, written provisionally as DimStructDyn\_P.

Besides the structure of dimensions, the qualification organization has two further layers: how these dimensions constitute one and the same probability question, and who formally bears relative generation. Are their shifts already absorbed by the structure of dimensions or by the first two branches? A case is constructed below for each, both keeping everything obtained before.

Case of a change in the structure of common binding. Keep ActDyn = 0, RateDyn = 0, and keep the formal set of dimensions and their Law-bearing unchanged. Let the formal structure of the common binding shift legally: for example, the formal generation schema of the generation relation to which the dimensions jointly belong shifts legally from g\_C to g\_C′, the dimensions still belonging to one generation relation and still mutually exclusive successors; or the formal internal relations of the common binding within the same declared domain are reorganized. B can be 1 at both ends. Then the structure of dimensions has not changed, nor action or rate relation, but the formal organization of "how these dimensions constitute one and the same probability question" has changed. If this did not count as Probability-Dynamics, the shift of this layer would have no place. So there must be a shift of the formal structure of common binding, written provisionally as BindStructDyn\_P.

Case of a change in the rate-bearing organization. Keep ActDyn = 0; keep V\_P, 𝓛\_P, the binding of each dimension to formal Law-bearing BearDim(L\_i, v\_i), the common binding B and Rel\_ρ itself all unchanged. Change only: among the same batch of already fixed Law-bearing duties, which formal load-bearing states, in what joint, distributed or redundant combination, actually bear the formation of Rel\_ρ. For example, at t\_k {W\_1, W\_2} jointly enter the formation of Rel\_ρ, and at t\_{k+1} {W\_1, W\_3} bear the same Rel\_ρ; W\_1, W\_2, W\_3 are always in the fixed 𝓛\_P, and their formal Law-bearing qualifications for the dimensions have not changed. What changes is only the rate-bearing organization, that is, how this relative-generation relation is formally borne inside the organization. It is not DimStructDyn, since the set of dimensions, the registration family and the Law-bearing bindings of the dimensions have not changed; not RateDyn, since the rate relation itself has not changed; nor realization replacement, since realization replacement changes the realization from r\_a to r\_b under one and the same formal bearing duty, whereas here what changes is the participation and organization of formal bearing duties themselves (see the realization-replacement case below). If this did not count as Probability-Dynamics, the shift of "how this relative generation is formally borne inside the organization" would have no place. So there must be a shift of the formal structure of the rate-bearing organization, written provisionally as RateBearingStructDyn\_P.

The three cases, each keeping the other two layers unchanged, force out three classes of structural shift respectively, none containing another. Together they make the formal structural shift of the qualification organization:

QualStructDyn\_P := DimStructDyn\_P ∨ BindStructDyn\_P ∨ RateBearingStructDyn\_P

QualStructDyn may not be reduced to D(t\_k) ≠ D(t\_{k+1}), B(t\_k) ≠ B(t\_{k+1}), R(t\_k) ≠ R(t\_{k+1}) or Probability(t\_k) ≠ Probability(t\_{k+1}): in all three cases the truth values at both ends are the same, while the internal formal structure has indeed changed.

Branch Three must also draw a line. Construct a realization-replacement case: keep V\_P, the formal Law-bearing registrations, the common binding, the qualification structure of rate bearing, Rel\_ρ and ProbabilityAct all unchanged, and let only the current realization of some formal duty be replaced from r\_a by r\_b. This is only a replacement of a running realization or witness; the formal organization of the probability has not changed. So:

RealizationReplacement ⇏ QualStructDyn\_P, hence RealizationReplacement ⇏ ProbabilityDyn

But if the change falls on the formal probability organization itself — the formal members of the probability dimensions change, the formal Law-bearing qualification binding of some dimension changes, the structure of common binding changes, or the qualification of the rate-bearing organization changes — it belongs to QualStructDyn. The determination of "two different Law identities" is not involved here: the question of the unified cross-time identity of Law has not yet been closed by this group form.

The three branches merge. What the three close are three aspects of one and the same question, "what is happening to this probability itself", none containing another: the action shift of Branch One can occur while the rate relation and organization are unchanged, the rate shift of Branch Two can occur while action and organization are unchanged, the organizational shift of Branch Three can occur while action and rate relation are unchanged. A real event of Probability-Dynamics needs only at least one branch to hold, not all three changing at once. So:

DynActual\_P^η = ActDyn\_P^η ∨ RateDyn\_P^η ∨ QualStructDyn\_P^η

They are alternative projections standing side by side, not a conjunction.

Then ask again: is it enough that the shifts of the three projections happen to this declared candidate?

Construct a non-qualified-zone case. Under the same real trajectory and legal updates, at some pair of adjacent evaluation points (t\_k, t\_{k+1}), the candidate has no probability qualification at either end (Probability(t\_k) = Probability(t\_{k+1}) = 0), while the rate relation of the candidate or the structure of its qualification organization changes in this step.

In this step there is no probability, only a candidate that has not yet become, or is no longer, a probability. Its structural change can count only as "a structural change of a probability candidate", not as the dynamics of this probability: the object entry speaks of a real shift of "the state as a probability".

Construct further a cross-zone stitching case. In the same stretch of running, the earlier segment has Probability = 1, but action, rate relation and qualification organization do not shift; the later segment has Probability = 0, and the candidate structure changes in it. If the probability qualification of the earlier segment and the candidate change of the later segment were put together to sign "this probability has dynamics", it would stitch a qualification witness at one place to a structural change in a wholly non-qualified zone at another.

So the witnessing steps of dynamic actuality must be in local contact with probability qualification. For an adjacent evaluation step k, write:

Contact\_P(k) := Probability(t\_k) = 1 ∨ Probability(t\_{k+1}) = 1

ActDyn still, as required by Branch One, has Probability = 1 at both ends; the witnessing steps of RateDyn and QualStructDyn must satisfy Contact\_P(k) = 1. And the signing of DynActual must be borne by at least one adjacent evaluation step in local contact with probability qualification; the probability qualification at one place and the candidate structural change in a wholly non-qualified zone at another may not be stitched into Probability-Dynamics. This discipline is written QualificationContactBind\_PDyn.

It is the witness well-formedness discipline of dynamic actuality, of the same nature as "consistency of witnesses within the same segment": not new dynamic content, not a fourth projection, and it does not change the two-part structure of N\_PDyn. At the step where the entry into (0 → 1) or exit from (1 → 0) qualification lies, Contact\_P(k) = 1, so the step straddling qualification is not excluded by the contact discipline: if there is an object-level witness of a change of the formal qualification organization, it is borne by QualStructDyn; if the organizational change is inferred backwards only from the straddling of the truth of qualification, H2 ∧ H3^red ∧ SC\_Hold must be inherited.

Then ask again: with three projections, does the task close?

Construct an after-the-fact re-reading case. Under the same real trajectory and legal updates, the result is seen first, and then it is chosen which variables to evaluate, at what resolution, in what direction to compare, which projection to read and at which time points to sample, so that some projection happens to show change.

Such a manner of reading can read "change" out of a stretch of running that is unchanged, and can also read a real change as "unchanged". So the reading protocol of dynamics must be frozen in advance: evaluation variables, evaluation resolution, direction of comparison, projection read and sampling points are all fixed before running, written FrozenEvalProtocol.

Construct a first-and-last comparison case. Action undergoes 0 → 1 → 0 in a stretch of running; or the rate relation undergoes R\_0 → R\_1 → R\_0; or the qualification organization undergoes V\_0 → V\_1 → V\_0. First and last are the same, while in between something really happens. If only first and last are compared, these real dynamics are all missed.

So all three projections must be read step by step along adjacent evaluation points (t\_k, t\_{k+1}); the difference between first and last cannot proxy for dynamics. Adjacent step-by-step evaluation can keep the intermediate changes covered by the evaluation points, but cannot guarantee to capture changes at an arbitrarily fine scale: back-and-forth can still occur between two adjacent points (for example, action undergoing 0 → 1 → 0 between two evaluation points, both ends reading 0). Whether such back-and-forth enters the judgment depends on the evaluation granularity declared by Γ\_PDyn (2.2). This requires the set of evaluation points T\_η = (t\_0, t\_1, …, t\_n) to be well-formed: strictly ordered; at least two evaluation points (n ≥ 1); every pair of adjacent evaluation points corresponds to a sub-segment of the real trajectory; undecidable points may not be deleted after the fact. Written WellFormedEvalSet.

The results of the first two rounds and the last two rounds are combined into one segment:

LegalProbabilityDynSegment^η := SameActualTrajectory^η ∧ FixedProbabilityAnchor^η ∧ AdmissibleUpdate^η ∧ FrozenEvalProtocol^η ∧ WellFormedEvalSet^η

Witnesses within the same segment must be consistent: all three projections are read on the same trajectory and under the same evaluation protocol of this segment. This discipline belongs to the internal well-formedness requirements of the legal dynamics segment and is not set up as a third ontological item side by side.

Now there are a legal dynamics segment and dynamic actuality. Does the task close?

Continue testing structures that might be added. The following all arise from one and the same question: is there yet a fourth independent dynamics projection belonging to this probability itself, which can be placed neither in the shift of action, of rate relation nor of qualification organization, and is not some other question?

Observation. A change of empirical frequency or outcome statistics is not a shift of the probability itself.

Representation. A change of numerical rate, readability or manner of recognition is not a change of the rate relation itself.

Execution. That some one continuation finally falls into v\_i or instead into v\_j is only the outcome of one execution, not a cross-time change of the probability organization.

Source. It asks how it forms, not how it changes after it has formed; it belongs to The Source Form of Probability.

Location. Crossing fields or hosts first needs a criterion of identity continuity, which is not involved here.

Relation. The relation of probability to objects such as Law, Principle and Intention (the relational side of corpus B2, "passes through the Law Ecology") is not an independent dynamics projection; if it makes the action, rate relation or qualification organization of this probability shift, it has already been placed in one of the three projections.

Replacement of realizations and witnesses. Already excluded by the realization-replacement case.

External contribution. Who makes the probability change and whether there is change in the probability are two questions. Even if this probability itself is not the cause of its change (DynContrib\_P = 0), ProbabilityDyn = 0 cannot be inferred.

Stability. It studies persistence, first actual failure and recovery, not what happens on a single dynamics segment.

Reachability. It studies whether there exists from a given initial state a legal finite path hitting a target, not the actual dynamics on a given real path.

Change of the truth of qualification. Does Probability going from 1 to 0 or from 0 to 1 form a separate projection? Proof

Conclusion

A probability can hold at different moments, each on its own.

But putting the states of different moments together is not dynamics; rewriting a state over is not dynamics; joining two stretches of running is not dynamics either.

So there must be a stretch of path walked by one and the same probability along one and the same real trajectory through legal updates, the manner of reading frozen in advance, the evaluation points joined one to the next.

The path has been walked.

But if nothing in this probability itself has changed, only the field moving and time passing, that is not Probability-Dynamics either.

So something must really happen to this probability itself: its action shifts, or the relative-generation relation it bears shifts, or the formal organization constituting its qualification shifts. At least one of the three.

Pressed further into one sentence:

The dynamics of probability is that one and the same probability, on a stretch of real and legal path, really shifts in its own action, rate relation or qualification organization.
