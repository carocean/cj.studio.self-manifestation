# The Mathematical Form of Probability-Dynamics

## Section One　Purpose

To establish a formal system for the necessary structure of Probability-Dynamics, such that the dynamic structure of any declared Probability candidate over a stretch of actual running can be actually evaluated starting from real running records and upstream formal interfaces, yielding a definite reading: holds (1), fails after complete evaluation (0), or insufficient legal formal material (⊥).

The structure formalized comes from The General Form of Probability-Dynamics v1.0:

N\_PDyn = LegalProbabilityDynSegment^η ∧ DynActual\_P^η

LegalProbabilityDynSegment^η = SameActualTrajectory^η ∧ FixedProbabilityAnchor^η ∧ AdmissibleUpdate^η ∧ FrozenEvalProtocol^η ∧ WellFormedEvalSet^η

DynActual\_P^η = ActDyn\_P^η ∨ RateDyn\_P^η ∨ QualStructDyn\_P^η

QualStructDyn\_P^η = DimStructDyn\_P^η ∨ BindStructDyn\_P^η ∨ RateBearingStructDyn\_P^η

together with its already frozen witness disciplines: ActDyn requires probability qualification at both adjacent ends and actual exercise within the sub-segment; the witnessing steps of RateDyn and QualStructDyn must be in contact with probability qualification (QualificationContactBind\_PDyn).

The formal objective is:

S\_PDyn ↦ M\_PDyn

that is: for every load-bearing formal quantity, to state from where in reality it is obtained, how it is computed, how it falls to ⊥ when material is insufficient, and as what structural fact it can be read back; and to give the order of solving, the coverage needed for negative read-outs at each layer, and the rules of read-back and condition propagation. Symbols that cannot answer these four questions do not enter the system here.

## Section Two　Core Thesis

Given one and the same declared Probability candidate χ̄\_PDyn = (F, 𝒫, C, 𝔯\_P, Γ\_PDyn) and a declared stretch of actual running η, how is it to be judged, from the real records of the operating substrate and the existing formal systems of probability:

(1) whether this is a segment walked by one and the same candidate along one and the same actual running, through legal updates, according to a manner of reading frozen in advance and well-formed evaluation points;

(2) whether in this segment at least one kind of dynamics on the probability side really occurred: the start, continuation or stop of action; a shift of the relative-generation relation itself; a shift of the formal organization of qualification;

(3) how false dynamics from endpoints, false changes of representation, changes of empirical frequency, candidate changes in non-qualified zones, pure realization replacement and stitching across histories are excluded;

(4) how 1, 0 and ⊥ are strictly distinguished: 1 for clearly holding, 0 for clearly failing after complete evaluation, ⊥ for insufficient legal formal material.

The following groups of objects must be distinguished formally:

endpoint signature ≠ actual exercise within the sub-segment

change of value or representation ≠ change of the relative-generation relation itself

change of qualification truth ≠ change of the formal organization of qualification

realization replacement ≠ change of the formal bearing organization

bearing combination on a single evaluation ≠ bearing organization over the whole rate domain

not having found positive evidence ≠ negation

### 3.1 Running data package

Definition 3.1 \[Aux\]

Ω^η\_PDyn = (RunID, BranchID, χ̄\_PDyn, Γ\_PDyn, T\_η, {I\_k}, {S\_k}, ℰ\_η, ℋ\_η)

RunID: the identifier of the actual running instance. BranchID: the identifier of the actual history branch. T\_η = (t\_0, …, t\_n), n ≥ 1: evaluation points frozen in advance. I\_k: the actual sub-segment of real running connecting t\_k and t\_{k+1}, k = 0, …, n − 1. S\_k: the formal state snapshot at evaluation point t\_k. ℰ\_η: the actual event record. ℋ\_η: the history of state updates, operations, sources and protocols.

These inputs are provided by the field system, the operating substrate and the upstream mathematical forms; no separate physical running system is built here. Ω^η\_PDyn is only the form of organization of the solving input, not a new ontology.

### 3.2 Three-valued logic

Definition 3.2 \[Aux\]

𝕋\_3 = {1, 0, ⊥}.

∧₃: any 0 gives 0; no 0 but some ⊥ gives ⊥; all 1 gives 1.

∨₃: any 1 gives 1; no 1 but some ⊥ gives ⊥; all 0 gives 0.

¬₃: 1 ↦ 0, 0 ↦ 1, ⊥ ↦ ⊥.

∃₃ k Q\_k (existential aggregation): 1 when there is a step with Q\_k = 1; ⊥ when there is no 1 and at least one load-bearing step is ⊥; 0 only when all declared load-bearing steps have completed evaluation and are all 0, with the corresponding coverage certificates (Definition 3.17).

Positive evidence first: a definite 1 is not washed out by the ⊥ of other steps or other branches. Not: not having found positive evidence ⇒ 0.

### 3.3 The same actual trajectory

Definition 3.3 \[Aux/S\]

TrajLink\_k ∈ 𝕋\_3. It takes 1 if and only if it can be verified that: the states at t\_k, t\_{k+1} and I\_k all belong to the same RunID and the same BranchID; I\_k really exists, Begin(I\_k) = t\_k, End(I\_k) = t\_{k+1}; I\_k is continuously joined in actual history with I\_{k−1} and I\_{k+1}; no current running in reality, replay, another history or another branch is stitched in. It takes 0 when there is definite stitching or branch inconsistency; ⊥ when the running or branch records cannot be obtained.

Read\_{SameActualTrajectory}^M = ∧₃\_k TrajLink\_k

### 3.4 The fixed probability anchor

Definition 3.4 \[S\]

Read\_{FixedProbabilityAnchor}^M = 1 if and only if the references of F, 𝒫, C, 𝔯\_P, Γ\_PDyn stay the same at all evaluation points. It takes 0 when some reference changes; ⊥ when the references cannot be obtained. V\_P(t), 𝓛\_P(t), Rel\_ρ(t), ProbabilityAct(t) are not listed in the fixed anchor: they are precisely the dynamic states allowed to change.

### 3.5 Legal updates

Definition 3.5 \[Aux/S\]

For each sub-segment I\_k a real update certificate is obtained:

κ\_k^upd = (Ops(I\_k), Sources(I\_k), ProtRef, TargetWriteLog(I\_k))

ProtocolAllowed\_k ∈ 𝕋\_3: every operation in Ops(I\_k) belongs to the field dynamics or running operations permitted by Γ\_PDyn (including the Mask, Avail, Freeze and the like already permitted by the substrate), and has a traceable source chain.

NoDirectTargetWrite\_k ∈ 𝕋\_3: TargetWriteLog(I\_k) has no record of writing directly, bypassing the legal running structure, into the following objects: ProbabilityAct; Rel\_ρ; formal probability dimensions; the truth values of D, B, R; QualStructDyn or other derived dynamics labels.

A legal external intervention is not itself an illegal update: as long as the state change has a traceable running source chain, it is a legal update.

UpdateOK\_k = ProtocolAllowed\_k ∧₃ NoDirectTargetWrite\_k

Read\_{AdmissibleUpdate}^M = ∧₃\_k UpdateOK\_k

When the source chain or write log cannot be obtained, take ⊥; 1 may not be signed on the ground that "no illegal operation was found".

### 3.6 Frozen evaluation protocol

Definition 3.6 \[S\]

Γ\_PDyn = (AnchorRef, VarSet, ProjSet, T\_η, Resolution, CmpRules, RepRules, FreezeStamp, Version)

Read\_{FrozenEvalProtocol}^M = 1 if and only if: FreezeStamp precedes every load-bearing evaluation; Version is consistent throughout; no evaluation points have been added or deleted after the fact; comparison rules, reading direction, resolution or the projection set have not been changed after the fact. It takes 0 when there is definite modification after the fact; ⊥ when the modification history of the protocol cannot be audited.

### 3.7 Well-formed set of evaluation points

Definition 3.7 \[S\]

The evaluation points declared in advance T\_η^decl are distinguished from the index actually used in evaluation T\_η^read.

Read\_{WellFormedEvalSet}^M = 1 if and only if: t\_0 \< t\_1 \< … \< t\_n, n ≥ 1; every pair of adjacent points has a real I\_k; Index(T\_η^read) = Index(T\_η^decl), that is, all points declared in advance are kept in the evaluation index. When some evaluation point is undecidable, its reading may be ⊥, but the point may not be deleted after the fact; a declared point having been deleted takes 0; when the index record cannot be obtained, take ⊥.

Definition 3.8 \[S\]

Read\_{LegalSeg}^M = Read\_{SameActualTrajectory}^M ∧₃ Read\_{FixedProbabilityAnchor}^M ∧₃ Read\_{AdmissibleUpdate}^M ∧₃ Read\_{FrozenEvalProtocol}^M ∧₃ Read\_{WellFormedEvalSet}^M

### 3.9 Same-witness binding key

Definition 3.9 \[Aux\]

K\_k^PDyn = (𝒫, RunID, η, t\_k, t\_{k+1}, I\_k, Γ\_PDyn)

Every piece of load-bearing evidence (trajectory, update, endpoint reading, action certificate, rate-relation representation, organization snapshot) must carry its K\_k^PDyn.

SamePDynBind\_k^M ∈ 𝕋\_3: 1, the keys of all load-bearing evidence used at step k agree, and agree with the η, RunID, Γ\_PDyn used by the legal dynamics segment; 0, the keys clearly disagree (for example the legal segment taken from η\_1 and the dynamics evidence from η\_2); ⊥, insufficient key material.

3.10 Endpoint qualification and qualification contact

Definition 3.10 \[Aux\]

P\_k := Read\_Probability(t\_k) ∈ 𝕋\_3, taken from the value at t\_k of the verdict Det\_P of The Determination Form of Probability, or from the reading of The Mathematical Form of Probability at t\_k through its read-back.

Contact\_k^M := P\_k ∨₃ P\_{k+1}: 1 when at least one end is definitely 1; 0 when both ends are definitely 0; ⊥ when no end is 1 and at least one end is ⊥.

Qualification contact is not a fourth dynamics projection, nor a third main item of N\_PDyn, but the gate of attribution of step-by-step dynamics witnesses.

### 3.11 Action dynamics

Definition 3.11 \[Aux/S\]

Upstream input: the current-action certificates in reality of The Mathematical Form of Probability-Action are reused; no second ProbabilityAct is defined. For the sub-segment I\_k, the legal positive action witnesses are collected:

𝒲\_k^Act = { w^M: the actual support of w^M falls within I\_k, Read\_{N\_PAct}^M(w^M) = 1, and it carries K\_k^PDyn }

Endpoint action reading: A\_k := the reading of ProbabilityAct at t\_k ∈ 𝕋\_3. 1 is taken from Read\_{N\_PAct}^M = 1 through the positive read-back of The Mathematical Form of Probability-Action and The Necessity Form of Probability-Action; 0 from Read\_{N\_PAct}^M = 0 through negative read-back; otherwise ⊥.

Action evaluation units: by Resolution\_Act given in advance in Γ\_PDyn, I\_k is divided into 𝒰\_k^Act = { u\_{k,1}, …, u\_{k,m\_k} }. It is not required that there be action at every instant on arbitrarily fine time.

Unit reading a\_u ∈ 𝕋\_3: 1, there exists w ∈ 𝒲\_k^Act with Support(w) ∩ u ≠ ∅; 0, the negative coverage of action events in unit u is complete (Cov^{Act}(u) = 1), and there is no such w; ⊥, other cases.

Source binding: OnsetBind\_k ∈ 𝕋\_3 is 1 if and only if the unit within the sub-segment where action enters 1 from 0 is joined to a legal update chain (Definition 3.5), and its evidence carries K\_k^PDyn; CessationBind\_k is defined likewise on the unit where it enters 0 from 1.

The step state StepAct\_k is evaluated only when P\_k = P\_{k+1} = 1:

Start: A\_k = 0, A\_{k+1} = 1, OnsetBind\_k = 1.

Continue: A\_k = A\_{k+1} = 1, and a\_u = 1 for every u ∈ 𝒰\_k^Act (ActCover\_k = 1).

Stop: A\_k = 1, A\_{k+1} = 0 (the negative read-out at t\_{k+1} complete), CessationBind\_k = 1.

Idle: A\_k = A\_{k+1} = 0, and a\_u = 0 for every u ∈ 𝒰\_k^Act. If A\_k = A\_{k+1} = 0 while some a\_u = 1, StepAct\_k = ⊥: this is not "not knowing whether there is action", but the current frozen evaluation granularity being incompatible with the observed fact of a start and stop within the segment, so that this step cannot be classified by the three current legal endpoint signatures; neither Idle is signed, nor ActDyn = 1 on its own.

⊥: the remaining cases, including only an endpoint change without source binding, endpoints 1 → 1 with some unit not 1, incomplete negative read-out and the like.

The step state has only four positive and negative types, Start, Continue, Stop, Idle, inheriting the endpoint signatures 0 → 1, 1 → 1, 1 → 0 of ActDyn of The General Form of Probability-Dynamics, 0 → 0 not counting. If the frozen evaluation granularity is so coarse that a "start and then stop" is packed into one adjacent segment, making the endpoints 0 → 0, this pair of endpoints cannot on its own be extended into action dynamics; it should be checked whether the resolution of FrozenEvalProtocol and WellFormedEvalSet suffices to expose the real start and stop as a change between adjacent evaluations. Endpoints 1 → 1, with some unit in between not being 1, do not constitute Continue; under the current object-level terms this step is recorded ⊥, its start and stop to be exposed separately by finer declared evaluation points.

If P\_k or P\_{k+1} is definitely 0, this step is not in the working domain of action, and StepAct\_k = NA (not applicable) is recorded; in the aggregation of ActDyn it is treated as 0; if P\_k or P\_{k+1} is ⊥ and neither is 0, StepAct\_k = ⊥.

Step reading: Q\_k^Act = 1 when StepAct\_k ∈ {Start, Continue, Stop} and SamePDynBind\_k^M = 1; Q\_k^Act = 0 when StepAct\_k ∈ {Idle, NA}; otherwise ⊥.

Read\_{ActDyn}^{M,η} = ∃₃ k Q\_k^Act

1 → 1 is only an endpoint signature, not a sufficient condition of Continue; Continue requires witnesses of actual exercise in all action units. "No action event was seen afterwards" does not proxy for stopping.

### 3.12 Rate-relation dynamics

Definition 3.12 \[Aux/S\]

The upstream relative-generation relation is defined per evaluation: Rel\_ρ^M(ν), ν ∈ Eval(𝔯\_P). So the rate relation at moment t\_k is taken as the family of relations over the whole rate domain:

ℛ\_k := \[ ν ↦ Rel\_ρ^M(ν; t\_k) \], ν ∈ Eval(𝔯\_P)

Evaluation-by-evaluation identity SameRel\_k(ν) ∈ 𝕋\_3:

struct → struct: by the identity criterion of the upstream struct representation; ⊥ when no legal comparison can be made.

weight → weight: compare the positive-scaling equivalence classes \[w\_k(ν)\]\_ρ and \[w\_{k+1}(ν)\]\_ρ; if w\_{k+1} = a·w\_k, a \> 0, it is the same relation. So (1, 2) → (3, 6) is not a change of relation, (1, 2) → (1, 3) is a change of relation.

struct ↔ weight: compared only when upstream already has a legal bridge to a common representation; ⊥ without a bridge. A change of representation mode itself does not imply a change of relation.

Object identity: SameRel\_k^M ∈ 𝕋\_3 answers whether the two ends represent one and the same formal relative-generation relation object. If the substrate unfolds it into the family of relations ν ↦ Rel\_ρ(ν), 1 or 0 is signed only when the frozen representation protocol can completely judge the identity of this family: 1 when SameRel\_k(ν) = 1 for all ν, 0 when there exists ν with SameRel\_k(ν) = 0, otherwise ⊥. The a.e. rule of DomainEvalMode is used for aggregating qualification and rate bearing over the rate domain, not for establishing the identity of the relation object: if the existing measure-type interface suffices only for qualification aggregation and not for judging the identity of the relation object, SameRel\_k^M = ⊥, and changes on null-measure evaluations are not arbitrarily declared not to count as changes.

RelChange\_k^M := ¬₃ SameRel\_k^M

The d\_ρ, ε\_ρ used for upstream rate-bearing diagnostics may not define a dynamics threshold: RateDyn = 1 ⇔ d\_ρ \> ε\_ρ may not be written. A real change of relation that cannot be distinguished at the current resolution of representation takes ⊥, not 0. Changes of empirical frequency, of numerical magnitude, of representation mode, of the action state or of the bearing organization do not proxy for RelChange.

Step reading: Q\_k^Rate := Contact\_k^M ∧₃ RelChange\_k^M ∧₃ SamePDynBind\_k^M

Read\_{RateDyn}^{M,η} = ∃₃ k Q\_k^Rate

A change of the candidate rate relation in a purely 0 → 0 non-qualified zone is not signed, because Contact = 0.

### 3.13 The formal dimension organization

Definition 3.13 \[Aux\]

𝔇\_k = (V\_k, 𝓛\_k, 𝔟\_k^Dim), 𝔟\_k^Dim = { (W\_i, v\_i): W\_i ∈ 𝓛\_k, BearDim^M(W\_i, v\_i) = 1 }

What is compared is the formal membership of dimensions, formal Law-bearing registrations and formal BearDim bindings, not the number of witnesses or of current realizations.

DimDiff\_k ∈ 𝕋\_3: 1, 𝔇\_k and 𝔇\_{k+1} clearly differ in formal dimension membership, registration or binding (for example a new dimension v\_3 having obtained formal Law-bearing and entered V\_P); 0, all three clearly the same; ⊥, the snapshot cannot be obtained, or the comparison depends on the cross-time identity of a Law or duty and that identity cannot be legally settled. No unified criterion of the cross-time identity of Law is set up on its own here.

### 3.14 The common-binding organization

Definition 3.14 \[Aux\]

\_k^Bind = (PosBind\_k, DomainBind\_k, GenBind\_k)

Its content includes at least: the formal identity of the Continuation-Position; the frozen generation domain; the formal generation-relation identity g\_C; the binding of dimensions to the generation relation; the formal successor-realization domains of the dimensions; the structure of mutually exclusive successors. It may not keep only the three Boolean values SameContinuationPosition, SameGenerationDomain, SameGenerationRelation.

BindDiff\_k ∈ 𝕋\_3: 1, the internal formal structure clearly differs (for example g\_C → g\_C′, or a legal reorganization of the binding schema), even if B is 1 at both ends; 0, clearly the same; ⊥, the snapshot cannot be obtained or cannot be compared.

3.15 The formal bearing organization at the level of the rate domain

Definition 3.15 \[Aux\]

For each evaluation point t\_k and legal evaluation ν ∈ Eval(𝔯\_P), let ℰ^real\_{k,ν} be the set of actual evaluation realizations legally readable at that time point and under that evaluation, its members and sources being provided by the upstream interface of evaluation realizations and the operating substrate. If this set cannot be obtained completely, the comparison of bearing organizations depending on it is ⊥.

RoleSet\_{k,ν}(G, e) = { W\_i ∈ 𝓛\_P: G ∩ GenIn(W\_i, ν) ≠ ∅ and EvalRateBind^M(W\_i, ν, e) = 1 }, G ∈ MinSuff\_ρ(ν), e ∈ ℰ^real\_{k,ν}

ℋ\_{k,ν}^ρ = { RoleSet\_{k,ν}(G, e): G ∈ MinSuff\_ρ(ν), e ∈ ℰ^real\_{k,ν}, RoleSet\_{k,ν}(G, e) ≠ ∅ }

Every RoleSet is obtained on one and the same actual realization e, certificates from different actual realizations under one evaluation not being stitched together; multiple realizations and hot redundancy are each kept as different hyperedges, not compressed into a unique realization.

Empty RoleSets do not enter: upstream allows a wholly external redundant mechanism to exist as a minimal sufficient group, as long as the declared organization has its own actual bearing; these purely external sufficient groups contain no duty of the declared organization and do not belong to the bearing organization of this probability itself. What is compared is always which formal Law-bearing duties inside the declared organization, in what combination, actually bear. The appearance, disappearance or reorganization of an external mechanism does not on its own sign a change of the bearing organization.

The formal bearing organization over the whole rate domain:

𝔅\_k^ρ = \[ ν ↦ ℋ\_{k,ν}^ρ \], compared evaluation by evaluation by the formal organizational identity that the frozen protocol can legally recognize. The a.e. rule of DomainEvalMode is used for the rate-domain aggregation of the qualification DomainRelativeRateBearing, not for establishing the identity of the formal bearing organization: with qualification keeping R = 1 at both ends, the internal bearing structure can still change.

Role combinations are kept as families of hyperedges, not compressed into the union of active roles: {{W\_1, W\_2}, {W\_3}} and {{W\_1}, {W\_2, W\_3}} have the same union of roles, but different bearing organizations.

Role projection RoleProj: the realization r\_i, contribution c\_i, sub-segment Z\_i^ρ and actual in-evaluation realization e in the upstream rate-bearing certificate are used only to prove that the formal duty W\_i really bears on one and the same e, and to group the duties into hyperedges accordingly; before cross-time comparison everything is projected to the layer of duties, each hyperedge keeping only W\_i. So when only W\_2(r\_a) → W\_2(r\_b) occurs, while formal duties, formal bindings and role combinations are unchanged, the bearing organization is unchanged.

BearOrgDiff\_k ∈ 𝕋\_3: 1, there exists some evaluation ν whose family of hyperedges ℋ\_{k,ν}^ρ clearly differs from ℋ\_{k+1,ν}^ρ; 0, clearly the same for all evaluations; ⊥, the minimal sufficient groups or bindings of some evaluations are unobtainable, or the substrate can provide only a.e. qualification aggregation and cannot settle the complete bearing organization.

### 3.16 Qualification-organization dynamics

Definition 3.16 \[S\]

Step-by-step readings of the three classes:

Q\_k^Dim := Contact\_k^M ∧₃ DimDiff\_k ∧₃ SamePDynBind\_k^M

Q\_k^Bind := Contact\_k^M ∧₃ BindDiff\_k ∧₃ SamePDynBind\_k^M

Q\_k^BearStruct := Contact\_k^M ∧₃ SameRel\_k^M ∧₃ BearOrgDiff\_k ∧₃ SamePDynBind\_k^M

Q\_k^BearStruct contains the relation-preserving gate SameRel\_k^M: only when the relative-generation relations at both ends are one and the same object does the change of bearing organization belong to RateBearingStructDyn. If the relation itself changes (RelChange\_k = 1, that is, SameRel\_k = 0), then Q\_k^BearStruct = 0, and the step belongs to RateDyn, without double signing. This is the encoding at the formal layer of "RateBearingStructDyn and RateDyn do not overlap" of The General Form of Probability-Dynamics. BearOrgDiff\_k is only an auxiliary quantity, recording whether the bearing organization formally differs; it does not directly sign RateBearingStructDyn.

Segment-level readings:

Read\_{DimStructDyn}^{M,η} = ∃₃ k Q\_k^Dim

Read\_{BindStructDyn}^{M,η} = ∃₃ k Q\_k^Bind

Read\_{RateBearingStructDyn}^{M,η} = ∃₃ k Q\_k^BearStruct

Read\_{QualStructDyn}^{M,η} = Read\_{DimStructDyn}^{M,η} ∨₃ Read\_{BindStructDyn}^{M,η} ∨₃ Read\_{RateBearingStructDyn}^{M,η}

corresponding one to one with the object-level QualStructDyn = DimStructDyn ∨ BindStructDyn ∨ RateBearingStructDyn. The auxiliary summary OrgDiff\_k := DimDiff\_k ∨₃ BindDiff\_k ∨₃ BearOrgDiff\_k may be recorded, but is not used to sign QualStructDyn.

D(t\_k) ≠ D(t\_{k+1}), B(t\_k) ≠ B(t\_{k+1}), R(t\_k) ≠ R(t\_{k+1}) or Probability(t\_k) ≠ Probability(t\_{k+1}) may not proxy for an organizational change; realization replacement may not be read as an organizational change; H2 ∧ H3^red may not be borrowed to sign an organizational change automatically from a change of qualification truth: when organizational material is missing, the corresponding difference predicate is ⊥.

3.17 Coverage certificates for negative read-outs

Definition 3.17 \[Aux\]

Every 0 must have completeness grounds at the corresponding layer; no unified completeness gate replaces coverage at each layer. Coverage is layered as follows, each taking 𝕋\_3:

Cov^{traj}: records of running, branch and adjacent sub-segments complete;

3.19 Declaration of the properties of relations

TrajLink, UpdateOK, SamePDynBind, Contact, Q\_k^Act, Q\_k^Rate, Q\_k^Dim, Q\_k^Bind, Q\_k^BearStruct: three-valued predicates; no reflexivity, symmetry or transitivity is declared.

SameRel\_k(ν): within one representation mode, the reading of positive-scaling equivalence (weight mode) or of the upstream identity criterion (struct mode); positive-scaling equivalence is an equivalence relation (reflexive, symmetric, transitive). Cross-mode comparison is made only when there is a legal bridge.

DimDiff, BindDiff, BearOrgDiff: difference predicates between snapshots, symmetric; transitivity is not declared (difference is not transitive). The corresponding "same" is identity of snapshots, treated as an equivalence relation.

Equivalence of 𝔅\_k^ρ: equality of families of hyperedges evaluation by evaluation, an equivalence relation; the identity of the formal organization is not defined by a.e. equality.

Equality of K\_k^PDyn: identity, an equivalence relation.

StepAct\_k: a classification label, not a relation.

### 4.2 Formal properties

Proposition 4.1　Positive evidence first. If some step has Q\_k^Act, Q\_k^Rate, Q\_k^Dim, Q\_k^Bind or Q\_k^BearStruct equal to 1, then Read\_{DynActual}^{M,η} = 1, whether or not other steps or other branches are ⊥.

Proof: by the definitions of ∃₃ and ∨₃, one 1 decides the result. □

Proposition 4.2　Proportional scaling does not sign rate-relation dynamics. If PD-M9 holds, both ends obtain legal comparable weight representations on all relevant evaluations, Cov^{Rel}\_k = 1, and for each ν, w\_{k+1}(ν) = a(ν)·w\_k(ν), a(ν) \> 0, then SameRel\_k^M = 1 and Q\_k^Rate = 0.

Proof: by Definition 3.12, two positively scaled equivalent weights belong to the same class. □

Proposition 4.3　Static endpoints do not sign continuing exercise. If A\_k = A\_{k+1} = 1, while there exists u ∈ 𝒰\_k^Act with a\_u ≠ 1, then StepAct\_k ≠ Continue.

Proof: Continue requires a\_u = 1 for every unit. □

Proposition 4.4　Realization replacement does not sign organizational change. If between t\_k and t\_{k+1} there is only replacement of realizations, while formal dimensions, formal bindings and role combinations at the level of the rate domain are all unchanged, then DimDiff\_k = BindDiff\_k = BearOrgDiff\_k = 0 (when coverage at each layer is complete).

Proof: 𝔇\_k contains only formal members, registrations and bindings; \_k^Bind contains only the formal binding structure; 𝔅\_k^ρ keeps only the layer of duties through RoleProj. None of the three contains realizations. □

Proposition 4.5　Non-qualified zones do not sign. If P\_k = P\_{k+1} = 0, then Q\_k^Rate = Q\_k^Dim = Q\_k^Bind = Q\_k^BearStruct = 0, and StepAct\_k = NA.

Proof: Contact\_k = 0, so ∧₃ is 0; ActDyn is evaluated only when both ends are 1. □

Proposition 4.6　A change of hyperedge combination can be signed. If the union of roles is unchanged while the family of hyperedges on some evaluation clearly changes, and the interface of complete organizational identity is decidable, then BearOrgDiff\_k = 1; DomainRelativeRateBearing being 1 at both ends does not hinder this conclusion. When Contact\_k = 1, SameRel\_k = 1, SamePDynBind\_k = 1, Q\_k^BearStruct = 1 follows.

Proof: by Definition 3.15, what is compared is the family of hyperedges, not the union. □

Proposition 4.7　Stitching across segments is excluded. If the legal segment and the dynamics evidence are taken from different η, RunID or Γ\_PDyn, then SamePDynBind\_k^M = 0, and the corresponding Q\_k is 0.

Proof: by Definition 3.9 and the ∧₃ in each step reading. □

1\. Check the declarations of χ̄\_PDyn, η, Γ\_PDyn, T\_η (PD-M1).

13\. Check the K\_k^PDyn of the evidence of each step of this branch, obtain SamePDynBind\_k^M (PD-M13), and then obtain Q\_k^Rate and Read\_{RateDyn}^{M,η}.

14\. Construct the snapshot of the formal dimension organization, and obtain DimDiff\_k (PD-M10).

15\. Construct the snapshot of common binding, and obtain BindDiff\_k (PD-M11).

16\. Construct the formal bearing organization at the level of the rate domain, and obtain BearOrgDiff\_k (PD-M12).

17\. Check the K\_k^PDyn of the evidence of each step of this branch, obtain SamePDynBind\_k^M (PD-M13); take the SameRel\_k^M of step 12; obtain Q\_k^Dim, Q\_k^Bind, Q\_k^BearStruct, and then the readings of the three classes and Read\_{QualStructDyn}^{M,η}.

K-PDynM5: realization replacement is read as QualStructDyn = 1.

K-PDynM6: when the truth values of D, B, R are unchanged, a real internal formal structural change is missed.

K-PDynM7: the rate-bearing organization is compared only on a single evaluation, missing distributed or redundant structural changes at the level of the rate domain.

K-PDynM8: the a.e. rule of qualification aggregation is used to establish the identity of the relation object or of the formal bearing organization, thereby missing real internal changes while the qualification truth is unchanged, or signing undecidable cases as 0.
