# The Necessity Form of Probability-Dynamics

## Section One　Purpose

To prove: a declared Probability candidate on a stretch of cross-time running η, as soon as it has obtained the complete Probability-Dynamics structure N\_PDyn set up by The General Form of Probability-Dynamics, cannot fail to obtain Probability-Dynamics in this running:

N\_PDyn ⇒ ProbabilityDyn^η

N\_PDyn = LegalProbabilityDynSegment^η ∧ DynActual\_P^η

DynActual\_P^η = ActDyn\_P^η ∨ RateDyn\_P^η ∨ QualStructDyn\_P^η

QualStructDyn\_P^η = DimStructDyn\_P^η ∨ BindStructDyn\_P^η ∨ RateBearingStructDyn\_P^η

That is: once the legal dynamics segment and dynamic actuality are both present, no additional independent dynamic content is needed, and there is no condition able on its own to cancel these dynamics. Whether there is environmental disturbance or a source-drive impulse, whether there is a history of formation by reward and punishment, whether the rate value changes numerically, whether the empirical frequency changes, whether these dynamics are recognized, whether the final landing in a dimension changes, whether the system is ontologically random, whether this probability is the cause of its own change, whether the underlying realization is replaced, whether it later loses stability, whether it can be reached from some initial state — none of these affects the conclusion.

Only the sufficient direction is proven here, not the necessary direction.

This text takes over the range of evaluation granularity of The General Form of Probability-Dynamics v1.1: judgments of dynamics are relative to the evaluation granularity declared by Γ\_PDyn. The ProbabilityDyn^η obtained by the main theorem is a judgment of dynamics at that granularity; back-and-forth within sub-segments finer than that granularity and wholly vanishing at it lies outside the antecedent and conclusion of this theorem.

## Section Two　Core Proposition

The Probability-Dynamics spoken of by the frozen dynamics corpus has exactly three classes of independent positive dynamic duty, holding disjunctively: the action of this probability starts, continues to be exercised or stops in a real cross-time sub-segment; the formal relative-generation relation it bears itself shifts; its formal qualification organization undergoes a structural shift. These three classes of shift must further be attributed to the shift of one and the same declared probability itself in one and the same legal running; attribution is a discipline, not a fourth class of content. In the frozen corpus there is no fourth class of positive duty, nor any condition able on its own to cancel dynamics once the positive duties have been realized and legally attributed. The three projections of N\_PDyn realize the three classes of duty respectively, and the legal dynamics segment and the discipline of qualification contact realize the attribution. Therefore, once N\_PDyn holds, Probability-Dynamics cannot fail to occur.

The proof does not take the route "ProbabilityDyn ⇒ N\_PDyn, so the converse also holds"; the minimality of the General Form proves only that no item can be lacking, not that together they suffice for dynamics. Nor does the proof define ProbabilityDyn as N\_PDyn, or let the formal reading Read\_{N\_PDyn}^M = 1 stand in for the object-level antecedent. The route is: frozen corpus domain → semantic atomization → classification and closure of positive duties → the three projections each realize the corresponding duty → the legal segment realizes attribution → the review of cancelling conditions is empty → ProbabilityDyn^η = 1. This agrees with the method of the sufficient direction in The Necessity Form of Probability and The Necessity Form of Probability-Action.

Entry condition: the twelfth text, The Mathematical Form of Probability-Dynamics, has already been established in advance, giving the complete formal system S\_PDyn ↦ M\_PDyn. So the entry condition of the Canon Chapter for a Necessity Form, "the formal system on which the operator mapping rests is already established", is satisfied here.

### 4.3 The fixed reading of B6.a

The whole sentence B6.a belongs to dynamics and is not demoted to representation. ### 4.4 The fixed reading of B2

B2, "Probability is the Law that passes through the Law Ecology", adds no fourth dynamic content. It states that probability is not a static registration side by side, but actually travels through the Law Ecology with its Law organization, rate relation and current action; therefore shifts of action, rate relation and qualification organization can be the dynamics of this probability itself, not merely changes of the surrounding environment. B2 mainly bears the semantics of the attribution of dynamics.

4.5 Three-way division of positive duties

In the frozen dynamics corpus, the duties that can independently decide that ProbabilityDyn holds positively are merged into three classes:

U\_ActDyn: this probability, as a probability organization, has its current action in reality start, continue to be exercised or stop in a real cross-time sub-segment.

U\_ρDyn: the formal relative-generation relation borne by this probability itself shifts across time.

U\_OrgDyn: this probability, as a probability organization, has its formal qualification organization undergo a cross-time structural shift, including the dimension organization, the common-binding organization or the formal rate-bearing organization.

The three are disjunctive: PositiveDuty\_PDyn = U\_ActDyn ∨ U\_ρDyn ∨ U\_OrgDyn. ProbabilityDyn does not require the three to occur at once.

4.6 Components that are not independent duties

None of the following constitutes on its own a positive duty of ProbabilityDyn: observation; representation; execution (into which dimension some continuation falls); static relation; location; source and first formation; identity; pure realization replacement; external contribution; reachability; stability; empirical frequency; numerical rate; observed results of statistical distributions. When they change in legal cases while none of the three classes of positive duty occurs, this is not enough to obtain ProbabilityDyn.

## Section Five　The Theorem Group

5.1 Theorem One　Theorem of the closure of the semantic duties of Probability-Dynamics

In Σ\_PDyn^cur, the independent dynamic duties deciding that ProbabilityDyn holds positively are only the three classes U\_ActDyn, U\_ρDyn, U\_OrgDyn, holding disjunctively: PositiveDuty\_PDyn = U\_ActDyn ∨ U\_ρDyn ∨ U\_OrgDyn. There is no fourth independent positive dynamic duty.

Proof

First, atomization. A3.c gives the shift of the continuation rate as Sedimentation-Degree changes (currently taken only in direction, see L1), a cross-time change of the relative-generation relation, falling into U\_ρDyn; the change of Sedimentation-Degree is the driver, not the content. B6.a is split according to 4.3: the growth of the direction dimensions falls into U\_OrgDyn; the change of the continuation rate in each direction falls into U\_ρDyn; the possibility space growing larger is a description of what accompanies or results from the first two, especially the expansion of the domain of directions, and constitutes no independent duty; the head of the sentence is driver and trigger. B2, according to 4.4, gives that the changes above belong to the probability's own running through the Law Ecology, not to an independent fourth quantity of change.

Second, the cross-time shift of action. The sealed ProbabilityAct system gives: ProbabilityAct is that probability, as a Law organization, actually bears the relative-generation relation among the dimensions in a current continuation in reality (action corpus B5.c, "to go returning to one"; B11.a, "go by this"). The start, continuation and stop of this state in legal cross-time running are exactly the cross-time shift of action, constituting U\_ActDyn. Start, continuation and stop must actually occur within the sub-segment: both endpoints exercising while there is no actual exercise within the sub-segment is not "continuation".

Third, the range of U\_OrgDyn. The load-bearing word of U\_OrgDyn is "structural shift of the formal qualification organization", not "increase in the number of dimensions" itself. "The direction dimensions are growing" of B6.a gives a direct corpus instance of this class of dynamics. Probability qualification has already been closed independently by the preceding sufficiency form into three formal duty layers, not substitutable for one another: dimension qualification, common continuation, rate bearing (The Necessity Form of Probability, Theorem One). Under the condition that this qualification is still declared as one and the same probability organization, a cross-time shift of the internal organization of any of these duty layers is a change in "the formal organization constituting the qualification of this probability" itself, not in external representation or underlying realization. So the three changes of the dimension organization, the common-binding organization and the rate-bearing organization are different structural instances of one and the same U\_OrgDyn: first there is the semantic class of "dynamics of the formal qualification organization", and then its possible places of realization are unfolded according to the already independently established structure of probability qualification; it is not that three semantic duties are manufactured backwards because The General Form of Probability-Dynamics listed three branches.

Fourth, there is no fourth duty. Reviewing class by class the observation, representation, execution, static relation, location, source, identity, realization replacement, external contribution, reachability and stability listed in 4.6: either they only state how some one occasion is observed, represented or executed, or they ask another question (how it forms, where it comes from, whether it can be reached, whether it can persist); when none of the three classes of duty occurs, none of them can on its own constitute the dynamics of this probability itself. This review is an independent analysis of the frozen corpus and does not cite the exclusion conclusion of K-PDyn4 of The General Form of Probability-Dynamics as an antecedent; it reaches only the finite completeness of Σ\_PDyn^cur and does not assert that future theory cannot add dynamic duties.

So the positive duties close in the three classes above. □

5.2 Theorem Two　Theorem of the realization of the action-dynamics duty

ActDyn\_P^η ⇒ U\_ActDyn

Proof

The object-level definition of ActDyn (The General Form of Probability-Dynamics, Section Five) requires: both ends of the witnessing step are within the working domain of probability qualification (Probability = 1); on the corresponding real sub-segment, ProbabilityAct does actually start, continue or stop; 1 → 1 requires that there be the fact of continuing exercise within the sub-segment, not two static endpoints; 0 → 0 does not count. So what ActDyn registers is not the passing of time, change of outcome, change of observation, change of representation or change of an external mechanism, but that the current action of this probability itself, as a probability organization, has shifted across time in real running. This is exactly U\_ActDyn. □

5.3 Theorem Three　Theorem of the realization of the rate-relation-dynamics duty

RateDyn\_P^η ⇒ U\_ρDyn

Proof

The object of RateDyn is the cross-time change of Rel\_ρ itself, its witnessing step in contact with probability qualification. The General Form of Probability-Dynamics and The Mathematical Form of Probability-Dynamics have strictly distinguished it from superficial changes of the numerical rate, changes of empirical frequency, changes of representation mode, positive scaling, and changes of the formal bearing organization. So when RateDyn holds, what occurs is a change of the relative-generation relation borne by this probability itself, realizing the dynamic duty, in A3.c and B6.a, that "the probability of being continued in each direction" changes. This is exactly U\_ρDyn. □

5.4 Theorem Four　Theorem of the realization of the qualification-organization-dynamics duty

QualStructDyn\_P^η ⇒ U\_OrgDyn

Proof

In three branches, the witnessing step of each in contact with probability qualification.

DimStructDyn: the formal probability dimensions, formal Law-bearing registrations or relations of dimension bearing change. It directly realizes "the direction dimensions are growing" of B6.a and more general changes of the formal dimension organization.

BindStructDyn: even if the qualification truth of common binding stays holding, the internal formal Continuation-Position, generation domain, binding of the generation relation or organization of successors changes. This is a formal structural shift of the probability organization itself.

RateBearingStructDyn: while Rel\_ρ itself is unchanged, the combination of formal Law-bearing roles bearing this relation changes. It is not RateDyn (the relation has not changed), nor realization replacement (what changes is the formal bearing organization, not the underlying realization under the same duty), but a change of the formal bearing organization itself.

All three branches belong to formal structural shifts of the probability qualification organization and, by the third point of Theorem One, all fall within the range of U\_OrgDyn. □

5.5 Theorem Five　Theorem of the realization of dynamic actuality

DynActual\_P^η ⇒ PositiveDuty\_PDyn

Proof

DynActual\_P^η = ActDyn\_P^η ∨ RateDyn\_P^η ∨ QualStructDyn\_P^η. When the first branch holds, Theorem Two gives U\_ActDyn; when the second holds, Theorem Three gives U\_ρDyn; when the third holds, Theorem Four gives U\_OrgDyn. Whichever branch holds, U\_ActDyn ∨ U\_ρDyn ∨ U\_OrgDyn, that is, PositiveDuty\_PDyn = 1. □

5.6 Theorem Six　Theorem of the legal attribution of dynamics

If LegalProbabilityDynSegment^η ∧ DynActual\_P^η holds, then the positive duty realized by DynActual belongs to the shift of one and the same declared Probability candidate itself in one and the same legal running η.

Proof

The five items of the legal dynamics segment exclude the following false attributions: one and the same declared candidate is guaranteed by FixedProbabilityAnchor, excluding attributing the change of another candidate to the current 𝒫; one and the same actual running is guaranteed by SameActualTrajectory, excluding stitching of different runs, histories or branches; legal updates are guaranteed by AdmissibleUpdate, excluding direct writing into derived labels or target states; a manner of reading frozen in advance is guaranteed by FrozenEvalProtocol, excluding changing evaluation points, comparison rules or resolution after the fact; adjacent step-by-step reading without deleting points after the fact is guaranteed by WellFormedEvalSet, excluding comparing only first and last after deleting undecidable points. The three projections are further constrained by the discipline of qualification contact: the witnessing step must be in contact with probability qualification, and candidate changes in purely non-qualified zones and cross-zone stitching are not signed. The two parts of N\_PDyn take values under one and the same η and one and the same Γ\_PDyn (L5), and the step at which DynActual takes 1 belongs to the set of evaluation points of the legal segment.

So the U\_ActDyn, U\_ρDyn or U\_OrgDyn realized by DynActual is not a bare change, but a shift on the probability side of this declared Probability candidate on one and the same real legal trajectory. The attribution required by B2 is thereby satisfied. □

5.7 Theorem Seven　Theorem that the independent cancelling conditions are empty

When LegalProbabilityDynSegment^η = 1 and PositiveDuty\_PDyn = 1, legally attributed according to Theorem Six, there is in the frozen dynamics corpus no independent condition able still on its own to make ProbabilityDyn^η = 0:

IndependentCancel\_PDyn = ∅

Proof

Class-by-class review.

Observation: it states only how something is observed or recorded and does not cancel dynamics of the probability itself that have already occurred. Changes or non-changes of empirical frequency or statistical distribution are not cancelling conditions.

Representation: it states only how probability, rate or possibility is represented; whether the numerical rate changes or is readable does not cancel the shift of the relation itself.

Execution: how the result of some one execution settles (L3) does not cancel the cross-time shift of action, rate relation or organization that has already occurred.

Static relation: static relational categories constitute no cancelling condition of dynamics; the relational side of B2 bears attribution.

Location: where it occurs does not cancel the holding of dynamics.

Source and first formation: how it first formed and whether there are dynamics in this segment are two questions.

Reachability: whether it can be reached from another initial state does not cancel dynamics that have already occurred in this segment.

Stability: whether it can later persist or recover does not cancel dynamics that have already held in this segment.

Realization replacement: when there is only realization replacement, it does not enter the three classes of positive duty in the first place; when the three classes of duty hold on other grounds, realization replacement cannot cancel them either.

External contribution: external mechanisms can take part in, trigger or influence actual running; it is not required here that this probability itself be the cause of the change. As long as the legal segment and object-level dynamic actuality have held, the causal source cannot on its own cancel dynamics of this probability itself that have already occurred.

Driving and triggering: the absence of environmental disturbance, of a source-drive impulse, of a history of formation by reward and punishment all belong to driving sources or generation processes and cannot retroactively cancel shifts that have already occurred.

Ontological randomness: the dynamics corpus does not require execution to be unpredictable; under L3 every execution returns to one anyway.

Sole bearing: U\_ρDyn and U\_OrgDyn require that the relation or organization borne by this organization itself shift, not that it bear exclusively.

The unified cross-time identity of Law not closed: U\_OrgDyn reads the shift of the formal organization of the probability itself and does not depend on the determination of "whether two different Laws are the same".

After the class-by-class review above, all components of the current frozen corpus adjacent to dynamics that might be mistaken for qualification restrictions have been assigned respectively to observation, representation, execution, relation, location, source, reachability, stability, realization, external contribution, driving or randomness; none of them can, after the three classes of positive duty have been realized and legally attributed, still on its own imply that dynamics fail. Nor does the frozen corpus give any further cancelling clause not covered by the classification above. So within the current finite domain of proof of Σ\_PDyn^cur, IndependentCancel\_PDyn = ∅. This conclusion, like Theorem One, reaches only the current frozen corpus and does not assert that future theory cannot have new cancelling conditions. □

5.8 Theorem Eight　Theorem of the necessity of Probability-Dynamics

N\_PDyn ⇒ ProbabilityDyn^η

Proof

Suppose N\_PDyn = 1; then LegalProbabilityDynSegment^η = 1 and DynActual\_P^η = 1. By Theorem One, within Σ\_PDyn^cur, all the independent positive dynamic content of ProbabilityDyn closes in U\_ActDyn ∨ U\_ρDyn ∨ U\_OrgDyn. By Theorem Five, DynActual\_P^η = 1 has already made at least one of them actually realized. By Theorem Six, this realization has already been legally attributed to the shift of one and the same declared Probability candidate itself in one and the same running η. By Theorem Seven, after these positive contents have been realized and legally attributed, there is no independent cancelling condition.

Hence, within the current frozen semantic domain Σ\_PDyn^cur, the content of the positive holding of ProbabilityDyn has been obtained, and no cancelling condition remains, so ProbabilityDyn^η = 1, that is, N\_PDyn ⇒ ProbabilityDyn^η. □

This proof depends only on two things already proven: the exhaustion of positive semantics (Theorem One, realized and attributed by Theorems Five and Six) and the emptiness of the cancelling side (Theorem Seven); no meta-rule "if it does not hold, there must be an unsatisfied duty or a cancelling item" is inserted separately.

Status: Established. H\_new = ∅, H\_inherit^ontology = ∅.

What is obtained here is not "a structure similar to dynamics": the theorem proves that N\_PDyn completely realizes the positive duties and attribution obtained by independent analysis of the frozen dynamics corpus, and that there is no independent cancelling condition. It does not define ProbabilityDyn as N\_PDyn, nor does it invert the necessary direction of The General Form of Probability-Dynamics.

## Section Six　Boundary Cases and Structural Breakdown Conditions

6.1 Cases that do not constitute a breach

Only one projection holds: DynActual is a disjunction, not requiring the three branches to occur at once.

Probability does not stay 1 over the whole segment: the General Form already allows this; the attribution of witnesses is handled by the discipline of qualification contact.

RateDyn occurs while RateBearingStructDyn does not, or RateBearingStructDyn occurs while Rel\_ρ is unchanged: the two are not required to occur together in the first place, and the latter is exactly the definition of that branch.

Realization replacement while the formal organization does not change: it is not QualStructDyn and does not enter the positive duties in the first place.

The empirical frequency is unstable or unreadable: ProbabilityDyn is not defined by empirical frequency.

Some formal readings are ⊥: the main theorem takes the object-level N\_PDyn = 1 as its antecedent and does not let formal readings stand in for object facts.

The real process has, at a scale finer than the declared granularity, further back-and-forth vanishing at that granularity: this lies outside the antecedent and conclusion of this theorem (The General Form of Probability-Dynamics v1.1, 2.2) and constitutes no breach.

### 6.2 Structural breakdown conditions

The appearance of any of the following breaches the main theorem or the corresponding theorem, or turns it pending.

Breach One　Semantic omission: there exists in the frozen dynamics corpus a fourth independent positive duty U\_4 that cannot be placed in the three classes of duty; or the corpus is revised and such sentences are added. Breaches Theorem One.

Breach Two　Omission of a cancelling condition: there exists an independent condition X such that, when the legal segment and the positive duties have both held, X still makes ProbabilityDyn = 0. Breaches Theorem Seven.

Breach Three　Failure of action realization: there exists a legal instance of ActDyn that does not realize an actual cross-time shift of the action state of the probability itself. Breaches Theorem Two.

Breach Four　Failure of rate-relation realization: there exists a legal instance of RateDyn that is only a change of representation, frequency or value, the formal Rel\_ρ itself not having changed. Breaches Theorem Three.

Breach Five　Failure of qualification-organization realization: there exists a positive instance of QualStructDyn that is only realization replacement or a rearrangement of external mechanisms, the formal probability organization not having changed. Breaches Theorem Four.

Breach Six　Failure of attribution: N\_PDyn = 1, while the witness of DynActual is not a change on the same 𝒫, the same η, the same legal trajectory or the same frozen protocol. Breaches Theorem Six.

Breach Seven　Double-signing error: in one step in which Rel\_ρ has already changed, the change of the bearing organization is still signed at the same time as RateBearingStructDyn, violating the non-overlap boundary frozen by the eleventh and twelfth texts.

Breach Eight　Demotion of B6.a: demoting "the direction dimensions are growing, the possibility space is growing larger, and the probability of being continued in each direction is growing smaller" as a whole to representation or non-dynamics, missing dynamics explicitly registered by the frozen corpus.

Breach Nine　Reification of possibility space: setting up "the possibility space is growing larger" of B6.a separately as an ontological dynamic entity or a fourth dynamic duty.
