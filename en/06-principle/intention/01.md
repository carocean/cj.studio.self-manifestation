# The General Form of Intention

This chapter attempts a general proposition:

To acknowledge the fact that “Intention holds” necessarily means this: in the Intention-Domain there is a formal probability organization that stands in a source-drive driving relation and itself bears the continuation of the source drive; its probability dimensions correspond one-to-one to Desire-Gates and each leads to its corresponding gate; and behind every Desire-Gate there is an interface for commanding tuning.

That is: Intention is a probabilized Law Ecology driven by source drive in the Intention-Domain. Viewed by its constitution, it is a Law Ecology; viewed by the organizational relation among the directions, it is Probability; viewed by its relation of selecting, biasing, breaking and altering the existing holding-tendency, it is Principle; viewed by the particular ecological identity in which this organization bears source-drive continuation, leads each direction to a Desire-Gate and goes on to command tuning, it is called Intention. These are not four things added together but four views of one and the same organization.

The question this raises is:

When Intention holds, what structure must it have at minimum in order still to be Intention in the sense of On Self-Manifestation? And what is its relation to Probability, to Law and to Principle?

It should be stated at the outset how far this chapter goes in answering this question. This chapter proves only the necessary direction: if Intention holds, it necessarily has a certain structure. It does not prove that this structure suffices for Intention to hold; the sufficient direction belongs to The Necessity Form of Intention. This chapter treats only Intention qualification; whether Intention is acting at this moment belongs to The General Form of Intention-Action.

In this chapter we set out, through sustained questioning, to find where the proof should lead, and to derive the general form of Intention.

Our objective is:

to state positively what Intention is; to obtain the minimal necessary structure of Intention qualification; to show that the relations of Intention to Probability, Law and Principle fall on one and the same organization; and to give the proof of minimality, the corollaries and the breach paths.

Abstract

This chapter answers four questions: what Intention is, and what its relation is to Probability, to Law and to Principle.

The inquiry starts from the fact that Intention holds. A Sedimentation-Field can contain a complex Law Ecology and yet have no organization of directions around one and the same Continuation-Position; that is not yet Intention, so Intention must be a formal probability organization within the Intention-Domain. A probability organization can be driven entirely by external input, unrelated to the source drive of the Intent-Field; that is not yet Intention, so the organization must stand in a source-drive driving relation and itself bear the continuation of the source drive. A probability organization driven by source drive may have directions that lead only to ordinary internal states or motor outputs and enter no Desire-Gate; that is not yet a “direction of desire,” so the probability dimensions must correspond one-to-one to Desire-Gates and lead to the corresponding gates. Stopping at the gate, with a dead end behind it, is still not Intention as On Self-Manifestation means it, so behind every Desire-Gate there must be an interface for commanding tuning.

None of the four items can be deleted (the deletion of P\_I carries H2 ∧ H3^red at this one point), and no two of them can be merged. Within the semantic scope of this chapter there is no fifth independent qualification and no independent cancelling condition.

Together the four items explain why one and the same organization obtains the identity of Intention: by constitution it is a Law Ecology; by the organization of its directions, Probability; by the break-holding relation, a Principle organization; and because it bears source-drive continuation, leads to Desire-Gates and commands tuning, it is Intention. Intention is a Principle organization, but it is not compressed into a single Principle of The Principle Group-Form. None of the following belongs to Intention qualification: the current source-drive pulse, actual Desire-Gate switching, actual tuning, manifestation and behavioral outcomes.

Introduction

We often say “I want to look,” “I want to listen,” “I remember now.” One can experience such stirrings of mind clearly, but the experience comes late. The process of switching Desire-Gates itself has no self-manifestation; only after switching to some Desire-Gate, when manifest circuits participate in the organization behind that gate, does one become aware: “I am going to look.” Intention-Action therefore occurs before awareness. A lower Sedimentation-Field has very low manifestation clarity and no such clear experience, yet it can have Intention.

This raises a question. The source drive only emits pulses: why does it go to this Desire-Gate and not to that one? Why does it go to these two at once and not to the other two? To answer “this direction has a higher probability than that one” is the observer judging; the system itself does not know these values. If the system could know them, the observer would be able to observe its own sedimentary organization, as if a person could feel the very circuits of the brain, which is absurd. So we must ask: between the source drive and the Desire-Gate, is there a path, a Law, that makes the pulse take this way and not that?

This calls for an answer: what, after all, is Intention? What structure must it have at minimum? What is its relation to Probability, Law and Principle? Starting from the fact that Intention holds, this chapter answers these questions through sustained questioning.

This chapter does not discuss whether Intention is acting at this moment; how it starts, turns and stops; where it first forms; how its operating substrate is constructed; whether it is reachable from an initial state; or whether it is stable; nor how Intention is to be determined by external observation. These questions belong to the subsequent chapters of this group form.

Preliminary Distinctions

## Section One　Intention Is Uncountable

Intention is one and uncountable: Intention is Intention itself. This chapter says only “Intention.” Direction dimensions, Desire-Gates, source-drive pulses, action events and successor paths can all be distinguished and counted, but counting them is not counting Intention.

## Section Two　Intention Runs on the Dynamics of the Field

In the Sedimentation-Field, nothing runs except upon its own dynamics. Intention runs on the dynamics of the Sedimentation-Field itself, more specifically of the Self-Manifestation-Field, not outside the dynamics of the field.

## Section Three　Qualification, Action and Manifestation

Intention qualification answers: what structure is this? Intention-Action (IntentAct) answers: is this structure acting at this moment? The two can come apart: Intention can hold while currently at rest, that is, Intent = 1 while IntentAct = 0.

Hence each qualification forced out below asks only whether a structure holds. Where the source drive is concerned, it does not require that a source-drive pulse has already been emitted at this moment; where what lies behind the Desire-Gate is concerned, it does not require that a tuning has already occurred at this moment, nor that some experience has already been changed, still less that anything has already manifested.

The process of switching Desire-Gates itself has no self-manifestation. Only after switching to some Desire-Gate, when manifest circuits participate in the organization behind that gate, does manifestation take place: the Tuning-Field opens some manifest circuit, and only then does one become aware: “I am going to look,” “I am going to taste.” So Intention-Action occurs before awareness:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Intent = 1 ⇏ Manifestation = 1,   IntentAct = 1 ⇏ Manifestation = 1

A lower Sedimentation-Field has very low manifestation clarity; it can have Intention without any clear manifest circuit. A higher Sedimentation-Field allows experiences that have already manifested, such as musing and thinking, to reenter through legal circuits and change the subsequent running of Intention; but such reentry is not a universal qualification of Intention, otherwise lower Sedimentation-Fields would be wrongly excluded.

## Section Four　Probability Dimensions and Desire-Gates

This chapter uses the following notation to distinguish probability dimensions from Desire-Gates. A probability dimension is not a Desire-Gate:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;v\_i ≠ G\_i

A probability dimension leads to a Desire-Gate, written:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;v\_i ⇝ G\_i

The Desire-Gate is the structural gate position of a direction; only behind the Desire-Gate does the tuning side begin. Where the probability organization lies, and how probability dimensions correspond to Desire-Gates, are derived in Steps One and Three; here only the notation is fixed.

## Section Five　Intention Is Not a Reading-Off of Probability

Intention is not a matter of reading off probabilities, comparing probability values and then choosing. The system does not know the rate values of its own directions; if it could, the observer would be able to observe its own sedimentary organization, as if a person could feel the very circuits of the brain. A probability value, if it can be read out at all, is only the observer's representation of an already formed anisotropic generation relation, not a control quantity read by some further observer within the field. That the pulse takes this dimension and not that one must be explained by the paths and Laws within the field between the source drive and the Desire-Gate; this receives a formal statement in Step One.

Line of Argument

We proceed by sustained questioning, step by step; the argument therefore forms a line, and is to be read from the top down.

Step One　Are a Law Ecology and Direction Alone Already Intention?

Intention holds. First we ask: there are Laws in the Sedimentation-Field, and there is direction; on these alone, can we already explain what Intention is?

Consider world W1: the Sedimentation-Field contains many already formed Laws; they can be re-walked and they organize later Causal-Response, forming a complex Law Ecology; continuation also has direction. But these Laws do not form, around one and the same continuation question, an organization of relative generation among directions: they are several continuation events proceeding separately, not several possible successors of one and the same continuation.

W1 is not Intention. Q10 says, “Experience-Field—Field of Law.” The Experience-Field itself contains a great many Laws, yet it is not Intention on that account. Q1 says that Intention is “the direction of desire” and that “Direction is selection, bias”; Q9 says that the Intention-Domain has “selectivity”; Q4 says of seeing, hearing … speaking that “the gate of opening and closing lies in one domain.” The direction of Intention is a direction biased among several destinations within one and the same continuation question.

What W1 lacks can be stated item by item.

First, the directions of Intention must be directions borne by Law. I3 says that “In its ontological bearing, Intention is a Law Ecology, because its direction dimensions are composed entirely of Law-bearing”; I2 says that Intention “is in essence a Law-Ecology organization”; P5 says that what probability generates are probability dimensions, and “every dimension is a Law”; P1 speaks of probability driven by source drive “generating familiar Law-continuation routes in every direction.” The destinations toward which Intention is biased are continuations borne by Law that can be re-walked, not one-off flows. So each direction dimension of Intention obtains formal Law-bearing: this is the content of MultiLawDimension.

Second, these directions must belong to one and the same continuation question: they are several possible successors of one and the same continuation, not several independent events existing at the same time. I4 says that these direction dimensions “jointly bear one Continuation-Position”; Q4 says of seeing, hearing … speaking that “the gate of opening and closing lies in one domain”; Q9 says that the Intention-Domain has “selectivity.” What is selected among are different destinations from one and the same place; two directions proceeding at the same time and unrelated to each other offer nothing to select between. This is the content of CommonContinuationBind.

Third, the relative generation among the directions must be borne by this organization itself; it cannot be supplied by an external selector, nor decided by the system's reading of rate values. I4 says that “the organization itself bears the relative generation”; I5 and I6 point out that the source drive goes to this Desire-Gate and not that one not because the system knows which direction has the higher rate (the system itself does not know these values), but because between the source drive and the Desire-Gate there are paths and Laws that make it take this way and not that; P4 says that the familiar routes of the directions are sedimented from the reward and punishment of each path direction, and appear as anisotropy and as probability values only “if recognized.” So the relative generation among the directions is borne by the paths and Laws already sedimented in the organization itself: this is the content of DomainRelativeRateBearing.

These three are exactly the three items of formal Probability in The Probability Group-Form. The Probability Group-Form also specifically distinguishes “two directions existing at the same time” from “several successor directions of one and the same continuation event,” only the latter constituting the common continuation question of probability; and it distinguishes the “outcome bearing” of an external selector from the “rate-bearing” of a probability organization. All three items have been forced out by the corpus and W1:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Intent ⇒ MultiLawDimension ∧ CommonContinuationBind ∧ DomainRelativeRateBearing

Hence the sufficient direction of The Probability Group-Form (which carries no H) can be invoked, giving:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Probability\_{F,t}(𝒫\_I; C\_I, 𝔯\_I) = 1

This does not say that Intention “additionally has” a probability; it says that Intention, viewed by the relations among its directions, simply is a formal probability organization. Its Law-bearing is borne by the formal dimensional structure of this organization, so no separate item “Law Ecology” needs to be listed.

A further question: where does this probability organization lie?

Consider world W1-B: a formal probability organization lies on the tuning side behind the Desire-Gates and consists in the relative generation among tuning Laws, while the directions in the Intention-Domain do not compose a probability organization.

The probability in W1-B is not the probability of Intention. Step Three　Is a Probability Organization Driven by Source Drive Already a “Direction of Desire”?

Keep P\_I = 1, SourceDrive = 1.

Consider world W3: the organization stands in a source-drive driving relation and does produce different continuations in different directions; but every direction leads only to ordinary internal states, motor outputs or other branches within the field, and none enters a Desire-Gate.

W3 can be an endogenous, probabilized continuation organization, but it is not yet a “direction of desire.” Q4 says of seeing, hearing … speaking that “the gate of opening and closing lies in one domain named the Intention-Domain”; Q11 says that “intention is the toward of going”; P3 says: “Animals gradually evolved Desire-Gates to correspond to the direction dimensions of probability.” The directions of Intention are directions that lead to Desire-Gates.

A further question: what correspondence between dimensions and gates is enough?

Consider world W3-B: two different probability dimensions v₁, v₂ lead to the same Desire-Gate G₁. At the gate the two directions become one destination: v₁ and v₂ are different courses within the probability organization but cannot be told apart at the Desire-Gate, and they are no longer two different “directions of desire.”

Consider world W3-C: one probability dimension v₁ leads to two Desire-Gates G₁, G₂ at once. Within the probability organization v₁ is a single course, yet at the gates it splits into two destinations; how it divides between G₁ and G₂ is not borne by the relative generation of the probability organization but decided by something else.

Neither W3-B nor W3-C is Intention: the former merges different directions of desire into one at the gate; the latter makes one direction split at the gate into a selection that the organization does not bear. I7 says that the probability dimensions lead to the Desire-Gates “in one-to-one correspondence”; P3 says that Desire-Gates exist “to correspond to the direction dimensions of probability.” The two failure worlds show why one-to-one correspondence enters qualification: the distinctions among probability dimensions must be preserved intact up to the gate positions of the Desire-Gates; otherwise either different directions merge at the gate, or one and the same direction splits there into a selection that the organization does not bear.

This yields the third item:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;GateCorrespondence\_{F,t}(𝒫\_I; 𝒢\_I) = 1

It means that between the set V\_I of formal probability dimensions of 𝒫\_I and the set 𝒢\_I of Desire-Gates there is a correspondence unique in both directions, and that each dimension leads to the Desire-Gate corresponding to it:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;∀v\_i ∈ V\_I, ∃! G\_i ∈ 𝒢\_I: Corr(v\_i, G\_i)  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;∀G\_i ∈ 𝒢\_I, ∃! v\_i ∈ V\_I: Corr(v\_i, G\_i)

A probability dimension is not a Desire-Gate (v\_i ≠ G\_i); the Desire-Gate is the structural gate position of that direction.

Corollary Two (from W2, W3). A probability organization driven by source drive is not necessarily Intention: its dimensions must lead one-to-one to Desire-Gates.

Next question: once each dimension leads one-to-one to its corresponding Desire-Gate, is that already Intention?

Step Four　Is Stopping at the Gate Already Intention?

Keep P\_I = 1, SourceDrive = 1, GateCorrespondence = 1.

Consider world W4: each probability dimension leads one-to-one to its Desire-Gate, but every Desire-Gate is a dead end, with no interface for commanding tuning behind it:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;v\_i ⇝ G\_i → ∅

In W4 the system can “get to some gate,” but cannot, from the direction of “desiring to see, to hear, to remember, to think,” go on to invoke the corresponding experiential relations.

Consider also world W4-B: of eight Desire-Gates, only one has a tuning interface behind it; the other seven are dead ends.

Neither W4 nor W4-B is Intention as On Self-Manifestation means it. Q5 says of the Intention-Domain: “It connects experience, passing through the Tuning-Field. &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;TuneInterface\_{F,t}(𝒢\_I) = 1

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;“Intention is a Principle organization” ≠ Principle(Intention; A, J)

Step Seven　Can the Four Items Be Deleted or Merged?

Deletion test.

Delete SourceDrive: keep the formal probability organization, the correspondence between probability dimensions and Desire-Gates, and the tuning interfaces, but let the organization be driven only by current external input. SourceDrive and GateCorrespondence cannot be merged: a probability organization driven by source drive can lead to arbitrary internal branches with no Desire-Gate.

Lemma One (exhaustion of a fifth qualification). Let X be a claimed fifth independent qualification whose carrier is some semantic atom, or an irreducible combination of several atoms.

The argument “no component is a qualification, so neither is their combination” is not used here, since two atoms that individually constitute no qualification may together produce a new force. The argument starts instead from the entries through which qualifying force can come in.

Intention qualification is the existing structure of the organization at the moment of evaluation. Any content, whether a single atom or a combination, can change the current qualification of Intention only through one of the following five channels:

First, changing the constitution of the current probability organization, that is, changing the dimensions borne by Law, their common Continuation-Position, the bearing of the relative generation among the dimensions, or the location of the organization;  
Second, changing the source-drive driving relation;  
Third, changing the correspondence between dimensions and Desire-Gates;  
Fourth, changing the tuning-command interfaces behind the Desire-Gates;

The first four channels are exactly the four items already obtained. Any content acting through them merely makes one of the four items hold or fail; its force is already borne by the four items, and it is not an independent fifth item.

Three Running Cases

The general form does not formalize the details of running, but the structure of this chapter can accommodate the following three cases.

No environmental disturbance. The pulse driven by source drive continues within the already sedimented probabilized Law Ecology. The system does not need to read rate values; the current field state and the already formed Law organization directly determine the real relation of generation, and the pulse proceeds along the corresponding probability dimension to the corresponding Desire-Gate.

With environmental disturbance. The disturbance changes the real field state, the availability of paths, impedance, sedimentary relations or other legal dynamical conditions; the real relative generation of the probability organization changes accordingly, and the source drive can therefore continue along a different dimension. This is not an external observer making a new choice.

Higher-order explicit reentry. Musing, thinking and already manifested experience return through legal circuits and change the current dynamical state of Intention; the new source drive still changes direction by passing through the same probabilized Law Ecology. This is not consciousness observing its own sedimentary organization and then assigning a Desire-Gate by hand.

K-I1: There is a case with Intent = 1 but P\_I = 0, that is, with no formal probability organization, or with the probability organization outside the Intention-Domain.
