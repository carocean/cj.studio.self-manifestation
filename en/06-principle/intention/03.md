# The Necessity Form of Intention

## Section One　Purpose

To prove: within the Intention-qualification working domain of the Self-Manifestation-Field, as long as a probability organization in the Intention-Domain has all four items of the following structure at the same time, Intention holds, and no fifth independent qualification condition needs to be satisfied.

The four-item structure consists of:

First, there is a formal probability organization 𝒫\_I in the Intention-Domain: it lies in the Intention-Domain, before the Desire-Gates; its dimensions are borne by Law and jointly bear the Continuation-Position C\_I, and the relative generation among the dimensions is borne by the organization itself.

Second, 𝒫\_I stands in a source-drive driving relation and itself bears the continuation of the source drive.

Third, the probability dimensions of 𝒫\_I correspond one-to-one to Desire-Gates and lead to the corresponding gates.

Fourth, behind every Desire-Gate there is an interface for commanding tuning.

In formulas:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;N\_I ⇒ Intent\_{F,t}

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;N\_I := P\_I ∧ SourceDrive\_{F,t}(𝒫\_I) ∧ GateCorrespondence\_{F,t}(𝒫\_I; 𝒢\_I) ∧ TuneInterface\_{F,t}(𝒢\_I)  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;P\_I := InIntentDomain\_{F,t}(𝒫\_I) ∧ Probability\_{F,t}(𝒫\_I; C\_I, 𝔯\_I)

“No fifth independent qualification condition needs to be satisfied” means specifically: whether manifestation is currently present, whether there is a clear experience of “I want to …”, whether there is external behavior, whether Desire-Gates are currently being switched, whether musing circuits or thinking circuits participate, whether some dimension currently conforms or has a zero rate, whether probability values can be read out, whether Intention is at rest, whether tuning is currently taking place, whether some action changed the final outcome, whether the system has higher explicit experience: none of these affects the conclusion. A lower Sedimentation-Field with no clear experience of the stirring of mind likewise obtains Intention qualification, as long as the four-item structure holds in full.

The proof does not take the route “The General Form of Intention has already proven that there is no fifth qualification and no independent cancelling condition, so having all four items suffices for Intention to hold.” In that chapter those two conclusions serve the necessary direction; this chapter does not take them as premises and uses them only for a consistency back-test in Section Eight. This chapter carries out the sufficiency procedure anew and independently on the same corpus domain.

The route of proof is:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;fixed source-text corpus domain → atom classification → review of irreducible conjunctions → closure of current qualification → N\_I completely realizes the qualification → Intent

The corresponding three theorems are: first obtain, on the fixed corpus, all the duties of current Intention qualification, and prove that there is no independent cancelling condition (Theorem One); then prove that the four-item structure of N\_I completely realizes these duties (Theorem Two); finally obtain the Theorem of the Necessity of Intention (Theorem Three).

## Section Four　Rules of Atomization and Classification

### 4.1 Classification

Each atom is assigned to one of the following classes by its dominant duty.

CurrentIntentQualification: by virtue of what an organization in the current field now obtains Intention qualification (the obtaining side). By the question answered it is further divided into four classes:

U\_P　probability organization: whether the Intention-Domain contains a directional organization borne by Law, with common continuation, whose relative generation is borne by the organization itself;  
U\_S　source-drive driving: whether this organization stands within source-drive driving, and whether the continuation of the source drive is borne by the organization itself;  
U\_G　correspondence between probability dimensions and Desire-Gates: whether these directions lead to Desire-Gates, and whether directions and Desire-Gates correspond one-to-one;  
U\_T　tuning interface: whether tuning can be commanded behind the Desire-Gates.

Disqualification: atoms that, when the conditions of obtaining of CurrentIntentQualification all hold, can on their own make the current Intention qualification fail (the cancelling side).

The remaining classes are together called U\_O:

Relation　the relation of Intention to Principle, Law, Probability and so on;  
Designation　naming;  
Location　the field where Intention and its components lie, or the source of a category;  
WorkDomain　working-domain constraints;  
Act　the current action or general action of Intention;  
Dyn　how Intention changes and how it maintains its state;  
Source　how Intention is generated and how it forms;  
Representation　representation, recognition, read-out;  
Boundary　ontic boundary, that is, the exclusion of something from belonging to Intention;  
Consequence　the consequences of Intention-Action, including manifestation;  
Implementation　manner of realization.

### 4.2 Criteria of classification

(a) Criterion of question: ask whether the atom answers “is there a formal directional organization” (U\_P), “is the source drive borne by this organization” (U\_S), “do the directions correspond one-to-one to Desire-Gates” (U\_G), “can tuning be commanded behind the Desire-Gates” (U\_T), or some other question (U\_O).

(b) Criterion of phase: ask whether the atom says “how it forms” (Source), “whether it now holds” (CurrentIntentQualification), “how it changes” (Dyn), “what it is doing” (Act), or “what it appears as” (Representation, Consequence).

(c) Criterion of deletion: suppose the atom is negated, with the other atoms unchanged. If the organization still has all four classes of obtaining-side content, and no sentence in the fixed corpus denies that Intention holds when that atom is negated, then the atom does not belong to CurrentIntentQualification. This criterion is used only to re-check atoms already assigned to U\_O by (a), not to produce duties of the obtaining side; duties of the obtaining side are read from the corpus only by (a).

(d) Review of the cancelling side: if an atom looks formally like a negation (“if some situation appears, it is not Intention”), it must be specially reviewed: supposing the conditions of obtaining all hold, can the atom on its own make Intent = 0? If, in order to take effect, it must make some condition of obtaining fail, then it is not an independent cancelling condition and is returned to its original class.

### 4.3 Review of irreducible conjunctions

After all single atoms have been classified, the irreducible conjunctions authorized by the fixed corpus must still be checked. Two atoms that individually constitute no qualification may together produce a new force, so “the conjunction is not a qualification” cannot be inferred from “none of the parts is a qualification.” If someone claims that a conjunction of two or more atoms

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;X = u\_{j1} ∧ … ∧ u\_{jn}

bears a new current Intention-qualification duty or cancelling duty, then grounds explicitly authorizing this combination to bear that duty must be found in the fixed corpus. Such joint authorization can occur within one sentence, or be formed by causal, conditional, referential, summarizing or anaphoric relations across sentences.

Conjunctions are reviewed in three classes:

(i) Conjunctions containing members of U\_P, U\_S, U\_G, U\_T: the qualification members among them are realized by the four-item structure; the remaining members can form a fifth duty only if the corpus explicitly gives the combination added current-qualification semantics or cancelling semantics.

(ii) Conjunctions made wholly of U\_O atoms: without authorization by the corpus, they cannot rise to qualification merely by being conjoined.

(iii) Claims that the conjunction produces new current-qualification semantics that existed in none of the atoms beforehand: if there is no authorization for these semantics in the fixed corpus, the claim has gone beyond the frozen corpus domain and does not enter the proof.

For a conjunction X to become a fifth qualification, it must also satisfy the following: while the four-item structure persists, negating X makes Intent = 0, and this cancellation does not appear as the exit of any of the four items. Otherwise X is only an unfolding of one of the four items.

## Section Six　Closure of Current Qualification

U\_P　probability organization: Intention has several distinguishable destinations (u4); each direction is borne by formal Law, a Law Ecology organized in the form of probability (u1, u2); the differences among the directions are borne by already sedimented familiar routes (u9), and the path between the source drive and the Desire-Gate is borne by Law, making the pulse take this path and not that one (u8); the directions jointly bear one Continuation-Position (u6); the selection and bias among the directions, that is, relative generation, are borne by the organization itself (u3, u5, u7); this organization lies in the Intention-Domain, not behind the Desire-Gates (u10).

## Section Eleven　Breach

The case that directly breaches the main theorem is:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;N\_I = 1 ∧ Intent = 0

Breaches fall into four classes according to the level at which they occur. If any of them appears, a fresh review is required:

Breach One　Omission in the corpus domain: outside the fixed corpus there exist sentences bearing current Intention qualification, or such sentences are added when the corpus is revised, and their atoms cannot be assigned to U\_P, U\_S, U\_G, U\_T or U\_O.

Breach Two　Counterexample of a fifth qualification: there exists a single atom or irreducible conjunction X with grounds in the corpus that can still on its own cancel the qualification when the conditions of obtaining all hold, and that can neither be assigned to the four-item structure nor be attributed to a downstream task.

Breach Three　Failure of structural realization: N\_I = 1, but some content of at least one of U\_P, U\_S, U\_G, U\_T is actually not realized, showing that the correspondence of Section Seven is mistaken.

Breach Four　Anti-sufficiency world: the four classes of duty have all actually been realized, yet some independent factor still makes Intent = 0, and this factor, in taking effect, does not appear as the exit of any item of the four-item structure.

The following cases do not constitute a breach:

IntentCoreCert^M = ⊥: this only means that the formal material is insufficient.

Some dimension currently having rate zero, or some dimension not currently conforming: a zero rate does not cause the dimension to exit.

Probability values being unreadable: this is a question of representation.

No current manifestation, no experience of “I want to …”, no external behavior: these belong to consequence.

No current source-drive pulse, no Desire-Gate switching, no tuning: these belong to action.

No participation of musing circuits or thinking circuits: this belongs to the mode of initiation.

Intention later losing stability: this falls within the scope of the Stability Form.

Intention being unreachable from some initial state: this falls within the scope of the Reachability Form.

The appearance of a new destination that has never obtained Law-bearing: it is not a formal dimension and does not enter N\_I.
