# The General Form of Intention-Action

This chapter attempts a general proposition:

The fact that Intention already holds and that, within the current declared unit, there is indeed one complete instance of Intention-Action necessarily means that in this one real current action, a source-drive continuation belonging to Intention itself actually occurs and enters the probability organization of Intention; in this continuation, the probability organization, as probability, actually bears the formation of relative generation among the formal directions; the directional continuation thus formed actually enters the Desire-Gate formally corresponding to that direction; and once past the Desire-Gate, it actually enters the tuning-command interface of that Desire-Gate, so that the tuning side obtains a current command from Intention.

The question this raises is:

When Intention already holds and one complete current instance of Intention-Action holds, which current duties must hold at minimum for it still to be Intention that is acting, and not merely Intention at rest, not merely source drive running or probability running, nor merely a direction that has arrived at the gate?

The degree to which this chapter answers the question must be stated in advance. This chapter proves only the necessary direction: if one complete instance of Intention-Action holds, there is necessarily a certain current structure. It does not prove that this structure suffices for Intention-Action to hold; the sufficient direction belongs to The Necessity Form of Intention-Action. This chapter does not prove Intention qualification anew; how Intention-Action starts, continues, stops and restarts belongs to the three Intention-Dynamics chapters; how the Desire-Gate switches from one to another belongs to the three Desire-Gate switching chapters.

In this chapter we set out, through sustained questioning, to find where the proof should lead, and to derive the general form of Intention-Action.

Our objective is:

to state where the difference between Intention qualification and Intention-Action lies; to obtain the minimal necessary structure of the current action of Intention; to show why the duties of the layers in this structure progress layer by layer and must fall on one and the same action chain; and to give the minimality argument, the boundaries and the breach paths.

The result first.

Formula of the Necessary Structure of Intention-Action

Within the working domain where Intent\_{F,t} = 1:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;IntentAct\_{F,t}(ξ\_t) ⇒ N\_IAct

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;N\_IAct := ∃w\_I \[ A1(w\_I) ∧ A2(w\_I) ∧ A3(w\_I) ∧ A4(w\_I) \]

where:

A1　Current occurrence of the source-drive duty (CurrentSourceOccurrence): in the current action unit, a continuation belonging to the source-drive duty of Intention itself actually occurs and enters 𝒫\_I.

A2　Current conformance of the probability duty (IntentProbabilityDuty): in the same continuation as A1, a legal current realization of 𝒫\_I, as a probability organization, actually bears the formation of relative generation among the formal directions.

A3　Current passage through the corresponding Desire-Gate (CurrentGatePass): the directional continuation actually formed by A2 actually enters, along the formal correspondence v\_i ⇝ G\_i, the Desire-Gate corresponding to that direction.

A4　Current conformance of tuning command (CurrentTuneCommand): once past the Desire-Gate, the same action chain actually enters the tuning-command interface of that Desire-Gate, so that the tuning side obtains a current command from Intention.

w\_I is the witness of this one action. The four items must hold on one and the same witness: the same 𝒫\_I, the same real continuation, the same conformance of the probability duty, the same actual direction, the same Desire-Gate corresponding to that direction, and the same tuning-command chain issuing from that Desire-Gate.

The four items do not imply one another. Only the cumulative stages, built up layer by layer,

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;R1 := A1,  R2 := R1 ∧ A2,  R3 := R2 ∧ A3,  R4 := R3 ∧ A4

have the inclusion relation R4 ⇒ R3 ⇒ R2 ⇒ R1; N\_IAct is just ∃w\_I R4(w\_I).

The above, placed at the head of the chapter, only announces the result and is not a premise of the subsequent derivation.

In one sentence

One complete current instance of Intention-Action is not a matter of Intention having come to hold, nor of where the outcome lands: its source drive is really running at this moment, probability is really running as probability at this moment, the direction has really entered the corresponding Desire-Gate at this moment, and once past the gate it has really commanded tuning; and these four things are one and the same action.

Abstract

This chapter answers: when Intention already holds, what current structure must one complete current instance of Intention-Action have at minimum in order to hold?

The inquiry starts from Intention that holds and is currently at rest. All four items of qualification are in place and there is no real continuation at all; that is not Intention acting, so it is required that a source-drive continuation of Intention itself actually occur at present. The source drive runs, but the continuation goes only along one fixed member Law, and the probability organization does not bear the relative generation among the directions; that is bare source drive, a single Law acting alone, not Intention acting, so current conformance of the probability duty in the same continuation is required. Source drive runs and probability runs, but the direction formed enters only ordinary internal states or general outputs and not the corresponding Desire-Gate; that is probability acting, not yet the “direction of desire” acting, so current passage through the corresponding Desire-Gate is required. Stopping at the Desire-Gate, with no tuning command behind it, is only a legal prefix of action, so current conformance of tuning command is required. The four layers must fall on one and the same action chain and cannot be spliced together from different events.

None of the four items can be deleted, weakened, replaced or merged. On the frozen action semantic domain there is no fifth universally necessary action duty, and no independent cancelling condition has been found. This chapter proves only the necessary direction and carries no new H.

Introduction

We often say “I want to go and look.” This “want” is usually known only after the fact: the process of switching Desire-Gates itself has no self-manifestation; only after switching to some Desire-Gate, when manifest circuits participate in the organization behind that gate, does one become aware: “I am going to look.” So Intention-Action occurs before awareness. That Intention is acting does not mean that it has already manifested, nor that it has already been experienced.

On the other hand, that Intention holds does not mean that Intention is acting. A person who is awake and thinking of nothing has Intention that holds, with the whole structure in place, yet at this moment it can be at rest. The main book says: “It must issue from the Intention-Domain itself. Therefore there must be an intention that first sets it in motion. This is called thought stirring within stillness.” To pass from stillness to motion, there must be a setting in motion by Intention. It also says: “Bias arises with the stirring of mind, selects the gate of desire and sets tuning to work; it must first determine, then enact.” The stirring of mind first determines a direction and selects a Desire-Gate, and then commands tuning.

The question this raises is: what current things must happen for there to be one complete instance of Intention-Action? Is it enough that source drive runs, or must probability run? Is it enough that the direction reaches the Desire-Gate, or must it also pass the gate and command tuning? Can these things happen separately, at different moments and in different directions, and then be put together and counted as one action?

This chapter does not discuss whether Intention qualification itself holds; how Intention-Action starts, continues, stops and restarts; how the Desire-Gate switches from one to another; nor how to determine by external observation whether Intention-Action holds. These questions belong to the other chapters of this group form.

Preliminary Distinctions

## Section One　Qualification, Action and Manifestation

Intention qualification answers: what structure is this? Intention-Action (IntentAct) answers: does this structure have one complete action within the current declared unit? The two can come apart: Intention can hold while currently at rest, that is, Intent = 1 while IntentAct = 0. So the entry condition of this chapter, Intent = 1, is only object qualification, not a component of the action structure, and IntentAct = 1 cannot be inferred from Intent = 1.

Nor is Intention-Action the same as manifestation. The process of switching Desire-Gates itself has no self-manifestation; manifestation takes place only when manifest circuits participate in the organization behind the Desire-Gate; Intention-Action occurs before awareness:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;IntentAct = 1 ⇏ Manifestation = 1

Nor is action the same as the success of its outcome. Even when the current duties have reached conformance, the final outcome can still be changed or offset by other mechanisms.

## Section Two　The Current Action Unit Is Not a Zero-Width Instant

ξ\_t is the declared situation of the current action, including the current field state, the current input, the context of the current real running, and the information needed for this declaration of action. It is not a zero-width mathematical instant. One action can have an internal order: first a direction is formed, then the Desire-Gate is passed, then tuning command is entered. The main book's “it must first determine, then enact” speaks of exactly this internal order.

But this internal order is only the progression of duties within one action unit, not a cross-time dynamic process. How action starts, continues, stops and restarts, and how the state of action changes across time, belong to the three Intention-Dynamics chapters; this chapter does not define them.

Hence IntentAct\_{F,t}(ξ\_t) in this chapter declares whether, within the current declared unit ξ\_t, one complete instance of Intention-Action holds. It does not take “action being in formation” at any intermediate point within this action as the criterion of a complete instance of Intention-Action. When an action is under way, having just reached the Desire-Gate and not yet entered tuning command, the action chain has already really occurred and is advancing, but as one complete instance of Intention-Action it has not yet closed. R1, R2 and R3 below are such legal duty prefixes. How a prefix starts, continues and passes into a complete action is handed to the three Intention-Dynamics chapters.

## Section Three　Atomic Duties and Cumulative Stages

This chapter distinguishes two sets of notation. A1 to A4 are atomic duties, each stating one current matter; R1 to R4 are cumulative stages, R\_k being the conjunction of the first k duties on the same witness. The atomic duties do not imply one another, because, as will be seen below, for each layer there is a world in which all the preceding layers hold and this layer does not. The inclusion relation among the cumulative stages

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;R4 ⇒ R3 ⇒ R2 ⇒ R1

is obtained by definition and is not a general causal Law in the field. Writing R1 ≺ R2 ≺ R3 ≺ R4 means that each later layer is evaluated only on the same real action chain on which all the preceding layers already hold.

Taken alone, each atomic duty asks only whether the content of its own layer holds on some continuation: A1 asks whether a source-drive continuation of Intention itself occurs; A2, whether the probability organization bears relative generation; A3, whether the directional continuation enters the corresponding Desire-Gate along the formal correspondence; A4, whether the tuning command behind the gate is entered. Expressions in the definitions such as “the same continuation,” “the directional continuation formed by A2” and “the same action chain” are the readings that apply when the four items are evaluated conjunctively on the same witness, and are supplied by the discipline of common witness below.

## Section Four　Desire-Gate Passage and Desire-Gate Switching

In this chapter CurrentGatePass means that this action has passed through the Desire-Gate corresponding to its direction. Desire-Gate switching (GateSwitch) means that the gate position changes from one Desire-Gate to another. The two differ:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;CurrentGatePass ≠ GateSwitch

If the previous action was v\_a ⇝ G\_a and this action is still v\_a ⇝ G\_a, both have passed through a Desire-Gate, yet the second involves no switching. Conversely, the state of a gate can also be changed by external forcing, ordinary gating or other mechanisms without going through the duty chain of Intention. So:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;IntentAct ⇏ GateSwitch,  GateSwitch ⇏ IntentAct

The event structure of Desire-Gate switching belongs to Chapters 7 to 9.

## Section Five　External Induction and External Bearing

External conditions can trigger, disturb, reenter or change the field state, and thereby take part in inducing Intention-Action; but no external mechanism can wholly replace Intention in bearing this continuation from source drive to direction. An external stimulus that gives rise to a source-drive continuation of Intention itself is legal action:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;external trigger → A1

If an external selector directly decides which Desire-Gate the action lands on, skipping the source-drive continuation and the probability bearing of Intention itself, then it is not Intention acting. External induction is not external bearing.

Line of Argument

We proceed by sustained questioning, step by step; the argument therefore forms a line, and is to be read from the top down.

Step One　Is Intention That Holds and Is at Rest Already Acting?

We begin by asking about the weakest case. Suppose Intent = 1: in the Intention-Domain there is a formal probability organization 𝒫\_I, which stands in source-drive driving and itself bears source-drive continuation; the probability dimensions correspond one-to-one to Desire-Gates, and behind every Desire-Gate there is a tuning-command interface. But at this moment no real continuation belonging to the duty chain of Intention itself is occurring.

W-A1　All four items of qualification are in place; there is currently no source-drive continuation of Intention itself.

W-A1 is Intention at rest. Not one item of qualification is missing, but Q12 says “there must be an intention that first sets it in motion”: to pass from stillness to motion, there must be a setting in motion by Intention. Intention at rest has no setting in motion, and cannot be said to be acting at this moment:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Intent = 1 ∧ no current source-drive continuation of Intention itself ⇒ IntentAct = 0

So Intention qualification alone cannot show that one complete instance of Intention-Action already holds within the current declared unit. What is missing is that a source-drive continuation belonging to Intention itself is in fact currently occurring. This gives the first duty:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A1 := CurrentSourceOccurrence: in the current action unit, a continuation belonging to the source-drive duty of Intention itself actually occurs and enters 𝒫\_I.

A1 differs from SourceDrive in the qualification. SourceDrive is a structure: 𝒫\_I stands in a source-drive driving relation and itself bears the continuation of the source drive. A1 is the real current conformance of this duty: such a continuation is in fact under way at this moment. I8 says that source drive belongs to Intention itself and that one “cannot say ‘Intention, when there is source drive’”; there is no contradiction here: that source drive belongs to Intention is qualification; that source drive is running at this moment is action.

A1 does not require that a pulse be newly emitted at this moment; it requires only that a source-drive continuation of Intention itself really exist in the current action chain. Nor does A1 exclude external participation: an external stimulus can trigger, disturb or change the field state and so give rise to this continuation. But what bears this continuation must be the source-drive duty of Intention itself.

W-A1-B　An external selector directly decides which Desire-Gate the action lands on, and post-gate tuning follows; the source-drive continuation of Intention itself takes no part.

In W-A1-B there is activity at both the Desire-Gate and the tuning, but the direction this time was not determined by Intention; it was selected externally. This is external bearing, not external induction, and not Intention acting. It likewise lacks A1.

Step Two　Is Source Drive Running Already Intention Acting?

Now keep R1 = A1 = 1: a source-drive continuation of Intention itself is in fact currently occurring and has entered 𝒫\_I.

We ask: is this already Intention acting?

W-A2　The current source-drive continuation enters 𝒫\_I but goes only along one fixed member Law; the probability organization does not bear the relative generation among the formal directions, the other directions play no role at all in this continuation, and this one Law alone decides where it goes.

In W-A2 source drive is running, but probability is not running as probability. This is bare source drive, or a single Law acting alone. P1 says that probability driven by source drive makes it possible, “while passing through many Law Ecologies, to go returning to one in every direction”: the going of Intention is source drive going returning to one among the directions by way of the probability organization, not one Law going by itself. I6 asks, “Between the source drive and the Desire-Gate, is there a path in between? Or rather, is there a Law that makes it take this path and not that one?”: taking this path and not that one means singling out this one within the generation relation among the directions, and that relation is borne by the organization; I5 further excludes “reading off the rate values of the directions and then choosing.” With a single Law acting alone, no relative generation among the directions is being formed, so there can be no talk of “selection” and “bias,” and the “first determine” of Q13 has nothing to rest on.

So W-A2 is not Intention acting. What is missing is that, in the same continuation, the probability organization, as probability, actually bears the formation of relative generation among the directions. This gives the second duty:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A2 := IntentProbabilityDuty: in the same real current continuation as A1, a legal current realization of 𝒫\_I, as a probability organization, actually bears the formation of the relative-generation relation among the formal directions.

Here a distinction must be drawn:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;some one member Law currently running ≠ the probability organization currently running as probability

A2 inherits the semantics of duty conformance from The General Form of Probability-Action, but is not Probability-Action itself: A2 is one layer in the action chain of Intention, and its identity requirement is laid down by the common witness below.

W-A2 is a legal world: R1 = 1 while A2 = 0. So A2 cannot be absorbed by A1.

Step Three　Is Probability Running Already Intention Acting?

Keep R2 = 1: a source-drive continuation of Intention itself is currently occurring, and in the same continuation the probability organization actually bears the relative generation among the directions.

We ask: is this already Intention acting?

W-A3　Source drive runs and probability runs, but the directional continuation actually formed this time enters an ordinary internal state, a general output, or some interface that is not a Desire-Gate, and does not enter the Desire-Gate formally corresponding to that direction.

In W-A3 there is source drive and there is probability, and action as probability has already reached conformance. But Q11 says that Intention is “the toward of going,” and Q1 that Intention is “the direction of desire”; Q13 says that the stirring of mind “selects the gate of desire”; I7 says that “the probability dimensions lead to the Desire-Gates in one-to-one correspondence.” In W-A3 the direction formed this time has not reached its Desire-Gate, and the “direction of desire” has no conformance in the current action. What is acting is probability driven by source drive, not yet Intention.

So W-A3 is not Intention acting. What is missing is that the directional continuation actually formed this time enters the Desire-Gate of that direction along the formal correspondence. This gives the third duty:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A3 := CurrentGatePass: in the same action chain, the directional continuation actually formed by A2 actually enters, along the formal correspondence v\_i ⇝ G\_i, the Desire-Gate corresponding to that direction.

A3 requires the corresponding Desire-Gate. If the directional continuation enters the Desire-Gate of another direction, or enters a gate to which no direction corresponds, then this action has not gone along the formal correspondence, and A3 does not hold.

A3 answers whether this action has passed through the corresponding Desire-Gate, not whether the gate position has changed. In successive actions along the same direction through the same Desire-Gate, each one can have A3 = 1 without any Desire-Gate switch.

W-A3 is a legal world: R2 = 1 while A3 = 0. So A3 cannot be absorbed by A2. It is also A3 that distinguishes Intention-Action from Probability-Action in general: Probability-Action requires only duty conformance of relative generation, not that the output end be a Desire-Gate.

Step Four　Is Stopping at the Gate Already Intention Acting?

Keep R3 = 1: source drive runs, probability runs, and the direction has actually entered its corresponding Desire-Gate.

We ask: is this already Intention acting?

W-A4　This action chain stops at the Desire-Gate. Behind the Desire-Gate there is a legal tuning-command interface, but this time it is not entered; the tuning side obtains no current command from Intention.

In W-A4 the direction has reached the gate. But Q13 says that it “selects the gate of desire and sets tuning to work; it must first determine, then enact”: to determine is to determine a direction and select a Desire-Gate, and to enact is to command tuning; the stirring of mind does not stop at selecting the gate, but must also enact. Q5 says, “It connects experience, passing through the Tuning-Field. That the Intent-Field can drive tuning is the work of the Intention-Domain”; I2 says that Intention, “once past the Desire-Gate, chiefly commands tuning to break the holding of Law in the Experience-Field.” Stopping at the gate involves only “determining” and no “enacting”; the “work” of Intention has no conformance.

So W-A4 is not a complete instance of Intention-Action. It is only a legal prefix of action: the duty chain of this declaration has advanced as far as the Desire-Gate, but the complete action has not yet closed. An action chain that halts at the Desire-Gate and an action that is under way but has not yet passed behind the gate are treated alike in this chapter: neither is yet one complete instance of Intention-Action, and the difference between them lies in whether the prefix goes on afterward, which belongs to the three Intention-Dynamics chapters. What is missing is actual entry into tuning command after passing the gate. This gives the fourth duty:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A4 := CurrentTuneCommand: once past the Desire-Gate, the same action chain actually enters the legal tuning-command interface of that Desire-Gate, so that the tuning side obtains a current command from Intention.

A4 is stronger than TuneInterface in the qualification: TuneInterface says only that behind the gate there is a legal tuning-command interface; A4 says that this action has really used that interface. A4 is also weaker than the success of the outcome:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A4 ⇏ TuneSuccess

A4 does not require that tuning has been completed, that the Experience-Field has changed, that the outcome of tuning prevails, or that some target has been achieved, nor that anything has already manifested or that there is already external behavior. The Tuning-Field's subsequent invoking of experience, and the manifestation that takes place as manifest circuits participate, all come after A4 and belong to consequence.

“It must first determine, then enact” supports R3 preceding R4. This is a progression of duties within one action unit, not the starting and stopping of action.

W-A4 is a legal world: R3 = 1 while A4 = 0. So A4 cannot be absorbed by A3. This agrees with Form Chapter 4's separate listing of the Boundary-Gate relation and the integrative relation: reaching the Desire-Gate is a Boundary-Gate relation; commanding tuning is an integrative relation.

Step Five　Can the Four Layers Be Spliced Together from Different Events?

A1 to A4 have now all been obtained. We ask: can they hold separately?

W-Splice　At this moment there is a source-drive continuation of Intention itself, but it is a single Law acting alone; elsewhere, the probability organization bears relative generation in another continuation, but does not reach a gate; in yet another continuation, a directional continuation enters its Desire-Gate along the formal correspondence, but the direction this time was selected by an external selector; and behind yet another Desire-Gate a tuning command takes place, but not on the same chain as any of the foregoing. Each of the four items holds somewhere.

In W-Splice no single action is Intention acting: every real chain breaks at some layer. Putting the four fragments together does not constitute one instance of Intention-Action. The wrong way to write it is:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(∃w1 A1(w1)) ∧ (∃w2 A2(w2)) ∧ (∃w3 A3(w3)) ∧ (∃w4 A4(w4))

The right way to write it is:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;∃w\_I \[ A1(w\_I) ∧ A2(w\_I) ∧ A3(w\_I) ∧ A4(w\_I) \]

Write this identity requirement as B\_same^IAct: A1 to A4 must belong to the same 𝒫\_I, the same real current continuation, the same conformance of the probability duty, the same actual direction, the same Desire-Gate formally corresponding to that direction, and the same tuning-command chain issuing from that Desire-Gate.

B\_same^IAct is not a fifth action duty; it is only the well-formed binding discipline of the witness, stating in what sense the four duties belong to one and the same action. This chapter fixes only this semantic identity; its formal objects, such as the identities of events, runs, branches, directional duties, Desire-Gate duties, tuning duties and source-drive links, are defined by The Mathematical Form of Intention-Action.

The cumulative stages are therefore all on the same witness: R1 is A1; R2 adds A2 on the same witness; R3 and R4 likewise. R1, R2 and R3 may be called legal duty prefixes of a complete instance of Intention-Action, meaning that the duty chain of this declaration has advanced to that layer but the complete action has not yet closed. They are not new states of action and receive no separate names; only R4 closes the necessary structure of this chapter.

Step Six　Can the Four Items Be Deleted, Weakened, Replaced or Merged?

Deletion. Delete A1, and Intention at rest would be wrongly judged to have one complete action (W-A1), and post-gate tuning decided by an external selector would also be wrongly judged to be Intention-Action (W-A1-B). Delete A2, and a single Law acting alone would be wrongly judged to be Intention-Action (W-A2). Delete A3, and Probability-Action driven by source drive would be wrongly judged to be Intention-Action even if it output only to an ordinary internal branch (W-A3), so that Intention-Action could no longer be distinguished from Probability-Action. Delete A4, and stopping at the gate would be wrongly judged to be a complete instance of Intention-Action (W-A4). None of the four items can be deleted.

Weakening. A1 cannot be weakened to “the system is currently active”; it must be a continuation belonging to the source-drive duty of Intention itself. A2 cannot be weakened to “the member Law of some probability dimension is running”; it must be the current conformance of the duty of relative generation under the identity of the probability organization. A3 cannot be weakened to “some gate is currently entered”; it must be the formal Desire-Gate corresponding to the current actual direction. A4 cannot be weakened to “some change of state has occurred behind the Desire-Gate”; it must be the current conformance of the legal tuning-command duty.

Replacement. None of the following can replace A1 to A4: manifestation, which is not a necessary condition of action; a clear experience of “I want to …,” which comes late; external behavior, which belongs to effects further downstream; final success of the outcome, which is not a current duty; attention, which is the gathering of Intention at the place it tunes and is one kind of object of action; Desire-Gate switching, since this action can continue on the same Desire-Gate without switching; the tuning result, since current command does not mean that tuning has been completed; reading out probability values, since the system need not read rate values; every dimension having a positive current rate, since a zero-rate dimension is still a formal direction; ontological randomness, since deterministic running does not cancel action; musing and thinking, which are ways of starting higher-order reentry; the Power-Ring, which is a conjecture about the manner of realization; a unique physical bearing, since one duty can be jointly borne by several realizations; and starting, continuing and stopping, which belong to the three Intention-Dynamics chapters.

Merging. A1 and A2 cannot be merged: A1 = 1 does not guarantee A2 = 1 (W-A2); conversely, when the probability organization is acting, what triggers it may not be the source-drive duty chain of Intention itself on this occasion, so the semantics of A2 cannot stand in for A1. A2 and A3 cannot be merged: Probability-Action requires only duty conformance of relative generation, and does not in itself require the output end to be a Desire-Gate (W-A3). A3 and A4 cannot be merged: reaching the Desire-Gate is not entering the post-gate tuning command (W-A4), and the Boundary-Gate relation and the integrative relation are already listed separately upstream. A1 and A4 cannot be joined directly, skipping the two middle layers: if only source-drive occurrence and tuning command were required, the identities of probability and of the Desire-Gate in between would vanish entirely, and any fixed “source drive to tuning” line would be wrongly judged to be Intention-Action.

Step Seven　Simultaneous Multiple Dimensions and Joint Use

Q3 says that experiences “can operate together and can also switch.” Can one instance of Intention-Action achieve conformance in only one direction and pass through only one Desire-Gate?

This chapter makes no such stipulation. As a necessary condition, it requires only that there be at least one legal current direction–Desire-Gate–tuning-command chain satisfying A2 to A4 on the same witness, that is, that there exist v\_i, G\_i and a post-gate tuning-command interface q\_i such that A2, A3 and A4 hold on w\_I. As for whether several formal dimensions can conform simultaneously, The Mathematical Form of Intention-Action formally admits multiple complete witnesses; whether several Desire-Gates can conform jointly, and how joint use is compatible with the discipline of mutually exclusive successors of Probability-Action, are handed to the chapters on Desire-Gate switching and Intention-Dynamics. This chapter does not stipulate in advance, for the sake of formal neatness, that one action can have only one dimension or one Desire-Gate.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;N\_IAct := ∃w\_I R4(w\_I) = ∃w\_I \[ A1(w\_I) ∧ A2(w\_I) ∧ A3(w\_I) ∧ A4(w\_I) \]

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;IntentAct\_{F,t}(ξ\_t) ⇒ N\_IAct

Proof. Suppose IntentAct\_{F,t}(ξ\_t) = 1.

By Q12 and u31, Intention-Action necessarily involves a setting in motion by Intention; W-A1 shows that full qualification without current source-drive continuation is not Intention-Action, and W-A1-B shows that post-gate tuning borne by an external selector is not Intention-Action (Step One). Three Running Cases

The general form does not formalize the details of running, but the structure of this chapter can accommodate the following three cases. In each case A1 to A4 conform on one and the same action chain.

No environmental disturbance. A source-drive continuation of Intention itself occurs (A1); in the probabilized Law Ecology already sedimented, the probability organization bears the relative generation among the directions, and one direction among them is taken (A2); the system need not read rate values, since the current field state and the existing Law organization directly decide the real generation relation. This direction enters its corresponding Desire-Gate (A3) and, once past the gate, commands tuning (A4).

With environmental disturbance. The disturbance changes the real field state, the availability of paths, impedance, sedimentary relations or other legal dynamic conditions, and the relative generation really borne by the probability organization changes accordingly (A2); the source drive can therefore continue along a different direction, enter a different corresponding Desire-Gate (A3), and command tuning there (A4). The disturbance took part in inducing, but what bears this continuation is still the source-drive duty of Intention itself (A1); this is not an external observer making the selection anew, nor an external selector selecting the Desire-Gate on Intention's behalf.

Higher-order explicit reentry. Musing, thinking and experiences that have already manifested return through legal circuits and change the current dynamic state of Intention; the new source-drive continuation still occurs in Intention itself (A1), still changes direction through the same probabilized Law Ecology (A2), enters the corresponding Desire-Gate (A3), and, once past the gate, commands tuning (A4). This is not consciousness observing its own sedimentary organization and then designating a Desire-Gate by hand. How reentry starts an action belongs to the three Intention-Dynamics chapters; whether a Desire-Gate switch brought about by it actually occurs belongs to the three Desire-Gate switching chapters; how a switch is started belongs to the three Intention-Dynamics chapters.

Breach Argument

K-IA1: There is a case with IntentAct = 1 but A1 = 0. Minimal counterexample form: there is no source-drive continuation of Intention itself, the direction and the Desire-Gate are decided by an external selector, tuning occurs behind the gate, and this case should still be judged Intention-Action.

K-IA3: There is a case with IntentAct = 1 but A3 = 0. Minimal counterexample form: Probability-Action driven by source drive outputs only to an ordinary internal branch or a non-corresponding gate, and this case should still be judged Intention-Action.

K-IA4: There is a case with IntentAct = 1 but A4 = 0. Minimal counterexample form: the direction enters the corresponding Desire-Gate and stops there, no tuning command is entered behind the gate, and this case should still be judged a complete instance of Intention-Action.

K-IA5: A1 to A4 can hold only separately, on different action units, different 𝒫\_I, different directions, different Desire-Gates or different tuning chains, and yet IntentAct = 1 can still be judged to hold.

K-IA6: In the frozen action semantic domain U\_IAct^src or an extension of it there appears a truly independent fifth universal duty that can be assigned neither to A1 to A4 nor to inducing, dynamics, switching, outcome, manifestation, realization or the other chapters of this group form.

K-IA7: If it can be proven that some item is wholly absorbed by the others, so that deleting it introduces no legal misjudgment, then the minimality of the four items is breached.

The following do not constitute a breach of this chapter: no change to another Desire-Gate; no manifestation; no awareness; no external behavior; no change in the final experience; the effect of tuning being offset by other mechanisms; no attention being formed; no musing; the system running deterministically; no probability values being read; some formal dimensions currently having zero rate; an external stimulus taking part in inducing; a world that satisfies N\_IAct yet is not Intention-Action, which belongs to the sufficient direction and is dealt with by The Necessity Form of Intention-Action.

Closing

Starting from Intention that holds and is at rest, this chapter has pressed its questions all the way through: why full qualification is still not action, why source drive running is not enough, why probability running is not enough, why reaching the Desire-Gate is not enough, and why the four layers cannot be spliced together. The conclusions of this chapter compress into two sentences:

One complete instance of Intention-Action is this: its source drive is really running at this moment, probability is really running as probability at this moment, the direction is really entering the corresponding Desire-Gate at this moment, and once past the gate it really commands tuning; these four layers are one and the same action.

Intention-Action is not Intention qualification, not Desire-Gate switching, not manifestation, and not the success of the outcome; it occurs before awareness, can continue on the same Desire-Gate, and can be induced externally while being borne by Intention itself.
