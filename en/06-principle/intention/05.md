# The Mathematical Form of Intention-Action

## Section One　Purpose

To establish a formal system for the necessary structure of Intention-Action, such that, for any declared instance that has already obtained Intention qualification, the four action duties and their common witness can be formally evaluated within the current action declaration unit ξ\_t, yielding a definite reading: holds (1), fails (0) or insufficient material (⊥). Of these, when the second duty yields 0 on the branch where “current occurrence already holds but duty conformance fails,” the strength of that 0 as a negation at the structural layer carries the reverse-representation condition H\_PAct-Rep; 0s at the other layers carry no such condition.

The structure formalized is:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;IntentAct\_{F,t}(ξ\_t) ⇒ N\_IAct  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;N\_IAct := ∃w\_I \[ A1(w\_I) ∧ A2(w\_I) ∧ A3(w\_I) ∧ A4(w\_I) \]

A1 is the current occurrence of the source-drive duty, A2 the current conformance of the probability duty, A3 the current passage through the corresponding Desire-Gate, and A4 the current conformance of tuning command; the four items progress layer by layer in the order R1 ≺ R2 ≺ R3 ≺ R4 and are borne jointly by one and the same witness.

The formal objective is:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;S\_IAct ↦ M\_IAct

That is: to give formal criteria for the four current duties, evaluated layer by layer on one and the same witness while keeping the progressive relation intact; and to give the enumeration of candidate witnesses, the coverage needed for negative read-outs, the solving procedure and the three-valued read-back rules.

## Section Two　Core Thesis

Given Intention qualification that already holds, together with its declared instance, and a current action declaration unit ξ\_t, how is the formal truth value of “there exists a witness on which all four current duties hold” to be obtained from real event records, registrations of current realizations, actual source chains, the duty identities of formal directions and Desire-Gates, post-gate tuning duty registrations and upstream current rate-bearing certificates, while strictly distinguishing structural failure 0 from insufficient formal material ⊥?

M\_IAct must express exactly:

(1) Distinguishing the current from qualification. A qualification reading says only whether the structure is present; an action reading says whether the action really occurs this time. SourceDrive^M = 1 does not mean that the source drive is currently at work; GateCorrespondence^M = 1 does not mean that a direction has currently passed its gate; TuneInterface^M = 1 does not mean that tuning is currently being commanded. Every item of action must read the events, realizations and path segments of current reality.

(2) The four current duties. In this event the source-drive duty is borne by the source-drive duty realization of Intention itself and actually enters 𝒫\_I; in the same event the probability organization bears relative generation as probability; the formal direction that thereby obtains the current duty actually enters its Desire-Gate along the formal correspondence; and after passing the gate, the same chain actually enters the post-gate tuning-command duty.

(3) Progression and common witness. Each later item is evaluated only on the same chain already obtained by the item before it; the four items may not be split into four independent existential formulas, nor spliced together from different events, organizations, directions, Desire-Gates or tuning chains. Common witness is a well-formedness requirement on the witness, not a fifth duty.

(4) The distinction between failure and insufficiency. Not having found a witness does not mean that no witness exists; a negative read-out must have coverage. If the declaration itself is invalid, only re-declaration can be required; this may not be read as a structural failure of Intention-Action. For the formal failure of the second item to be read back as a structural failure, the reverse representation of upstream duty conformance is also needed.

The following groups of objects must be distinguished formally:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;qualification reading ≠ current action reading  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;current gate passage ≠ Desire-Gate switching  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;current tuning command ≠ tuning result, experience change, manifestation  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;external induction ≠ complete external bearing  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;conformance of the second duty ≠ Probability-Action itself  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;structural witness ≠ formal witness

This system represents the necessary structure of Intention-Action; it does not prove its sufficient direction. What can be distinguished and counted are events, realizations, path segments, directions, Desire-Gates, tuning duties and witnesses; several complete witnesses may exist at the same time.

### 3.2 Definition sequence

What is evaluated for action is the real running, within one current action unit, of a declared instance that has already obtained Intention qualification. The declared instance therefore consists of the qualification instance together with the action declaration unit; the identities relevant to action, such as which event, which set of realizations and which path segment, must all be frozen before solving and may not be rewritten after the result of running has been seen.

Definition 1 (Action solving instance) \[S\]

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;χ\_IAct := (χ\_I, ξ\_t, Γ\_IAct)

χ\_I is the already established declared instance of Intention qualification; ξ\_t is the current declared unit of complete action of The General Form of Intention-Action, not a zero-width instant; Γ\_IAct is the solving protocol and frozen-identity registration, which freezes at least: the current-event identity protocol; the identity of the current Intention organization realization; the versions of the source-drive duty class, the formal directional duty family, the Desire-Gate duty class and the post-gate tuning duty registration; the identity rules for path segments; the candidate enumeration protocol; the coverage protocol; and the declared-boundary rule. Γ\_IAct is a solving protocol, not an action duty and not an ontological predicate.

Entry condition: Intent\_{F,t} = 1. This system does not solve Intention qualification anew; if Intention qualification was obtained from an upstream formal reading, its read-back conditions keep their original upstream attribution.

The witness in the General Form speaks of six aspects of one and the same action: one and the same 𝒫\_I, one and the same real continuation, one and the same conformance of the probability duty, one and the same actual direction, the one Desire-Gate corresponding to that direction, and the one tuning-command chain issuing from that Desire-Gate. The formal layer unfolds it into a tuple and grounds each aspect in readable events, realizations and path segments.

Definition 2 (Structural witness) \[Reg\]　The witness w\_I of The General Form of Intention-Action is written w\_I^S at the structural layer.

Definition 3 (Formal witness) \[Reg\]

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;w\_I^M := (e\_t, Π\_t^I, Z\_src, Z\_ρ, v\_i, \[G\_i\], Z\_G, q\_i, Z\_T)

e\_t is the current real continuation event; Π\_t^I is the current realization package of the Intention organization in this action, whose probability-organization part is written Π\_t^𝒫 and is identical with the current organizational realization package of The Mathematical Form of Probability-Action; Z\_src is the actual current continuation sub-segment from the source-drive duty to 𝒫\_I; Z\_ρ is the actual generation sub-segment in which 𝒫\_I currently bears relative generation; v\_i is the formal direction dimension that obtains the current duty in the real generative chain of this occasion; \[G\_i\] is the Desire-Gate duty class formally corresponding to v\_i, counted by the Desire-Gate duty quotient class 𝒢̄\_I and not by single physical nodes; Z\_G is the actual current passage sub-segment from v\_i to \[G\_i\]; q\_i is the legal tuning-command duty realization after \[G\_i\]; Z\_T is the actual current entry sub-segment from \[G\_i\] to q\_i.

The correspondence between formal witnesses and structural witnesses is written as the set-valued map 𝔉\_IAct: w\_I^M ∈ 𝔉\_IAct(w\_I^S) means that w\_I^M is a legal formal registration of w\_I^S. One and the same structural witness can have several legal formal registrations, for example when realizations are registered at different granularities; the number of actions may not be inferred from the number of formal registrations. Below, w at the formal layer always means a formal witness.

A1 reads whether the source-drive duty is really at work at this moment. SourceDrive^M at the qualification layer says only that 𝒫\_I is driven by source drive, not that there is continuation at this moment, so it cannot stand in for A1. A1 must read four things: whether this is an event in current reality; whether what bears the source drive on this occasion is the source-drive duty realization of Intention itself; whether this stretch of continuation really enters 𝒫\_I; and whether all of the above are the same set of objects as the qualification anchor.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A1^M(w) := CurrentEvent\_I^M ∧₃ CurrentSrcRoleReal^M ∧₃ ActualSrcEnter^M ∧₃ SrcAnchorBind^M

The outside world can trigger, perturb or change the field state and thereby give rise to the source-drive continuation of Intention itself; this system does not require the outermost cause to lie inside the system, only that the continuation from source drive to direction on this occasion be borne by the duties of Intention itself. So when an external trigger enters 𝒫\_I through \[S\_I\]\_src, A1^M may take 1; when an external selector bypasses \[S\_I\]\_src and 𝒫\_I and directly decides the Desire-Gate, A1^M may not take 1. An external cause is not complete external bearing.

SrcAnchorBind^M = 0 on a candidate witness shows that the source-drive bearing of this candidate does not belong to the Intention organization on this declared anchor; it makes this candidate fail but does not make the declaration invalid. A declared instance that is itself cross-anchor or cross-version falls under DeclarationZero of Definition 29.

The formal criterion of this duty has already been established by The Mathematical Form of Probability-Action; this system builds no second one and only embeds it in the same witness. A2 is not Probability-Action itself: this system does not let the total reading of Probability-Action stand in for A2; A2 is only the reading of current duty conformance on the same witness.

A4 must read four things: whether this post-gate duty is a legal tuning command; whether it has a legal current realization at this moment; whether there is a real path segment entering it from this Desire-Gate; and whether this path continues from the same Desire-Gate as in A3.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A4^M(w) := PostGateTuneRoleRead^M ∧₃ CurrentTuneRoleReal^M ∧₃ ActualTuneEntrySeg^M ∧₃ GateTuneChainBind^M

Separate readings of the four items are not yet enough: if the four 1s are taken from different events, directions, Desire-Gates or tuning chains, putting them together does not describe one and the same action. Common witness grounds the identity requirement of the General Form in the components of the formal witness. What it checks is whether a candidate witness is well-formed; it is not a fifth duty.

Properties of relations: identity of events, identity of realization packages, identity of directions, identity of Desire-Gate duty classes and identity of tuning duties are all identities, treated as equivalence relations (reflexive, symmetric, transitive). The successor relation ⇝ between chain segments is a heterogeneous directed relation; no reflexivity, symmetry or transitivity is declared, and it is not a partial order. 𝔉\_IAct is a set-valued correspondence; no single-valuedness, injectivity, surjectivity or uniqueness is declared, nor reflexivity, symmetry or transitivity. DirGateChainBind^M, GateTuneChainBind^M and SrcAnchorBind^M are heterogeneous three-valued predicates; no reflexivity, symmetry or transitivity is declared. Candidate sets and coverage certificates are sets or predicates, not binary relations.

## Section Four　Equations

This system has no dynamical equations; its relations are all static relations, that is, relations of composition among readings.

### 4.1 Progressive evaluation

The General Form prescribes that each later layer is evaluated only on the same action chain on which all earlier layers already hold. So the formal layer may not evaluate the four items unconditionally at the same time, but must evaluate them layer by layer in progressive order: A2 has a legal object of evaluation only after A1 has obtained the current event and the entry of the source drive; A3 is evaluated only after the real direction of A2 has been obtained; A4 is evaluated only after the real Desire-Gate of A3 has been obtained. When a preceding layer is ⊥, the next layer has no legal object of evaluation, and one may not step past this ⊥ to look for a 0 in a later layer.

Definition 23 (Witness-by-witness progressive reading) \[Aux\]

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Q1(w) := A1^M(w)  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Q2(w) := 0, when Q1(w) = 0; ⊥, when Q1(w) = ⊥; A2^M(w), when Q1(w) = 1  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Q3(w) := 0, when Q2(w) = 0; ⊥, when Q2(w) = ⊥; A3^M(w), when Q2(w) = 1  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Q4(w) := 0, when Q3(w) = 0; ⊥, when Q3(w) = ⊥; A4^M(w), when Q3(w) = 1

The formal representation of the cumulative stages is R\_k^M(w) := Q\_k(w), k = 1, 2, 3, 4.

Definition 24 (Witness-by-witness complete-action reading) \[Aux\]

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Q\_IAct(w) := SameIActBind^M(w) ∧₃ Q4(w)

SameIActBind^M is the guard of the witness, not a fifth-layer duty.

### 4.2 Candidates and coverage

A positive reading needs only one legal common witness. A negative reading is different: not having found a witness does not mean that no witness exists; to sign 0, it must be proven that all the required candidates have been enumerated. Coverage is frozen separately for each layer of the progression and is used only when preparing to sign the failure of the corresponding layer.

Definition 25 (Candidate witness domain) \[Aux\]　Ŵ\_IAct: the candidate set of formal witnesses enumerated, according to Γ\_IAct, from real events, current realizations, source chains, directions, Desire-Gates and tuning duty registrations. The hatted set is only an enumeration domain and is not inherently equal to the set of all legal witnesses.

The coverage certificates are layered as follows:

EventCover^M: all events in current reality within the current declared unit that are related to the declared anchor have been covered.  
SrcRoleCover^M(e): all current realizations in event e that are qualified to bear the formal source-drive duty have been covered.  
SrcSegCover^M(e, s): all relevant current path segments from the source-drive duty to 𝒫\_I have been covered.  
PRealCover^M(e\_t): all legal current organizational realization packages of 𝒫\_I in event e\_t have been covered, that is, the PRealCover^M of upstream Definition 3.14.  
PGenSegCover^M(e\_t, Π\_t^𝒫): all generation sub-segments actually borne by Π\_t^𝒫 in e\_t have been covered, that is, the SegCover^M of upstream Definition 3.14.  
The two items above serve negative read-outs of the current-occurrence sub-layer of A2.  
PActCertCover^M(w): all candidates for the current rate-bearing certificate needed by A2 have been covered, that is, upstream RateCertCover^M(w\_ρ) = 1; it serves only negative read-outs of the duty-conformance sub-layer of A2. ### 4.3 Total reading

Definition 27 (Total reading) \[S\]

Piecewise: takes 1 when at least one candidate w makes Q\_IAct(w) = 1; takes 0 when NegComplete\_IAct^M = 1, all candidates have Q\_IAct(w) = 0, and none of these 0s is a DeclarationZero; otherwise takes ⊥, including cases where some candidate is undecided, coverage is not closed, upstream formal solvability conditions are not satisfied, the declaration is invalid or identity is non-adjudicable. ### 4.4 Zero types and failure layers

A 0 can arise for two entirely different reasons. In one, some layer of the action structurally did not in fact occur; in the other, the declaration itself is not valid, for example the version is wrong, the anchor is crossed, or the protocol was altered after running was seen. The latter shows that this declaration cannot be used, not that the action did not occur.

Definition 28 (StructuralZero) \[Aux\]　The decisive 0 comes from failure at a layer of actual duty: A1, A2, A3 or A4 fails structurally, or all candidate common witnesses are excluded by legal structural refutation.

Definition 29 (DeclarationZero) \[Aux\]　The decisive formal 0 comes only from the invalidity of the declaration or protocol, for example: the identity of χ\_I disagrees with the object currently running; the version of the Desire-Gate duty table is wrong; Γ\_IAct is rewritten after running has been observed; the declared unit ξ\_t is not legal; a 𝒫 not belonging to K\_I^M is used; the identity anchor crosses versions; the action candidate domain arises from illegal post hoc selective deletion. DeclarationZero is not read back as N\_IAct = 0 and finally reverts to ⊥ (DeclarationInvalid).

Definition 30 (Failure layer) \[Aux\]　When Read\_{N\_IAct}^M = 0, every candidate obtains 0 at some layer: Q1 = 0 is recorded at A1; Q1 = 1 with Q2 = 0 at A2; Q2 = 1 with Q3 = 0 at A3; Q3 = 1 with Q4 = 0 at A4; SameIActBind^M = 0 with Q4 not 0 is recorded at Bind. The last case includes both Q4 = 1 and Q4 = ⊥: by Definition 24, when SameIActBind^M = 0, Q\_IAct = 0, and this candidate is already determined not to be a legal common witness, with no need to wait for the read-out of each layer. The failure-layer signature FailLayer\_IAct^M ⊆ {A1, A2, A3, A4, Bind} is the set of the failure layers of all candidates; when the reading is 1 or ⊥, FailLayer\_IAct^M = ∅. When A2 ∈ FailLayer\_IAct^M, a sub-failure signature FailSub\_A2^M ∈ {PCurrent, PDuty} is also recorded: if all candidates recorded at A2 have CurrentProbabilityOccurrence^M(w\_ρ) = 0, PCurrent is recorded; if at least one of them has CurrentProbabilityOccurrence^M(w\_ρ) = 1 and ProbabilityDutyConformance^M(w\_ρ) = 0, PDuty is recorded. As long as there is a candidate that entered the duty-conformance sub-layer and failed, PDuty is recorded; this is the conservative choice. Bind is not a fifth duty; it indicates only that all four layers have been obtained while same-chain binding does not hold. Neither FailLayer nor FailSub\_A2 is a duty; both are only diagnostic signatures of negative read-outs.

## Section Five　Running and Solving

This system represents the structure of current action and does not evolve over time; “running” means evaluating an action solving instance χ\_IAct.

### 5.1 Order of solving

(1) Check χ\_IAct, the entry condition and Γ\_IAct. If the declaration is invalid, return ⊥ (DeclarationInvalid).  
(2) Enumerate the relevant events in current reality e\_t within ξ\_t, and determine EventCover^M.  
(3) For each e\_t, read Π\_t^I and check that it is same-anchor with K\_I^M.  
(4) Enumerate the current realizations of the formal source-drive duty and Z\_src, and obtain A1^M.  
(5) For candidates with Q1 = 1, identify ν\_t^cur, read κ\_ρ, and obtain A2^M.  
(6) For candidates with Q2 = 1, read the formal direction v\_i in the real generation of this occasion and its corresponding \[G\_i\], enumerate Z\_G, and obtain A3^M.  
(7) For candidates with Q3 = 1, read the legal q\_i after \[G\_i\], enumerate Z\_T, and obtain A4^M.  
(8) For the complete chain of each candidate, obtain SameIActBind^M, giving Q\_IAct(w).  
(9) If any Q\_IAct(w) = 1, the reading is 1, and enumeration may stop.  
(10) When there is no positive witness, check the corresponding coverage by failure layer: if coverage is not closed, return the corresponding ⊥; if the 0s of the candidates include a DeclarationZero, return ⊥ (DeclarationInvalid); otherwise sign 0 and give FailLayer and FailSub\_A2. Once 0 has been signed, the structural verdict is determined from the failure-layer set by 7.3: if the set does not contain A2, or contains A2 with FailSub\_A2 = PCurrent, the structural negative verdict does not carry H\_PAct-Rep; if the set contains A2 with FailSub\_A2 = PDuty and H\_PAct-Rep is not closed, the formal reading is still 0 and the structural verdict is ⊥ (PActRepOpen).

### 5.2 Reasons for ⊥

When the formal reading takes ⊥, one of the following reasons must be attached: DeclarationInvalid, declaration, version or anchor invalid; EventOpen, current real event record incomplete; SourceRoleOpen, current source-drive duty realization non-adjudicable; SourcePathOpen, source chain of the source drive's entry into 𝒫\_I incomplete; ProbabilityCertOpen, current rate-bearing certificate of A2 not obtainable; DirectionOpen, current formal direction non-adjudicable; GateIdentityOpen, correspondence identity between formal direction and Desire-Gate duty non-adjudicable; GatePathOpen, coverage of the current gate-passage paths not closed; TuneRoleOpen, identity or current realization of the post-gate tuning duty non-adjudicable; TunePathOpen, coverage of the current paths from Desire-Gate to tuning duty not closed; SameBindOpen, identity binding of the same event, direction, Desire-Gate and tuning chain non-adjudicable.

Two further reasons belong not to the formal reading but to the structural verdict obtained by reading back the formal reading: PActRepOpen, where the formal reading is 0, the failure-layer set contains A2 with FailSub\_A2 = PDuty, and H\_PAct-Rep is not closed, so that the structural verdict cannot sign N\_IAct = 0; LawReadOpen, where the formal reading is 1 while H\_LawRead of the Law-bearing witness actually used by the positive read-back is not closed, so that the structural verdict cannot sign N\_IAct = 1 unconditionally.

These reasons are all solving diagnoses, not ontological conditions.

### 5.3 Solvability

The following are conditions of formal representation and solving, written IA-M1 to IA-M12. They are not ontological conditions of Intention-Action, nor are they H. If any condition is missing, only the items depending on it revert to ⊥; nothing is automatically judged a structural 0.

IA-M1 The solving instance is identifiable: every item of χ\_IAct can be effectively referred to; χ\_I is an already holding instance of Intention qualification; Γ\_IAct is frozen before solving.  
IA-M2 Events in current reality are readable: the operating substrate can give the record of events in current reality within ξ\_t, and can distinguish current running from historical recurrence, offline re-entry and qualification re-run.  
IA-M3 The current Intention realization package is readable: the identities of the current Π\_t^I, of the Π\_t^𝒫 within it, of the qualification anchor K\_I^M and of the running instance can be obtained.  
IA-M4 Current realization of the source-drive duty is adjudicable: the duty identity of \[S\_I\]\_src and the realization currently bearing it are adjudicable, the system-boundary rule is frozen, and external induction can be distinguished from complete external bearing.  
IA-M5 The source chain is traceable: the real source chains, predecessor–successor relations and bearers of Z\_src, Z\_ρ, Z\_G, Z\_T can be read.  
IA-M6 The current probability evaluation is identifiable: ν\_t^cur can be legally identified, and the evaluation of a historical recurrence is not substituted for the current evaluation.  
IA-M7 The upstream certificates of the probability duty are obtainable: κ\_ρ, MinSuff\_ρ, GenIn, EvalRateBind and CurrentRateRealize required by The Mathematical Form of Probability-Action can be obtained on the current evaluation and satisfy their upstream solvability conditions.  
IA-M8 Formal directions are adjudicable: V\_I is frozen; which formal direction obtains the current successor duty in the real chain of this occasion is adjudicable; final outcome labels are not substituted for directional duties.  
IA-M9 The Desire-Gate duty identity is adjudicable: the Desire-Gate duty quotient class 𝒢̄\_I, the formal correspondence and GateRoleID are adjudicable; single physical nodes are not substituted for Desire-Gate duties.  
IA-M10 Tuning-duty identity is adjudicable: the tuning-duty qualification of the post-gate q\_i, its current realization and PostGateTuneRoleRead^M are adjudicable; arbitrary post-gate change is not substituted for tuning command.  
IA-M11 Same-chain identity is adjudicable: the identity and predecessor–successor binding of events, realizations, path segments, directions, Desire-Gates and tuning duties are adjudicable, and cross-event and cross-chain splicing can be detected.  
IA-M12 Coverage is adjudicable: the coverage certificate of every layer can be determined as the failure layer requires.

Under the conditions that Γ\_IAct is frozen, all candidate domains involved are finite and enumerable, and IA-M1 to IA-M12 hold, each step terminates after finitely many operations, and Read\_{N\_IAct}^M has a definite three-valued reading, with a reason attached when it takes ⊥. A declared instance that does not satisfy these conditions does not thereby take 0; its reading remains ⊥.

### 5.4 Standing of the solution

Read\_{N\_IAct}^M = 1 is a formal solution, denoting that there exists a common witness on which all four current duties hold. Read\_{N\_IAct}^M = 0 denotes that under complete coverage there is no such formal witness; the strength of its negation at the structural layer is distinguished by failure layer (7.3). Read\_{N\_IAct}^M = ⊥ denotes insufficient material and may not be read as N\_IAct not holding, still less as Intention-Action not holding.
