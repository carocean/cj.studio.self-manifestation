# The Necessity Form of Intention-Action

## Section One　Purpose

To prove: once Intention holds, within the current action declaration unit ξ\_t, provided that the following four current duties are all fulfilled on one and the same real witness, one complete instance of Intention-Action holds, and no fifth independent condition of action need be satisfied.

The four current duties are:

First, current occurrence of the source-drive duty: in this real continuation, the source-drive duty of Intention itself actually occurs and enters 𝒫\_I.

Second, current conformance of the probability duty: in the same continuation, 𝒫\_I, as a probability organization, actually bears the formation of relative generation among the formal directions.

Third, current passage through the corresponding Desire-Gate: the directional continuation so formed actually enters, along the formal correspondence, the Desire-Gate of that direction.

Fourth, current conformance of tuning command: after passing the gate, the same action chain actually enters the tuning-command interface of that Desire-Gate.

In formulas:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;N\_IAct ⇒ IntentAct\_{F,t}(ξ\_t)

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;N\_IAct := ∃w\_I \[ A1(w\_I) ∧ A2(w\_I) ∧ A3(w\_I) ∧ A4(w\_I) \]

The working domain is Intent\_{F,t} = 1.

“No fifth independent condition of action need be satisfied” means specifically: whether Desire-Gate switching has occurred, whether there has been manifestation, whether there has been awareness, whether tuning has succeeded, whether experience has finally changed, whether there is external behavior or effect, whether attention has formed, whether musing or thinking circuits take part, whether this route is deterministic, whether some formal dimensions currently have rate zero, whether the action is induced by an external stimulus, and whether there is another complete action chain within the same unit: none of these affects the conclusion.

## Section Four　Rules of Classification

### 4.1 Classification

Each atom is assigned to one of the following classes according to its duty with respect to the question “does this complete action hold?”

Positive action semantics U\_IAct^+: the current matters by virtue of which one complete action holds. By the question answered, this chapter reads three load-bearing points from the corpus:

U\_Init　Initiation: whether Intention performs the first setting in motion.  
U\_ChooseCommand　Deciding, choosing, commanding: whether a direction is first decided and a Desire-Gate selected, and tuning then commanded.  
U\_ProbRun　Source drive runs through Probability: whether probability driven by source drive goes returning to one across the directions.

The three load-bearing points do not correspond one-to-one to A1 to A4; they are read from the corpus independently, and their number is decided by the corpus, not padded out to match the number of the four duties.

Cancelling side: atoms that, when the positive action semantics are all realized, can on their own make IntentAct = 0.

Remaining semantics U\_IAct^O: the range of capacities or functions, a class of objects of action, and the consequences and boundaries of action. Each has theoretical significance, but none bears the universal duty of obtaining with respect to “does this complete action hold?”

### 4.2 Criteria of classification

(a) Criterion of question. Ask whether the atom answers “by virtue of what does this action hold,” or “what can action do,” “on what does action act,” “what happens after action.”

(b) Criterion of phase. Ask whether the atom speaks of what lies within this action, or of the consequences after action, or of a capacity that action has in general.

(c) Criterion of deletion. Suppose the atom does not appear in this action, with the other atoms unchanged. If no sentence in the corpus thereby denies that this action holds, the atom does not belong to the positive action semantics. This criterion is used only to re-check atoms already assigned to U\_IAct^O by (a).

(d) Review of the cancelling side. If an atom looks formally like a negation, it must be specially reviewed: supposing the positive action semantics are all realized, can it on its own make IntentAct = 0? If, in order to take effect, it must make some positive semantics fail, it is not an independent cancelling condition.

### 4.3 Review of irreducible conjunctions

After the single atoms have been classified, the irreducible conjunctions authorized by the frozen corpus must still be checked. Two atoms that individually do not decide the truth of action may together produce a new force, so “no part is a condition of action” does not license “the conjunction is not a condition of action.” If someone claims that some conjunction bears a new duty of obtaining or cancelling action, grounds explicitly authorizing that combination to bear the duty must be found in the frozen corpus; such authorization can occur within one sentence, or be formed by causal, conditional, referential, summarizing or anaphoric relations across sentences.

For a conjunction to become a fifth action duty, it must further satisfy this: when the positive action semantics are all realized and the four action duties hold on the same witness, its failure makes IntentAct = 0, and this cancellation does not appear as the exit of any one of the four items.

The atoms that look formally like negations or limitations are u48, u49 and u50.

u48, “has no self-manifestation”: what it denies is the self-manifestation of the switching process, not action. Suppose the positive action semantics are all realized without any self-manifestation; no sentence in the corpus thereby makes IntentAct = 0. u50, “occurs before awareness”: it directly excludes taking awareness as a condition of action. Suppose the positive action semantics are all realized and there is not yet awareness; the corpus says explicitly that action has already occurred.

## Section Six　Closure of Action Semantics

6.1 Theorem One　Theorem of the Closure of the Current Action Semantics of Intention

On the frozen action semantic domain U\_IAct^src: the positive semantics of current complete Intention-Action are exactly u31, u32, u33; the remaining atoms do not decide the truth of every action; the cancelling side is empty; and there is in the frozen corpus no irreducible conjunction bearing a fifth action duty or a cancelling duty. That is:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;U\_IAct^+ = { u31, u32, u33 },  U\_IAct^O = { u30, u34, u48, u49, u50, u51 }  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;FifthIndependentActDuty\_I = ∅,  IndependentCancel\_IAct = ∅

Proof

First, the positive semantics are exhausted. U\_IAct^src is a finite set, and all nine atoms have been classified by the criteria of Section Four. Only u31, u32, u33 are assigned to the positive side; the other six are assigned by criteria (a) and (b) to capacity, object, boundary or consequence, and re-checked by criterion (c).

Second, no single atom bears a fifth duty. u31 bears initiation through “first sets it in motion”; u32 bears deciding, choosing, commanding and their order; u33 bears source drive running through Probability. u30 is a range of capacity, u34 a class of objects of action, u48 and u50 boundaries, u49 a consequence of manifestation, u51 a capacity after action; none of them is a universal condition of obtaining for every action.

Third, the cancelling side is empty. u48, u49, u50 have been reviewed item by item under criterion (d); none can on its own make IntentAct = 0 when the positive semantics are all realized. None of the following candidates is an independent cancelling condition either:

No Desire-Gate switching: two successive complete actions on the same Desire-Gate are both legal; u30 says only that switching is possible.  
No manifestation: by u48 and u49, action can precede manifestation; manifestation is a consequence of the participation of manifest circuits behind the gate.  
No awareness: by u50, action occurs before awareness.  
No external behavior, no effect output: behavior and effect come after tuning command; u51 speaks of a capacity after action.  
Tuning not succeeding, experience not finally changing, subsequent results being offset: u32 speaks of “sets tuning to work”; a command, once issued, is already in conformance, and the results belong to what comes after.  
No attention formed: by u34, attention is only one class of objects of action.  
No participation of musing, introspection, recollection or thinking circuits: these are one way in which higher-order reentry begins; u31 requires only that Intention itself set things in motion, and source drive can trigger action directly.  
This route being deterministic: u33 speaks of going returning to one among the directions; which dimension a single continuation falls on does not cancel “proceeding by way of Probability.”  
Some formal dimensions currently having rate zero: a zero-rate dimension does not exit the formal directions and does not affect complete action on another dimension.  
Induction by an external stimulus: u31 requires that the setting in motion belong to Intention itself; the outside can trigger, but what bears the setting in motion is still the source-drive duty of Intention itself.  
Inhibition of the Intention-Domain by the Sleep-Domain: this item is not in the frozen action semantic domain (u37, dynamics), but it is the one most easily read as a cancelling condition, so it is reviewed here as well. If the inhibition prevents source-drive continuation from occurring, or prevents post-gate tuning command from occurring, then u31 or u32 is not realized, which is a lack of positive semantics; when the positive semantics are all realized, no sentence in the corpus prescribes that the inhibition still makes action fail to hold.  
Another complete action chain within the same unit: no sentence in the corpus requires that there be only one action chain at a time.

Fourth, there is no irreducible conjunction bearing a fifth duty or a cancelling duty. What is reviewed here is not “several atoms appearing together in one sentence,” but whether the frozen corpus gives a joint authorization among two or more atoms, that is, takes the joint holding of these atoms itself as grounds for obtaining or cancelling this action.

Authorization within one sentence is reviewed first.

Q13 writes “Bias,” “the stirring of mind,” “selects the gate of desire,” “sets tuning to work” and “first determine, then enact” in one sentence, all belonging to u32; the sentence states the composition and order of one and the same action and adds no condition.

I9 writes u48, u49, u50 in one sentence, all belonging to U\_IAct^O. The conclusion of the sentence is “Intention-Action occurs before awareness,” which precisely excludes the reading that adds manifestation or awareness as a condition of action; it does not authorize the combination of these consequences to bear an action duty.

Q3 writes u30, “can operate together and can also switch,” in one sentence with the qualification atom u21, “What enables this is Intent.” u21 answers by whom the joint use and switching of experiences is commanded; it belongs to Intention qualification and answers the content of the working domain itself. u30 speaks of what action can do. The sentence does not prescribe that only “currently operating together or switching” is action.

T1 sets u51, the “Effect Line,” side by side with the qualification atom u20, the “Tuning Line.” Juxtaposition is not authorization: T1 merely lists the buses of the Intent-Field and does not prescribe that one action must both tune and invoke effects.

P1 writes u33 in one sentence with the qualification atom u1, the relation atom u23 and the formation atom u40. In the sentence, “because it breaks the holding of Laws, … is Principle” states where “is Principle” comes from, and the result component of “generating familiar Law-continuation routes in every direction” is qualification; none of them is given added semantics of “whether this action holds.”

Next, conjunctions among the core atoms, and between core atoms and the remaining atoms, are reviewed. u31 ∧ u32, u31 ∧ u33, u32 ∧ u33, u31 ∧ u32 ∧ u33: these are only combinations of the three load-bearing points, and the corpus adds no duty on top of them. u32 ∧ u34 describes a class of instances in which action tunes onto attention; u32 ∧ u49 describes a class of instances in which manifestation occurs after action; neither is a universal condition of action. As for conjunctions within U\_IAct^O, such as u30 ∧ u34 and u48 ∧ u49 ∧ u50, the frozen corpus does not authorize these combinations themselves to decide the truth of action.

Then authorization across sentences is reviewed. The “first sets it in motion” of Q12 and the “stirring of mind” of Q13 correspond to each other, both saying that this action is initiated by Intention itself; the “selects the gate of desire and sets tuning to work” of Q13 is matched against the correspondence between probability dimensions and Desire-Gates and against the post-gate tuning interface at the qualification layer, the former being the current conformance, the latter the structure; the “go returning to one” of P1 and “the system does not read rate values” and “there is a path, there is a Law, that makes it take this path” of I5 and I6 limit one another, showing that going returning to one is borne by the organization and is not a choice made after reading out rate values; “the stirring of mind begins in the musing circuits” of T4 corresponds to Q12, the former speaking of one way of beginning, the latter of the universal setting in motion. All these cross-sentence relations serve only to clarify single semantics or to state ways of beginning or consequences; no cross-sentence relation separately authorizes the joint holding of two or more atoms that originally do not decide the truth of action as a fifth action duty or an independent cancelling condition.

The cases that can really block action all take effect by making some positive semantics fail: if source drive does not set things in motion, u31 is not realized; if a single Law acts alone and the probability organization does not bear the relative generation, u33 is not realized; if the direction does not enter the corresponding Desire-Gate, or stops at the gate without commanding tuning, u32 is not realized. These are lacks of positive semantics, not independent cancelling conditions.

By the four points above, Theorem One is proven. □

This conclusion is obtained by this chapter independently on the frozen action semantic domain; it does not cite the result of the checks of the same name in The General Form of Intention-Action.

## Section Seven　Duty Realization

7.1 Theorem Two　Theorem of the Realization of Intention-Action Duties

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;N\_IAct ⇒ u31 ∧ u32 ∧ u33

Specifically:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A1 ⇒ u31  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A2 ∧ A3 ∧ A4 ⇒ u32  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A1 ∧ A2 ⇒ u33

All three are read on the same witness w\_I. u32 and u33 are joint realizations across duties: their full meaning requires two or three duties to be accomplished together on the same action chain, and cannot be falsely presented as “each duty alone realizing one complete semantic atom.”

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A1    A2    A3    A4  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;u31     ✓  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;u32           ✓     ✓     ✓  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;u33     ✓     ✓

Proof

First, A1 ⇒ u31. A1 gives: in this real continuation, the source-drive duty of Intention itself actually occurs and enters 𝒫\_I. This is exactly “Intention first sets it in motion”: from stillness to motion, there is a setting in motion that belongs to Intention itself. The outside can trigger, but what bears this setting in motion is the source-drive duty of Intention itself, so the setting in motion by “an intention” in u31 is realized.

Second, A2 ∧ A3 ∧ A4 ⇒ u32. u32 has three links and one order. “First determine”: A2 gives that, in the same continuation, the probability organization, as probability, bears the formation of the relative generation among the formal directions, and thereby decides the direction of this occasion. “Selects the gate of desire”: A3 gives that the directional continuation formed by A2 actually enters, along the formal correspondence, the Desire-Gate of that direction. “Sets tuning to work”: A4 gives that the same chain, after passing the gate, actually enters the post-gate tuning command. “It must first determine, then enact”: by R2 ≺ R3 ≺ R4, each later layer is obtained only on the same chain on which all earlier layers already hold; the command of A4 follows the same Desire-Gate as A3, and the direction of A3 is exactly the direction decided on that occasion by A2. The order here is a semantic order within one and the same action, not a general causal law. If any one of the three is lacking, u32 is incomplete: with A2 alone, the direction is decided but no gate is selected; with A2 and A3 alone, the gate is selected but no command is issued.

Third, A1 ∧ A2 ⇒ u33. u33 speaks of “probability driven by source drive” that “goes returning to one” across the directions. A1 gives the real occurrence, on this occasion, of driving by source drive; A2 gives that the probability organization, in the same continuation, bears the formation of the relative generation among the directions, so that this continuation makes its way out among the directions, that is, goes returning to one. “Go returning to one” here is how the current relative generation shows itself in execution; it does not require ending up on only one dimension, nor does it exclude another action chain within the same unit. With A2 and without A1, probability is going, but it is not driven by source drive; with A1 and without A2, source drive is going, but as a single Law acting alone, not proceeding by way of Probability. So u33 must be realized jointly by the two on the same witness.

Fourth, the same witness. The antecedent of N\_IAct is ∃w\_I \[ A1(w\_I) ∧ A2(w\_I) ∧ A3(w\_I) ∧ A4(w\_I) \], not (∃w A1) ∧ (∃w A2) ∧ (∃w A3) ∧ (∃w A4). The common witness B\_same^IAct guarantees that the four items belong to the same 𝒫\_I, the same real continuation, the same conformance of the probability duty, the same actual direction, the same Desire-Gate corresponding to that direction, and the same tuning-command chain issuing from that Desire-Gate. So the three realizations above all occur in one and the same action: Intention itself initiates, probability itself bears the formation of the direction, the direction actually enters the corresponding Desire-Gate, and the same chain actually enters tuning command. What this proves is not that “four things once occurred separately” but that there is one action.

By the four points above, N\_IAct ⇒ u31 ∧ u32 ∧ u33, that is, N\_IAct completely realizes U\_IAct^+. □

7.2 A complete action unit is not a fifth condition

No separate fifth antecedent “the action unit is already complete” is set up. The General Form of Intention-Action has already placed the closure of one complete action within R4 = A1 ∧ A2 ∧ A3 ∧ A4 and the common witness: R1, R2, R3 are duty prefixes of one and the same complete action, closing at A4. Setting up a further item “complete” would double-count what R4 and the common witness have already realized.

## Section Eight　Theorem of the Necessity of Intention-Action

8.1 Theorem Three　Theorem of the Necessity of Intention-Action

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;N\_IAct ⇒ IntentAct\_{F,t}(ξ\_t)

Proof

Suppose Intent\_{F,t} = 1, and suppose N\_IAct = 1. By Theorem Two, all the positive action semantics in the frozen action semantic domain, u31, u32, u33, are completely realized in one and the same real action. By Theorem One, on the frozen action semantic domain the positive action semantics are exactly these three; there is no fifth action duty and no independent cancelling condition, and there is no irreducible conjunction bearing a fifth duty or a cancelling duty. Therefore all the positive semantics of current complete Intention-Action have been obtained, and no independent factor cancels it. By the corpus-domain declaration of Section Three, IntentAct\_{F,t}(ξ\_t) = 1. □

What is obtained here is not “a structure similar to Intention-Action.” The four current duties, on the same witness, completely realize all the positive action semantics obtained by independent analysis of the frozen corpus; the proof does not depend on defining IntentAct as N\_IAct.

### 8.2 Anti-sufficiency world

Let

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;W\_IAct^- := { w ∣ N\_IAct(w) = 1 ∧ IntentAct(w) = 0 }

Within the frozen action semantic domain and the working domain Intent = 1,

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;W\_IAct^- = ∅

Proof: suppose w ∈ W\_IAct^-. IntentAct(w) = 0, so by the corpus-domain declaration either some positive action semantics is not realized, or some cancelling condition takes effect. The former contradicts Theorem Two: N\_IAct(w) = 1 already realizes u31, u32, u33 on the same witness. The latter contradicts Theorem One: the set of cancelling conditions is empty, and there is no irreducible conjunction bearing a cancelling duty. So W\_IAct^- is empty. □

W\_IAct^- = ∅ is a conclusion within the frozen action semantic domain and the working domain, not an absolute assertion about all possible theories. It shows where the necessity comes from: not from Intention-Action having been defined as the four duties, but from all the positive action semantics having been completely realized by the four duties on the same witness, with no independent cancelling item.

### 8.3 Consistency back-test

The General Form of Intention-Action checked, on its frozen action semantic domain, that there is no fifth universal action duty and no independent cancelling condition. This agrees with the conclusion of Theorem One of this chapter. The necessary direction and the sufficient direction have obtained compatible results on the same semantic domain. ### 8.4 Two-way closure

## Section Nine　Boundary Cases

9.1 Successive actions on the same Desire-Gate

Suppose two complete actions occur one after the other on the same Desire-Gate G\_a, each with A1 to A4 holding on the same witness. IntentAct holds both times; the absence of Desire-Gate switching cancels neither.

### 9.2 No manifestation, no awareness

Suppose A1 to A4 hold on the same witness, no manifest circuits participate behind the Desire-Gate, and the system is not yet aware. IntentAct = 1. Intention-Action occurs before awareness; manifestation is a consequence of the participation of manifest circuits behind the gate.

9.3 Tuning failure, unchanged experience, no behavior

Suppose A4 is already in conformance, the tuning is then offset by other mechanisms, experience in the end does not change, and there is no external behavior or effect output. IntentAct = 1. The command is already in conformance, and the current duties of action have closed; the results belong to what comes after.

### 9.4 No attention, no musing

Suppose the object of this tuning is not attention, and its beginning involves no musing, introspection, recollection or thinking circuits, the action being triggered directly by source drive. When A1 to A4 hold, IntentAct = 1.

9.5 External induction and complete external bearing

Suppose an external stimulus triggers, the source-drive duty of Intention itself bears the continuation, and A2 to A4 then hold on the same chain. IntentAct = 1: external induction is not external bearing.

Suppose an external selector directly decides the direction or the Desire-Gate, and the declared organization only executes the result. Then A1 or A2 does not hold; the case does not fall under the antecedent of Theorem Three, nor does it constitute an anti-sufficiency world.

9.6 Cases where the antecedent is not satisfied

A single Law acting alone, with A1 holding and A2 not; the direction not entering the corresponding Desire-Gate, A3 = 0; stopping at the gate, A4 = 0; the four items belonging to different events, or the four kinds of fact each existing while the common witness does not hold: N\_IAct = 0. None of these falls under the antecedent of Theorem Three, and none constitutes an anti-sufficiency world.

9.7 Multiple complete action chains and zero-rate dimensions

Suppose there are two legal complete action chains within the same unit: IntentAct = 1; uniqueness is not required. Action chains, directions and Desire-Gates can all be distinguished and counted; the number of action chains is not a count of Intention.

Suppose some formal dimension currently has rate zero and another dimension forms a complete action chain: IntentAct = 1. A zero-rate dimension does not exit the formal directions.

### 9.8 Deterministic single settling

Suppose that in the current environment every occasion falls on the same direction, while the qualification layer is still a legal multidirectional probability organization, and A1 to A4 hold. IntentAct = 1. Which dimension a single continuation falls on does not cancel proceeding by way of Probability.

### 9.9 Incidental consequences

Suppose A1 to A4 hold, and manifestation, behavior and effect occur at the same time. IntentAct = 1; the appearance of consequences changes neither the holding of the action nor its strength.

## Section Eleven　Breach

The case that directly breaches the main theorem is:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;N\_IAct = 1 ∧ IntentAct = 0

By the level at which they occur, such cases fall into the following classes; if any of them appears, a re-review is required:

Breach One　Omission in the semantic domain: outside the frozen action semantic domain there exist sentences that already belong to current theory and directly decide the truth of IntentAct, and they cannot be assigned to U\_IAct^+ or U\_IAct^O.

Breach Two　Counterexample of a fifth duty: there exists a single atom or irreducible conjunction, with grounds in the frozen corpus, that is still a universal additional condition of obtaining for IntentAct when A1 to A4 hold on the same witness.

Breach Three　Failure of the realization mapping: N\_IAct = 1, while at least one of u31, u32, u33 is actually not realized, showing that the mapping of Section Seven is mistaken.

Breach Four　Anti-sufficiency world: there exists a legal w with N\_IAct(w) = 1 and IntentAct(w) = 0.

Breach Five　Counterexample of independent cancellation: there exists X such that, with A1 to A4 and the common witness all persisting, X still independently makes IntentAct = 0, and X is authorized by the frozen corpus.

The following erroneous formulations also breach this chapter: splitting N\_IAct into four independent existential statements, allowing cross-event splicing; assigning u32 wholly to any single one of A2, A3, A4, losing the joint meaning of “first determining, selecting the gate, commanding”; assigning u33 wholly to A2, losing A1's bearing of “driven by source drive”; elevating Desire-Gate switching, manifestation, awareness, tuning success, experience change, behavior or effect to a universal condition of obtaining for IntentAct; using Read\_{N\_IAct}^M = 1 directly as an antecedent of the main proof without reading back through The Mathematical Form of Intention-Action to N\_IAct at the object layer; adding H\_PAct-Rep to the main sufficient direction of this chapter; treating the number of action chains as a count of Intention; setting up “the action unit is complete” as a fifth duty or an independent sufficient antecedent.
