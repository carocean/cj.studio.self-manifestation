# The Mathematical Form of Intention Desire-Gate Switching

## Section One　Purpose

To establish a formal system for the necessary structure of Intention Desire-Gate switching, such that for any declared instance that has already obtained Intention qualification, on one fixed declared switching comparison sequence σ, the four items (real prior-gate passage, Intention's own current passage through the later gate, prior-gate exit and handoff bearing) together with their co-binding can all be formally evaluated, yielding a definite reading: holds (1), fails (0) or insufficient material (⊥). Of these, the 0 that the probability duty-conformance sub-layer inside the later-gate item obtains on the branch "current occurrence already holds while duty conformance fails" carries, in its strength of negation at the structural layer, the reverse-representation condition H\_PAct-Rep; the 0 of every other item carries none.

The structure formalized is:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;IntentGateSwitch\_F(σ) ⇒ N\_IGS(σ)  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;N\_IGS(σ) := ∃w\_a, w\_b, \[G\_a\], \[G\_b\] \[ S1 ∧ S2 ∧ S3 ∧ S4 ∧ B\_same^IGS \]

S1 is real prior-gate passage, Pass\_σ^-(\[G\_a\]) = 1; S2 is Intention's own current passage through the later gate, A1(w\_b) ∧ A2(w\_b) ∧ A3(w\_b, \[G\_b\]), with this gate passage belonging to the later state of σ; S3 is prior-gate exit, Pass\_σ^+(\[G\_a\]) = 0; S4 is handoff bearing: in the later state of σ, the bearing state of \[G\_a\] and the acquisition of \[G\_b\] belong to one and the same real gate-bearing event on w\_b; B\_same^IGS is the co-binding discipline of the two ends, not a fifth duty. The four items do not absorb one another and hold side by side; that the two gates differ in duty is a corollary, not a component.

The formal objective is:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;S\_IGS ↦ M\_IGS

That is: to give formal criteria for the four items and the co-binding, evaluated side by side on the same candidate witness; to carry over, inside the later-gate item, the progression of the first three layers of Intention-Action; and to give the enumeration of candidate witnesses, the coverage required for negative read-outs, the solving procedure and the three-valued read-back rules.

## Section Two　Core Thesis

Given Intention qualification that already holds and its declared instance, together with a comparison sequence σ frozen before solving: how is the formal truth value of "there exists a candidate witness on which the four items and the co-binding all hold" to be obtained from the real gate-passage records in the prior and later evaluation domains, the candidate-prefix registrations of the first three layers of Intention-Action in the later state, the bearing-state records of each Desire-Gate duty class in the later state, the identity keys of real events, and the anchor, order and sequence-identity material of the two ends, while strictly distinguishing structural failure 0 from insufficient formal material ⊥?

M\_IGS must express exactly:

(1) Switching versus gate passage. A3^M = 1 says only that this action passed through the corresponding Desire-Gate, not that the gate position changed; a different GateRoleID, the old gate being absent, the new gate being acquired: none of these alone is switching. A reading of switching can be given only by the four items and the co-binding together.

(2) Each of the four items reads its own object. S1 reads only one real, legal gate passage in the prior state, without tracing its source drive or probability source; S2 reads a gate passage in the later state obtained by Intention's own source drive, probability and direction, with this gate passage falling in the later state of σ; S3 reads only the bearing state of the old gate in the later state of σ; S4 reads only the event attribution of the later-state records, not whose continuation it is, nor whether the old gate has already exited. Each of the four items has its own independent object of evaluation, so they are evaluated side by side at the top level and not arranged as a progression.

(3) Exit versus not seen. Old-gate exit can be read out only after the coverage of later-state gate-bearing has closed; not having found a record of the old gate in the later state does not mean that the old gate has exited.

(4) Event attribution versus temporal adjacency. S4 uses only the real-event identity keys frozen in advance to judge whether the two groups of relevant later-state records all belong to one and the same event; temporal adjacency, correlation of states and sameness of results cannot stand in for event identity; S4 performs no counterfactual, interventional or causal test.

(5) Local attribution versus binding across prior and later. S4 is event attribution within the later state; B\_same^IGS is the same-anchor, ordered, same-sequence and same-organization binding between the prior state and the later state. The two are evaluated separately and must not be merged.

(6) Failure versus insufficiency. Not having found a positive witness does not mean that no positive witness exists; a negative read-out must have coverage. An invalid declaration can only call for re-declaration; it cannot be read as a structural failure of switching.

The following pairs of objects must be distinguished formally:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;current gate passage ≠ Desire-Gate switching  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;general gate-position switching ≠ Intention Desire-Gate switching  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;old gate not seen ≠ old gate already exited  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;temporal adjacency ≠ same event  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;later-state event attribution ≠ prior–later sequence binding  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;structural witness ≠ formal witness

This system represents the necessary structure of Intention Desire-Gate switching and does not prove its sufficient direction. What can be distinguished and counted are comparison sequences, events, records, path segments, Desire-Gate duty classes and witnesses; one and the same σ can have several candidate witnesses, and neither a unique later gate nor a unique switching candidate is required.

### 3.2 Definition sequence

The object of evaluation of switching is the two segments, prior and later, of the real running of a declared instance that has already obtained Intention qualification, on one comparison sequence. So the declared instance consists of the qualification instance plus the comparison sequence; the identities that bear on switching, such as which segment counts as the prior state and which as the later state, which records belong to one and the same event, and which prior and later records belong to one and the same sequence, must all be frozen before solving and may not be rewritten after the prior and later states of the gates have been seen.

Definition 1 (Switching solving instance) \[S\]

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;χ\_IGS^M := (χ\_I, σ^M, Γ\_IGS)

χ\_I is the already established declared instance of Intention qualification, which contains the Sedimentation-Field F, the qualification anchor K\_I^M, the formal direction family V\_I, the Desire-Gate duty quotient class 𝒢̄\_I and GateRoleID; σ^M is the switching sequence declared for comparison (Definition 2); Γ\_IGS is the solving protocol and frozen-identity registration, and freezes at least: the identity protocol for the qualification anchor and the running instance; the division and ordering of the prior and later evaluation domains; the version of GateRoleID; the scheme of real-event identity keys and the identity material permitted for use; the identity material for sequence attribution; the candidate enumeration protocol; the coverage protocol; and the declared-boundary rule. Γ\_IGS is a solving protocol, not a switching duty and not an ontological predicate.

Entry condition: Intent\_{F,t} = 1. This system does not solve Intention qualification anew; if Intention qualification was obtained by an upstream formal reading, its read-back conditions keep their original upstream attribution.

Definition 2 (Comparison-sequence registration) \[Reg\]

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;σ^M := (D\_σ^-, D\_σ^+, ≺\_σ)

D\_σ^- is the prior-state evaluation domain of σ, D\_σ^+ is the later-state evaluation domain of σ, and ≺\_σ is the precedence relation frozen in Γ\_IGS, with D\_σ^- ≺\_σ D\_σ^+. ≺\_σ says only that the prior-state evaluation domain as a whole precedes the later-state evaluation domain; it requires neither adjacency nor the absence of gaps; there may be an interval between the two, or a stretch of transition. The transition segment does not belong to D\_σ^+, so a brief coexistence of the old gate and the new gate during the transition does not enter the reading of S3; where the later-state evaluation domain begins is frozen in advance by Γ\_IGS and may not be moved after the states of the gates have been seen. σ^M is not a Host-Body, a slot or an entity, only the registration of the prior–later gate-passage relation declared for comparison.

The necessary core of the General Form is written on a fixed σ as N\_IGS(σ) := ∃w\_a, w\_b, \[G\_a\], \[G\_b\] \[ … \], and the global closure is written separately as N\_IGS := ∃σ N\_IGS(σ). The proof of the General Form obtains S1 through B\_same^IGS item by item for the one σ given in the antecedent, and uses B3 to require that the two ends belong to this same σ. So, on one fixed declared σ, the object of formalization is written:

Definition 3 (Necessary core of the fixed sequence) \[Reg\]

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;N\_IGS(σ) := ∃w\_a, w\_b, \[G\_a\], \[G\_b\] \[ S1 ∧ S2 ∧ S3 ∧ S4 ∧ B\_same^IGS \]

The N\_IGS of the General Form is ∃σ N\_IGS(σ); the main theorem of the General Form reads, on a fixed σ, as IntentGateSwitch\_F(σ) ⇒ N\_IGS(σ). Obtaining N\_IGS(σ) = 0 for one σ says only that there is no necessary core on this σ, not that there is none on other comparison sequences.

The witness in the General Form speaks of four aspects of one and the same switching: one real gate passage in the prior state, one instance of the first three layers of Intention-Action in the later state, the duty classes of the two gates, and that one real event in the later state. The formal layer unfolds it into a tuple, bringing each aspect down onto readable events, records, path segments and identity keys.

Definition 4 (Structural witness) \[Reg\]　The witnessing group (w\_a, w\_b, \[G\_a\], \[G\_b\]) of The General Form of Intention Desire-Gate Switching is written w\_IGS^S at the structural layer.

Definition 5 (Formal witness) \[Reg\]

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;w\_IGS^M := (w\_a^M, w\_b^M, \[G\_a\], \[G\_b\], κ\_b, Λ\_IGS^M)  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;w\_a^M := (e\_a, Z\_a)  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;w\_b^M := (e\_b, Π\_b^I, Z\_src, Z\_ρ, v\_b, Z\_G)

w\_a^M is the prior-state candidate registration: e\_a is an event registered in the prior-state evaluation domain, and Z\_a is the gate-passage path segment registered in this event. It serves only S1 and carries no source-drive, probability or direction component. w\_b^M is the later-state candidate-prefix registration: e\_b, Π\_b^I, Z\_src, Z\_ρ, v\_b, Z\_G are of the same type as e\_t, Π\_t^I, Z\_src, Z\_ρ, v\_i, Z\_G of the formal witness w\_I^M of The Mathematical Form of Intention-Action, taken only up to the gate-passage layer; they contain neither the post-gate tuning duty q\_i nor the tuning-entry segment Z\_T, and do not presuppose any complete-action witness containing q\_i or Z\_T.

Both registrations are candidate registrations, not already holding witnesses. Candidates are generated before the judgment of any item; candidates cannot be defined backward from S1 or S2 holding. Each component of a registration may be a legal actual segment, a definitely mismatched actual segment, or missing or undecided: for example, in a world where a complete external selector directly changes the gate state to \[G\_b\], the later-state registration has e\_b and the actual gate-passage segment Z\_G entering \[G\_b\], while Z\_src is missing or Z\_ρ does not belong to the generation of 𝒫\_I; such a registration still enters the candidate domain and obtains 0 on S2 (when the records are complete) or ⊥ (when the records are incomplete), rather than vanishing at the enumeration stage. Registrations that fail at any of the layers A1, A2, A3 likewise enter the candidate domain. When v\_b is a formal direction, its formally corresponding Desire-Gate is written \[G\_b\]; when v\_b is missing, \[G\_b\] takes the Desire-Gate duty class that Z\_G actually enters. \[G\_a\] and \[G\_b\] are the Desire-Gate duty classes of the two gates, counted by 𝒢̄\_I, not by single physical nodes. κ\_b is the real-event identity key of e\_b (Definition 17). Λ\_IGS^M is the comparison key (Definition 23).

The correspondence between formal witnesses and structural witnesses is written as the set-valued map 𝔉\_IGS: w\_IGS^M ∈ 𝔉\_IGS(w\_IGS^S) means that w\_IGS^M is a legal formal registration of w\_IGS^S. One and the same structural witness can have several legal formal registrations, for example when the records differ in registration granularity; the number of switchings may not be inferred from the number of formal registrations. Below, w at the formal layer always means a formal witness.

S1 reads whether there is, in the prior state, one real, legal gate passage through \[G\_a\]. The General Form stipulates that S1 does not trace how the prior gate was originally obtained, so S1 cannot be read with A3^M of The Mathematical Form of Intention-Action: A3^M already binds the gate passage to the direction formed by that one duty conformance of A2, and reading the prior gate with it would judge a legal prior state permitted by the General Form as ⊥ or 0. S1 reads only four things: whether this is a real event in the prior-state evaluation domain; whether \[G\_a\] is a formal Desire-Gate duty class; whether there is in this event a real path segment entering \[G\_a\]; and whether this gate passage belongs to the prior state of σ.

Definition 6 (Prior-state real event) \[S\]　PriorEventReal^M(e\_a; σ): takes 1 when e\_a is a real running event, not a historical label, an observer's annotation, a static reachability relation or a record of offline re-enactment; whether it belongs to the prior state of σ is read by Definition 9; takes 0 when the prior-state event records are complete and e\_a definitely does not belong to real running; otherwise takes ⊥.

Definition 7 (Prior-gate duty-class registration) \[Inh\]　PriorGateRoleRead^M(\[G\_a\]): \[G\_a\] ∈ 𝒢̄\_I, evaluated by the frozen GateRoleID, that is, the registration of the Desire-Gate duty quotient class of The Mathematical Form of Intention.

Definition 8 (Actual prior-state gate passage) \[S\]　ActualPriorGatePass^M(Z\_a; e\_a, \[G\_a\]): takes 1 when Z\_a is a real path segment in e\_a that actually enters the Desire-Gate duty class \[G\_a\], that is, passes in reality through some physical realization bearing \[G\_a\]; takes 0 when the coverage of the gate-passage candidates and path segments of e\_a is complete and it definitely does not enter \[G\_a\]; otherwise takes ⊥. This reading does not read the source-drive segment and generation segment before Z\_a, and does not ask by whom the direction entering \[G\_a\] was formed.

Definition 9 (Prior-state sequence binding) \[Aux\]　PriorPassSeqBind^M(e\_a, Z\_a; σ): takes 1 when, by the identity material frozen in Γ\_IGS, this gate passage Z\_a belongs to the prior-state evaluation domain D\_σ^- of σ; takes 0 when the identity material is complete and it definitely does not belong; otherwise takes ⊥. That it belongs to the prior state of σ may not be established backward from "the gate was later seen to be different."

Definition 10 (Reading of S1) \[S\]

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;S1^M(w) := PriorEventReal^M ∧₃ PriorGateRoleRead^M ∧₃ ActualPriorGatePass^M ∧₃ PriorPassSeqBind^M

S1^M does not require that the prior state have complete Intention-Action, does not require A1, A2 or A4 of the prior state, and does not require that the prior gate be unique in the whole system.

S2 reads whether the later gate was obtained in reality by Intention itself through the chain of source drive, probability and direction, and whether this gate passage falls in the later state of σ. For the first part, The Mathematical Form of Intention-Action has already given formal criteria; this system builds no second set, but takes only its first three layers and binds them to the later state of σ.

Definition 11 (Later-state progressive reading of the first three layers) \[Aux\]

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Q1^≤3(w\_b) := A1^M(w\_b)  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Q2^≤3(w\_b) := 0 when Q1^≤3(w\_b) = 0; ⊥ when Q1^≤3(w\_b) = ⊥; A2^M(w\_b) when Q1^≤3(w\_b) = 1  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Q3^≤3(w\_b) := 0 when Q2^≤3(w\_b) = 0; ⊥ when Q2^≤3(w\_b) = ⊥; A3^M(w\_b) when Q2^≤3(w\_b) = 1

Here A1^M, A2^M, A3^M are exactly the readings of Definitions 8, 9 and 14 of The Mathematical Form of Intention-Action, evaluated directly on the later-state candidate prefix w\_b^M: in that chapter each of these three readings reads only the components of its own layer, namely the event, the realization package, Z\_src, Z\_ρ, v\_b, \[G\_b\] and Z\_G, and does not read q\_i or Z\_T, so no complete-action witness containing post-gate components is needed first. The action declaration unit ξ\_t that Definition 4 of that chapter needs in order to read the real current event is here taken to be the declared unit in D\_σ^+ that contains e\_b, frozen in advance by Γ\_IGS. The form of the progression is the same as the first three layers of Definition 23 of that chapter, and the progression discipline is inherited unchanged: A2^M is evaluated only on the same witness with A1^M = 1, and A3^M only on the same witness with A2^M = 1; when an upper layer is ⊥, one does not step past this ⊥ to look for a 0 in a lower layer. The two kinds of 0 of A2^M are also inherited unchanged: a 0 of current occurrence is recorded as PCurrent, and a 0 of duty conformance when current occurrence is 1 is recorded as PDuty.

Definition 12 (Same-chain binding of the first three layers) \[Reg\]　SameIActBind\_≤3^M(w\_b): takes 1 if and only if all of the following hold at once: A1^M, A2^M, A3^M all cite the same K\_I^M; all cite the same real event e\_b; the realization of the probability organization in Π\_b^I is identical with the realization of the probability organization invoked by A2^M; the successor of Z\_src enters the same 𝒫\_I; Z\_ρ belongs to the same current generative chain; the actual direction of A2 is exactly the v\_b of A3; the order and connection of the chain segments are legal, Z\_src ⇝ Z\_ρ ⇝ Z\_G; and there is no splicing across events, organizations, directions or Desire-Gates. Takes 0 when the identity material is complete and one of the above definitely fails; otherwise takes ⊥. It is the part of Definition 22 of The Mathematical Form of Intention-Action on the first three layers, and contains neither q\_i, Z\_T, the registration of post-gate tuning duty nor A4.

Definition 13 (Later-state sequence binding) \[Aux\]　PostPassSeqBind^M(e\_b, Z\_G; σ): takes 1 when, by the identity material frozen in Γ\_IGS, this gate passage Z\_G entering \[G\_b\] belongs to the later-state evaluation domain D\_σ^+ of σ; takes 0 when the identity material is complete and it definitely does not belong; otherwise takes ⊥.

Definition 14 (Reading of S2) \[S\]

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;S2^M(w) := Q3^≤3(w\_b) ∧₃ SameIActBind\_≤3^M(w\_b) ∧₃ PostPassSeqBind^M(e\_b, Z\_G; σ)

S2^M = 1 gives: on the later-state witness, A1, A2, A3 hold on the same chain, and this gate passage is exactly the passage through \[G\_b\] in the later state of σ, that is, Pass\_σ^+(\[G\_b\]) = 1. When an external stimulus enters 𝒫\_I through the formal source-drive duty and induces this continuation, S2^M can take 1; when a complete external selector bypasses the source-drive duty and 𝒫\_I and directly changes the gate state to \[G\_b\], then by Proposition 4 of The Mathematical Form of Intention-Action, A1^M or the same-chain binding is not 1, and S2^M may not take 1. S2^M does not read post-gate tuning, nor manifestation or consciousness.

S3 reads whether the old gate still bears gate passage in the later state of σ. The main thing to guard against here is taking "not seen" for "already exited." So the reading of later-state gate-bearing is defined first, and S3 is then obtained from it by negation.

Definition 15 (Later-state gate-bearing reading) \[S\]　PostGateBearRead^M(σ, \[G\]): takes 1 when a real record in D\_σ^+ shows explicitly that in the later state of σ gate passage is borne by the Desire-Gate duty class \[G\], that is, passes in reality through some physical realization bearing \[G\], and this record belongs, according to Γ\_IGS, to the later state of σ; takes 0 when PostGateStateCover^M(σ, \[G\]) = 1, that is, all physical realizations bearing \[G\] in D\_σ^+, the common bearing nodes and their gate-passage records have been covered, and it is definite that \[G\] bears no gate passage in the later state of σ; otherwise takes ⊥, including the cases where the later-state records are incomplete, the common bearing nodes are not fully covered, GateRoleID is undecided, or the later-state boundary is undecided.

Definition 16 (Reading of S3) \[S\]

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;S3^M(w) := ¬₃ PostGateBearRead^M(σ, \[G\_a\])

That is: S3^M = 1 if and only if the later-state bearing coverage is closed and it is definite that \[G\_a\] does not bear; S3^M = 0 if and only if a real record shows explicitly that \[G\_a\] still bears in the later state of σ; otherwise ⊥. Taking 0 does not need closure of coverage: one bearing record belonging to the later state of σ is already positive evidence that the old gate is still there. Taking 1 requires closure of coverage:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;no later-state record of \[G\_a\] found ≠ \[G\_a\] already exited

S3^M reads only the later state of σ, not \[G\_a\] elsewhere in the system, in other jointly used chains or in other duties; S3^M = 1 is not the global deactivation of the old gate.

S4 reads the event attribution of two groups of later-state records: whether all the records that determine the bearing state of the old gate in the later state, and all the records showing the acquisition of the new gate in the later state, belong to one and the same real event on w\_b. One Desire-Gate duty can be borne jointly by several physical realizations, so the old gate's state in the later state and the acquisition of the new gate may each be determined jointly by several records; S4 must read all of these records and may not pick out just one. The General Form stipulates that S4 does not say whether this event is Intention's own continuation, nor whether the old gate has already exited; so S4 at the formal layer reads neither A1^M nor A2^M, nor takes S3^M = 1 as an antecedent, but reads only event identity keys.

Definition 17 (Event identity key) \[Aux\]　EventKey^M(r): for every real gate-bearing record or gate-state record r, reads the key of the real event to which it belongs according to the event identity scheme frozen in advance in Γ\_IGS, for example a run number, a branch number, an actual-update number, a transaction or event number, or a contemporaneous event lineage. Obtaining a key must rest on identity material permitted by the frozen scheme; identical keys may not be manufactured after the fact because "the gate seems to have changed"; temporal adjacency, correlation of states and sameness of results are not identity material. When the key cannot be read out, takes ⊥. κ\_b := EventKey^M(e\_b).

Definition 18 (Later-state family of new-gate acquisition records) \[S\]　ℛ\_new^M(σ, \[G\_b\]): all real records in D\_σ^+ showing that \[G\_b\] has been obtained, that is, that in the later state of σ gate passage is borne by \[G\_b\], including the records of each physical realization bearing \[G\_b\] and of the common bearing nodes; its set of event keys is written K\_new^M := { EventKey^M(r) \| r ∈ ℛ\_new^M }. This record family is read off the later state of σ and is not obtained through S2^M: in a world of externally forced gate alteration, the records of the new gate's acquisition still exist; only their event is not Intention's own continuation.

Definition 19 (Later-state family of old-gate bearing-state records) \[S\]　ℛ\_old^M(σ, \[G\_a\]): all real records in D\_σ^+ that determine the bearing state of \[G\_a\] in the later state of σ, whether this state is still bearing or no longer bearing, including the records of each physical realization bearing \[G\_a\] and of the common bearing nodes; its set of event keys is written K\_old^M := { EventKey^M(r) \| r ∈ ℛ\_old^M }. What is read are records of the "bearing state," without presupposing S3 = 1: under same-gate continuation, they are records that \[G\_a\] still bears; under old-gate exit, they are records that \[G\_a\] no longer bears.

Reading of the empty old-gate state domain, OldStateDomainEmpty^M(σ, \[G\_a\]): takes 1 when all physical realizations and records bearing \[G\_a\] in the prior and later evaluation domains of σ have been covered, and it is confirmed that \[G\_a\] has no bearing whatever on σ and no change whatever of bearing state, so that there is no old-gate state event requiring attribution; takes 0 when there is indeed bearing of \[G\_a\] or a change of its bearing state on σ, so that the old-gate state must be attributed through the records in ℛ\_old^M; otherwise takes ⊥, that is, it is not known whether the domain is truly empty or the material is missing. It is a purely formal auxiliary reading, serving only the vacuous branch of S4^M.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;old-gate state domain confirmed empty ≠ attribution material for the old-gate state missing

The former is what the General Form calls "no exit that needs to be borne separately"; the latter can only be ⊥. When there is indeed a passage through \[G\_a\] in the prior state, OldStateDomainEmpty^M = 0; the old gate's state in the later state must have records, each attributed one by one, and 1 may not be signed by way of the vacuous branch.

Coverage HandoffAttributionCover^M(σ, \[G\_a\], \[G\_b\]): ℛ\_old^M and ℛ\_new^M have both been completely enumerated, the event identity key of every record in them has been read out, and OldStateDomainEmpty^M has obtained 1 or 0. "No other mechanism seen" cannot stand in for this coverage.

Definition 20 (Reading of S4) \[S\]

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;S4^M(w) := 1, when HandoffAttributionCover^M = 1, ℛ\_new^M ≠ ∅, K\_new^M = {κ\_b}, and one of the following two branches holds:  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;substantive attribution branch: ℛ\_old^M ≠ ∅, and K\_old^M = {κ\_b};  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;vacuous attribution branch: OldStateDomainEmpty^M = 1  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;S4^M(w) := 0, when there exists r ∈ ℛ\_old^M ∪ ℛ\_new^M such that r belongs to the later state of σ, EventKey^M(r) is readable and EventKey^M(r) ≠ κ\_b  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;S4^M(w) := ⊥, otherwise

The substantive attribution branch is equivalent to the universal statement: ∀r ∈ ℛ\_old^M ∪ ℛ\_new^M, EventKey^M(r) = κ\_b. It requires closure of coverage, because S4 of the General Form contains the universal content "the later-state bearing state of \[G\_a\] is not borne separately by another, independent mechanism": only when all relevant records have been covered and every one of them belongs to κ\_b can it be said that the state of the old gate and the acquisition of the new gate belong to one and the same event on w\_b, with no other event taking part in the bearing. Records of the new gate's acquisition must exist in both branches: if no record shows the acquisition of the new gate, there is no material to attribute.

The vacuous attribution branch corresponds to the world of the General Form in which S1 is deleted: on σ there was no bearing of \[G\_a\] at all, \[G\_b\] is obtained by w\_b in the later state, the old-gate side has no event whatever needing attribution, and S4 holds vacuously. This branch is open only when OldStateDomainEmpty^M = 1; if the record family of the old-gate state is empty but the empty domain has not been confirmed, the reading is ⊥, and 1 is not signed.

The readings in the various cases are as follows: no old gate on σ needs a handoff and all records of the new gate's acquisition belong to κ\_b, S4^M = 1; there is an old-gate state, and all records of the old-gate state and the new gate's acquisition belong to κ\_b, S4^M = 1; one relevant record belongs to another event, S4^M = 0; it is not known whether the old-gate state domain is empty, or the event key of some record cannot be read out, S4^M = ⊥.

Taking 0 does not need closure of coverage: as soon as one relevant record belonging to the later state of σ explicitly belongs to another event, another event already takes part in bearing the state of the old gate or the acquisition of the new gate, and S4 already fails; the reasoning is the same as for S3^M taking 0. The typical case is that a record of the old-gate state belongs to another, independent event; it also includes the case where all records belong together to another event κ ≠ κ\_b, in which case the w\_b of this candidate is not the event that bears this handoff. If the operating substrate provides no event identity keys at all, S4^M can only take ⊥; no representation condition may be set up separately just to make it adjudicable.

The independence of S4^M from S2^M and S3^M holds at the formal layer as follows: under same-gate continuation, all records that \[G\_a\] still bears and the acquisition records of \[G\_b\] = \[G\_a\] can all belong to κ\_b, with S4^M = 1 and S3^M = 0; under externally forced gate alteration, all records of the old gate's exit and the new gate's acquisition belong to the event of that one external execution, with S4^M = 1 and S2^M = 0; under coincidental exit, one record in the record family of the old-gate state belongs to another event, with S4^M = 0 and S2^M = S3^M = 1.

The readings of the four items taken one by one are not yet enough: four 1s taken from different anchors or different sequences do not, when put together, describe one and the same switching. Co-binding brings B1 to B4 of the General Form down onto the identity material of the two ends. What it checks is whether a candidate witness is well-formed; it is not a fifth duty.

Definition 21 (The four aspects of co-binding) \[Aux\]  
AnchorBind\_IGS^M(w): takes 1 when w\_a^M and w\_b^M are anchored to the same K\_I^M; takes 0 when the identity material is complete and the two are definitely cross-anchor; otherwise takes ⊥.  
OrderBind\_IGS^M(w): takes 1 when, by ≺\_σ, the prior state in which e\_a lies really precedes the later state in which e\_b lies; takes 0 when the order material is complete and either e\_a definitely does not precede e\_b, or the two are simultaneous and unordered and have been arbitrarily put in an order; otherwise takes ⊥.  
SequenceBind\_IGS^M(w): takes 1 when, by the event lineage, control lineage, action records or other frozen identity material permitted by Γ\_IGS, w\_a^M and w\_b^M are the prior and later states of the same gate-change sequence σ; takes 0 when the identity material is complete and the two definitely belong to different sequences, for example to two independent action chains; otherwise takes ⊥. Membership of the same sequence may not be established backward from "the prior and later gates differ."  
OrganizationBind\_IGS^M(w): takes 1 when the two ends belong to the same qualification organization; takes 0 when the identity material is complete and the two definitely cross qualification organizations; otherwise takes ⊥.

Definition 22 (Co-binding) \[Reg\]

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;SameIGSBind^M(w) := AnchorBind\_IGS^M ∧₃ OrderBind\_IGS^M ∧₃ SequenceBind\_IGS^M ∧₃ OrganizationBind\_IGS^M

SameIGSBind^M = 0 means: this group of formal fragments is not the two ends, prior and later, of one and the same switching. It is not a fifth duty taking 0. SameIGSBind^M and S4^M are evaluated separately: the former binds the prior state to the later state, while the latter looks only at the event attribution of the two groups of relevant records within the later state; neither implies the other. SameIGSBind^M also differs from SameIActBind\_≤3^M: the latter binds only the first three layers within the later-state end.

Definition 23 (Switching comparison key) \[Aux\]　Λ\_IGS^M := (K\_I^M, σ^M, e\_a, e\_b, \[G\_a\], \[G\_b\], κ\_b). It is used only for registration and comparison and adds no ontological object.

Properties of relations: identity of events, equality of event keys, identity of qualification anchors, identity of Desire-Gate duty classes and identity of sequences are all identities, treated as equivalence relations (reflexive, symmetric, transitive). ≺\_σ is the strict precedence between the prior and later evaluation domains, declared irreflexive, antisymmetric and transitive, but used only between the two evaluation domains of σ, and not declared a total order. The successor relation ⇝ between chain segments is a heterogeneous directed relation, declared neither reflexive, symmetric nor transitive, and is not a partial order. 𝔉\_IGS is a set-valued correspondence, declared neither single-valued, injective, surjective nor unique, and neither reflexive, symmetric nor transitive. PriorPassSeqBind^M, PostPassSeqBind^M and the four aspects of co-binding are all heterogeneous three-valued predicates, declared neither reflexive, symmetric nor transitive. Candidate sets and coverage certificates are sets or predicates, not binary relations.

(Definitions 24 to 30 are relations and readings composed of the objects above, and are listed in Section Four.)

## Section Four　Equations

This system has no dynamical equations; its relations are all static relations, that is, relations of composition among readings. How switching begins, how long it takes and how competition proceeds in between are not represented in this system.

### 4.1 Parallel evaluation

The four items of The Mathematical Form of Intention-Action are cumulative stages: a later item has an object of evaluation only on the same chain obtained by the earlier item, so there they proceed layer by layer. The four items of this system are different: the object of S1 is the gate-passage record of the prior state, the object of S2 is the action chain of the later state, the object of S3 is the bearing record of the old gate in the later state, and the object of S4 is the event keys of the two relevant record families of the later state. The object of evaluation of no item is produced by the value of another item; the General Form has also given, for each of the four items, a counterexample lacking only that item. So the four top-level items are evaluated side by side and not arranged as a progression; were they arranged as a progression, an item that could have been judged 0 or 1 independently would be illegitimately masked by the ⊥ of an earlier item. Only inside S2 is the progression of the first three layers of Intention-Action carried over.

Definition 24 (Witness-by-witness parallel reading) \[Aux\]

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Q\_IGS(w) := S1^M(w) ∧₃ S2^M(w) ∧₃ S3^M(w) ∧₃ S4^M(w) ∧₃ SameIGSBind^M(w)

SameIGSBind^M is the guard of the witness, not a fifth duty. That the duty classes of the two gates differ does not enter Q\_IGS; it is a corollary (Proposition 1).

### 4.2 Candidates and coverage

A positive reading needs only one legal candidate witness. A negative reading is different: not having found a witness does not mean that no witness exists; to sign 0, it must be proven that all the required candidates have been enumerated.

Definition 25 (Candidate witness domain) \[Aux\]　Ŵ\_IGS(σ): according to Γ\_IGS, the prior-state candidate registrations and later-state candidate-prefix registrations are enumerated from the prior and later evaluation domains of σ, the GateRoleID registration, the real-event records and the event identity records, and composed into a candidate set of formal witnesses. The hatted set is only an enumeration domain and does not by nature equal all legal witnesses. Enumeration discipline: candidates may not be filtered first by S1 to S4 or the co-binding being 1, or negative cases would be silently deleted at the enumeration stage; candidates of same-gate continuation, joint use with a newly added gate, external forcing, coincidental exit and cross-sequence splicing, as well as candidates failing at any of the later-state layers A1, A2, A3, must all be able to enter the candidate domain and obtain their own readings; the positive witness is not required to be unique.

The coverage certificates are layered as follows:

S1: PriorEventCover^M, the real events in D\_σ^- relevant to the declared anchor have all been covered; PriorGatePassCover^M(e\_a), the gate-passage candidates and path segments in e\_a have all been covered; PriorSeqBindCover^M, the sequence identity material of the prior-state gate passage is complete.

S2: directly inherits from The Mathematical Form of Intention-Action EventCover^M, SrcRoleCover^M, SrcSegCover^M, PRealCover^M, PGenSegCover^M, PActCertCover^M, DirRealCover^M, GateSegCover^M and the corresponding identity coverage, restricted to the later-state evaluation domain; in addition, PrefixBindCover\_≤3^M, the identity material for the same chain of the first three layers is complete; and PostSeqBindCover^M, the sequence identity material of the later-state gate passage is complete.

S3: PostGateStateCover^M(σ, \[G\]), Definition 15.

S4: HandoffAttributionCover^M(σ, \[G\_a\], \[G\_b\]), after Definition 19; it includes the complete enumeration of the two record families ℛ\_old^M and ℛ\_new^M, the read-out of the event identity key of every record, and the determination of the empty old-gate state domain. It serves only S4^M taking 1; a 0 can be given by a single relevant record belonging to another event.

Co-binding: AnchorCover^M, OrderCover^M, SequenceIdentityCover^M, OrganizationCover^M, the completeness of the material for anchor, order, sequence identity and organizational identity respectively.

Candidates: CandidatePairCover\_IGS^M, the prior-state candidate registrations, later-state candidate-prefix registrations and duty-class combinations on σ that could compose candidate witnesses have all been enumerated.

These are all conditions of formal solvability and coverage; they are not H, nor conditions of Intention Desire-Gate switching at the object layer.

Definition 26 (Coverage for negative read-outs) \[Aux\]　NegComplete\_IGS^M = 1 if and only if: CandidatePairCover\_IGS^M = 1, and every candidate has at least one usable refutation path (Definition 30): whatever coverage that path needs has closed; a 0 of S3 or S4 obtained from positive evidence needs no coverage. NegComplete\_IGS^M is not an ontological condition and does not enter N\_IGS(σ); once a positive reading appears, 1 may be signed without it.

### 4.3 Total reading

Definition 27 (Total reading) \[S\]

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Read\_{N\_IGS(σ)}^M := ∃₃ w ∈ Ŵ\_IGS(σ) · Q\_IGS(w)

Piecewise: takes 1 when at least one candidate w has Q\_IGS(w) = 1; takes 0 when NegComplete\_IGS^M = 1, all candidates have Q\_IGS(w) = 0, and none of these 0s is a DeclarationZero; otherwise takes ⊥, including the cases where there is an undecided candidate, coverage is not closed, an upstream condition of formal solvability is not met, the declaration is invalid, or identity is non-adjudicable. The total reading is determined only by the formal material, the coverage, the identities and the legality of the declaration; whether read-back conditions such as H\_PAct-Rep and H\_LawRead are closed does not change the total reading, but affects only its strength when it is read back as a structural verdict (7.3, 7.7).

### 4.4 Zero types and failing items

Definition 28 (StructuralZero) \[Aux\]　A decisive 0 comes from a failure of actual structure: there is definitely no legal gate passage in the prior state; the first three layers of action in the later state fail structurally, or the same chain of the first three layers does not hold, or the later gate definitely does not belong to the later state of σ; the old gate definitely still bears in the later state of σ; one record in the relevant later-state record families definitely belongs to an event other than w\_b; the two ends are definitely cross-anchor, unordered, cross-sequence or cross-organization.

Definition 29 (DeclarationZero) \[Aux\]　A decisive formal 0 comes only from invalidity of the declaration or protocol, for example: the prior and later evaluation domains of σ^M are written the wrong way round or contain each other; Γ\_IGS was rewritten after the states of the gates were observed; the scheme of event identity keys was not frozen in advance; the version of GateRoleID does not agree with the version of the qualification anchor; the qualification anchor spans versions; the candidate domain was produced by illegitimate after-the-fact deletion. DeclarationZero is not read back as N\_IGS(σ) = 0, and ultimately falls back to ⊥ (DeclarationInvalid).

Definition 30 (Failing items and refutation paths) \[Aux\]　When Read\_{N\_IGS(σ)}^M = 0, every candidate w has Q\_IGS(w) = 0. Write Fail\_IGS^M(w) ⊆ {S1, S2, S3, S4, Bind} for the set of items taking 0 on w, where Bind means SameIGSBind^M = 0; because the top level is parallel, one candidate can take 0 on several items at once. When S2 ∈ Fail\_IGS^M(w), a sub-failure signature FailSub\_S2^M(w) ⊆ {A1, A2-PCurrent, A2-PDuty, A3, PrefixBind, PostSeqBind} is recorded in addition: a 0 of Q3^≤3 is recorded under A1, A2-PCurrent, A2-PDuty or A3 according to how Definition 30 of The Mathematical Form of Intention-Action assigns it on the first three layers; SameIActBind\_≤3^M = 0 is recorded as PrefixBind; PostPassSeqBind^M = 0 is recorded as PostSeqBind. The refutation path RefuteRoute(w) is the one item of Fail\_IGS^M(w) chosen to sign the 0 of this candidate (when S2 is chosen, one sub-failure is selected as well); the chosen path must satisfy the coverage and read-back conditions of that item (7.3). The items of the chosen paths of all candidates compose the failure-layer signature FailLayer\_IGS^M; when the reading is 1 or ⊥, FailLayer\_IGS^M = ∅. Bind is not a fifth duty; it indicates only that the two ends are not the two ends of one and the same switching. Fail, FailSub, RefuteRoute and FailLayer are none of them duties; they are only diagnostic signatures of a negative read-out.

As soon as one item of a candidate is structurally definite as 0, the conjunction is already 0; so a negative verdict needs only at least one valid refutation path for each candidate, and does not require that the readings of that candidate on its other items also be evaluated to closure. This differs from the layer-by-layer progression of The Mathematical Form of Intention-Action: there each candidate has only one failure layer; here, with parallel evaluation, one candidate can have several refutation paths, and any one usable path suffices.

The properties of these relations are as follows.

Proposition 1 (Gate-Duty Difference). S2^M(w) = 1 ∧ S3^M(w) = 1 ⇒ GateRoleID(\[G\_a\]) ≠ GateRoleID(\[G\_b\]). Proof: S2^M = 1 gives a real gate-passage record belonging to the later state of σ, showing that \[G\_b\] bears gate passage in the later state of σ; if \[G\_a\] and \[G\_b\] were the same duty class, this record would be a record that \[G\_a\] still bears in the later state of σ, so by Definition 15, PostGateBearRead^M(σ, \[G\_a\]) = 1, whence S3^M = 0, a contradiction. □ This corollary is used for consistency review; it is not a component of N\_IGS(σ).

Proposition 2 (Quantifiers Cannot Be Split). (∃₃w S1^M(w)) ∧₃ (∃₃w S2^M(w)) ∧₃ (∃₃w S3^M(w)) ∧₃ (∃₃w S4^M(w)) = 1 does not imply Read\_{N\_IGS(σ)}^M = 1. Proof: take a prior-state gate passage on action sequence I making S1^M take 1, and a later-state action chain on action sequence II, independent of I, making S2^M, S3^M, S4^M take 1; any w that splices the two into one candidate has SequenceBind\_IGS^M(w) = 0, so Q\_IGS(w) = 0. □

Proposition 3 (Same-Gate Continuation). If in the later state Intention's own continuation walks into \[G\_a\] again, and this record belongs to the later state of σ, then PostGateBearRead^M(σ, \[G\_a\]) = 1, S3^M = 0 and Q\_IGS = 0; here S1^M, S2^M and S4^M can all be 1. □

Proposition 4 (Joint Use and Addition). If in the later state Intention itself newly obtains \[G\_b\] while \[G\_a\] still has bearing records belonging to σ in the later state of σ, then S3^M = 0; if the continued bearing of \[G\_a\] belongs to another sequence unrelated to w\_a, then SequenceBind\_IGS^M = 0. In both cases Q\_IGS = 0, even if \[G\_a\] ≠ \[G\_b\]. □

Proposition 5 (Externally Forced Gate Alteration). If \[G\_a\] is passed in the prior state, and a complete external selector or ordinary forced gating directly changes the gate state to \[G\_b\], the old gate exiting accordingly, with the organization merely executing this result, then S2^M is not 1: by Proposition 4 of The Mathematical Form of Intention-Action, A1^M or the same-chain binding of the later-state candidate is not 1. Here S1^M, S3^M and S4^M can all be 1; this is the standard form in which general gate-position switching can hold while the necessary core of Intention Desire-Gate switching does not. □

Proposition 6 (Coincidental Exit). If in the later state \[G\_b\] is obtained by Intention itself, S1^M = S2^M = S3^M = 1, while one record in the family of \[G\_a\]'s state records in the later state belongs to another, independent event, EventKey^M(r) ≠ κ\_b, then S4^M = 0 and Q\_IGS = 0. When the old gate is borne jointly by several physical realizations, and one record belongs to κ\_b while another belongs to another event, likewise S4^M = 0: one may not pick out only the record belonging to κ\_b and sign 1. □

Proposition 7 (Event Attribution Unknown). If it is known from the states that the old gate no longer bears in the later state and the new gate has been obtained, but the event key of a record of the old-gate state cannot be read out, then S4^M = ⊥; if that candidate has no other decisive 0, Q\_IGS = ⊥, and it is not filled in as 1 on the strength of temporal adjacency or agreement of results. □

Proposition 8 (Local Switching). If on σ the old gate exits, and the new gate is obtained by Intention itself, both belonging to κ\_b, while elsewhere in the system another legal action chain still passes through \[G\_a\], then as long as the records of that chain do not belong to the later state of σ, PostGateBearRead^M(σ, \[G\_a\]) does not take 1 on its account, and S3^M is unaffected. This system does not require the global deactivation of the old gate. □

Proposition 9 (The Post-Gate Side, Manifestation and Consciousness Do Not Enter the Reading). If the later gate has been obtained by Intention itself and the four items and the co-binding are all 1, while this action then stops at the gate, A4^M = 0, or there is no manifestation, no awareness, and no tuning result or behavior, Read\_{N\_IGS(σ)}^M can still take 1. Proof: no item among Definitions 6 to 24 reads q\_i, Z\_T, A4^M, tuning results, experience change, manifestation or consciousness. □

Proposition 10 (Multiple Candidates, and Rate Values Not Read). If on one and the same σ there are several legal candidate witnesses, Read\_{N\_IGS(σ)}^M = 1; this system requires neither a unique later gate nor a unique switching candidate. That the new gate is not the gate corresponding to the direction with the greatest current rate, or that the rate value of the old gate's direction has not fallen to zero, does not affect the reading: no item among Definitions 6 to 24 reads rate values, the maximum rate, rate-value crossings or thresholds. □

Proposition 11 (Lacking Evidence, Exit Is Not Written). If no bearing record of \[G\_a\] has been found in the later state while PostGateStateCover^M(σ, \[G\_a\]) is not closed, then S3^M = ⊥, and 1 is not signed; if no other mechanism changing the old-gate state has been seen while HandoffAttributionCover^M is not closed, then S4^M = ⊥, and 1 is not signed; if no positive witness has been found while CandidatePairCover\_IGS^M is not closed, then Read\_{N\_IGS(σ)}^M = ⊥. □

Proposition 12 (The Inducing Source Does Not Enter the Reading). If musing, thinking or already manifested experience returns through legal circuits and induces the change of direction of the later-state continuation, this system does not read this inducing source; as long as the source-drive continuation, probability bearing and gate passage of the later state are still borne by Intention's own chain, S2^M can take 1. If a so-called reentry directly specifies the later gate, bypassing the source-drive duty and 𝒫\_I, then A1^M or the same-chain binding is not 1, and S2^M may not take 1. Direct source-drive switching and switching induced by explicit reentry read the same group S2^M, S3^M, S4^M at the formal layer. □

Proposition 13 (Invalid Declaration). If the switching chain appears complete, but the starting point of the later-state evaluation domain was fixed only after the states of the gates were seen, or the scheme of event identity keys was not frozen in advance, then the reading is ⊥ (DeclarationInvalid), and N\_IGS(σ) = 0 is not signed. □

## Section Five　Running and Solving

This system represents the necessary structure of switching on one comparison sequence and does not evolve in time; "running" means evaluating a switching solving instance χ\_IGS^M.

### 5.1 Order of solving

(1) Check χ\_IGS^M, the entry condition and Γ\_IGS; check the prior and later evaluation domains of σ^M and ≺\_σ. If the declaration is invalid, return ⊥ (DeclarationInvalid).  
(2) Enumerate the real events and gate-passage path segments in D\_σ^-, and obtain S1^M for every candidate (e\_a, Z\_a, \[G\_a\]).  
(3) Enumerate the later-state candidate-prefix registrations w\_b^M in D\_σ^+, including registrations in which A1, A2, A3 may fail and registrations of externally forced gate alteration; obtain Q3^≤3 progressively by Definition 11, obtain SameIActBind\_≤3^M and PostPassSeqBind^M, and so obtain S2^M.  
(4) For every candidate \[G\_a\], read the records bearing \[G\_a\] in the later state of σ, judge PostGateStateCover^M, and obtain S3^M.  
(5) Enumerate the family ℛ\_new^M of acquisition records of \[G\_b\] and the family ℛ\_old^M of bearing-state records of \[G\_a\] in the later state of σ, and read the event key of every record and κ\_b by the frozen scheme; if one readable key is not equal to κ\_b, S4^M is 0; otherwise judge OldStateDomainEmpty^M and HandoffAttributionCover^M, and obtain S4^M.  
(6) Obtain the four aspects of co-binding for the two ends, giving SameIGSBind^M.  
(7) Obtain Q\_IGS(w) for every candidate. The items do not depend on one another for their order of evaluation and may be evaluated in any order; once some item is 0, that candidate is already 0, and the remaining items need not be evaluated.  
(8) If any Q\_IGS(w) = 1, the reading is 1, and enumeration may stop.  
(9) When there is no positive witness, judge CandidatePairCover\_IGS^M, choose one refutation path for every candidate, and check the coverage that path requires: if any candidate has no usable refutation path, return the corresponding ⊥; if the 0s of the candidates include a DeclarationZero, return ⊥ (DeclarationInvalid); otherwise sign 0, and give FailLayer\_IGS^M and the FailSub\_S2^M of the chosen paths. After 0 is signed, the structural verdict is determined from the chosen paths according to 7.3: if the only usable refutation path of some candidate is the A2-PDuty sub-failure of S2 and H\_PAct-Rep is not closed, the formal reading remains 0 and the structural verdict is ⊥ (PActRepOpen).

### 5.2 Reasons for ⊥

When the formal reading takes ⊥, a reason must be attached, from among the following: DeclarationInvalid, the declaration, version, anchor or sequence registration is invalid; PriorEventOpen, the record of prior-state real events is incomplete; PriorPassOpen, the coverage of prior-state gate-passage paths is not closed; PriorSeqOpen, the sequence identity of the prior-state gate passage is non-adjudicable; PostActOpen, one of the first three layers of later-state action is non-adjudicable, the reasons being subdivided as in 5.2 of The Mathematical Form of Intention-Action into EventOpen, SourceRoleOpen, SourcePathOpen, ProbabilityCertOpen, DirectionOpen, GateIdentityOpen, GatePathOpen; PrefixBindOpen, the same-chain identity of the first three layers is non-adjudicable; PostSeqOpen, the sequence identity of the later-state gate passage is non-adjudicable; GateStateOpen, the coverage of later-state gate-bearing is not closed; EventKeyOpen, the event identity key cannot be read out or its coverage is not closed; OldDomainOpen, the empty old-gate state domain is non-adjudicable, it not being known whether the domain is truly empty or the material is missing; BindOpen, the anchor, order, sequence or organizational identity of the two ends is non-adjudicable; CandidateOpen, the enumeration of candidate combinations is not closed.
