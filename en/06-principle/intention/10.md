# The General Form of Intention-Dynamics

This chapter attempts a general proposition:

The fact that Intention already holds and that, on a segment of a real running trajectory of the Sedimentation-Field, it indeed has dynamics necessarily means this: the trajectory is a segment of the real running of the Sedimentation-Field and belongs to one and the same qualification anchor, every state within the segment is produced by the legal running of the field, and Intention qualification persists over the whole segment; and on this trajectory at least one of the following three really occurs: the source-drive duty of Intention itself really launches, continues issuing, stops issuing or issues again after stopping; a bearing Law that constitutes a formal direction of Intention undergoes real dynamics of degree in its directional duty; the formal probability organization in the Intention-Domain undergoes real Probability-Dynamics. The start, continuation, stop and restart of Intention-Action, as well as its strengthening and weakening, are all projections of these three in running; Desire-Gate switching is a gate-layer event that invokes the current occurrence of the source drive and the current conformance of the probability duty; post-gate Principle-Action is the coupled output of Intention, which feeds back through the real running of the field and is then taken over by these three.

The question this raises is:

When Intention already holds, when can it be said to be moving? If something in the field is moving, does that count as Intention moving? Is it enough that the probability organization of the Intention-Domain is moving? Is it enough that the Laws bearing the directions of Intention are moving? If the source drive is issuing while probability is not in conformance, does that count as Intention moving? When Intention is not acting at this moment, is it at rest? Are the start, strengthening and switching of action separate engines, or manifestations of these internal dynamics?

The degree to which this chapter answers the question must be stated in advance. This chapter proves only the necessary direction: if Intention-Dynamics occurs, there is necessarily a certain structure. It does not prove that this structure suffices for Intention-Dynamics to occur; the sufficient direction belongs to The Necessity Form of Intention-Dynamics. This chapter does not re-prove Intention qualification, Intention-Action or Intention Desire-Gate switching, nor does it rebuild Law-Dynamics or Probability-Dynamics; it invokes only their already closed object-layer conclusions. Concrete readings, coverage and comparison coordinates are handed over to The Mathematical Form of Intention-Dynamics. How Intention qualification first forms, whether it can be reached, how it persists and recovers, and where it truly exits belong respectively to The Source Form of Intention, The Reachability Form of Intention, The Stability Form of Intention and The Limit Form of Intention.

In this chapter we set out, through sustained questioning, to find where the proof ought to go, and to derive the general form of Intention-Dynamics.

Our objective is:

first to take stock of the layers of dynamics on which Intention rests; to show why Intention-Dynamics must fall on three sites, namely the source drive of Intention itself, the bearing Laws that constitute the directions of Intention, and the probability organization of Intention itself, why the three do not absorb one another, and why all of them must belong to one and the same qualification anchor; to show how the start, continuation, stop and restart of action, together with strengthening and weakening and Desire-Gate switching, run out of these three sites; to show how post-gate Principle-Action constitutes output and feedback rather than standing as an axis of its own; to run through the three running cases and the two inducing mechanisms one by one; and to give the deletion test, the boundaries and the breach paths.

Abstract

This chapter answers: when Intention already holds and indeed has dynamics, what structure it must have at minimum.

First, take stock of the dynamics. All actual motion comes from the real running of the Sedimentation-Field: Trace-Retention, sedimentation, decay, continuation, field pressure, path impedance, availability and masking, deepening, reentry, flow competition, tuning, fullness, incumbent-closing and bypass competition. Above this, Law has Law-Dynamics and Probability has Probability-Dynamics; both group forms have been sealed. Viewed by its constitution, Intention is a Law Ecology; viewed by the relations among its directions, a probability organization; viewed as what bears source-drive continuation, Intention; and the source drive belongs to Intention itself. For post-gate Principle-Action, only Principle qualification and current counter-holding have been sealed at present; Principle-Dynamics has not yet been established as a form-text.

Questioning starts from “something is moving in the field.” Laws moving elsewhere in the field, or other probability organizations moving, are not Intention moving; so dynamics must belong to the source drive of Intention, to the directional bearing Laws of Intention or to the probability organization of Intention, and to one and the same qualification anchor. Is it enough to look only at the probability organization of Intention? The degree profiles of the Laws bearing the directions of Intention can really rise or fall while the relative relations among the directions remain unchanged; so carrier dynamics is needed. Is it enough to look only at the carrier? The probability organization can be really rearranged in its common binding and rate-bearing organization without this being attributable to any single Law; so Intention Probability-Dynamics is needed. Looking only at these two is still not enough: the source drive of Intention can really launch, stop issuing and issue again while the probability organization has no conformance as probability and the degree of the bearing Laws does not change; The General Form of Intention-Action allows the source drive to proceed while probability is not in conformance, and the realization of the source-drive duty need not be a Law; so source-drive occurrence dynamics is needed. If the evidence for the three sites is taken from different anchors or different runs, or involves out-of-segment write-in, or qualification exits midway and re-forms, it cannot be pieced together into the motion of Intention; so the legal segment is needed.

Then look at running. The start, continuation, stop and restart of action are the cross-time joint projection, over the complete action chain, of the source drive, Probability-Action, gate passage and post-gate command; they require real sub-segments and are not the 0 and 1 of two endpoints. When Intention is not acting at this moment, Intention can still be moving internally: the main book, using the image of a spider spreading its web and waiting, says that Intention has Biased-Flow even while waiting. Strengthening and weakening divide into the directional type and the carrier type, each falling on a sealed real object, and they cannot be combined into one total strength; the strength and rhythm of the source drive have at present no formal object and do not enter. The mutual inhibition of the Sleep-Domain and the Intention-Domain is real dynamics acting on the Intention-Domain through the running of the field, and it enters the three sites. Desire-Gate switching is a running event formed at the gate layer by the current occurrence of the source drive through the probability organization; whether it constitutes dynamics across time is determined separately; direct source-drive switching and switching induced by explicit reentry both take the same internal chain. Post-gate Principle-Action, if it really occurs, feeds back to the three sites through the field's rerouting, Trace-Retention and sedimentation, and then through legal circuits. The three running cases (no disturbance, with disturbance, and explicit reentry) all run on these three sites.

Each of the three sites has a world lacking only it, and none of them can be deleted. This chapter proves only the necessary direction and carries no new H.

Introduction

We say “I want to go and look.” Before the words are spoken, Intention is already moving: Intention itself launches again and again, and the pulses enter the Intention-Domain; along the route of looking, the Law that bears it is changing in degree; the bias among the directions is changing; sometimes a pulse reaches the Desire-Gate of looking, sometimes it does not. Saying “I want to go and look” only manifests some result of this motion.

The main book says that Intention is “the direction of desire,” that the Intention-Domain is “the inexhaustible core of motion in the Intent-Field,” that Intention “awaits the Biased-Flow of the Experience-Target like a spider stretching and tying its web,” that the Intention-Domain has “spontaneity, selectivity, statehood,” and that the Intent-Field contains a Sleep-Domain, with which the Intention-Domain is in mutual inhibition. All these words speak of Intention moving: the source drive is inexhaustible, there is Biased-Flow in waiting, states can differ, and the Intention-Domain and the Sleep-Domain press upon each other.

The first nine chapters have shown what Intention is, what one action consists of and what Desire-Gate switching consists of, and have handed over to the three dynamics chapters how action starts, continues, strengthens, weakens, stops and restarts. What this chapter must answer is: on what, after all, do these motions of Intention fall? They cannot hang in the air: the motion of Intention must be its own source drive issuing, its own directional bearing Laws changing and its own probability organization moving, held up by the real running of the Sedimentation-Field.

This chapter does not discuss whether Intention qualification itself holds; it does not re-prove whether one action or one switching holds; it does not rebuild Law-Dynamics or Probability-Dynamics; it does not study the formation, reachability, stability and exit of qualification; nor does it discuss how dynamics is to be determined by external observation. These questions belong to the other chapters of this group form and of other group forms.

## Section Two　Taking Stock of Dynamics

The first layer: Sedimentation-Field dynamics. This is the only physical base. All actual motion is produced by the real running of the Sedimentation-Field: Trace-Retention, sedimentation, decay, continuation, field-pressure transfer, path impedance, availability and masking, deepening, reentry, flow competition, tuning, fullness, incumbent-closing, historical openness, bypass competition, and the Trace-Retention and sedimentation of actually executed paths. Any “strength,” “activation,” “dynamic potential” or “pulse amplitude” newly made up for Intention-Dynamics that cannot be derived from these real field states or their legal extensions is a dangling operator.

The second layer: Law-Dynamics. The Law-Dynamics Group-Form has been sealed: Law-Dynamics consists of a legal Law segment together with the action dynamics (start, continuation, stop) or the dynamics of degree (rise, fall, migration) of the Law; the degree of a Law is not a total score but a many-sided profile drawn from real field quantities, and it allows incomparable migration: namely the Degree profile domain J\_D of The Grading Form of Law v1.1; a rise or fall of the degree profile is not the same as becoming more familiar or shallowing, and a change in depth cannot be judged from a rise or fall of the profile alone; the comparison of familiarity relies separately on δ̄ in J\_δ. One and the same change can satisfy both criteria at once. The formal direction dimensions of Intention are all borne by Law, so motion in the degree of the Laws bearing the directions is motion in the constitution of Intention.

The third layer: Probability-Dynamics. The Probability Group-Form has been sealed: Probability-Dynamics consists of a legal Probability-Dynamics segment together with the start, continuation or stop of Probability-Action, a real change of the relative-generation relation Rel\_ρ itself, or a structural change of the formal dimensions, the common binding or the rate-bearing organization; the start, continuation and stop of Probability-Action must have real sub-segments and occurrences and are not 0/1 patterns at two endpoints; a change of Rel\_ρ is not replaced by empirical frequencies, by the difference of single numerical values or by positive scaling. 𝒫\_I simply is the formal probability organization in the Intention-Domain, so the Probability-Dynamics of 𝒫\_I is motion in the organization of Intention.

The fourth layer: the source drive. Intention must have source drive, and the source drive belongs to Intention itself; the main book says that the Intention-Domain is “the inexhaustible core of motion in the Intent-Field.” The Mathematical Form of Intention registers source-drive duty realizations as a separate family, counted by source-drive duty identity, and does not prescribe that they be borne by Law; the first duty A1 of The General Form of Intention-Action speaks precisely of whether this duty really occurs at this moment and enters 𝒫\_I, and it allows A1 to hold while A2 does not. So the real occurrence of the source drive is a motion of Intention itself and cannot by default be taken as part of Law-Dynamics or Probability-Dynamics. The source-drive objects currently given upstream reach only occurrence and continuation; no comparable quantities such as the amplitude, frequency, rhythm or degree of the source drive have been given.

The fifth layer: the Principle side. What The Principle Group-Form has sealed is Principle qualification and current counter-holding CurAnti: one and the same Principle-conferring duty currently occurs, its current legal realization enters the holding-tendency bearing of the target Law, and counter-holding attains conformance on the dynamical degrees of freedom actually held by the target; the full-dynamics Sedimentation-Field has already realized the entry of counter-holding into the target's degrees of freedom, which changes actual continuation through tuning, blocking, fullness, incumbent-closing and bypass competition; actually executed paths retain traces and sediment, and repeated history can bring about the Law-formation of a new route. But the current strength, net effect, duration and aftereffects of counter-holding, that is, Principle-Dynamics itself, have not yet been established as a form-text of their own. So this chapter does not invent Principle-Dynamics, nor does it merge it into Intention-Dynamics.

Intention is several views of one and the same organization, not a sum: viewed by its constitution, it is a Law Ecology; viewed by the relations among its directions, Probability; viewed by its relation to the Laws whose holding it breaks, Principle; viewed as what bears source-drive continuation, Intention. So the motion of Intention is not the same as Probability-Dynamics, nor the same as the sum of Law-Dynamics, Probability-Dynamics and Principle-Dynamics; it is the motion of one and the same organization in its source drive, in its constitution and in its organization, and it obtains its attribution to Intention through source-drive driving, the correspondence between probability dimensions and Desire-Gates, and the post-gate tuning interface.

## Section Five　Waiting Is Not Stillness; Stopping Is Not Exit

The main book says that Intention “awaits the Biased-Flow of the Experience-Target like a spider stretching and tying its web.” The spider does not move; the web is spread. On the spread web, the tension of every thread changes with the wind and with time. When Intention has no complete action at this moment, its source drive can be issuing without conformance, the degree of its bearing Laws can be rising or falling, the relative generation of its probability organization can be changing, and its common binding can be rearranging. &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;IntentAct = 0 ⇏ IntentDyn = 0  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;stopping of action ≠ exit from Intention qualification

Waiting says only that complete action is not currently in conformance; it does not say that Intention is internally still. The exit of qualification itself belongs to The Limit Form of Intention and The Stability Form of Intention and does not enter this chapter.

Line of Argument

We proceed by sustained questioning, step by step; the argument therefore forms a line, and is to be read from the top down.

Step One　Does Every Motion in the Sedimentation-Field Count as Intention Moving?

We begin with the widest case. Intention qualification holds, and something in the field is moving.

W-ID1　In the field, the degree of a Law L\_x is rising, but L\_x bears no formal direction of 𝒫\_I.

W-ID2　In the field, the relative-generation relation of another probability organization 𝒫\_X is changing, but 𝒫\_X does not belong to K\_I, has no source-drive driving, and has no correspondence between probability dimensions and Desire-Gates.

Both worlds have real dynamics, yet neither is Intention moving. What is lacking is that the dynamics must belong to Intention. This yields the attribution requirement:

On the source-drive side, it must be a real occurrence of the formal source-drive duty class \[S\_I\]\_src belonging to K\_I, and it must actually enter the same 𝒫\_I.

On the Law side, it must be a formal directional bearing Law of 𝒫\_I, and the dynamics must occur precisely when and where it bears this directional duty. If the same Law undergoes dynamics elsewhere in a non-Intention duty, this may not be pieced into Intention-Dynamics by borrowing the same physical identity.

On the probability side, it must be the same 𝒫\_I in K\_I, with the same set of formal directions and the same Desire-Gate duty family, and it must still lie within the Intention organization fixed by source-drive driving, the correspondence between probability dimensions and Desire-Gates, and the post-gate tuning interface. Ordinary Probability-Dynamics cannot be automatically promoted to the Probability-Dynamics of Intention.

Step Two　Is It Enough to Look Only at the Probability Organization of Intention?

Keep the attribution. Question: is the dynamics of Intention simply the Probability-Dynamics of 𝒫\_I?

W-ID3　Freeze the formal dimensions, the relative-generation relation, the common binding and the rate-bearing organization of 𝒫\_I; Intention is waiting, and the source drive does not occur on γ, so there is no start, sustaining, stop or re-onset of occurrence either; the degree profile of the bearing Law of some formal direction really rises within the interval in which qualification persists; but the degrees of the bearing Laws of all directions rise together in the same proportion, or redundant compensation takes place, so that the relative-generation relation among the directions is unchanged, Probability-Action has no new start, continuation or stop, and the organizational structure is unchanged.

In W-ID3, 𝒫\_I has no Probability-Dynamics, yet a Law bearing a direction of Intention has really changed: viewed by its constitution, Intention is a Law Ecology, and its constitution is moving. Looking only at Probability-Dynamics misses it. What is lacking is carrier dynamics:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;CarrierDyn\_I^γ := ∃ v\_i ∈ V\_I, L\_i ∈ ℒ\_I · BearDim(L\_i, v\_i, K\_I) ∧ DegDyn\_F^γ(L\_i) ∧ SameCarrierDynBind\_I

BearDim is the attribution of a bearing Law to a formal direction, not a new Law; DegDyn is the dynamics of degree sealed in The Law-Dynamics Group-Form, namely rise, fall or migration (relations on the Degree profile domain J\_D, Law-Dynamics Group-Form Chapter 4 v1.1), and the duty correspondence in its upstream degree-reading regime Γ\_D is fixed as the formal directional bearing of v\_i by L\_i. The degree of a Law is in any case read by duty and by evaluation unit, and one and the same Law can have different degrees in different duties, entries and contexts; carrier dynamics only invokes the dynamics of degree under this existing duty-based reading regime; it does not cut the Law apart, nor does it newly create a direction-by-direction degree convention. SameCarrierDynBind guarantees that this dynamics of degree occurs on the same realization in which L\_i bears v\_i, and that it belongs to the same γ, the same run and the same K\_I.

Carrier dynamics takes only dynamics of degree, not all the Law-Dynamics of the bearing Law.
