# The Mathematical Form of Intention-Dynamics

## Section One　Purpose

To establish a formal system for the necessary structure of Intention-Dynamics, such that, for any declared instance that has already obtained Intention qualification, on a real running trajectory γ declared in advance, the six items of the legal dynamics segment and the three sites of source-drive occurrence dynamics, carrier dynamics and Intention Probability-Dynamics, together with the action projection produced by the three sites in running, typed strengthening and weakening, and the invocation of Desire-Gate switching, can all be formally evaluated, yielding a definite reading: holds (1), fails (0) or insufficient material (⊥). Of these, the 0 of the probability site carries, in its strength of negation at the structural layer, the conditions of the necessary direction of The General Form of Probability-Dynamics and the reverse-representation conditions carried by the upstream failure paths; the 0 of the carrier site and of the item of whole-segment persistence of qualification inherits the conditions of the actual upstream negative paths; the 0 of every other item carries no condition.

The structure formalized is:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;IntentDyn\_F^γ(K\_I) ⇒ N\_IDyn^γ  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;N\_IDyn^γ := LegalIntentDynSegment\_F^γ(K\_I; Γ\_IDyn) ∧ DynActual\_I^γ  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;DynActual\_I^γ := SourceOccurrenceDyn\_I^γ ∨ CarrierDyn\_I^γ ∨ IntentProbDyn\_I^γ

The legal dynamics segment consists of six items: L1 real trajectory, L2 legal running, L3 same-anchor, L4 whole-segment persistence of qualification, L5 same-trajectory evidence, L6 protocol freezing. The three sites do not absorb one another and appear as a disjunction. The start, continuation, stop and restart of action, and directional-type and carrier-type strengthening and weakening, are projections of the three sites in running; Desire-Gate switching is a gate-layer event that invokes the current occurrence of the source drive and the current conformance of the probability duty, and whether across time it constitutes dynamics at the three sites must be determined separately by the corresponding cross-time sub-segment on γ. None of these is a further axis.

The formal objective is:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;S\_IDyn ↦ M\_IDyn

That is: to give formal criteria for the six items of the legal dynamics segment and for the three sites, evaluated on one and the same real running; the source-drive site reads the real source-drive continuation within the evaluation units and distinguishes its cross-time type by real records of start, continuation and stop, without substituting the end states of units for cross-time facts; the carrier site and the probability site invoke the existing formal systems of Law-Dynamics and Probability-Dynamics; to give formal criteria for each running projection; and to give the enumeration of candidates, the coverage required for negative read-outs, the solving procedure and the three-valued read-back rules.

## Section Two　Core Thesis

Given Intention qualification that already holds and its declared instance, together with a real running trajectory γ whose evaluation points, evaluation units, continuity granularity and comparison protocol were frozen before solving: how is the formal truth value of “γ is a legal dynamics segment of one and the same Intention, and at least one of the three sites really moves on γ” to be obtained from the records of running instances and branches, the state updates and source histories, the real events and source-drive duty realizations within each evaluation unit, the degree readings of the formal directional bearing Laws, the Probability-Dynamics readings of the same 𝒫\_I, the Intention-Action readings of each time window, and the Desire-Gate switching readings on comparison sequences, while strictly distinguishing structural failure 0 from insufficient formal material ⊥?

M\_IDyn must express exactly:

(1) The distinction among the three sites. The source-drive site reads only the real continuation of Intention's own source-drive duty and its start, continuation and stop, not whether the probability duty is in conformance; the carrier site reads only the dynamics of degree of the formal directional bearing Laws on their directional duties, not the start, continuation and stop of member Laws; the probability site reads only the Probability-Dynamics of the same 𝒫\_I, not other probability organizations in the field. Each of the three sites has a world in which only it takes 1, so they are combined at the top level by disjunction and not arranged as a progression.

(2) Cross-time versus end state. All three sites read what happens on real sub-segments; this is not replaced by the readings of two endpoints or two units. That two adjacent units each have source-drive occurrence does not imply that the source drive is sustained across the two units: in between there can be a real cessation of issuing followed by a re-onset; that one unit has occurrence and no occurrence is found in the next unit does not imply a stop either. Sustaining, start and stop are all determined by real continuation sub-segments and the continuity granularity frozen in advance; stop and start further require negative coverage of the corresponding time windows. The carrier site and the probability site keep the upstream discipline of step-by-step comparison of adjacent evaluation points and of actual exercise within the segment.

(3) Existence versus classification. Whether dynamics occurs at a site, and whether it belongs to a start, a continuation or a stop, are two questions. A real nonzero source-drive continuation falling within γ suffices to establish that the source drive has cross-time motion on γ; whether at a given moment it belongs to a start, a continuation or a stop requires corresponding cross-time evidence. An undecided classification does not wash away an occurrence already established; nor does an established occurrence license writing in, on the strength of end states, a classification not yet proven.

(4) Projection versus axis. The action projection, typed strengthening and weakening, and the invocation of switching are all evaluated alongside the readings of the three sites; they only report, do not in turn determine the readings of the three sites, and do not enter the reading of the necessary core.

(5) Type versus total. Strengthening and weakening come in only two types, directional and carrier, each falling on an object already sealed; if a composite profile is declared, it can be compared only by product order, mixed Rise and Fall is incomparable, and when some required coordinate has no comparable relation on its object, not applicable is recorded, which is not to be confused with insufficient material; this system outputs no total Intention strength whatever.

(6) Failure versus insufficiency. Not having found positive evidence does not mean that positive evidence does not exist; a negative read-out must have coverage. An invalid declaration can only call for re-declaration; it cannot be read as a structural failure of Intention-Dynamics. Probability evidence brought in that belongs to another object shows only that this evidence cannot be used, not that the 𝒫\_I of this declaration has no Probability-Dynamics; if the load-bearing evidence of the three sites is taken from different runs or qualification anchors and adopted jointly, this group of spliced material cannot obtain a certificate of the dynamics of one and the same Intention, but neither does it show that there is no Intention-Dynamics on γ. A mismatch of evidence identity can only make a certificate fail; it cannot manufacture a negation at the object layer.

The following pairs of objects must be distinguished formally:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;end-state reading ≠ actual occurrence in a sub-segment  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;occurrence at some time within a unit ≠ sustaining across units  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;source-drive occurrence ≠ Probability-Action  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;action dynamics of member Laws ≠ dynamics of degree of directional bearing Laws  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Probability-Dynamics in the field ≠ Probability-Dynamics of the same 𝒫\_I  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;foreign evidence unusable ≠ this object has no dynamics  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;change of the relative-generation relation ≠ change of rate values  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;directional-type strengthening ≠ carrier-type strengthening  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;current switching event ≠ classification of cross-time dynamics  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;read-out ≠ write-in

This system represents the necessary structure of Intention-Dynamics and does not prove its sufficient direction. What can be distinguished and counted are runs, sub-segments, evaluation points, evaluation units, time windows, events, source-drive duty realizations, bearing Laws, and evaluation and comparison coordinates; this system does not compute the strength of Intention.

Negation ¬₃ exchanges 1 and 0 and leaves ⊥ unchanged. ### 3.2 Definition sequence

The object of evaluation of dynamics is the change, over a segment of real running, of a declared instance that has already obtained Intention qualification. So what is frozen before solving are the identities of objects and duties, the candidate domains, the ways of reading, the evaluation points, the evaluation units, the continuity granularity, the comparison conventions and the attribution rules; none of these may be rewritten after the results of running have been seen. What is not frozen is the running state that dynamics is precisely meant to read: the identity system of the formal directions can be frozen, but which Law actually bears a given direction at a given moment may change legally; the candidate carrier domain can be frozen, but the actual bearing state cannot; the representation protocol of Rel\_ρ can be frozen, but Rel\_ρ itself cannot.

Definition 1 (Dynamics solving instance) \[S\]

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;χ\_IDyn^M := (χ\_I, γ, Γ\_IDyn)

χ\_I is the already established declared instance of Intention qualification, which contains the Sedimentation-Field F, the qualification anchor K\_I^M, 𝒫\_I, C\_I, 𝔯\_I, the formal direction family V\_I, the source-drive duty class \[S\_I\]\_src and the Desire-Gate duty quotient class 𝒢̄\_I; γ is the declared real running trajectory segment; Γ\_IDyn is the protocol frozen in advance, and freezes at least: the identity of the qualification anchor and of the qualification organization; the versions of \[S\_I\]\_src and SrcRoleID; the identity system of formal directional duties and the version of GateRoleID; the discovery rule of the carrier candidate domain and its BearDim reading rule; the evaluation points T\_γ, the division into evaluation units and the continuity granularity δ\_src, δ\_act; the running protocol of the field and the permitted operations; the scheme of same-evidence keys; the degree protocol of each carrier candidate; the Γ\_PDyn^I used by the probability site; the direction-comparison instances; if a composite profile is declared, its coordinate set and the domain rule of the coordinates; the binding rules of the action and switching projections; the coverage protocol; and the declared boundary. Γ\_IDyn does not freeze the actual bearing state in running, the degree, Rel\_ρ, the current organization of the formal dimensions, the common binding, the rate-bearing organization, the action state or the gate-bearing state. Γ\_IDyn is a solving protocol, not a dynamics duty and not an ontological predicate. χ\_IDyn^M is the formal registration of the dynamics declaration χ\_IDyn = (F, K\_I, γ, Γ\_IDyn) of the General Form.

Entry condition: at the starting point of γ, Intent\_{F,t\_0} = 1. This system does not solve anew whether Intention qualification holds; whether qualification persists over the whole segment is evaluated by L4.

Definition 2 (Running data package) \[Aux\]

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Ω\_IDyn^γ := (RunID, BranchID, χ\_IDyn^M, T\_γ, {I\_k}, U\_γ, {S\_k}, ℰ\_γ, ℋ\_γ)

RunID is the identifier of the actual running instance, BranchID is the identifier of the actual history branch, ℰ\_γ is the actual event record, ℋ\_γ is the history of state updates, operations, sources and protocol freezing, and S\_k is the formal state snapshot at the evaluation point t\_k. These inputs are provided by the field system and the operating substrate; this system builds no separate physical running system. Ω\_IDyn^γ is only the organizational form of the solving input, not a new ontology.

Definition 3 (Evaluation points, evaluation units and continuity granularity) \[Aux\]　T\_γ := (t\_0, …, t\_n), n ≥ 1, are the evaluation points frozen in advance; I\_k is the actual sub-segment of the real running that connects t\_k and t\_{k+1}. U\_γ := (u\_1, …, u\_m), m ≥ 1, is the sequence of evaluation units frozen in advance: each u\_j is an actual sub-segment of nonzero width within γ; the units are arranged in temporal order, do not overlap, and together cover γ without gaps. The evaluation unit is the reading granularity of source-drive occurrence and of coverage, not the definition of continuity: that two adjacent units each have occurrence does not mean that occurrence is sustained across the two units. δ\_src \> 0 and δ\_act \> 0 are the continuity granularities frozen in advance, that is, the formal registration of the General Form's “the granularity of continuity is frozen by Γ\_IDyn”: if the gap between two real continuations of the same duty is shorter than this granularity, they count as continuous occurrence; a gap of length not less than this granularity counts as a cessation of issuing if negative coverage confirms that there is no occurrence within it. A time window means an actual sub-segment within γ of length not less than the corresponding continuity granularity; Γ\_IDyn stipulates that every evaluation unit and every such time window is a legal action declaration unit in the sense of The Mathematical Form of Intention-Action.

Definition 4 (Same-evidence key and same-evidence binding) \[Aux\]　Every load-bearing record r carries its same-evidence key

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;K^IDyn(r) := (K\_I^M, RunID, BranchID, γ, Γ\_IDyn version, the sub-segment, unit or time window it belongs to)

SameIDynBind^M(x) is evaluated on all the load-bearing records actually adopted by a reading x: it takes 1 when the keys of these records agree with the declared K\_I, RunID, BranchID, γ and Γ\_IDyn, and with one another; it takes ⊥ (EvidenceSplice) when the identity material is complete and some necessary load-bearing record of x definitely comes from another qualification anchor, another run, another branch, another declared segment or another protocol version; it takes ⊥ (EvidenceKeyOpen) when the identity itself is non-adjudicable. This reading does not take 0: confirming that a record is foreign establishes only that it cannot prove this object, not that this object lacks the corresponding fact. Foreign records in the data package that are irrelevant to the current evaluation and have not been adopted as load-bearing do not enter the load-bearing record set of x and can simply be ignored; the candidates at each site are enumerated only from records carrying the key of this declaration. Below, “binding” always means SameIDynBind^M of the records used by the corresponding reading. For every reading conjoined by ∧₃ with a binding reading, including the candidate terms of SrcOcc^M, Q^Car and the witnesses of each projection, when the binding is not 1 the reading takes ⊥, and does not take 0 merely because the other components are 0: material taken by mistake or of unknown identity serves neither as positive evidence nor as negative evidence.

The legal dynamics segment brings L1 to L6 of the General Form down onto running records, update histories, qualification readings and protocol histories.

Definition 5 (L1, real trajectory) \[S\]　Read\_{ActualTrajectory}^M: takes 1 when it can be verified that all sub-segments of γ belong to the same RunID and the same BranchID, each I\_k really exists, Begin(I\_k) = t\_k, End(I\_k) = t\_{k+1}, and adjacent sub-segments join continuously in actual history, that is, there exists a real running trajectory τ such that γ = τ\|\_{\[t\_0, t\_n\]}; takes 0 when the declared γ itself has definite cross-run, cross-branch, re-walk or snapshot splicing; otherwise takes ⊥, for example when the records of the run or the branch cannot be obtained.

Definition 6 (L2, legal running) \[S\]　Read\_{AdmissibleIntentRun}^M: for each sub-segment a real update certificate is obtained, containing the operations, source chain, protocol references and history of writing of authoritative state. It takes 1 when all updates of load-bearing state within γ belong to the field running or running operations permitted by Γ\_IDyn and have a traceable source chain, and none of the following objects has been written directly, bypassing the running of the field, so as to become authoritative underlying state: source-drive occurrence, the degree of Law, relative-generation relations, formal dimensions, common binding or rate-bearing organization, action, switching or counter-holding, and any dynamics label; it takes 0 when there is definite illegal direct writing, label injection or tampering with state that oversteps the underlying mechanism; otherwise it takes ⊥, for example when the source chain or the history of writing cannot be obtained. Passively generated event logs, audit records, read-out caches and derived certificates may exist, provided they only record running that has already occurred and do not become a source that drives the underlying state. Environmental disturbances that enter through the legal interfaces of the field with a traceable source chain belong to legal running. A 1 may not be signed on the ground that “no illegal operation was found.”

Definition 7 (L3, same-anchor) \[S\]　Read\_{FixedIntentAnchor}^M: takes 1 when at all evaluation points F, K\_I^M, 𝒫\_I, C\_I, 𝔯\_I, \[S\_I\]\_src, the identity system of formal directional duties and the identity system of Desire-Gate duties are all references to the same qualification anchor; takes 0 when the references to the qualification anchor, the probability organization, the source-drive duty, the formal directional duty system or the Desire-Gate duty system undergo a replacement across organizations; otherwise takes ⊥. The current bearer of a formal direction, the degree of the bearing Law, Rel\_ρ, the current organization of the formal dimensions, the common binding, the rate-bearing organization, the action state and the gate-bearing state are not listed under same-anchor: they are precisely the states that are allowed to change legally and that dynamics reads.

Definition 8 (L4, whole-segment persistence of qualification) \[S\]　Read\_{IntentQualWholeSegment}^M: takes 1 when IntentCoreCert^M = 1 at all evaluation points t\_k and the record of qualification-change events covers the whole segment γ (QualEventCover^M = 1), with no event of exit from qualification or re-formation within it; takes 0 when IntentCoreCert^M = 0 at some evaluation point, or there is a real record of exit from qualification or re-formation; otherwise takes ⊥. If qualification cannot be read out at some evaluation point, the result is only ⊥, not 0; it is not permitted to check only t\_0 and t\_n. The record of qualification-change events is the ground on which this item can pass from discrete evaluation points to “persists at all field states”; without it, this item cannot take 1.

Definition 9 (L5, same-trajectory evidence) \[S\]

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Read\_{SameIDynEvidenceRun}^M := ∧₃ SameIDynBind^M(x)

x ranges over all load-bearing evidence groups actually adopted in this evaluation, including the evidence used by the other five items of the legal segment and by the readings of the three sites. If all are on the same trajectory, it takes 1; if load-bearing material across anchors, runs, branches or declared segments has actually been adopted, it takes ⊥ (EvidenceSplice); if identity is non-adjudicable, it takes ⊥ (EvidenceKeyOpen). This item does not take 0. This is exactly the formal representation of W-ID6 of the General Form: material taken from different places and pieced together cannot obtain a certificate of the dynamics of one and the same Intention; it is not a proof that “there is no Intention-Dynamics on γ.” Evaluation must be redone with same-trajectory material. This item is an identity guard of the evidence, not a fourth site of dynamics.

Definition 10 (L6, protocol freezing) \[S\]　Read\_{FrozenIDynProtocol}^M: takes 1 when the freezing time of Γ\_IDyn precedes all load-bearing evaluation and the version is consistent throughout; no evaluation points, evaluation units or time windows have been added or removed afterward, and neither the division into units nor the continuity granularity has been changed afterward; neither the discovery rule of the carrier candidate domain, nor the duty-identity rules, the degree protocols, Γ\_PDyn^I, the direction comparison sets, the profile coordinates, the coverage rules or the attribution rules have been rewritten afterward; and the index used in evaluation is the same as the declared index; takes 0 when there is a definite after-the-fact modification, a selective deletion of unfavorable evaluation points, an after-the-fact enlargement or reduction of a candidate domain, a re-specification of duty identity or a change of comparison rules; otherwise takes ⊥, for example when the modification history cannot be audited. What is frozen here is how to identify, compare, enumerate and attribute, not a ban on change of running state: legally occurring bearer succession, change in the structure of formal dimensions, change in Rel\_ρ, and change in common binding and rate-bearing organization do not make this item 0. If some evaluation point or unit yields no reading, this only makes the corresponding reading ⊥; it may not be deleted from the index.

Definition 11 (Legal dynamics segment) \[S\]

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Read\_{LegalIDynSeg}^M := Read\_{ActualTrajectory}^M ∧₃ Read\_{AdmissibleIntentRun}^M ∧₃ Read\_{FixedIntentAnchor}^M ∧₃ Read\_{IntentQualWholeSegment}^M ∧₃ Read\_{SameIDynEvidenceRun}^M ∧₃ Read\_{FrozenIDynProtocol}^M

The six items correspond one-to-one to L1 to L6 of the General Form. Failure to read out produces only ⊥ and may not be rewritten as an illegal trajectory.

What the source-drive site reads is: whether, on γ, Intention's own source-drive duty really continues and enters 𝒫\_I, and at what moment this continuation starts, on which sub-segments it is sustained and at what moment it stops. The readings within a unit directly invoke the four readings of the A1 layer of The Mathematical Form of Intention-Action and do not read A2: if the source drive is issuing while probability is not in conformance, the reading is still 1.

Definition 12 (Unit source-drive occurrence reading and positive occurrence interval) \[S, Aux\]　For each u ∈ U\_γ, source-drive candidates o := (e, Π^I, Z\_src) are enumerated according to Γ\_IDyn: e is a real event within u, Π^I is the current realization package of the Intention organization in this event, and Z\_src is the actual nonzero continuation sub-segment from the source-drive duty to 𝒫\_I in this event; the candidate set is written SrcCand(u). Write

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A1src^M(o) := CurrentEvent\_I^M ∧₃ CurrentSrcRoleReal^M ∧₃ ActualSrcEnter^M ∧₃ SrcAnchorBind^M

The four items are Definitions 4 to 7 of The Mathematical Form of Intention-Action, evaluated with u as the action declaration unit.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;SrcOcc^M(u) := ∃₃ o ∈ SrcCand(u) · \[ A1src^M(o) ∧₃ SameIDynBind^M(o) \]

Taking 0 requires coverage Cov^Src(u) = 1, that is, EventCover^M, SrcRoleCover^M and SrcSegCover^M are closed on u; if coverage is not closed and no positive candidate has been found, the reading is ⊥. SrcOcc^M(u) = 1 says that in u there is a real nonzero continuation belonging to \[S\_I\]\_src that actually enters the same 𝒫\_I. It does not say whether this continuation, relative to what comes before and after, is a start, a continuation or a stop. Definition 32 (StructuralZero) \[Aux\]　A decisive structural 0 comes from the failure of actual structure: the declared γ itself indeed has splicing across runs, across branches or of snapshots; there is indeed illegal direct writing within γ; the qualification anchor or the duty-identity system indeed undergoes a replacement across organizations; qualification indeed exits or re-forms within γ; a legal declaration was indeed rewritten after the results were seen; or all three sites are determinately without dynamics on correct objects with complete coverage. ## Section Five　Running and Solving

“Running” means evaluating a dynamics solving instance χ\_IDyn^M. This system reads running records and does not drive running.

### 5.1 Order of solving

(1) Check χ\_IDyn^M, the entry condition and Γ\_IDyn; check the declaration of T\_γ, U\_γ and the continuity granularity. If the declaration is invalid, return ⊥ (DeclarationInvalid).  
(2) Reconstruct RunID, BranchID and all I\_k, and evaluate Read\_{ActualTrajectory}^M.  
(3) Read the update logs, source chains and history of writing of authoritative state, and evaluate Read\_{AdmissibleIntentRun}^M.  
(4) Check the qualification anchor and duty-identity systems at each evaluation point, and evaluate Read\_{FixedIntentAnchor}^M; legal changes of running state are not included here.  
(5) Evaluate IntentCoreCert^M at each evaluation point, read the record of qualification-change events, and evaluate Read\_{IntentQualWholeSegment}^M.  
(6) Check same-evidence keys: foreign records not adopted are ignored; foreign records adopted as load-bearing make L5 ⊥ (EvidenceSplice), and evaluation must be redone with same-trajectory material. Evaluate Read\_{SameIDynEvidenceRun}^M.  
(7) Audit the freezing history of protocol and index, and evaluate Read\_{FrozenIDynProtocol}^M.  
(8) Compose Read\_{LegalIDynSeg}^M. If it is 0, the total reading is 0 and solving may stop; if it is ⊥, go on to evaluate dynamic actuality.  
(9) Source-drive site: for each unit enumerate SrcCand(u), evaluate A1src^M and the binding, and obtain SrcOcc^M(u); aggregate Read\_{SourceOccurrenceDyn}^{M,γ}. Separately, from the positive occurrence interval and confirmed non-occurrence, evaluate sustaining, stop, start and SrcRestart^M as a classification report. The cross-time type may not be defined directly from the values of SrcOcc^M on adjacent units.  
(10) Carrier site: build 𝒦^car by the discovery rule and judge CarRegCover^M; for each step of each candidate evaluate BearHold, invoke StepDeg under Γ\_D^{ij}, and evaluate the binding and Q^Car; aggregate Read\_{CarrierDyn}^{M,γ}.  
(11) Probability site: check SameIntentProbDynBind^M; when it is 1, invoke the order of solving of The Mathematical Form of Probability-Dynamics with χ̄\_PDyn^I and γ to obtain Read\_{IntentProbDyn}^{M,γ}; when it is 0, remove the foreign evidence and re-evaluate on the correct object, and if material for the correct object still cannot be obtained, return ⊥ (ProbBindMismatch).  
(12) Compose Read\_{DynActual\_I}^{M,γ} and Read\_{N\_IDyn^γ}^M. As soon as any site obtains 1, dynamic actuality can be signed 1 and the other sites may stop; bindings must be checked on the spot before the readings of each unit and each step are evaluated.  
(13) Separately evaluate the action projection: evaluate the complete-action positive interval and, for each relevant time window, NoAct^M and Reach^M; evaluate start, continuation, stop, the stop layer, ProbSide and IActRestart^M. End states of time windows serve only as consistency checks.  
(14) Separately evaluate the directional-type and carrier-type readings; if a composite profile has been declared, first decide applicability, then evaluate the comparison of ≻\_Π.  
(15) Evaluate SwitchCall^M(σ) for the comparison sequences falling within γ.  
(16) When there is no positive reading, check the coverage of the refutation path chosen for signing 0: if coverage is not closed, return the corresponding ⊥; if there is only DeclarationZero, return ⊥ (DeclarationInvalid); otherwise sign 0 and give FailBranch^M. After signing 0, determine the structural verdict by 7.3.  
(17) Output the total reading, the attached reasons, the paths used, the readings of the three sites, the reports of the projections and the record of condition propagation.

Steps (13) to (15) are only reports of projections and do not in turn determine the readings of steps (9) to (12).

### 5.2 Reasons for ⊥

When a formal reading takes ⊥, a reason must be attached, as follows: DeclarationInvalid, the declaration, version, division into units or continuity granularity is invalid; TrajectoryOpen, the records of run or branch are incomplete; UpdateOpen, the update operations, source chains or history of writing are incomplete; AnchorOpen, the references to the anchor or the duty-identity systems are non-adjudicable; QualificationOpen, the qualification reading at some evaluation point is ⊥, or the record of qualification-change events is incomplete; EvidenceKeyOpen, the same-evidence key is non-adjudicable; EvidenceSplice, the adopted load-bearing material contains confirmed foreign records; ProtocolOpen, the modification history of protocol or index cannot be audited; SourceOpen, some unit of the source-drive site is non-adjudicable, subdivided as in The Mathematical Form of Intention-Action into EventOpen, SourceRoleOpen and SourcePathOpen; CarrierRegistryOpen, the coverage of the carrier candidate domain is not closed; BearDimOpen, the bearing reading is non-adjudicable; DegreeOpen, the degree reading or comparison is ⊥\_D; CarrierBindMismatch, the load-bearing records adopted by a carrier step belong to another anchor, direction, Law, degree protocol or run; ProbabilityDynOpen, the upstream Probability-Dynamics reading is ⊥, subdivided by the upstream reasons; ProbBindOpen, the attribution of Intention Probability-Dynamics is non-adjudicable; ProbBindMismatch, the Probability-Dynamics evidence obtained belongs to another object, and material for the correct object has not yet been obtained; ReaderImplementationInvalid, the test of read-out non-interference fails (5.3, IDyn-M14).

Reports of projections have reasons of their own: SourceTransitionOpen, the records required for the cross-time classification of the source drive are insufficient; ActOpen, the action reading of a time window is ⊥; ActTransitionOpen, the records required for the cross-time classification of complete action are insufficient; StopLayerOpen, the reach layer is non-adjudicable; ProbSideOpen, the state of Probability-Action before the start is non-adjudicable; DirectionOpen, the directional-type comparison is non-adjudicable; ProfileOpen, the profile comparison is ⊥; SwitchOpen, the switching reading is ⊥ or the sequence is not within γ. They do not affect the total reading. Not applicable and profile not applicable are not reasons for ⊥; they are determinate results.

Several further reasons belong not to formal readings but to structural verdicts obtained by read-back from formal readings: PDynNegOpen, the formal reading is 0, but the conditions required in read-back for the 0 of the probability site are not closed; CarrierNegOpen, the upstream negative conditions required in read-back for the 0 of the carrier site are not closed; QualExitOpen, the chosen path is the 0 of L4, but the structural determination of qualification exiting within γ has not yet been closed by an upstream negative path; LawReadOpen, the formal reading is 1, but the H\_LawRead of the Law-bearing witnesses actually used in positive read-back is not closed.

These reasons are all solving diagnoses, not ontological conditions. It is not permitted to lump every ⊥ together as insufficient material.

### 5.3 Solvability

The following are conditions of formal representation and solving, written IDyn-M1 to IDyn-M14. They are not ontological conditions of Intention-Dynamics, nor are they H.

Under the conditions that Γ\_IDyn is frozen, all candidate domains involved are finite and enumerable, and IDyn-M1 to IDyn-M14 hold, every step completes in finitely many steps, and Read\_{N\_IDyn^γ}^M has a definite three-valued reading, with a reason attached when it takes ⊥. A declared instance that does not satisfy these conditions does not thereby take 0; its reading stays ⊥.

### 5.4 Standing of the solution

Read\_{N\_IDyn^γ}^M = 1 is a formal solution, meaning that γ is a legal dynamics segment of one and the same Intention and that at least one of the three sites has a formal certificate of dynamics. Read\_{N\_IDyn^γ}^M = 0 means that some item of the legal segment fails structurally, or that none of the three sites has dynamics on correct objects with complete coverage; its strength of negation at the structural layer is distinguished by the chosen refutation path (7.3). Read\_{N\_IDyn^γ}^M = ⊥ means that N\_IDyn^γ cannot be decided formally; it may not be read as N\_IDyn^γ failing, still less as Intention having no dynamics. Profile not applicable says only that the declared profile has no complete common domain of comparison at some step, and does not affect the total reading.
