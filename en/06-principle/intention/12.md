# The Necessity Form of Intention-Dynamics

## Section One　Purpose

To prove: when Intention already holds, provided that a declared trajectory γ is a legal dynamics segment of one and the same Intention, and at least one of the three sites, source drive, bearing Law and probability organization, really moves on γ, Intention has dynamics on γ, and no fifth independent dynamics condition need be satisfied.

A legal dynamics segment is this: γ is a segment of a real running trajectory of the Sedimentation-Field; all states within the segment are produced by the legal running of the field, with no out-of-segment write-in; the qualification anchor is not replaced; Intention qualification persists at every field state of γ; the evidence for the three sites is taken from the same γ and the same run; and the protocols for comparison and attribution are frozen before the results are seen.

The three sites are:

First, source-drive occurrence dynamics: the source-drive duty of Intention itself really continues on γ and enters the same 𝒫\_I, and across time it starts, is sustained, stops, or undergoes re-onset after stopping.

Second, carrier dynamics: the bearing Law of some formal direction of 𝒫\_I undergoes, on the same realization in which it bears the duty of that direction, a rise, fall or migration of degree.

Third, Intention Probability-Dynamics: the formal probability organization 𝒫\_I in the Intention-Domain itself undergoes on γ the start, continuation or stop of Probability-Action, a real change in the relative-generation relation, or a structural change of the formal dimensions, the common binding or the rate-bearing organization.

In formulas:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;N\_IDyn^γ ⇒ IntentDyn\_F^γ(K\_I)

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;N\_IDyn^γ := LegalIntentDynSegment\_F^γ(K\_I; Γ\_IDyn) ∧ ( SourceOccurrenceDyn\_I^γ ∨ CarrierDyn\_I^γ ∨ IntentProbDyn\_I^γ )

The working domain is: at the start of γ, Intent\_{F,t\_0} = 1. The persistence of qualification over the whole segment is L4 in the antecedent and is not made a separate assumption of the working domain.

“No fifth independent dynamics condition need be satisfied” means specifically: whether there is a complete Intention-Action on γ; whether there is Desire-Gate switching; whether there is current counter-holding or post-gate Principle-Action; whether there is manifestation, awareness, tuning success, experience change, behavior or effect; whether there is strengthening or weakening; whether one site is moving or several at once; whether the states at the start and the end of the segment are the same; whether the induction comes from the field state, from the external world or from explicit reentry; whether there is suppression by the Sleep-Domain; whether musing circuits take part: none of these affects the conclusion.

### 2.2 Route of proof

On the same range of dynamics corpus, this chapter independently carries out the sufficiency procedure anew.

The route of proof is:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;frozen dynamics semantic domain → atom classification → review of irreducible conjunctions → closure of dynamics semantics → the legal segment together with any one of the three sites completely realizes the dynamics semantics → IntentDyn

The corresponding three theorems are: first, obtain on the frozen dynamics semantic domain all the positive semantics of Intention having dynamics on a segment of trajectory, and prove that there is neither a fifth duty nor an independent cancelling condition (Theorem One); then prove that the legal segment together with each one of the three sites, taken separately, completely realizes these semantics (Theorem Two, in three branches); finally, obtain the Theorem of the Necessity of Intention-Dynamics (Theorem Three).

## Section Four　Rules of Classification

### 4.1 Classification

Each atom is assigned to one of the following classes according to its duty with respect to the question “does Intention have dynamics on γ?” Two layers must be kept apart here: the frozen corpus atoms are the semantic grounds; what is extracted from these grounds and must be realized by every instance of Intention-Dynamics are the universal positive duties. The concrete situations that the corpus atoms speak of, such as waiting or suppression, are not required to appear item by item in every instance of Intention-Dynamics.

Semantic grounds: atoms that directly state by virtue of what Intention has dynamics on a segment of trajectory.

Positive dynamics semantics U\_IDyn^+: the universal obtaining duties extracted from the semantic grounds. By the question answered, this chapter reads two load-bearing points:

U\_Move　real movement: in a segment of real running there is indeed cross-time change on the side of Intention, rather than stillness; identical states at the two ends do not by that alone count as no movement; and the change is neither a written-in label nor an appearance produced by replacing the objects of comparison after the fact.  
U\_Own　Intention's own movement: this change falls on Intention itself, that is, on the Intention-Domain's own launching, constitution or organization, and belongs to Intention that is one and the same over the whole segment; it is not movement elsewhere in the field, not movement of some other organization, and it is not pieced together across what is not one and the same Intention, or across different runs or segments.

The two load-bearing points correspond one-to-one neither to the three sites nor to the six items of the legal segment; they are read independently from the corpus, and their number is decided by the corpus, not padded out to match the number of sites.

Cancelling side: atoms that, when the positive dynamics semantics are all realized, can on their own make IntentDyn = 0.

Remaining semantics U\_IDyn^O: atoms in U\_IDyn^src that are neither semantic grounds nor bearers of a cancelling duty.

Boundary review: the atoms of B\_IDyn^edge take no part in the classification; they are only reviewed item by item as to whether they form a fifth duty or a cancelling duty at the boundary. What they speak of are the attribution relation of dynamics, modes of onset, the naming of state differences and conjectures about implementation.

### 4.2 Criteria of classification

(a) Criterion of question. See whether the atom answers “has Intention moved on this segment, and is it Intention itself that is moving,” or rather “where does the source drive come from,” “how does the stirring of thought begin,” “what is the state difference called,” or “by what mechanism is dynamics implemented.”

(b) Criterion of phase. See whether the atom speaks of the change on a segment of trajectory itself, or of the source of the change, the route by which it begins, or a conjecture about structure.

(c) Criterion of deletion. Suppose that what the atom speaks of does not appear on this segment, the other atoms unchanged. If no sentence in the corpus therefore denies that Intention has dynamics on this segment, the atom is not a semantic ground and bears no obtaining duty. This criterion is used only to re-check atoms already assigned to U\_IDyn^O under (a) and the atoms of the boundary review set.

(d) Review of the cancelling side. If an atom looks formally like a negation or a restriction, it must be specially reviewed: supposing the positive dynamics semantics are all realized, can it on its own make IntentDyn = 0? If, in order to take effect, it must make some positive semantics fail, it is not an independent cancelling condition.

### 4.3 Review of irreducible conjunctions

After the single atoms have been classified, the irreducible conjunctions authorized by the frozen corpus must still be checked. Two atoms that individually do not decide the truth value of dynamics may together produce a new force, so “the conjunction is not a dynamics condition either” cannot be inferred from “none of the parts is a dynamics condition.” If someone claims that some conjunction bears a new obtaining duty or cancelling duty, grounds explicitly authorizing that combination to bear the duty must be found in the frozen corpus; such authorization can occur within one sentence, or be formed by causal, conditional, referential, summarizing or anaphoric relations across sentences.

For a conjunction to become a fifth duty, it must further satisfy this: when the positive dynamics semantics are all realized and the legal segment and one of the three sites hold together, its failure makes IntentDyn = 0, and this cancellation does not appear as the exit of some item of the legal segment or as the failure of all three sites.

## Section Five　Item-by-Item Classification of the Atoms

### 5.1 Semantic grounds

u35, u36, u37 are all semantic grounds. From three sides they jointly constrain the universal positive duties this chapter is to extract; they are not three duties each of which must be brought into conformance in every instance of Intention-Dynamics.

u35　Intention awaits the Biased-Flow of the Experience-Target. Semantic ground; supports U\_Move and U\_Own, and draws the boundary “no complete action does not mean no dynamics.” Q8 speaks of the time when Intention “awaits”: at that time there is no complete action toward any Experience-Target, yet Intention has “Biased-Flow,” like a spider stretching its web, the tension in every thread of which is changing. Biased-Flow is flow: on the reading of The General Form of Intention-Dynamics v1.1, its actual launching falls on the source drive, and the bias among directions falls on the relative-generation relation of 𝒫\_I, whether the latter constitutes Intention Probability-Dynamics still depending on the upstream cross-time criteria. It lies within waiting, which shows that the movement of Intention is not confined to complete action; it is the Biased-Flow of “Intention,” which shows that this movement belongs to Intention itself. What it answers is: when not acting, Intention can still be moving, and it is Intention itself that moves.

u36　Statehood. Semantic ground; supports U\_Move and U\_Own. Q9 says that the Intention-Domain has statehood: the Intention-Domain can be in different states. This shows that the state of the Intention-Domain can change, and that what changes is the Intention-Domain's own state, the different states of one and the same Intention-Domain at different moments. According to The General Form of Intention-Dynamics, the state of the Intention-Domain is precisely the real condition, at a given moment, of its source-drive occurrence, its bearing Laws and its probability organization, and differences of state fall on the three sites; so u36 shows that such a change of state accords with the semantics of Intention-Dynamics, but it does not on its own establish a “state dynamics” outside the three sites: a difference of state is the movement of Intention only when it actually falls into a real cross-time change at one of the three sites. What it answers is: what kind of change counts as Intention moving, and what it is that changes.

u37　The Sleep-Domain and the Intention-Domain mutually inhibit one another. Semantic ground; supports U\_Move and U\_Own, and draws the boundary “the suppression and counter-pressure of the field enter the Intention-Domain and do not set up an axis of their own.” T2 says that the Intent-Field contains a Sleep-Domain, and that it and the Intention-Domain mutually inhibit one another: the Sleep-Domain suppresses the Intention-Domain, and the Intention-Domain also suppresses the Sleep-Domain. This is the action and reaction of real field dynamics upon the Intention-Domain. It is counted as Intention's own movement only insofar as it shows in the Intention-Domain's own state: when the Intention-Domain changes under suppression, or changes by prevailing over the other, it is the Intention-Domain that is moving; a change in the Sleep-Domain itself is not the movement of Intention. What it answers is: the movement of Intention can come from the suppression and counter-pressure of the field, but the movement must fall on the Intention-Domain. It does not require that every instance of Intention-Dynamics involve suppression by the Sleep-Domain.

### 5.2 Boundary review

u12　The Intention-Domain is the inexhaustible core of motion in the Intent-Field. Boundary; attribution. It says that the source drive issues from the Intention-Domain itself, so that when the source-drive duty of Intention itself really occurs, it is Intention launching, not an external push. It serves to clarify that the scope of U\_Own includes the source drive, and bears no separate obtaining duty: it says that the source drive belongs to Intention, not whether the source drive has issued on this segment.

u31　Thought stirring within stillness must issue from the Intention-Domain. Boundary; mode of onset. It says that, for the kind of beginning that goes from stillness to movement, what first sets things in motion is Intention itself. It explains one origin of a beginning and does not stipulate that every segment of dynamics must have such a beginning: on a segment of trajectory Intention may be continuing all along, or may change only in constitution or in organization.

u34　Attention is the gathering of Intention at the place it tunes. Boundary; naming of a state difference. It says that the difference between gathered and ungathered exists, and this difference is already covered by the statehood of u36; it does not authorize a total strength, nor does it require that Intention be gathered in order to count as moving.

u42　The stirring of mind begins in the musing circuits. Boundary; route of onset. It speaks of one explicit mode of onset in a higher Sedimentation-Field, not of a condition of every segment of dynamics; continuation and organizational change caused directly by the source drive are equally legal.

u52　Power-Ring. Boundary; conjecture about implementation. The main book puts it forward with “may” and “provisionally”; it is a conjecture about how dynamics is implemented, and bears neither an obtaining duty nor a cancelling duty.

### 5.3 Review of the cancelling side

The atoms that look formally like negations or restrictions are u35, u37 and the boundary atom u31.

The “awaits” of u35: waiting looks like stillness. But what Q8 says is precisely that there is Biased-Flow within waiting; what it denies is “not acting means being still,” not dynamics. Suppose the positive dynamics semantics are all realized while Intention is waiting: no sentence in the corpus therefore makes IntentDyn = 0.

The “inhibit” of u37: suppression looks as if it could cancel the movement of Intention. But suppression is itself field dynamics acting on the Intention-Domain, and the Intention-Domain changing because of suppression is precisely moving; even if suppression makes the source drive cease issuing and action stop, stopping is also a movement. If suppression makes Intention qualification itself exit, that is a matter of qualification: when qualification exits within γ, L4 of the legal segment does not hold and the antecedent already fails; it is not a case of “the positive semantics all realized and then cancelled.”

The “within stillness” of u31: it says that a stirring of thought arises from stillness; stillness is the state before the stirring of thought, not a denial of dynamics on a segment of trajectory.

None of the three can on its own make IntentDyn = 0 when the positive dynamics semantics are all realized.

## Section Six　Closure of Dynamics Semantics

6.1 Theorem One　Theorem of the Semantic Closure of Intention-Dynamics

As obtained by independent analysis on the frozen dynamics semantic domain U\_IDyn^src: the universal positive duties of Intention having dynamics on γ close into two items, real movement U\_Move and Intention's own movement U\_Own; u35, u36, u37 jointly support and constrain these two duties from three sides, namely internal dynamics within waiting, the changeability of state, and the real action of field dynamics on the Intention-Domain, without requiring any instance of Intention-Dynamics to realize their literal situations item by item; none of the atoms of the boundary review set B\_IDyn^edge forms a fifth duty or a cancelling duty; the cancelling side is empty; and there is in the frozen corpus no irreducible conjunction bearing a fifth duty or a cancelling duty. That is:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;U\_IDyn^+ = { U\_Move, U\_Own },   U\_IDyn^O = ∅  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;FifthIndependentIDynDuty\_I = ∅,   IndependentCancel\_IDyn^suff = ∅

Proof

First, the positive semantics are exhausted. The three dynamics semantic atoms of U\_IDyn^src have all been classified, and the five boundary atoms of B\_IDyn^edge have all been reviewed item by item. An exhaustive analysis of the entire propositional contribution of the three semantic grounds extracts only two classes of universal obtaining duty, and no third: the flow going on within waiting of u35, the differing states of u36, and the suppression and counter-pressure of u37 are all “there is indeed cross-time change”; the “Intention” of u35, the “Intention-Domain” of u36 and the “with the Intention-Domain” of u37 are all “the change falls on Intention itself.” The “awaits” of u35 and the “suppression” of u37 speak of situations in which Intention-Dynamics can occur, not of conditions that every instance of Intention-Dynamics must meet: to say that Intention is moving on a segment of trajectory does not require that it happen to be waiting, nor that the Sleep-Domain happen to be suppressing it. By criteria (a) and (b) the five boundary atoms belong to attribution, mode of onset, naming and conjecture, and they are re-checked by criterion (c): if on a segment of trajectory there is no beginning from stillness to movement, no participation of musing circuits, no gathering, or it is unknown whether the Power-Ring exists, no sentence in the corpus therefore denies that Intention has dynamics on this segment.

Second, no single atom bears a fifth duty. u35, u36, u37 support real movement and Intention's own movement and set up no separate duty of their own; u12 shows that the source drive belongs to Intention, u31 explains the origin of one kind of beginning, u34 names a state difference, u42 explains one route of onset, and u52 is a conjecture. None of them is a universal obtaining condition of every segment of dynamics.

Third, the cancelling side is empty. u35, u37, u31 have been reviewed item by item under criterion (d). The following candidates are not independent cancelling conditions either:

No complete Intention-Action: by u35, there is still Biased-Flow within waiting, and the movement of Intention is not confined to complete action.  
No Desire-Gate switching: switching is a current event at the gate layer, not a condition of dynamics; on a segment of trajectory there may be only continuation, a rise or fall in the degree of bearing Laws, or organizational change.  
No current counter-holding, no post-gate Principle-Action: these lie behind the gate and are outputs of Intention; the corpus does not take them as conditions of Intention moving.  
No manifestation, no awareness: no sentence in the frozen corpus takes manifestation or awareness as a condition of Intention moving; corpus item I9 says that Intention-Action occurs before awareness (u50, belonging to the corpus of Desire-Gate switching), and the Biased-Flow of Intention in waiting is all the less conditioned on manifestation.  
Tuning not succeeding, experience not changing, no behavior or effect: these are post-gate consequences.  
No strengthening or weakening: u36 says that states can differ, and does not require states to rise or fall in some direction; the source drive occurring continuously while degree and relations both stay unchanged is also a segment of movement.  
Only one site moving: the corpus does not require source drive, constitution and organization to change at the same time; Q8, Q9 and T2 each speak of the movement of the Intention-Domain or of its changeable state, and do not say that it must move in several respects at once.  
The states at the start and the end of the segment being the same: if a real cross-time change has already been obtained within γ at one of the three sites, for example a bearing Law first rising and then falling, or the source drive launching and then ceasing to issue, then even if the states at the start and the end of the segment are the same, this does not cancel the movement that has already occurred on the segment; the restoration of the endpoints cannot erase the real motion in between.  
Induction from the external world, from the field state or from explicit reentry: this is the source of the induction, not the bearer of the movement; as long as the change falls on Intention itself, U\_Own is realized all the same.  
Suppression by the Sleep-Domain: by u37, suppression is itself dynamics acting on the Intention-Domain, not a cancellation of dynamics.  
Exit of Intention qualification: for it to take effect, it must make the content of U\_Own, “Intention that is one and the same over the whole segment,” fail; in the antecedent of this chapter it appears as the failure of L4, not as an independent cancelling condition.

Fourth, there is no irreducible conjunction bearing a fifth duty or a cancelling duty.

First, authorization within one sentence is reviewed. Q8 says only that there is Biased-Flow within waiting, and adds no third thing beyond “waiting” and “Biased-Flow,” such as that the Biased-Flow must reach some strength or must go to some Desire-Gate. Q9 lists the three properties side by side; of these, only statehood bears on the semantic domain of this chapter, while the other two properties in the same sentence lie outside it, and the sentence does not authorize the combination of the three properties to decide dynamics on a segment of trajectory. T2 says only that they mutually inhibit one another, and authorizes neither “suppression must reach some degree to count as movement” nor “suppression makes dynamics invalid.”

Next, the conjunctions between semantic grounds and boundary atoms are reviewed. u35 ∧ u12: putting the Biased-Flow within waiting together with the inexhaustible core of motion says only that Intention can still launch while waiting, and produces no new obtaining condition. u36 ∧ u34: putting statehood together with gathered Intention says only that differences of state include being gathered or not; it does not authorize determining dynamics by the degree of gathering, nor does it set up a total strength. u35 ∧ u31, u36 ∧ u42: putting waiting together with thought stirring within stillness, and state together with musing circuits, says only that Intention's beginning to act from waiting or from stillness can have these origins; it does not raise them into conditions of every segment of dynamics. u37 ∧ u52: mutual inhibition is put together with the Power-Ring, but the main book offers the Power-Ring only as a conjecture, which cannot authorize any duty. u12 ∧ u31 ∧ u42: the core of the source drive, thought stirring within stillness and the musing circuits all answer where movement starts from, not whether there is movement on this segment.

Then authorization across sentences is reviewed.
