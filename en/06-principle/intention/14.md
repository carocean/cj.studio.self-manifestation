# The Determination Form of Intention

## Section One　Purpose

For real declared instances frozen in advance, under a legal and operable determination protocol, to invoke the formal systems, read-back rules and object-layer closures already closed in the first thirteen chapters; to give a final verdict separately on each of the four classes of object currently closed in the Intention Group-Form; and to prove that when the verdict is 1 the object necessarily holds, and when the verdict is 0 the object necessarily does not hold (each carrying the conditions of the path used); that when the working domain of the object has not been entered, the verdict is OutDom, not 0; and that whenever correctness cannot be guaranteed, the verdict is ⊥, with the reason stated.

The four classes of object and the four verdicts are:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Det\_I(χ\_I) ∈ {1, 0, ⊥}: whether Intention holds on the declared qualification instance;  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Det\_IAct(χ\_IAct) ∈ {1, 0, ⊥, OutDom}: whether one complete instance of Intention-Action has occurred on the declared current action unit;  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Det\_IGS(χ\_IGS) ∈ {1, 0, ⊥, OutDom}: whether one Intention Desire-Gate switching has occurred on the declared comparison sequence σ;  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Det\_IDyn(χ\_IDyn) ∈ {1, 0, ⊥, OutDom}: whether Intention has dynamics on the declared trajectory segment γ.

Together they are called the group-level determination family:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;𝔇\_I^Π := ( Det\_I, Det\_IAct, Det\_IGS, Det\_IDyn )

The four verdicts answer four different questions; they are not combined into one total verdict, they do not vote against one another, and they do not fill in for one another.

This chapter does not redefine Intent, IntentAct, IntentGateSwitch or IntentDyn, adds no component to any object, does not rebuild the formal system of any Mathematical Form, and sets up no new H.

## Section Two　Determination Instance, Determination Protocol and Layering of Evidence

### 2.1 Determination instance

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;χ\_IJudge := ( χ\_I, χ\_IAct, χ\_IGS, χ\_IDyn )

Each sub-instance follows the upstream solving instance and is not rebuilt:

χ\_I := (F, t, χ\_P, 𝒢\_I^raw, Σ\_I^src, 𝒯\_I^tune, Γ\_I), Definition 1 of The Mathematical Form of Intention; the qualification anchor K\_I^M is given by it.  
χ\_IAct := (χ\_I, ξ\_t, Γ\_IAct), Definition 1 of The Mathematical Form of Intention-Action.  
χ\_IGS := (χ\_I, σ^M, Γ\_IGS), Definition 1 of The Mathematical Form of Intention Desire-Gate Switching.  
χ\_IDyn := (χ\_I, γ, Γ\_IDyn), Definition 1 of The Mathematical Form of Intention-Dynamics; the χ\_I in it is taken at the starting point t\_0 of γ, and the qualification instances at the evaluation points t\_k are same-anchor with it.

Only declared sub-instances are evaluated. The qualification instance cited by each upper-level sub-instance must be same-anchor with the qualification anchor K\_I^M of this declaration, and Det\_I is invoked at the respective time point; citing another anchor is a declaration invalidity.

All of these are declared objects, not objects that have already obtained qualification. They must be frozen before determination; during determination they may not be added or deleted, narrowed in domain, re-anchored, re-windowed or re-declared in order to pass a check.

### 2.2 Determination protocol

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Π\_IJudge := ( Freeze^inst, VersionSet, DeclRules, CoverRules, BindRules, FailRules )

Freeze^inst: the object of determination, the declared window, the candidate identities and the qualification anchor are frozen before determination.  
VersionSet: the versions of the upstream formal systems invoked and of the duty-identity system are frozen.  
DeclRules: the rules of declaration eligibility for each sub-instance, following the definitions of declaration zero in each Mathematical Form.  
CoverRules: the coverage required for signing 0 along each negative path, following the coverage certificates of each Mathematical Form.  
BindRules: the binding rules of same anchor, same chain, same sequence, same run, same carrier and same probability organization, following each Mathematical Form.  
FailRules: the permitted structural refutation paths, following each Mathematical Form; this chapter adds only one rule: the negative verdict on Intention-Dynamics does not use the L4 path.

Each sub-determination still invokes its own dedicated protocol Γ\_I, Γ\_IAct, Γ\_IGS, Γ\_IDyn. This chapter does not override these protocols; it only confirms that they have been legally frozen with consistent versions, and unifies the signing rules of the final verdicts.

### 2.3 Layering of evidence

The interfaces read by determination are divided into four layers according to the object they serve:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Y\_det = ( Y\_I, Y\_IAct, Y\_IGS, Y\_IDyn ) and Y\_cov

Y\_I: IntentCoreCert^M and its four item readings, anchor qualification, the probability reading and zero type of 𝒫\_I, and the Det\_P of The Determination Form of Probability.  
Y\_IAct: the current event, the current realization, the four-segment source chain, duty identities, same-chain binding, and FailLayer and FailSub\_A2.  
Y\_IGS: the real events of the prior and later evaluation domains, the gate-passage path segments, the later-state gate-bearing records, the event identity keys, the sequence identity, and FailLayer and FailSub\_S2.  
Y\_IDyn: the records of runs and branches, the history of updates and write-ins, the qualification readings at each evaluation point and the qualification-change records, the same-evidence key, the protocol history, source-drive continuation, carrier registration and degree, Probability-Dynamics, and FailBranch and RefuteRoute.  
Y\_cov: the coverage certificates of each layer and the completeness of negative read-outs, that is, NegComplete\_IAct^M, NegComplete\_IGS^M, NegComplete\_IDyn^M and so on.

Y\_cov is not a component of any object; it determines only whether the method of determination is qualified to sign 1 or 0.

The forward sources of each layer are given by forms already established: Y\_I comes from The Mathematical Form of Intention, The Mathematical Form of Probability and The Determination Form of Probability; Y\_IAct from the definitions of The Mathematical Form of Intention-Action and IA-M1 to IA-M12; Y\_IGS from the definitions of The Mathematical Form of Intention Desire-Gate Switching and IGS-M1 to IGS-M10; Y\_IDyn from the definitions of The Mathematical Form of Intention-Dynamics and IDyn-M1 to IDyn-M14.

Behavior, manifestation, effects, tuning results, empirical frequency or rate values may not serve as input to any verdict: The Inversion Form of Intention has already proven that such external results cannot in general identify Intention-Dynamics, and none of them is a condition of qualification, action or switching either.

## Section Three　Group-Level Determination Discipline

### 3.1 Four-valued verdicts

1: the object formally holds.  
0: the object formally does not hold.  
⊥: 1 or 0 cannot legally be signed for the current instance.  
OutDom: the working domain of the object has not been entered; the current declared question is not posed on this object.

The four values are pairwise distinct. Neither ⊥ nor OutDom is a third truth value of the object: at the object layer the object is still two-valued; ⊥ says that determination has not been completed, and OutDom says that this question does not apply to the current instance. OutDom is merged neither into 0 nor into ⊥: merging it into 0 would write a failure of the working domain as a negation of the object, and merging it into ⊥ would discard the knowledge already obtained that “the working domain has definitely not been entered.”

Det\_I does not use OutDom: what it judges is qualification itself. The working domain of qualification is the Intention-qualification working domain in the Self-Manifestation-Field; if the declared F is not such a field, that is a declaration invalidity, and the verdict is ⊥ (DeclarationInvalid).

### 3.2 Six general disciplines

J1　A formal reading is not a final verdict. Read^M = 1 does not amount to a verdict of 1: the object-layer read-back must still be completed and the conditions of the path used closed. Read^M = 0 does not amount to a verdict of 0: a legal structural zero must still be obtained and the conditions of the chosen path closed.

J2　A declaration zero is not a structural zero. Whenever a 0 comes only from declaration invalidity, the verdict is ⊥ (DeclarationInvalid), and re-declaration is required.

J3　Failing to find positive evidence is not a negative verdict. NoPositiveWitness ⇏ Det = 0.

J4　No 0 is signed while coverage is not closed. Whenever the candidate domain and the coverage required for signing 0 are not closed, the verdict is ⊥.

J5　Mismatched evidence does not manufacture a 0 for the object. EvidenceSplice, CarrierBindMismatch, ProbBindMismatch, and the identity mismatches of same chain, same sequence or same anchor in each layer, only make the corresponding reading or verdict ⊥.

J6　Conditions propagate only along the actual path. A verdict carries the conditions of whichever path it takes; the conditions of the various objects and paths are not mechanically conjoined.

### 3.3 Zero types

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;ZeroType ∈ { StructuralZero, DeclarationZero }

StructuralZero\_T: for the target T, the decisive 0 comes from a structural failure path permitted upstream, the candidate coverage is complete, the binding is correct, and the negative-representation conditions of the chosen path are closed, so that it can be legally read back as the structural core N\_T = 0. DeclarationZero\_T: the decisive 0 comes only from declaration invalidity.

No evidence zero is set up separately: evidence splicing and mismatch are both ⊥, not a kind of 0.

### 3.4 Dependence structure

Det\_I is the common entry of the other three verdicts: action, switching and dynamics are all defined within the working domain of Intent = 1. Among the three there is no ordering of stages:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The determination of IntentGateSwitch does not require IntentAct = 1: S2 invokes only A1, A2, A3 of action;  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The determination of IntentDyn requires neither IntentAct = 1 nor IntentGateSwitch = 1: neither complete action nor Desire-Gate switching is a condition of obtaining dynamics;  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The determination of IntentAct requires no switching or dynamics.

So the dependence graph of determination consists of three branches going down from Intent, not the chain Intent → IntentAct → IntentGateSwitch → IntentDyn. The entry of Intention-Dynamics has a further requirement of whole-segment applicability (Section Seven).

## Section Four　Determination of Intention Qualification

### 4.1 Positive verdict

Det\_I(χ\_I) = 1 if and only if IntentCoreCert^M(K\_I^M) = 1 and H\_LawRead of each Law-bearing witness actually used in the Probability read-back path has been closed.

### 4.2 Negative verdict

The Mathematical Form of Intention stipulates in 7.3 that the formal zeros of the atoms newly added in this group form (the atomic readings of InIntentDomain, SourceDrive, GateCorrespondence and TuneInterface) are not read back as structural zeros. So the only path by which a structural zero of qualification can currently be obtained is the probability side within P\_I:

Det\_I(χ\_I) = 0 if and only if AnchorQual\_I^M = 1; χ\_P is the probability solving instance of the 𝒫\_I declared by K\_I^M; and The Determination Form of Probability signs Det\_P(χ\_P) = 0 for χ\_P.

The definition of Det\_P = 0 already requires that Read\_{N\_P}^M = 0 be a structural zero, that the H^-\_M or H\_ρRep^- required by the failure path be closed, and that H2 ∧ H3^red be closed; these conditions accordingly enter Det\_I = 0.

Det\_I = 0 is a verdict relative to the declared anchor K\_I^M and the time point t: it says that this anchor bears no Intention at t, not that there is no Intention elsewhere in the field.

### 4.3 Undecided

In all other cases Det\_I = ⊥, with a reason attached:

DeclarationInvalid: declaration invalidity, including anchor invalidity (AnchorQual\_I^M = 0) and a declaration zero of the probability reading.  
AnchorOpen: anchor qualification is non-adjudicable.  
IntentCoverOpen: the coverage of some atomic reading is not closed, or material is insufficient.  
ProbabilityOpen: the probability verdict on 𝒫\_I is ⊥, with the reason from The Determination Form of Probability attached, such as OntologyBridgeOpen, UpstreamLawOpen, RateRepOpen.  
IntentAtomRepOpen: IntentCoreCert^M = 0, while the formal zero comes only from atoms newly added in this group form; their negative representation has not been proven, and a structural zero cannot be signed.  
LawReadOpen: IntentCoreCert^M = 1, while H\_LawRead of the witnesses used is not closed.

4.4 Qualification determination as the entry of the three branches

The qualification instance cited by an action, switching or dynamics sub-instance at its respective time point is written χ\_I.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Det\_I(χ\_I) = 0　⇒　Det\_IAct = OutDom, Det\_IGS = OutDom, reason IntentDomainFail  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Det\_I(χ\_I) = ⊥　⇒　Det\_IAct = ⊥, Det\_IGS = ⊥, reason IntentDomainOpen

The entry of Intention-Dynamics is determined separately according to Section Seven: failure and undecidedness of starting-point qualification are recorded as StartDomainFail and StartDomainOpen respectively, that is, as the names of IntentDomainFail and IntentDomainOpen at the starting point of γ.

Each upper-level sub-instance first checks its own declaration: if the declaration is invalid, the verdict is ⊥ (DeclarationInvalid), and entry determination is not reached; only after the declaration is eligible is Det\_I invoked according to the entry rules above. This agrees with the order of determination for dynamics in 7.7.

IntentDomainFail does not say IntentAct = 0 or IntentGateSwitch = 0: these objects have no working domain on this declared anchor, and the question is not posed on it.

## Section Seven　Determination of Intention-Dynamics

7.1 First checkpoint: starting-point qualification

Invoke Det\_I(χ\_I), with χ\_I taken at the starting point t\_0 of γ.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Det\_I(χ\_I) = 0　⇒　Det\_IDyn = OutDom (StartDomainFail)  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Det\_I(χ\_I) = ⊥　⇒　Det\_IDyn = ⊥ (StartDomainOpen)

When starting-point qualification does not hold, the base working domain W\_IDyn has not been entered, and the dynamics question of this group form is not posed on this segment; IntentDyn = 0 is not signed.

7.2 Second checkpoint: whole-segment qualification persistence

After starting-point qualification has been judged 1, judge L4:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;L4Det ∈ {1, 0, ⊥}

L4Det = 1 if and only if Read\_{IntentQualWholeSegment}^M = 1, that is, IntentCoreCert^M = 1 at all evaluation points t\_k, and the records of qualification-change events cover the whole segment (QualEventCover^M = 1) and contain no event of exit or re-formation; and H\_LawRead of the Law-bearing witnesses used by the qualification readings at the evaluation points has been closed. Equivalently: Det\_I = 1 at all evaluation points, and the qualification-change records cover the whole segment with no exit event.

L4Det = 0 if and only if at least one of the following two negative paths is closed, corresponding to the two sources of formal zero in Definition 8 of The Mathematical Form of Intention-Dynamics:

QualPointFail: there exists an evaluation point t\_k within γ at which Det\_I = 0 is signed for the same anchor K\_I^M, and the material used by this verdict belongs to the same run and the same branch as γ, without splicing.

QualExitEvent: there exists a real event record e\_q of exit from or re-formation of qualification, which belongs to this γ and has the same run, the same branch and the same anchor K\_I^M as γ; the identity, time point and anchor of the event are all adjudicable; it is not a spliced log, a conjecture or an external label; by Definition 8 of The Mathematical Form of Intention-Dynamics it is read as exit from or re-formation of qualification, making Read\_{IntentQualWholeSegment}^M = 0; and the failure of qualification shown by this record satisfies, by 7.3 of The Mathematical Form of Intention, the conditions of structural read-back: the negative determination domain of the failing item is closed, and the negative-representation conditions of that failure path have been closed.

The second path does not require that Det\_I = 0 first be obtained at some discrete evaluation point; for example, when exit and re-formation both occur between adjacent evaluation points, they can be read out only from the event record. But the two paths bear the same kind of read-back condition: whichever item the failure of qualification falls on, the negative-representation condition of that item is carried. In the current upstream state, the atoms newly added in this group form have no negative representation; if the failure shown by a qualification-change record falls on these items, this path likewise cannot be closed; when the record does not show which item the failure falls on, the condition cannot be determined, and the path cannot be closed either. Both cases revert to ⊥ (QualExitOpen). A failure on the probability side carries conditions according to The Determination Form of Probability.

L4Det = ⊥: all other cases (QualExitOpen).

Signing L4Det = 0 needs only one closed negative path; it needs neither the qualification readings at all evaluation points nor whole-segment coverage by the qualification-change records: the latter two are requirements for signing L4Det = 1. Whether qualification re-forms after exiting, and whether the identity of the re-formation event is separable, do not affect L4Det = 0; they serve only to determine the boundaries of re-segmentation and are output as reports.

When L4Det = 0:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Det\_IDyn = OutDom (QualExit)

In this case the structural verdicts L4 = 0 and N\_IDyn^γ = 0 may be signed as report items; IntentDyn = 0 is not signed. This declared segment should be re-segmented at the qualification boundaries, and the new declared segments determined anew.

When L4Det = ⊥: Det\_IDyn = ⊥ (QualExitOpen).

When both starting-point qualification and whole-segment qualification are judged 1, write Dom = In and proceed to the determination of dynamics.

### 7.3 Positive verdict

Det\_IDyn(χ\_IDyn) = 1 if and only if: Dom = In; Read\_{N\_IDyn^γ}^M = 1; and the conditions of the positive reading path used have been closed, that is, the applicable H\_LawRead of the Law-bearing witnesses actually used in the L4 qualification readings, in the bearing and degree readings of the carrier site and in the positive reading path of the probability site, together with the conditions of the upstream positive-reading path.

A positive verdict needs only that L1 to L6 all be 1 and that at least one of the three sites be 1. Once any site has obtained 1, the other sites need not be evaluated for the sake of the positive verdict. Neither complete action nor Desire-Gate switching, behavior, manifestation or tuning success is a condition of the positive verdict.

### 7.4 Negative-verdict paths

When Dom = In, the only permitted refutation paths are:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;RefuteRoute\_IDyn ∈ { L1, L2, L3, L6, DynActual }

L4 does not enter the negative verdict on Intention-Dynamics: its 0 goes only to OutDom at the second checkpoint. L5 takes only 1 or ⊥ and is not a negative-verdict path; evidence splicing yields only ⊥ (EvidenceSplice).

Paths L1, L2, L3, L6: the corresponding coverage Cov^traj, Cov^upd, Cov^anchor, Cov^prot is closed, and there is an explicit trajectory splice, out-of-segment direct writing, anchor change or after-the-fact rewriting. No H is carried.

Path DynActual: all three sites obtain structural 0 at once.

Source-drive site: Cov^Src\_γ is closed, and SrcOcc^M(u) = 0 for all units. No H is carried. When coverage is not closed, it is ⊥ (SourceOpen).

Carrier site: CarRegCover^M is closed; every step of every candidate has Q\_{ij,k}^Car = 0; the bearing readings and degree readings used by these 0s satisfy the upstream negative read-back conditions, and these conditions have been closed. When the conditions are not closed, it is ⊥ (CarrierNegOpen); when the evidence is mismatched, it is ⊥ (CarrierBindMismatch).

Probability site: SameIntentProbDynBind^M = 1; Read\_{N\_PDyn}^M = 0 is a structural zero; the H\_PAct-Rep, H\_ρRep^- or H^-\_M required by the actual failure path has been closed; and H2 ∧ H3^red of the necessary direction of The General Form of Probability-Dynamics has been closed. When the conditions are not closed, it is ⊥ (PDynNegOpen); when the evidence is mismatched, it is ⊥ (ProbBindMismatch).

If one of the three sites is ⊥, path DynActual is unavailable; dynamic actuality may not be signed 0 on the strength of 0s at two sites plus an undecided third.

### 7.5 Negative verdict

Det\_IDyn(χ\_IDyn) = 0 if and only if: Dom = In; Read\_{N\_IDyn^γ}^M = 0, and this 0 is a structural zero; and the chosen refutation path is one of the five above and satisfies that path's coverage and conditions.

### 7.6 Undecided

In all other cases Det\_IDyn = ⊥, with reasons following 5.2 of The Mathematical Form of Intention-Dynamics: DeclarationInvalid, TrajectoryOpen, UpdateOpen, AnchorOpen, QualificationOpen, EvidenceKeyOpen, EvidenceSplice, ProtocolOpen, SourceOpen, CarrierRegistryOpen, BearDimOpen, DegreeOpen, CarrierBindMismatch, ProbabilityDynOpen, ProbBindOpen, ProbBindMismatch, ReaderImplementationInvalid; PDynNegOpen, CarrierNegOpen, QualExitOpen and LawReadOpen at the layer of the structural verdict; and StartDomainOpen of this chapter.

### 7.7 Order of determination

(1) Freeze the declaration, and check the versions, the declared window and the protocol. If the declaration is invalid, the verdict is ⊥ (DeclarationInvalid).  
(2) Judge starting-point qualification. 0 gives OutDom (StartDomainFail); ⊥ gives ⊥ (StartDomainOpen).

8.5 Theorem Five　Theorem of the Independence of Verdicts

Among the four verdicts, apart from the entry relation, there is no mutual implication:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Det\_IAct = 0 ⇏ Det\_I = 0; Det\_IGS = 0 ⇏ Det\_IAct = 0; Det\_IDyn = 0 ⇏ Det\_IAct = 0 or Det\_IGS = 0;

Proof: upstream has already proven that the objects are mutually independent only within the working domain of Intent = 1: the failure of action does not cancel qualification (7.3 of The Mathematical Form of Intention-Action); switching invokes only the first three layers of action and does not require complete action (the interface of The Necessity Form of Intention Desire-Gate Switching); neither complete action nor Desire-Gate switching is a condition of obtaining Intention-Dynamics (Section One of The Necessity Form of Intention-Dynamics); and a conclusion on one σ implies nothing about other sequences. So upstream admits these non-implying combinations among the objects. A verdict agrees with the truth value of its object only when it takes a definite value, 1 or 0 (Theorems One and Two); if the definite value of one verdict were inferred from another verdict, then on an instance where the objects take such a combination, the inferred verdict would disagree with the truth value of its object, violating Theorem One or Theorem Two. So each verdict can only be evaluated separately, according to its own definition. Equality between a verdict and the truth value of its object holds only within the respective complete determination domain of Section Ten. □

So the four verdicts can be combined freely, for example Det\_I = 1, Det\_IAct = 0, Det\_IGS = 1, Det\_IDyn = 1. Majority voting is not permitted, and the definite value of one verdict may not be used to fill in the ⊥ of another.

## Section Nine　Misjudgment Bound

This chapter introduces no statistical misjudgment rate. The discipline of determination is: whenever correctness cannot be guaranteed, revert to ⊥.

Positive-verdict domain: by Theorem One, whenever 1 is signed, the object necessarily holds, with the conditions written into the definition.

Negative-verdict domain: by Theorem Two, whenever 0 is signed, the object necessarily does not hold, with the conditions of the chosen path written into the definition.

Working domain: by Theorem Three, whenever OutDom is signed, the working domain has necessarily not been entered, and the object has not been negated.

Where both cannot be secured, the current state as declared upstream is as follows:

The negative verdict on Intention qualification has only one path, on the probability side. It depends on H2 ∧ H3^red and, according to the path, on H^-\_M or H\_ρRep^-; The Determination Form of Probability declares that these conditions are not yet closed. Until they are closed, Det\_I = 0 cannot be signed and reverts to ⊥ (ProbabilityOpen, with OntologyBridgeOpen and the like attached). Formal zeros of the atoms newly added in this group form revert to ⊥ (IntentAtomRepOpen).

Consequently, IntentDomainFail and StartDomainFail depend on Det\_I = 0, and the two negative paths of QualExit bear the same negative read-back conditions of qualification as Det\_I = 0, so all three revert to ⊥ along with it: until the upstream conditions are closed, the determiner cannot obtain OutDom and gives only IntentDomainOpen, StartDomainOpen or QualExitOpen.

Action and switching: the negative verdict via A2-PDuty depends on H\_PAct-Rep; while that condition is not yet closed, it reverts to ⊥ (PActRepOpen). Negative verdicts on the other failure layers and paths carry no H and can be signed.

Dynamics: paths L1, L2, L3, L6 carry no H and can be signed; path DynActual depends on the upstream negative conditions of the carrier site and on H2 ∧ H3^red and the like of the probability site, and until they are closed it reverts to ⊥ (CarrierNegOpen or PDynNegOpen). L5 does not take 0; an instance whose load-bearing evidence is cross-track can only be judged ⊥ (EvidenceSplice).

Each “can be signed” above presupposes that the entry qualification is judged 1, that is, that H\_LawRead of the witnesses used by the entry qualification reading has been closed.

So the coverage of the current determiner in the negative direction and in the working-domain direction is smaller than in the positive direction: it does not misjudge, but it yields ⊥ more often. This is an explicitly declared misjudgment bound, not an error of the determiner. The real boundary of determination is the adjudicable domain, not a statistical confidence interval.

## Section Fifteen　Interfaces

### 15.1 Upstream

The General Form of Intention, The Necessity Form of Intention, The Mathematical Form of Intention: the two-way closure of Intention qualification, the formal entry, zero types, anchor qualification and the boundary of negative read-back.  
The General Form of Intention-Action, The Necessity Form of Intention-Action, The Mathematical Form of Intention-Action: the two-way closure of action, three-valued readings, failure layers, coverage, reasons for ⊥ and the propagation of H\_PAct-Rep.  
The General Form of Intention Desire-Gate Switching, The Necessity Form of Intention Desire-Gate Switching, The Mathematical Form of Intention Desire-Gate Switching: the two-way closure of switching, parallel evaluation, refutation paths, coverage, reasons for ⊥ and condition propagation.  
The General Form of Intention-Dynamics, The Necessity Form of Intention-Dynamics, The Mathematical Form of Intention-Dynamics: the two-way closure of dynamics, the legal segment and the three sites, FailBranch, coverage, reasons for ⊥, the special-case discipline of L4 and condition propagation.  
The Inversion Form of Intention: the classification of residues and the whole-segment applicability labels.  
The Determination Form of Probability: the probability verdict on 𝒫\_I and its conditions.  
The field system and the operating substrate: the real sources of each determination interface.

15.2 Connection with The Inversion Form of Intention

By invoking legal internal interfaces on a real instance to sign verdicts, this chapter can close ClassOpen in inversion: once the object truth value of a candidate has been judged 1 or 0 by this chapter, its label closes accordingly. Within DomOpen, whatever can be closed is closed by the determination of starting-point qualification and of L4, with the result In, StartDomainFail or QualExit. InvOpen is a different question: directly determining one instance does not complete the preimage enumeration under the original inversion protocol. Mix is not eliminated by this chapter: that two legal candidates are observationally equivalent under the external protocol is a structural unidentifiability of the protocol; running determination once more on the same instance does not add any separating power to the original protocol.

### 15.3 Hand-down

The Limit Form of Intention: receives StartDomainFail, QualExit, the time points of qualification failure and the boundaries of re-segmentation, and studies at what limits qualification and each object truly exit.  
The Construction Form of the Intention Operating Substrate: must provide all the legal interfaces required by the four verdicts of this chapter; lacking any one of them, an operating substrate cannot claim to implement this determiner completely.  
The Reachability Form of Intention: may use the verdicts of this chapter to certify whether a target state has truly been reached.  
The Source Form of Intention: may invoke Det\_I and the other verdicts to determine the historical endpoint of first holding; verdicts at the negative end carry conditions along their original paths.  
The Stability Form of Intention: may take the verdicts of this chapter as window-level state signatures; stability itself is not determined in this chapter.  
The upgrade of The Mathematical Form of Intention: supplies the reverse representation of the atoms newly added in this group form; only once it is supplied can the negative verdict and complete determination of Intention qualification extend to these atoms.

### 15.4 What this chapter does not do

This chapter does not redefine Intention, action, switching or dynamics; adds no component to any object; does not rebuild the Mathematical Forms; sets up no new H; does not write conditions at the layer of the method of determination as conditions of objects; does not use behavior, manifestation, effects, empirical frequency or rate values as criteria; introduces no Bayesian prior, posterior or statistical confidence; does not treat a failure to find a witness as the nonexistence of the structure; and does not study limits, the operating substrate, reachability, source or stability.
