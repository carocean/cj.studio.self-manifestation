# The Limit Form of Intention

This chapter carries out a limit review of the four kinds of object already closed in the Intention Group-Form, along legal comparison directions declared in advance, to settle three things: whether the object still holds when the peripheral properties usually taken as “Intention at work” retreat to their extremes; where the boundary lies that each kind of object really cannot cross; and, once it is crossed, which object exits and into what it degenerates.

The conclusion is: a direction whose current rate is zero, the absence of a current source-drive pulse, no manifestation, no complete action, no switching, identical states at the two ends, and migration of the dynamics position are all endpoints compatible with the corresponding objects; the boundary of Intention qualification is characterized exactly by the four qualification structures, and the boundaries of Intention-Action and Intention Desire-Gate switching exactly by their respective structural cores; the boundary of Intention-Dynamics divides into two kinds, one an exit inside the object, the other an exit of whole-segment applicability, and the latter is not a negation of dynamics. Intention qualification is the common working domain of the other three kinds of object; its boundary is the common domain boundary of the three, and none of the three objects' own boundaries is its boundary. Within the currently sealed semantic domain, entry into and exit across these boundaries carry no H. The limit invariant of Intention is not behavior, manifestation or effect, but the structural core of each object.

### 1.3 Limit comparison protocol

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Π\_ILim := ( Σ\_I^cur, T, A, Ord\_A, E\_A, Fix\_A )

T: the target of the present review, T ∈ { Intent, IntentAct, IntentGateSwitch, IntentDyn }. Only one target is declared at a time.

A: the comparison axis. It may be some item of the target's structural core, a legal auxiliary quantity of the target, a peripheral result or strength, or a quantity of realization resources. Every limit conclusion must declare in advance which axis is compared; one may not move several axes at once and then attribute the result to one of them.

Ord\_A: the direction of degeneration of that axis, for example the current rate of some dimension decreasing to zero, the clarity of manifestation decreasing to zero, or the dynamics at some site retreating to rest. The direction is frozen before the results are seen.

E\_A: the declared endpoint or boundary, of which four kinds must be distinguished: endpoint compatible, endpoint only approachable, open soft range, and crossing the boundary of the object.

Fix\_A: the identities and protocols that must be fixed in comparison. The same kind of target; the same Σ\_I^cur; the same qualification-anchor rule and object-identity rule; action comparisons fix the declaration rule of the current action unit, switching comparisons fix σ, and dynamics comparisons fix the declared window and Γ\_IDyn; the resolution protocol is unchanged; the structure outside the declared axis is unchanged. When the comparison family consists of different candidates, the comparison-family protocol must be frozen in advance, and the anchor, unit, sequence or declared segment of each member is frozen before testing. One may not change anchors, change the candidate domain or re-cut declared segments after seeing the results; one may not splice across protocols or across working domains into one limit sequence; and one may not merge the endpoints of different objects into one boundary.

### 1.4 Limit direction and limit conditions

Limit direction: along Ord\_A, push the comparison axis toward its degenerate end.

Limit conditions: every member of the comparison family {χ\_α} satisfies Fix\_A, lies under the semantics of Σ\_I^cur, and consists of legal candidates, legal instances or legal representations.

1.5 Comparison is not dynamics, nor stability, reachability or source

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;{χ\_α} ≠ χ(t\_0) → χ(t\_1) → …　　LimitComparison ≠ IntentDyn

What is compared is a family of legal candidates, not how one and the same system changes from one state to another. Reducing the source-drive pulse step by step to zero can be a comparison axis, but it does not say that any real system actually underwent such a decrease; “gate-bearing approaching disappearance” can be a comparison axis, but it does not say that a switching occurred in reality; a qualification boundary is not an event of exit from qualification within dynamics.

This chapter does not study how long something persists, whether it recovers after a disturbance, first failure and return, hysteresis or stability width; these belong to The Stability Form of Intention. It does not study whether one can actually go from a given initial state to some endpoint; this belongs to The Reachability Form of Intention. It does not study from which segment of history the structures on the two sides of a boundary first formed; this belongs to The Source Form of Intention.

### 1.6 What “endpoint compatible” means

“Endpoint compatible” (EndpointCompatible) means: among the definitions of the items of the target's structural core, none excludes the endpoint, so a legal candidate that is at the endpoint and in which the structural core holds is, by the sufficient direction, certainly the target object. It does not assert that some specific physical realization already exists; the construction of realizations belongs to The Construction Form of the Intention Operating Substrate. Nor does it assert that the endpoint can be reached from any initial state; that is a question for The Reachability Form of Intention. This chapter does not use the term “endpoint reachable,” so as not to confuse it with path reachability in The Reachability Form of Intention.

“Endpoint only approachable” (ApproachableEndpoint) means: there exist legal candidates arbitrarily close to the endpoint in which the structural core holds, while the endpoint itself is incompatible with the structural core. This is an existential conclusion and requires real witnesses.

“Open soft range” (OpenSoftRange) means: some non-endpoint range of degeneration is compatible with the structural core and does not constitute a boundary; but it has not been proven that there exist candidates in which the structural core holds that approach the endpoint all the way.

## Section Two　Inevitability Argument

Why must the limit review fall on the structural core of each object, and not on behavior, manifestation, effect or strength?

First, the side of soft limits. The structural core of each object is the sufficient core of that object within its working domain. For any comparison family, so long as every member lies within the working domain and its structural core continues to hold, the object holds in every member, wherever the comparison axis goes. This side carries no H.

Second, the side of the exhaustion of hard bounds. In the current sealed state the necessary direction of each object carries no H, so within the working domain the holding of the object implies the holding of the structural core; any comparison that goes from inside the object to outside it must make at least one item of the structural core change from 1 to 0. A peripheral property can become a boundary of the object only by breaking some item of the structural core.

Third, therefore any comparison axis has only two possibilities: its degenerate end is compatible with the structural core, in which case it is a soft limit and the object continues to hold; or its degenerate end forces some item of the structural core to fail, in which case it crosses the hard bound, and the cause of the crossing is that structural item, not the axis itself.

Fourth, action, switching and dynamics have a third possibility as well: the comparison causes the working domain itself to be lost, that is, qualification exits, or, for dynamics, starting-point qualification does not hold or qualification does not persist through the declared segment. The object then does not become 0; rather, the question of the object is not raised on this instance.

This bifurcation is unavoidable within Σ\_I^cur, and carries no H for any of the four kinds of object.

## Section Three　Core Definitions

### 3.1 Soft limit

A property not belonging to the target's structural core is called a soft limit when its degenerate end, or every value approaching the degenerate end, is compatible with the structural core; soft limits divide into EndpointCompatible, ApproachableEndpoint and OpenSoftRange (1.6).

### 3.2 Hard bound and domain boundary

Hard bound (HardBoundary\_T): the structural boundary at which T exits from 1 to 0 while the working domain of T persists.

Domain boundary (DomainBoundary\_T): the boundary at which the working domain of T is lost. Beyond the domain boundary, the question of T is not raised on this instance, which is written OutDom; it is not T = 0.

The boundaries of the four kinds of object are called respectively: the qualification boundary QualificationBoundary\_I of Intention qualification; the action boundary ActionBoundary of Intention-Action; the switching boundary SwitchBoundary of Intention Desire-Gate switching; and the dynamics boundary DynBoundary of Intention-Dynamics, the latter dividing further into the internal dynamics boundary DynInternalBoundary and the dynamics applicability boundary DynApplicabilityBoundary. “Qualification boundary” is used only for Intention qualification; the boundaries of action, switching and dynamics are not called qualification boundaries. The group-level collective name is object boundary, ObjectBoundary.

3.3 Boundary value and boundary attainment

When the comparison axis has a frozen order, write the axis values in the comparison family at which the object holds as

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A^+ := { A(χ\_α) ∣ T(χ\_α) = 1 }

Taking the infimum or supremum b of A^+ along Ord\_A gives only the position of the boundary:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;BoundaryValue\_T(A) := b

It does not give the value of the object at b. Whether the boundary is actually attained is recorded separately:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;BoundaryAttained\_T(A; b) ∈ { 1, 0, ⊥ }

1: the comparison family has a legal candidate whose axis value is b, and its object value and failure structure have been determined; 0: it has been proven that the comparison family has no legal candidate whose axis value is b; ⊥: all other cases.

No topology is defined on the space of candidate systems, nor any boundary operator; the boundary value here is only an order value on the comparison axis, and a hard bound means the exit condition of the structural core, not a topological boundary.

3.4 Failure signature and classes of degeneration beyond the boundary

For a candidate crossing the hard bound, its failure signature FailSig\_T is the set of items of the structural core that do not hold. Several items may fail at once; a unique responsible item is not required. Classes of degeneration beyond the boundary are named by the items in the failure signature: Deg\_X means X ∈ FailSig\_T. Classes may coexist; divided by failure-signature set, the region beyond the boundary is partitioned without overlap or omission, with the Intention-Dynamics side subject to the restriction in 4.2.

Intention qualification: FailSig\_I ⊆ { P, S, G, T }, referring in order to P\_I, SourceDrive, GateCorrespondence, TuneInterface. When P ∈ FailSig\_I, a subtype is attached: Deg\_P,Dom, InIntentDomain does not hold; Deg\_P,Prob, Probability does not hold. The subtype is only a supplementary report, not a fifth qualification item.

Intention-Action: N\_IAct is an existential quantification over action witnesses, the four items holding progressively on the same witness. N\_IAct = 0 means that every possible witness fails somewhere; the failure of each witness is recorded at the first layer that does not hold; where the material of every layer can be found separately, yet there exists no legal common witness on which the four items hold on the same chain, the failure is recorded at Bind. FailSig\_IAct ⊆ { A1, A2, A3, A4, Bind } is the set of the failure positions of all witnesses. Bind is not a fifth structural duty beyond A1 to A4: the same chain is borne by one and the same witness w\_I, and Bind only indicates that the common witness cannot hold; it is one way in which the existential quantification N\_IAct fails, not an added conjunct.

Intention Desire-Gate switching: the four top-level items are parallel, and one witnessing group can fail on several items at once. FailSig\_IGS ⊆ { S1, S2, S3, S4, Bind } is the set of the failing items of all witnessing groups, Bind referring to the failure of the co-binding B\_same^IGS. In N\_IGS(σ) the co-binding is a guard on the witnessing group, not a fifth duty (The General Form of Intention Desire-Gate Switching).

Intention-Dynamics: the internal failure signature of dynamics is FailSig\_IDyn ⊆ { L1, L2, L3, L6, DynActual }, DynActual referring to all three sites being at rest; the dynamics applicability boundary is recorded separately as { StartDomainFail, QualExit }. L4 does not enter the internal failure signature. L5 currently has no established object-layer negative characterization; this chapter declares no degeneration class of type L5, nor does it claim that L5 constitutes no boundary at the object layer (Section Five, Theorem Six).

FailSig and the degeneration classes are all labels of limit classification, not new ontological predicates.

3.5 Ontic boundary and formal adjudicability boundary

The boundaries of this chapter all lie at the object layer: the object exits where its structural core fails. Whether The Determination Form of Intention can sign this exit on some instance is another question. Where the determiner can only return ⊥ because of a representation gap, an unclosed condition or insufficient material, this is recorded as the formal adjudicability boundary FormalDecidabilityBoundary\_T, and is given only as a supplementary report:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;FormalDecidabilityBoundary\_T ≠ OnticBoundary\_T

Likewise, Mix, InvOpen, ClassOpen and DomOpen of The Inversion Form of Intention are identifiability boundaries relative to the observation protocol, not boundaries of the object.

## Section Five　Main Conclusions

5.1 Theorem One　Theorem of Structural Persistence

Under Π\_ILim, if every member of the comparison family lies within the working domain of T and the structural core of T holds, then whichever legal degenerate end the comparison axis approaches, every member has T = 1. For Intention-Dynamics, the working domain means W\_IDyn, and the structural core includes L4.

Proof: the sufficient direction of each object (The Necessity Form of Intention, The Necessity Form of Intention-Action, The Necessity Form of Intention Desire-Gate Switching, The Necessity Form of Intention-Dynamics), applied member by member. □

This carries no H. It is only the limit-form reading of the sufficient direction and adds no ontology.

### 5.2 Theorem Two　Theorem of Soft Limits

Under Π\_ILim, the following endpoints are compatible with the corresponding objects (EndpointCompatible).

Intention qualification:

(1) The current rate of some formal dimension is zero. V\_I is taken from the qualification instance and does not gain or lose directions as current rate values change; a direction whose current rate is 0 still belongs to V\_I (The Mathematical Form of Intention, 7.4); zero rate of a single dimension is compatible with N\_P = 1 (Theorem One of The Limit Form of Probability). None of P\_I, SourceDrive, GateCorrespondence and TuneInterface requires the current rate of every dimension to be positive.

(2) There is currently no source-drive pulse. SourceDrive is the qualification of 𝒫\_I to stand in a source-drive driving relation and bear source-drive continuation, not the presence of a current pulse (Theorem 2 and 7.4 of The Mathematical Form of Intention).

(3) There is currently no manifestation and no tuning in progress. TuneInterface means that every Desire-Gate duty class has a legal post-gate tuning interface, not that tuning is currently in progress (Theorem 4 of The Mathematical Form of Intention); the current absence of manifestation does not cancel N\_I (ibid., 7.4).

(4) There is currently no complete action, no Desire-Gate switching, and no dynamics on the declared segment. The four items of N\_I are all qualification structures and contain no current event; action, switching and dynamics are all raised only after qualification holds.

(5) Zero anisotropy, full determinacy, an unreadable value. These are compatible with Probability (Theorem One of The Limit Form of Probability), enter through P\_I, and do not touch the other three items. Partial coverage is an open soft range, inheriting the conclusion and open question of that chapter.

Intention-Action (within Intent = 1, A1 to A4 holding on the same chain):

(6) After tuning, the effect is offset by other mechanisms, experience does not change, no manifest circuit takes part, and there is no manifestation. A4 is the actual entry of the same chain, after gate passage, into tuning command, not tuning success, experience change or manifestation (The General Form of Intention-Action; for the formal layer see Proposition 8 of The Mathematical Form of Intention-Action).

(7) Two successive actions take the same direction and the same Desire-Gate, with no Desire-Gate switching. Action does not require switching (ibid.).

(8) The current rate of some formal dimension is zero while another dimension forms a complete action chain; there are several complete witnesses within the same unit. Action requires neither conformance in every dimension nor a unique witness (Proposition 9 of The Mathematical Form of Intention-Action).

(9) The action is triggered by an external stimulus, while continuation is borne by Intention's own source-drive duty. A1 asks about the bearer, not about what induces it (Proposition 4 of The Mathematical Form of Intention-Action).

Intention Desire-Gate switching (within Intent = 1, S1 to S4 and the co-binding holding on fixed σ):

(10) The later state has no A4 and no complete Intention-Action. S2 invokes only A1, A2, A3 of action (the interface of The Necessity Form of Intention Desire-Gate Switching).

(11) After switching there is no manifestation and no change of behavior or experience. Neither the four items nor the co-binding contain these results.

(12) The old gate is returned to on another comparison sequence. The conclusion on this σ does not concern other sequences.

Intention-Dynamics (within W\_IDyn, L1 to L6 holding):

(13) Only one site is moving. Dynamic actuality is the disjunction of the three sites (The General Form of Intention-Dynamics).

(14) The states at the two ends are identical, though change occurred in between. The object-layer definitions of the three sites all read real cross-time change, not the difference of end states (boundary cases of The Necessity Form of Intention-Dynamics).

(15) Complete action stops while the source drive continues; there is no Desire-Gate switching; there is no manifestation, tuning success, behavior or effect. None of these is a condition for obtaining dynamics (Section One of The Necessity Form of Intention-Dynamics).

(16) The change is induced by an external stimulus or by explicit reentry but falls on Intention's own source drive, bearing Laws or organization; the suppression by the Sleep-Domain grows heavier. What induces it is not the bearer of the motion; suppression acts on the Intention-Domain, and the Intention-Domain changes on that account, which is precisely Intention moving (boundary cases of The Necessity Form of Intention-Dynamics).

(17) Migration of the dynamics position: the members of the comparison family differ in position, for example from only the source drive moving to only the carrier moving, while every member has at least one site moving. By (13), every member has dynamic actuality.

Proof: each item is an item-by-item check of the definitions of the structural core, none of which excludes the listed endpoint; a candidate at that endpoint in which the structural core holds is, by Theorem One, the target object. □

So none of the above properties is a hard bound of the current objects. There is currently no proven ApproachableEndpoint; apart from the partial coverage inherited in (5), this chapter declares no new open soft range.

This carries no H.

5.3 Theorem Three　Theorem of the Hard Bound

Within Σ\_I^cur:

(1) Intention qualification: Intent ⇔ N\_I. The qualification boundary is characterized exactly by the four items P\_I, SourceDrive, GateCorrespondence, TuneInterface; entry and exit both carry no H.

(2) Intention-Action: within Intent = 1, IntentAct ⇔ N\_IAct. The action boundary is characterized exactly by the common-witness structure of A1 to A4: degeneration appears as the failure of some layer, or as the inability of a common witness to hold on the same chain; carrying no H.

(3) Intention Desire-Gate switching: within Intent = 1, for fixed σ, IntentGateSwitch\_F(σ) ⇔ N\_IGS(σ). The switching boundary is characterized exactly by S1 to S4 and the co-binding, carrying no H.

(4) Intention-Dynamics: within W\_IDyn and with L4 = 1, IntentDyn ⇔ N\_IDyn^γ, carrying no H. Within the range for which an object-layer negative characterization has currently been established, the internal hard bound of dynamics is characterized by the failure of L1, L2, L3, L6 and of dynamic actuality; the boundary of dynamic actuality is all three sites being at rest at once, not any one site being at rest alone. L5 currently has no legal object-layer negative-verdict path: this chapter does not establish a hard bound of dynamics from L5 being formally undecided or from evidence splicing, nor does it declare a degeneration class of type L5; whether an independent object-layer L5 failure boundary exists has not been proven upstream and remains open here.

Proof: the necessary and sufficient directions of each object have been sealed, with Status Established, and none of them carries H; taken together, the holding region coincides with the region in which the structural core holds (4.1), so entry into and exit from the object are both characterized by the structural core. For the treatment of L5 in (4), see Theorem Six. □

When the boundary is further localized to structural items of upstream group forms, the upstream conditions are inherited: when the qualification boundary falls on the side of Probability and is to be further localized to D, B, R of probability qualification, the exit direction carries H2 ∧ H3^red (Theorem Two of The Limit Form of Probability); when the dynamics boundary falls on the probability site and is to be further localized to the items of the necessary core of Probability-Dynamics, the exit direction carries the H2 ∧ H3^red of the necessary direction of The General Form of Probability-Dynamics. These conditions propagate only with further localization and do not enter Theorem Three itself: Theorem Three takes object-layer facts such as Probability and IntentProbDyn as its boundary items.

The ontic boundary does not change with the current capacity for determination. For example, when SourceDrive does not hold at the object layer, taking the contrapositive of The General Form of Intention gives Intent = 0; that The Determination Form of Intention cannot currently sign Det\_I = 0 from a formal zero of SourceDrive shows only that this boundary is non-adjudicable in the current formal representation, not that it is not a qualification boundary.

5.4 Theorem Four　Theorem of Degeneration beyond the Boundary

Within Σ\_I^cur, for Intention qualification, Intention-Action and Intention Desire-Gate switching, every candidate crossing the hard bound fails to be the corresponding object, and its failure signature determines that it belongs to one or several of the following degeneration classes. For Intention-Dynamics, this theorem covers only the internal hard bound for which an object-layer negative characterization has currently been established, that is, crossings obtained, within W\_IDyn and with L4 = 1 and L5 = 1, through the failure of L1, L2, L3, L6 or of dynamic actuality.

Intention qualification:

Deg\_P,Dom　Location degeneration: 𝒫\_I is not in the Intention-Domain (for example, it lies behind the Desire-Gate); it can be a formal probability organization yet is not an organization of Intention.  
Deg\_P,Prob　Probability degeneration: the declared organization is not a formal probability organization; under H2 ∧ H3^red, by The Limit Form of Probability it degenerates further into collapse of the dimensional base, dissolution of common binding or failure of the bearing of relative generation.  
Deg\_S　Source-drive degeneration: the organization does not stand in a source-drive driving relation, or does not bear source-drive continuation, for example a probability organization pushed from outside. It can still be a probability organization, yet is not Intention.  
Deg\_G　Correspondence degeneration: the bijection between formal dimensions and Desire-Gate duty classes is lost, for example two directions merge into one Desire-Gate duty class, one formal dimension corresponds to two Desire-Gate duty classes, there exists a formal Desire-Gate duty class with no corresponding direction, or the correspondence is not legal pass-through.  
Deg\_T　Interface degeneration: behind some Desire-Gate duty class there is no legal tuning interface, only effect output or a general change of state.

Intention-Action (qualification persisting):

Deg\_A1　No current source-drive occurrence: qualification is complete but at rest, or the witnessed event is a re-walk or a re-entry, or continuation is borne entirely from outside.  
Deg\_A2　No current conformance of the probability duty: the probability organization does not currently occur, or it occurs but does not bear the formation of relative generation among the directions, for example when continuation is decided only by one fixed member Law.  
Deg\_A3　The direction does not enter the corresponding Desire-Gate: it enters another Desire-Gate, or makes no gate passage.  
Deg\_A4　After gate passage there is no entry into tuning command, only entry into the effect side.  
Deg\_Bind　The four layers are not on one and the same action chain: a splice from different events, directions, Desire-Gates or tuning chains.

Intention Desire-Gate switching (qualification persisting, σ fixed):

Deg\_S1　The prior state has no real legal gate passage.  
Deg\_S2　The gate passage of the later state is not driven by Intention: there is no current bearing chain from formal source drive to 𝒫\_I, for example an externally forced gate swap; or the first three layers are not on the same chain, or the later gate does not belong to the later state of σ.  
Deg\_S3　The old gate still bears in the later state, for example same-gate continuation, or joint use with a newly added gate.  
Deg\_S4　The exit of the old gate and the acquisition of the new gate do not belong to one and the same real gate-bearing event, for example a coincidental exit, or another mechanism bearing the exit of the old gate.

## Section Eight　Interpretation (Not Part of the Formal Structure)

What follows only explains the foregoing conclusions; it takes no part in the proofs and bears no load.

One usually supposes that when Intention is present, something must be happening: a thought surfacing, an action, something felt. By the conclusions here, all of these can retreat to zero while Intention remains: no pulse, no manifestation, no action, no switching, even nothing moving at all on this segment, and Intention qualification still holds. An action, too, can have no visible consequence whatever; a switching can take place without a single complete action being accomplished; a segment of dynamics can be the same at its beginning and its end, can have only one site moving, and can also shift from this site to that one.

There are only four places where Intention disappears: the organization is not in the Intention-Domain or is not a formal probability organization; it no longer bears the source drive; the correspondence between directions and Desire-Gates has come apart; behind a Desire-Gate there is no interface for tuning. Action, switching and dynamics each have their own places of disappearance, but they all lie within Intention; once Intention is gone, they do not become “nothing,” but are no longer asked about.

So the limit of Intention lies not in its effects, but in its structure.

### 9.4 Breach method

The breach of a limit form is: the limit conditions already hold, yet the asserted limit property does not appear.

K-ILim1　Wrong boundary at a soft end: claiming that some endpoint is compatible with the structural core, when it can be proven that some item of the structural core excludes that endpoint.  
K-ILim2　Wrong boundary at the periphery: writing as a hard bound of the object an axis that does not belong to the structural core, is not a working-domain condition, and has no established bridge implying the failure of a structural item.  
K-ILim3　Counterexample inside the hard bound: within the working domain there exists a case in which the object holds while the structural core does not.  
K-ILim4　Counterexample outside the hard bound: within the working domain there exists a case in which the structural core holds while the object does not; this at the same time directly breaches the corresponding Necessity Form.  
K-ILim5　Misclassification of degeneration: claiming that a candidate belongs to some degeneration class when that structural item in fact holds; or overlap or omission appearing in the partition by failure signature.  
K-ILim6　Contamination of the comparison protocol: the limit family obtains its limit result only by changing anchors, changing units, changing σ, re-cutting declared segments, changing the candidate domain, altering the resolution protocol or splicing across protocols.  
K-ILim7　Smuggling in dynamics: writing the comparison family as the real temporal evolution of one and the same system, and on that basis asserting causes of formation, persistence or recovery.  
K-ILim8　Writing an approachable endpoint as a compatible endpoint; or writing a compatible endpoint as path-reachable.  
K-ILim9　Reading the object value or failure signature at an endpoint directly from the boundary value.  
K-ILim10　Several axes change, and responsibility for the exit is attributed to one of them.  
K-ILim11　Taking the ⊥ of determination, a formal adjudicability boundary or the unidentifiability of inversion as a boundary of the object.  
K-ILim12　Writing an internal boundary of action, switching or dynamics as a qualification boundary.  
K-ILim13　After qualification exits, writing action, switching or dynamics as 0 rather than OutDom.  
K-ILim14　L4 = 0 is written as IntentDyn = 0; failure of starting-point qualification is written as IntentDyn = 0.  
K-ILim15　Evidence splicing or the undecidedness of L5 is promoted to an object boundary of dynamics; or, conversely, the current absence of a negative characterization of L5 is written as L5 constituting no boundary at the object layer.  
K-ILim16　Single-site loss, site migration or identical ends are written as an exit of dynamics.  
K-ILim17　Re-formation of qualification is taken as the originally declared segment returning inside the boundary.  
K-ILim18　The limit form studies persistence, recovery, stability time, path reachability or first formation.  
K-ILim19　Adding an H, a qualification item or a component of an object in order to obtain a complete boundary.  
K-ILim20　Overstepping into absolute uniqueness: writing the exact boundaries within Σ\_I^cur as ontic boundaries eternally unique for future theories.
