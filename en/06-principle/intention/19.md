# The Stability Form of Intention

Intention qualification already holds. Under permitted variation and along legal successor trajectories, does it persist? When does it first truly fail? Once it has failed, can it return? And when it returns, what is it that persists?

The conclusion is this: the stability of Intention is not that some group of realizations stays unchanged throughout, nor that action, pulses or Desire-Gate switching keep occurring. Before a stability claim is judged, it must first be stated which identity is to persist: the originally declared segment of qualification; qualification on the same specified group of bearers; or that, among the candidate bearers frozen in advance, some one is qualified throughout, or at every moment some one is qualified. Once the original segment exits it cannot be re-entered; if qualification holds again after exit, it must be re-declared as a new segment. A return on the same bearer must come after a first failure actually attained, must come back to qualification within the declared deadline and last to the end of the observation window, and must pass the verification length. “At every moment some bearer is qualified” does not yield “some bearer is qualified at every moment.” The internal witnesses can all be replaced while the bearer and the qualification remain unchanged; when the components are each stable, their conjunction need not be stable; the three levels of a single trajectory, a declared trajectory family and all legal trajectories differ in strength and may not pass as one another. This chapter cannot prove whether the returned organization is ontologically the same as the original organization; it gives only a reviewable layered relation.

## Section One　Object Declaration

### 1.1 Form-kind and single task

Stability Form; auxiliary form; standalone text. Under the Stability Form specification of the Canon Chapter, Part Four, Chapter Fifteen: the object is a qualification that already holds; first the identity level, the domain of permitted variation, the observation protocol and the trajectories are frozen, and only then are persistence, actual failure and return judged; the persistence of a fixed object is kept apart from persistence at the existence level; return comes after the first actual failure; boundaries must be actually attained; and the quantifiers of single trajectory, trajectory family and all legal trajectories may not be swapped.

The entry conditions are met item by item as that chapter requires: the qualification being stabilized has a closed definition and holds at the entry (1.2, 1.3); the identity level of the object of stability has been declared (1.6); the domain of permitted variation is declared in advance (1.3, 1.4), and qualification after variation is recomputed at every evaluation point by the upstream four-item same-anchor core, without carrying over readings from before the variation (1.2, 3.2); the legal successor dynamics used for dynamic return are the field dynamics and operating-substrate dynamics of The Construction Form of the Intention Operating Substrate, while the time domain and the observation protocol are declared in 1.3 and 3.1 of this chapter; when a guarantee over all legal trajectories is claimed, the full set of legal trajectories is delimited as in 5.11, and non-adjudicable trajectories are not deleted.

Single task: with what identity, and at what quantifier level, Intention qualification that already holds persists, actually fails and returns, under a domain of permitted variation frozen in advance and along legal successor trajectories. It divides into six questions:

(1) whether the originally declared segment can persist within the observation window;  
(2) whether qualification on the same specified group of bearers, after failure, can return within the declared deadline and pass verification;  
(3) whether, among the candidate bearers frozen in advance, some one persists throughout the whole window (∃e∀t);  
(4) whether, among the candidate bearers, at every moment some one is qualified (∀t∃e), and whether, after a break-off, this can return;  
(5) whether the succession of internal witnesses changes the verdicts of the levels above;  
(6) what strength the claims above attain at each of the three levels of single trajectory, declared trajectory family and all legal trajectories.

Not studied here: whether Intention qualification holds (The General Form of Intention, The Necessity Form of Intention, The Determination Form of Intention); where the hard boundary of qualification lies (The Limit Form of Intention); whether it can be reached from a given initial state (The Reachability Form of Intention); by what history the first holding of qualification is formed, including the formation of a re-declared new segment (The Source Form of Intention); how an existing structure runs in the field (The Construction Form of the Intention Operating Substrate); how Intention is generated from the base Sedimentation-Field (Chapter 21); how Intention acts at this moment, how it switches Desire-Gates, and whether it has dynamics (the action, Desire-Gate switching and dynamics chapters). No fifth qualification is added, no ontological H is added, and no upstream definition is rewritten.

Six closures are kept apart here:

~~~text
    qualification closure   the four-item structure holds at the evaluation point                     signs Intention qualification at that time
    running closure         an existing structure actually operates in the field                        signs the running of an existing Intention
    path closure            a legal path reaches it from a given initial state                          signs reachability under a specified task
    source closure          the first holding is borne by a certified actual history                    signs the source under a given protocol
    persistence closure     1 at every point on the frozen identity level                               signs persistence on that level, in that window, on that trajectory
    return closure          after the first actual failure, back to 1 within the deadline and lasting to the end of the window, passing the verification length   signs return on that level, under that protocol
~~~

An earlier closure does not yield a later one; persistence and return hold only for the declared identity level, observation protocol and trajectories.

### 1.2 Upstream frozen structure

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;N\_I := P\_I ∧ SD ∧ GC ∧ TI

SD, GC and TI denote SourceDrive(𝒫\_I), GateCorrespondence(𝒫\_I; 𝒢\_I) and TuneInterface(𝒢\_I) respectively; P\_I := InIntentDomain(𝒫\_I) ∧ Probability(𝒫\_I; C\_I, 𝔯\_I). The four items fall on the same Intention organization and the same qualification anchor. The General Form of Intention: Intent ⇒ N\_I; The Necessity Form of Intention: N\_I ⇒ Intent; both directions are Established, carrying no H.

Formal layer: The Mathematical Form of Intention gives IntentCoreCert^M and the formal readings of each item. A formal reading of 1 is read back to the object layer with H\_LawRead inherited according to the Law-bearing witness used; a formal reading of 0 is read back to the object layer according to The Determination Form of Intention: a negative reading on the probability side carries H2 ∧ H3^red together with H^-\_M or H\_ρRep^- of the corresponding failure path, and the formal zero of an atom newly added by this group form is not read back as a structural zero (IntentAtomRepOpen). These are upstream read-back conditions, not H newly added here.

Qualification is not action. Qualification reads a duty structure that has already sedimented and holds, not whether a pulse is currently occurring, whether a source-drive session is open, or whether some edge on the intention side can currently be traversed. The qualification reader of The Construction Form of the Intention Operating Substrate is designed accordingly: the current cessation of issuing and the availability settings of the five intention-side edges belong to occurrence and action, and do not erase a structural qualification that already holds. Closing availability at the running layer can cut off the carrier of the probability organization and so make qualification exit (V4 of that chapter); that is a change of qualification, not a change of occurrence. So every qualification reading in this chapter invokes the upstream four-item same-anchor core rather than substituting the number of actions, the pulse frequency or whether switching occurs (Section Seven, 7.2).

### 1.3 Stability instance

A stability inquiry takes one frozen stability instance as its object:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Σ\_I^st = (χ̄\_I, X\_st, 𝒞\_I, Elig\_I, Δ\_I, Dyn, Judge, H)

χ̄\_I: the cross-time frozen declaration of the originally declared segment, as in 1.3 of The Source Form of Intention: χ̄\_I = (F, 𝒫\_I, C\_I, 𝔯\_I, V\_I, Anc\_I, Duty\_I, Rec\_I), where Rec\_I is the declaration record (registration point and declaration sequence number).

X\_st: the entry state. For the original segment, the entry is the registration point; for a new segment re-declared after exit, the registration point precedes the re-establishment (1.6), and the entry is taken as the first evaluation point after registration that reads 1. The verdict used at the entry must give qualification 1. When entry qualification is not 1, the stability question does not arise: an entry of 0 is recorded as OutDom, an entry of ⊥ as ⊥.

𝒞\_I: the candidate bearer domain frozen in advance. Each member e is a host (a field together with the operating substrate on it) along with its bearer designation Des\_e (which edge bears the source drive, which dimension corresponds to which gate duty, which gate node bears the gate duty, which post-gate edge bears tuning, and which probability organization is declared to lie in the Intention-Domain; as Des\_I in 1.3 of The Source Form of Intention). The bearer of the original segment is written e\_0 ∈ 𝒞\_I. 𝒞\_I carries in addition a declaration DomClosed\_I ∈ {1, 0}: when it is 1, the candidate domain is closed as the universe of discourse for negative verdicts; when it is 0, the existence level can only give positive verdicts.

Elig\_I: the same-task candidate mark, computed in advance from the frozen declaration of each member and not altered after failure (1.5).

Δ\_I: the permitted-variation class. It permits only the operations in the legal control set of the operating substrate and the declared disturbance operations; it does not permit writing into qualification readings or the labels derived from them, nor altering χ̄\_I, Des\_e, 𝒞\_I, Elig\_I or H.

Dyn: the field dynamics and operating-substrate dynamics of The Construction Form of the Intention Operating Substrate.

Judge: the qualification verdict. At the formal layer it is IntentCoreCert^M, written Q^M; at the object layer it is Det\_I of The Determination Form of Intention, written Q; when the structural fact at the object layer serves as the verdict, it is Intent\_t itself (N\_I ⇔ Intent, both directions Established).

H = (Pts, c, T\_obs, T\_rec, h): the observation protocol. Pts is the evaluation-point rule; c is the clock, mapping each evaluation point to a moment; T\_obs is the observation window; T\_rec is the recovery deadline; h is the verification length. All five are frozen in advance.

### 1.4 Freezing discipline

χ̄\_I, every Des\_e, 𝒞\_I (including DomClosed\_I), Elig\_I, Δ\_I and H are all frozen before the stability inquiry begins and may not be modified after failure. Whatever holds after any of them has been altered does not belong to the persistence or return of the original stability instance; it is another stability instance. In particular, it is not permitted, after seeing a break-off, to add a newly appearing bearer to 𝒞\_I and then claim “there was never a break-off”; it is not permitted, after seeing a failure, to relax T\_rec or shorten h and then claim “it has returned”; and it is not permitted to re-record a bearer of another task as one of the same task.

If the bearer designation is rewritten within the window, then, by analogy with 3.6 of The Source Form of Intention, this is a designation change: it is neither persistence nor return within the field; the bearer has changed its declared identity, the readings thereby leave the stability inquiry of that bearer, and under 1.7 they are recorded as OutDom.

### 1.5 Same-task candidate mark

Elig\_I(e) ∈ {1, 0}: it is 1 when the duty identities in the frozen declaration of e (the identities of the source-drive, Desire-Gate and tuning duties), the pairing of dimensions with gate duties in Des\_e, and the formal dimensions and direction domain are identical, item by item, to the frozen declaration of the original segment and to Des\_{e\_0}; otherwise it is 0. It is computed for every member of 𝒞\_I before the inquiry begins and does not change thereafter.

Elig\_I is a comparison protocol, not a fifth qualification, and not an identity certification. It says only that “by comparison of the frozen duties, e is declared a candidate for bearing the same task”; it does not say that e and e\_0 are the same organization, nor that e takes over anything from e\_0, nor that there is any causal or registered handover between them. So on top of Elig\_I only readings at the existence level can be defined (Section Four, 4.6); any claim of “taking over” requires a separate handover certificate, which this chapter has not constructed (O-ST3).

### 1.6 Four identity levels

The stability of Intention must first state which identity persists. The four levels are as follows (the implications among the last three are given in Theorem Three):

Witness level: the internal witnesses on which the bearing structure rests at the time, for example the repetition records on a bearing edge, or certain particular events. Witnesses can be replaced one by one.

Original-segment level: the originally declared segment S\_0 = (χ̄\_I, e\_0), from the entry (for S\_0, the registration point) up to the point at which qualification is first determined to be 0. When qualification goes 1 → 0, the original segment crosses the qualification boundary (QualExit); by the proposition of 5.11 of The Limit Form of Intention, “re-formation is not re-entry into the original segment” (applied to qualification segments through 1.5 of The Source Form of Intention), there is no re-entry into the original segment; qualification that holds again thereafter belongs to a re-declared new segment S′, whose declaration record Rec′ differs from Rec\_I, and whose registration point lies after the exit of the original segment and before the re-establishment (1.5 of The Source Form of Intention).

Fixed-bearer level: one and the same e, that is, the same frozen designation Des\_e on the same host. It can cross segments: after the original segment has exited, the re-establishment of qualification on the same specified group of bearers is a question of return at this level; whether it returns is determined by the seven items of 4.2. The “sameness” here is sameness of declaration record: the host and Des\_e have not been rewritten within the window. It is not a proof that the returned organization is ontologically the same as the original organization (Section Six).

Existence level: the bearers in 𝒞\_I with Elig\_I = 1. There are two quantifier forms: some one bearer is qualified throughout the whole window (∃e∀t), or at every moment some bearer is qualified (∀t∃e).

Succession at the witness level is not bearer succession; the return of a fixed bearer is not re-entry into the original segment; the absence of a break-off at the existence level is not the continuation of the same bearer.

1.7 Three values, two layers and the working domain

The qualification reading at each evaluation point takes 1, 0 or ⊥. Failure admits only a determinate 0; ⊥ counts neither as failure nor as persistence.

The formal layer and the object layer are registered separately. A 0 at the formal layer does not automatically become a 0 at the object layer: a negative reading at the object layer must be obtained under the conditions of 1.2, and the formal zero of an atom newly added by this group form is recorded as ⊥ (IntentAtomRepOpen); for a 1 at the formal layer to be read back to the object layer, H\_LawRead of the witnesses used must already be closed, otherwise it is recorded as ⊥ (LawReadOpen).

OutDom differs from both 0 and ⊥. The following cases are recorded as OutDom, not as loss of stability: entry qualification is 0; a frozen item of the stability instance has been altered; the program fails numerically in the middle of an operation and there is no successor (as in The Reachability Form of Intention: every history consists of completed operations, and a failure in the middle of an operation produces no successor).

## Section Two　Exclusion of the Presumed Stability Claim

The following are claims easily taken for stability, failure or return; all of them are invalid inferences. Some follow directly from the definitions, others are established by theorems or reference instances; the grounds are given in the places noted.

W-S1　Occurrence passing as qualification: cessation of issuing of the source drive, closing of availability on the intention side, or a stretch of time with no pulse ⇏ exit from qualification (1.2; Section Seven, 7.2; for no pulse, see rounds 1 to 10 of γ\_idle in 7.4). Closing the availability of the probability carrier at the running layer can make qualification exit (1.2), and does not fall under this item.  
W-S2　Running passing as persistence: organizational events keep occurring ⇏ qualification persists (Section Seven, 7.4).  
W-S3　Endpoint sampling passing as persistence: Q(t\_0) = Q(t\_end) = 1 ⇏ Keep = 1 (Theorem Seven; Section Seven, 7.5).  
W-S4　Coarse-grained sampling passing as pointwise persistence: at the end of every round some bearer is qualified ⇏ at every evaluation point some bearer is qualified (Theorem Seven; Section Seven, 7.7).  
W-S5　Re-entry into the original segment: 1 → 0 → 1 ⇏ the original segment persists, and also ⇏ the original segment returns (4.4, Theorem Eight).  
W-S6　Momentary re-establishment passing as return: a reading of 1 at some point after failure ⇏ Recover = 1 (Theorem Five; Section Seven, 7.5).  
W-S7　Deadline reset: there is a later failure, and the deadline is counted from that later failure ⇏ a legal determination of return (Theorem Five; Section Seven, 7.8).  
W-S8　The infimum passing as the first actual failure: inf Fail ⇏ actual failure point (3.3).  
W-S9　No break-off passing as the same bearer continuing: ∀t∃e ⇏ ∃e∀t (Theorem Four; Section Seven, 7.7).  
W-S10　Cross-task splicing: bearers of different tasks being qualified in turn ⇏ no break-off for this task (Theorem Nine; Section Seven, 7.7).  
W-S11　Witness succession passing as bearer succession, or an unchanged bearer passing as unchanged witnesses (Theorem Ten; Section Seven, 7.3).  
W-S12　Components each stable passing as conjunctive stability: Stable(SD) ∧ Stable(GC) ⇏ Stable(Q) (Theorem Six; Section Seven, 7.8).  
W-S13　A single trajectory or trajectory family passing as a guarantee: one or several trajectories are stable ⇏ all legal trajectories are stable (Theorem Eleven; Section Seven, 7.9).  
W-S14　A qualification boundary passing as a stability boundary (Theorem Twelve; Section Seven, 7.10).  
W-S15　Formal zero passing as object zero: Q^M = 0 ⇏ Q = 0 (1.7; Section Seven, 7.11).  
W-S16　Record sameness passing as ontological sameness: the same host, the same group of designations, the same running identity, the same task name ⇏ the returned organization is the same as the original organization (Section Six).  
W-S17　Numerical terminus passing as failure: the program fails numerically in the middle of an operation ⇏ Q = 0 (1.7).  
W-S18　Still calling it the original instance after frozen items have been altered after the fact (1.4).  
W-S19　High-probability invariance passing as stability: persisting on most trajectories or with high probability ⇏ stable; stability is judged trajectory by trajectory at the declared quantifier level (Theorem Eleven).  
W-S20　Removal of the original witnesses passing as loss of stability: all witnesses at the entry have been removed ⇏ qualification has lost stability; the replacement witnesses at the same level must be checked (Theorem Ten; Section Seven, 7.3).  
W-S21　An uncovered window passing as persistence: the trajectory does not cover the observation window, and the missing part makes “every evaluation point is 1” vacuously true ⇏ Keep = 1 (Theorem Seven).  
W-S22　Local results passing as stability: local results in limit boundaries, reachable paths or necessary consequences ⇏ the conclusions of the Stability Form (Section Eleven).

## Section Three　Evaluation Points, Instantaneous Readings and First Actual Failure

### 3.1 Evaluation points and clock

Pts: the frozen rule for evaluation points. The reference instance takes one point at the entry and one point after each permitted operation is completed; when several hosts are examined together, the hosts execute within the same round in the declared order, and all hosts are read after every operation. c(t): the round in which evaluation point t lies, the entry being round 0. Deadlines and verification lengths are both counted in rounds.

In the reference operating substrate the system state changes only within operations; reading after each operation covers every state that exists between two operations. Intermediate states inside an operation are not system states at the granularity of this protocol. A claim about continuous time requires a separate in-segment event record and coverage certificate; this chapter makes no claim about continuous time (O-ST5).

### 3.2 Instantaneous readings at each level

Fixed-bearer level: Q\_e(t) is the qualification reading of host e under Des\_e at evaluation point t (at the formal layer Q\_e^M(t), at the object layer Q\_e(t)).

Original-segment level: Q\_{S\_0}(t) := Q\_{e\_0}(t), defined only at points where the original segment persists, that is, from the entry (1.3) up to the point at which qualification is first determined to be 0.

Existence level:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Q\_∃(t) := ∨₃ { Q\_e(t) : e ∈ 𝒞\_I, Elig\_I(e) = 1 }

Three-valued disjunction: 1 if at least one is 1; 0 if all are 0 and DomClosed\_I = 1; ⊥ otherwise. When DomClosed\_I = 0, “all bearers retrieved are 0” does not give 0; it gives only ⊥.

Witness level: W\_e(t) is the set of internal witnesses on which the bearing structures of e rest at t. The reference instance takes the repetition records on each specified bearing edge; the binding records of the two dimensions are counted separately and are not included in W\_e.

3.3 Failure-time set and first actual failure

For a reading sequence Q(·):

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Fail(Q) := { t ∈ Pts ∩ T\_obs : Q(t) = 0 }

Only a determinate 0 is admitted.

FirstFail(Q) = b: b ∈ Fail(Q), and Q(t) = 1 for every t \< b. If there is a ⊥ before the first determinate 0, FirstFail = ⊥: an earlier failure cannot be excluded. If Fail(Q) is non-empty but its infimum does not belong to Fail(Q) (for example, in continuous time Fail = (3, 4\], and the reading at 3 is not 0), the infimum is not actually attained and is not an actual failure point; FirstFail = ⊥. Here ⊥ means that the first actual failure has not been attained and return cannot be signed, even when all readings are determinate; Recover is recorded as ⊥ under 4.2, or as 0 when the reading at the end of the window is 0. If Fail(Q) is empty and every reading is 1, FirstFail = none is recorded; if Fail(Q) is empty and there is a ⊥, FirstFail = ⊥.

On a finite discrete set of evaluation points, the earliest determinate 0 is always actually attained; the difficulty of “first failure” lies only in two places: a ⊥ preceding it, and the infimum in continuous time.

## Section Four　Persistence, Return and Stability

### 4.1 Persistence

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Keep(Q) := ∧₃ { Q(t) : t ∈ Pts ∩ T\_obs }

Three-valued conjunction: 0 if any point is 0; 1 if all are 1; ⊥ otherwise. Persistence is required point by point; it is not enough to check round by round or to look only at the two ends.

### 4.2 Return

For Recover(Q) to take 1, all of the following must hold:

(1) the entry is 1;  
(2) the first actual failure is attained: FirstFail(Q) = b, where b is an evaluation point;  
(3) there is a point of return t\_r \> b such that every point from t\_r up to the last point of the observation window is 1;  
(4) the deadline is counted from b: c(t\_r) − c(b) ≤ T\_rec, and it is not reset by a renewed failure after b;  
(5) t\_r is the earliest point satisfying (3);  
(6) verification length: c(t\_end) − c(t\_r) ≥ h;  
(7) b and t\_r are both attained under the same frozen instance and the same H.

When the entry is 1, Recover(Q) takes 0 if: FirstFail(Q) = none (1 at every point throughout, nothing to return from); or the last point of the window is 0 (no return lasting to the end of the window); or FirstFail(Q) = b is attained, and even with every ⊥ after b taken as 1, the earliest possible point of return already exceeds the deadline.

When the entry is not 1, the question of return does not arise (1.3). When the entry is 1, Recover(Q) takes ⊥ in the remaining cases, for example: FirstFail = ⊥ while the end of the window is not 0; the point of return lies within the deadline but the verification length cannot be observed within the window (when condition (6) cannot be satisfied within the window, verification is treated as incomplete and recorded as ⊥, not as 0); the end of the window is ⊥.

### 4.3 Stability

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Stable(Q) := Keep(Q) ∨₃ Recover(Q)

When the entry is not 1, the question of stability does not arise (1.3). Stable = 1 must be supported by determinate 1s: 1 at every point throughout, or an actual first failure together with the completion of all conditions of return; Stable = 0 must be supported by a determinate failure together with a determinate non-satisfaction of the deadline condition or the end-of-window condition; all else is ⊥.

### 4.4 Stability of the original segment

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Stable\_{S\_0} := Keep(Q\_{S\_0})

The original segment has no return term. The reason: the original segment crosses the qualification boundary at the point first determined to be 0 and, by The Limit Form of Intention, does not re-enter; in 1 → 0 → 1 the later 1 belongs to a re-declared new segment S′. So the original-segment level has persistence only: when Keep(Q\_{S\_0}) = 1 the original segment persists within the window; when there is a determinate 0, the original segment exits at that point and Stable\_{S\_0} = 0, whether or not qualification holds again afterwards.

The first holding of qualification of the new segment S′ can, on the set of evaluation points of S′, be a first qualification event in the sense of The Source Form of Intention (Genesis is determined under 1.5 of that chapter); by what history it is formed is a question of source, handed to that chapter. S′ sets up a stability instance of its own: its entry is the first point at which S′ reads 1 (1.3), and from there on it has its own question of persistence at the original-segment level.

### 4.5 Stability at the fixed-bearer level

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Keep\_fix(e) := Keep(Q\_e),  Recover\_fix(e) := Recover(Q\_e),  Stable\_fix(e) := Stable(Q\_e)

The fixed-bearer level admits cross-segment return: on the same host and the same group of frozen designations, after the first failure of qualification, qualification comes back to 1 within the deadline and stays there. It says “on this specified group of bearers, qualification has come back”; it does not say “the original segment has come back,” nor “what has come back is the same organization.”

### 4.6 Stability at the existence level

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Keep\_∃∀ := ∨₃ { Keep(Q\_e) : e ∈ 𝒞\_I, Elig\_I(e) = 1 } (three-valued disjunction as in 3.2)  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Keep\_∀∃ := Keep(Q\_∃),  Recover\_∀∃ := Recover(Q\_∃),  Stable\_∀∃ := Stable(Q\_∃)

A 0 of Keep\_∃∀ and a 0 of Q\_∃ both require DomClosed\_I = 1. Recover\_∀∃ = 1 says only this: after a break-off in “some one of the frozen candidates is qualified,” this comes back to 1 within the deadline and stays there. It does not say that there is a takeover between the bearers before and after the break-off, nor that any bearer has returned.

### 4.7 Witness level

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;WitnessSame\_e(t\_0, t) := \[W\_e(t) ∩ W\_e(t\_0) ≠ ∅\]

It is used only to review the succession of witnesses; it does not enter the qualification reading, nor is it a stability quantity of a level of its own: qualification reads whether the structure holds, not which batch of witnesses the structure holds by. When the witnesses are all replaced while Q\_e is 1 at every point, Keep\_fix(e) = 1.

## Section Five　Theorems

5.1 Theorem One (Instantaneous Inter-Level Implication)

At points where the original segment persists, Q\_{S\_0}(t) = Q\_{e\_0}(t); for any e ∈ 𝒞\_I, if Elig\_I(e) = 1, then Q\_e(t) = 1 ⇒ Q\_∃(t) = 1.

Proof. The first formula follows from the definition in 3.2. The second: when one term of a three-valued disjunction is 1, the disjunction takes 1. □

Status = Established. The converse does not hold: Q\_∃(t) = 1 while Q\_{e\_0}(t) = 0, as at every point after the failure of e\_1 in the relay instance of Section Seven, 7.7.

5.2 Theorem Two (Dynamic Stability Monotonicity Theorem)

Let two reading sequences Q\_1 and Q\_2 lie on the same trajectory, under the same H and in the common adjudicable domain (at every point within the observation window both take 1 or 0), let both have entry 1, and let Q\_1(t) = 1 ⇒ Q\_2(t) = 1 hold point by point. Then Keep(Q\_1) = 1 ⇒ Keep(Q\_2) = 1, and Stable(Q\_1) = 1 ⇒ Stable(Q\_2) = 1.

Proof. The formula for Keep follows directly from the pointwise implication. Stable: if Q\_1 falls under Keep, as above. If Q\_1 falls under Recover, the pointwise implication gives Fail(Q\_2) ⊆ Fail(Q\_1). If Fail(Q\_2) is empty, Q\_2 is 1 at every point and falls under Keep. If it is non-empty, let b\_1 and b\_2 be the first failures of the two; the inclusion gives b\_2 ≥ b\_1. Take the point of return t\_r of Q\_1: from t\_r on, Q\_1 is 1 at every point, so Q\_2 is 1 at every point from t\_r on; the point of return t\_r′ of Q\_2 satisfies t\_r′ ≤ t\_r, and also t\_r′ \> b\_2 (Q\_2 is 0 at b\_2). Hence c(t\_r′) − c(b\_2) ≤ c(t\_r) − c(b\_1) ≤ T\_rec, and c(t\_end) − c(t\_r′) ≥ c(t\_end) − c(t\_r) ≥ h. All seven items are satisfied, and Q\_2 falls under Recover. □

Status = Established. The antecedent must be explicit: the same trajectory, the same H, the common adjudicable domain. At points with ⊥ the pointwise implication cannot be checked, and the theorem does not apply.

### 5.3 Theorem Three (Stability Chain)

From Theorem One and Theorem Two:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Keep\_{S\_0} = 1 ⇒ Keep\_fix(e\_0) = 1 ⇒ Keep\_∃∀ = 1 ⇒ Keep\_∀∃ = 1  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Stable\_{S\_0} = 1 ⇒ Stable\_fix(e\_0) = 1 ⇒ Stable\_∀∃ = 1

Proof. Forward direction: take the same e as the witness at every moment. Status = Established; the negation of the converse is established by a reference instance. In one sentence: the same bearer continuing entails no break-off; no break-off does not entail the same bearer continuing.

5.5 Theorem Five (Necessary Conditions of Return)

Recover(Q) = 1 ⇒ Fail(Q) ≠ ∅, FirstFail(Q) is actually attained, and there exists t\_r such that every point from t\_r to the end of the window is 1, c(t\_r) − c(FirstFail) ≤ T\_rec, and c(t\_end) − c(t\_r) ≥ h.

Proof. Read off item by item from the definition in 4.2. □

Status = Established. Three corollaries follow:

(1) Without failure there is no return: for a sequence that is 1 throughout, Recover = 0 and Stable = Keep = 1.  
(2) Momentary re-establishment is not return: a 1 read after failure, if it becomes 0 again within the window later on, is not a return lasting to the end of the window; in the momentary instance of 7.5, qualification comes back to 1 after the organizational event of round 6 and is 0 again within round 10, Recover = 0.  
(3) The deadline is not reset: the deadline is counted from the first actual failure; when a failure recurs later, the deadline may not be counted from that later failure. In the asynchronous instance of 7.8, the first failure of qualification is in round 4 and the lasting return begins in round 16; with a deadline of 3 rounds, Recover = 0; counting from the renewed failure in round 14 would wrongly give 1.

5.12 Theorem Twelve (Qualification Boundary and Stability Boundary Are Incomparable)

Status = Established; the first direction is a reference instance, the second an abstract model (a formal model, which does not claim that a corresponding physical instance already exists); the monotonicity of the stability verdict value with respect to the disturbance axis is not yet established (O-ST6). Corollary: q^∂\_Q ≤ q^∂\_St may not be signed unconditionally, nor may its converse. A hysteresis width is taken as the difference between the forward exit boundary and the reverse entry boundary on the same axis only after both have been actually attained; it is not the same quantity as the recovery deadline T\_rec.

## Section Six　The Re-formed Organization and the Original Organization

The Limit Form of Intention hands “the relation between the re-formed organization and the original organization” over to this chapter. This chapter's answer is layered:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;same original segment                  does not hold: the original segment exits at the point first determined to be 0 and does not re-enter (Theorem Eight)  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;same specified group of bearers        reviewable: whether the host and Des\_e have been rewritten within the window (fixed-bearer level, 4.5)  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;same task                              reviewable: given by Elig\_I through comparison of the frozen duties (1.5); a comparison protocol  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;same organization (ontological)        unproven

The first three levels are all relations on declaration records and comparison protocols. The fourth level requires cross-segment certification of bearer identity: between exit and re-establishment, whether the physical carrier of the bearing structure is continuous, whether the bearing sedimentation is carried over rather than obtained anew, and what kind of carry-over suffices to say “the same organization.” No such sufficient theorem exists upstream: the duty invariant of The Universal Invariant Form of Intention is an invariant among realizations that obtain the same role, and does not yield the sameness of the physical bearer; The Source Form of Intention reaches no conclusion on whether a re-declared candidate is the same as the original candidate. So this chapter signs only the first three levels and records the fourth as unproven (O-ST1).

The interfaces that need to be built are specified as follows (a specification is not a construction):

(1) a continuity certificate for the bearing carrier over the exit interval: between the exit point and the point of re-establishment, the topology, existence and sedimentation variables of the specified bearing edges have not been deleted and rebuilt;  
(2) a decomposition of sedimentation carry-over: of the sedimentation on which re-establishment rests, which part comes from before the exit, and which part is obtained by the events of re-establishment (the threshold-crossing discrimination of 3.6 (1) of The Source Form of Intention can serve as one input; for an instance, see 7.7 of that chapter);  
(3) the criterion of identity itself: under what carry-over the organization is called the same must be given by the identity chapters, and may not be fixed by the Stability Form on its own.

Until these three are built, “the same Intention organization has returned” is not a verdict that can be signed; what can be signed is “qualification has returned on the same specified group of bearers.”

def _relay(e2_from, include_e3=False, e1_full_until=10, e2_fresh=False):
    """Hosts e1 (kept, then eroded from round e1_full_until+1), e2 (pre-eroded before the window, maintenance resumes at
    round e2_from; or, with e2_fresh, an X_st host maintained throughout), optional e3 (qualified throughout, declared
    for another task)."""
    e1 = X_st(); e2 = X_st()
    if not e2_fresh:
        for _ in range(10):
            for op in MP(omit=('sd',)): do(e2, op)
    hosts = {'e1': e1, 'e2': e2}
    if include_e3: hosts['e3'] = rename_duties(X_st(), 'B')
    plan = []
    for r in range(1, 31):
        rnd = {'e1': MP() if r <= e1_full_until else MP(omit=('sd',)), 'e2': MP(omit=('sd',)) if r < e2_from else MP()}
        if include_e3: rnd['e3'] = MP()
        plan.append(rnd)
    T = task_key(e1)
    elig = {h: task_key(I) == T for h, I in hosts.items()}
    pts = run(hosts, plan); rnd_ = rounds_of(pts)
    per = {h: series(pts, h) for h in hosts}
    qset = [or3(*[per[h][i] for h in hosts if elig[h]]) for i in range(len(pts))]
    qany = [or3(*[per[h][i] for h in hosts]) for i in range(len(pts))]
    first1 = {h: next((rnd_[i] for i, x in enumerate(per[h]) if x is True), None) for h in hosts}
    out = dict(eligible={h: t3(x) for h, x in elig.items()}, first_1_round=first1,
               per_host={h: stats(per[h], rnd_, T_REC, HOLD) for h in hosts},
               Keep_common=t3(or3(*[keep(per[h]) for h in hosts if elig[h]])),
               Set=stats(qset, rnd_, T_REC, HOLD),
               Set_round_end_only=t3(keep([qset[i] for i in round_end(pts)])))
    if include_e3: out['Set_if_task_ignored'] = stats(qany, rnd_, T_REC, HOLD)
    return out

def org_successors(I):

    random source with these weights); each branch consumes the same single draw and then runs the event on that dimension."""
    rel = dict(I.P.Rel()); out = []

def cmd_guarantee(horizon=6):
    """Finite admissible class Δ': each round is a maintenance round, optionally followed by an organisation event;
    every successor of every organisation event (each dimension with Rel > 0) is a separate branch; all trajectories
    enumerated depth-first.  Class Δ adds idle rounds: one legal trajectory (30 idle rounds) is a determinate unstable
    witness."""
    n = [0]; leaves = []; nonone = [0]; branch_dims = set()
    def dfs(I, depth, path, kp):
        if depth == horizon:
            n[0] += 1; leaves.append(kp); return
        J = copy.deepcopy(I); k = kp
        for op in MP():
            do(J, op); x = qual(J)['Intent']; k = and3(k, x)
            if x is None: nonone[0] += 1
        dfs(J, depth + 1, path + 'm', k)
        for d, K in org_successors(J):
            branch_dims.add(d); x = qual(K)['Intent']
            if x is None: nonone[0] += 1
            dfs(K, depth + 1, path + d, and3(k, x))
    I = X_st(); dfs(I, 0, '', qual(I)['Intent'])
    gk = and3(*leaves)
    pts = run({'e0': X_st()}, [{'e0': IDLE} for _ in range(30)])
    w = stats(series(pts, 'e0'), rounds_of(pts), T_REC, HOLD)
    return dict(finite_class=dict(horizon_rounds=horizon, trajectories=n[0], organisation_event_dimensions=sorted(branch_dims),
                                  undecided_points=nonone[0], GuaranteedKeep=t3(gk)),
                class_with_idle=dict(witness='30 idle rounds', witness_Stable=w['Stable'], GuaranteedStable=0 if w['Stable'] == 0 else '⊥'))
