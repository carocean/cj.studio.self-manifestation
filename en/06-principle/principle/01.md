# The General Form of Principle

What follows attempts one general proposition:

what, exactly, does a Law have over and above being a Law, once it becomes a Principle relative to another Law?

The General Form of Law has already shown that for a relation to be a Law it cannot be merely one instance of correspondence, one current response, or one trace left behind; it must already have formed, and must really and stably bear what comes after.

The argument continues from there.

Fix first a target Law A that already stands. Some action now arises in the field and begins to interfere with the holding-tendency A has acquired. When can one say only that "an action occurred," and when may one say that "a Principle is already here"?

This is the irreducible task to be borne.

Under sustained questioning we are to find where the proof must go, and from that obtain the general form of Principle.

The aim:

to obtain the minimal necessary structure, no part of which can be deleted, for a candidate P to acquire Principle qualification relative to an already standing Law A; and to give its minimality, its boundaries, and the conditions under which it breaks down.

The result first.

When the declared working domain, the inherited conditions, and the H conditions hold:

　　Law\_F,t(A, I\_A)  
　　∧ P ≠ A  
　　∧ Principle\_F,t(P; A, J)

　　⇒

　　Law\_F,t(P, I\_P)  
　　∧ CounterHoldRole\_F,t(P; A, J)

Here:

　　Law\_F,t(P, I\_P)

says that P has itself acquired the holding-tendency of a Law;

　　CounterHoldRole\_F,t(P; A, J)

says that, in the relational evaluations prescribed by J — those in which A's holding-tendency legally enters bearing — the Rel(P) by which P acquired Law qualification carries a duty of one of the two following types:

　　delivery-type — its Read restricts P's contribution entering A's bearing quantity, that is, the quantity P generates which serves as an argument of the local generation function of A's bearing quantity, and this Read takes the entry of that contribution into the generation of A's bearing quantity as its condition of realization — the site at which A's bearing quantity is generated lies within the necessary bearing organization by which P completes that duty;  
　　result-type — its Read restricts the value of A's bearing quantity itself, and that restriction is really discharged by the same DynamicRole through which Law(P, I\_P) was acquired, with no requirement that P have a contribution entering A's bearing quantity directly;

and the side so restricted runs counter to A's holding-direction on a dynamical degree of freedom A actually holds.

"Counter-holding" does not require that A's duty has already been made to fail, does not require that A hold nothing at all, and does not require that what comes after finally departs from Rel(A). It is a relation of dynamical direction with respect to a holding-tendency A has already formed, not a result.

Only the necessary direction is proved here. The converse is borne by The Necessity Form of Principle.

Where Principle Stands

In nature there is flux, interaction, and the repeated recurrence of relations. Whether nature itself "possesses" Law and Principle is left open here and not decided a priori.

A human being, as observer, recognizes Law and Principle. As objects of recognition, Law and Principle are generated in the observer's Sedimentation-Field: one can say "this is a Law" or "there is a hold-breaking relation here" because the corresponding relation has already formed, in that Sedimentation-Field, a structure that can be stably re-walked and told apart. In this Law and Principle are alike. This does not make Law and Principle mere subjective fictions; the relations the observer sediments come from experience of real flux.

A different proposition, on another level, is this: the most primitive generation of Principle requires no subject and no intention. A Sedimentation-Field may bring forth the hold-breaking of one Law by another before it has brought forth any subject, thought, or intention. What follows uses no subject, goal, evaluation, intention, or representation to explain Principle.

Both propositions hold. They are not merged here.

What is studied is this: once a relation has been generated in a Sedimentation-Field and has acquired the structural qualification of "Principle," what minimally does it have over and above an ordinary Law?

In One Sentence

A Law holds what comes after it.

A Principle is first of all a Law; by its own duty it forms a counter-hold against the holding-tendency another Law is currently exercising.

Abstract

Taking an already standing target Law A as the working domain, the question is only this: what kind of P can acquire Principle qualification relative to A?

The argument starts from one current action. Even an action that really does suppress, deflect, or cut off A is not yet a "tendency that breaks the hold"; hence the first thing forced out is that P must itself already stand as a Law. Keeping that, and now letting P stably damage A, two kinds of case still stand. P's duty is to consume some resource on which A happens to depend for its Continuation, and A is damaged accordingly; yet with A absent, P's duty is discharged in full just the same. Or P, in discharging its own duty, imposes a stable incidental load on A, while P's duty makes no requirement whatever on A's value. Neither is "interference aimed at A": in neither case is P's duty about A. So the argument forces out more: Rel(P) must carry a duty directed toward A's holding-tendency, in one of two legal types. Delivery-type: the Read restricts the contribution P delivers directly into A's bearing quantity, and takes the entry of that contribution into A's generation as its condition of realization; what P's duty prescribes is what it delivers to A, not what A finally amounts to, nor what P itself puts out. Result-type: the Read restricts A's bearing quantity directly, and that restriction is really discharged by the same DynamicRole through which Law(P) was acquired; the route may run through threshold, impedance, or Ω indifferently. Keeping the first two rounds, and now adding a duty so directed toward A's holding-tendency, same-direction support, orthogonal modulation, and legal branch switching still stand; so the argument forces out further that the side restricted must run counter to A's holding-direction on a dynamical degree of freedom A actually holds — not with it, and not indifferent to it.

At that point the new structure closes. Temporary wiring needs no separate round of exclusion: a piece of unsedimented wiring cannot turn the duty witnessed by Law(P) into a duty about A. What remains is only to settle what "counter-holding" minimally means. Complete cut-off is not the threshold of qualification; a change in the final outcome is not a necessary condition; neither failure of Read nor departure from the successor support set is a criterion. Counter-holding may be very weak; it may not have won. P's duty may prescribe only what it delivers into A — and even if that contribution is fully compensated by another Law, so that A's bearing quantity does not change at all, P is still a Principle. P's duty may instead prescribe A's bearing quantity itself, in which case its realization must really enter A. What Principle bears upon is the holding-tendency A is legally exercising, not A's structure at rest, not the resources A uses, and not the final read-off value.

Hence Principle is not law-change, not mere conflict, not a side effect, not control, and not the new Law itself. It is a relational qualification acquired by an already standing Law P relative to another already standing Law A. What distinguishes Principle from an ordinary Law is not some other mechanism of sedimentation, stability, or re-walking, but the object restricted by the duty through which P already stands as a Law, and the direction that restriction takes.

Working Domain

Let:

　　F　be the Sedimentation-Field;  
　　t　the current moment;  
　　A　the target Law;  
　　P　the candidate Principle;  
　　I\_A　A's own domain of Law application;  
　　I\_P　the pre-declared domain of Law application for the candidate P;  
　　J　the Principle relation domain of P relative to A.

Before entry it is required that

　　Law\_F,t(A, I\_A)  
　　P ≠ A  
　　I\_P ≠ ∅

be already fixed.

Two distinguishable Law identities, A and P, are fixed here. One and the same physical carrier may witness several Laws; Principle is adjudicated according to the Law identity taken. P ≠ A is non-identity of Law identity; it does not require physical separation of carriers. The strict criterion for identity of Law identity is not defined here and belongs to the task of Law identity.

I\_P is the pre-declared domain of application by which candidate P would stand as a Law; a smaller domain must not be chosen after the fact once the argument is over.

Law(A, I\_A) guarantees that A is not one instance of flux, one response, a mere record, or an old groove left as structural trace, but has acquired the holding-tendency guaranteed by Law qualification.

J ≠ ∅. J must be declared before P's Principle qualification relative to A is tested, and must not be narrowed after the fact because a counterexample has appeared.

The covering semantics of J is fixed as follows:

　　every legal relational evaluation in J must, on the A side, project onto legal conditions within I\_A covered by Law(A, I\_A), and in that evaluation A's holding-tendency must actually enter bearing;  
　　on the P side it must project onto legal conditions within I\_P covered by Law(P, I\_P);  
　　P's duty relative to A is tested within that same evaluation;  
　　every legal relational evaluation that J declares itself to cover must be tested under the same CounterHoldRole qualification; one must not pick out only the single most favourable instance and let it stand for the rest;  
　　Principle qualification may be retained while A and P are currently at rest, since it is adjudicated by the legal replay evaluations J prescribes, not by current actual inflow.

The element type of J, the exact quantifier notation, and the form of projection are left to H\_P3 for The Mathematical Form of Principle to close.

Inherited:

　　Law\_F,t(L,I)  
　　⇒  
　　∃R \[  
　　　　Established\_F,t(R)  
　　　　∧ Govern\_F,t(L;R,I)  
　　\]

and:

　　Govern\_F,t(L;R,I)  
　　⇔  
　　DynamicRole\_F,t(L;R,I)  
　　∧ ΩSupport\_F,t(L;R,I)

The senses of Established, Govern, DynamicRole, ΩSupport, Rel(L), and I are all inherited from the Law Group-Form and are not redefined here.

Inherited from The Mathematical Form of Law: the time-unfolded generation graph, local generation functions, and bearing arguments. Every generable quantity has its own set of local arguments, and substitution-elimination is not permitted. That a quantity "is an argument of what some node generates" means that it belongs to a minimal μ-sufficient set for that node's generation; its objective truth value falls to H3, and is settled neither by the counterfactual of simple deletion nor by statistical correlation.

"A's bearing quantity," as used here, means a node generated by a local generation function on A's bearing path within the evaluation in question — the Continuation tendency on A's segment, A's gating output, A's value on the degree of freedom it holds. These are the quantities A is producing. The thresholds, impedances, resources, and field pressures on which A relies in continuing are arguments A reads when generating those quantities; they are not A's bearing quantities. On a shared base, a quantity generated by a local generation function on a shared segment from both sides' contributions lies on both Laws' bearing paths and is a bearing quantity of both at once; no exclusive component is required. Resource occupancy is generated by the field's resource rules and is not any Law's bearing quantity.

"P's contribution entering A's bearing quantity," as used here, means a node generated by P's realization which is also an argument in the generation of some bearing quantity of A: P generates it, A's local generation function reads it. It is the quantity P delivers to A's holding-tendency.

The "directedness binding" of a delivery-type duty, as used here, means that the duty's Read not only restricts the value of that contribution but takes the contribution's entry into the generation of A's bearing quantity as its condition of realization: what the Read reads is "the value of c as an argument of y\_A," not "the value of c." Its upstream expression: the site at which A's bearing quantity is generated lies within the necessary bearing organization by which P completes that duty — the necessary Continuation organization borne by ΩWit constitutively reaches and contains it, and that reaching belongs to the discharge of the duty, not to A's input path. It need not be the terminus of that organization: P's duty may, after delivering the contribution, continue on through records generated by A's bearing quantity, feedback, or other quantities, so long as the site of A's generation lies within its necessary bearing organization. A quantity that P puts out and A happens to read is not this: its Read is discharged at P's output, the site of A's generation lies outside P's necessary bearing organization, and it is not directed toward A. A quantity P writes into a structural state that A reads — a threshold, an impedance, a resource — is likewise not this: such quantities enter the updating of a structural state, not the generation of A's bearing quantity; what A's bearing quantity reads is the updated structural state, not P's write. On a shared base, where the Continuation tendency of a shared segment is generated by that segment's local generation function from both sides' contributions, P's contribution is a contribution entering A's bearing quantity, and A's contribution is likewise one entering P's.

"A duty's Read restricts the value of some quantity," as used here, means that the projection of the duty's satisfaction set onto that quantity is a proper subset of its value range: there exist values of that quantity at which the duty cannot be discharged, whatever the other quantities may be. If for every value of that quantity the duty can be discharged by suitable values of the others, then the duty does not restrict it, even if it entered the evaluation of the Read. The "quantity" a duty restricts is, for delivery-type, P's contribution entering A; for result-type, A's bearing quantity itself. A result-type duty's projection onto P's contribution may be the whole range: what it prescribes is that A be held in, not how much P delivers.

The restriction must fall on the duty's Read, that is, on what the duty discharges; falling on the duty's antecedent does not count. A duty triggered only when A's bearing quantity lies in some range, but whose Read requires nothing of P's contribution entering A or of A's bearing quantity, restricts neither. That is a Law responding to A, not a Law interfering with A.

"Holding-tendency," as used here, means a holding whose Law qualification has already been acquired and which has entered bearing within a legal evaluation.

"Hold-breaking," as used here, takes at the outset only the task-sense of the canonical source's "tendency that breaks the hold." It is not defined in advance as failure of Read, rerouting, non-completion of a duty, total cut-off, or any other specific criterion on outcomes. What it minimally means is forced out by the argument below and then pressed further.

The Line of Argument

We proceed by sustained questioning, one step at a time; the argument therefore forms a line, and is to be read from the top down.

First question:

A already stands as a Law.

Some candidate p now acts once upon A's running, so that A's holding-tendency undergoes suppression, deflection, cut-off, or some other counter-directed interference.

On that alone, may one say that p is already a Principle of A?

No.

Construct the weakest case.

A already stands as a Law. Some one-off event p strikes into A's running and forms one counter-directed action upon A's holding-tendency. When the action is over, p vanishes, and no already formed relation remains in the field capable of bearing counter-holding action of that kind again.

A counter-holding event has occurred this once. But there is here no "tendency that breaks the hold."

If such a p could be a Principle, then any impact, any noise pulse, any instantaneous gate, any chance wiring would acquire Principle qualification as soon as it happened to act counter to A's holding-tendency. That would collapse one counter-holding event and a hold-breaking tendency capable of stable re-walking into one thing.

The original task is therefore not closed.

The failure lies here: what has been observed is one counter-holding action only, and there is as yet no hold-breaker that has itself acquired a holding-tendency.

Inner Chapter 9 · Experience-Field says: "Law is the tendency of holding; Principle is the tendency that breaks the hold." The same word, tendency, is said of both. A hold-breaker without a holding-tendency is merely a change in the field.

Hence, if P is already a Principle relative to A, P must itself first acquire Law qualification over its pre-declared domain I\_P:

　　Law\_F,t(P, I\_P)

This is not a presupposition of the working domain; it is the necessary result forced out by the first round of questioning.

P ≠ A is fixed as a working-domain restriction of "one Law becoming a Principle relative to a target Law," and is not forced out by this round. If only the strength of A's own single Law alters as it runs, that belongs to a Law's own dynamics, or to law-change; only where there exists a candidate P distinguishable from A in Law identity does the present task begin.

Obtained so far:

　　A already stands as a Law;  
　　P and A are non-identical in Law identity;  
　　Principle(P;A,J) ⇒ Law(P,I\_P).

Then the next question:

Given that P and A are both Laws, and that P stably damages A's running, is the original task closed?

Not yet.

Keep everything obtained so far.

A is a Law, P is another Law, and P's running stably damages A.

Two kinds of case can be constructed which keep all of the previous round, are stable, are repeatable, and yet are not "this would hold, that breaks it."

The resource-consumption case. P's duty is to consume one unit of resource r whenever C arrives. A is holding, and A's Continuation tendency takes the remaining r directly as an argument. There is no adjudicating step in the field: the update simply subtracts P's consumption from r, and A then reads r. Each time P consumes, A's holding-tendency drops. P's consumption belongs to a minimal sufficient set for the discharge of P's duty; it stably alters the resource quantity read in the generation of A's holding-tendency, in a direction that damages A; and A is holding.

But take A out of the relation — let A continue on some other resource — and P's structure, inputs, and consumption are all unchanged; P's duty is discharged in full just the same. Replace A with B, C, or D: anything running on r is slowed just as much. P's duty is about r, not about A. P holds P, A holds A, and A happens to use what P consumes. The variant in which the field adjudicates resource shares is only another way of writing the same thing.

The side-effect case. P is a stable measurement Law, with Rel(P): A's activity ↦ reading z. P's realization is physically coupled to A's activity and, in taking the reading, imposes a small stable load on A's Continuation tendency. The load is a quantity P generates which enters the generation of A's Continuation tendency directly — it is precisely P's contribution entering A's bearing quantity — counter-directed and repeatable. But P's duty requires nothing of that load, and nothing of A's value: the load may be larger or smaller, A higher or lower, and z is read out and the duty discharged all the same. The load is merely a by-product of P's discharging its duty. Friction, heat dissipation, bandwidth occupancy, and coupling loss all belong here.

In both cases P is a Law, A is stably damaged, and P's action enters A's generation directly. If both counted as Principle, a "tendency that breaks the hold" would degenerate into "any Law that stably has a negative effect on another Law."

What the two have in common: P's duty prescribes nothing about what P delivers to A's holding-tendency. The resource-consuming Law's duty restricts the value of r — the consumption enters the updating of r, not the generation of A's Continuation tendency. The measurement Law's duty restricts the relation between z and A, and sets no restriction on its own load entering A. Take A away, and the first is discharged just the same, while the second has nothing to measure and equally nothing to break.

The Experience-Field says: "This would hold, that breaks it; it breaks it, and then it does not hold; the pursuit of the two makes Causal-Response transform without cease." Pursuit requires that the one's breaking take the other's holding as that upon which it bears. That one consumes r and this happens to use r is not pursuit; that one measures this while this still holds itself is not pursuit either.

The original task is therefore still not closed.

The previous round forced out that P must itself have a holding-tendency; the gap remaining after this round narrows to:

what, exactly, does P's duty prescribe about what it delivers to A's holding-tendency?

To exclude the two kinds of case, more must be forced out:

within the legal relational evaluations of J, Rel(P) carries a duty directed toward A's holding-tendency: either its Read restricts P's contribution entering A's bearing quantity and takes that contribution's entry into A as its condition of realization (delivery-type), or its Read restricts the value of A's bearing quantity itself and that restriction is really discharged by the same DynamicRole (result-type). The first half is obtained below, and "takes its entry into A as its condition of realization" and "really discharged" are then pressed home in turn.

"P's contribution entering A's bearing quantity" is a fact of the field: some node P generates is an argument read by the local generation function of A's bearing quantity. "Restricts" is likewise a fact of the field: whether the projection of the duty's satisfaction set onto that contribution is a proper subset. A dedicated inhibitory Law's duty — to deliver a negative quantity into A's Continuation tendency — is discharged only when that contribution is negative; take A away, there is nowhere to deliver, and the duty cannot be discharged at all. The resource-consuming Law's satisfaction set restricts the value of r, its consumption enters r's update and not the generation of A's Continuation tendency, and P has no contribution entering A's bearing quantity to restrict. The measurement Law does have a contribution entering A — the load — but the projection of its duty's satisfaction set onto that load is the whole range: whatever the load, z is read out.

This condition prescribes what P delivers to A, not what A finally amounts to. Construct the compensation case: A is holding and contributes +10 to the quantity it holds; P's already formed duty is to deliver −1 to that quantity each time; and another Law Q happens to deliver +1 to it. A's bearing quantity is still 10, unchanged. P really and stably delivers −1 every time, and that is P's duty. P is a Principle of A. P's duty can prescribe only what it delivers, not what A finally takes — Q may compensate, and A may contribute more strongly itself. Were qualification to require that P's duty restrict A's actual bearing quantity, this most standard weak Principle would be missed, and the requirement would contradict both "counter-holding may not have won" and "a change in the final outcome is not a necessary condition."

The delivery-type must be questioned once more. Construct the shared modulation bus case: P's already formed duty is to write −1 into a public modulation quantity each time C arrives. A is holding, and A's local generation function happens to read that quantity directly, which runs counter to A's holding-direction. P stands as a Law; the quantity is generated by P; it is an argument of A's bearing quantity; P's Read restricts it to −1; the direction is counter. If delivery-type required only this, P would be a Principle of A. But take A away, and P writes −1 just the same, its duty discharged in full; hook up B, C, or D later, and they are suppressed by it just as much. P's duty is to put out −1, not to deliver −1 to A; A merely happens to read it. This is of a kind with resource consumption, only bypassing the update of a resource state and landing directly on A's argument.

So the delivery-type's Read cannot restrict the value of the contribution alone. It must also take that contribution's entry into the generation of A's bearing quantity as its condition of realization: what the Read reads is "the value of c as an argument of y\_A," not "the value of c." This is directedness binding. Its upstream expression: the site at which A's bearing quantity is generated lies within the necessary bearing organization by which P completes that duty — P's ΩWit constitutively reaches and contains it, and that reaching is part of the discharge of P's duty, not part of A's input path. It is not required to be the terminus: a duty may be "deliver −1 to A, then generate a feedback quantity z by way of A's bearing quantity," where neither y\_A nor z may be dropped; y\_A is not the terminus yet still lies within P's necessary bearing organization, and P is still a Principle. Written as "with A as terminus," such a duty would be missed, and the wording would amount to requiring a "duty terminus" in another guise — and a duty terminus is not a necessary structure (see the closure of necessary constitution). In the bus case, P's necessary bearing organization stops at the public quantity, and the passage from that quantity to A is A's input; the site of A's generation lies outside it. For the dedicated inhibitory Law, the necessary bearing organization contains the site at which A's bearing quantity is generated; with A absent, the Read cannot be discharged. On the same bus structure, should there be another Law P₂ whose duty is "deliver −1 to A," and whose ΩWit contains a segment reaching A and has a witness, then P₂ under that Law identity is a Principle of A.

Directedness binding does not require that A activate P, does not require reading before returning, and does not require an exclusive component. It requires only that the content of P's duty relation be the delivery of a contribution to A's holding-tendency — and that content is given by the field fact of whether the site of A's bearing-quantity generation lies within P's necessary bearing organization.

Directedness binding has one consequence: the legal domain of a delivery-type duty is conditioned on A's holding-tendency entering bearing. With A not bearing, y\_A is not being generated, and "the value of c as an argument of y\_A" cannot be evaluated at all; the duty cannot be discharged. Hence the coverage of that duty by Law(P, I\_P) must have, in the legal evaluation of its inflow conditions, A's holding-tendency entering bearing — the corresponding element of I\_P is a joint inflow arriving together with A's inflow, not A's state itself. A duty discharged just the same whether A is present or not is not delivery-type; this is the other face of the bus case's exclusion. None of this means P must be activated by A: P may be activated by C, and it is only that when C arrives while A is not holding, this duty lies outside its legal domain.

The second branch of this condition is the result-type duty. A duty prescribing that A's Continuation tendency not exceed some value restricts A's bearing quantity itself. It does not require that P have a contribution entering A's bearing quantity directly: P may suppress A by way of threshold, impedance, Ω, or a resource route, while what A's Continuation tendency reads is the updated structural state and P's write does not appear in A's local generation function. But for it to be a duty of Law(P), it must have the witness of DynamicRole and ΩSupport: P's state really enters the generation chain of A's bearing quantity, and P's necessary Continuation segment bears that discharge with Ω — it is not that A happens to fall within what is restricted, but that P holds it there. Such a duty fails in the compensation case: Q compensates, A is not suppressed, the Read does not hold, and P does not stand as a Law over that domain. This is no omission on the part of the present form; it is that this Law has not itself stood. That delivery-type and result-type duties meet different fates under compensation is precisely the exact expression of "qualification ≠ victory ≠ final outcome."

The two types are one and the same "what the duty restricts" unfolded in two kinds of legal realization, not two top-level necessary items. Delivery-type restricts the quantity P delivers to A and takes entry into A as its condition of realization; result-type restricts the quantity A is held in and is really discharged by P. What they share is that A's bearing quantity enters the evaluation of P's duty Read — in the first as the direction of the contribution, in the second as the quantity restricted. P's duty is directed toward A's holding-tendency, and not about a resource, a threshold, a public quantity, or somewhere else.

This condition contains "P's action belongs to a minimal sufficient set for P's duty" and is stronger than it. That P's contribution entering A belongs to a minimal sufficient set for P's duty Read does not mean P's duty restricts it: the measurement Law's Read reads A by way of the load, yet does not restrict the load.

This condition does not restrict the route of action, though the two types require different things of the route. P delivers a negative quantity directly into A's Continuation tendency: that negative quantity is the contribution entering A — delivery-type. P raises the threshold or impedance of the segment A is running on, or lowers its Ω: what is written is A's structural state; what A's Continuation tendency reads is the updated threshold, not P's write — P has no contribution entering A's bearing quantity, and delivery-type does not hold. Here P is a Principle just in case its duty prescribes A's Continuation tendency itself and its DynamicRole really holds that down by way of the threshold — result-type. Construct the stable gating hold-breaking case: A is holding, and P's already formed duty, when condition C appears, is that the Continuation tendency of the segment A is running on be pressed down, realized by raising that segment's threshold. This is a Principle. With the same manner of realization, if P's duty is merely "that segment's threshold is high," setting no restriction on A's Continuation tendency and having no contribution entering A, then with A removed the duty is discharged just the same: not a Principle, but a Law that alters running conditions — of a kind with resource consumption.

This condition does not require P's contribution to vary with A's current state. An open-loop dedicated inhibitory Law — activated by a third Law C, delivering a fixed negative quantity −δ into A's Continuation tendency each time, its duty being precisely to deliver that −δ — restricts the contribution entering A, and is a Principle. A feedback inhibitory Law is a Principle too.

Nor does this condition require that P be activated by A, that P read A before returning into A, or that P and A have an exclusive component. P may be activated by the same signal, by a third Law, or by other conditions, and may be active beforehand; in continuous coupling there are no two discrete steps.

This condition cannot be acquired by the wording of Rel(P). On the very carrier of the resource consumer one may declare a further Rel(P₂): "when C arrives, A's Continuation tendency is pressed down." Whether P₂ stands as a Law is a fact of the field: its Read restricts A's Continuation tendency, its realization must really deliver a contribution entering A, and the Ω-witnessed segment must bear that discharge. If P's consumption really does stably suppress A, P₂ may stand as a Law, and P₂ under that Law identity is a Principle of A — on one carrier, the consuming Law is not a Principle and the suppressing Law is. Principle qualification is adjudicated by Law identity, just as Law qualification is adjudicated by Rel and I. This is no play on words: declaring a Rel cannot conjure its witness out of nothing.

This condition also excludes temporary wiring. P stands as a Law elsewhere, and a one-off event g wires P's output temporarily onto A's bearing quantity: the duty of Rel(P) restricts a quantity elsewhere, not the contribution entering A. Were one to declare instead a Rel(P′) restricting the contribution entering A, P′'s carrier would have to take g as a necessary link of access; but g is instantaneous, CarrierNonInstant fails, Law(P′) does not hold, and the first round has already excluded it.

The abrasion case needs no separate exclusion here. When A is at rest it has no bearing quantity — its Continuation tendency is not being generated — so P has nowhere to deliver a contribution entering A, and Rel(P) can restrict only the structural state of R\_A. The A-side clause of J places this outside the relational evaluation of Principle. It is one of the sources of law-change. The same P, if its duty prescribes the contribution delivered while A is holding, or A's Continuation tendency, is a Principle.

Obtained so far:

　　P itself already stands as a Law;  
　　Rel(P) carries a duty directed toward A's holding-tendency, either restricting P's contribution entering A's bearing quantity and taking its entry into A as the condition of realization (delivery-type), or restricting A's bearing quantity itself and being really discharged by the same DynamicRole (result-type).

Then the next question:

If P's duty restricts what it contributes into A, or restricts A's bearing quantity, is the original task closed?

Still not.

Keep everything obtained in the previous two rounds.

P is a Law, A is a Law, and the duty of Rel(P) restricts either P's contribution entering A's bearing quantity or A's bearing quantity.

Four kinds of case can still be constructed.

The supporting case. The duty of Rel(P) is to deliver a positive quantity into A's Continuation tendency, or to prescribe that A's Continuation tendency not fall below some value: A's holding-tendency has weakened and P pulls it back to its original holding-direction; some role contribution of A is disturbed and P makes it stable again. P's duty does restrict the contribution entering A — restricts it to the side A itself holds.

The orthogonal modulation case. The duty of Rel(P) prescribes a contribution entering some bearing quantity of A, but one on a degree of freedom A generates and does not hold. What A holds is some amplitude relation, and what P delivers is a phase taking no part in that holding-direction; or what A holds is a path organization, and what P delivers is a quantity contributing neither for nor against that holding-direction.

The legal branch case. Rel(A) permits two branches under the current conditions, and A's DynamicRole has no holding-direction as between them — both are legal Continuations of A, and A holds neither side. The duty of Rel(P) restricts A's branch value to one of them. P's duty does restrict A's bearing quantity, but the degree of freedom restricted is not one A holds: no holding-direction of A is run counter to. If A does have a holding-direction under the current conditions — its Ω-supported Continuation pointing to one branch — and P's duty restricts it to the other, that is the counter-directed case, and is a Principle. If what P alters is the input entering A, so that A reselects its branch by its own relation, then what P restricts is A's input and not A's bearing quantity, and the second round has already excluded it.

The counter-directed case. The contribution restricted by the duty of Rel(P) runs counter to A's holding-direction on a degree of freedom A actually holds.

All four keep what the previous two rounds obtained. But only the fourth is the "hold-breaking" of which the canonical source speaks. If the first three were also called Principle, "a tendency that breaks the hold" would still have acquired no power to discriminate.

The original task is therefore still not closed.

The gap left by this round is direction alone, and it is a trichotomy, not a dichotomy:

is the quantity restricted by P's duty, relative to the dynamical degree of freedom A actually holds at present, with it, indifferent to it, or counter to it?

To exclude support, strengthening, repair, stabilization, orthogonal modulation, and legal branch switching, more must be forced out:

the direction of P's contribution relative to A must be counter-holding — not with it, and not indifferent to it.

"Holding-direction" here does not mean a spatial path alone, but the direction A actually bears by its DynamicRole on the current duty type. Path, weight, strength, timing, duration, and other duty types each have their own formal reference for holding-direction; these are closed by H\_P2.

Direction is evaluated on the quantity restricted by P's duty. If what is restricted is the contribution entering A, look at the sign with which that contribution lands on the degree of freedom A holds, through the local generation function of that bearing quantity of A — one step, A's own function. If what is restricted is A's bearing quantity itself, look at the relation between A's holding-direction and the side into which the duty holds A — the side on which the satisfaction set lies: A holds toward the high side while the duty holds A to the low side, and the direction is counter; holds A to the high side, and it is with. "The side restricted" means the side into which the duty holds A, not the side excluded. A result-type duty may be realized by way of threshold, impedance, Ω, or resource indifferently; the route does not enter the adjudication of direction.

"Counter-holding" says: P's contribution, on the very dynamical degree of freedom A actually holds, runs counter to A's holding-direction. It may appear as suppression, cut-off, deflection, redistribution, or withdrawal of a hold; or it may be no more than a counter-directed contribution not yet sufficient to change the final outcome.

Only at this point is a "hold-breaking direction" in the sense used here acquired for the first time.

Obtained so far:

　　P itself stands as a Law;  
　　Rel(P) carries a duty directed toward A's holding-tendency, either restricting P's contribution entering A's bearing quantity and taking its entry into A as the condition of realization (delivery-type), or restricting A's bearing quantity itself and being really discharged by the same DynamicRole (result-type);  
　　the side restricted is counter-holding in direction relative to A.

Closure of the Necessary Constitution

We now try once more to construct a degenerate case.

A single impact was excluded in the first round. Resource consumption, side effects, and temporary wiring were excluded in the second; abrasion is excluded by the A-side clause of J. Support, strengthening, repair, stabilization, orthogonal modulation, and legal branch switching were excluded in the third.

The following candidates were also tried and rejected one by one.

"P's contribution must take A's current holding-tendency state as a generative term" — too strong: the fixed contribution of an open-loop dedicated inhibitory Law does not vary with A, yet it is plainly a Principle. This tightens "interference aimed at A" into "interference varying with state," and the latter is only one realization of the former. It is also not enough: the measurement Law's load really does take A's state as a generative term, yet is merely a side effect. State dependence is wrong at both ends.

"P's duty takes the breaking of A as its terminus rather than as a means" — adjudicating a terminus by the wording of Rel, when one carrier may witness several Rel. Adjudicating by the satisfaction set of the Read and by the necessary bearing organization of ΩWit is a fact of the field.

"P's contribution must land on A's active nodes (intermediate generated quantities) and not on structural-state nodes (threshold, impedance, Ω)" — too strong: in the stable gating hold-breaking case, P's duty, while A is holding, presses down A's Continuation tendency by raising a threshold; that is plainly a Principle, yet would be excluded. The route of action is a level of realization, not an ontological boundary.

"P's dutiful contribution must be an argument of the generation of A's holding-tendency directly, and not enter through an adjudicating node" — still too wide: in the resource-consumption case P consumes r, A reads r directly, there is no adjudicating step, everything passes — yet P's duty has nothing to do with A, and with A removed P is discharged just the same. "Whether there is an adjudicating node" adjudicates how direct the route of action is; it cannot adjudicate whether breaking A is P's duty. That an action on P's side belongs to P's duty does not entail that the effect of that action on A belongs to P's duty.

"The counter-hold must belong to the same already formed duty organization through which P acquired Law qualification" — correct, but already contained in what the duty restricts as established in the second round (what a duty restricts is relative to the Rel(P) through which Law was acquired), and so does not constitute an independent round.

"P must read A before returning into A"; "P and A must have an exclusive component" — manners of realization, not qualification.

"A must still be re-walked outside J"; "A's Continuation must have a delay margin" — these are, respectively, dynamical conditions for whether a Principle relation can persist and whether it can be generated; they are not conditions for its standing at the present moment.

At present no new degenerate case has been obtained that keeps all three rounds and still makes the original task fail for want of another independent top-level structure.

The constraint recursion of the newly added structure stops here.

Discriminating the Breaking of a Hold

The new duty has been forced out, but "counter-holding" still admits several competing readings. What follows adds no further top-level necessary structure; it only settles what the hold-breaking direction already forced out minimally means.

Given that the counter-holding direction has been forced out, the next question:

must P already have made A hold nothing at all, in order to acquire Principle qualification?

No.

Principle qualification adjudicates whether P's duty is directed toward A's holding-tendency and restricted to the counter-holding side. It does not take as a necessary condition whether this particular counter-hold is strong enough, or whether it finally dominates what comes after.

Hence: P's counter-hold may be very weak; A may go on bearing; P may even, in some run, not have changed what finally came after. "Losing all hold" is only one possible outcome after a strong counter-hold has succeeded; it is not the threshold of qualification.

So "none of A's live successors are discharged any longer," "Read\_A = 0," and "the actual successor must leave A's original support set" must not be written as general necessary conditions of Principle. They are at most phenomena in certain cases of strong action.

Given that a change in the final outcome is not a necessary condition, the next question:

with what comes after externally identical, can A's holding-tendency still be broken?

It can.

Construct the takeover case. A formerly bore some outcome by its own holding-tendency. P's duty forms a counter-hold on A's bearing quantity, while another organization takes over that same outcome. The final path value, weight value, and timing value may all be the same as when A held it itself.

But if A's own former bearing has been pressed back, withdrawn, or replaced by P, an identical external result is no evidence that A was not broken in its hold.

This differs from hot redundancy. In hot redundancy, A and the other bearer both really contribute to the same duty; the other's duty neither delivers a counter-directed contribution into A's bearing quantity nor holds A's bearing quantity to the counter side. In the takeover case, P's duty requires that A's holding-tendency be pressed, and it is only that another organization makes up the outcome afterwards.

Hence the hold-breaking of a Principle cannot be defined on the final read-off value of Rel(A). It bears upon A's bearing quantity, not upon whether the result remains the same.

Given that hold-breaking bears upon A's bearing quantity, the next question:

a Law dedicated to suppressing A, realized by raising the threshold of one of A's segments — is that a Principle?

It depends on what its duty restricts.

A has not yet entered bearing, and P raises the threshold of its entry segment so that A does not start at all: A has no bearing quantity to restrict, and the A-side clause of J places this outside. This is blocking, of a kind with abrasion.

A is holding, and the duty of Rel(P) is that the Continuation tendency of the segment A is running on fall below some value, realized by raising that segment's threshold: this is a Principle. The duty restricts A's bearing quantity (result-type), and whether the realization runs through a threshold or not is irrelevant to qualification.

A is holding, and the duty of Rel(P) is merely that that segment's threshold exceed some value, so that A's Continuation tendency is thereby pressed down, while the duty sets no restriction on A's Continuation tendency and P has no contribution entering the generation of A's Continuation tendency — the threshold's update reads P's write, and A's Continuation tendency reads the updated threshold. Take A away, the threshold stays high and the duty is discharged just the same: not a Principle. This is a Law that alters running conditions.

So the line between Principle and law-change lies not in the route by which P acts, but in whether P's duty prescribes what it delivers to A's holding-tendency, or that holding-tendency itself.

Given that the line lies in what the duty restricts, the next question:

P stably consumes resource r, and A is holding and takes r directly as an argument — does it matter whether there is an adjudicating step?

It does not. Neither way is a Principle.

P's duty restricts the value of r, or its own occupancy. P's consumption enters r's update, and A's Continuation tendency reads the updated r — P has no contribution entering A's bearing quantity, and A's bearing quantity is not in the projection of its satisfaction set either. Take A away, and P is discharged just the same. With an adjudicating step, P's demand is apportioned a share by the field; without one, P's consumption subtracts from r directly. The two differ only in how the resource rule is written; P's duty is about r either way.

If on that same carrier there is another Law P₂ whose duty restricts A's Continuation tendency, with the witness of Established ∧ Govern, then P₂ is a Principle of A. Resource consumption does sometimes stably suppress A; but "P's consumption harmed A" and "there exists a Law whose duty is to suppress A" are two different things.

Given that the line lies in what the duty restricts, the next question:

two Laws each deliver contributions of opposite direction into the Continuation tendency on one shared segment, each taking that delivery as its duty — is that a Principle?

It is, and each is a Principle of the other.

The Continuation tendency on a shared segment is generated by that segment's local generation function from both sides' contributions; it lies on both Laws' bearing paths and is a bearing quantity of both. P's contribution is a contribution entering A's bearing quantity, and A's contribution likewise. A's duty is to deliver a contribution toward σ\_A, P's duty is to deliver a contribution toward σ\_P, the two run opposite, and each takes entry into that Continuation tendency as its condition of realization; so P bears a delivery-type counter-holding duty toward A, and A toward P. Both contributions can be discharged at once — the Continuation tendency is composed from the two — so two Laws may stand as Laws simultaneously and each be a Principle of the other. This is two already formed holding-tendencies in pursuit of each other on one and the same Continuation.

If instead the two duties are result-type — each requiring that the Continuation tendency fall on its own side — then the two Reads cannot both hold within one evaluation, and at most one stands as a Law. The one that stands is a Principle of the other, and the other does not stand as a Law over that domain. A contest of result-types cannot be mutual Principle; what can be mutual is the pursuit of delivery-types.

A consequence of this adjudication: two Laws on a shared base whose duties are to deliver opposite contributions are Principles of each other. This agrees with "sedimentary competition just is Principle," and with the canonical source's "the pursuit of the two."

The General Form

What the line of argument has obtained may now be read back formally.

When the declared working domain, the inherited conditions, and the H conditions hold:

　　Law\_F,t(A, I\_A)  
　　∧ P ≠ A  
　　∧ Principle\_F,t(P; A, J)

　　⇒

　　Law\_F,t(P, I\_P)  
　　∧ CounterHoldRole\_F,t(P; A, J)

Here CounterHoldRole\_F,t(P; A, J) is not a new field quantity but a compressed notation for the relation the line of argument has already forced out. It contains at least two layers, neither deletable.

First, what the duty restricts. In the legal relational evaluations of J, A's holding-tendency actually enters bearing; and the Rel(P) through which Law(P, I\_P) was acquired carries a duty of one of the two following types.

　　Delivery-type: its Read restricts P's contribution entering A's bearing quantity — the quantity P generates which is an argument of the local generation function of A's bearing quantity — and takes the entry of that contribution into the generation of A's bearing quantity as its condition of realization: the site at which A's bearing quantity is generated lies within the necessary bearing organization by which P completes that duty, though not necessarily as its terminus. What P's duty prescribes is what it delivers to A's holding-tendency, not what A finally takes, and not what P itself puts out; compensation does not alter qualification.  
　　Result-type: its Read restricts the value of A's bearing quantity itself, and that restriction is really discharged by the same DynamicRole through which Law(P, I\_P) was acquired — P's state enters the generation chain of A's bearing quantity, and P's necessary Continuation segment bears that discharge with Ω. It does not require that P have a contribution entering A's bearing quantity directly; the route may run through threshold, impedance, Ω, or resource indifferently.

Under either type, take A away and the duty cannot be discharged. This excludes the consuming and conditioning Laws whose duties are about resources or conditions alone, with neither a contribution entering A nor a restriction on A's bearing quantity; excludes the measurement side effect, which has a contribution entering A that its duty does not restrict; excludes public modulation, whose duty restricts its own output while A happens to read it; excludes the empty declaration of a result-type, where A happens to fall within what is restricted and P bears nothing; and excludes unsedimented temporary wiring.

Second, direction. The side restricted runs counter to A's holding-direction on a dynamical degree of freedom A actually holds — and is not same-direction support, nor indifferent, nor orthogonal, nor a choice among branches A holds no direction between.

I\_A is A's own domain of Law application; I\_P is P's own domain of Law application, forced out in the first round; J is the Principle relation domain of P relative to A. The three may coincide or differ. J projects, on the A side, onto the Law coverage of I\_A, and on the P side onto the Law coverage of I\_P; that the concrete realization of P's action on A takes place inside A's internal running is no ground for rewriting J as P's own domain of Law inflow.

The Principal Theorem

When the declared entry conditions, the inherited conditions, and the H conditions hold:

if A already stands as a Law, and P acquires Principle qualification relative to A over a non-empty, pre-declared relation domain J, then: P and A are non-identical in Law identity; P has itself acquired Law qualification; and the Rel(P) through which that qualification was acquired carries a duty directed toward A's holding-tendency — either restricting P's contribution entering A's bearing quantity and taking its entry into A as the condition of realization, or restricting A's bearing quantity itself and being really discharged by the same DynamicRole — the side so restricted running counter to A's holding-direction on a degree of freedom A holds.

That is:

　　Law\_F,t(A, I\_A)  
　　∧ P ≠ A  
　　∧ Principle\_F,t(P; A, J)

　　⇒

　　Law\_F,t(P, I\_P)  
　　∧ CounterHoldRole\_F,t(P; A, J)

Only the necessary direction is proved.

The sufficient direction:

　　Law\_F,t(A, I\_A)  
　　∧ P ≠ A  
　　∧ Law\_F,t(P, I\_P)  
　　∧ CounterHoldRole\_F,t(P; A, J)

　　⇒

　　Principle\_F,t(P; A, J)

is not borne here.

A note on notation. In the formula above, "the Rel(P) through which that qualification was acquired carries a duty directed toward A's holding-tendency" refers to one fixed duty; from The Mathematical Form of Principle onward that duty is written d*. Hence the requirement of "the same established duty" is written here inside the content of CounterHoldRole, and is written explicitly as d* in the texts that follow. The two refer to the same thing; they are not two conditions.

Minimality

Only the newly added structure that the line of argument really forced out is tested here; inherited items of the working domain and manners of realization are not mixed in.

What the three rounds obtained is progressive, not parallel: what the second round obtained presupposes what the first obtained, because what a duty restricts is relative to the Read of the Rel(P) through which Law was acquired. This is of a kind with Govern containing DynamicRole in The General Form of Law.

Delete Law(P, I\_P), obtained in the first round, and with it the part of the second round that presupposes it, and one impact, one instantaneous gate, or one noise pulse may pass itself off as a Principle.

Keep P as another already standing Law but delete what the second round obtained — "Rel(P) carries a duty directed toward A's holding-tendency" — and resource consumption, conditioning Laws, measurement side effects, public modulation, and temporary wiring all come in.

Within what the second round obtained there are three separately weakenable components. Delete "restricts" and require only that P's contribution enter A's generation, and the measurement side effect comes in. Delete the delivery-type's "takes its entry into A as the condition of realization," and public modulation comes in. Delete the result-type's "really discharged by the same DynamicRole," and the empty declaration of a result-type comes in. Each has a case among the substitution failures as its witness.

Keep only the delivery-type and delete the result-type, and gating hold-breaking realized through threshold, impedance, or Ω is missed. Keep only the result-type and delete the delivery-type, and the compensated weak Principle is missed. Neither type may be dropped; yet they are two branches of one and the same "what the duty restricts," not two top-level items: delete either branch and what is missed is, in both cases, one class of legal realization of "the duty being about A's holding-tendency."

Keep what the second round obtained but delete what the third obtained — "the side restricted runs counter to A's holding-direction" — and support, repair, stabilization, strengthening, orthogonal modulation, and legal branch switching all come in.

The second and third rounds are independent: the supporting case satisfies the second and not the third; the resource-consumption case has an action direction counter to A, and does not satisfy the second.

P ≠ A and Law(A, I\_A) are working-domain restrictions and do not take part in the deletion test.

Substitution Failures

Replace counter-holding with "none of A's live successors are discharged any longer," and whether it has failed completely at present is wrongly raised into a threshold of qualification.

Replace it with "Read\_A = 0," and qualification flips with the declared threshold and granularity of the Read, conflating an external result with a relation of holding-tendency.

Replace it with "the actual successor must leave A's original support set," and the takeover case, action within the support set, and counter-holds that have not yet changed the final outcome are all missed.

Replace the duty's directedness with "P's contribution takes A's current holding-tendency state as a generative term," and the open-loop dedicated inhibitory Law is missed while the measurement load is wrongly admitted.

Replace it with "P's duty takes the breaking of A as its terminus," adjudicating the terminus by the wording of Rel, and on one carrier a change of Rel suffices to write resource conflict up as a Principle.

Replace it with "P must read A before returning into A," and open-loop dedicated inhibition, activation by a third Law, P active before acting on A, and continuous coupling are all missed.

Replace it with "P must be activated by A," and activation by a third Law and a P already active are missed.

Require P and A each to have an exclusive component, or to be physically separate, and two Laws with opposite duties on a shared base are missed.

Replace "what the duty restricts" with "P's dutiful contribution is directly an argument of the generation of A's holding-tendency," and the resource-consumption case is wrongly admitted: P consumes r, A reads r directly, and P's duty has nothing to do with A.

Replace it with "the contribution lands on A's active nodes rather than structural-state nodes," and stable gating hold-breaking is missed.

Replace it with "A's bearing quantity enters a minimal sufficient set for P's duty Read," and the measurement Law is wrongly admitted: its Read reads A without restricting A.

Write the delivery-type as "P's duty restricts the value of some quantity that P generates and that is an argument of A's bearing quantity," without requiring directedness binding, and the shared modulation bus case is wrongly admitted: P puts out −1, A happens to read it, and with A removed P is discharged just the same.

Replace it with "P's duty restricts the value of A's own bearing quantity," and the compensation case is missed: P stably delivers −1, Q compensates +1, and A does not change; P's duty can prescribe only what it delivers, not what A finally takes. This substitution also contradicts "counter-holding may not have won."

Replace it with "P's duty has the breaking of A as its purpose," and subject semantics are introduced.

Hence "state dependence," "read before returning," "exclusive component," "grafted prefix," "total cut-off," "failure of Read," "deflection outside the support set," "duty terminus," "node type," "direct argument," and "adjudicating node" may serve as realization schemes or strong witnesses in later mathematical, construction, or determination forms; but they must not be raised into necessary structures of the general form of Principle.

Breaking, Biasing, Choosing

The canonical source says:

　　"Law is the tendency of holding; Principle is the tendency that breaks the hold."  
　　"Law — repetition and depth; Principle — choosing and bias."

What follows does not set out "breaking, biasing, choosing" in advance as three parallel necessary items. What the argument finally forced out as the ontological core is a counter-holding duty directed toward A's holding-tendency.

Biasing is a possible appearance of counter-holding within an actual Continuation organization, and may show as path, weight, strength, timing, duration, or distribution; but these are appearances after counter-holding action has taken effect, not necessary read-off values of Principle qualification itself.

Choosing is the concrete outcome shown when counter-holding takes part in what comes after, where several continuable organizations exist.

"Biasing" and "choosing" add no further top-level condition to the general form.

Principle and Law-Change

What is adjudicated here is this: whether there exists a P that itself already stands as a Law, is non-identical with A in Law identity, and whose Law duty prescribes its contribution entering the bearing quantity A is currently exercising (or that bearing quantity itself), restricted to the side A does not hold.

The Law-Dynamics Group-Form later handles a Law's generation, strengthening, shallowing, drift, competition, replacement, recombination, and loss. Such changes may involve no Principle at all. Abrading a structure at rest, blocking an A that has not yet started, consuming the resource A depends on, altering the conditions A depends on so that A is damaged — all belong to law-change or conflict, not to Principle. One and the same action of altering a threshold, an impedance, or Ω is law-change if the duty restricts the threshold, impedance, or Ω itself, and is Principle if the duty restricts the Continuation tendency A is currently holding.

The following boundaries must be stated.

Normalization and lateral inhibition. If A and Q each take as their duty the delivery of opposite-direction contributions into the same Continuation tendency, each is a Principle of the other, and normalization of the result does not annul this. If instead each takes as its duty that that Continuation tendency fall on its own side (result-type), then at most one stands as a Law, and they cannot be Principles of each other. If A and Q merely consume the same resource, there is no Principle.

Several degrees of freedom. The quantity restricted by P's duty may be with, indifferent to, and counter to A on several degrees of freedom A holds, all at once. So long as it is counter on some degree of freedom A actually holds, the direction condition of the present form holds. This accords with "counter-holding may be very weak"; its consequence is that Principle relations are common in complex coupling, and the strength of a Principle relation belongs to The Grading Form of Principle.

Side effects and Law identity. The load of a measurement Law P is not a Principle under P's Law identity (Rel(P): A ↦ z, I\_P). If a further Law P′ is declared whose Rel lists "loading A" as a duty, and that load really has the witness of Established ∧ Govern, then P′ under its Law identity is a weak Principle of A. This is no play on words: whether P′ stands as a Law is a fact of the field. Principle qualification is adjudicated by Law identity, just as Law qualification is adjudicated by Rel and I; nothing ontological is added here for the purpose.

Dominant Principle. What the present form adjudicates is P's Principle relation to A while A still holds Law qualification. A result-type Principle coexisting with Law(A) requires that what is restricted not touch the Read of A's own duty: A holds toward the high side, its Read holds above w, P restricts A below v with v ≥ w — A still stands as a Law and P is a Principle; with v \< w, A loses Law over that domain. The delivery-type carries no such restriction, since it prescribes no value for A. If P's counter-hold comes to dominate over a long period, so that A's DynamicRole or some other necessary Law qualification on I\_A ∩ J really lapses, then from the moment A exits Law qualification, Principle(P;A,J) no longer holds by the present form. This is no omission of a strong Principle; it is that Principle-action has already destroyed its own target relational object. If P stably overpowers A at the very moment P stands as a Law, so that A's Law lapses at that moment, there was no interval in which the Principle relation existed, and the case belongs to law-change and replacement. The Principle-action before that, A's lapse, and the formation of new relations afterwards belong to the tasks of Principle-dynamics and Law-dynamics; among them, the generation reachability and generation attribution of a new Law are later borne by The Reachability Form of Principle Generation and The Determination Form of Principle Generation, while the remaining items have no form-text to date.

Hence: law-change ≠ Principle. A Principle may take part in law-change, and may even cost the target Law its qualification; but the terminal state after that lapse cannot define Principle in reverse, nor can it retroactively annul a Principle relation that had already stood.

Principle and the New Law

A Principle is not the new Law produced after it acts.

Let A be the old Law and P a Principle relative to A. After P acts on A, the current actual Continuation forms a new relation B\_t. Then A supplies the old hold, P supplies the tendency that breaks it, and B\_t is the actual Continuation result obtained after the pursuit of the two.

So it must be kept that

　　A ≠ P ≠ B\_t.

Whether B\_t subsequently acquires Law(B) is proved by later form-texts. What follows does not trade "Principle-action generates a new Law" for "Principle just is the new Law."

Principle and Intention

What follows has used no subject, goal, value, evaluation, intention, thought, representation, or argmin.

So the most primitive Principle may be generated before any subject or intention. Intention may later activate, organize, or combine Principles already formed, without becoming an antecedent of the general form of Principle.

The generative interface

　　Law → Principle → intention

is left open.

H\_P1　The reduction bridge for what a duty restricts

It has been forced out above that the Rel(P) through which Law(P, I\_P) was acquired must carry a duty directed toward A's holding-tendency: either restricting P's contribution entering A's bearing quantity and taking its entry into A as the condition of realization (delivery-type), or restricting A's bearing quantity itself and being really discharged by the same DynamicRole (result-type).

The Mathematical Form of Principle must give the formal reduction of the two types over Type(d), Read(d), JointReal, RolePath, ActArg^μ, ΩWit, Hosts, and the update rules of The Mathematical Form of Field: taking as A's bearing quantity the nodes generated by local generation functions on A's bearing path, with quantities generated by field rules such as resource occupancy excluded; taking as P's contribution entering A the nodes "generated by P and belonging to ActArg^μ of some bearing quantity of A," with quantities P writes into structural-state updates excluded; adjudicating "restricts" by whether the projection of each Rel(P) duty's Read satisfaction set onto that contribution (delivery-type) or onto A's bearing quantity (result-type) is a proper subset. For the result-type it must further adjudicate, by DynamicRole and ΩWit, that the restriction is discharged by P — P's state lying in ActArg^μ of A's bearing-quantity generation chain, and P's necessary Continuation segment for completing that Read having Ω — rather than A's happening to fall within what is restricted. It must be proved that the following cases fall as adjudicated — the first five excluded, the last three admitted or adjudicated by Law identity:

　　resource consumption and conditioning Laws (P's write enters the update of r or θ, with no contribution entering A's bearing quantity; the duty does not restrict A's bearing quantity either; take A away and the duty is discharged just the same);  
　　measurement side effects (there is a contribution entering A, but the projection of the satisfaction set onto that contribution is the whole range);  
　　empty declaration of a result-type (the Read restricts A's bearing quantity, but P's state does not enter its generation chain, and A's falling within what is restricted has nothing to do with P; Law(P) does not hold for that Rel);  
　　public modulation (a quantity P generates is an argument of A's bearing quantity and the Read restricts its value, but the site of A's bearing-quantity generation lies outside P's necessary bearing organization and the Read does not take entry into A as its condition of realization; take A away and the duty is discharged just the same);  
　　temporary wiring (what the duty restricts lies elsewhere; a new Rel taking A as what is restricted fails to stand as a Law because the access is instantaneous);  
　　duties continuing after delivery (P's duty, after delivering a contribution to A, goes on to generate further quantities by way of A's bearing quantity; the site of A's generation lies within its necessary bearing organization without being the terminus; admitted);  
　　delivery-type under compensation (the contribution is compensated by another Law and A's bearing quantity does not change; admitted);  
　　another Law identity on the same carrier (one directed toward A's holding-tendency with an independent witness is a Principle under that identity).

The reduction of "restricts" must not be implemented as "delete some contribution of P and the Read fails": two redundant counter-holding contributions of P each really bear, and H3 has already dealt with this point. What this bridge adjudicates is the satisfaction set of the Read, not the deletion counterfactual of a contribution.

The reduction must take up H3's distinction between bearing and correlation, the discipline of The Mathematical Form of Law that local generation admits no substitution-elimination, and Bind's closure over witness families. It must not be traded for "P's contribution must depend on A's state," "P must be activated by A," "read before returning," "the contribution must land on nodes of some class," or "P has the breaking of A as its purpose."

Status: pending closure.

H\_P2　The bridge for holding-direction and counter-holding direction

It has been proved above that counter-holding is not synonymous with "the final duty fails"; and that "not supporting the hold" is not counter-holding either, since there remains the indifferent or orthogonal modulation.

The Mathematical Form of Principle must give, for each of A's duty types, the formal reference for its actual holding-direction — path, weight, strength, timing, and duration each have their own reference, and what upstream makes available are the direction indicated by A's Ω-supported Continuation segment and the sign of the Continuation tendency — and must make same-direction support, indifferent modulation, and counter-directed hold-breaking mutually separable.

Direction is evaluated on the quantity restricted by P's duty. Where what is restricted is the contribution entering A, by the sign with which that contribution lands on the degree of freedom A holds through the local generation function of that bearing quantity of A — one step, A's own function, not substitution across carriers. Where what is restricted is A's bearing quantity, by the relation between A's holding-direction and the side into which the duty holds A (the side on which the satisfaction set lies, not the side excluded). The realization route of a result-type duty does not enter the adjudication of direction.

It must also declare how direction is aggregated within one legal evaluation: A's holding-direction may change sign within a single evaluation, and the quantity restricted by P's duty may be now counter and now with within that same evaluation. Whether the adjudication is moment-by-moment, existential, or over the whole stretch must be fixed as one of these.

Whether the final outcome changes must not be made a necessary condition of counter-holding qualification.

Status: pending closure.

Breakdown Conditions

Any one of the following breaks through the necessary structure established above.

Construct a case that is plainly a Principle by the semantics of the canonical source, in which: P and A are not two distinguishable Laws; or P lacks Law qualification; or, in a relational evaluation where A's holding-tendency legally enters bearing, Rel(P) has no duty bound in directedness to A that restricts P's contribution entering A's bearing quantity, and no duty really discharged by its DynamicRole that restricts A's bearing quantity; or the side restricted does not run counter to A's holding-direction on a degree of freedom A holds.

Construct a case in which A already stands as a Law, P is another already standing Law, Rel(P) carries a delivery-type or result-type duty, and the side restricted is counter to A's holding-direction — all holding — yet the relation is plainly not a Principle by the semantics of the canonical source, and does not fall under the declared boundaries (several degrees of freedom, mutual Principle on a shared base, side effects and Law identity, dominant Principle).

Prove that "the projection of a Read's satisfaction set onto P's contribution entering A, or onto A's bearing quantity" has no objective truth value; or that "the site at which A's bearing quantity is generated belongs to P's necessary bearing organization" has no objective locus in ΩWit; or that "A's bearing quantity" cannot be characterized on a shared base.

Prove that any one item among the structures forced out in the three rounds can be wholly absorbed by the others, so that the original task remains closed after its deletion.

Prove that, beyond the structures forced out above, there exists a further independent necessary structure that they cannot absorb and that belongs neither to the H conditions, nor to realization specification, dynamical strength, or the tasks of later form-texts.

Prove that "a duty's Read restricts some quantity" has no objective truth value over Rel(P), that is, that H\_P1 cannot be closed.

The following do not constitute breakdown: that some concrete realization is not at present computable; that CounterHoldRole cannot be adjudicated from the observations at hand; which of P and A finally dominates in some particular run; whether P's counter-hold has already changed the final duty value; when a new Continuation acquires Law qualification after Principle-action; how a Principle relation persists, shallows, transfers, or terminates. These belong respectively to the later mathematical, determination, necessity, and dynamics forms.

Final Compression

The General Form of Law has already shown that a Law, by a relation already formed, really and stably bears what comes after.

Further obtained:

Principle has no second kind of qualification for being formed. A Principle is first of all a Law in full. What it adds over an ordinary Law is neither another kind of tendency, nor the result of "having already defeated another Law."

What it adds is a dynamical relation between Laws: the duty of one already standing Law is directed toward the holding-tendency another already standing Law is currently exercising — either prescribing that it deliver a counter-directed contribution into that holding-tendency, or holding that holding-tendency to the side it does not hold. Principle qualification does not take as necessary whether that holding-tendency finally changes: a delivery-type duty is still discharged when its contribution is compensated; a result-type duty is not discharged when what it restricts does not come about, but qualification adjudicates what the duty restricts, not who won this time.

Hence:

　　A Principle is a Law;  
　　by its own hold it breaks the hold of another.

I. Boundaries declared

Principle qualification lies in what the duty restricts, in two branches, delivery-type and result-type. If P's duty restricts its contribution entering A and takes entry into A as its condition of realization, it is a Principle even if fully compensated. If P's duty restricts A's bearing quantity itself and is really discharged by its DynamicRole, it is a Principle. If P's duty restricts only a threshold, only a resource, or only its own output, and A happens to be damaged, it is not a Principle.

Resource consumption is not a Principle, with or without an adjudicating step; another Law identity on the same carrier, directed toward A's holding-tendency and independently witnessed, is a Principle under that identity.

Two Laws on a shared base whose duties are to deliver opposite contributions into the same Continuation tendency are Principles of each other; a contest of result-types admits at most one winner and cannot be mutual.

A side effect is not a Principle; another Law identity taking the side effect as its duty, if witnessed, is a weak Principle under that identity.

What Principle interferes with is the holding-tendency A is currently holding, by any route; abrasion while A is at rest, and blocking an A that has not yet started, belong to law-change.

A dominant Principle terminates from the moment A loses Law; one that overpowers at the very moment it stands as a Law belongs to law-change.

Counterexamples are called "cases" throughout. "World" is not a term of On Self-Manifestation and is not used below.

II. What is not done

The sufficient direction is not proved. No formal system is built. The persistence, shallowing, detachment, and termination of a Principle relation are not treated. The degree of counter-holding is not treated. The generative conditions and delay antecedents of Principle are not treated. Cross-temporal identity of a Law is not defined.
