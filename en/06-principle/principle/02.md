# The Mathematical Form of Principle

## Section One　Purpose

The General Form of Principle has already given the structural conditions of Principle: P is first of all a Law in full; within a pre-given relation domain J, one and the same established duty of P is directed toward the holding-tendency another Law A is currently exercising, and forms a counter-hold on at least one dynamical degree of freedom A actually holds. What the duty restricts is of two kinds: restricting the contribution P actually delivers into the generation of A's bearing quantity, or restricting A's bearing quantity itself or its legal result description.

The problem this Mathematical Form treats is:

　how to write the structure of "bearing a counter-holding duty toward another Law" as a formal system that can be evaluated item by item, run, adjudicated, and read back.

The problem has three formal gaps that must be closed together.

　First, how to adjudicate that P's duty really restricts "the contribution entering A" or "A's bearing result," rather than merely restricting P's own ordinary output, altering some threshold or resource, correlating with A by accident, or simply renaming A's own role as P.

　Second, how to obtain A's holding-direction from the dynamical role A itself has already established and is currently bearing, and then adjudicate whether P's duty restriction is with it, indifferent to it, or counter to it; one must not infer A's holding-direction backwards from "P makes some number smaller," nor delete counter-holding qualification because some other action has compensated P's counter-directed contribution.

　Third, how to guarantee, within the relation domain J, that A's holding-tendency, P's duty restriction, the counter-holding direction, DynamicRole, ΩWit, and Bind all come from one and the same legal joint evaluation and one and the same fixed duty — forbidding evidence drawn severally from different evaluations, different duties, or different witnesses and then assembled into one Principle relation.

The structural input is written:

　S = On Self-Manifestation · Principle Chapter · Principle Group-Form I v1.0 · The General Form of Principle.

The formal output is written:

　M = the Principle formal system.

The mapping is:

　S ↦ M.

The core output of M is:

　CounterHoldRole^M\_F,t(P;A,J),

which gives the formal qualification, with its certificate, for "one and the same established duty of P forms a counter-hold against A's actual holding-tendency within J."

This form-text therefore establishes:

　the formal criterion for what a duty restricts;  
　the formal criterion for A's holding-direction and P's counter-holding direction;  
　the joint evaluation of J together with same-duty and same-witness closure;  
　a three-valued solution procedure;  
　the two-way representation and read-back rules with respect to The General Form of Principle.

M formalizes only the necessary structure The General Form of Principle has already obtained; it does not rewrite the necessary direction as the sufficient direction. Inferring

　Principle(P;A,J)

from

　Law(P,I\_P)  
　∧ CounterHoldRole^M(P;A,J)

is the task of The Necessity Form of Principle.

## Section Two　The Core Thesis

Fix:

　F\_t　the current Sedimentation-Field;  
　A　the target Law;  
　P　the candidate Principle;  
　I\_A　A's domain of Law application;  
　I\_P　P's pre-declared domain of Law application;  
　J　P's pre-declared relation domain relative to A.

Required:

　Law\_F,t(A,I\_A)=1,  
　P≠A,  
　I\_P≠∅,  
　J≠∅.

The non-identity of P and A is non-identity of Law identity; it does not require physical separation of carriers.

The formal structure of a Principle relation has three layers.

The first layer is what the duty restricts.

P must first acquire Law(P,I\_P). Within the Rel(P) through which that Law qualification was acquired, there exists a fixed duty d which, in the legal relational evaluations of J, forms a delivery-type or result-type restriction. The restriction must not be counterfeited by the applicability conditions of Ante, and must not be raised after the fact out of P's ordinary public output, resource consumption, threshold change, or other dynamical consequence.

The second layer is the counter-holding direction.

A's holding-direction must not be inferred backwards from P's action. It must first be obtained from A's own established DynamicRole, actual bearing, and Ω support within the current relational evaluation; only then is the direction of P's restriction adjudicated, and only on degrees of freedom A already actually holds.

The third layer is closure of the relation domain.

The A side and the P side within J must come from one and the same joint evaluation. P's Law, duty d, route of action, counter-holding direction, Ω, and Bind must not be drawn severally from different evaluations, different duties, or different witness families.

The formal goal is therefore:

　CounterHoldRole\_F,t(P;A,J)  
　↦  
　CounterHoldRole^M\_F,t(P;A,J)

closing, respectively, the three formal bridges left by The General Form of Principle:

　H\_P1　the reduction bridge for what a duty restricts;  
　H\_P2　the bridge for holding-direction and counter-holding direction;  
　H\_P3　the relation-domain bridge.

### 3.2　Joint relational context

Definition 3.1　\[S\]〔relational context〕

An element of J is written:

　j=(Ctx\_J,Π\_A,Π\_P).

Here:

　Ctx\_J

is the joint evaluation context;

　Π\_A, Π\_P

are the pre-fixed projection specifications on the A side and the P side.

J must be fixed before the success or failure of CounterHoldRole is adjudicated.

J, Π\_A, and Π\_P must not be modified after a failing evaluation has appeared.

Definition 3.2　\[aux\]〔joint evaluation family〕

For j∈J:

　Γ\_J(j):=Eval(Ctx\_J(j)).

If Type requires measure semantics, then:

　𝔈\_J(j):=(Γ\_J(j),Σ\_J(j),μ\_J(j))

is induced by the local and joint generation kernels of that same Ctx\_J.

The A side and the P side must not each generate an Eval independently and then take the Cartesian product.

Definition 3.3　\[aux\]〔joint projection〕

For η∈Γ\_J(j):

　π\_A^j(η), π\_P^j(η)

are the legal restrictions of η under Π\_A and Π\_P respectively.

JointProj\_j(η)=1 if and only if:

　(i) the shared structural-state values on the two sides are the same;  
　(ii) the shared resource and gating values are the same;  
　(iii) no joint node is independently resampled on the two sides;  
　(iv) measure-type projections come from the marginal or pushforward of the same μ\_J;  
　(v) there is no joint state manufactured out of a side projection.

If a joint node or shared quantity cannot be adjudicated, then JointProj\_j(η)=⊥.

Declaration of relational properties:

　π\_A^j and π\_P^j are maps, not binary relations;  
　JointProj\_j is a three-valued predicate; reflexivity, symmetry, antisymmetry, and transitivity are not discussed for it.

### 3.4　Direct entry

Definition 3.6　\[aux\]〔direct entry〕

For a contribution block C of P:

　DirectIn\_A(C,y\_A,η)∈{1,0,⊥}.

Here:

　DirectIn\_A=1

if and only if the contribution variables read by the duty within C belong to the actual input list, in η, of the original local generation function or original local generation kernel of y\_A:

　C⊆Arg\_η(y\_A)

or the corresponding input set in kernel form.

　DirectIn\_A=0

if and only if it can be determined that C does not belong to that original generative input.

　DirectIn\_A=⊥

if and only if the identity of the input is itself not adjudicable.

DirectIn answers only "does it enter the original generation directly."

It does not replace Arg with a minimal sufficient set, a Type coarsening, or necessity after compensation.

### 3.5　Result description

Definition 3.7　\[aux\]〔pure result description chain〕

Let:

　y\_A=z₀ → z₁ → … → z\_n.

If for every i≥1:

　Arg(z\_i)  
　⊆  
　{z₀,…,z\_{i-1}} ∪ Const\_j,

where Const\_j contains only structural constants fixed within j, and contains none of:

　the previous-moment state of z\_i itself;  
　dynamical nodes of other Laws;  
　competing inputs outside that description chain;  
　new gates;  
　independent threshold states;  
　other field quantities that carry an independent dynamical degree of freedom varying with the evaluation,

then z\_n is called a pure result description of y\_A, written:

　z\_n∈Desc\_A(y\_A;j).

A description node is permitted to read earlier time-unfolded nodes on the chain; hence derivatives, differences, and fixed-window temporal functionals may still belong to Desc, so long as they introduce no new independent dynamical degree of freedom.

If any node on the chain introduces an independent state, competition, an input from another Law, or its own dynamical update, the description chain terminates there.

Parallel recomputation:

　y\_A=f(a,q,c),  
　z=g(a,q,c)

does not yield z∈Desc\_A(y\_A;j), even where z≡y\_A.

Declaration of relational properties:

　Desc\_A is reflexive under the zero-length-chain convention;  
　Desc\_A is transitive;  
　Desc\_A is in general not symmetric;  
　Desc\_A is in general not antisymmetric.

Lemma 3.1〔pullback of a result description〕

If z=h(y\_A) with z∈Desc\_A(y\_A;j), and the satisfaction set of Read on z is S\_z, then its result restriction on y\_A is equivalent to:

　S\_y=h⁻¹(S\_z).

If S\_y=X\_A^+, then Read imposes no real restriction on y\_A.

Proof.

At every level of a pure result description chain there is no new generative argument beyond the predecessors and fixed constants, so all variation in z is obtained from variation in y\_A through fixed maps. The satisfaction condition of Read(z) can therefore be pulled back to the result domain of y\_A. If the preimage is the whole domain, then every result of y\_A satisfies that Read, and no result restriction exists. □

### 3.6　Delivery-type restriction

Definition 3.8　\[S\]〔what a delivery-type duty restricts〕

Fix one duty d in Rel(P).

DeliveryLimit\_d(P;A,j,η)=1 if and only if:

　(i) the object restricted by Read(d) is the contribution block C;  
　(ii) Read(d) forms a real restriction on the correct Type domain of C;  
　(iii) DirectIn\_A(C,y\_A,η)=1;  
　(iv) Read(d) takes C's entry into the generation of y\_A as its condition of realization;  
　(v) within that same η there exists an actual duty chain running from P's duty role through C and y\_A to Read(d).

Joint restriction is permitted:

　C=(c₁,…,c\_m).

It is not required that each c\_i be restricted on its own, nor that every route be irreplaceable.

Definition 3.9　\[aux\]〔delivery-type failure〕

DeliveryLimit\_d=0 if any of the following holds:

　Read restricts only the public output C without requiring that C be delivered into y\_A;  
　"delivered into y\_A" is written only in Ante(d);  
　C reaches Read only through a parallel same-valued recomputation node;  
　C influences y\_A only indirectly through multi-level state updates, without belonging to Arg\_η(y\_A).

If the key generative relation is not adjudicable, then DeliveryLimit\_d=⊥.

### 3.7　Result-type restriction

Definition 3.10　\[S\]〔what a result-type duty restricts〕

ResultLimit\_d(P;A,j,η)=1 if and only if:

　(i) Read(d) reads y\_A, Desc\_A(y\_A;j), or a distribution, weight, or expectation functional already declared by its Type;  
　(ii) pulling Read back to X\_A^+ yields a real restriction S\_d⊊X\_A^+;  
　(iii) the DynamicRole of the same duty d through which Law(P,I\_P) was acquired actually enters the formation chain of y\_A or of its pure result description;  
　(iv) that actualization belongs to one and the same legal duty realization and to the Ω/Bind witness.

If Read restricts only θ, a resource, a threshold, or P's own output, and these subsequently influence y\_A, ResultLimit must not be inferred from the dynamical consequence.

Definition 3.11　\[aux\]〔consequences must not be raised〕

If:

　Read(d)=R(θ)

while:

　θ→…→y\_A,

and Read has not declared y\_A or Desc\_A(y\_A;j) in advance as its result object, then:

　ResultLimit\_d(P;A,j,η)=0

even where:

　R(θ)=1 ⇒ y\_A∈S

holds identically in that dynamical system.

3.8　The restricted part and role non-degeneracy

Definition 3.12　\[aux\]〔restricted part〕

LimitPart\_d^A is exactly that semantic part of duty d used to obtain DeliveryLimit or ResultLimit.

In the delivery-type it consists of:

　the real restriction on the contribution C;  
　C's direct entry into y\_A;  
　the corresponding discharge of the duty.

In the result-type it consists of:

　the real restriction on y\_A or Desc\_A(y\_A;j);  
　the actual discharge of that restriction by the same duty of P.

Diagnostics, side branches, additional satisfiable clauses, and unused edges unrelated to that restriction do not enter LimitPart.

Definition 3.13　\[aux\]〔Type-sensitive sufficient role set〕

Fix j, η, d, and one and the same legal realization bundle Π.

Let RoleEvents\_d(η,Π) be the full set of role events of duty d available, in that realization, for LimitPart.

For G⊆RoleEvents\_d(η,Π), define:

　Suff\_T(G;LimitPart\_d^A,η)=1

if and only if, under the original semantics of Type(d) and Read(d), G suffices for LimitPart to acquire, within η, the qualification of being discharged.

Define the minimal sufficient role sets:

　MinRoleSuff\_T  
　:=  
　{G \|  
　 Suff\_T(G)=1  
　 ∧ ¬∃G'⊊G, Suff\_T(G')=1}.

Set-type is adjudicated under set semantics;

measure-type under the probability, weight, or expectation semantics of the same μ\_J and Read;

temporal and durational types under their original window and quantifier semantics.

Definition 3.14　\[aux\]〔A's own roles〕

Let:

　D\_A(y\_A,η)

be the full set of duties by which A jointly bears y\_A in η.

For each:

　d\_A∈D\_A(y\_A,η),

define:

　Self\_A(η,y\_A;d\_A)

as the set of role events A uses, under duty d\_A, to acquire and actually execute the current holding-tendency of y\_A.

Define the total bearing role set:

　Self\_A(η,y\_A)  
　:=  
　⋃\_{d\_A∈D\_A(y\_A,η)}  
　Self\_A(η,y\_A;d\_A).

RoleNonDegenerate is compared against the total bearing role set Self\_A(η,y\_A), not against some single duty of A alone.

If the candidate P is merely one of A's bearing sub-duties redeclared as another Law, whether P≠A still holds is governed by the representation antecedent M8 and by the upstream task of Law identity; a difference of duty name alone must not be used to bypass that antecedent.

Definition 3.15　\[aux\]〔role non-degeneracy〕

RoleNonDegenerate\_d(P,A,j,η,Π)=1

if and only if there exist:

　G∈MinRoleSuff\_T(LimitPart\_d^A,η)

and q∈G such that:

　q∉Self\_A(η,y\_A).

RoleNonDegenerate excludes P's being a pure relabelling of A.

It does not require that P and A be physically separate carriers, nor that q alone be irreplaceable.

Lemma 3.2〔a zero-contribution private edge yields no non-degeneracy〕

If P's actual H\_P1 action on y\_A wholly reuses Self\_A(η,y\_A), with only a private event q besides, and q contributes nothing to LimitPart, then:

　RoleNonDegenerate\_d=0.

Proof.

q does not alter the discharge qualification of LimitPart, so any sufficient role set containing q remains sufficient once q is deleted. Hence q belongs to no minimal sufficient role set. If all other events belong to Self\_A(η,y\_A), then no minimal sufficient set containing a non-Self\_A event exists. □

### 3.9　Fixed duty and witness family

Definition 3.16　\[S\]〔fixed counter-holding duty〕

A candidate Principle relation may use only one fixed duty in Rel(P):

　d\*∈Duties(Rel(P)).

d\* is fixed across the whole of J.

Different j may be borne by different legal carrier solutions R;

within one j, different evaluations may select different Π from the same Bind family;

but different duties d must not be assembled across different relational evaluations into one counter-holding duty.

Definition 3.17　\[aux\]〔Bind family〕

Fix:

　R∈𝒮(F\_t,P,I\_P),  
　d\*∈Duties(Rel(P)).

Let:

　ℱ\_B(P,d\*;R)

be the realization family to which d\* belongs within the legal Scheme/ΩWit/Bind of the carrier solution R.

For a legal evaluation η within one and the same relational context j, the Π\_η used must satisfy:

　Π\_η∈ℱ\_B(P,d\*;R).

The duty restriction of H\_P1, the direction of H\_P2, RoleNonDegenerate, and Ω/Bind must be conjoined on one and the same Π\_η.

Different j may select different legal R\_j, and with R\_j the corresponding ℱ\_B(P,d\*;R\_j); this does not constitute cross-evaluation assembly. Within one j, R and ℱ\_B are fixed, and neither the carrier solution nor the realization family may be changed from one η to the next.

Definition 3.19a　\[aux\]〔A-k one-step induced generation map and role output vectors〕

Fix j, η, and k∈K\_A(j).

Define:

　G^{A,k}\_{j,η}(x;a,p)

as follows: in the remaining background of fixed j and η, taking x as the current state of the k-degree of freedom, connect A's same Law, same duty, and same DynamicRole into the original local generation function or local generation kernel of y\_A, where a is the full vector of A's current relevant duty-role outputs and p the full vector of P's current relevant duty-role outputs; the result is then projected onto the k-degree of freedom, giving the one-step result. If the original generation of y\_A does not read its own previous value, then G^{A,k}\_{j,η} does not depend on x.

Define:

　Out\_A(j,η)

as the full vector of A-side duty-role outputs entering A's current relevant local generation interface in the current joint evaluation η;

　RestOut\_A(j,η)

as the full vector of structural rest outputs at that same local interface, under the current Estab\_t, when all of A's relevant duty roles are at rest;

　Out\_P(j,η)

as the full vector of P-side duty-role outputs entering A's current relevant local generation interface in the current joint evaluation η;

　RestOut\_P(j,η)

as the full vector of structural rest outputs at that same local interface, under the current Estab\_t, when all of P's relevant duty roles are at rest.

Define further the state-parametrized recomputed output with P at rest:

　Out\_A^{P←rest}(j,η;x)

as follows: taking x as the state of the k-degree of freedom, set all of P's relevant duty-role outputs to RestOut\_P(j,η), keep the remaining non-P background consistent with the current j and η, and let A's own same RoleReal / local generation interface re-obtain, at x, the full vector of A-side duty-role outputs.

Out\_A^{P←rest} is neither the current Out\_A frozen as it stands, nor a value recomputed at x\_cur and then frozen and carried to some other state; it must be re-evaluated at the very state x under discussion, under the P-at-rest reference. If the original generation of y\_A does not read its own previous value, then Out\_A^{P←rest}(j,η;x) does not depend on x and is written Out\_A^{P←rest}(j,η). If that re-evaluation is not adjudicable, then A's corresponding holding-direction reference is ⊥.

G^{A,k}\_{j,η}, Out\_A, Out\_A^{P←rest}, RestOut\_A, Out\_P, and RestOut\_P only rewrite the already existing upstream G\_y / local generation kernel, Type(d), RoleReal, and Estab\_t; they add no dynamical object.

If any load-bearing rest output cannot be obtained from the current structure or from a declared interface, the corresponding direction value is ⊥, and no mathematically neutral element may be filled in at will.

Definition 3.20　\[aux\]〔one-step self-holding relation〕

Fix j, η, and k∈K\_A(j).

First divide by type, according to whether the original generation of y\_A reads its own previous value.

(i) Temporal type

If the next-step generation of y\_A reads its own previous value — that is, if its original generative input list contains the prior state of y\_A — then the holding-direction of k is obtained solely from A's own actual Continuation.

Define:

　x →^dyn\_{A,k\|j} y

if and only if there is a legal j-embedding of x such that A's same Law, same duty, and same DynamicRole actually continue at x; where, in computing that Continuation:

　A's relevant duty-role outputs are taken as Out\_A^{P←rest}(j,η;x),  
　all of P's relevant duty-role outputs are taken as RestOut\_P(j,η),  
　the remaining non-A, non-P background is fixed as in the current j and η;

and the resulting next state on the k-degree of freedom is y.

If, under the reference RestOut\_P(j,η), A's role output cannot enter the generation of y\_A — the channel is closed under that rest reference, the interface is absent, or the original generation does not hold under it — then A has no self-holding Continuation on that k, and the Continuation result from x is written †; the background decay or drift that then occurs must not be written as an edge of A's Continuation.

For the temporal type:

　→\_{A,k\|j}  
　:=  
　→^dyn\_{A,k\|j}.

The temporal type adds no further local-contribution-effect edges. Recovery, hysteresis, capacity saturation, and the Ω ceiling are all settled by the real Continuation; if A does not move at a saturation point, no extra holding-direction edge is generated.

(ii) One-shot generation type

If the original generation of y\_A does not read its own previous value, state transition cannot give A's holding-direction; here only the contribution effect of A's own duty role is used.

Define A's role contribution effect on k:

　Δ^role\_{A,k}(j,η)  
　:=  
　G^{A,k}\_{j,η}  
　(  
　 x\_ref;  
　 Out\_A^{P←rest}(j,η),  
　 RestOut\_P(j,η)  
　)  
　−  
　G^{A,k}\_{j,η}  
　(  
　 x\_ref;  
　 RestOut\_A(j,η),  
　 RestOut\_P(j,η)  
　).

In the one-shot generation type the original generation does not read the previous value of y\_A, so x\_ref serves only as a uniform interface placeholder and must not affect Δ^role; if in the actual realization the result does depend on x\_ref, that instance does not belong to the one-shot generation type.

If Δ^role\_{A,k}(j,η) is adjudicable and non-zero, then:

　x →^loc\_{A,k\|j} y

if and only if:

　y−x ∈ cone({Δ^role\_{A,k}(j,η)})\\{0}.

For the one-shot generation type:

　→\_{A,k\|j}  
　:=  
　→^loc\_{A,k\|j}.

If Δ^role=0, then A forms no direction edge on that degree of freedom through its own duty role.

Changes caused by noise, by other Laws, by external perturbation, or by background drift not belonging to A's current duty role must not be written into →^dyn or →^loc.

(iii) Relational properties

In both types the base relation → is in general:

　not reflexive;  
　not symmetric;  
　not antisymmetric;  
　not transitive.

The temporal type and the one-shot generation type select their holding-direction generator exclusively; the two classes of edge are not unioned.

Definition 3.21　\[aux\]〔self-holding reachability preorder〕

Define:

　x ≼\_{A,k\|j} y

if and only if x=y, or there is a finite chain:

　x=x₀ → x₁ → … → x\_n=y.

Relational properties:

　≼\_{A,k\|j} is reflexive;  
　≼\_{A,k\|j} is transitive;  
　in general not symmetric;  
　in general not antisymmetric.

It is therefore a preorder, and not by default a partial order.

Define the strict part:

　x ≺\_{A,k\|j} y  
　⇔  
　x ≼\_{A,k\|j} y  
　∧ ¬(y ≼\_{A,k\|j} x).

Relational properties:

　≺\_{A,k\|j} is irreflexive;  
　≺\_{A,k\|j} is asymmetric;  
　≺\_{A,k\|j} is transitive.

Define:

　x \~\_{A,k\|j} y  
　⇔  
　x≼y ∧ y≼x.

Relational properties:

　\~ is reflexive;  
　\~ is symmetric;  
　\~ is transitive.

\~ is therefore an equivalence relation.

3.12　j-relative holding-direction terminals

Definition 3.22　\[aux\]〔current attractor class and unbounded holding-direction〕

Let A's current state on degree of freedom k in η be x\_cur.

Let:

　Reach\_A^+(x\_cur)  
　:=  
　{y∈X^leg\_{A,k}(j) \| x\_cur≼\_{A,k\|j}y}.

On the quotient:

　Reach\_A^+(x\_cur)/\~

take the set of maximal equivalence classes:

　Att\_k(j,η)  
　:=  
　Max(Reach\_A^+(x\_cur)/\~).

Let:

　UAtt\_k(j,η)  
　:=  
　⋃ Att\_k(j,η)

be the union of the elements of all maximal classes.

If Reach\_A^+ contains an unbounded strictly ascending chain that is dominated by no maximal class — there is no τ∈Att\_k(j,η) such that every element of the chain is ≼\_{A,k\|j} some element of τ — and no legal limit class takes it in, then write:

　Unbd\_k(j,η)=1.

Att\_k non-empty and Unbd\_k=1 may hold at once on different branches of Reach\_A^+: one branch converging on a maximal class, another ascending without bound. The two are evaluated separately and adjudicated together by Definition 3.23(iii).

In the one-shot generation type, Unbd\_k=1 where Δ^role≠0 is the normal case, not a defect: an A that generates in one shot has no terminal, only a direction.

If neither Att can be obtained nor an undominated unbounded strict ascent proved, the direction information is written ⊥.

Relational properties:

　Att\_k and UAtt\_k are set-valued objects;  
　Unbd\_k is a three-valued predicate;  
　neither is a binary relation.

In a continuous dynamical realization, where a declared limit set exists, the corresponding limit equivalence class may be used to realize Att.

### 3.13　Result-type counter-holding

Definition 3.23　\[S\]〔result-type counter-holding degree of freedom〕

Define the comparison domain:

　D\_k(j)  
　:=  
　pr\_k(X\_A^+) ∩ X^leg\_{A,k}(j).

Let the legal satisfaction set of a result-type duty, pulled back and projected onto k, be:

　S\_k⊆D\_k(j).

Result direction divides into a finite-terminal component and an unbounded component, evaluated separately and adjudicated together at the end.

(i) Finite-terminal component

If Att\_k is adjudicable and non-empty, first check:

　UAtt\_k(j,η) = {†}.

If this holds, it says that under the reference in which all of P's roles are at rest and A's output has been re-evaluated as Out\_A^{P←rest}, A forms no successor of its own holding-direction on that k. Then:

　FiniteNoDir\_k=1,

and that finite-terminal component constitutes no Anti.

If:

　UAtt\_k(j,η) ≠ {†},

then define:

　FiniteAnti\_k=1

if and only if:

　S\_k≠∅,  
　S\_k⊊D\_k(j),

and one of the following two holds:

　1\. S\_k∩UAtt\_k(j,η)=∅;

　2\. there exists τ∈Att\_k(j,η) such that:  
　　　　τ∩S\_k≠∅  
　　　　and  
　　　　τ⊄S\_k.

Clause 1 says the duty restriction wholly excludes the finite terminal class of A's current holding-direction; clause 2 says the duty cuts into one and the same cyclic or equivalent terminal class, so that A cannot realize that holding-direction class in full.

Define:

　FiniteSame\_k=1  
　⇔  
　UAtt\_k(j,η)⊆S\_k.

Other adjudicable finite-terminal situations are written:

　FiniteNoDir\_k=1.

(ii) Unbounded component

If Unbd\_k(j,η)=1, define:

　UnbdAnti\_k=1

if and only if:

　S\_k≠∅,  
　S\_k⊊D\_k(j),  
　S\_k∩Reach\_A^+(x\_cur)≠∅,

and there exists y∈Reach\_A^+(x\_cur)\\S\_k such that:

　∀x∈S\_k∩Reach\_A^+(x\_cur),  
　x≺\_{A,k\|j}y.

That is, S\_k is not cofinal in that unbounded holding-direction.

Define:

　UnbdSame\_k=1

if and only if S\_k is cofinal on every current unbounded strictly ascending branch.

If Unbd is adjudicable but the case is neither Anti nor Same, then:

　UnbdNoDir\_k=1.

The explicit non-empty intersection condition here excludes vacuous truth; if in some η the duty is never discharged within the currently reachable domain, that η does not acquire Anti under the unbounded clause.

(iii) Joint adjudication

If:

　FiniteAnti\_k=1  
　or  
　UnbdAnti\_k=1,

then:

　ResultAnti\_k=1.

If no Anti component exists, and every holding-direction component that actually exists is Same — that is:

　the finite-terminal component does not exist, or FiniteSame\_k=1,

and:

　the unbounded component does not exist, or UnbdSame\_k=1,

then:

　ResultSame\_k=1.

Other adjudicable situations:

　ResultNoDir\_k=1.

If the relevant Att, Unbd, S\_k, or cofinality facts are not adjudicable, and no Anti has already been definitely established, the corresponding result direction is ⊥.

Att\_k and Unbd\_k may hold at once on different reachable branches; joint adjudication forbids a finite-terminal Same from pre-empting an unbounded Anti, and equally forbids an unbounded Same from pre-empting a finite-terminal Anti.

Proposition 3.1〔recovery type〕

If A's self-holding limit class from the current state within j is {10}, then any real result restriction not containing 10 is ResultAnti; any result restriction containing 10 and not cutting that terminal class constitutes no Anti on that degree of freedom.

Proof.

Att={{10}}, UAtt={10}. Where 10 is absent, clause 1 of Definition 3.23(i) is satisfied; where 10 is present and that singleton class is not cut, Anti is not satisfied. □

Proposition 3.2〔branch indifference〕

If under the current j A can hold its way to both terminal classes b₁ and b₂:

　Att\_k={{b₁},{b₂}},

then restricting to {b₂} alone constitutes no ResultAnti.

Proof.

S\_k intersects UAtt, and no terminal equivalence class is cut; hence ResultAnti\_k=0. □

Proposition 3.3〔cyclic cut〕

If A's current holding-direction terminal is a cyclic equivalence class τ, and P's result duty permits only a proper non-empty subset of τ, then ResultAnti\_k=1.

Proof.

τ∩S\_k≠∅ and τ⊄S\_k, satisfying clause 2 of Definition 3.23(i). □

### 3.14　P's rest output

Definition 3.24　\[aux\]〔contribution-block rest component and substituted output〕

RestOut\_P(j,η) and Out\_P(j,η) are obtained by Definition 3.19a.

If C is the contribution block restricted by a delivery-type duty, define:

　Rest\_C^P(j,η)

as the component of RestOut\_P(j,η) on C.

Define further the full output vector with only the C component substituted:

　Out\_P^{C←rest}(j,η)

whose components satisfy:

　the C component = Rest\_C^P(j,η);

　every other P-side role output component = its current actual value in Out\_P(j,η).

Rest\_C^P comes from the structural rest output of the same field's currently established structure when all of P's duty roles are at rest; it does not require the existence of a joint sample in which "P is at rest while A actually bears."

If C is not generated at rest, † may be taken; if that rest output is not adjudicable, then Rest\_C^P=⊥.

If Rest\_C^P(j,η)=†, that component is read as "this interface has no such input / the contribution block is absent." Only where the original local generation interface of y\_A already defines a generation rule for the absence of that input may † be written into Out\_P^{C←rest} and Y\_rest computed further; where the interface has no semantics of absence, then:

　Y\_rest,k=⊥.

Rest is not an a priori mathematically neutral element.

### 3.15　Delivery-type counter-holding

Definition 3.25　\[S\]〔delivery-type marginal direction〕

Let the contribution block restricted by a delivery-type duty be C.

For k∈K\_A(j), define by means of the same auxiliary generation map:

　Y\_P,k  
　:=  
　G^{A,k}\_{j,η}  
　(  
　 x\_cur;  
　 Out\_A(j,η),  
　 Out\_P(j,η)  
　),

　Y\_rest,k  
　:=  
　G^{A,k}\_{j,η}  
　(  
　 x\_cur;  
　 Out\_A(j,η),  
　 Out\_P^{C←rest}(j,η)  
　).

Both generations therefore use the full P-side role output vector; the sole difference is that:

　Y\_P,k uses C's current actual output,

　Y\_rest,k substitutes only the C component with Rest\_C^P(j,η),

while the remaining P-side outputs, the A-side outputs, and the whole non-P background are held unchanged.

This comparison measures only the marginal direction of the contribution block C restricted by the duty; it does not withdraw P's other duty actions along with it.

In the deterministic single-valued case:

　Y\_P,k ≺\_{A,k\|j} Y\_rest,k  
　⇒ DeliveryAnti\_k=1;

　Y\_rest,k ≺\_{A,k\|j} Y\_P,k  
　⇒ DeliverySame\_k=1.

If C\_P or Rest is a set-valued or measure-valued object, then the basic direction event:

　E^-\_k  
　:=  
　{(y\_p,y\_r) \| y\_p≺\_{A,k\|j}y\_r}

is aggregated under the original set, measure, or temporal semantics of Type(d\*).

A set-type full-coverage duty, for instance, requires:

　∀y\_p∈Y\_P,k, ∀y\_r∈Y\_rest,k:  
　y\_p≺\_{A,k\|j}y\_r.

Measure-type applies the original Read threshold to E^-\_k under the same legal joint measure; existential and temporal types follow their original Type quantifiers.

A portion of counter-direction must not be automatically raised into Anti for the whole item.

If the actual and the rest results are equivalent, incomparable, or the Type aggregation does not satisfy the counter-directed duty, the value is NoDir; where a key quantity is not adjudicable, it is ⊥.

Proposition 3.4〔compensation does not remove delivery-type counter-holding〕

Take a one-shot generation type:

　y\_A=a+q+c.

A's own duty role currently delivers:

　a=10,

with structural rest output:

　Rest\_a^A=0;

other background action:

　q=1;

P currently delivers:

　c=-1,

with:

　Rest\_c^P=0.

Then the contribution effect of A's own role is:

　Δ^role\_A  
　\=  
　(10+1+0)−(0+1+0)  
　\=  
　10  
　\>0.

Definition 3.20 therefore yields a contribution-effect edge consistently toward the high side.

At the same time, against the same background:

　with P actually delivering c=-1, y\_A=10;

　substituting only c with its structural rest value 0, y\_A=11.

Hence:

　10 ≺\_A 11,

and P's delivered contribution is counter-holding.

If instead:

　c=+5,

then:

　11 ≺\_A 16,

so it is Same, and is not misjudged as Anti.

Proof.

The one-shot generation type obtains A's holding-direction not from state displacement but from the local generation difference between Out\_A^{P←rest}, re-obtained after P is wholly at rest, and RestOut\_A, which gives the direction of the contribution effect. P's marginal comparison substitutes only the C component, so a negative contribution falls on the counter-holding side and a positive contribution on the same-direction side. □

### 3.16　Multi-degree-of-freedom direction

Definition 3.26　\[S\]〔counter-holding〕

For the fixed duty d\*:

　Anti\_d\*(P,A,j,η)=1

if and only if there exists:

　k∈K\_A(j)

such that:

　DeliveryLimit\_d\*=1  
　∧ DeliveryAnti\_k=1

or:

　ResultLimit\_d\*=1  
　∧ ResultAnti\_k=1.

That the same duty is Same or NoDir on other degrees of freedom does not cancel Anti.

"Mixed," therefore, is only a structure of action; it is not a negation of Anti.

Lemma 3.3〔projection cannot turn an existing attractor intersection into counter-holding〕

Let S∩Att≠∅. For any fixed projection φ:

　φ(S)∩φ(Att)≠∅.

If Att⊆S, then:

　φ(Att)⊆φ(S).

Proof.

Take x∈S∩Att; then φ(x) belongs to both projections. Preservation of inclusion under projection is likewise immediate. □

Hence an after-the-fact projection cannot forge a restriction that originally contained or met Att into one disjoint from Att; the pre-declared duties of K\_A serve to exclude orthogonal degrees of freedom, not to manufacture direction.

## Section Four　The Equations

### 4.1　The general formal formula

When M1 through M8 of Section Five and the inherited upstream formal representation antecedents hold, the formal necessary formula of the Principle relation is:

　Principle\_F,t(P;A,J)  
　⇒  
　Law\_F,t(P,I\_P)  
　∧  
　CounterHoldRole^M\_F,t(P;A,J).

Here:

　CounterHoldRole^M=1

unfolds as:

　∃d\*∈Duties(Rel(P))  
　∀j∈J  
　∃R\_j∈𝒮(F\_t,P,I\_P)  
　∃ℱ\_{B,j}=ℱ\_B(P,d\*;R\_j)  
　\[  
　 RelPass\_{d\*}(P;A,j;R\_j,ℱ\_{B,j})=1  
　\].

The single-evaluation Sat kernel within that j is:

　Sat(η)=1  
　⇔  
　∃Π\_η∈ℱ\_{B,j}  
　\[  
　 JointProj\_j(η)=1  
　∧ AHold\_A(j,η)=1  
　∧ LimitRole\_{d\*}(P;A,j,η,R\_j,Π\_η)=1  
　∧ Anti\_{d\*}(P,A,j,η)=1  
　\].

d\* is fixed outside J;

R\_j and ℱ\_{B,j} may use legal alternative solutions from one j to the next;

within one and the same j, R\_j and ℱ\_{B,j} must not be changed from one η to the next;

Π\_η may be selected per η from the ℱ\_{B,j} fixed for that j.

The quantifiers must not be interchanged to give:

　∀j∃d\_j

or:

　∀η∃R\_η

or:

　∀η∃ℱ\_η.

### 4.2　The delivery-type formula

Fix j, η, d\*, R, Π. Define:

　Delivery^M\_{d\*}(P;A,j,η,R,Π)=1

if and only if:

　Π∈ℱ\_B(P,d\*;R);  
　DeliveryLimit\_{d\*}(P;A,j,η)=1;  
　RoleNonDegenerate\_{d\*}(P,A,j,η,Π)=1;  
　the duty role, ΩWit, and Bind used by DeliveryLimit belong to that same Π.

The delivery-type direction is:

　DeliveryAnti^M\_{d\*}(P;A,j,η)=1

if and only if there exists k∈K\_A(j) such that DeliveryAnti\_k=1 of Definition 3.25 holds.

Set-, measure-, and temporal types all aggregate that marginal direction event under the original semantics of Type(d\*).

### 4.3　The result-type formula

Fix j, η, d\*, R, Π. Define:

　Result^M\_{d\*}(P;A,j,η,R,Π)=1

if and only if:

　Π∈ℱ\_B(P,d\*;R);  
　ResultLimit\_{d\*}(P;A,j,η)=1;  
　RoleNonDegenerate\_{d\*}(P,A,j,η,Π)=1;  
　the DynamicRole, ΩWit, and Bind used by ResultLimit belong to that same Π.

The result-type direction is:

　ResultAnti^M\_{d\*}(P;A,j,η)=1

if and only if there exists k∈K\_A(j) such that ResultAnti\_k=1 of Definition 3.23 holds.

### 4.4　Disjunction of the two types

Define:

　DutyLimit^M\_{d\*}(P;A,j,η,R,Π)  
　:=  
　Delivery^M\_{d\*}(P;A,j,η,R,Π)  
　∨  
　Result^M\_{d\*}(P;A,j,η,R,Π).

This disjunction is not exclusive.

One and the same duty may restrict both the contribution it delivers into A and A's bearing result.

### 4.5　Relational properties

In this formal system:

　→\_{A,k\|j} carries in general no guarantee of reflexivity, symmetry, antisymmetry, or transitivity;

　≼\_{A,k\|j} is reflexive and transitive, in general not symmetric, in general not antisymmetric;

　≺\_{A,k\|j} is irreflexive, asymmetric, transitive;

　\~\_{A,k\|j} is reflexive, symmetric, transitive;

　Desc\_A is reflexive and transitive, in general not symmetric, in general not antisymmetric;

　set inclusion ⊆ is a partial order;

　non-identity of Law identity ≠ is a symmetric and irreflexive relation of non-identity;

　"P is a Principle of A" is in general neither symmetric nor transitive:  
　that P counter-holds A does not entail that A counter-holds P;  
　that P counter-holds A and A counter-holds B does not entail that P counter-holds B.

The Principle relation must not be taken by default to be a partial order, a total order, or a transitive relation.

## Section Five　Running and Solving

### 5.1　Order of solution

Given:

　(F\_t,A,P,I\_A,I\_P,J)

solve in the following order.

Step one: obtain A's legal Law witness.

If:

　Law\_F,t(A,I\_A)≠1,

that instance does not enter the solving of a Principle relation.

Step two: obtain P's Law solution set:

　𝒮(F\_t,P,I\_P).

If:

　𝒮=∅,

then CounterHoldRole^M=0.

If the qualification of the solution set is not adjudicable, the value is ⊥.

Step three: fix a candidate duty d within Rel(P).

d must be tested as one and the same duty across the whole of J.

Step four: for each j∈J generate the native joint evaluation:

　Γ\_J(j)

or:

　(Γ\_J,Σ\_J,μ\_J).

Generating the A side and the P side separately and then assembling them is forbidden.

Step five: on each legal η obtain:

　JointProj,  
　AHold,  
　DeliveryLimit / ResultLimit,  
　RoleNonDegenerate,  
　K\_A(j),  
　RestOut\_P, RestOut\_A, Out\_A^{P←rest},  
　≼\_{A,k\|j},  
　Att\_k / Unbd\_k,  
　Anti.

Step six: for the current j fix one R\_j together with its Bind family ℱ\_B(P,d\*;R\_j), and then, from within that family, find a Π\_η for each η such that H\_P1, H\_P2, Ω, and Bind all hold on one and the same Π\_η.

Step seven: obtain RelPass within j under the original semantics of the duty's Type.

Step eight: take full coverage over all j∈J.

If there is one fixed d\* and every j has its own legal R\_j with the corresponding Bind family covering that j, then:

　CounterHoldRole^M=1.

If every j can pass, but only by using a different d\_j for each, the whole is 0.

### 5.2　Current actual running

## Section Seven　Read-Back Rules

### 7.1　H\_P1

Theorem 7.1〔reduction of what a duty restricts〕

When M1 through M8 and the upstream Law formal interfaces hold, if there are legal j, η, R, Π such that:

　Delivery^M\_{d\*}(P;A,j,η,R,Π)=1,

then that single-evaluation certificate reads back as the delivery-type duty restriction of The General Form of Principle;

if there are legal j, η, R, Π such that:

　Result^M\_{d\*}(P;A,j,η,R,Π)=1,

then that single-evaluation certificate reads back as the result-type duty restriction of The General Form of Principle.

Proof.

In the delivery type, Read forms a real restriction on C; DirectIn guarantees that C is a real input of the original generation of y\_A; the same-evaluation duty chain of Definition 3.8, together with the same Π, guarantees that this delivery belongs to the same duty realization through which Law(P) was acquired; RoleNonDegenerate excludes renaming A's own holding-tendency role as P. Its read-back is therefore exactly the delivery type.

In the result type, Read reads y\_A directly, or a pure result description pullable back to y\_A; Definition 3.10 requires a real restriction on the pullback domain; the same DynamicRole actually discharges that restriction; Definition 3.11 excludes passing off an upstream cause or an incidental consequence as a result duty; RoleNonDegenerate excludes pure relabelling. Its read-back is therefore exactly the result type. □

### 7.2　H\_P2

Theorem 7.2〔reduction of counter-holding direction〕

If:

　Anti\_d\*(P,A,j,η)=1,

then on at least one degree of freedom A actually holds at present, what P's duty restricts runs counter to A's holding-direction.

Proof.

In the delivery type, what is compared are two A-Type states, obtained against the same background by one step of A's own local generation: one from P's actual dutiful contribution, the other from its structural rest output. A's holding-direction takes its reference by generation type: the temporal type generates it solely from the real Continuation edge obtained after P is wholly at rest and A's output has been re-evaluated at the state x under discussion as Out\_A^{P←rest}(j,η;x); the one-shot generation type generates it solely from the contribution-effect edge of Out\_A^{P←rest} relative to RestOut\_A. The two types are exclusive, and state Continuation and contribution effect are not unioned. If the state obtained from P's actual contribution lies strictly on the counter-holding side of the state obtained from its rest output, that contribution's marginal action on A is counter.

In the result type, P's current action does not enter A's holding-direction reference; in computing A's Continuation, the P-side role outputs are set to their structural rest values and A's role outputs are recomputed state by state. If S\_k is disjoint from the current UAtt\_k, or cuts one and the same terminal cycle or equivalence class, then the duty excludes or breaks up the result class toward which A's own holding-tendency tends; where the holding-direction is unbounded, adjudication proceeds by whether S\_k is cofinal in Reach^+; the finite-terminal component and the unbounded component are evaluated separately and then adjudicated together.

Both types are adjudicated only on k∈K\_A(j). That other degrees of freedom are with or indifferent does not cancel a counter-holding degree of freedom that exists. In the recovery type, "high" and "low" are settled solely by the current Att, and not by the literal magnitude of the numbers. □

Each j's evaluation is first generated natively by Ctx\_J and then projected by JointProj, so A and P cannot come from two incompatible evaluations. K11　Solo-domain transport: a holding-direction comparison made while A runs alone cannot be lifted to the joint evaluation of J, yet is still used for Anti.

K12　Cartesian assembly of evaluations: successful evaluations exist separately on the A side and the P side, but the shared resource values conflict, and a joint relational evaluation is nevertheless obtained.

K13　Shared quantity not preserved: π\_A and π\_P read different values for the same shared resource or gate.

K14　Independent resampling: a joint node is sampled separately on the two sides, manufacturing a joint success that does not exist in the original joint kernel.

K15　Measure pooling: a duty fails within some j, yet is pooled with the success rate of another j to reach the threshold and so pass J.

K16　Vacuous truth on an empty domain: Γ\_J(j)=∅, yet that j passes by universal vacuous truth.

K17　After-the-fact narrowing: failing or unknown j are deleted from J and the original Principle(P;A,J) is still claimed to hold.

K18　Manufactured replay: a replay evaluation uses stable structures not belonging to the current Estab\_t, or a new stable bearing segment is manufactured in order to make Principle hold.
