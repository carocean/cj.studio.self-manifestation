# The Necessity Form of Principle

## Section One　Purpose

When does a Law cease to be merely a Law and become a Principle?

The General Form of Principle has already answered the necessary direction: a Principle must first be a Law; and the same established duty through which Law qualification was acquired must take another Law — one already standing and currently exercising a holding-tendency — into a relation of action, forming a counter-hold on a dynamical degree of freedom that other Law actually holds.

That says what a Principle must have. It has not yet answered the converse question:

　　if all these structures already stand, is that already enough to be a Principle?

One case remains unexcluded:

　　P already stands as a Law;  
　　P and A are two distinguishable Law identities;  
　　P's same established duty is already directed toward A's holding-tendency;  
　　that duty already forms a counter-hold on a dynamical degree of freedom A actually holds;

and yet:

　　P is still not a Principle relative to A.

If such a case can stand, the structure given by The General Form of Principle can be only a necessary condition of Principle and not yet a sufficient criterion; and the question of just what further boundary an ordinary Law must cross in order to become a Principle would likewise remain unclosed.

The Necessity Form of Principle treats this gap and nothing else.

What is to be proved: within the given working domain, a P that already stands as a Law needs, once its same established duty has acquired counter-holding qualification against the holding-tendency of another Law A, no subject, no intention, no victory, no actual deflection of flow, no generation of a new Law, and no further qualification of any kind; these structures already suffice for P to acquire Principle qualification relative to A.

Formally, the proposition to be proven is:

　　Law\_F,t(A,I\_A)  
　　∧ P ≠ A  
　　∧ Law\_F,t(P,I\_P)  
　　∧ CounterHoldRole\_F,t(P;A,J)

　　⇒

　　Principle\_F,t(P;A,J).

Here:

　　I\_P ≠ ∅;  
　　J ≠ ∅;

J must be declared in advance of adjudication, and must not be narrowed after the fact once a counterexample has appeared.

The General Form of Principle has already proved the necessary direction:

　　Law\_F,t(A,I\_A)  
　　∧ P ≠ A  
　　∧ Principle\_F,t(P;A,J)

　　⇒

　　Law\_F,t(P,I\_P)  
　　∧ CounterHoldRole\_F,t(P;A,J).

The task of The Necessity Form of Principle is therefore not to prove again that "a Principle must have these structures," but, with the above working domain fixed, to eliminate the residual case:

　　Law(A,I\_A)=1  
　　∧ P ≠ A  
　　∧ I\_P ≠ ∅  
　　∧ J ≠ ∅  
　　∧ Law(P,I\_P)=1  
　　∧ CounterHoldRole(P;A,J)=1  
　　∧ Principle(P;A,J)=0.

So long as that case is unexcluded, the structural boundary of "Law gives birth to Principle" remains unclosed.

What is to be settled, then, is not what outward marks a Principle has, but:

　　what structural boundary an ordinary Law has crossed once it has necessarily become a Principle.

The necessary direction cannot simply be inverted.

The following inference is invalid:

　　Principle  
　　⇒  
　　Law(P) ∧ CounterHoldRole

therefore:

　　Law(P) ∧ CounterHoldRole  
　　⇒  
　　Principle.

Nor may "a Principle is a tendency that breaks the hold" be inverted directly into a conclusion as though it were a natural-language definition.

Sufficiency must be obtained independently:

　　fix the canonical source material  
　　→ atomize the canonical propositions  
　　→ separate current qualification for Principle from the other duties  
　　→ exhaust current qualification  
　　→ check for independent disqualifiers  
　　→ realize every current qualification, item by item, through upstream structure  
　　→ Principle.

"A hold-breaker that already has a tendency," "the same established counter-holding duty," "directedness toward the target holding-tendency," "counter-holding direction," and the like serve only as names of duties within the analysis of canonical semantics. They are not object-layer predicates, bear no independent truth value, and must all be unfolded back into canonical sentences and into structures already established by The General Form of Principle.

What is proved is only the current sufficient qualification of Principle.

Not treated:

　　how a Principle first comes to be;  
　　how the strength of a Principle is graded;  
　　how a Principle makes an old Law withdraw;  
　　how a Principle generates a new Law;  
　　how a Principle issues in action;  
　　how a Principle generates intention;  
　　whether some particular exercise of a Principle finally prevails;  
　　how many distinguishable Law identities exist in some particular neural network or Sedimentation-Field.

Completion Conditions:

　　CPL-1　every load-bearing atom of the frozen canonical source M1 through M12 has been registered, its semantic decomposition items classified, with no item left unsorted, and every category used has been declared in Section Four;

　　CPL-2　every CurrentQualification atom reduces to Q\_a through Q\_d, and every possible independent Disqualification atom has been checked;

　　CPL-3　the item-by-item structural mapping between Q\_a through Q\_d and Law(P,I\_P), CounterHoldRole(P;A,J) is complete, with no name substituted for a proof;

　　CPL-4　the principal sufficiency theorem is proved, and a legal direct breakdown condition of the form C=1 ∧ Principle=0, together with boundary cases and counterexample back-testing, is given;

　　CPL-5　the Status stratification of the structural layer, the formal layer, and the two-way necessary–sufficient closure is explicit, with no interface antecedents traded for one another;

　　CPL-6　no undeclared H exists; if any completion condition above can rest only on an external proposition still awaiting verification, Established must not be signed.

Only when CPL-1 through CPL-6 all hold does this form-text acquire completion qualification as a Necessity Form.

## Section Two　Working Domain and Upstream Inputs

### 2.1　Working domain

Fix:

　　F　the current Sedimentation-Field;  
　　t　the current moment;  
　　A　the target Law;  
　　P　the candidate Principle;  
　　I\_A　A's domain of Law application;  
　　I\_P　P's pre-declared domain of Law application;  
　　J　P's pre-declared Principle relation domain relative to A.

The working domain requires:

　　Law\_F,t(A,I\_A)=1;  
　　P ≠ A;  
　　I\_P ≠ ∅;  
　　J ≠ ∅.

P ≠ A means non-identity of Law identity; it does not require physical separation of carriers. One and the same physical carrier may witness several distinct Law identities; Principle is adjudicated according to the Law identity taken. The strict criterion of Law identity belongs to the task of Law identity; only the antecedent "distinguishable" is inherited here.

J must be fixed before P's Principle qualification relative to A is tested.

A legal relational evaluation of J must satisfy:

　　the A side projects onto legal conditions within I\_A covered by Law(A,I\_A);  
　　the P side projects onto legal conditions within I\_P covered by Law(P,I\_P);  
　　within that same evaluation, A's holding-tendency actually enters bearing;  
　　P's relevant duty is tested within that same evaluation;  
　　every legal evaluation J declares itself to cover is bound by the same CounterHoldRole qualification; one must not pick out only the single most favourable instance.

Principle qualification may be retained while A and P are currently at rest; adjudication proceeds by the legal replay evaluations J prescribes, and does not take the presence of actual inflow at this very moment as its sole condition.

2.2　Structural input from The General Form of Principle

Inherited directly:

　　CounterHoldRole\_F,t(P;A,J).

It says: within the legal relational evaluations prescribed by J, the same established duty through which P acquired Law qualification is directed toward the holding-tendency A is currently exercising, and on at least one dynamical degree of freedom A actually holds, forms a counter-hold relative to A's holding-direction.

What the duty restricts is of two legal types.

Delivery-type:

P's Read restricts the contribution P actually delivers into the generation of A's bearing quantity; that contribution is generated by P and enters A's bearing organization as a real variable of the local generation of A's bearing quantity; and Read takes that contribution's entry into A's generation as its condition of realization. What P's duty prescribes is what it delivers to A, not what A finally amounts to. Compensation of that contribution by a third Law on A's side does not cancel the delivery-type counter-holding qualification P has already acquired.

Result-type:

P's Read restricts A's bearing quantity itself or its legal result description; that restriction must be really discharged by the same DynamicRole through which Law(P,I\_P) was acquired. A result-type cannot stand merely on "P intends to restrict A" or "P correlates with A," nor may an unsedimented piece of temporary wiring be passed off as an established duty of P.

Both types require that the side restricted run counter to A's own holding-direction on at least one dynamical degree of freedom A actually holds.

Counter-holding is not a criterion on outcomes.

It does not require:

　　that A's duty has already failed;  
　　that A already holds nothing at all;  
　　that P has already prevailed;  
　　that A's final value has already changed;  
　　that what comes after has already departed from Rel(A).

It is first of all a relation of dynamical direction whose duty qualification has already been acquired.

2.3　Formal input from The Mathematical Form of Principle

The Mathematical Form of Principle has already established the system of formal representation and read-back:

　　CounterHoldRole\_F,t(P;A,J)  
　　↔  
　　CounterHoldRole^M\_F,t(P;A,J)

and has closed, on the Principle side:

　　H\_P1　the reduction bridge for what a duty restricts;  
　　H\_P2　the bridge for holding-direction and counter-holding direction;  
　　H\_P3　the relation-domain bridge.

That two-way representation is valid only when the M conditions declared by The Mathematical Form of Principle, and the Law-side formal antecedents it invokes, hold.

The structural principal theorem uses CounterHoldRole directly, not by way of CounterHoldRole^M.

H\_P1 through H\_P3 are therefore not copied into the antecedent of the structural principal theorem; nor does the existence of the formal bridges reduce the structural sufficient direction to Conditional of itself.

An inference at the formal layer, entering Principle from CounterHoldRole^M, must still retain the current formal antecedents of The Mathematical Form of Principle.

### 2.4　The type of necessary bearing

What is to be proved is not a new event at t+Δt, nor an added trajectory of temporal evolution, but whether the candidate relation has already acquired Principle qualification at t.

No further dynamical equation is therefore constructed.

The necessary bearing is carried out by two current dynamical structures already established:

　　Law\_F,t(P,I\_P)

gives the Law qualification of P — that P has already acquired a tendency and can bear, by structures already formed, the Continuation that comes after;

　　CounterHoldRole\_F,t(P;A,J)

gives how the same established duty of that Law takes A's currently exercised holding-tendency into a relation of bearing, and forms Anti on a degree of freedom A already holds.

The structural role of the condition set is therefore this: Law(P,I\_P) and CounterHoldRole(P;A,J) jointly realize all the duties of current Principle qualification. This is a relation of realization, by existing structures, of the qualification duties set by the canonical source; it is not a newly added dynamical operator.

Were one to construct a t→t+Δt equation as the load-bearing condition of necessity, one would have rewritten a "sufficient formula for current qualification" into a "formula for the occurrence of a successor event," which does not answer the proposition to be proven.

## Section Four　Atomization and Rules of Classification

### 4.1　Categories

Canonical sentences are not labelled whole.

Each sentence is first split into the minimal propositional components bearing on the target predicate Principle, and each component is then assigned, by its governing duty, to one of the following declared categories:

　　Formation　　　　　　how a Principle comes to be from nothing;  
　　CurrentQualification　by what a candidate relation has, at present, already acquired Principle qualification;  
　　Disqualification　　　conditions that independently cancel Principle while every current qualification is retained;  
　　Character　　　　　　characterizations of the nature of Principle;  
　　Degree　　　　　　　the degree and strength of a Principle's action;  
　　Dynamics　　　　　　the running relations of a Principle once qualification is acquired;  
　　Consequence　　　　　what a Principle generates once qualification is acquired;  
　　Relation　　　　　　accounts of Principle's relation to Law, Axiom, action, choosing, and the like;  
　　CategoryOrigin　　　by what generative mechanism a Principle arises;  
　　Recognition　　　　　whether a subject has recognized or come to know it;  
　　Location　　　　　　in what field a Principle bears its position;  
　　Application　　　　　judgements upon instances in particular systems.

For convenience of registration, the following sub-labels are also declared; each belongs under one of the categories above and forms no category of its own:

　　Manifestation　　　　⊂ Dynamics / Consequence; appearances that may arise once qualification is acquired;  
　　Exclusion　　　　　　⊂ Relation; boundary exclusions where some current qualification has not been acquired — not Disqualification;  
　　Support　　　　　　　⊂ CurrentQualification; components supplying canonical warrant for some current qualification;  
　　Source　　　　　　　⊂ Formation / CategoryOrigin; accounts of where an action comes from;  
　　Comparison　　　　　⊂ Relation; comparisons among Law, Principle, and Axiom;  
　　Example　　　　　　　⊂ Application; positive instances given by the canonical source.

The sufficiency proof is borne by two categories only:

　　CurrentQualification;  
　　Disqualification.

The other categories may be used for exposition, boundaries, and back-testing, but must not be slipped into the sufficient antecedent.

### 4.2　The temporal-phase criterion

If an item accounts for:

　　how a Principle comes to be from nothing,

it goes to Formation.

If an item answers:

　　by what a candidate relation has, at present, already acquired Principle qualification,

it goes to CurrentQualification.

If an item only accounts for:

　　how things appear once Principle is acquired, how the contest goes, how paths change, how successors are generated,

it goes to Dynamics or Consequence.

If an item only accounts for:

　　in what field a Principle chiefly arises, from what generative mechanism it comes,

it goes to CategoryOrigin or Location.

### 4.3　The deletion criterion

Hold the other current qualifications fixed and delete the candidate component.

If Principle can still stand, that component is not a CurrentQualification.

If deleting it necessarily lets in an ordinary Law, a single action, ordinary conflict, same-direction support, or some other non-Principle case, then that component bears current qualification.

4.4　The independent-disqualifier criterion

Suppose every current qualification obtained remains in force.

If there is an X such that:

　　X=1  
　　∧ every CurrentQualification=1  
　　⇒  
　　Principle=0,

then and only then is X an independent Disqualification.

If the appearance of X necessarily makes one of the qualifying conditions itself withdraw, X is not an independent disqualifier but merely the lapse of that original qualifying condition.

4.5　Parallel syntax and the classification of qualification

"Law — repetition and depth; Principle — choosing and bias" is syntactically parallel. The Necessity Form of Law assigns "repetition" to CurrentQualification (the qualification of stable re-walking) and "depth" to Degree.

Parallel syntax does not by itself entail that "choosing" and "repetition" must bear the same class of proof duty; classification is still settled by the criteria of 4.2 through 4.4.

Test "choosing" by the deletion criterion: hold Q\_a through Q\_d fixed and delete actual choosing — in the compensation case A's flow has not in fact been chosen against — and Principle still stands. Hence "choosing" bears no current qualification. What it characterizes is the nature of Principle (Character) and the appearance once counter-holding has taken effect (Manifestation). The sealed General Form of Principle has already placed choosing and bias at the layer of appearance and adds no further top-level condition; that is inherited here, and no qualification component is extracted from "choosing" or "bias."

The nature "choosing" characterizes — that a Principle can bias the flow of another Law — does not conflict with the compensation case, because that nature is already guaranteed by Q\_c and Q\_d: a duty directed toward A's holding-tendency and counter to it has, by that very fact, the qualification to bias A's flow, whether or not it is compensated this time. The nature is covered by the qualification; the nature does not bear the qualification.

"Bias" likewise: its nature goes to Character, actual deflection of flow to Manifestation, the magnitude of deflection to Degree. The load-bearing of direction itself is borne by M9's "made to run counter" and by M11's summary (Q\_d), and not by "bias."

The handling of "choosing" and "bias" is therefore of a kind with the handling of "depth" in The Necessity Form of Law: nature and degree do not enter current qualification.

## Section Five　Semantic Sorting of the Canonical Source

### 5.1　M1

M1 gives a prototype expressly called Principle.

Within it, "the information does not go, and it goes to the NOT gate alone" appears as the concrete running result after an original Continuation has been altered by another relation of action.

It shows that Principle has to do with a change in the direction of Continuation, but it does not prove that "a visible path change has already occurred at present" is a necessary qualification of Principle.

Classification: Relation / Dynamics / Example.

"This is Principle" supplies a canonical positive instance, but cannot by itself serve as a formal definition.

### 5.2　M2

"It has arrived at the NOT gate. This is action."

This sentence separates "action" from the Principle relation spoken of before it.

Hence:

　　ActualAction  
　　ActualArrival

Classification: Consequence, not CurrentQualification.

A Principle may already stand while the concrete succeeding behaviour has not yet been completed.

### 5.3　M3

"Bias" shows that the action of a Principle has direction.

But "a visible bias has already appeared" is not taken as a condition of qualification.

At the structural layer the duty of direction is borne by A's own holding-direction and by P's Anti relation to it; the degree of actual bias and its visible result do not enter the sufficient antecedent.

Classification: Character / Relation.

### 5.4　M4

"To choose is to bias the flow."

This sentence says that the running nature of "choosing" has to do with biasing a flow.

By 4.5, actual choosing and actual deflection of flow are not read-off values that Principle must already exhibit at present: the sealed General Form of Principle already permits a very weak counter-hold, permits A to keep its original holding-tendency, permits a delivery-type counter-directed contribution to be wholly compensated by a third Law, and permits the final net value to be unchanged. No qualification component is extracted from "choosing."

Classification: Relation / Manifestation.

### 5.5　M5

"It does not follow its own flow, but is forced to be biased. This is why it is called choosing."

This sentence shows how one kind of already actualized counter-holding appears as choosing.

It has two uses.

First, it shows that choosing is not the ordinary self-following of the thing acted upon.

Second, it supplies the semantic boundary between active counter-holding and ordinary obstruction.

But one cannot infer from it that:

　　CounterHoldRole  
　　⇒  
　　actual deflection has already occurred  
　　⇒  
　　choosing  
　　⇒  
　　Principle.

For CounterHoldRole may stand while no actual net deflection has yet appeared.

Classification: Dynamics / Manifestation; it also serves as the boundary exposition of Exclusion.

### 5.6　M6

"Choosing is a disturbing, and there must be one that presides over it; call it the active factor."

"One that presides" must not be presupposed to be a psychological subject, a conscious subject, or an intentional subject.

Within the given working domain, its content bearing on current qualification falls respectively to:

　　the non-identity of the Law identities of P and A;  
　　P's having itself acquired a Law holding-tendency;  
　　the counter-hold belonging to P's own established duty;  
　　that duty being really directed toward A's holding-tendency.

Hence "one that presides" leaves no fifth qualification independent of Law(P), P≠A, and CounterHoldRole.

Classification: Relation / Source.

### 5.7　M7

"Whatever takes on shape by following its tendency and meeting obstruction — none of these is choosing."

The grammatical subject of this sentence is "a thing that receives"; it speaks from the side of what is acted upon: where a thing follows its tendency, strikes an obstruction, and takes on shape, that deformation is not choosing. It cannot be read as:

　　a structure that physically resembles a wall is never a Principle.

The unit of adjudication is a Law identity and the duties of that identity, not a physical shape.

If the duty of the wall's Law identity P₁ is only to maintain the wall itself, and A's striking the wall and being deflected is merely a side effect, then:

　　CounterHoldRole(P₁;A,J)=0,

and under the identity P₁ it is not a Principle.

If on the same physical carrier there is another distinguishable Law identity P₂, whose own established duty is precisely to restrict A's bearing, and which satisfies every condition of result-type CounterHoldRole, then:

　　CounterHoldRole(P₂;A,J)=1,

and P₂ may be a weak Principle relative to A.

What this sentence excludes is therefore:

　　raising an obstacle itself into a Principle merely because passive obstruction has produced deformation or deflection.

Classification: Exclusion / Relation. It adds no CurrentQualification, and it is not a Disqualification.

### 5.8　M8

"That which, by opening and closing within itself, receives or refuses a following tendency — that is the active."

This sentence shows that the active is not something merely present alongside, but something that takes another tendency-following relation into the duties of its own opening and closing.

It supplies canonical support for:

　　the duty being really directed toward A's holding-tendency.

Note "receives or refuses": one that receives a following tendency is active too. The extension of the active is therefore wider than counter-holding — the active includes same-direction incorporation. M8 supports Q\_c (directedness) only, and not Q\_d (counter-direction); one that receives a following tendency is excluded from Principle by Q\_d.

Classification: Relation; its content "taking the other's following tendency as what it is directed toward" is Support for Q\_c. It adds no independent object-layer predicate.

### 5.9　M9

"If a tendency-following system is made to run counter, the active factor must come from without; and two tendency-following systems, disturbing each other, may run counter to each other."

"Made to run counter" — making another tendency-following system's Continuation reverse — is the most direct statement of counter-holding direction in the canonical source, and supplies support for Q\_d.

"Without" is relative to the self-following system acted upon; it does not require leaving the Sedimentation-Field as a whole, nor physical separation of carriers.

"Two tendency-following systems, disturbing each other, may run counter to each other" shows that two systems each already having a holding-tendency of its own may become sources of counter-holding for each other within their relation.

Hence:

　　P≠A

is non-identity of Law identity, not spatial externality.

Classification: Relation; "made to run counter" is Support for Q\_d; "must come from without" is borne by P≠A; "disturbing each other, may run counter" is the boundary of mutual counter-holding.

### 5.10　M10

"Law — repetition and depth; Principle — choosing and bias."

For Principle, by 4.5:

　　"choosing" goes to Character / Relation, its actualized appearance to Manifestation;  
　　"bias" goes to Character / Relation, actual deflection of flow to Manifestation, the magnitude of deflection to Degree.

The natures the two characterize are covered by Q\_c ∧ Q\_d, but bear no current qualification.

"Bias" may itself appear as change in path, weight, strength, timing, duration, or distribution, and must not be compressed into a single condition of degree.

"These three differ in that they differ in where they are born" belongs to CategoryOrigin.

It shows that Law, Principle, and Axiom have different chief generative mechanisms. It does not entail:

　　only a relation arising in a mature Intent-Field has Principle qualification.

The most primitive Principle may appear before a subject, thought, or explicit intention has formed. A counter-holding duty formed by pure sedimented re-walking still acquires current qualification; provenance does not enter current qualification, by the same treatment The Necessity Form of Law gives to "where they are born."

Hence:

　　where born ∉ CurrentQualification.

### 5.11　M11

M11 is the core canonical source of current qualification.

"Law is the tendency of holding; Principle is the tendency that breaks the hold."

For an analysis of Principle qualification, "the tendency that breaks the hold" cannot be treated merely as a defining phrase.

It must be unfolded, together with M4 through M9, with the successive failing cases of The General Form of Principle, and with the boundaries already in place, into a duty structure that can be deleted item by item and back-tested.

"This would hold, that breaks it; it breaks it, and then it does not hold" — the clause "and then it does not hold" belongs to Consequence.

It is not a necessary antecedent of Principle.

"The pursuit of the two" belongs to Dynamics / Relation; it shows that holding and counter-holding may coexist and pursue each other, and does not require that one has already annihilated the other.

"Makes Causal-Response transform without cease" belongs to Dynamics / Consequence, and does not enter current qualification.

### 5.12　M12

"The Intent-Field is the field of Principle; the Experience-Field is the field of Law."

It does not entail:

### 5.13　Registration of canonical atoms

So that exhaustion may be a proof over a finite domain rather than a summary after sentence-by-sentence exposition, the load-bearing components of M1 through M12 bearing on Principle qualification, disqualification, boundary, or consequence are registered as the finite total set of atoms A\_M.

Registration has two layers. The first layer is the canonical atom: the minimal load-bearing fragment of an original canonical sentence, containing no exposition; A\_M consists of this layer alone and is a frozen finite domain. The second layer is semantic decomposition: where one canonical atom bears more than one semantic content under the target predicate, decomposition items are registered beneath it and distinguished by superscript; classification proceeds by decomposition item. A decomposition item is not a new canonical atom, does not enlarge A\_M, and no qualification component may be extracted from an atom at the layer of appearance or of nature.

Registration bears only the classification for the target predicate Principle; it does not imply that these sentences can have this one reading alone in other form-kinds.

M1:

　　A1　"from the AND gate it goes to the NOT gate";  
　　A2　"knowing the OR gate, the NOR gate";  
　　A3　"the information does not go, and it goes to the NOT gate alone";  
　　A4　"this is Principle".

## Section Six　Unfolding Current Qualification for Principle

### 6.1　General

Taking M1 through M12 together, and keeping the boundaries already frozen by The General Form of Principle, "the tendency that breaks the hold" cannot be compressed into two unexplicated names. Its current qualification unfolds into four semantic duties.

These four are only names of duties within the analysis of the proof; they add no four object-layer predicates.

6.2　Q\_a: the hold-breaker has itself already acquired a tendency

P cannot be merely one impact, one gating, one burst of noise, or one unsedimented current action.

Even where a single action really does suppress, deflect, or cut off A, there is only one "breaking," and no "tendency that breaks the hold."

Hence it is first required that:

　　P has itself acquired Law qualification.

Realized structurally as:

　　Law\_F,t(P,I\_P)=1.

Q\_a bears:

　　the hold-breaker itself already has a holding-tendency.

6.3　Q\_b: the counter-hold belongs to P's same established duty

That P itself already stands as a Law is still not enough.

If P has Law qualification elsewhere, but some temporary wiring g connects P's dynamics into A for the occasion and forms a counter-directed action this once, then one must not assemble across structures:

　　P's Law qualification elsewhere

with:

　　this one counter-directed action here

into a Principle.

The counter-hold against A must therefore belong to an established duty borne by the same Rel(P) through which Law(P,I\_P) was acquired, and be borne by the same legal DynamicRole and witness family.

Q\_b bears:

　　the hold-breaking is not a borrowed connection for the occasion, but has already become a stable duty within P's own holding-tendency.

6.4　Q\_c: the duty is directed toward the holding-tendency A is currently exercising

If P's duty is only to:

　　consume a resource;  
　　maintain itself;  
　　output some public value;  
　　alter a threshold;  
　　occupy shared capacity;  
　　produce an ordinary load;

while A happens thereby to be damaged, this is still only a side effect or an ordinary stable conflict.

P's duty must really take the holding-tendency A is legally exercising into its own relation of bearing.

Its only legal modes are:

　　delivery-type;  
　　result-type.

Q\_c bears:

　　the hold-breaking duty is about A's holding-tendency, and does not merely happen to damage A.

M8's "by opening and closing within itself, receives or refuses a following tendency" is the canonical support of Q\_c: taking the other's following tendency into the duties of one's own opening and closing.

6.5　Q\_d: counter on a degree of freedom A actually holds

Even where P's established duty is really directed toward A, it may still be:

　　same-direction support;  
　　strengthening;  
　　repair;  
　　stabilization;  
　　orthogonal modulation;  
　　an indifferent change.

None of these is "breaking the hold." M8's "receives" is excluded precisely here.

It is therefore required that, on at least one dynamical degree of freedom A already actually holds, the duty be Anti relative to A's own holding-direction.

Q\_d bears:

　　the direction of the hold-breaking.

M9's "a tendency-following system is made to run counter" is the canonical support of Q\_d.

The conjunction of Q\_c and Q\_d covers the nature of "choosing" as characterized in M10: an established duty has the qualification to bias A's flow. The nature is covered by the qualification, and does not bear the qualification in return.

### 6.6　The double seating of "tendency"

The "tendency" in "the tendency that breaks the hold" cannot be borne by Law(P) alone.

Law(P) guarantees:

　　the hold-breaker P has itself acquired a tendency.

Q\_b, together with its relation-domain closure, further guarantees:

　　this counter-hold against A already belongs to P's same established duty, and is covered within the pre-declared J by that same duty and by a legal witness.

So:

　　Law(P)

prevents "one that acts once" from passing itself off as one that has a tendency;

while:

　　same-duty closure + coverage of J

prevents "one that has a tendency and happened to break a hold once" from passing itself off as "a tendency that breaks the hold."

The current qualification of the canonical "tendency that breaks the hold" may therefore be compressed as:

　　a P that has a tendency,  
　　whose own established duty  
　　is directed toward A's current holding-tendency,  
　　and forms Anti on a degree of freedom A already holds.

These four together constitute the cluster of current qualification duties.

## Section Seven　Deletion Tests on the Four Qualifications

### 7.1　Deleting Q\_a

Let P be merely one instantaneous counter-directed impact.

At t it really does knock A off course.

But P has no Law qualification of its own and stably bears nothing that comes after.

The verdict must be:

　　Principle=0.

Q\_a is therefore not deletable.

### 7.2　Deleting Q\_b

Let P already be a Law.

Let there also be an unsedimented temporary wiring g, connecting P's current dynamics into A and forming a counter-directed contribution.

But the counter-hold against A is not the same established duty through which P acquired Law qualification.

　　Law(P)=1;  
　　A is really affected in a counter direction;

Q\_b is therefore not deletable.

### 7.3　Deleting Q\_c

Let P already stand as a Law and stably consume some public resource; and let A also depend on that resource.

P's prolonged running weakens A.

P's duty is discharged in full even where A does not exist, and its Read requires nothing of A's bearing.

This is ordinary resource competition.

Let P instead be a stable wall whose Law duty is only to maintain its own structure; and let A strike the wall and be deflected.

This is a side effect produced by obstruction.

　　Q\_a=1;  
　　Q\_b may hold for P's own duty;

　　duty of P directed toward A's holding-tendency.

Hence not a Principle.

Q\_c is not deletable.

### 7.4　Deleting Q\_d

Let P already stand as a Law, and let its same duty be really directed toward A's holding-tendency.

But it only:

　　strengthens A;  
　　maintains A;  
　　supplies A in the same direction;

or alters only a degree of freedom A does not hold.

Then it is no adversary of A's holding-tendency.

Q\_d is therefore not deletable.

### 7.5　The Law-identity test for the wall

Sameness of physical carrier is not sameness of Law identity.

If on one and the same sedimented wall there is:

　　P₁: whose duty is to maintain the wall itself;

then the deflection produced by A's striking the wall does not of itself make:

　　CounterHoldRole(P₁;A,J)=1.

If there is another distinguishable Law identity:

　　P₂: whose own established duty is precisely to restrict A's bearing quantity or its legal result description;

and P₂ satisfies the result-type conditions of real discharge by the same DynamicRole, relation-domain closure, and Anti,

then:

　　CounterHoldRole(P₂;A,J)=1;

and under the identity P₂, Principle may be acquired.

The object of adjudication is therefore:

　　a Law identity and its duties,

and not the name or the shape of a physical object.

## Section Twenty　Conclusion

What has been proved is this.

A Law P that already stands does not become a Principle merely by existing, by conflicting, by occupying a resource, by producing a side effect, or by incidentally altering another Law A.

For P to become a Principle relative to A, what is sufficiently required is:

　　that P has itself acquired a Law holding-tendency;

and:

　　that the counter-hold against A belongs to the same established duty through which P acquired that Law qualification;  
　　that this duty is really directed toward the holding-tendency A is currently exercising;  
　　that this duty runs counter to A's holding-direction on at least one dynamical degree of freedom A actually holds;  
　　that these relations are maintained, across the pre-declared non-empty J, under the same duty, the same evaluation, and legal coverage.

This is the sufficient realization, at the present structural layer, of the canonical "tendency that breaks the hold."

It does not require that the hold-breaking has already prevailed, that A has already lost its Law, that the net outcome has already changed, that actual choosing and bias are already displayed, or that a subject, a goal, or an intention has appeared first.

Hence, within the working domain above:

　　Law\_F,t(A,I\_A)  
　　∧ P ≠ A  
　　∧ Law\_F,t(P,I\_P)  
　　∧ CounterHoldRole\_F,t(P;A,J)

　　⇒

　　Principle\_F,t(P;A,J).

A Principle is first of all a Law.

It is not a second kind of being, of a different order from Law; it is, within a holding-tendency that already stands as a Law, the formation of a counter-holding duty directed toward another holding-tendency currently being exercised.

A Law holds what comes after it.

A Principle, by its own hold, breaks the hold of another.
