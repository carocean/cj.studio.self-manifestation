# The General Form of Principle-Action

What follows attempts one general proposition:

once a Principle already stands, how does it pass from "being a Principle" into "acting"?

The General Form of Principle, The Mathematical Form of Principle, and The Necessity Form of Principle have, within their common working domain and their respective inherited conditions, closed the necessary–sufficient structure of a Principle's standing: for P to be a Principle relative to A, the structural boundary is that P is first of all a Law in full, and that the same established duty through which Law qualification was acquired takes as its object the holding-tendency A is currently exercising, forming a counter-hold on at least one dynamical degree of freedom A actually holds. The sufficient direction is Established; the necessary direction and the two-way closure remain Conditional as declared upstream. What is adjudicated there is qualification: whether a relation has already become a Principle.

But to be a Principle and to be acting are not the same thing. On the Primordial says: "the information of the NAND gate does not go, and it goes to the NOT gate alone — this is Principle," and "from the AND gate it has arrived at the NOT gate — this is action." The canonical source sets Principle and action on two layers. A Principle may already stand and be currently at rest; a counter-directed action may be occurring and yet not be Principle-action. One question therefore remains unanswered:

　　when does an already standing Principle cease merely to hold Principle qualification, and come to be acting?

That is the single irreducible task.

What follows does not prove again what a Principle is, and redefines none of Principle, Law, CounterHoldRole, DynamicRole, AHold, LimitRole, Anti, Bind, or Ω. All are inherited from sealed upstream texts and are here only used.

The result first.

Within the working domain Principle\_F,t(P;A,J)=1, if P's Principle relative to A is acting in the relational context j∈J, then:

　　Act\_F,t(P;A,j)  
　　⇒  
　　CurAnti\_F,t(d\*;P,A,j)

where d\* is the same established duty through which P became a Principle relative to A, and CurAnti says: the legal realization of d\* enters, within the current actual Continuation, A's current bearing of its holding-tendency, and there discharges its established counter-hold. CurAnti is only a compressed notation; it acquires no independent ontological or formal predicate standing. It unfolds back into the same duty, legal realization, A's current bearing of its holding-tendency, what the duty restricts, and counter-holding direction, each already independently defined upstream. It alters no upstream counter-holding structure; it adds only one restriction: the realization used to prove this instance of action must come from the current actual Continuation, and a legal replay evaluation must not be put in place of current actual occurrence. Wherever proof, minimality, or breakdown is at issue, the unfolded structure governs.

Only this necessary direction is proved. The converse is treated in the section on the sufficient direction.

In One Sentence

A Principle stands first; action is the discharge, in the current actual Continuation, of that already established counter-holding duty.

Abstract

Taking an already standing Principle as the working domain, the only question asked is when it is acting.

The argument starts from the weakest case: the Principle stands, its structure is intact, every legal replay evaluation passes — and yet no bearing relation of P upon A is occurring at present. There is a Principle, and it is not acting. So the first thing forced out is that, besides Principle qualification, there must be current occurrence. Keeping that, and now letting d\* start up at present, cases still fail: a signal is in transit and has not entered A's bearing; a result-type module is executing and does not at present restrict the degree of freedom A holds. The running of a duty is not Principle-action. So the next thing forced out is that the current running must enter the very bearing the Principle relation is directed toward. Keeping the first two rounds, and now letting d\* enter A's bearing at present, cases still fail: what enters is with A's direction or indifferent to it. Entering is not counter-holding. So the argument forces out that the same duty must, in this current bearing, discharge its established counter-hold. At that point no legal case remains that could still be called "standing but not acting," and the task closes.

What follows then settles only what "current" and "discharge" minimally mean. Current is not a replay evaluation, not the structure still being in place, not Ω still being positive; discharge is not arrival, not completion of the duty, not a change in the final outcome, not prevailing in the net composition. A delivery-type counter-hold compensated by a third Law, a result-type redundant with other Laws, a duty episode not yet ended, A's instantaneous value not yet moved — each may already be Principle-action. Whereas being at rest, a replay evaluation merely passing, a duty started but not entering A, same-direction or indifferent action, an empty restriction, resource competition, a measurement side effect, a same-shaped substitute by another duty, and the after-effects of past action are none of them Principle-action.

Hence a Principle's standing and a Principle's acting are two axes of adjudication: the first is that the counter-holding duty relation has come to stand; the second is that this already standing relation enters the current actual Continuation and occurs.

Working Domain

Let:

　　F　the Sedimentation-Field;  
　　t　the current moment;  
　　A　the target Law;  
　　P　the Law that already stands as a Principle relative to A;  
　　J　the pre-declared relation domain of that Principle relation;  
　　j∈J　the relational context currently at issue;  
　　d\*　the same established duty through which P became a Principle relative to A, d\*∈Duties(Rel(P)).

Before entry it is required that:

　　Law\_F,t(A,I\_A)=1;  
　　P≠A;  
　　I\_P≠∅;  
　　J≠∅;  
　　Principle\_F,t(P;A,J)=1

be already fixed. The first four are the entry conditions of The General Form of Principle and are not entailed by Principle; under those entry conditions and the inherited conditions, Law(P,I\_P)=1 and CounterHoldRole(P;A,J)=1 are inherited from the necessary direction of The General Form of Principle, and by The Mathematical Form of Principle, d\* is fixed across the whole of J. All of this is working domain, not structure forced out below.

The relational instance currently at issue must belong to J: j∈J. Principle-action cannot borrow one Principle qualification to cover contexts outside its relation domain; where the current occurrence is in some j′∉J, qualification for that relation domain must be acquired separately, and the case lies outside the working domain.

"Current actual Continuation," as used here, means the Continuation actually occurring in the Sedimentation-Field at t — actual inflow, actual generation, actual bearing — and not the legal replay evaluation prescribed by The Mathematical Form of Principle, nor the structure's capacity to be re-walked, nor a bearing that once stood historically. Upstream has already separated the two: a current actual run, if it satisfies the legal evaluation conditions of Ctx\_J(j), is one evaluation within Γ\_J(j); a replay evaluation is a legal re-enactment starting from Estab\_t. What follows only uses that existing distinction; it erects no field quality or dynamical layer called "current."

"Action," as used here, takes at the outset only the task-sense of the canonical "this is action." It is not defined in advance as arrival, completion, prevailing, or any read-off value of outcome. What it minimally means is forced out by the questioning below.

The unit of adjudication is (P,d*), not the net composition of all of P's action. P may at the same time have another duty d₂ supporting A; whether P's Principle relative to A is acting turns on d* alone.

The Line of Argument

We proceed by sustained questioning, one step at a time; the argument therefore forms a line, and is to be read from the top down.

First question:

P already stands as a Principle relative to A. On that alone, may one say that P's Principle is acting?

No.

Construct the weakest case.

P's Law identity, its Principle-conferring duty d*, every component of CounterHoldRole, its stable structure and its legal replay qualification are all retained. The replay evaluation passes in every relational context of J. But at the current moment t, the joint evaluation j refers to has not actually occurred: A has no inflow, or A has inflow while d*'s trigger conditions are absent. No actual bearing relation of P upon A is under way in the field.

This is a Principle standing at rest.

The General Form of Principle already permits Principle qualification to be retained while A and P are currently at rest; The Mathematical Form of Principle further gives legal replay evaluations based on the current Estab\_t. Principle qualification therefore does not require that an actual bearing relation obtain at this very moment. Qualification and current occurrence are two different things from the outset. Were a Principle at rest also called acting, "acting" would have no power to discriminate at all: every Principle would be acting.

The original task is therefore not closed.

The failure lies here: Principle qualification is a relation that already stands, and it contains no item of "occurring at this moment."

Hence, if P's Principle is acting, then besides Principle qualification there must be current occurrence: the duty d\* that made P a Principle actually occurs in the current actual Continuation.

This round forces out current occurrence itself, and nothing more. What d\* currently does, and how far it currently reaches, are asked below.

Obtained so far:

　　Principle(P;A,J)=1;  
　　Act(P;A,j) ⇒ d\* actually occurs at present.

Then the next question:

d\* has actually started up at present. Is the original task closed?

Not yet.

Keep everything obtained so far.

P now stands as a Principle and d\* actually starts up at present. The following cases can still be constructed.

The transit case. d\* is triggered by condition C, and its signal is travelling through P's structure but has not yet reached A's bearing organization; A is currently holding, and P's contribution has not entered the generation of A's bearing quantity.

The module case. d\* is a result-type duty and its realizing module is executing — a threshold is being tuned, a gate is moving — but at this moment the degree of freedom A holds is not restricted by that execution: the restriction falls on a quantity A does not hold, or on a segment A has not yet entered in bearing, or the restriction set on this occasion is, on the degree of freedom A holds, exactly the whole domain, restricting nothing at all. Upstream ResultLimit requires a real restriction evaluation by evaluation; on this occasion it does not hold.

The ordinary-running case. d\* is running, and has indeed altered some quantities in the field, but none of those alterations touches the holding-tendency A is currently exercising; A continues wholly by its own relation.

In all three, d\* is running, and none of them is P's action upon A. Were they too called acting, "acting" would amount to "a duty running" — and a duty runs whenever any Law whatever is activated.

These cases do not conflict with the working domain Principle=1. Principle qualification is adjudicated across the whole of J under the duty's Type: The Mathematical Form of Principle first obtains CounterEval evaluation by evaluation, then forms Sat and RelPass under the original Type and Read of the duty, and finally covers J. For a duty whose original Type and Read permit local evaluations to fail while RelPass still holds overall, this legal actual evaluation may be precisely one in which no counter-hold is formed. The qualification is there; on this occasion it does not act. If the current Continuation does not satisfy the legal evaluation conditions of Ctx\_J(j), then it is not an evaluation within j: P has no action in j at this moment. That follows directly from the working-domain condition j∈J; it is of a kind with the resting case of the first round, and needs no separate counterexample here.

The original task is therefore still not closed.

The failure lies here: a duty's current running and the bearing the duty is directed toward are two different things. What Principle qualification is directed toward is one particular bearing — the holding-tendency A is currently exercising. If d\*'s running has not entered that bearing, it has not yet reached the place the Principle relation points to.

Hence, if P's Principle is acting, d*'s current actual occurrence must enter the bearing structure the Principle relation is directed toward: within the current actual Continuation, A's holding-tendency actually enters bearing, and d*'s legal realization actually enters that bearing.

This is no new ontological item. That A's holding-tendency enters bearing is already borne upstream by AHold; that d\*'s realization enters A's contribution chain or result-restriction chain is already borne upstream by the delivery type and result type of LimitRole. What this round forces out is only that both must hold within the current actual Continuation, and not within a replay evaluation.

Obtained so far:

　　Principle(P;A,J)=1;  
　　d\* actually occurs at present;  
　　its legal realization currently enters A's bearing of its holding-tendency.

Then the next question:

d\* has currently entered A's bearing of its holding-tendency. Is the original task closed?

Still not.

Keep everything obtained in the previous two rounds.

P now stands as a Principle, d\* actually occurs at present, and its realization currently enters A's bearing of its holding-tendency. The following cases can still be constructed.

The same-direction case. d\*'s current realization enters the generation of A's bearing quantity, but this contribution is, on the degree of freedom A holds, with A's holding-direction. For a duty whose original Type and Read permit local evaluations to fail while Principle qualification still holds overall, there can be a current legal evaluation in which LimitRole holds and Anti does not; this is one such.

The indifferent case. d\*'s current realization enters A's bearing, and what it alters is a degree of freedom A does not currently hold.

In both, d\* has entered A's bearing, and neither forms, on this occasion, a counter-hold against A's holding-tendency. The condition on which they are compatible with Principle=1 is the same as in the second round: it holds only for duties whose original Type and Read permit local evaluations to fail while RelPass still holds overall; and to prove that Principle does not entail current action, one legal class of case is enough; it need not hold for every Type. A Principle is a Principle because d\* is counter to A's holding-tendency; if this entry is not counter, then on this occasion there is no hold-breaking, only entering.

The original task is therefore still not closed.

The failure lies here: entering a bearing is not discharging a counter-hold. What Principle qualification is directed toward is not merely "d\* with respect to A's holding-tendency," but "d\* counter to A on a degree of freedom A holds"; and what current action must discharge is this latter.

Hence, if P's Principle is acting, then after d\*'s current realization has entered A's bearing of its holding-tendency, it must further discharge, in that bearing, its established counter-hold: on at least one dynamical degree of freedom A currently actually holds, the contribution or restriction of this realization is counter relative to A's holding-direction.

This too is no new ontological item. Counter-holding direction is already borne upstream by Anti; what this round forces out is only that it must hold within the current actual Continuation.

Now set what each round obtained side by side:

　　P already stands as a Principle relative to A (working domain);  
　　the same duty d\* that made P a Principle actually occurs in the current actual Continuation;  
　　its legal realization currently enters the bearing of the holding-tendency A is exercising;  
　　and in that bearing, on a degree of freedom A actually holds, it discharges its established counter-hold.

We now try again to construct a degenerate case.

Being at rest was excluded in the first round. Transit, module execution (including the empty restriction), and ordinary running were excluded in the second. Same-direction and indifferent action were excluded in the third.

The following candidates were also tried and rejected one by one; the reasons are given under the discrimination of action and under substitution failures:

　　A's final outcome has already changed;  
　　P prevails in the net composition, or has already defeated A;  
　　withdraw P and the outcome differs;  
　　the counter-hold reaches some uniform strength or lasts some minimum time;  
　　the duty episode has already been completed;  
　　P touches A directly, has a unique site of action, a unique or minimal witness, a single node or a single path;  
　　no other Law compensates or is redundant with it;  
　　A currently loses its Law, or its holding-tendency fails altogether;  
　　a separate predicate of "duty continuation."

Each is either a consequence, or a degree, or a manner of realization, or already borne by upstream structure. None of them can let a case of "standing but not acting" re-enter once the four items above all hold.

At present no new degenerate case has been obtained that keeps all of the above and still makes the original task fail for want of another independent top-level structure.

The constraint recursion of the newly added structure stops here.

Discriminating Action

The new structure has been forced out, but "current" and "discharge" still admit several competing readings. What follows adds no further top-level necessary structure; it only settles what the two items already forced out minimally mean.

Given that action requires current occurrence, the next question:

do the structure's still being in place, Ω's still being positive, and the existence of a legal replay evaluation already count as current occurrence?

No.

All three speak of qualification: the structure being in place is Established; Ω being positive is the capacity for stable re-walking; a replay evaluation passing is the standing of CounterHoldRole's qualification in that context. Taken together they give exactly what the working domain has already frozen: Principle=1. Were they to count as current occurrence, the resting case of the first round would re-enter.

Upstream has already separated current actual running from legal replay: the former is one actual evaluation within Γ\_J(j), the latter a legal re-enactment starting from Estab\_t. Qualification may be maintained by the latter; occurrence can be taken only from the former. When the current actual evaluation fails, one must not take a successful CounterEval from a replay evaluation and assemble it into "current action." The layer of qualification and the layer of occurrence must not borrow from each other.

Hence none of the following may be written as "acting":

　　Ω\>0;  
　　DynamicRole already stands;  
　　the structure is currently still in place;  
　　a legal replay evaluation exists.

They are the premises of action, not action.

Given that current occurrence must be taken from the actual Continuation, the next question:

must d\*'s realization in the current actual Continuation touch A directly, have a unique site of action, or be borne by a single node or a single path?

No.

A legal realization may run through a long chain of intermediaries, change carriers, be realized on one and the same physical host under a different Law identity, be borne jointly by a distributed structure, contain cycles, have no unique first node of counter-holding, and have no single node that suffices on its own to bear the whole counter-hold. All of these are forms of realization already permitted upstream by LimitRole and Bind. What decides acting or not acting is whether the legal current realization of the same Principle-conferring duty actually discharges its counter-hold; not how many intermediaries it passed through, nor which node it landed on.

Likewise, "the same duty" is not "the same shape of behaviour." If another independent duty dX produces behaviour exactly identical to d*'s while d* currently bears nothing, one must not use dX's realization to prove that d\* is acting. The upstream requirements of a fixed d*, the same legal realization, and the same witness family already exclude that counterfeit; no separate predicate of "duty continuation" is erected for it. If a new Law brought about by d* later acquires its own independent DynamicRole and duty dX, and dX independently takes over the counter-hold against A, then P's Principle-action ends at the point of takeover, even where the outward phenomenon is continuous — the witness of the original d\*'s current legal realization no longer stands, and with it the qualification to be acting.

Given that action requires discharging a counter-hold, the next question:

must discharge appear as a change in A's final outcome, as P's prevailing in the net composition, or as the outcome's differing when P is withdrawn?

No.

Construct the compensation case. A contributes +10 on the quantity it holds; P's d\* currently delivers −1 into that quantity; another Law Q delivers +1 at the same time. The final value is still 10, and withdrawing P still leaves 10. But d*'s −1 is the contribution its established duty requires, it really enters the generation of A's bearing quantity at present, and its direction is counter. The General Form of Principle has already adjudicated that compensation does not cancel delivery-type counter-holding qualification. By the same token, compensation does not cancel the current discharge of a delivery-type counter-hold — what is discharged is what d* delivers to A, not what A finally amounts to.

Construct the redundancy case. P and Q both really restrict A to the same side by result-type duties; withdraw P and Q still maintains the same outcome. P's marginal effect is zero, and that does not cancel the result-type restriction P has itself actually brought to stand; whether Q also bears it at the same time does not alter the adjudication of P's own current discharge of its duty — the upstream criteria adjudicate each duty by that duty and its own legal realization, and nowhere list "the outcome must change once this bearer is removed" as a condition of a duty's standing. Hence MarginalEffect(P\|Q)=0 does not entail that P's Principle is not acting.

Construct the mixed case. P has two duties: d\* counter-holds A and d₂ supports A; the net direction of P's host supports A. But the unit of adjudication is (P,d*). So long as d* itself currently discharges its Principle-conferring counter-hold, P's Principle relative to A is still acting.

Principle-action therefore cannot be defined on the final outcome. None of the following is a necessary condition of acting:

　　A's final outcome changes;  
　　P prevails or defeats A;  
　　the outcome differs when P is withdrawn;  
　　an observable endpoint changes;  
　　the counter-hold reaches a uniform strength threshold.

Given that discharge is not an outcome, the next question:

must discharge wait for the duty episode to be completed, or must it appear as an instantaneous counter-directed displacement in A's value?

Neither.

Completion first. A temporal or durational duty may be actually discharging its counter-hold while the full episode has not yet ended; the counter-hold is occurring, and one cannot say it is not yet acting merely because it has not finished. Principle-action is first of all a process relation now occurring, not a completed event adjudicable only in retrospect. But conversely, not every moment of a duty's execution may be called acting: if the earlier stretch of that duty is mere preparation and transit, and no counter-hold has actually been formed yet, the duty is executing and the Principle is not yet acting. Action begins at the real occurrence of the counter-hold within the current actual Continuation; it is not mechanically identified with the moment the duty starts, nor deferred to the moment the duty completes.

Now the value. In the result type one misreading especially must be guarded against: P may currently close off part of the future reachable degrees of freedom A holds while A's instantaneous value has not yet moved. So long as those future reachable degrees of freedom were themselves part of A's current holding-tendency, and P has currently and really narrowed them, the counter-hold is already occurring at present. What "current" qualifies is that the counter-holding duty is occurring within the current actual Continuation; not that the consequence has already shown as some state quantity falling at this moment.

Given that action is current occurrence, the next question:

do the after-effects left by past action count as current action?

No.

Principle-action currently alters A's bearing and flow; those alterations then form traces, sediment, changes of organization, and changes in future Continuation by ordinary Sedimentation-Field dynamics. That generative chain runs:

　　current Principle-action  
　　→ change in current flow and bearing  
　　→ signal and trace  
　　→ ordinary Sedimentation-Field dynamics  
　　→ subsequent structural change.

After-effects are downstream of the chain and are borne by ordinary field dynamics; Principle does not write sediment directly, and has no special sedimentation operator. If only after-effects are present at present — d\* no longer occurs, or P's Principle qualification has already lapsed while the dynamical residue remains — that is not current action.

With this, the minimal content of "current" and "discharge" is closed.

The General Form

What the line of argument has obtained may now be read back formally.

Within the working domain Principle\_F,t(P;A,J)=1 and j∈J, if P's Principle relative to A is acting in j, then:

　　Act\_F,t(P;A,j)  
　　⇒  
　　CurAnti\_F,t(d\*;P,A,j)

where CurAnti\_F,t(d\*;P,A,j) is not a new field quantity and acquires no independent definitional standing, but is a compressed notation for the relation the line of argument has already forced out; wherever proof, minimality, or breakdown is at issue, the unfolded structure below governs. It contains at least three layers, none deletable, and all three are evaluated within the current actual Continuation.

First, current occurrence. The same established duty d\* through which P became a Principle relative to A actually occurs within the current actual Continuation — not an occurrence within a replay evaluation, and not the structure's being in place or Ω's being positive.

Second, current bearing. Within that same current Continuation, A's holding-tendency actually enters bearing, and d\*'s legal realization actually enters that bearing: for the delivery type, its contribution currently enters the generation of A's bearing quantity; for the result type, its restriction currently falls on a degree of freedom A holds and is discharged by the same DynamicRole. This is the standing, within the current actual Continuation, of the upstream AHold and LimitRole.

Third, current counter-hold. On at least one dynamical degree of freedom A actually holds, this realization is counter relative to A's holding-direction. This is the standing, within the current actual Continuation, of the upstream Anti.

In each legal single evaluation that constitutes the current discharge, current bearing, what the duty restricts, and the counter-hold must all be conjoined within one and the same legal witness Π\_η of that evaluation; across evaluations, the fixed duty d\*, the legal solution R\_j of the same j, and its Bind family must not drift, while Π\_η may be taken evaluation by evaluation from that fixed family under the upstream rules. One instance of action must not be assembled across evaluations, across duties, across carrier solutions, or across Bind families.

The three layers are not three parallel ontological items. Current bearing and current counter-hold are the internal structure of the upstream counter-holding duty; only one difference is added: this already established duty relation has landed in the current actual Continuation — the realization proving this instance of action is taken from actual occurrence, and a legal replay may not stand in for it.

Read back to the canonical source: a Principle's standing is that the counter-holding duty relation has come to stand; a Principle's acting is that this already standing duty relation enters the current actual Continuation and occurs. It is not potentiality becoming actuality — a Principle is not a potentiality; it is already a relation that stands. It is the current occurrence of a relation that already stands.

The Principal Theorem

Within the working domain Principle\_F,t(P;A,J)=1 and j∈J:

if P's Principle relative to A is acting in j, then the same duty d\* that made P a Principle actually occurs within the current actual Continuation; its legal realization actually enters A's current bearing of its holding-tendency; and on at least one dynamical degree of freedom A actually holds, it discharges its established counter-hold.

That is:

　　Principle\_F,t(P;A,J)=1  
　　∧ j∈J  
　　∧ Act\_F,t(P;A,j)

　　⇒

　　CurAnti\_F,t(d\*;P,A,j).

Only the necessary direction is proved.

Minimality

Only the newly added structure that the line of argument really forced out is tested here; inherited items of the working domain and manners of realization are not mixed in. Principle=1 and j∈J are working domain and take no part in the deletion test.

Delete current occurrence and keep only Principle qualification, and a Principle at rest is adjudicated as acting; the case of the first round re-enters.

Fold current occurrence into Principle qualification — taking "Ω still positive," "the structure still in place," or "a replay evaluation passing" as occurrence — and a Principle that passes replay while currently at rest is adjudicated as acting; the case of the first question under the discrimination of action re-enters.

Keep only current ordinary running and delete current bearing, and the cases of a duty started while the signal has not entered A, and of a result-type module executing without restricting the degree of freedom A holds or with an empty restriction, are adjudicated as acting; the cases of the second round re-enter.

Keep current bearing and delete current counter-hold, and same-direction and indifferent entries are adjudicated as acting; the cases of the third round re-enter.

The three layers are progressive: current counter-hold presupposes current bearing, and current bearing presupposes current occurrence. This is of a kind with the upstream Anti presupposing LimitRole and LimitRole presupposing d\*. The deletion test must therefore carry through: deleting a lower layer deletes with it the higher layer that presupposes it.

The independence of the three is witnessed at one end each: being at rest — occurrence does not hold, and bearing and counter-hold are out of the question; transit — occurrence holds and bearing does not; same-direction — occurrence and bearing hold and counter-hold does not.

Substitution Failures

Replace current counter-hold with "A's final outcome changes," and the compensation case is missed: P delivers −1 and Q makes up +1; the counter-hold is really discharged at present and the outcome is unchanged.

Replace it with "the outcome differs when P is withdrawn," and the redundancy case is missed: P and Q both really restrict A, and P's marginal effect is zero.

Replace it with "P prevails in the net composition," and the mixed case is missed: d\* counter-holds while d₂ supports, and the host's net direction supports A.

Replace current occurrence with "the duty is completed," and the case of a durational duty discharging its counter-hold with the episode unfinished is missed.

Replace current bearing with "the duty starts up," and the transit and module-execution cases are wrongly admitted.

Replace the same duty with "the same shape of behaviour," and the case of dX counterfeiting d\* is wrongly admitted.

Replace current counter-hold with "an instantaneous counter-directed displacement in A's value," and the result-type case of currently closing off A's future reachable degrees of freedom without the value moving is missed.

Require direct contact, a unique site of action, a unique or minimal witness, or a single node or single path, and legal action through long intermediaries, changed carriers, distributed bearing, and cyclic structures is missed.

Require "no compensation" or "no redundancy," and the compensation and redundancy cases are missed.

Require "A currently loses its Law" or "A's holding-tendency fails altogether," and weak action — the counter-hold discharged at present while A still prevails — is missed.

Erect a separate predicate of "duty continuation" as necessary, and it merely repeats the upstream requirements of a fixed d\*, the same realization, and the same witness family; delete it, and the dX counterfeit is still excluded upstream.

These may therefore serve as realization schemes or strong witnesses in later mathematical, determination, or grading tasks; they must not be raised into necessary structures of the general form of Principle-action.

Boundaries

The following boundaries must be stated.

The relation domain is not lent out. The current relational instance of Principle-action must belong to the J of the Principle relation. Where the current occurrence is in some j′∉J, it must not be called the action of the same Principle on the strength of the original Principle qualification; qualification for that relation domain must be acquired separately. Likewise, a successful replay evaluation in one legal j must not be spliced onto a failing current evaluation in another.

Qualification is not re-proved. The working domain has frozen Principle=1; only current occurrence is adjudicated. It is not required that the qualification across the whole of J be proved afresh each time an action occurs. Qualification is maintained by the whole of J, by Type-sensitive Sat and RelPass, and by legal replay evaluations; occurrence is taken from the current actual Continuation. Qualification and occurrence are two axes of adjudication: there is a Principle with no action (at rest), and there is counter-directed occurrence that is not Principle-action (ordinary collision, incidental inhibition, resource competition, temporary control, counter-directed action by something that has acquired neither Law nor Principle qualification). Both classes of degeneration exist independently, and so the two are not the same thing.

The unit of action is faithful to the duty's Type. An upstream duty may be point-type, set-type, measure-type, temporal, or durational; the granularity of current actual realization may correspondingly be one evaluation, a group of concurrent evaluations, a stretch of sequence, a durational interval, or an actual evaluation unit that carries an established measure structure. No mathematics of action aggregation is built here; it is declared only that current occurrence must be faithful to the legal realization semantics of the original Type and Read of the duty. One thing is forbidden above all: to see on which samples the counter-hold succeeded, and then to pick those samples out after the fact and define them as the "unit of action." The boundary of an action evaluation must be obtained from the original duty's Type, Read, relational context, actual running structure, or a pre-prescribed evaluation regime, and must not be selected in reverse according to the success or failure of the counter-hold. This is the anti-cheating interface delivered to The Mathematical Form of Principle-Action; no ontology of the unit of action is erected for it.

Pseudo-action. None of the following is current Principle-action: a Principle standing at rest; only a replay evaluation passing; a duty started without entering A's bearing; same-direction support or indifferent modulation; an empty result-type restriction; ordinary resource competition; an ordinary measurement side effect; ordinary prediction; a same-shaped substitute by another duty; after-effects left by past action; dynamical residue after Principle qualification has lapsed.

Action and arrival. The canonical "arrived at the NOT gate — this is action" is action within one concrete example. It must not be generalized into "all Principle-action must have a final arrival." Continuous restriction, path closure, gating, distributed action, and a compensated delivery-type counter-hold may each already be Principle-action while the final state has not changed or the net outcome is wholly unaltered. ActualAction ≠ ActualArrival.

The Sufficient Direction

The form-kind is a General Form, and bears the necessary direction.

In development, the sufficient direction was attacked to its limit: within the frozen working domain and the boundaries already excluded, no legal case was found in which all three layers of CurAnti hold within the current actual Continuation and which still could not be called Principle-action. That result is reported here, but is not signed as a theorem. Whether it is to be raised into an independent Necessity Form is subject to the specification of form-kinds and the configuration of later texts, and is not decided here: this form-text does not reach beyond its authority to settle it.

None.

The Mathematical Form of Principle-Action: to map the standing of the three layers within the current actual Continuation precisely into the formal system of Γ\_J, η, Π, and CounterEval; to give the rules by which a current actual evaluation unit is formed under the duty's Type; to formalize "the unit of action must not be selected in reverse according to the success or failure of the counter-hold" as an anti-splicing clause; and to treat the formal distinction between current evaluation and replay evaluation.

Final Compression

A Law holds what comes after it; a Principle, by its own hold, breaks the hold of another. That is a Principle's standing.

A Principle's acting is the discharge of that already established counter-holding duty within the current actual Continuation — the same duty occurring at present, entering the bearing of the holding-tendency the other is exercising, and being counter on a degree of freedom that other holds.

A Principle does not become a Principle by acting at present; a Principle stands first. Acting is what already stands, now occurring.

Compressed into one sentence:

　　What is a Principle: one whose counter-holding duty has come to stand. What is acting: that duty's discharge at this moment.

Status

Status: Established (the ontological necessary direction, within the frozen working domain Principle=1 and j∈J).

Inherited upstream conditions are transmitted under their original attributions, and this status does not upgrade them.

New H: none.

Breakdown: construct a legal case in which P's Principle is plainly acting by the semantics of the canonical source, while one of the following does not hold within the current actual Continuation — the same duty d\* that made P a Principle actually occurs at present; its legal realization enters A's current bearing of its holding-tendency; this realization is counter on a degree of freedom A actually holds; or prove that one of the three can be wholly absorbed by the others; or prove that the current actual Continuation and legal replay evaluation have no objective distinction upstream.
