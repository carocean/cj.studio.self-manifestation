# The Mathematical Form of Principle-Action

## Section One　Purpose

The Mathematical Form of Principle can already adjudicate whether a counter-holding duty suffices to make P a Principle relative to A. But that the same duty has become a Principle is not that it is discharging at this moment. The General Form of Principle-Action has already given the necessary direction within a frozen working domain:

　　Act\_F,t(P;A,j)  
　　⇒  
　　CurAnti\_F,t(d\*;P,A,j),

where d\* is the same fixed duty through which P became a Principle relative to A, and the ontological content of CurAnti has been sealed in three layers: the same d\* actually occurs within the current actual Continuation; that same current legal realization actually enters A's bearing of the holding-tendency it is exercising; and that same current realization discharges its established counter-hold on at least one dynamical degree of freedom A actually holds.

The remaining question is:

　　how is "actually occurring at present — entering bearing — forming a counter-hold" to be written as a formal system that can be evaluated item by item, adjudicated three-valuedly, and read back?

This question cannot be answered by running the qualification kernel of The Mathematical Form of Principle again. That text's single-evaluation kernel

　　CounterEval\_d\*  
　　⇔  
　　JointProj ∧ AHold ∧ LimitRole\_d\* ∧ Anti\_d\*

adjudicates qualification: whether, within one legal relational evaluation, what d*'s duty restricts and its counter-holding direction hold. A qualification evaluation may stand within a role evaluation or a legal replay evaluation while d* is not generating at the current moment. The Mathematical Form of Law has already distinguished three layers of chain: StructChains asks whether the structural position is there; ReplayChains asks whether it could be done again later; ActChain\_t asks whether generation is under way at present. The qualification structure of The Mathematical Form of Principle inherits the structural and replay layers through the Law-side realization interface, and does not require the Principle-conferring duty to have a non-empty ActChain\_t at this moment; action adjudication must add that current-chain constraint. Hence:

　　CounterEval\_d\*=1  
　　⇏  
　　CurAnti\_F,t(d\*;P,A,j).

There is one genuinely new formal difference and no more: to connect the current dynamical chain ActChain\_t(d*) of the same Principle-conferring duty d* into the counter-holding structure, and to require that entry into bearing and the counter-hold be discharged precisely by role events lying on that current chain.

The structural input is written S = On Self-Manifestation · Principle Chapter · Principle Group-Form IV v1.0 · The General Form of Principle-Action; the formal output is written M = the Principle-action formal system; the mapping is S ↦ M. The core output of M is:

　　CurAnti^M\_F,t(d\*;P,A,j).

M formalizes CurAnti only; it does not bear CurAnti ⇒ Act. Throughout, this is kept:

　　Act ⇒ CurAnti ↔ CurAnti^M,

and must not be written as Act ↔ CurAnti^M. The sufficient direction belongs to The Necessity Form of Principle-Action.

## Section Two　The Core Thesis

Fix:

　　F\_t　the current Sedimentation-Field;  
　　ν\_t　the current actual assignment;  
　　A　the target Law;  
　　P　the Law already standing as a Principle relative to A;  
　　J　the pre-declared relation domain of that Principle relation;  
　　j∈J　the relational context currently at issue;  
　　d\*∈Duties(Rel(P))　the fixed duty through which P became a Principle relative to A.

The working domain is fixed as in The General Form of Principle-Action: before entry, Law\_F,t(A,I\_A)=1, P≠A, I\_P≠∅, J≠∅, and Principle\_F,t(P;A,J)=1 are already fixed; the first four are the entry conditions of The General Form of Principle and are not entailed by Principle; under those entry conditions and the inherited conditions, Law(P,I\_P)=1 and CounterHoldRole(P;A,J)=1 are inherited from the necessary direction of The General Form of Principle, and from The Mathematical Form of Principle it is inherited that d\* is fixed across the whole of J; and j∈J. All of this is working domain.

The formal structure has three layers.

The first layer is the current factual base. The current actual occurrence of d\* can be given only by the current dynamical chain ActChain\_t(d*) of The Mathematical Form of Law — grounded in the current actual assignment ν\_t and the current inflow, and empty where ν\_t does not satisfy Ante(d*) or where the chain does not pass through its antecedent path. It cannot be replaced by the existence of structure, by Ω, by Bind, by a role evaluation, by a replay evaluation, or by the standing of CounterEval. RoleReal retains its upstream duties of qualification and legal realization; its semantics is unaltered, and it does not serve as a certificate of current fact.

The second layer is same-chain binding. The group of role events that suffices to discharge LimitPart must actually lie on d*'s current dynamical chain; it must not be made up from a structural chain, a replay chain, another duty, or another evaluation. Within a single evaluation the order of dependence is fixed: ActChain\_t(d*) → the current group of discharging events → Anti, and never in reverse.

The third layer is the current unit of discharge. One current discharge need not be a single evaluation; it is constituted by an evaluation family obtained in advance from d\*'s original Type/Read and the joint evaluation specification of j — including the current window and, for the measure type, the current measure — and its boundary must be fixed before any success or failure is read.

The formal goal is therefore:

　　CurAnti\_F,t(d*;P,A,j) ↦ CurAnti^M\_F,t(d*;P,A,j).

### 3.2　Current evaluation witness

Definition 3.2　\[aux\]〔current evaluation witness〕

Fix j. For η∈Γ\_J(j):

　　CurEval\_t(j;η)∈{1,0,⊥}.

CurEval\_t(j;η)=1 if and only if the current actual Continuation forms a legal evaluation η under Ctx\_J(j), and η is compatible with ν\_t, with the current inflow, and with that context's fixed background. η may still read existing structures within Existing\_t, the current background, and states left by historical sedimentation; what CurEval restricts is only that the witness of the source of occurrence must not be replaced by a replay; it does not restrict the provenance of η's structural components.

CurEval\_t(j;η)=0 if and only if it is determined that no such inducing exists: there is at present no actual run satisfying Ctx\_J(j), or ν\_t is inconsistent with η on the variables involved.

Where it cannot be adjudicated, the value is ⊥.

The witness is obtained under the EvalMode declared by Type(d\*), and may carry a time subscript. In the set type or the determinate type, the current actual Continuation induces at t a unique evaluation η\_cur, and CurEval\_t is 1 for it alone. In the measure type, the current generation kernel induces under ν\_t a current measure μ^cur\_J on Γ\_J(j); CurEval\_t is 1 for the η within the support of μ^cur\_J, and the weights of μ^cur\_J are carried along as part of the witness — the measure of a replay kernel must not be put in its place. In the temporal and durational types, the witness is obtained separately for each moment t\_a within a current window W taken in advance, by the actual assignment ν\_{t\_a} at that moment, and is written CurEval\_{t\_a}(j;η). Below, wherever CurEval\_t is written, it reads as CurEval\_{t\_a} in the window case.

CurEval is a witness relation between ν\_t and η; it is not an intrinsic property of η. A legal replay evaluation may agree with some current evaluation on every upstream predicate, and may even be equal to it as an assignment; in that case the same η has both a replay witness and, possibly, a current witness, and the two do not conflict — what CurEval records is "this η has a current factual witness," not "this η is current rather than replayed."

Declaration of relational properties: with F, t, ν\_t, and j fixed, CurEval is a three-valued witness predicate on η, its truth value given by the relation between ν\_t and η; reflexivity, symmetry, and transitivity are not discussed for it.

Why no upstream object can simply take its place: upstream 5.2 and 5.3 distinguish two ways of producing an evaluation, current actual running and legal replay; but qualification adjudication has no need to ask of a single η whether it has a current witness, since qualification is covered over J by Type-sensitive aggregation. Action adjudication does need it, because action is taken from current fact alone. Were provenance written as a property of η, a replay and a current evaluation of equal value would make that property contradict itself; written as a witness relation, it does not.

### 3.3　The current duty chain

Definition 3.3　\[aux\]〔the current occurrence of d\*〕

Inheriting Definition 3.19 of The Mathematical Form of Law:

　　ActChain\_t(d*) = Chain\_{ν\_t}^{(Existing\_t, current inflow, BaseFix(ν\_t), fixed)}(Read(d*)),

which is that chain where ν\_t ⊨ Ante(d*) and the chain passes through d*'s antecedent path, and ∅ otherwise.

Within the formal domain of this form-text,

　　ActChain\_t(d\*) ≠ ∅

serves as the formal candidate for "d\* actually occurs at present." Its read-back to the ontological structure of The General Form of Principle-Action inherits the corresponding H and Read conditions that The Mathematical Form of Law places on ActChain, and is not upgraded here into an unconditional ontological equivalence; the Law side has declared the formalization of H3 complete while its ontological read-back truth value is Conditional, and this form-text transmits that as it stands.

This formal candidate says only that d\* is generating at present. It naturally excludes: d*'s antecedent not holding at present; only a replay evaluation being present; only structural states left by past action being present with no current generation — in each of these ActChain\_t(d*)=∅. It does not entail that d\* has entered A's bearing, does not entail that Anti has been formed, and does not entail that the Principle is acting. The following must be kept:

　　ActChain\_t(d*)≠∅ ⇏ LimitRole\_d*=1;  
　　ActChain\_t(d*)≠∅ ∧ LimitRole\_d*=1 ⇏ Anti\_d\*=1.

Failing cases for the first: the current chain stops at a preparatory or transit stretch inside P and has not reached A's bearing organization; or a result-type restriction currently falls on a quantity A does not hold. For the second: the chain has entered A's bearing, but on this occasion it is with A's direction or indifferent to it.

Declaration of relational properties: ActChain\_t(d*) is a set of nodes; ActBear\_{R\_j}(d*)=R\_j∩Comp(ActChain\_t(d\*)) is a set of components; neither is a binary relation.

Why no upstream object can simply take its place: this is precisely an upstream object, not a new one; all that is added is taking its value at the fixed d\* and connecting it into the counter-holding structure. RoleReal cannot take its place: RoleReal is the legal realization witness used for the formation-state qualification of DynamicRole, and does not require an actual input at this moment.

3.4　The current group of discharging events

Definition 3.4　\[aux\]〔the group of discharging events on the current chain〕

Fix j, η, R\_j, and Π\_η∈ℱ\_B(P,d\*;R\_j).

Inheriting 3.13 of The Mathematical Form of Principle: RoleEvents\_{d*}(η,Π\_η) is the full set of role events of duty d* available for LimitPart in that realization, and MinRoleSuff\_T(LimitPart\_{d\*}^A,η) is the family of minimal groups of role events that, under the original semantics of Type/Read, suffice for LimitPart to acquire the qualification of being discharged.

An event-level relation of belonging is needed between role events and the current chain. Upstream, ActChain\_t(d*) is a set of nodes and ActBear a set of components; the two give which nodes and components the current chain passes through, but they do not give the event-level relation "this role event is an event instance of this very generation on that current chain." Co-location at a node cannot bear it: on one and the same node q a past event e\_old may have occurred that belongs to some minimal sufficient group, while what occurs at q as the current chain passes through is a different event e\_new, one that has not yet discharged LimitPart; e\_old's bearing node lies within Comp(ActChain\_t(d*)), and yet e\_old is not a current event. An event-instance map and an event-level predicate of belonging are therefore introduced.

Let ι\_t(e) be the event instance of role event e within the current time-unfolded generation graph — identified jointly by e's bearing node, the moment of e's occurrence at that node, and the generation step it belongs to. ι\_t is a representation map from RoleEvents to event instances of the current time-unfolded generation graph; its obtainability is entered under M-A1 (iii).

Define:

　　CurEvent\_t(e;d\*)∈{1,0,⊥},

　　CurEvent\_t(e;d*)=1 if and only if ι\_t(e) exists and that instance belongs to this very ActChain\_t(d*) — that is, its moment of occurrence and its generation step fall within the sequence of bearing nodes that chain yields when traced back from ν\_t and the current inflow;  
　　CurEvent\_t(e;d*)=0 if and only if ι\_t(e) determinately belongs to a generation at another moment, to a replay chain, or to structural history, and not to this very ActChain\_t(d*);  
　　CurEvent\_t(e;d\*)=⊥ if and only if ι\_t(e) cannot be obtained, or its belonging to this chain cannot be adjudicated.

CurEvent\_t can be 1 only where ActChain\_t(d\*)≠∅. It rewrites no ActChain\_t and adds no ontology: that an event belongs to this very generation is a fact of the current generation itself; ι\_t merely joins the role events already present upstream to the instances of the time-unfolded generation graph already present upstream. It is a newly added formal representation bridge, not a new ontological object.

　　CurLimit\_{d\*}(P;A,j,η,R\_j,Π\_η)=1

if and only if:

　　(i) LimitRole\_{d\*}(P;A,j,η,R\_j,Π\_η)=1;  
　　(ii) within the one legal duty realization that makes (i) hold, there exists a minimal sufficient group bearing its LimitPart,  
　　　　G∈MinRoleSuff\_T(LimitPart\_{d*}^A,η), G⊆RoleEvents\_{d*}(η,Π\_η),  
　　　　such that ∀e∈G, CurEvent\_t(e;d\*)=1.

The G in (ii) must be a sufficient group within the legal realization that makes this very LimitRole=1; one must not take some other group merely satisfying Suff\_T in order to make up currency.

CurLimit is a currency-adding condition upon LimitRole, not a second LimitRole. The whole content of the upstream LimitRole — DeliveryLimit or ResultLimit, RoleNonDegenerate, and belonging with DynamicRole, ΩWit, and Bind to one R\_j and one Π\_η — is inherited unaltered by (i); CurLimit asks one further thing only: whether the group of events discharging LimitPart are events of this very generation on the current chain.

Hence CurLimit=1 ⇒ LimitRole=1 is an immediate implication by definition. Conversely, LimitRole=1 does not entail CurLimit=1: LimitRole's witnessing group may be constituted by events on a structural chain, on a replay chain, or by past events at the same node. Nor does it suffice merely to have some G∈MinRoleSuff\_T consisting wholly of current events: if G⊆Self\_A, then RoleNonDegenerate=0, LimitRole=0, and CurLimit is 0 by (i).

Event-level belonging does not narrow the forms of realization permitted by The General Form of Principle-Action: the current chain may be distributed over several components, may contain cycles, may span several steps; for a durational duty the current chain extends with the Continuation, and the discharging group may be distributed over different nodes and different moments on that chain; the narrowing of A's future reachable degrees of freedom by a result type is adjudicated by Anti on the current evaluation under the upstream Att exclusion rule, and does not require A's value to move at this moment. CurEvent excludes only "the event does not belong to this generation"; it does not exclude "the events of this generation are widely distributed."

The order of dependence is fixed:

　　ActChain\_t(d\*)≠∅  
　　→ CurEvent\_t / CurLimit\_{d\*}  
　　→ Anti\_{d\*}.

Anti takes no part in obtaining ActChain; CurLimit does not define "current occurrence."

Declaration of relational properties: ι\_t is a partial map; CurEvent\_t(·;d\*) is a three-valued unary predicate on role events; CurLimit is a three-valued predicate.

Why no upstream object can simply take its place: the upstream LimitRole requires only that there exist a legal realization under which what the duty restricts holds, belonging with Ω/Bind to one Π, and asks nothing of the present; the upstream ActChain\_t gives the nodes and components of the current chain, and does not give the belonging of events. Action adjudication needs both at once, and needs them joined together at the event level.

### 3.5　The current single-evaluation kernel

Definition 3.5　\[S\]〔current single-evaluation discharge of a counter-hold〕

Fix j, η, R\_j, Π\_η∈ℱ\_B(P,d\*;R\_j), and the moment t\_a to which that evaluation belongs (point type: t\_a=t; window type: t\_a∈W).

　　CurCounterEval\_{d\*}(P;A,j,η,R\_j,Π\_η;t\_a)=1

if and only if:

　　(i) CurEval\_{t\_a}(j;η)=1;  
　　(ii) ActChain\_{t\_a}(d\*)≠∅;  
　　(iii) CurLimit\_{d\*}(P;A,j,η,R\_j,Π\_η)=1, with CurEvent taken as CurEvent\_{t\_a};  
　　(iv) within the same η, the same Π\_η, the fixed R\_j, and the fixed ℱ\_B: JointProj\_j(η)=1, AHold\_A(j,η)=1, Anti\_{d\*}(P,A,j,η)=1, and the restriction to which Anti corresponds is precisely the restriction discharged in (iii) by the current group of events.

Where any item determinately fails, the value is 0; with no determinate failure but a key ⊥ present, the value is ⊥. Where t\_a is omitted, t is meant.

The four items are the formal representations of the three layers of The General Form of Principle-Action: (i) and (ii) are current occurrence; (iii), together with AHold in (iv), is current bearing; Anti in (iv) is current counter-hold; and the same η, same Π\_η, and same current chain across (iii) and (iv) are the requirement of same evaluation, same witness, and same realization.

Declaration of relational properties: CurCounterEval is a three-valued predicate.

Lemma 3.1〔the current kernel entails the qualification kernel〕

　　CurCounterEval\_{d*}=1 ⇒ CounterEval\_{d*}=1.

Proof. (iv) gives JointProj, AHold, and Anti; (iii), CurLimit=1, gives LimitRole\_{d\*}=1 directly by Definition 3.4 (i). All four items of Definition 3.29 of The Mathematical Form of Principle therefore hold. □

Lemma 3.2〔the qualification kernel does not entail the current kernel〕

In general: CounterEval\_{d*}=1 ⇏ CurCounterEval\_{d*}=1.

Proof. Take η to be a legal replay evaluation while there is at present no actual run satisfying Ctx\_J(j): CounterEval may be 1 on the replay evaluation while CurEval\_t(j;η)=0, so (i) fails. This one case suffices to prove the non-implication. □

Two further independent failing types are registered here, though they do not bear this lemma: where ν\_t⊭Ante(d*), or where only structural states left by past action are present with no current generation, ActChain\_t(d*)=∅ and (ii) fails; where the current chain is non-empty and LimitRole=1, but within the realization that makes it hold every minimal sufficient group discharging LimitPart contains a non-current event — the chain stopping at a transit stretch, or co-located with past events — (iii) fails.

3.6　The current family of discharge evaluations

Definition 3.6　\[aux\]〔current unit of discharge〕

Fix j, d\*, R\_j, ℱ\_B.

　　𝒜\_F,t(d\*,j)

is the finite or measurable set obtained in advance under the three items below, its members being a=(t\_a,η,Π\_η), where t\_a∈W, W being a current window obtained in advance (for the point type and the measure type, W={t}; for the temporal and durational types, W is an interval or sequence ending at t), η∈Γ\_J(j) with CurEval\_{t\_a}(j;η)=1, and Π\_η∈ℱ\_B(P,d*;R\_j). In the measure type, 𝒜 carries the restriction of the current measure μ^cur\_J to 𝒜 as the measure used in reading out; the measure of a replay kernel or of a historical kernel must not be put in its place. In the measure type the current chain ActChain\_t(d*) is obtained from ν\_t by way of ActArg^μ and is shared by all η within the support; the role events of each η belong, through ι\_t, to one and the same current generation step, and their CurEvent\_t is adjudicated by whether that generation step is this very one — the differences among the η are different results of one and the same current generation step, not different current generations.

There are three grounds of obtaining and no others: the legal-unit semantics that d*'s original Type(d*) and Read(d\*) give for "what evaluation structure constitutes one discharge of a duty"; the existing joint evaluation specification Ctx\_J(j) of j; and the running structure of this very current actual Continuation.

On those grounds, the forms 𝒜 may take include: a single evaluation (W={t}, one member); an evaluation unit carrying a current measure structure (W={t}, members being the η within the support of μ^cur\_J); a stretch of sequence or a durational interval (W spanning several moments, the members at each moment witnessed by its own ν\_{t\_a}). Type by itself does not always determine the unit of occurrence uniquely; W and the members are obtained jointly from the three grounds, and are not fixed one to one by the name of the Type. The boundaries of W and 𝒜 are alike bound by outcome-blindness.

Across members: d\* is fixed; j is fixed; R\_j is fixed; ℱ\_B is fixed; Π\_η may be obtained evaluation by evaluation from that fixed family under 3.17 of The Mathematical Form of Principle; each member's ActChain\_{t\_a} and CurEvent\_{t\_a} are obtained at its own moment, and the current chain of one moment must not be carried over to another.

The boundary of 𝒜 must be obtained before any success or failure of CounterEval, CurCounterEval, Anti, or Sat is read. Any construction that filters by success or failure is forbidden. This requirement of outcome-blindness is not deletable.

Declaration of relational properties: 𝒜 is a set-valued object, not a binary relation.

Why no upstream object can simply take its place: upstream, Sat and RelPass aggregate CounterEval by Type over the whole of j, and then ∀j∈J covers it up to qualification — that is the unit of the qualification layer. The action layer needs the unit of "this discharge"; it takes the unit semantics of the same Type/Read, but takes neither ∀j∈J nor the totality of evaluations on j — only the family constituted by this very current Continuation.

### 3.7　CurAnti^M

Definition 3.7　\[S\]〔formal representation of the current discharge of a counter-hold〕

　　CurAnti^M\_F,t(d\*;P,A,j)∈{1,0,⊥}

is read out from the CurCounterEval values of the members of 𝒜\_F,t(d*,j), under the original unit semantics that Type(d*) and Read(d\*) give for one discharge: a single-member unit is read at that member; the measure type applies the original Read's threshold, weight, or a.e. requirement to the set of successful members under the current measure μ^cur\_J carried by 𝒜; the temporal and durational types read out over W under the original Read's window, whole-stretch, or durational requirement. No second Read is erected here; the original Type/Read is simply applied to a current unit of discharge already legally obtained.

Three-valued:

　　1: the dynamical chain of the current fixed d\* is non-empty, the group of discharging events are all events of this very chain, A's current bearing is entered, Anti holds, and all the requirements of same evaluation, same witness, and same chain determinately hold on 𝒜 under the original unit semantics;  
　　0: at least one necessary structure of CurAnti determinately fails on 𝒜 under the original unit semantics;  
　　⊥: there is no decisive 0, and the current witness, the current chain, event-level belonging, the boundary of discharge, or a load-bearing formal value does not suffice for adjudication, or the representation conditions of M-A1 required to complete the current adjudication are not satisfied.

If the obtaining of 𝒜 violates the requirement of outcome-blindness, this evaluation is void: the protocol yields no truth value for CurAnti^M, and neither 0 nor 1 may be recorded. A violation of outcome-blindness is an illegality of the adjudication protocol, not an ontological negation of CurAnti.

If it is determinate that no η attains CurEval\_{t\_a}(j;η)=1 anywhere in W, then there is at present no evaluation in j, and CurAnti^M=0; if the witness cannot be adjudicated, then ⊥.

## Section Four　The Equations

### 4.1　The general formal formula

When M-A1 of Section Five and the inherited upstream formal representation antecedents hold:

　　CurAnti\_F,t(d*;P,A,j) ↔ CurAnti^M\_F,t(d*;P,A,j).

CurAnti^M=1 unfolds as:

　　𝒜\_F,t(d\*,j) is obtained outcome-blind  
　　∧ under the unit semantics of Type(d*)/Read(d*), for a=(t\_a,η,Π\_η)∈𝒜, by the original quantifier (the measure type taking μ^cur\_J as its measure):  
　　　 CurEval\_{t\_a}(j;η)=1  
　　　 ∧ ActChain\_{t\_a}(d\*)≠∅  
　　　 ∧ LimitRole\_{d*}=1 ∧ within its legal realization ∃G∈MinRoleSuff\_T(LimitPart\_{d*}^A,η): G⊆RoleEvents\_{d*}(η,Π\_η) ∧ ∀e∈G, CurEvent\_{t\_a}(e;d*)=1  
　　　 ∧ JointProj\_j(η)=1 ∧ AHold\_A(j,η)=1 ∧ Anti\_{d\*}=1,  
　　　 the restriction to which Anti corresponds being discharged by G, and all four conjoined within the same η, the same Π\_η, the fixed R\_j, and the fixed ℱ\_B.

Together with The General Form of Principle-Action:

　　Act\_F,t(P;A,j) ⇒ CurAnti\_F,t(d*;P,A,j) ⇒ CurAnti^M\_F,t(d*;P,A,j).

Act must not be inferred from CurAnti^M.

4.2　The qualification layer and the action layer

Qualification layer: CounterEval → Sat → RelPass(j) → ∀j∈J → CounterHoldRole.

Action layer: ActChain\_t(d*) → CurLimit → CurCounterEval → 𝒜\_F,t(d*,j) → CurAnti^M.

The two layers share the unit semantics of Type/Read; they do not share the total quantifier over the relation domain. Hence RelPass ≠ CurAnti^M, and CurAnti^M must not be defined as any form of RelPass^Active.

The three layers of chain on the Law side supply the interfaces of structure, replay, and current generation respectively: StructChains asks whether the structural position is there; ReplayChains asks whether it could be done again later; ActChain asks whether generation is under way at present. The qualification structure does not require the fixed d\* to have a non-empty ActChain\_t at this moment; this form-text additionally invokes the current-chain interface.

## Section Five　Running and Solving

### 5.1　Order of solution

Given (F\_t,ν\_t,A,P,J,j,d\*), with Principle=1 and j∈J already fixed.

Solving observes one overriding rule: ⊥ is recorded only, and never terminates early; only a decisive 0 may terminate early.

Step one: obtain in advance the current window W under Type(d*)/Read(d*) and the running structure (for the point type and the measure type, W={t}); for each t\_a in W take the actual assignment ν\_{t\_a} and the current inflow, and find in Γ\_J(j) the evaluations with CurEval\_{t\_a}(j;η)=1, taking also, in the measure type, the current measure μ^cur\_J. If it is determinate that there is no current evaluation anywhere in W, then CurAnti^M=0 and solving terminates; if the witness cannot be adjudicated, record ⊥ and go on to the other solvable items.

Step two: for each t\_a obtain ActChain\_{t\_a}(d\*). Where it is determinately empty at some t\_a, the member at that moment is recorded as a decisive 0; if those emptinesses have already formed a determinate overall failure over W under the original Read — for the point type, emptiness at t; for a universal or whole-stretch type, emptiness at any moment — then CurAnti^M=0 and solving terminates; otherwise record and go on. Where it cannot be adjudicated, record ⊥ and go on.

Step three: obtain 𝒜\_F,t(d*,j) outcome-blind, under Type(d*)/Read(d\*), Ctx\_J(j), and the running structure of this Continuation. This step is completed before step four, and the boundary once obtained must not be altered; if the boundary is found to have been obtained or altered after successes and failures were read, this evaluation is void and no truth value is output.

Step four: for each member (t\_a,η,Π\_η) of 𝒜: first obtain LimitRole\_{d*} by The Mathematical Form of Principle; where it holds, take MinRoleSuff\_T(LimitPart\_{d*}^A,η) within the legal realization that makes it hold, obtain CurEvent\_{t\_a}(e;d\*) for the events of each group, and adjudicate whether there exists a minimal sufficient group consisting wholly of current events and belonging to Π\_η, giving CurLimit; then obtain JointProj, AHold, and Anti, and verify that the restriction to which Anti corresponds is discharged by that group, giving CurCounterEval.

Step five: read CurCounterEval up to CurAnti^M under the unit semantics of Type(d*)/Read(d*).

Step six: propagate the three values at two layers. Adjudication is made here and nowhere earlier; the ⊥ recorded before are handled at this step together with the rest, and solving must not be ended ahead of it. Within a member (the conjunction of the items of CurCounterEval): any decisive 0 makes that member 0; no 0 with a ⊥ present makes it ⊥; only all 1 makes it 1. Across members (reading CurAnti^M out on 𝒜): evaluate strictly under the original unit semantics of Type(d*)/Read(d*), and do not use "any member 0 makes the whole 0" — for a measure-type duty, one member being 0 still leaves CurAnti^M at 1 so long as the measure of the successful members still reaches the threshold of the original Read; only where the original Read itself demands a universal, whole-stretch, or full-coverage condition does one member's 0 make the whole 0 directly. The propagation of ⊥ likewise follows the quantifier and measure semantics of the original Read: where an unadjudicable member makes the required quantifier or measure unadjudicable, the whole is ⊥; otherwise it has no effect.

### 5.2　Three-valued discipline

MissingEvidence → ⊥; ContradictoryEvidence → 0. Within a member the conjunction follows this; across members, aggregation follows the original Type/Read semantics of step six in 5.1, and is not propagated mechanically.

"No relevant value of ν\_t has been recorded" must not be written as ActChain\_t(d*)=∅; only a determinate ν\_t⊭Ante(d*), or a determinate absence of current generation, makes it empty. "No current witness has been obtained" must not be written as CurEval=0; only a determinate absence of an actual run satisfying Ctx\_J(j), or a determinate inconsistency, makes it 0. Where the event-instance map ι\_t cannot be obtained, or belonging cannot be adjudicated, CurEvent is ⊥; co-location at a node must not be put in its place.

0 takes precedence over ⊥ — this applies only within one and the same necessary conjunction, or where an overall adjudication has already been formed under the original Read; across the members of 𝒜 there is no general precedence of 0.

### 5.3　Current and replay

A legal replay evaluation is obtained from Estab\_t under 5.3 of The Mathematical Form of Principle, and may be equal in value to a current evaluation; qualification adjudication may use either, while action adjudication uses only what has a current witness. Where action fails on the current evaluation, one must not make up or replace the members of 𝒜 with successful members from a replay evaluation; one must not make up CurLimit with events on a replay chain, on a structural chain, or with past events at the same node; and in the measure type the measure of a replay kernel must not be put in place of the current measure μ^cur\_J.

5.4　Solvability and representation conditions

M1 through M8 of The Mathematical Form of Principle, and the Law-side formal representation antecedents they invoke, are inherited — in particular that ν\_t and the current inflow and BaseFix on which ActChain\_t depends are obtainable.

Newly added:

M-A1　Conditions for the representability of current discharge

(i) The evaluation witness of ν\_t and the current inflow under Ctx\_J(j) is obtainable. (ii) ActChain\_t(d*) is adjudicable. (iii) The representation map ι\_t from role events to event instances of the current time-unfolded generation graph is obtainable, so that CurEvent\_t(e;d*) is adjudicable — an event-to-node correspondence alone will not do, since it cannot distinguish a past event at the same node from a current one; ι\_t must give the moment of occurrence and the generation step, not the node alone. (iv) The boundary of this current discharge evaluation as determined by the original Type/Read — including the current window W — is obtainable within the existing system of joint evaluation, R\_j, Π\_η, and Bind. (v) In the measure type, the current measure μ^cur\_J induced under ν\_t by the current generation kernel is obtainable and distinguishable from a replay-kernel measure; and every load-bearing difference that would change the truth value of CurAnti is preserved.

M-A1 does not require that the complete microscopic history be recoverable, that every actual process be observable of itself, that the representation map be unique, or that every evaluation variable be measured directly; it requires only that the action-relevant differences not be wholly lost in the formal representation. Where some representation condition of M-A1 is missing: if a decisive 0 on a necessary item of CurAnti is already in hand, the 0 stands; if there is no decisive 0 and the representation conditions required to complete the current adjudication are missing, the output is ⊥. A missing representation does not override a 0 already determined.

Where any condition is missing, no value may be filled in at will.

## Section Seven　Read-Back Rules and Theorems

Theorem 7.1〔the qualification kernel is not the action kernel〕

　　CurCounterEval\_{d*}=1 ⇒ CounterEval\_{d*}=1;  
　　CounterEval\_{d*}=1 ⇏ CurCounterEval\_{d*}=1.

Proof. The first half by Lemma 3.1; the second by the replay counterexample of Lemma 3.2. An empty current chain, and a discharging group containing non-current events, are two further independent failing types; see the note to Lemma 3.2. □

Corollary: The Mathematical Form of Principle does not suffice to take the place of this form-text; qualification adjudication and action adjudication are two formal objects.

Theorem 7.2〔same-chain closure〕

If CurCounterEval\_{d*}(P;A,j,η,R\_j,Π\_η)=1, then the dynamical chain proving current occurrence, the minimal sufficient group of role events discharging LimitPart, and the restriction to which Anti corresponds all belong to one and the same current chain ActChain\_{t\_a}(d*) of the same d\* (at that member's moment), to the same η, to the same Π\_η, to the fixed R\_j, and to the fixed ℱ\_B; not across duties, not across evaluations, not across carrier solutions, not across Bind families, and not made up from a structural chain, a replay chain, past events at the same node, or events of another duty.

Proof. Items (ii), (iii), and (iv) of Definition 3.5 bind all three to the same ActChain\_t(d\*), the same η, and the same Π\_η; Definition 3.4 requires LimitRole to hold and, within the realization that makes it hold, every event of the discharging group to be an event of this very chain and to belong to that Π\_η; Definition 3.3's ActChain\_t contains current generation only; Definition 3.2 excludes evaluations without a current witness. □

Theorem 7.3〔outcome-blindness of the boundary of current discharge〕

𝒜\_F,t(d\*,j) must be fixed before any success or failure of CounterEval, CurCounterEval, Anti, or Sat is read. Otherwise there exists a post-selection construction: retaining from a legal evaluation family only the successful members, so that a current failure or non-action is represented as CurAnti^M=1.

Proof. Take a legal set-type or measure-type instance whose family 𝒜′ has some members with CurCounterEval=0, so that CurAnti^M=0 under the original unit semantics, while 𝒜″={a∈𝒜′ \| CurCounterEval(a)=1}, obtained by deleting the failing members, passes under that same Read — under a universal Read this holds of any family containing a failing member, and under a threshold Read it suffices to take an instance whose failing members carry enough measure that the original family falls short of the threshold while the family with them deleted reaches it. Were filtering by success or failure permitted, one and the same instance would receive two values, and the representation would be distorted. The existence of such an instance suffices to prove that post-selection must be forbidden. □

Theorem 7.4〔formal read-back〕

When the inherited upstream read-back conditions and M-A1 hold:

　　CurAnti^M=1 ⇒ CurAnti=1.

If CurAnti^M=0 arises from a necessary item that has ontological read-back standing, and that failure has, under the original Type/Read unit semantics of current discharge, already formed a determinate overall failure — a determinate absence of a current witness, a determinately empty ActChain\_t(d\*), or a failure of CurLimit or Anti already sufficient under the original Read to make the current discharge not hold — then:

　　CurAnti^M=0 ⇒ CurAnti=0.

CurAnti^M=⊥ makes no commitment as to the truth or falsity of CurAnti.

Proof. If CurAnti^M=1, then on 𝒜, under the original unit semantics: d\*'s current dynamical chain is non-empty and has a current witness, and this, under the inherited Law-side read-back conditions on ActChain, is the first layer; the group of events discharging LimitPart lies on that chain and stands in the same evaluation as AHold (the second layer); that same group of events discharges Anti (the third layer); and same evaluation, same witness, same chain are guaranteed by Theorem 7.2. The three layers are exactly the unfolded structure of CurAnti in The General Form of Principle-Action; that the discharging group consists wholly of current events guarantees that the second and third layers are borne by this very realization, and not by a past realization merely co-located with it. Each decisive source of CurAnti^M=0 corresponds to one of the three layers determinately failing to hold within the current actual Continuation, that failure being already such as to make the current discharge not hold under the original Type/Read; where a single member's Anti or CurLimit is 0 while the whole still passes under the original Read, no CurAnti^M=0 arises, and such a case does not enter this clause. A violation of outcome-blindness yields no 0 but only voids this evaluation, and so does not enter read-back. ⊥ signifies, by 5.2, insufficiency of evidence only. □

Theorem 7.5〔conditional representation〕

If M-A1 holds, and the inherited representation antecedents hold, then:

　　CurAnti=1 ⇒ CurAnti^M=1.

Proof. CurAnti=1 gives ontological current occurrence; under the inherited Law-side converse representation conditions, that structure receives the formal representation ActChain\_t(d\*)≠∅. Its current legal realization enters A's current bearing and discharges the established counter-hold, with the same evaluation, the same witness, and the same realization. M-A1 guarantees that the current witness, the current chain, event-level belonging, the current window, the current measure, and the boundary of discharge are all obtainable within the existing system without loss of load-bearing differences; and CurAnti's "the current realization discharges" gives a group of events discharging LimitPart, which are precisely events of this very generation, so that CurEvent is 1 for them. An 𝒜 may therefore be taken on whose members CurEval, ActChain, CurLimit, JointProj, AHold, and Anti hold item by item, and CurAnti^M=1 is read out under the original unit semantics. □

This theorem bears formal representability only. It asserts nothing about an actual CurAnti being observable, recordable, or encodable of itself.

Theorem 7.6〔the principal general formula〕

Within the working domain Principle=1, j∈J, d\* fixed, and under M-A1 and the inherited antecedents, Theorems 7.4 and 7.5 give:

　　CurAnti\_F,t(d*;P,A,j) ↔ CurAnti^M\_F,t(d*;P,A,j).

Together with the necessary direction of The General Form of Principle-Action:

　　Act\_F,t(P;A,j) ⇒ CurAnti^M\_F,t(d\*;P,A,j).

CurAnti^M ⇒ Act is not signed.

### 7.7　Corollaries

Corollary one〔replay does not take the place of current〕. A replay evaluation may be equal in value to a current evaluation, with CounterEval the same; but a current witness is a relation between ν\_t and η, and a replay witness does not give it. Nor are events on a replay chain events of this very ActChain\_t(d\*).

Corollary two〔freezing does not suffice〕. ActChain\_t is given by ν\_t and the current inflow, not by Freeze\_t. There exist two instances whose frozen field states are equal in every value, while in one d\* is generating at this moment and in the other only structural states left by past generation remain; their ActChain\_t(d\*) are, respectively, non-empty and empty. Freeze(F\_t) is therefore in general not a sufficient statistic for CurAnti.

Corollary three〔same shape of behaviour does not suffice〕. If another duty d\_X currently produces exactly the same restricting result as d*, while ActChain\_t(d*)=∅, then CurAnti^M(d\*)=0. Identity of behavioural result is not identity of duty.

Corollary four〔a duty's running does not suffice〕. ActChain\_t(d\*)≠∅ does not entail CurAnti^M=1: the current chain may stop at a preparatory or transit stretch, or may have entered A while being with its direction or indifferent to it. A duty's current generation is not Principle-action.

Corollary five〔completion is not necessary〕. CurAnti^M=1 requires only that the three layers hold within the current Continuation under the original unit semantics; it does not require that the whole duty episode has ended.

## Section Eight　Back-Testing Against Instances

### 8.1　Protocol for the positive instance

The positive instance does not acquire Principle qualification here from nothing. Let an instance have acquired its working domain upstream: Law(A,I\_A), Law(P,I\_P), R\_j∈𝒮, DynamicRole, Ω, Bind, with RelPass and CounterHoldRole holding across the whole of J, Principle=1, and d\* its Principle-conferring duty. What follows only adds the current-chain back-test upon that working domain, to show its form.

Let the instance be two Laws on a shared carrier: R={e\_A,e\_P}, with common entry q, e\_A: q→a, e\_P: q→p; the common generation kernel gives π\_A=μ(Y=e\_A) and π\_P=μ(Y=e\_P); A's duty Read\_A: π\_A≥1/8; P's fixed duty d*, Read\_P: π\_P≥3/4, equivalently π\_A≤1/4; A's current holding-direction is π\_A rising; d* is result-type, restricting π\_A to the side A does not hold, Anti=1, the restriction being discharged through the common kernel by P's same DynamicRole. This is a working-domain assumption and is not proved here.

The action back-test: deliver one current actual unit pulse into q, giving ν\_t; under Ctx\_J(j) obtain the current evaluation η, with CurEval\_t(j;η)=1. ν\_t⊨Ante(d*), d* generates at present by way of e\_P, and ActChain\_t(d*)≠∅. The current generation node of the common kernel lies on that chain; within MinRoleSuff\_T(LimitPart\_{d*}^A,η) there exists a minimal sufficient group constituted by the current gating output of e\_P, all of whose events are current generation events under this very pulse, so CurEvent=1 and CurLimit=1. g\_A actually takes part in the generation at present, so AHold=1; that restriction is opposite to A's holding-direction on π\_A, so Anti=1, and it is discharged by that same group. The unit is a single member, 𝒜={(η,Π\_η)}, obtained outcome-blind. Hence CurCounterEval=1 and CurAnti^M=1.

Whether Y finally draws e\_A is not a necessary condition of this result-type duty's current discharge: what d\* currently restricts is the measure structure μ(Y) already actually generated, not the outcome of a single draw. The load-bearing measure actually generated ≠ the final sampling outcome.

### 8.2　Negative instances

N1　At rest. The Principle structure, Ω, Bind, and replay evaluations are retained; ν\_t⊭Ante(d*), or there is determinately no current generation, so ActChain\_t(d*)=∅. CurAnti^M=0.

N2　Replay only. An equal-valued CounterEval may be obtained on a replay evaluation, but there is determinately no current actual run satisfying Ctx\_J(j) and no evaluation with CurEval=1. CurAnti^M=0. The same holds in the measure type where a replay-kernel measure is passed off as μ^cur\_J.

N3　Started but not entered. ActChain\_t(d\*)≠∅, but the chain stops at a preparatory or internal transit stretch; LimitRole does not hold on that η, or it holds while its realization contains no minimal sufficient group consisting wholly of current events; CurLimit=0. CurAnti^M=0.

N4　Entered but not counter. CurEval, ActChain, CurLimit, and AHold hold; Anti=0. CurAnti^M=0.

N5　After-effect. d\* once formed Anti in the past and the current structural state still preserves its result, but under the current ν\_t d\* has no generation, so ActChain\_t(d\*)=∅. CurAnti^M=0.

N6　Duty substitution. d\_X≠d\* currently produces the same restricting effect, while ActChain\_t(d*)=∅. CurAnti^M(d*)=0.

N7　Cross-chain or co-located splicing. The current chain is non-empty and LimitRole=1, but within the realization that makes it hold, every minimal sufficient group discharging LimitPart contains a non-current event — from a structural chain, from a replay chain, or a past event co-located with the current chain — and no group consists wholly of current events. CurAnti^M=0.

N7′　Degenerate counterfeit. The current chain is non-empty and there exists a G∈MinRoleSuff\_T consisting wholly of current events, but G⊆Self\_A, so RoleNonDegenerate=0 and LimitRole=0. CurLimit is 0 by Definition 3.4 (i). CurAnti^M=0.

N8　Cross-evaluation or cross-witness splicing. AHold, CurLimit, and Anti hold only under incompatible η, Π, R, or ℱ\_B respectively. CurAnti^M=0.

N9　Post-selected family. CurCounterEval is computed first, and 𝒜 is then defined by the successful members. The protocol is illegal, this evaluation is void, and no truth value is yielded.

N10　Insufficient evidence. It is confirmed that a relevant current run exists, but ν\_t is unadjudicable on Ante(d\*), or the event-instance map ι\_t cannot be obtained or its belonging cannot be adjudicated; there is no decisive 0. CurAnti^M=⊥.
