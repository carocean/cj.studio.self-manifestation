# The Necessity Form of Principle-Action

## Section One　Purpose

When is an already standing Principle already acting?

The General Form of Principle-Action has answered the necessary direction: if a Principle is acting, then the same duty that made it a Principle must have actually occurred within the current actual Continuation; that same current legal realization must have entered the bearing of a holding-tendency another Law is exercising; and on a dynamical degree of freedom that other Law actually holds, it must have discharged its established counter-hold. These three layers are compressed as CurAnti.

That says what Principle-action must have. It has not yet answered the converse question:

　　if all three layers already hold in current fact, is that already enough to say that this Principle is acting?

One case remains unexcluded:

　　P already stands as a Principle relative to A;  
　　the same duty d\* that made P a Principle actually occurs at present;  
　　that same current legal realization has entered A's bearing of the holding-tendency it is exercising;  
　　and it has discharged its established counter-hold on a degree of freedom A actually holds;

and yet:

　　this Principle is still not acting — something is still wanting: a making-so, or a driver, or an arrival.

If such a case can stand, CurAnti can be only a necessary condition of acting and cannot yet serve as a sufficient criterion; and the boundary between "being a Principle" and "acting" would remain unclosed.

What follows treats this gap and nothing else.

What is to be proved: within the given working domain, the frozen canonical semantic domain, and the inherited conditions,

　　CurAnti\_F,t(d\*;P,A,j)  
　　⇒  
　　Act\_F,t(P;A,j),

and, joined with the necessary direction of The General Form of Principle-Action:

　　Act\_F,t(P;A,j)  
　　⇔  
　　CurAnti\_F,t(d\*;P,A,j).

The necessary direction cannot simply be inverted. The following inference is invalid:

　　Act ⇒ CurAnti, therefore CurAnti ⇒ Act.

Nor may one define Act := CurAnti and then obtain the sufficient direction from the definition; that hides the conclusion to be proved inside the definition.

Sufficiency must be obtained independently:

　　fix the canonical semantic domain  
　　→ unitize  
　　→ separate current-realization semantics from the rest  
　　→ force out and exhaust the components of current realization  
　　→ check for counter-realization  
　　→ CurAnti realizes every component of current realization, item by item  
　　→ Act.

One point must be made first. Act is not some new qualification acquired after Principle. Principle is the qualification of a relation already formed; Act is whether that already formed qualification is being discharged at present. What follows therefore does not speak of "Act acquiring some further current qualification," and constructs no object such as a "law of action," an "action qualification," or a "second sedimentation qualification of action." Principle=1 with Act=0 is perfectly legal: the Principle stands and is currently at rest.

What is proved is only the current sufficient criterion of Principle-action. Not treated: who makes this Principle begin to act; the strength of acting; the persistence and termination of acting; its after-effects; how acting generates a new Law; whether acting is recognized.

"Executing identity," "relational entry," "currency of the counter-hold," and the R1, R2, R3, U\_Act and the like appearing below are intermediate names within the canonical analysis and the proof only. They acquire no independent ontological predicate standing, and must all be unfolded back into canonical sentences and into structures already standing upstream.

## Section Two　Working Domain and Upstream Inputs

### 2.1　Working domain

Fixed before entry:

　　CounterHoldRole\_F,t(P;A,J)=1;

and from The Mathematical Form of Principle it is inherited that d\* is the same fixed duty through which P became a Principle relative to A, its identity not drifting across the whole of J.

### 2.2　Structural upstream

The General Form of Principle-Action gives:

　　Act\_F,t(P;A,j)  
　　⇒  
　　CurAnti\_F,t(d\*;P,A,j).

CurAnti is a compressed notation only, and its ontological unfolding is fixed in three layers:

　　C1　the same d\* actually occurs within the current actual Continuation;  
　　C2　that same current legal realization actually enters A's bearing of the holding-tendency it is exercising;  
　　C3　that same current realization discharges the established counter-hold on a dynamical degree of freedom A actually holds.

The three layers must be conjoined within the same current Continuation, the same legal relational instance, and the same realization. What follows neither rewrites the three layers nor infers Act back from them. The numbering C1–C3 serves for comparison only and adds no ontology.

### 2.3　Formal upstream

The Mathematical Form of Principle-Action has established a conditional formal representation:

　　CurAnti ↔ CurAnti^M,

whose standing retains that text's M-A1, its read-back conditions on ActChain, and every upstream H status. The structural proof below uses CurAnti directly, not by way of CurAnti^M, and does not use CurAnti^M ⇒ Act unconditionally.

### 2.4　The predicate character of Act

Act\_F,t(P;A,j) is a predicate of current occurrence, and at the structural layer it is two-valued: either it is acting at this moment or it is not. The ⊥ of The Mathematical Form of Principle-Action signifies only insufficiency of formal representation, of evidence, or of adjudication; an illegal protocol does not even yield a CurAnti^M truth value. Neither constitutes a third ontological state of "half-acting."

## Section Four　Unitization and Rules of Partition

### 4.1　Units

Canonical sentences are not labelled whole.

Each sentence is first split into the minimal propositional components bearing on the target predicate Act; any condition, relation, negation, or conjunctive structure expressly borne by the canonical source, and not further divisible without loss of meaning, is registered as a unit along with them. All the units together constitute the finite frozen semantic domain U\_Act.

U\_Act is a finite set, obtained by registration alone. Two units already classed as non-current-realization must not afterwards be freely combined into a new condition; a combination not borne by the canonical source is a new proposition, and must not pass itself off as existing Act semantics.

### 4.2　Partition

Each unit is assigned, by its governing duty toward the target predicate Act, to one of the following declared categories:

　　CurrentRealization　a component that directly decides whether an already standing Principle is now discharged in fact;  
　　Source　　　　　　by what the acting is driven, and by whom it is made to act;  
　　Propensity　　　　the tendency to act, the possibility of acting, a state of readiness, energy;  
　　Degree　　　　　　the strength of acting;  
　　Persistence　　　the persistence of acting;  
　　Completion　　　　the completion and ending of acting;  
　　Consequence　　　what acting generates once acquired;  
　　Recognition　　　whether the acting is known or recognized;  
　　InstanceForm　　　the form acting takes in one concrete example;  
　　Generation　　　　the generative layers and categorial contrast of Principle and action;  
　　Exclusion　　　　a boundary exclusion where some component of current realization has not been acquired — not an independent counter-realization condition;  
　　PositiveInstance　the canonical source's verdict "this is action" upon a concrete fact, used for consistency checking and not as a source of definition.

The sufficiency proof is borne by CurrentRealization and the counter-realization check alone. The other categories serve for exposition, boundary, and back-testing, and must not be added to the sufficient antecedent.

### 4.3　The phase criterion

If a unit answers "is this already standing Principle now discharged in fact," it goes to CurrentRealization.

If it answers only "why it began," "who made it so," or "whether there is a tendency," it goes to Source or Propensity.

If it answers only "what follows the discharge, how strong, how long, whether completed, what is generated," it goes to Consequence, Degree, Persistence, or Completion.

If it answers only "what form it takes in some example," it goes to InstanceForm.

### 4.4　The deletion criterion

Hold the other components of current realization fixed and delete the candidate component. If "the Principle is acting" can still stand, that component is not a component of current realization. If deleting it necessarily lets in a case of "standing but not acting" — at rest, not entered, same-direction — then that component bears current realization.

### 4.5　The counter-realization criterion

Suppose every component of current realization remains in force. If the canonical source expressly bears a condition D such that, with D=1 and every component =1, Act=0 is still required, then D is an independent counter-realization condition. If the appearance of D necessarily makes some component itself withdraw, D is not an independent counter-realization condition but merely the lapse of that component.

The counter-realization check is a checking procedure within the proof only; its result forms no ontological object such as a "disqualification of acting."

## Section Six　Forcing Out the Components of Current Realization

### 6.1　General

U2, "this is action," supplies only the canonical source's positive-instance verdict, and is not a source of definition for R1–R3. The components of current realization are forced out independently from three places: who is the one executed (U5, U6, U19); what a making-so is and where the executed relation lands (U7, U8, U11, U12); and what the relational content of this Principle is — inherited from the frozen Principle upstream: the distinguishing content that makes P a Principle relative to A is the established counter-hold of the fixed d\* against A's holding-tendency. Once the three are forced out, one returns to the positive instance of U2 for a consistency check. The three serve the analysis of the proof only and add no ontological predicate.

### 6.2　R1　Executing identity

U5, U6, and U19 say: what the canonical source asks after is the execution of a Principle, and what is asked after, and driven into acting, is that Principle itself.

An already standing Principle is borne by its Principle-conferring duty d*. If what occurs in the current actual Continuation is another duty d\_X of P, then even where d\_X produces exactly the same behavioural result as d*, what is executed is not this Principle. Identity of behavioural result is not identity of executing identity.

If d\* does not occur at all in the current Continuation — the Principle standing at rest — then the Principle is not being executed.

Hence the first component:

　　R1　the same duty d\* that makes the current Principle stand has entered the current actual Continuation.

The core of R1 is not "P has activity," but that what is executed is this one already standing Principle.

### 6.3　R2　Relational entry

U8 and U12 say: acting is a making-so — making something become fact. The canonical source requires only "this Principle is executed and made so"; the working domain has already been fixed upstream: the relational object of this Principle is A, and its distinguishing content is a counter-hold against A's holding-tendency. Taken together: within the working domain, to make "this Principle" become current fact requires at least that the same realization actually enter A's current bearing of its holding-tendency. This step is a joint derivation from the canonical making-so semantics and the upstream Principle structure; the canonical source does not directly write "A's bearing of its holding-tendency."

If d\* merely starts, prepares, propagates, or accumulates inside P, without yet entering A's bearing of the holding-tendency it is exercising, then what has been made so is not yet this relation; U16 can support only "starting is not already discharged."

Hence the second component:

　　R2　the same current legal realization of d\* has actually entered A's bearing of the holding-tendency it is exercising.

### 6.4　R3　Currency of the counter-hold

R3 is not borne by U21. Its source is the conjunction of three: the retention of identity in U6 — what is executed is this one Principle; the making-so of U8 and U12 — making this Principle become fact; and the relational content already frozen upstream in Principle — the distinguishing content that makes P a Principle relative to A is the established counter-hold of the fixed d\* against A's holding-tendency. To make "this Principle" into current fact is to make its distinguishing content into current fact — that is, the current discharge of the established counter-hold of that same d\*.

If d\* has entered A's bearing but is on this occasion with A's direction or indifferent to it, then what has become fact is not this Principle's distinguishing content; entering is not discharging.

Hence the third component:

　　R3　that same current realization has discharged, on a dynamical degree of freedom A actually holds, the established counter-hold that makes P a Principle relative to A.

### 6.5　The requirement of identity

The three must belong to one and the same instance of acting. U5 and U6 require that what is executed be the same Principle; U8 requires that what is made so be that same relation; and the working domain fixes the same d\* through which that Principle stands. R1 through R3 must therefore not be spliced together from different duties, different relational instances, or different phases: the same d\*, the same current discharge, the same legal relational instance, the same realization, and the same Principle relation must be kept throughout. A past execution plus a current entry plus a counter-direction elsewhere does not constitute one instance of acting.

### 6.6　The deletion test

Delete R1: let another duty d\_X of P produce, in the current actual Continuation, the same restricting effect as d*, while d* is at rest; or let d\* be at rest with only the after-effects of past action present. By U5, U6, and U19, what is executed, or driven into acting, is not this already standing Principle. R1 is therefore not deletable.

Delete R2: let d\* start up at present with its signal travelling inside P, or its result-type module executing while the restriction has not landed on a degree of freedom A holds. By U8 and U12, this relation has not yet been made fact; U16 supports only that starting is not discharging. R2 is therefore not deletable.

Delete R3: let d\* currently enter A's bearing but be, on this occasion, with A's holding-direction or indifferent to it. By the executing identity of U6 and the making-so requirement of U8 and U12, together with the relational content already frozen upstream in Principle, the established counter-hold that makes P a Principle relative to A has not become current fact. R3 is therefore not deletable.

The three bear different undeletable constraints, but they are not pairwise independent; their structure is one of progressive dependence: R2 has R1 as its precondition, and R3 has R2 as its precondition. What the deletion test proves is that no layer can be eliminated from the structure of current realization, not that the three can take their values independently of one another. The three classes of failing case fall at the three breaking points of that progression: being at rest breaks at R1, transit at R2, same-direction at R3.

Consistency check: in the example of M1, what the author adjudicates as action — from the AND gate it has arrived at the NOT gate — is precisely the actual running of the very bias relation that has been named Principle in that example, and neither another naming of that relation nor a preparation that never ran. The "current discharge of an already formed relation" that the three components delineate does not conflict with that positive instance. The check goes no further; the upstream structure is not read back into M1.

## Section Seven　The Absorption of Making-So

U8, "making-so," is the unit most easily erected in error as a fourth component.

Suppose R1, R2, and R3 all hold: the same duty of this Principle occurs at present in the actual Continuation, that same realization has entered A's bearing of the holding-tendency it is exercising, and it has discharged the established counter-hold on a degree of freedom A holds. Ask again: what "making-so" is still wanting?

If "not yet made so" means that A's value has not yet changed — that is Consequence; that P has not yet prevailed — that is Degree and Consequence; that the duty is not yet completed — that is Completion; that no new Law has yet been generated — that is Consequence; that one does not know who drives it — that is Source; that the counter-hold has not yet become fact — that is precisely R3 not holding, and not a fourth item.

After this item-by-item check, within the frozen semantic domain the whole minimal content of "making-so" bearing on Act is absorbed by R2 and R3: to make something fact = to enter the other's bearing of its holding-tendency and to discharge the established counter-hold. No residual content of current realization remains.

No R4 is therefore erected. To set up a further item under the name of "making-so" is merely to name R2 ∧ R3 over again.

## Section Eight　The Sorting of Driving

U9, U13, and U18 say: acting has a beginning in driving, it is driven by the Causal-Base, and driving brings about.

Driving answers "why this Principle begins to act" and "who makes it act." The target predicate Act answers "whether this Principle is now acting." The two differ in phase: the former lies before the acting or at its source, the latter at the acting's present moment.

Suppose R1, R2, and R3 all hold while the source of driving is unknown: this Principle is at present being executed, has entered A's bearing, and has discharged its counter-hold; not knowing who drives it does not make it not acting at this moment. Suppose the source of driving is known while R1 does not hold: one knows who would drive it, but it is not being executed at this moment, and it is not acting. Driving is therefore neither a component of current realization nor a counter-realization condition.

Hence:

　　Driver, the Causal-Base, SourceOfAct, DriverKnown, DriverIdentified, intention, a subject's choice

all go to Source, and do not enter the current-realization semantics of Act.

U20, the "tendency to act," goes likewise to Propensity: energy, a driving tendency, a state of readiness, an executable capacity — all are possibility of acting and not already acting. A Principle may have ample tendency to act and be currently at rest.

Who chooses and drives a Principle into Act is a task for the Intentionality Group-Form and for the Form of Principle-Dynamics; an interface is kept for them here and the question is not answered in advance.

## Section Nine　Exhaustion of the Current-Realization Semantics

### 9.1　The question and the finite domain

The domain of exhaustion is fixed as U\_Act = {U1, …, U21}.

Suppose there is a fourth independent component of current realization, R4. Then R4 must be borne by at least one registered unit of U\_Act or by an irreducible conjunction of such units, and must at the same time satisfy: R4 directly alters the truth value of "the Principle is acting"; R4 cannot be reduced to R1 through R3; R4 is not a working-domain condition; R4 is not a manner of realization; R4 is not a matter of degree, persistence, completion, consequence, source, tendency, or recognition; and R4 cannot be obtained merely by renaming a unit of U\_O already classed as non-current-realization.

If R4 cannot fall within U\_Act, it is a condition imposed from outside the frozen canonical source.

### 9.2　Item-by-item check

Arrival (U4): InstanceForm. By the deletion criterion: keep R1 through R3 and delete "arrival," and no unit of M1 through M9 requires Act=0 — M1 adjudicates arrival at the NOT gate as action in one example only, and no unit lists arrival as a general requirement of acting. The boundaries already declared by The General Form of Principle-Action agree: continuous restriction, gating, path closure, narrowing of the future reachable domain, full compensation by a third Law, an instantaneous value unmoved, and an episode uncompleted — none of these takes arrival as necessary. U4 is the form of M1's one example, not a component.

Making-so (U8, U12): proved absorbed by R2 ∧ R3 in Section Seven.

Driving, the Causal-Base, starting (U9, U13, U16, U18): proved to go to Source in Section Eight.

The tendency to act, energy (U20): Propensity.

At work (U14): it says that acting is a process now in progress; its current-realization content is just R1 through R3 holding within the current Continuation, and adds no further item.

Stop, under way (U15, U17): Completion, Persistence. Acting requires neither completion nor persistence to some minimum duration.

The object of the tendency to act (U21): Propensity and auxiliary boundary; it bears no component. The directional content of R3 comes from the established counter-hold of the upstream Principle, not from U21.

The bias of the element of Principle, the contrast of layers (U1, U3, U10): Generation.

Prevailing, a change in the net outcome, A losing its Law, a new Law being generated, an observable endpoint changing, A's instantaneous value moving, being recognized, a subject or an intention being present — none of these appears in U\_Act as a unit of current realization; some are consequential readings of items in U\_O, others are conditions from outside the frozen canonical source. They cannot be raised into an R4.

### 9.3　The exhaustion theorem

Theorem 9.1　Finite-domain exhaustion of the components of current realization

Within the finite semantic domain U\_Act of the frozen canonical source M1 through M9:

　　the current-realization semantics within U\_R ≡ R1 ∧ R2 ∧ R3.

There is no fourth independent component of current realization, R4.

Proof. Every unit of U\_Act has been registered as U1 through U21. Apart from U5, U6, U7, U8, U11, U12, U14, and U19, all the remaining units have gone into U\_O, among them the expository categories Source, Propensity, Persistence, Completion, InstanceForm, Generation, PositiveInstance, and Exclusion. Section Six forces out R1 through R3 independently from those units together with the already frozen relational content of the upstream Principle; the units go respectively to R1, to R2 ∧ R3, or to R3, while U2 serves only for the positive-instance check and not as a source of definition. The deletion test of Section Six proves that none of the three is deletable. Section Seven proves that the current content of U8 and U12 is absorbed by R2 ∧ R3. Section Eight and 9.2 prove that every remaining candidate is a matter of Source, Propensity, Degree, Persistence, Completion, Consequence, InstanceForm, or Generation, or a condition from outside the domain, and cannot be raised out of U\_O. Whoever still maintains that an R4 exists must point to the specific misclassified unit among U1 through U21, or to a specific load-bearing component of M1 through M9 not yet registered; failing which, R4 lies outside the frozen canonical semantic domain. □

The equality sign says only that the current-realization semantics is exhausted within the finite domain of the frozen canonical source; it adds no object-layer set predicate.

## Section Ten　The Counter-Realization Check

### 10.1　The procedure

Suppose R1=R2=R3=1 are all retained. Check item by item whether U\_Act contains an expressly borne condition D such that, with D=1, the canonical source still requires Act=0.

### 10.2　Item by item

A's value unmoved, P not prevailing, a net outcome of zero, full compensation by a third Law: all Consequence or Degree; a counter-hold already discharged under R3 does not vanish because its consequence is cancelled afterwards. Not a D.

The duty episode uncompleted, the acting stopping immediately after (U15, U17): Completion, Persistence. Current realization is a process now in progress (U14) and is not conditional upon completion; stopping immediately after only makes R1 withdraw at the next moment, and does not retroactively alter this one. Not a D.

The source of driving unknown, no subject, no intention (U9, U13, U18): Source. Not a D.

Only the tendency to act (U20): this is the case of R1 not holding — a tendency without execution — and not a counter-realization after R1 through R3 hold.

Not arrived (U4): InstanceForm. Not a D.

Another duty d\_X producing a result of the same shape: this is the case of R1 not holding, not a D.

R1, R2, R3 spliced across time, across evaluations, or across witnesses: this is the requirement of identity in 6.5 not being met, that is, the three not truly holding at once in one and the same instance of acting; not a D.

Not recognized: Recognition. Not a D.

### 10.3　Result of the check

Within the finite semantic domain of the frozen canonical source M1 through M9, there is no unit, and no irreducible conjunction, constituting an independent condition D that makes Act=0 while R1=R2=R3=1 are all retained. Every negative and exclusionary unit in the canonical source either excludes a case in which some component has not been acquired, or accounts for a consequence, a source, a tendency, or a form.

This result is a conclusion of the checking procedure within the proof only; it forms no ontological object such as a "disqualification of acting."

## Section Twelve　The Principal Sufficiency Theorem

Theorem 12.1　Sufficiency theorem for Principle-action

Within the working domain of Section Two, if:

　　CurAnti\_F,t(d\*;P,A,j)=1,

then:

　　Act\_F,t(P;A,j)=1.

Proof.

The current-realization semantics of "acting" in Act is given solely by the frozen canonical source M1 through M9 (Section Three); the concrete relational content of the Principle so discharged is inherited from the upstream structures listed in Section Two.

By Theorem 11.1, CurAnti=1 gives R1=R2=R3=1, with the three conjoined within one and the same instance of acting.

By Theorem 9.1, R1 through R3 have exhausted the current-realization semantics of "the Principle is acting" within U\_Act; there is no R4.

By Section Ten, U\_Act contains no condition that, with R1 through R3 all retained, independently makes Act=0.

Suppose for contradiction that Act\_F,t(P;A,j)=0. Under the exhaustion established within the frozen canonical semantic domain, if Act does not hold, then either at least one item of the exhausted current-realization semantics has not become fact, or, with all three holding, some independent counter-realization semantics holds besides. There is no third semantic possibility, because the units of U\_Act bearing directly on the truth value of Act have all been assigned by Theorem 9.1 to R1 through R3, and the remaining units, checked in Section Ten, constitute no independent counter-realization condition. The former can, by Theorem 9.1, only be one of R1 through R3 failing to become fact, which contradicts R1=R2=R3=1; the latter contradicts Section Ten. The domain of interpretation of this theorem has been frozen as M1 through M9; a load-bearing sentence newly added outside the frozen domain does not lie within the sufficient domain already proved, and cannot be used undeclared, in the present proof, as a third ground. Should such a sentence be found in future, it triggers the re-examination of Section Three; it is not to be slipped into the present proof. Within the frozen domain, therefore, the supposition does not stand.

Hence Act\_F,t(P;A,j)=1. □

## Section Fourteen　Boundaries and Counterexample Back-Testing

## Section Eighteen　Conclusion

A Principle does not become a Principle by acting at present; a Principle stands first. Principle-action is the discharge, within the current actual Continuation, of that already standing counter-holding duty: the same duty that made it a Principle is currently executed, enters the bearing of the holding-tendency the other is exercising, and discharges the established counter-hold on a degree of freedom that other holds.

Where these three hold, there is acting; and within the frozen semantic domain M1 through M9, no independent component of current realization is required beyond them. The Causal-Base that drives it, the tendency it possesses, the place it reaches, whether it prevails, and what it leaves behind are none of them the acting itself.

　　What is a Principle: one whose counter-holding duty has come to stand;  
　　what is acting: that duty's discharge at this moment;  
　　and what is so discharged at this moment is the acting itself.
