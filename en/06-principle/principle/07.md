# The Construction Form of the Principle Running Base

## Section One　Purpose and Entry Conditions

### 1.1　Purpose

The first six texts of the Principle Group-Form have given and frozen the qualification of Principle and its current action: Law, Principle, CounterHoldRole, Act, CurAnti, together with the object-level requirements of the same duty, the target's current bearing, Read, and counter-holding direction. The Law Group-Form has given the Sedimentation-Field base and the Law running base, so that a Law can form from a weak state within a runnable field and be adjudicated by one and the same criterion.

What remains is a problem of construction: can these object-level requirements obtain executable witnesses together within one and the same full-dynamics Sedimentation-Field?

This form-text gives an explicit construction of that problem. What is constructed is a running specification Spec\_PB together with its construction procedure, such that a legal instance of that same specification can actually pass through: the formation of several Laws → P acquires Principle qualification relative to A by the same duty through which it became a Law → that same duty currently acts upon A → the field dynamics reroute → an actual execution history → sedimentation → one and the same general Law criterion adjudicates a new Law N → with P withdrawn, N sustains itself → N, as an ordinary Law, is targeted in turn by P2.

This form-text signs constructive capacity only. It signs none of the following: that Principle necessarily generates a new Law; general conditions of generation reachability; the boundary, stability, or grading of Principle; intentionality. General reachability conditions belong to The Reachability Form of Principle Generation.

### 1.2　Entry conditions

　　(i) Compatibility: the question of coexistence between the object-level requirements of the first six texts of the Principle Group-Form and the Sedimentation-Field base and Law running base of the Law Group-Form is handled, under the entry conditions of the construction-form specification, as "advanced in parallel with this form-text and mutually declared" — the legal instance of the same specification given here is the explicit witness of that coexistence; should an independent Compatibility Form bear it separately, the verification here may serve as an instance of that.  
　　(ii) Closure of the condition set: C1 through C11 of Section Two are each independently checkable, all derived from requirements already standing upstream or from the task of this form-text itself, and contain no undeclared antecedent.  
　　(iii) The question to be answered: what such a running specification looks like.

1.3　Division of labour with neighbouring form-texts

The Construction Form of the Law Running Base constructs Spec\_LB, the running base on which a Law can form and be adjudicated; this form-text extends it with the duties, targets, and adjudication interfaces required by Principle and Principle-action. The first six texts give the criteria themselves; this form-text does not prove them again, but constructs their executable witnesses. The Reachability Form of Principle Generation bears the reachability theorem for a general permitted domain, general field states, and general P/A/B conditions; this form-text does not bear it.

## Section Two　Declaration of the Condition Set

The conditions are given one by one, each independently checkable, with its source noted. Those marked "upstream" come from sealed form-texts; those marked "task" come from the construction task of 1.1. Section Six verifies them item by item under the same numbering.

C1　Inheritance of the full-dynamics Sedimentation-Field. The specification retains the complete actual running chain of field pressure q, flow, accumulated depth h, fullness, impedance, blocking, tuning, bypass competition, actual trace τ, sedimentation, depth g, re-walk rep, and Ω; trace and sedimentation accept only the history of actual execution — candidates, predictions, and undischarged branches do not enter sedimentation. 〔upstream: the Sedimentation-Field base and the Law running base〕

C2　Several Laws forming in one field. A, P\_R, P\_D, Q, and B can each acquire Law qualification from a weak state within one and the same specification and stand together on one and the same frozen state, while the candidate N does not stand in the initial state. 〔task〕

C3　Closure of same-duty identity. The specification of a Law bearing a Principle explicitly binds its forming duty, so that "the duty that makes P a Law, the duty that makes P a Principle, and the duty by which P acts are one and the same duty" is adjudicable; one of the same shape that is not that bound duty must acquire neither Principle qualification nor acting. 〔upstream: the same-duty requirement of The General Form of Principle and The Necessity Form of Principle〕

C4　Principle qualification is executable. The adjudication of Principle qualification executes, on a frozen persistent field, one non-sedimenting legal replay probe, which checks the same duty, the Law qualification of the bearing Law and of the target Law, the degree of freedom the target holds, terminal identity and material, direction, and Read; the probe writes no trace, writes no depth, registers no re-walk, alters no accumulated depth, and alters no tuning. 〔upstream: the legal replay evaluation requirement of The Mathematical Form of Principle〕

C5　Principle-action is executable. The adjudication of acting requires: the bearing event occurs at present; the target Law has inflow on this beat; the target still currently holds the corresponding degree of freedom; the duty and what the bearing Law binds are one and the same; and this Read is satisfied on the actual quantities. Where several duties fall on the same beat, each keeps its own Principle-qualification result, and they do not witness for one another. 〔upstream: the three layers of The General Form of Principle-Action, and the current-witness and same-chain binding requirements of The Mathematical Form of Principle-Action〕

C6　External mechanisms are distinguishable. Gating, external editing, and counter-holding are three different mechanisms: gating alters the availability of the current path, editing alters the generation of the target's result, and counter-holding enters the degree of freedom the target holds by way of the bound duty. The first two register no acting. A duty of the same physical effect as a legal duty but not identical with it likewise registers no acting. 〔upstream: the requirement of The Necessity Form of Principle-Action concerning generative identity〕

C7　The field dynamics reroute. A legal instance of acting actually enters the impedance or the contribution on the degree of freedom the target holds, and, by way of blocking, tuning, and bypass competition, brings about a change in the actual Continuation; tuning alters only the current openness, and does not write sedimented depth directly. 〔task〕

C8　Actual history makes a Law. Only actually executed paths leave a trace and sediment; unexecuted candidates do not sediment; one instance of actual rerouting does not suffice to make a Law; with sedimentation forbidden, actual rerouting likewise makes no Law; after repeated actual rerouting, N acquires Law qualification by one and the same general Law criterion. 〔upstream: the Law adjudication protocol; task〕

C9　The new Law becomes ordinary and self-sustaining. Once N stands, the original Principle is withdrawn, and N sustains itself on ordinary inflow alone, keeping the identity of an ordinary Law and retaining no special type of "a Law that was generated." 〔task〕

C10　Re-targeting. A new Law identity P2 can acquire Principle qualification relative to N, and act, through the same general duty and target interface. 〔task〕

C11　Controls and breakability. The controls — no P, no B, a non-identical duty, a lapsed target, the target having no inflow on this beat, gating, editing, ghost history, short history, forbidden sedimentation — can be distinguished as expected; interchange of material roles, ablation of each item of field dynamics, the at-rest lifetime, and the parameter neighbourhood are all measurable. 〔task〕

The numbering C1 through C11 marks construction conditions, not experiments. The PB numbers in Section Six are the names of verification protocols, and must not serve in reverse as conditions.

## Section Three　Upstream Inputs and the Boundary of Inheritance

### 3.1　Inheritance

　　The Sedimentation-Field base: the field-dynamics interfaces of node, edge, depth, trace, re-walk, Ω, decay, and rest;  
　　The Law running base Spec\_LB: Law specifications, carrier solutions, the general Law criterion, and the procedures of formation and maintenance;  
　　The Law adjudication interface: exhaustion of candidate carriers with anchors, restricted Read, sedimentation and re-walk of the same candidate, anchor ablation, edge-by-edge Ω over the bearing stretch, and the witness of the same candidate;  
　　The General Form of Principle, The Mathematical Form of Principle, The Necessity Form of Principle: Law(P), the same duty, the two types of what a duty restricts, counter-holding direction, the relation domain;  
　　The General Form of Principle-Action, The Mathematical Form of Principle-Action, The Necessity Form of Principle-Action: the three layers of CurAnti, the current witness, same-chain binding, and the current evaluation of Read.

### 3.2　The boundary of inheritance

The Law adjudication, the Principle-qualification adjudication, and the acting adjudication in Appendix B are only realization projections of the upstream adjudication protocols onto a "frozen finite linear-path toy domain." They are not the full three-valued and endpoint-grid protocol of The Determination Form of Law, nor an item-by-item equivalent implementation of the full formal systems of The Mathematical Form of Principle and The Mathematical Form of Principle-Action. Whoever reads a general upstream conclusion back from the implementation details of this construction must check it separately against the determination forms.

The model is a discrete first-generation full-dynamics Sedimentation-Field toy model, not a biological neural model. The material roles P, E, and I within it are material types of this specification, not the only materials real in biology; the counts, thresholds, and parameters appearing in it are phenomena of the implementation, not theoretical constants.

3.3　Extension and the stratification of state

Spec\_PB = Augment(Spec\_LB, A\_PB).

What A\_PB adds is of three kinds only: first, the binding of a forming duty upon a Law specification; second, target specifications and duty specifications, together with the Principle-qualification adjudication and the acting adjudication upon them; third, the bypass registration and branch specification of a target. The field-dynamics equations, the core CR/CNI/Dyn/Ω/Bind adjudication kernel of Law, and their numerical parameters are unaltered by A\_PB; for a bearing Law that bears a Principle identity, a law-duty identity binding check is added, so as to provide the same-duty interface.

Stratification of state:

　　persistent state — depth g, the re-walk queue rep, accumulated depth h, the availability and masking of an edge;  
　　instantaneous quantities — field pressure q, flow, trace τ, impedance r, fullness, blocking, tuning, shallowing, openness;  
　　derived quantities — Ω derived from depth and the re-walk queue, fullness derived from accumulated depth, result quantities and blocking derived from current quantities;  
　　the current target state — whether the target currently holds the declared degree of freedom; this mark takes part in the acting adjudication directly, and is not a diagnostic quantity;  
　　exogenous control — the inflow set, the set of activated duties, branch forcing, sedimentation permission, the assignment of a compensating Law, gating, and editing; the last three serve only for controls or the compensation protocol, and are not duties;  
　　the diagnostic interface — the set of duties registered as acting, each duty's Read value and quantities on this occasion, and the running record of each experiment.

The construction goal is written:

　　∃ Spec\_PB \[ SedFieldSpec(Spec\_PB) ∧ LawBaseCapable(Spec\_PB) ∧ PrincipleBaseCapable(Spec\_PB) \],

where PrincipleBaseCapable says only that C1 through C11 can each obtain a witness from a legal instance of the same specification; it does not state the general theorem Principle ⇒ ∃N Law(N).

## Section Four　The Construct Spec\_PB

### 4.1　Substrate and materials

Three node materials: P is the entry pressure-bearing material, E the relay material, and I the material of reverse field output. Where the terminal of a bearing Law is I, it outputs a reverse quantity; where it is E, a forward quantity. Where the material duty is not satisfied, that Law's path does not propagate and there is no output.

Nodes: s, m, a, c, x, n, d, z are E; hR, hD, hQ, h2 are P; pR, pD, pQ, p2 are E; tR, tD, t2 are I; tQ is E.

### 4.2　Edges

　　s→m, m→a, a→c;  
　　m→x, m→z;  
　　x→n, n→d;  
　　hR→pR, pR→tR; hD→pD, pD→tD; hQ→pQ, pQ→tQ; h2→p2, p2→t2.

Every edge has initial depth g0=0.04.

### 4.3　Law specifications

　　A: antecedent s, read node c, path s→m→a→c, anchor m→a, branch mode;  
　　B: antecedent x, read node d, path x→n→d, anchor x→n, direct mode;  
　　P\_R: antecedent hR, read node tR, path hR→pR→tR, anchor pR→tR, bearing mode, entry P, relay E, terminal I, bound forming duty dR;  
　　P\_D: isomorphic to P\_R, antecedent hD, terminal tD, bound dD;  
　　Q: antecedent hQ, read node tQ, path hQ→pQ→tQ, anchor pQ→tQ, bearing mode, terminal tQ being E; it is a Law only and bears no Principle duty, its forward terminal output being used for the compensation control under the compensation configuration of 4.5;  
　　P2: isomorphic to P\_R, antecedent h2, terminal t2, bound d2;  
　　N: antecedent s, read node d, path s→m→x→n→d, anchor m→x, branch mode.

### 4.4　Target specifications

　　Target A: held edge m→a, result quantity y\_A, diagnostic mark "whether currently held";  
　　Target N: held edge m→x, result quantity y\_N, likewise.

### 4.5　Duty specifications

　　dR: bearing P\_R, result-type, target A, target edge m→a, terminal tR, reverse, Read constraining y\_A;  
　　dD: bearing P\_D, delivery-type, target A, target edge m→a, terminal tD, reverse, Read constraining the delivered quantity;  
　　d2: bearing P2, result-type, target N, target edge m→x, terminal t2, reverse;  
　　dX: bearing P\_R, of the same shape as dR — same target, same target edge, same gain, same terminal — but not the forming duty bound to P\_R, used for the same-duty control.

Compensation configuration Comp\_Q: Q does not serve as a Principle duty and registers no duty specification. In the compensation control, only where Q runs on that beat and its Law qualification passes is its forward terminal output admitted, at a fixed compensation gain of 0.55, into the generation of A's result. That gain is one uniform calibrated value, and is not fitted seed by seed.

### 4.6　Branches and bypasses

Branch specification J\_main: entry s, prefix s→m, branch point m, three branches m→a, m→x, m→z, each with its suffix a→c, x→n→d, and empty respectively.

Target A registers bypasses m→x and m→z, the first bypass taking full tuning and the second partial.

### 4.7　Parameters and initial state

The parameters take the values carried in Appendix B; the working point is counter-holding gain 1.05, tuning gain 2.20, sedimentation rate 0.22, re-walk window 80, re-walk threshold 3, depth threshold 1.0, read threshold 0.90. The initial state gives every edge the initial depth g0 and every node an accumulated depth of zero.

## Section Five　The Construction Procedure

The procedure is explicit step by step, and every step can be executed by the code of Appendix B.

Step 1　Form the initial set of Laws from a weak state. For A, P\_R, P\_D, Q, and B in turn, execute the general formation procedure: re-enact along that Law's specification within the same field dynamics, forcing the branch point along that Law's path, until one and the same general Law criterion first passes. A Law that bears a Principle re-enacts, during its formation, upon the target Law by means of the duty it binds, so as to keep the formation under the same duty. During formation the bound duty is permitted to take part in the formation process of the bearing Law: its sole effect is that, while the bearing Law has not yet acquired Law qualification, the current contribution of that bound duty is still admitted into the field, so that "the formation history of the bearing Law" and "the actual occurrence history of that same bound duty" can be obtained in parallel within one and the same formation trajectory. This permission does not mean that the duty's contribution is the cause of the bearing Law acquiring Law qualification — the bearing Law still acquires its qualification through the actual execution of its own path, its trace, sedimentation, re-walk, and the general Law criterion; without activating that duty it forms just the same (re-checked over 5 seeds, forming at step 27 in each). The permission relaxes only the antecedent "the bearing Law must already be a Law" for the duty's entry, and holds only for the same duty bound to that bearing Law; the adjudication of Principle qualification and of acting is not thereby relaxed — Principle qualification is still adjudicated independently by the probe of Step 3, and acting must still pass every condition of Step 5. Once formation ends, the permission is no longer used.

Step 2　Freeze a common initial state. Maintain the necessary Laws already formed — A, P\_R, P\_D, Q, B — in a loop until, on one and the same frozen state, they all pass the Law criterion while N does not. The compensating Law Q must be within the maintained set, otherwise its re-walk record would expire with time and it would lose Law qualification. That frozen state is written F₀; thereafter every verification protocol starts from an independent copy of F₀, and they do not contaminate one another.

Step 3　The Principle-qualification probe. For a candidate duty, execute on F₀ or on the current sedimented state one non-sedimenting legal replay probe: freeze the persistent field, propagate along the bearing Law's path and materials, take the terminal sign, compute the counter-holding quantity or the delivered quantity, and check item by item the same duty, the Law qualification of both Laws, the edge the target holds, terminal identity and material, direction, and Read. The probe writes no trace, writes no depth, registers no re-walk, alters no accumulated depth, and alters no tuning.

Step 4　Run the bearer and the target in the current experiment. Trigger the bearing Law and the target Law with the inflow set: the bearing Law propagates along its own path and yields a terminal output; the target Law enters the branch from its antecedent.

Step 5　The duty enters the held degree of freedom, and acting is adjudicated. First adjudicate entry: the bearing Law has a terminal output on this beat, and the bearing Law is already a Law (during formation, see the permission of Step 1); the target Law has inflow on this beat, and the target currently holds the declared degree of freedom. What enters does so by type — a result-type adds its reverse output, multiplied by the gain, to the impedance of the edge the target holds; a delivery-type adds its signed output, multiplied by the gain, to the target's result contribution — and is recorded as pending. Entry itself is not acting: the contribution of one of the same shape as a legal duty but not bound likewise enters the field, only never to be registered as acting. Then adjudicate item by item: the duty and what the bearing Law binds are one and the same; this Read is satisfied on the actual quantities; and that duty's own Principle qualification stands (taken from the probe result of Step 3, kept duty by duty, not shared with other duties on the same beat). Only where all three pass is acting registered.

Step 6　Blocking, tuning, and competition produce the actual Continuation. Blocking is computed from the result quantity and the fullness of the branch point; where blocking exceeds the threshold, the openness of the held edge is lowered and shallowed, and the openness of the registered bypasses is raised; the branch point scores by openness, depth, Ω, the target's accumulated depth, source pressure, impedance, and a redistribution bonus, and softly selects the actual course.

Step 7　Leave a trace and sediment along the actually executed path. Edges actually traversed leave a trace in proportion to flow, the trace converts to depth at the sedimentation rate, the moment of re-walk is registered, and the target node accumulates depth; edges not traversed enter the candidate record only.

Step 8　The general Law criterion adjudicates N. In the finite implementation here, an edge acquires Ω where its depth exceeds the threshold and the number of events within the re-walk window reaches the threshold; once every edge of N's path has depth, re-walk, and Ω, N is adjudicated by the same general Law criterion as A.

Step 9　Withdraw the Principle and sustain. Stop activating any duty and run on ordinary inflow alone; count whether the actual Continuation still takes the new path, and re-adjudicate N's Law qualification.

Step 10　Form P2 and re-target. On the trajectory of Step 9, form P2 by the general formation procedure, target N's held edge by its bound duty d2 through the same duty and target interface, and compare the impedance and result quantity of N's held edge between the two experiments, with d2 activated and not activated.

No step depends on an undeclared antecedent; the executability of each is witnessed by the code of Appendix B and the running records of Appendix A.

## Section Six　Item-by-Item Verification of the Condition Set

Verification follows the order C1 through C11. Each item gives its protocol, measured results, grounds of satisfaction, and what that experiment does not prove. Every figure comes from the actual runs recorded in Appendix A.

C1　Inheritance of the full-dynamics Sedimentation-Field

Protocol: formation-stage ablation (30 seeds, adjudicating that the five Laws A, P\_R, P\_D, Q, B stand together in one field while N does not), executing Steps 1–2 with field-pressure transfer or trace-response switched off; the ghost-history protocol PB10ghost (200 seeds), taking the old path 20 times and then inspecting the bypass's re-walk and depth; the no-sedimentation protocol PB11b (200 seeds), 15 actual reroutings with sedimentation not permitted.

Results: formation-stage baseline 30/30; field-pressure transfer off 0/30; trace-response off 0/30. PB10ghost 200/200: the bypass's re-walk queue is empty and its depth does not increase. PB11b 200/200: N does not become a Law and re-walk is empty.

Satisfied: the complete running chain is present; without field-pressure transfer or trace-response the five Laws cannot form together from a weak state, so neither is an empty operator. Trace and sedimentation accept only the history of actual execution; neither candidates nor executions with sedimentation forbidden enter the structure.

Does not prove: it does not prove that these two are indispensable under any topology or any parameters.

C2　Several Laws forming in one field

Protocol: PB1 and PB2 (200 seeds each) adjudicate on F₀ the Law qualification of A, and that A, P\_R, P\_D, Q, B are Laws together while N is not.

Results: PB1 200/200; PB2 200/200 (PB2 officially requires the five Laws A, P\_R, P\_D, Q, B to stand simultaneously while N does not). Q's Law qualification on the frozen initial state is tested separately: 30/30 pass. Witness for seed 0: A passes with {s→m, m→a, a→c}, P\_R with {hR→pR, pR→tR}, P\_D with {hD→pD, pD→tD}, Q with {hQ→pQ, pQ→tQ}, B with {x→n, n→d}; N fails on the read item and has no witness.

Satisfied: the five Laws form from a weak state within one and the same specification and stand together on one and the same frozen state, while the candidate new Law does not stand in the initial state; hence the N spoken of below is not preset. The compensating Law Q is likewise brought into common maintenance, and its Law qualification on F₀ is thereby guaranteed.

Does not prove: it does not prove that any set of Laws whatever can form together within this specification.

C3　Closure of same-duty identity

Protocol: the same-duty control (30 seeds), running the same experiment with dR and with dX on two copies of F₀ respectively, comparing result quantity, the impedance of the held edge, Principle qualification, and acting; PB7a (200 seeds) with dX counterfeiting.

Results: dR's Principle qualification 30/30, acting 30/30; dX's Principle qualification 0/30, acting 0/30; the result quantities the two produce on A's held edge agree digit for digit 30/30, and the impedances agree digit for digit 30/30. PB7a 200/200.

Satisfied: the binding makes the same duty adjudicable; the physical effect is exactly the same while the Principle identity differs, so Principle qualification and acting are not determined by physical quantities. This is the strongest control in this construction:

　　a physically identical counter-hold is not the same Principle identity.

Does not prove: it does not prove that dX could not, under long-range repetition, sediment out the same new Law — this form-text has not run that experiment, and so makes no such assertion.

C4　Principle qualification is executable

Protocol: PB3 and PB4 (200 seeds each) adjudicate the Principle qualification of P\_R and P\_D; the probe-writes-nothing check (30 seeds, Appendix D command probe): record every persistent state and instantaneous quantity on F₀, execute one Principle-qualification adjudication, and compare item by item.

Results: PB3 200/200; PB4 200/200. Probe-writes-nothing 30/30: an edge's depth, trace, impedance, openness, re-walk queue, Ω, availability, masking, tuning, and shallowing; a node's material, field pressure, accumulated depth, and fullness; and the clock — unaltered item by item.

Satisfied: Principle qualification is given by one non-sedimenting replay probe executed on a frozen persistent field, within which the same duty, the Law qualification of both Laws, the edge the target holds, terminal identity and material, direction, and Read are checked item by item; the probe does not alter the field, so the adjudication of Principle qualification does not manufacture the fact it is to adjudicate.

Does not prove: it does not prove that this probe is item-by-item equivalent to the legal replay evaluation of The Mathematical Form of Principle; it is a realization projection of that protocol onto this toy domain.

C5　Principle-action is executable

Protocol: PB5 (acting), PB6 (the Principle stands while the bearer is not triggered), PB7b (a lapsed target: the Law identity is retained while the held degree of freedom is not in bearing), PB8 (a delivery-type exactly compensated by the compensating Law), 200 seeds each; the target having no inflow on this beat (30 seeds): give only the bearer's antecedent and not the target's, contrasted with giving both; several duties on one beat (30 seeds): dR and dD activated on the same beat.

Results: PB5 200/200; PB6 200/200; PB7b 200/200; PB8 200/200. In PB8 the compensating Law Q has its forward output admitted as compensation only where its Law qualification passes; the compensation returns the target's result quantity exactly to the value it would have without any counter-hold, while the delivery-type duty is still registered as acting. Target with no inflow on this beat: Principle qualification stands 30/30, acting 0/30; the same batch of seeds gives acting 30/30 when both antecedents are supplied. Several duties on one beat: dR acts 30/30, dD acts 30/30.

Satisfied: acting requires that the bearer occur at present and that the bearing Law be a Law, that the target have inflow on this beat, that the target currently hold that degree of freedom, that the duty be the same, that this Read be satisfied, and that the duty's own Principle qualification stand. Entry and registration as acting are two layers: a duty of the same shape but not bound may enter and alter the field, yet is never registered as acting (C3, C6). The three different failures each have an independent witness: the bearer not triggered (PB6), the target not running on this beat (the no-inflow protocol), and the target running while the held degree of freedom is not in bearing (PB7b). Where several duties fall on one beat, each keeps its own Principle qualification item by item, and they do not witness for one another. PB8 witnesses a further point: acting is not conditional upon a change in the final result — another Law, itself now standing, restores the result to its original value, and the delivery-type is still acting.

Does not prove: it does not prove that these three layers are item-by-item equivalent to the formal criteria of The Mathematical Form of Principle-Action.

C6　External mechanisms are distinguishable

Protocol: PB9 (200 seeds) executes gating, editing, and counter-holding on three copies of F₀ respectively, 60 times each in succession, adjudicating the acting on the first beat and the N of the terminal state; a supplementary protocol (30 seeds, Appendix D command three) runs the three mechanisms independently under the same protocol, counting N and the history of acting for each.

Results: PB9 200/200: gating and editing register no acting, counter-holding registers acting, and all three terminal states make N. Supplementary protocol: gating 30/30, editing 30/30, counter-holding 30/30 form N; gating and editing have a history of acting of zero across the 60 times, while counter-holding has a history of acting 30/30.

Satisfied: the three mechanisms differ — gating alters the availability of the current path, editing alters the generation of the result, counter-holding enters the held degree of freedom by way of the bound duty — while the terminal states agree in the Law identity adjudicated: all three trajectories are adjudicated by one and the same general Law criterion to make the same new Law N stand (the microscopic terminal states of the three trajectories are not thereby the same, and this form-text asserts no such thing). Of these, only the counter-holding branch has the identity Law(P) → Principle(P;A) → Act(P;A); the other two have a history of acting of zero. Hence

　　Law(N)=1 does not entail that N was generated by Principle;

the same Law identity is not the same history of generation. Generative identity can only be read back from the historical chain: Law(P) → Principle(P;A) → Act(P;A) → actual trace → sedimentation → Law(N).

Does not prove: it does not prove that gating and editing can form N under any parameters or any gating strength; it does not prove that the terminal field states of the three trajectories are the same; the proportions above are measured values of this environment under the declared protocol.

C7　The field dynamics reroute

Protocol: generation-stage ablation (30 seeds), executing Steps 4–8 after F₀ with tuning, fullness, or incumbent-closing switched off in turn.

Results: tuning off 30/30/0/0; fullness off 30/30/0/0; incumbent-closing off 30/30/0/0 (the four columns being Principle qualification, acting, N, self-sustaining).

Satisfied: with these three off, the Principle still stands and is still acting, while new Laws are zero — a counter-hold generates no new Law without the field dynamics rerouting. This is a direct witness of "acting ≠ already rerouted ≠ already a new Law." Tuning alters only the current openness; depth comes only from the sedimentation of an actual trace.

Does not prove: it does not prove that these three are indispensable under any topology.

C8　Actual history makes a Law

Protocol: PB10 (200 seeds) runs 60 successive main experiments and records the ordinal at which N first becomes a Law; PB11a (200 seeds) adjudicates N after one forced actual rerouting; PB11b and PB10ghost as in C1; the depth\_accumulation ablation (30 seeds).

Results: PB10 200/200, first becoming a Law at the 12th in 1 case, the 13th in 13, the 14th in 72, the 15th in 98, and the 16th in 16. PB11a 200/200 not a Law. PB11b and PB10ghost 200/200 each. Accumulated-depth accumulation off 30/30/0/0.

Satisfied: only actually executed paths sediment; one rerouting does not suffice; with sedimentation forbidden no Law results; after repeated actual rerouting, N acquires qualification by the same general Law criterion as A. Seed 0 first becomes a Law at the 15th, with witness {s→m, m→x, x→n, n→d}.

        if admission and tau>0:
            old=st.g
            st.g=min(self.p.g_max,st.g+self.p.sediment_rate*tau*(1-st.g/self.p.g_max))
            st.rep.append(self.t)
            log.sedimented[e]=st.g-old
            dst=e[1]
            self.nodes[dst].h=min(self.p.h_cap,self.nodes[dst].h+self.p.h_gain*tau)

    def _material_roles_ok(self,law:LawSpec):
        if law.entry_kind is not None and self.nodes[law.ante].kind != law.entry_kind:
            return False
        if law.terminal_kind is not None and self.nodes[law.read_node].kind != law.terminal_kind:
            return False
        if law.relay_kind is not None and len(law.path)>1:
            # sources after the first edge are interior relay sites
            for e in law.path[1:]:
                if self.nodes[e[0]].kind != law.relay_kind:
                    return False
        return True

    def terminal_output(self,law:LawSpec,q:float):
        if not self._material_roles_ok(law):
            return 0.0
        k=self.nodes[law.read_node].kind
        if k=="I": return -self.p.i_output_gain*q
        if k=="E": return +self.p.e_relay_gain*q
        return q

    def propagate_path(self,path,input_q,log,admission=True,law:Optional[LawSpec]=None):
        if not path:return 0.0
        if law is not None and not self._material_roles_ok(law):
            return 0.0
        q=input_q
        if law is not None and law.entry_kind=="P":
            q*=self.p.p_entry_gain
        if q<=self.p.q_floor:return 0.0
        for idx,e in enumerate(path):
            st=self.edges[e]
            if st.masked or not st.available:
                return 0.0
            if law is not None and idx>0 and law.relay_kind=="E":
                q*=self.p.e_relay_gain
            if q<=self.p.q_floor:return 0.0
            self.nodes[e[0]].q=max(self.nodes[e[0]].q,q)
            flow=q*self.p.q_transfer*self.edge_open(e)
            if flow<=self.p.q_floor:return 0.0
            self.trace_exec(e,flow,log,admission)
            log.executed.append(e)
            self.nodes[e[1]].q=max(self.nodes[e[1]].q,flow)
            q=flow
        return q

    def softmax_choice(self,source,q_src,extra_r,log,force:Optional[Edge]=None,bonus=None):
        bonus=bonus or defaultdict(float)
        vals=[]
        for e in self.out_edges.get(source,[]):
            vals.append((e,self.score(e,q_src,extra_r.get(e,0.0))+bonus.get(e,0.0)))
        if not vals:return None
        mx=max(v for _,v in vals)
        exps=[0.0 if v<-1e8 else math.exp((v-mx)/self.p.temperature) for _,v in vals]
        z=sum(exps); probs=[x/z if z else 0 for x in exps]
        log.candidates[source]=[(vals[i][0],probs[i]) for i in range(len(vals))]
        if force:return force
        r=self.rng.random();acc=0.0
        for (e,_),pr in zip(vals,probs):
            acc+=pr
            if r<=acc:return e
        return vals[-1][0]

    def path_read(self,law:LawSpec):
        v=1.0
        for e in law.path:
            st=self.edges[e]
            if st.masked or not st.available:return 0.0
            local=min(1.0,st.g/self.p.g_thresh)
            if st.omega<=0:local*=0.45
            v*=local
        return v

    def _restricted_read(self, law:LawSpec, subset:Set[Edge]):
        """
        PR1 for this finite linear-path toy domain.
        Only edges admitted by subset are available to the restricted kernel.
        Missing a required continuation produces failure mass (Read=0), not unknown.
        """
        v=1.0
        for e in law.path:
            if e not in subset:
                return 0.0
            st=self.edges[e]
            if st.masked or not st.available:
                return 0.0
            local=min(1.0,st.g/self.p.g_thresh)
            if st.omega<=0:
                local*=0.45
            v*=local
        return v

    def _candidate_subsets(self,law:LawSpec):
        """Enumerate every P⊆R containing Anchor, as required by the fixed finite domain."""
        rest=[e for e in law.path if e!=law.anchor]
        for mask in range(1<<len(rest)):
            P={law.anchor}
            for i,e in enumerate(rest):
                if mask & (1<<i):
                    P.add(e)
            yield P

    def law_check_detail(self,law_name:str):
        law=self.laws[law_name]
        # Explicit Law-duty binding for carrier laws. The Law witness, Principle
        # identity and later Act must point to the same DutySpec.
        if law.law_duty_name is not None:
            if law.law_duty_name not in self.duties:
                return False,"BindDuty",None
            d=self.duties[law.law_duty_name]
            if d.carrier_law!=law.name or d.terminal_node!=law.read_node:
                return False,"BindDuty",None
        # CC: current carrier states exist and are positive.
        if any(e not in self.edges or self.edges[e].g<=0 for e in law.path):
            return False,"CC",None

        best_failure="CR"
        for P in self._candidate_subsets(law):
            # PR1 / CarrierRealizes under restricted kernel.
            read=self._restricted_read(law,P)
            if read<self.p.read_thresh:
                continue

            # CNI: the same candidate carrier must be sedimented and replay-supported.
            cni=True
            for e in P:
                st=self.edges[e]
                if st.g<self.p.g_thresh or len(st.rep)<self.p.omega_n:
                    cni=False; break
            if not cni:
                best_failure="CNI"
                continue

            # PR2 DynamicRole: Anchor structural availability must matter to the same Read.
            anc=law.anchor
            base=read
            saved_mask=self.edges[anc].masked
            self.edges[anc].masked=True
            ablated=self._restricted_read(law,P)
            self.edges[anc].masked=saved_mask
            if base-ablated<0.01:
                best_failure="Dyn"
                continue

            # PR4 RolePath and PR3 ΩSupport on the bearing segment from Anchor to Read.
            try:
                idx=law.path.index(anc)
            except ValueError:
                best_failure="Dyn"; continue
            bear=law.path[idx:]
            if any(e not in P for e in bear):
                best_failure="Dyn"
                continue
            if any(self.edges[e].omega<=0 for e in bear):
                best_failure="Omega"
                continue

            # Bind: the very same P supplies CR, CNI, Dyn and ΩSupport.
            witness=tuple(e for e in law.path if e in P)
            return True,"PASS",witness

        return False,best_failure,None

    def law_check(self,law_name:str):
        ok,why,_=self.law_check_detail(law_name)
        return ok,why

    def _probe_edge_open(self,e:Edge):
        """
        Persistent-state opening used by Principle qualification.
        It deliberately ignores instantaneous Tune from the preceding episode:
        Principle qualification is evaluated on the frozen persistent field.
        """
        st=self.edges[e]
        mature=self.p.open_from_g*min(1.0,st.g/self.p.g_thresh)
        return min(1.0,max(0.0,self.p.open_base+mature))

    def _legal_duty_probe(self,duty_name:str):
        """
        Non-sedimenting legal re-entry probe for CounterHoldRole qualification.
        Returns (ok, value): for result-type, value is the probed target result;
        for delivery-type, value is the probed signed delivered contribution.
        No Trace/Sediment/rep/h/tune is written.
        """
        if duty_name not in self.duties:
            return False,None
        d=self.duties[duty_name]
        if d.carrier_law not in self.laws or d.target_law not in self.laws:
            return False,None
        carrier=self.laws[d.carrier_law]

        # The probe must be carried by the same declared terminal/material chain.
        if carrier.read_node!=d.terminal_node or not self._material_roles_ok(carrier):
            return False,None

        # Frozen persistent carrier propagation, with no current-history writes.
        q=self.p.q_input
        if carrier.entry_kind=="P":
            q*=self.p.p_entry_gain
        if q<=self.p.q_floor:
            return False,None
        for idx,e in enumerate(carrier.path):
            st=self.edges[e]
            if st.masked or not st.available:
                return False,None
            if idx>0 and carrier.relay_kind=="E":
                q*=self.p.e_relay_gain
            q=q*self.p.q_transfer*self._probe_edge_open(e)
            if q<=self.p.q_floor:
                return False,None

        k=self.nodes[d.terminal_node].kind
        terminal=(-self.p.i_output_gain*q) if k=="I" else (
                 self.p.e_relay_gain*q if k=="E" else q)

        if d.duty_type=="result":
            if terminal>=0 or d.result_limit is None:
                return False,None
            anti=max(0.0,-terminal)*d.gain
            if anti<=self.p.q_floor:
                return False,None
            y=max(0.0,1.0-anti)
            return y<=d.result_limit, y

        if d.duty_type=="delivery":
            if terminal>=0 or d.delivery_limit is None:
                return False,None
            contribution=d.sign*abs(terminal)*d.gain
            return contribution<=d.delivery_limit, contribution

        return False,None

    def principle_check(self,duty_name:str):
        if duty_name not in self.duties:
            return False
        d=self.duties[duty_name]
        carrier=self.laws[d.carrier_law]

        # Same-duty identity: the carrier Law, Principle and later Act
        # must all point to exactly the same DutySpec.
        if carrier.law_duty_name != duty_name:
            return False
        if not self.law_check(d.carrier_law)[0]:
            return False
        if not self.law_check(d.target_law)[0]:
            return False

        # Target binding is part of CounterHoldRole.
        if d.target_law not in self.targets:
            return False
        ts=self.targets[d.target_law]
        if d.target_edge != ts.held_edge:
            return False

        # Terminal identity/material and Read semantics must survive a legal,
        # non-sedimenting re-entry probe on the frozen persistent field.
        ok,_=self._legal_duty_probe(duty_name)
        return ok

    def run_episode(self,inputs:Set[str],active_duties:Set[str]=set(),admission=True,
                    force_by_source:Optional[Dict[str,str]]=None,
                    external_gate:Optional[Dict[str,float]]=None,
                    external_edit:Optional[Dict[str,float]]=None,
                    compensation:Optional[Dict[str,float]]=None,
                    compensation_laws:Optional[Dict[str,str]]=None,
                    allow_law_duty_training:bool=False):
        self.reset_current()
        log=EpisodeLog(self.t,set(inputs))
        force_by_source=force_by_source or {}
        external_gate=external_gate or {}
        external_edit=external_edit or {}
        compensation=compensation or {}
        compensation_laws=compensation_laws or {}

        for n in inputs:
            if n in self.nodes:self.nodes[n].q+=self.p.q_input

        # Run every configured carrier law whose ante is present.
        terminal={}
        for lname,law in self.laws.items():
            if law.execution_mode=="carrier" and law.ante in inputs:
                q=self.propagate_path(law.path,self.p.q_input,log,admission,law=law)
                terminal[lname]=self.terminal_output(law,q)

        # Optional compensation must come from an actually running declared Law.
        # This is used by PB8 so Q, not an external scalar, restores A's net result.
        law_comp=defaultdict(float)
        for tname,lname in compensation_laws.items():
            if lname in terminal and self.law_check(lname)[0]:
                law_comp[tname]+=self.p.q_comp_gain*max(0.0,terminal[lname])

        # Direct field laws (non-junction laws) run from their own environmental ante.
        for lname,law in self.laws.items():
            if law.execution_mode=="direct" and law.ante in inputs:
                self.propagate_path(law.path,self.p.q_input,log,admission,law=law)

        # Evaluate duties generically. Principle qualification is structural;
        # Act additionally requires current carrier event, current target holding,
        # same-duty identity, and the duty's own Read to be satisfied in this episode.
        target_extra_r=defaultdict(lambda:defaultdict(float))
        target_delivery=defaultdict(float)
        pending_duties={}
        for dname in active_duties:
            if dname not in self.duties:
                continue
            d=self.duties[dname]
            carrier_out=terminal.get(d.carrier_law,0.0)
            if abs(carrier_out)<=self.p.q_floor:
                continue
            principle_ok=self.principle_check(dname)
            carrier=self.laws[d.carrier_law]
            same_duty=(carrier.law_duty_name==dname)
            # A configured current contribution may physically occur even when it is
            # not the carrier's Law-duty (wrong-duty world). Such a contribution can
            # affect the field but can never count as Principle/Act.
            if not self.law_check(d.carrier_law)[0] and not (allow_law_duty_training and same_duty):
                continue
            ts=self.targets[d.target_law]
            target_law=self.laws[d.target_law]
            target_current=(target_law.ante in inputs)
            if not ts.currently_holds or not target_current:
                continue
            # R2: the same current realization enters the declared held freedom
            # of a target Law that is actually current in this episode.
            if d.duty_type=="result":
                anti=max(0.0,-carrier_out)*d.gain
                if anti<=self.p.q_floor:
                    continue
                target_extra_r[d.target_law][d.target_edge]+=anti
                pending_duties[dname]=("result",anti,principle_ok)
                log.duty_values[dname]=anti
            elif d.duty_type=="delivery":
                contribution=d.sign*abs(carrier_out)*d.gain
                target_delivery[d.target_law]+=contribution
                pending_duties[dname]=("delivery",contribution,principle_ok)
                log.duty_values[dname]=contribution

        # External diagnostic worlds are deliberately different mechanisms.
        # Gate changes the held edge's current availability/opening only.
        gate_saved={}
        for tname,val in external_gate.items():
            if tname not in self.targets:
                continue
            held=self.targets[tname].held_edge
            st=self.edges[held]
            gate_saved[held]=(st.available,st.tune)
            if val>=1.0:
                # Hard external path gate: close the incumbent and, when a bypass set
                # is declared, close nonselected alternates so the first bypass is
                # externally selected. This is intentionally not field Tune.
                st.available=False
                bypasses=self.bypass_sets.get(tname,[])
                for alt in bypasses[1:]:
                    ast=self.edges[alt]
                    gate_saved[alt]=(ast.available,ast.tune)
                    ast.available=False
            else:
                # Partial gate closes current openness without adding resistance.
                st.tune-=abs(val)
            log.external_events.setdefault("gate",{})[tname]=val

        # Edit directly changes target result generation, not edge resistance or availability.
        edit_delta=defaultdict(float)
        for tname,val in external_edit.items():
            if tname in self.targets:
                edit_delta[tname]-=abs(val)
                log.external_events.setdefault("edit",{})[tname]=val

        # Generic target response.
        bonus=defaultdict(float); extra=defaultdict(float)
        for tname,tspec in self.targets.items():
            held=tspec.held_edge
            antiR=target_extra_r[tname].get(held,0.0)
            y=max(0.0,1.0-antiR+target_delivery[tname]
                  +compensation.get(tname,0.0)+law_comp[tname]+edit_delta[tname])
            log.result_values[tname]=y

            # Finalize each pending duty only after its own Read is evaluated.
            for dname,(dtype,val,duty_principle_ok) in list(pending_duties.items()):
                d=self.duties[dname]
                if d.target_law!=tname:
                    continue
                if dtype=="result":
                    lim=d.result_limit if d.result_limit is not None else d.read_limit
                    read_ok=(y<=lim)
                else:
                    lim=d.delivery_limit if d.delivery_limit is not None else d.read_limit
                    read_ok=(val<=lim)
                log.duty_reads[dname]=read_ok
                if read_ok and duty_principle_ok:
                    # R3: established Anti/limit is actually realized on the currently held DOF.
                    log.act_duties.add(dname)

            mfull=self.nodes[held[0]].m
            block=max(0.0,self.p.block_gain*((1-y)+self.p.fullness_block_gain*mfull))
            log.block[tname]=block
            # Actual principle Anti enters the held DOF's current resistance.
            extra[held]+=antiR
            # Edit influences result/block but never enters resistance.
            # Gate influences current availability/opening but never counts as Act.
            if block>self.p.tune_threshold and self.bypass_sets.get(tname):
                gain=self.p.tune_gain*(block-self.p.tune_threshold)
                self.edges[held].tune-=self.p.incumbent_close_gain*gain
                self.edges[held].shallow+=self.p.shallow_gain*block
                for j,e in enumerate(self.bypass_sets[tname]):
                    frac=1.0 if j==0 else 0.35
                    self.edges[e].tune+=frac*gain
                    bonus[e]+=frac*self.p.redistrib_score_gain*block

        # Configured field junctions: prefix pressure -> local competition -> configured suffix.
        for j in self.junctions.values():
            if j.input_node not in inputs:
                continue
            q_front=self.propagate_path(j.prefix_path,self.p.q_input,log,admission)
            if q_front<=self.p.q_floor:
                continue
            f=force_by_source.get(j.source)
            forced=(j.source,f) if f else None
            e=self.softmax_choice(j.source,q_front,extra,log,forced,bonus)
            if e is None:
                continue
            flow=q_front*self.p.q_transfer*self.edge_open(e)
            if flow<=self.p.q_floor:
                continue
            self.trace_exec(e,flow,log,admission);log.executed.append(e)
            self.nodes[e[1]].q=max(self.nodes[e[1]].q,flow)
            suffix=j.branch_suffix.get(e,[])
            if suffix:
                self.propagate_path(suffix,flow,log,admission)

        log.q_snapshot={n:s.q for n,s in self.nodes.items()}
        log.h_snapshot={n:s.h for n,s in self.nodes.items()}
        log.m_snapshot={n:s.m for n,s in self.nodes.items()}
        # Gate is instantaneous: restore structural availability after the current episode.
        for e,(avail,tune0) in gate_saved.items():
            self.edges[e].available=avail
            self.edges[e].tune=tune0
        self.logs.append(log);self.decay_step()
        return log

def build_model(seed=0,p:Optional[Params]=None):
    f=SedimentFieldEngine(seed,p)
    # nodes/materials
    for n in ["s","m","a","c","x","n","d","z"]:f.add_node(n,"E")
    for prefix,termkind in [("R","I"),("D","I"),("Q","E"),("2","I")]:
        f.add_node(f"h{prefix}","P");f.add_node(f"p{prefix}","E");f.add_node(f"t{prefix}",termkind)
    # edges
    for e in [("s","m"),("m","a"),("a","c"),("m","x"),("m","z"),("x","n"),("n","d")]:
        f.add_edge(*e)
    for prefix in ["R","D","Q","2"]:
        f.add_edge(f"h{prefix}",f"p{prefix}");f.add_edge(f"p{prefix}",f"t{prefix}")
    # laws
    f.add_law(LawSpec("A","s","c",[("s","m"),("m","a"),("a","c")],("m","a"),execution_mode="junction"))
    f.add_law(LawSpec("B","x","d",[("x","n"),("n","d")],("x","n"),execution_mode="direct"))
    f.add_law(LawSpec("P_R","hR","tR",[("hR","pR"),("pR","tR")],("pR","tR"),
                      execution_mode="carrier",entry_kind="P",relay_kind="E",terminal_kind="I",
                      law_duty_name="dR"))
    f.add_law(LawSpec("P_D","hD","tD",[("hD","pD"),("pD","tD")],("pD","tD"),
                      execution_mode="carrier",entry_kind="P",relay_kind="E",terminal_kind="I",
                      law_duty_name="dD"))
    f.add_law(LawSpec("Q","hQ","tQ",[("hQ","pQ"),("pQ","tQ")],("pQ","tQ"),
                      execution_mode="carrier",entry_kind="P",relay_kind="E",terminal_kind="E"))
    f.add_law(LawSpec("P2","h2","t2",[("h2","p2"),("p2","t2")],("p2","t2"),
                      execution_mode="carrier",entry_kind="P",relay_kind="E",terminal_kind="I",
                      law_duty_name="d2"))
    f.add_law(LawSpec("N","s","d",[("s","m"),("m","x"),("x","n"),("n","d")],("m","x"),execution_mode="junction"))
    f.add_target(TargetSpec("A",("m","a"),"yA"))
    f.add_target(TargetSpec("N",("m","x"),"yN"))
    f.add_duty(DutySpec("dR","P_R","result","A",("m","a"),f.p.lambda_result,0.50,"tR",-1,
                              result_limit=0.80))
    f.add_duty(DutySpec("dD","P_D","delivery","A",("m","a"),f.p.lambda_delivery,-0.10,"tD",-1,
                              delivery_limit=-0.10))
    f.add_duty(DutySpec("d2","P2","result","N",("m","x"),f.p.lambda_result,0.50,"t2",-1,
                              result_limit=0.80))
    # dX is physically identical to dR at the target but is not P_R's Law-duty.
    # It is the wrong-duty control: physical effect without Principle/Act identity.
    f.add_duty(DutySpec("dX","P_R","result","A",("m","a"),f.p.lambda_result,0.50,"tR",-1,
                              result_limit=0.80))
    f.register_bypasses("A",[("m","x"),("m","z")])
    f.add_junction(JunctionSpec(
        "J_main","s",[("s","m")],"m",
        {
            ("m","a"):[("a","c")],
            ("m","x"):[("x","n"),("n","d")],
            ("m","z"):[]
        }
    ))
    return f

def _force_map_for_law(f:SedimentFieldEngine, law_name:str):
    law=f.laws[law_name]
    # Only branch points need forcing. For this topology, derive from the law path.
    forced={}
    for e in law.path:
        if len(f.out_edges.get(e[0],[]))>1:
            forced[e[0]]=e[1]
    return forced

def form_law_until(f:SedimentFieldEngine, law_name:str, duty_name:Optional[str]=None,
                   max_steps:int=300):
    """
    Generic formation: replay the declared LawSpec through the same field dynamics
    until the common Law judge first passes. No law-specific episode count.
    If the Law is also a principle carrier, replay its declared duty against the
    target law in the same episode, preserving same-duty formation.
    """
    law=f.laws[law_name]
    duty=f.duties.get(duty_name) if duty_name else None
    inputs={law.ante}
    active=set()
    force=_force_map_for_law(f,law_name)
    if duty is not None:
        target=f.laws[duty.target_law]
        inputs.add(target.ante)
        active.add(duty_name)
        force.update(_force_map_for_law(f,duty.target_law))
    for k in range(1,max_steps+1):
        f.run_episode(inputs,active,force_by_source=force,
                      allow_law_duty_training=(duty is not None))
        if f.law_check(law_name)[0]:
            return k
    return None

def maintain_law(f:SedimentFieldEngine, law_name:str, duty_name:Optional[str]=None, steps:int=3):
    law=f.laws[law_name]
    duty=f.duties.get(duty_name) if duty_name else None
    inputs={law.ante}; active=set(); force=_force_map_for_law(f,law_name)
    if duty:
        inputs.add(f.laws[duty.target_law].ante); active.add(duty_name)
        force.update(_force_map_for_law(f,duty.target_law))
    for _ in range(steps):
        f.run_episode(inputs,active,force_by_source=force,
                      allow_law_duty_training=(duty is not None))

def train_initial(f:SedimentFieldEngine):
    formed={}
    formed["A"]=form_law_until(f,"A")
    formed["P_R"]=form_law_until(f,"P_R","dR")
    formed["P_D"]=form_law_until(f,"P_D","dD")
    formed["Q"]=form_law_until(f,"Q")
    formed["B"]=form_law_until(f,"B")

    # Generic simultaneous-current-qualification convergence.
    # Cycle required pre-existing laws until they are all PASS at the same freeze.
    # Principle carriers replay against their configured target while target continuation
    # is forced along its incumbent path, so candidate N is not trained.
    required=[("A",None),("P_R","dR"),("P_D","dD"),("Q",None),("B",None)]
    convergence=None
    for cyc in range(1,81):
        for lname,dname in required:
            maintain_law(f,lname,dname,steps=1)
        states={lname:f.law_check(lname) for lname,_ in required}
        if all(v[0] for v in states.values()) and not f.law_check("N")[0]:
            convergence=cyc
            break

    status={k:f.law_check(k) for k in ["A","P_R","P_D","Q","B","N"]}
    status["_formed_steps"]=formed
    status["_maintenance_cycles"]=convergence
    return status

    cnt=0
    for _ in range(20):

def run_suite(nseed=50):
    """
    Every PB is isolated from the same frozen initial field by deepcopy.
    PB12a/b continue only from their own clean PB10 lineage.
    """
    s=defaultdict(int); firsts=[]
    for seed in range(nseed):
        base=build_model(seed)
        init=train_initial(base)
        # Audit logs are not part of field state; discard before cloning PB branches.
        base.logs.clear()
        s["PB1_A"]+=int(init["A"][0])
        s["PB2_multi"]+=int(init["A"][0] and init["P_R"][0] and init["P_D"][0] and init["Q"][0] and init["B"][0] and not init["N"][0])

        # PB3 / PB4 structural Principle qualification
        f=copy.deepcopy(base)
        s["PB3_PR"]+=int(f.principle_check("dR"))
        s["PB4_PD"]+=int(f.principle_check("dD"))

        # PB5 current Act
        f=copy.deepcopy(base)
        l=f.run_episode({"s","hR"},{"dR"})
        s["PB5_act"]+=int("dR" in l.act_duties and l.duty_reads.get("dR",False))

        # PB6 Principle but no current trigger
        f=copy.deepcopy(base)
        l=f.run_episode({"s"})
        s["PB6_idle"]+=int(f.principle_check("dR") and "dR" not in l.act_duties)

        # PB7a wrong duty: same physical counterhold through dX, but dX is not
        # the Law-duty of P_R and therefore cannot be Principle/Act.
        fR=copy.deepcopy(base); fX=copy.deepcopy(base)
        lr=fR.run_episode({"s","hR"},{"dR"})
        lx=fX.run_episode({"s","hR"},{"dX"})
        held=fR.targets["A"].held_edge
        same_physical=(abs(lr.result_values["A"]-lx.result_values["A"])<1e-12
                       and abs(fR.edges[held].r-fX.edges[held].r)<1e-12)
        s["PB7_wrong"]+=int(
            fX.principle_check("dR")
            and not fX.principle_check("dX")
            and "dX" not in lx.act_duties
            and same_physical
        )

        # PB7b stale target: A remains Law and Principle remains true,
        # but A no longer currently holds the designated freedom.

        before_principle=f.principle_check("dR")
        before_A=f.law_check("A")[0]
        l=f.run_episode({"s","hR"},{"dR"})
        s["PB7_stale"]+=int(before_principle and before_A and "dR" not in l.act_duties)

        # PB8: P_D acts while running Law Q restores net result.
        f=copy.deepcopy(base)
        # First run without Q to confirm dD current delivery.
        l0=f.run_episode({"s","hD"},{"dD"},force_by_source={"m":"a"})
        dD_ok=("dD" in l0.act_duties)
        # Clean clone, now run Q as an actual Law and use its positive terminal output.
        f=copy.deepcopy(base)
        l=f.run_episode({"s","hD","hQ"},{"dD"},
                        force_by_source={"m":"a"},
                        compensation_laws={"A":"Q"})
        s["PB8_comp"]+=int(dD_ok and "dD" in l.act_duties and abs(l.result_values["A"]-1.0)<1e-9)

        # PB9 three distinct mechanisms. Each is run from the same frozen field.
        # Gate alters edge availability, Edit alters result generation, Counterhold
        # is the Law-duty history. All may eventually yield the same Law N, but only
        # Counterhold may record Act.
        fg=copy.deepcopy(base); fe=copy.deepcopy(base); fc=copy.deepcopy(base)
        g0=fg.run_episode({"s"},set(),external_gate={"A":1.0})
        e0=fe.run_episode({"s"},set(),external_edit={"A":0.8})
        c0=fc.run_episode({"s","hR"},{"dR"})
        for _ in range(59):
            fg.run_episode({"s"},set(),external_gate={"A":1.0})
            fe.run_episode({"s"},set(),external_edit={"A":0.8})
            fc.run_episode({"s","hR"},{"dR"})
        s["PB9_triple"]+=int(
            "dR" not in g0.act_duties and "dR" not in e0.act_duties and "dR" in c0.act_duties
            and g0.external_events.get("gate",{}).get("A")==1.0
            and e0.external_events.get("edit",{}).get("A")==0.8
            and fg.law_check("N")[0] and fe.law_check("N")[0] and fc.law_check("N")[0]
        )

        # PB10 clean generation lineage.
        f=copy.deepcopy(base)
        first=None
        for k in range(60):
            f.run_episode({"s","hR"},{"dR"})
            if first is None and f.law_check("N")[0]:
                first=k+1
        s["PB10_N"]+=int(f.law_check("N")[0])
        if first is not None:
            firsts.append(first)

        # PB10 ghost: candidate never executed -> no history/sediment.
        fg=copy.deepcopy(base); g0=fg.edges[("m","x")].g; rep0=len(fg.edges[("m","x")].rep)
        for _ in range(20):
            fg.run_episode({"s"},force_by_source={"m":"a"})
        s["PB10_ghost"]+=int(
            len(fg.edges[("m","x")].rep)==rep0
            and fg.edges[("m","x")].g<=g0+1e-12
        )

        # PB11a one real reroute insufficient
        fs=copy.deepcopy(base)
        fs.run_episode({"s","hR"},{"dR"},force_by_source={"m":"x"})
        s["PB11_short"]+=int(not fs.law_check("N")[0])

        # PB11b actual reroutes but no sediment admission
        fa=copy.deepcopy(base)
        rep0=len(fa.edges[("m","x")].rep)
        for _ in range(15):
            fa.run_episode({"s","hR"},{"dR"},admission=False,force_by_source={"m":"x"})
        s["PB11_no_sed"]+=int(
            not fa.law_check("N")[0]
            and len(fa.edges[("m","x")].rep)==rep0
        )

        # PB12a continue from clean PB10 lineage.
        new=0
        for _ in range(20):
            lg=f.run_episode({"s"})
            new+=int(("m","x") in lg.executed)
        s["PB12_self"]+=int(new>=18 and f.law_check("N")[0])

        # PB12b form P2 and retarget mature N through same generic interface.
        form_law_until(f,"P2","d2",max_steps=300)
        f.logs.clear()
        f0=copy.deepcopy(f); f1=copy.deepcopy(f)
        l0=f0.run_episode({"s","h2"},set())
        l1=f1.run_episode({"s","h2"},{"d2"})
        heldN=f1.targets["N"].held_edge
        r0=f0.edges[heldN].r; r1=f1.edges[heldN].r
        y0=l0.result_values.get("N",1.0); y1=l1.result_values.get("N",1.0)
        s["PB12_retarget"]+=int(
            f1.principle_check("d2")
            and "d2" in l1.act_duties
            and l1.duty_reads.get("d2",False)
            and r1>r0+1e-9 and y1<y0-1e-9
        )
    return dict(s),firsts
