# The Determination Form of Principle Generation

## Section One　Use, Entry Conditions, and Declaration of Dependence

### 1.1　Use

The Construction Form of the Principle Running Base and The Reachability Form of Principle Generation have arrived at a result that recurs: one and the same new Law can be made by several histories of wholly different source — an already standing Law counter-holding another by its same duty; a duty whose physical effect is the same but which is not the duty through which the bearing Law acquired its Law qualification; external gating; external editing. All four can make the candidate Law acquire qualification, and of them only the first has the source identity of Principle and Principle-action. Hence "the new Law stands" and "the new Law was generated by Principle" are two different things, and the latter can be adjudicated only from the generative history, never inferred backwards from a result of qualification.

This form-text gives that adjudication: taking a clearly delimited observation set as input, it gives a procedure for deciding whether the property "this candidate Law's standing was generated by Principle-action" holds, proves the validity of that procedure in both directions, and lists explicitly, as bounds of misjudgement, those places where the two cannot both be had. The property adjudicated is an executable narrowing of the upstream direct-type source; the extent and the cost of that narrowing are declared explicitly in 2.1 and 5.7.

### 1.2　Entry conditions

　　The definition of the property is already closed: the property adjudicated is a narrowing of the direct-type source restriction of Section 4.2 of The Reachability Form of Principle Generation — the locally counterfactually discernible type; see 2.1. The narrowed adjudication domain is declared explicitly by this form-text, and every constituent used is taken from upstream; no new ontological predicate is made here.  
　　The observation set is clearly delimited: Section Two declares four classes of observation and states item by item what each can read.  
　　The forward relation between the property and the observations has been given by form-texts already standing: how a generative history produces observable records is given by the running specification of The Construction Form of the Principle Running Base and by the path argument of The Reachability Form of Principle Generation.

### 1.3　Declaration of Dependence

This form-text is a determination form standing as its own text, and depends on the following already standing form-texts: The Construction Form of the Principle Running Base v1.0 and its official construction model; The Reachability Form of Principle Generation v1.0; and the Law, Principle, and Act criteria of the first six texts of the Principle Group-Form. Should a principal form on which it depends be broken through or withdrawn, the conclusions of this form-text lapse or become pending.

A determination form is an auxiliary form and does not cover the basic directions of proof. This form-text specifically does not do three things: it does not prove that Principle necessarily generates a new Law; it does not prove general conditions of reachability; and it does not redefine the standing of Principle or Principle-action.

This form-text modifies no upstream definition, adds no ontological predicate, and adds no H. The observation set and criterion erected within it serve the adjudication of source alone, and do not enter the ontology.

## Section Two　Declaration of the Property and the Observation Set

### 2.1　The property adjudicated

Fix a candidate Law N together with one legal witness it has acquired in the terminal state. The property adjudicated is written

　　P\_gen^cf: the standing of this candidate Law in the terminal state has a locally counterfactually discernible direct-type source — that is, it was generated by Principle-action, and that source can be discerned by a local counterfactual.

Below, wherever P\_gen is written, P\_gen^cf is meant.

The duty at issue is fixed as d\* — the one duty through which the bearing Law acquired its Law qualification, bound upon the Law specification by the construction form depended on. It is not changed at any point of the adjudication, and is not made to refer to some other duty merely because another duty happened to be active in a given run.

The direct-type source restriction frozen in Section 4.2 of The Reachability Form of Principle Generation reads: the counter-hold discharged by d\* enters, by way of the normal field dynamics, the actual execution, trace, and sedimentation history of some legal witness of the terminal state. That formulation does not prescribe how "enters" is to be verified. This form-text adopts one executable narrowing of it — the locally counterfactually discernible type — and defines the property adjudicated by the following four items:

P\_gen holds if and only if, within that run, there is an experiment in which all four of the following occur together:

　　(a) d\* is registered as acting in that experiment — by the first six texts of the Principle Group-Form, being registered as acting already entails that the bearing Law is a Law, that d\* is identical with the duty it binds, that Principle qualification stands, that the target is in bearing on this beat, and that this Read is satisfied; these conditions are therefore not listed again here;  
　　(b) within that same experiment, some edge of the terminal witness W is actually executed;  
　　(c) that execution leaves sedimentation within that same experiment;  
　　(d) that execution and sedimentation are brought about by d*'s acting: with the same prior state, the same normal inflow, and the same random state, withdraw the activation of d* and re-run that experiment, and the edge is not executed in a way that leaves positive sedimentation.

Items (b), (c), and (d) take an existential quantifier over the edges of the witness, and fix no particular edge. In the reference implementation depended on, the edge that in fact closes the witness is the bypass edge, but that is a fact of the instance and does not enter the general definition.

The declaration of the narrowing must be set out first. Item (d) is a condition of "withdraw it and it does not occur," and is stronger than the upstream "enters the generative history": where, after d\* is withdrawn, that sedimenting execution still occurs and only the quantity of sedimentation is smaller, the upstream formulation does not necessarily exclude it, while (d) does. Hence what this form-text adjudicates is the locally counterfactually discernible type among direct-type sources, and it makes no claim to exhaust every direct-type source of the eighth text. Whoever cites a verdict of 0 from this form-text may read it only as "it does not have a locally counterfactually discernible source," and must not read it as "the direct-type source of the eighth text is false." The cost of this narrowing is listed in 5.7.

The strength of P\_gen must be seen clearly at the same time. It is existential: one sedimenting execution of a witness edge brought about by d*'s acting suffices for truth; it does not require that the closing of the witness be borne entirely by acting, nor that no other mechanism take part thereafter. There is therefore a class of run on which P\_gen is true while the closing was not completed by acting: d* did bring about the first sedimenting execution of the bypass edge, and the advance and the closing thereafter were taken over by gating. This form-text adjudicates such a run as 1, and that is a verdict in accord with the direct-type definition frozen upstream, not a misjudgement of it. If one wishes to ask "was the closing borne entirely by acting" or "how large a part did acting play in the generation," that is another and stronger property, which this form-text neither defines nor adjudicates; see 5.6.

Item (d) cannot be dispensed with. A witness often contains mature public edges — in the reference implementation, the trunk edges — which are executed and sediment in almost every experiment, and which therefore co-occur naturally with any instance of acting on the same beat. With (a), (b), and (c) alone, a run of mixed source — let d\* act for one beat, and let everything thereafter be generated by gating or by editing — would be adjudicated true: there really is acting on the first beat, there really is a sedimenting execution of a trunk edge on the first beat, and the trunk edge really is within the terminal witness, while the edge that actually closes the witness was made entirely by gating or editing. What (d) requires is that this sedimenting execution be brought about by that instance of acting, and not merely occur at the same time as it. Co-occurrence is not source.

The cross-experiment initiation-and-completion type does not fall within P\_gen: where acting first alters the field state and the bearing Law then falls at rest, the generation being completed by later experiments without acting, P\_gen is false. This does not mean that it constitutes no source relation whatever; it may constitute some other, indirect type of generative property, one that this form-text neither defines nor adjudicates; see Section Five. Two things are to be kept apart: for the P\_gen already defined, the answer is 0; for the indirect-type property not yet defined, this form-text gives no answer.

### 2.2　The observation set

Four classes of observation are declared layer by layer, with what each can read written out. The first three bear on the input of adjudication; Y\_full is the higher observation of the whole history, and the criterion does not use it.

Y\_end　Terminal structural observation. Readable: the depth and re-walk queue length of every edge in the terminal state, the accumulated depth of every node, the Law-criterion result for the candidate Law, and its set of witness edges. Not readable: the on-beat record of any experiment.

Y\_traj　Running-trajectory observation. Readable: the set of duties registered as acting in each experiment, the set of edges actually executed in that experiment, and the sedimentation increment of each executed edge in that experiment. These are all records produced by the running specification itself, not probes added on top.

Y\_cf　Paired-intervention observation. Readable: for each experiment, re-run it once with the same prior state, the same normal inflow, and the same random state, with the activation of d\* withdrawn, and take the set of executed edges and the sedimentation increment of each. This yields, for that experiment, the edges whose execution with positive sedimentation depends on d\*'s acting; these are called that experiment's source-chain edge set.

Y\_cf is an interventional observation, not a passive record, but it is executable: all that is needed is to save the prior state and the random state and re-run one beat, which is precisely the local counterfactual protocol used in Section 10.1 of The Reachability Form of Principle Generation, and its execution depends on no unobservable quantity. Where the running environment does not permit that re-run, Y\_cf cannot be obtained, and the criterion returns undecided by Section Three.

Two things are to be kept apart: that an experiment's Y\_cf has been obtained and its source chain is empty, and that its Y\_cf was never obtained at all. The former is a settled negative fact and takes part in adjudication; the latter is an insufficiency of the conditions of adjudication and makes the criterion return undecided. The record must mark the two separately: an empty set must not be passed off as not obtained, nor not obtained as an empty set. Where an experiment did not activate d\*, withdrawing a duty that was not activated to begin with yields the positive branch itself, so that its source chain is empty is a settled result and not an unobtained one, and no separate re-run is needed there.

Y\_full　Whole-history observation. Readable beyond the above: the inputs of each experiment, the Law-criterion results for the bearing Law and the target Law, the results of the Principle-qualification adjudication, and the beat-by-beat state of every edge.

The inputs of the criterion are three: Y\_traj; the set of witness edges within Y\_end — the witness alone, and no terminal numerical value; and Y\_cf. The criterion uses no additional content from Y\_full and depends on no unobservable quantity. How Y\_end behaves as a sole input belongs to the bounds of misjudgement; see Section Five. The undecidability reported in 5.1 holds under the condition that a complete Y\_end is in hand while Y\_traj and Y\_cf are not.

## Section Three　Construction of the Criterion

Let the records left by one run be

　　r₁, r₂, …, r\_m,

where r\_k records the set of duties registered as acting in the k-th experiment, the set of edges actually executed, the sedimentation increment of each executed edge, and the source-chain edge set obtained from Y\_cf. Let the edge set of the terminal witness be W.

The criterion D\_gen is defined as follows. The d\* within it is the one duty fixed in 2.1; the criterion asks after that same d\* for any run whatever, and does not make it refer to whichever duty happened to be active in that run. An experiment k is called a joint event if and only if d\* ∈ the set of acting duties of r\_k, and there exists an e ∈ W such that all three hold: e ∈ the set of executed edges of r\_k; the sedimentation increment of e in r\_k is positive; and e belongs to the source-chain edge set of r\_k. The third item is the formalization of (d); without it, the whole falls back to mere co-occurrence.

　　If the candidate Law does not stand in the terminal state, D\_gen is recorded as not applicable — source adjudication has not yet been entered;  
　　If the records are incomplete — the number of experiments does not agree with the number of records, or any record lacks one of the four items, or the source-chain edge set of any record is marked as not obtained — then D\_gen = undecided;  
　　If the records are complete and a joint event exists, then D\_gen = 1;  
　　If the records are complete and no joint event exists, then D\_gen = 0.

The third and fourth branches exhaust every case in which the records are complete, so the criterion is two-valued upon complete records; undecided is left to incomplete records or a failed protocol, and is left to no class of real generative history whatever. This accords with the "if and only if" of 2.1: where a complete trajectory contains no joint event, P\_gen is false and the criterion adjudicates 0.

Undetermined signifies only an insufficiency of the conditions of adjudication — incomplete records, Y\_cf unobtainable, or a failed pairing protocol. It does not signify a third kind of generative fact.

The completeness check is placed inside the criterion so that "returning undecided rather than 0 where the records are incomplete" is a step of the criterion itself and not an admonition outside it. The number of experiments here is the number of experiments actually run in that run, given by the running side along with the records; what the criterion compares is whether the number of records agrees with that number, so any truncation of the records is caught by this branch. Appendix A carries the measurement of that branch.

Execution of the criterion: take each experiment's set of acting duties, and where it contains d*, check on each edge of the terminal witness the conjunction of three items — execution, positive sedimentation increment, and membership of the source chain. The number of experiments and the number of witness edges are both finite; for experiments that activated d*, one paired re-run is made to obtain the source chain, while for experiments that did not activate d\* no re-run is needed and the source chain is directly settled as empty. The whole adjudication therefore completes in finitely many steps.

## Section Four　Validity of the Criterion

### 4.1　True adjudicated true

Suppose the records are complete and P\_gen holds. By 2.1 there is an experiment possessing (a), (b), (c), and (d) together: in that experiment's record r\_k, d\* is within the set of acting duties, some edge of the witness W is within its set of executed edges with a positive sedimentation increment, and that edge is within that experiment's source-chain edge set. That is exactly the joint event of Section Three, so D\_gen = 1.

### 4.2　False adjudicated false

Suppose the records are complete and P\_gen does not hold. By the "if and only if" of 2.1, the trajectory then contains no joint event, so the fourth branch of D\_gen holds and D\_gen = 0.

This branch covers three classes of run and need not treat them separately. First, d\* is never registered as acting: gating, external editing, and a same-shaped non-bound duty all belong here — gating and editing activate no duty; and a same-shaped non-bound duty, though it produces in the field a physical effect agreeing digit for digit with d*'s, is not registered as acting, because it is not the duty through which the bearing Law acquired its Law qualification; the total count of acting events in such runs is measured at 0. Second, d* was once registered as acting while no experiment has both that acting and a sedimenting execution of a witness edge. Third, there is acting, and a sedimenting execution of a witness edge on the same beat, while that sedimenting execution is not brought about by that instance of acting — a run of mixed source belongs here: there is acting on the first beat and the trunk edge sediments, while all the generation thereafter is made by gating or editing; withdraw the acting of the first beat and the trunk edge is executed and sediments just the same, so (d) does not hold, there is no joint event, and the verdict is 0. All three are false by 2.1, and the criterion adjudicates 0. Whether any of them contains some not-yet-defined indirect or weaker source relation lies outside the adjudicative range of this form-text, and does not on that account turn the verdict to undecided. This must be kept apart from the case described in 5.6: there, d\* did bring about the sedimenting execution of a witness edge, (d) holds, and by 2.1 it is true and adjudicated 1; here, the third class has (d) not holding, and by 2.1 it is false. The dividing line is (d), not who completed the closing.

### 4.3　Measurements

On the construction model depended on, 30 seeds are run for each of the four classes of generative history:

　　counter-holding: the candidate Law stands 30/30, D\_gen = 1 in 30/30;  
　　the same-shaped non-bound duty: the candidate Law stands 30/30, D\_gen = 0 in 30/30;  
　　gating: the candidate Law stands 30/30, D\_gen = 0 in 30/30;  
　　editing: the candidate Law stands 30/30, D\_gen = 0 in 30/30.

Thirty seeds are also run for each of two classes of mixed-source run: d\* is activated on the first beat, and everything thereafter is generated by gating or by editing. The candidate Law stands 30/30 in both classes; adjudicated by (a), (b), and (c) alone, the same-beat co-occurrence of both is true 30/30, that is, a bare co-occurrence criterion would give 1; with (d) added, D\_gen = 0 in 30/30. That control is direct evidence that (d) cannot be dispensed with.

An upstream identity re-verification is also made: it reads the same set of records and is not a source of truth independent of the criterion; what it verifies separately is the identity side — whether d\* is the Law-conferring duty bound to the bearing Law, and whether its Principle qualification stands in that run. The results are in Appendix A: for counter-holding, the joint event is true, the identity re-verification passes, and the criterion gives 1, each 30/30; for the same-shaped non-bound duty, the joint event is false while the identity re-verification passes, and the criterion gives 0, each 30/30; for gating and editing, the joint event is false, the identity re-verification does not pass, and the criterion gives 0, each 30/30.

## Section Five　Bounds of Misjudgement

The items below are the places where both cannot be had at once, listed explicitly one by one.

5.1　Terminal observation does not suffice for adjudication

If the criterion takes Y\_end alone as input, then on the model depended on it must misjudge. The measurement compares the complete Y\_end declared in 2.2 — the depth and re-walk queue length of every edge, the accumulated depth of every node, the Law-criterion result, and the set of witness edges — and the terminal snapshots of the two classes of run, counter-holding and the same-shaped non-bound duty, agree item by item on 20 seeds (20/20). One is true and the other false, while the complete terminal observation reads out no difference whatever; any criterion taking Y\_end alone as input must therefore err on one of the two here.

The complete terminal snapshots of gating and of editing differ from that of counter-holding (0/20 the same under the same comparison), but that is only a numerical difference of this implementation and not a mark of identity: the witness and the Law identity remain the same, and no terminal quantity whatever can mark the source in general. Neither, therefore, may "the terminal numbers differ" serve as a criterion of source.

Conclusion: on this family of instances of the model depended on, P\_gen is undecidable upon Y\_end — a pair of runs of different source with terminal observations agreeing digit for digit is already in evidence. This is undecidability at the level of a counterexample, not a universal proof completed over all observations within Y\_end; that belongs to an Indistinguishability Form, see Section Seven. Hence one cannot adjudicate from Y\_end alone, and must enter the trajectory records and the paired-intervention observation, that is, take Y\_traj, the set of witness edges within Y\_end, and Y\_cf as input. That is precisely why the criterion of Section Three takes those three.

5.2　The cross-experiment initiation-and-completion type: an undefined property, not an undecided adjudication

For the P\_gen^cf already defined, that type is false, the criterion adjudicates 0, and there is no misjudgement. The first-beat co-occurrence type of mixed source described in 4.3 is the same: there really is one instance of acting within it, but no instance of acting satisfies the source-chain joint event defined by this form-text, so for P\_gen^cf it is false. This must be kept apart from the partial-source type of 5.6 — there an instance of acting does satisfy the joint event, and the verdict is 1. The bound reported here lies not in the adjudication but in the definition: this form-text defines no cross-experiment indirect-type source property, and defines neither partial source nor a share of source, so it gives no answer as to whether they constitute some other, weaker source.

Its reachability is measured on the model depended on: running counter-holding for 6, 8, and 10 experiments and then withdrawing the duty alone, the bearing Law still keeping its normal inflow — a single-variable control whose only difference is whether the duty is activated — 30 seeds fail to make the candidate Law stand at all three switching points (not standing at the switch 30/30, and still not standing in the terminal state 30/30). Under that protocol on that model, therefore, this type has not yet appeared as an instance. That is a fact of the instance, and must not be read as this type being impossible in general.

### 5.3　Completeness of the records

The correctness of the criterion depends on the completeness and truthfulness of the trajectory records. Completeness is written into the criterion itself: the corresponding branch of Section Three returns undecided where the number of experiments does not agree with the number of records, where any record lacks one of the four items — the set of acting duties, the set of executed edges, the sedimentation increments, and the source-chain edge set — or where the source chain of any record is marked as not obtained; so "incomplete records misjudged as 0" cannot occur. An empty source chain and an unobtained source chain are marked separately in the record, and the criterion treats them separately: the former is adjudicated 0, the latter undecided.

Truthfulness, however, is not within the criterion. Where records are forged — the number and the fields complete while the content is untrue — the criterion will still give 1 or 0 according to the forged content, and its output no longer carries the guarantees of 4.1 and 4.2. This form-text supplies no means of authenticating the records themselves; that is part of the bounds of misjudgement reported here.

5.4　The source chain depends on a re-runnable intervention

Verifying (d) requires one paired re-run for each experiment. Where the running environment does not save the prior state and the random state, or does not permit a re-run, that experiment's Y\_cf is marked as not obtained and the criterion returns undecided; it does not fall back to the bare co-occurrence criterion, nor take not obtained for an empty source chain and adjudicate 0. This is a prerequisite of execution, not a refinement that may be skipped: the bare co-occurrence criterion has already been proved by the mixed-source control of 4.3 to give false positives.

5.5　What is adjudicated is the source of a single run

What the criterion adjudicates is the generative source of one particular run, not a property of the candidate Law itself. One and the same Law identity may be made by different sources in different runs; the criterion gives its conclusion separately for each run, and makes no general attribution to that Law identity.

5.6　Existential source and exclusive source

P\_gen is an existential source: one sedimenting execution of a witness edge brought about by acting suffices for truth. It is not an exclusive source, nor a load-bearing source. Measurement: let d\* run until it first brings about a sedimenting execution of the bypass edge (occurring, over 30 seeds, at experiments 3, 4, 5, and 6 respectively), and let everything thereafter be advanced to closing by gating; the candidate Law stands 30/30 and the criterion adjudicates 1 in 30/30. This differs from the mixed-source control of 4.3 — there what sediments on the first beat is the mature trunk edge, which sediments just the same when acting is withdrawn, so (d) does not hold; here what sediments is the bypass edge, which would not occur at all were acting withdrawn, so (d) holds.

The dividing line between the two is therefore clear: what the criterion adjudicates is "whether there was one instance of acting that really brought about the sedimentation of a witness edge," not "who completed the closing." The latter requires an exclusive source or a load-bearing source to be defined separately, with a separate determination form; this form-text gives no answer, and does not, for want of an answer to that question, turn such runs to 0 or to undecided.

5.7　Quantity-difference sources lie outside the narrowed adjudication domain

Item (d) takes "withdraw it and it does not occur." Where, after d\* is withdrawn, that witness edge is still executed and sediments, only in a smaller quantity, that edge does not enter the source chain and the criterion does not adjudicate 1 on its account. Such quantity differences do exist in the model depended on, and can be re-checked by the quant command of Appendix B: running counter-holding until the candidate Law stands and comparing the pairs experiment by experiment, among the execution events with positive sedimentation on the positive branch across all experiments of 20 seeds, 660 are of the "withdraw it and it does not occur" kind, 891 have sedimentation exactly the same on both branches, and 154 sediment on both branches in differing quantity; the quantity-difference cases are concentrated on the edge the target Law itself holds, for example seed 0, experiment 1, that edge sedimenting 0.010222 on the positive branch and 0.049887 on the negative.

Whether such events should also count as "entering the generative history" in the upstream sense, this form-text does not adjudicate. The cost of the narrowing lies just here: with respect to the general direct-type source of the eighth text, a verdict of 1 from this form-text is sufficient, while a verdict of 0 says only "it does not have a locally counterfactually discernible source." To take in the quantity-difference type, one must first define a measure and a threshold for the contribution of sedimentation, and that is another property, requiring a separate determination form.

## Section Six　The Determination Theorem

Theorem 6.1　Determination theorem for the locally counterfactually discernible direct-type source

Working domain: the narrowed property P\_gen^cf declared in 2.1, that is, the locally counterfactually discernible type among the direct-type sources of the eighth text. Under the entry conditions declared in Section One, the criterion D\_gen, taking as input Y\_traj, the set of witness edges within Y\_end, and Y\_cf, satisfies:

　　(i) if the records are complete and P\_gen^cf holds, then D\_gen = 1;  
　　(ii) if the records are complete and P\_gen^cf does not hold, then D\_gen = 0;  
　　(iii) if the trajectory records are incomplete, if some experiment's Y\_cf was not obtained, or if the pairing protocol failed, then D\_gen = undecided; a source chain obtained and found empty does not fall under this branch, and is adjudicated 0 by (ii);  
　　(iv) if the candidate Law does not stand in the terminal state, D\_gen is not applicable;  
　　(v) the criterion completes execution in finitely many steps, and uses only the records produced by the running specification itself together with the source chain — the latter obtained, for experiments that activated d*, by one paired re-run, and settled directly as empty for those that did not — the duty asked after being fixed as d*, and the witness edge taken being any edge of the terminal witness.

On runs where the records are complete and every experiment's Y\_cf has been obtained, therefore, the criterion is two-valued upon P\_gen^cf: true adjudicated true, false adjudicated false, with no undecided region.

The strength signed can accordingly be compressed into one sentence: what this form-text adjudicates is the locally counterfactually discernible direct-type existential source. It makes no claim to exhaust every direct-type source of the eighth text, does not adjudicate the cross-experiment indirect type, does not adjudicate the share of a source, and does not adjudicate who completed the closing of the witness.

The bounds of misjudgement are as listed in Section Five: undecidable with Y\_end as input (5.1); undecided where the records are incomplete, Y\_cf was not obtained, or the pairing protocol failed (5.3, 5.4); records may be forged and this form-text supplies no authentication (5.3); what is adjudicated is the source of a single run and not a general attribution to a Law identity (5.5); what is adjudicated is an existential source and not an exclusive or load-bearing source (5.6); what is adjudicated is the locally counterfactually discernible type, and quantity-difference sources lie outside the adjudication domain (5.7); the cross-experiment indirect type is undefined in this form-text and therefore not adjudicated (5.2).

Proof. (i) by 4.1; (ii) by 4.2; (iii) and (iv) by the corresponding branches of the criterion in Section Three; (v) by the definition of the criterion in Section Three — checking the set of acting duties experiment by experiment, and taking an existential quantifier over the set of witness edges to check execution, sedimentation increment, and membership of the source chain; the number of experiments and the number of witness edges are both finite, the first three items are taken from Y\_traj and the fourth from Y\_cf — one paired re-run for experiments that activated d*, and directly settled as empty for those that did not — while d* is fixed by 2.1. □

This theorem does not entail: that the standing of a candidate Law entails any source whatever; that Principle-action entails that the candidate Law must stand; that the criterion can work upon Y\_end; that the criterion can adjudicate the indirect-type source property this form-text leaves undefined; or that a verdict of 0 from the criterion entails that the general direct-type source of the eighth text is false.

## Section Seven　Interfaces

To the Law-Change Group-Form: the result of adjudicating the generative source of a single run is delivered, for distinguishing "a new Law with a locally counterfactually discernible source" from "the same Law identity sedimented out by some other mechanism." Whoever cites it must carry the narrowing declared in 2.1 along with it, and must not read a verdict of 0 as the general direct-type source of the upstream text being false.

To The Reachability Form of Principle Generation: the criterion of this form-text may serve as an executable sufficient verification procedure for the source condition of its Section Four — a verdict of 1 satisfies that text's source condition; a verdict of 0 does not suffice to negate it, because the adjudication domain of this form-text is narrowed.

To an Indistinguishability Form: the terminal indistinguishability measured in 5.1 is a candidate task that could be raised into an independent Indistinguishability Form — that would require a universal proof completed over all observations within Y\_end, whereas this form-text gives measurements only and does not pass them off as universal.

To the tasks of diagnosis and inversion: the sufficient observation set this form-text adopts is three items — the three records of Y\_traj, the set of witness edges within Y\_end (the witness alone, and no terminal numerical value), and the source chain of Y\_cf; the criterion completes its adjudication using those three alone. Whether it is minimal — whether any one of the three could be deleted with the two-way validity still standing — this form-text does not prove; from the mixed-source control of 4.3 it is known that at least Y\_cf cannot be deleted.

　　Two-way validity (command judge, 30 seeds): the criterion asks after the fixed d\* for all four classes of run alike; counter-holding D\_gen = 1 in 30/30; the same-shaped non-bound duty, gating, and editing each D\_gen = 0 in 30/30; the candidate Law stands 30/30 in all four. Also checked: the total count of acting events in runs with the same-shaped non-bound duty is 0.  
　　Upstream identity re-verification (command oracle, 30 seeds): this command is not a source of truth independent of the criterion — it reads the same set of trajectory records, and what it verifies separately is the identity side: whether d\* is the Law-conferring duty bound to the bearing Law, and whether its Principle qualification stands in that run. Note: the determination-side script is not within the model code, and is a rebuild against the engine's public interfaces. The three trajectory records the criterion uses — each experiment's set of acting duties, set of actually executed edges, and sedimentation increment of each executed edge — are all taken directly from the experiment records of the running specification, with no probe added. As to the fourth item, the source-chain edge set: for experiments that activated d\* it is obtained by one paired re-run, which uses the saved prior state and random state, withdraws the activation of d\* alone, and leaves every other input and external condition unaltered; for experiments that did not activate d\*, no re-run is needed and the source chain is directly settled as empty.

"""Decision-side protocols for the judgement form (rebuilt from the engine's public API).
Commands: judge | terminal | indirect | oracle | truncate | mixed | partial | quant
Run in the same directory as full_dynamics_sediment_field_v4_12.py (SHA-256
84307a1a049953cf8d4b65088b9f8a4afe69b74221555f8505d81d5aed13258b)."""
import json,sys,copy
from collections import defaultdict,Counter
import full_dynamics_sediment_field_v4_12 as M

TARGET=("m","x")          # in v4.12 this is the edge whose execution-sediment history
                          # closes the terminal witness of N; the criterion below does
                          # NOT privilege it — it quantifies over the whole witness W.
MECHS={                    # the four generating mechanisms compared throughout
 "counterhold":({"s","hR"},{"dR"},{}),
 "nonbound"   :({"s","hR"},{"dX"},{}),
 "gate"       :({"s"},set(),{"external_gate":{"A":1.0}}),
 "edit"       :({"s"},set(),{"external_edit":{"A":0.8}}),
}

def _episode_record(log,srclink=None):
    """One episode's observation. The first three fields are Y_traj — which duties acted,
    which edges were actually executed, how much each executed edge sedimented. The fourth
    is Y_cf: the set of edges whose executed-and-sedimented status in THIS episode depends
    on d* being active, established by the paired counterfactual re-run (see _paired)."""
    return {"acts":set(log.act_duties),
            "executed":set(log.executed),
            "sedimented":{e:v for e,v in log.sedimented.items() if v>0},
            # None means Y_cf was never obtained for this episode; an empty set means the
            # paired re-run WAS performed and produced no source link. The two must not be
            # conflated: the first makes the criterion undecided, the second is a decided
            # negative fact about this episode.
            "srclink":None if srclink is None else set(srclink)}

def _paired(f,inp,du,kw):
    """Run one episode twice from the same prior state, same ordinary inflow and the same
    random state; the sole difference is whether d* is activated. Returns the positive
    log and the set of witness-eligible edges that owe their sedimenting execution in this
    episode to d* — i.e. executed with positive sediment on the positive branch and not so
    on the negative branch. This is the same local-counterfactual protocol the reach form
    uses; it is an executable intervention on the model, not an unobservable quantity."""
    if DSTAR not in du:
        # d* is not activated in this episode, so the counterfactual branch (same state,
        # same inflow, d* withdrawn) is the positive branch itself. Y_cf is therefore
        # determined without a second run, and its source link is the empty set — an
        # obtained-and-empty result, not a missing one.
        return f.run_episode(inp,du,**kw), frozenset()
    st=f.rng.getstate(); snap=copy.deepcopy(f)
    pos=f.run_episode(inp,du,**kw)
    neg_field=copy.deepcopy(snap); neg_field.rng.setstate(st)
    neg=neg_field.run_episode(inp,du-{DSTAR},**kw)
    link=set()
    for e in pos.executed:
        if pos.sedimented.get(e,0.0)>0 and not (e in neg.executed and neg.sedimented.get(e,0.0)>0):
            link.add(e)
    return pos,frozenset(link)

def record_complete(rec,n_expected):
    """Completeness precondition: exactly one record per episode actually run, each
    carrying the four fields the criterion reads — acts, executed, sedimented, and a
    source link that is present AND obtained (srclink is not None). Failing any of these,
    the criterion returns undecided rather than 0 (see the misjudgement bound on record
    integrity). An obtained-but-empty source link passes this check: it is a decided
    negative, not a missing observation."""
    if rec is None or len(rec)!=n_expected: return False
    return all(("acts" in r and "executed" in r and "sedimented" in r
                and "srclink" in r and r["srclink"] is not None)
               for r in rec)

def _run(base,mech,n=60):
    """Returns the field, the per-episode records, and the number of episodes actually
    run, so that the completeness check can compare counts rather than assume them."""
    inp,du,kw=MECHS[mech]; f=copy.deepcopy(base); rec=[]; ran=0
    for _ in range(n):
        log,link=_paired(f,inp,du,kw)
        rec.append(_episode_record(log,link)); ran+=1
        if f.law_check("N")[0]: break
    return f,rec,ran

DSTAR="dR"   # the one duty by which the carrier law P_R holds its Law status

def criterion(f,rec,n_expected,duty=DSTAR):
    """D_gen for the narrowed property P_gen^cf: the locally-counterfactually-discriminable
    direct-type existential source. Inputs are Y_traj (per-episode acts / executed edges /
    sediment increments), the terminal witness W from Y_end, and Y_cf (the per-episode
    source-link set produced by the paired re-run in _paired).

    The duty is fixed to d* for every run and never re-pointed at whatever duty happened
    to be active. Returns 1 / 0 / undecided / n/a:

    n/a       — N does not hold in the terminal state; source judgement not entered.
    undecided — the record is incomplete (episode count mismatch, or some episode record
                missing one of its four fields, source-link included). This says the
                judging conditions are insufficient; it does not name a third kind of
                generating fact.
    1         — some episode carries Act(d*) together with an edge e of W that is executed
                in that episode, sediments positively in it, AND lies in that episode's
                source-link set, i.e. without d* — same prior state, same ordinary inflow,
                same random state — e would not have executed-and-sedimented there.
    0         — the record is complete and no such episode exists.

    Scope: 0 means "no locally-counterfactually-discriminable source"; it does NOT mean
    the upstream general direct-type source is false. An event that still sediments without
    d*, only by a smaller amount, is outside this criterion's positive domain by design."""
    if not record_complete(rec,n_expected):
        return "undecided"      # incomplete record: never report 0
    ok,_,wit=f.law_check_detail("N")
    if not ok: return "n/a"
    W=set(map(tuple,wit)) if wit else set()
    if not W: return "n/a"
    # Joint event with source link: some episode carries the act of d* together with an
    # actual execution-with-sediment of SOME witness edge, AND that very sedimenting
    # execution is owed to d* — without d*, in the same prior state, same inflow and same
    # random state, that edge would not have executed-and-sedimented in that episode.
    # Co-occurrence alone is not enough: a mature shared edge such as the trunk sediments
    # in every episode regardless of d*, so it can be co-present with an act while the
    # edge that actually closes the witness is produced by something else entirely.
    joint=any(duty in r["acts"] and
              any(e in r["executed"] and r["sedimented"].get(e,0)>0 and e in r["srclink"]
                  for e in W)
              for r in rec)
    return 1 if joint else 0

def judge(n=30):
    """Two-way validity: the criterion on all four mechanisms."""
    out=defaultdict(Counter)
    for seed in range(n):
        base=M.build_model(seed); M.train_initial(base)
        for mech in MECHS:
            f,rec,ran=_run(base,mech)
            out[mech][str(criterion(f,rec,ran))]+=1   # d* fixed, never re-pointed
            out[mech]["LawN="+str(f.law_check("N")[0])]+=1
    return {k:dict(v) for k,v in out.items()}

def _y_end(f):
    """The full Y_end declared in the text: for every edge its depth and replay-queue
    length, for every node its accumulated depth, plus the law verdict and witness."""
    edges=tuple(sorted((e,round(st.g,9),len(st.rep)) for e,st in f.edges.items()))
    nodes=tuple(sorted((x,round(ns.h,9)) for x,ns in f.nodes.items()))
    ok,_,w=f.law_check_detail("N")
    wit=tuple(sorted(map(tuple,w))) if w else ()
    return (edges,nodes,ok,wit)

def terminal(n=20):
    """Y_end: is the whole terminal structure enough to tell the mechanisms apart?"""
    rows=defaultdict(list); wits=defaultdict(set)
    for seed in range(n):
        base=M.build_model(seed); M.train_initial(base)
        for mech in MECHS:
            f,_,_r=_run(base,mech)
            rows[mech].append(_y_end(f))
            _ok,_why,w=f.law_check_detail("N")
            if w: wits[mech].add(tuple(map(tuple,w)))
    same=lambda a,b: sum(1 for x,y in zip(rows[a],rows[b]) if x==y)
    e=lambda m,i: [x for x in rows[m][i][0] if x[0]==TARGET][0]
    return {"comparison":"full Y_end snapshot (all edges: depth+replay count; all nodes: "
                         "accumulated depth; law verdict; witness)",
            "witness_sets":{k:[list(map(list,w)) for w in v] for k,v in wits.items()},
            "counterhold_eq_nonbound":same("counterhold","nonbound"),
            "counterhold_eq_gate":same("counterhold","gate"),
            "counterhold_eq_edit":same("counterhold","edit"),
            "n":n,
            "sample_target_edge":{m:e(m,0) for m in MECHS}}

def indirect(n=30):
    """Single-variable control: act for k episodes, then withdraw ONLY the duty while the
    carrier law keeps its ordinary inflow, so the difference is duty activation alone."""
    c=Counter()
    for seed in range(n):
        base=M.build_model(seed); M.train_initial(base)
        for k0 in (6,8,10):
            f=copy.deepcopy(base); acts=0
            for _ in range(k0):
                acts+=len(f.run_episode({"s","hR"},{"dR"}).act_duties)
            c[(k0,"N_at_stop",f.law_check("N")[0])]+=1
            for _ in range(60-k0): f.run_episode({"s","hR"},set())   # withdraw only the duty
            c[(k0,"N_at_end",f.law_check("N")[0])]+=1
            c[(k0,"acts",acts)]+=1
    return {str(k):v for k,v in sorted(c.items(),key=str)}

def oracle(n=30):
    """Upstream identity cross-check. NOT an independent oracle for P_gen: it reads the
    same Y_traj records as the criterion. It verifies the identity side separately —
    that d* is the carrier law's bound Law-duty and that Principle(d*) holds in this run."""
    out=defaultdict(Counter)
    for seed in range(n):
        base=M.build_model(seed); M.train_initial(base)
        for mech in MECHS:
            f,rec,ran=_run(base,mech)
            ok,_,wit=f.law_check_detail("N"); W=set(map(tuple,wit)) if wit else set()
            joint=any(DSTAR in r["acts"] and any(e in r["executed"] and
                      r["sedimented"].get(e,0)>0 and e in r["srclink"] for e in W)
                      for r in rec)
            # Upstream identity cross-check, not an independent oracle: the criterion and
            # this check read the same trajectory records; what is verified here is that
            # the duty the criterion asks about is indeed the carrier's Law-duty and holds
            # Principle in this run, i.e. the identity side the criterion does not re-derive.
            out[mech]["joint="+str(bool(joint))]+=1
            out[mech]["identity_ok="+str(bool(f.principle_check(DSTAR) and
                       f.laws["P_R"].law_duty_name==DSTAR))]+=1
            out[mech]["crit="+str(criterion(f,rec,ran))]+=1
    return {k:dict(v) for k,v in out.items()}

def truncate(n=30):
    """Completeness branch, two independent failures evaluated separately.
    (a) count mismatch: drop the last record, keep n_expected — caught by the count test.
    (b) field loss with the count intact: replace one record by a field-short copy and
        pass the matching n_expected — caught by the field test alone, not by the count.
    (c) Y_cf never obtained: every record carries srclink=None — undecided.
    (d) Y_cf obtained and empty: every record carries srclink=set() — a decided negative,
        so the criterion returns 0, not undecided. (c) and (d) must differ.
    Each is reported with the branch that caught it, so neither result borrows the other."""
    c=Counter()
    for seed in range(n):
        base=M.build_model(seed); M.train_initial(base)
        f,rec,ran=_run(base,"counterhold")
        c["full="+str(criterion(f,rec,ran))]+=1

        nocf=[dict(r,srclink=None) for r in rec]
        c["c_cf_missing="+str(criterion(f,nocf,ran))]+=1
        empty=[dict(r,srclink=set()) for r in rec]
        c["d_cf_empty="+str(criterion(f,empty,ran))]+=1

        cut=rec[:-1]
        c["a_count_mismatch="+str(criterion(f,cut,ran))]+=1
        c["a_caught_by_count="+str(len(cut)!=ran)]+=1

        short=[{k:v for k,v in r.items() if k!="sedimented"} if i==0 else r
               for i,r in enumerate(rec)]
        c["b_field_dropped="+str(criterion(f,short,ran))]+=1
        c["b_count_ok="+str(len(short)==ran)]+=1
        c["b_caught_by_field="+str(not all(("acts" in r and "executed" in r
                                            and "sedimented" in r) for r in short))]+=1
    return dict(c)

MIXED={"dR_then_gate":({"s"},set(),{"external_gate":{"A":1.0}}),
       "dR_then_edit":({"s"},set(),{"external_edit":{"A":0.8}})}

def mixed(n=30):
    """Mixed-origin runs: one genuine act of d* in the first episode, then the whole
    generation carried by gate or edit. The trunk edge sediments in that first episode and
    belongs to the terminal witness, so bare co-occurrence would report 1; the source-link
    condition must bring it back to 0."""
    c=defaultdict(Counter)
    for seed in range(n):
        base=M.build_model(seed); M.train_initial(base)
        for nm,(inp,du,kw) in MIXED.items():
            f=copy.deepcopy(base); rec=[]; ran=0
            for k in range(60):
                if k==0: log,link=_paired(f,{"s","hR"},{DSTAR},{})
                else:    log,link=_paired(f,inp,du,kw)
                rec.append(_episode_record(log,link)); ran+=1
                if f.law_check("N")[0]: break
            W=set(map(tuple,f.law_check_detail("N")[2] or []))
            bare=any(DSTAR in r["acts"] and any(e in r["executed"] and
                     r["sedimented"].get(e,0)>0 for e in W) for r in rec)
            c[nm]["LawN="+str(f.law_check("N")[0])]+=1
            c[nm]["H="+str(ran)]+=1
            c[nm]["bare_cooccurrence="+str(bare)]+=1
            c[nm]["crit="+str(criterion(f,rec,ran))]+=1
    return {k:dict(v) for k,v in c.items()}

def partial(n=30):
    """Partial-source runs. Here d* genuinely causes the first sedimenting execution of
    the bypass edge (source link true on a witness edge), and only afterwards is the
    closure completed by gate. By the frozen upstream direct-type definition this IS
    P_gen: it asks for at least one act-caused sedimenting execution of a witness edge,
    not that the act carry the whole closure. Reported so the boundary is on record:
    the criterion returns 1 here, and that is correct against the declared property,
    not a false positive against it."""
    c=Counter()
    for seed in range(n):
        base=M.build_model(seed); M.train_initial(base)
        f=copy.deepcopy(base); rec=[]; k0=None
        for k in range(30):                      # run d* until it first causes the bypass
            log,link=_paired(f,{"s","hR"},{DSTAR},{})
            rec.append(_episode_record(log,link))
            if TARGET in link: k0=k+1; break
            if f.law_check("N")[0]: break
        if k0 is None: c["no_source_link_before_N"]+=1; continue
        for _ in range(60):                      # then hand the rest over to the gate
            log,link=_paired(f,{"s"},set(),{"external_gate":{"A":1.0}})
            rec.append(_episode_record(log,link))
            if f.law_check("N")[0]: break
        c["first_source_link_at="+str(k0)]+=1
        c["LawN="+str(f.law_check("N")[0])]+=1
        c["crit="+str(criterion(f,rec,len(rec)))]+=1
    return dict(c)

def quant(n=20):
    """Quantitative-difference census behind the narrowing bound. For every episode of a
    counterhold run up to the formation of N, the paired re-run is compared edge by edge:
    butfor      — positive branch sediments the edge, negative branch does not execute-and-
                  sediment it at all (this is what enters the source link);
    identical   — both branches sediment it by the same amount;
    quant_diff  — both branches sediment it, by different amounts. These lie outside the
                  positive domain of the criterion by design, and are the cost of the
                  narrowing declared in the text."""
    c=Counter(); ex=[]
    for seed in range(n):
        base=M.build_model(seed); M.train_initial(base)
        f=copy.deepcopy(base)
        for k in range(60):
            st=f.rng.getstate(); snap=copy.deepcopy(f)
            pos=f.run_episode({"s","hR"},{DSTAR})
            g=copy.deepcopy(snap); g.rng.setstate(st)
            neg=g.run_episode({"s","hR"},set())
            for e in pos.executed:
                p=pos.sedimented.get(e,0.0)
                q=neg.sedimented.get(e,0.0) if e in neg.executed else 0.0
                if p<=0: continue
                if q<=0: c["butfor"]+=1
                elif abs(p-q)<=1e-12: c["identical"]+=1
                else:
                    c["quant_diff"]+=1
                    if len(ex)<3: ex.append({"seed":seed,"episode":k+1,"edge":list(e),
                                             "pos":round(p,6),"neg":round(q,6)})
            if f.law_check("N")[0]: break
    return {"n_seeds":n,"counts":dict(c),"examples":ex}

                      "oracle":oracle,"truncate":truncate,"mixed":mixed,
                      "partial":partial,"quant":quant}[sys.argv[1]]()))
