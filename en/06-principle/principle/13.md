# The Source Form of Principle

## Section One　Purpose, Form-Kind, and Entry Conditions

### 1.1　Form-kind

This form-text is a Source Form, an auxiliary form standing as its own text. It does not ask what a Principle is, nor when it stands, when it crosses a boundary, whether it is stable, or how strong it is; it asks one thing only: of a Principle that has already come about, how did it come about?

### 1.2　The single irreducible task

Given a Principle that has passed for the first time from not standing to standing, to discern whence its coming about proceeded, to establish legal conditions of source bearing and source recognition, and to determine whether among them there is a source of this kind — one where the same established duty through which P became a Law acquires, within a legal Sedimentation-Field history, a counter-holding role it did not have before, so that Principle is born of Law.

Seven questions must be answered. Once Principle stands, can one infer from the terminal state alone how it came about? What history is qualified to be called a real source of coming-about? How are redundant, multi-route, and jointly borne sources to be handled? Where a legal replay is impossible, why can one not adjudicate "there is no source"? Under what conditions may one say that a Principle truly has a Law-born source? What relation of inclusion holds between a source in general and a Law-born source? In which cases can the source not be uniquely determined?

Not answered here: what a Principle is; the necessary and sufficient conditions of its qualification; when its boundary is crossed; whether it is stable; its strength and grading; whether it is acting; how a Principle generates a new Law; how a real source is to be inverted from finite observation; the probability of a source; and the construction of any concrete running base.

### 1.3　Dependence

It depends on the sealed The General Form of Principle v1.0, The Mathematical Form of Principle v1.0, The Necessity Form of Principle v1.0, The Boundary Form of Principle v1.0, The Construction Form of the Principle Running Base v1.0, and The Grading Form of Principle v1.0. It modifies no upstream definition, adds no ontological predicate, and adds no H. The protocol predicates set up within it serve the adjudication of source alone and do not enter the ontology.

The upstream same-duty requirement must above all be kept: a Principle must have CounterHoldRole borne by the same established duty through which P acquired its Law qualification. The following formulation never stands in this form-text — "P has a Law, and another duty counter-holds A, so P is a Principle." That is duty substitution, and it is not a Principle.

What is called in-field formation is throughout dependent upon the existing Sedimentation-Field and the dynamics of a legal running base; no abstract host is made anew here.

### 1.4　Entry conditions

The object of this form-text is not any Principle whatever that already stands, but one event of first coming-about. Freeze

　　Genesis\_τ(P; A, J),

whose standing requires

　　Principle\_{τ⁻}(P; A, J) = 0　and　Principle\_τ(P; A, J) = 1,

where τ is the boundary of Principle's first standing within the declared historical range. This form-text does not prove the Boundary Form again; it only cites this one event of first passing from 0 to 1 as the object of the study of source.

Where what is at issue is a standing again of the form 1 → 0 → 1, that belongs to recovery or re-entry and does not fall within the first genesis of this form-text's principal proposition. The source criteria here may equally be used for the source of a recovery, but a recovery must not be written as a first genesis.

## Section Two　Why the Terminal State Cannot Account for the Source

The standing of a Principle is a result. The same result may come by wholly different routes. If inferring the source backwards from the terminal state were permitted, then wherever Principle stands one could declare Law-born, and that declaration would bear no history at all.

This section therefore first excludes six classes of default pseudo-source. None is excluded for "insufficient evidence"; each is excluded because it is, in form, no source bearing at all.

E1　Terminal inference. LawBorn must not be inferred from Principle\_τ = 1. The same terminal state may come from different histories; see the two trajectories given in W-S1 of Section Thirteen.

E2　Temporal precedence. That d\* changed first and Principle stood after must not yield that d\* is the source. Precedence is a temporal relation, not a relation of bearing.

E3　Synchronous correlation. That d\* and Principle changed at the same time must not yield a source. Correlation, synchrony, and predictability can none of them stand in for the bearing of a coming-about.

E4　Pure A-side change passing for Law-born. If P and d\* have themselves formed no new counter-holding role, and it is only because A's holding-direction has changed that the original duty is now Anti relative to A, then this may be a source of the Principle's coming-about, but it cannot automatically be called LawBorn.

E5　Duty substitution. If the duty through which P became a Law and the duty bearing the counter-hold are not one and the same, that is, d\_Law ≠ d\_Anti, then it must not be called "the same duty through which P became a Law generating into a Principle."

E6　Discovery by observation passing for generation. If CounterHoldRole had long stood and was only measured later, that is a history of recognition, not a history of coming-about.

The six together give this form-text's basic stance: a source is not read off from a result, but borne out by a history.

## Section Three　The Source Protocol and Candidate History Units

### 3.1　The source protocol

One examination of source must freeze the source protocol Π\_src in advance, containing at least: the initial state; the rules of identity for P, A, J, and d\*; the candidate family of source history units ℋ^Π; the permitted external inputs; the dynamical rules of the host and of the Sedimentation-Field; the permitted operations of freezing, masking, and removal; and finally this one item, that the original Principle criterion is uniformly used in evaluation.

The candidate family of history units must be frozen before any source result is seen. To alter the granularity after the fact so as to manufacture a "minimal source" is to look at the answer and then draw the question.

Its finiteness must be frozen along with it:

　　\| ℋ^Π \| \< ∞.

This item is no technical convenience but the precondition of the minimality of Section Five. On a finite family, so long as some non-empty set is sufficient while the empty set is not, descent along proper subsets must halt in finitely many steps, so a sufficient subset minimal under inclusion must exist, and the non-emptiness of 𝒮\_src^Π is guaranteed. Were an infinite family permitted, one could construct this case: every sufficient set has a proper subset still sufficient, so that no set is minimal and 𝒮\_src^Π is empty — and that is not "there is no source," but only that the source is a distributed bearing with no minimal element. This form-text does not adjudicate that class, and it must not be misread as the source being zero.

What this form-text adjudicates, therefore, is one frozen finite source protocol. A continuous or unbounded history must first be divided, by protocol, into finitely many legal source units before it can enter here.

### 3.2　Prohibitions

The initial state must not be altered; the criteria of Principle or of Law must not be altered; other states must not be compensated directly so as to maintain the original trajectory; the consequences must not be patched up by hand after a history unit has been deleted; Principle = 1 must not be written in directly; and the history units must not be re-divided after the fact so as to obtain a desired result.

### 3.3　Protocol relativity

A source conclusion is by default written in a protocol-relative form:

　　Source^Π,　LawBorn^Π.

If several permitted source protocols give different source conclusions, only the protocol-relative result may be reported. Only where the conclusion holds unchanged across the whole class of permitted protocols may it be raised to a protocol-independent conclusion.

## Section Four　Legal Replay

### 4.1　Three-valued semantics

For a candidate history subset K ⊆ ℋ^Π define

　　Replayable\_Π(K) ∈ { 1, 0, ⊥ }.

The domain of definition contains the empty set. The empty set has here one definite and single role: it is background control, not a source candidate. Replayable\_Π(∅) asks whether the background control run itself — "retaining none of the declared source units" — is legal; Suff\_Π(∅) asks whether the frozen background itself already suffices for Principle to stand. Both must be evaluable — otherwise, when Section Five adjudicates the minimality of a singleton set, whose sole proper subset is precisely the empty set, minimality could not be adjudicated at all.

The hierarchy is thereby clear: Replayable and Suff are defined for every K ⊆ ℋ^Π, the empty set included; whereas 𝒮\_src^Π admits only non-empty K.

Replayable\_Π(K) = 1: there is a legal replay conforming to Π\_src — the same initial state, the same rules of identity, the same permitted inputs, the same dynamical laws; for source units not selected into K, only the freezing or masking the protocol permits is used; and the system is then let evolve of itself.

Replayable\_Π(K) = 0: such a counterfactual replay is itself illegal. For instance, it could be completed only by changing the host, changing an identity, changing a dynamical law, or changing the criterion of qualification.

Replayable\_Π(K) = ⊥: it ought in theory to be adjudicable, while the facts at hand do not suffice to confirm whether that replay is legal.

### 4.2　Unreplayable is not not-a-source

This item must be written out on its own:

　　cannot be legally replayed　≠　the counterfactual fails　≠　is not a source.

Replayable = 0 says that this protocol cannot answer that counterfactual; it does not say that the history deleted bears no source. A stretch of history may be unintervenable, irreversible, impossible to delete legally — and be precisely the real source. To read unreplayable as the source being zero is the misjudgement this form-text has most to guard against.

## Section Five　Sufficient Sources and Certified Minimal Sources

### 5.1　Sufficiency

Only after Replayable\_Π(K) = 1 may one evaluate

　　Suff\_Π(K) ∈ { 1, 0, ⊥ }.

If within that legal replay Principle = 0 at the starting point while Principle = 1 by the moment τ of actual first standing, then Suff\_Π(K) = 1.

It is not required here that Principle in the counterfactual also stand first at exactly τ: once some inhibiting factor is removed, it may stand earlier. What this form-text studies is the source of a qualification's coming-about, not the whole of the timing causes of the moment of its coming-about.

### 5.2　Certified minimality

Define Minimal\_Π(K) ∈ { 1, 0, ⊥ }.

If Suff\_Π(K) = 1, and for every proper subset K′ ⊊ K one already has Replayable\_Π(K′) = 1 and Suff\_Π(K′) = 0, then Minimal\_Π(K) = 1.

If there is some key proper subset whose Replayable is 0 or ⊥, then K must not be declared minimal, and only Minimal\_Π(K) = ⊥ may be recorded.

It must not be taken for granted that checking single-element deletions suffices. Unless there is some explicit condition of monotonicity or closure besides, the definitional layer must run through every proper subset.

5.3　The family of minimal sufficient source sets

Define

　　𝒮\_src^Π = { K \| K ≠ ∅ ∧ Suff\_Π(K) = 1 ∧ Minimal\_Π(K) = 1 }.

The requirement of non-emptiness cannot be dispensed with. If the empty set already suffices — that is, Suff\_Π(∅) = 1, the frozen background dynamics by itself already making Principle stand — then the declared candidate family of source units has failed to capture the real bearing of the coming-about, or the source lies within the frozen background conditions.

Two things must therefore be kept at once, and neither may swallow the other.

First, the empty set still must not enter 𝒮\_src^Π. A source with no bearer at all is not a source.

Second, the source at the event layer must not on that account be adjudicated 0. On the contrary, Suff\_Π(∅) = 1 itself proves the current candidate source domain incomplete — one cannot say on the one hand that the real source may lie hidden in the frozen background, and on the other that the candidate domain has been exhausted and so there is no source. The load-bearing background should then be divided back into ℋ^Π and the adjudication made afresh.

Hence this discipline:

　　Suff\_Π(∅) = 1　⇒　Source^Π = ⊥.

Not having been captured by the candidate domain is not the same as there being no source. The "unreplayable is not not-a-source" of Section Four and this are two shapes of one and the same discipline: the former says the protocol's power to delete is limited, the latter that its power to divide is limited, and neither constitutes evidence that the source is zero. The reference implementation of the appendix accordingly enumerates from singletons upward, and sets up a separate test verifying that where the background suffices, 𝒮\_src^Π is empty while both the event-layer source and source bearing stop at undecided.

This is the genuinely certifiable family of minimal sufficient source sets. Whatever this form-text calls a "source" hereafter is to be taken by it throughout.

Its non-emptiness is guaranteed by the finiteness of 3.1: if a non-empty sufficient set exists while the empty set is not sufficient, then on a finite family descent along proper subsets must halt in finitely many steps, and where it halts is a minimal sufficient source set. Within this form-text's working domain, therefore, "𝒮\_src^Π = ∅" can come only from a genuine absence of source or from insufficient exhaustion, and never from the lack of a minimal element.

### 5.4　The event-level source

Define

　　Source^Π(H; P, A, J) = 1　⟺　𝒮\_src^Π ≠ ∅.

Its three-valued boundary: if the relevant candidate source domain and the legal replays have both been exhausted while 𝒮\_src^Π = ∅, then Source^Π = 0; if exhaustion is insufficient, or a key replay is illegal or undecided, or Suff\_Π(∅) = 1, then Source^Π = ⊥.

The third case must be noted particularly: Suff\_Π(∅) = 1 directly negates the premise of exhaustion, so it falls not into the branch of 0 but into ⊥.

This predicate is where the two theorems of Section Nine come to rest, and it must not be passed over by leaning on the non-emptiness of 𝒮\_src^Π and nothing more.

## Section Six　Redundant, Necessary, and Multi-Route Sources

### 6.1　Source bearing

For a history unit h: if there exists a K ∈ 𝒮\_src^Π with h ∈ K, then SourceCarrier\_Π(h) = 1.

To adjudicate SourceCarrier\_Π(h) = 0, one must already have exhausted the relevant candidate source domain, the relevant legal replays, and all certifiable minimal sufficient source sets, and have confirmed that h belongs to none of them. Otherwise ⊥ is kept throughout.

In one sentence: not having found a source certificate is not the same as not being a source.

### 6.2　Necessary sources

An incidental definition: NecessarySource\_Π(h) = 1 if and only if the source family has been completely identified and

　　h ∈ ⋂\_{K ∈ 𝒮\_src^Π} K.

The premise "completely identified" cannot be dispensed with. If some key replay is illegal, or if the background itself already suffices, there may be minimal sufficient source sets not yet touched, and some one of them need not contain h. Necessity must not then be asserted from the portion already found; only ⊥ may be recorded. The reference implementation of the appendix accordingly returns undecided first in these two cases, and has tests verifying it.

This is only an incidental classification of source, not the principal proposition of this form-text.

### 6.3　Redundant sources

Let 𝒮\_src^Π = { { h₁ }, { h₂ } }. Then after deleting h₁, h₂ still suffices to generate Principle, and after deleting h₂, h₁ does likewise.

So "Principle still stands after a single deletion" cannot yield "h is not a source." This is measured in the reference implementation of the appendix: two units each constitute a minimal sufficient source set, both are source bearers, and neither is a necessary source.

Three classes are distinguished accordingly:

　　necessary source — belonging to every minimal sufficient source set;  
　　redundant but real source — belonging to at least one, but not to all;  
　　non-source concomitant — belonging to no completely identified minimal sufficient source set.

The adjudication of the third class requires particular care: it requires "completely identified," failing which one can only stop at ⊥.

## Section Seven　The Same-Duty Conditions of Coming-About for Law-Born Principle

A Principle's source in general permits several kinds of bearing: change on the P side, change on the A side, joint change, and even direct external writing. LawBorn is only one among them, and its minimal structure is fixed as the seven items below; that it is indeed a proper subclass of source in general is proved by the two theorems of Section Nine, and is not drawn upon in advance here.

G1　Prior Law qualification. There is a t₀ \< τ with Law\_{t₀}(P) = 1.

G2　The same law-conferring duty. There is a d\*, and it is just that duty through which P acquired its Law qualification at t₀.

G3　Carrying through the duty's identity. d*\_{t₀} ≡ d*\_τ. The duty's state, relations of action, and role are permitted to change; its identity is not permitted to be substituted.

G4　Law qualification still stands at the genesis end. Law\_τ(P) = 1, and the Law qualification at the genesis end is still borne by the same d*. It is not written here that Law = 1 throughout \[t₀, τ\] — that would be an unproved strengthening. The minimal requirement locks three points only: standing as a Law at the start, still standing as a Law at the end, and the same d* bearing at both.

G5　The relational qualification of counter-holding is newly arisen. At the source's starting point CounterHoldRole\_{t₀}(d*; P, A, J) = 0, while at the genesis end CounterHoldRole\_τ(d*; P, A, J) = 1.

G5 adjudicates only that this relational qualification has gone from 0 to 1; it does not adjudicate whether that change was brought about by the P side, the A side, or jointly. Source attribution is borne entirely by G6. A purely A-side coming-about may therefore perfectly well satisfy G5 — A's holding-direction has changed, and the same duty is thereby Anti relative to A — and still not be LawBorn, because G6 does not hold.

G6　The role-formation history of that same duty is a real source bearing. There exists a K ∈ 𝒮\_src^Π such that the role-formation history h\_{d*} of that same d* satisfies h\_{d\*} ∈ K.

By h\_{d*} is meant here: those history units, within the candidate family frozen by Π\_src, which bear d*'s own transition from having no counter-holding relational qualification to having one. It must be a history of that same d\* — not a history of some other duty of P, nor a history on the A side.

G7　That role formation must not be manufactured by a direct write of qualification. h\_{d\*} must be formed by permitted Sedimentation-Field or running-base dynamics — the permitted dynamics here are the host and Sedimentation-Field dynamical rules frozen as the fifth item of Π\_src, which depend upon The Construction Form of the Principle Running Base and are not made anew here. A direct write such as CounterHoldRole := 1, Principle := 1, DeliveryAnti := 1, or ResultAnti := 1 must not serve as the generative mechanism of LawBorn.

The division of labour between G5 and G6 must be seen clearly: G5 says only that this relational qualification is newly arisen; G6 says that behind that new arising there is a role-formation history of the same duty itself, and that this history really does bear the source. A qualification newly arisen is not a source borne — this is precisely the structural-layer counterpart of the synchronous correlation excluded by E3 of Section Two.

## Section Eight　The Law-Born Source Theorem

Theorem 8.1　Law-born source theorem

Suppose that for the first event Genesis\_τ(P; A, J) there exist a duty d\* and a source history H such that G1 through G7 all hold. Then

　　LawBornSource^Π(H; P, A, J) = 1.

The proof runs in two chains, conjoined at the end.

The structural chain. G4 gives Law\_τ(P) = 1; G5 gives CounterHoldRole\_τ(d*; P, A, J) = 1; and by G2 and G3 the d* here is just the same duty through which P acquired its Law qualification, its identity agreeing at both ends. Citing then the sufficient direction of the sealed Necessity Form of Principle,

　　Law\_τ(P) ∧ CounterHoldRole\_τ(d\*; P, A, J) ⇒ Principle\_τ(P; A, J),

gives Principle\_τ = 1.

What this step does must be made plain, lest it be read as redundant. Principle\_τ = 1 is given already by the entry conditions of Section One and needs no re-proof here; what the structural chain really establishes is the manner in which it is borne — that this Principle at τ is borne by the two items Law\_τ(P) and the CounterHoldRole\_τ of the same d*, and not by some other duty or some other route. Without this step, the h\_{d*} of G6 might be a stretch of history unrelated to the actual bearing at τ.

As to the firstness of τ, that too is given by the entry conditions — Genesis\_τ has already frozen τ as the boundary of first standing. It is not inferred here from G5 that "there was no counter-holding relational qualification before τ": G5 gives the values at the two moments t₀ and τ only, and cannot yield the whole open interval.

The source chain. By G6, the role-formation history h\_{d*} of that same d* belongs to some certified minimal sufficient source set K ∈ 𝒮\_src^Π. By Section Five, the sufficiency of K is given by legal replay and its minimality by the examination of every proper subset. So h\_{d\*} is not a synchronous concomitant but a real source bearing. And by G7, that history is formed by permitted dynamics and is not a direct write of qualification.

Conjoining the two chains gives LawBornSource^Π = 1. □

It must be recorded: the structural chain guarantees only that Principle stands at τ, and the source chain only that the history bears the source; lacking either, what is obtained is not LawBorn. A role newly arisen must not be identified directly with a source borne, nor the standing of a qualification directly with a source adjudicated.

## Section Nine　The Boundary Between Source in General and Law-Born Source

Theorem 9.1　The relation of inclusion

　　LawBornSource^Π ⇒ Source^Π.

Proof. By G6 there is a K ∈ 𝒮\_src^Π containing h\_{d\*}, so 𝒮\_src^Π is non-empty, and by Section Five that is Source^Π standing. □

Theorem 9.2　The converse does not hold

　　Source^Π ⇏ LawBornSource^Π.

Proved by two classes of counterexample.

First, pure A-side change. P and d\* form no new structure of their own; A's holding-direction changes, making the original duty Anti relative to A, and Principle goes from 0 to 1. That history constitutes a legal minimal sufficient source set, so Source^Π = 1. Here G5 may even hold — the counter-holding relational qualification does go from 0 to 1. What really fails is G6: taking h\_{d*} for the role-formation history of that same d*, there is no K ∈ 𝒮\_src^Π with h\_{d\*} ∈ K, because no such stretch of history bears the source at all. So LawBorn does not hold. This case is measured separately in the appendix.

Second, direct external writing. The qualification structure is written in directly and so stands, Principle goes from 0 to 1, and that history of writing likewise constitutes a minimal sufficient source set, so Source^Π = 1; but by G7 a direct write must not serve as the generative mechanism of LawBorn.

Both are measured in the reference implementation of the appendix, each giving Source = 1 while LawBorn = 0.

Corollary 9.3

　　Genesis\_τ ⇏ LawBornSource^Π.

That a Principle has come about is not that it was born of Law.

## Section Ten　Pure Law-Born, Co-Law-Born, and Other Source Types

The four labels below are only incidental classifications of source attribution. They constitute no grading and are extended no further downward.

PureLawBorn: there is a minimal sufficient source set all of whose members are the h\_{d*} of G6, that is, the in-field role-formation history of that same d* itself.

CoLawBorn: there is a minimal sufficient source set containing h\_{d*} as a real and undeletable source member, while that set does not stop at h\_{d*} — it must bear jointly with A-side change, legal inputs, or other joint histories besides.

ASource: there is a source route on which the pure A side already suffices.

DirectWriteSource: there is a minimal sufficient source route borne by a direct write of the qualification structure.

The first two are both subtypes of LawBorn, so

　　PureLawBorn ⇒ LawBorn,　CoLawBorn ⇒ LawBorn.

Where any one of G1 through G7 fails, both labels close together, and they must not be affixed merely because the shape of some source set happens to suit. The reference implementation of the appendix accordingly checks LawBorn before adjudicating either label.

The four are not required to be mutually exclusive. One and the same Genesis event may perfectly well have both LawBorn = 1 and ASource = 1 — which means it has two independently sufficient routes of coming-about. A source may have several attributions.

It must be stressed: Pure and Co are no difference of strength, only a difference of the structure of bearing. To read them as grades is to confound this form-text with The Grading Form of Principle.

## Section Eleven　Non-Uniqueness of Source and Protocol Relativity

The non-uniqueness of source has at least four kinds, which must be declared explicitly.

U1　Several minimal sufficient source sets. 𝒮\_src^Π may contain several K, each independently sufficient.

U2　Several source attributions may hold at once. LawBorn, ASource, and DirectWriteSource need not be mutually exclusive.

U3　PureLawBorn and CoLawBorn may hold at once by different source routes: one minimal sufficient source set consisting wholly of h\_{d\*}, another requiring joint bearing; their coexistence is no contradiction. A single label is not forced.

U4　Protocol relativity. Different permitted protocols may give different source decompositions, owing to differences in the division of history or in the definition of legal replay.

Source conclusions are therefore written by default as Source^Π and LawBorn^Π, and must not be quietly raised into absolute source conclusions. Only where invariance of the conclusion has been proved across the class of permitted protocols may the superscript be dropped.

## Section Twelve　Three-Valued Adjudication and the Boundary Between 0 and ⊥

This is one of the core matters this form-text must write out clearly.

### 12.1　When LawBorn = 1 may be adjudicated

The structural conditions G1 through G5 all hold — prior Law qualification, the same law-conferring duty, the duty's identity carried through, Law at the genesis end still borne by that duty, and the counter-holding relational qualification gone from 0 to 1; and there is at least one certified minimal sufficient source route on which the role-formation history h\_{d*} of that same d* is a real source bearing (G6), and is not a direct write of qualification (G7).

Lacking any one of the seven, 1 must not be adjudicated.

### 12.2　When LawBorn = 0 may be adjudicated

Two cases only.

First, structural exclusion: any one of G1 through G5 determinately fails — prior Law qualification does not hold; or the duty through which P became a Law and the bearer of the counter-hold are determinately not the same one; or the duty's identity is not carried through; or the Law at the genesis end is no longer borne by that duty; or the counter-holding relational qualification did not go from 0 to 1. Besides, one for which no legal same-duty source is possible at all under that protocol falls likewise under this branch.

Second, exclusion by exhaustion: the candidate source domain is frozen and sufficiently complete; every relevant legal replay has been exhausted; every certifiable minimal sufficient source set has been obtained; and none of them contains the role-formation history h\_{d\*} of the same law-conferring duty as a source bearing.

This branch has one further premise that must be written out: Suff\_Π(∅) = 0. If the background itself already suffices, then the completeness of the candidate domain has been negated and exhaustion cannot be spoken of at all; only ⊥ may then be recorded; see 5.3.

### 12.3　Everything else is ⊥

Three disciplines must be written out side by side, each stopping up one way of reading a limitation of the protocol as a fact in the field:

　　Replayable = 0 ⇏ LawBorn = 0 — that it cannot be deleted is not that it does not bear;  
　　Suff\_Π(∅) = 1 ⇏ Source = 0 — that it was not captured by the candidate domain is not that it does not exist;  
　　no source certificate found ⇏ not a source — that exhaustion is incomplete is not that it has been refuted.

Unintervenable, irreversible, impossible to delete legally, improperly divided, not yet fully examined — none of these is evidence of "no source."

## Section Thirteen　Contrasting Cases

The five cases below serve to draw boundaries, and each is measured in the reference implementation of the appendix.

W-S1　Same terminal state, different sources. History one: the same duty forms CounterHoldRole within the field. History two: direct external writing. Both finally give Principle = 1. So the terminal state cannot uniquely yield the source backwards. Measured: the former LawBorn = 1, the latter LawBorn = 0.

W-S2　Purely A-side coming-about. P and d\* form no new role; A's holding-direction changes; and finally Principle = 1. So Genesis ⇏ LawBorn. Measured: Source = 1, ASource = 1, LawBorn = 0.

W-S3　Redundant double source. K₁ = { h₁ } and K₂ = { h₂ } are two independently sufficient routes. So necessity under single deletion is no criterion of source. Measured: 𝒮\_src contains two singleton sets, both units are source bearers, and neither is a necessary source.

W-S4　Not legally replayable. The real history may have a source, while deleting the candidate history would break an identity or a legal dynamical rule. So unreplayable ⇏ not a source. Measured: Replayable = 0, while Suff and SourceCarrier both stop at ⊥ and do not fall to 0 with it.

W-S5　Duty substitution. d₁ makes P a Law, and thereafter d₂ counter-holds A. So d\_Law ≠ d\_Anti cannot constitute a positive instance of "the same law-conferring duty generating into a Principle." Measured: Source = 1, LawBorn = 0.

## Section Fourteen　Bounds of Misjudgement

K-SRC1　Where the entry conditions of Genesis are satisfied and G1 through G7 all hold, this form-text still cannot obtain the LawBorn it declares.

K-SRC2　The source theorem has depended on an undeclared external condition.

K-SRC3　A legal replay proves that the h\_{d\*} identified does not in fact bear the source role declared of it.

K-SRC4　A history of the pure A-side, of direct writing, or of duty substitution is misjudged as LawBorn.

K-SRC5　A redundant real source is wrongly adjudicated a non-source because "it still stands under single deletion."

K-SRC6　Being not legally replayable is interpreted directly as the source being 0.

## Section Fifteen　Completion Conditions, Interfaces, and Conclusion

### 15.1　Completion Conditions

　　CPL-SRC1　The object is confined to a first passage of Principle from 0 to 1, and Principle is not redefined.  
　　CPL-SRC2　Six classes of pseudo-source are excluded: terminal inference, temporal precedence, synchronous correlation, the pure A side, duty substitution, and discovery by observation.  
　　CPL-SRC3　Π\_src and the candidate family of history units are frozen, and the finiteness of that family is frozen with them, so that the existence of minimal sufficient source sets is guaranteed.  
　　CPL-SRC4　The three-valued semantics of Replayable, Suff, and Minimal is established, and an illegal replay is expressly not a non-source.  
　　CPL-SRC5　Multi-route, redundant, and distributed minimal sufficient source sets are supported; the empty set must not enter 𝒮\_src^Π.  
　　CPL-SRC5b　The event-level Source^Π is officially defined with its three-valued boundary given; where the empty set suffices, undecided is adjudicated and not 0.  
　　CPL-SRC5c　The domains of Replayable and Suff contain the empty set, the empty set serving as background control; 𝒮\_src^Π admits only non-empty sets.  
　　CPL-SRC6　It is proved that G1 through G7 entail LawBornSource^Π.  
　　CPL-SRC7　It is proved that LawBornSource^Π entails Source^Π, and proved by counterexample that the converse does not hold.  
　　CPL-SRC8　The multiplicity of solutions, the multiplicity of attributions, and protocol relativity are all explicitly declared.  
　　CPL-SRC9　The downstream tasks of the running base, reachability, boundary, stability, grading, a Principle generating a new Law, and inversion from finite observation are not borne.

### 15.2　Interfaces

To The Construction Form of the Principle Running Base: the in-field role formation spoken of here depends throughout on the running-base dynamics it has constructed; no host is made anew here.

To The Boundary Form of Principle: this form-text cites its first crossing as an object, and does not adjudicate the boundary again.

To The Stability Form of Principle and The Grading Form of Principle: a source adjudicates neither stability nor strength; nor the converse.

To the downstream tasks of a Principle generating a new Law, of inversion from observation, and the like: this form-text gives only the criteria of the source of a coming-about; it gives no method of inverting a real source from finite observation, and no probability of a source.

### 15.3　Conclusion

The fact that a Principle stands does not uniquely determine the source of its coming-about.

A real source must be borne by a history of coming-about under a legal source protocol, and cannot be inferred backwards from the terminal state, from correlation, or from temporal precedence.

One coming-about may have several minimal sufficient source sets; a source may be redundant, may be distributed, may have several attributions.

Not being legally replayable means only that the current protocol cannot answer that counterfactual; it does not mean that the history is not a source.

What is called Principle born of Law is no second definition of Principle's qualification, but a property of source: the same established duty through which P became a Law, while keeping its bearing of that Law identity, acquires through a legal Sedimentation-Field history a CounterHoldRole it did not have before, and that role-formation history h\_{d\*} really belongs to at least one certified minimal sufficient source route, and is not manufactured by a direct write of qualification.

Hence

　　LawBornSource^Π ⇒ Source^Π,  
　　Source^Π ⇏ LawBornSource^Π,  
　　Principle = 1 ⇏ LawBornSource^Π = 1.

That a Principle stands is not that it was born of Law; that it was born of Law is a thing that must be borne out by a history.

"""Minimal finite model for the source certificates of the Principle-origin form.

It carries no ontological proof. On a finite reference model it only checks that the
source-certificate system behaves as the text states: redundant sources, several minimal
sufficient source sets, the separation of illegal replay from non-source, the pure and
cooperative law-born types, and the two directions between Source and LawBorn.

Every history unit below is an opaque token. The model never writes Principle directly;
it evaluates the frozen Principle criterion on whatever the replay produces.
"""
from itertools import combinations
from enum import Enum


class Tri(Enum):
    TRUE = 1
    FALSE = 0
    UNDEC = "⊥"

    def __repr__(self):
        return {1: "1", 0: "0", "⊥": "⊥"}[self.value]


class Scenario:
    """One frozen source protocol Π_src over one Genesis event.

    units          — the frozen candidate history-unit family ℋ^Π, required to be finite:
                     over a finite family, a non-empty sufficient set always contains a
                     minimal sufficient one, so 𝒮_src^Π cannot be empty for the wrong
                     reason. Continuous or unbounded histories must first be cut into
                     finitely many legal source units by the protocol.
    law_at         — for each unit, whether it carries Law(P) at the source origin.
    role_units     — units that are role-formation history of the SAME duty d* that
                     carries Law(P); these are the only candidates for a law-born path.
    direct_write   — units that obtain the qualification by writing it in rather than by
                     admissible field dynamics; such a unit can be a Source but never
                     grounds LawBorn.
    a_side         — units that are purely A-side changes; likewise never LawBorn.
    unreplayable   — subsets whose removal cannot be legally replayed at all.
    holds          — the frozen Principle criterion, evaluated on the surviving units.
    Besides the history data, three structural inputs are carried explicitly so that the
    law-born test encodes G1–G7 rather than only the source-certificate part:
    duty_persists (G3), law_at_tau (G4) and role_novel (G5). They are supplied by the
    upstream forms; the model does not derive them.
    """
    def __init__(self, units, holds, role_units=frozenset(), direct_write=frozenset(),
                 a_side=frozenset(), unreplayable=(), prior_law=True, same_duty=True,
                 duty_persists=True, law_at_tau=True, role_novel=True):
        self.units = frozenset(units)
        self.holds = holds
        self.role_units = frozenset(role_units)
        self.direct_write = frozenset(direct_write)
        self.a_side = frozenset(a_side)
        self.unreplayable = {frozenset(s) for s in unreplayable}
        self.prior_law = prior_law            # G1 Law holds at the source origin
        self.same_duty = same_duty            # G2 that Law is carried by d*
        self.duty_persists = duty_persists    # G3 the duty identity is carried through
        self.law_at_tau = law_at_tau          # G4 Law still holds at τ, borne by the same d*
        self.role_novel = role_novel          # G5 CounterHoldRole goes 0 → 1

    # ---- replay ---------------------------------------------------------------
    def replayable(self, K):
        """Whether removing everything outside K admits a legal replay.

        Defined for EVERY K ⊆ ℋ^Π, the empty set included: Replayable(∅) asks whether the
        background control run — no declared source unit retained — is itself legal. The
        empty set is a control input, not a source candidate; only 𝒮_src^Π excludes it.

        An illegal replay is a fact about the protocol, not about the field: it says the
        counterfactual cannot be run here, never that the removed history is no source.
        """
        return Tri.FALSE if frozenset(K) in self.unreplayable else Tri.TRUE

    def suff(self, K):
        """Source sufficiency of K: the replayed run still reaches Principle = 1.

        Also defined for the empty set, where it asks whether the frozen background alone
        already yields Principle = 1. Only asked after the replay is legal; an illegal
        replay leaves this undecided.
        """
        if self.replayable(K) is not Tri.TRUE:
            return Tri.UNDEC
        return Tri.TRUE if self.holds(frozenset(K)) else Tri.FALSE

    def minimal(self, K):
        """Certifiable minimality: every proper subset is legally replayable and insufficient.

        If some proper subset cannot be legally replayed, minimality is undecided — it is
        not declared, and it is not denied. Single-element deletion is never enough.
        """
        K = frozenset(K)
        if self.suff(K) is not Tri.TRUE:
            return Tri.FALSE if self.suff(K) is Tri.FALSE else Tri.UNDEC
        undecided = False
        for r in range(len(K)):
            for sub in combinations(sorted(K), r):
                sub = frozenset(sub)
                if self.replayable(sub) is not Tri.TRUE:
                    undecided = True
                    continue
                if self.suff(sub) is Tri.TRUE:
                    return Tri.FALSE
        return Tri.UNDEC if undecided else Tri.TRUE

    def empty_suffices(self):
        """Whether the frozen background control alone already yields Principle = 1.

        If it does, the declared candidate family has failed to capture the bearing
        history, or the source lies inside the frozen background conditions. Two things
        follow, and they must not be confused:

          the empty set still never enters 𝒮_src^Π — a source with no carrier is no source;
          but the event-level verdict cannot be 0 either, because the very fact that the
          background suffices shows the candidate domain is incomplete. Exhaustion has not
          been established, so Source^Π stays undecided and the protocol must be rebuilt
          with the bearing background split back into ℋ^Π.

        Not captured by the candidate domain is not the same as no source at all.
        """
        return self.holds(frozenset())

    def minimal_sets(self):
        """𝒮_src^Π: all certifiable minimal sufficient source sets. Non-empty only."""
        out = []
        for r in range(1, len(self.units) + 1):
            for K in combinations(sorted(self.units), r):
                K = frozenset(K)
                if self.suff(K) is Tri.TRUE and self.minimal(K) is Tri.TRUE:
                    out.append(K)
        return out

    # ---- source carriage ------------------------------------------------------
    def source_carrier(self, h):
        """Whether h belongs to some certifiable minimal sufficient source set.

        A negative verdict requires the candidate domain to be exhausted; where some
        relevant replay is illegal, the verdict stays undecided rather than 0.
        """
        sets = self.minimal_sets()
        if any(h in K for K in sets):
            return Tri.TRUE
        if self.empty_suffices() or self.unreplayable:
            return Tri.UNDEC          # exhaustion not established
        return Tri.FALSE

    def necessary_source(self, h):
        """h lies in every certifiable minimal sufficient source set. A side label only.

        The text requires the source family to be COMPLETELY identified first. Where some
        relevant replay is illegal, or the background alone suffices, further minimal sets
        may exist that were never reachable, and one of them might omit h. In that case the
        label stays undecided rather than claiming necessity from a partial family.
        """
        sets = self.minimal_sets()
        if not sets:
            return Tri.UNDEC
        if self.empty_suffices() or self.unreplayable:
            return Tri.UNDEC          # family not completely identified
        return Tri.TRUE if all(h in K for K in sets) else Tri.FALSE

    # ---- source types ---------------------------------------------------------
    def is_source(self):
        """Source^Π at the event level: 𝒮_src^Π is non-empty.

        0 requires the candidate domain and the relevant replays to be exhausted; where
        exhaustion is not established, the verdict stays undecided rather than 0.
        """
        if self.minimal_sets():
            return Tri.TRUE
        if self.empty_suffices():
            return Tri.UNDEC      # candidate domain incomplete, not an absence of source
        return Tri.UNDEC if self.unreplayable else Tri.FALSE

    def law_born(self):
        """LawBorn: G1–G7.

        G1–G5 are the structural conditions supplied above: prior Law, the same duty
        carrying it, that duty's identity carried through, Law still standing at τ borne by
        the same duty, and the counter-hold relation going 0 → 1. G6 requires that duty's
        own role-formation history to sit inside some certifiable minimal sufficient source
        set; G7 requires that history not to be a direct qualification write.

        Note G5 only records that the relation changed from 0 to 1; it says nothing about
        which side caused the change. Attribution is G6's job, which is exactly why a pure
        A-side genesis can satisfy G5 and still fail LawBorn.
        """
        if not (self.prior_law and self.same_duty and self.duty_persists
                and self.law_at_tau and self.role_novel):
            return Tri.FALSE
        sets = self.minimal_sets()
        if not sets:
            return Tri.UNDEC if (self.empty_suffices() or self.unreplayable) else Tri.FALSE
        ok = any(any(h in self.role_units and h not in self.direct_write for h in K)
                 for K in sets)
        if ok:
            return Tri.TRUE
        return Tri.UNDEC if (self.empty_suffices() or self.unreplayable) else Tri.FALSE

    def pure_law_born(self):
        """Some minimal sufficient set whose whole bearing source is that duty's own history.

        PureLawBorn ⇒ LawBorn, so the structural conditions are checked first.
        """
        if self.law_born() is not Tri.TRUE:
            return Tri.FALSE
        return Tri.TRUE if any(K and K <= self.role_units and not (K & self.direct_write)
                               for K in self.minimal_sets()) else Tri.FALSE

    def co_law_born(self):
        """Some minimal sufficient set where the duty's history is an undeletable member
        but must be carried together with A-side or other legal history.

        CoLawBorn ⇒ LawBorn, so the structural conditions are checked first.
        """
        if self.law_born() is not Tri.TRUE:
            return Tri.FALSE
        for K in self.minimal_sets():
            if (K & self.role_units) and not (K <= self.role_units):
                return Tri.TRUE
        return Tri.FALSE

    def a_source(self):
        """Some minimal sufficient set lying wholly on the A side."""
        return Tri.TRUE if any(K and K <= self.a_side for K in self.minimal_sets()) else Tri.FALSE

    def direct_write_source(self):
        """Some minimal sufficient set lying wholly in direct qualification writes."""
        return Tri.TRUE if any(K and K <= self.direct_write
                               for K in self.minimal_sets()) else Tri.FALSE
