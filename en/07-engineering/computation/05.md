# The General Form of Generative Computation

This chapter attempts a general proposition:

For a run segment σ on a computing host P that bears a one-step task τ\_Φ to be a stretch of generative computation, what must it minimally have?

*The General Form of Computational Bearing* gave what a host must minimally have in order to bear a mathematical task, and *The General Form of Computational Execution* gave what one actual execution must minimally have. Both treat rule computation and generative computation alike: for the program in a game in which a ball bounces off a wall, bearing and executing each of its steps satisfies all the structure of both chapters. Here we ask about something else: by virtue of what is a stretch of step-by-step computation not merely rules running, but a computation in which dynamical conditions have entered the transformation from the current state to the next — in which the potential of history's Continuation takes part in the transformation?

Generative computation is not the generation of laws, nor is it the Sedimentation-Field itself. Generative computation drives the dynamics of a Sedimentation-Field, and laws are generated by the field's dynamics; a Sedimentation-Field can also be realized in a non-computing host.

How far this question is answered must be stated in advance. First edition. The initial question on entering the proof is this: on bearing and execution, the stipulations of generative computation, the upstream results already laid down on fields and laws, and the non-degeneracy condition alone, does "σ is a stretch of generative computation" already hold? The subquestions actually generated in the course of the inquiry, and their standing, are as follows ("proved" always means the necessary direction of the item as a necessary condition of GenComp, not that the item yields generative computation):  
First: on bearing a one-step task alone, does generative computation already hold? It does not; Step is a necessary condition, proved relative to G1 and the working domain, its two clauses of Ex carrying Computational Execution General Form-H3; whether continuously free-running processes fall within the working domain belongs to H1 (Section Two).  
Second: given Step, does it hold? It does not; the form "D enters the generation of the next structure" is forced by G2 (Section Three), and superseded by the Gov of Section Four.  
Third: once D is read in, is it already governing generation? It is not; Gov is a necessary condition, conditional on H2 (Section Four).  
Fourth: given Gov, does it hold? It does not; Sed is a necessary condition, proved, the clause of Sed(i) on persistent field states carrying C0 of *The General Form of Law* v2.5, and Sed(ii), for fields realized by deployment coupling, carrying H3 (Section Five).  
Fifth: given Sed, does it hold? It does not; ¬Intr is a necessary condition, proved (Section Six).  
Sixth: given ¬Intr, does it hold? It does not; Carry is a necessary condition, proved (Section Seven).  
Seventh: given Carry, does it hold? It does not; Hold is a necessary condition, proved (Section Eight).  
Eighth: given Hold, does it hold? It does not; Multi is a necessary condition, proved; its independent indispensability is conditional on Computational Execution General Form-H2 (Section Nine).  
Ninth: must proposed additions be set up separately? Seven items (updating D at every step, hold-breaking at every step, generating a law in every segment, correctness of the result, D coming from the history of this instance, the initial value of D in the segment being formed by actual Continuation, observation of the read-out) are excluded by the standard positive cases, and are proved to need no separate item; the two items "every step is governed by potential" and "all the duties of the field are realized by P's computation" are not established as necessary conditions, and whether they are necessary is unanswered (U3, U4) (Section Ten).  
General conclusion: the necessary direction only; whether the seven items yield generative computation (U1), restricted minimality among admissible candidate structures (U2), and the step-by-step strength of Gov and qualification over the whole segment (U3) are unanswered.

In this chapter we shall find, through persistent questioning, where the proof must go, and derive the general form of generative computation.

Our objective is:

taking bearing and execution as antecedents, to obtain the minimal necessary structure of a stretch of generative computation, and to give a proof of minimality and the ways of breach; to make this structure accommodate a network in training, a network frozen after training, sedimentation moved into another instance, initial values of a qualified field written in by hand, and generative computation with distorted results; and to separate these from rule programs, logs that only record, caches that only change the route, parameters given by a schedule, constants written into the rules, parameters that carry only the name of sedimentation, bare rewriting within the segment, externally inserted evaluation, external custody, shadow copies and relay splicing.

The result first.

The general form of the minimal structure of generative computation

H2 ∧ GenComp(P, σ) ⇒ ∃π ∃r: Step ∧ Carry ∧ Gov ∧ Sed ∧ ¬Intr ∧ Hold ∧ Multi

The part not depending on H2: GenComp(P, σ) ⇒ ∃π ∃r: Step ∧ Carry ∧ Sed ∧ ¬Intr ∧ Hold ∧ Multi

In both formulas, the two clauses of Ex contained in Step carry Computational Execution General Form-H3, the clause of Sed(i) on persistent field states carries C0 of *The General Form of Law* v2.5, and Sed(ii), for the deployment-coupling kind, carries H3; the determination of each item carries H1, Computational Execution General Form-H1–H3 and Computational Bearing General Form-H1–H3.

And:

π is a legitimate, individuable step partition of σ, and r is one run on one and the same instance of P;  
Gov: ∃k ∈ {1, …, n}, D ∈ ActArg^μ(x′\_k) (D as a composite argument);  
Sed: FieldQualified(F) ∧ D is a persistent field state of F ∧ in the declared system the sedimentary duties of F take D as their object ∧ σ is a legitimate trajectory segment of F;  
π covers σ (each step, each junction and the final segment); Step, Carry, ¬Intr and Hold are stated relative to the steps and junctions of π (including the final segment), and Multi requires the whole of σ to belong to r and to contain at least two steps.

That is: σ can be divided into steps, each of which is an execution of τ\_Φ by P; between steps, the structure and the dynamical conditions are held by P itself; under H2, at one step at least, the dynamical condition D really enters the generation of the next structure; D is the persistent field state of a Sedimentation-Field that has obtained full field qualification, changes within the segment only by the field's protocol, and every update the protocol requires actually takes place; between steps no process outside the boundary inserts evaluation; what each step writes out is exactly what the next step reads, and what the last step writes out is exactly the final state; the whole segment belongs to one and the same run.

In One Sentence

Execution says "this step really was computed"; generative computation says "in the step that was computed, history was pushing it on."

Abstract

*The General Form of Computational Execution* has already shown that, when a host executes, on one occasion, a task it bears, there must be a run in which the task's initiation event occurs, the host operates under the enabling conditions without proxy evaluation, the transforming part of the borne task — for a non-trivial task, together with the data-carrying part — is set going by this initiation, and a read-out is obtained at the read-out condition; the items are witnessed by one and the same run (the two clauses of Ex conditional on Computational Execution General Form-H3). Rule computation satisfies all of this. So the question presses on: what more does a stretch of generative computation require?

A host whose clock steps as usual while its arithmetic unit is not enabled and whose state is changed by drift of the medium is not computing; so every step must be an execution (Step). A rule program executes step by step for any number of steps with no quantity entering the transformation in the capacity of potential; a log that is read in and merely stored as it is, and a cache that only changes the route, are read without entering generation; so, under H2, the dynamical condition D must be a distribution-bearing argument of the generation kernel of the next structure (Gov). An annealing temperature lowered by the clock, constants written into the rules, and parameters that carry the name of sedimentation operators while those operators behave in no sedimentary way, all govern generation, yet are not potential; for a sedimentary quantity rewritten bare within the segment, what is read after the point of rewriting is an implanted setting; sedimentation declared as switched on writes into a copy, and the field state is not carried into the successor the protocol requires; so D must be the persistent field state of a Sedimentation-Field that has obtained full field qualification, changing within the segment by the field's protocol (Sed). Shallowing at a junction evaluated on the host's behalf by a machine outside the boundary shows that no external evaluation may be inserted at junctions (¬Intr). A case in which each step is executed fully on the host but the state is handed to external storage for custody between steps shows that the host must hold its state between steps (Carry). An update of the structure written into a copy, or a junction at which the value is changed back to a preset value, shows that what is changed must be carried over (Hold). Steps relayed one by one by separately initiated runs show that the run must be the same (Multi). Updating D at every step, hold-breaking at every step, generating a law in every segment, correctness of the result, D coming from the history of this instance, the initial value of D in the segment being formed by actual Continuation, and observation of the read-out are each tested and found not to require a separate item.

The core conclusion is this: the general form of execution says how a step is made; the general form of generative computation says what more the steps so made must at least have in order to be a stretch of computation that history is driving.

Introduction

To say that a machine "is generating" can mean several different things: it goes on computing step by step according to rules; each step it computes is swayed by the history it has accumulated; computing in this way, it drives the dynamics of a Sedimentation-Field; and that field grows laws in its dynamics. The first is rule computation, the second is generative computation, the third is the layer in which generative computation stands, and the fourth is a matter for the field.

Older ways of speaking often recognize generation by the number of steps, by changing parameters or by the sensitivity of results to history. Yet a rule program that runs ten thousand steps is still rules running; an annealing temperature following a schedule keeps changing, yet does not change with the Continuation that actually occurs; a lookup table compiled from run logs is sensitive to history, yet history in it is only an argument of the table-compiling program. On the other hand, a network frozen after training does not move a single parameter during inference, yet each of its steps is still swayed by the Familiar Routes pressed out by training.

The minimal content of generative computation lies between these two groups of cases.

Preliminary Distinctions

Generative computation and the Sedimentation-Field. A Sedimentation-Field can be realized in a non-computing host: the dynamics of the brain's Sedimentation-Field is driven by nutrition and ionic permeability, and has nothing to do with computation. Generative computation is the kind of computation that drives the dynamics of a Sedimentation-Field; the object asked about is computation, not the field.

Generative computation and the generation of laws. Generative computation stands in the driving layer: it first drives the dynamics of the field, and the field's dynamics then generates laws and Imagery. A stretch of generative computation need not generate a new law, and computation makes no direct contribution to the result of a law either.

Two senses of "Principle." *Inner Chapter 9, The Experience-Field*, says: "Computer — Principle; within it, no Law. Transformer Sedimentation-Field — Law; within it, no Principle." Principle here is the formal running of program logic, and belongs to rule transformation. There is another sense of Principle: according to Chapter 1 of *The Principle Group-Form*, *The General Form of Principle* v1.0, a Principle is a law already formed, whose formed duty forms a counter-hold against the holding-tendency of another law; it is a duty of sedimentary bearing in the field, and can take part in dynamical transformation as a potential for hold-breaking. The former is not a dynamical condition; the latter can be.

A step of execution and a step of the target law. In generative computation, what the host executes is one step of the field's transformation Φ: it reads the current structure and the dynamical condition, rewrites the structure, and may update the dynamical condition. How the target law "goes at the next step" is not prescribed directly by the rules of Φ, but given by the dynamical condition through Φ; in rule computation, the rules of the task are the node rules of the target law.

Changing parameters and carrying potential. An annealing temperature following a schedule, or a learning rate following a schedule, is updated, yet does not change with the Continuation that actually occurs; changing is not the same as carrying the potential of Continuation.

The qualification of potential is current. Theorem Two of *The Necessity Form of Law* v1.3 has proved that the current qualification of a law lies only in the two levels of sedimentary formation and stable holding-tendency, while the history of formation belongs to formation and not to current qualification. The qualification of potential likewise depends on where it currently is and what it currently does, not on how its current value came about.

Terms such as "stable holding-tendency" and "freezing" are used from here on as defined upstream; "potential" means the potential of holding or of counter-holding possessed by the sedimentary bearing organizations in a Sedimentation-Field, not any quantity whatever that can sway the next step. "Continuation" has two senses: the field's Continuation, in which the field goes on along laws already present (the sense of the potential of Continuation, the Continuation kernel and the Continuation strength κ); and the continuation of a run, in which one and the same run proceeds from its held state after a pause, a checkpoint recovery or a migration (the usage of *The General Form of Computational Execution*).

Fourth, the stipulations of generative computation. GenComp(P, σ) means "σ is a stretch of generative computation on P," delimited by the following six stipulations, relative to which the necessity below holds:  
　G1 (being computation)　The one-step transformation of the extended state in σ — reading the current structure, the dynamical condition and the input, and giving the successor of the extended state — is completed by P's execution of the task it bears, each one-step transformation being completed at the read-out of that execution; it is not a physical change of P's medium while P is not executing. What G1 quantifies over is only one-step transformations; evolution of state brought about between steps by the field protocol within the same run (shallowing, freezing and other operations at junctions) is not a one-step transformation and is not within the quantification of G1; who evaluates it, and whether it accords with the protocol, are constrained by G4 and by G2 and G3 respectively.  
　G2 (dynamical transformation)　σ is a stretch of dynamical transformation: dynamical conditions have entered the transformation from the current state to the next, and the potential of history's Continuation formed by the current structures and relations — the tendency to continue along laws already present, or the potential for hold-breaking of a Principle already formed in the field — requires the next step to go this way; history becomes dynamics.  
　G3 (holding)　Computation realizes structures, relations and dynamical conditions in the host, transforms the current structures and relations, and holds the structures and relations as changed by the dynamical conditions.  
　G4 (non-intrusion)　Inserting additional computation into nodes from outside the history is intrusion, and does not belong to the dynamics of generative computation.  
　G5 (multiple steps)　Generative computation is multi-step computation driven by dynamical conditions, and these steps are steps of one and the same computation.  
　G6 (layer)　Computation acts on the structures, relations and dynamical conditions of the field, standing in the driving layer; the target law is generated by the field's dynamics, and computation makes no direct contribution to its result.

Two

On these alone, does "σ is a stretch of generative computation" already hold?

Take a neuromorphic chip that bears τ\_Φ: it reads the current activations and weights, gives the next activations, and deepens the connections passed through; τ\_Φ is declared as clock-driven, each step initiated by a clock edge and ending with a register latch. In σ the clock steps and the registers latch as usual; but the enable of the arithmetic units has been disconnected, and the chip does no evaluation of τ\_Φ at all. Its analogue memory cells, however, drift physically: cells repeatedly passed through by current rise slightly in conductance, idle ones fall slightly, activations diffuse between cells with the residual currents, and what each clock edge latches is the result of the drift. This change of the medium looks like the Continuation and sedimentation of a Sedimentation-Field, and may even agree with the result of some execution.

This is not generative computation: the transformation in σ is not P's execution but a physical change of P's medium while P is not executing, contrary to G1; it is the dynamics of a field in a non-computing medium, of the same kind as the dynamics of the brain's Sedimentation-Field. What is currently known does not exclude it: bearing says only that P can do τ\_Φ, and the working domain only restricts how executions, if any, are arranged; neither says whether the change in σ is P's doing.

By G1, the transformations in σ must be completed by P's executions; these transformations are one transformation after another, so each must be one execution. By *The General Form of Computational Execution*, an execution has initiation, operation and read-out. So σ must be read as step after step, and it must be required that there is an execution at each step.

Definition One: step partition  
A step partition π of σ divides σ into successive steps 1, …, n: step k begins at an initiation event s\_k and ends at a write event e\_k; the interval J\_k = (e\_k, s\_{k+1}) between adjacent steps (k = 1, …, n−1) is called a junction; the interval J\_n = (e\_n, t\_end\] from the last step to the declared end point t\_end of σ is called the final segment, also counted as a junction, and s\_{n+1} ≔ t\_end is written. σ runs from s\_1 to t\_end, and π covers σ: σ = \[s\_1, e\_1\] ∪ J\_1 ∪ \[s\_2, e\_2\] ∪ … ∪ \[s\_n, e\_n\] ∪ J\_n; every moment of σ lies either within some step or within some junction (including the final segment), and no part is unassigned; a junction may be empty (the next step being initiated at the moment of the previous step's read-out), adjacent parts may share end points, and at an end point the read-out event belongs to the earlier step and the initiation event to the later. Every statement about junctions takes k = 1, …, n; for the final segment, "the state carrier and initial state of step k+1" is read as the state on the final-state carrier at t\_end; only "the initiation of step k+1" is restricted to k = 1, …, n−1. π is legitimate and individuable if and only if π covers σ as above, each s\_k is issued by P's own control (a clock edge, the issuing of an instruction, one iteration of the running loop) or by the declared protocol, rather than being an analytic moment marked out by an observer, and each e\_k is the event at which the result of step k is written or latched into P's output carrier (see below). Step-by-step declaration: the input reading scheme of τ\_Φ reads the (x, D) held at the head of the step together with u, the output reading scheme reads the (x′, D′) written at e\_k, and the read-out condition is e\_k. Declared carriers are of two kinds, both declared component by component before σ: the state carrier In\_k is the position from which step k reads (x, D) at its head, and the output carrier Out\_k is the position into which (x′, D′) is written at e\_k; together they are called declared carriers Car, identified by physical position, and a position obtained by storage-only or transfer-only copying belongs to one carrier lineage with the original position. Whether Out\_k and In\_{k+1} join up component by component is not asked here.

Step (step-by-step execution): σ has a legitimate, individuable step partition π such that, under step-by-step declaration, each step of π satisfies the Ini, Run (En, ¬Sub, Ex\_α, Ex\_β) and Out of *The General Form of Computational Execution*.

Its necessity is shown in three stages. (1) The unit of execution: by G1, each one-step transformation of the extended state in σ is completed by one execution, by P, of the task it bears, and is completed at that execution's read-out; by the task-side declaration, the task that takes the extended state as operand and its successor as result is τ\_Φ; so each one-step transformation of the extended state in σ is one complete execution of τ\_Φ by P, protocol operations at junctions not being among them. (2) The step partition: by the working domain, these executions follow one another without overlapping; taking the initiation event of each execution as s\_k and its read-out event as e\_k gives a step partition π of σ whose steps are exactly these executions. σ begins at the initiation of the first execution (task-side declaration); between executions are junctions, and after the last execution up to t\_end is the final segment, so π covers σ; the final segment, like the internal junctions, is subject to the determinations of Carry, ¬Intr, Hold and Sed(iii). The initiation event of an execution belongs to the declared protocol or to P's own dynamics (the Ini of *The General Form of Computational Execution*), and the read-out event under step-by-step declaration is the writing of the result into the output carrier (Out), so π is legitimate and individuable. (3) Obtaining at each step: the general theorem of *The General Form of Computational Execution* gives Ini, Run and Out on each execution (the two clauses of Ex conditional on Computational Execution General Form-H3), that is, on each step of π.  
Note that π is taken from the initiations and read-outs of the executions themselves, not from control cycles. When one execution of τ\_Φ spans several control cycles, a legitimate step partition drawn by control cycles does not satisfy Step, but this does not affect the satisfaction of Step by the π delimited by the executions. The working domain and the task-side declaration give only the arrangement of executions and the unit of a step; they contain neither Ini, Run nor Out, and do not presuppose Step.

Lemma One (step-partition lemma)　Suppose that P runs the declared update dynamics in one and the same continuous stretch of process, that operation proceeds only under the enabling conditions and the state within a step is held by P, and that each update is initiated by an event issued by P's own control and ends with a write or latch; if these events are taken as the boundaries of steps and the declaration is step by step, then π is legitimate and individuable; if the update is borne by P itself without proxy evaluation, and its transforming part is driven by that initiation, then Ini, Run and Out hold on that step (the two clauses of Ex conditional on Computational Execution General Form-H3).  
Proof: The initiation is issued by P's control and belongs to the declared protocol, and the state at the head of the step is read by the input reading scheme as (x, D, u); hence Ini. By hypothesis, the step operates under the enabling conditions and the state is held by P; hence En. ¬Sub by hypothesis. The transforming part is driven by that initiation, and, for a non-trivial τ\_Φ, the data-carrying part is the part that reads in x and D and writes out x′ and D′; hence Ex. The write event is the read-out condition; hence Out. ∎

Clock-synchronized digital hosts, and discrete realizations initiating and writing hop by hop, satisfy the hypotheses of the lemma. Sampling moments marked out by an observer do not: they can be boundaries of analysis, but not events at which the host actually initiates the transformation of that step.

Three

Step has been obtained. Given step-by-step execution, does generative computation hold?

Take a computer game: each frame, the program rewrites the ball's position and velocity by rule, the ball bouncing off the wall on impact, for any number of steps. Take also the Game of Life: a fixed local rule acts on the current pattern, executed generation by generation. Every step of both is one execution, by P, of a one-step task, and Step holds; whichever component is declared as D, the next step is given directly by the rules.

These are not generative computation: how the next state goes at each step is prescribed directly by the rules, and no quantity enters the transformation in the capacity of the potential of history's Continuation, contrary to G2. Step does not exclude them: Step asks only whether each step is an execution, not what kind of transformation is executed.

By G2, potential requires the next step to go this way, that is, potential takes part in the generation of the next structure. Hence: the declared D must, at some step, enter the generation of the next structure x′, that is, the generation of x′ takes D as an argument.

This is still only a form: it says that D appears among the arguments of generation, without asking whether D, once read in, really has an effect.

Four

D appears among the arguments of the generation of the next structure at some step. Is potential thereby already governing generation?

Take a program that, at each step, reads in a run log as input, while Φ merely stores it as it is, the next structure having nothing to do with the content of the log. Take also a memoizing cache: with historical results as its table, on a hit it skips the computation and fetches the result directly, and on a miss it computes as usual; x′ is the same whether or not there is a hit, and only the route and the time taken differ. In both, D is read in at every step and lies among the arguments of generation, and Step holds.

These are not generative computation: the log is read without entering the generation of x′, and the cache changes only the route by which x′ is found; potential does not require the next step to go any way, contrary to G2. The form of Section Three does not exclude them: being read in satisfies it.

By G2, potential must really enter the generation of the next structure — not as a bystander, not as a record, not as an accompaniment; upstream records this duty as ActualHold, and gives its formal criterion through distribution-bearing arguments.

Definition Two: generation kernel  
The generation distribution of the result x′\_k of the structural component at step k, under the operand (x, D, u) and given the exogenous control configuration c in which that step stands, is written K\_k(· \| x, D, u; c); the c after the semicolon is a control configuration declared in advance and changed only by protocol interventions (running mode, Mask, Avail and so on), and does not belong to the dynamical condition. For a deterministic transformation, the kernel is a point measure. Two generation kernels are compared by total variation, with a declared resolution ε\_role.

Gov (governance by potential): for at least one step k of π, D ∈ ActArg^μ(x′\_k); D enters the determination as a composite argument, its variable domain frozen as declared; the legitimate counterfactuals are drawn from AdmVar, and the resolution is taken as ε\_role.

The criterion must keep several levels apart. What is compared is generation kernels, not single samples, and not raw physical states uninterpreted by the reading scheme; route and time taken are not in x′, so the cache does not qualify. The counterfactuals take values in AdmVar; these values need only be legitimate under the field's constraints, and are not required to be physically reachable within the same run; that D does not change in a frozen segment does not prevent Gov. Where D has only one legitimate value in AdmVar, it belongs to no minimal sufficient set, and Gov fails.

Inferring the bearing argument from a difference of kernels requires comparability: both states compared belong to AdmVar and are consistent in field state, the derived quantities agreeing with the basic quantities; outside the variable domain of D, the other arguments actually read by the generation kernel (x, u) and the control configuration c take the same values. Under this condition, suppose the total variation between K\_k(· \| x, D₁, u; c) and K\_k(· \| x, D₂, u; c) exceeds ε\_role: any set of conditions not containing D fixes only the arguments other than D, both states agree with it and belong together to its family of legitimate variations, so it is not μ-sufficient; hence D belongs to every μ-sufficient set, minimal sufficient sets exist when the set of arguments is finite, and D ∈ ActArg^μ(x′\_k). If the two states also differ in some kernel argument outside D, the difference of kernels cannot be attributed to D; at steps at which the actual state itself is inconsistent in field state, Gov is not judged by differences of kernels. That D is judged, as a composite argument, to be a bearing argument does not mean that each of its components is. The ontological content of Gov is ActualHold; ActArg^μ is a formal criterion, and the operable subfamily is only a capacity for determination.

Its necessity: by G2, dynamical conditions have entered the transformation in σ, and at one step at least, potential requires the next step to go this way — the step here being a step of the π obtained from G1 in Section Two, the transformations in σ being just these steps — that is, the dynamical condition at that step has the duty of which ActualHold speaks; by the task-side declaration, the dynamical condition in σ is D, whose variable domain contains all the persistent components of the field (where potential resides is asked separately in Section Five), so D has this duty at that step. What upstream has proved is DynamicRole ⇒ ActualHold (Theorem Three of *The Necessity Form of Law* v1.3); what the necessary direction needs is the other direction: that ActualHold in the generation of x′\_k yields D ∈ ActArg^μ(x′\_k). This is H2. Under H2, Gov holds. The direction needed falls into three stages, recorded in H2(b) as (b1), (b2) and (b3): (b1) ActualHold yields DynamicRole, which is a question of how G2 is read — ActualHold is the name of an intermediate duty and carries no independent truth value, and upstream has proved only the converse; (b2) DynamicRole yields its formal representation, namely that, at the generation node where the duty resides, some component of D belongs to ActArg^μ under the declaration of that duty (kernel, admissible counterfactual family, resolution), which is H\_RepD of *The Mathematical Form of Law*; (b3) passing from the declaration of the duty to the declaration of the present step (K\_k, AdmVar, ε\_role), which is a mathematical question between two sets of declarations. (b3) has been proved by Chapter 6 under conditions stated explicitly (Chapter 6, Proposition 7.30, Corollary 7.2); (b1) and (b2) are not proved, and H2 is not thereby closed. The comparability argument of the preceding paragraph is one sufficient route for (b3): if the admissible counterfactual family of the present step contains a pair of states differing only in D whose kernels differ by more than ε\_role, D belongs to ActArg^μ(x′\_k), whatever the declaration of the duty; but it says only that "when there is such a witnessing pair, D obtains the qualification of the present step," and does not prove that ActualHold necessarily brings such a witnessing pair with it — that still goes through (b1) and (b2).  
Discipline of argument: whoever infers, from an actual dynamical duty, the qualification of bearing generation at the present step must go through (b1), (b2) and (b3) stage by stage, without skipping any; while (b1) and (b2) are not proved, the conclusion carries them as conditions; (b3) must be checked item by item under the conditions given. The relative size of the resolution declared for the present step and the resolution of the duty does not on its own guarantee the transfer of qualification: the qualification of bearing is not monotone in resolution, and it can be lost whether the resolution is coarser or finer (Chapter 6, Example 7.4, Proposition 7.31). The correspondence between the generation node where the duty resides and the output x′\_k of the present step may not be assumed without being checked: qualification obtained by the duty at an internal node can be erased by the map from that node to the output (the note after Chapter 6, Proposition 7.30).

Gov requires only at least one step: G2 does not lay down "every step is governed by potential" (the strong-input step of GS10); "at least one step" is the lower bound derived from G2. Gov is determined relative to the declared π; whether it is preserved under refinement and coarsening belongs to H1.

Gov supersedes the form of Section Three.

Five

Gov has been obtained. Is the quantity that governs generation thereby potential?

Gov asks only whether D really enters generation, not what kind of quantity D is, nor how D changes within the segment. This divides into two branches, with Gov and what was obtained before as their common premise: branch A asks where D lies, and branch B asks how D changes within the segment; the counterexamples of branch B keep the location required by branch A. The two branches close separately and then merge.

(A)

Take simulated annealing: the temperature is lowered on a schedule fixed in advance, and governs the acceptance probability at each step, thereby changing the generation kernel of x; Gov holds. Take a rule program in which a set of constants is written so that the program reproduces, step by step, the ball bouncing off the wall; the constants are read at every step and govern x′, and in no mode does any sedimentation act on them; Gov holds. Take an ordinary program that compiles a lookup table from actual run logs and writes it into P's rule-configuration area, which P reads at every step thereafter; Gov holds. Take a rule program that stores a set of weights W, together with three functions named Trace, Sediment and Decay that the protocol permits to be called in a certain mode, but which, when acting on W, do not respond through traces, do not deepen with repetition and do not shallow with idleness; in normal running W does not change and determines the next state; Gov holds. Take also a set-table — set to a fixed value on being passed through once, and thereafter neither deepening nor fading; and a global counter — any passage through increases all positions alike, so that the trace does not differ with the source.

None of these is generative computation. The change of temperature is given by the clock, and no Trace-Response writes into it; the constants, the lookup table in the configuration area, and the W carrying the name of sedimentation operators take values given by setting in every mode, and the dependence of x′ on them is a dependence on rules; the lookup table is compiled from history, but history is only an argument of the table-compiling program; the set-table is a record, and the global counter is a counter. They govern generation, but what governs is neither the tendency to continue along laws already present nor the potential for hold-breaking of a Principle already formed in the field, of which G2 speaks, contrary to G2. Gov does not exclude them.

By G2, potential is the tendency to continue along laws already present, or the potential for hold-breaking of a Principle already formed in the field. Two stages are needed here: first ask to what kind of field potential belongs, then ask what position D holds in that field.

To what kind of field potential belongs. According to upstream, law resides in sedimentation (*Outer Chapter 7, Information-Transformation*: "the transformation of signal enters sediment and is regulated therein"), and a law is a holding-tendency (*Inner Chapter 9, The Experience-Field*: "Law holds."); the general theorem of *The General Form of Law* v2.5 takes "the candidate law L is established in the Sedimentation-Field F" as its antecedent, and the Sedimentation-Field there is the field laid down by *Sedimentation-Field Theory* v1.2, having the three basic properties of Sedimentability Nature, Organizability Nature and State-Flux Nature, whose dynamical structure is given by *The Mathematical Form of Field* v2.6. According to Chapter 1 of *The Principle Group-Form*, *The General Form of Principle* v1.0, a Principle is a law already formed, so a Principle too lies in such a field. Hence the field in which the "laws already present" and "Principle already formed" of G2 lie is, by the upstream definitions, a Sedimentation-Field in this sense: this is not a field qualification inferred separately from "some organization has borne a dynamical duty," but the identity of field that the term "law" presupposes upstream. A system that carries only the name of sedimentation, whose carriers under the action of its operators do not respond through traces, neither deepen nor fade, and do not differ with the source, lacks these three properties; there is no law in it in the upstream sense, and so none of the potential of which G2 speaks. Field qualification is constituted by the behaviour of the carriers under the action of the operators, not by what the operators are called or by whether the protocol permits rewriting; "being a program" does not cancel field qualification, and "having writable parameters" does not confer it. *The Construction Form of the Minimal Sedimentation Field* v1.2 unfolds the three properties into the construction interfaces C1–C7 and gives a construction satisfying them; the success of that construction is only a witness that fields of this kind exist, not the general argument here.

What position D holds in that field. By C0 of *The General Form of Law* v2.5 (inheritance of laws into sedimentation, with upstream status as inheritance pending verification), together with that form's restriction of "established" in its working domain, the establishment of a law requires that the relational organization bearing it be a bearing organization currently existing in F and non-instantaneous, not maintained merely and immediately by the current input (that is, CurrentCarrier ∧ CarrierRealizes ∧ CarrierNonInstant, obtained by the first round of questioning in the necessary direction of that form; this step does not invoke that form's H1, which is needed only for passing to Established, not used here); Theorem Two of *The Necessity Form of Law* v1.3 gives the semantic content of the same level (sedimentary formation) on its corpus domain M1–M8, but that form proves only the sufficient direction, and inferring from "the law is established" that "the bearing organization is a sedimentary bearing within the field" belongs to the necessary direction and still carries C0 (the conditional inheritance in the synthesis of Sections One and Nine of that form, and Section Fourteen (2)); by Definitions 3.3 and 3.4 of *The Mathematical Form of Law* v1.5, freezing strips away only immediate driving, and what still exists after freezing is formed structure. So the D that enters generation as potential at the step of which Gov speaks is a state of sedimentary bearing of this field, and still exists after freezing; that is, it is a persistent field state. Where history is retained without entering the generation of what follows, it is inert dynamically (*The General Form of Sedimentation Dynamics* v1.1); this has already been dealt with by Gov.

How this field relates to this stretch of computation. By G3, computation realizes dynamical conditions in the host, so the state carrier of D is in P; by G6, this stretch of computation acts on the structures, relations and dynamical conditions of the field, and what it drives is the dynamics of this field. Taken together, the carrier in P must be a carrier of this field, not merely a place storing a value equal to some state of the field: in the declared system of this stretch of computation, there must be some mode of the field's protocol whose sedimentary duties take D as their object — reading the current value of D and giving its update according to the protocol. Sedimentation takes the state of the field as its object; this is the very duty of a Sedimentation-Field (Sedimentability Nature); if in no mode of the declared system does any sedimentary duty take this D as its object, then the value of D in this stretch of computation is given only by setting, and what σ acts on is a configuration value, not a dynamical condition of the field, contrary to G6. Where the update is written and whether the next step reads it are not asked here, but in Section Eight. This relation of realization can be realized by P itself (sedimentation switched on within the segment, or switched off through the protocol's freezing), or by P together with a declared sedimentation implementer through coupling; when the latter holds is what H3 asks.

Field qualification is a property of carriers and operators, and has nothing to do with what value a carrier currently stores. How the current value of D came about — formed by actual Continuation, moved in, written in, initialized — does not change whether it stands in the position of potential; the transplantation clause and the lookup-table clause of the upstream Established give the same boundary.

Definition Three: field qualification and persistent field state  
FieldQualified(F) means that F is a Sedimentation-Field in the sense of *Sedimentation-Field Theory* v1.2 — having Sedimentability Nature, Organizability Nature and State-Flux Nature — that is, the field presupposed by *The General Form of Law* v2.5 when it takes "the law is established in the Sedimentation-Field F" as antecedent; C1–C7 of *The Construction Form of the Minimal Sedimentation Field* v1.2 are its construction interfaces. A persistent field state is a field state that still exists after freezing, as distinct from a driving quantity maintained merely and immediately by the current input. The protocol Prot\_F of F lists the permitted state updates and operations: sedimentation, shallowing, freezing, thawing, role masking and so on; an operation scheduled by the protocol at some junction is called a scheduled operation of that junction.

(B)

Take a network realizing a qualified field: D lies on the persistent field state of the field, and Gov holds; but every three steps P itself rewrites D, according to a schedule, into a preset pattern, at the same time maintaining the field's derived quantities so that the field state is consistent.

It is not generative computation: what is read after the point of rewriting is an implanted setting, the "history becomes dynamics" of G2 is broken at that point, and this segment is no longer a legitimate trajectory segment of the field, contrary to G2. Branch A does not exclude it: the position of D is qualified.

That no change contrary to the protocol occurs is not yet enough. Take again a network realizing a qualified field, declared to be in the switched-on mode: the protocol requires that at each step D be carried, along the route passed through, into the D′ after sedimentation. D′ is computed, but written into a copy at another position in P (declared as the output carrier of D′); the next step still reads D from the original position, and what it reads is not updated. No change outside the protocol has occurred in the field state, yet the update the protocol requires has not occurred. This too is not generative computation: the dynamical condition as changed by the dynamical conditions has not been held (G3), and the field declared as switched on is in fact running frozen. This is exactly shadow computation: carrying out the corresponding computation on the target field state without bearing its update and subsequent Continuation. By Definition One of *The General Form of Law-Dynamics* v1.0, a legitimate trajectory segment is a stretch of one and the same legitimate trajectory, within which every state update obeys the protocol and the earlier state is really carried into the later one; here the earlier D has not been carried into the later D that the switched-on mode requires, so this segment is not a legitimate trajectory segment under the declared mode. Nor is it a legitimate segment under the frozen mode: freezing is a mode of the protocol that must be declared, and what is declared here is switched on. Branch A does not exclude it.

Merging: the cases from here on must keep both the position of D and the discipline within the segment.

Sed (potential resides in sedimentation): (i) the Sedimentation-Field F to which the carrier of D belongs satisfies FieldQualified(F), and D is a persistent field state of F, neither an immediate driving quantity nor a configuration constant in the rules of τ\_Φ; (ii) in the declared system of this stretch of computation, the protocol of F has some mode whose sedimentary duties take D as their object (reading D in and giving its update according to the protocol), this mode being realized by P (sedimentation switched on within the segment, or switched off through the protocol's freezing), or by P together with a declared sedimentation implementer through coupling; (iii) the trajectory of D is a legitimate trajectory segment of F under the declared mode (Definition One of *The General Form of Law-Dynamics* v1.0): in the sequence of values of D read from the state carrier of each step and at the junctions, every change is a protocol operation, and every update the declared mode requires actually carries the earlier D into the later D. What is taken here is legitimacy on the projection onto D: conditional on the x actually passed through in σ and on the deliveries, the sequence of D satisfies the field protocol's update equation for D. "Actually passed through" takes its sense from the upstream sedimentation gate. By 3.7 and 4.3 of *The Mathematical Form of Law-Dynamics* v1.0, a legitimate actual execution is taken as a writer according to the protocol, and its sedimentation enters Trace and Sediment from the output Y\_r of that execution through the route projection π\_tr; candidate evaluations, hypothetical branches, replay probes, non-actual executions and unpermitted manual rewriting are all excluded — the gate "separates 'actually happened' from 'merely evaluated.'" Accordingly, what step k in σ actually passed through is the actual transition of the execution of that step: from the structure read in by that step from the state carrier (together with the content entering the input slots by delivery legitimate under T1 and T2 of Section Six) to the structure read out by that step as its result at e\_k. It is the path actually walked this time, not a path that was evaluated.

This delimitation asks only "what did this step actually walk," not "did the place it reached thereafter become the starting point of the next step": the latter is carry-over, determined in Section Eight. If "already taken up by the next step" were merged into what is actually passed through, Sed would swallow Hold. Three boundaries follow from this. First, an exploratory action in training that is actually executed and read out as the result of that step is actually passed through, and enters sedimentation; distortion of the training result does not change this (GS9). Second, candidates computed within a step but not read out as the result of that step (unselected branches of beam search, look-ahead evaluations, replay probes) are not actually passed through, and where they enter sedimentation Sed(iii) fails. Third, in the structural shadow of Section Eight, x′ is the structure read out as the result of the execution of that step, the transition walked is actually passed through, and sedimentation on its basis accords with the gate; its failure lies in x′ not being taken up by the next step, which is a failure of Hold, not of Sed. If sedimentation is based on a route record that is neither the actual transition of some step nor a legitimate delivery (one replaced or forged), the update of D does not satisfy the protocol equation conditional on what is actually passed through, and Sed(iii) fails. The ground truth written in by teacher forcing enters the declared input slot by legitimate delivery, is thereafter actually processed by the network, and what is passed through enters sedimentation (GS6); the scalar delivered through the reward channel is a delivery, and what is updated on its basis is the sedimentation on the route actually passed through (GS7). The variable domain of D already contains all persistent components of F and the derived quantities updated along with them (task-side declaration), so no component on the persistent side of the field falls outside the determination. The remaining field state of F is x — the current structures and relations transformed by the computation — whose transformation within one step is determined by Step, and whose carry-over between steps is determined by Section Eight. The whole trajectory of F (together with x) is not required to be legitimate: failures on the side of x have their own items, and merging them into Sed would let the content of Hold be swallowed by Sed.

Its necessity: (i) by G2 and upstream — potential is the potential of a law already present or a Principle already formed, and law and Principle lie, by upstream, only in a Sedimentation-Field in the sense of *Sedimentation-Field Theory*, so FieldQualified(F); under C0 of *The General Form of Law* v2.5 (through the first round of questioning in that form's necessary direction, without invoking that form's H1), the bearing organization of a law is a bearing organization currently existing in F and non-instantaneous, and further, by Definitions 3.3 and 3.4 of *The Mathematical Form of Law* v1.5, it still exists after freezing, so D ∈ Pers(F) and is not an immediate driving quantity; it is a state of the field, not a constituent of the rules of τ\_Φ, and so not a configuration constant. This step (D ∈ Pers(F)), together with "not an immediate driving quantity" and "not a configuration constant" obtained from it (the three together called the persistent-field-state clause of Sed(i); corresponding, in item 3 of Part Five of the Appendix, to the two clauses D ∉ Pers(F) and configuration constant), carries C0; the clause FieldQualified(F) does not pass through C0. (ii) By G3 and G6 — the carrier of D is in P, and what the computation drives is the dynamics of the field, so in the declared system some sedimentary duty must take this D as its object; otherwise what σ acts on is a configuration value. (iii) The first half by G2 — where D is rewritten bare within the segment, what is read after the point of rewriting is an implanted setting, and the "history becomes dynamics" of G2 is broken; the second half by G3 — where an update the declared mode requires is computed without carrying the field state into its successor, the dynamical condition as changed by the dynamical conditions has not been held.

(i) requires only full upstream field qualification; whether behavioural testing on a reference implementation is equivalent to it is not proved (U6). A frozen segment satisfies (ii). Where a field is realized by deployment coupling — the host realizes no sedimentation operator and only runs the Continuation kernel with D, and sedimentation takes place in another training system — whether its D lies on the persistent field state of a qualified field depends on whether the host realizes the frozen mode of this field; upstream uses frozen running as a contrast without making this determination, so for such cases Sed is ⊥ before H3 is determined, not 0. Where a sedimentation mode is declared on the host while the operators of that mode lack the behaviour of a field, Sed is 0. (iii) does not restrict the value at the head of the segment; the initial state of a legitimate trajectory segment is unrestricted.

Six

Sed has been obtained. Updates at the junctions — who evaluates them?

Sed(iii) asks only whether the changes of D within the segment accord with the protocol, not who computes them. The protocol also schedules operations between steps — shallowing, updating path quantities according to the route already walked — which take place at the junctions, not within the execution of any step.

Take a network in training that satisfies all the items above: at each step, reading (x, D), selecting x′ and sedimenting along the route passed through are all computed by P itself, and Step holds; but at every junction, the shallowing scheduled by the protocol is evaluated on P's behalf by a machine outside the declared boundary: it reads a copy of D from P, computes the new value by the same shallowing law of F, and at the end of the junction writes it into the state carrier of D in P; throughout the junction, D and all the carried-over components remain on P's carriers, held by P, the external machine only taking a copy and keeping nothing in custody. Every write accords with the value prescribed by the protocol, and Sed and Gov hold; the state never leaves P.

It is not P's generative computation: the dynamical formation between nodes is given by inserted computation from outside the boundary, contrary to G4; by the delimitation of proxy evaluation, a link outside the boundary has carried out part of the evaluation, and the subsequent read depends on that work. What has been obtained so far does not exclude it: the ¬Sub contained in Step governs only within each step, and Sed governs only the form of change.

¬Intr (no intrusion at junctions): at each junction of π, no process outside the boundary carries out the evaluation (or part of it) of D or of the carried-over components of x such that the state read by step k+1 depends on that work.

Its necessity follows from G4 and the delimitation of proxy evaluation. The proxy evaluation of *The General Form of Computational Bearing* takes the task function as its object; protocol operations at junctions are not one-step transformations of τ\_Φ, and here the delimitation of proxy evaluation is extended to the update functions scheduled by the protocol at junctions (in agreement with Definition 3.7(iii) of Chapter 6, which takes the update functions of Prot\_F under the declared mode as the functions evaluated by proxy); this extension is this chapter's reading of the "inserting additional computation" of G4, not content inherited from *The General Form of Computational Bearing*. Where an external machine computes the D′ of the present step and writes it back, the proxy evaluation lies within one step and is excluded by the ¬Sub of Step, not by ¬Intr. ¬Intr is the necessitation, for generative computation, of K2 of Corollary Five of *The General Form of Computational Execution* (no proxy evaluation at junctions).

Not intrusion: external noise, faults and environmental action, which do not evaluate updates, their consequences being determined by Step or as computational distortion; storage-only and transfer-only handling, determined in Section Seven; protocol operations issued by the declared operating party (freezing, thawing, role masking, changes of availability, mode switching). A delivery must pass two tests: T1 (not bypassing carry-over) — by the update equation of the protocol declared for σ at that junction, the content of this slot at step k+1 does not take the output of step k, that is, at that junction it is an input slot and not a carried-over component; where Φ writes it under this protocol and a delivery overwrites it, the carried-over state that the dynamics within the field should have formed is replaced — if the overwriting content is computed by an evaluation of Φ outside the boundary, the case belongs to ¬Intr, otherwise to Section Eight; what is determined is the protocol equation, not the name of the input slot. T2 (no proxy evaluation) — the delivered content is not obtained by an evaluation of Φ outside the boundary on the state of P in this segment, and the delivery protocol is declared before the run under test. D is not rewritten by delivery: whatever changes D is either a protocol operation of the field, or violates Sed(iii).

Hence teacher forcing, which writes ground truth into the "previous token" declared as an input slot (GS6), passes T1 and T2, and is a delivery; in a protocol declared as free generation, the same token is a carried-over component, and the same practice does not pass T1. A scalar delivered through the reward channel, on the basis of which P computes the update of D (GS7), is a delivery. A parameter server within the declared boundary (GS8) is not intrusion.

Seven

¬Intr has been obtained. Between steps, who holds the potential and the structure?

¬Intr requires that evaluation at junctions be carried out by P; where the state is kept between steps it does not ask.

Take a device satisfying all the items above: at the head of each step P fetches (x, D) from a storage device outside the boundary into its local carriers, all reads and writes within the step are within P, and after writing out at the end of the step the whole is handed back to the storage device for custody, P keeping no copy; the storage device only stores and does not compute. The Ini, Run (including En within the step) and Out of each step hold, and Step holds; evaluation at the junctions is all within P, and ¬Intr holds; Gov and Sed hold.

It is not P's generative computation: between steps, the changed structures and dynamical conditions are not in P, contrary to G3, "computation realizes structures, relations and dynamical conditions in the host"; it is the generative computation of the larger system of P together with the external storage. What has been obtained so far does not exclude it: Step contains only holding within each step, and ¬Intr asks only about evaluation. Where every read and write falls directly on the external storage, the case is excluded on the side of bearing: by Leg₁ of *The General Form of Computational Bearing*, the reading schemes of τ\_Φ are defined on the state type of P; where the carriers read and written within a step lie outside the declared boundary, the states on them are not states of P, and P does not bear a τ\_Φ taking them as operand and result position; execution has bearing as its antecedent, so the steps are not executions of τ\_Φ by P, and Step fails (the determination carrying Computational Bearing General Form-H1).

Carry (host holding): at each junction of π (including the final segment), the carried-over components of x and D are held by P; D is a state of P, not an input fetched or delivered from outside the boundary at each step.

Its necessity follows from the second half of G3. Carry is the necessitation, for generative computation, of K4 of Corollary Five (En at junctions). Carry does not require D to come from P's own history: what is moved in is held by P just the same.

Eight

Carry has been obtained. Is the changed structure exactly what is read next?

Carry requires the state to be held by P; whether what is held is the very one written out by step k it does not ask.

Take a network satisfying all the items above, declared as follows: the state carrier and the output carrier of D are one and the same position L\_D, and sedimentation, by the protocol of the switched-on mode, carries D into D′ and writes it back into L\_D; the state carrier of x is L\_x, but the output carrier of x′ is declared as another position C in P (a copy). Each step reads (x, D) from L\_x and L\_D, computes (x′, D′), writes D′ into L\_D and x′ into C; the next step still reads from L\_x, and the structure it reads is not updated. Checking item by item: each step initiates, operates, writes into the declared output carrier and obtains the read-out at e\_k, so Ini, Run and Out hold, and Step holds; all states are within P, and Carry holds; there is no external evaluation at the junctions, and ¬Intr holds; the D on L\_D enters the generation of x′ at every step, and Gov holds; F is qualified, D is its persistent field state, sedimentation takes D as its object, and the trajectory of D is a legitimate trajectory segment under the switched-on mode, so Sed holds; the steps belong to the same run, and Multi holds.

This construction is hereafter called the shadow copy of the structure. It is not generative computation: the structure as changed by the dynamical conditions has not become the current structure of what follows, contrary to the first half of G3 — the structure stays where it was, while the field sediments as usual. What has been obtained so far does not exclude it: Carry asks only who holds, and Sed concerns only the trajectory of D.

Writing to the right carrier is not yet enough. Take the same network, with the output carrier of each component joined to the state carrier of the next step; but at each junction a routine of P's own changes the carried-over components of x back to a preset value, leaving D untouched. Carry, ¬Intr and Sed hold, and the write positions are correct. It too is not generative computation: the changed structure is erased at the junction, contrary to the first half of G3.

Carry-over holds between all adjacent steps; what of the last step? Take the same network, the outputs of the first n−1 steps being joined to the state carriers of the next step, with no rewriting at the junctions either; but the x′ of step n is written into a position that thereafter no longer bears the field state, while D′ lands as usual on the final-state carrier (the trajectory of D is legitimate, and Sed holds); when σ ends, the structure on the final-state carrier is still the one from before step n. Looking only at the joining of adjacent steps does not exclude it. Take another: the output of step n is written into the final-state carrier, but within the final segment, before σ ends, a routine of P's own restores the x there to its original value. Looking only at the joining of carriers does not exclude this either. Neither is generative computation of this segment: the structure and dynamical conditions as last changed have not been held (G3). If the stretch after the last step were not brought within the determination, any unqualified tail could escape the test on the ground that it lies "within no step and no junction"; so the final segment is subject to determination in the same way as the internal junctions.

Hold (carry-over of what is changed): for k = 1, …, n (for k = n the junction is the final segment, and the state carrier of step k+1 means the final-state carrier, and its initial state the state on that carrier at t\_end), (a) on every component of the carried-over components and of D, the output carrier Out\_k of step k is joined to the state carrier In\_{k+1} of step k+1 — the same position, or connected by storage-only or transfer-only copying; (b) the carried-over components and D of the initial state of step k+1 are, in value, what is obtained from the state written out by step k through the action within the junction (instantaneous quantities being recomputed within the subsequent execution, input slots obtained by delivery, and exogenous control quantities evolving by the protocol, none of which is within this comparison); (c) within the junction, the carried-over components of x are not rewritten by P or by the declared protocol; of the carried-over components and the input slots, only input slots may change within the junction, and then through declared delivery (instantaneous quantities, recomputed within the subsequent execution, fall outside this clause); protocol operations at junctions that act on exogenous control quantities (such as role masking, changes of availability, mode switching) or on D are not within the prohibition of (c), the former being permitted by the protocol and the latter determined by Sed(iii). (a) concerns the identity of carriers, and (b) and (c) concern the continuation of values: where carriers are joined but the values are changed back, (b) and (c) exclude it.

Its necessity follows from the first half of G3: (a) excludes the shadow copy of the structure and the discarding of the last step's output, (c) excludes changing back at junctions, and (b) links the two into "the subsequent initial state is written out by the previous step." Where a momentary external disturbance rewrites carried-over components, it does not evaluate an update, is classed as computational distortion, and does not violate (c). On the side of D: the shadow of D in branch B of Section Five has already been excluded by Sed(iii); under Step and Sed(iii), the D on the state carrier of the next step is in value the update the protocol requires, and what Hold adds for the components of D is only the joining of carriers; the independent content of Hold lies in the carry-over of the components of x and in (c). Where D is changed back at a junction by P itself to an old or preset value without going through the protocol, this is a failure of Sed(iii). Hold does not require D to be updated at every step: in a frozen segment only x is rewritten and carried over (GS2). In pulsed running, a structural position is given by entry delivery at the start of each pulse; that position is declared at this junction as an input slot, and this does not violate (c).

What (c) prohibits is only the rewriting of carried-over components, not legitimate protocol operations at junctions. The protocol operations permitted upstream are Mode\_exo, Mode\_endo, Freeze, Thaw, IdleStep, do(Mask), do(Avail) (*The Construction Form of the Law Operating Substrate* v1.2): IdleStep only shallows and only expires windows, acting on D; Freeze, Thaw and mode switching change the sedimentation switch and the running mode; do(Mask) changes only the role-reading mask — "cutting off the path by which that structure enters current generation, without changing g, S, rep, Ω" — and do(Avail) changes only availability; Mask and Avail are exogenous control quantities. No permitted operation rewrites the current structure written out by the previous step. Thus applying role masking at a junction, so that the current effective relation read by the next step changes from R to R\*, is a legitimate intervention on exogenous control quantities: the current effective relation, by the reading in the sixth item of Section One, belongs to the instantaneous quantities, is recomputed from Mask and D within the next step, and is not carried across steps in the first place; its change does not violate (c). Declaring the current effective relation as a carried-over component would disagree with the classification by which upstream lists κ among the instantaneous quantities and this chapter classes the Mask·g read by κ among them too; it would be a misdeclaration of the division of the extended state (H1(a)), not a counterexample to (c).

Nine

Hold has been obtained. The steps are linked into a chain by carry-over — do they belong to one stretch of computation?

Hold links the writing and the reading of adjacent steps; whether they are two steps of one and the same computation it does not ask.

Take a process satisfying all the items above: step k is executed by one run, which writes out the state, and that run ends; step k+1 is executed by a separately initiated new process — not issued by the dynamics of the earlier run or by the declared protocol, but taking as its initial state the state the earlier run left in P's medium. The state is in P throughout, and Carry holds; what the later step reads is exactly what the earlier step wrote, and Hold holds; each step satisfies Step, there is no external evaluation at the junctions, and D accords with the protocol and governs generation. The six steps belong to six separate runs, and the trajectory pieced together is exactly the same as that of executing the six steps continuously in one run.

It is not a stretch of generative computation: multi-step computation must be multiple steps of one and the same computation (G5); by *The General Form of Computational Execution*, one computation is undertaken by one run; rerunning with recorded inputs, or a process beginning from an initial state that does not belong to the run, by a separate initiation not issued by that run's dynamics or by the declared protocol, is a new run (for checkpoint recovery, see below). Hold does not exclude it: Hold only links reading and writing, without asking to which run the two ends belong.

Multi (the same run): σ has at least two steps, and the whole of σ (each step and each junction) belongs to one and the same run r on one and the same instance of P — one continuous stretch of process, not restarted from an initial state, such as one produced by reset, that does not belong to r, the initiation of step k+1 being issued within this process by P's own dynamics or by the declared protocol.

Its necessity follows from G5 and the delimitation of the same run. Checkpoint recovery and live migration within the declared boundary are continuations of the same run (GS8); the distinction between relay and recovery is determined by Computational Execution General Form-H2. A reset-and-restart violates both Hold and Multi. The scope of ∃r is fixed by this.

Ten

Step, Gov, Sed, ¬Intr, Carry, Hold and Multi have been obtained. Must anything else be set up?

Updating D at every step: in frozen inference D does not change, yet potential governs as usual (GS2). No separate item is needed.  
Hold-breaking at every step: holding-tendency alone can be a dynamical condition (the first kind in G2). No separate item is needed.  
Generating a new law in every segment: laws are generated by the field's dynamics (G6), and an early stretch of training in which no new law has yet been generated is generative computation (GS9). No separate item is needed.  
Correctness of the result: distorted generative computation is still generative computation (GS9). No separate item is needed.  
D coming from the history of this instance: sedimentation moved in (GS3). No separate item is needed.  
The initial value of D in the segment being formed by actual Continuation: initial values written in by hand (GS4); the initial state of a legitimate trajectory segment is unrestricted, and, under Established, qualification is not lost because of source. No separate item is needed.  
The read-out being observed and the target law being declared: unattended training is generative computation; what is borne and executed is τ\_Φ, not the target law. No separate item is needed.  
The seven items above are excluded by the standard positive cases, and none needs to be set up separately. Two further items are neither excluded nor established as necessary conditions:  
Every step governed by potential: if, at the strong-input step of GS10, the generation kernel does not vary with D beyond ε\_role, this requirement would exclude it; but G2 leaves it undetermined whether that step still belongs to dynamical transformation. What follows from G2 is only the lower bound "at least one step"; whether this item is necessary is unanswered (U3).  
All the duties of the field realized by P's computation: in training, external field-induction enters (GS7); Sed(i) requires only that the field in which D lies obtain full field qualification. Which of the field's duties must be realized by P is unanswered (U4).  
Neither item enters the general formula; if either is later proved necessary, the general formula will be revised in a new version.

Back to the same task: if σ is a stretch of generative computation on P, it can necessarily be divided step by step, each step being one execution of τ\_Φ by P; between steps, the structure and the dynamical conditions are held by P, and evaluation at junctions is carried out by P; under H2, at one step at least, D really enters the generation of the next structure; D is the persistent field state of a Sedimentation-Field that has obtained full field qualification, changes within the segment only through the protocol, and every update the protocol requires actually takes place; what each step writes out is exactly what the next step reads, and what the last step writes out is exactly the final state; the whole segment belongs to one and the same run. The failure cases of Sections Two to Nine have all been excluded, none of the proposed additions excluded by the positive cases opens a new constraint, and the remaining two items are listed as unanswered; the original question closes in the necessary direction. Closing means that no known unclosed point remains, not sufficiency (U1).

Eleven

At this point the general form of generative computation has been forced out step by step. It can be written out formally, together with the general theorem.

The general form of the minimal structure of generative computation

H2 ∧ GenComp(P, σ) ⇒ ∃π ∃r: Step ∧ Carry ∧ Gov ∧ Sed ∧ ¬Intr ∧ Hold ∧ Multi

(The two clauses of Ex contained in Step carry Computational Execution General Form-H3, the clause of Sed(i) on persistent field states carries C0 of *The General Form of Law* v2.5, and Sed(ii), for the deployment-coupling kind, carries H3; for the six-item conclusion not depending on H2, see part (1) of the general theorem.)

General Theorem　Suppose that P bears τ\_Φ within the declared period Θ, that σ ⊆ Θ, that the division of the extended state, the variable domain of D, the delivery protocols, the specification and protocol of the field F, and the boundary are all declared before and independently of σ, that σ lies within the working domain, and that generative computation is delimited by G1–G6. If σ is a stretch of generative computation on P, then:  
(1) there exist a legitimate, individuable step partition π of σ and a run r on one and the same instance of P such that Step, Carry, Sed, ¬Intr, Hold and Multi hold together (the two clauses of Ex in Step being conditional on Computational Execution General Form-H3, the clause of Sed(i) on persistent field states conditional on C0 of *The General Form of Law* v2.5, and, in Sed(ii), realization by deployment coupling having H3 as its condition of determination);  
(2) under H2, there exist π and r such that the seven items hold together; the π and r of (1) are witnesses.  
The full formal statement of each item is in Part Three of the Appendix.

The proof is as follows. First obtain π from G1: σ is a succession of executions of τ\_Φ by P, and taking the initiation and read-out events of these executions as step boundaries gives π (Section Two). Step, Gov, Sed, ¬Intr, Carry and Hold are all stated relative to this π:  
without Step, the transformations in σ are not successive executions of τ\_Φ by P, contrary to G1 (Section Two; its step boundaries give a legitimate π, and the general theorem of *The General Form of Computational Execution* gives Ini, Run and Out on each step);  
without Gov, at no step does potential really enter the generation of the next structure, contrary to G2 (Section Four; the step from ActualHold to D ∈ ActArg^μ is under H2, whose (b) divides into the three stages (b1), (b2), (b3));  
without Sed, what enters generation is not an organization of sedimentary bearing in a Sedimentation-Field, or the field in which it lies is not realized in this stretch of computation, or the change within the segment is an implanted setting, or an update the declared mode requires does not take place, contrary to G2, G3 and G6 (Section Five);  
without ¬Intr, the dynamical formation at junctions is given by inserted computation from outside the boundary, contrary to G4 (Section Six);  
without Carry, the structures and dynamical conditions between steps are not in P, contrary to the second half of G3 (Section Seven);  
without Hold, the changed structure has not become the current state of what follows, contrary to the first half of G3 (Section Eight);  
then obtain r from G5: the steps of π are steps of one and the same computation, and by the delimitation of the same run there is a run r on one and the same instance of P such that the whole of σ (each step and each junction of π) belongs to r, and n ≥ 2; without Multi, the steps are not steps of one and the same computation, contrary to G5 (Section Nine).  
To draw these together: π is obtained from G1, r is obtained from G5 together with the delimitation of the same run in *The General Form of Computational Execution*, and the whole of σ belongs to r and satisfies Multi; Step, Sed, ¬Intr, Carry and Hold all hold for the same π, so the six items hold together on the same π and r, which is (1). Under H2, Gov also holds for this π, so the seven items hold together on the same π and r, which is (2). ∎

Strength: the general theorem is in the necessary direction. The necessity of the six items in (1) does not depend on H2, and is unconditional relative to G1–G6 and the working domain, except that the clause of Sed(i) on persistent field states carries C0 of *The General Form of Law* v2.5, the two clauses of Ex contained in Step are conditional on Computational Execution General Form-H3, and Sed(ii), for the deployment-coupling kind, has H3 as its condition of determination; the concrete determination of each item carries H1, Computational Execution General Form-H1–H3 and Computational Bearing General Form-H1–H3. The necessity of Gov in (2) is conditional on H2, relative to the declared π, and gives only "at least one step"; the conclusion of the seven-item conjunction therefore carries H2, while the six-item conclusion is not thereby reduced to a conditional one. Whether the seven items yield generative computation is unanswered (U1).

Reading back in words: if a stretch of running on a computing host that bears a one-step task is generative computation, then that running can necessarily be divided step by step, each step initiated by the host itself and ending with a write, being one complete execution by it of this one-step task; between steps, the current structure and the dynamical conditions are kept by the host itself, and the evaluations scheduled by the protocol are also done by it, with no process outside the boundary inserted to compute on its behalf; the dynamical condition is the persistent field state of a Sedimentation-Field that has obtained full field qualification, the declared system of this stretch of computation has sedimentary duties of the field taking it as their object, and within the segment it changes only according to the field's protocol, every update the protocol requires actually taking place — how its current value came about is not in question; under H2, at one step at least, this dynamical condition really enters the generation of the next structure: replace it under legitimate counterfactuals, and the generation distribution of the next structure at that step will differ; what each step writes out is exactly what the next step reads, and what the last step writes out is exactly the state at the end of the segment; the whole segment belongs to one and the same run, of at least two steps. Of these, the two clauses contained in "each step is one complete execution," to the effect that the transforming part is set going by the initiation, carry Computational Execution General Form-H3; the clause "the dynamical condition is a persistent field state" carries C0 of *The General Form of Law* v2.5. The order of the items is the order of proof, and indicates no temporal or causal order.

Twelve: Corollaries

Corollary One (independence of source)　Under one and the same qualified field specification, whether the initial value of D in the segment is formed by actual Continuation, moved in from another instance, written in by hand, or given by initialization does not change the truth value of Sed.  
Proof: Sed(i) and (ii) concern only the qualification and modes of the field to which the carrier belongs, and have nothing to do with the value stored in the carrier; (iii) concerns only changes within the segment, and the initial state of a legitimate trajectory segment is unrestricted. ∎  
It does not follow that these cases agree in truth value on Gov or on the other items; nor does it follow that constants written into the rules are of the same kind as weights written into a qualified field — the former do not satisfy Sed(i).

Corollary Two (identical step-by-step Continuation with different classification, testable)　The following five pairs of processes can be constructed such that, in each pair, under the same initial state, the same deliveries and the same random source, the (x, D, u) read in at each step is the same and the generation kernel K\_k of x′ is the same, so that the choice made at each step and the trajectory of x are the same, while one member satisfies, and the other does not, some item of the general formula: (1) the shadow copy of D in branch B of Section Five, and the frozen running of the same network (the former does not satisfy Sed(iii), and Hold(a) on the components of D fails as well); (2) shallowing at junctions evaluated by proxy outside the boundary by the same shallowing law, and its computation by P itself (¬Intr); (3) custody between steps by external storage that only stores and does not compute, and holding by P (Carry); (4) a rule program in which the weights obtained by training are written as constants, its rules constructed as the same transformation as the Continuation kernel of the field, with like-named operators lacking sedimentary behaviour attached, and the frozen running of the same weights in a qualified field (Sed); (5) relayed runs, and continuous execution of the same process in one run (Multi).  
Proof: (1) in the shadow of D, the D read by the next step is always the value at the head of the segment, and x′ is carried over as usual, so the same (x, D, u) is read in at each step as in frozen running; (2) and (3) the two ways of evaluating or of keeping custody give the same value; (4) the constants are those weights, and the rules are that Continuation kernel; (5) the state is passed on unchanged through the medium. In each pair, the (x, D, u) read in at each step is the same, the generation kernel of x′ is the same, and the random source is the same, so the choices and the trajectory of x are the same; for the items not satisfied, see Sections Five to Nine. ∎  
The sameness asserted by the corollary extends only to the generation kernel of x′, the choices made and the trajectory of x, not to the complete output of the extended state: in (1) the shadow also writes out D′, which frozen running does not; in (2) the D written by the two is the same, but the evaluator differs.  
The corollary asserts only that such paired constructions exist, not that any two processes of the same kind are identical step by step.  
Hence Hold, ¬Intr, Carry, Sed and Multi cannot be determined by looking only at the output of each step or at the trajectory of x.

Corollary Three (evidence for discrimination)　That D is read in does not yield Gov (the log); that D changes within the segment is not a necessary condition (GS2), nor does it yield Sed (the scheduled temperature); that what follows is sensitive to history does not yield Sed (the lookup table compiled from logs); that the output trajectory of x is the same as that of some generative computation does not yield any of Hold, ¬Intr, Carry, Sed, Multi (Corollary Two); that there are functions named as sedimentation operators, or that the protocol permits rewriting D, does not yield Sed(i) (the like-named operators).

Thirteen: Minimality and Load-Bearing

Classification by load-bearing role: all seven items are forced items — Step refers back to the chip of Section Two, Gov to the rule programs of Section Three and the log and cache of Section Four, Sed(i) and (ii) to branch A of Section Five and (iii) to the bare rewriting within the segment and the shadow copy of D in branch B, ¬Intr to the external computation at junctions in Section Six, Carry to the external custody of Section Seven, Hold(a) and (b) to the shadow copy of the structure and the discarding of the last step's output in Section Eight and (c) to changing back at junctions, and Multi to the relay of Section Nine. Lemma One and Corollaries One to Three are local results. The inherited items are the general theorems of *The General Form of Computational Bearing* and *The General Form of Computational Execution* together with the corollaries cited, and, from upstream, field qualification, persistent field state, freezing, legitimate trajectory segment, Established, closure of current qualification (Theorem Two of *The Necessity Form of Law* v1.3, restricted to its corpus domain M1–M8, taking only its semantic content), inheritance of laws into sedimentation (C0 of *The General Form of Law* v2.5, carried by the necessity of the persistent-field-state clause; that form is further cited for the definition of Established and for its transplantation and lookup-table clauses), ActualHold, AdmVar and ActArg^μ. Their conditions of citation hold within the working domain: *The General Form of Computational Bearing* takes P's bearing τ\_Φ as antecedent, which is given by the task-side conditions; *The General Form of Computational Execution* takes one execution as its object, which is given by Step on each step; the upstream results on fields and laws take the declared field F and its protocol as their object, which is given by the declaration of F in the task-side conditions. The antecedent items are G1–G6, GS1–GS10, the task-side conditions, the working domain and the non-degeneracy condition. The conditional items are H1–H3, Computational Execution General Form-H1–H3, Computational Bearing General Form-H1–H3, and, from upstream, C0 of *The General Form of Law* v2.5 (extending only to the persistent-field-state clause of Sed(i)).

Independent degeneration: removing any one item while keeping the others in their original sense leaves, in each case, a non-generative computation that would be counted as generative computation.  
Remove Step: a network realizing a qualified field, in which, at each step, the sedimentary update D′ due under the protocol is evaluated on its behalf, within that step, by a machine outside the boundary according to the same sedimentation law, and written back; between steps the state is held by P, there is no external evaluation at the junctions, the changes of D all take the values prescribed by the protocol, D governs x′, what is written is what is read, and the run is the same — only Step is missing (¬Sub fails at that step). In the chip example of Section Two, the drift of the medium is not an operation of the field protocol, so Sed fails as well; the chip example therefore serves only to force out Step, not as a witness of independent degeneration.  
Remove Gov: a network realizing a qualified field, in which, through role masking by the protocol, sedimentation does not enter Continuation, the weights of the remaining terms in the Continuation kernel that read D are zero, and sedimentation is still written as usual — only Gov is missing.  
Remove Sed: the scheduled temperature, rule constants, like-named operators (missing (i)), bare rewriting within the segment (missing (iii)) — Gov and the other items hold, and only Sed is missing.  
Remove ¬Intr: external computation of shallowing at junctions — only ¬Intr is missing.  
Remove Carry: external custody between steps — only Carry is missing.  
Remove Hold: the shadow copy of the structure (the components of x missing (a)), changing the carried-over components back at junctions (missing (c)), discarding the structural output of the last step (for k = n, the components of x missing (a)) — only Hold is missing. The shadow copy of D also lacks Sed(iii), and does not serve as an independent witness for Hold.  
Remove Multi: relay — only Multi is missing; the distinction between relay and recovery is determined under Computational Execution General Form-H2, so the independent indispensability of Multi holds under that condition.

Non-mergeability: Gov and Sed (role masking satisfies Sed but not Gov; the scheduled temperature the reverse); Sed and ¬Intr (external computation at junctions satisfies Sed but not ¬Intr; bare rewriting within the segment the reverse); Step and ¬Intr (external computation at junctions satisfies Step but not ¬Intr; external computation of the present step's sedimentation the reverse); Carry and ¬Intr (external custody satisfies ¬Intr but not Carry; external computation at junctions the reverse); Carry and Step (external custody satisfies Step but not Carry; external computation of the present step's sedimentation the reverse); Carry and Hold (external custody satisfies Hold but not Carry; the shadow copy of the structure the reverse); Hold and Multi (the shadow copy of the structure satisfies Multi but not Hold; relay the reverse); Carry and Multi (external custody satisfies Multi but not Carry; relay the reverse). The three clauses of Sed and the three clauses of Hold each have a determinable sense only for one and the same D and one and the same junction, so Sed and Hold are taken as the units of the removal test; within Sed, bare rewriting satisfies (i) and (ii) but not (iii); within Hold, the shadow satisfies (c) but not (a), and changing back satisfies (a) but not (c).

Dependence: Gov, Carry, ¬Intr and Hold are all stated relative to the π of Step, Multi constrains the whole of σ and each step of π, and Sed concerns only D and the field. The order of proof indicates no temporal or causal order.

Each overstrong substitute excludes one class of standard positive cases: requiring D to be updated at every step (excludes GS2); requiring hold-breaking at every step (excludes the GS1 and GS2 cases driven purely by holding-tendency); requiring a new law in every segment, or requiring correctness of the result (excludes GS9); requiring D to come from the history of this instance (excludes GS3); requiring the initial value of D in the segment to be formed by actual Continuation (excludes GS4); requiring every step to be governed by potential (excludes GS10 if the generation kernel at its strong-input step does not vary with D; this depends on U3); requiring all the duties of the field to be realized by the host and allowing no external field-induction (excludes GS7 if external field-induction is counted among the field's duties; which of the field's duties must be realized by P is left to U4); requiring that no external action whatever enter the junctions (excludes the deliveries of GS6 and GS7); requiring a single process and allowing no recovery (excludes GS8); requiring the read-out to be observed (excludes unattended GS1). Any proposed addition tested in Section Ten, if added to the general formula, is one of these formulations.

The meaning of minimality: each item of the general formula is forced out by a case that keeps all the preceding items yet is not generative computation; under G1–G6 and the corresponding H, removing any item leaves a non-generative computation counted as generative computation; adding any of the seven proposed additions excluded by the standard positive cases in Section Ten excludes some standard positive case; the other two items (every step governed by potential, the field's duties realized by P) are listed as U3 and U4, and fall outside this closure. This is indispensability relative to the items forced out, and closure relative to the seven proposed additions above; writing N for the necessary structure on the right-hand side of the general formula (N₆ or N₇ of Part Four of the Appendix), take any condition Q that is not a tautology; then N ∨ Q is also a necessary condition and is weaker, so minimality can only be put forward relative to a family of admissible candidate structures — those whose components are facts of the host layer and the dynamics layer and which can exclude each failure case of Sections Two to Nine — and whether there is within it a weaker group of components is unanswered (U2). The general formula gives minimal necessity in function and structure; it does not prescribe a unique physical realization, nor the concrete construction of the field.

Breach Argument

Ways of breach: while the premises of the general theorem, G1–G6, the task-side conditions, the working domain and the corresponding H are kept, exhibit a stretch of generative computation on P for which there is no legitimate, individuable step partition and run making the seven items hold together; or exhibit a case among GS1–GS10 lacking one of Step, Carry, Sed, ¬Intr, Hold, Multi; or, under H2, exhibit one of these cases in which D is a distribution-bearing argument of x′ at no step. For minimality: prove that some two items can be merged, or that some item can be derived from the others together with the antecedents, or that some proposed addition excluded in Section Ten is in fact necessary. For Sed: exhibit a D lying on a persistent field state in a field satisfying FieldQualified, really entering generation and changing within the segment only through the protocol, that fails to be potential solely because of the source of its initial value in the segment — this would at the same time breach the transplantation clause of the upstream Established.

Counterexample One: a host deployed for inference only.  
The host realizes no sedimentation operator but only runs the Continuation kernel with the D obtained by training, and sedimentation takes place in another training system. Sed(ii) is ⊥: whether it realizes the frozen mode of some qualified field belongs to H3. Before H3 is determined, it is neither excluded by the general formula nor a breach. Where D is written into read-only storage and no coupling whatever is possible, this is a boundary case in the determination of H3.

Counterexample Two: one lookup table in two positions.  
A lookup table compiled from logs and written into the rule-configuration area is excluded by the persistent-field-state clause of Sed(i) (carrying C0 of *The General Form of Law* v2.5); written into the sedimentary bearing carrier of a qualified field, with the field's sedimentary duties taking it as their object in the protocol, it is not excluded by Sed, and is determined by the other items and by the upstream Established and Govern; where it is compiled and written step by step within the segment by an external program, it is excluded by ¬Intr or Step. This is not a breach: the distinction falls on the field qualification of the carrier, not on the content.

Counterexample Three: reservoir computing, LMS filtering and linear Kalman filtering.  
The internal random weights of a reservoir are not the object of any sedimentary duty, and are rule configuration; the read-out weights can be D in training. The weights of LMS are updated by the actual error, and whether they satisfy the items depends on their field qualification. The gain of a Kalman filter is given by a recursion independent of the data, no Trace-Response writes into it, and as D it is excluded by the persistent-field-state clause of Sed(i) (carrying C0 of *The General Form of Law* v2.5). Whether the first two, when they satisfy the items, are generative computation belongs to sufficiency (U1); they do not breach the necessary direction.

Counterexample Four: segments of purely external Trace-Response.  
The route at each step is given entirely by delivery, the Continuation kernel is not used, and the field only receives Trace-Response from external action. D does not enter the generation of x′, so under H2 the segment is excluded by Gov. If D's governing its own increment (sedimentation saturating with existing depth) is held to count as a dynamical condition too, Gov would have to be extended to D′; such an extension must first distinguish purely Trace-Response updating from real governance by the dynamical condition of its own subsequent increment, and must recheck that ordinary self-recursive state machines and scheduled parameters are not admitted (U5).

Counterexample Five: mixed computation.  
Of a hundred steps, only one is governed by sedimentary dynamics, and the rest are executed by ordinary rules. It is not excluded by Gov; whether the whole segment is generative computation belongs to U3; the case does not breach the necessary direction.

Counterexample Six: fields that do not compute.  
The dynamics of the brain's Sedimentation-Field, the physical drift of a medium. They do not satisfy G1 and are not among the objects of this chapter; they are a boundary, not a breach.

Counterexample Seven: paired processes with identical step-by-step Continuation.  
The five pairs of processes of Corollary Two have the same output trajectories but different classifications. This is not a breach but indistinguishability at the effect level: the differences the general formula distinguishes do not lie in the output.

Hence: none of these — many steps, changing parameters, sensitivity to history — makes a stretch of computation generative computation; a stretch of generative computation must, at minimum, on top of step-by-step execution, be held by the host itself, carried over by the host itself, and proceed within one and the same run; and under H2, the potential of sedimentary bearing in the field must, at some step, really enter the generation of the next structure.

Closing

Compressed finally into three sentences:

Bearing settles "can it compute,"  
Execution settles "did it compute this step,"  
Generative computation settles "in the step it computed, was history pushing?"

Compressed again into the simplest single sentence:

Without execution, there is no step of generative computation; under H2, without potential in sedimentation really entering the generation of the next step, no amount of execution is generative computation; without the host itself holding, carrying over and proceeding within one and the same run, it is not this host's generative computation.

Appendix: The General Form of Generative Computation · Formalization

This appendix gives the formal objects and formal statements of the general form of generative computation, so that the structures, propositions and theorems of the main text can be checked item by item at the formal level.

One. Basic Objects and Types

P: a computing host, with boundary, entry, read-out, delivery protocols, operating conditions and declared period Θ declared as in *The General Form of Computational Bearing*.  
τ\_Φ: a one-step task, with operand (x, D, u) and result (x′, D′), having the extension of its function (or kernel) as its identity.  
s = (x, D): the declared extended state; x = (x\_carry, x\_input, x\_inst) — carried-over components (written out by step k and read by step k+1), input slots (filled by declared delivery), instantaneous quantities (computed from the current state within one step, not carried over across steps); D is a composite quantity whose variable domain Dom(D) contains all persistent components of F and the derived quantities updated along with them, the domain being frozen before σ.  
c: the exogenous control configuration (running mode, Mask, Avail and so on), belonging neither to x nor to D, not written by sedimentation and not subject to shallowing, changed only through permitted interventions in Prot, and kept across steps until the next intervention (*The Construction Form of the Law Operating Substrate* v1.2); the sequence of values of c at each step and junction is read out together with σ. To which of x\_carry, x\_input, x\_inst, D, c each component belongs is fixed junction by junction according to the declared protocol and declared before σ (one and the same structural position may be an input slot at some junction and a carried-over component at the others, as with a position given by entry delivery at the start of a pulse); the uniqueness of the classification belongs to H1(a).  
σ ⊆ Θ: a stretch of actual process on P.  
π = ((s\_k, e\_k))\_{k=1..n}: the step partition of σ; t\_end: the declared end point of σ; s\_{n+1} ≔ t\_end; J\_k ≔ (e\_k, s\_{k+1}), k = 1, …, n (J\_n being the final segment): junctions; In\_{n+1} ≔ Fin.  
Cover(σ, π) ⟺ σ = \[s\_1, t\_end\] and σ = ⋃\_{k=1}^{n} (\[s\_k, e\_k\] ∪ J\_k), the interiors of the parts being pairwise disjoint, end points being allowed to coincide; J\_k is empty when s\_{k+1} = e\_k, and the final segment is empty when e\_n = t\_end, both being allowed. Events at a shared end point are assigned by their identity: e\_k is the read-out event of step k and belongs to step k; s\_{k+1} is the initiation event of step k+1 and belongs to step k+1.  
In what follows, wherever J\_k, In\_{k+1} or the initial state of step k+1 is involved, ∀k means ∀k ∈ {1, …, n}; wherever the initiation of step k+1 is involved, ∀k means ∀k ∈ {1, …, n−1}.  
RunOf(r, P): r is one run on one and the same instance of P.  
Fin: the declared final-state carrier (where the extended state lies when σ ends, and the read position for Continuation after σ).  
In\_k, Out\_k: the state carrier of step k (the position from which (x, D) is read at the head of the step) and its output carrier (the position into which (x′, D′) is written at e\_k), declared component by component before σ; Car ≔ ⋃\_k (In\_k ∪ Out\_k), including lineages of storage-only and transfer-only copying; Link(A, B) means that A and B are the same position or are connected by storage-only or transfer-only copying. Out\_k is an output carrier, distinct from the execution item Out (reaching read-out) of *The General Form of Computational Execution*.  
r: a continuous stretch of actual process on one and the same instance of P (a run in the sense of *The General Form of Computational Execution*).  
K\_k(· \| x, D, u; c): the generation kernel of x′ at step k, c being the exogenous control configuration of that step. c is a declared control parameter of the one-step task, whose rules of change are fixed by the declared legitimate modes; it is not a sedimentary dynamical condition; when generation kernels under the same mode are compared, c is fixed, and switching across modes must obey the declared protocol, and a change of c may not be passed off as D's bearing role. TV: total variation; ε\_role: the declared resolution.  
F: a Sedimentation-Field; Prot\_F: the protocol of the field (sedimentation, shallowing, freezing, thawing, role masking and so on); FieldQualified(F): F is a Sedimentation-Field in the sense of *Sedimentation-Field Theory* v1.2 (Sedimentability Nature, Organizability Nature, State-Flux Nature), that is, the field presupposed by *The General Form of Law* v2.5; Pers(F): the set of persistent field states of F.  
AdmVar, ActArg^μ: as in Definitions 3.9′ and 3.10′ of *The Mathematical Form of Law* v1.5.  
Path(σ) ≔ (ρ\_k)\_{k=1..n}, where ρ\_k is the actual transition of the execution of step k — from the x read in on In\_k (together with the content entering the input slots by delivery legitimate under T1 ∧ T2) to the x′ read out at e\_k as the result of that step (that is, the route projection π\_tr(Y\_k) of that step's output Y\_k, 3.7 and 4.3 of *The Mathematical Form of Law-Dynamics* v1.0). Candidates not read out as results within a step, hypothetical branches and replay probes are not included. ρ\_k is not conditional on x′ being taken up by step k+1 (carry-over belongs to Hold).  
Legal(σ, F): conditional on Path(σ), the sequence of values of D read from the state carrier of each step and at the junctions satisfies the update equation of Prot\_F for D under the declared mode (Definition One of *The General Form of Law-Dynamics* v1.0 applied on the projection onto D) — every change is a protocol operation, and every update the declared mode requires carries the earlier D into the later D. D contains all persistent components of F and their derived quantities; the whole trajectory of F together with x is not required to be legitimate.

Two. Legitimacy and Individuability

LegalPart(π) ⟺ Cover(σ, π) ∧ ∀k ∈ {1, …, n}: s\_k ∈ Ctrl(P) ∪ Prot (issued by P's own control or by the declared protocol), and e\_k is the event at which the result of step k is written or latched into Out\_k.  
Exec\_Φ(I): the interval I is one execution of τ\_Φ by P (in the sense of *The General Form of Computational Execution*).  
Working domain: the interiors of the Exec\_Φ intervals in σ are pairwise disjoint, the initiation of a later interval is no earlier than the read-out of the earlier one, and the read-out end point of the earlier interval may coincide with the initiation end point of the later one (end-point events being assigned as laid down in Cover).  
The step partition delimited by executions: π\_Exec takes the initiations and read-outs of the Exec\_Φ intervals in σ as step boundaries. By G1 and the task-side declaration, every one-step transformation of the extended state in σ is an Exec\_Φ interval; by the working domain, π\_Exec is a step partition.

Three. Formal Statements of the Seven Items

Step(σ, π) ⟺ LegalPart(π) ∧ ∀k ∈ {1, …, n}: Ini(\[s\_k, e\_k\]) ∧ Run(\[s\_k, e\_k\]) ∧ Out(\[s\_k, e\_k\]) (step-by-step declaration; the two clauses of Ex conditional on Computational Execution General Form-H3).

Gov(σ, π) ⟺ ∃k ∈ {1, …, n}: D ∈ ActArg^μ(x′\_k), D as a composite argument taking values in Dom(D), the counterfactuals drawn from AdmVar, at resolution ε\_role.  
Comparability lemma: if (x, D₁, u; c), (x, D₂, u; c) ∈ AdmVar, the two are consistent in field state, the arguments other than D actually read by the generation kernel, and the control configuration c, take the same values, and TV(K\_k(·\|x, D₁, u; c), K\_k(·\|x, D₂, u; c)) \> ε\_role, then D ∈ ActArg^μ(x′\_k). The counterfactuals of Gov vary only D, c being fixed at the configuration in which that step actually stands.

Sed(σ) ⟺ (i) FieldQualified(F) ∧ D ∈ Pers(F) ∧ D is not an immediate driving quantity ∧ D is not a configuration constant in the rules of τ\_Φ; (ii) ∃ a mode m ∈ Prot\_F: the sedimentary duties of m take D as their object (reading D in and giving its update according to the protocol), and m is realized by P, or by P together with a declared sedimentation implementer through coupling (H3); (iii) Legal(σ, F): every change is an operation in Prot\_F, and every update the declared mode requires actually takes place.  
For the deployment-coupling kind before H3 is determined: Sed = ⊥; where the operators in a declared sedimentation mode lack the behaviour of a field: Sed = 0.

¬Intr(σ, π) ⟺ ∀k ∈ {1, …, n}: there is no process Q outside the boundary such that Q, within J\_k, carries out the evaluation (or part of it) of D or of the carried-over components of x, and the state read at s\_{k+1} depends on this work of Q.  
Delivery permission: T1 ∧ T2. T1: by the update equation of the protocol declared for σ at J\_k, the content of this slot at step k+1 does not take the output of step k (the slot belongs to x\_input at J\_k). T2: the delivered content is not obtained by an evaluation of Φ outside the boundary on the state of P in this segment, and the delivery protocol is declared before the run under test.

Carry(σ, π) ⟺ ∀k ∈ {1, …, n}: within J\_k, x\_carry and D are held by P, and D is not an input delivered from outside the boundary at each step.

Hold(σ, π) ⟺ ∀k ∈ {1, …, n}: (a) on every component of x\_carry and D, Link(Out\_k, In\_{k+1}) (for k = n, In\_{n+1} ≔ Fin, that is, the final-state check extends only to x\_carry and D; x\_inst is recomputed at the next step, and c is read out separately); (b) pr\_{x\_carry, D}(the initial state at s\_{k+1}) = pr\_{x\_carry, D}(the result of applying the action within J\_k to the state written out at e\_k) — only the persistent carried-over projection is compared: changes of D at junctions must satisfy Sed(iii), x\_input is obtained by legitimate delivery, x\_inst is recomputed within the subsequent execution, and c evolves separately according to the protocol; (c) within J\_k, x\_carry is not rewritten by P or by Prot; within J\_k, x changes only on x\_input, through declared delivery; actions of Prot on c or D are not subject to this restriction, the former being permitted by the protocol and the latter determined by Sed(iii) (momentary external disturbances counting as distortion).

Multi(σ, π, r) ⟺ n ≥ 2 ∧ RunOf(r, P) ∧ σ ⊆ r ∧ ∀k ∈ {1, …, n}: \[s\_k, e\_k\] ⊆ r ∧ ∀k ∈ {1, …, n−1}: s\_{k+1} is issued within r by P's own dynamics or by the declared protocol; r is one continuous stretch of process, not restarted from an initial state, such as one produced by reset, that does not belong to r.

Four. The General Theorem (Formal)

Write N₆(σ, π, r) ≔ Step(σ, π) ∧ Carry(σ, π) ∧ Sed(σ) ∧ ¬Intr(σ, π) ∧ Hold(σ, π) ∧ Multi(σ, π, r), and N₇(σ, π, r) ≔ N₆(σ, π, r) ∧ Gov(σ, π). Under the task-side conditions, the working domain and G1–G6:  
(1) GenComp(P, σ) ⇒ ∃π ∃r \[LegalPart(π) ∧ RunOf(r, P) ∧ N₆(σ, π, r)\] (the two clauses of Ex in Step carrying Computational Execution General Form-H3, the clause of Sed(i) on persistent field states carrying C0 of *The General Form of Law* v2.5, and Sed(ii), for the deployment-coupling kind, carrying H3);  
(2) H2 ∧ GenComp(P, σ) ⇒ ∃π ∃r \[LegalPart(π) ∧ RunOf(r, P) ∧ N₇(σ, π, r)\], and the witnesses π, r of (1) are witnesses of (2).  
(1) does not depend on H2; the Gov item of (2) depends on H2.

Five. Conditions of Non-Generative Computation

By the contrapositives of (1) and (2) of Part Four of this appendix:  
(1′) ∀π ∀r \[LegalPart(π) ∧ RunOf(r, P) ⇒ ¬N₆(σ, π, r)\] ⇒ ¬GenComp(P, σ);  
(2′) under H2, ∀π ∀r \[LegalPart(π) ∧ RunOf(r, P) ⇒ ¬N₇(σ, π, r)\] ⇒ ¬GenComp(P, σ).  
Exclusion must hold for every legitimate pair π, r; failure on one step partition or one run does not exclude holding on another legitimate step partition and run.  
For given π, r, the concrete ways in which ¬N₆ or ¬N₇ fails are as follows (item 2 is used only for (2′); in item 3, the two clauses D ∉ Pers(F) and configuration constant carry C0 of *The General Form of Law* v2.5; item 4, for the deployment-coupling kind, awaits H3). The nine items are the unfolding of ¬N₆ and ¬N₇ on given π, r, not independent theorems each holding unconditionally; any item holding on some π, r gives only ¬N on that π, r, not ¬GenComp:  
1\. some step of π does not satisfy Ini, Run, Out;  
2\. for every step k of π, D ∉ ActArg^μ(x′\_k);  
3\. ¬FieldQualified(F), or D ∉ Pers(F), or D is a configuration constant;  
4\. in the declared system there is no mode of Prot\_F whose sedimentary duties take D as their object;  
5\. ¬Legal(σ, F);  
6\. within some J\_k (k ≤ n, including the final segment) a process outside the boundary evaluates D or x\_carry and what follows depends on it;  
7\. within some J\_k, x\_carry or D is not held by P;  
8\. for some k ≤ n and some component, ¬Link(Out\_k, In\_{k+1}) (In\_{n+1} ≔ Fin), or within some J\_k the carried-over components x\_carry of x are rewritten by P or by Prot (protocol updates of D are not included here, being determined by item 5);  
9\. π has only one step (n \< 2), or σ does not belong as a whole to r.  
Items 3–5 do not depend on π and r, and if any of them holds it holds for all π, r; items 1, 2 and 6–9 must hold separately for each legitimate π (item 9 together with each r) in order to give exclusion.

Six. Classification of Runs

In what follows, "fails" means fails for every legitimate, individuable step partition and run.

Multi-step rule programs, cellular automata: Gov fails. Logs that only record, caches that only change the route: Gov fails. (Excluding generative computation by the failure of Gov is by (2′), carrying H2.) Scheduled temperatures and learning-rate schedules as D: Sed(i) fails. Rule constants, lookup tables in the configuration area, like-named operators, set-tables, global counters: Sed(i) fails. (For both of these kinds, generative computation is excluded by the failure of the persistent-field-state clause of Sed(i), by (1′), carrying C0 of *The General Form of Law* v2.5; among them, the carriers of like-named operators lack the behaviour of a field under the action of the operators and are further excluded by ¬FieldQualified(F), a clause not passing through C0.) Bare rewriting within the segment, and P itself changing D back at a junction to an old or preset value: Sed(iii) fails. The shadow copy of D (in the switched-on mode, the update not landing on the state carrier): Sed(iii) fails, and Hold(a) on the components of D fails as well. Proxy evaluation outside the boundary at junctions: ¬Intr fails. The D′ of the present step evaluated by proxy outside the boundary: Step fails (¬Sub at that step). Reading and writing external storage directly within a step: P does not bear a τ\_Φ whose read and write positions are carriers outside the boundary (Leg₁ of *The General Form of Computational Bearing*, carrying Computational Bearing General Form-H1), and Step fails accordingly. Custody by external storage between steps: Carry fails (it is the generative computation of P together with the external storage). The shadow copy of the structure, changing the carried-over components back at junctions, the last step's output discarded or changed back in the final segment: Hold fails. Relay (not declared as a recovery protocol): Multi fails (Computational Execution General Form-H2). Reset at every step: Hold and Multi fail. Deployed inference only: Sed(ii) is ⊥, awaiting H3. All seven items hold: not excluded; for those that are generative computation by the standard positive cases, see GS1–GS10, and the classification of the rest belongs to U1. The kinds may combine; where they do, the first item that fails is taken as the ground of exclusion.
