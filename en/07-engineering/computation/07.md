# The General Form of Computational Layering

This chapter attempts a general proposition:

If a stretch of computation σ on a computing host P makes history become dynamics, into how many layers at least is its necessary structure divided? And if it goes further and makes a law continue in the system borne by P, into how many layers at least?

*The General Form of Computational Bearing* gave what a host must minimally have in order to bear a task, *The General Form of Computational Execution* gave what one execution must minimally have, and *The General Form of Generative Computation* gave six items that a stretch of generative computation must minimally have, together with governance by potential under that chapter's H2, seven in all. *The General Form of Generative Computation* delimits generative computation by G1–G6, of which G6 stipulates: "Computation acts on the structures, relations and dynamical conditions of the field, standing in the driving layer; the target law is generated by the field's dynamics, and computation makes no direct contribution to its result." There, the placement in layers is a stipulation, not a conclusion.

This group form's initial stipulation on computation says: computation drives the dynamics of the Sedimentation-Field, and the dynamics of the Sedimentation-Field generates laws and Principles; this is layering (initial stipulation, item 7); it also says: computation makes no direct contribution to the result of a law, the two being separated by the dynamics layer (initial stipulation, the latter clause of item 5). The fundamental difference between this chapter and Chapter 5 is this: Chapter 5 takes the placement in layers as one of its stipulations, while this chapter takes it as a conclusion pending verification. This chapter gives only the duties of generation, not layers, nor in advance the relations that discriminate layers; it first asks what structure these duties minimally force out, and, once the structure has closed, tests it against the generative hierarchy of the initial stipulation: what qualification each level has obtained, whether that qualification can be borne by the level below, and hence whether there is layering and into how many layers.

How far this question is answered must be stated in advance. First edition. General conclusion: the necessary direction, restricted to 𝔇. Without objects, at least two layers: the host layer and the dynamics layer, carrying Computational Execution General Form-H3, Generative Computation General Form-H1, H2 and this chapter's H1. When a law continues, at least three layers — the host layer, the dynamics layer and the object layer, carrying in addition H\_G of the Law Group-Form, the bearing declarations of E5 and the initial restriction E5\_host, together with H\_cert and this chapter's H2; the exact figures of two and three layers depend on the convention for counting layers of Section Eleven. This is the minimum number of layers of the declared chain of generative qualification, not the number of functional layers of any concrete computing system. The object layer does not require a separate, independent physical mechanism, and may be realized by the host through dynamics; it is the level at which the formed organization of dynamics obtains law qualification.

In this chapter we shall find, through persistent questioning, where the proof must go, and derive the general form of computational layering.

The action of law is actually realized by the host and borne through dynamics, and this does not prevent law from being the level above in the generative hierarchy.

Abstract

*The General Form of Generative Computation* has already shown that a stretch of generative computation must have seven items: step-by-step execution, governance by potential, potential residing in sedimentation, no intrusion at junctions, host holding, carry-over of what is changed, and the same run (governance by potential carrying Generative Computation General Form-H2). But it stipulates through G6 that computation stands in the driving layer, and the placement in layers itself has not been proved. So the question presses on: with the stipulation of placement in layers removed, and only the two duties "history becomes dynamics" and "a law continues" left, is the structure still layered? Into how many layers?

A pure-function program (computed out) has no cross-step factors: where two current states are the same, what follows must be the same; so there must be cross-step factors beyond the comparison projection that enter execution. A frame-by-frame simulation of a ball bouncing off a wall (run out) has cross-step states, but they are states of the rules, not deepening with passage nor shallowing with little use; so what bears the differences must be sedimented by actual passage. The physical Trace-Response of a storage device itself deepens or shallows according to passage, but is not driven by this stretch of execution; so the attribution of E3 must be applied to the sedimentary updates within the segment: the sedimentary updates bearing the witness must be driven by execution (W). The history bearing the differences must enter what comes later; by E2 and the definition of cross-step factors, what is sedimented must be read in by subsequent executions (Rd). At this point the duties without objects close: the execution S of a fixed transformation relation, and the historical condition D, one that satisfies Sedimentability Nature, is read in by execution and is rewritten within the segment by execution — sedimentation fixes where the flow goes, and the flow in turn rewrites sedimentation. This to and fro is the very meaning of the dynamics layer; rule computation can also have feedback and state updates, but feedback by itself does not constitute sedimentary dynamics.

Turning to law. A law written in the rules is a hostless law, so the bearer of the law must lie in the sedimentation within the field and be read in through execution (G\_loc); yet the new groove of one strong impulse lies in the field and is read in, and still does not become a law, so the bearer must further be formed and have obtained Govern (O). The facts of computational implementation — the content of the rules, the reproduction of trajectories — cannot substitute for the field bearing that law requires.

Layering is tested by the generative hierarchy and by qualification. Between host and dynamics: fixed rules cannot bear history, and what is computed out or run out is complete computation with a host but no dynamics. Between dynamics and law: the new groove left by one strong impulse has entered the field and really governs the next step, yet does not re-walk stably — dynamics is present, law is not formed, and dynamics stays at simple running; the upstream judgement kernel delivers its verdict at the stable re-walk link. Conversely, an organization compressed by repeated passage until it shows stable re-walk within the declared window obtains law qualification (conditionally, carrying H\_cert; an upstream positive case; a strong-impulse field whose read sedimentation is the same as that of a formed law but which does not form a law is, under re-entry, through the reaction of the dynamics layer itself, judged by the judgement kernel to turn from FAIL to PASS, its rise to a law carrying H\_cert). The qualification of law cannot be borne by a dynamical organization that has not obtained it; where the field has qualified sedimentation but Continuation is prescribed by the program's rules, the sedimentation does not bear the law either. So, under the conditions listed in parts (1) and (2) of the general theorem, without objects there are at least two layers; when a law continues, at least three: each rise by one level must fully obtain one declared generative relation for which the organization was not previously qualified, and this chapter declares only two, "history becomes dynamics" and "the law is established," so within this chapter's working domain there are also at most three levels; the satisfiability of a continuing law is given, under the upstream read-back antecedents and this chapter's attribution conditions, by the positive cases of the operating substrate. The reaction of law through its holding-tendency is actually realized by the host and borne through dynamics, which does not change the position of law in the generative hierarchy. What is obtained here is the minimum number of layers of the declared chain of generative qualification, not the number of functional layers of any computing system.

The core conclusion is this: computation drives dynamics, and dynamics generates laws; this is the layering of which the initial stipulation speaks; with the stipulation of placement in layers removed, it is forced out by the duties of generation and established by the test of the non-substitutability of each level's qualification. What the latter clause of item 5 of the initial stipulation says — that computation makes no direct contribution to the result of a law, the two being separated by the dynamics layer — thereby obtains, within a restricted working domain, a conditional theorematic statement (Corollary Two); the clause itself remains an initial stipulation, serving only for reading back.

Introduction

To say that a machine "has computed out a law" can mean several different things: it has given the results of the law by rule; it has simulated the process of the law step by step by rule; it has set the dynamics of a field running, and the law has grown out of that dynamics and holds. The initial stipulation calls these three computed out, run out and generated.

There are three readings of "how many layers separate computation from law," each giving a different number of layers.

The first: computation gives the law directly. There is only one layer, and the law is a product of computation. This is the reading of superstition about computing power; the initial stipulation denies it: one cannot say that computation directly computed out the question-answer law in GPT (initial stipulation, item 7).

The second: dynamics is nothing but the state of a program. A program runs on carrying cross-step variables, and the so-called historical conditions are only states of rule computation, merged into the host; above computation there is only law. There are two layers: computation and law.

The third: law is nothing but dynamics. The initial stipulation also says that objects are in essence dynamics too, and that if they are not recognized they remain dynamical transformation (initial stipulation, item 12); if there is law wherever there is dynamics, or if the action of law, being realized through dynamics, is on the same level as dynamics, objects are merged into dynamics. There are two layers: computation and dynamics.

The initial stipulation of computation is: computation drives the dynamics of the Sedimentation-Field, and the dynamics of the Sedimentation-Field generates laws and Principles; this is layering (initial stipulation, item 7). It also speaks of two reactions: after the water has flowed, it erodes the network of channels in turn, and this is the generation of the dynamics layer (initial stipulation, item 9); part of the holding-tendency is judged to be law, and objects act back on dynamics through their holding, selecting, biasing, breaking and other actions (initial stipulation, item 10). The four readings can be entirely the same in output: a rule program can reproduce the whole trajectory of a law frame by frame, and so can a network in training. So the question cannot be answered by looking at outputs.

So the question is no longer: can computation reproduce law?

Nor is it only: in which layer does computation stand?

It is an earlier question: if only the duties of generation are given, and not layers, will the structure forced out collapse into two layers, or even one?

Preliminary Distinctions

One. Layers. The layers spoken of in this chapter are the generative hierarchy: the lower level drives and generates the upper level, the upper level obtains a qualification that the lower level lacks, and in turn constrains the lower level (initial stipulation, the former clause of item 5, items 7, 9 and 10). The unit of counting layers is the generative hierarchy and the relation of reaction of which the initial stipulation speaks: layers are distinguished by the generative qualification each level has obtained, not counted by position in the representation of computation (transformation relation, arguments), nor by the number of physical mechanisms, variables, computing cores or stores, nor by the number of logical atoms or task clauses; the placement in layers is invariant under the way logical formulas are split, the naming of variables and the merging of execution mechanisms. One and the same program, store or carrier can comprise several levels at once. The upper level need not have a mode of execution feedback that the lower level lacks: the object layer and the dynamics layer can have the same actual flux within a legitimate comparison, their difference lying in qualification. The arrow of generation denotes taking-over, not that the lower level necessarily generates the upper. Nor do layers mean processes causally independent of one another: the action of the upper level is actually realized by the host and borne through the lower level; the distinction of layers is not conditional on the upper level having a separate execution mechanism or being causally independent of the lower level.  
No discriminating relation for layers, and no number of layers, is stipulated here. Whether layers hold, and how many there are, is tested after the Line of Argument has closed (Section Eleven).

Two. Host, host layer and rule computation. The initial stipulation: host rules are the Trace-Response relation between signal and host medium, and the dynamical conditions are the current conditions at each occurrence of Trace-Response that produces a transformation (initial stipulation, item 15); generative computation contains rule computation, and rule computation takes place in the host layer of generative computation (initial stipulation, item 16). The host P is the system that bears computation; the term host layer means the fixed transformation relation on P and its execution, not hardware. Rule computation, by the initial stipulation, is rule transformation: each execution node is a fixed rule. What the host layer of generative computation executes are the basic rules of the host (the Trace-Response relation, sedimentation laws), which contain reaction rules — deepening with passage, shallowing with little use; the rules are written in the host, and by these rules the host can automatically generate the dynamics layer (initial stipulation, item 12). It is not a task of simulating the rules of an external object, nor does it specify in advance which law will be generated. Computation itself has states: it proceeds from input through state, transformation and state again, in turn, until output (initial stipulation, item 1); the states of rule computation — the position and velocity of the ball, counters, caches — belong to computation itself.

Three. Dynamical conditions and dynamical rules. The rules are written in the host, and are fixed; the dynamical conditions are carved by history under the rules, and change with the flow. In neural plasticity, places through which the flow often passes deepen, and places through which it seldom passes become shallow (initial stipulation, item 11); this is the rule, and the current depth of each connection is the dynamical condition. The two can be realized in one and the same program and store, but in duty they are not the same thing.

Four. Object layer and recognition. The initial stipulation says that objects are in essence dynamics too, only having gradually formed an object form; we recognize objects at this layer, and if they are not recognized they remain dynamical transformation (initial stipulation, item 12); it also says that part of the holding-tendency is judged to be law (initial stipulation, item 10). There are two things here: the object form takes shape in the field; an Observer recognizes it and judges it. The O of this chapter denotes only the former — the formed organization of a law in the field and its governance, taken from the Law Group-Form as Established ∧ Govern; this is a fact of the field, not conditional on recognition; recognition and judgement belong to other group forms. The object form comes about by dynamics being compressed into stability (initial stipulation, item 12: the dynamics layer thereby gradually compresses input and produces objects).

Five. The duties of generation and generative computation. This chapter's duty Φ is not Chapter 5's GenComp: Φ contains neither G6 nor the structural content of G1–G5, but only the initial stipulation's stipulations on generation (E1–E5 of Section One of the Line of Argument). The criteria of the items of Chapter 5 can be cited, but its general theorem has G6 as an antecedent and is not a premise in this chapter.

Six. Continuation and formation; training and field-induction. The general proposition asks about Continuation: what a stretch of computation must minimally have while it bears the duties. How a law grows from nothing belongs to formation, whose minimal structure is listed as unanswered (U6); the leap witness of Section Nine is used for distinguishing levels. Adjusting weights by error in training is field-induction, not a basic rule of the Sedimentation-Field (*The General Form of Field-Induction in the Self-Manifestation-Field*): it changes the routes of flow from outside, making some regions deeper and some shallower, while the rules of the Sedimentation-Field remain unchanged.

The three names "host layer," "dynamics layer" and "object layer" are used as names of layers only after the test of Section Eleven; until then, they are called only S, D and O.

Four

κ\_sed has been obtained. It varies with passage — is it this stretch of execution that makes it vary?

Take a storage device that has its own physical Trace-Response: the more current passes, the greater the conductance, and when current has not passed for a long time it falls back. This physical process is not within the declared implementation of τ, and exists only as an environmental property of the device; the execution of τ does not drive it, and its occurrence does not depend on the present initiation. P executes τ as usual, τ reads these cells, and the drift of the device makes what follows differ with history. E2 holds; the difference is within P, not written from outside the boundary; the difference deepens or shallows according to passage, and E4 holds.

It does not bear Φ(∅): the difference is produced by physical changes of the device, not by the execution of σ, so Φ cannot be attributed to σ, and E3 fails. These are exactly the two kinds of dynamics the initial stipulation distinguishes: in a computing host, dynamics occurs when a computational transformation is really carried out through the rules (initial stipulation, item 15); in a non-computing host, dynamics occurs in the material interaction of signal and medium. The drift of the device is of the latter kind, even though it occurs in a computing device. The standing of this example is that of a degeneration witness of E3(ii): it lies within P and within the run, satisfies the "not outside the boundary, not outside the run" requirement and the current execution contribution of E3(i), and κ\_sed varies with passage as usual; if the duties had only E3(i), it would be counted as bearing Φ(∅). E3(ii) — that the within-segment formation of the historical conditions on which the witness depends must be dynamics of a computing host (initial stipulation, item 15) — is exactly the clause that excludes it. Below, W is derived from E3(ii), with no new requirement set up beyond E3.

W (within-segment sedimentation is driven by execution): for a witness chain χ of E2, the update events of κ\_sed on χ within σ are all driven by some execution in σ — the present initiation is one of the actual causes of the update (the Ex of *The General Form of Computational Execution*, carrying that form's H3). What W constrains are the sedimentary updates occurring within σ on the witness chain borne by the declared execution, not all state updates in P, not updates on all witness chains, and not the formation before σ of the historical conditions on which the witness depends. Physical state transitions legitimately declared as constituents of the implementation of τ and driven by the present execution are driven by execution; physical changes of the device not within the declared implementation and occurring independently of the present initiation are not — if such changes bear the within-segment formation of χ, they still fall within the scrutiny of W, and make W fail. When there are no such update events within σ (frozen running, read-only deployment), W holds trivially.

Its necessity: E2 is an existential proposition; E3(ii) requires that, if the historical conditions on which the witness depends are formed or changed within σ, this formation be driven by execution. If a witness chain has updates of κ\_sed within σ not driven by execution, the within-segment formation of that witness is a physical change of the medium itself (this example), or a change from outside the boundary or outside the run, and does not satisfy E3(ii); it may still have a current execution contribution of σ (E3(i)) — the execution does read this difference — but this does not make up for (ii). By Ex, a change is attributed to execution if and only if the present initiation is one of the actual causes of its present evolution. So a Φ(∅) satisfying E3 has at least one witness chain whose updates of κ\_sed within σ are all driven by execution: W must hold. This derivation uses the constraint of E3(ii) on within-segment formation; it does not extend the attribution of current evolution by Ex to all events of the history of sedimentation: autonomous formation before σ is not constrained by W.  
Mixed modes are not excluded: during the execution of σ, some components of the field state in P may change autonomously, while execution reads them as usual and governs what follows; as long as there is another witness chain wholly attributed to execution, Φ(∅) holds as usual. Autonomous changes are not counted among the sedimentary results of σ; if the witness of E2 can be borne only by them, E3 fails.

The form of realization is unrestricted: digital writes and legitimately declared physical implementations can both satisfy W; the distinction falls on attribution, not on physical form. How the value of κ\_sed at the head of the segment came about — training, moving in, writing in — is not within W (the transplantation clause). W does not require the extension of τ to contain sedimentation laws. Note: W holding trivially shows only that there are no updates within the segment not attributed to execution; it does not show that sedimentation occurred within the segment, nor does it certify the field qualification of κ\_sed; for the latter, see Section Six.

Registered: W is the spelling-out of E3(ii) on the sedimentary updates of the witness chain, its criterion inherited from the Ex of *The General Form of Computational Execution*, and is not counted as a new item. What this section obtains is this: sedimentation in the device's environment witnesses the necessity of E3(ii), and W applies (ii) to the sedimentary update events themselves within σ on one and the same witness chain χ.

For cases with no update within the segment, one more question must be asked: in frozen running and read-only deployment, W holds trivially and Rd certifies only that something is read; what then bears the Sedimentability Nature that E4 requires? Under this condition, the unclosed points of each clause of E2, E3 and E4 have all been closed, and E1 is given by inheritance. There is no longer any constraint opened by E1–E4 and not yet closed. It closes; the forcing of items stops.

By E1 and extensional identity, τ is fixed within Θ, which is inherited; Sections Three to Five make κ\_sed an argument that satisfies Sedimentability Nature, is updated within the segment as driven by execution, and is read in by execution. These two kinds of bearing together make up the very meaning of the dynamics layer: Rd is the channel fixing the flow — sedimentation is read in as an argument and fixes where what follows flows; W and Pass are the flow reshaping the channel — what is actually passed through is sedimented through execution, rewriting the values read thereafter. The former is the qualification of D, which any σ satisfying Φ(∅) must have; the latter occurs only in switched-on segments. Nine

O has been obtained, and the generative side closes on E1–E5. The initial stipulation says of generation not only "driving" and "generating" but also "reaction," and two reactions at that:  
First, the dynamics layer itself: after the water has flowed, it erodes the network of channels in turn, and subsequent flows go on interacting with the network of channels in this way; this process shows itself as the dynamics of the network of channels, and this is the generation of the dynamics layer (initial stipulation, item 9).  
Second, objects: changes of dynamics generate objects, and objects act back on dynamics through their holding, selecting, biasing, breaking and other actions, so that subsequent flows can only go along certain tendencies; these motions all really come from the host (initial stipulation, item 10).  
Now we ask: where does each of the two reactions lie in the structure already forced out; does either open a new item; and what distinguishes the levels of generation? This working domain contains only law, so for the reaction of objects only "holding" is tested; selecting, biasing and breaking belong to Principle, Probability and Intention, in U3.

The first is the very meaning of the dynamics layer. The Rd obtained in Sections Five and Six is the channel fixing the flow — sedimentation is read in by execution as an argument and fixes where what follows flows; W and Pass are the flow reshaping the channel — what is actually passed through is sedimented through execution, rewriting the values read thereafter. Together they make one to and fro: after reaction the potential has changed from before, and subsequent flows interact with it again. Rule computation can have feedback, state updates and even self-modification, but feedback by itself does not constitute sedimentary dynamics: what is run out has states and feedback, yet its states neither deepen with passage nor shallow with little use; only under qualified Sedimentability Nature and attribution to execution does the passage of history become a dynamical condition of what follows. If reaction rules are written in the host's rules (deepening with passage, shallowing with little use), this set of rules becomes the driving substrate of generative computation; and the concrete content of the reaction is formed by actual passage, not exhausted in advance by the rules (Section Three, E4; Section Four, W). The first cannot be replaced by general execution of the host's rules: a fixed τ gives the same successors for the same arguments (Section Two). It opens no new item — it is D.

The second — law reacting on dynamics through its holding-tendency — is actually realized by the host and borne through dynamics. According to upstream, the governance of a law is that the relevant states of R really enter the subsequent generation involved in Rel(L) (DynamicRole), and its stable re-walk is the capacity to re-enter and bear under every class of applicable condition (ΩSupport). In a computing host this action is realized through the chain of execution (Section Seven, Lemma Two, by E5\_host), and the constrained flow in turn carves R deeper by the sedimentation gate — still the to and fro of channel and flow. On the upstream operating substrate F\_LB this can be checked as follows (the same-depth check, Appendix B, item A-1): the Continuation kernel reads only the local depth of sedimentation; a strong-impulse groove whose read sedimentation is the same as that of a formed law but which does not form a law exerts, under a shared random source, the same constraint on subsequent flows (in 100 alternating re-entries, the 50 Continuations from a1 go a1→m1→c on both sides, and the read-outs of g and Ω at the end of the window are the same; under 30 competing perturbations, the probabilities of edge selection, the hit counts and the law-side pre-stage read-outs at the final time (Established^inst, ΩSupport^pre) are the same item by item on both sides, and at 60 and above both sides flip alike, g differing only by 0.001 in the third decimal place). This shows that the action of law is actually realized by the host and borne through dynamics, exactly as the initial stipulation says: these motions all really come from the host (initial stipulation, item 10).  
It does not show that law and dynamics are on the same level. Levels are not distinguished by the mechanism by which an action is realized (Preliminary Distinctions, One), but by the qualification obtained. The initial stipulation: above the dynamics layer, part of the holding-tendency is judged to be law (initial stipulation, item 10) — holding-tendency is a matter of the dynamics layer, and what is judged to be law is the part of it compressed into stability.

This separates where the dynamics layer can stay from where it leaps.  
Staying: one strong impulse enters the field and, through actual passage, leaves a marked new groove; the new groove has entered the field and really governs the next stretch of successors (Established = 1, DynamicRole = 1), while it lacks the historical qualification of stable re-walk (Ω = 0, under the current upstream Ω\_W = 0); the upstream judgement kernel delivers its verdict at the ΩSupport link, and for the declared a1 ⇒ c no law is formed (Theorem 4.13 of *The Mathematical Form of Law*, LB2 of *The Construction Form of the Law Operating Substrate*; written as a computation in Section Eight). Eleven

The structure has closed, and the two reactions have been placed. Now back to the general proposition: is there layering? Into how many layers?

The unit of counting layers is as in Preliminary Distinctions, One: the generative hierarchy and the relation of reaction of which the initial stipulation speaks (initial stipulation, the former clause of item 5, items 7, 9 and 10). Layers are distinguished by generative qualification, and are invariant under the splitting of logical formulas, the naming of variables and the merging of execution mechanisms. Counting layers by the number of atoms, by the number of task clauses, or by the number of function positions or physical mechanisms is not adopted: a program going from having no cache to having a cache, or from having no velocity variable to having one, does not mean that it has generated a new dynamics layer; splitting Established into three finer conditions does not mean that the object layer gains two more levels.

For a lower-level candidate X and an upper-level candidate Y, Y is a level above X if and only if each of the following five conditions has an independent witness:  
(1) New qualification: Y has a qualification that X does not have;  
(2) Possibility of staying: there is a legitimate complete case in which X holds and Y does not — X can stay, without having to generate Y;  
(3) Not certifiable by the lower level: Y cannot be certified on the existing qualification of X alone; certification takes the criteria and witnesses established upstream, and requires an independent degeneration witness; it may not be derived merely from the definition of Y;  
(4) Needed by the task: this qualification is necessary for the declared task;  
(5) Hierarchical character of generative qualification: the qualification of Y has a source that legitimately rests on generation of the X kind — obtained through the legitimate generative history of the X kind and its organization, or belonging to a legitimate taking-over under such a source that satisfies the upstream conditions of transplantation and preservation; it is not required that Y be formed within the σ under consideration, nor that it have been generated by the current P itself; preloaded qualification is still determined by the upstream conditions, and does not qualify automatically by being preloaded. This qualification enables the organization to bear a declared generative relation for which it was not previously qualified — "previously" referring to the state of the generative hierarchy before the qualification was obtained, not necessarily the preceding moment of the present segment's computation; this change may be stable holding-tendency, coverage of application or another object qualification certified upstream, and no new execution mechanism is required.  
Condition (5) guarantees that the qualification of the upper level really has a legitimate generative source, rather than being an arbitrarily stipulated name — if there were only (3), any artificially stipulated qualification would be "not certifiable by the lower level"; it also lays down what counts as one rise in level: fully obtaining one new declared generative relation, intermediate qualifications not counting. The source of this counting convention is the initial stipulation's stipulation on layers, not an exclusionary condition added in order to arrive at some number of layers: the initial stipulation names layers by what is generated — generative computation first drives and generates field dynamics, this level being called the dynamics layer, and then field dynamics generates laws, Imagery and other upper-layer objects, this level being called the object layer (initial stipulation, the former clause of item 5); the members of the object layer are those judged to be laws and other objects, and above the dynamics layer part of the holding-tendency is judged to be law (initial stipulation, item 10); objects are in essence dynamics too (initial stipulation, item 12), and holding-tendency not judged to be law remains in the dynamics layer. Accordingly, a layer is delimited by the kind of complete qualification of what it generates: what has obtained the complete qualification of law is in the object layer, and what has partial law qualification without the complete qualification (such as holding-tendency with current governance but no stable re-walk) remains in the dynamics layer; intermediate qualifications do not form further layers. This is a counting convention made according to the initial stipulation, used within 𝔇; it is not derived from E1–E5 alone, so the upper bound obtained from it is called the counting theorem for the hierarchy of declared qualifications, and is not extended into a theorem on the absolute minimum number of layers of generative structure. It does not require Y to have a mode of execution feedback that X lacks: as Section Nine has shown, organizations that have and have not formed a law can have the same actual flux within a legitimate comparison. Skipping levels is tested separately: the facts of the level two below Y cannot substitute for X's bearing of Y (levels cannot be skipped).  
All judgements in this section use only the necessary direction: what lacks one of the proved necessary conditions of a qualification cannot obtain that qualification; what has all the necessary conditions must still pass complete certification — for Rel0, the task certification of E1–E4; for RelL, the complete certification of the Law Group-Form.

Corollary One (the host layer is rule transformation)　Stated in two parts. Generality: any σ bearing Φ(∅) contains S, S is the execution of a fixed τ on P, each execution node is a fixed rule, and by the initial stipulation it belongs to rule transformation (initial stipulation, item 3). Specificity: in a σ bearing Φ(∅), the legitimate source of D is sedimentation written by execution and carved by passage (Section Eleven, S → D (5)); the rules bearing this writing — basic host rules such as the Trace-Response relation and sedimentation laws — are constituents of τ, or a D taken over legitimately was formed by execution with rules of this kind. So generative computation contains rule computation, and rule computation takes place in the host layer of generative computation (initial stipulation, item 16). Rule computation here means the execution of the basic host rules; it does not mean that generative computation also bears a task of simulating the rules of an external object.  
Proof: Generality by general theorem (1) and E1: S holds, and τ is fixed within Θ. Specificity by Sections Three and Four (Pass, W) and Section Eleven, S → D (5); the item "τ is fixed within Θ" alone does not yield that τ is the basic sedimentation rule of the host. ∎  
What is computed out and what is run out are rule computations in which S holds alone, their τ being object rules; the host layer of generative computation is also rule computation, its τ being the basic rules of the host. Both are rule transformation, differing in the rules executed.

Corollary Two (computational implementation cannot bypass the bearing of the Sedimentation-Field to obtain law qualification directly)　Within 𝒲, under Φ(L), carrying the conditions of general theorem (2) (using H\_G and the antecedents of Lemma One through the non-substitution lemma, E5\_run, E5\_host, Computational Execution General Form-H3 and Generative Computation General Form-H2 through Lemma Two, and this chapter's H2 through CtPath), computational implementation cannot bypass the bearing of the actual Sedimentation-Field to obtain law qualification directly: the bearer of law qualification is the R in Sed\_P, whose relevant state Q enters Continuation through the chain of execution (Lemma Two); the execution subchain that actually contributes may take part in current Continuation (CtPath), including signals and states outside R; law qualification is borne by the field organization. Delivered input and permitted interventions of the exogenous control configuration can affect the Continuation of a law; see the boundaries below.  
Proof: By the non-substitution lemma, Lemma Two and CtPath. ∎

That deleting items one by one leads to failure does not mean that no weaker substitute structure can succeed: writing Ψ for the conjunction of the necessary structure obtained in this chapter, take any condition X that is not a tautology; then Ψ ∨ X is also a necessary condition and is weaker, so minimality can only be put forward relative to a family of admissible candidate structures — those whose components are facts of the host layer and of the field and which can exclude each failure case of Sections Two to Eight — and whether there is within it a weaker or finer grading is unanswered (U2).

Breach Argument

Ways of breach: while the premises of the general theorem, E1–E5, the task-side conditions, 𝒲 and the corresponding H are kept, exhibit: Φ(∅) holding while one of κ, Pass, W, Rd fails; or an organization in S that has not obtained the qualification of D bearing historical difference; or exhibit a law continuing in the system borne by P as driven by σ while its governing structure is not in P's Sedimentation-Field Sed\_P, or σ reads in none of its relevant states in the Continuation of L; or D implying O — any dynamics whatever already being law; or a dynamical organization that has not obtained law qualification bearing law qualification (a breach of the upstream determination); or some fact of computational implementation itself bearing Established, DynamicRole or ΩSupport. For the minimum number of layers: prove that S and D, or D and O, can be merged into one level; or give, within 𝔇, an upper level that satisfies the five conditions of Section Eleven but does not fully obtain any new declared generative relation; or give an organization that lacks a proved necessary condition of Rel0 or RelL and still obtains that relation.  
Not a breach: the action of law being realized by the host through dynamics, without requiring a separate, independent execution mechanism (the same-depth check of Section Nine) — levels are not counted by mechanism.

Counterexample One: the physical Trace-Response of a device.  
One and the same storage device that drifts with current is classified differently under two declarations: as an environmental property of the device, the updates within the segment are not driven by execution, W fails, and it belongs to the dynamics of a non-computing host (Section Four); legitimately declared as a constituent of the implementation of τ and driven by the present execution, W holds. The distinction falls on attribution, agreeing with the initial stipulation's distinction between the two kinds of host dynamics, and is not a breach. If the action of the bearer of a law on Continuation goes through drift of the former kind and not through execution, then in that part the law resides in the medium and not in computation, and E5\_host fails (Section Seven (2)).

Counterexample Two: a host deployed for inference only.  
The host realizes no sedimentation operator, running only with the κ\_sed obtained by training, while sedimentation takes place in another system. There is no sedimentary update within σ, so W holds trivially; Rd holds. Whether its κ\_sed satisfies Sedimentability Nature must be determined by this chapter's H1 and Generative Computation General Form-H3; before that determination, the corresponding conclusion is ⊥, not 0. It is neither excluded by the general formula nor a breach.

Counterexample Three: preloaded states and sedimentation moved in.  
The field state of Sed\_P at the head of the segment comes from elsewhere, and O\_P can already hold at the head of the segment. This lies within the boundaries listed in the restricted proposition, and is not a substitution of computation for O\_P. G\_loc and O\_P are as usual. Not a breach.

Counterexample Four: role masking and delivered input.  
R is masked through a permitted intervention of the exogenous control configuration, or the delivered input is changed, and the Continuation of L changes accordingly while Sed\_P has not sedimented in the present segment. This lies within the scope excluded by Corollary Two and the restricted proposition; the bearer is still the R in Sed\_P. Not a breach.

Counterexample Five: training.  
External error and an optimizer decide where D sediments. By the initial stipulation, this is field-induction, not a basic rule of the Sedimentation-Field; the sedimentary updates within the segment are still driven by execution. Not a breach, nor a further layer.

Counterexample Six: the brain.  
The brain has dynamics and has laws and Principles, while its driving is nutrition and its dynamical conditions have nothing to do with computation (initial stipulation, item 6). It does not satisfy E1, and is not within 𝒲; it shows that dynamics and law can exist without a host layer of computation; it is a boundary, not a breach.

Counterexample Seven: one program serving as both S and D, one carrier holding both D and O.  
Layers are not counted by modules, carriers or mechanisms (Preliminary Distinctions, One). Not a breach.

Counterexample Eight: a unified field-transformation kernel and "same depth, different law."  
One kernel realizes at once sedimentation, carry-over and the governance of a formed law; for two fields whose read sedimentation is the same, one forming a law and the other not, subsequent flux is the same in the read-outs of the same-depth check of Section Nine (the Continuations of a1 the same each time, the read-outs of sedimentation at the end of the window the same; Appendix B, item A-1). This shows that the action of law is realized by the host through dynamics; it does not show that law and dynamics are on the same level: the one that does not form a law has no stable re-walk, upstream judges that it does not form a law, and it is a holding-tendency staying in the dynamics layer. Not a breach.

Counterexample Nine: "computation computed out the question-answer law."  
Calling the question-answer law in a language model a direct product of computation. By Corollary Two and the non-substitution lemma, this is a cross-layer misreading, not a counterexample to the general formula; its dissolution belongs to Chapter 9, *The Dissolution Form of Cross-Layer Misplacement in Computation*. Training can change weights and influence behaviour by gradients and other methods; but from "the weights are adjusted to certain values" or "the output meets the target" it does not follow that the corresponding question-answer law has been established with the qualification of a Sedimentation-Field.

Hence: having states is not having dynamics, and having dynamics is not having law; rules can reproduce the trajectory of a law but cannot take the place of the field the law requires; dynamics must be compressed into stability to enter the level of law; between computation and law there must lie a dynamics layer, driven by computation, in which channel and flow go to and fro and law obtains its qualification.

Closing

With the stipulation of placement in layers removed, and only "history becomes dynamics" and "a law continues" left, the structure forced out is still layered once it has closed: the host executes rules written down, among which are reaction rules, while what will grow out is not written down; dynamics carves history in the to and fro of channel and flow, which rules cannot carve on its behalf; law is the level that dynamics becomes when compressed into stability, dynamics can stay without reaching this level, and computation cannot get past dynamics to obtain it. The action of law is actually realized by the host and borne through dynamics, which does not change its position in generation. What this chapter obtains is the minimum number of layers of the declared chain of generative qualification — two layers without objects, three when a law continues — and not a claim that any given computing system has only two or three functional layers.

The conclusions of this chapter, compressed into three sentences:

The host layer settles "by what things change,"  
The dynamics layer settles "how history pushes it on in the to and fro of channel and flow,"  
The object layer settles "what of what is pushed out already holds stably, and how it constrains the flow thereafter."

Compressed again into the simplest single sentence:

Without the host layer, there is no computation bearing the duties of generation; without the dynamics layer, however much is computed, it is only rules running; without compression into stability, history is pushing but the law is not yet formed; and computation, without going through the dynamics layer, cannot reach the qualification of law.

Appendix: The General Form of Computational Layering · Formalization

This appendix gives the formal objects and formal statements of the general form of computational layering, so that the structures, propositions and theorems of the main text can be checked item by item at the formal level.

One. Basic Objects and Types

P: a computing host, with boundary, entries, read-out, delivery protocols and declared period Θ declared as in *The General Form of Computational Bearing*.  
τ: the task borne by P within Θ, having the extension of its function (or kernel) as its identity.  
σ ⊆ Θ: a stretch of actual process on P; Exe(σ): the executions of τ by P in σ.  
Decl: the complete reading of the state held by P (division of components, π\_cmp, cross-step carry-over, modes of update), declared before σ (Generative Computation General Form-H1).  
π\_cmp: the declared comparison projection, containing τ, the input of the present step, the exogenous control configuration c and the declared read-out of the current structure, fixed before σ.  
c: the exogenous control configuration, changed only through permitted interventions of the declared protocol, not belonging to Exe(σ); u: delivered input.  
Ob ∈ {∅, {L}}: the declared set of objects; Rel(L), I: the relation and domain of application of the law.  
𝒲: the working domain — computational implementation, sedimentary dynamics and the Continuation of at most one law within a single host P.  
HistVar: the family of legitimate variations of historical conditions (possibly including legitimate counterfactuals).  
Ex(e, r): event e is driven by execution r (the Ex of *The General Form of Computational Execution*, carrying that form's H3); ImplDecl(e): e is a physical state transition legitimately declared as a constituent of the implementation of τ.  
Witness(σ): the witness chains of E2.  
Sed\_P: the persistent field state of the Sedimentation-Field in P that has obtained field qualification; D\_P: the part of Sed\_P read in by execution; R ⊆ Org(Sed\_P): the bearer of the law L; Q ⊆ R: its relevant state read in by the present segment (Q ⊆ D\_P); ActBind: action binding; O\_P, O\_σ: upstream law object qualification, and its conjunction with the present segment's location.  
FieldQual(κ\_sed): κ\_sed is the persistent field state of a Sedimentation-Field that has obtained full field qualification (what this chapter's H1 corresponds to).

Two. Stipulations

E1(σ) ⟺ P bears τ within Θ ∧ the transformations in σ ⊆ Exe(σ).  
E2(σ) ⟺ ∃ h₁, h₂ ∈ HistVar, ∃ s₁, s₂: π\_cmp(s₁) = π\_cmp(s₂) ∧ some subsequent actual transformation in σ differs under h₁ and h₂.

# ---------- kernel & exact path measure ----------
def kernel(F,q,avail=None,mask=None):
    """K_y at position q: distribution over out-edges, from state only (exact)."""
    avail=avail or F.Avail; mask=mask or F.mask
    cand=[e for e in F.out[q] if avail[e]]
    if not cand: return {}
    ks=[]
    for e in cand:
        k=F.k0+F.w_g*mask[e]*F.g[e]*(1.0 if F.S(e) else F.s_weak)+F.w_d*F.d[e[1]]
        ks.append(k/F.T)
    m=max(ks); ws=[math.exp(k-m) for k in ks]; z=sum(ws)
    return {e:w/z for e,w in zip(cand,ws)}

def eval_paths(F,entry,edges_allowed=None,maxhop=MAXHOP):
    """Exhaustive Eval(Ctx): all routes with exact μ. edges_allowed=None → all; else restrict (κ_P)."""
    out=[]
    def rec(q,route,prob,hops):
        K=kernel(F,q)
        if edges_allowed is not None:
            K={e:p for e,p in K.items() if e in edges_allowed}
            z=sum(K.values())
            if z==0: out.append((tuple(route),prob,'dead')); return
            K={e:p/z for e,p in K.items()}   # 受限评价：P 外结构不可用，核在 P 内重归一
        if not K or hops>=maxhop: out.append((tuple(route),prob,'end')); return
        for e,p in K.items(): rec(e[1],route+[e[1]],prob*p,hops+1)
    rec(entry,[entry],1.0,0)
    return out

def mu_read(F,entry,target,edges_allowed=None):
    return sum(p for r,p,_ in eval_paths(F,entry,edges_allowed) if target in r)

# ---------- ActArg^μ at a choice node (weighted duty) ----------
def actargs_at(F,q,eps=0.01):
    """minimal μ-sufficient groups over per-edge state fixings at position q.
    AdmVar: each out-edge's g varies over its admissible domain. The implementation evaluates
    endpoint grids {0,g_max} (S recomputed): single-free-variable sufficiency is exact, while
    multi-variable positive sufficiency is conditional on PR2-valid (paper §4 PR2, §7.1);
    negative verdicts from grid counterexamples are unconditionally sound."""
    cand=[e for e in F.out[q] if F.Avail[e]]
    def dist(gvals):
        ks=[]
        for e in cand:
            g=gvals[e]; S=1.0 if g>=F.th[e] else 0.0
            k=F.k0+F.w_g*F.mask[e]*g*(1.0 if S else F.s_weak)+F.w_d*F.d[e[1]]
            ks.append(k/F.T)
        m=max(ks); ws=[math.exp(k-m) for k in ks]; z=sum(ws)
        return [w/z for w in ws]
    def tv(p,qq): return 0.5*sum(abs(a-b) for a,b in zip(p,qq))
    base={e:F.g[e] for e in cand}
    def sufficient(C):
        freevars=[e for e in cand if e not in C]
        if not freevars: return True
        grids=[[0.0,F.g_max] for _ in freevars]
        dists=[]
        for combo in itertools.product(*grids):
            gv=dict(base); gv.update({e:v for e,v in zip(freevars,combo)})
            for e in C: gv[e]=base[e]
            dists.append(dist(gv))
        return max(tv(a,b) for a in dists for b in dists)<=eps
    minimal=[]
    for r in range(len(cand)+1):
        for C in itertools.combinations(cand,r):
            if sufficient(set(C)) and not any(set(M)<=set(C) for M in minimal):
                minimal.append(C)
    return set(u for M in minimal for u in M), minimal

def _judge_P(F,L,P,R,pi0,eps):

    Returns (depth_reached, detail). Depth order: CR=1, CNI=2, Dyn=3, ΩSupport=4 (all-pass)."""
