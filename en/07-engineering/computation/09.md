# The Dissolution Form of Cross-Layer Misplacement in Computation

General Proposition

"Computation directly computes out the law," "computational power determines the object," "every step is a rule, so it is not generation," "dynamics is nothing but program state," "where there is dynamics there is law" — these five claims share one form: they judge the qualification of a higher layer by facts obtainable on the computation side (rules, numbers of steps, computational power, outputs, program variables, updates in execution). At the level of structure they do not hold. They are pressed out by one and the same structural process: each claim rests on a local fact that is true on the computation side; a layer can stay at the level below, and staying and not staying cannot be told apart on the computation side, so the local fact is generalized into a judgement of the qualification of the higher layer, crossing the layer. Once the claims are read apart by layer, several groups of seemingly opposed claims in the initial stipulation hold at the same time.

Scope of dissolution. One thing is dissolved, one thing is kept, one thing is not adjudicated:  
Dissolved: inferences that derive (or deny) the qualification of the dynamics layer or the object layer from facts obtainable on the computation side.  
Kept: computation is present everywhere at the level of realization — every transformation in a computing host is executed by computation; the host layer is rule throughout; computational power is a resource for the formation of sedimentation; dynamical conditions reside in the device; dynamics can generate law.  
Not adjudicated: what qualification a concrete actual system (a particular language model, a particular brain) has actually obtained.

In One Sentence

Everything in a computing host is done by computation, yet law is not borne by computation: law is borne by the Sedimentation-Field that computation drives. To read "done" as "borne" is to cross the layer.

Two. The Misplaced Claims

### 2.1　The five misplacements

W1　Computation directly computes out the law. Its variant: the question-answer law can be directly made or controlled by computation.  
W2　Computational power determines the object: the greater the computational power, the more numerous and the stronger the laws.  
W3　Every step is a rule, so it is not generation. Its converse: with enough steps, it becomes generation.  
W4　Dynamics is nothing but program state: dynamical conditions reside in program variables and are updated by the program, so they are just the state of rule computation.  
W5　Where there is dynamics there is law: once the field is carved in execution, the law already holds.

2.2　Five groups of surface oppositions in the initial stipulation

(A) These motions all really come from the host, and if the host is a computing device, they come from computation (initial stipulation, item 7) — one cannot say that computation directly computed out the question-answer law in GPT (initial stipulation, item 5); computation makes no direct contribution to the result of a law (initial stipulation, item 3).  
(B) Each execution node is a logical rule (initial stipulation, item 2), and generative computation contains rule computation (initial stipulation, item 11) — the difference between generative computation and rule computation lies in whether the transformation is dynamical transformation or rule transformation (initial stipulation, item 14).  
(C) "Its computational power is used to construct the Sedimentation-Field" (*Judgment of Intelligence*) — the superstition about computational power (initial stipulation, item 13).  
(D) Computation "holds the structures and relations as changed by the dynamical conditions," the carrier of the dynamical conditions being in the host P (G3 of *The General Form of Generative Computation*) — dynamical conditions that bear history must satisfy Sedimentability Nature, and rule state does not guarantee this (Section Three of *The General Form of Computational Layering*).  
(E) Changes of dynamics generate objects (initial stipulation, item 7) — the dynamics layer can stay without law (Corollary Six of *The General Form of Computational Layering*).  
In each group both sides come from texts already established; if the two sides speak of the same subject with the same predicate, they contradict each other.

### 2.3　Concepts used in the statement

Layers, qualifications and criteria are all taken from what is inherited: Φ(∅) is defined by E1–E4; the truth value of Law(L) at the moment of comparison is obtained by the judgement kernel of the Law Group-Form (where the verdict is "holds," it is raised to Law(L), carrying Computational Layering General Form-H\_cert. Three readings and measures of established objects are set up in addition:

Definition One (computation-side obtainable quantities)　For a run segment σ in 𝒲₈, Λ(σ) = (Λ\_rule, Λ\_op, Λ\_io, Λ\_ex): Λ\_rule is the rule text of τ (up to renaming of structural positions); Λ\_op is the sequence of types of operator calls of P in σ — each call of an operator declared by τ (evaluation of the Continuation kernel, Continuation sampling, sedimentation Pass, shallowing Idle and so on) is recorded as one entry, the instructions within an operator not being further subdivided (the number of steps is obtained from this); Λ\_io is the sequence of deliveries and the output trajectory; Λ\_ex comprises the facts of execution — whether each execution node is a fixed rule (written RuleFixed), where cross-step quantities are stored, and whether there are sedimentation update events and how many. Λ contains neither the content of D nor the history of the field. The part with Λ\_rule removed is written Λ⁻.  
Definition Two (computational power)　For any declared non-negative pricing w (giving each operator type a non-negative number), c\_w(σ) = the sum of w over the entries of Λ\_op(σ). If two runs have sequences of types of operator calls identical entry by entry, their computational power is the same under every such pricing.  
Definition Three (cross-layer judgement)　A judgement of the qualification Q of a higher layer that takes certain components of Λ as arguments. Q is one of the following: Φ(∅); whether the component bearing cross-step differences satisfies Sedimentability Nature (E4); Law(L) at the moment of comparison; the bearer of law qualification when L forms a law. Each misplacement consists of two parts: the local fact F on which it rests — taken from Λ and true within the range it states; and the cross-layer generalization that derives Q from F.  
　W1　F: the behaviour in σ conforming to L is produced directly by P's execution of τ (Λ\_io, Λ\_ex). Generalization: the law qualification of L is borne by the computation side — the rule content of τ or the events of execution.  
　W2　F: there is a positive pricing w and a history of growth in which an increase of c\_w accompanies the law's passing from not holding to holding (local co-occurrence for some w). Generalization: Law(L) is determined by c\_w, or is an increasing function of c\_w.  
　W3　F: each execution node is a fixed rule (RuleFixed). Generalization: Φ(∅) does not hold. Its converse　F: the number of execution steps is very large. Generalization: Φ(∅) holds.  
　W4　F: cross-step quantities are stored in program variables and updated step by step by fixed rules (Λ\_ex). Generalization: they do not satisfy Sedimentability Nature and are only rule state.  
　W5　F: there are sedimentation updates in execution, and the field state is actually carved (Λ\_ex). Generalization: Law(L) holds.

Three. Locating the Source

3.1　Lemma One (indistinguishability at the effect level)

(i) The determination of law is not fixed by the complete computation-side obtainable quantities: there are two runs σ, σ′ in 𝒲₈ starting from the same moment t₀ with Λ(σ) = Λ(σ′) — the same rule text, sequences of types of operator calls identical entry by entry, the same deliveries and the same output — while at the moment of comparison t₀ the verdict of the judgement kernel of the Law Group-Form on L is "holds" in σ and "does not hold" in σ′. Where the verdict is "holds," it is raised to Law(L), carrying Computational Layering General Form-H\_cert; where the verdict is "does not hold," Law(L) is false by contraposition of the necessary direction under H\_G, through the check listed under "Ground of failure at the object layer" in the proof, following the discipline of negative reading of 2.3.  
(ii) Φ(∅) is not determined by Λ⁻: there are σ, σ′ with Λ⁻(σ) = Λ⁻(σ′) while Φ(∅) holds in σ and does not hold in σ′. The side on which it holds carries Computational Execution General Form-H3.  
(iii) By (i), for any pricing w by operator type, the determination of law is not a function of c\_w.

Proof　(i) has two levels: the sameness of the computation-side obtainable quantities is a mathematical derivation, and the verdicts' being true in one run and false in the other is an instance certification.  
　Mathematical derivation. Take A to be a field of a formed law on F\_LB; take B\* to be the field identical with A item by item in edge traces g, position traces d, masks, thresholds and availability, but with the re-walk records cleared and Ω zero. B\* is obtained by writing in initial values, which is legitimate taking-over (Corollary One of *The General Form of Generative Computation*; Counterexample Three of *The General Form of Computational Layering*). At t₀ the two fields differ only in the re-walk records and Ω, and these two items are not in Λ. Both execute the same rules F\_LB and run with the same deliveries (a1 and a2 alternating, strength 1) and the same random source. The Continuation kernel of F\_LB reads only g, d, masks and availability, and reads neither the re-walk records nor Ω (a source-code fact checked in Appendix B, item A-1 of *The General Form of Computational Layering*); the rewriting of g and d by sedimentation and shallowing does not read the re-walk records or Ω either. By induction on steps: at the start of each step the quantities read in from the two fields are the same, the same random number gives the same Continuation, sedimentation and shallowing give the same g and d, and the outputs are the same.  
　The components of the complete Λ are identical item by item. Λ\_rule: the same rules. Λ\_op: one pulse of F\_LB calls shallowing once, then calls the Continuation kernel and sampling at each position passed, and calls sedimentation once for each edge passed; which operator is called, and how many times, is determined only by the current position, the available outgoing edges and the Continuation drawn. The maintenance of the re-walk records and Ω takes place unconditionally within the two operators sedimentation and shallowing — sedimentation appends a record each time and recomputes Ω, shallowing removes expired records each time and recomputes Ω — and does not determine whether any operator is called or how many times; since the Continuations are already the same step by step, the sequences of types of calls are identical entry by entry. Λ\_io: the deliveries and outputs are the same. Λ\_ex: each execution node is the same fixed rule, cross-step quantities are stored in the same place, and the numbers of sedimentation calls are the same. Hence Λ(σ) = Λ(σ′), holding for segments of any length.  
　The amount of instructions within operators is not wholly the same: the number of times expired re-walk records are removed in shallowing is greater for A than for B\* (Appendix B, item B-2: 200 against 138 in 100 pulses), the difference being bounded by the number of records A held at t₀. This difference is the maintenance of the re-walk records in D; Λ counts by operator call and does not reach it; the lemma makes no assertion as to whether computational power priced by instructions within operators can determine the verdict on law (ρ9).  
　Instance certification. At t₀, the judgement kernel judges L = a1 ⇒ c to hold for A and not to hold for B*, the latter falling at ΩSupport (insufficient stable re-walk). This difference of verdict comes from the re-walk records and Ω; it is given by the upstream judgement kernel, not by the derivation of the preceding paragraph. Appendix B, item B-2 checks, under the two rules with Continuation temperatures 0.15 and 3.0, that the total variation of the step-by-step Continuation distributions is exactly zero and that the sequences of operator calls are the same, as well as the verdicts above; at the moments of comparison after the first two re-entries of a1, the verdicts are still one "holds" and one "does not hold," and after the third re-entry both "hold." A field formed by history whose read sedimentation matches A to within 10⁻³ (the B of A-1 of *The General Form of Computational Layering\*) gives the same difference of verdict, with routes and sequences of operator calls the same step by step (Appendix B, item B-1); since its step-by-step sameness depends on the Continuations of the two fields being nearly deterministic, it serves only as a supplement.  
　Ground of failure at the object layer (v1.1, following round 1 of the external review). The judgement kernel's FAIL for B\* means only that the candidate examined does not pass that determination task; that Law(L) is false in B\* is obtained by the following check, not read off directly from the machine verdict. (a) Declaration: B\* is a field set up independently at t₀ by writing initial values; "legitimate taking-over" above means that the written g, d, masks, thresholds and availabilities are legitimate initial values; B\* is not declared as a transplant of A's bearing organization and does not take over A's actual history of passage. The declared window is W = \[t₀ − W\_rep, t₀\] (W\_rep being the re-walk window of Spec\_LB of *The Construction Form of the Law Operating Substrate*); within W the actual history of B\* is empty: B\* does not exist before t₀, and writing initial values is not a passage event. (b) By (a), no path in B\* has a countable actual re-walk event within W; of the three facts of Ω\_W, fact one (the re-walk record reaches the declared criterion) and fact three (every sub-segment has re-walk events) fail for every candidate organization in B*; hence no candidate organization satisfies ΩSupport for the applicable re-entry conditions of L, and Govern fails. This conclusion does not depend on which candidate is examined, and no other bearing organization can make up the qualification. (c) Hence there is no organization in B* satisfying Established ∧ Govern, and under H\_G, by contraposition of the necessary direction of the Law Group-Form, Law(L) is false in B*. What is used is B*'s declared construction and its actual history of passage within the window, not the machine's Ω\_e = 0 (Ω\_e = 0 does not directly yield Ω\_W = 0; upstream has a witness of consecutive weak pulses giving Ω\_W = 1 while Ω\_e = 0); merely clearing the re-walk records and setting the machine's Ω to zero does not by itself prove that the actual historical qualification has vanished, and the object-layer failure of B\* comes from the declaration of independent set-up in (a). If B\* were instead declared as a transplant taking over A's bearing organization, its historical qualification would have to be examined separately under the upstream transplantation clause, (c) would no longer hold, and (i) would hold only at the level of the judgement kernel's verdicts. (d) This lemma is load-bearing only at the moment of comparison t₀; the verdicts after the first two re-entries at a1 are only recorded, their object-layer reading requiring a separate check of the actual re-walk events within W, and they bear no load here. This negative reasoning does not pass through the sufficient direction and carries no SC\_Hold in addition.  
(ii) Take σ to be a generative run on a new field of F\_LB: first the exogenous experience a1→m1→c, then a delivery to a1 (strength 1). The new field of F\_LB satisfies the model-level antecedents of Theorem One-B of *The Necessity Form of Generative Computation*: C₁ (P bears F\_LB), C₂ (p = 1 ≤ g\_max/β, F\_LB belonging to 𝔗, 3.3 of that chapter), C₄ (a1 has two available outgoing edges a1→m1 and a1→m2), C₈ (the comparison projection is declared before the run); σ is executed by P, with SED = 1 and no other protocol operation, and is one and the same legitimate run, so C\_run(σ) holds. The witness configuration Γ(σ) holds item by item: Γ1 is the actual passage through a1→m1 at a1 in the first pulse, the actual history being denoted h₁; Γ2 replaces it by the exogenous experience a1→m2→x permitted by the same protocol, giving h₂, the subsequent deliveries and random source being shared; Γ3 is the reading-in of Continuation at a1 in the second pulse, the two histories having at the comparison point the same rules, input, exogenous control configuration, current position and available outgoing edges; Γ4: (i) between the two pulses the edge traces of A(a1) undergo in both histories only the one Idle at the start of the second pulse, (ii) n\_m1 in h₁ and n\_m2 in h₂ undergo one more Pass, the other heads being the same in the two histories, and the required ⪰ follows from TR2; Γ5 is RT₁(m⁰) on the new field (C₆). By Theorem One-B of *The Necessity Form of Generative Computation* (for existence see also its Theorem One-A), Φ(∅) holds in σ, carrying Computational Execution General Form-H3; the Continuation distributions of the two comparable histories at re-entry differ (Appendix B, item B-3: total variation 0.3149). Take σ′ to be a shadow run on the same field: the Continuation kernel reads only a snapshot of the field state at the head of the segment, sedimentation and shallowing write into the field state as usual, and what is written is never read in (the D shadow of pair (1) of Corollary Two of *The General Form of Generative Computation*; the "copy written but not read" of Appendix B, item A-5 of *The General Form of Computational Layering*). In both, each execution node is a fixed rule; the sequences of types of operator calls of execution — evaluation of the Continuation kernel, Continuation sampling, sedimentation, shallowing — are identical entry by entry, the numbers of sedimentation update events are the same, and cross-step quantities are stored in the same place; under the same random source the outputs are the same (B-3: 8 of 10 random seeds give routes and sequences of operator calls identical entry by entry), so Λ⁻(σ) = Λ⁻(σ′). The Continuation of σ′ reads only the snapshot at the head of the segment, and sedimentation formed within the segment never enters Continuation; on a new field the snapshot is zero everywhere, the Continuation kernel is at each position a fixed distribution that does not change with time, and any two comparison points whose current situation is the same have the same successor distributions (total variation 0 at re-entry for the two comparable histories, B-3), so E2 does not hold and Φ(∅) does not hold. When only some of the components of Λ⁻ are taken (each step being a fixed rule, the number of steps, deliveries and output), lookup-table replay gives the same contrast as well: the table entries are program constants and the index is rule state, bearing no sedimentable historical dependence, so E4 does not hold (the lookup-table program listed under S → D (2) in Section Eleven of *The General Form of Computational Layering*); for paired constructions of the same type see pair (4) of Corollary Two of *The General Form of Generative Computation*.  
(iii) If the sequences of types of operator calls are identical entry by entry, computational power is the same under every pricing by operator type; the result follows from (i). ∎

Strength　(i) and (ii) are existence statements. The lemma makes no assertion as to whether Λ including the rule text can determine Φ(∅): Φ(∅) further requires events in the run and no intrusion, the latter taking place outside P's execution. Qualification can be determined by the criteria of the layer in which it lies — reading the content of D and the history of the field, by E1–E4 or the judgement kernel — and this is precisely the part Λ does not contain.

### 3.2　Theorem One (the Source Theorem)

(1) Limits of determination functions. (a) Every function that takes Λ as argument to determine the verdict on law at the moment of comparison errs at least once on some Λ-class; the same holds where law qualification is the object of determination, carrying H\_cert; and a function that reads the verdict "does not hold" as law qualification being false, through the discipline of negative reading of 2.3 (the check of the failure of the formal necessary items), carries in addition H\_G. (b) Every function that takes Λ⁻ as argument to determine Φ(∅) errs at least once on some Λ⁻-class, carrying Computational Execution General Form-H3. Determining Φ(∅) by the complete Λ is not covered here (ρ6); the judgements of where the bearer of law qualification lies and of Sedimentability Nature are dealt with separately by the results cited in (2).

Four. The Dissolution Condition

Definition Four (layer-marked reading)　For every statement about computation, mark which of the following it speaks of:  
　Realization — an event is executed by P;  
　Kind of rule — whether τ is a basic rule of the host, or contains object rules (the definition in Section Five of *The Necessity Form of Generative Computation*);

Qualification is determined only by the criteria of the layer in which it lies: Φ(∅) by E1–E4 and the witness configuration; Law(L) by Established ∧ Govern of the Law Group-Form and the judgement kernel.

Five. The Dissolution Theorem

Theorem Two (the Dissolution Theorem)　Under the dissolution condition, both sides of the five groups of surface oppositions of 2.2 hold at the same time, and the contradiction cannot be reconstructed. Stated separately:

(A) Realization side: within 𝒲₈, every transformation in σ is carried out by P's execution of τ (E1); the action of law on Continuation is realized through computational execution (E5\_host, the initial restriction). Universal.  
　Bearing side: whenever L forms a law in σ, its bearer is an R in Sed\_P satisfying Established ∧ Govern; the rule content of τ, rule state, and the events and trajectories of execution are not bearers of Established, DynamicRole or ΩSupport (the non-substitution lemma, carrying H\_G and E5\_car). Universal. When τ ∈ 𝔗, τ contains no object rule (Theorem Four of *The Necessity Form of Generative Computation*). Universal.  
　The branches of W1: suppose τ ∈ 𝔗 (containing no object rule, the Continuation kernel reading in trace states: TR4, TR5). If L forms a law in σ, law qualification is borne by R in D; if the target candidate relation L has not yet formed a law, there is no law qualification to be borne, and the subsequent Continuation is given by the D read in by the Continuation kernel. In both branches the generalization of W1 does not hold, and this conclusion does not depend on whether L forms a law in a concrete system. In a rule simulation in which L is written into τ, the behaviour conforming to L is indeed given directly by the execution of rules, yet L is a hostless law (Corollary 2.1 (i)): the local fact holds, and the law qualification the generalization speaks of does not exist.

(B) Rule side: every σ bearing Φ(∅) contains S, which is the execution of a fixed τ, and each execution node is a rule (Corollary One of *The General Form of Computational Layering*). Universal.  
　Generation side: when τ ∈ 𝔗, under the group of conditions there is a legitimate run in which Φ(∅) holds (Theorem One-A of *The Necessity Form of Generative Computation*, carrying Computational Execution General Form-H3). Existential.  
　Hence being rule-executed is not a negative criterion of Φ(∅); nor is the number of steps a positive criterion of Φ(∅) (the conclusion at the end of the Breach Argument section of *The General Form of Generative Computation*; initial stipulation, item 12). The difference spoken of in item 14 of the initial stipulation falls on the kind of rule and on the events in the run: whether what is executed is the Trace-Response rule of the host or an object rule can be read from the rule text; whether "history becoming dynamics" actually occurs is determined by the witness configuration.

(C) Resource side: when τ ∈ 𝔗, SED = 1 in σ, and every change of D within σ goes only through Idle and Pass (hereafter called no intrusion within the segment; C₇(σ) of *The Necessity Form of Generative Computation* imposes this requirement only on the read-in set A(x) of the branch position, and here it is extended to all trace components of D), changes of D occur only within the calls of Idle and Pass executed by P (C₂: Idle and Pass are executed by P). Write N\_sed(σ) for the number of calls of Idle and Pass executed within the segment and N\_op(σ) for the total number of operator calls; then N\_sed(σ) ≤ N\_op(σ): formation within the segment presupposes execution, and the number of its calls is bounded by the total number of operator calls. One call can rewrite several trace components; this inequality counts calls, not rewrites of components. If the pricing w has a positive lower bound w\_min on the two operator types Idle and Pass, then in addition N\_sed(σ) ≤ c\_w(σ)/w\_min; for a general non-negative pricing, computational power gives no upper bound on the number of sedimentation calls. Universal under the conditions stated.  
　Determinant side: computational power does not fix the determination of law (Lemma One (iii)); more computation can make a law lose its qualification (Appendix B, item B-4 (b)); rule simulation with any computational power does not obtain law qualification (B-4 (c)). Existential.

(D) Carrier side: the state carrier of D is in P, and may be program variables updated by fixed rules (G3 and Section Five of *The General Form of Generative Computation*). Universal.  
　Qualification side: a component that bears historical differences as a dynamical condition must satisfy Sedimentability Nature (E4); having only the qualification of rule state does not guarantee Sedimentability Nature, and such a component must be determined separately by Sedimentability Nature and the corresponding duties (Sections Three and Eleven of *The General Form of Computational Layering*: S and D cannot be merged). Universal. The manner of storage and update is neither a negative criterion nor a positive criterion of dynamical qualification.

(E) Generation side: within 𝒲, an organization of the dynamics layer obtains the qualification of a law object if and only if it obtains Established ∧ Govern by the upstream criteria (Corollary Six of *The General Form of Computational Layering*, carrying its conditions: H\_G for the necessary direction; for the sufficient direction, through *The Necessity Form of Law* v1.4, SC\_Hold); under the same host rules, some histories leap to law (Appendix B, item A-2 of *The General Form of Computational Layering*).  
　Staying side: in one strong impulse, dynamics is present and the law does not hold (as above; the B of Appendix B, item B-1; the object-layer "does not hold" follows the discipline of negative reading of 2.3, by contraposition under H\_G on the ground that the actual passage within the window is only one strong impulse, not read off directly from the machine FAIL). Existential.

Consistency witness: a switched-on run of a field of a formed law of F\_LB has, at the same time and in the same system — every transformation executed by P; each execution node being a Trace-Response rule of the host (F\_LB belonging to 𝔗 at strength p ≤ g\_max/β, containing no object rule); D stored in P's storage; the judgement kernel judging L = a1 ⇒ c to hold (raising this to Law carries H\_cert), its bearer being R1 = {a1→m1, m1→c} in Sed\_P (Appendix B, items A-1 and A-2 of *The General Form of Computational Layering*; Appendix B, item B-1 of this chapter). Both sides of (A), the rule side of (B), both sides of (D) and the generation side of (E) are true together in this system. The remaining sides are existence statements, witnessed by their respective paired systems.

Non-reconstructibility: the subjects of the two sides of the five groups all differ — the realizer and the bearer, the basic rule of the host and the object rule, resource and determinant, carrier and qualification, dynamics and law. The universal statement on each side does not mention the subject of the other side. Under the layer-marked reading there is no pair of statements with the same subject and the same predicate and opposite truth values, and the surface oppositions cannot be reconstructed. ∎

Corollary 2.1 (making only through D)　Within 𝒲₈ (carrying H\_G and E5\_car), every way of making L obtain law qualification in P, in the sense of the Law Group-Form, goes through D; hosts and paths of generation outside the working domain are not covered:  
　(i) Writing L into τ as an object rule that wholly determines Continuation at that place: there is no dynamical reading-in at that place (Corollary 4.4 of *The Necessity Form of Generative Computation*), and L does not obtain law qualification there (Appendix B, item A-3 of *The General Form of Computational Layering*: the determination falls at DynamicRole; A-4: it falls at CC). What is obtained is a hostless law.  
　(ii) Training that adjusts weights by error: this is field-induction (initial stipulation, item 9), and what it changes is D; the qualification of L is still obtained by an organization in D.  
　(iii) Writing weights: as initial values at the head of the segment, this is legitimate taking-over, and can give L law qualification already at the head of the segment (Corollary One of *The General Form of Generative Computation*; Counterexample Three of *The General Form of Computational Layering*); written in the course of running, it is intrusion (initial stipulation, item 10). What both write is D.  
Proof: by the non-substitution lemma, whenever L forms a law its bearer is in Sed\_P, so every way of obtaining law qualification goes through D. (i) to (iii) are three kinds of approach taken from the computation side, and the result of each is given by what is cited: what is written into the rules does not obtain qualification, and the other two kinds change D. ∎  
What the initial stipulation says — that artificial neural networks can only adjust weights, and cannot directly control or make the question-answer law (initial stipulation, item 8) — is this corollary: all that can be done is to change D, and qualification is still borne by D.

Corollary 2.2 (the standing of computational power)　Computational power is a measure of resources obtained by pricing on the host layer. Sedimentation calls within the segment presuppose execution, and are bounded by computational power when the pricing has a positive lower bound on the sedimentation operators; computational power does not determine law qualification, its effect on law qualification is not monotone, and no computational power however great suffices to make a rule simulation obtain law qualification.  
Proof: by Theorem Two (C). ∎  
What *Judgment of Intelligence* says — "Its computational power is used to construct the Sedimentation-Field, not to obtain the law of Symbolic-Flow. The law is obtained only through sedimentation" — is the separation of the resource side from the determinant side.

Everything that happens in a computing host is done by computation; yet law is obtained and borne by the Sedimentation-Field that computation drives. The rules are written in the host, history is written in the field, and law grows within the field. To take the facts produced by computation as the qualification of law is to cross over the dynamics layer; to take the fact that everything in the host layer is rule as the absence of generation is to fail to see the dynamics layer. Inherited from Canonical F* v1.2 (最小沉积场构造式 v0.9): node depth d, relation sediment g (saturating),

import math, random, copy
from collections import defaultdict, deque

class FLB:
    def __init__(self, nodes, medium, alpha=0.05, beta=0.25, g_max=20.0, lam_d=0.01, lam_g=0.01,
                 theta=1.0, W=30, n_omega=3, kappa0=0.05, w_g=1.0, w_d=0.02, s_weak=0.4, T=0.15, seed=0):
        self.nodes=nodes; self.E=list(medium); self.out=defaultdict(list)
        for e in self.E: self.out[e[0]].append(e)
        # inherited persistent state
        self.d={n:0.0 for n in nodes}; self.g={e:0.0 for e in self.E}
        # added persistent state
        self.rep={e:deque() for e in self.E}; self.Om={e:0.0 for e in self.E}
        self.th={e:theta for e in self.E}; self.Avail={e:1 for e in self.E}
        self.alpha,self.beta,self.g_max,self.lam_d,self.lam_g=alpha,beta,g_max,lam_d,lam_g
        self.W,self.n_omega,self.k0,self.w_g,self.w_d,self.s_weak,self.T=W,n_omega,kappa0,w_g,w_d,s_weak,T
        self.t_run=0; self.t_sed=0; self.SED=True; self.rng=random.Random(seed); self.log=[]; self.macro=None
        self.mask={e:1 for e in self.E}   # RoleReadMask: exogenous control quantity (not sediment, not decayed, persists until re-intervened)
    # ---------- inherited operators
    def Decay(self):
        """one sediment-clock step: t_sed advances; depths decay; replay windows (on t_sed) expire."""
        self.t_sed+=1
        for n in self.nodes: self.d[n]=max(0.0,self.d[n]-self.lam_d)
        for e in self.E:
            self.g[e]=max(0.0,self.g[e]-self.lam_g)
            while self.rep[e] and self.t_sed-self.rep[e][0]>self.W: self.rep[e].popleft()
            self.Om[e]=self._omega(e)
    def Trace(self,e,p): return {'e':e,'p':p}
    def Sediment(self,tau):
        e,p=tau['e'],tau['p']
        self.d[e[0]]+=self.alpha*p; self.d[e[1]]+=self.alpha*p
        self.g[e]=min(self.g_max,self.g[e]+self.beta*p*(1-self.g[e]/self.g_max))
        self.rep[e].append(self.t_sed); self.Om[e]=self._omega(e)
    def kappa(self,e):
        if not self.Avail[e]: return -1e9
        return self.k0+self.w_g*self.mask[e]*self.g[e]*(1.0 if self.S(e) else self.s_weak)+self.w_d*self.d[e[1]]
    def Continuation(self,q):
        cand=[e for e in self.out[q] if self.Avail[e]]
        if not cand: return None
        ks=[self.kappa(e)/self.T for e in cand]; m=max(ks); ws=[math.exp(k-m) for k in ks]
        r=self.rng.random()*sum(ws)
        for e,w in zip(cand,ws):
            r-=w
            if r<=0: return e
        return cand[-1]
    def probs(self,q):
        cand=[e for e in self.out[q] if self.Avail[e]]
        ks=[self.kappa(e)/self.T for e in cand]; m=max(ks); ws=[math.exp(k-m) for k in ks]; z=sum(ws)
        return {e[1]:w/z for e,w in zip(cand,ws)}
    # ---------- added layer
    def S(self,e): return self.g[e]>=self.th[e]
    def Deposited(self,e): return self.g[e]>0
    def Groove(self,e): return self.S(e) and len(self.rep[e])>=2
    def Gamma(self): return {e for e in self.E if self.Groove(e)}
    def _omega(self,e):
        n=len(self.rep[e]); return 0.0 if (n<self.n_omega or not self.S(e)) else min(1.0,n/(2*self.n_omega))
    def Freeze(self): self.SED=False
    def Thaw(self): self.SED=True
    def SetAvail(self,e,v): self.Avail[e]=v
    def IdleStep(self,n=1):

    def SetMask(self,e,v): self.mask[e]=v          # targeted role intervention (structure untouched)
    # ---------- pulse
    def pulse(self,entry,p=1.0,path=None,maxhop=6):
        self.t_run+=1
        if self.SED: self.Decay()                 # t_sed advances only when sediment law is on
        q=entry; route=[q]; inst=[]
        for h in range(maxhop):
            if path is not None:
                if h>=len(path)-1: break
                e=(path[h],path[h+1])
            else:
                e=self.Continuation(q)
                if e is None: break
            if not self.Deposited(e): inst.append(e)
            if self.SED: self.Sediment(self.Trace(e,p))
            q=e[1]; route.append(q)
            if not self.out[q]: break
        self.log.append((self.t_run,entry,tuple(route),self.macro))
        return route,inst
    # ---------- law-side readouts
    # duty = dict(ante=entry node, anchor=edge whose head state is the relation's antecedent, read=target node)
    def _closure(self,start,edges):
        adj=defaultdict(list)
        for (i,j) in edges: adj[i].append(j)
        seen={start}; st=[start]
        while st:
            x=st.pop()
            for y in adj[x]:
                if y not in seen: seen.add(y); st.append(y)
        return seen
    def CarrierRealizes_inst(self,R,duty):
        """R alone (restricted) carries Ante ⇝ Anchor.tail —Anchor→ Anchor.head ⇝ Read, all on deposited edges of R;
        the anchor edge itself must be traversed (it is deposited and on the path)."""
        a=duty['anchor']
        if a not in R or not self.Deposited(a): return False
        dep=[e for e in R if self.Deposited(e)]
        return a[0] in self._closure(duty['ante'],dep) and duty['read'] in self._closure(a[1],dep)
    def Established_inst(self,R,duty):
        """R ⊆ E ∧ ∀e∈R Deposited ∧ CarrierRealizes_inst. CarrierNonInstant is a type invariant here:
        g is persistent state, so Deposited(e) ⇒ not flow-only."""
        return set(R)<=set(self.E) and all(self.Deposited(e) for e in R) and self.CarrierRealizes_inst(R,duty)
    def MediumReach_inst(self,entry,Q):
        """medium access only: entry reaches the head of some Avail=1 edge in Q via Avail=1 edges. Does not require Q deposited."""
        seen=self._closure(entry,[e for e in self.E if self.Avail[e]])
        return any(self.Avail[e] and e[0] in seen for e in Q)
    def RolePath_inst(self,R,duty):
        """a deposited, mask-open path inside R from the anchor state to Read."""
        a=duty['anchor']
        if a not in R or not (self.Deposited(a) and self.mask[a]): return False
        open_edges=[e for e in R if self.Deposited(e) and self.mask[e]]
        return a[0] in self._closure(duty['ante'],open_edges) and duty['read'] in self._closure(a[1],open_edges)
