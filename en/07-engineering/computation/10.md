# The Source Form of Reproduction Distortion in Computation

General Proposition

The distortion that appears in a reproduction is not given by computation itself, nor by the amount of computational power. It is pressed out by declarable actual processes in the construction of the reproduction: where the dynamics of the source system is written as rules in the host, distortion arises through not following wherever the history of the source system changes its dynamics; where a difference is written into the host's field state or deliveries — intrusion and purification — the difference spreads through the host's own generative loop, being retained, read in, rerouted and written again. All three kinds of process can appear without pressing out distortion; one and the same distortion can have several legitimate sources, and pointwise records on the host side at the evaluation points alone cannot tell which it is.

In One Sentence

Distortion is not a product of computation: it is the result of rules or differences written into a reproduction either not following, or following astray, in the host's generation.

Two. Declaration of the Object

### 2.1　Declaration of the reproduction

ℛ = (A, P, κ, Bg): A is the declared model of the source system, with rules τ\_A and field state D^A; P is the computing host, with rules τ\_P and field state D^P; κ is the correspondence of the structural positions and targets of the two; Bg is the frozen background — the shared delivery sequence, the shared random source (each side holding a random source with the same seed), and P's execution.

### 2.2　Distortion target and reading

The distortion target χ\_D = (T, κ, ε, T\_Π), frozen in advance: T is taken as a law target — the verdict given by the judgement kernel of the Law Group-Form on a candidate L in A and in P — or a Continuation target — the Continuation distribution on a frozen set of structural positions X\_T; ε is the resolution; T\_Π is a finite set of evaluation points.  
The distortion reading Dist\_t ∈ {1, 0, ⊥} (t ∈ T\_Π): for a law target, it is 1 when one side's verdict is "holds" and the other's "does not hold," 0 when they are the same, and ⊥ when the verdict on either side cannot be determined; for a Continuation target, it is 1 when max\_{x∈X\_T} TV(K^A\_x(t), K^P\_x(t)) \> ε, and 0 otherwise. For a law target, raising the verdict "holds" to law qualification carries Computational Layering General Form-H\_cert (what is taken being its upstream conditions pending verification and its restriction of the domain of determination of *The Determination Form of Law*); distortion is stated at the level of verdicts. A verdict "does not hold" is recorded only as that side's verdict, and this chapter does not read it as law qualification being false (the discipline of negative reading of 2.3 of *The Dissolution Form of Cross-Layer Misplacement in Computation* v1.1: a machine FAIL means only that the candidate examined does not pass the determination task, and ¬Law requires a separate check of the failure of the formal necessary qualification). When P's Continuation kernel is rewritten by an extraction unit, the verdict on the P side is given by the extended judgement kernel of Appendix B, item A-3 of *The General Form of Computational Layering* (a faithful extension in implementation: the effective arguments are found from the Continuation kernel actually executed by the instance under examination); instances whose Continuation kernel is not rewritten are still determined by the original judgement kernel (Appendix A, exp9.py, function judge); the grounds for applying the extended judgement kernel are inherited along with the restriction of the domain of determination of Computational Layering General Form-H\_cert.

### 2.3　First distortion

Genesis\_Dist^Π\_{t*} ∈ {1, 0, ⊥} (t* ∈ T\_Π being the evaluation moment under discussion): it is 1 when Dist\_{t*} = 1 and Dist\_t = 0 for every t ∈ T\_Π with t \< t*; 0 when Dist\_{t*} = 0, or there is an earlier t with Dist\_t = 1; and ⊥ otherwise. The rules of value assignment are the same as in 3.5 of *The Source Form of Law\*, with Dist in place of the verdict.

2.4　The source protocol and the construction history

The source protocol is frozen according to 3.2 of *The Source Form of Law*, the history H\_obs being an ordered finite sequence of construction units of the reproduction. Units are of three kinds:  
　Extraction unit — writing into some structural position x of τ\_P an object rule that wholly determines Continuation, what is written being a value obtained by extraction from A;  
　Purification unit — processing with a declared encoding π the handover field state (the field state of D^P at the head of the segment when handed over from A to P, before the first pulse) or some component of some delivery;  
　Intrusion unit — in the course of running, that is, after some pulse (including the interval after the first pulse and before the first evaluation point), a declared write to D^P other than Idle and Pass.  
The categories of units are frozen in advance by this protocol and determined by the object and temporal position of each unit's action, not by the constructor's naming the same operation otherwise: a change to the field state at the head of the segment before the first pulse is not an intrusion unit: if an encoding π is declared, it is a purification unit; if no encoding π is declared and it makes the field state at the head of the segment differ from D^A, it is neither a unit of this protocol nor background, and constructions containing such changes lie outside the scope of this protocol (the permitted reductions cannot delete this change, and deleting all units does not yield H\_∅), so this chapter gives no source solution for them (the change must first be declared as an encoding π and treated as a purification unit; see also "Transplantation and preloading" under the boundary cases of Section Seven); a write to D^P after the first pulse is an intrusion unit; writing an object rule into τ\_P is an extraction unit; the three kinds are pairwise disjoint. The categories here are types of units in the protocol of reproduction sources: they determine which kind of unit put in the reproduction difference relative to the source system A, not whether the qualification of the resulting field state was obtained legitimately — the latter is determined by Corollary 2.1 (iii) of *The Dissolution Form of Cross-Layer Misplacement in Computation*.  
Permitted reductions: deleting an extraction unit restores that structural position to τ\_A; deleting a purification unit restores the identity encoding; deleting an intrusion unit omits that write. Deliveries, the random source and P's execution belong to the background and are not units. The domain of reduction is finite, and the replay semantics of each reduction is explicit (Sections Five and Six of *The Source Form of Law*).

### 2.5　The empty-history control

H\_∅ is taken as faithful transplantation: τ\_P = τ\_A; the value of D^P at the head of the segment is taken from D^A by legitimate taking-over (Corollary One of *The General Form of Generative Computation*); no writes. SuffDist(H\_∅) and SourceDomainIncomplete are evaluated according to 3.4 of *The Source Form of Law*.

2.6　The actual distortion chain and the source core

The actual distortion chain ActualDistLink = ExtLink ∨₃ DevLink, the content of the two branches being characterized in Section Four; ∨₃ is three-valued disjunction. The source core:

if any of the three items is 0, DistSource is 0, and if there is no 0 but there is a ⊥, it is ⊥. Three. Exclusion of the Presumed Sources

W-D1　Inference backward from the final state. The moment and reading of first distortion do not determine the family of source solutions: the same first distortion can come from different units (Theorem Five).  
W-D2　Computation itself, the digital device itself. Excluded by Theorem One when a faithful transplantation exists.  
W-D3　Computational power, precision. Whether there is distortion is not a function of P's computational power (Theorem One (iii)); the precision of encoding does not fix source qualification — three grid spacings of one and the same encoding give no distortion, delayed distortion and immediate distortion respectively (Appendix B, item C-4).  
W-D4　Temporal precedence, correlation. By W-S2 and W-S3 of *The Source Form of Law*: preceding first distortion or co-occurring with it does not imply bearing.  
W-D5　Background enabling. Without deliveries, the random source and P's execution there is no run, but they are background, not units, and do not enter the source (W-S7 of *The Source Form of Law*).  
W-D6　Illegal deletion. Replayable = 0 ⇏ necessary bearing (W-S6 of *The Source Form of Law*).

Theorem One (the empty-history control: computation itself is not a source)　Suppose a faithful transplantation exists.  
(i) Under faithful transplantation, the readings of the two sides are the same at every evaluation point; SuffDist(H\_∅) = 0 and SourceDomainIncomplete = 0.  
(ii) P's execution, computational power and digital representation are all present in H\_∅ and in the background; they are not construction units and enter no source solution; the background by itself is not sufficient.  
(iii) On the run segment up to first distortion, whether there is distortion is not a function of P's computational power (in the sense of Definition Two of *The Dissolution Form of Cross-Layer Misplacement in Computation*).  
Proof　(i) By induction on steps: τ\_P = τ\_A, and the field states at the head of the segment are the same; at each step the states, deliveries and random numbers of the two sides are the same, and the same deterministic rule gives the same Continuation and the same write, so the readings are the same and Dist\_t = 0. (ii) By (i) and the definitions: with no units, under the background alone, no distortion appears. (iii) Take a construction containing only one intrusion unit: the write takes place outside P's execution, and up to first distortion the sequence of P's operator calls is the same as under faithful transplantation (Appendix B, item C-3: the evaluation point immediately after the write is already distorted), while one has distortion and the other none. ∎  
Restriction　If the source system A cannot be represented in P, faithful transplantation does not exist, Replayable(H\_∅) = 0 and SourceDomainIncomplete = ⊥: under this protocol it cannot be determined whether purification itself is a source. The analogue source and digital host spoken of in the initial stipulation belong to this case, and the strong reading of item 5 of the initial stipulation is neither proved nor refuted by Theorem One.  
Instance: under faithful transplantation of a field of a formed law of F\_LB, neither target is distorted at any of the 120 evaluation points (Appendix B, item C-1).

Four. Characterization of the Generating Process

### 4.1　The two modes

The two branches of the actual distortion chain correspond to two modes. Not following: where A has dynamical reading-in, P replaces it by a rule; the history of A changes A's Continuation, and P does not follow. Following astray: the difference is written into the field state or deliveries that P reads, and P takes it over through its own generative loop and deviates accordingly. The two are not presupposed to be mutually exclusive: in one and the same reproduction both branches can hold.  
Readings of the chains. ExtLink, on a law target, reads as: the object rule written by the extraction unit is present, and the set of effective arguments of Continuation at x is empty; on a Continuation target, it reads as: K^P\_x does not vary with the values of the field state, while at an evaluation point K^A\_x deviates from the extracted value by more than ε. DevLink reads as: the difference put in by a unit is taken up in the actual run by the score of some actual Continuation or by the target reading at some evaluation point, or falls on a bearing component of the law so that that component of qualification does not hold (here "that component of qualification does not hold" is read by the determination component that the judgement kernel reads, the re-walk support component reading Ω\_e; it is a reading at the level of verdicts and is not read directly as the formal component of qualification (Ω\_W) failing). Both branches are read on the states of the actual run, and "delete it and there is no distortion" is not to stand in for them (7.5 of *The Source Form of Law*).

### 4.2　Extraction: not following

Theorem Two (the extraction source)　Suppose an extraction unit writes at structural position x an object rule that wholly determines Continuation, P's Continuation kernel at x being constantly K̂.  
(a) Law target: suppose x is the starting point of the anchor edge in the declared duties of L — by the DynamicRole item of the judgement kernel of the Law Group-Form, for any candidate organization R in P to obtain Govern(R, L), the anchor edge must be an effective argument of Continuation at x, a requirement that concerns only the Continuation kernel at x and is independent of which candidate organization is in question — and that A judges L to hold at the first evaluation point. Then Dist is 1 at the first evaluation point, regardless of whether K̂ equals A's Continuation.  
(b) Continuation target: suppose x ∈ X\_T is covered by the extraction, and write TV\_x(t) = TV(K^A\_x(t), K̂). For t ∈ T\_Π: TV\_x(t) \> ε ⇒ Dist\_t = 1; when X\_T = {x}, or when at that evaluation point the readings at all other positions z ∈ X\_T do not exceed ε, Dist\_t = 1 if and only if TV\_x(t) \> ε. When the extraction is accurate (K̂ = K^A\_x(t\_e), t\_e being the moment of extraction) and the readings at the other positions do not exceed ε at the evaluation points before first distortion, first distortion is at the evaluation point in T\_Π at which TV\_x first exceeds ε — not at the moment at which the threshold is first crossed in continuous history. When the extraction diverges (TV\_x(t\_e) \> ε) and t\_e ∈ T\_Π, Dist\_{t\_e} = 1; when t\_e ∉ T\_Π, one can only say that at extraction there is already a local distributional difference exceeding ε, and first distortion must still be read off at a subsequent evaluation point.  
Existence: suppose A belongs to the working domain 𝒲₇ of *The Necessity Form of Generative Computation*, and that its field state at t\_e satisfies that chapter's C\_pre (taking it as the field state at the head of the segment D₀, with x as the branch position); then from t\_e on there are two comparable legitimate histories giving different K^A\_x at re-entry (Theorem One-A, carrying Computational Execution General Form-H3), so at least one of them makes K^A\_x deviate from K̂; whether the deviation exceeds ε depends on the instance. The deliveries of these two histories are obtained by the construction of *The Necessity Form of Generative Computation*, and need not be the shared deliveries of the declaration of the reproduction; for the declared delivery sequence, whether K^A\_x deviates likewise depends on the instance.  
(c) When (a) or (b) gives distortion, in a construction containing only this one unit it is the unique source: first distortion holds, ExtLink holds by the readings above, and deleting it gives the empty history, which is not sufficient.  
Proof　(a) By Corollary 4.4 of *The Necessity Form of Generative Computation*, Continuation at x does not read the field state, no field-state component is an effective argument of Continuation at x, and the anchor edge is not an effective argument. This fact is determined by the Continuation kernel at x alone, so for every candidate organization R in P, DynamicRole does not hold and Govern(R, L) does not hold; law qualification is an existential quantification over candidate organizations (Law\_P(L) ⇒ ∃R\[Established(R) ∧ Govern(R, L)\], necessary direction, carrying H\_G), so L does not form a law in P, the verdict is "does not hold," while in A it is "holds." Substitute organizations are thereby excluded as well: no organization bypassing x satisfies the requirement on the anchor edge at x (Appendix B, item A-3 of *The General Form of Computational Layering*: τ\_C has an empty set of effective arguments at m1, the starting point of the anchor edge, and the determination falls at DynamicRole). (b) P's Continuation kernel at x is constantly K̂, so the reading at x is TV\_x(t); Dist\_t is whether the greatest of the readings at the positions of X\_T exceeds ε, so TV\_x(t) \> ε gives Dist\_t = 1, and the two are equivalent when none of the readings at the other positions exceeds ε. Existence: both histories take the field state at t\_e as the head of the segment, K̂ is a fixed distribution, and the K^A\_x of the two differ from each other, so they cannot both equal K̂. (c) Deleting it gives H\_∅, which by Theorem One (i) is not sufficient; by the premise it is itself sufficient. The actual distortion chain is ExtLink. ∎  
Instance: τ\_C (a1 always takes m1, m1 always takes c) is loaded with A's field state; the declared anchor edge of L = a1 ⇒ c is m1→c, whose starting point m1 lies where τ\_C covers. The law target is distorted at the first evaluation point; the Continuation target is first distorted at point 73, after A has turned under consecutive exogenous experience a1→m2→x. Take another protocol frozen in advance: the deliveries take only the first 20 alternations of a1 and a2, T\_Π is its 20 evaluation points, and the rest is as in C-2; its run is by causality a prefix of the run of C-2, and the records of points 0–19 of C-2 are its run records — at these evaluation points the difference between A's Continuation at a1 and m1 and the extracted values does not exceed the resolution, and the Continuation target is not distorted (Appendix B, item C-2). This is not an after-the-fact truncation of the T\_Π of the C-2 protocol.  
Reading back: what item 4 of the initial stipulation says, that where extraction diverges accurate reproduction is hard, is the latter clause of (b); the difference between simulation and reproduction spoken of in the same item is (a) and the former clause of (b) — even if the extraction is accurate, once A's history has changed A's dynamics, the rule does not follow.

4.3　Intrusion and purification: following astray

Theorem Three (the intrusion source and the purification source)　Suppose τ\_P ∈ 𝔗 (the medium being a finite simple directed graph), with shared deliveries and a shared random source, and suppose one intrusion or purification unit makes, at moment t\_w (for purification, the head of the segment), a trace component ζ of D^P differ from that of A, the two sides being otherwise the same.  
(a) Suppose x has at least two available outgoing edges, all open for reading; ζ is the edge trace of one of these outgoing edges e, or is the head-position trace of e with the score S strictly increasing in position traces; the difference is retained until the next reading-in of Continuation at x (at reading-in, the readings of ζ on the two sides still differ), and up to that reading-in the two sides differ only in ζ. Then on any state in which the two sides differ only in ζ, K^P\_x ≠ K^A\_x, and that reading-in takes place on such a state. Continuation target: if at some evaluation point the two sides differ only in ζ, then when ζ is an edge trace only the Continuation at x can differ at that evaluation point, and Dist = 1 if and only if x ∈ X\_T and the total variation there is greater than ε; when ζ is a position trace, every position in X\_T having an outgoing edge whose head is that position can differ, and Dist is determined by definition from the greatest of the readings at the positions. Evaluation points after the two sides fork are not within this clause.  
(b) In the case of (a), on a set of positive measure of samplings of the shared random source the actual outgoing edges of the two sides at x differ. On such samplings, of the two outgoing edges passed by the two sides at the step of the fork, at least one had the same edge trace on both sides before; that edge undergoes Pass on one side only, and when unsaturated its trace differs between the two sides thereafter — at least one component that no unit wrote, and that was the same on both sides before, becomes different. This clause asserts neither that the number of differing components increases monotonically nor that the amplitude of distortion grows.  
(c) Suppose there is a moment t′ such that before t′ the difference changes neither the score of any actual Continuation nor the target reading at any evaluation point (the distribution on X\_T for a Continuation target, the verdict for a law target), and at t′ the states of the two sides become the same again (for example, the difference having been smoothed away by shallowing). Then in the construction containing only this unit Dist\_t = 0 at every evaluation point, and SuffDist = 0. Knowing only that the difference is smoothed away before some reading-in yields only the local conclusion that that reading-in is not distorted. This clause corresponds to the "smoothing away" world with Γ5 removed in 6.1 of *The Necessity Form of Generative Computation*.  
(d) If ζ bears the law (re-walk records, Ω and so on) and is not read in by the Continuation kernel, the law target can be distorted while the Continuation target is unchanged.  
Proof　(a) In a simple graph the two different outgoing edges of x have different heads, so ζ enters only the score s\_e of e, not the scores of the other outgoing edges of x. By TR5 (edge trace) or the assumed strict increase (position trace), s\_e differs between the two sides while the other scores are the same; the Continuation kernel is of Luce type, K\_x(e)/K\_x(e′) = φ(s\_e)/φ(s\_{e′}) with φ strictly increasing, so for another available outgoing edge e′ this ratio differs between the two sides, and the Continuation kernels differ (the same argument as Lemma 2 of *The Necessity Form of Generative Computation*, the difference coming from the unit's write rather than from one passage). With only one available outgoing edge the Continuation kernel is constantly a point distribution, so at least two are required. The two cases of the Continuation target: an edge trace is read in only at its starting position; a position trace is read in at every outgoing edge having that position as head. (b) By Lemma 2 of *The Necessity Form of Generative Computation*, the two distributions differ, and inverse-transform sampling gives different outgoing edges e\_A ≠ e\_P on an interval of positive measure. Before this the two sides differ only in ζ, and ζ is the edge trace of at most one of e\_A and e\_P (when ζ is a position trace, the edge traces of both are the same), so of the two at least one edge trace is the same on both sides; by TR1 it undergoes Pass on one side only, and by TR2 its reading strictly increases when unsaturated, so the two sides differ thereafter, while no unit has written it. Five. Source Theorems

Theorem Five (insufficiency of the final state for source)　There is no function that determines the family of source solutions from the moment and reading of first distortion alone: there are two constructions with the same moment and reading of first distortion whose source solutions belong to units of different types.  
Proof　Construction 1 contains the purification unit "discarding the re-walk records at handover" (before the first pulse, the encoding π deletes the re-walk records of the field state at the head of the segment and sets Ω to zero); construction 2 contains the intrusion unit "after the first pulse and before the first evaluation point, deleting from the re-walk records the entries taken over at the head of the segment, and recomputing Ω by the rules" (a write in the course of running). The two units belong to the two kinds by the temporal positions frozen in 2.4, not distinguished by naming. Item-by-item certification (Appendix B, items C-5 (i) and C-6):  
　First distortion: both at the first evaluation point, the law target judged to hold in A and not to hold in P (falling at ΩSupport), Genesis\_Dist = 1.

　Minimal sufficiency: deleting the unit gives the empty history, and faithful transplantation is not distorted (Theorem One); the unit itself is sufficient.  
Hence the source solution of construction 1 is {purification unit} and that of construction 2 is {intrusion unit}: the moment and reading of first distortion are the same while the types of source differ. ∎

Theorem Six (the source is indistinguishable from the host side)　There are two constructions whose pointwise run records of P on the set of evaluation points T\_Π — routes, operator calls, verdicts, Continuation distributions — are wholly the same, with the same distortion, while their source solutions belong to a purification unit and an intrusion unit respectively. Hence the source is not a function of the pointwise observable quantities on the P side on T\_Π; these pointwise records alone cannot certify the source, and to distinguish these two constructions one must obtain information beyond these records that can distinguish them, for example information on construction and temporal position.  
Proof　The two constructions of Theorem Five are pointwise the same on the P side (Appendix B, item C-5 (i): at all 120 evaluation points of T\_Π, P's routes, verdicts, Continuation distributions at each position and complete field states are pointwise the same; the write of construction 2 takes place outside P's execution and is not an operator call of P, and since the routes are the same, the sequences of types of operator calls of each pulse are the same). The two constructions differ in the field state at the head of the segment before the first pulse, and also in the number of expired re-walk records removed by shallowing in the first pulse (granularity of instructions within operators, as in ρ9 of *The Dissolution Form of Cross-Layer Misplacement in Computation*), neither of which lies within the pointwise records on T\_Π; precisely because their temporal positions differ, the two are separable by the protocol. Since these pointwise records of the two constructions are the same while their sources differ, any discrimination that can separate the two must use information beyond these records. ∎  
Note　This chapter, following the source protocol frozen in advance (2.4; the three items of certification are in 2.6), reads the declarations of constructions and the records of their implementation in order to fix the categories of units and certify sources; this is one route of certification adopted by this chapter's protocol, and Theorem Six does not prove it to be the only necessary route. The field state at the head of the segment mentioned above is a state on the P side fixed by the construction before the first pulse; it likewise lies outside the pointwise records on T\_Π, and also suffices to separate the pair of constructions given by this theorem.

　Under faithful transplantation there is no distortion; computation itself and computational power are not sources (when the source system can be represented in the host)　　(Theorem One)  
　Extraction: the law target is distorted immediately; on the Continuation target the reading at x is TV(K^A\_x, K̂), and distortion results once the source system's Continuation deviates from the extracted value by more than the resolution — not following　　(Theorem Two; the existence clause of (b) carrying Computational Execution General Form-H3, restricted to 𝒲₇)

Distortion in reproduction comes from somewhere. Copy the dynamics of the source system as rules, and the rules stop at the moment they were copied, while the history of the source system goes on changing its dynamics, and the reproduction falls behind; write a difference into the field of the host, and the host takes up the difference with its own generation, and the difference travels elsewhere along with reading-in and writing. In both cases computation runs as usual, and computational power is present in both; they are not where distortion comes from.

        self.g[e]=min(self.g_max,self.g[e]+self.beta*p*(1-self.g[e]/self.g_max))
        self.rep[e].append(self.t_sed); self.Om[e]=self._omega(e)
    def kappa(self,e):
        if not self.Avail[e]: return -1e9
        return self.k0+self.w_g*self.mask[e]*self.g[e]*(1.0 if self.S(e) else self.s_weak)+self.w_d*self.d[e[1]]
    def Continuation(self,q):
        cand=[e for e in self.out[q] if self.Avail[e]]
        if not cand: return None
        ks=[self.kappa(e)/self.T for e in cand]; m=max(ks); ws=[math.exp(k-m) for k in ks]
        r=self.rng.random()*sum(ws)
        for e,w in zip(cand,ws):
            r-=w
            if r<=0: return e
        return cand[-1]
    def probs(self,q):
        cand=[e for e in self.out[q] if self.Avail[e]]
        ks=[self.kappa(e)/self.T for e in cand]; m=max(ks); ws=[math.exp(k-m) for k in ks]; z=sum(ws)
        return {e[1]:w/z for e,w in zip(cand,ws)}

    # ---------- pulse
    def pulse(self,entry,p=1.0,path=None,maxhop=6):

        if self.SED: self.Decay()                 # t_sed advances only when sediment law is on
        q=entry; route=[q]; inst=[]
        for h in range(maxhop):
            if path is not None:
                if h>=len(path)-1: break
                e=(path[h],path[h+1])
            else:

                if e is None: break

            if self.SED: self.Sediment(self.Trace(e,p))

        return route,inst

    # duty = dict(ante=entry node, anchor=edge whose head state is the relation's antecedent, read=target node)
    def _closure(self,start,edges):
        adj=defaultdict(list)
        for (i,j) in edges: adj[i].append(j)
        seen={start}; st=[start]
        while st:
            x=st.pop()
            for y in adj[x]:
                if y not in seen: seen.add(y); st.append(y)
        return seen
    def CarrierRealizes_inst(self,R,duty):
        """R alone (restricted) carries Ante ⇝ Anchor.tail —Anchor→ Anchor.head ⇝ Read, all on deposited edges of R;
        the anchor edge itself must be traversed (it is deposited and on the path)."""
        a=duty['anchor']
        if a not in R or not self.Deposited(a): return False
        dep=[e for e in R if self.Deposited(e)]
        return a[0] in self._closure(duty['ante'],dep) and duty['read'] in self._closure(a[1],dep)
    def Established_inst(self,R,duty):
        """R ⊆ E ∧ ∀e∈R Deposited ∧ CarrierRealizes_inst. CarrierNonInstant is a type invariant here:
        g is persistent state, so Deposited(e) ⇒ not flow-only."""
        return set(R)<=set(self.E) and all(self.Deposited(e) for e in R) and self.CarrierRealizes_inst(R,duty)
    def MediumReach_inst(self,entry,Q):
        """medium access only: entry reaches the head of some Avail=1 edge in Q via Avail=1 edges. Does not require Q deposited."""
        seen=self._closure(entry,[e for e in self.E if self.Avail[e]])
        return any(self.Avail[e] and e[0] in seen for e in Q)
    def RolePath_inst(self,R,duty):
        """a deposited, mask-open path inside R from the anchor state to Read."""
        a=duty['anchor']
        if a not in R or not (self.Deposited(a) and self.mask[a]): return False
        open_edges=[e for e in R if self.Deposited(e) and self.mask[e]]
        return a[0] in self._closure(duty['ante'],open_edges) and duty['read'] in self._closure(a[1],open_edges)

# ---------- kernel & exact path measure ----------
def kernel(F,q,avail=None,mask=None):
    """K_y at position q: distribution over out-edges, from state only (exact)."""
    avail=avail or F.Avail; mask=mask or F.mask
    cand=[e for e in F.out[q] if avail[e]]

    ks=[]

def eval_paths(F,entry,edges_allowed=None,maxhop=MAXHOP):
    """Exhaustive Eval(Ctx): all routes with exact μ. edges_allowed=None → all; else restrict (κ_P)."""
    out=[]
    def rec(q,route,prob,hops):
        K=kernel(F,q)
        if edges_allowed is not None:
            K={e:p for e,p in K.items() if e in edges_allowed}
            z=sum(K.values())
            if z==0: out.append((tuple(route),prob,'dead')); return
            K={e:p/z for e,p in K.items()}   # 受限评价：P 外结构不可用，核在 P 内重归一
        if not K or hops>=maxhop: out.append((tuple(route),prob,'end')); return
        for e,p in K.items(): rec(e[1],route+[e[1]],prob*p,hops+1)
    rec(entry,[entry],1.0,0)
    return out

def _judge_P(F,L,P,R,pi0,eps):
    """evaluate CR/CNI/Dyn/ΩSupport on one implementation P ⊆ R (canonical witness Π_P: W=ResEval(κ_P), PR1).
    Returns (depth_reached, detail). Depth order: CR=1, CNI=2, Dyn=3, ΩSupport=4 (all-pass)."""

    # CNI by reduction lemma: frozen ⇒ Estab={g>0}; witnesses under PR1 have Q(Π)⊆P⊆R⊆Estab (CC);

    seen={anchor[1]}; st=[anchor[1]]
    while st:

        for y2 in adj[x]:
            if y2 not in seen: seen.add(y2); st.append(y2)
    rolepath=(anchor in aa) and (read in seen or read==anchor[1])
    d['Dyn']=dict(ActArg_at_anchor_tail=[f"{e[0]}→{e[1]}" for e in sorted(aa)],minimal_groups=[[f"{e[0]}→{e[1]}" for e in M] for M in minimal],anchor_in_ActArg=anchor in aa,rolepath=rolepath)
    if not rolepath: return 2,d
    # ΩSupport on Π_P
    seenA=F._closure(ante,[e for e in F.E if F.Avail[e]])
    reach=any(F.Avail[e] and e[0] in seenA for e in P)
    bear=[e for e in P if e==anchor or (anchor[1]==e[0] or e[0] in seen)]
    omega_ok=all(F.Om[e]>0 for e in bear)
    d['ΩSupport']=dict(Reach=reach,Bear=[f"{e[0]}→{e[1]}" for e in bear],Omega={f"{e[0]}→{e[1]}":round(F.Om[e],2) for e in bear})
    if not reach: return 3,dict(d,fail='Reach')

    the input F_LB state is converted to a Freeze^inst snapshot before judging.
    Existential over implementations: R passes iff ∃P ⊆ R (anchor ∈ P) whose canonical witness Π_P

    same Π_P bears ΩWit and ρ_P ∈ RoleReal via its Dyn check). fail_at = deepest level reached over all P."""
