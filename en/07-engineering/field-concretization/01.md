# The General Form of Field Concretization

General Proposition  
This paper takes up one general proposition:  
For a concrete host, within the declared level, object domain, and history window, by virtue of what does the action of signals on it actually constitute a Sedimentation-Field, that is, constitute “the Trace-Response set that signals leave in a medium, together with the mode of organization of this set, transforming under the action of historical dynamics, and its activity”? What we seek is the minimal structure N that this fact of constitution O (active concretization, in which every component of the definition has a bearer within the declared history window) requires of the host, in the form O ⇒ N.  
In this paper we set out, through persistent questioning, to find where the proof must go, and to derive the General Form of Field Concretization.  
Goal of proof:  
Within the working domain of a declared host instance, starting from the weakest fact, the action of signals, force out the minimal structure that a Sedimentation-Field must have to be actively concretized over the declared history window; prove its necessity and its stepwise irreplaceability; exclude unnecessary additions; and give the formal master formula, read-back, boundaries, breach modes, and interfaces.  
Completion conditions:  
The constraint recursion closes under a single irreducible task; every forced item has a countermodel that preserves everything obtained before it; every inherited item cites its source and scope accurately; the formal master formula corresponds item by item to the preview at the head of the paper; the minimality test and the exclusion of additions are complete; conditions, boundaries, and unanswered questions are declared truthfully.  
Breach modes:  
Within the working domain declared in this paper, exhibit a host instance on which a Sedimentation-Field is actively concretized over the declared history window while some item of the necessary structure listed here fails; or prove that some item listed as necessary can be deleted or weakened while concretization still holds. Exhibiting another physical realization of the same duty does not constitute a breach.  
We begin with the result. (What follows only previews the result and is not a premise of the later derivation; the formal master formula is given in Section 5.)  
Necessary Form of Field Concretization (abbreviated, to be proved)  
On a declared host instance χ = (P, L, B, W):  
&nbsp;&nbsp;Fld(χ) ⇒ ∃T \[ TraceSet(χ, T) ∧ Part(χ, T) ∧ HDyn(χ, T) \]  
· Trace-Response-set duty TraceSet(χ, T): T is a nonempty Trace-Response set in B that persists within W; the formative history of T contains Trace-Responses left by signals acting on the medium. The other elements of T may be produced by in-field continuation.  
· Organizational-participation duty Part(χ, T): the mode of organization of T participates in the actual processes of the host within W.  
· Historical-dynamics duty HDyn(χ, T): within W, at least one actual process acts on the mode of organization of T, forming, maintaining, or transforming it (the actions may cancel, with zero net change), and this process issues from the Trace-Responses that T has already left.  
· All three take the same T as argument. The dynamics that issues from the Trace-Responses and acts on the organization is not required to run along a complete material route within B; that is a condition of a stronger field type (Section 7).  
In one sentence: for a host to actually become a Sedimentation-Field, signals must leave Trace-Responses in its medium, the organization of these Trace-Responses must enter its actual processes over the history in question, and a dynamics issuing from these Trace-Responses must actually act on this organization over the history in question.

Abstract  
*The Ontological Definition of the Sedimentation-Field* says what a Sedimentation-Field is. Beyond its main text, its symbol definitions define Tᵢ as “the current Trace-Response set,” Dᵢ as dynamics, and D′ᵢ as dynamic re-action, and its diagram draws dynamics out of the Trace-Response set and has it act again on the succeeding current Trace-Response set. *The Mathematical Form of the Field* v2.7 gives the Trace-Response (the effective change a Structural Position sustains and retains), the pulse (the effective action that produces a Trace-Response, which may come from input entering the field or from in-field continuation), and the two-term evolution of Sedimentation-Degree by deepening and shallowing. What remains unclosed is the concretization problem: for a concrete physical host, onto which facts of the host must the terms of the definition fall for that host to actually constitute a Sedimentation-Field?  
The questioning starts from the weakest available fact. Signals act on the host: not enough, since an action may leave no change at all. This yields the Trace-Response-set duty, inherited from the definition and the Field Chapters. A Trace-Response set is not enough: it may merely be stored, entering no actual process over the history in question. This yields the organizational-participation duty. Participation is still not enough: the organization of the Trace-Responses may only be read, with no process acting on it. This yields the requirement that some actual process act on this organization. Acting on the organization is still not enough: the action may have nothing to do with the Trace-Responses and be applied on an external schedule. The structure given by the definition's symbol definitions and diagram is a dynamics that issues from the Trace-Response set and acts on it again; brought into the time window in question under the bearing reading of this Form, it yields the requirement that at least one process acting on the organization have its action determined in part, through an actual material route, by the Trace-Responses already left. Ask again at this point, and every term of the definition has a bearer; forcing stops. Closure of the trace-issued dynamics within B is not a necessary condition for a Sedimentation-Field in general.  
Result: on a declared host instance, a Sedimentation-Field can be constituted only if three duties hold (the Trace-Response-set, organizational-participation, and historical-dynamics duties), all with the same Trace-Response set as their object. The direction of necessity is proved; the converse is a definitional correspondence.

1\. Version Position and Declaration of Degree of Answer  
This is the first paper of the Field Concretization Group Form.  
The task this paper sets out to close is this: given a declared host instance on which signals act, which facts of the host must bear each term of the definition for the host to actually constitute a Sedimentation-Field over the declared history window.  
The sub-questions that actually arise and are answered in this paper are as follows:  
(1) Whether signal action suffices to establish Trace-Responses — proved: it does not (First Round); what is needed is the Trace-Response of the Field Chapters, and the signal requirement is inherited from the definition and the Field Chapters.  
(2) Whether the Trace-Response set must participate in actual processes over the history in question — proved: it must (Second Round, forcing out Part).  
(3) Whether, beyond participation, some actual process must act on the mode of organization — proved: it must (Third Round, forcing out Act).  
(4) Whether, beyond action, there must be an action issuing from the Trace-Responses — proved: there must (Fourth Round; the structure is inherited from the definition's symbol definitions and diagram, the bearing reading belongs to this paper, and P₄ shows that it cannot be replaced by Act).  
(5) Whether the dynamics issuing from the Trace-Responses and acting on the organization must run along a complete route within B — proved: it need not (Convergence Round, witness χ\_e); whether the acting process must be identical with the participating process — proved: a trace-issued action process executed by a mechanism within B itself bears Part (Convergence Round lemma), so identity of the two processes is not an independent addition.  
(6) Whether multiple elements, interconnection, some specified topology, nonzero change at every moment, or a signal origin for every element is required — proved: none is required (Sections 5 and 6, witnesses χ₀, χ\*).  
(7) Whether the three duties imply that a Sedimentation-Field is constituted — definitional correspondence (Corollary 1).  
(8) How the three duties are adjudicated in a concrete host — unanswered; this belongs to the adjudication task.  
(9) The general identity condition for modes of organization — unanswered.  
(10) Whether all endogenous Trace-Responses can be traced back to signals — unanswered; not load-bearing.  
Uniqueness, global independent minimality, and uniqueness of realization are not proved.

2\. Working Domain and Inheritance Boundary  
Working domain. A host instance is χ = (P, L, B, W). P is the physical host. L is the declared descriptive level and resolution; the resolution is part of the declaration and specifies which changes count as effective at that level, not the detection capability of some measuring instrument. B is the declared object domain: the part of the medium of P examined at level L, with its set of Structural Positions Ξ\_B, together with the mechanism carriers declared to be components of the host; whatever lies outside B is outside the boundary. W is the declared finite history window.  
Active-concretization stipulation. The fact of constitution O treated by this Form is active concretization over W: every component of the definition (the Trace-Response set, its mode of organization and its activity, and its transformation under the action of historical dynamics) has a bearer within W; Trace-Responses may form before W, provided they persist within W. This relativization to a time window is a stipulation of the working domain, not something drawn from the definition: the definition's main text, symbol definitions, and diagram fix no time scale and do not require every finite time window to contain a dynamic re-action. A host whose dynamic re-actions occur only in history outside W is not an active concretization over W; declaring it with a time window that covers that history gives another instance.  
Non-degeneracy condition: there are instances in which signals act on P and no Sedimentation-Field is constituted (First Round, P₁), and instances in which a Sedimentation-Field is constituted (Section 6, χ₀, χ\*); hence O is neither always true nor always false.

Whether these structures hold in a given host, and how they are certified by intervention and observation protocols, belong to the adjudication task; the classification of types of transformation of the mode of organization belongs to the grading task; there is as yet no established result on the general identity condition for modes of organization.

3\. Preliminary Distinctions  
Bearing. The bearers on χ of the terms of *The Ontological Definition of the Sedimentation-Field* are the states and processes of P at level L, within object domain B, in history window W. Fld(χ) says that the definition is satisfied under this bearing relation; this is the entire content of the fact of constitution O. The bearing relation directly yields that the changes bearing Trace-Responses lie in the medium of B. When a process acts on the medium of B, the action falls within B and is borne within B; the mechanism carrier of the process producing it may lie outside the boundary, as with an out-of-boundary signal. A change in an external recorder is not a Trace-Response on χ, even if it is caused by a signal received by P; declaring the recorder separately gives another instance. The same physical device under a different level, object domain, or history window is another instance; the failure of one instance to constitute a Sedimentation-Field does not imply that the device has no Sedimentation-Field under other declarations.  
Signals, pulses, and Trace-Responses. A signal is an action from outside B that actually acts on the medium to which B belongs, whatever the channel; it corresponds to the Sᵢ that enters from outside in the definition's diagram. By *The Mathematical Form of the Field*, a signal entering the field forms pulses, and pulses produce Trace-Responses at Structural Positions; a pulse may also be an in-field continuation action. A Trace-Response set may therefore contain two kinds of elements: those left by pulses formed as signals enter the field, and those left by in-field continuation. The definition speaks of “the Trace-Response set that signals leave in a medium,” and the phrase qualifies the set. It requires that the formative history of the set contain Trace-Responses left by signals, not that the pulse chain of every element trace back to some signal. Nor is the Sᵢ drawn at each step of the definition's diagram required at every step: over a stretch of history, T may receive no signal and undergo only dynamic re-action.  
Trace-Response elements, depth, and mode of organization. A Trace-Response element is a change left at some Structural Position; its depth is the Sedimentation-Degree δ. By the definition's explanatory note, a single Trace-Response only deepens or shallows. The mode of organization Org(T) of T comprises the depths of the elements of T, together with the relations and structures formed over these elements. T denotes one and the same set advancing through history; T1, T2, T3 in the definition's symbols are its states at successive points of history. Org(T) is the organizational state of T over the declared history, including its past states.  
Organizational conditioning. That the course of a process π is conditioned on Org(T), written Cond(π, Org(T)), means that the actual course of π (including its conditions of occurrence and its site, direction, or strength of action at the declared level) is conditioned on the element depths, distribution, or organizational relations that T has already left. This conditioning may be transmitted through an actual material route and may enter π from past states of Org(T) through historical continuity; it is not restricted to direct reading at the same moment, and it does not require every link of π to be determined by T. What serves as the condition must be the organizational state of the same declared T; another group of Trace-Responses in B unrelated to T, or any other historical variable, does not constitute Cond(π, Org(T)).  
Action. A process ρ acts on Org(T) when, within W, ρ actually applies, by material means, a deepening, a shallowing, or a change of relation to the elements or relations of T, and this action is effective at the declared resolution of L; it may cancel against other actions. Action is an object-level fact, not the detection result of an observation protocol. The unit of process identification is the mechanism process: a process is a stretch of actual operation of one mechanism carrier within W, and a process whose carrier lies within B is a mechanism process within B. When several carriers act together only inseparably and no legitimate process-by-process decomposition exists, their union is taken as one process; when a legitimate process-by-process decomposition exists, the mechanism processes to which the components of the decomposition belong are decisive, and decomposable processes may not be merged into a composite process.  
Component criterion. When the hosting mechanism admits a legitimate process-by-process decomposition at L, each step of change of T being written as the sum of the components that the actual processes contribute through their own material mechanisms (as with the deepening term Dτ and the shallowing term Eλ in Equation 2 of *The Mathematical Form of the Field*), action has an exact criterion: ρ acts on Org(T) if and only if the component of ρ within W is nonzero and is an effective change. Then, if two components are +a and −a with zero net change, each is still an action, whether or not any observation protocol can separate them; whether they can be separated is a question of adjudicative evidence. A process that by its mechanism has no effective component at L, such as the light on the mirror in the First Round, is not an action on Org(T), even if it is in contact with T. Process-by-process decomposition is not a universal form for all hosts: nonlinear coupling or inseparable joint action need not have a unique decomposition. In that case the general characterization of action applies with the joint process as the unit, and the criterion of action for a joint process with zero net change remains open (Section 8). The counterexamples of Section 4 and the witnesses of Sections 4 and 6 all admit legitimate process-by-process decompositions.  
Maintenance and rest. By the explanatory note, no change is zero change, so “transformation” includes maintenance. Maintenance must be borne by action: remaining unchanged because effective actions cancel is maintenance; remaining unchanged with no effective action at all is rest, not maintenance under the action of dynamics.  
Historical dynamics. The definition says “transforming under the action of historical dynamics.” By the symbol definitions and diagram, dynamics Dᵢ is drawn out of the current Trace-Response set Tᵢ and, as dynamic re-action D′ᵢ, acts on the succeeding current Trace-Response set; the explanatory note adds that “the Trace-Response set is organized by dynamics.” The historical dynamics here is therefore a dynamics that issues from the Trace-Responses this set has already left and acts again on this set. This structure is inherited. Its bearing reading on χ is as follows: “drawn out of Tᵢ” in the diagram is borne by an actual material route from Trace-Responses T has already left to the action of the process; by the active-concretization stipulation, this re-action occurs within W. The Third and Fourth Rounds below test the necessity of the two layers, “action” and “issuing from the Trace-Responses,” respectively.  
History window. A Sedimentation-Field is constituted relative to W: Trace-Responses may form before W, provided they persist within W; activity and dynamic action must occur within W.  
A set presupposes no particular number of elements. A Trace-Response set may have a single element.

4\. Constraint Recursion  
First Round. On what is known so far (the definition, the pulse and Trace-Response of the Field Chapters, and the declaration and bearing relation of instance χ), is the task already closed? The definition says what a Sedimentation-Field is, and the bearing relation says where the bearers must be, but neither says which fact of P bears “the Trace-Response set that signals leave in a medium.” The weakest available fact is that signals act on P.  
Let P₁ be a flat mirror, with the level taken as optical reflection, the resolution as the declared precision of reflectance and surface figure, B as the mirror surface, and W as a time window of illumination. Within W, incident light acts on the mirror surface and is reflected; once the action stops, the state of the mirror surface at that level and resolution is indistinguishable from its state before the action. Signal action holds, but no change persists at the declared resolution. By the Field Chapters there is no Trace-Response here, and Fld fails on the instance of P₁. This conclusion concerns only the declared level and resolution.  
Hence what bears the first component must be the Trace-Response set that signals leave in a medium. By the definition and the Field Chapters, its items are:  
(q) each element is an effective change sustained and retained by some Structural Position in B and persists within W (*The Mathematical Form of the Field*, Definition 3);  
(r) in the formative history of T (W and before), pulses formed by signals entering the field leave Trace-Responses at Structural Positions of T (the definition, “the Trace-Response set that signals leave in a medium”; *The Mathematical Form of the Field*, Axioms 1, 2); the other elements of T may be produced by in-field continuation (*The Mathematical Form of the Field*, Definition 2).  
Both are readings on χ of inherited items. The Trace-Response-set duty TraceSet(χ, T) is fixed as: T is nonempty, its elements lie in B, and (q)(r) hold.  
Second Round. Given TraceSet(χ, T), is the task closed? Let P₂ be a fuse-programmed read-only memory, with the level taken as logical bits and B as all the fuse bits. Before W, external programming pulses blow some of the fuses; the bit pattern is kept throughout W, but within W no circuit reads it, no process runs conditioned on it, and no process acts on it. TraceSet(χ₂, T) holds. The second component of the definition requires “the mode of organization … of this set, and its activity,” and Fld is constitution over W: within W the mode of organization of T participates in no actual process, and “and its activity” has no bearer on this instance.  
Forced out:  
(p) the mode of organization of T participates in the actual processes of the host within W: there exists a process, actually occurring within W and with its mechanism carrier in B, whose course is conditioned on Org(T).  
The organizational-participation duty Part(χ, T) is fixed.  
Third Round. Given TraceSet and Part, is the task closed? Let P₃ be a grooved plate together with an optical scanner, both belonging to B, with the level taken as the surface topography of the grooves and the resolution as the declared depth precision. The grooves were punched earlier by steel balls falling from outside the boundary (the impacts of the balls are signals) and belong to the Trace-Response set T within B. Within W, the scanner scans the groove profile with weak light, and its output is determined by the grooves: Part holds. The plate sits in an inert sealed environment: within W no abrasion, weathering, deposition, or other process gives, by its mechanism, an effective component on the grooves at the declared level; the action of the scanning light on the plate surface, like the light on the mirror in the First Round, has no effective component at that level. These are stipulations of the mechanism of P₃ itself. So within W the mode of organization of the grooves is only read, and its remaining unchanged is rest, not maintenance under the action of dynamics. The definition requires “the mode of organization of this set, transforming under the action of historical dynamics”: within W the mode of organization of T is under no dynamic action, and this phrase has no bearer.  
Forced out:  
(a) within W some actual process acts on Org(T): by material means it actually applies to the elements or relations of T a deepening, shallowing, or change of relation that is effective at L; the actions may cancel, and the net change may be zero.  
Fourth Round. Given TraceSet, Part, and (a), is the task closed? Let P₄ consist of the plate and scanner of P₃ plus a sandblaster outside the boundary that blasts a few fixed positions on a fixed schedule, each time removing a fixed thickness of the plate surface where it reaches and so changing the depth of the grooves there. Within W the scan output is determined by the grooves, so Part holds; the sandblasting gives an effective component on the grooves, so (a) holds; apart from this, no other process within W gives an effective component on the grooves. The position, timing, and strength of the sandblasting are determined by the schedule alone; executing the schedule reads neither the pattern of the grooves, nor the scan output, nor any previously left Trace-Response, and is not materially influenced by them. In P₄ the scanning and the sandblasting are borne by different mechanisms and have a legitimate process-by-process decomposition at the declared level; by the process-identification rules of Section 3 they are separate mechanism processes and are not merged. The scanning is conditioned on the groove organization but applies no effective action to it; the sandblasting applies effective action to the grooves, but its occurrence, timing, position, and strength are all determined by an independent schedule, and the Trace-Responses T has already left take no part in determining them. Hence within W no process satisfies Act(ρ, Org(T)) ∧ HistCond(ρ, T). P₄ retains TraceSet, Part, and Act but does not satisfy (h).  
The agent the definition requires is “historical dynamics,” and its symbol definitions and diagram say what this dynamics is: dynamics Dᵢ is drawn out of the current Trace-Response set Tᵢ and, as dynamic re-action D′ᵢ, acts on the succeeding current Trace-Response set; the explanatory note says “the Trace-Response set is organized by dynamics.” The only process in P₄ that acts on T is not drawn out of T, so the branch of the diagram that is drawn out of Tᵢ and acts again on T has no bearer on P₄.  
Inherited:  
(h) at least one process satisfying (a) issues from the Trace-Responses T has already left: its action on Org(T) within W (its occurrence, timing, position, strength, or direction) is determined in part, through an actual material route, by Trace-Responses T has already left (whether left before W or within W).  
The structure of (h), dynamics drawn out of the Trace-Response set and acting again on this set, is inherited from the definition's symbol definitions and diagram. “Determined in part through an actual material route” is the bearing reading of “drawn out of” on a physical host, taken in its weakest form: the Trace-Responses need only take part in determining the action, not determine it alone. “Within W” comes from the active-concretization stipulation. P₄ shows that (h) cannot be replaced by (a). It is a conditioning relation within actual processes, of the same kind as Cond in Part; intervention and counterfactual comparison are methods of certifying it and are not part of its content. (h) is an existence requirement: actions that coexist with the trace-issued action but are not conditioned on the Trace-Responses (such as adding the sandblasting of P₄ to an already constituted Sedimentation-Field) do not destroy it. (h) says that the dynamics itself issues from the Trace-Responses, not that the values of T depend on history: the latter holds even under mere persistence.  
The historical-dynamics duty HDyn(χ, T) is fixed: (a) and (h) hold for one and the same acting process.  
Convergence Round. Given TraceSet, Part, and HDyn, with the same T as argument, is the task closed? Checking term by term: “Trace-Responses that signals leave in a medium” is borne by (q)(r); “set” by T; “the mode of organization … of this set, and its activity” by Part; “transforming under the action of historical dynamics” by HDyn, where (a) bears “transforming under the action of dynamics,” maintenance included, and (h) bears the relation, within “historical dynamics,” by which the dynamics issues from the Trace-Response set and acts on it again. Every component of the definition has a bearer, and the task is closed.  
We next ask about possible additions, beginning with the relation between the acting process and the participating process.  
Lemma (historical conditioning implies organizational participation). Under the process identification and the semantics of organizational conditioning fixed in Section 3, if HistCond(ρ, T), then Cond(ρ, Org(T)); if moreover ρ ∈ Proc\_W(B), then ρ itself satisfies the participation condition required by Part.  
Proof. HistCond(ρ, T) requires that at least one of the occurrence, timing, position, strength, or direction of the actual action of ρ on Org(T) be determined in part, through an actual material route, by Trace-Responses already left in T. At the declared level these Trace-Responses enter Org(T) as element depths, distributions, or relational states; hence the corresponding course of ρ is conditioned on the state of Org(T) (including its past states), and by the characterization of organizational conditioning in Section 3, Cond(ρ, Org(T)). This neither identifies T with Org(T) nor requires Org(T) to determine ρ alone. If the mechanism carrier of ρ lies in B, then ρ ∈ Proc\_W(B), and by the definition of Part, ρ itself bears the organizational-participation duty. ∎  
Scope of application. The lemma does not imply that a trace-issued action process must be executed by a mechanism within B; when the mechanism carrier of ρ lies outside the boundary, Part cannot be obtained from the lemma, and a separate participating process within B is needed.  
Hence the requirement that “the process acting on the organization be identical with a process in which the organization participates” opens no new constraint: every trace-issued action process executed by a mechanism within B is itself a participating process. Only one addition remains: whether the trace-issued dynamics must run along a complete material route within B. It answers no new unclosed constraint, as the following witness shows.  
Let χ\_e be as follows. B contains only a Structural Position m and its readout circuit; δ\_m is deepened by external write pulses and persists, so TraceSet holds; within W, m does not shallow within B, and no other mechanism within B acts on m. The output current of the readout circuit is determined by δ\_m, so Part holds. A controller E outside B records the output current and, according to the record, sends pulses to m; the pulses act directly on m, and no mechanism within B executes the write. These pulses give an effective component on m, so (a) holds; their timing and strength are determined by the output recorded by E, and the output is determined by δ\_m, so this action issues, through an actual material route, from the Trace-Response of m, and (h) holds. The action falls within B; the material route from the Trace-Response of m to this action passes through E, outside the boundary. Within W the only process acting effectively on m is the sending by E, whose mechanism carrier is outside the boundary; the readout circuit is conditioned on δ\_m but does not act on m. The mechanism carrier of E lies outside B throughout; that the sent pulses produce an effective change within B shows only that the object of the action lies within B, and does not change the mechanism attribution of the sending process. The readout process and the sending process are identified separately under the rules of Section 3. Hence, although χ\_e satisfies HDyn, it has no trace-issued action process that is borne by a mechanism within B and whose relevant material route lies entirely within B. All three duties have bearers on χ\_e, so by the term-by-term check above (Section 5, Corollary 1), χ\_e is a Sedimentation-Field; what E sends are legitimate signals, and the deepening they leave is a Trace-Response.  
Hence closure of the trace-issued dynamics within B is not a necessary condition for a Sedimentation-Field in general. It delimits a stronger field type (Section 7). Forcing stops.

5\. Formal Master Formula, Read-Back, and Main Theorems  
Symbols and types.  
· χ = (P, L, B, W); Ξ\_B: the set of Structural Positions of B.  
· τ: a change event at a Structural Position; δ: its depth.  
· T: a nonempty set of Trace-Response elements in B; Org(T): the mode of organization of T (Section 3).  
· Hist(T): the formative history of T (W and before).  
· Proc\_W: the set of processes actually occurring within W, individuated by the identification rules of Section 3; Proc\_W(B) ⊆ Proc\_W: those whose mechanism carriers lie in B.  
· Cond(π, Org(T)): the actual course of process π (including its conditions of occurrence and its site, direction, or strength of action) is conditioned on the organizational state of the same T (including its past states), possibly transmitted through an actual material route (Section 3).  
· Act(ρ, Org(T)): within W, ρ actually applies, by material means, an action effective at L to Org(T) (Section 3); ρ is a mechanism process under the identification rules of Section 3, and a joint process only when inseparable.  
· c\_ρ: when the hosting mechanism admits a legitimate process-by-process decomposition at L, the component of ρ on Org(T); then Act(ρ, Org(T)) ⇔ c\_ρ is nonzero and effective within W.

· Fld(χ): *The Ontological Definition of the Sedimentation-Field* is satisfied on χ under the bearing relation of Section 3, with every component borne within W (active-concretization stipulation).

Read-back.  
· τ on Ξ\_B: a consequence of the bearing relation.  
· q(τ): τ is an effective change sustained and retained by a Structural Position and persists within W.  
· r(T): in Hist(T), pulses formed by signals entering the field leave Trace-Responses at Structural Positions of T; the signal action may precede W, whatever its channel; the other elements of T may be produced by in-field continuation and need not trace back to signals.  
· Part: there exists a process, actually occurring within W and with its mechanism carrier in B, whose actual course is conditioned on the organizational state of the same T (including its past states).  
· HDyn: there exists a process ρ actually occurring within W that actually applies, by material means, an action effective at L to the mode of organization of T (actions may cancel and the net change may be zero; under a legitimate process-by-process decomposition, this means that the component of ρ is nonzero and effective), and the action of ρ is determined in part, through an actual material route, by Trace-Responses T has already left. The mechanism carrier of ρ may lie outside the boundary, with its action falling in the medium of B; when ρ is executed by a mechanism within B, it itself bears Part (Convergence Round lemma). Other actions not issuing from the Trace-Responses may coexist.  
· The tuple is not a temporal order; TraceSet is written first to indicate that Part and HDyn take T as argument.  
Theorem 1 (necessary structure of Field Concretization). Within the working domain of Section 2 (including the active-concretization stipulation), for any declared host instance χ, if Fld(χ), then there exists T such that TraceSet(χ, T), Part(χ, T), and HDyn(χ, T) hold together.  
Proof. Suppose Fld(χ). The first component of the definition is borne on χ by some nonempty set T; by the bearing relation, its elements lie on Ξ\_B. As “Trace-Responses left,” each element must meet the Field-Chapter qualification for a Trace-Response and persist within W, for otherwise there is no Trace-Response (First Round, type P₁); this gives q. As a set “that signals leave in a medium,” T must have in its formative history Trace-Responses left by signals, for otherwise the set is not one left by signals; by the field-entry axiom and the Trace-Response axiom, a signal's leaving a Trace-Response means that the signal enters the field as pulses and the pulses leave Trace-Responses at Structural Positions; this gives r. Hence TraceSet(χ, T).  
The second component of the definition takes the same T as its object and is borne on χ within W. “And its activity” requires a process within W, with its mechanism carrier in B, that is conditioned on Org(T), for otherwise the mode of organization has no activity (Second Round, type P₂); this gives Part. “Transforming under the action of dynamics” must, by the active-concretization stipulation, be borne within W, and it requires a process within W that actually applies an effective action to Org(T), for otherwise the mode of organization is merely at rest (Third Round, type P₃); this gives Act. This step uses only the general characterization of action, not process-by-process decomposition. “Historical dynamics” is, by the symbol definitions and diagram, a dynamics drawn out of the Trace-Response set and acting again on this set, and by the active-concretization stipulation it must be borne within W. Hence among the acting processes above there is at least one whose action is determined in part, through an actual material route, by Trace-Responses T has already left, for otherwise the branch of the diagram that is drawn out of Tᵢ and acts again on T has no bearer over W (Fourth Round, type P₄); this gives HistCond. Hence HDyn(χ, T). ∎  
Corollary 1 (definitional correspondence). If there exists T such that TraceSet, Part, and HDyn hold on χ, then Fld(χ). The reason is the term-by-term check of the Convergence Round: the three duties assign bearers on χ to every term of the definition and to T, S, D, D′ in the symbol definitions. This direction follows from the item-by-item correspondence between the terms of the definition and the duties; it supplies no procedure for adjudicating whether the duties hold.  
Theorem 2 (irreplaceability). Within the working domain of Section 2:  
(i) signals act on P ⇏ there exists an element satisfying q (P₁);  
(ii) TraceSet ⇏ TraceSet ∧ Part (P₂);  
(iii) TraceSet ∧ Part ⇏ ∃ρ Act (P₃);  
(iv) TraceSet ∧ Part ∧ ∃ρ Act ⇏ ∃ρ \[Act ∧ HistCond\] (P₄).  
Proof. Check each instance constructed in Section 4. ∎  
Theorem 3 (non-necessity of additions). None of the following is a necessary condition for a Sedimentation-Field to be constituted:  
(i) the existence of a process that issues from the Trace-Responses, acts on the organization, and has its material route entirely within B (witness χ\_e);  
(ii) more than one element in the Trace-Response set; interconnection among elements; any specified topology among elements (such as a chain, ring, or lattice); nonzero net change at every moment (witness χ₀, Section 6);  
(iii) every element of T being produced directly by a signal (witness χ\*, Section 6: Trace-Responses left by endogenous continuation).  
Proof. Each witness satisfies TraceSet, Part, and HDyn, and so is a Sedimentation-Field by Corollary 1, yet fails the listed condition. ∎  
By the Convergence Round lemma, identity of the acting process and the participating process holds automatically for trace-issued action processes executed by mechanisms within B, so it is not listed as a separate addition.

6\. Minimality and Load-Bearing Classification  
Load-bearing classification.  
　Component: τ on Ξ\_B; category: consequence of the working domain; source: the bearing relation.  
　Component: activity, action, and re-action borne within W; category: stipulation of the working domain; source: the active-concretization stipulation (Section 2).  
　Component: q; category: inherited; source: *The Mathematical Form of the Field* v2.7, Definition 3.  
　Component: r; category: inherited; source: the definition, “the Trace-Response set that signals leave in a medium”; *The Mathematical Form of the Field* v2.7, Axioms 1, 2, Definition 2.  
　Component: Part; category: forced out in this paper; source: Second Round, P₂.  
　Component: Act; category: forced out in this paper; source: Third Round, P₃; the process-by-process component is the exact criterion when a legitimate decomposition exists, with reference to *The Mathematical Form of the Field*, Equation 2.  
　Component: HistCond; category: structurally inherited, with the bearing reading belonging to this paper; source: for the structure, the definition's symbol definitions (Tᵢ, Dᵢ, D′ᵢ), diagram, and explanatory notes; for “determined in part through an actual material route,” the bearing reading of this paper; for “within W,” the active-concretization stipulation; Fourth Round, P₄, shows that it cannot be replaced by Act.  
　Component: transformation includes zero change; elements only deepen and shallow; category: inherited; source: the explanatory notes of *The Ontological Definition of the Sedimentation-Field*.  
What this paper forces out are two bearing conditions for the second component of the definition on a concrete host: the activity must actually occur, and the organization must actually be acted on. That some action must issue from the Trace-Responses is inherited from the definition's symbols and diagram, and this paper proves that it cannot be replaced by the first two. The items of the first component are inherited from the definition and the Field Chapters.  
Removal and weakening test. In the recursive sense, each instance satisfies all preceding items and lacks only the item under test: P₂ satisfies TraceSet and lacks Part; P₃ satisfies TraceSet ∧ Part and lacks Act; P₄ satisfies TraceSet ∧ Part ∧ Act and lacks HistCond. q is an inherited item, and P₁ shows that signal action is not enough to provide it.  
Dependency types. Part and HDyn take T as argument and are object-dependent on TraceSet; “Part ∧ ¬TraceSet” is meaningless and is not used as a counterexample. HistCond implies Cond (Convergence Round lemma): when a trace-issued action process is executed by a mechanism within B, it itself bears Part; when it is executed by an out-of-boundary mechanism (χ\_e), Part is borne separately by a process within B. The two jointly take the same T as object and may be causally dependent in particular realizations (as in χ₀ and χ\_e). Within HDyn, Act → HistCond is a linear dependency: HistCond speaks of the acting process in Act.  
Witness χ₀ for a single element and zero change (abstract model). B contains a single Structural Position x with its readout and write-back mechanisms; the level is the model's real-valued Sedimentation-Degree, and any nonzero component is an effective change. The state is δ ∈ \[0, δ\_max\]; an external signal uₜ ∈ \[0, ū\] acts on x, with ū ≥ 1/(λ − β); the activity is aₜ = δₜ (readout is conditioned on δ); the update is δₜ₊₁ = min{δ\_max, δₜ + α·uₜ + β·aₜ − λ·δₜ}, with parameter domain α \> 0, 0 \< β \< λ \< 1, α/(λ − β) \< δ\_max. The three components belong, in order, to three processes: signal entry, readout write-back, and shallowing. Signals deepen δ and form the Trace-Response τ\_x, so (q)(r) hold; readout is conditioned on δ, so Part holds; the readout write-back component β·aₜ and the shallowing component −λ·δₜ are both nonzero when δₜ \> 0, so (a) holds; the readout write-back component β·aₜ = β·δₜ is determined by the Trace-Response x has already left, so (h) holds. Write δ\* = α/(λ − β) \< δ\_max. Declared history: before W begins, let δ₀ = 0 and apply one external signal u₀ = 1/(λ − β). After this update, δ₁ = α·u₀ = α/(λ − β) = δ*, and δ₁ is taken as the initial state of W. At each step within W take uₜ = 1; then δₜ₊₁ = δ* + α + β·δ\* − λ·δ\* = δ*, so δₜ stays at δ*. The net change is zero, while the three mechanism components of signal entry, readout write-back, and shallowing, α, β·δ*, and −λ·δ*, are each nonzero. T = {τ\_x} has a single element, with no relations among elements and no topology. The readout and write-back mechanisms are mechanism carriers within B, not elements of T. The readout write-back process of χ₀ is executed by a mechanism within B, and its route from the Trace-Response lies entirely within B, so it belongs to the in-boundary self-feedback type of Section 7; this does not require all Sedimentation-Fields to use feedback mechanisms of the same kind.  
The three duties can be realized together (computational realization). Declare χ\* to be Canonical F\* of *The Construction Form of the Minimal Sedimentation-Field* v1.3: B\* contains the substrate Ξ = {A, B, C, D}, the persistent field states d and g, and the execution carriers of the four operators; the level is that Form's state variables, and any nonzero component is an effective change; the external signals are the external permitted events in exogenous experience mode; the declared history comprises an exogenous experience phase followed by an endogenous continuation phase, and W covers the endogenous continuation phase. 7\. Stronger Field Type  
On top of the three duties, a stronger field type can be delimited. It is not a necessary condition for a Sedimentation-Field in general (Theorem 3) but a subclass of Sedimentation-Fields:  
· In-boundary self-feedback type: the dynamics that issues from Trace-Responses T has already left and actually acts again on its organization is borne by a mechanism process within B, and the relevant material route from the Trace-Responses to that action lies entirely within B. Formally, there exists ρ ∈ Proc\_W(B) with Act(ρ, Org(T)) ∧ HistCond(ρ, T), and the material route through which HistCond runs lies entirely within B. Relative to a Sedimentation-Field in general, this type adds only two items: the acting process is borne by a mechanism within B (the quantifier range narrows from Proc\_W to Proc\_W(B)), and the relevant material route lies entirely within B. That a single process bears both Cond and Act is a consequence of the Convergence Round lemma, not a separately listed qualification. χ₀ belongs to this type; χ\_e does not.  
That HistCond implies Cond does not imply that every Sedimentation-Field is of this type: HDyn requires only ρ ∈ Proc\_W, a quantifier range different from that of this type. Processes are individuated by the identification rules of Section 3, so this type cannot be obtained by splicing an in-boundary participating process and an out-of-boundary acting process into a composite process. Studies whose object is the transformation of the mode of organization in processes that it itself guides (such as tendency-type grading) have working domains of the in-boundary self-feedback type and must declare that the host in question satisfies the conditions of that type.

8\. Boundaries, Breach Modes, H Group, and Unanswered Questions  
Boundaries.  
· All conclusions are relative to the declared instance χ and its level and resolution. P₁ at another level, P₂ read or acted on in another time window, P₃ placed in an environment with abrasion or deposition, P₄ with the sandblasting made to depend on the grooves, and χ\_e with E brought into the object domain are all other instances.  
· Every instance of Section 4 preserves the working domain and the non-degeneracy condition of Section 2 and is a genuine failure within the working domain.  
· Theorems 1 to 3 are conclusions about active concretization over W. The definition does not require every finite time window to contain a dynamic re-action; a host whose dynamic re-actions occur only outside W does not satisfy the premises of these theorems.  
· “Effective change” is taken in the Field-Chapter sense, with its threshold given by the declared resolution of L; action is an object-level fact. The process-by-process component serves as the exact criterion of action only when the hosting mechanism admits a legitimate decomposition; joint action that cannot be uniquely decomposed takes the joint process as the unit. Protocols that certify action and HistCond through observation, intervention, counterfactual comparison, and random coupling belong to the adjudication task. That an observation protocol cannot separate cancelling actions does not imply that the actions do not exist; that an observation protocol detects no change does not imply that the action is zero.  
· The structural basis of HistCond is the definition's symbol definitions and diagram: Dᵢ is drawn out of Tᵢ, and D′ᵢ acts again on the succeeding current Trace-Response set. Its physical bearing reading is given by this paper, and the requirement that it hold within W comes from the active-concretization stipulation.  
Breach modes. Within the working domain of Section 2, exhibit χ such that Fld(χ) holds while, for every T bearing the definition, one of q, r, Part, Act, HistCond fails; or prove that Part and HDyn need not take as object the same T that bears the Trace-Response set; or prove that the definition's symbol definitions and diagram do not require a dynamics issuing from the Trace-Response set to act again on this set; or, in an instance of active concretization over W, exhibit a dynamics drawn out of the Trace-Response set and acting again on it whose bearer is connected to the Trace-Responses of T by no actual material route. Exhibiting another physical realization of the same duty does not constitute a breach.  
H Group. Theorems 1 to 3 depend on no unproven antecedents; there are no load-bearing H entries.  
Unanswered questions (these do not support the conclusions of this Form).  
· How the three duties are adjudicated in a concrete host, and what the bounds of Misjudgment are.  
· The general identity condition for modes of organization.  
· Whether all Trace-Responses left by in-field continuation can be traced back to signals; the relation between initial conditions and endogenous pulses.  
· The criterion of action for joint action that cannot be uniquely decomposed, when the net change is zero.  
· The time-window conditions for a Sedimentation-Field to be constituted when active concretization is not the object (for example, when dynamic re-action occurs only outside the declared time window).  
· The relation of Familiar Routes, closure, Law, Principle, intention, the Observer, and other structures to the necessary structure of this Form.
