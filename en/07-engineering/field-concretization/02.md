# The Mathematical Form of Field Concretization

Section 1  
Purpose

### 1.1 What Problem This System Solves

*The General Form of Field Concretization* has proved that if a declared host instance actively concretizes as a Sedimentation-Field over its history window, then there exists a Trace-Response set T on which the Trace-Response-set duty, the organizational-participation duty and the historical-dynamics duty hold simultaneously. The three duties state mechanism facts: which mechanism process is conditioned on the organization of T; which process exerts an effective action on the organization of T; whether that action is determined in part, through an actual material route, by Trace-Responses T has already left; and whether the carrier of the process lies inside or outside the object domain.  
The most direct way to represent, run and solve the three duties on a concrete host model is to record the state trajectory. That is not enough. When two components +a and −a cancel, the trajectory is indistinguishable from rest, yet the General Form counts cancelling actions as actions. A write applied on a fixed schedule and a write whose strength is determined by the Trace-Responses can give pointwise identical trajectories, yet the former does not issue from the Trace-Responses. Qualification is not a function of the state trajectory.  
This system provides a formal language that preserves mechanism witnesses: every transition is recorded together with the mechanism events taking part in it and, for each event, its components, carrier attribution, action target and source path; the three duties are evaluated three-valuedly on these witnesses. The system has two layers. The relational layer R accommodates multi-valued, nonlinear and indecomposable joint mechanisms; the finite separable layer F gives numerical recursion and executable solution on a finite subdomain of separable mechanisms. F enters R through the witness-preserving embedding J. The formal correspondence from host mechanisms to the three duties is borne by this system's correspondence table, embedding and read-back rules.

### 1.2 The Structure Formalized

The structure formalized is the formal master formula of Section 5 of *The General Form of Field Concretization*:  
　　S\_Fld: Fld(χ) ⇒ ∃T \[ TraceSet(χ, T) ∧ Part(χ, T) ∧ HDyn(χ, T) \]  
together with the bearing relation, the organizational condition, action and the process-identification rule, the active-concretization stipulation and the Convergence Round lemma of Section 3 of that paper. This system establishes a formal system for it:  
　　S\_Fld ⟼ M\_Fld  
The Form kind is Mathematical Form: a principal form, an independent text, first edition. It is assigned per Canon Chapter 1, Part Three, Chapter Two. Entry conditions: the structure S\_Fld has been established by the proof principal form *The General Form of Field Concretization*, and its components already have stable terminology.

### 1.3 Boundary

M\_Fld takes declared evidence as the input of its evaluation and outputs a three-valued reading. The reading 1 reads back the three duties of the General Form when the read-back antecedents listed in Section 8 hold. The read-back antecedents themselves—the formation and retention of Trace-Responses, the cross-time identity of the Trace-Response set, the individuation and carrier attribution of mechanisms, the material reality of organizational conditions and source paths—are supplied by host-side certification and are not produced by running this system.  
The F layer covers only separable mechanisms with finitely many Structural Positions, a finite discrete history window and numerical laws of three classes: constant, linear and bilinear. The finite explicit part of the R layer can be enumerated completely; implicit, infinite or continuous relations lie outside the solvable domain. The General Form has as yet no conclusion on “the action criterion for an indecomposable joint action whose net change is zero”; this system keeps an undetermined value for it.

Section 2  
Core Thesis

### 2.1 Central Content

The central content this system must express precisely is this: the concretization qualification of a Sedimentation-Field is a function of mechanism witnesses, not a function of the state trajectory.  
Three requirements follow. First, the representation must carry mechanism events and their components, carrier attributions, action targets and source paths, and must not be compressed into net state transitions (Section 10, Theorem 1). Second, correspondences between layers must preserve these witnesses event by event; only then is qualification preserved (Theorem 2). Third, whatever cannot be read returns the third value; undetermined is not false, and refining the evidence can only turn an undetermined reading into a determined one, never flip a reading already determined (Theorem 4).

### 2.2 Structure Inherited from Upstream

*The General Form of Field Concretization* has established the following, which this system inherits as it stands:  
　　Declared host instance  χ = (P, L, B, W)  
　　Active-concretization stipulation  stipulates that every component is borne within W; Trace-Responses may form before W and be retained within W  
　　TraceSet(χ, T)  T is nonempty and its elements lie on Ξ\_B; every element satisfies q (an effective change sustained and retained by a Structural Position, persisting within W), and T satisfies r (the formative history of T contains Trace-Responses left by signals; the other elements may be caused by in-field continuation)  
　　Part(χ, T)  there exists a process π actually occurring within W, with mechanism carrier inside B, such that Cond(π, Org(T))  
　　HDyn(χ, T)  there exists a process ρ actually occurring within W such that Act(ρ, Org(T)) ∧ HistCond(ρ, T)  
　　Cond  the actual course of the process (including its occurrence conditions and the position, direction or strength of its action) is conditioned on the organizational state of the same T (including past states), and the conditioning may be transmitted via an actual material route  
　　Act  the process materially exerts on Org(T) an action effective at L; actions may cancel; when a legitimate process-by-process decomposition exists, the exact criterion is that the component be nonzero and effective  
　　HistCond  the process's action on Org(T) (its occurrence, timing, position, strength or direction) is determined in part, through an actual material route, by Trace-Responses T has already left  
　　Process-identification rule  the unit is the mechanism process; the joint process is the unit only when no legitimate process-by-process decomposition exists  
　　Convergence Round lemma  HistCond(ρ, T) ⇒ Cond(ρ, Org(T))  
A signal is an action that actually acts, from outside B, on the medium to which B belongs, whatever the channel.

The components:

### 2.4 Two Layers and the Embedding

The R layer answers “how to represent”: all legitimately individuated mechanism events and transitions, multi-valued and indecomposable ones included. The F layer answers “how to run and solve”: numerical recursion and qualification evaluation for finite separable mechanisms. J answers “how to couple”: every run of F enters R, with witnesses preserved event by event.

Section 3  
Basic Objects and the Definition Sequence

### 3.1 Three Sources of Objects

First, ontological objects from upstream. This system only invokes them and does not redefine them:  
　　χ, P, L, B, W, Ξ\_B, T, Org(T), Fld, TraceSet, Part, HDyn, Cond, Act, HistCond, signal, mechanism process, joint process  
Second, existing formal objects. They are inherited from *The Mathematical Form of the Field* and not reconstructed:  
　　Structural Position, Trace-Response, Sedimentation-Degree δ, the deepening term Dτ and the shallowing term Eλ of Equation 2  
Third, formal objects newly introduced in this paper, defined one by one below. An object marked as a purely formal auxiliary object is not a field quantity, is not an ontological predicate and does not enter the host's operating law; the complete list is in 8.6.

### 3.2 The Definition Sequence

The entries below are numbered. For every entry that involves a relation, its properties are declared item by item in Section 7.  
D1  Three-Valued Evidence Domain  
　　𝔻 = { 1, 0, ⊥ }  
1 means certified to hold, 0 means certified not to hold, ⊥ means undetermined. Conjunction and disjunction follow the strong Kleene rules: a conjunction is 0 if it contains a 0, otherwise ⊥ if it contains a ⊥, otherwise 1; disjunction is dual. ⊥ is not 0.  
D2  Declared Set and Time Window  
T is a finite set of Structural Positions, T ⊆ Ξ\_B. The history window of the F layer is the integer interval W = { t₀, t₀ + 1, …, t₁ }; the formative history before W is represented in F by the entry beat t₀ − 1.  
D3  Organizational State  
O\_t is the typed organizational state at time t; it may be a scalar, a vector or a general state. O\_t is not the same as T: T is a set of elements, while O\_t is the value at time t of the depths, distribution and relations of those elements.  
D4  Mechanism Event  
A mechanism event e is the actual run, within one beat, of a legitimately individuated mechanism process, individuated by the process-identification rule of the General Form. It carries an identifier id(e), a time t(e), a carrier attribution car(e) ∈ { B, out-of-boundary } and an action target tgt(e). When a legitimate process-by-process decomposition exists, decomposable events must not be merged into one composite event.  
D5  R Transition Relation and Legal Histories  
　　K\_t ⊆ O\_t × E\_t × O\_{t+1}  
E\_t is the set of mechanism events at time t. A legal history is h = ( o₀, e₀, o₁, …, e\_{n−1}, o\_n ) satisfying ( o\_k, e\_k, o\_{k+1} ) ∈ K\_k at every step. K\_t need not be a function and need not be defined everywhere; a pre-state may have several successors.  
D6  Event Witness  
　　ω = ( id, t, car, tgt, c, a, κ, η )  
c is the component (when separable); a, κ, η ∈ 𝔻 are, in order, the event's evidence values for Act, Cond and HistCond.  
D7  Witness Step and Witness History  
　　RStep = ( t, o\_before, o\_after, Ω\_t, Π\_t )  
　　RPath = ( X, Trace, Ident, ( RStep\_k )\_k, T )  
Ω\_t is the finite set of event witnesses of that beat, Π\_t is the finite set of pulse contributions of that beat, and X is the set of Structural Positions. Trace is the evidence value of the Trace-Response-set duty, and Ident is the evidence value of the cross-time identity of T (η\_T). RPath is the sole transition-and-qualification type of the R layer.  
D8  R-Layer Qualification Evaluation  
　　Part\_R = ⋁ { κ(ω) : ω in the steps within W, car(ω) = B }  
　　HDyn\_R = ⋁ { \[tgt(ω) ∈ T\] ∧ a(ω) ∧ η(ω) : ω in the steps within W }  
　　Q\_R = Trace ∧ Ident ∧ Part\_R ∧ HDyn\_R  
⋁ and ∧ follow the strong Kleene rules of D1; the empty disjunction is 0. \[·\] is the 0–1 indicator.  
D9  Indecomposable Joint Event  
　　j = ( id, car, o\_b, After(j), φ, κ, η, tgt )  
After(j) is a finite set of successors, and φ ∈ 𝔻 is the physical-action evidence. Let the actually realized transition be ( o\_b, o\_a ), o\_a ∈ After(j). Then  
　　Act(j, o\_a) = φ        if o\_a ≠ o\_b  
　　Act(j, o\_a) = 0        if o\_a = o\_b and φ = 0  
　　Act(j, o\_a) = ⊥        if o\_a = o\_b and φ ≠ 0  
The last line inherits the unanswered scope of the General Form: when an indecomposable joint process has zero net change, its action qualification is not settled by this system. Act is evaluated on the actually realized transition, not on all of the event's possible successors. Through adaptation a joint event is written as an event witness (the a of ω takes Act(j, o\_a), κ and η take the declared values, tgt takes tgt(j)) and evaluated by D8.  
D10  F-Layer State  
I is a finite set of Structural Positions, I ⊆ Ξ\_B. The state is s\_t : I → ℚ\_{≥0}; depth is not truncated at zero, and an update whose value is negative leaves the domain (D20). All numerical values in F are rationals computed exactly: a declared number is taken as the exact rational given by its decimal representation. ε ≥ 0 is the threshold in F for the declared resolution: a change whose absolute value does not exceed ε is not an effective change.  
D11  Pulse  
　　p = ( id, t, tgt, a\_p, ext, cert )  
a\_p ≥ 0 is the deepening amount; ext marks whether the pulse comes from outside the boundary (a signal) or from within the field. cert is the certification of whether the pulse actually occurred: 1 for occurred, 0 for not occurred, ⊥ for occurrence undetermined. F evaluates an already given actual event course and accepts only cert ∈ { 1, 0 }. A pulse with cert = ⊥ would change the post-state without its being known whether it occurred; it lies outside the single-trajectory domain of F and is represented in R by two realization branches, occurred and not occurred, with qualification evaluated on each branch separately (5.2). ext is an attribution already decided; undetermined occurrence must not be read as undetermined out-of-boundary attribution. In F a pulse represents deepening only; shallowing caused by out-of-boundary action is represented by a mechanism with an out-of-boundary carrier.  
D12  Separable Mechanism  
　　m = ( id, t, tgt, car, law, R(m), k, b, cert\_eff, cert\_read )  
The component is computed from the pre-state according to the numerical law:  
　　Constant law  c\_m = b  
　　Linear law  c\_m = b + k · Σ\_{j ∈ R(m)} s\_t(j)  
　　Bilinear law  c\_m = b + k · s\_t(j₁) · s\_t(j₂), R(m) = ( j₁, j₂ )  
R(m) gives the read positions. Every mechanism reads the pre-beat state s\_t. cert\_eff, cert\_read ∈ 𝔻 are the action certification and the read certification respectively; cert\_eff determines the numerical trajectory and must be decided in F (D20).  
D13  Evidence-Path Graph  
The nodes are state nodes (i, t) (at time t) and event nodes id (at time t + ½); an edge ( u, v ) carries a certification value cert(u, v) ∈ 𝔻 and requires time(u) \< time(v). The closure convention is Γ\_closed ∈ { closed, open }.  
D14  Path Status  
For a start set S and an event e:  
　　Path(S, e) = 1    if e is reachable from S through edges with certification value 1 only  
　　Path(S, e) = ⊥    if not, but e is reachable from S through edges with certification value other than 0  
　　Path(S, e) = 0    if neither of the above holds and Γ\_closed = closed  
　　Path(S, e) = ⊥    if neither of the above holds and Γ\_closed = open  
Reachability in the graph is only a formal witness of the source path; the reality of the material route is a read-back antecedent.  
D15  Functional Support  
　　Supp\_act(m, s\_t): the read positions with respect to which the numerical law has a nonzero partial derivative  
　　　　empty for the constant law; for the linear law, R(m) when k ≠ 0 and empty otherwise; for the bilinear law, when k ≠ 0, { j₁ : s\_t(j₂) ≠ 0 } ∪ { j₂ : s\_t(j₁) ≠ 0 }  
　　Supp\_cond(m, s\_t) = Supp\_act(m, s\_t) ∪ ( R(m), if m follows the linear law and \|c\_m\| ≤ ε )  
“Nonzero” is meant in the mathematical sense: whether k and the partner reading are exactly zero has nothing to do with the declared resolution ε. ε only judges whether a change is effective (D10), not whether a functional dependence exists: however small the coefficient, as long as it is not zero, the strength is still determined in part by the read positions. The second term admits pure-read processes: processes whose component is not an effective change (\|c\_m\| ≤ ε) but which run conditioned on their read positions (such as scan readout). A process whose component is an effective change, positive or negative, is not a pure read; whether a pure read can bear participation further requires read certification (D18) and the read-back antecedents.  
D16  Formation Record and Out-of-Boundary Cause  
　　Effective out-of-boundary change: an out-of-boundary action on Structural Position i whose change has absolute value greater than ε. Out-of-boundary actions are pulses with ext = out-of-boundary (change a\_p) and mechanisms whose carrier is outside the boundary (change c\_m).  
　　Form(i): the record of the events that make Structural Position i a trace-retaining element. Certified effective pulses are entered; a certified effective mechanism component is entered if s\_t(i) ≤ ε \< s\_t(i) + c\_m, as formation by in-field continuation if its carrier is inside B and as formation by signal if its carrier is outside the boundary.  
　　Mark(i): the certified effective out-of-boundary changes on Structural Position i, whether the element is new or old and whether the change is positive or negative.  
Form and Mark have different duties. Form bears “the element has formed” in q and “formation precedes reading” in D17, and records only the production of new elements. Mark bears “Trace-Responses left by signals” in r, and records every effective change left by out-of-boundary action, including the deepening and shallowing of existing elements. One event may enter both: an out-of-boundary pulse that produces a new element enters both Form and Mark.  
D17  Available Positions  
　　Avail\_x(m, t) = { i ∈ Supp\_x(m, s\_t) ∩ T : s\_t(i) \> ε, and Form(i) has a record earlier than t }, x ∈ { act, cond }  
D18  F-Layer Event Atoms  
　　Act\_F(m) = \[ \|c\_m\| \> ε \] ∧ cert\_eff  
　　Cond\_F(m) = \[ Avail\_cond(m, t) ≠ ∅ \] ∧ cert\_read  
　　HistCond\_F(m) = \[ Avail\_act(m, t) ≠ ∅ \] ∧ cert\_read ∧ Path( { (i, t) : i ∈ Avail\_act(m, t) }, m )  
HistCond\_F does not contain Act\_F; HDyn is the conjunction of the two (D19). A path label cannot substitute for functional support: when Supp\_act is empty, HistCond\_F = 0, however the links are declared.  
In F, the time, target and read positions of a mechanism are given by declaration, and the numerical law expresses only how the strength of the action depends on the read positions. Hence HistCond\_F encodes only one case, “strength determined in part by the Trace-Responses”, and Cond\_F encodes only two, strength dependence and pure read. Cases in which the Trace-Responses take part in determining the occurrence, timing or position of the action are not encoded in F; they must be represented in the R layer through the κ and η of event witnesses.  
D19  F-Layer Qualification  
　　Src = \[ ∃ i ∈ T, Mark(i) ≠ ∅ \], evaluated on the decided event course  
　　Ret = \[ ∀ i ∈ T, s\_{t₁+1}(i) \> ε and Form(i) ≠ ∅ \]  
　　Trace\_F = Src ∧ Ret ∧ Ident ∧ HistEv  
　　Part\_F = ⋁ { Cond\_F(m) : t(m) ∈ W, car(m) = B }  
　　HDyn\_F = ⋁ { \[tgt(m) ∈ T\] ∧ Act\_F(m) ∧ HistCond\_F(m) : t(m) ∈ W }  
　　Q\_F = Trace\_F ∧ Part\_F ∧ HDyn\_F  
Src is determined only by “effective out-of-boundary changes on T”: out-of-boundary actions on positions outside T, and out-of-boundary actions whose change does not exceed ε, do not enter Src. In F the occurrence of pulses is decided (D11, D20), so Src takes only 0 and 1; cases of undetermined occurrence belong to the branches of R, and Src is evaluated on each branch separately.  
Ident is the evidence for the cross-time identity of T, and HistEv is the evidence for the formative-history record itself; both are declared three-valued atoms.  
D20  F-Layer Domain  
A run of F requires: Structural Positions and event identifiers are pairwise distinct; event times lie in \[t₀ − 1, t₁\]; numerical laws, read positions and parameters are legal and finite; the edges of the evidence-path graph point forward in time; the exact update value at every beat is not less than zero; cert\_eff ≠ ⊥; every pulse has occurrence certification cert ∈ { 1, 0 }. There is no tolerance: an update value below zero leaves the domain. Mechanisms with undetermined action and pulses with undetermined occurrence cannot determine the numerical trajectory and belong to the R layer; F refuses to run on them rather than returning Q = 0 or Q = ⊥. Numerical inputs may be integers, rationals or decimals; decimals are taken as the exact rational given by their decimal representation.  
D21  Witness-Preserving Embedding  
　　J: a run of F ⟼ RPath  
For each beat in W, J takes the pre-state and post-state of that beat, the witness ω = ( id, t, car, tgt, c\_m, Act\_F, Cond\_F, HistCond\_F ) of each mechanism and the contribution of each certified effective pulse; Trace takes Trace\_F, Ident takes Ident, and T is unchanged.  
D22  State-Trajectory Projection  
　　π(h) = ( o₀, o₁, …, o\_n ); in F, ( s\_{t₀}, …, s\_{t₁+1} )  
π discards events, components, carriers, targets and paths, and keeps only the state sequence.

Section 4  
Equations and Criteria

### 4.1 Relational Evolution in the R Layer

　　o\_{t+1} ∈ K\_t( o\_t, e\_t )  
The set of legal histories over W is denoted ℋ\_W. Qualification is evaluated on every complete history; “it holds on some history” does not replace “it holds on every history”.

### 4.2 Numerical Recursion in the F Layer

　　s\_{t+1}(i) = s\_t(i) + Σ\_{p : tgt(p) = i, cert(p) = 1, a\_p \> ε} a\_p + Σ\_{m : tgt(m) = i, Act\_F(m) = 1} c\_m  
Components are always computed from the pre-state s\_t. Components of different mechanisms on the same Structural Position may cancel. The update is not truncated at zero: an input whose right-hand side is negative leaves the domain (D20), while one whose right-hand side is zero is legal.

### 4.3 Criterion Equations

R layer: D8. F layer: D18, D19. The qualification formulas of the two have the same shape:  
　　Q = Trace ∧ Part ∧ HDyn; Part counts only carriers inside B, HDyn counts only targets in T, and HistCond does not contain Act

### 4.4 The Shape of Three-Valued Evaluation

The readings of all three duties are strong Kleene conjunction–disjunction compounds of finitely many atoms. The atoms are of two kinds. One kind is fixed by the numerical laws and the formation records (\[\|c\_m\| \> ε\], Supp, Form, Mark, Ret) and takes only 0 and 1; the other kind is declared evidence (cert\_read, path-edge certifications, Ident, HistEv) and may take ⊥. The action certification cert\_eff and the pulse-occurrence certification cert determine the numerical trajectory and must be decided in F (D20).

Section 5  
Running and Solving

### 5.1 The F-Layer Solver

Given the F part of 𝕀\_Fld, execute beat by beat from t₀ − 1 to t₁:  
　　Step 1  Verify D20 and reject if it fails; reject any input containing a pulse of undetermined occurrence or a mechanism of undetermined action, and pass it to the R layer  
　　Step 2  Register the pulses of the beat that are certified as occurred and effective, together with their formation records and out-of-boundary causes; pulses certified as not occurred are not counted  
　　Step 3  For each mechanism of the beat, compute c\_m from the pre-state and evaluate Act\_F, Cond\_F, HistCond\_F  
　　Step 4  Add the components of the mechanisms with Act\_F = 1, and register the formation of new elements and the out-of-boundary causes  
　　Step 5  Update the state by 4.2  
　　Step 6  For t ∈ W, record the pre-state, post-state, components and event atoms  
　　Step 7  At the end of the window, evaluate Trace\_F, Part\_F, HDyn\_F, Q\_F by D19  
The evidence-path check at each beat is reachability on a finite graph, so the solver terminates in finitely many steps.

### 5.2 R-Layer Enumeration

The input is an initial state and, for each beat, an explicit finite set of joint events. Beat by beat, every legal prefix is extended by all of its legal successors, yielding ℋ\_W; on each complete history, the Act of each event is evaluated by D9 on the actually realized transition, and qualification is evaluated through adaptation by D8. An enumeration bound is set; when the bound is reached, the enumeration reports truncation and does not sign off on “fully enumerated”.  
A pulse of undetermined occurrence is represented in R as two realization branches: one in which it occurs and one in which it does not. If all other events are separable and decided, each branch is a legal run of F, and its image under J is the witness history of that branch; Trace, Part, HDyn and Q are evaluated on each branch separately. The branches of R are only formally possible histories; they do not certify which branch is the material history that actually occurred.

### 5.3 Three-Valued Propagation

0 and ⊥ must not be flattened into one another: missing evidence returns ⊥; no path on a closed graph returns 0, and no path on an open graph returns ⊥; a net-zero transition of an indecomposable joint event returns ⊥ when its action evidence is not 0.  
An undetermined evidence atom and an undetermined event occurrence are two different problems. The former does not change the numerical trajectory and propagates by the strong Kleene rules. The latter changes the numerical trajectory and is not handled within a single-trajectory run of F; instead R enumerates the realization branches, each with a determinate trajectory and its own qualification evaluation. The states of different branches must not be spliced into one numerical state with ⊥ masking the difference between the branches.

### 5.4 Solvability

F layer: within the domain of D20, every run has a unique numerical trajectory and a unique qualification reading (Section 10, Proposition 1). R layer: the finite explicit part can be solved completely, at a cost that may grow exponentially with the window length (Theorem 3); no general solution procedure is provided for implicit, infinite or continuous transition relations.

Section 6  
Coupling Relations  
6.1 With *The Mathematical Form of the Field*  
Correspondence: Structural Position ↔ element of I; Sedimentation-Degree δ ↔ s; Trace-Response ↔ the effective changes recorded by Form and Mark.  
Equation 2 of *The Mathematical Form of the Field* is δ\_{t+1}(x) = δ\_t(x) + Dτ\_t(x) − Eλ\_t(x). At the same level and on the same timescale, and when every component has a legitimate mechanism attribution, the components of F are assigned to Equation 2 by mechanism duty, not by sign:  
　　Dτ ↔ deepening components caused by pulses, by Trace-Responses and by continuation  
　　Eλ ↔ components of field-intrinsic shallowing mechanisms  
　　R\_ext, R\_other ↔ components with no counterpart term in Equation 2, such as shallowing caused by out-of-boundary action  
The bookkeeping of F can thus be written as  
　　s\_{t+1} = s\_t + Dτ\_t − Eλ\_t + R\_ext(t) + R\_other(t)  
This is correspondence bookkeeping on the F side; it does not alter Equation 2 of *The Mathematical Form of the Field*. A component whose mechanism attribution cannot be established is recorded as undetermined and is not forced into a term. Indecomposable joint events of the R layer are not split into components of Equation 2.  
6.2 With Canonical F\* of *The Construction Form of the Minimal Sedimentation-Field*  
A single-step update of F\* can be declared as a finite explicit transition of the R layer, and its Sediment write and shallowing components can serve as event witnesses. The choice of successor by continuation tendency in F\* does not belong to the three classes of numerical laws of F, so F\* is not evaluated directly in F.  
6.3 With *The Adjudication Form of the Sedimentation-Field*  
The operational predicates of the Adjudication Form are candidate sources of host-side evidence, corresponding as follows:  
　　Tr^rest, Tr^cut (retention difference)  ↔  Ret and the retention evidence of q  
　　Hist (historical retention difference)  ↔  the formative-history evidence for Form, Mark, HistEv  
　　Act\_𝓟 (in-protocol active-participation witness)  ↔  the participation evidence for cert\_read in Part  
　　Dep (historical change in dependency effect)  ↔  candidate evidence for functional support and read certification in HistCond  
These correspondences only say where the evidence goes. That form's own bridging decides when its predicates suffice to certify the atoms above; until the bridging holds, values taken from the Adjudication Form are not promoted to certified values of this system's atoms. By Theorem 1, a criterion whose only input is the actual historical state trajectory cannot decide Act and HistCond; the interventions, counterfactual comparisons and protocol witnesses of the Adjudication Form are precisely sources of information beyond the state trajectory.  
6.4 With *The General Form of Field Concretization*  
The item-by-item correspondence is given in Section 9. M\_Fld adds no ontological condition: every conjunct of D8, D18 and D19 refers back to a bearing condition of the General Form.

Section 7  
Relation Properties

### 7.1 The R Transition Relation K\_t (D5)

K\_t is a ternary heterogeneous relation on O\_t × E\_t × O\_{t+1}. Reflexivity, symmetry, antisymmetry and transitivity are undefined for a ternary heterogeneous relation and do not apply. For the binary state relation obtained by fixing an event and projecting, none of the four properties holds in general.  
7.2 Cond, Act, HistCond (as Relations between Processes and Trace-Response Sets)  
All three are heterogeneous binary relations between the set of mechanism processes and Trace-Response sets (or their organizational states). The four same-domain properties do not apply. Neither symmetry nor transitivity may be assumed.

### 7.3 Temporal Precedence \< (D13)

Strict precedence on node times: irreflexive, asymmetric, antisymmetric (vacuously), transitive.  
7.4 Certified Reachability ⇝⁺ (D14, Length at Least One)  
Reachability of length at least one through edges with certification value 1 only: by 7.3 the edges point forward in time, so it is irreflexive, asymmetric, antisymmetric (vacuously) and transitive (by path concatenation). Reachability through edges with certification value other than 0 has the same four properties, but it only yields ⊥ and is not certified reachability.

### 7.5 Zero-Length Reachability ⇝\*

Reachability that allows length zero: reflexive, antisymmetric, transitive, not symmetric. The evaluation of HistCond uses only ⇝⁺ and does not take zero-length reachability as source evidence.  
7.6 Cross-Time Identity Evidence Ident (η\_T)  
Ident is a three-valued atom about the declared T, not a binary relation between sets; the four properties do not apply. This system does not use it as an equivalence relation and does not infer cross-time identity from numerical equality.

### 7.7 Information Order ⪯ (D1)

⊥ ⪯ 0, ⊥ ⪯ 1, and every value ⪯ itself: reflexive, antisymmetric, transitive; not a total order (0 and 1 are incomparable).

### 7.8 Truth Order ≤ (D1)

0 ≤ ⊥ ≤ 1: reflexive, antisymmetric, transitive; a total order. The truth order and the information order have different semantics and must not be substituted for each other.

### 7.9 The Embedding J (D21)

J is a map from F runs to RPath, not a same-domain binary relation; the four properties do not apply. J is not surjective (Theorem 2(3)); its invertibility is unproved.

Section 10  
Formal Realization Theorems  
The proofs of the results in this section are all elementary: constructing counterexamples, verifying by construction, induction on the number of steps, induction on formula structure. Their role is to fix the representation requirements, the inter-layer coupling and the solvable range of this formal system; they are not theorems of high mathematical difficulty.  
Proposition 1 (Uniqueness in the F Layer)  
Within the domain of D20, every run of F has a unique numerical trajectory and a unique qualification reading.  
Proof. The components of each beat are single-valued functions of the pre-state, the pulse contributions are given by the input, and the event atoms are determined by the pre-state, the input and reachability on a finite graph; by induction on the number of beats, the trajectory and the reading are unique. ∎  
This proposition is only the well-definedness of the numerical recursion, not a claim that the physical mechanism is unique.  
Theorem 1 (Incompressibility of Mechanism Witnesses)  
There exist legal witness histories h, h′ with π(h) = π(h′) but Act\_W(h) ≠ Act\_W(h′); there also exist legal witness histories g, g′ with π(g) = π(g′) but HistCond\_W(g) ≠ HistCond\_W(g′); and within each pair the Q values also differ. Hence there is no function with π as its sole input that gives Act\_W, HistCond\_W or Q correctly on all legal histories.  
Proof. Act: take the same declared instance and T, with initial state s = 1. h has no mechanism event within W; h′ has, at one beat, two legitimately individuated mechanism events with components +a and −a, a \> ε, the former conditioned on reading T and issuing from the Trace-Responses. Then π(h) = π(h′) = (1, 1); the Act and Part of h are 0, while the Act, Part and HDyn of h′ are 1.  
HistCond: take the same declared instance and T, formed and retained before W, with s = 1, and with a separate pure-read process within W bearing Part. In g the only write mechanism follows the constant law c = 0.2; in g′ it follows the linear law c = 0.2 · s, with a certified evidence path. Both give +0.2 at s = 1, and π(g) = π(g′) = (1, 1.2). In g, Supp\_act is empty, HistCond = 0 and Q = 0; in g′, HistCond = 1 and Q = 1.  
If some function f with π as its sole input were correct everywhere, f would have to take two different values on the same input, a contradiction. ∎  
Both examples lie within the range the General Form permits: cancelling actions are actions, and whether an action issues from the Trace-Responses does not depend on its value at a particular state. The theorem states a requirement on formal representation: the representation must carry mechanism witnesses. It is not a conclusion about observational distinguishability; the latter involves interventions and protocols and belongs to the adjudication task.  
Theorem 2 (Witness-Preserving Embedding and Preservation of Qualification)  
For every F run F within the domain of D20:  
(1) Trace, Part, HDyn and Q are equal item by item on F and on J(F);  
(2) at every step of J(F), the post-state of every Structural Position equals its pre-state plus the sum of the components of the events with action certification 1 at that step and the certified pulse contributions;  
(3) J is not surjective: there exists an RPath that is not the image of any F run.  
Proof. (1) By D21, each event witness of J(F) takes its fields, one by one, from the same event of F: carrier, target, component, Act\_F, Cond\_F, HistCond\_F; Trace and T are unchanged. The qualification formulas of D8 and D19 take the same disjunctions and conjunctions over the same witness sets, and the strong Kleene operations give equal values on equal inputs; the Ident in Q\_R is already contained in Trace\_F, and strong Kleene conjunction is idempotent, so the reading does not change. (2) This holds beat by beat by 4.2: 4.2 involves no truncation at zero, so the post-state is the pre-state plus the certified effective components and pulse contributions of that beat; inputs whose right-hand side is negative are not in the domain. Since F computes exactly over the rationals, the equality holds exactly position by position, and the program verifies it as an equality. (3) Within the domain of F, action evidence does not take ⊥ (D20), so every event witness in the image of J has a ∈ { 0, 1 }; an RPath containing a net-zero indecomposable joint event whose action evidence φ is not 0 has a = ⊥ (D9) and is not in the image. ∎  
By Theorem 1, if J compressed the witnesses of a step into a net state transition, (1) could not hold in general.  
Theorem 3 (Full-History Solution of Finite Explicit R)  
Let W have n beats; suppose that at every beat all finite successors of every legal pre-state can be effectively listed, that state equality is decidable, and that the enumeration does not reach its bound. Then the set of histories output by the enumeration of 5.2 is exactly ℋ\_W. If at every beat each pre-state has at most b legal successors, then \|ℋ\_W\| ≤ bⁿ; outputting all histories explicitly takes time and space no less than the total length of the output.  
Proof. Induction on the number of beats. With zero beats there is only the initial history. Suppose the enumeration lists exactly all legal histories of length k; it extends each history by all and only its legal successors, so it obtains exactly all legal histories of length k + 1. Each beat multiplies the count by at most b, which gives bⁿ. ∎  
The theorem gives no polynomial full enumeration for multi-valued relations, and it does not cover implicit, infinite or continuous transition relations.  
Theorem 4 (Monotonicity under Refinement of Three-Valued Evidence)  
Fix the structure of F (the Structural Positions, T, W, the pulses and their certifications, the mechanisms with their numerical laws and action certifications, and the edge set and closure convention of the evidence-path graph). Let two evidence assignments satisfy ν ⪯ ν′, that is, every atom among the read certifications, path-edge certifications, Ident and HistEv is either ⊥ under ν or has the same value under ν as under ν′. Then the readings of Trace, Part, HDyn and Q satisfy Eval\_ν ⪯ Eval\_ν′; the same holds in the R layer for the a, κ, η of event witnesses and for Trace and Ident.  
Proof. The numerical trajectory depends only on the structure, not on the atoms listed above (D12, 4.2), so Supp, Form, Mark, Ret and \[\|c\_m\| \> ε\] are the same under ν and ν′. The path status of D14 is monotone in the edge certifications: a status of 1 under ν remains 1 under ν′, and where the status is 0 under ν (no nonzero path on a closed graph), there is still no nonzero path under ν′. The qualification formulas are strong Kleene conjunction–disjunction compounds of atoms, and strong Kleene conjunction and disjunction are monotone in the information order; induction on formula structure gives the result. ∎  
Changing a pulse certification or an action certification changes the numerical trajectory and is not evidence refinement; neither is changing T, changing a mechanism law, or switching to a different history.  
Theorem 5 (Conditional Read-Back)  
Under the read-back antecedents of 8.2, for the declared T: Q = 1 if and only if T satisfies TraceSet, Part and HDyn on χ.  
Proof. By item-by-item correspondence. Src and Mark correspond to r: Mark records the changes that out-of-boundary actions leave on the Structural Positions of T, including those from out-of-boundary pulses and the components of mechanisms with out-of-boundary carriers (antecedents (1), (3)). The conjuncts correspond one to one, and the W-level existential quantifiers range over the same witness sets on both sides. ∎

~~~python
def exact(v):

    return Fraction(repr(v)) if isinstance(v, float) else Fraction(v)
def tri_and(*xs):

    if None in xs:return None
    return True

def _num(v, nonneg=False):

    if isinstance(v, bool) or not isinstance(v, (int, float, Fraction)): return False
    if isinstance(v, float) and not isfinite(v): return False

def check(x):

        names.add(e.name)
        if e.step<x.window[0]-1 or e.step>x.window[-1]:raise ValueError('event time')

            # Declared reads do not establish a genuine dependence: compute the law's support.

            # Support for the organizational condition: the numerical law depends on the read positions, or the process is a pure read (component not an effective change, |c| ≤ ε); read certification enters as a separate conjunct, and an undetermined value is not truncated to false in advance
            # Functional support is decided by a mathematically nonzero partial derivative (coefficient and partner reading exactly nonzero), not conflated with the effective-change threshold ε
            if m.law=='linear' and (k!=0 or abs(value)<=eps): support=m.reads
            elif m.law=='bilinear' and k!=0:
                support=tuple(s for i,s in enumerate(m.reads) if before[m.reads[1-i]]!=0)
            # For every element in the declared T, historical formation must precede the read.
            active_T=set(x.declared_T)

            # Action provenance must be supported by a nonzero functional dependence,
            # not merely a certified read that has no effect on the action.
            action_support=()
            if m.law=='linear' and k!=0:action_support=m.reads
            elif m.law=='bilinear' and k!=0:
                action_support=tuple(s for i,s in enumerate(m.reads) if before[m.reads[1-i]]!=0)
            action_available=[s for s in action_support if s in active_T and before[s]>eps and any(a[1]<t for a in formation[s])]
            action_nodes=[f'state:{s}:{t}' for s in action_available]
            action_route=path_status(x.links,action_nodes,m.name,x.closed_graph) if action_nodes else False
            is_hist=tri_and(bool(action_available),m.certified_read,action_route)
            if is_action is True:
                contributions[m.target].append((m.name,value))
                # Formation of a new element: with its carrier inside B it is formation by in-field continuation; with its carrier outside the boundary it is formation by signal
                if value>eps and before[m.target]<=eps:
                    formation[m.target].append((m.name,t,not m.carrier_inside))
                # An effective component of a process whose carrier is outside the boundary is a change left by a signal, whether the element is new or old and whether the component is positive or negative
                if not m.carrier_inside:
                    signal_marks[m.target].append((m.name,t))
            elif is_action is None: # defensive check: an undetermined action certification is already excluded from the domain by check()
                raise ValueError('unresolved effect cannot determine numeric trajectory')
            if t in x.window:
                if m.carrier_inside:part.append(is_cond)
                hdyn.append(tri_and(m.target in active_T,is_action,is_hist))
            events.append({'id':m.name,'delta':value,'act':is_action,'cond':is_cond,'histcond':is_hist,'route':route})

            state[s]=new
            if state[s]>eps and formation[s]:history_snapshots[s].append(t+1)
        if t in x.window:reports.append({'step':t,'before':before,'after':dict(state),'contributions':dict(contributions),'events':events})

    eligible=all(state[s]>eps and formation[s] for s in ts)
    sources={origin for site in ts for origin,t in signal_marks[site]}
    trace=tri_and(bool(sources),eligible,x.identity,x.trace_history)
    pw=tri_or(part); hw=tri_or(hdyn)
~~~

A.3  relational\_extension.py  R-layer indecomposable joint events, finite explicit enumeration and adaptation (D9, Section 5, 5.2)

~~~python
"""The Mathematical Form of Field Concretization v1.0, Appendix A: finite explicit relational solving and adaptation for R-layer indecomposable joint events.
Does not infer physical causation from the declared transition relation.
"""
from dataclasses import dataclass
from embedding_verified import EventWitness, RStep, RPath, evaluate_rpath
@dataclass(frozen=True)
class JointEvent:
    name: str
    carrier_inside: bool
    before: tuple
    after_options: tuple[tuple,...]
    physical_effect: bool|None
    organized_read: bool|None
    trace_causal_path: bool|None
    target: str  # action target (required)
    # net change 0 need independent effect witness; no invented decomposition
    def act(self,after=None):
        # Neither state inequality nor an uncertified flag proves a physical Act.
        # Nonseparable net-zero remains unresolved in the sealed upstream text.
        if after is None: return None # a branched event alone is not a realized transition
        if after not in self.after_options: raise ValueError("unrealized transition")
        if self.before == after:
            return False if self.physical_effect is False else None
        return self.physical_effect
    def histcond(self):
        return self.trace_causal_path
    def cond(self):
        return self.organized_read

def t_and(*args):
    if False in args:return False
    if None in args:return None
    return True

def solve_relational(initial, stages, max_paths=100000):
    """Finite set-valued successor relation: all paths, not an arbitrarily chosen unique path."""
    paths=[(initial,)]
    for stage in stages:
        nxt=[]
        for p in paths:
            for ev in stage:
                if p[-1]!=ev.before: continue
                for end in ev.after_options:
                    nxt.append(p+(end,))
                    if len(nxt)>max_paths:raise OverflowError('path cap, no completeness claim')
        paths=nxt
    return tuple(paths)

def window_quality(trace_status, events, identity_status, declared_T):
    """Requires explicit true/false/unknown declarations; these are not material certification."""
    def t_or(xs):
        xs=list(xs)
        if True in xs:return True
        if None in xs:return None
        return False
    events=tuple((e,e.after_options[0] if len(e.after_options)==1 else None) if isinstance(e,JointEvent) else e for e in events)
    # JointEvent is an adapter to the shared witnessed R transition semantics.
    # The event is indivisible; a net-zero joint effect remains unknown.
    witness_rows=tuple(EventWitness(e.name,i,e.carrier_inside,e.target,0.0 if after==e.before else 1.0,e.act(after),e.cond(),e.histcond(),'joint') for i,(e,after) in enumerate(events))
    canonical=RPath(tuple(declared_T),trace_status,identity_status,(RStep(0,(0.,),(0.,),witness_rows,()),),tuple(declared_T))
    return evaluate_rpath(canonical)

def qualify_paths(initial,stages,trace_status,identity_status,declared_T):
    # Models may branch; qualify each complete history, do not existentially select a good history
    paths=[((initial,),())]
    for stage in stages:
        nxt=[]
        for states,events in paths:
            for event in stage:
                if event.before!=states[-1]:continue
                for after in event.after_options:
                    nxt.append((states+(after,),events+((event,after),)))
        paths=nxt
    return tuple((states,window_quality(trace_status,events,identity_status,declared_T)) for states,events in paths)
~~~
