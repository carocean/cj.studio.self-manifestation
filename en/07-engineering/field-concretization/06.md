# The Construction Form of Field Concretization

1\. Declaration of Form Kind  
This paper is a Construction Form (Group Three, an auxiliary form of establishment proof). Its sole task: in two families, flow-network sedimentation and biological neural networks, to give for each an explicit construction of a declared physical mechanism model and of its formal encoding; to verify on the mechanism model, item by item, the three duties of Field Concretization; to verify, item by item, the correspondence obligations between the mechanism model and the formal encoding; to give the construction domain and decompose the failures; to set the two families side by side under the same condition group and the same verification table; and then to give the item-by-item correspondence between artificial neural networks and the biological construction.  
The conclusions of this paper fall into five groups.  
　1\. Condition group: declaration of the physical mechanism model (dimensions, balance laws and budgets, locality, energy account, parameters and domain of validity), the three duties and the five bearing contents, eight classes of correspondence obligations between the mechanism model and the formal encoding, and the side-by-side comparison of the two families (Section 3).  
　2\. Construction: the compilation rule J compiles the declared mechanism model into an alphabet-gated F model; Family A is a constant-head two-channel flow network, and Family E is a firing-rate network in which one presynaptic neuron drives two postsynaptic neurons through two synapses (Section 4).  
　3\. Construction theorems: the Projection-Omission Lemma (Lemma 1); the construction theorem for the two families (Theorem 1); the unit closed-form construction domain (Proposition 1); the exact condition for state fidelity and the sufficient condition for the certificate (Proposition 2); the drive-free retention domain (Proposition 3); the sharing between positive and negative components (Proposition 4); the failure verdicts, listed separately (Section 6).  
　4\. Side-by-side comparison: the two families compile to the same parameterized encoding and their duty readings are identical, while their physical declarations, budgets, energy accounts and routes differ item by item; identical formal encodings do not mean that the physical construction is usable (Proposition 5); the correspondence of artificial neural networks under two modes of operation, online learning and frozen inference (Proposition 6) (Section 7).  
　5\. Strength, breach modes, open items and interfaces (Sections 8–12).  
Strength position:  
　The strength attained is construction strength: within the declared mechanism model, an object satisfying the condition group is explicitly built. Reachability (arrival from a given initial state by permitted operations) and realization (completion in real materials) are not within this strength, and neither is material qualification.  
　Three objects are discussed: the declared physical mechanism model χ; the formal encoding M that carries its Structural Positions; and the real host. For the real host only a conditional interface is given: separate evidence is still required that the real system preserves the eight classes of correspondence facts. “The real system obeys the same equations of state” does not suffice to fulfil this interface.  
　“Different hosts share the same bearing principle” is unproved as a general conclusion; what is proved are item-by-item conclusions between two declared constructions.  
Theoretical increment: upstream there already exist the abstract model χ₀, Canonical F*, the mechanism-witness template of 6.3 and the two-tube model of *The Grading Form of Tendency Types*, and the minimal representative model of the artificial network in 7.5 (3) of *The Adjudication Form of the Sedimentation-Field\*; none of these carries a physical declaration with dimensions and budgets, and none verifies item by item the correspondence obligations between mechanism and encoding. The increment of this paper is:  
　physical mechanism constructions of the two families, with dimensions and with budgets and energy accounts verified beat by beat, among which the physical-mechanism-layer construction of a biological neural network is given in the Group Form for the first time;  
　certification values derived from the declared route chains, not taken as 1 by declaration;  
　the exact condition for state fidelity, and the constraint that the resolution semantics of the F layer of the Mathematical Form places on physical constructions (Proposition 2);  
　qualification preservation for projection encodings and omitted processes (Lemma 1);  
　the structural-class isomorphism of the Structural-Position encodings of the two families, and the four-layer separation of the formal and physical layers (Proposition 5).  
The proofs are elementary (item-by-item substitution, induction, strong Kleene conjunction).

### 2.2  Model and Evaluation

The encoding layer is 𝔐\_GF with RD; the evaluator is reach\_core.py (Appendix A.8, verbatim identical to Appendix A.1 of the Reachability Form).  
　Effective change: pulse amplitude a \> ε, or process component \|c\| \> ε; only effective changes are counted into the state.  
　Formation registration: an effective pulse registers formation; an effective positive component acting on a position with pre-beat s ≤ ε also registers formation. Mark registration: out-of-boundary pulses and effective processes with out-of-boundary carriers register marks.  
　Qualification: Q(T) = TraceSet(T) ∧ Part(T) ∧ HDyn(T), by strong Kleene conjunction; the evaluator additionally reports the in-boundary self-feedback reading.  
　The evaluator declares Ident and HistEv to be 1; this paper takes the structural declaration check of C-C (7) in Section 3 as the in-model basis for this.  
The mechanism layer is χ\_A and χ\_E, declared in Section 4: states carry dimensions, and their values are rational numbers in the declared system of units; each beat is computed from the declared constitutive antecedents, and the budgets (for Family A, carrier fluid and structural material; for Family E, receptors) and the energy account are verified beat by beat.

### 2.3  Entry Conditions

　(1) Closure of the condition group: every clause of C-A, C-B, C-C and C-D in Section 3 has a source.  
　(2) Compatibility: whether the physical mechanism declaration can coexist with the three duties and the correspondence obligations has not been proved upstream. This paper closes compatibility together with the construction: one and the same construct obtains all clauses of the condition group at once, which is a constructive compatibility witness.  
　(3) The question “what does a satisfier look like” must be answered: whether the bearing principles of hosts such as neural networks, flow networks, river channels and slime moulds can all be brought down onto one and the same set of duties and construction conditions requires explicit instances with physical declarations.

3\. Declaration of the Condition Group  
C-A  Declaration of the physical mechanism model (filled in separately by each family; verifiable clauses are checked beat by beat by the programs)  
　P1  Dimensions and state: Structural Positions carry dimensions; the relation between the structural quantity and the conductance (or synaptic conductance) is declared as a constitutive antecedent.  
　P2  Balance laws and budgets: for each conserved quantity, list beat by beat the inflow, the outflow, the conversion, the entry position of external injection, and the sources and destinations.  
　P3  Locality: coupling runs only through declared physical links; when quasi-static elimination is declared, its conditions are stated.  
　P4  Energy account: supply power = rate of change of stored energy + dissipation, with dissipation ≥ 0; “no increase of structural quantity without supply” is not required, nor is it attributed to the second law of thermodynamics.  
　P5  Parameters: dimensionally consistent, with values in physically interpretable ranges; each constitutive antecedent declares its domain of validity, which is checked beat by beat.  
　Constitutive antecedents are declared independently, and phenomenological laws are declared with their status as assumptions; the construction is derived from the complete set of antecedents and is not claimed to follow uniquely from P1–P5.  
　Criterion for new clauses: each component of the update equation can be traced to one declared balance law and one declared constitutive antecedent, and corresponds to one physical quantity with dimensions; the new clauses are listed by item-by-item comparison with Canonical F\* and the template of 6.3 of *The Grading Form of Tendency Types* (7.5).  
C-B  The three duties: on the declared instance, the Trace-Response set T, the window W and the resolution ε, TraceSet (Src, Ret), Part and HDyn (Act ∧ HistCond) hold item by item; positive and negative effective components are checked alike; the in-boundary self-feedback type is determined. The five bearing contents—Trace-Response, formation and retention, organizational participation, historical dynamics, actual route of action—are written out family by family (5.2).  
C-C  Correspondence obligations between the mechanism model χ and the encoding M  
　(1) Processes (projection-coverage obligation): all processes of the mechanism model are listed in χ; among them, for each process that changes an encoded Structural Position, every effective component is compiled into a process component of M; processes acting on unencoded state may be omitted, but they must satisfy the antecedents K1–K4 of Lemma 1 and the declaration-completeness condition K5, and Lemma 1 guarantees that the omission does not change the qualification issued. A process whose two coefficients b and k are both 0 under some letter of the encoding does not appear (omission of identically-zero processes; generation is determined by the coefficients alone, whichever physical quantity is 0); a process with nonzero coefficients that takes the value 0 at some state is kept.  
　(2) State: the complete mechanism state X consists of the Structural Positions and the unencoded state (the loose-material store of Family A, the receptor pool of Family E); the positions of M take only the projection π(X), i.e. the Structural Positions (above-baseline amounts) as dimensionless numbers in the declared units.  
　(3) Records: formation events are compiled into pulses of M, and state does not stand in for records; a triggering carrier outside B\_phys is marked out-of-boundary (Mark). This is a declaration about the two formation events of this paper: they are coarse-grained into macroscopically effective out-of-boundary pulses, i.e. the signal acts on the medium from outside the boundary and completes the structural change within one beat. It is not a general compilation theorem: an endogenous process is not recorded as an out-of-boundary change merely because it has a remote cause outside the boundary.  
　(4) Carriers: a process carrier inside B\_phys is marked in-boundary.  
　(5) Routes: cert\_read and cert\_path are derived from the declared route chains—the carrier of the read position is the head of the chain; each segment is a declared physical link; each node lies inside B\_phys; a missing chain is recorded as unknown, and a contradiction with the declaration as 0.  
　(6) Time: the node times of a route chain run strictly forward from t and end at t + 1/2 (the action-event time of D13 of the Mathematical Form).  
　(7) Identity and historical events: each position is bound to a unique carrier, invariant across letters; formation events are recorded in the letter declaration of χ. This is a structural declaration check and is the in-model basis on which the evaluator declares Ident and HistEv to be 1; it does not test physical certification of identity over time or of historical events, which belongs to the real-host interface.  
　(8) Resolution compatibility: along the input history in question, every nonzero pulse amplitude and process component satisfies \|c\| \> ε. This is a sufficient condition for the state of M to coincide beat by beat with π(X) (Proposition 2); the construction certificate of this paper takes it as its condition of issue. When it fails, the certificate is not issued, but this does not mean that read-back is impossible.  
C-D  Side-by-side comparison: the five bearing contents are set out item by item for the two families, stating the common structure and the differences; for artificial neural networks, each item is marked as corresponding, as replaced by the program-host declaration, or as not holding under the stated mode of operation; conclusions are issued only between the two declared constructions.

4\. Construction Procedure

### 4.1  Compilation J (Appendix A.1)

J(χ) → (M, compilation record): following C-C (1)–(7) item by item, J generates the position set of M, ε, the initial state, the pulses, the processes (the law is linear: value = b(u) + k(u)·Σ read positions), the carrier marks and the certification values. b and k are obtained as products of declared physical quantities; the program asserts that k is dimensionless and that b has the same dimension as the Structural Position. Certification values are not filled in by hand; all of them are derived from route chains and physical links.  
Resolution-compatibility check fidelity(M, h): checks C-C (8) beat by beat and returns the first incompatible pulse or component.  
Check of the projection and omission antecedents coverage(χ): χ additionally lists the unencoded state and the omitted processes (name, action target, quantities read); the program checks K1–K5 (Lemma 1) one by one. This is a structural auxiliary check for the two declarations of this paper, not a semantic certifier for arbitrary models: the basis for K2 and for “all processes listed” is the explicit coefficient formulas, the beat-by-beat physical updates and the process inventories of the two families (4.2, 4.3).  
4.2  Family A: Constant-Head Two-Channel Flow Network χ\_A (Appendix A.2)  
Physical boundary B\_phys = {inlet, outlet, bed1, bed2, flow1, flow2, loose1, loose2}: the inlet from the upstream reservoir, the downstream outlet, the walls of the two channels and the carrier fluid in each channel, and the loose-material store of each channel (a temporary holding place for shed structural material).  
Physical links: inlet→flowᵢ, flowᵢ→outlet; bedᵢ→flowᵢ (the structure on the wall determines conductance); flowᵢ→bedᵢ (shear and transport act on the wall); bedᵢ→bedᵢ (local processes of the wall); bedᵢ→looseᵢ (shedding into the store); looseᵢ→flowᵢ (the store is carried off by the carrier fluid).  
Dimensions: pressure P = M L⁻¹ T⁻²; hydraulic conductance G = L⁴ T M⁻¹ (Q = gΔP); structural quantity A = L²; time T. System of units P\_u, G\_u, A\_u, Δt, with length unit L\_u = G\_u P\_u Δt / A\_u (the program asserts that its dimension is L).  
Quantities and interpretation: Qᵢ is the volumetric flow rate of the carrier fluid; the structural material carried by the fluid is a dilute phase (volume fraction c ≪ 1) whose volume is not counted in Q; zᵢ is the cross-sectional area of above-baseline structural material on the wall of channel i, i.e. the structural volume per unit length, and is the Trace-Response; structural volume and carrier-fluid volume do not convert into each other; that conductance increases with z is the phenomenological antecedent C1.  
Structural Positions: zᵢ, bound to carrier bedᵢ, initial state 0. Complete mechanism state X = (z₁, z₂, L₁, L₂), where Lᵢ is the loose-material store (a volume).  
Constitutive antecedents (phenomenological; declared with their status as assumptions):  
　C1  Linearization of conductance in structure: gᵢ = g₀ + κzᵢ, domain of validity 0 ≤ zᵢ ≤ z\_max;  
　C2  Constant-head flow: Qᵢ = gᵢΔP (quasi-static: hydraulic relaxation time ≪ Δt ≪ structural relaxation time 1/γ);  
　C3  Flux builds structure: each beat writes dzᵢ = φcQᵢΔt/ℓ, where φ ∈ \[0, 1\] is the fraction of the carried material converted into structure;  
　C4  Structural relaxation: −γzᵢΔt per beat; the shed structural material enters the store Lᵢ of the same channel at the end of the beat;  
　C4′  Store output: each beat the carrier fluid carries off min(Lᵢ, eQᵢΔt), using the store at the start of the beat, where e is the transport capacity of the carrier fluid for loose material (a volume fraction); beats with zero flow have no output;  
　C5  External dumping: the out-of-boundary dump point dump adds a volume V of structural material to channel 1, increasing z₁ by V/ℓ.  
Drive letters: flow (ΔP = 1 P\_u); seed (ΔP = 0, accompanied by an external dump with V/ℓ = 2 A\_u); rest (ΔP = 0).  
Parameters: g₀ = 1 G\_u, κ = 1 G\_u/A\_u, c = 1/5, φ = 1/2, ℓ = 1 L\_u, γ = 1/10 Δt⁻¹, e = 1/5, ε = 1/1000 A\_u, z\_max = 10 A\_u.  
Budgets (beat by beat, recomputed independently by the program):  
　Carrier fluid: the carrier-fluid content of each channel is constant (incompressible, full pipe); the total flow at the inlet node and the total flow at the outlet node are computed separately and checked to be equal.  
　Structural material: the inlet load is cQᵢΔt; the write is φ times the inlet load and does not exceed it; the shedding γzᵢΔtℓ goes into the store; the store output exceeds neither the store at the start of the beat nor eQᵢΔt; outlet load = inlet load − write + store output; in beats with zero flow, store output and outlet load are both 0.  
　Global: the change in the structural-material inventory Σzᵢℓ + ΣLᵢ (computed from the state difference) = inlet load + external dump − outlet load (computed from the two end nodes), exactly, beat by beat.  
Energy account: this model is idealized—the free-energy change of the structural material, and the additional dissipation of writing, shedding and store transport, are all taken as 0; the work done by the external dump lies outside B\_phys and does not enter this account. This is a model assumption, not a deduction. Under this assumption the hydraulic account is the whole energy account: supply power ΔP·ΣQᵢ = viscous dissipation ΣQᵢ²/gᵢ (derived from C2 and Poiseuille dissipation), and the change in hydraulic stored energy is 0. In beats with zero flow both sides are 0, yet structure can still be shed; this is permitted in the idealization adopted; whether it holds for real materials is unproved.  
Route chains: writeᵢ: bedᵢ(t) → flowᵢ(t + 1/4) → bedᵢ(t + 1/2); relaxᵢ: bedᵢ(t) → bedᵢ(t + 1/2).  
Omitted processes: intakeᵢ (intake of relaxation-shed material into the store, the store-side component of relaxᵢ, target Lᵢ); exportᵢ (store output, target Lᵢ, reading Lᵢ, and reading zᵢ through Qᵢ = (g₀ + κzᵢ)ΔP). Store output reads the store, but the store does not feed back into the update of the Structural Positions: neither the coefficients nor the occurrence nor the routes of write and relaxation read L. The encoding takes only π(X) = (z₁, z₂). What is omitted is an encoding component; the real budget is not deleted; the material coupling between the store-side components and write and relaxation is kept as declared, and these side components are not declared as physical mechanisms that can be knocked out independently.  
Readings: in the slime-mould tube reading, z is the increment of the cross-section of structural material in the tube wall, and the structural material is transported by protoplasmic streaming; this is the budget direction of C3. In the river-channel reading, incision enlarges the flow cross-section and bed material is exported to the fluid, so the budget direction is opposite to that of C3; the encoding is unchanged, but the material budget must be separately declared and verified in the opposite direction, and this verification remains open (U2).  
4.3  Family E: Firing-Rate Model of a Biological Neural Network χ\_E (Appendix A.3)  
Physical boundary B\_phys = {pre, syn1, syn2, N1, N2}: the presynaptic neuron, the two synapses, the two postsynaptic neurons.  
Physical links: pre→synᵢ (transmitter release); synᵢ→Nᵢ (synaptic transmission); Nᵢ→synᵢ (backpropagating action potentials reach the synapse); synᵢ→synᵢ (local synaptic processes).  
Dimensions: conductance S = M⁻¹ L⁻² T³ I²; firing rate Hz = T⁻¹; energy E = M L² T⁻²; time T. Units S\_u, x\_u, Δt.  
Structural Positions: zᵢ = wᵢ − w₀, the above-baseline conductance of synapse i, is the Trace-Response; bound to carrier synᵢ, initial state 0. Complete mechanism state X = (z₁, z₂, R₁, R₂), where Rᵢ is the receptor pool of postsynaptic neuron i (a number of receptors).  
Constitutive antecedents (phenomenological; declared with their status as assumptions):  
　N1  Linear firing rate: yᵢ = βwᵢx (x is the firing rate of the presynaptic neuron; membrane time constant ≪ Δt; domain of validity yᵢ ≤ y\_max);  
　N2  Hebbian plasticity: each beat writes dzᵢ = ηxyᵢΔt (joint pre- and postsynaptic activity: the backpropagating action potential of the postsynaptic neuron coincides at the synapse with transmitter release);  
　N3  Weight decay: −γzᵢΔt per beat (receptor endocytosis returns receptors to the pool);  
　N4  External paired stimulation: paired stimulation from an out-of-boundary electrode induces a potentiation of synapse 1 by Δw = 2 S\_u, with the receptors taken from the receptor pool of postsynaptic neuron 1;  
　N5  Receptor budget: w = number of receptors × single-receptor conductance g₁; write and induction take receptors from the pool, decay returns receptors to the pool, and the pool is always nonnegative.  
Drive letters: drive (x = 1 x\_u); pair (x = 0, accompanied by paired stimulation); rest (x = 0).  
Parameters: w₀ = 1 S\_u, β = 1 S\_u⁻¹, η = 1/10 S\_u·Δt, γ = 1/10 Δt⁻¹, g₁ = 1/10 S\_u, initial receptor pool 40, y\_max = 100 x\_u, ε = 1/1000 S\_u.  
Budget (verified beat by beat): the receptor pool decreases by (write + induction − decay)/g₁ and is always nonnegative; the firing rate does not exceed y\_max; the structural quantity is nonnegative.  
Energy account (phenomenological): metabolic supply power = e\_spk·Σyᵢ + e\_syn·x·Σwᵢ + e\_pl·Σ\|Δwᵢ\|/Δt, where e\_spk is the energy cost per spike (dimension E), and e\_syn and e\_pl are the transmission and plasticity energy costs per unit conductance (dimension E/S); all of it is booked as dissipation, and the change in chemical stored energy is taken as 0; the program asserts that the supply has the dimension of power. This account is not derived from any dissipation law; “dissipation = supply” is a realization of the declared assumption, not a derivation of real metabolism (U3).  
Route chains: hebbᵢ: synᵢ(t) → Nᵢ(t + 1/4) → synᵢ(t + 1/2); decayᵢ: synᵢ(t) → synᵢ(t + 1/2).  
Omitted processes: poolᵢ (the receptor-pool budget, i.e. the pool-side components of write, induction and decay; target Rᵢ, reading zᵢ). The receptor pool acts only as a resource constraint (nonnegativity of the pool is a condition of physical validity) and does not enter the coefficients of write and decay; the encoding takes only π(X) = (z₁, z₂).

### 4.4  Compilation Results (Appendix B.1)

For both families, all certification values are derived from the route chains as 1 (all five checks true: read\_pos\_carrier, links, in\_boundary, timing, ends\_at\_proc\_carrier); the projection-omission antecedents K1–K5 are all true for both families.  
Formation events: the triggering carrier dump of seed in Family A and the triggering carrier electrode of pair in Family E both lie outside B\_phys, and both are compiled into out-of-boundary pulses.  
Process coefficients: in drive beats the write has b = 1/10, k = 1/10 (Family A: b = φcΔPg₀Δt/ℓ, k = φcΔPκΔt/ℓ; Family E: b = ηxβw₀xΔt, k = ηxβxΔt); the relaxation has k = −1/10 in every beat; in rest and formation beats both write coefficients are 0, and the write is omitted as an identically-zero process.

5.2  The Five Bearing Contents (Family by Family, Side by Side)  
　(1) Trace-Response  
　　Family A: the cross-sectional area z₁ (dimension L²) of above-baseline structural material on the wall of channel 1; the baseline corresponds to g₀ and does not enter the Trace-Response.  
　　Family E: the above-baseline conductance z₁ of synapse 1; the baseline w₀ does not enter the Trace-Response.  
　　Common: the Trace-Response is the structural quantity in excess of the baseline, bound to a unique carrier; the baseline has conducting or transmitting capacity but no formation record, and does not count as Trace-Response (consistent with the distinction between record and state in Proposition 1 of *The Dynamics-Source Form of Field Concretization*).  
　(2) Formation and retention  
　　Formation: in Family A it is an out-of-boundary dump, with external material entering B\_phys; in Family E it is paired stimulation by an out-of-boundary electrode, where the outside supplies only the trigger and the material is taken from the in-boundary receptor pool. Both are encoded as out-of-boundary pulses and recorded in Form and Mark.  
　　Retention: without drive there is only relaxation, z₁ = 2·(9/10)^m; within the resolution-compatible domain, bearing still holds for m ≤ 50 (Proposition 3).  
　(3) Organizational participation  
　　Family A: the write process write₁ (flow determined by g₁ = g₀ + κz₁) and the relaxation relax₁ are organizationally conditioned on z₁, with carriers inside the boundary.  
　　Family E: the Hebbian write hebb₁ (postsynaptic firing y₁ = β(w₀ + z₁)x) and the decay decay₁ are organizationally conditioned on z₁.  
　(4) Historical dynamics  
　　Drive beat: the write component 3/10 and the relaxation component −1/5 are both effective, both read the already formed, suprathreshold z₁, both have certification value 1, and both act on z₁ ∈ T.  
　　Process-by-process isolation (Proposition 4): write alone or relaxation alone each gives Q{z1} = 1; when the write does not read the Trace-Response and there is no relaxation, it is 0.  
　(5) Actual route of action  
　　Family A: z₁ (wall) → carrier fluid of channel 1 (t + 1/4, flow determined by the structure on the wall) → write on the wall (t + 1/2), entirely inside B\_phys, closing through the channel's carrier fluid back onto the carrier on which the Trace-Response sits.  
　　Family E: z₁ (synapse 1) → postsynaptic neuron 1 (t + 1/4, firing determined by the synaptic conductance) → the backpropagating action potential returns to synapse 1 and writes (t + 1/2), entirely inside B\_phys.  
　　Route of the negative component: in both families, a process local to the carrier.

Lemma 1 (Projection-Omission Lemma) \[the histories compared must lie in the valid domain common to the models\]  
　Let the complete state of χ be X = (z, Y), where z are the encoded Structural Positions and Y is the unencoded state; M = J(χ) contains only processes acting on z; χ also has omitted processes, all of whose action targets lie in Y. Assume:  
　　K1  omitted processes do not act on Structural Positions;  
　　K2  encoded processes read only Structural Positions, and their coefficients, occurrence and route certification are determined by the letter alone and do not read Y;  
　　K3  formation events act only on Structural Positions, and omitted processes produce no pulses on Structural Positions;  
　　K4  all encoded processes are inside the boundary, with certification value 1.  
　Let M⁺ be the mechanism-layer encoding obtained by adding the omitted processes to M (as processes acting on Y, with arbitrary certification values), with any valid initial value of Y; M⁺ is evaluated under the same semantics of D15–D18 of the Mathematical Form, and the laws of the omitted processes may go beyond D12 (such as the min law of exportᵢ), provided that their action targets, functional support and effectiveness are defined (for the min law, the support at branch junctions is declared according to the semantics used in this paper). M⁺ is an auxiliary semantics of this paper's mechanism layer, not an extension of the evaluator; the evaluator does not execute M⁺, and the conclusion of this lemma is given by the proof below, not by a program run. Then for the same input history, the same window and any T ⊆ z:  
　(i) M⁺ and M have the same z trajectory, and the same Form and Mark on the z positions;  
　(ii) Src(T), Ret(T) and HDyn(T) are the same on both;  
　(iii) Q\_{M⁺}(T) = Q\_M(T), and the in-boundary self-feedback readings are the same.  
Proof. (i) By K2, the increment of z in each beat depends only on the pre-beat z and the letter; by K1, omitted processes do not change z; by K3, formation and marking of the z positions come only from formation events and encoded processes, which are the same in M and M⁺; induction gives the claim. (ii) Src and Ret depend only on Mark, Form and the final state of the z positions, which are the same by (i). HDyn(T) requires the action target to belong to T ⊆ z; by K1, the targets of omitted processes are not in z, so omitted processes are not bearers of HDyn(T), and the set of bearers and their values are therefore the same. (iii) Q = Src ∧ Ret ∧ Part ∧ HDyn. Omitted processes may be organizationally conditioned on z (for example, exportᵢ reads zᵢ through Qᵢ), so that Part\_{M⁺}(T) may be greater than Part\_M(T). But by K4 every bearer of HDyn(T) is inside the boundary, and Supp\_act ⊆ Supp\_cond, so HDyn(T) = 1 implies Part\_M(T) = 1; when HDyn(T) = 0, Q is 0 on both; with certification value 1, HDyn does not take the value unknown. Hence Q is the same on both; the same argument applies to the in-boundary self-feedback reading, whose bearing term also requires the action target to belong to T. ∎  
　Application: for both families, K1–K4 and the declaration-completeness condition K5 (the unencoded state and the omitted processes are each listed completely against the other) are checked true by coverage (Appendix B.1). For Family A, Y = (L₁, L₂), with omitted processes intakeᵢ and exportᵢ; for Family E, Y = (R₁, R₂), with omitted process poolᵢ.  
　Limits: the lemma holds only for T ⊆ Structural Positions; it draws no conclusion about candidate T containing the store or the receptor pool, nor does it state that all duty atoms of the mechanism model (such as actions targeting the store) coincide with those of the encoding. The validity of Y (store and pool nonnegative) is a condition of the physical construction certificate and is not given by the lemma.

Proposition 1 (unit closed-form construction domain)  
　Unit model: a formation pulse a, then m rest beats (relaxation −γz), then one drive beat (write b + kz, relaxation −γz), W the last beat, T = {z1}. Proposition 2 (exact condition for state fidelity; resolution compatibility as a sufficient condition)  
　Let M = J(χ), where every component of χ acting on Structural Positions is counted in full into the state. Along an input history h:  
　(i) the state trajectory of M coincides beat by beat with the trajectory of the projection π(X) if and only if, in every beat and at every position, the sum of the components discarded by M (pulse amplitudes and process components with 0 \< \|c\| ≤ ε) is 0;  
　(ii) in particular, resolution compatibility implies identical trajectories. If in some beat the pre-beat states of the two layers coincide and the sum of the discarded components at some position in that beat is nonzero, then the two states differ after the update of that beat; this condition holds when that position has exactly one nonzero discarded component, or when all its discarded components have the same sign. In particular, if t is the beat in which the sum of discarded components first becomes nonzero at some position, then the two layers diverge for the first time after the update of beat t. After divergence, later discarded components may cancel the existing difference and make the two states equal again; regained equality does not mean that the whole trajectory has always agreed.  
Proof. The evaluator counts into the state only components with \|c\| \> ε (likewise for effective pulses). Suppose the two states coincide in the previous beat; in the current beat, at a given position, the increment of χ minus the increment of M is exactly the sum of the discarded components at that position. (i) follows by induction over beats. (ii) Under compatibility there are no discarded components. When the pre-beat states coincide, the difference at a position after the update of that beat is exactly the sum of the discarded components at that position, and this sum is nonzero when there is only one such component or when they all have the same sign; in every beat before the first beat with a nonzero sum, the sums are 0, so by (i) the pre-beat states coincide, and that beat is therefore the first divergence. ∎ Example: ε = 1/100, initial state 1, two beats with only the constant components +1/200 and then −1/200; the encoded trajectory is 1, 1, 1 and the mechanism trajectory is 1, 201/200, 1: they first diverge in beat 1 and are equal again in beat 2.  
　When components cancel (+1/200 and −1/200 at the same position in the same beat, ε = 1/100), resolution is incompatible yet the trajectories coincide (Appendix B.2, Part 5). Hence C-C (8) is a checkable sufficient condition for identical trajectories, not a necessary one.  
　Corollary: when resolution compatibility fails, this check alone cannot guarantee read-back, and the construction certificate of this paper is not issued; if it is separately proved, item by item, that the state and the relevant qualification atoms and correspondence obligations still agree, read-back may be carried out separately. Example: an effective relaxation −1/5 plus two cancelling subthreshold components +1/200 and −1/200 (ε = 1/100): the compatibility check fails, yet the final states of mechanism and encoding are both 9/5, the subthreshold components are ineffective in both layers, and the formal qualification is 1 (Appendix B.2, Part 5).

Discarding sub-resolution components produces in the encoding a retention that the mechanism model does not have.  
　The three cases are kept apart: the sufficient condition holds, and the certificate can be issued; the sufficient condition fails and nothing further has been proved, and the certificate is not issued; divergence has been observed, and there is actual unfaithfulness.

9\. Final Statement  
In each of the two families, flow-network sedimentation and biological neural networks, there is a physical mechanism model with dimensions, closed budgets and a completely declared energy account, whose Structural Positions are encoded by the compilation rule J into an alphabet-gated F model: certification values are derived from the declared route chains, formation events are encoded as out-of-boundary pulses, and processes acting on unencoded state are omitted according to the Projection-Omission Lemma. An artificial neural network in online learning corresponds to the biological construction in duty structure; in frozen inference after training, it lacks historical dynamics on the listed T.

~~~python
"""The Construction Form of Field Concretization: declaration of the physical mechanism model χ_model, dimensional algebra, and compilation J(χ_model) → M (the alphabet-gated F model of The Reachability Form of Field Concretization).
Exact rational arithmetic, Python standard library; evaluation uses reach_core.py of The Reachability Form of Field Concretization v1.0, Appendix A.1 (in the same directory, identical, character for character, to that appendix).
Compilation rules (the correspondence obligations follow from the declaration; no value is assigned by hand):
  position ↔ structural quantity (each position is bound to a unique carrier, invariant across letters: the in-model basis of Ident);
  pulse ↔ declared formation event; if the triggering carrier lies outside B_phys, record ext='out' (Mark), otherwise 'in';
  process ↔ declared physical process; in letter u the process does not appear when both coefficients b and k are 0 (identically-zero processes are omitted; generation is decided by the coefficients alone,
        not by which physical quantity is 0; a process with nonzero coefficients whose value is 0 in some state is kept);
  car ↔ whether the process carrier lies in B_phys;
  cert_read ↔ the carrier of the read positions is the head of the route chain, and the head carrier is the bound carrier of that position;
  cert_path ↔ every segment of the route chain is a declared physical link, every node lies in B_phys, the node times lie in (t, t+1/2] and are strictly forward, and the last time is t+1/2;
  a missing route chain is recorded as None (unknown); a chain that contradicts the declaration is recorded as 0.
"""
from fractions import Fraction as F
from reach_core import GF, Proc
BASE = ('M', 'L', 'T', 'I')

class Dim:
    def __init__(self, **e):
        self.e = tuple(F(e.get(b, 0)) for b in BASE)
    def __mul__(self, o):
        d = Dim(); d.e = tuple(a + b for a, b in zip(self.e, o.e)); return d
    def __truediv__(self, o):
        d = Dim(); d.e = tuple(a - b for a, b in zip(self.e, o.e)); return d
    def __pow__(self, n):
        d = Dim(); d.e = tuple(a * n for a in self.e); return d
    def __eq__(self, o):
        return self.e == o.e
    def __repr__(self):
        s = ' '.join(f'{b}^{x}' for b, x in zip(BASE, self.e) if x)
        return s or '1'

ONE = Dim()

class Qty:
    """Declared quantity with a dimension: the value is expressed in the declared system of units (a rational), dim is its dimension."""
    def __init__(self, v, dim):
        self.v, self.dim = F(v), dim
    def __mul__(self, o):
        return Qty(self.v * o.v, self.dim * o.dim)
    def __truediv__(self, o):
        return Qty(self.v / o.v, self.dim / o.dim)
    def __add__(self, o):
        assert self.dim == o.dim, ('dimension mismatch', self.dim, o.dim)
        return Qty(self.v + o.v, self.dim)
    def __sub__(self, o):
        assert self.dim == o.dim, ('dimension mismatch', self.dim, o.dim)
        return Qty(self.v - o.v, self.dim)

def dimensionless(q, unit):
    """Turn a quantity measured in unit into a dimensionless number: requires q.dim == unit.dim."""
    assert q.dim == unit.dim, ('dimension mismatch', q.dim, unit.dim)
    return q.v / unit.v

class Process:
    """Declared physical process: value = b(u) + k(u)·Σ read positions. b, k are Qty under the given letter and must be compatible with the units of the structural quantities.
    chain: route chain [(carrier, time offset)], from the carrier of the read positions to the process carrier; None means the route is undeclared."""
    def __init__(self, pid, tgt, carrier, reads, coef, chain):
        self.pid, self.tgt, self.carrier, self.reads = pid, tgt, carrier, tuple(reads)
        self.coef, self.chain = coef, chain     # coef(u) -> (b: Qty|None, k: Qty|None); (None, None) means the process is absent in this beat

class Model:
    """χ_model: B_phys (carrier set), links (physical links, directed), positions {position: (carrier, unit Qty)} (the encoded Structural Positions),
    letters {u: [(position, Qty amplitude, triggering carrier)]} (formation events), processes [Process] (processes acting on Structural Positions, all encoded),
    eps (Qty, in structural-quantity units), extra (names of mechanism states that are not encoded), omitted [(process name, action target, quantities read, note)] (processes that are not encoded).
    Complete mechanism state X = (Structural Positions, extra); the encoding takes only the projection π(X) = Structural Positions / declared units."""
    def __init__(self, name, B_phys, links, positions, letters, processes, eps, s0, extra=(), omitted=()):
        self.name, self.B, self.links = name, set(B_phys), set(links)
        self.pos, self.letters, self.procs, self.eps, self.s0 = positions, letters, processes, eps, s0
        self.extra, self.omitted = tuple(extra), list(omitted)

def coverage(model):
    """Coverage and omission antecedents of the projection encoding (K1–K4 of Lemma 1 and declaration completeness K5), returned clause by clause as truth values:
    K1 every process that changes a Structural Position is in procs (no omitted process has a Structural Position as its action target);
    K2 the coefficients of encoded processes are determined by the letter alone (Process.coef takes only the letter), and every quantity they read is a Structural Position (no unencoded state is read);
    K3 formation events act only on Structural Positions; omitted processes produce no pulse, formation or marking of a Structural Position (their targets are not Structural Positions);
    K4 every encoded process is in-boundary and its route certification follows from the chain as 1 (the HDyn bearer is in-boundary, HDyn ⇒ Part);
    K5 the unencoded states and the omitted processes are listed in full in the declaration (extra and the targets of omitted cover each other)."""
    pos = set(model.pos)
    k1 = all(o[1] not in pos for o in model.omitted) and all(p.tgt in pos for p in model.procs)
    k2 = all(set(p.reads) <= pos for p in model.procs) and \
        all(p.coef.__code__.co_argcount == 1 for p in model.procs)
    k3 = all(i in pos for evs in model.letters.values() for (i, _, _) in evs)
    k4 = all(p.carrier in model.B and cert_of(model, p)[:2] == (1, 1) for p in model.procs)
    k5 = {o[1] for o in model.omitted} == set(model.extra)        # every unencoded state has a declared process, and vice versa
    return dict(K1=k1, K2=k2, K3=k3, K4=k4, K5=k5)

def cert_of(model, p):
    """Derive (cert_read, cert_path, reason) from the route declaration."""
    if p.chain is None:
        return None, None, 'route undeclared'
    nodes = [c for c, _ in p.chain]
    times = [t for _, t in p.chain]
    rd = 1 if all(model.pos[j][0] == nodes[0] for j in p.reads) else 0
    ok_links = all((a, b) in model.links for a, b in zip(nodes, nodes[1:]))
    ok_B = all(c in model.B for c in nodes)
    ok_t = times[0] == 0 and times[-1] == F(1, 2) and all(a < b for a, b in zip(times, times[1:])) \
        if len(times) > 1 else times == [F(1, 2)]
    ok_end = nodes[-1] == p.carrier
    path = 1 if (ok_links and ok_B and ok_t and ok_end) else 0
    why = dict(read_pos_carrier=rd, links=ok_links, in_boundary=ok_B, timing=ok_t, ends_at_proc_carrier=ok_end)
    return rd, path, why

def compile_model(model):
    """J(χ_model) → (GF, compilation record)."""
    I = tuple(model.pos)
    unit = {i: model.pos[i][1] for i in I}
    rec = {'Ident': {i: model.pos[i][0] for i in I}, 'cert': {}, 'pulses': {}, 'procs': {}}
    letters = {}
    for u, events in model.letters.items():
        pulses = []
        for (i, amt, trig) in events:
            a = dimensionless(amt, unit[i])
            ext = 'in' if trig in model.B else 'out'
            pulses.append((i, a, ext))
            rec['pulses'][(u, i)] = (a, ext, trig)
        procs = []
        for p in model.procs:
            b, k = p.coef(u)
            if b is None and k is None:
                continue
            bv = dimensionless(b, unit[p.tgt]) if b is not None else F(0)
            kv = F(0)
            if k is not None:
                assert k.dim == ONE, ('k must be dimensionless (equal position units)', p.pid, k.dim)
                kv = k.v * unit[p.reads[0]].v / unit[p.tgt].v
            if bv == 0 and kv == 0:
                continue                      # identically-zero process omitted (generation is decided by the coefficients alone)
            rd, path, why = cert_of(model, p)
            rec['cert'][p.pid] = (rd, path, why)
            car = 'B' if p.carrier in model.B else 'out'
            procs.append(Proc(f'{p.pid}@{u}', p.tgt, car, 'lin', R=p.reads, k=kv, b=bv,
                              cert_read=rd, cert_path=path))
            rec['procs'][(u, p.pid)] = (bv, kv, car)
        letters[u] = (pulses, procs)
    eps = dimensionless(model.eps, unit[I[0]])
    s0 = tuple(dimensionless(model.s0[i], unit[i]) for i in I)
    return GF(I, eps, letters, s0), rec

def fmt(x):
    return str(x) if not isinstance(x, (set, frozenset)) else '{' + ','.join(sorted(x)) + '}'

def fidelity(M, hist):
    """Resolution-compatibility check: along hist, at every beat, each pulse amplitude and each process component c must be 0 or satisfy |c| > ε; otherwise the F encoding discards that component and diverges from the mechanism model.
    Returns None (compatible) or (beat index, process, c)."""
    s = M.s0
    for t, u in enumerate(hist, 1):
        sd = dict(zip(M.I, s))
        for (tgt, a, ext) in M.letters[u][0]:
            if a != 0 and abs(a) <= M.eps:
                return t, f'pulse→{tgt}', a
        for p in M.letters[u][1]:
            c = p.value(sd)
            if c != 0 and abs(c) <= M.eps:
                return t, p.pid, c
        r = M.step(s, u)
        if r is None:
            return t, 'out of domain', None
        s = r[0]
    return None
~~~

A.2  fam\_flow.py  Family A: mechanism-model declaration and beat-by-beat physical verification of the constant-head two-channel flow network (Section 4, 4.2)

~~~python
"""Family A: declaration χ_A of the physical mechanism model of a constant-head two-channel flow network (a common abstraction of river-channel incision and slime-mould tube thickening).
Constitutive antecedents (phenomenological; declared with their status as assumptions):
  C1 conductance–structure linearization: g_i = g0 + κ·z_i (z_i is the above-baseline cross-sectional area; domain of validity 0 ≤ z_i ≤ z_max);
  C2 constant-head flow: Q_i = g_i·ΔP (quasi-static; hydraulic relaxation time ≪ Δt);
  C3 flux builds structure: each beat writes dz_i = φ·c·Q_i·Δt/ℓ (φ is the fraction of the carried material that turns into structure, 0 ≤ φ ≤ 1);
  C4 structural relaxation: −γ·z_i·Δt per beat; the shed structural material enters the loose-material store L_i of the same channel (carrier loose_i, in B_phys);
  C4′ store output: each beat the fluid carries off min(L_i, e·Q_i·Δt) (e is the transport capacity of the carrier fluid for loose material, a volume fraction);
      the store at the start of the beat is used, a zero-flow beat outputs nothing, and material shed in a beat enters the store at the end of that beat;
  C5 external dumping: an out-of-boundary source (the dump point) adds a volume V of structural material to channel 1; z_1 increases by V/ℓ.
Quantities and interpretation: Q is the volumetric flow rate of the carrier fluid; the carried structural material is a dilute phase (volume fraction c ≪ 1), and its volume is not counted in Q; z is the cross-sectional area of wall structural material
  (structural volume per unit length); structural volume and carrier-fluid volume do not convert into each other; that conductance grows with z is the declared phenomenological antecedent C1.
Energy account (idealized, declared): the free-energy change of the structural material and the additional dissipation of writing, shedding and store transport are taken as 0; the work done by external dumping lies outside B_phys
  and does not enter this account. Under this idealization the hydraulic account, supply ΔP·ΣQ = viscous dissipation ΣQ²/g (stored-energy change 0), is the whole energy account.
Complete mechanism state X = (z_1, z_2, L_1, L_2). The store-output process reads L_i (and, through Q_i, z_i) and acts on L_i; the entry of shed material into the store is the counterpart component of relaxation
  on the store side. The store does not feed back into the update of the Structural Positions (neither the coefficients nor the occurrence and routes of writing and relaxation read L). The encoding M takes only the projection π(X) = (z_1, z_2);
  the store and its processes are omitted by the Projection-Omission Lemma (Lemma 1), rather than “encoding every physical process one to one”.
Time: state t → flow division t+1/4 → write event t+1/2 → state t+1 (D13 of the Mathematical Form).
"""
from fractions import Fraction as F
from ctor_core import Dim, Qty, ONE, Process, Model
Pd = Dim(M=1, L=-1, T=-2)                  # pressure
Gd = Dim(L=4, T=1, M=-1)                   # hydraulic conductance (Q = g·ΔP)
Ad = Dim(L=2)
Td = Dim(T=1)
Ld = Dim(L=1)
P_u, G_u, A_u, dt = Qty(1, Pd), Qty(1, Gd), Qty(1, Ad), Qty(1, Td)
L_u = G_u * P_u * dt / A_u                 # self-consistent system of units: A_u·L_u = g_u·P_u·Δt
assert L_u.dim == Ld and L_u.v == 1

def params(**over):
    p = dict(g0=Qty(1, Gd), kappa=Qty(1, Gd / Ad), c=Qty(F(1, 5), ONE), phi=Qty(F(1, 2), ONE),
             ell=Qty(1, Ld), gamma=Qty(F(1, 10), Td ** -1), dP_flow=Qty(1, Pd), seed=Qty(2, Ad),
             eps=Qty(F(1, 1000), Ad), z_max=Qty(10, Ad), e=Qty(F(1, 5), ONE))
    p.update(over)
    return p

def model(p=None, chains=True):
    p = p or params()
    dP = {'flow': p['dP_flow'], 'seed': Qty(0, Pd), 'rest': Qty(0, Pd)}
    alpha = p['phi'] * p['c'] / p['ell']                      # dimension L^-1
    def write_coef(u):
        return alpha * dP[u] * p['g0'] * dt, alpha * dP[u] * p['kappa'] * dt
    def relax_coef(u):
        return None, Qty(-1, ONE) * p['gamma'] * dt
    procs = []
    for i in (1, 2):
        b, f = f'bed{i}', f'flow{i}'
        procs.append(Process(f'write{i}', f'z{i}', b, (f'z{i}',), write_coef,
                             [(b, F(0)), (f, F(1, 4)), (b, F(1, 2))] if chains else None))
        procs.append(Process(f'relax{i}', f'z{i}', b, (f'z{i}',), relax_coef,
                             [(b, F(0)), (b, F(1, 2))] if chains else None))
    B = {'inlet', 'outlet', 'bed1', 'bed2', 'flow1', 'flow2', 'loose1', 'loose2'}
    links = {('inlet', 'flow1'), ('inlet', 'flow2'), ('flow1', 'outlet'), ('flow2', 'outlet'),
             ('bed1', 'loose1'), ('bed2', 'loose2'), ('loose1', 'flow1'), ('loose2', 'flow2'),
             ('bed1', 'flow1'), ('flow1', 'bed1'), ('bed2', 'flow2'), ('flow2', 'bed2'),
             ('bed1', 'bed1'), ('bed2', 'bed2')}
    letters = {'seed': [('z1', p['seed'], 'dump')], 'flow': [], 'rest': []}
    pos = {'z1': ('bed1', A_u), 'z2': ('bed2', A_u)}
    omitted = [(f'intake{i}', f'L{i}', (f'z{i}',), 'relaxation-shed material enters the store (store-side component of relax)') for i in (1, 2)] + \
              [(f'export{i}', f'L{i}', (f'L{i}', f'z{i}'), 'store carried off by the carrier fluid, min(L, eQΔt); Q reads z through g') for i in (1, 2)]
    return Model('Family A · constant-head two-channel flow network', B, links, pos, letters, procs, p['eps'],
                 {'z1': Qty(0, Ad), 'z2': Qty(0, Ad)}, extra=('L1', 'L2'), omitted=omitted), dP, alpha

def physics(hist, p=None, L0=0):
    """Compute directly on χ_A, beat by beat, in the declared physical quantities, and recompute the three accounts independently; return the z trajectory (in A_u) and the verification record.
    Fluid: the carrier-fluid content of each channel is constant (incompressible, full pipe), inflow = outflow; the inlet total and the outlet total are recomputed separately from the two end nodes.
    Structural material: per channel, the structural volume zℓ and the loose-material store L; boundary flux = inlet load + external dumping − outlet load;
      each beat checks Δ(Σzℓ + ΣL) = boundary flux (the inventory is computed from the state difference, the boundary flux from the two end nodes)."""
    p = p or params()
    M, dP, alpha = model(p)
    V3 = Ld ** 3
    z = {1: Qty(0, Ad), 2: Qty(0, Ad)}
    L = {1: Qty(L0, V3), 2: Qty(L0, V3)}
    traj, log = [(z[1].v, z[2].v)], []
    for u in hist:
        g = {i: p['g0'] + p['kappa'] * z[i] for i in (1, 2)}
        Q = {i: g[i] * dP[u] for i in (1, 2)}                          # carrier-fluid flow rate L^3/T
        load_in = {i: p['c'] * Q[i] * dt for i in (1, 2)}               # volume of structural material carried in at the inlet
        dep = {i: p['phi'] * load_in[i] for i in (1, 2)}                # turned into structure
        ero = {i: p['gamma'] * dt * z[i] * p['ell'] for i in (1, 2)}    # shed into the store
        cap = {i: p['e'] * Q[i] * dt for i in (1, 2)}
        exp_ = {i: L[i] if L[i].v <= cap[i].v else cap[i] for i in (1, 2)}   # store output (start-of-beat store)
        inj = {1: p['seed'] * p['ell'] if u == 'seed' else Qty(0, V3), 2: Qty(0, V3)}
        carry_out = {i: load_in[i] - dep[i] + exp_[i] for i in (1, 2)}  # outlet load
        znew = {i: z[i] + (dep[i] - ero[i] + inj[i]) / p['ell'] for i in (1, 2)}
        Lnew = {i: L[i] - exp_[i] + ero[i] for i in (1, 2)}
        for i in (1, 2):
            assert dep[i].v <= load_in[i].v and exp_[i].v <= L[i].v and exp_[i].v <= cap[i].v
            assert Lnew[i].v >= 0 and carry_out[i].v >= 0
            assert g[i].v > 0 and 0 <= znew[i].v <= p['z_max'].v        # constitutive domain of validity
            if Q[i].v == 0:
                assert exp_[i].v == 0 and carry_out[i].v == 0           # no transport in a zero-flow beat
        # fluid: inlet node and outlet node recomputed separately
        q_in = sum((g[i] * dP[u] for i in (1, 2)), Qty(0, Dim(L=3, T=-1)))
        q_out = sum((Q[i] for i in (1, 2)), Qty(0, Dim(L=3, T=-1)))
        assert q_in.v == q_out.v
        # structural material: inventory difference (state) = boundary flux (two end nodes)
        inv0 = sum((z[i] * p['ell'] + L[i] for i in (1, 2)), Qty(0, V3))
        inv1 = sum((znew[i] * p['ell'] + Lnew[i] for i in (1, 2)), Qty(0, V3))
        bnd = sum((load_in[i] + inj[i] - carry_out[i] for i in (1, 2)), Qty(0, V3))
        assert (inv1 - inv0).v == bnd.v
        # energy account (idealization: see the module docstring): supply power = viscous dissipation, stored-energy change 0
        supply = dP[u] * (Q[1] + Q[2])
        diss = sum(((Q[i] * Q[i] / g[i]) for i in (1, 2)), Qty(0, supply.dim))
        assert supply.dim == diss.dim and supply.v == diss.v and diss.v >= 0
        log.append(dict(u=u, Q=(Q[1].v, Q[2].v), dep=(dep[1].v, dep[2].v), ero=(ero[1].v, ero[2].v),
                        exp=(exp_[1].v, exp_[2].v), L=(Lnew[1].v, Lnew[2].v), inj=inj[1].v,
                        carry_out=(carry_out[1].v, carry_out[2].v), supply=supply.v))
        z, L = znew, Lnew
        traj.append((z[1].v, z[2].v))
    return traj, log
~~~

A.3  fam\_neuro.py  Family E: mechanism-model declaration and beat-by-beat physical verification of the firing-rate model of a biological neural network (Section 4, 4.3)

~~~python
"""Family E: declaration χ_E of the physical mechanism model of a firing-rate model of a biological neural network (one presynaptic neuron drives two postsynaptic neurons through two synapses).
Constitutive antecedents (phenomenological; declared with their status as assumptions):
  N1 linear firing rate: y_i = β·w_i·x (x is the firing rate of the presynaptic neuron; domain of validity y_i ≤ y_max; membrane time constant ≪ Δt);
  N2 Hebbian plasticity: each beat writes dz_i = η·x·y_i·Δt (joint pre- and postsynaptic activity; the backpropagating action potential of the postsynaptic neuron reaches the synapse);
  N3 weight decay: −γ·z_i·Δt per beat (receptor endocytosis returns receptors to the dendritic receptor pool);
  N4 external paired stimulation: paired stimulation from an out-of-boundary electrode induces a potentiation Δw of synapse 1 (the receptors are taken from the receptor pool of postsynaptic neuron 1);
  N5 receptor budget: w = number of receptors × single-receptor conductance g₁; writing takes receptors from the pool, decay returns them to it; the pool is nonnegative.
z_i = w_i − w0 is the above-baseline synaptic conductance (the Trace-Response). Time: state t → synaptic transmission and firing t+1/4 → write event t+1/2 → state t+1.
"""
from fractions import Fraction as F
from ctor_core import Dim, Qty, ONE, Process, Model
Sd = Dim(M=-1, L=-2, T=3, I=2)             # conductance (siemens)
Hz = Dim(T=-1)
Td = Dim(T=1)
Ed = Dim(M=1, L=2, T=-2)                   # energy
Wd = Ed / Td                               # power
S_u, x_u, dt = Qty(1, Sd), Qty(1, Hz), Qty(1, Td)

def params(**over):
    p = dict(w0=Qty(1, Sd), beta=Qty(1, Sd ** -1), eta=Qty(F(1, 10), Sd * Td),
             gamma=Qty(F(1, 10), Td ** -1), x_drive=Qty(1, Hz), seed=Qty(2, Sd),
             eps=Qty(F(1, 1000), Sd), g1=Qty(F(1, 10), Sd), pool0=40, y_max=Qty(100, Hz),
             e_spk=Qty(1, Ed), e_syn=Qty(1, Ed / Sd), e_pl=Qty(1, Ed / Sd))
    p.update(over)
    return p

def model(p=None, chains=True):
    p = p or params()
    x = {'drive': p['x_drive'], 'pair': Qty(0, Hz), 'rest': Qty(0, Hz)}
    def write_coef(u):

        heb = {i: p['eta'] * x[u] * y[i] * dt for i in (1, 2)}            # conductance increment
        dec = {i: p['gamma'] * dt * z[i] for i in (1, 2)}

        znew = {i: z[i] + heb[i] - dec[i] + ind[i] for i in (1, 2)}
        for i in (1, 2):
            take = (heb[i] + ind[i] - dec[i]) / p['g1']                    # net number of receptors taken (dimensionless)
            assert take.dim == ONE
            pool[i] -= take.v
            assert pool[i] >= 0 and y[i].v <= p['y_max'].v and znew[i].v >= 0 and w[i].v > 0
        # metabolic energy account (phenomenological): supply = firing cost + synaptic-transmission cost + plasticity cost; all dissipated, chemical stored-energy change taken as 0
        # e_spk: energy cost per spike (energy); e_syn: transmission cost per presynaptic spike and per unit synaptic conductance; e_pl: plasticity cost per unit conductance change
        dw_abs = {i: Qty(abs((heb[i] - dec[i] + ind[i]).v), Sd) for i in (1, 2)}
        supply = (p['e_spk'] * (y[1] + y[2]) + p['e_syn'] * x[u] * (w[1] + w[2]) +
                  p['e_pl'] * (dw_abs[1] + dw_abs[2]) / dt)
        diss = supply
        assert supply.dim == Wd and diss.v >= 0
        log.append(dict(u=u, y=(y[1].v, y[2].v), heb=(heb[1].v, heb[2].v), dec=(dec[1].v, dec[2].v),
                        ind=ind[1].v, pool=(pool[1], pool[2]), supply=supply.v))
        z = znew
        traj.append((z[1].v, z[2].v))
    return traj, log
~~~

A.8  reach\_core.py  Alphabet-gated F model and window-reading evaluator of Appendix A.1 of *The Reachability Form of Field Concretization* v1.0; verbatim identical to Appendix A.1 of that paper

~~~python
"""The Reachability Form of Field Concretization: alphabet-gated F model, window reading, augmented reachability search and brute-force comparison (exact rational arithmetic, strong Kleene three-valued logic)
Model class 𝔐_GF (alphabet-gated F models): a finite position set I and a resolution ε; an alphabet U (containing the rest letter 'rest');
each letter u gives the pulse table [(tgt, a_p, ext)] and the process table [Proc] of its beat; processes follow the numeric laws of the three classes of D12 of The Mathematical Form of Field Concretization
and read the pre-beat state; cert_eff = 1; cert_read and the certification of path edges are declared by the process (direct edges: read position → process, Γ_closed = closed);
Ident and HistEv are declared as 1. Window reading of an input history h (this paper's reading RD): W takes the last w beats of h, and all beats before W are the formative history,
whose Form and Mark records are represented in F by entry-beat records (D2 of the Mathematical Form); the starting state of W is the final state of the formative history.
"""
from fractions import Fraction as Fr
from itertools import combinations, product
from collections import deque
ONE, ZERO, BOT = 1, 0, None

def k_and(*xs):
    if any(x == 0 for x in xs):
        return 0
    if any(x is None for x in xs):
        return None
    return 1

def k_or(*xs):
    if any(x == 1 for x in xs):
        return 1
    if any(x is None for x in xs):
        return None
    return 0

class Proc:
    def __init__(self, pid, tgt, car, law, R=(), k=0, b=0, cert_read=1, cert_path=1):
        self.pid, self.tgt, self.car, self.law = pid, tgt, car, law
        self.R, self.k, self.b = tuple(R), Fr(k), Fr(b)
        self.cert_read, self.cert_path = cert_read, cert_path
    def value(self, s):
        if self.law == 'const':
            return self.b
        if self.law == 'lin':
            return self.b + self.k * sum(s[j] for j in self.R)
        j1, j2 = self.R
        return self.b + self.k * s[j1] * s[j2]
    def supp_act(self, s):
        if self.law == 'const' or self.k == 0:
            return set()
        if self.law == 'lin':
            return set(self.R)
        j1, j2 = self.R
        out = set()
        if s[j2] != 0:
            out.add(j1)
        if s[j1] != 0:
            out.add(j2)
        return out
    def supp_cond(self, s, eps):
        sa = self.supp_act(s)
        if self.law == 'lin' and abs(self.value(s)) <= eps:
            sa |= set(self.R)
        return sa

class GF:
    """Alphabet-gated F model. letters: {u: (pulses, procs)}; pulses = [(tgt, a, ext)], ext ∈ {'out','in'}."""
    def __init__(self, I, eps, letters, s0):
        """Validity antecedents (Definition 3): position names are nonempty and pairwise distinct; ε, the initial state and the pulse amplitudes are nonnegative rationals (int or Fraction; floats are not accepted);
        the alphabet is finite and contains 'rest'; process labels are pairwise distinct within a letter; law ∈ {const, lin, bilin}, lin has nonempty read positions, bilin exactly two,
        const none; read positions and action targets belong to the position set; cert_read, cert_path ∈ {1, 0, None}; pulse ext ∈ {'out','in'}.
        Invalid input raises ValueError and does not enter evaluation."""
        def rat(v, nonneg=False):
            if isinstance(v, bool) or not isinstance(v, (int, Fr)):
                raise ValueError('a value must be an int or a Fraction')
            if nonneg and v < 0:
                raise ValueError('must be nonnegative')
            return Fr(v)
        I = tuple(I)
        if not I or len(set(I)) != len(I) or any(not i for i in I):
            raise ValueError('position set')
        if 'rest' not in letters:
            raise ValueError('the alphabet must contain rest')
        for u, (pulses, procs) in letters.items():
            for (tgt, a, ext) in pulses:
                if tgt not in I or ext not in ('out', 'in'):
                    raise ValueError('pulse')
                rat(a, True)
            ids = [p.pid for p in procs]
            if len(set(ids)) != len(ids):
                raise ValueError('duplicate process label')
            for p in procs:
                if p.tgt not in I or any(j not in I for j in p.R) or p.car not in ('B', 'out'):
                    raise ValueError('process target or read positions')
                if p.law not in ('const', 'lin', 'bilin') or (p.law == 'lin' and not p.R) or \
                   (p.law == 'bilin' and len(p.R) != 2) or (p.law == 'const' and p.R):
                    raise ValueError('law and read positions')
                if p.cert_read not in (1, 0, None) or p.cert_path not in (1, 0, None):
                    raise ValueError('certification value')
        if len(s0) != len(I):
            raise ValueError('initial-state length')
        self.I, self.eps, self.letters = I, rat(eps, True), letters
        self.s0 = tuple(rat(x, True) for x in s0)
    def idx(self, i):
        return self.I.index(i)
    def step(self, s, u):
        """One beat (4.2 and 5.1 of the Mathematical Form): returns (post-state, set of newly formed positions, set of newly marked positions, process event table); returns None when the step leaves the domain."""
        eps = self.eps
        pulses, procs = self.letters[u]
        d = {i: Fr(0) for i in self.I}
        sd = dict(zip(self.I, s))
        formed, marked, events = set(), set(), []
        for (tgt, a, ext) in pulses:
            a = Fr(a)
            if a > eps:                      # effective pulse: always counted in Form (D16; as in upstream field_final.py lines 123–126), whatever the pre-beat depth
                formed.add(tgt)
                if ext == 'out':
                    marked.add(tgt)
                d[tgt] += a
        for p in procs:
            c = p.value(sd)
            act = 1 if abs(c) > eps else 0
            events.append((p, c, act))
            if act:
                if c > eps and sd[p.tgt] <= eps:   # an effective positive component makes an empty position a trace-retaining element (D16; upstream line 162)
                    formed.add(p.tgt)
                if p.car == 'out':
                    marked.add(p.tgt)        # an effective change by a mechanism with an out-of-boundary carrier enters Mark
                d[p.tgt] += c
        s2 = tuple(sd[i] + d[i] for i in self.I)
        if any(x < 0 for x in s2):

def window_eval(M, s_start, F_rec, M_rec, word):
    """Window reading: entry records F_rec, M_rec (position sets), W = word. Returns a dict with Q for every nonempty T, Fld and the in-boundary self-feedback type."""
    eps = M.eps
    s = s_start
    Form = set(F_rec)           # formation records “earlier than t” (the entry-beat records precede every beat in W)
    Mark = set(M_rec)
    ev_log = []                 # (full avail_cond, full avail_act, p, act); the part relevant to each T is extracted afterwards
    for u in word:
        r = M.step(s, u)
        if r is None:
            return None
        s2, formed, marked, events = r
        sd = dict(zip(M.I, s))
        for (p, c, act) in events:
            ac = {i for i in p.supp_cond(sd, eps) if sd[i] > eps and i in Form}
            aa = {i for i in p.supp_act(sd) if sd[i] > eps and i in Form}
            ev_log.append((ac, aa, p, act))
        Form |= formed
        Mark |= marked
        s = s2
    end = dict(zip(M.I, s))
    out = {}
    fld, selfref = 0, 0
    for n in range(1, len(M.I) + 1):
        for T in combinations(M.I, n):
            T = set(T)
            src = 1 if any(i in Mark for i in T) else 0
            ret = 1 if all(end[i] > eps and i in Form for i in T) else 0
            trace = k_and(src, ret, 1, 1)   # Ident and HistEv declared as 1
            part = k_or(*[k_and(1 if (ac & T) else 0, p.cert_read)
                          for (ac, aa, p, act) in ev_log if p.car == 'B']) if ev_log else 0
            hd_terms, sr_terms = [], []
            for (ac, aa, p, act) in ev_log:
                hc = k_and(1 if (aa & T) else 0, p.cert_read, p.cert_path if (aa & T) else 0)
                hd_terms.append(k_and(1 if p.tgt in T else 0, act, hc))
                if p.car == 'B':
                    sr_terms.append(k_and(1 if p.tgt in T else 0, act, hc))
            hdyn = k_or(*hd_terms) if hd_terms else 0
            q = k_and(trace, part, hdyn)
            out[frozenset(T)] = q
            fld = k_or(fld, q)
            sr = k_and(q, k_or(*sr_terms) if sr_terms else 0)
            selfref = k_or(selfref, sr)
    return {'Q': out, 'Fld': fld, 'self': selfref, 'end': s}

def run_prefix(M, h):
    """Formative history: apply h from s0 and return (final state, Form position set, Mark position set)."""
    s, F, Mk = M.s0, set(), set()
    for u in h:
        r = M.step(s, u)
        if r is None:
            return None
        s, fo, ma, _ = r
        F |= fo
        Mk |= ma
    return s, frozenset(F), frozenset(Mk)

def fld_of_history(M, h, w):
    """Window-reading qualification of the input history h (W = last w beats, w ≥ 1)."""
    if w < 1:
        raise ValueError('w ≥ 1')
    if len(h) < w:
        return None
    pre = run_prefix(M, h[:len(h) - w])
    if pre is None:
        return None
    s, F, Mk = pre
    return window_eval(M, s, F, Mk, h[len(h) - w:])

def physical_reach(M, letters=None):
    letters = letters or list(M.letters)
    seen, dq = {M.s0}, deque([M.s0])
    while dq:
        s = dq.popleft()
        for u in letters:
            r = M.step(s, u)
            if r and r[0] not in seen:
                seen.add(r[0])
                dq.append(r[0])
    return seen

def augmented_search(M, w, target='Fld', letters=None):
    """Augmented search: nodes (s, Form, Mark); for each node, enumerate the window words of length w. Returns the shortest satisfying history and node statistics."""
    letters = letters or list(M.letters)
    start = (M.s0, frozenset(), frozenset())
    dist, par = {start: 0}, {start: None}
    dq = deque([start])
    while dq:
        node = dq.popleft()
        s, F, Mk = node
        for u in letters:
            r = M.step(s, u)
            if r is None:
                continue
            s2, fo, ma, _ = r
            nxt = (s2, F | fo, Mk | ma)
            if nxt not in dist:
                dist[nxt] = dist[node] + 1
                par[nxt] = (node, u)
                dq.append(nxt)
    best = None
    for node in sorted(dist, key=lambda n: dist[n]):
        if best is not None and dist[node] + w >= best[0]:
            break
        s, F, Mk = node
        for word in product(letters, repeat=w):
            e = window_eval(M, s, F, Mk, word)
            if e is not None and e[target] == 1:
                path, n = [], node
                while par[n] is not None:
                    n, u = par[n]
                    path.append(u)
                h = tuple(reversed(path)) + word
                if best is None or len(h) < best[0]:
                    best = (len(h), h)
                break
    N = len(physical_reach(M, letters))
    return {'best': best, 'nodes': len(dist), 'N': N,
            'max_prefix_dist': max(dist.values()), 'bound_prefix': (2 * len(M.I) + 1) * N - 1}

def brute_min(M, w, Lmax, target='Fld', letters=None):
    letters = letters or list(M.letters)
    for L in range(w, Lmax + 1):
        for h in product(letters, repeat=L):
            e = fld_of_history(M, h, w)
            if e is not None and e[target] == 1:
                return L, h
    return None
~~~
