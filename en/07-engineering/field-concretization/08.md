# The Dynamics-Source Form of Field Concretization

1\. Declaration of Form Kind  
This paper is a Source Form (an auxiliary form of the second, source-and-transformation group). Its sole task: in the alphabet-gated F model under the window reading, with the model, the mechanism, the actual history, the evaluation point and the target action frozen, to take the action event that bears historical dynamics on this occasion and characterize which events in the actual history activate and contribute the part of it that “issues from the Trace-Responses” (the availability of the positions read, the exact nonzeroness of the bilinear partner, the effectiveness of the action); how these events stand to one another under replay interventions as sufficient, necessary and substitute; and, where the source is not unique, how far, at most, the theory can determine it.  
Scope of responsibility: the present action of historical dynamics. The objects whose sources are traced include neither the first formation of qualification as a whole nor the generation of the dynamics laws, the couplings or the process table themselves.  
The conclusions of this paper fall into five groups.  
　1\. Exclusion of the default source: the target action depends on the past only through the pre-beat state and the pre-beat formation record (Theorem 1); constant laws and zero-coefficient processes do not bear historical dynamics (Corollary 1); whether the process table alone determines the present historical dynamics depends on whether Φ\_e is constant on the declared family of histories, which in general fails, and each of the three channels has a witness (Corollary 2); formation records are in general not functions of the state (Proposition 1).  
　2\. Generative process: the actual ancestor graph is finite, backtracking stops at three kinds of roots, and the number of nodes has an explicit upper bound (Theorem 2).  
　3\. Source theorems: in the nonnegative family, formation records are determined position by position by the state and the effective pulses (Theorem 3); in the nonnegative family, all three target layers are monotone in the retained events, necessary events are decided exactly by single-deletion tests, every minimal sufficient set is contained in the actual ancestors, and when the actual ancestors are disjoint from the frozen background, deleting all ancestors in the candidate domain makes the target fail (Theorem 4); several failure phenomena outside the family (Proposition 2); the bilinear partner need only be exactly nonzero, need not be formed or suprathreshold, and may come from the initial state or be formed by the history (Theorem 5); the signal condition of qualification and the source of the action are kept in separate accounts (Proposition 3).  
　4\. Where the source cannot be uniquely determined: seven classes (overdetermination, substitute formation with preemption, joint sufficiency, the out-of-domain comparison gap, legal but undetermined, separate accounts across layers, redundant ancestors) and the sets that can be determined in each class (Proposition 4).  
　5\. Instances: the cap-free F variant of χ₀ and the bridge domain of the original χ₀ (Proposition 5); Instance A of the Adjudication Form (Proposition 6).  
Strength position:  
　The “source” issued here is the counterfactual retention-set structure and the actual-ancestor constraint under a frozen background: sufficiency, necessity, ancestor containment. It is not an apportionment of actual formation responsibility, nor the first existence or first reachability of the object.  
　All conclusions lie within the formal model and constitute no source certification of any material host.  
Theoretical increment: the upstream papers have proved the constitution, adjudication, reachability, realization and maintenance of Sedimentation-Field qualification; all of them take historical dynamics as a reading, and none traces which actual events produced it. The increment of this paper falls on three structures specific to Sedimentation-Fields:  
　substitute formation under replay, brought about by formation registration;  
　the mixture of exact nonzeroness and threshold effectiveness;  
　leaving the domain, and non-monotonicity, caused by negative changes.  
In the nonnegative family, leaving the domain and non-monotonicity are excluded; preemption through formation registration still occurs but is confined within the actual ancestors (Proposition 4(1), Theorem 4(v)), and the source structure can be characterized exactly. Outside the family, only finite tables of legal masks can be given. The proofs are elementary (monotonicity, induction, closure).

### 2.2  Model and Evaluation

The model class of this paper is 𝔐\_GF, the reading is RD, and the evaluator is reach\_core.py (Appendix A.7, verbatim identical to Appendix A.1 of the Reachability Form).  
　Model: a finite position set I, a resolution ε ≥ 0, an alphabet (containing rest), and a nonnegative rational initial state s₀. Each letter gives the pulse table and the process table of the current beat.  
　Effective change: a pulse amplitude a \> ε, or a process component \|c\| \> ε; only effective changes are counted into the state.  
　Formation registration: an effective pulse registers formation; an effective positive component acting on a position with pre-beat s ≤ ε also registers formation.  
　Mark registration: out-of-boundary pulses and effective processes with out-of-boundary carriers register marks.  
　Leaving the domain: a beat leaves the domain of definition when its post-state has a negative component.

### 2.3  Entry Conditions

　(1) The structure is already constituted: on the actual history, the target action event already bears HDyn of the T in question (O₁ = 1 in Definition 1).  
　(2) The source is taken by default as given: in the F-layer model, the numerical law, read positions and run conditions of an acting process are given by the process table. The default reading is “historical dynamics is the process in the process table that reads T”, that is, the frozen process table alone suffices to determine the present HDyn. Section 4 excludes or restricts this default.  
　(3) The source protocol is frozen in advance (Definition 3).

3\. Declaration of Objects  
Definition 1 (target and evaluation point). Freeze M ∈ 𝔐\_GF, a window length w ≥ 1 and an actual history h; the set of evaluation points is E(h) = {t : w ≤ t ≤ \|h\|}, and t\* ∈ E(h) is chosen; the bearing set is ∅ ≠ T ⊆ I; the target event e is the process instance p of beat b (b is a beat index in h with t\* − w \< b ≤ t*, i.e. the beat belongs to the window of t*). Write the pre-beat state s = s\_{b−1} and the pre-beat formation record Fo = Form\_{b−1}. Three target layers:  
　O₁ (main line, fixed action event): HD\_e(T) = τ ∧ η ∧ H.  
　　τ = \[the action target of p ∈ T\];  
　　η = \[\|c\_p(s)\| \> ε\];  
　　H = α ∧ κ, α = \[∃i ∈ Supp\_act,p(s) ∩ T : s\_i \> ε and i ∈ Fo\], κ = cert\_read ∧ cert\_path; when α is 0, H = 0;  
　　conjunctions follow strong Kleene.  
　This is how \[tgt ∈ T\] ∧ Act\_F ∧ HistCond\_F of D18 and D19 is written in 𝔐\_GF: in 𝔐\_GF cert\_eff = 1, so Act\_F = η; the evidence path is the direct edge from the read positions to the process, and its path status is cert\_path; α is Avail\_act ≠ ∅ (D17).  
　O₂ (parallel): HDyn(T) at the same t\*, i.e. the disjunction of the HD terms of the process events in the window.  
　O₃ (parallel): Fld\_RD at the same t\*.  
When the target action disappears and another action takes over, O₁ is judged not preserved, while O₂ and O₃ may be judged preserved; the three are kept in separate accounts and do not stand in for one another. Switching under replay to a later evaluation point or to another bearing set does not belong to this protocol.  
Definition 2 (three-valued firstness). Genesis(h, t) = Fld\_RD(h≤t) ∧ ⋀\_{t′∈E(h), t′\<t} ¬Fld\_RD(h≤t′) (strong Kleene).  
　Currently 1 and all earlier evaluation points 0: certified first holding;  
　currently 0, or some earlier 1: Genesis = 0;  
　the remaining cases (currently 1 with an earlier ⊥ and no earlier 1; currently ⊥ with no earlier 1): firstness undetermined.  
Firstness serves only as a qualifier: when Genesis = 1, a source conclusion may carry the annotation “case of first formation”; otherwise no firstness is issued. The first positive reading is not called a first holding.  
Definition 3 (source protocol Π\_src).  
　(1) Frozen items: M (including the alphabet, the process table, the numerical laws, the coefficients and the certification atoms), w, h, t\*, and the target layer with its parameters (T, b, p).  
　(2) Individuation of events: an event is either “the k-th pulse instance of beat t” (t, pulse, k) or “the process instance pid of beat t” (t, proc, pid). Instances that are ineffective in the actual history are also events.  
　(3) Intervention: delete specified individual events of a beat, i.e. use at that beat a letter that lacks only these events; the other events of the same beat and all other beats are unchanged. The name of that letter must be fresh, and no original letter is ever overwritten; process labels and beat labels are unchanged.  
　(4) Recomputation: the replay runs from s₀, and the components, effectiveness, support, formation and marks of the retained events are all recomputed from the replayed pre-state. “Frozen-value” interventions without recomputation do not belong to the intervention class here (U6).  
　(5) Candidate domain and background: a finite candidate event domain C is frozen in advance, containing only events of beat t\* and earlier; events outside C form the background and are retained in every replay; the target event is not in C. All source conclusions are relative to (C, background).  
　(5′) Evaluation scope: all three target layers are read at t\* (O₁ at beat b, t\* − w \< b ≤ t*). The replay runs from s₀ up to beat t*; beats after t\* are not objects of comparison, do not enter the replay, and their legality does not affect the verdict.  
　(6) Three-way division of the legal domain: for a retention set K ⊆ C (deleting C∖K), replay up to t\*:  
　　leaves the domain of definition: recorded as “illegal”, excluded from comparison, taken neither as 0 nor as evidence of a necessary source;  
　　legal with target verdict ⊥: recorded as “legal but undetermined”;  
　　legal with verdict 0 or 1.  
Definition 4 (set semantics). For a target layer O:  
　the family of sufficient retention sets is 𝒮 = {K ⊆ C : the replay is legal and O(K) = 1}; the family of minimal sufficient sets 𝒦\_min consists of the minimal elements of 𝒮;  
　legal-deletion necessary event: 𝒮 is nonempty, x ∈ C, there is a legal replay with x deleted, and every member of 𝒮 contains x; the set of all such events is written N (when 𝒮 is empty, N = ∅; no vacuous necessity is taken);  
　the set of actual ancestor events A: the effective pulse instances and effective mechanism instances in the actual ancestor graph of Section 5.  
The four are defined separately and are in general pairwise unequal. 𝒮, 𝒦\_min and N are counterfactual retention-set semantics under a frozen background, not actual formation responsibility; A is the lineage on the actual history, and is not responsibility either.  
Definition 5 (nonnegative family). 𝔐\_GF⁺: every process in 𝔐\_GF has k ≥ 0 and b ≥ 0 (constant law b ≥ 0; likewise for bilinear laws); the initial state is any nonnegative rational; pulse amplitudes are already nonnegative in 𝔐\_GF. 𝔐\_GF⁺₀: the subfamily of 𝔐\_GF⁺ in which every component of s₀ is ≤ ε.

4\. Exclusion of the Default Source  
Theorem 1 (factorization). With M and the letter of beat b frozen, O₁ is a function Φ\_e of (s\_{b−1}, Form\_{b−1}, T), written exactly as the expression of Definition 1. The past enters only through the pre-beat state and the pre-beat formation record.  
Proof:  
　By D15, D17, D18 and 4.2, each factor of O₁ reads only the process table of beat b, the pre-beat state and the formation records earlier than that beat.  
　The window reading of RD does not change this: the formation records of each beat in the window are the union of the entry records and the formations of earlier beats in the window (Lemma 1 of the Reachability Form).  
　Under replay the same expression holds for the replayed pre-beat state and records. ∎  
Finite verification: in random general models (including negative coefficients, suprathreshold initial states and ⊥ certification), O₁ readings reached at the same (pre-beat state, pre-beat record, letter, process, T) within one model never conflict; O₁ is computed separately from the event expression, and the self-computed Q(T) is also compared item by item with the evaluator's Q(T) (Appendix B.1 (1)). O₁ and Q(T) are not the same verdict (in Proposition 3(A), O₁ = 1 while Fld = 0).  
Readings at internal evaluation points (t\* \< \|h\|) and the replay scope are given in Appendix B.1 (5).  
Corollary 1 (constant-valued laws do not bear). Constant-law instances, and linear and bilinear instances with k = 0, have empty action support, so α ≡ 0 and O₁ ≡ 0: they bear HDyn of no T. A bearer of HDyn must be a linear or bilinear instance with k ≠ 0.  
A constant-law instance can still be an ancestor of a bearer: “does not bear” differs from “is not a source”. Verification in Appendix B.1 (2).  
Corollary 2 (criterion for excluding the default source). Let 𝓗 be a declared family of actual histories that have the same letter at beat b. The process table alone suffices to determine the present HDyn if and only if Φ\_e is constant on {(s\_{b−1}(h), Form\_{b−1}(h)) : h ∈ 𝓗}.  
Proof: immediate from Theorem 1. ∎  
Each of the three channels has a witness not determined by the process table (Appendix B.1 (3-a)–(3-c)):  
　availability (record) channel: ε = 1/10, s₀ = 1/2 (suprathreshold, empty record), use reads x: O₁ = 0;  
　action-support channel: on sx·sy·bl and sx·rest·bl, the O₁ of bl for T = {x} is 1 and 0 respectively;  
　effectiveness channel: at the 12th and 13th a beats of Instance A, the O₁ of opt is 1 and 0; at the 13th, w is already formed and suprathreshold, but the component is ≤ ε.  
So the default reading fails in general on 𝔐\_GF. The exclusion goes only this far: the alphabet, the numerical laws, the coefficients and the certification atoms remain given mechanisms throughout, and none of the given conditions is thereby excluded.  
Proposition 1 (the record channel is independent of the state). In 𝔐\_GF, formation records are in general not functions of the state.  
Witness (Appendix B.2 (2-iii′)): s₀ = 1 \> ε. The histories rest·use and sx·drain·use have the same state x = 1 before the use beat, with formation records ∅ and {x}, and O₁ equal to 0 and 1.  
So the “historical part” cannot be reduced to the current state: formation records carry history beyond the state. This witness uses both a suprathreshold initial state and a negative bias, and is not used to isolate either antecedent.

5\. Characterizing the Generative Process: The Actual Ancestor Graph  
Definition 6 (actual ancestor graph). For an actual history h and a target event e:  
　Numeric node (i, t): the value of position i before beat t (1 ≤ t ≤ \|h\|). Its inputs are the initial-state boundary node E0(i) and all effective events targeting i before beat t.  
　Initial-state boundary node E0(i): set for every component of s₀, zero values included. It serves as a truncation boundary and is not said to explain its own source.  
　Event nodes:  
　　an effective pulse instance has no inputs;  
　　an effective mechanism instance has as inputs the numeric nodes of its read positions before that beat (state-dependent inputs) and, when b ≠ 0, one labeled bias input node offset(t, pid). A constant-law instance has only the bias input.  
　The bias input is only a lineage representation; it changes neither the identity of the process nor the granularity of deletion (deletion is still by whole process instance). The numerical value of b is a mechanism parameter; offset(t, pid) records the actual execution of that instance in this beat.  
　Backtracking starts from (i, b) (i ∈ R\_e).  
　Roots fall into two classes:  
　　contributing roots: nonzero E0 nodes, effective pulse instances, bias inputs;  
　　zero-value boundaries: E0 nodes that read a zero value; they record only syntactic dependence and certify no formation responsibility.  
　Event sources, numerical inputs, certification atoms and mechanism parameters are recorded in four separate ledgers.  
Theorem 2 (finiteness of the ancestor graph, root coverage and upper bound on the node count). For a finite actual history, the actual ancestor graph is finite, and every backtracking path stops at one of three kinds of roots: initial-state boundary nodes, effective pulse instances, bias inputs. The total number of nodes is ≤ \|I\|·(\|h\| + 1) + E + B ≤ \|I\|·(\|h\| + 1) + 2E, where E is the number of event instances in h and B the number of process instances in h with b ≠ 0.  
Proof:  
　The inputs of a numeric node stop at E0 nodes and at effective events targeting that position. Pulses have no inputs. The inputs of a mechanism instance stop at numeric nodes of earlier times and at bias inputs.  
　By induction on t, every backtracking path moves strictly back in time and stops at an E0 node, a pulse or a bias input. Hence a nonempty graph must have roots.  
　Counting: numeric nodes ≤ \|I\|·\|h\|; initial-state boundary nodes ≤ \|I\|; event nodes ≤ E; bias input nodes ≤ B (at most one per instance), and B ≤ E. ∎  
Finite verification in Appendix B.1 (4), (4′). (4′) is an example with 12 constant-law ancestors: 26 nodes, against a bound of 28.  
Root coverage does not mean that the source is closed; a contributing root is not a synonym for certified formation responsibility.

6\. Source Theorems  
Theorem 3 (formation records in the nonnegative family). In 𝔐\_GF⁺, at every time of every legitimate history, the following holds position by position:  
　(A) for a position with s₀,i ≤ ε: i ∈ Form ⇔ s\_i \> ε;  
　(B) for a position with s₀,i \> ε: i ∈ Form ⇔ an effective pulse targeting i has occurred before.  
Global form (𝔐\_GF⁺₀): i ∈ Form ⇔ s\_i \> ε.  
Proof:  
　In 𝔐\_GF⁺ every effective change is nonnegative, and the state is nondecreasing and always nonnegative. Formation is registered by only two routes: an effective pulse; an effective positive component acting on a position with pre-beat s\_i ≤ ε.  
　(A ⇒) After either route s\_i \> ε, and the state does not decrease, so s\_i \> ε ever after.  
　(A ⇐) If s\_i \> ε at some time, take the first effective change of i: when it is applied, the pre-beat s\_i = s₀,i ≤ ε. If it is a pulse, it registers formation; if it is a process component, c \> ε acts on s\_i ≤ ε and also registers formation.  
　(B) s\_i ≥ s₀,i \> ε always holds, so the process route is never available; hence formation is registered exactly by effective pulses. ∎  
Corollary: in 𝔐\_GF⁺ formation records are determined by the state and the history of effective pulses; in 𝔐\_GF⁺₀ they are determined by the state alone, and the record channel collapses into the state.  
Boundary (Appendix B.2 (2-iii)): with ε = 0, s₀ = 1 and only rest, s \> ε while Form = ∅; the global form fails, and the position-wise form (B) holds.  
Finite verification in Appendix B.2 (1) b.  
Theorem 4 (monotone source structure of the nonnegative family). Let M ∈ 𝔐\_GF⁺, freeze (h, w, t\*, e, T, C, background) per Definition 3, and suppose that actually O₁ = 1. Then:  
　(i) every replay is legal;  
　(ii) K ⊆ K′ ⇒ s^K ≤ s^{K′} beat by beat (componentwise order), and Form^K ⊆ Form^{K′};  
　(iii) O₁ is monotone in K (truth order 0 \< ⊥ \< 1); 𝒮 is upward closed;  
　(iii′) O₂ and O₃ are also monotone in K;  
　(iv) N = {x ∈ C : O₁(C∖{x}) ≠ 1} = ∩𝒦\_min: beyond the actual history, only \|C\| single-deletion replays are needed. When O₂ or O₃ is actually 1, its necessary events are likewise decided exactly by single-deletion tests;  
　(v) non-ancestor candidates are irrelevant: for every K and every X ⊆ C∖A, O₁(K) = O₁(K ∪ X). Hence every member of 𝒦\_min is contained in A;  
　(vi) if A is disjoint from the background, then O₁ = 0 once all candidates are deleted: the actual ancestors in the candidate domain are jointly necessary. When A meets the background, this clause is not issued (ancestors in the background are retained in every replay).  
Proof:  
　(i) Deletion only removes events; the edited model still belongs to 𝔐\_GF⁺, and the state is always nonnegative.  
　(ii) Induction on the number of beats. At beat t, every event occurring in the K-replay also occurs in the K′-replay.  
　　Events occurring on both sides: pulse increments are equal; the process component b + kΣs\_j or b + k·s\_j·s\_l is nondecreasing in s on nonnegative states; the increment c·\[c \> ε\] is nondecreasing in c. Events occurring only in K′ have nonnegative increments. Hence s^K\_t ≤ s^{K′}\_t.  
　　Inclusion of the formation records follows position by position from Theorem 3: class-(A) positions are monotone through the state; class-(B) positions are registered only by effective pulses, and the pulses of the K-replay all occur in the K′-replay.  
　(iii) τ and κ are constants. η = \[c\_e(s) \> ε\] is monotone. α ⇔ ∃i ∈ Supp\_act(s) ∩ T : s\_i \> ε and i ∈ Fo, and both conditions are monotone in K. For the linear law, Supp\_act is always R when k ≠ 0; for the bilinear law, j₁ ∈ Supp\_act ⇔ s\_{j₂} ≠ 0 ⇔ s\_{j₂} \> 0, which is monotone. Strong Kleene conjunction is monotone.  
　(iii′) O₂ is the disjunction of the HD terms of the events in the window; the events that occur increase as K grows, and each term is monotone by (iii). O₃ = ⋁\_T (Src ∧ Ret ∧ Part ∧ HDyn), monotone term by term:  
　　Src: marks are registered by effective out-of-boundary pulses and effective processes with out-of-boundary carriers, and effectiveness is monotone;  
　　Ret: s\_i \> ε at the end of the window and already formed, monotone by (ii);  
　　Part: in 𝔐\_GF⁺, Supp\_cond of in-boundary processes is empty for the constant law, constant for the linear law (R when k ≠ 0; when k = 0, R if \|b\| ≤ ε and empty otherwise), and equal to Supp\_act for the bilinear law; availability is monotone;  
　　HDyn: as for O₂.  
　(iv) 𝒮 is upward closed and C ∈ 𝒮. If C∖{x} ∈ 𝒮, it is a sufficient set not containing x; conversely, if some K ∈ 𝒮 does not contain x, then K ⊆ C∖{x}, so C∖{x} ∈ 𝒮. Replays deleting x are all legal.  
　(v) Induction on t over ancestor nodes (i, t): s^K\_i(t) = s₀,i + the sum of the replay increments targeting i before beat t.  
　　The events targeting i that are actually effective all lie in A (ancestor closure). Actually ineffective events: a pulse has a ≤ ε and its increment is always 0; a process has c^K ≤ c^C ≤ ε in the replay (by (ii), s^K ≤ s^C = actual) and remains ineffective.  
　　The component of an ancestor event reads only the values of its read positions at earlier times, and these are also ancestor nodes. So the replay values of ancestor nodes depend only on K ∩ A.  
　　Formation at ancestor nodes: for class-(A) positions it is determined by the replay value; for class-(B) positions, by the earlier effective pulses targeting i, all of which lie in A.  
　　O₁ reads only the values and formation at (i, b) (i ∈ R\_e), which are all ancestor nodes.  
　　If K is minimal sufficient, then O₁(K ∩ A) = O₁(K) = 1, and by minimality K = K ∩ A.  
　(vi) By (v), once all candidates are deleted, the positions of R\_e keep only their initial state, with no ancestor events: class-(A) positions have value s₀,i ≤ ε; class-(B) positions have no effective pulses and are not formed. Hence α = 0. ∎  
Finite verification in Appendix B.2 (1): on random instances of 𝔐\_GF⁺, including suprathreshold initial states and nonempty backgrounds, (i)–(vi) and the monotonicity and single-deletion results for O₂ and O₃ all hold.  
The containment in (v) is in general strict: in seed·grow·use, grow is booked as effective and is an actual ancestor, but enters no minimal sufficient set (Appendix B.4 (7)).  
Proposition 2 (several failure phenomena outside the family). The antecedents of Theorem 4 serve only as sufficient conditions; they are not claimed to be necessary one by one. Each of the following witnesses removes only one antecedent, keeps the other antecedents and the actual O₁ = 1, and breaches one clause of Theorem 4 (Appendix B.2 (2-i), (2-ii), (2-iv)):  
　(a) Removing only k ≥ 0, substitute formation: seed·alt·use, alt = 1 − y (b = 1). 𝒦\_min = {{seed}, {alt}}, yet alt is ineffective in the actual history (actual increment 0) and not in A. (v) fails.  
　(b) Removing only b ≥ 0, leaving the domain: seed·seed·drain·use, where drain is a constant law with b = −1. The replay with everything deleted leaves the domain and is excluded from comparison. (i) fails.  
　(c) Removing only k ≥ 0, inhibition: sy·sy·sz·inh·use, inh = −z. Keeping {beat 1} gives O₁ = 1; keeping {beat 1, beat 3} gives O₁ = 0; keeping everything gives O₁ = 1. (iii) fails.  
Upstream instances also contain non-monotonicity witnesses internally: Instance A with n = 13 (Proposition 6(iii)).  
Initial state: Theorem 4 does not need s₀ ≤ ε; that antecedent affects only the global form of Theorem 3.  
These are known phenomena and not necessarily exhaustive. In the general 𝔐\_GF, “withdrawing all actual ancestors makes the target fail” is not issued; source conclusions outside the family are given only as finite tables of legal masks (Section 7).  
Theorem 5 (the exact nonzero channel of the partner). Let e be a bilinear instance with R = (j₁, j₂), j₁ ≠ j₂, j₁ ∈ T, j₂ ∉ T. Then  
　α ⇔ k ≠ 0 and s\_{j₁} \> ε and j₁ ∈ Fo and s\_{j₂} ≠ 0.  
When k = 0 the action support is empty and α = 0 (consistent with Corollary 1).  
Proof: by D15, the bilinear action support is first restricted by k ≠ 0; when k ≠ 0, j₁ ∈ Supp\_act ⇔ s\_{j₂} ≠ 0. Substituting into the definition of α gives the result. ∎  
The partner condition requires only exact nonzeroness: it requires neither formation, nor being suprathreshold, nor membership in T. So the partner's contribution to the present action is recorded in the numerical-input ledger and is not conditioned on Trace-Responses: it may come solely from the initial-state boundary with no historical event at all, or it may be formed by the history (as in sx·sy·bl, where the partner y is already formed and suprathreshold). This holds only for the current T; it does not assert that j₂ cannot be a Trace-Response in some other bearing set.  
A further state fact (independent of the antecedent on k): in 𝔐\_GF⁺ with s₀,j₂ = 0, the partner is nonzero ⇔ the partner is formed. Reason: effective increments are all \> ε, so s \> 0 ⇔ s \> ε, and Theorem 3(A) then applies. In this case the partner must have been produced by the history.

The boundary grid and the random verification are in Appendix B.3 (1′), (2).  
This is historical activation of the partner position under the same given bilinear law, not generation of the dynamics law, the coupling or the process table by sedimentation.  
Proposition 3 (separation of the signal condition of qualification from the source of the action). (Appendix B.3 (3))  
　(A) A bearing action can hold with no signal at all: on bias·use, (O₁, O₂, Fld) = (1, 1, 0).  
　(B) When qualification holds, the signal need not lie among the ancestors of the bearing action either: on sig·bias·use, Fld = 1, the only ancestor event of the bearing action is bias, and sig enters qualification only through Src.

Strength: this is a separation of objects and accounts, not a proof of logical independence between two universal propositions.

Proposition 4 (classification of multiple solutions and degree of determinability). On a finite history and a finite candidate domain, 2^\|C\| replays give 𝒮, 𝒦\_min, N and the illegal retention sets exactly. This is a computation, not a new universal theorem. """The Dynamics-Source Form of Field Concretization: reference implementation of the source protocol Π_src (standard library only; exact rational arithmetic; strong Kleene three-valued logic).
Calls only reach_core (Appendix A.1 of The Reachability Form of Field Concretization) and does not modify the upstream rules.
  edited     within-beat individual deletion (pulse instances or process instances); new letter names are genuinely fresh; retention outside the mask is checked beat by beat;
  evaluate   (O1, O2, Fld) at the evaluation point t*: O1 is the HDyn term of the fixed action event; O2 is the HDyn of the fixed T; Fld is the existential layer;

  genesis    three-valued firstness (frozen set of evaluation points E(h) = {w, …, |h|});

  ancestors  actual ancestors (contributing roots / zero-value boundaries / intermediate events); ancestor_events folds them into event identifiers;
  ancestor_nodes  all nodes of the ancestor graph (numeric nodes, initial-state boundary nodes, event nodes, bias-input nodes), for checking the upper bound on the node count.

from fractions import Fraction as F
from hashlib import sha256

def P(pid, tgt, reads, k, b=0, car='B', cert=1, law='lin'):
    return Proc(pid, tgt, car, law, R=reads, k=F(k), b=F(b), cert_read=cert)

# ---------- Intervention: within-beat individual deletion ----------
def edited(M, h, deleted):
    """deleted: a set of events {(beat, 'pulse', k) | (beat, 'proc', pid)}, with beats counted from 1.
    Returns (M', h'): each beat holding deleted events gets a separate letter that lacks only those events; the other events of that beat are kept.
    Each new letter name is checked to lie outside the original alphabet and the names already assigned (genuinely fresh); no original letter is ever overwritten;
    process labels and beat indices are unchanged. Before returning, checks beat by beat that every event outside the mask is kept and every event inside the mask is absent."""
    letters = dict(M.letters)
    h2 = []
    for t, u in enumerate(h, 1):
        dp = {k for (b, kind, k) in deleted if b == t and kind == 'pulse'}
        dq = {k for (b, kind, k) in deleted if b == t and kind == 'proc'}
        if not dp and not dq:
            h2.append(u)
            continue
        pulses, procs = M.letters[u]
        n = 0
        while ('del', t, n) in letters:
            n += 1
        nu = ('del', t, n)                  # a non-string tuple name, also checked with in, so it can never clash with an existing letter
        letters[nu] = ([x for k, x in enumerate(pulses) if k not in dp],
                       [p for p in procs if p.pid not in dq])
        h2.append(nu)
    M2, h2 = GF(M.I, M.eps, letters, M.s0), tuple(h2)
    for t, (u, u2) in enumerate(zip(h, h2), 1):   # check that events outside the mask are kept
        (p1, q1), (p2, q2) = M.letters[u], M2.letters[u2]
        keep_p = [x for k, x in enumerate(p1) if (t, 'pulse', k) not in deleted]
        keep_q = [p for p in q1 if (t, 'proc', p.pid) not in deleted]
        assert p2 == keep_p and q2 == keep_q, ('event outside the mask not kept', t)
    return M2, h2

def events_of(M, h):
    out = []
    for t, u in enumerate(h, 1):
        pulses, procs = M.letters[u]
        out += [(t, 'pulse', k) for k in range(len(pulses))]
        out += [(t, 'proc', p.pid) for p in procs]
    return out

# ---------- Evaluation: event-level HDyn terms, cross-checked against Q ----------
def trace_run(M, h):
    """Returns, beat by beat, (pre-beat state dict, pre-beat Form, events), plus the final-state records; returns None on leaving the domain."""
    s, Form, Mark, rows = M.s0, set(), set(), []
    for u in h:
        r = M.step(s, u)
        if r is None:
            return None
        s2, fo, ma, ev = r
        rows.append((dict(zip(M.I, s)), set(Form), ev))
        Form |= fo
        Mark |= ma
        s = s2
    return rows, dict(zip(M.I, s)), Form, Mark

def hd_term(M, row, p, act, T):
    sd, Form, _ = row
    aa = {i for i in p.supp_act(sd) if sd[i] > M.eps and i in Form}
    hc = k_and(1 if aa & T else 0, p.cert_read, p.cert_path if aa & T else 0)
    return k_and(1 if p.tgt in T else 0, act, hc)

def evaluate(M, h, w, T, beat, pid, tstar=None):
    """Returns (O1, O2, Fld) at the evaluation point t* (default |h|); returns ILLEGAL if the replay leaves the domain.
    O1: the HDyn term of process pid at beat `beat` (recorded as 0 if that process instance is absent); requires t* − w < beat ≤ t*.
    The replay runs only up to t*: beats after t* are not compared and do not enter the replay, and their legality does not affect the verdict (Definition 3)."""
    tstar = len(h) if tstar is None else tstar
    assert w <= tstar <= len(h) and tstar - w < beat <= tstar, ('evaluation point or target beat out of range', tstar, beat)
    h = tuple(h[:tstar])
    T = set(T)
    tr = trace_run(M, h)
    if tr is None:
        return ILLEGAL
    rows, end, Form, Mark = tr
    n = len(h)
    win = range(n - w, n)
    o1 = 0
    for (p, c, act) in rows[beat - 1][2]:
        if p.pid == pid:
            o1 = hd_term(M, rows[beat - 1], p, act, T)
    o2 = k_or(*[hd_term(M, rows[t], p, act, T) for t in win for (p, c, act) in rows[t][2]] or [0])
    # cross-check: the self-computed Q(T) = Trace ∧ Part ∧ HDyn agrees with reach_core
    src = 1 if T & Mark else 0
    ret = 1 if all(end[i] > M.eps and i in Form for i in T) else 0
    part = k_or(*[k_and(1 if ({i for i in p.supp_cond(rows[t][0], M.eps)
                               if rows[t][0][i] > M.eps and i in rows[t][1]} & T) else 0, p.cert_read)
                  for t in win for (p, c, act) in rows[t][2] if p.car == 'B'] or [0])
    q = k_and(src, ret, part, o2)
    r = fld_of_history(M, h, w)
    assert r is not None and r['Q'][frozenset(T)] == q, ('cross-check failed', h, T)
    return o1, o2, r['Fld']

# ---------- G: three-valued firstness ----------
def k_not(x):
    return None if x is None else 1 - x

def genesis(M, h, w):
    reads = [fld_of_history(M, h[:t], w)['Fld'] for t in range(w, len(h) + 1)]
    g = [k_and(reads[j], *[k_not(x) for x in reads[:j]]) if j else reads[0] for j in range(len(reads))]
    return reads, g

def ancestors(M, h, beat, reads):
    """Actual ancestors (a lineage representation only, not responsibility).
    Nodes: the value (i, t) of position i before beat t; the effective events of beat t (pulse instances, mechanism instances).
    Inputs of (i, t) = the initial-state boundary node E0(i) (listed even when zero) + all effective events before beat t that act on i.
    Inputs of a mechanism instance = the pre-beat values of its read positions (state-dependent inputs) + the bias input ('offset', t, pid) (when b ≠ 0; a constant law has only the bias input).
    The bias input is only a labeled input in the lineage representation; it changes neither the process identity nor the deletion granularity (deletion is still by whole process instance).
    Returns (contributing roots, zero-value boundaries, intermediate events):
      contributing roots = nonzero initial-state boundaries, effective pulse instances, nonzero bias inputs of executed instances;
      zero-value boundaries = initial-state boundary nodes that read zero; they record only syntactic dependence and certify no formation responsibility.
    By construction every backtracking path from (i, t) ends at an E0 node, a pulse or a bias input, so whenever the intermediate events are nonempty the roots (both kinds together) are nonempty."""
    tr = trace_run(M, h)
    rows = tr[0]
    seen, roots, zeros, mids = set(), set(), set(), set()
    def pos(i, t):                       # source of the value of position i before beat t (pre-beat)
        if (i, t) in seen:
            return
        seen.add((i, t))
        if M.s0[M.idx(i)] != 0:
            roots.add(('E0', i))
        else:
            zeros.add(('E0', i, 0))
        for t2 in range(1, t):
            pulses, _ = M.letters[h[t2 - 1]]
            for k, (tgt, a, ext) in enumerate(pulses):
                if tgt == i and F(a) > M.eps:
                    roots.add(('pulse-' + ext, t2, k))
            for (p, c, act) in rows[t2 - 1][2]:
                if p.tgt == i and act:
                    mids.add(('mech-' + ('ext' if p.car == 'out' else 'in') + '/' + p.law, t2, p.pid))
                    if p.b != 0:
                        roots.add(('offset', t2, p.pid))
                    for j in p.R:
                        pos(j, t2)
    for i in reads:
        pos(i, beat)
    assert not mids or roots or zeros, 'nonempty graph without roots'
    return sorted(roots, key=str), sorted(zeros, key=str), sorted(mids, key=str)



def ancestor_events(M, h, beat, reads):
    """The set of event identifiers among the actual ancestors: pulses (t,'pulse',k); mechanism instances (t,'proc',pid) (a bias input is assigned to its instance)."""
    roots, zeros, mids = ancestors(M, h, beat, reads)

"""The Reachability Form of Field Concretization: alphabet-gated F model, window reading, augmented reachability search and brute-force comparison (exact rational arithmetic, strong Kleene three-valued logic)
Model class 𝔐_GF (alphabet-gated F models): a finite position set I and a resolution ε; an alphabet U (containing the rest letter 'rest');
each letter u gives the pulse table [(tgt, a_p, ext)] and the process table [Proc] of its beat; processes follow the numeric laws of the three classes of D12 of The Mathematical Form of Field Concretization
and read the pre-beat state; cert_eff = 1; cert_read and the certification of path edges are declared by the process (direct edges: read position → process, Γ_closed = closed);
Ident and HistEv are declared as 1. Window reading of an input history h (this paper's reading RD): W takes the last w beats of h, and all beats before W are the formative history,
whose Form and Mark records are represented in F by entry-beat records (D2 of the Mathematical Form); the starting state of W is the final state of the formative history.
"""
from fractions import Fraction as Fr
from itertools import combinations, product
from collections import deque
ONE, ZERO, BOT = 1, 0, None

def k_and(*xs):
    if any(x == 0 for x in xs):
        return 0
    if any(x is None for x in xs):
        return None
    return 1

def k_or(*xs):
    if any(x == 1 for x in xs):
        return 1
    if any(x is None for x in xs):
        return None
    return 0

class Proc:
    def __init__(self, pid, tgt, car, law, R=(), k=0, b=0, cert_read=1, cert_path=1):
        self.pid, self.tgt, self.car, self.law = pid, tgt, car, law
        self.R, self.k, self.b = tuple(R), Fr(k), Fr(b)
        self.cert_read, self.cert_path = cert_read, cert_path
    def value(self, s):
        if self.law == 'const':
            return self.b
        if self.law == 'lin':
            return self.b + self.k * sum(s[j] for j in self.R)
        j1, j2 = self.R
        return self.b + self.k * s[j1] * s[j2]
    def supp_act(self, s):
        if self.law == 'const' or self.k == 0:
            return set()
        if self.law == 'lin':
            return set(self.R)
        j1, j2 = self.R
        out = set()
        if s[j2] != 0:
            out.add(j1)
        if s[j1] != 0:
            out.add(j2)
        return out
    def supp_cond(self, s, eps):
        sa = self.supp_act(s)
        if self.law == 'lin' and abs(self.value(s)) <= eps:
            sa |= set(self.R)
        return sa

class GF:
    """Alphabet-gated F model. letters: {u: (pulses, procs)}; pulses = [(tgt, a, ext)], ext ∈ {'out','in'}."""
    def __init__(self, I, eps, letters, s0):
        """Validity antecedents (Definition 3): position names are nonempty and pairwise distinct; ε, the initial state and the pulse amplitudes are nonnegative rationals (int or Fraction; floats are not accepted);
        the alphabet is finite and contains 'rest'; process labels are pairwise distinct within a letter; law ∈ {const, lin, bilin}, lin has nonempty read positions, bilin exactly two,
        const none; read positions and action targets belong to the position set; cert_read, cert_path ∈ {1, 0, None}; pulse ext ∈ {'out','in'}.
        Invalid input raises ValueError and does not enter evaluation."""
        def rat(v, nonneg=False):
            if isinstance(v, bool) or not isinstance(v, (int, Fr)):
                raise ValueError('a value must be an int or a Fraction')
            if nonneg and v < 0:
                raise ValueError('must be nonnegative')
            return Fr(v)
        I = tuple(I)
        if not I or len(set(I)) != len(I) or any(not i for i in I):
            raise ValueError('position set')
        if 'rest' not in letters:
            raise ValueError('the alphabet must contain rest')
        for u, (pulses, procs) in letters.items():
            for (tgt, a, ext) in pulses:
                if tgt not in I or ext not in ('out', 'in'):
                    raise ValueError('pulse')
                rat(a, True)
            ids = [p.pid for p in procs]
            if len(set(ids)) != len(ids):
                raise ValueError('duplicate process label')
            for p in procs:
                if p.tgt not in I or any(j not in I for j in p.R) or p.car not in ('B', 'out'):
                    raise ValueError('process target or read positions')
                if p.law not in ('const', 'lin', 'bilin') or (p.law == 'lin' and not p.R) or \
                   (p.law == 'bilin' and len(p.R) != 2) or (p.law == 'const' and p.R):
                    raise ValueError('law and read positions')
                if p.cert_read not in (1, 0, None) or p.cert_path not in (1, 0, None):
                    raise ValueError('certification value')

            raise ValueError('initial-state length')

        return self.I.index(i)
    def step(self, s, u):

        eps = self.eps
        pulses, procs = self.letters[u]
        d = {i: Fr(0) for i in self.I}
        sd = dict(zip(self.I, s))
        formed, marked, events = set(), set(), []
        for (tgt, a, ext) in pulses:
            a = Fr(a)

                formed.add(tgt)
                if ext == 'out':

def window_eval(M, s_start, F_rec, M_rec, word):
    """Window reading: entry records F_rec, M_rec (position sets), W = word. Returns a dict with Q for every nonempty T, Fld and the in-boundary self-feedback type."""
    eps = M.eps
    s = s_start
    Form = set(F_rec)           # formation records “earlier than t” (the entry-beat records precede every beat in W)
    Mark = set(M_rec)
    ev_log = []                 # (full avail_cond, full avail_act, p, act); the part relevant to each T is extracted afterwards
    for u in word:
        r = M.step(s, u)
        if r is None:
            return None
        s2, formed, marked, events = r
        sd = dict(zip(M.I, s))
        for (p, c, act) in events:
            ac = {i for i in p.supp_cond(sd, eps) if sd[i] > eps and i in Form}
            aa = {i for i in p.supp_act(sd) if sd[i] > eps and i in Form}
            ev_log.append((ac, aa, p, act))
        Form |= formed
        Mark |= marked
        s = s2
    end = dict(zip(M.I, s))
    out = {}
    fld, selfref = 0, 0
    for n in range(1, len(M.I) + 1):
        for T in combinations(M.I, n):
            T = set(T)
            src = 1 if any(i in Mark for i in T) else 0
            ret = 1 if all(end[i] > eps and i in Form for i in T) else 0
            trace = k_and(src, ret, 1, 1)   # Ident and HistEv declared as 1
            part = k_or(*[k_and(1 if (ac & T) else 0, p.cert_read)
                          for (ac, aa, p, act) in ev_log if p.car == 'B']) if ev_log else 0
            hd_terms, sr_terms = [], []
            for (ac, aa, p, act) in ev_log:
                hc = k_and(1 if (aa & T) else 0, p.cert_read, p.cert_path if (aa & T) else 0)
                hd_terms.append(k_and(1 if p.tgt in T else 0, act, hc))
                if p.car == 'B':
                    sr_terms.append(k_and(1 if p.tgt in T else 0, act, hc))
            hdyn = k_or(*hd_terms) if hd_terms else 0
            q = k_and(trace, part, hdyn)
            out[frozenset(T)] = q
            fld = k_or(fld, q)
            sr = k_and(q, k_or(*sr_terms) if sr_terms else 0)
            selfref = k_or(selfref, sr)
    return {'Q': out, 'Fld': fld, 'self': selfref, 'end': s}

def run_prefix(M, h):
    """Formative history: apply h from s0 and return (final state, Form position set, Mark position set)."""
    s, F, Mk = M.s0, set(), set()
    for u in h:
        r = M.step(s, u)
        if r is None:
            return None
        s, fo, ma, _ = r
        F |= fo
        Mk |= ma
    return s, frozenset(F), frozenset(Mk)

def fld_of_history(M, h, w):
    """Window-reading qualification of the input history h (W = last w beats, w ≥ 1)."""
    if w < 1:
        raise ValueError('w ≥ 1')
    if len(h) < w:
        return None
    pre = run_prefix(M, h[:len(h) - w])
    if pre is None:
        return None
    s, F, Mk = pre
    return window_eval(M, s, F, Mk, h[len(h) - w:])

def physical_reach(M, letters=None):
    letters = letters or list(M.letters)
    seen, dq = {M.s0}, deque([M.s0])
    while dq:
        s = dq.popleft()
        for u in letters:
            r = M.step(s, u)
            if r and r[0] not in seen:
                seen.add(r[0])
                dq.append(r[0])
    return seen

def augmented_search(M, w, target='Fld', letters=None):
    """Augmented search: nodes (s, Form, Mark); for each node, enumerate the window words of length w. Returns the shortest satisfying history and node statistics."""
    letters = letters or list(M.letters)
    start = (M.s0, frozenset(), frozenset())
    dist, par = {start: 0}, {start: None}
    dq = deque([start])
    while dq:
        node = dq.popleft()
        s, F, Mk = node
        for u in letters:
            r = M.step(s, u)
            if r is None:
                continue
            s2, fo, ma, _ = r
            nxt = (s2, F | fo, Mk | ma)
            if nxt not in dist:
                dist[nxt] = dist[node] + 1
                par[nxt] = (node, u)
                dq.append(nxt)
    best = None
    for node in sorted(dist, key=lambda n: dist[n]):
        if best is not None and dist[node] + w >= best[0]:
            break
        s, F, Mk = node
        for word in product(letters, repeat=w):
            e = window_eval(M, s, F, Mk, word)
            if e is not None and e[target] == 1:
                path, n = [], node
                while par[n] is not None:
                    n, u = par[n]
                    path.append(u)
                h = tuple(reversed(path)) + word
                if best is None or len(h) < best[0]:
                    best = (len(h), h)
                break
    N = len(physical_reach(M, letters))
    return {'best': best, 'nodes': len(dist), 'N': N,
            'max_prefix_dist': max(dist.values()), 'bound_prefix': (2 * len(M.I) + 1) * N - 1}

def brute_min(M, w, Lmax, target='Fld', letters=None):
    letters = letters or list(M.letters)
    for L in range(w, Lmax + 1):
        for h in product(letters, repeat=L):
            e = fld_of_history(M, h, w)
            if e is not None and e[target] == 1:
                return L, h
    return None
