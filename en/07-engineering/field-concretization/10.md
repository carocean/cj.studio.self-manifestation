# The Stability Form of Field Concretization

1\. Declaration of Form Kind  
This paper is a Stability Form (Group Five, an auxiliary form of maintenance and stability). Its sole task is to determine, for a Sedimentation-Field qualification that has already been established, under what identity and at what quantifier level it is maintained, actually fails and returns within a domain of permitted variation and along legitimate successor trajectories frozen in advance.  
The conclusions of this paper fall into five groups.  
　1\. Instantaneous readings: in the alphabet-gated F model, the qualification reading at the existence layer is determined by the unique maximal bearing set (Theorem 1), and failure attribution can be read off component by component on this set (Corollary 1).  
　2\. Substitution: that qualification holds at every moment does not imply that the same Trace-Response set bears it throughout (Proposition 1); that the processes bearing historical dynamics are replaced does not imply that qualification fails (Proposition 2).  
　3\. Observation windows and guarantees: the qualification reading does not decrease as the window lengthens (Theorem 2); deciding maintenance on all legitimate successors requires keeping the state and records at the window start (Proposition 3, Theorem 3), and in the finite case admits a three-valued decision (Theorem 4).  
　4\. Recovery: two kinds of obstruction to return—the necessary passage of a signal (Theorem 5) and stationary-point obstruction (Theorem 6); Instance A of the Adjudication Form exits under continued training and cannot return (Corollary 3).  
　5\. Boundaries and quantifiers: finite segmentation under replay comparison (Theorem 7) and its failure to extend to the infinite horizon (Proposition 6); decision and separation of the fixed layer and the trajectory quantifiers (Corollary 4, Proposition 7, Proposition 8).  
Strength position: what a Stability Form proves is the maintenance of an established qualification under change, not the first existence, first reachability or first occurrence of the object. All conclusions of this paper lie within formal models; they certify the stability of no material host.  
Theoretical increment: the upstream papers prove, respectively, the establishment, adjudication, reachability and realization of Sedimentation-Field qualification; none of them takes on whether qualification is maintained as the time window advances and along legitimate successors, or whether it can return after it exits. The increment of this paper lies in the specific information structure of qualification:  
　the existence layer reduces to a single maximal bearing set;  
　return must be split into two kinds of obstruction according to failure attribution;  
　monitoring of sliding-window qualification must keep the window start and cannot look only at the final state.  
The proofs of the theorems are elementary (monotonicity, induction, finiteness of polynomial roots, graph search). Three-valued algebra, the safety product construction and the finiteness of roots are all existing methods; no claim of methodological novelty is made.

### 2.2  Entry Conditions

The entry conditions of the Canon Chapters, Part Four, Chapter Fifteen are met in this paper as follows:  
　(1) The qualification Q to be stabilized has a closed definition: Fld\_RD and each Q\_F(h, T) (F6).  
　(2) Q already holds at the entry: the entry history h₀ of a stability instance satisfies Fld\_RD(h₀) = 1. For χ₀ and Instance A the entry is taken at the arrival points proved in the Reachability Form; for separately declared instances the entry is evaluated directly in the text.  
　(3) The identity layer of the object of stability has been declared (Definition 3).  
　(4) The domain of permitted variation is declared in advance, and the recomputation rule after variation is established (Definitions 1 and 2).  
　(5) The legitimate successor dynamics, the time domain and the observation protocol are given by the beat-wise recursion of 𝔐\_GF (4.2 of the Mathematical Form) and by Definitions 1 and 4.  
　(6) When guaranteed stability over all legitimate futures is declared, the evaluation domain is delimited in Definitions 1, 2 and 7.

### 2.3  Outside the Scope

　(1) The establishment, adjudication, reachability and realization of qualification belong respectively to the General Form, the Adjudication Form, the Reachability Form and the Realization Form;  
　(2) online variation that changes parameters or state in mid-run (beyond the alphabet-gated form of 𝔐\_GF) is not in the domain of permitted variation (U2);  
　(3) recovery at the fixed layer, decision algorithms for stability with recovery, stochastic families and continuous-time models have no conclusions yet (U1, U3, U4);  
　(4) the conclusions below certify the maintenance, exit or return of no material host (U5).

3\. Objects of Stability and the Original Qualification  
Definition 1 (stability instance and domain of permitted variation). A stability instance is Σ = (M, w, h₀, 𝓛):  
　M ∈ 𝔐\_GF (fixed position set I, resolution ε, alphabet and initial state s₀);  
　w ≥ 1 is the window length;  
　h₀ is the entry history, \|h₀\| ≥ w, Fld\_RD(h₀) = 1;  
　𝓛 gives the legitimate successors, under one of two regimes:  
　　Alphabet regime: a letter set U′ (possibly empty, in which case there is only the empty extension, i.e. the entry itself); every finite word over U′ is an evaluation point.  
　　Regular regime: an automaton (Q, q₀, δ, F), where δ is a partial transition function and F ⊆ Q is the set of accepting states.  
　　　q₀ is the state on reading the empty extension word; h₀ is not read in.  
　　　An extension word v is executable if δ is defined everywhere along v and no prefix leaves the domain of definition.  
　　　v is an evaluation point if and only if v is executable and δ(q₀, v) ∈ F. Non-accepting prefixes advance the physical state as usual but are not evaluation points.  
　　The prefix-closed regime is the special case F = Q: every executable prefix is an evaluation point. Acceptance semantics and prefix-closed semantics differ and must not be conflated.  
The domain of permitted variation is exactly extension according to 𝓛, with the model, ε and w unchanged. Changing any one of them gives another instance, and the original instance is then not said to be maintained or recovered.  
Definition 2 (evaluation points, recomputation and actual failure). An evaluation point is h = h₀·v, where v is an extension word evaluated according to Definition 1.  
　Recomputation rule: at each evaluation point, all T and the three-valued reading Fld(h) := Fld\_RD(h) are recomputed by RD on the actual history h; earlier readings are not carried over.  
　The entry keeps all Form and Mark records and the final window of h₀; it is not reset to an empty record.  
　Actual failure: Fld(h) = 0 at an evaluation point.  
　⊥ is not actual failure. An extension that leaves the domain of definition is not executable, is not an evaluation point, and is not extended further.  
Definition 3 (identity layers).  
　Fixed layer: a nonempty T₀ ⊆ I is specified in advance, with reading a(h) = Q\_F(h, T₀). The stability task at the fixed layer further requires Q\_F(h₀, T₀) = 1 at the entry. An unqualified T₀ can still be used to classify instantaneous readings (Lemma 1), but this is not called maintenance of an established qualification. Fld(h₀) = 1 guarantees only that some T qualifies, not that the specified T₀ qualifies.  
　Existence layer: reading b(h) = Fld(h).  
　Internal-witness layer: the process events that bear HDyn. 𝔐\_GF guarantees only that process labels are pairwise distinct within a letter; process identity across letters must be declared separately.  
　Ident and HistEv are declared 1 in 𝔐\_GF; formal maintenance of the coordinate set T does not prove the continuous identity of material Trace-Responses.  
Definition 4 (stability protocol, failure-time set and recovery).  
　The stability protocol 𝒫 is frozen before observation and declared together with the stability instance:  
　　the identity layer judged;  
　　the evaluation domain and the entry;  
　　the recovery deadline D and the continued-verification length L (each may be “not declared”);  
　　the timing rule: counted by the index of evaluation points; the deadline runs from the first actual failure, and later failures do not reset it; continued verification is counted consecutively from the return point, the return point included.  
　Every recovery conclusion cites the same 𝒫. Parameterized queries over different (D, L) are allowed, but their results are not conclusions under a pre-declared protocol.  
　Existence layer: along a legitimate trajectory ν (a sequence of extensions), the failure-time set is F(ν) = {t : Fld\_t = 0}.  
　　The first actual failure is τ\_f = min F(ν) (when F(ν) is nonempty);  
　　a return point is τ\_r \> τ\_f with Fld\_{τ\_r} = 1;  
　　recovery holds if and only if τ\_r − τ\_f ≤ D and Fld = 1 at L consecutive evaluation points starting from τ\_r; items (D or L) not declared in 𝒫 are not required, and the conclusion then speaks only to the declared items.  
　　Fld becoming 1 again with no preceding actual failure does not constitute recovery.  
　Fixed layer: replace Fld by Q\_F(·, T₀) and define F, τ\_f, τ\_r word for word as above, without substituting the times of the existence layer. Conclusions on failure and recovery are issued only at the existence layer.

4\. Instantaneous Readings: The Maximal Bearing Set and Failure Decomposition  
Definition 5 (maximal bearing set). At an evaluation point h, R(h) = {i ∈ I : s\_i \> ε at the end of the window, and i ∈ Form(h)}, where Form(h) is the formation record of the whole history.  
Theorem 1 (maximal bearing set). For any evaluation point h of 𝔐\_GF:  
　(a) if R(h) = ∅, then Fld(h) = 0; if R(h) ≠ ∅, then Fld(h) = Q\_F(h, R(h)).  
　(b) For T ⊆ T′ ⊆ R(h), Q\_F(h, T) ≤ Q\_F(h, T′) (truth order); if T ⊄ R(h), then Q\_F(h, T) = 0.  
Proof.  
　Ret\_T = 1 if and only if T ⊆ R(h).  
　Src\_T = \[T ∩ Mark ≠ ∅\], which does not decrease as T grows.  
　Part\_T is a strong Kleene disjunction over the in-window events inside B; each term \[ac ∩ T ≠ ∅\] ∧ cert\_read is nondecreasing in T (ac is the set of condition-support positions available to the event).  
　HDyn\_T is a disjunction over the in-window events; each term \[tgt ∈ T\] ∧ act ∧ (\[aa ∩ T ≠ ∅\] ∧ cert\_read ∧ cert\_path) is nondecreasing in T (aa is the set of available action-support positions).  
　Strong Kleene conjunction and disjunction are monotone in the truth order, so (b) holds.  
　Fld is the disjunction over all nonempty T: those with T ⊄ R(h) give 0, and those with T ⊆ R(h) do not exceed Q\_F(h, R(h)). Hence (a) holds. ∎  
Verification (Appendix B.1 (1)): at 8798 evaluation points of random valid models (including ⊥ certification), Fld always equals Q\_F(h, R(h)).  
Corollary 1 (failure decomposition). Fld(h) = 0 if and only if one of the following four holds:  
　R(h) = ∅ (retention collapse);  
　Src(R(h)) = 0;  
　Part(R(h)) = 0;  
　HDyn(R(h)) = 0.  
Fld(h) = ⊥ if and only if R(h) ≠ ∅ and, among these four components, none is 0 and some is ⊥.  
Hence failure attribution at the existence layer need only be read off on the unique set R(h). The failure points below have different attributions:  
　the first failure of the χ₀ variant (the instance in Section 7): R = {x}, Part = 1, HDyn = 0;  
　the failure point of Proposition 4: R = {x}, Part = HDyn = 0;  
　the failure point of the example after Theorem 5: R = ∅.  
Lemma 1 (pairing of the readings of the two layers). a(h) ≤ b(h). Hence (a, b) can only be one of the following six:  
　(1, 1): both layers maintained;  
　(0, 1): the fixed layer definitely exits, and the existence layer is maintained by substitution;  
　(0, 0): the existence layer definitely collapses;  
　(⊥, 1): the existence layer definitely maintained, the fixed layer unknown;  
　(0, ⊥): the fixed layer definitely exits, the existence layer unknown;  
　(⊥, ⊥): both layers unknown.  
Maintenance at the fixed layer implies maintenance at the existence layer; the converse fails (Proposition 1).  
Proof. Fld is a strong Kleene disjunction that contains Q\_F(h, T₀). ∎  
The classification keeps both readings of the pair: in (⊥, 1) the unknown fixed-layer reading is not to be read as the existence layer failing to hold. Verification (Appendix B.1 (2)): all six pairings occur in random models, and no other pairing was observed.

5\. Substitution: Object Substitution and Internal-Witness Substitution  
Corollary 2 (maximal candidate at the fixed layer). Along a set of evaluation points E, ∃T ∀h ∈ E \[Q\_F(h, T) = 1\] holds if and only if  
　R\_E := ⋂\_{h∈E} R(h) ≠ ∅, and ∀h ∈ E \[Q\_F(h, R\_E) = 1\].  
Proof. By Theorem 1 (b), any qualifying T must be contained in every R(h), so T ⊆ R\_E and Q\_F(h, T) ≤ Q\_F(h, R\_E). ∎  
Since I is finite, the intersection stabilizes after finitely many steps, so this applies equally to infinite trajectories.  
Proposition 1 (object substitution: a bearer at every moment, but no bearer throughout).  
　Model: I = {x, y}, ε = 0, w = 2.  
　　X: out-of-boundary pulse x +1; process ky inside B, reads y, acts on y, component −y.  
　　Y: out-of-boundary pulse y +1; process kx inside B, reads x, acts on x, component −x.  
　Entry X·Y·X, successors (Y·X)^∞.  
　Conclusion: Fld = 1 at every evaluation point; R alternates between {x} and {y}, and the intersection is empty; each of the three nonempty T fails to qualify at some evaluation point. Hence ∀t∃T holds and ∃T∀t does not.  
Proof. From the entry on, the state alternates between (1, 0) and (0, 1), and the records and the state have period 2.  
　At an evaluation point ending with X, in the window \[Y, X\], kx reads the pre-beat value x = 1, x has already formed, and the component −1 is effective and acts on x; so Src, Ret, Part and HDyn of Q({x}) are all 1.  
　Evaluation points ending with Y are symmetric.  
　R alternates, so by Corollary 2 there is no fixed T. ∎  
Verification (Appendix B.1 (3)): 41 evaluation points.  
Proposition 2 (internal-witness substitution).  
　Model: I = {x}, ε = 0, w = 1.  
　　seed: out-of-boundary pulse +1;  
　　A: process p inside B, reads x, acts on x, component x/2;  
　　B: process q inside B, reads x, acts on x, component −x/4.  
　Entry seed·A, successors (B·A)^∞.  
　Conclusion: T = {x} qualifies throughout, and the process bearing HDyn alternates between p and q. The original witness p does not appear in the window of a q beat, yet qualification is not lost.  
Proof. x stays positive; the single process of each beat reads x and acts on x, with a nonzero component. ∎  
Exit of the original witness is not loss of stability: a substitute witness at the same layer must be checked.

6\. Dynamic Trajectories, Observation Windows and Decidable Domains  
Theorem 2 (window-length monotonicity). For a fixed history h and 1 ≤ w \< \|h\|, Fld(h, w) ≤ Fld(h, w + 1) (truth order). Hence the positive-determination region of window lengths may be empty; if a positive-determination window length exists, the positive-determination region is an upper tail of {1, …, \|h\|}.  
Proof.  
　Src and Ret depend only on the Mark and Form of the whole history and on the state at the end of the window; they do not depend on w.  
　The event terms of an in-window beat j depend on only two things: the pre-beat state and the pre-beat formation records. The latter are the union of the entry-beat records and the formation earlier in the window, which is exactly all formation before j, independent of the window start.  
　Hence the event terms shared by w and w + 1 are the same, and the longer window only adds disjuncts. Strong Kleene disjunction is monotone. ∎  
Witness (Appendix B.3 (4)): Instance A χ\_in, history (init, aⁿ); the positive-determination window lengths are w ≥ n − 11 (n = 13, 20, 40)—the window must cover the last beat on which opt is effective.  
Definition 6 (window-start monitor node). The node of an evaluation point h is ν(h) = (Z(h), buf(h)):  
　Z(h) = (s, Form, Mark) is the window's starting state and records, obtained by running the first \|h\| − w beats of h;  
　buf(h) is the last w letters.  
Advancing by one beat u: update Z with the first letter of buf (records accumulating per D16 of the Mathematical Form), delete that first letter, and append u.  
Proposition 3 (insufficiency of final-state monitoring).  
　Model: I = {x}, ε = 0, w = 1.  
　　small: out-of-boundary pulse +1/2;  
　　large: out-of-boundary pulse +1;  
　　c: process inside B, reads x, acts on x, component 1 − x.  
　The histories small·c and large·c have the same final state x = 1, the same accumulated Form and Mark, and the same final-window letter, yet their Fld are 1 and 0 respectively.  
　Their window starting states differ (1/2 and 1): in the former the component of c is 1/2 and effective; in the latter it is 0 and ineffective, so HDyn = 0.  
Hence “final state + accumulated records + final-window letters” cannot determine qualification.  
Theorem 3 (window-start monitoring).  
　(a) Fld(h) depends only on ν(h): it equals the window reading with Z(h) as the window start and buf(h) as the in-window word (Definition 4 and Lemma 1 of the Reachability Form).  
　(b) ν(h·u) is determined by ν(h) and u (the advance of Definition 6).  
　(c) Hence the readings on all extensions of h depend only on ν(h).  
　(d) Count: let N be the number of physical states reachable from the entry window's starting state under all letters (including the letters in the final window of the entry), d = \|I\|, and A the number of letters in the union of the legitimate successor letters and the letters of the final window of the entry. Then the number of executable monitor nodes is K ≤ N·4^d·A^w. In the regular regime, multiply further by the number of automaton states \|Q\|. Transitions that leave the domain of definition halt and are not counted in K.  
Proof.  
　(a) The definition of RD.  
　(b) Z(h·u) is the result of running the first \|h\| + 1 − w beats, i.e. Z(h) advanced by the first letter of buf(h), with records accumulating per D16; the update of buf is evident.  
　(c) Induction on the length of the extension.  
　(d) Form, Mark ⊆ I, each with at most 2^d possibilities. ∎  
Proposition 3 shows that (Z\_t, buf) cannot replace (Z\_{t−w}, buf).  
Verification (Appendix B.4 (2)): over 387 random finite models (including ⊥ certification):  
　the monitor label sets to depth ≤ 5 agree case by case with enumeration by history-by-history evaluation;  
　so do the shortest word lengths of the first 0 label and of the first ⊥ label;  
　K never exceeds N·4^d·A^w.  
Definition 7 (levels of maintenance and the three-valued guarantee reading).  
　Single-trajectory maintenance: Fld = 1 at every evaluation point of one trajectory.  
　Maintenance over a declared trajectory family: every trajectory in the family is maintained.  
　Guaranteed maintenance: Fld = 1 at every evaluation point of 𝓛.  
　The three-valued guarantee reading G(Σ): G = 0 if some evaluation point reads 0; G = ⊥ if none reads 0 and some reads ⊥; G = 1 if all read 1.  
　G = ⊥ neither certifies maintenance nor proves actual failure; unknown trajectories stay in the quantifier domain and are not deleted.  
　Evaluation domain: all evaluation points declared by Definitions 1 and 2. Under the prefix-closed regime, the set of evaluation points of an infinite legitimate trajectory is the union of those of its finite prefixes; when the executable graph has no dead ends, the two evaluation domains give the same G. When there are dead ends, evaluation points on dead-end prefixes are still counted, and the dead end itself is not read as failure.  
　Empty evaluation domain: the regular regime may have no executable accepting node at all (e.g. F = ∅). Then G = 1 is only logical truth over an empty domain; it is listed separately as an empty evaluation domain and is not recorded as evaluated maintenance.  
Theorem 4 (decision of the three-valued guarantee reading). When the executable product graph (monitor nodes × automaton states) is finite, G(Σ) is obtained by breadth-first traversal of all reachable product nodes, with labels read only at evaluated nodes (all nodes in the alphabet regime; nodes whose state lies in F in the regular regime):  
　if there is an evaluated 0 label, G = 0;  
　otherwise, if there is an evaluated ⊥ label, G = ⊥;  
　otherwise G = 1; when the number of evaluated nodes is 0, the case is listed separately as an empty evaluation domain.  
Transitions that leave the domain of definition halt. For the first evaluated 0 (or ⊥) label, the shortest extension word has length at most K − 1.  
Proof. By Theorem 3 (c), the label set of the evaluation points equals the label set of the evaluated reachable product nodes; non-accepting nodes are expanded as usual, only their labels are not read. Breadth-first search gives shortest paths, and a shortest path contains no cycle. ∎  
Witnesses (Appendix B.4 (3)(6)(7)):  
　the model of Proposition 1 has G = 1 under the alternating prefix-closed regime (3 nodes); under the full alphabet G = 0, with shortest counterexample rest.  
　A flip model in which the read certification of one process is ⊥: G = ⊥.  
　Acceptance semantics versus prefix-closed semantics:  
　　entry seed·b (seed is an out-of-boundary pulse +1; b is a flip process inside B, reads x, acts on x, component 3/2 − 2x; a has no events), extension language (ab)\*.  
　　Under acceptance semantics only (ab)ⁿ is evaluated: x alternates between 1/2 and 1, the component of b in the last beat always has absolute value 1/2, and G = 1.  
　　Evaluated over its prefix closure: Fld = 0 on the non-accepting prefix a, and G = 0.  
　Other boundary cases:  
　　with a ⊥ accepting endpoint, G = ⊥;  
　　with a branch that leaves the domain of definition, the branch halts and G = 1;  
　　when only ab is accepted, the empty extension is not an evaluation point;  
　　an explicitly empty letter set has only the empty extension, and G = 1;  
　　with F = ∅, the evaluation domain is empty.  
Scope: this theorem decides maintenance throughout. Deciding stability with recovery (maintenance or recovery) requires separately defining monitors for recovery timing and verification length; no such result exists yet (U1). That a 0 has occurred at an evaluation point does not rule out recovery.

7\. Recovery  
Proposition 4 (return without a new signal).  
　Model: I = {x}, ε = 1, w = 1.  
　　seed: out-of-boundary pulse +4;  
　　a: process p inside B, reads x, acts on x, component x/2;  
　　rest: no events.  
　The histories seed·a, seed·a·rest, seed·a·rest·a have Fld 1, 0, 1 in turn (x = 6, 6, 9).  
　At the failure point R = {x}, Src = Ret = 1, Part = HDyn = 0: there is no process in the window, while the old Trace-Response is still present.  
　After the failure there is no out-of-boundary change at all and the old Mark is kept, yet qualification returns. Nor is this return prevented by the absence of any effective positive component below threshold (x ≤ 1).  
Theorem 5 (necessary passage of a signal). Let τ be an actual failure point on a trajectory and Mark\_τ its whole-history mark set, and suppose:  
　(i) at τ, s\_i ≤ ε at the end of the window for every i ∈ Mark\_τ;  
　(ii) in every signal-free successor, the state of every i ∈ Mark\_τ stays ≤ ε.  
A signal-free successor is a legitimate successor no beat of which contains an effective out-of-boundary change: no out-of-boundary pulse of amplitude greater than ε, and no effective component of a process with an out-of-boundary carrier. This is decided beat by beat, not by whether the set Mark grows—an out-of-boundary pulse applied again to an already marked position does not enlarge Mark, yet it is still a signal.  
Then Fld = 0 at every evaluation point of every signal-free successor. Hence return must pass through a new effective out-of-boundary change. This condition is necessary for return, not sufficient.  
Proof. Mark is registered only by effective out-of-boundary pulses and effective components with out-of-boundary carriers (D16 of the Mathematical Form), so in a signal-free successor Mark remains Mark\_τ. For any nonempty T:  
　if T ∩ Mark\_τ = ∅, then Src = 0;  
　otherwise take i ∈ T ∩ Mark\_τ; by (ii), s\_i ≤ ε, so Ret = 0.  
In both cases Q\_F = 0, so Fld = 0. ∎  
A checkable sufficient condition: for every applicable non-signal letter and every subthreshold position in Mark\_τ, the sum of the effective pulses and effective components at that position does not lift it above ε.  
Example (Appendix B.2 (4)):  
　Model: I = {x}, ε = 1/10, w = 1.  
　　sig: out-of-boundary pulse +1;  
　　g: process inside B, reads x, acts on x, component x;  
　　d: process inside B, reads x, acts on x, component −x;  
　　inp: in-field pulse +1.  
　Entry sig·g; after d is applied, x = 0: the first actual failure (R = ∅).  
　The non-signal letters rest, d, g all keep x ≤ ε. All 1093 non-signal successors of length ≤ 6 have Fld = 0.  
　Return via sig·g: sig hits an already marked position; Mark does not grow, yet it is still a signal.  
　Antecedent (ii) cannot be dropped: when inp is allowed, inp·g gives Fld = 1 while Mark is still only the old {x}; this is a return without a new out-of-boundary change.  
Theorem 6 (stationary-point obstruction). Suppose the trajectory is in state s\* at time σ, and for every letter u legitimate after σ, every pulse amplitude of u is ≤ ε and every process of u has \|c\| ≤ ε at s\*. Then:  
　(a) the state remains s\* thereafter;  
　(b) Fld = 0 at every evaluation point whose window lies entirely after σ.  
Proof.  
　(a) There is no effective component; induction on the number of beats.  
　(b) act is 0 for every in-window event, and every term of HDyn contains a factor 0, so HDyn = 0. Hence Q\_F = 0 for every T, whether or not certification is ⊥. ∎  
Corollary 3 (exit and non-return of Instance A under continued training).  
　Instance A χ\_in: ε = 1/100, w = 1, the regime of legitimate histories 𝓗\_A (init only at beat 0), along (init, a, a, …).  
　Conclusion: the first actual failure occurs at the 13th a beat; thereafter Fld = 0 on every successor over a and rest, and qualification never returns.  
Proof.  
　On the stretch where effective updates continue (n = 0, 1, …, 12), w\_n = 2 − (3/4)^n, and the component of opt at the nth a beat is (1/4)(3/4)^(n−1) \> ε.

8\. Stability Boundaries  
Definition 8 (parameter regions under replay comparison). For a declared one-parameter family {M\_θ}, a fixed history h and a window length w, each θ is run independently from the original s₀.  
　The positive-determination region is {θ : Fld = 1}, the zero-determination region is {θ : Fld = 0}, and the undetermined region is {θ : Fld = ⊥}.  
　This is a comparison between members of a parameter family; it does not describe change in mid-run and is not called maintenance, failure or return; the boundary of a parameter region is not a dynamic actual-failure event.  
Theorem 7 (finite segmentation of one-parameter polynomial families).  
　Suppose: a fixed finite history h and window length w; the parameter θ takes only rational values, θ ∈ J ∩ ℚ (J an interval). The following discrete data are fixed in θ:  
　　the position set I, the alphabet, and the pulse table and process table of each letter;  
　　law types, read positions, action targets, carriers, and the out-of-boundary or in-field flags of pulses;  
　　all certification atoms (cert\_read, cert\_path).  
　Only the initial state, the pulse amplitudes, the process coefficients and biases, and ε vary with θ, and all of them are polynomials in θ with rational coefficients.  
　Write the real-algebraic validity domain J\_alg = {θ ∈ J : the validity antecedents, such as nonnegativity, hold, and h never leaves the domain of definition}, and the valid parameter domain of the original model J\* = J\_alg ∩ ℚ. Invalid parameter points and points that leave the domain are given no reading and are not merged into ⊥.  
　Conclusion: the real interval J can be divided into finitely many points and open intervals (cells); for each cell whose intersection with J\* is nonempty, Fld(θ) and all Q\_F(θ, T) are constant on that intersection.  
Proof. Induction on beats. On each branch on which the outcomes of the comparisons are fixed, the state coordinates and the components are polynomials in θ. Each comparison is a sign test of some polynomial:  
　x − ε, c − ε, −c − ε, s − ε, ε − s (effective, available, formed);  
　exact nonzeroness of bilinear partner readings;  
　nonnegativity of post-states.  
　There are finitely many branches, and each involves finitely many polynomials. The discrete data and the certification atoms are fixed, so the three-valued combination changes only with the outcomes of these comparisons.  
　Take all roots in J of those polynomials that are not identically zero, together with the roots of the polynomials in the sign conditions of J\_alg; this gives finitely many cells. Within a cell every polynomial keeps its sign, so the valid rational parameters in the cell follow the same branch and have the same readings. Roots that are rational are recomputed directly.  
　J\_alg is a union of finitely many points and intervals; J\* = J\_alg ∩ ℚ in general is not, so the conclusion is stated for the intersections of cells with J\*. ∎  
Generalization: when the numerical data are rational functions whose denominators do not vanish on J, the states and components on each branch are still rational functions; the comparison f/g \> 0 is equivalent to f·g \> 0 (multiplying by the square of a nonzero denominator preserves sign), and the root set is again finite.  
The antecedents cannot be relaxed:  
　if the discrete data are not fixed: with all numerical values constant but cert\_read(θ) equal to 1 on countably many intervals and 0 elsewhere, Fld equals exactly cert\_read(θ), and the positive-determination region has infinitely many segments;  
　if the parameter dependence is not restricted to polynomials: with a coefficient k(θ) equal to 1 on countably many intervals and 0 elsewhere, there are likewise infinitely many segments.  
Endpoints: readings at breakpoints must be recomputed directly; they cannot be inferred merely from the fact that each atom is a strict inequality.  
Witnesses for parameter regions (the family ε = θ, a special case of Theorem 7; Appendix B.3 (1)(3)).

Proposition 6 (a positive margin on finite horizons does not guarantee a positive margin on the infinite horizon). Let χ₀ take ε = θ, along (u1, rest, rest, …). Fld = 1 at every evaluation point if and only if θ = 0.  
Proof.  
　θ = 0: by Proposition 5.  
　θ \> 0: under rest, δ does not increase. wb effective implies dec effective (3δ/10 \> δ/10), so as long as some process is effective, δ is multiplied by at most 4/5 per beat. After finitely many steps (3/10)δ ≤ θ, both processes are ineffective, a stationary point is entered, and by Theorem 6 Fld = 0 thereafter. ∎  
For each finite n, the positive-determination region is \[0, θ\_n), with θ\_n decreasing to 0 (for any θ \> 0, θ ∉ \[0, θ\_n) once n is large enough); the positive-determination region of the infinite horizon is only {0}.  
Scope: {0} is itself still a finite segmentation, so this example does not show that the infinite horizon lacks finite segmentation. What it shows is that a positive margin θ\_n \> 0 on each finite horizon does not imply a uniform positive margin on the infinite horizon. Theorem 7 is issued only for finite histories.

9\. Quantifier Checks across Levels  
Time quantifiers and trajectory quantifiers must be checked together. Each of the following three statements implies the next, and none of the converses holds:  
　the same T qualifies throughout on all trajectories (∃T ∀ν ∀t);  
　each trajectory has its own fixed T that qualifies throughout (∀ν ∃T ∀t);  
　on each trajectory, at each moment, some T qualifies (∀ν ∀t ∃T).  
Proposition 7 shows that the second does not imply the first, and Proposition 1 that the third does not imply the second.  
Corollary 4 (the all-trajectory quantifier at the fixed layer). When the executable product graph is finite, “there exists a T that qualifies at the entry and at all evaluated reachable product nodes” is decidable:  
　take the candidate T\_E = R(h₀) ∩ ⋂{R(ν) : ν an evaluated reachable product node};  
　the reading is the strong Kleene conjunction of Q\_F of T\_E at the entry and at all evaluated nodes (0 when T\_E = ∅).  
　The reading is 1 if and only if such a T exists; when the reading is 0, every T reads 0 at some node; when the reading is ⊥, it neither certifies nor refutes.  
Proof. By Corollary 2 and Theorem 1 (b), any qualifying T satisfies T ⊆ T\_E, and Q\_F(T) ≤ Q\_F(T\_E) at every node. ∎  
Two requirements:  
　intersection and checking use only evaluated nodes; non-accepting nodes are expanded as usual and do not prune paths.  
　The entry is counted in the candidate condition whether or not q₀ is accepting. What is judged is whether the bearer that already qualified at the original entry is maintained; a T that fails to qualify at the entry and qualifies only later must not be passed off as the original bearer. If only the mathematical existence statement over the evaluation domain E is issued, it must be marked separately; it is not the stability task of the fixed layer.  
Witnesses (Appendix B.4 (4)(7)):  
　a model with entry seed·A and legitimate letters A, B (seed consists of out-of-boundary pulses x +1, y +1; A flips x and removes y, B flips x): 5 nodes, T\_E = {x}, reading 1.  
　Under (ab)\* acceptance semantics, T\_E = {x} and the reading is 1; under its prefix closure the reading is 0.  
　When only ab is accepted, the reading is 1; with a ⊥ accepting endpoint, the reading is ⊥.  
　R is smaller at non-accepting intermediate points: entry seed·B, extension (a·B)\*, R = {x} at intermediate points. Under acceptance semantics T\_E = {x, y} and the reading is 1; under the prefix closure the candidate shrinks to {x}.

　Model: I = {x, y}, ε = 0, w = 1.  
　　seed: out-of-boundary pulses x +1, y +1;

Proof. Flipping moves the flipped position back and forth within {1/2, 1}, and the component has absolute value 1/2 \> 0. The conclusion follows by applying Corollary 2 to the intersection along each of the two trajectories, and then intersecting over all evaluated nodes together with the entry. ∎

Proposition 8 (decision of ∀ν∃T∀t).  
　Suppose: the prefix-closed regime; the executable monitor graph is finite and every reachable node in it has an executable successor (no dead ends); a trajectory means an infinite executable path from the entry.  
　Attach to the nodes a violated set V ⊆ 𝒫⁺(I): initially V₀ = {T : Q\_F(h₀, T) ≠ 1} (the entry itself is an evaluation point, and Fld(h₀) = 1 does not guarantee that every T qualifies at the entry); along each edge, add the T with Q\_F ≠ 1 at the new evaluation point.  
　Then “there exists a trajectory on which every T is violated” if and only if some product node with V = 𝒫⁺(I) is reachable.  
Proof.  
　(⇐) A finite path reaches a node with V = 𝒫⁺(I). Since there are no dead ends, extend this finite prefix to an infinite trajectory; V only grows, so on that trajectory every T fails to qualify at some evaluation point.  
　(⇒) On some infinite trajectory no fixed T qualifies throughout, i.e. every T has an evaluation point of first violation. There are only finitely many T; take the maximum of these first-violation times; the product node at the end of that finite prefix already has V = 𝒫⁺(I). ∎  
The number of product nodes is at most K·2^(2^d − 1), where K is the number of nodes of the executable monitor graph. Violations at the entry must not be omitted: one can construct a model in which only {x} qualifies at the entry and {x, y} does not, after which {x, y} qualifies forever and {x} subsequently exits. With the entry counted, all candidates are violated; ignoring the entry wrongly keeps {x, y}.

12\. Final Statement  
Given that the stability instance Σ and the stability protocol 𝒫 are frozen in advance:  
　At every evaluation point, Sedimentation-Field qualification is determined by the unique maximal bearing set, and failure attribution is read off component by component on this set.  
　That qualification holds at every moment does not mean that the same Trace-Response set bears it throughout; replacement of the bearing processes does not mean failure of qualification.  
　The qualification reading does not decrease as the window lengthens. Deciding maintenance on all legitimate successors requires keeping the state and records at the window start; in the finite case a three-valued decision is possible, unknowns are not deleted, and the empty evaluation domain is listed separately.  
　Return can occur without a new signal (the window temporarily lacks an action witness while the Trace-Response remains); when at failure all marked positions are subthreshold and stay subthreshold in every signal-free successor, there is no return without a signal (if an in-field pulse deepens a marked position again, return can occur without a new out-of-boundary change); when no successor contains an effective change, return is impossible. Instance A of the Adjudication Form exits under continued training because of learning convergence, and cannot return.  
　Parameter segmentation on finite histories holds under frozen discrete data; positive margins on each finite horizon do not imply a uniform margin on the infinite horizon.

~~~python
Evaluation points: the legitimate extensions h0·v of the entry history (v a finite legitimate word), with reading Fld_RD(h0·v), where W is the last w beats.

from collections import deque

import reach_core as rc
ORDER = {0: 0, None: 1, 1: 2}          # truth order 0 < ⊥ < 1

def window_record(M, h, w):
    """Returns (window start Z = (s, Form, Mark), window-end state, whole-history Form, whole-history Mark); returns None if the history leaves the domain."""
    pre = rc.run_prefix(M, h[:len(h) - w])
    if pre is None:
        return None
    s, F, Mk = pre
    z = (s, F, Mk)
    F2, M2 = set(F), set(Mk)
    for u in h[len(h) - w:]:
        r = M.step(s, u)
        if r is None:
            return None
        s, fo, ma, _ = r
        F2 |= fo
        M2 |= ma
    return z, s, frozenset(F2), frozenset(M2)

def canon(M, h, w):
    """Maximal bearing set R = {i : window-end s_i > ε and i ∈ Form} (Theorem 1)."""
    rec = window_record(M, h, w)
    if rec is None:
        return None
    _, s, F, _ = rec
    return frozenset(i for i, v in zip(M.I, s) if v > M.eps and i in F)

def evaluate(M, h, w):
    """Full reading at an evaluation point: Fld, the Q of each T, and the maximal bearing set R. Returns None if the history leaves the domain."""
    e = rc.fld_of_history(M, h, w)
    if e is None:
        return None
    return {'Fld': e['Fld'], 'Q': e['Q'], 'R': canon(M, h, w), 'end': e['end']}

def components(M, h, w, T):
    """The four components (Src, Ret, Part, HDyn) of a single T, for failure decomposition; identical term by term to reach_core.window_eval."""
    rec = window_record(M, h, w)
    (s0, F0, M0), _, _, _ = rec
    eps = M.eps
    s, Form, Mark, ev = s0, set(F0), set(M0), []
    for u in h[len(h) - w:]:
        s2, fo, ma, events = M.step(s, u)
        sd = dict(zip(M.I, s))
        for (p, c, act) in events:
            ac = {i for i in p.supp_cond(sd, eps) if sd[i] > eps and i in Form}
            aa = {i for i in p.supp_act(sd) if sd[i] > eps and i in Form}
            ev.append((ac, aa, p, act))
        Form |= fo
        Mark |= ma
        s = s2
    end = dict(zip(M.I, s))
    T = set(T)
    src = 1 if T & Mark else 0
    ret = 1 if all(end[i] > eps and i in Form for i in T) else 0
    part = rc.k_or(*[rc.k_and(1 if (ac & T) else 0, p.cert_read) for (ac, aa, p, act) in ev if p.car == 'B']) if ev else 0
    hd = [rc.k_and(1 if p.tgt in T else 0, act, rc.k_and(1 if (aa & T) else 0, p.cert_read, p.cert_path if (aa & T) else 0))
          for (ac, aa, p, act) in ev]
    hdyn = rc.k_or(*hd) if hd else 0
    return {'Src': src, 'Ret': ret, 'Part': part, 'HDyn': hdyn}

def monitor_init(M, h0, w):

    rec = window_record(M, h0, w)

    return (s, frozenset(F), frozenset(Mk), tuple(h0[len(h0) - w:]))

def monitor_step(M, node, u):

    s, F, Mk, buf = node
    r = M.step(s, buf[0])

    return (s2, F | frozenset(fo), Mk | frozenset(ma), buf[1:] + (u,))

    letters: the legitimate letter set (default: all letters).
    dfa: (q0, δ, F), the regular regime declaring the extension words v — q0 is the automaton state on reading the empty extension word (h0 is not read), δ(q, u) returns the successor state or None
         (infeasible), and F is the set of accepting states; if F is omitted, all states are taken (prefix-closed regime: every feasible prefix is an evaluation point).
         The evaluation points are exactly the h0·v with v accepted; non-accepting prefixes advance the physical state and the monitor node as usual but are not evaluation points.
    stop_on_zero: stop at the first evaluated 0 label (the reading 0 is then settled; the search is breadth-first, so the counterexample word returned is a shortest one; K is then only the number of nodes expanded so far).
    Returns a dict: verdict (1: every evaluation point is 1; 0: some evaluation point is 0; ⊥: no 0 but some ⊥), K (the number of reachable and executable product nodes;
    nodes that leave the domain are not counted), first0 / firstB (the shortest evaluated 0 / ⊥ extension words), dead (the number of transitions halted for leaving the domain), finite,
    evaluated (the number of evaluated nodes); when evaluated = 0, verdict = 1 is only the logical truth over an empty evaluation domain, vacuous is set to True, and the result is not an evaluated record of maintenance."""
    letters = list(M.letters if letters is None else letters)   # None means the default (all letters); an explicit empty set is kept as given
    if dfa:
        q0, delta = dfa[0], dfa[1]
        acc = dfa[2] if len(dfa) > 2 and dfa[2] is not None else None
    else:
        q0, delta, acc = None, None, None
    accepted = (lambda q: True) if acc is None else (lambda q: q in acc)
    start = (monitor_init(M, h0, w), q0)
    seen = {start: ()}
    dq = deque([start])
    first = {0: None, None: None}
    dead = 0
    evaluated = 0
    lab0 = monitor_label(M, start[0])
    if accepted(q0):
        evaluated += 1
        if lab0 in (0, None):
            first[lab0] = ()
    while dq:
        node, q = dq.popleft()
        for u in letters:
            q2 = q
            if delta:
                q2 = delta(q, u)
                if q2 is None:
                    continue
            n2 = (monitor_step(M, node, u), q2)
            if n2 in seen:
                continue
            lab = monitor_label(M, n2[0])
            if lab == 'dead':
                dead += 1
                continue                        # leaves the domain: not executable, not an evaluation point, and not extended further
            seen[n2] = seen[(node, q)] + (u,)
            if accepted(q2):
                evaluated += 1
            if accepted(q2) and lab in (0, None) and first[lab] is None:
                first[lab] = seen[n2]
                if lab == 0 and stop_on_zero:
                    return {'verdict': 0, 'K': len(seen), 'finite': None, 'first0': first[0], 'firstB': first[None], 'dead': dead,
                            'evaluated': evaluated, 'vacuous': False}
            dq.append(n2)
            if len(seen) > cap:
                return {'verdict': 'not closed', 'K': len(seen), 'finite': False, 'first0': first[0], 'firstB': first[None], 'dead': dead,
                        'evaluated': evaluated, 'vacuous': None}
    v = 0 if first[0] is not None else (None if first[None] is not None else 1)
    return {'verdict': v, 'K': len(seen), 'finite': True, 'first0': first[0], 'firstB': first[None], 'dead': dead,
            'evaluated': evaluated, 'vacuous': evaluated == 0}

def node_R_Q(M, node):
    """Maximal bearing set R and all Q at a monitor node (Theorem 1, Theorem 3 (a))."""
    s, F, Mk, buf = node
    e = rc.window_eval(M, s, F, Mk, buf)
    Ftot, ss = set(F), s
    for u in buf:
        ss, fo, _, _ = M.step(ss, u)
        Ftot |= fo
    end = dict(zip(M.I, e['end']))
    return frozenset(i for i in M.I if end[i] > M.eps and i in Ftot), e['Q']

def fixed_layer(M, h0, w, letters=None, dfa=None, cap=200000):
    """All-trajectory quantifier at the fixed layer (Corollary 4): is there a T that qualifies at the entry and at every evaluated reachable product node?
    Non-accepting nodes are expanded as usual and are filtered out only when intersecting and checking Q; the entry counts toward the candidate condition whether or not q0 is accepting (the original bearer must already qualify at the entry).
    Returns T (candidate = R(entry) ∩ the intersection of R over the evaluated nodes), verdict (the strong Kleene conjunction of the candidate's Q at the entry and at all evaluated nodes; 0 if the candidate is empty),
    evaluated (the number of evaluated nodes) and K. By Theorem 1 (b), verdict = 1 if and only if such a T exists; when verdict = 0, every T reads 0 at some node."""
    letters = list(M.letters if letters is None else letters)
    if dfa:
        q0, delta = dfa[0], dfa[1]
        acc = dfa[2] if len(dfa) > 2 and dfa[2] is not None else None
    else:
        q0, delta, acc = None, None, None
    accepted = (lambda q: True) if acc is None else (lambda q: q in acc)
    start = (monitor_init(M, h0, w), q0)
    seen, dq = {start}, deque([start])
    Rs, Qs = [], []
    R0, Q0 = node_R_Q(M, start[0])
    Rs.append(R0); Qs.append(Q0)                       # entry: candidate condition
    evaluated = 1 if accepted(q0) else 0
    while dq:
        node, q = dq.popleft()
        for u in letters:
            q2 = q
            if delta:
                q2 = delta(q, u)
                if q2 is None:
                    continue
            n2 = (monitor_step(M, node, u), q2)
            if n2 in seen or monitor_label(M, n2[0]) == 'dead':
                continue
            seen.add(n2); dq.append(n2)
            if accepted(q2):
                R, Q = node_R_Q(M, n2[0])
                Rs.append(R); Qs.append(Q); evaluated += 1
            if len(seen) > cap:
                raise RuntimeError('product graph did not close within cap')
    T = frozenset.intersection(*Rs)
    v = rc.k_and(*[Q[T] for Q in Qs]) if T else 0
    return {'T': T, 'verdict': v, 'evaluated': evaluated, 'K': len(seen)}

def brute_labels(M, h0, w, letters, L):
    """Brute-force comparison: evaluates every legitimate extension of length ≤ L one by one (extensions that leave the domain are discarded and not extended); returns the label set and the shortest word for each label."""
    out, first = set(), {}
    level = [()]
    for n in range(L + 1):
        nxt = []
        for v in level:
            e = rc.fld_of_history(M, h0 + v, w)
            if e is None:
                continue
            f = e['Fld']
            out.add(f)
            first.setdefault(f, v)
            if n < L:
                nxt += [v + (u,) for u in letters]
        level = nxt
    return out, first
~~~

A.4  s4\_boundary.py  Parameter regions under replay comparison, attainment of the boundaries, and window-length parameters (Theorems 2 and 7, Proposition 6)

~~~python
"""S4: parameter partition and attainment of the boundaries under replay-comparison semantics (fixed finite history).
The parameter is the resolution ε = θ (a declared one-parameter family: the initial state, pulse amplitudes and coefficients do not vary with θ, only the threshold does; this is a first-degree special case of the polynomial families treated in Theorem 7).
Each value of θ is a separate valid 𝔐_GF model, rerun from the original s₀; what is compared here is the parameter family, not dynamic maintenance or failure.
Method: run the original evaluator with ε replaced by a threshold class Thr that records everything it is compared with, which yields all comparison values encountered at that point; on an open interval containing no comparison value the branches do not change,
so the reading does not change (the skeleton of the proof of Theorem 7). The breakpoints are found one by one, and the reading is recomputed directly at each breakpoint.
(1) The ε-parameter family of χ₀, history (u1, rest^n), n = 5, 12, 30: the positive-determination region, the zero-determination region, and which of them attains each breakpoint.
(2) Infinite horizon: for each n the positive-determination region is [0, θ_n), with θ_n decreasing to 0; the only parameter that gives 1 for all n is θ = 0 (Proposition 6).
(3) The positive-determination region need not be an interval: among random models there occur parameter families whose reading along θ is 1, 0, 1 (one example is given explicitly).
(4) The window length w is a discrete parameter, monotone by Theorem 2; the minimal positive-determination window length on Instance A (init, a^n)."""
import random
from fractions import Fraction as Fr
import reach_core as rc
from stab_core import evaluate

class Thr:
    """Threshold θ: every rational number compared with it is recorded in seen."""
    def __init__(self, v):
        self.v, self.seen = Fr(v), set()
    def _r(self, x):
~~~

~~~python
"""The Reachability Form of Field Concretization: alphabet-gated F model, window reading, augmented reachability search and brute-force comparison (exact rational arithmetic, strong Kleene three-valued logic)
Model class 𝔐_GF (alphabet-gated F models): a finite position set I and a resolution ε; an alphabet U (containing the rest letter 'rest');
each letter u gives the pulse table [(tgt, a_p, ext)] and the process table [Proc] of its beat; processes follow the numeric laws of the three classes of D12 of The Mathematical Form of Field Concretization
and read the pre-beat state; cert_eff = 1; cert_read and the certification of path edges are declared by the process (direct edges: read position → process, Γ_closed = closed);
Ident and HistEv are declared as 1. Window reading of an input history h (this paper's reading RD): W takes the last w beats of h, and all beats before W are the formative history,
whose Form and Mark records are represented in F by entry-beat records (D2 of the Mathematical Form); the starting state of W is the final state of the formative history.
"""
from fractions import Fraction as Fr
from itertools import combinations, product
from collections import deque
ONE, ZERO, BOT = 1, 0, None

def k_and(*xs):
    if any(x == 0 for x in xs):
        return 0
    if any(x is None for x in xs):
        return None
    return 1

def k_or(*xs):
    if any(x == 1 for x in xs):
        return 1
    if any(x is None for x in xs):
        return None
    return 0

class Proc:
    def __init__(self, pid, tgt, car, law, R=(), k=0, b=0, cert_read=1, cert_path=1):
        self.pid, self.tgt, self.car, self.law = pid, tgt, car, law
        self.R, self.k, self.b = tuple(R), Fr(k), Fr(b)
        self.cert_read, self.cert_path = cert_read, cert_path
    def value(self, s):
        if self.law == 'const':
            return self.b
        if self.law == 'lin':
            return self.b + self.k * sum(s[j] for j in self.R)
        j1, j2 = self.R
        return self.b + self.k * s[j1] * s[j2]
    def supp_act(self, s):
        if self.law == 'const' or self.k == 0:
            return set()
        if self.law == 'lin':
            return set(self.R)
        j1, j2 = self.R
        out = set()
        if s[j2] != 0:
            out.add(j1)
        if s[j1] != 0:
            out.add(j2)
        return out
    def supp_cond(self, s, eps):
        sa = self.supp_act(s)
        if self.law == 'lin' and abs(self.value(s)) <= eps:
            sa |= set(self.R)
        return sa

class GF:
    """Alphabet-gated F model. letters: {u: (pulses, procs)}; pulses = [(tgt, a, ext)], ext ∈ {'out','in'}."""
    def __init__(self, I, eps, letters, s0):
        """Validity antecedents (Definition 3): position names are nonempty and pairwise distinct; ε, the initial state and the pulse amplitudes are nonnegative rationals (int or Fraction; floats are not accepted);
        the alphabet is finite and contains 'rest'; process labels are pairwise distinct within a letter; law ∈ {const, lin, bilin}, lin has nonempty read positions, bilin exactly two,
        const none; read positions and action targets belong to the position set; cert_read, cert_path ∈ {1, 0, None}; pulse ext ∈ {'out','in'}.
        Invalid input raises ValueError and does not enter evaluation."""
        def rat(v, nonneg=False):
            if isinstance(v, bool) or not isinstance(v, (int, Fr)):
                raise ValueError('a value must be an int or a Fraction')
            if nonneg and v < 0:
                raise ValueError('must be nonnegative')
            return Fr(v)
        I = tuple(I)
        if not I or len(set(I)) != len(I) or any(not i for i in I):
            raise ValueError('position set')
        if 'rest' not in letters:
            raise ValueError('the alphabet must contain rest')
        for u, (pulses, procs) in letters.items():
            for (tgt, a, ext) in pulses:
                if tgt not in I or ext not in ('out', 'in'):
                    raise ValueError('pulse')
                rat(a, True)
            ids = [p.pid for p in procs]
            if len(set(ids)) != len(ids):
                raise ValueError('duplicate process label')
            for p in procs:
                if p.tgt not in I or any(j not in I for j in p.R) or p.car not in ('B', 'out'):
                    raise ValueError('process target or read positions')
                if p.law not in ('const', 'lin', 'bilin') or (p.law == 'lin' and not p.R) or \
                   (p.law == 'bilin' and len(p.R) != 2) or (p.law == 'const' and p.R):
                    raise ValueError('law and read positions')
                if p.cert_read not in (1, 0, None) or p.cert_path not in (1, 0, None):
                    raise ValueError('certification value')
        if len(s0) != len(I):
            raise ValueError('initial-state length')
        self.I, self.eps, self.letters = I, rat(eps, True), letters
        self.s0 = tuple(rat(x, True) for x in s0)
    def idx(self, i):
        return self.I.index(i)
    def step(self, s, u):
        """One beat (4.2 and 5.1 of the Mathematical Form): returns (post-state, set of newly formed positions, set of newly marked positions, process event table); returns None when the step leaves the domain."""
        eps = self.eps
        pulses, procs = self.letters[u]
        d = {i: Fr(0) for i in self.I}
        sd = dict(zip(self.I, s))
        formed, marked, events = set(), set(), []
        for (tgt, a, ext) in pulses:
            a = Fr(a)
            if a > eps:                      # effective pulse: always counted in Form (D16; as in upstream field_final.py lines 123–126), whatever the pre-beat depth
                formed.add(tgt)
                if ext == 'out':
                    marked.add(tgt)
                d[tgt] += a
        for p in procs:
            c = p.value(sd)
            act = 1 if abs(c) > eps else 0
            events.append((p, c, act))
            if act:
                if c > eps and sd[p.tgt] <= eps:   # an effective positive component makes an empty position a trace-retaining element (D16; upstream line 162)
                    formed.add(p.tgt)
                if p.car == 'out':
                    marked.add(p.tgt)        # an effective change by a mechanism with an out-of-boundary carrier enters Mark
                d[p.tgt] += c
        s2 = tuple(sd[i] + d[i] for i in self.I)
        if any(x < 0 for x in s2):
            return None
        return s2, formed, marked, events

def window_eval(M, s_start, F_rec, M_rec, word):
    """Window reading: entry records F_rec, M_rec (position sets), W = word. Returns a dict with Q for every nonempty T, Fld and the in-boundary self-feedback type."""
    eps = M.eps
    s = s_start
    Form = set(F_rec)           # formation records “earlier than t” (the entry-beat records precede every beat in W)
    Mark = set(M_rec)
    ev_log = []                 # (full avail_cond, full avail_act, p, act); the part relevant to each T is extracted afterwards
    for u in word:
        r = M.step(s, u)
        if r is None:
            return None
        s2, formed, marked, events = r
        sd = dict(zip(M.I, s))
        for (p, c, act) in events:
            ac = {i for i in p.supp_cond(sd, eps) if sd[i] > eps and i in Form}
            aa = {i for i in p.supp_act(sd) if sd[i] > eps and i in Form}
            ev_log.append((ac, aa, p, act))
        Form |= formed
        Mark |= marked
        s = s2
    end = dict(zip(M.I, s))
    out = {}
    fld, selfref = 0, 0
    for n in range(1, len(M.I) + 1):
        for T in combinations(M.I, n):
            T = set(T)
            src = 1 if any(i in Mark for i in T) else 0
            ret = 1 if all(end[i] > eps and i in Form for i in T) else 0
            trace = k_and(src, ret, 1, 1)   # Ident and HistEv declared as 1
            part = k_or(*[k_and(1 if (ac & T) else 0, p.cert_read)
                          for (ac, aa, p, act) in ev_log if p.car == 'B']) if ev_log else 0
            hd_terms, sr_terms = [], []
            for (ac, aa, p, act) in ev_log:
                hc = k_and(1 if (aa & T) else 0, p.cert_read, p.cert_path if (aa & T) else 0)
                hd_terms.append(k_and(1 if p.tgt in T else 0, act, hc))
                if p.car == 'B':
                    sr_terms.append(k_and(1 if p.tgt in T else 0, act, hc))
            hdyn = k_or(*hd_terms) if hd_terms else 0
            q = k_and(trace, part, hdyn)
            out[frozenset(T)] = q
            fld = k_or(fld, q)
            sr = k_and(q, k_or(*sr_terms) if sr_terms else 0)
            selfref = k_or(selfref, sr)
    return {'Q': out, 'Fld': fld, 'self': selfref, 'end': s}

def run_prefix(M, h):
    """Formative history: apply h from s0 and return (final state, Form position set, Mark position set)."""
    s, F, Mk = M.s0, set(), set()
    for u in h:
        r = M.step(s, u)
        if r is None:
            return None
        s, fo, ma, _ = r
        F |= fo
        Mk |= ma
    return s, frozenset(F), frozenset(Mk)

def fld_of_history(M, h, w):
    """Window-reading qualification of the input history h (W = last w beats, w ≥ 1)."""
    if w < 1:
        raise ValueError('w ≥ 1')
    if len(h) < w:
        return None
    pre = run_prefix(M, h[:len(h) - w])
    if pre is None:
        return None
    s, F, Mk = pre
    return window_eval(M, s, F, Mk, h[len(h) - w:])

def physical_reach(M, letters=None):
    letters = letters or list(M.letters)
    seen, dq = {M.s0}, deque([M.s0])
    while dq:
        s = dq.popleft()
        for u in letters:
            r = M.step(s, u)
            if r and r[0] not in seen:
                seen.add(r[0])
                dq.append(r[0])
    return seen

def augmented_search(M, w, target='Fld', letters=None):
    """Augmented search: nodes (s, Form, Mark); for each node, enumerate the window words of length w. Returns the shortest satisfying history and node statistics."""
    letters = letters or list(M.letters)
    start = (M.s0, frozenset(), frozenset())
    dist, par = {start: 0}, {start: None}
    dq = deque([start])
    while dq:
        node = dq.popleft()
        s, F, Mk = node
        for u in letters:
            r = M.step(s, u)
            if r is None:
                continue
            s2, fo, ma, _ = r
            nxt = (s2, F | fo, Mk | ma)
            if nxt not in dist:
                dist[nxt] = dist[node] + 1
                par[nxt] = (node, u)
                dq.append(nxt)
    best = None
    for node in sorted(dist, key=lambda n: dist[n]):
        if best is not None and dist[node] + w >= best[0]:
            break
        s, F, Mk = node
        for word in product(letters, repeat=w):
            e = window_eval(M, s, F, Mk, word)
            if e is not None and e[target] == 1:
                path, n = [], node
                while par[n] is not None:
                    n, u = par[n]
                    path.append(u)
                h = tuple(reversed(path)) + word
                if best is None or len(h) < best[0]:
                    best = (len(h), h)
                break
    N = len(physical_reach(M, letters))
    return {'best': best, 'nodes': len(dist), 'N': N,
            'max_prefix_dist': max(dist.values()), 'bound_prefix': (2 * len(M.I) + 1) * N - 1}

def brute_min(M, w, Lmax, target='Fld', letters=None):
    letters = letters or list(M.letters)
    for L in range(w, Lmax + 1):
        for h in product(letters, repeat=L):
            e = fld_of_history(M, h, w)
            if e is not None and e[target] == 1:
                return L, h
    return None
~~~
