# The General Form of Tuning

This paper takes up one master proposition:

When a tuning actually occurs — whether the regulated field is neural tissue, an artificial network, or some other concretized field; whether the actor is an intention, a rule retained in the field, or a device outside the field; whichever pathway it opens and whichever branch it suppresses — what structure must it carry that it cannot lose? This Group Form's initial stipulation on tuning is as follows. Tuning is one mode of action of Field Induction; its actual change to the regulated target is to open and close existing pathways by suppression and disinhibition and adjust their degree of permeability, constituting, on top of the existing physical connections, a dynamic logical structure that is a subset of the current Trace-Response-set structure. Tuning does not directly add or delete connections and does not directly rewrite parameters; what directly transforms the physical layer is host adjustment. An action that only enters as signals and is processed by the field's own basic rules is input; the deepening and shallowing that occur by the field's own basic rules after tuning has channeled the flow is sedimentation and does not change the classification. Who the actor is, and whether it is inside or outside the field, plays no part in this distinction. Tendency is the inclination that the organizational state of a field possesses, and the organizational state includes the patency of pathways and their degree of permeability; hence changing only patency, without changing any physical parameter, can also change the tendency. These are stipulations that this Group Form takes as premises without proof. *The General Form of Field Induction* has proved that an act of Field Induction necessarily has six duty groups on the same instance. Beyond these six groups, what else does tuning necessarily carry?

Object: a tuning Tune(κ), κ = (V, Xg, Xa, a, W, L, 𝓘), with an already established tuning as antecedent. V is the regulated field, that is, the induced field of this act of Field Induction (in the instance of *The General Form of Field Induction*, take F := V); Xg, Xa, a, W, L, 𝓘 are as in *The General Form of Field Induction*; Φ\_𝓘 reads the tendency of V. a may be a compound process; the part of it that acts by suppression and disinhibition is called the gating part. V may be one piece of a larger system; that larger system does not bear the arguments of this Form. Xg and Xa may be inside or outside V. Each of the above items stays fixed throughout every round of questioning.

Working domain and inheritance boundary: the antecedent is that Tune(κ) is established. V must on its own satisfy the working domain of *The General Form of Field Induction*: V is a concretized field (its declared mechanism admits, at the rule level, legitimate witnesses of the three duties of Trace-Response, organizational participation, and historical dynamics (historical action may be borne through retention or out-of-boundary processes), and it acquires Field-Concretization Qualification in some declared actual window, which need not be W); the reading is Φ\_𝓘 on L and 𝓘, and “changing the tendency” is read as the net tendency difference within W, ΔΦ\_W ≠ 0; V does not thereby acquire qualification merely by being a subdomain of some concretized field. The host and the dynamic layer must be declared: the physical layer of V (connections, weights, rules) and its dynamic layer (the current patency and degree of permeability on each existing connection) are written out separately; the input channels of V are written out in its field specification; the side to which cross-boundary connections belong is fixed in advance and not adjusted afterward. The inherited items are: the master theorem of *The General Form of Field Induction* v1.0 (six necessary duty groups, necessary direction) and its branching read-back of modes of action; the delimitation of direct write, input, and sedimentation in *The General Form of Host Adjustment* v1.0, and its reading of compound events marked by parts; this Group Form's initial stipulations on tuning and tendency; the delimitation of action and condition in Section 3 of *The General Form of Field Concretization*. The internal structure of tendency raising is developed by the other Forms of this Group Form; their conclusions are not premises of this Form.

We state in advance how far this question is answered. The sub-questions that actually arise and are answered are as follows.  
First, if a tuning has the six duty groups of Field Induction, is tuning thereby unfolded? No: there must be a gating part that opens and closes existing pathways by suppression and disinhibition (Argument Flow 1). Proved.  
Second, with a gating part as well, is it unfolded? No: the bearing of the tendency must originate in this gating change (Argument Flow 2). Proved. The bearing chain is constrained only at its starting point; the subsequent route may be the dynamic structure formed by the gating itself, or may pass through sedimentation by the field's own rules after the flow has been channeled.  
Third, how is an act to be read that contains gating, direct-write, and input parts at once, or that affects the regulated field through another subfield? First mark the gating, direct-write, input, and sedimentation parts by their actual mechanism; mechanism labels do not confer the event qualification of tuning or host adjustment; an influence through another subfield is reported separately by its actual landing point and route (Argument Flow 3). Proved.  
Fourth, are the following necessary: the goal specification being borne by an explicit intention; iteration; the reading reaching the target and stopping tuning in finite time; weight change or accompanying sedimentation within W; the actor being outside the regulated field? None is necessary. Proved (Argument Flow 5). Hijacking plays no part in the classification of tuning. Whether Tuning-Balance (also called Experience-Balance) is necessary: this Form gives no criterion; unanswered.  
Fifth, is it necessary that the content goal already be manifested, that the field be in a waking state, or that conscription pass through a convergence relation with Law qualification? Unanswered. Whether the dynamic structure is maintained after suppression and disinhibition are withdrawn: unanswered.  
Overall conclusion: a tuning necessarily has the six duty groups of Field Induction plus two items: a gating part that opens and closes existing pathways of the regulated field by suppression and disinhibition (Gate), and a bearing chain originating in this gating change (Bear\_G). Neither can be replaced by the six duty groups, and the latter cannot be replaced by the former; the converse is unproved.

In this paper we set out, through persistent questioning, to find where the proof must go, and to derive the General Form of Tuning.

Our goal is as follows:

Starting from the conclusions of *The General Form of Field Induction* and the initial stipulation on tuning, to unfold the mode stipulation “constituting a dynamic logical structure by suppression and disinhibition” into the duties that tuning necessarily carries; to prove that they are required by any established tuning and that they cannot be replaced by the six duty groups of Field Induction; to treat compound events and influences transmitted through another subfield; to exclude features often mistaken as necessary for tuning; and to mark the strength of what this Form proves.

We begin with the result.

What follows is the abbreviated form of the results of this paper. The proofs are in the Argument Flow, the Proof of Minimal Structure, and “Master Formula and Master Theorem”; the formal definitions are in the Appendix.

　　Tune(κ) ⇒ the six duty groups of Ind(κ) (F := V) ∧ Gate(a, V) ∧ Bear\_G(a, Φ\_𝓘; W)  
　　Bear\_G(a, Φ\_𝓘; W) :⇔ ∃τ ∈ W · BearAt\_G(a, Φ\_𝓘, τ)

Here Gate(a, V) says that at least one part of a opens and closes existing pathways of V by suppression and disinhibition and adjusts their degree of permeability, and that the dynamic logical structure so formed is a subset of V's current Trace-Response-set structure; BearAt\_G(a, Φ\_𝓘, τ) says that a has some actual gating part on V such that the patency change left on V by this same part takes part, as the start of an actual material route, in determining that part of the organization or conditions bearing Φ\_𝓘 at τ on which Φ\_𝓘 depends. The six duty groups and these two items take the same V, Xg, Xa, a, W, 𝓘 as arguments. Bear\_G implies Gate and Bear, while Gate does not imply Bear\_G: the two items are progressive duties, “there is gating” and “that gating bears the tendency”. This is the necessary direction; the converse is unproved.

In One Sentence

Within the declared net-tendency-difference working domain, an established tuning necessarily has the six duty groups of Field Induction, necessarily has a gating part that opens and closes existing pathways of the regulated field by suppression and disinhibition, and the bearing of the tendency originates in this gating change. Whether these suffice to identify tuning has not yet been proved.

Abstract

*The General Form of Field Induction* has shown that an established act of Field Induction necessarily has six duty groups on the same instance — starting organization, goal specification, net tendency difference, the act's action on the field, bearing chain, goal condition — and that it branches by implementation layer into host adjustment and tuning. *The General Form of Host Adjustment* has unfolded the branch that directly transforms the physical layer. This paper unfolds the other branch. Tuning, as a mode of Field Induction, carries all six duty groups; but the six groups do not restrict the route, and an act may write parameters directly or merely send in signals. Starting from an established tuning, this paper asks what more the mode stipulation forces beyond the six groups.

An act that writes connection weights directly according to a goal, and a process that sends in signals according to a goal and lets the field's own rules deepen connections, can both have all six duty groups; but the former is host adjustment and the latter is input plus sedimentation, and neither is tuning. Hence there must be a gating part that opens and closes existing pathways by suppression and disinhibition. Even given a gating part, that part may suppress a branch irrelevant to the reading while the change in tendency is borne entirely by simultaneously sent signals and sedimentation; tuning causes the field to have an inclination by way of gating, so the bearing must originate in this gating change. After the starting point of bearing, the route is unrestricted: the dynamic structure formed by gating can itself bear the tendency, and when gating has channeled the flow, the deepening of the connections of a disinhibited branch by the field's own rules is likewise a successor in the same bearing chain.

When an act contains gating, direct-write, and input parts at once, first mark each part by its actual mechanism; that some part carries a gating or direct-write label does not mean it is already established as a tuning or a host adjustment. Influences transmitted through another subfield are reported separately by actual landing point and route, not classified by source. For each of the following there is a tuning, established under the initial stipulation, that lacks it, so none is necessary structure: the goal specification being borne by an explicit intention; iteration; the reading reaching the target and stopping tuning in finite time; weight change or accompanying sedimentation within W; the actor being outside the field.

Proved: an established tuning has, on the same instance, the six duty groups, Gate, and Bear\_G; Gate cannot be replaced by the six groups, and Bear\_G cannot be replaced by the six groups together with Gate. Unanswered: the converse; whether Tuning-Balance is necessary; whether the content goal must already be manifested; field wakefulness; whether conscription must pass through a convergence relation with Law qualification; maintenance of the dynamic structure after control is withdrawn; whether the degree of integration can be regulated by suppression and disinhibition; uniqueness and global minimality.

Preliminary Distinctions

What follows only fixes objects and terms; it bears no part of the necessity of the structural items.

Gating, direct write, input, and sedimentation. Opening and closing existing pathways of V by suppression and disinhibition and adjusting their degree of permeability is called gating; gating acts only on connections that already exist, and the dynamic logical structure it forms (the currently open pathways and their degree of permeability) is a subset of the current Trace-Response-set structure. Changing the rules, parameters, or organization of V's host level, executed by the mechanism of the actor (or of a device it drives) itself, is called direct write; it is the mode of implementation of host adjustment. A signal entering V and being processed by V's own basic rules is called input; a physical-layer change caused by V's own basic rules is called sedimentation. The distinction looks at which layer the change falls on and by which route, not at variable names and not at the actor's position. One act may contain gating, direct-write, and input parts at once; these are marked by parts. Gating and direct write are labels of the mode of implementation, not event qualifications: a gating segment is not a tuning merely because it is gating, and a direct-write segment is not a host adjustment merely because it is direct write.

Bearing. Bearing is read as in *The General Form of Field Induction*: it requires only participation, not sole determination; the route may be direct, or may pass through retention, successor mechanisms, out-of-boundary processes, and round trips, or indecomposable joint processes; it suffices that one bearing moment exists within W, with no requirement of immediate visibility or of persistence to the end point; counterfactual comparison is a method of certification, not the content of bearing.

Hijacking. Hijacking means taking over the intention or replacing the tuning law; it is a separate concept and plays no part in the classification of tuning.

Gating host G₀. All constructed examples in the Argument Flow take the following declared host as the regulated field V.  
Physical layer: nodes x, h1, h2, h3, y1, y2; existing connections x→h1, h1→y1, x→h2, h2→y1, x→h3, h3→y2, each with a weight w(e); the basic rule of V is given below.  
Dynamic layer: on each existing connection e, a patency coefficient m(e) ∈ \[0, 1\] (0 is full suppression, 1 is fully open), determined by the current action of suppression and disinhibition; it is not a host parameter; m is defined only on existing connections. This construction does not stipulate whether m reverts after suppression and disinhibition are withdrawn.  
Reading: Φ = Σ\_{p: x→y1} Π\_{e∈p} w(e)·m(e), that is, the flux reaching y1 through open pathways when x = 1 is read in (𝓘 = {x = 1}; the reading is a mathematical read-out and injects no signal into V); the h3 branch is not on the reading path.  
Basic rule (flux write-back): when an input signal s enters x, compute for each pathway p from x to an output (y1 or y2) its flux f\_p = s·Π\_{e∈p} w(e)·m(e), and each connection e writes w(e) ← w(e) + (1/10)·Σ\_{p∋e} f\_p. With no input there is no flux on V and the weights are unchanged; connections with no flux are unchanged.  
Qualification window W\_q (prior to the W of each example): at the start every weight is 1, except w(h2→y1) = 2; the gating pattern is x→h1 and x→h2 fully suppressed, x→h3 fully open; within W\_q an input s = 1 enters once, the flux on the h3 branch is 1, w(x→h3) and w(h3→y2) change from 1 to 11/10, and the other connections have no flux and are unchanged.  
Lemma 0  G₀ acquires Field-Concretization Qualification on W\_q.  
Proof: Trace-Response: the input s leaves a change on w(x→h3) and w(h3→y2) (1 → 11/10); the change is retained beyond W\_q, and its source is the passage of a signal. Organizational participation: this write computes the flux from the current w and the current m. Historical dynamics: the increment (1/10)·f of this actual write within W\_q is determined by the w already left, and acts again on the same w. All three have actual bearing on the same Trace-Response set {w(e)} within W\_q. ∎  
The W of each example lies after W\_q. Start: the weights take their values after W\_q (11/10 on the two connections of the h3 branch, w(h2→y1) = 2, 1 elsewhere); the gating pattern m\_r is x→h1 fully open, x→h2 fully suppressed, x→h3 fully open, all others fully open; Φ = 1. Which inputs are sent within W, and who performs which gating, is stated in place for each example; unless stated otherwise, there is no input within W. Lemma 0 gives qualification on W\_q and does not imply qualification within the W of each example; the working domain of this Form also requires only the former.

Argument Flow

1

What is currently known is the master theorem of *The General Form of Field Induction* (F := V), the initial stipulation on tuning, the instance declaration, and the working domain. On these alone, is it already clear which structures a tuning carries at minimum?

It is not. A tuning is an act of Field Induction, and all six duty groups are present: V has a starting organization; Xg bears the goal specification; the net tendency difference within W is nonzero; a acts on V; a bears the tendency at some moment within W; the goal takes part in determining a. But the six duty groups do not restrict the route.

Consider two processes. First: V = G₀; Xg holds the goal specification “strengthen the output from x to y1”, and Xa accordingly writes w(h1→y1) directly from 1 to 5; Φ goes from 1 to 5. Second: the same goal; Xa, according to the goal, sends in one input s = 1, V's basic rule writes back by flux, w(x→h1) and w(h1→y1) change from 1 to 11/10, and Φ goes from 1 to 121/100. Each of the six duty groups can be read off in both processes: the starting organization is there; the goal specification is borne by Xg; the net tendency difference is nonzero; a acts on V; the change made by a (written directly in the former, via V's rules in the latter) bears Φ; a is determined by the goal. But the former directly transforms the physical layer and is host adjustment; in the latter every change in V is executed by V's own rules, and it is input followed by sedimentation. Neither involves any suppression or disinhibition. Under the initial stipulation, neither is tuning. The six duty groups are not enough.

Lemma 1 (Gating)  In any Tune(κ), at least one part of a opens and closes existing pathways of V by suppression and disinhibition and adjusts their degree of permeability, and the dynamic logical structure so formed is a subset of V's current Trace-Response-set structure. Write Gate(a, V).  
Proof: Take any established Tune(κ). By the initial stipulation on tuning, the actual change this action makes to V is to open and close existing pathways by suppression and disinhibition and adjust their degree of permeability, constituting a dynamic logical structure that is a subset of the current Trace-Response-set structure; this actual change is executed by some part of a, and that part is the gating part on V, so Gate(a, V) holds. If a had no such part, the instance would not satisfy the mode condition of the initial stipulation, contradicting Tune(κ). ∎  
Direct write, and action that only enters as signals (with the sedimentation that follows it), are both examples that fail this mode condition; we do not claim that they exhaust all non-gating action. A gating part only opens and closes existing connections; it neither adds nor deletes connections and does not rewrite parameters. If a also contains direct-write or input parts, these are marked by parts.

2

We now have the six duty groups and Gate. Is tuning closed?

No. Let V = G₀, let Xg hold the goal specification “strengthen the output from x to y1”, and let Xa issue a accordingly, a consisting of two parts: the gating part fully suppresses x→h3 (m from 1 to 0); then the input part sends in one input s = 1, V's basic rule writes back by flux, the two connections of the h1 branch change from 1 to 11/10, and Φ goes from 1 to 121/100. Both parts are determined by the goal. The six duty groups hold — the bearing chain runs through the signal sent in and sedimentation; Gate also holds — x→h3 is suppressed. But remove the gating part and Φ still goes from 1 to 121/100; remove the input part and Φ is unchanged. The ground is not this comparison alone: the h3 branch is not on the reading path, and the patency of x→h3 does not affect the flux or write-back of the h1 and h2 branches, so the gating change takes no part in bearing the tendency anywhere in W. The change in tendency is borne entirely by input and sedimentation. This is a witness that a deletion candidate is insufficient, not a positive instance of tuning.

Lemma 2 (Bearing originating in the gating change)  In any Tune(κ), there exist τ ∈ W and some actual gating part a\_G of a on V such that the patency change left on V by this same a\_G takes part, as the start of an actual material route, in determining that part of the organization or conditions bearing Φ\_𝓘 at τ on which Φ\_𝓘 depends. Write BearAt\_G(a, Φ\_𝓘, τ), and Bear\_G(a, Φ\_𝓘; W) :⇔ ∃τ ∈ W · BearAt\_G(a, Φ\_𝓘, τ).  
Proof: Tuning is Field Induction carried out in the mode of constituting a dynamic logical structure by suppression and disinhibition; in tuning, the bearing chain of *The General Form of Field Induction* (“causing the field to have an inclination”) must be borne by this mode. Suppose the patency change left on V by every gating part of a takes no part in bearing the tendency anywhere in W. Then a's bearing of the tendency runs entirely through its non-gating parts; the “causing the field to have an inclination” of this act of Field Induction is not borne by gating and is not achieved in the gating mode, contradicting Tune(κ). ∎  
The same a may contain several gating segments: it suffices that one of them satisfies Lemma 2, and not every gating segment is required to bear; nor may a bearing chain be pieced together from different segments or different events. In the previous example the change that suppresses x→h3 satisfies Lemma 2 at no moment of W, and is excluded.

Lemma 2 constrains only the starting point of the bearing chain, not the route after it. Consider two examples. First: V = G₀; Xg holds the goal specification “strengthen the output from x to y1”, and Xa accordingly suppresses x→h1 and disinhibits x→h2; the weights are unchanged and Φ goes from 1 to 2; the bearing is given directly by the dynamic structure that the gating forms. Second: after the same gating, one input s = 1 enters and V's basic rule writes back by flux: the suppressed h1 branch has no flux and its weights are unchanged; the disinhibited h2 branch has flux 2, w(x→h2) changes from 1 to 6/5 and w(h2→y1) from 2 to 11/5; Φ goes from 2 to 66/25. The weight change is executed by V's own rules; it is sedimentation, not gating; but which branch has flux, and hence which branch is deepened, is decided by the gating. The bearing chain a\_G →(patency) flux routing →(V's own rules) w(h2 branch) → Φ passes through sedimentation without changing the classification. With all pathways open, the reading changes from 3 to 91/25; the deepening remains in the physical layer after this gating pattern is withdrawn; this is the retention of sedimentation, not the maintenance of the dynamic structure formed by gating. If the bearing chain were required to be given directly by the dynamic structure alone, this successor route would be excluded.

Lemma 2 is a specialization of Bear in *The General Form of Field Induction*: the starting point is restricted to the patency change left on V by some actual gating part, and the reading is otherwise unchanged; no in-boundary, immediacy, or end-point requirement is added.

3

We now have the six duty groups, Gate, and Bear\_G. When an act contains several kinds of parts, or affects V through another subfield, how is it read relative to V?

A compound act is read in two layers. First layer, marking by actual mechanism: gating parts, direct-write parts, input parts, and the physical write-back subsequently formed by V's own rules (sedimentation). Second layer, event qualification: only a part that has independently been established as a tuning or a host adjustment has the necessary duties of the corresponding General Form read on its event arguments; a mere gating or direct-write label does not confer the corresponding event qualification. The countermodel of Argument Flow 2 is a case in point: the part in it that suppresses x→h3 is gating, yet does not bear the tendency declared for this event and is not a positive instance of tuning. If an ineffective gating segment is added to an act that contains effective gating, the added segment does not acquire qualification merely by belonging to the same act; the reading, time window, or event arguments may not be changed to supply it with qualification. Lemma 1 of the two General Forms is, respectively, tuning ⇒ Gate and host adjustment ⇒ Rewrite; each is used only as a necessary condition, never for identification in the reverse direction.

Next consider signals from outside V. Suppose there is a control unit U outside V: an external signal s′ enters U's input, and U, according to the goal specification it holds, “strengthen the output from x to y1”, selects a gating pattern, suppressing x→h1 and disinhibiting x→h2; Φ goes from 1 to 2. Under the initial stipulation this act is tuning: it is led by the goal specification U holds, opens and closes existing pathways of V by suppression and disinhibition, and changes V's tendency. Reported separately: s′ is input relative to U; U's suppression and disinhibition of V is gating and satisfies Gate relative to V. The source of the external signal does not change the classification of this tuning.

If an act acts only on another subfield V″, its influence on V enters only through declared input channels and is processed by V's own basic rules, and it has no gating or direct-write landing point on V, then relative to V it is not tuning: the cross-boundary part is input, and the physical write-back formed in V by V's own rules is sedimentation; relative to V there is no gating part, so Gate(a, V), the conclusion of Lemma 1, fails. Whether it is input, gating, or direct write at V is not decided by source alone: if the output of V″ opens and closes existing pathways of V through a suppression channel declared as belonging to V, what happens at the V end is gating; if it drives a direct write of V's physical parameters, there is a direct-write landing point at V. These must be determined from the actual chain and the prior declaration.

4

We now have the six duty groups, Gate, and Bear\_G. Is tuning closed?

Check the initial stipulation word by word. Tuning is Field Induction — the six duty groups (inherited). “Opening and closing existing pathways by suppression and disinhibition and adjusting their degree of permeability”, “constituting a dynamic logical structure”, “a subset of the current Trace-Response-set structure” — Gate (Lemma 1). Gating “thereby” causing the field to have an inclination — Bear\_G (Lemma 2) and the net tendency difference. The initial stipulation states explicitly that the actor's position plays no part in the distinction, so no position item is set up. Forcing stops.

5

We now have the six duty groups, Gate, and Bear\_G. What other features are often thought necessary for tuning? We test them one by one. In each example below V is G₀ (with the start as in the Preliminary Distinctions), and there is no input within W unless stated otherwise. Each example is directly a tuning under the initial stipulation: the act is led by a goal specification, opens and closes existing pathways of V by suppression and disinhibition, and changes V's tendency. The examples are not obtained by reasoning backward from the necessary conditions; the six duty groups, Gate, and Bear\_G can be read off one by one in each.

The actor being outside the regulated field: not necessary. Besides the physical and dynamic layers of G₀, V contains an internal control unit: it holds the goal specification “strengthen the output from x to y1” and accordingly suppresses x→h1 and disinhibits x→h2; Φ goes from 1 to 2. The actor is inside V; the same gating pattern applied by a device outside V is equally tuning, see Argument Flow 3.

The goal specification being borne by an explicit intention: not necessary. V contains a retained Biased-Flow rule: it reads in an experiential differential d (d \> 0 indicates an Experience-Target on the y1 side) and selects a gating pattern according to d; when d \> 0 it suppresses x→h1 and disinhibits x→h2. In this example d = 1 and Φ goes from 1 to 2. The goal specification (“strengthen the output from x to y1”) is borne by this retained rule, with no intention involved. This example shows only that the bearer of the goal specification need not be an explicit intention; it does not certify whether this retained rule has the qualification of a Law.

Iteration, repetition: not necessary. The two examples above each have only one gating, one step, with Φ going from 1 to 2.

The reading reaching the target and stopping tuning in finite time: not necessary. Xg holds the fixed goal specification “the output strength from x to y1 reaches 3”, with Φ\* = 3 as the reading target and the stopping rule “stop when Φ = 3”; Xa contains a two-valued state s\_k, s\_1 = 1, s\_{k+1} = 1 − s\_k; at step k it applies gating according to s\_k: when s\_k = 0, x→h1 is fully open and x→h2 fully suppressed; when s\_k = 1, x→h1 is fully suppressed and x→h2 fully open; the two branches are never open together. Throughout the run (all k ≥ 1) there is no input and the weights are unchanged. Induction: for all k, the gating pattern is determined by s\_k, and s\_k ∈ {0, 1}; when s\_k = 0, Φ = 1, and when s\_k = 1, Φ = 2; hence for all k, Φ\_k ∈ {1, 2} ≠ 3, the stopping rule never fires, and the goal, though unchanged, is never reached and tuning never stops; each step has \|ΔΦ\| = 1, with no monotone progress. Under the net-difference reading, only windows of an odd number of steps are taken. If there were input during the run, the write-back rule of the same V would change the weights and Φ would leave {1, 2}, and the induction of this example would no longer apply; the conclusion of this example holds only under the protocol with no input throughout.

Weight change within W, accompanying sedimentation: not necessary. In the in-field control unit example above, there is no input within W, no connection weight changes at all, and Φ goes from 1 to 2; there is no physical-layer write-back whatever within W. We claim here only that there is no physical write-back within W, not that every state reverts after the gating is withdrawn; whether the dynamic structure is maintained after control is withdrawn is unanswered. Tuning may also be accompanied by sedimentation; see the second example of Argument Flow 2.

Each example above is a tuning that lacks the corresponding feature; the missing features are therefore not necessary structure of tuning.

Changing connections and rewriting parameters are not contained in the gating part of tuning: this is excluded by the initial stipulation, not by a test of non-necessity. If an act contains either, that part is marked as direct write, and whether it is established as host adjustment is checked separately under *The General Form of Host Adjustment*. Hijacking plays no part in the classification of tuning: suppose there is, outside V, a rule H that replaces the originally designated gating rule and, according to the goal held by H, fully suppresses x→h1 and x→h2; Φ goes from 1 to 0. If this act is established as an act of Field Induction, its route is still suppression and disinhibition, and its classification under the initial stipulation is tuning; whether it is hijacking is judged separately and does not change the classification.

Tuning-Balance. The alternating-gating example proves only that the reading never equals the target Φ\* = 3 and that the equality stopping rule never fires. This Form gives no criterion for “balance” and no correspondence between balance and the reading target or the stopping rule; hence it does not infer from this that balance fails, nor does it read any oscillation as imbalance. Whether Tuning-Balance is necessary for tuning is unanswered.

Proof of Minimal Structure

Load-bearing classification. The six duty groups are inherited from the master theorem of *The General Form of Field Induction* v1.0 (taking F := V; the condition for citing it is that V on its own satisfies that Form's working domain, which the working-domain declaration of this Form guarantees); Gate is proved by Lemma 1; Bear\_G is proved by Lemma 2, with the reading of bearing inherited from *The General Form of Field Induction*. Each of the two lemmas is drawn out by a gap in the corresponding round of the Argument Flow: a countermodel shows that what was obtained before is insufficient, and the lemma proves, from the initial stipulation on tuning, that the item is necessary for any established tuning. This Form has no external conditions awaiting verification: the Field-Concretization Qualification of V and the declaration of its host and dynamic layer are working-domain stipulations, not antecedents awaiting proof, but downstream citations must retain them.

Irreplaceability. The following are witnesses that deletion candidates are insufficient, not breaches of the master theorem:  
(i) six duty groups ⇏ Gate (Argument Flow 1: weights written directly; signals sent in followed by sedimentation);  
(ii) six duty groups ∧ Gate ⇏ Bear\_G (Argument Flow 2: the gating falls on the h3 branch, which is not on the reading path, and the tendency is borne by simultaneously sent signals and sedimentation).  
Both countermodels preserve everything obtained before and lack only the item under test.

Implication and non-equivalence. Bear\_G implies Gate (the bearing originates in some actual gating part) and Bear; Gate does not imply Bear\_G, nor does Bear imply Bear\_G (for both, see Argument Flow 2). Logically the two admit absorption, but their duties differ: Gate expresses “there is gating”, Bear\_G expresses “that gating bears the tendency”. Keeping both is a progressive unfolding, not a conjunction of mutually non-implying independent items.

Exclusion of proposed additions. For each item listed in Argument Flow 5 there is a tuning, established under the initial stipulation, that lacks it; none enters the master formula.

Scope. Minimality is limited to this: the listed weaker candidates cannot replace the duties unfolded. Uniqueness and global minimality are unproved.

Master Formula and Master Theorem

Master formula (necessary direction):

　　Tune(κ) ⇒ ∃T, g \[ Org₀(V, T) ∧ GoalRep(Xg, g) ∧ Target(g, V) ∧ ΔΦ\_W ≠ 0 ∧ Eff(a, V) ∧ Bear(a, Φ\_𝓘; W) ∧ GoalCond(a, g) ∧ Gate(a, V) ∧ Bear\_G(a, Φ\_𝓘; W) \]

Read-back. κ = (V, Xg, Xa, a, W, L, 𝓘); V is a concretized field that on its own satisfies the working domain of *The General Form of Field Induction*, with its physical and dynamic layers declared. The first seven atomic predicates are read back as in *The General Form of Field Induction* and together form the six duty groups. Gate(a, V): at least one part of a opens and closes existing pathways of V by suppression and disinhibition and adjusts their degree of permeability; the dynamic logical structure so formed is a subset of V's current Trace-Response-set structure; it neither adds nor deletes connections and does not rewrite parameters. Bear\_G(a, Φ\_𝓘; W): there exist a moment τ within W and some actual gating part a\_G of a on V such that the patency change left on V by this same a\_G takes part, as the start of an actual material route, in determining that part of the organization or conditions bearing Φ\_𝓘 at τ on which Φ\_𝓘 structurally depends; the route after that is unrestricted and may run through the dynamic structure itself or through sedimentation after the flow has been channeled. All items share the same V, Xg, Xa, a, W, 𝓘. Bear\_G implies Gate and Bear.

Master Theorem  Within the working domain, for any Tune(κ), all items on the right-hand side of the master formula hold simultaneously.

Proof: Tuning is Field Induction, so by the master theorem of *The General Form of Field Induction* v1.0 (F := V, with V satisfying that Form's working domain) it has the six duty groups; Lemma 1 gives Gate; Lemma 2 gives Bear\_G. All items take the same arguments of the same instance κ, so the conjunction holds. ∎

Strength. The master theorem states the necessary direction. Whether the right-hand side implies Tune(κ) is unproved; its items do not serve as criteria for identifying tuning.

Corollary (Separate reporting through another subfield)  If an act acts only on another subfield V″, its influence on V enters only through declared input channels and is processed by V's own basic rules, and it has no gating or direct-write landing point on V, then relative to V it is not tuning (Argument Flow 3).  
Proof: a has no gating landing point on V, so Gate(a, V) fails, and by the contrapositive of Lemma 1 it is not tuning relative to V. ∎  
For the two-layer reading of compound acts, mechanism marking and event qualification, see Argument Flow 3; mechanism labels do not confer event qualification.

Breach Modes

With the object domain, working domain, and physical-layer and dynamic-layer declarations stated by this Form all kept, exhibit a tuning in which Gate or Bear\_G fails; or prove that one of the two can be deleted, or weakened to a tested candidate, while tuning still holds; or show that the proof of Lemma 1 or Lemma 2 fails. Exhibiting only another physical realization of the same duty is not a breach; if the V of a counterexample lacks Field-Concretization Qualification, or its physical and dynamic layers were not declared in advance or were altered afterward, it is a boundary case, not a breach.
