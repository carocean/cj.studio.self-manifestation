# The General Form of Tendency Raising

This paper takes up one master proposition:

When a tendency raising is actually completed — whether the field whose tendency is raised is neural tissue, an artificial network, or some other concretized field; whether the acquirer is an initialization program outside the field, a maintenance mechanism within the field, or the formation of the host itself; and whether the starting organization is written, restored, or randomly generated — what structure must it carry that it cannot lose? This Group Form's initial stipulation on tendency raising is as follows: tendency raising pre-builds a concretized field and acquires an initial organizational state of the Trace-Response set, and Field Induction starts from this organizational state. Host adjustment and tuning are both adjustment, and nothing can be adjusted unless there is something to adjust; so there must first be a network, which need not be uniform at the outset but must have a tendency; what tendency raising acquires is the first thing to be adjusted. Tendency raising is delimited by this position it holds in Field Induction, not by whether the acquirer has a purpose, whether it serves some particular act of Field Induction, or whether it adopts a generalized structure. These are stipulations that this Group Form takes as premises without proof. Within this stipulation, which structures does a tendency raising necessarily carry; what does each of them bear, and how are they mutually irreplaceable?

Object: a tendency raising Prime(κ), κ = (F, H, M, R, o, t*, T*), with an already completed tendency raising as antecedent. F is the field whose tendency is raised, H its host, and M its declared mechanism; R = \[t₀, t₁\] is the run window that the tendency raising serves; o is the tendency-raising process under adjudication, and t\* is the time at which o is completed; T\* is the organization of the Trace-Response set of F when o is completed. Each of the above items stays fixed throughout every round of questioning; changing any of them changes the instance under adjudication.

Working domain and inheritance boundary: the antecedent is that Prime(κ) has been completed. A single field and a single host; the mechanism of F, the host boundary, and the run window are declared in advance. The route of acquisition may be writing, construction, moving in, restoration, or formation along with the host; the constructed examples of this paper use only writing and restoration among these, and this paper prescribes no uniform port for all tendency raising. Concretization qualification is adjudicated by the criteria of the Forms of Field Concretization; this paper does not certify material hosts. Mechanism events are exhausted by the declaration: the declaration of M lists all mechanism events that can occur on the host of F, including out-of-boundary processes that act on F through declared channels; undeclared events are not counted. This restriction takes effect before Lemma 2 is first invoked. Absence of a connection is represented by an uninstalled connection, not by a zero weight. The inherited items are: the initial stipulations of this Group Form for tendency raising, Field Induction, and tendency; the reading in *The General Form of Field Induction* v1.0 of the starting organization Org₀ and of “bearing a tendency” (the part on which Φ depends); the three duties of the Sedimentation-Field in *The General Form of Field Concretization* v1.0 — Trace-Response set (TraceSet), organizational participation (Part), and historical dynamics (HDyn) — which take the same Trace-Response set as their object, organizational participation and historical dynamics being existential process duties, and in which historical conditions may take part, via retention or out-of-boundary routes, in determining the occurrence, timing, position, direction, or strength of an action; Section 2 of *The Mathematical Form of Field Concretization* v1.0, “the concretization qualification of a Sedimentation-Field is a function of mechanism witnesses, not a function of the state trajectory,” restricted to its corresponding systems and read-back conditions; the correspondence conditions in Sections 1 and 2 of *The Mathematical Form of Duty Correspondence in Field Concretization* v1.0 for the transfer of duties, qualification, and dynamics between different declared instances, these conditions being sufficient conditions of the corresponding preservation theorems; and the stipulations of the Computation Group Form on initial field states, legitimate taking-over, reading-in of field states, and object rules. The internal structures of host adjustment and tuning are developed by the other Forms of this Group Form, and their conclusions are not premises of this Form.

We state in advance how far this question is answered. The sub-questions that actually arise and are answered are as follows.  
(1) Given only the host, the mechanism, and the temporal position, is there already a tendency raising? No; there must be an initial organization that is in place, nonempty, and tendency-bearing (Argument Flow 1). Proved. No nonzero threshold is set for any reading.  
(2) Given such an organization as well, is there already a tendency raising? No; the declared mechanism of F must, at the rule level, permit legitimate witnesses of the three duties of Trace-Response, organizational participation, and historical dynamics (Argument Flow 2). Proved. The three are not required to be borne by the same process, and write-back is not required to read the current field state directly at the moment of writing. This is a mechanism condition at the rule level, not Field-Concretization Qualification over any actual window.  
(3) Given the mechanism condition as well, is there already a tendency raising? No; the organization must be the starting organization of the run window served (Argument Flow 3). Proved.  
(4) Given the temporal position as well, is there already a tendency raising? No; the process under adjudication must actually acquire the organization (Argument Flow 4). Proved.  
(5) Are the following necessary: an acquirer outside the field; a uniform, flat, or nonzero-reading starting point; a starting organization acquired by prior learning inside the run served; an acquirer that has a purpose or serves some particular act of Field Induction; a generalized structure obtained by content alignment taken as the pre-building scheme; Field Induction actually occurring after the tendency raising? None of them is necessary. Proved (Argument Flow 6). Whether the acquired organization has the structural properties of a generalized structure is something for which this Form has no criterion; unanswered.  
(6) Whether the starting organization lends itself to some kind of Field Induction (inducibility): unanswered.  
Overall conclusion: a tendency raising necessarily has four duties on the same instance — an initial organization in place and bearing a tendency, a mechanism condition at the rule level, a starting temporal position relative to the run window served, and actual acquisition by the process under adjudication. They cannot be replaced by the weaker conditions examined; whether they suffice to establish tendency raising (the converse) is unproved.

In this paper we set out, through persistent questioning, to find where the proof must go, and to derive the General Form of Tendency Raising.

Our goal is as follows:

Starting from the initial stipulation and the working domain, unfold item by item the arguments and relations implicit in the four terms “the first thing to be adjusted,” “pre-build a concretized field,” “initial,” and “acquire”; prove that each item is required by any completed tendency raising; prove that they cannot be replaced by the weaker conditions examined; exclude non-necessary features that are often mistaken for necessary ones; separate the establishment of tendency raising, source and taking-over, and prior intervention; and mark the strength of what this Form proves.

We begin with the result.

What follows is the abbreviated form of the results of this paper. The proofs are in the Argument Flow, the Proof of Minimal Structure, and “Master Formula and Master Theorem”; the formal definitions are in the Appendix.

　　Prime(κ) ⇒ Pot(T*, F; R) ∧ MechC(F, M) ∧ Init(T*; R) ∧ Acq(o, T\*; H)

Here Pot says that T\* lies on F's own host, is nonempty, and bears the tendency of F; MechC says that the declared mechanism of F permits, at the rule level, legitimate witnesses of the three duties of Trace-Response, organizational participation, and historical dynamics; Init says that t\* ≤ t₀ and T\* is the starting organization of R; Acq says that the actual change o makes to H constitutes T*, and that T* persists from t\* to t₀. The four items take the same F, H, M, R, o, t*, T* as arguments. This is the necessary direction; the converse is unproved.

In One Sentence

A completed tendency raising must have an initial organization that lies on the host of the field whose tendency is raised, is nonempty, and bears a tendency, and must have a mechanism that permits the formation of sedimentation at the rule level; this organization must be the starting point of the run served, and must be actually acquired by the process under adjudication. Whether these suffice to establish tendency raising has not yet been proved.

Abstract

Host adjustment and tuning are both adjustment, and nothing can be adjusted unless there is something to adjust. What tendency raising acquires is the first thing to be adjusted: a network that need not be uniform at the outset but has a tendency, and from which Field Induction starts. Starting from a tendency raising already completed, this paper asks what each of the four terms of this stipulation carries.

The host may be there, the mechanism there, and the temporal position right, and yet there may be no organization at all: not a single connection on the host is installed. Or a set of numbers may be stored in a log outside the field that the field never reads in. Neither gives the first thing to be adjusted; hence there must be an initial organization on the field's own host that is nonempty and bears a tendency. Bearing a tendency is not the same as a nonzero reading: an organization whose excitatory and inhibitory branches cancel, giving a reading of zero, bears a tendency all the same. With such an organization in place, the mechanism may still be frozen: the reading depends on the weights, but no event on the host changes any connection, and no run can form sedimentation. What tendency raising pre-builds is a concretized field; hence there must be a mechanism condition at the rule level — the declared mechanism permits legitimate witnesses of the three duties, which need not be borne by the same process and need not read the current field state at the moment of write-back. This condition is not the same as Field-Concretization Qualification over any actual window. With organization and mechanism both present, the organization may still have been written partway through the run; that is an intervention within the run, not tendency raising; hence there must be a starting temporal position. With the first three all present, the process under adjudication may still have nothing to do with the acquisition; hence there must be actual acquisition.

The acquirer may be a maintenance mechanism within the field; the starting point need not be uniform, and the reading need not be nonzero; the starting organization may be written before the run it serves; the acquirer may have no purpose, serve no particular act of Field Induction, and take no generalized structure previously obtained by content alignment as its pre-building scheme; and after the tendency raising there may be only the field's own running. For each of these, some tendency raising completed according to the initial stipulation lacks it, so none of them is a necessary structure. The tendency structure that tendency raising acquires may have a long formative history; the “starting point” is relative only to the run served and does not erase the source history; the establishment of tendency raising, the legitimacy of the source, and the nature of prior intervention are recorded separately.

Proved: a completed tendency raising has, on the same instance, the four duties Pot, MechC, Init, and Acq, and none of them can be replaced by the weaker conditions examined. Unanswered: the converse; inducibility; the degree of tendency bearing; actual Field-Concretization Qualification within the run served; a structural criterion for whether the acquired organization has a generalized structure; the conditions under which a generalized structure passes into tendency raising; the qualification of material hosts; uniqueness and global minimality.

Preliminary Distinctions

What follows only fixes objects and terms; it bears no part of the necessity of the structural items.

Starting point and source history. The “starting point” is relative only to the run window R served. The same organization can be the starting point of R and also have a long formative history; establishing tendency raising does not erase the source history. The tendency structure that tendency raising acquires may first be formed elsewhere through content alignment and then be acquired directly at tendency raising: for example, some animals can walk from birth, and the tendency structure of their walking is already present when the brain's tendency is raised; it may have sedimented into heredity through long content alignment and passed from there into tendency raising. This only illustrates the distinction between source history and starting point; it is not offered as a proved biological mechanism.

Establishment of tendency raising, legitimacy of the source, prior intervention. The three are recorded separately: whether an organization became the starting point of R through a tendency raising is adjudicated by the necessary structure and the initial stipulation of this Form; whether the source of the organization is legitimate (conditions of formation, persistence, and moving in) is adjudicated by the stipulations of the Computation Group Form on legitimate taking-over; if the organization was formed in an earlier window by an intervention within the run, the nature of that intervention (host adjustment or intrusion) is registered as is and does not vanish with the change of window.

Routes of acquisition. The name of a route confers no tendency-raising qualification: whether a process is a tendency raising is adjudicated for the instance by the initial stipulation and the four necessary structures; it is not established by the name of the route, nor inferred backward from the necessary structures. Pre-building: acquisition by writing or construction; the acquirer may be within or outside the field. Restoration: restoring from a saved organization. Predetermination: arising with the formation of the host. Taking-over has three layers, which are not to be conflated: taking-over of numerical or field-state initial values must satisfy the conditions of formation, persistence, and moving in of the Computation Group Form; taking-over of a source organization or of object identity requires a separate declaration of the object taken over and of the correspondence map; transfer of conclusions about duties, qualification, or dynamics follows the stipulations of *The Mathematical Form of Duty Correspondence in Field Concretization* v1.0 on transfer between different declared instances, whose correspondence conditions are sufficient conditions of the corresponding preservation theorems, and failure to meet these conditions does not yield a verdict that taking-over or transfer is impossible. Intrusion is an intervention within the run window, not a route of acquisition for tendency raising.

Mechanism condition and Field-Concretization Qualification. The mechanism condition is a stipulation at the rule level: whether the complete declared mechanism of F permits legitimate witnesses of the three duties of Trace-Response, organizational participation, and historical dynamics. Field-Concretization Qualification is a fact over some actual window: whether the three duties of the same Trace-Response set are actually borne within that window. The former is a necessary mechanism premise for the latter but does not imply it; when tendency raising is completed, R has not yet run, and this Form outputs no Field-Concretization Qualification within R.

Host Q. The constructed examples in the Argument Flow all lie on the declared host below; the organization and rules declared separately for each example are written in place.  
Nodes x, h1, h2, y. The connections that can be installed are x→h1, h1→y, x→h2, h2→y; the set E of connections installed at a given time is part of the host's organization, and each installed connection e has a weight w(e) \> 0 and a sign σ(e) ∈ {+1, −1} (excitatory or inhibitory; +1 unless otherwise stated). Each installed connection also carries a patency coefficient m(e) ∈ \[0, 1\]; each is fully patent unless otherwise stated.  
Reading: Φ = Σ\_{p: x→y, p ⊆ E} Π\_{e∈p} σ(e)·w(e)·m(e) (𝓘 = {x = 1}; a mathematical read-out, injecting no signal into F).  
Mechanism M\_sed (flux write-back): when an input s enters x, compute f\_p = s·Π\_{e∈p} σ(e)·w(e)·m(e) for every pathway p made up entirely of installed connections, and write each connection as w(e) ← w(e) + (1/10)·Σ\_{p∋e} \|f\_p\|. With no input the weights do not change; both the reading and the write-back read in the current E, w, σ, m.  
Mechanism M\_frz (frozen): reading as above; when input passes through, no connection is written back.  
Time is counted in integer beats.

Argument Flow

1

What is currently known is the initial stipulation, the instance declaration, and the working domain. On these alone, is it already clear what structures a tendency raising carries at minimum?

It is not. The initial stipulation says that tendency raising acquires the first thing to be adjusted, but it does not say what this thing must have at the starting point.

Consider two cases. Case 1: the host of F is Q, the mechanism is M\_sed, the declaration is complete, and the temporal position is right: t\* = t₀; but at t₀, E = ∅, and not a single connection is installed. The reading is constantly 0; no input has a pathway to pass along, no write-back has a connection to land on, and no opening or closing has a connection to act on. Host, mechanism, and temporal position are all present, yet there is no organization at all on F. Case 2: the organization T\* under adjudication is a set of numbers (1, 1, 1, 2), stored in a log outside F and recorded in the order x→h1, h1→y, x→h2, h2→y; F's mechanism never reads in this log, and F's reading and trajectory do not change with the log. These numbers are nonempty and nonuniform, but they are not on F and are not a field state of F. Neither case gives the first thing to be adjusted.

Lemma 1 (Initial organization in place and bearing a tendency)  In any Prime(κ), the same T\* satisfies: (a) placement — T\* persists on F's own host H, is a field state of F, and is in a position where it can be read in within R (only being readable in is required, not that it be actually read in entirely within R); (b) nonemptiness — T\* contains at least one retained change of a connection or a Structural Position; (c) tendency bearing — the successor response of F under the declared inputs is a function of T\* and is not constant over the possible values of some part of T*, and the successor of F is not wholly determined by object rules that bypass the field state. Write Pot(T*, F; R).  
Proof: Take any completed Prime(κ). By the initial stipulation, what tendency raising acquires is the initial organizational state of the Trace-Response set of F, so T\* is a field state of F and lies on F's own host; it is the organization from which Field Induction starts, so it is in a position where it can be read in within R — (a). The initial stipulation says that what tendency raising acquires is “a network,” the first thing to be adjusted; when the Trace-Response set is empty there is nothing to adjust, a contradiction — (b). The initial stipulation says that this network “must have a tendency”; a tendency is the inclination that an organizational state has, read according to *The General Form of Field Induction* as the part on which Φ depends; if the successor response of F depends on no part of T*, then T* has no tendency — (c). ∎  
The three clauses are components of one duty: the actual conditioning in (c) presupposes (a); this Form does not claim that the three clauses are mutually non-implying. Case 1 above lacks (b), on the ground that the Trace-Response set is empty, not that the reading is zero; Case 2 lacks (a) for the same T\*, and (c) fails along with it.

Lemma 1 sets no nonzero threshold on the reading. An example: the host of F is Q, with mechanism M\_sed; E is all four connections, all weights are 1, and h2→y is inhibitory (σ = −1). Φ = 1 − 1 = 0. But changing w(h2→y) to 2 gives Φ = −1, and fully suppressing x→h2 gives Φ = 1. The response depends on the parts of T\*, so (c) holds; the organization is nonempty and on the host, so Pot holds, yet the reading is zero. Shifting the origin of the reading, Φ̃ = Φ + c, does not change the truth value of Pot either.

2

Pot is in hand. Is tendency raising closed yet?

No. Let the host of F be Q, with E all four connections and w = (1, 1, 1, 2) (in the order above), all excitatory; Φ = 1 + 2 = 3. The reading reads in the current w: changing w(h2→y) to 1 turns Φ into 2, so Pot holds. But the mechanism of F is M\_frz: when input passes through, no connection is written back, and w never changes in any run window. The initial stipulation says that what tendency raising pre-builds is a concretized field; in the complete declaration of M\_frz there is no event on the host of F that changes any connection or weight, neither within the field nor outside it: no run window can form new Trace-Responses, and there is no process that acts on the organization of the Trace-Response set. By *The Mathematical Form of Field Concretization*, qualification is a function of mechanism witnesses; if the declared mechanism permits no witness of the corresponding duties, there is none over any actual window. This is a judgment by the rules, not by a constant trajectory.

Lemma 2 (Mechanism condition)  In any Prime(κ), the complete declared mechanism M of F permits, at the rule level, legitimate witnesses of the following three duties: (1) Trace-Response — when a signal passes, a retained change can be left on a Structural Position of F; (2) organizational participation — a process conditioned on the organization of the same Trace-Response set can occur; (3) historical dynamics — a process can occur that acts on the organization of the same Trace-Response set and in whose determination (of at least one of its occurrence, timing, position, direction, or strength) the past organization of that set takes part through an actual material route. Write MechC(F, M). The three are not required to be borne by the same process; write-back is not required at every input, nor required to read the current field state directly at the moment of writing; the historical route may run through retention, out-of-boundary processes, or round trips.  
Proof: By the initial stipulation, what tendency raising pre-builds is a concretized field, that is, a field that can obtain Field-Concretization Qualification over some actual window. By *The General Form of Field Concretization* v1.0, qualification must be given over some actual window by the actual bearing of the three duties of Trace-Response, organizational participation, and historical dynamics of the same Trace-Response set; by Section 2 of *The Mathematical Form of Field Concretization* v1.0, qualification is a function of mechanism witnesses. In a working domain where mechanism events are exhausted by the declaration, if the complete declaration of M permits no legitimate witness of one of the three, then no actual window can realize that duty, F cannot obtain qualification in any window, and what is pre-built is not a concretized field, contradicting Prime(κ). ∎  
MechC is a necessary mechanism premise for Field-Concretization Qualification but does not imply it: qualification requires that the three duties actually be borne within some actual window, and when tendency raising is completed R has not yet run, so this Form outputs no qualification within R. If the source qualification of the starting organization T\* itself needs to be stated, one may choose another window that has actually run, list the actual bearing of the three duties in that window on the same Trace-Response set, and then state how it supplies T\*; this is a source record, not a necessary item of this Form. M\_sed satisfies MechC: when input passes, it writes back according to the flux (1); the write-back is conditioned on the current E, w, σ, m (2); the write-back acts on w, and its increment (1/10)·\|f\_p\| is determined by the w already left (3). M\_sed satisfies all three with a single process that reads the current state, which is stronger than what MechC requires and is not a requirement of MechC: for example, an out-of-boundary process may first read the state already left at some Structural Position and retain it, and later rewrite the same Structural Position according to the retained value without reading the current value at the moment of write-back; historical dynamics is then still borne by the past organization of the same Trace-Response set through an out-of-boundary route. M\_frz lacks (1) and (3).

3

Pot and MechC are in hand. Is tendency raising closed yet?

No. Let R = \[0, 6\], with the host of F being Q and the mechanism M\_sed; at t₀ = 0, E is all four connections with all weights 1, and Φ = 2. The process under adjudication o changes w(h2→y) to 2 at t = 2, and the resulting organization T\* has Φ = 3 thereafter. For T\*, Pot and MechC both hold. But what tendency raising acquires is the starting organization of R; a write partway through the run is an intervention within R (host adjustment or intrusion, adjudicated separately), not the tendency raising of R.

Lemma 3 (Starting temporal position)  In any Prime(κ), t\* ≤ t₀, and T\* is the starting organization of R. Write Init(T\*; R).  
Proof: The initial stipulation says that tendency raising acquires the “initial” organizational state and that Field Induction “starts from this organizational state.” “Initial” is relative to the run window R served: if t\* \> t₀, or T\* is not the organization of F at the start of R, then what o acquires is not the initial organization of R, contradicting Prime(κ). ∎  
Lemma 3 is only a necessary temporal-position condition relative to R. If the same organization was formed in an earlier window R₀ by an intervention within the run, it can be the starting organization of a later window R; but the nature of its intervention in R₀ is registered as is and does not vanish with the change of window.

4

Pot, MechC, and Init are in hand. Is tendency raising closed yet?

No. Let T\* be written into Q by some process P at t\* = t₀ = 0: E is all four connections, w = (1, 1, 1, 2), and the mechanism is M\_sed; Pot, MechC, and Init all hold for T*. There is also a process o′: it writes a one-line record into a log outside F, does not touch H, and ends exactly at t*. With o′ as the process under adjudication, the three items still all hold for T*; but o′ did not make F acquire T*, and it is not this tendency raising.

Lemma 4 (Actual acquisition)  In any Prime(κ), the actual change that the process under adjudication o makes to H (writing, construction, moving in, restoration, formation along with the host, and so on) constitutes T*, and T* persists from t\* to t₀. Write Acq(o, T\*; H).  
Proof: The initial stipulation says that tendency raising “acquires” the initial organizational state. If o makes no actual change to H that constitutes T*, then T* is not acquired by o, and o is not a tendency raising that acquires T*; if T* does not persist between t\* and t₀, then the organization at the start of R is not the one o acquired, which disagrees with the starting point read in Lemma 3. Both contradict Prime(κ). ∎  
Lemma 4 connects the process under adjudication with the organization described by the first three items, on the same F, T*, o, t*.

5

Pot, MechC, Init, and Acq are in hand. Is tendency raising closed yet?

Check the initial stipulation term by term: “the first thing to be adjusted,” “a network … has a tendency,” “organizational state of the Trace-Response set” — Pot (Lemma 1); “pre-build a concretized field” — MechC (Lemma 2); “initial,” “Field Induction starts from this organizational state” — Init (Lemma 3); “acquire” — Acq (Lemma 4). The initial stipulation states explicitly that tendency raising is not delimited by the acquirer's purpose, by whether it serves some particular act of Field Induction, or by whether it adopts a generalized structure, so no items are set up for these. Forcing stops.

6

Four items are in hand. What other features are often thought necessary for tendency raising? We examine them one by one. Each example below lies on host Q with mechanism M\_sed. Each example is a tendency raising directly by the initial stipulation: before the run window served begins, the process under adjudication actually acquires a network on F's own host that has a tendency, as the starting organization of R; the examples are not obtained by inference backward from the four necessary structures, and the four items can be read off one by one in each of them.

A uniform, flat, or zero-tendency starting point; a starting organization acquired by prior learning inside the run served; Field Induction actually occurring after tendency raising: none of these is necessary. R = \[0, 6\]. At t\* = t₀ = 0, an initialization process o outside the field writes E as all four connections, w = (1, 1, 1, 2), all excitatory; the starting point is not uniform, Φ = 3. T\* is acquired by writing before R begins and does not come from any learning within R. Within R there is only the field's own running: at t = 1 an environmental input s = 1 unrelated to any goal enters, the h1 branch carries flux 1 and the h2 branch flux 2, w becomes (11/10, 11/10, 6/5, 11/5), and Φ = 77/20; there is no Field Induction within R.

A nonzero reading is not necessary. At t\* = t₀, an initialization process writes into Q the organization of Argument Flow 1 whose excitatory and inhibitory branches cancel: E is all four connections, all weights are 1, and h2→y is inhibitory. At the starting point Φ = 0; the organization is nonempty, on the host, and bears a tendency (changing w(h2→y) or suppressing x→h2 changes the reading). This is a tendency raising whose starting reading is zero.

An acquirer outside the field is not necessary. Let the run of the previous example be R₀ = \[0, 1\]; at the end of R₀ the organization of F is S: w = (11/10, 11/10, 6/5, 11/5), Φ = 77/20. F's own host additionally declares a retention region, which does not enter the reading and is not subject to flux write-back; within F there is a maintenance mechanism (listed in the declaration of M): at the end of R₀ it stores S in the retention region and clears the run region (all connections in the run region are disconnected, E = ∅), and before the next run window R₁ begins it restores S from the retention region to the run region. The starting organization of R₁ is S, Φ = 77/20; after an input s = 1 enters once within R₁, Φ = 5098137/1000000, identical beat by beat to uninterrupted continuation. The acquirer is within F, and the route is restoration. The source history of S includes the run of R₀, which is registered as is.

An acquirer that has a purpose, serves some particular act of Field Induction, or takes a generalized structure obtained by content alignment as its pre-building scheme: none of these is necessary. At t\* = t₀, an initialization process writes all four connections according to a fixed sequence of pseudorandom numbers, w = (3/5, 7/5, 6/5, 4/5), Φ = 21/25 + 24/25 = 9/5. The acquiring process holds no content goal and serves no specified act of Field Induction, and the numbers it writes are not taken from any tendency structure obtained by content alignment: this acquiring process did not take a generalized structure previously obtained by content alignment as its pre-building scheme, and tendency raising does not require such a scheme to be already in hand. It actually acquired a network on F that has a tendency, as the starting organization of R. This example does not prove that the acquired organization has no generalized structure: this Form gives no structural criterion for generalized structure; whether the acquired organization has a generalized structure, and whether a generalized structure was adopted as a scheme, are two separate matters.

Each example above is a tendency raising that lacks the corresponding feature, so the missing feature is not a necessary structure of tendency raising.

Whether the starting organization permits or lends itself to some kind of Field Induction (inducibility) is unanswered: the absence of an actual act does not prove non-inducibility; nor does Pot guarantee that some specific mode of Field Induction can be carried out — for example, an organization that has a tendency does not thereby supply an execution mechanism for suppression and disinhibition.

Proof of Minimal Structure

Load-bearing classification. Pot is proved by Lemma 1; its “organizational state of the Trace-Response set” and “has a tendency” are inherited from the initial stipulation, and the reading of “bearing a tendency” from *The General Form of Field Induction*. MechC is proved by Lemma 2; the three duties are inherited from *The General Form of Field Concretization* v1.0, and “qualification is a function of mechanism witnesses” from Section 2 of *The Mathematical Form of Field Concretization* v1.0. Init is proved by Lemma 3; Acq is proved by Lemma 4. Each of the four lemmas is drawn out of the gap found in the corresponding round of the Argument Flow: a countermodel shows that what was obtained before is insufficient, and the lemma proves, from the corresponding term of the initial stipulation, that the item is necessary for any completed tendency raising. This Form has no external conditions awaiting verification: the declarations of mechanism, host boundary, and run window are working-domain stipulations, not antecedents awaiting proof, but downstream citations must keep them; in Lemma 2, “no corresponding mechanism, no witness” is restricted to the working domain in which mechanism events are exhausted by the declaration, and this restriction is given in advance in the working domain.

Irreplaceability. The following are witnesses that deletion candidates are insufficient, not breaches of the master theorem:  
(i) host, mechanism, and temporal position ⇏ Pot (Argument Flow 1: E = ∅ lacks (b); the log outside the field lacks (a) for the same T\*);  
(ii) Pot ⇏ MechC (Argument Flow 2: M\_frz);  
(iii) Pot ∧ MechC ⇏ Init (Argument Flow 3: the write at t = 2 within R);  
(iv) Pot ∧ MechC ∧ Init ⇏ Acq (Argument Flow 4: the unrelated process o′).  
Each countermodel keeps everything obtained before it and lacks only the item under test. No claim is made that the three clauses within Pot are mutually non-implying.

Exclusion of proposed additions. For each item listed in Argument Flow 6 there is a tendency raising, completed according to the initial stipulation, that lacks it; none of them enters the master formula.

Scope. Minimality is limited to this: the listed weaker candidates cannot replace the duties unfolded. Uniqueness and global minimality are unproved.

Master Formula and Master Theorem

Master formula (necessary direction):

　　Prime(κ) ⇒ Pot(T*, F; R) ∧ MechC(F, M) ∧ Init(T*; R) ∧ Acq(o, T\*; H)

Read-back. κ = (F, H, M, R, o, t*, T*) is the declared instance, R = \[t₀, t₁\]. Pot(T*, F; R): T* persists on F's own host H, is a field state of F, and is in a position where it can be read in within R; it contains at least one retained change of a connection or a Structural Position; and the successor response of F under the declared inputs is a function of T\* and is not constant over the possible values of some part of T*. MechC(F, M): the complete declared mechanism M of F permits, at the rule level, legitimate witnesses of the three duties of Trace-Response, organizational participation, and historical dynamics; the three need not be borne by the same process and need not read the current field state at the moment of write-back, and the historical route may run through retention or outside the boundary; it is not Field-Concretization Qualification over any actual window. Init(T*; R): t\* ≤ t₀, and T\* is the starting organization of R. Acq(o, T*; H): the actual change o makes to H constitutes T*, and T\* persists from t\* to t₀. The four items share the same F, H, M, R, o, t*, T*; the tuple is not a causal chain, and the order of proof is not temporal order.

Master Theorem  Within the working domain, for any Prime(κ), the four items on the right-hand side of the master formula hold simultaneously.

Proof: Lemma 1 gives Pot, Lemma 2 gives MechC, Lemma 3 gives Init, and Lemma 4 gives Acq. Each item takes the same arguments of the same instance κ, so the conjunction holds. ∎

Strength. The master theorem states the necessary direction. Whether the right-hand side implies Prime(κ) is unproved; its items do not serve as criteria for establishing tendency raising.

Corollary (Interface with Field Induction)  The T\* acquired by a tendency raising is the starting organization of R and can serve as one source of Org₀(F, T*) in *The General Form of Field Induction\*; Org₀ does not require acquisition through tendency raising, since the field may already be running.  
Proof: By Init and Pot, T\* is the organization of the Trace-Response set that F already has at the start of R; Org₀ requires only that F already have its organization at the start of W, and makes no requirement on the source of that organization. ∎

Unanswered and not load-bearing (later matters; they do not support the master theorem):

Converse: whether the joint presence of the four items on the right-hand side implies tendency raising.  
Inducibility: whether the starting organization permits or lends itself to some kind of Field Induction; to what degree a tendency must be borne.

Concretization qualification on material hosts.  
Uniqueness and global minimality.

Breach Modes

With the object domain, working domain, and declarations stated by this Form all kept, exhibit a tendency raising for which some item on the right-hand side of the master formula fails; or prove that some item can be deleted, or weakened to one of the candidates examined, while tendency raising still holds; or show that the proof of one of the four lemmas fails. Exhibiting only another physical realization of the same duty is not a breach; if the mechanism, host boundary, or run window of a counterexample was not declared in advance or was altered afterward, it is a boundary case, not a breach.

Appendix: Formalization

Notation. Host Q: nodes x, h1, h2, y; set of installed connections E ⊆ {x→h1, h1→y, x→h2, h2→y}; weight w: E → (0, ∞), sign σ: E → {+1, −1}, patency m: E → \[0, 1\]; reading Φ = Σ\_{p: x→y, p⊆E} Π\_{e∈p} σ(e)·w(e)·m(e). M\_sed: when an input s enters, w(e) ← w(e) + (1/10)·Σ\_{p∋e} \|s·Π\_{e′∈p} σ(e′)·w(e′)·m(e′)\|; M\_frz: no write-back. Uninstalled connections are not in E.

Definition 1 (Bearing a tendency)  T\* bears the tendency of F if there exist a part π of T\* and two possible values v ≠ v′ of it such that, with the other parts and the declared inputs unchanged, the successor response of F (including the reading Φ) differs between π = v and π = v′, and the successor of F is given by rules that read in the field state.

Definition 2 (Mechanism condition)  MechC(F, M) :⇔ within the working domain in which mechanism events are exhausted by the declaration, the complete declaration of M permits: (1) the existence of a retained change left on a Structural Position of F when a signal passes; (2) the existence of a process conditioned on the organization of the same Trace-Response set T; (3) the existence of a process that acts on the organization of T and in whose determination of at least one of its occurrence, timing, position, direction, or strength the past organization of T takes part through an actual material route. The processes for the three need not be the same; the route in (3) may run through retention, out-of-boundary processes, or round trips, and is not required to read the current value of T at the moment of action.

Definition 3 (Actual acquisition)  Acq(o, T*; H) :⇔ the actual change o makes on H results in T*, and T\* persists on \[t\*, t₀\].

Values of the constructions on host Q (w in the order x→h1, h1→y, x→h2, h2→y):  
Uninstalled: E = ∅, Φ ≡ 0, no input writes back.  
Log outside the field: (1, 1, 1, 2), not on H, not read in by F.
