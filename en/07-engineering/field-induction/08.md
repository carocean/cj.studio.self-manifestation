# The Possibility Form of Tuning in Feedforward Networks

## Section 1  Question and Strength

### 1.1  Question

*The General Form of Tuning* v1.0 proves that an established tuning necessarily has a gating part that opens and closes existing pathways of the regulated field by suppression and disinhibition and adjusts their degree of permeability (Gate), and that the bearing of the tendency originates in this gating change. An ordinary dense feedforward network has only weights, biases, inputs and elementwise nonlinearities; under a given input it cuts off some units, which looks like opening and closing pathways. Hence the question: with an ordinary dense feedforward network as the regulated field, can a tuning occur?

### 1.2  Sole Task and Form Kind

Within the closed abstract host run specification 𝔖\_ffn (Section 3) and its exhaustive set of legitimate transitions, decide whether there exists a tuning whose regulated field V is a network of 𝔖\_ffn. The conclusion is negative:

　　¬∃κ · (Tune(κ) ∧ V(κ) ⊨ 𝔖\_ffn)

The Form kind is Possibility Form: a principal form, first edition. The conclusion is the refutation of an existence assertion; it belongs to the witness direction and is handled by the breach modes of a Possibility Form (Section 8).

### 1.3  Upper Bound of Strength

The quantifier of the conclusion ranges only over the legitimate histories of 𝔖\_ffn. It does not extend to: networks with attention, multiplicative gating, recurrence or lateral inhibition (these violate B1 or B2 of 3.1); any concrete hardware implementation (𝔖\_ffn is an abstract host); networks with gating units or in-field control units. Whether networks of the last kind can be tuned is not decided by the conclusion on 𝔖\_ffn; it is checked item by item in the order of Section 7.

## Section 2  Condition Group

Taken over from *The General Form of Tuning* v1.0 and numbered item by item:

C1  Tune(κ) is an act of Field Induction: it has the six duty groups of Field Induction (F := V).  
C2  Gate(a, V): at least one part of the act a opens and closes existing pathways of V by suppression and disinhibition and adjusts their degree of permeability, and the dynamic logical structure so formed is a subset of V's current Trace-Response-set structure; the gating part neither adds nor deletes connections and does not rewrite parameters.  
C3  Bear\_G: the bearing of the tendency originates in the patency change that some actual gating part leaves on V.  
C4  Classification criterion: an action that only enters as signals and is processed by the field's own basic rules is input; what directly transforms the physical layer is direct write, the mode of host adjustment; the identity and location of the actor play no part in the classification. An in-field control unit can also be an actor, so an action's occurring inside the field does not suffice to decide that it is not an act.  
C5  Across subfields: whether an action is input, gating or direct write at the V end is determined by the actual chain and the prior declaration; if some output opens and closes existing pathways of V through a suppression channel declared as belonging to V, what happens at the V end is gating.

The negative argument uses only the necessary direction Tune ⇒ Gate (C2) and the classification criteria C4 and C5.

## Section 3  Abstract Host Run Specification 𝔖\_ffn

### 3.1  Organization

B1 (Network)  Finitely many units, topologically ordered by layer; connections run only from earlier layers to later layers, with no cycles and no lateral connections.  
B2 (Operation)  The sole operation of each unit j is h\_j = σ\_j(Σ\_i w\_ji·h\_i + b\_j), where σ\_j is an elementwise function fixed in advance for that unit (e.g. ReLU: σ(z) = max(0, z)); the identity function is admitted. The output of a unit enters other units in one way only: it is multiplied by the weight of its connection and added to the sum of the target unit. No unit's output acts on the coefficient of another connection.  
B3 (Host and state)  The θ layer is the weights w and biases b; together with the fixed σ\_j and the connection set it constitutes the physical layer. The r layer is the input x and the activations h of the units. The state is (θ, x, h). In addition, record for each unit the segment index stamp(j) of its most recent evaluation; at entry it is 0.  
B4 (Reading)  Φ takes the output value at the read-out probe; it is a mathematical read-out, not an event.

### 3.2  Transition Generators

A run consists of inference segments. The segment index k ranges over the positive integers and increases step by step; segment k consists of the phases (k, ·). 0 is not a segment index; it is reserved as the entry timestamp (stamp = 0 means not evaluated since entry). The legitimate transitions are exhausted by three classes:

T2  Parameter direct write: executed only in a pre-segment slot (k, 0, j) (j = 1, 2, …); writes only θ. Within an inference segment θ does not change. A training write-back acting on V belongs to this class and falls between two inference segments; the optimizer that computes the written-back values is outside V.  
T1  Input setting: executed at phase (k, 1); writes only x and opens segment k.  
T3  Layer-by-layer pure evaluation: executed at phases (k, i) (i ≥ 2), once per unit in topological order. The evaluation of unit j reads θ and the predecessors already evaluated in this segment (inputs must have been set in this segment), writes h\_j = σ\_j(Σ w\_ji·h\_i + b\_j), and sets stamp(j) = k. The unit, read set, write set and function of a T3 event are uniquely determined by what B2 stipulates for that unit.

Other transitions — taking 0 or a non-positive integer as segment index, writing θ within an inference segment, skipping the topological order, evaluating before the input is set, setting up a separate run component that writes activations, gating events, evaluating under the name of some unit while using another function or read set — are not legitimate transitions of 𝔖\_ffn.

### 3.3  Ports

The ports available to an act are exhausted by two: the parameter direct-write port of T2 and the input port of T1. T3 is a fixed basic rule of the field; its function, read set and write set do not change under any act.  
B3 contains no dynamic layer; this is how 𝔖\_ffn is represented. The proof of Section 5 starts from the transition generators and does not presuppose “undeclared dynamic layer”.

## Section 4  Qualification Antecedent

*The General Form of Tuning* requires that the regulated field V on its own satisfy the working domain of *The General Form of Field Induction*, that is, obtain Field-Concretization Qualification in some declared actual window. The proof of Section 5 does not use this antecedent; hence:

(1) The mechanism negation holds for the whole abstract class 𝔖\_ffn: no network of 𝔖\_ffn has a landing point for an act that could serve as a gating part.  
(2) If some member of the class obtains Field-Concretization Qualification in some actual window, the negation reads back on the qualification domain as: in its legitimate histories satisfying 𝔖\_ffn, that member is not the regulated field of any tuning.  
(3) Whether the qualification subdomain is nonempty has not been proved. To establish nonemptiness, one must give, for the same V and the same 𝔖\_ffn, qualification evidence for some actual window (actual trace-leaving, organizational participation, historical action), and show that the rules, mechanism and host identity of that window are compatible with the inference segments; the mechanism condition MechC holds at the rule level and cannot replace qualification in an actual window.  
For the position of training write-back see 3.2: it is T2, falls between inference segments, and is a parameter direct write within 𝔖\_ffn.

## Section 5  Impossibility Argument

Lemma 1 (Phase Lemma)  In any legitimate history of 𝔖\_ffn, at any phase, the value of each unit j equals F\_j(x\_s, θ\_s), s = stamp(j), where (x\_s, θ\_s) are the input and parameters at the time the input of segment s was set, and F is the forward function computed layer by layer by B2. A unit with stamp(j) = 0 keeps its entry value.  
Proof: Induction on phases. T2 writes only θ and changes no h and no stamp; it is executed in a pre-segment slot, and from then until the next T1 there is no T3, so it does not change the (x\_s, θ\_s) corresponding to any evaluated unit. T1 writes only x and opens a new segment; it changes no h and no stamp. When T3 evaluates unit j, its predecessors have all been evaluated or set in the current segment k, and by the induction hypothesis each equals the corresponding component of F(x\_k, θ\_k); θ does not change within the segment. Hence the value written is F\_j(x\_k, θ\_k), and stamp(j) = k is set. ∎  
After a segment's input has been set and before evaluation, the units not yet evaluated in this segment still hold the values obtained at their most recent evaluation, and units not evaluated since entry keep their entry values (see the example in 6.1); the lemma is stated in terms of stamp and covers this phase.

Lemma 2 (Exhaustion of landing points and routes)  Let a be an act acting on V. Every actual landing point of a on V is a T2 or a T1; T3 cannot be set or changed by any part of a. Every influence of a on the activations passes through T1 or T2 and is then transmitted by T3.  
Proof: By 3.2, the legitimate transitions of V are only T1, T2 and T3. The function, read set and write set of T3 are uniquely determined by B2: in any history it is the evaluation of the same rule on the current values, with no component an act can select; an evaluation that changes the function or read set is not a legitimate transition. Hence if the action of a on V has a landing point, it can pass only through the two ports of 3.3, that is, T2 or T1. By Lemma 1, every change in the activations comes from the input or parameters of some segment and is transmitted by T3. ∎

Lemma 3 (Sole coordinate of permeability)  In 𝔖\_ffn, an existing connection i→j transmits w\_ji·h\_i, which is added to the sum of j and then passes through σ\_j. The permeability coefficient of the connection is w\_ji; it belongs to the θ layer, and only T2 can change it. Whether unit j passes anything on (whether σ\_j cuts off) is computed by B2 from the sum entering j; it is the processing, by j's basic rule, of the signals entering j. No transition in 𝔖\_ffn sets the patency of a connection by suppression and disinhibition while neither rewriting w nor entering as signals: by B2, a unit's output acts only by being added to the sum of the target unit and does not act on the coefficient of another connection, so 𝔖\_ffn has no suppression channel in the sense of C5.  
Proof: Check class by class from 3.2. T2 rewrites θ, that is, rewrites connection coefficients or biases; T1 writes x, that is, changes the signals entering the first layer; T3 computes unit values by B2, in which each connection takes part with its fixed w\_ji and the cutoff of σ\_j is decided by the current sum. There are no transitions outside these three classes. ∎  
Reading ReLU: when σ\_j is ReLU, writing σ\_j(z) = max(0, z) as z·𝟙\[z \> 0\] formally produces a multiplicative factor, but that factor is computed by T3 from the current z and is not the landing point of any act. The criterion is the actual landing point and route, not whether the formula contains a multiplication sign or the code contains a mask variable. Likewise, a 0 appearing in the activations cannot be used to deny suppressive action in some concrete hardware; 𝔖\_ffn characterizes only an abstract host.

Theorem (Impossibility)  There is no tuning whose regulated field V ⊨ 𝔖\_ffn.  
Proof: Suppose Tune(κ) and V ⊨ 𝔖\_ffn. By C2, a has a gating part a\_G that opens and closes existing pathways of V by suppression and disinhibition and adjusts their degree of permeability, without rewriting parameters. By Lemma 2, the landing point of a\_G on V is a T2 or a T1. If it is a T2, it rewrites parameters; by C2 it is not a gating part, and by C4 it is direct write. If it is a T1, it is a signal entering through the input port and processed by T3; by C4 it is input. By Lemma 3, this route sets the patency of no connection and only changes the signals in the sums entering the units, so it is not a suppression channel in the sense of C5. Hence a has no gating part, ¬Gate(a, V), contradicting C2. By the contrapositive of the necessary direction Tune ⇒ Gate, ¬Tune(κ). ∎  
Used in the proof: 3.1–3.3, Lemmas 1–3, and the necessary direction and classification criteria of *The General Form of Tuning*. The qualification antecedent is not used, nor is “undeclared dynamic layer”. Input and the subsequent action triggered via input are reported separately: within 𝔖\_ffn, the only subsequent action is T3.

## Section 6  Items Prone to Misreading

### 6.1  Example Network

The examples below all use the same network: input x = (x1, x2); hidden layer h1 = σ\_h1(w11·x1 + w12·x2 + b1), h2 = σ\_h2(w21·x1 + w22·x2 + b2); output y = σ\_y(u1·h1 + u2·h2); σ\_h1 = σ\_h2 = ReLU, and σ\_y is the identity function (with u1 = −1, u2 = 0, y = −1 under x = (1, 0)). At entry w11 = w12 = w21 = 1, w22 = −2, b1 = b2 = 0, u1 = u2 = 1, and all activations are 0. Read-out probe x = (1, 0), Φ = u1·σ\_h1(w11 + b1) + u2·σ\_h2(w21 + b2); at entry Φ = 2.

Phases and old values: segment 1 sets x = (1, 0) and evaluates, giving h1 = 1, h2 = 1, y = 2. When segment 2 has set x2 = 1 but not yet evaluated, h2 is still 1 and stamp(h2) = 1, equal to the F value of segment 1; after evaluation h2 = σ\_h2(1 − 2) = 0. Lemma 1 holds at every phase. If b2 is written to −2 in the pre-segment slot before segment 2, the h2 obtained in segment 1 still corresponds to the (x, θ) of segment 1, and segment 2 evaluates under the new θ.

### 6.2  Item by Item

\| Item \| Actual landing point and route in 𝔖\_ffn \| Reading \|  
\| ReLU inactivation: h2 = 1 at x = (1, 0), h2 = 0 at x = (1, 1) \| T1, then via T3 \| Result of an ordinary forward run; the only landing point of the act is the input \|  
\| Context and prompt inputs \| Numerical vectors through the same T1 port, then via T3 \| Input; the classification rests on landing point and route, not on the semantics of the input \|

## Section 7  Extended Class

A network with multiplicative gating, feedback, lateral inhibition, clampable activations, settable masks or an in-field control unit is not tunable merely because it has such units, nor is it excluded because the control quantities can be computed from the input or because it lacks independently stored control coordinates. Its tuning is checked in the following order:

1\. Actual landing point and execution mechanism: mark input, gating and direct write part by part along the actual chain. The actions of an in-field control unit, of Biased-Flow rules retained in the field and of a control unit outside V are likewise checked by path (Argument Flow 3 and 5 of *The General Form of Tuning*). A control unit may compute the patency deterministically from the input and the goal specification it already holds, and then act on existing pathways through an actual port; determinism is compatible with actual gating.  
2\. The six duty groups, among which the goal condition GoalCond checks whether the goal actually takes part in determining the gating.  
3\. Gate: some part opens and closes existing pathways by suppression and disinhibition and adjusts their degree of permeability, and the structure so formed is a subset of the current Trace-Response-set structure.  
4\. Bear\_G: the bearing of the tendency originates in the patency change of that gating part.

Representation: patency can be represented as a dynamic control coordinate m supported on existing connections; this is one way of representing an implementation, not a necessary condition.  
Example: in the network of 6.1, add a dynamic coordinate m2 on the connection h2→y, so that the forward pass becomes y = u1·h1 + u2·m2·h2, with the reading changed in the same way. At entry m2 = 1, and at x = (1, 0), y = Φ = 2; after a gating event sets m2 = 0, under the same input y = Φ = 1. This gating acts on the actual transmission, and the formal conditions of Gate and Bear\_G can be checked; whether it constitutes a tuning still requires checking the six duty groups and the goal condition.  
Unanswered: whether conscription must pass through a convergence relation with Law qualification, and whether there must be an independent conscription chain. Their general necessity is left unanswered in *The General Form of Tuning*, and they are not listed as conditions of the extended class.

## Section 8  Strength and Breach

Strength: impossibility, under 𝔖\_ffn (3.1–3.3) and the necessary direction of *The General Form of Tuning*; the object is an abstract host; the mechanism negation holds for the whole abstract class, on the qualification domain it is a conditional read-back, and nonemptiness of the qualification subdomain is not proved. For the extended class this Form gives only the order of checking and the upstream necessary structure; it certifies neither existence nor nonexistence.  
Breach: exhibit a network satisfying 𝔖\_ffn and an act within its legitimate transitions that is established as a tuning under the initial stipulation of *The General Form of Tuning*. 𝔖\_ffn may not be modified after the fact to expel the counterexample.

## Section 9  Interfaces

Input: the necessary direction, the classification criteria and the separate reporting across subfields of *The General Form of Tuning* v1.0; the six duty groups and the working domain of *The General Form of Field Induction* v1.0.

Output: the impossibility theorem on 𝔖\_ffn, the phase lemma, the exhaustion of landing points and routes, the sole coordinate of permeability; the order of checking for the extended class. Downstream use must carry over the clauses of 𝔖\_ffn and the qualification conditions of Section 4 together with them.  
— To *The General Form of Tuning*: this Form is a negative application of its necessary direction on 𝔖\_ffn.  
— To the Adjudication Forms: on 𝔖\_ffn, tuning can be excluded directly, with the qualification conditions carried over together; the extended class is checked item by item per Section 7.  
— To *The Construction Form of Tuning in Spiking Networks*: delivers the upstream necessary structure (the six duty groups, Gate, Bear\_G) and the order of checking and the optional representation of Section 7; a construction must satisfy these structures, and no further necessary conditions are added.  
— To *The Mathematical Form of Error-Driven Host Adjustment*: training write-back on 𝔖\_ffn is T2, a direct write in the mode of host adjustment, consistent with that Form.  
— To *The Mathematical Form of Field Induction*: T1, T2 and T3 are, respectively, the In event through the input port, the Rw event through the direct-write port and the Run event citing the field-rule component in Definition 3.2 of that Form; pre-segment slots and in-segment phases are ordered by the lexicographic order of its Definition 3.3; T3, bound to the rule of B2, is the realization on 𝔖\_ffn of that Form's trusted premise “kind and src conform to the actual route”.

Unanswered: nonemptiness of the qualification subdomain; a complete comparison for the training phase (dropout, batch-normalization running statistics); real hardware implementations (cutoff implemented by inhibitory currents, and the like); positive existence for the extended class; whether conscription must pass through a convergence relation with Law qualification.
