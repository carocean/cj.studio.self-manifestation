# The Compatibility Form of Tuning Conditions

## Section 1  List of Antecedents

### 1.1  Question

*The General Form of Tuning* v1.0 gives the structure that every tuning must have: the six duty groups, a gating part Gate and gating bearing Bear\_G, and the requirement that the regulated field itself have Field-Concretization Qualification. *The Possibility Form of Tuning in Feedforward Networks* v1.0 proves that in the legitimate histories of the abstract host 𝔖\_ffn it declares (a conditional read-back on the qualification domain), the landing points of an act are only direct write and input, and there is no tuning; this conclusion does not extend to concrete hardware or to extended networks with gating. In spiking networks, tuning is often described as “the physical connections stay unchanged, the permeability varies, and it is conscripted by rules within the field.” Can these items and the necessary structure of tuning hold together on one and the same concretized field and one and the same act?

### 1.2  Antecedents

For the same regulated field F, act I (a gating act, namely the action of an in-field interneuron through an inhibitory channel; its part is labeled I), time window W, reading Φ, input class 𝓘, and goal specification g:  
P1 Physical-layer preservation: no event within W actually changes any θ variable; field-rule events that make no actual rewrite may occur. This is a strengthened assumption of the present model, not a general necessary condition of tuning. \[declared, H14-0\]  
P2 Variable permeability of existing connections: the dynamic-layer variable m is supported on installed connections, is actually changed by gating events, and is actually read by transmission. \[*The General Form of Tuning*, Gate; *The Mathematical Form of Field Induction* v1.0, Definitions 3.1 (m layer, Supp) and 3.2 (Gt port)\]  
P3 In-field conscription (a declared in-field control rule): gating is determined by an in-field control rule — the rule of the in-field interneuron determines its firing, and its output writes m through the declared inhibitory channel (Gt port); control participation is witnessed by a value-read chain (the gating event reads in the interneuron's firing, with dependence 1), not certified by proxy through a control-edge label. \[declared, H14-1\]  
P4 Necessary structure of tuning: on the same instance, starting organization Org₀, GoalRep and Target, a nonzero net tendency difference within W, Eff, Bear, goal condition GoalCond, Gate, Bear\_G; the goal chain binds this gating landing point. \[*The General Form of Tuning*, master formula; *The General Form of Field Induction*, six duty groups\]  
P5 Field-Concretization Qualification: at the rule level, the mechanism witness P10 of the declared mechanism on a legitimate protocol; for the actual window, the same F actually bears the three items Trace-Response, organizational participation, and historical dynamics in some actual window, which may differ from W. \[*The General Form of Tuning*, working domain; *The Mathematical Form of Field Induction*, P10, 7.1\]  
In-field Law conscription by a standing Law (the conscriptor itself having acquired Law qualification) is not among the antecedents listed above.

## Section 2  Analysis of Candidate Conflict Points

K1 Where the inhibition lands. An inhibitory current added to the membrane potential is, by its route, a term of the field's own membrane-potential rule (Run), or, at the boundary of the regulated field, an input (In); it is not gating. The same holds in feedforward networks, where truncating a unit is an operation on the unit's sum. Gating must directly set the patency of existing connections, and transmission must actually read it. A shunting or multiplicative form does not by itself confer gating qualification; the criterion is the actual landing point at the Gt port and the actual dependence of transmission on m.  
K2 In-field conscription and the classification of acts. The actor may be inside the field; an action is registered by its actual landing point and port, not by whether the actor is inside or outside. The firing rule of the interneuron is the running of the field (Run); only the step in which its output changes m through the Gt port is the landing point of the act.  
K3 Goal condition. The goal specification must take part, through an actual route, in determining this gating landing point, not other outputs of the same act. Under in-field conscription, the goal enters m through the value-write chain of input, interneuron firing, and gating read-in.  
K4 P1 and the field's own sedimentation. Within W, field rules (such as STDP) execute as usual; P1 requires that they not actually rewrite θ. If plasticity is driven by presynaptic release, presynaptic inhibition blocks that term as well. \[declared, H14-2\]  
K5 Bear\_G and P1. In this example the reading reads m directly: the dependence of the reading on m must pass through existing connections, and must still be 1 (as a dependence value) while θ is preserved. This is how the direct reading of this example bears; it is not a general requirement of Bear\_G.  
K6 Qualification and windows. The actual window of Field-Concretization Qualification may differ from the gating window W but must belong to the same F; rule-level evidence and actual-window evidence are delivered separately.

## Section 3  Coexistence Model

### 3.1  Regulated Field

F is a minimal spiking network. Value domains (Definition 3.1):  
\| Variable \| Layer \| Value domain \| Substitute sequence \|  
\| w \| θ \| rational ≥ 0 \| {j/10 : 0 ≤ j ≤ 30} \|  
\| m (supported on w) \| m \| {0, 1} \| full enumeration \|  
\| u, uI, xpre, xpost \| r \| rational ≥ 0 \| {j/4 : 0 ≤ j ≤ 12} \|  
\| z, zpre, d, ctx, zI \| r \| {0, 1} \| full enumeration \|  
\| ref \| r \| {0, 1, 2} \| full enumeration \|  
\| g \| ext \| {0, 1} \| full enumeration \|  
Ports: the gating port may write m; the input port may write zpre, d, ctx.  
Field rules (executed in order at each beat; right-hand sides are pre-write values):  
\- Interneuron inter: uI ← ctx; zI ← 1 (when ctx ≥ 1) or 0;  
\- Gating (act I, through the gating port): m ← 1 − zI;  
\- Membrane potential mem: when ref = 0, u ← max(0, u/2 + m·w·zpre + d); when ref ≠ 0, u ← 0;  
\- Firing fire: when u ≥ 1, z ← 1, u ← 0, ref ← 2; otherwise z ← 0, u unchanged, ref ← max(ref − 1, 0);  
\- Traces: xpre ← xpre/2 + zpre; xpost ← xpost/2 + z;  
\- STDP (sedimentation): w ← max(0, w + A·xpre·z − A·xpost·m·zpre), A = 1/10 (the LTD term takes the release quantity m·zpre, H14-2).  
Context input (part labeled c, through the input port): ctx ← 1 − g. The background drive d is 0 at every beat. The goal g is held by Xg and may be written by the goal-setting component.  
Reading Φ = m·w, with declared degrees 1 in w and 1 in m. The input class 𝓘 is a unit presynaptic spike; Φ is the mathematical read-out under this input and reads only the θ and m layers.  
Declared mechanism routes (exhaustive): inter, mem, fire, the two traces, STDP, the gating port, the input port, goal setting; the permitted input is the input port.

### 3.2  Run and Windows

Entry: w = 3/2, m = 1, g = 1; u, uI, xpre, xpost, z, zpre, d, ctx, zI, ref are 0. At beats 1–4, zpre = 1, 0, 0, 1; at the end of beat 4 a goal-setting event writes g to 0 (registered as the goal source); at beats 5–8, zpre = 1, 0, 1, 0; d = 0 throughout. Order within each beat: presynaptic input, background drive, context, interneuron, gating, membrane potential, firing, presynaptic trace, STDP, postsynaptic trace. The background drive is part of the specification: if d at beat 7 is changed to 1 with everything else unchanged, STDP at beat 7 actually rewrites w, w at the end of W is 1169/640, and P1 fails.  
W′ is beats 1–4; the entry of W is the snapshot after the goal setting, and the events within W are those of beats 5–8. The items of P4 are evaluated on W: the events of I are those within W, and bearing moments are taken at snapshots within W (*The Mathematical Form of Field Induction*, Definition 3.9).

### 3.4  Control for K1

Control model: remove the gating event (m stays 1) and change the membrane-potential rule to u ← max(0, u/2 + m·w·zpre + d − zI) when ref = 0; the other rules, the run schedule and the entry are the same. The action of the interneuron is borne entirely by field rules; part I has no events, and Gate(I) = 0; within W, STDP actually rewrites w, and ΔΦ\_W = −9/64. This control proves only the difference between the two kinds of implementation landing point; it does not claim that the two kinds of inhibition are equivalent in transmission.

## Section 4  Compatibility Theorem

Theorem (Compatibility)  Under H14-0, H14-1, H14-2, and the trusted premises of *The Mathematical Form of Field Induction* (5.2), P1, P2, P3, P4, and P5 have a valid coexistence model: the minimal spiking network of Section 3 and its run.  
Proof: Section 3 gives the windowed formal values item by item. The items of P4 are 1 on the same F, act I, W, Φ, 𝓘, g (Org₀ including entry bearing and positive dependence; Eff\_W, Gate\_W, Bear\_W, Bear\_G,W; GoalCond with Π = {Gt} × {m}) or nonzero (ΔΦ\_W), and GoalRep and Target agree; the goal chain binds this gating landing point through a version path (K3). P1 and P2 are read off the event record within W; P3 is read off the read-in and dependence of the gating event (K2); the rule-level evidence (P10) and the actual-window evidence for P5 are delivered separately (K6). K1 is handled by the gating landing point of Section 3 and the control of 3.4; K4 by H14-2 and the same-value writes of STDP within W; K5 by w ≠ 0 at the entry and within W. The bridging conditions used for the read-back of each item are listed item by item in 3.3. ∎

## Section 5  Declaration of Strength

The conclusion is only that “these established structures and the declared assumptions have a valid coexistence model.”  
\- It does not certify from the conjunction of the necessary items that Tune holds: the converse of *The General Form of Tuning* is not proved.  
\- It does not certify that an in-field tuning law has already been established: P3 takes a declared in-field control rule, not conscription by a standing Law.  
\- No commitment is made to any biological neural mechanism; H14-2 is the declaration of a model rule.  
\- P1 takes the strengthened reading “no actual change of θ”; under the reading that requires only an unchanged connection topology, STDP may actually rewrite within W and the handling of K4 can be relaxed; coexistence under that reading has not been worked out.  
\- The generality, reachability, and realization of the construction, and the reachability of Tuning-Balance, lie outside compatibility.
