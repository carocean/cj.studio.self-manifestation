# The Construction Form of Field Induction Law Engineering

## Section 1  Declaration of the Condition Group

### 1.1  Question

*The Discrimination Form of Trace-Response Transformation in Artificial Neural Networks* (Field Concretization Group Form, Chapter 20) gives an interceptable, predictable, and recomputable correspondence from host-level change to dynamics-level change, and registers “how Field Induction Law Engineering intercepts the cross-layer law changes of a target field, generates Field Induction laws within its own field and regulates repeatedly on that basis” as a task of the Field Induction Group Form. What does an object that fits this description look like? We are required to construct explicitly the induced field, the Law Engineering, the ports, and the run between them, and to verify them item by item.

### 1.2  Condition Group

For an induced field F, a Law Engineering G, a goal specification g, a reading Φ and an input class 𝓘:  
C1 Induced field F: a declared concretized field whose Field-Concretization Qualification is proved in two separate parts—a rule-level mechanism witness (P10 of Definition 3.10 of *The Mathematical Form of Field Induction* v1.0) and the actual bearing of Trace-Response, organizational participation and historical dynamics in some actual window.  
C2 Law Engineering G: another declared concretized field whose variables are disjoint from those of F and which is connected to F through declared ports; its rule-level and actual-window qualifications are likewise proved separately.  
C3 Intercepting the cross-layer law changes: through declared ports, G acquires the correspondence between the host-level change δ of F and the reading change ΔΦ (the read-out map of 11.3 of Field Concretization Group Form, Chapter 20), and retains it in its own Trace-Response. The following are frozen item by item: the intercepted correspondence, the observations and inputs, the domain of applicability, and the bounds of the reading fiber. Genuine interception is distinguished from merely recording samples: the retained correspondence must give correct predictions for cases that did not occur during the formation period.  
C4 Qualification of the Field Induction law: a Law candidate L in G, whose carrying organization R is formed by the write law of G through the signal history and which, through a value-read chain, determines the values G writes when it acts on F. Qualification is graded: formal law formation requires that the same carrying organization satisfy the criteria of *The Mathematical Form of Law* of the Law Group Form, obtain Established ∧ Govern through its read-back conditions, and then obtain Law through the sufficient direction of *The Necessity Form of Law*; when these are not met, the construction of a Law candidate is delivered and this is stated explicitly.  
C5 Regulating repeatedly on that basis: following L, G acts on F at least twice, in disjoint time windows; each act is delivered in three separate items: the route of implementation (the implementation label of *The Adjudication Form of Field-Induction Action Modes* v1.0), the formal read-back of the six necessary duty groups (P1–P7 of Definition 3.9 of *The Mathematical Form of Field Induction*, and 7.1, 7.2), and event qualification (upstream registers its affirmative direction as unanswered; this Form does not sign it).  
C6 Historical bearing: under the same external goal and subsequent inputs, different legitimate formation histories give different carrying organizations, and the values written by the act change accordingly; when the formed organization is tested according to protocol, the act changes accordingly; the read-in evidence of formation and of use is kept separately; “formed within G” is checked against the source and retention of D18-2 and D18-3 of *The Mathematical Form of Trace-Response Set Transformation* (Field Concretization Group Form, Chapter 18); a fixed schedule satisfies none of the above.  
C7 Maintenance configuration: G acts within a maintenance window of F; the comparison used (Comparison A or Comparison B of Theorem 20-8 of Field Concretization Group Form, Chapter 20), the protected reading, the window and the input family are frozen and verified separately, not inherited on the strength of a name.

## Section 2  Construction Procedure

### 2.1  Induced Field F

Variables: host level w0, w1; run level: inputs x0, x1, membrane drive u, read-out y; goal g—an out-of-boundary variable held by the goal holder, whose goal declaration points to F, and which belongs to neither the host level nor the run level of F. The field's own rules: membrane drive u := w0·x0 + w1·x1; sedimentation (STDP type): when x1 = 1 and u ≥ 1, w1 increases by 1/8; read-out y := w0 + p·w1. Reading Φ = w0 + p·w1. Ports: direct-write port {w0, w1}, input port {x0, x1}. Take p = 2.

### 2.2  Law Engineering G

Variables: host level c, C; run level: the read-in reading yG, the read-in goal gG, the command cmd, the command copy dlt, the read-in reading difference obs. The field's own rules: plan cmd := (gG − yG)/C; copy dlt := cmd; Sedimentation 1 (normalized least mean squares): c += (obs − c·dlt)/dlt; Sedimentation 2: C := c. Input port {yG, gG, obs}: these read in, respectively, the reading of F, the goal of F, and the reading difference before and after the act.  
Trigger condition: the reading-difference read-in, the copy, Sedimentation 1 and Sedimentation 2 execute only when the command of the current window is cmd ≠ 0 and the reading-difference observation has arrived; in a window with cmd = 0 these four do not execute (no division by zero). The entry value of C is 1, and Sedimentation 1 executes only when dlt ≠ 0, with obs = p·dlt; hence c and C remain nonzero in the signed runs (1 and 2 when p = 2; 1 and 3 when p = 3), and the division in the plan is defined.

### 2.3  Entry (Complete Initial State)

### 2.4  Joint Run

A single global event table executes in phase order; each event is recorded simultaneously in the formal models of F and of G, appearing in the other side's model as a declared out-of-boundary component, and the values on the two sides are synchronized event by event. Every transfer across fields is an actual event: the input port of G reads y, g and the reading difference of F; the plan of G reads C and issues the command; the direct-write-port event of F (actor G) reads the command and writes w1.  
　W1 (beat 20): F reads out → G reads in y, g → G plans (C = 1) → G writes w1 += cmd through the direct-write port of F → F reads out → (if cmd ≠ 0 and the reading-difference observation has arrived) G reads in the reading difference, records the command copy, and sediments c and C.  
　Beats 25 and 26: F runs normally (input (1, 1); sedimentation makes the reading drift).  
　W2 (beat 30), beats 35 and 36, W3 (beat 40): the same event sequence as W1, with the plan reading the formed C.  
The formation data of G are taken from the actual consequences of its act in W1; no probe writes and no test weight-setting are used.

## Section 3  Verification Item by Item

C1  Rule level: P10 = 1 (declared routes: membrane drive, sedimentation, read-out, goal setting, out-of-boundary components, input port, direct-write port; exhaustive): the Trace-Response version is the w1 written by sedimentation at beat 25 (its source contains the input event), the read-in event is the membrane drive at beat 26 (which actually changes u), and the successor write is the w1 written by sedimentation at beat 26. If the mechanism is undeclared, the value is ⊥. Actual window: in the same run, beats 25 and 26 give the version path w1 (beat 25) —value-write→ u (beat 26) —value-write→ w1 (beat 26).

C2  Rule level: P10 = 1 (declared routes: plan, copy, the two sedimentations, out-of-boundary components, input port; exhaustive): the Trace-Response version is the c written by Sedimentation 1 in W1 (its source contains the input event of G), the read-in event is Sedimentation 2 (which actually changes C), and the path is c —value-write→ C. Actual window: the formation in W1; the baseline (entry C) is 1, the formed value is 2, the resolution is ε\_L = 0, and the value is retained from the end of W1 to the end of W3.

C3  Interception  
(a) Correspondence: the reading slope in the w1 direction, ΔΦ = p·δ; the conclusion about action is limited to the affine reading slope in the w1 direction.  
(b) Observations and inputs: G reads the reading, the goal and the reading difference of F only through the input port; the formation data are the command copy of G's own act and the reading difference before and after the act.  
(c) Domain of applicability: read-out mode (1, p); any state of F; δ a nonzero rational.  
(d) Fiber: G reads only differences of Φ; the correspondence in the w0 direction is not intercepted.  
Moment of formation: the observation of W1 (δ = 1/2, ΔΦ = 1) is the first effective observation, and Sedimentation 1 changes c from 1 to 2; the observations of W2 and W3, (−1/2, −1) and (−1/4, −1/2), make Sedimentation 1 execute while leaving c unchanged, and serve as verification after use.  
Discrimination condition: for δ ∈ {3/2, −2, 1/3}, which did not occur during the formation period, G's predictions C·δ = 3, −4, 2/3 agree exactly with the true values; a lookup table that records only the samples seen makes no prediction on the three cases. Another induced field with p = 3: the entry reading 1/2 + 3/2 = 2 already equals the goal, the command in W1 is 0, and by the trigger condition there is no reading-difference read-in and none of the subsequent copy and sedimentation (the goal and the reading are read in as usual); the drift at beats 25 and 26 makes the command in W2 nonzero, and G forms C = 3 in W2; the three predictions 9/2, −6, 1 agree exactly.

C4  Qualification of the Field Induction law  
Law candidate L: “with the slope stored in C, convert the reading deviation into the amount to be written to w1”; carrying organization R = {c, C}.  
Proved: R is formed by the write law of G through the input history (its source contains input events, and the entry value 1 differs from the formed value 2); R is current and retained when the acts of W2 and W3 take place; the values written by the act depend on R through a value-read chain—the plan of G in W2 reads in the version of C formed at the end of W1, and the source of the command contains that version; in F, the sources of the w1 versions written in W2 and W3 contain that version of C, the version of c, and the goal holding anchor; resetting R by a test intervention changes the act accordingly (C6(b)).  
Unproved: the formal evaluation of Definitions 3.34–3.47 of *The Mathematical Form of Law* of the Law Group Form on G; the bridging of *The Determination Form of Law* to fields other than the operating substrate; a formal certificate and read-back conditions on the same carrying organization. The sufficient direction of *The Necessity Form of Law* carries the carry-over condition SC\_Hold, which the Law Group Form registers as a semantic carry-over condition, yet to be proved; it is not closed within the domain of this construction.  
Conclusion: this Form delivers the construction of a Law candidate; formal law formation is not attained.

C6  Historical bearing  
(a) The same F, the same goal and subsequent inputs, two legitimate histories: H1 (after W1, G reads in the reading difference and sediments): C = 2 before W2, and after the writes of the three acts, (w1, reading) = (1, 5/2), (3/4, 2), (3/4, 2). H0 (after W1, G's reading-difference input is absent): C = 1 before W2, and after the writes, (1, 5/2), (1/4, 1), (3/4, 2) (G forms after reading in the reading difference in W2). The value written in W2 changes with the formed organization.  
(b) Test intervention: under H1, resetting C to its entry value 1 before W2 gives (1/4, 1) after the W2 write, the same as W2 under H0. The test intervention serves only for verification.  
(c) Formation evidence: the reading-difference read-in, the command copy, Sedimentation 1 and Sedimentation 2 in W1; in-use evidence: the plan reading C in W2 and W3, and the act reading the command.  
(d) Correspondence with D18-3: source—c and C are written by the write law of G through inputs, not written directly by out-of-boundary values (the act on F still lies on the causal chain of observation: the reading difference comes from the consequences of G's act on F); retention—C from the end of W1 to the end of W3; level—the host level of G.  
(e) Fixed schedule: under each of the two histories, G's read-ins and sedimentations execute as usual, and only at the acting end is the same schedule (1/2, −1/2, −1/4) written. Entry C of W2: 2 under H1, 1 under H0; the write and reading of W2 are the same under both histories ((3/4, 2)), whereas the W2 writes of the Law Engineering under the two histories are (3/4, 2) and (1/4, 1). The conclusion on historical bearing falls on W2. (In W2 under H0, G's command copy differs from the amount actually written, and the subsequent sedimentation makes no re-estimate against the amount actually written; W3 is recorded only as fixed execution.)  
Cross-field transplant control: placing the C = 3 formed with p = 3 into the G for p = 2, with no further formation, gives w1 = 2/3 and reading 11/6 after the W1 write: not aligned.

The firing reading and Comparison B are not signed.  
Permitted operations: read-ins at G's input port; G's own field rules; G's writes to w1 through the direct-write port of F; F's own field rules and inputs. Test interventions (for verification only): resetting C, cross-field transplant, fixed schedule.

## Section 4  Construction Theorem

## Section 5  Declaration of Strength

\- Only constructive strength is claimed: we do not prove that the Law Engineering is unique, minimal or general.  
\- Formal law formation is not attained: none of the following has been carried out—the formal evaluation of the criteria of *The Mathematical Form of Law* on G, the bridging to fields other than the operating substrate, and a formal certificate on the same carrying organization; SC\_Hold in the sufficient direction of *The Necessity Form of Law* is not closed within the domain of this construction.  
\- Each regulation delivers only the read-back of the necessary structure; event qualification is not signed.  
\- We do not prove that host-adjustment equilibrium is reachable, nor that it substitutes for training or constitutes weight-space programming, and we sign nothing about biological sleep and dreams.  
\- Interception is limited to the affine reading slope in the w1 direction; the maintenance configuration signs only Comparison A on the declared input family, window and membrane drive.  
H15-1  The declaration that the mechanism routes of F and G are exhaustive is truthful.  
H15-2  The read-out map of F is linear in the w1 direction (given by the declared model).
