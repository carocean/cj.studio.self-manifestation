# The Necessity Form of Content-Aligned Structure

## Section 1  Purpose

Content alignment makes the regulated field realize the goal on the declared inputs. Host adjustment transforms the host level; tuning transforms the dynamic layer. The question is this: under a frozen content task, a frozen structural class, and declared conditions, does content alignment necessarily transform the structure of the field into a specific structure corresponding to the content goal? Does host adjustment necessarily transform the structure of the Trace-Response set, and does tuning necessarily transform the structure of the dynamic layer? “A change of state has occurred” does not count as a structural transformation; the structure must be defined in advance in the language of organization, and it must be proved that it corresponds to the goal and actually bears the reading.

## Section 2  Core Propositions

2.1  The General Propositions and What This Form Proves  
General propositions (open):  
　(Host adjustment) Host adjustment necessarily transforms the structure of the Trace-Response set into a specific structure corresponding to the content goal;  
　(Tuning) The structure that tuning transforms lies only in the dynamic layer; tuning does not necessarily grow a structure of the Trace-Response set, but it necessarily transforms the structure of the dynamic layer into a specific structure corresponding to the content goal.  
What this Form proves: on two declared hosts and tasks, the conditional forms of these two propositions — host adjustment along two paths (direct write as the starting point, with the Trace-Response formed by the field's own write law; direct write changing the Sedimentation-Degree of an existing Trace-Response), and tuning with the physical layer held fixed; the complete antecedents and conclusions of each are given in Section 7. The general propositions remain open on other hosts and tasks.

2.2  Trace-Response Qualification and Trace-Response Organization  
Two things are checked separately:  
(a) Qualification (*The Mathematical Form of Trace-Response Set Transformation*, Field Concretization Group Form, Chapter 18, D18-3): each qualification interval of a position x begins with a formation — a version generated by the field's own write law under input and deviating from the baseline, which is the formation version of that interval. Rewrites within the interval (direct writes included) change only the Sedimentation-Degree and leave in the source a component of out-of-boundary cause (D18-2); a return to the baseline terminates the interval; after termination, being raised again by direct write alone does not restore qualification, which requires a new formation. Current identity reads the formation version of the current interval. The baseline is the entry value, with resolution ε\_L = 0. Members: the current qualification interval extends to the moment of judgment, and the number of beats actually executed after the current depth version is no less than the retention window L = 2.  
(b) Organization (D18-4): the labeled organization description comprises the members, the connections and the Sedimentation-Degree of each Trace-Response. Sedimentation-Degree is borne by the hosting mechanism (the Sedimentation-Degree δ of Field Chapter 1, *The Mathematical Form of the Field*, with the deepening and shallowing of Equation 2); depth affects passage (Equation 3a and Law 5; Outer Chapter 3, “the deeper the Sedimentation-Degree, the smaller its threshold-value” and “the deeper receives the flow first”; Inner Chapter 6, “repeated, the trace deepens; seldom used, the trace fades”). With the same batch of Trace-Responses and the same connection network, when some Trace-Response becomes deeper or shallower, the organization of the Trace-Response set changes. A direct write that lands on the Sedimentation-Degree of an existing Trace-Response directly changes the Trace-Response organization by way of direct write (at the level of the mode of act and the necessary items; full host-adjustment event qualification is judged separately); “there is an out-of-boundary direct write” does not imply “the existing Trace-Response organization is unchanged.”

### 2.3  The Requirement of Nontriviality

The structural class is defined by a finite Boolean formula over organizational relations, frozen in advance, and saturated under equivalence of the values of the relations used; each relation class contains a legitimate alignment witness (corresponding to the goal); the relations used actually bear the reading in the aligned state (breaking them gives a mismatch); the entry is not in the structural class; there exist states that have changed and are not in the structural class; the structural class is not equal to the alignment set.

## Section 4  Dynamics

### 4.1  Spiking Host 𝔐\_I

Input s ∈ D = {A = (1, 0), B = (0, 1), AB = (1, 1)}. Interneuron z\_I(s) = \[a\_A s\_A + a\_B s\_B ≥ 1\]; output z(s) = \[m\_A w\_A s\_A + m\_B w\_B s\_B − m\_u u z\_I(s) ≥ 1\]. w and a are excitatory connections, u is the inhibitory connection from the interneuron to the output, and the weights are nonnegative (H17-1); m ∈ {0, 1} is supported on w\_A, w\_B, u. Task: z(A) = z(B) = 1, z(AB) = 0.  
Run: each beat consists, in order, of input (s\_A, s\_B and the teaching signal t), the interneuron, the membrane drive v = m\_A w\_A s\_A + m\_B w\_B s\_B − m\_u u z\_I, firing z = \[v ≥ 1\], excitatory sedimentation w\_X += t·s\_X/2, recruitment sedimentation a\_X += t·s\_X/4, and inhibitory sedimentation u += κ·z\_I·z (κ being the law-layer plasticity coefficient). Ports: input, direct write, gating; the declaration of mechanism routes is exhaustive. Readings: Φ\_z = z(AB) (a state-level probe) and Φ\_v = m\_A w\_A + m\_B w\_B − m\_u u·\[a\_A + a\_B ≥ 1\] (the membrane drive under AB, a run-level scalar reading). Formation history: A, A, B, B (t = 1); entry retention part: A, B, A, B (t = 0).  
Rule-level P10 = 1: the Trace-Response version is the w\_A written by the excitatory sedimentation at beat 1 (its source contains the input event), the successor version is the w\_A written at beat 2, and the path is w\_A —value-write→ w\_A; the membrane drive at beat 2 reads in the first version and actually changes v. The actual window of each example is taken from the formative part of its own run.

### 4.2  Linear Host 𝔐\_L

y(s) = w1 s\_A + w2 s\_B + w3 s\_A s\_B (linear in the parameters). Host write law: w\_i += e·k\_i·(t − y)·φ\_i(s), φ = (s\_A, s\_B, s\_A s\_B), where t is the target input and e ∈ {0, 1} is the teaching-enable input; each beat executes input, read-out and write, and when e = 0 the write writes the same value. k is the law-layer plasticity coefficient. The task is as above (A and B output 1, AB outputs 0). Readings: the task readings are the three outputs and the loss ℓ = ½Σ(y − target)²; the tendency reading is declared before the run, taken as Φ\_L = y(AB) or Φ\_A = y(A).

## Section 5  Theorem Group

5.1  Structural Classes of the Spiking Host  
Relation vocabulary 𝓡: r₁ = z\_I(A), r₂ = z\_I(B), r₃ = z\_I(AB), r₄ = \[m\_u u \> 0\], r₅ = \[m\_A w\_A \> 0\], r₆ = \[m\_B w\_B \> 0\]. Derivation rule: the excitatory drive is monotone in the input set and the task read-out is nonmonotone, so there must be an effective negative pathway recruited only on AB, and both excitatory pathways are effective.  
Numerical structural class S\_c^num: with all gates open, 𝓡 = (0, 0, 1, 1, 1, 1) (reads only values and gates).  
Coarse structural class S\_c: {w\_A, w\_B, a\_A, a\_B, u} are all members, and the state belongs to S\_c^num; that is, it is defined over the extended vocabulary 𝓡⁺ = 𝓡 ∪ {mem(x)}.  
Depth relation D\_T: {w\_A, w\_B, u} are all members, and δ\_u \> δ\_wA + δ\_wB − 1.  
Dynamic-layer structural class 𝒮^m: 𝓡 takes (0, 0, 1, 1, 1, 1) on the gated effective weights. Host-organization structural class 𝒮\_θ: 𝓡 takes this value on θ, irrespective of where the members come from.

Theorem 1 (Characterization of alignment)  Write w̃ = m·w, ũ = m\_u u. Alignment ⇔ w̃\_A ≥ 1, w̃\_B ≥ 1, a\_A \< 1, a\_B \< 1, a\_A + a\_B ≥ 1, ũ \> w̃\_A + w̃\_B − 1.  
Proof: If z\_I(AB) = 0, then z\_I(A) = z\_I(B) = 0 and z(AB) = \[w̃\_A + w̃\_B ≥ 1\], while z(A) = 1 gives w̃\_A ≥ 1, a contradiction; hence z\_I(AB) = 1, a\_A + a\_B ≥ 1, and ũ \> w̃\_A + w̃\_B − 1. If z\_I(A) = 1, then z(A) = 1 gives ũ ≤ w̃\_A − 1, while z(B) = 1 gives w̃\_B ≥ 1, so that ũ \> w̃\_A, a contradiction; hence a\_A \< 1 and w̃\_A ≥ 1, and likewise for B. The converse follows by substitution. ∎  
Corollary: Alignment ⇒ 𝓡 = (0, 0, 1, 1, 1, 1) and ũ \> w̃\_A + w̃\_B − 1. In the full model the latter numerical relation is only a necessary condition (θ = (1/2, 1, 1/2, 1/2, 2) satisfies it without alignment); on the cross-section on which w̃\_A ≥ 1, w̃\_B ≥ 1, a\_A \< 1, a\_B \< 1, a\_A + a\_B ≥ 1 already hold, it is equivalent to alignment.

Theorem 2 (Nontriviality and bearing)  
(a) Layered saturation: S\_c^num is defined by the values of 𝓡 and is saturated under 𝓡-equivalence; S\_c is defined by the values of 𝓡⁺ and is saturated under 𝓡⁺-equivalence. Two run histories can have the same read-out weights and recruitment weights at the end of W (the same values of 𝓡) and yet differ in S\_c — Examples 5 and 2 are such a pair, differing only in whether u is a member.  
(b) State level: the entry (1, 1, 1/2, 1/2, 0) is not in S\_c^num; the state that changes only w\_A := 2 is not in S\_c^num; S\_c^num contains non-aligned states (u = 1/2); the numerical relation of D\_T contains non-aligned states (the example above). Membership level: the entry of Example 1 is not in S\_c (u is not a member); the W entry of Example 2 is in S\_c but not aligned (a non-aligned witness with retention); the W end and the retention end of Example 2 are in S\_c and aligned (a legitimate alignment witness of the membership class). For the linear host, the numerical equality of D^L is already true at the entry of Example 6; it is not required that every subpredicate be false at the entry.  
(c) Both routes, host adjustment and tuning, have legitimate alignment witnesses (5.3).  
(d) S\_c^num has only one 𝓡 relation class, and it contains an alignment witness; for the witnesses of the membership class S\_c see (b). Relation classes without an alignment witness may not be merged in (“the alignment set together with an isolated class” does not pass).  
(e) Bearing (for every nonnegative rational θ satisfying Theorem 1): u := 0 → z(AB) = 1, a mismatch on AB; a\_A := 1 → z(A) = 0 (since u \> w\_A), a mismatch on A; a\_A := a\_B := 0 → a mismatch on AB; w\_A := 0 → a mismatch on A.  
Proof: (e) by substituting item by item into the conditions of Theorem 1. ∎

Theorem 3 (Necessary writes)  
(Q1) Without redundancy: from (1, 1, 1/2, 1/2, 0), every aligned state changes u; from (1, 1, 0, 0, 2), every aligned state changes a\_A and a\_B.  
(Q2) Redundant extension (declared: k interneurons, recruitment weights a\_{j,A}, a\_{j,B} ≥ 0, inhibitory weights u\_j ≥ 0, every connection gateable): alignment ⇒ Σ\_{j ∈ J₀} ũ\_j \> 1, where J₀ is the set of units recruited only on AB.  
Proof: Write S\_s = Σ\_j ũ\_j z\_j(s). z(A) = 1 gives S\_A ≤ w̃\_A − 1, z(B) = 1 gives S\_B ≤ w̃\_B − 1, and z(AB) = 0 gives S\_AB \> w̃\_A + w̃\_B − 1. Recruitment is monotone in the input set, so S\_AB ≤ Σ\_{J₀} ũ\_j + S\_A + S\_B; combining these gives Σ\_{J₀} ũ\_j \> 1. ∎  
Whole-group bearing: setting the inhibition of the whole group J₀ to zero leaves A and B unchanged, while the membrane drive under AB is ≥ w̃\_A + w̃\_B − (S\_A + S\_B) ≥ 2, so AB fires: a mismatch. Ablating a single unit need not give a mismatch (with u₁ = u₂ = 2, deleting either one still leaves alignment). The bearing is signed on the aggregate relation and the whole-group intervention.  
The candidate-position family K = {u\_j, m\_uj, a\_{j,A}, a\_{j,B}} and the end-state predicate “j ∈ J₀” are frozen in advance. When every ũ\_j = 0 at entry, every legitimate history reaching alignment changes some u\_j or its gate, and that unit satisfies the end-state predicate in the end state. For a general entry: the aggregate relation depends only on K; when the entry does not satisfy it and the end state does, something in K must change, and the change may be in the recruitment weights alone (example: w = (1, 1), u₁ = 2, u₂ = 0, all recruitment weights 1/4; raising only the recruitment weights of the first unit to 1/2 gives alignment).

Theorem 4 (Existence premise of the tuning branch)  For fixed θ, an aligned gate state exists ⇔ θ is aligned with all gates open; the aligned gate state is uniquely the fully open one.  
Proof: Gates only set effective weights to zero; alignment requires w̃\_A, w̃\_B ≥ 1 and ũ \> 0. ∎

Theorem 5 (Path 1: direct write as the starting point, the successor Trace-Response formed by the field's own write law)  Suppose that at entry {w\_A, w\_B, a\_A, a\_B} are members and u is at baseline. If a run segment reaches alignment, at the end state u has a qualification interval that began under input by the field's own write law and continues, the current depth versions of u and of the other members have been retained, and the other members keep their qualification throughout the segment, then S\_c and D\_T hold at the end state.  
Proof: The values of 𝓡 and the numerical relation follow from the corollary to Theorem 1; qualification and retention are given by the antecedent. ∎

Theorem 6 (Path 2: direct write changes the Sedimentation-Degree of an existing Trace-Response)  
(i) If at entry δ\_u ≤ δ\_wA + δ\_wB − 1, the end state of any run that reaches alignment satisfies δ\_u \> δ\_wA + δ\_wB − 1.  
(ii) If the qualification intervals of u, w\_A, w\_B continue from the entry to the end state, the identities of these three Trace-Responses are continuous; if at both the entry and the end state all three are members with retention, then the flip in (i) is a flip of the depth relation D\_T among one and the same group of member Trace-Responses — the organization of the Trace-Response set changes, while membership and identity do not. To sign that the membership of the entire Trace-Response set is unchanged, the entries and exits of the other members must be checked separately; cases with reconstruction after a return to baseline are judged separately.  
(iii) If the change in (ii) is borne by the actor directly writing the Sedimentation-Degree of an existing Trace-Response, then it is a direct change of the Trace-Response organization by way of direct write (at the level of the mode of act and the necessary items; the full host-adjustment event is not signed).  
Proof: (i) From the corollary to Theorem 1. (ii) Continuation of the qualification interval means that identity is unchanged (2.2(a)), and a direct write changes only the Sedimentation-Degree. (iii) From (ii) and the source of the direct write. ∎

5.2  Structural Classes of the Linear Host  
Theorem L1  Alignment ⇔ (w1, w2, w3) = (1, 1, −2).  
Proof: y(A) = w1 = 1, y(B) = w2 = 1, y(AB) = w1 + w2 + w3 = 0; elimination gives w3 = −2; the converse follows by substitution. ∎  
Derivation rule: the contribution of the excitatory features is monotone in the input set and the task read-out is nonmonotone, so the co-occurrence Trace-Response is negative and its depth cancels the sum of the two excitatory Trace-Responses. Coarse structure S\_c^L: w1 \> 0, w2 \> 0, w3 \< 0; depth relation D^L: −w3 = w1 + w2. Alignment ⇒ S\_c^L ∧ D^L (numerical reading; the membership reading further requires all three positions to be members with retention). Nontriviality: S\_c^L and D^L each contain non-aligned states (e.g., (1/2, 1/2, −1/2) and (2, 2, −4)); bearing: in the aligned state, w3 := 0 makes the AB output 2, and w1 := 0 makes the A output 0.

Theorem L2 (Closed-form recursion for Path 1)  Entry θ = 0, plasticity coefficients k = (1/3, 1/3, 1/3), cyclic teaching of A, B, AB. At the end of the nth cycle θ = (x\_n, x\_n, −2x\_n), x\_n = 1 − (8/9)^n.  
Proof: The entry satisfies w1 = w2 = x, w3 = −2x (x = 0). The two steps A and B give w1 = w2 = x̂ = x + (1 − x)/3; at the AB step the output is 2x̂ − 2x, and each of the three positions decreases by (2x̂ − 2x)/3, giving w1 = w2 = x + (1 − x)/9 and w3 = −2(x + (1 − x)/9). ∎  
Corollary: θ\_n → (1, 1, −2); the limit state is aligned, with zero write-back in the next cycle. This is the mathematical limit of the recursion, not a retention that has occurred.

## Section 6  Boundary Cases and Conditions of Structural Collapse

\- Direct write of a position with no formation record: the host organization changes, the Trace-Response organization does not (Example 5).  
\- Only input and the field's own sedimentation: the structure grows all the same, but this is not host adjustment (Example 3).  
\- Redundant realization: the common must-change coordinates may be empty; only the aggregate relation and the necessary hit on the candidate-position family are signed (Theorem 3).  
\- Tuning branch: when θ is not aligned with all gates open, there is no alignment witness (Theorem 4); after tuning, the field's own write law may write the host level on the basis of the changed activity; this is the field's own sedimentation, neither a landing point of tuning nor a necessary result.  
\- When qualification is discontinuous (a return to baseline), identity is not continued from the old formation version.  
\- The linear host has only one aligned point; in this host D^L is just the alignment condition of the AB component.  
\- Counterexample clause: for the conditional propositions proved in this Form, a breach is a run in which all the antecedents of some formula of Section 7 hold while its conclusion fails. For the general propositions of 2.1 (open), if some instance is genuinely a host adjustment and reaches alignment while the members of the Trace-Response set, the declared organizational relations and the Sedimentation-Degrees of existing Trace-Responses all remain unchanged, it serves as a counterexample to that proposition within that working domain.
