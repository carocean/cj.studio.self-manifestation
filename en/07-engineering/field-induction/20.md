# The Source Form of Generalized Structure

## Section 1  Declaration of the Object

### 1.1  Question

On the declared spiking host, *The Necessity Form of Content-Aligned Structure* proves a proposition of conditional form: under the antecedents it lists, the aligned final state satisfies the relation and depth conditions of “the AB-specific inhibitory pathway and its depth relation”; the membership reading additionally requires the antecedent of Trace-Response qualification, and a direct write into an empty position can reach the same numerical relations without membership qualification. Is this structure a generalized structure — does it have a determinate identity within the same host, and does it bear alignment over a family of tasks? If so, by what process is it generated in content alignment, rather than given? Is its source unique?

### 1.2  Host and Working Domain

Host 𝔐\_I: input s ∈ D = {A = (1, 0), B = (0, 1), AB = (1, 1)}; interneuron z\_I(s) = \[a\_A s\_A + a\_B s\_B ≥ 1\]; output z(s) = \[m\_A w\_A s\_A + m\_B w\_B s\_B − m\_u u z\_I(s) ≥ 1\]; weights are nonnegative; gates m ∈ {0, 1} are supported on w\_A, w\_B and u. Each beat consists, in order, of input (s and the teaching signal t), interneuron, membrane drive, firing, excitatory sedimentation w\_X += t·s\_X/2, recruitment sedimentation a\_X += t·s\_X/4, and inhibitory sedimentation u += κ·z\_I·z (κ is the law-layer plasticity coefficient). Ports: input, direct write, gating; the declared mechanism routes are exhaustive.  
Working domain D\_mem: gates always open (m\_A = m\_B = m\_u = 1, no gating events). In this domain the effective weights equal the host values, and the structural relations are borne only by the physical Sedimentation-Degree; the instantaneous permeability of the gates does not enter the present object.  
Trace-Response qualification (*The Necessity Form of Content-Aligned Structure*, 2.2(a)): the qualification interval of a position is opened when the field's own write law, under input, writes a version that deviates from the baseline; rewrites within the interval (including direct writes) change only the Sedimentation-Degree; returning to the baseline terminates the interval; a direct write that raises a position outside any interval does not open an interval. Membership mem(x): the qualification interval continues, and at least 2 beats have actually been executed after the current depth version. The baseline is taken as the entry value.

### 1.3  Structure Type

A task τ is an independently given goal truth table τ: D → {0, 1}, written τ = (τ(A), τ(B), τ(AB)); a state is aligned with τ ⟺ ∀s ∈ D, z(s) = τ(s). The positive single-input set is P(τ) = {X ∈ {A, B} : τ(X) = 1}; the two-input nonmonotone tasks are T2 = {(1, 1, 0), (1, 0, 0), (0, 1, 0)}.  
Relation template Σ(τ) (reading host values in D\_mem):  
　R1 z\_I(AB) = 1; R2 ∀X ∈ P(τ), z\_I(X) = 0; R3 u \> 0; R4 ∀X ∈ P(τ), w\_X \> 0; D u \> w\_A + w\_B − 1.  
Object (conjunctive inhibitory Trace-Response structure): Σ\_mem(τ) = Σ(τ) ∧ mem(u). For τ = (1, 1, 0) this is “the AB-specific inhibitory pathway and its depth relation”; on single-positive-input tasks R2 constrains only the positive input, and the structure is “a conjunctive inhibitory pathway outside the positive input”. The positions bearing the excitatory and recruitment relations may be given at entry and left unchanged; R3 and D are borne by the Sedimentation-Degree of the inhibitory position u.

### 1.4  Identity

Two criteria are kept apart:  
(I) Same template type: (σ, ℛ) (task τ) and (σ′, ℛ′) (task τ′) are of the same type ⟺ they satisfy Σ\_mem(τ) and Σ\_mem(τ′) respectively, with the roles taking values according to their respective tasks.  
(II) Instance identity up to relabeling (D18-5 of *The Mathematical Form of Trace-Response Set Transformation*, Field Concretization Group Form, Chapter 18): take the declared subgroup G = {id, swap}, where swap exchanges the position labels A and B (in w, a, the gates m\_A, m\_B and the position labels in the formation record), exchanges the inputs A and B, leaves u, m\_u and κ fixed, and takes the identity on the output; (σ, ℛ) ≈\_G (σ′, ℛ′) ⟺ there exists g ∈ G with σ′ = π·σ and ℛ′ = π·ℛ. The action of g on tasks is (g·τ)(s) = τ(σ\_g⁻¹ s).  
(II) constrains only states and records, not tasks, and contains no qualification: any valid entry (e.g. w = (1, 1), a = (1/2, 1/2), u = 0, κ = 0, empty record) witnesses (II) via id, yet lacks Σ\_mem qualification, so (I) fails. The relation between the two is given by the preservation result below.  
Equivariance: recruitment, membrane drive and firing are equivariant under the simultaneous exchange, and the reading z(AB) and the AB membrane drive are invariant; the three sedimentations are relabeled term by term, and inhibitory sedimentation is invariant; processes are paired by name, and their contributions are identical pair by pair; direct-write positions are relabeled in step; the opening, continuation and termination of a qualification interval depend only on the value of the position and its baseline, the kind of process and the state of the interval, all three of which are carried along by π; by induction on the length of the history, the formation versions, intervals, retention counts and member sets are all π-images.  
Proposition 1 (Preservation of qualification under transport)  If Σ\_mem(σ, ℛ; τ), g ∈ G, (σ′, ℛ′) = g·(σ, ℛ) and τ′ = g·τ, then Σ\_mem(σ′, ℛ′; τ′), and the two instances are of the same type under (I). If the original instance is in addition aligned with τ, its image is aligned with τ′.  
Proof: P(τ′) = π·P(τ); R1–R4 and D correspond term by term by the equivariance of recruitment and of the host values under the simultaneous exchange; mem(u) is preserved by the π-transport of the record (u is fixed). The additional conclusion on alignment: by equivariance, z(σ′, σ\_g s) = z(σ, s), so if the original instance is aligned with τ, its image is aligned with τ′. ∎  
Σ\_mem does not contain alignment: with all gates open, w = (1/2, 1/2), a = (1/2, 1/2), entry u = 0, κ = 1/2 and t = 0, execute AB, A, A — at the first beat AB fires and inhibitory sedimentation forms u = 1/2, which is retained over the next two beats; R1–R4, D and mem(u) all hold, yet the actual output for τ = (1, 1, 0) is (0, 0, 0), not aligned.  
The task must be transported along with the instance: a qualifying instance for (1, 0, 0) qualifies for (0, 1, 0) after swap, but not for the untransported (1, 0, 0). (I) does not imply (II): with w = (1, 1), a = (1/2, 1/2) and all gates open, u formed by in-field sedimentation up to 3/2 and retained, and the same Trace-Response subsequently written directly to 2 and retained, are of the same type yet belong to different concrete state orbits.  
The orbits of the tasks under G are {(1, 0, 0), (0, 1, 0)} and {(1, 1, 0)}; what the three tasks share is a template parameterized by role, and reuse across orbits is proved separately (1.5).

### 1.5  Reuse Domain

Theorem R (Numerical necessity)  On 𝔐\_I (nonnegative weights, arbitrary gates), alignment with τ ∈ T2 ⇒ Σ(τ) (read with the effective weights m·w, m\_u·u); moreover, bearing holds relation by relation: u := 0 → AB mismatch; a\_A := a\_B := 0 → AB mismatch; for X ∈ P(τ), a\_X := 1 → X mismatch; w\_X := 0 → X mismatch.  
Proof: Take X ∈ P(τ). z(X) = 1 gives w̃\_X ≥ 1 (R4). If z\_I(AB) = 0, then, since recruitment is monotone in the input set, z\_I(X) = 0 and v(AB) = w̃\_A + w̃\_B ≥ w̃\_X ≥ 1, so AB fires, a contradiction; hence R1. z(AB) = 0 gives ũ \> w̃\_A + w̃\_B − 1 (D), and w̃\_A + w̃\_B − 1 ≥ w̃\_X − 1 ≥ 0; hence R3. If z\_I(X) = 1, then ũ ≤ w̃\_X − 1 ≤ w̃\_A + w̃\_B − 1 \< ũ, a contradiction; hence R2. Bearing follows by substitution item by item. ∎  
Among two-input tasks the reusable domain is exactly T2: every monotone task has an aligned state with u = 0 ((0,0,0): w = 0; (0,0,1): w = (1/2, 1/2); (0,1,1): w = (0, 1); (1,0,1): w = (1, 0); (1,1,1): w = (1, 1); a is 0). Three or more inputs lie outside the reuse domain: with excitatory weight 1 for each of three inputs, and for each of the three pairs of inputs one inhibitory unit recruited only when that pair occurs together, with inhibitory weight 4/5 and threshold 1, the membrane drive is 1 for a single input, 6/5 for any pair and 3/5 for all inputs — every proper subset fires and the full conjunction does not, yet there is no unit recruited only on the full conjunction.

Theorem M (Reuse at the membership level)  Working domain D\_F ⊂ D\_mem: at entry u = 0 (at baseline); κ ≥ 0 is the entry law-layer value; every change of a host position in the history is made by the field's own three sedimentations in actually executed beats. The moment of adjudication is taken once ≥ 2 beats have actually been executed after the current depth version of u. Then alignment with τ ∈ T2 ⇒ Σ\_mem(τ).  
Proof: By Theorem R, Σ(τ) holds, with u \> 0. u changes only through inhibitory sedimentation, is monotone nondecreasing, and does not return to baseline after its first change; the first change is a version deviating from the baseline, written by the field's own write law under input, which opens a qualification interval, and later changes change only the Sedimentation-Degree; by the retention clause, mem(u). ∎  
This necessary direction, “content alignment must generate u”, is limited to D\_F with u starting from baseline; the route in which an already formed u continues to bear and later has its depth changed by direct write remains open (S3 of Section 3). Outside D\_F, with κ = 0, entry w = (1, 1) and a = (1/2, 1/2), a direct write u: 0 → 2 gives alignment with (1, 1, 0) over the three beats A, B, AB, and Σ holds, yet u has no qualification interval and Σ\_mem fails.  
Each of the three tasks has a history in which all bearing positions are members (κ = 1/2; two teaching beats, four AB beats, four retention beats): (1, 1, 0) goes from (w, a, u) = (1/2, 1/2, 1/4, 1/4, 0) to (1, 1, 1/2, 1/2, 3/2); (1, 0, 0) from (1/2, 0, 1/4, 1/4, 0) to (1, 1/2, 1/2, 1/2, 1); (0, 1, 0) is its relabeled image. In each case the qualification interval of u begins with the inhibitory sedimentation under the AB input at beat 3.

## Section 2  Exclusion of Default Sources

The starting point does not have the structure: the entry (w, a, u) = (1, 1, 1/2, 1/2, 0) does not belong to Σ under either κ = 0 or κ = 1/2 (u = 0), and is not aligned either.  
Three things are kept apart:  
(i) Wholesale external placement: κ = 0, u is written directly from baseline to 2, and afterward there are only beats that do not change u — aligned, Σ holds, u has no qualification interval, and Σ\_mem fails. External placement gives the numerical relations of the host, not the present object; it is not a source of Σ\_mem.  
(ii) Direct write as starting point: a direct write of the plasticity coefficient κ, or of u itself, as the starting point for the field's own subsequent sedimentation.  
(iii) In-field formation: the qualification interval of u is opened by the field's own inhibitory sedimentation under AB input.

## Section 3  Characterization of the Generative Process

Declared history family 𝓗: entry set — for τ = (1, 1, 0) take w = (1, 1), for τ = (1, 0, 0) take w = (1, 1/2), for τ = (0, 1, 0) take w = (1/2, 1); in all cases a = (1/2, 1/2), u = 0, κ ∈ {0, 1/2}; gates always open. Set of legitimate operations — input beats A, B, AB (t = 0), the actor's direct write κ := 1/2 (law layer), and direct writes u := 1/2, 3/2, 2, 0.  
Source classification (for the current qualification interval of u at the moment of adjudication): starting point — before formation u is at baseline, or holds a direct-write value; κ source — at formation κ is the entry value (even if a same-value write occurred before formation, there was no actual change of κ), or was acquired by an actual direct write; depth — rewrites within the interval are made only by the field's own sedimentation, or include a direct write.  
Four generation chains (each ends with two retention beats A, B; the final state is aligned with (1, 1, 0) and belongs to Σ\_mem, with u = 3/2):  
　S1 The field's own sedimentation: κ entry 1/2, three AB beats — baseline / entry / sedimentation only.  
　S2 Direct write of the plasticity coefficient as starting point: κ entry 0, direct write κ := 1/2, three AB beats — baseline / direct write / sedimentation only; u is formed by the field's own write law.  
　S3 Direct write deepening an existing Trace-Response: κ entry 1/2, one AB beat forms u = 1/2, retained for two beats, direct write u := 3/2 — baseline / entry / with direct write; the interval continues, and the direct write changes only the Sedimentation-Degree.  
　S4 Direct write of the position itself as starting point: κ entry 1/2, direct write u := 1/2, two AB beats — direct-write start / entry / sedimentation only; the Trace-Response is formed by the field's own subsequent sedimentation.

## Section 4  Source Theorem

Theorem S (Source on 𝓗)  In 𝓗, if at some moment of adjudication the state is aligned with τ ∈ T2 and Σ\_mem(τ) holds, then:  
(a) the current qualification interval of u is opened by the field's own inhibitory sedimentation, and the formation version comes from u += κ·z\_I·z (z\_I = z = 1) under AB input at that beat;  
(b) κ \> 0 at formation; when the entry κ = 0, there must be a prior actual direct write of κ;  
(c) the history belongs to one class of “starting point × κ source × depth”. Under the entry κ = 1/2, the permitted same-value write κ := 1/2 does not constitute an actual change of κ, and the κ source is the entry; under the entry κ = 0, there must be an actual change of κ before formation, and the κ source is direct write. Hence there are exactly four classes for a fixed entry; for each task the union of the classes over the two κ entries comprises eight classes; each of the four classes of each entry has actual histories in 𝓗.  
Wholesale external placement (u without a formation record) is not a source of Σ\_mem.  
Proof: (a) mem(u) requires a qualification interval, and an interval is opened only when the field's own sedimentation writes a version deviating from the baseline; the only sedimentation component on u is inhibitory sedimentation, which is nonzero only if κ \> 0, z\_I = 1 and z = 1, and among the inputs of 𝓗, z\_I = 1 only on AB; returning to baseline terminates the interval. (b) From (a): κ has only two sources, the entry value and direct write. (c) Each of the three axes is a dichotomy and exhaustive, and this holds for histories of any length; actual histories of each class are given by all operation sequences of length ≤ 6. ∎

## Section 5  Where the Source Cannot Be Uniquely Determined

Same identity, same final state, different sources: the final states of S1, S2, S3 and S4 are all (w\_A, w\_B, a\_A, a\_B, u, κ) = (1, 1, 1/2, 1/2, 3/2, 1/2), all belong to Σ\_mem(1, 1, 0), u opens its qualification interval at beat 1 in all of them, and their source classes differ pairwise. Under the same entry κ = 1/2, S1, S3 and S4 already give three histories with the same final state and different sources; S2 belongs to the κ = 0 entry. The final state does not determine the source; the source must be read from the generating history.  
Control: from the κ = 0 entry, directly write κ := 1/2 and then directly write u := 3/2 into the empty position, without sedimentation: the final state is the same, it is aligned and Σ holds, yet u has no qualification interval — the same final-state values can lack membership qualification.  
All the multiple solutions for the source are listed: four classes for each entry in 𝓗. Outside 𝓗: the three histories of 1.5 in which all bearing positions are members (with teaching input t = 1) are source witnesses outside 𝓗; other entries and other hosts have not been exhausted.

## Section 6  Declaration of Strength

\- The object is limited to the host-level membership structure Σ\_mem with two inputs and gates always open; the reuse domain is T2; three or more inputs, and objects of the dynamic layer (gating), are not covered by this Form.  
\- Theorem R is universal on 𝔐\_I; Theorem M is limited to D\_F and the retention clause; the completeness of Theorem S is limited to 𝓗 and the declared classification.  
\- The interventions bear only the listed causes and process segments, and do not extend to interventions not performed.  
\- Acceleration by tendency raising (whether raising a tendency with this structure makes subsequent alignment faster or cheaper) is not proved.  
H20-1  The declaration that the mechanism routes of the host are exhaustive is truthful.  
H20-2  Weights are nonnegative; the retention window is 2 beats, and the baseline is taken as the entry value.
