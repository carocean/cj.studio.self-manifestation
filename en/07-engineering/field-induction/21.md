# The Discrimination Form of Training Substitution

## Section 1  Declaration of the Candidate Pair

### 1.1  Question

The Law Engineering G given by *The Construction Form of Field Induction Law Engineering* rewrites the induced field F through the direct-write port, so that its reading reaches the goal and is maintained during continuation; the same goal can also be reached by additional training—giving F a training rule of its own, driven by teaching input. Under the same host, the same goal-reaching criterion, and the same permitted subsequent history, is there an executable observation that separates “Law Engineering substituting for training” from “additional training”?

### 1.2  Common Host

F⁺: the F of *The Construction Form of Field Induction Law Engineering* (p = 2; host level w0, w1; run level x0, x1, u, y; out-of-boundary goal g = 2) with a training component and a teaching-input port added. The training component (sedimentation, writing w1): w1 += η·e·(g′ − y), η = 1/2, where y reads the read-out just executed within the same window; the teaching inputs g′, e enter through the input port. The declaration of mechanism routes is exhaustive: membrane drive, host sedimentation, training, read-out, goal setting, input port, direct-write port. The entry is the complete entry table of 2.3 of *The Construction Form of Field Induction Law Engineering*, with g′ = e = 0 in addition.

### 1.3  Candidates

H\_L (Law Engineering substitution): F⁺ executes jointly with the Law Engineering G. Within a window, in order: F read-out (phase 1) → G reads in y, g (3, 4) → plan (5) → G writes w1 through the direct-write port (6) → F read-out (7) → (when cmd ≠ 0 and the reading-difference observation arrives) G reads in the reading difference, records the copy, and performs the two sedimentations (8–11) → teaching input (g′, e) := (g, 0) (12) → training component (13) → teaching input e := 0 (14). In H\_L, e ≡ 0: the training component executes, but its write increment is zero and it does not change w1.  
H\_T (additional training): F⁺ executes alone. The regulation windows are at the same positions as in H\_L; within a window: F read-out → teaching input g′ := g, e := e\_on → training component → F read-out → teaching input e := 0. e\_on is 1 in the teaching period and 0 in the withdrawal period.  
In both arms the windows fall at beats 20, 30, 40, …; between windows both arms take the same continuation: K0 (no input between windows), Kp (input (1, 0) at two beats between windows), or Km (input (1, 1) at two beats between windows).

### 1.4  Common Goal-Reaching Criterion

Adjudication cross-section σ (the entry state or a window-end state, as in 1.4 of *The Reachability Form of Maintenance Law Engineering*). Ach\_K(σ): Φ(σ) = g, and when execution continues from σ under continuation K and the window protocol of that arm, the effective correction of that arm in every subsequent window—the actual change of w1, whether from G's direct write or from the training component—is zero.  
The effective correction of H\_L is G's direct-write amount, and Ach\_K reads back as the equilibrium set B\_K of *The Reachability Form of Maintenance Law Engineering*. The effective correction of H\_T in the teaching period is η·(g − y), which is zero when Φ = g. Stopping writing after teaching is withdrawn does not by itself constitute reaching the goal: Ach\_K is judged under the window protocol of the teaching period. Law-candidate qualification is listed separately—only H\_L has a Law candidate, in G (C4 of *The Construction Form of Field Induction Law Engineering*), and it does not enter Ach\_K; formal law formation is not signed.

### 1.5  Permitted Subsequent History

From the cross-section at which each arm first belongs to Ach\_K, that arm enters the withdrawal period: in H\_T, e\_on = 0; in H\_L, G keeps its permitted operations. The Law Engineering's right of correction is its right to act; this is the structure of H\_L as an object of comparison, not a difference between the histories of the two arms. Test intervention: before the F read-out of the first regulation window after withdrawal (phase (k, 0) of that window), directly write w1 += δ, δ ∈ Δ\_δ = {±1/8, ±1/4, ±1/2}; thereafter continue under K0 or Kp, with no teaching. First-proof domain: K ∈ {K0, Kp}, δ ∈ Δ\_δ, and the reading-difference observation of H\_L's window 1 arrives (the first effective observation; through it G forms C = p). The H\_L arm in which the reading-difference observation does not arrive—its readings at the ends of windows 1 and 2 are 5/2 and 3/2, C stays 1, and it does not enter the goal-reaching and withdrawal comparison on the declared schedule—lies outside the first-proof domain and is judged undecided.

## Section 2  Forward Map

### 2.1  Reaching the Goal

H\_T: in window 1, y = 3/2, w1 += (1/2)·(1/2) = 1/4, Φ = 1/2 + 2·(3/4) = 2; in each later window y = 2 and the correction is zero. At the end of window 1 it first belongs to Ach\_K (K0, Kp).  
H\_L: in window 1, cmd = (2 − 3/2)/1 = 1/2, Φ = 5/2; the reading-difference observation arrives, and G forms C = 2; in window 2, cmd = (2 − 5/2)/2 = −1/4, Φ = 2; thereafter the correction is zero. At the end of window 2 it first belongs to Ach\_K, that is, to B\_K (the case of Theorem 2 of *The Reachability Form of Maintenance Law Engineering*).  
Under K0 and Kp each arm executes six windows, window by window as above; in none of the events of H\_L does the training component make an actual change.

### 2.2  Perturbation Window

The entry of the perturbation window is Φ\_in = g + p·δ.  
H\_L: C = p = 2, cmd = (g − Φ\_in)/C = −δ, and at the end of the window Φ = g; the reading-difference observation arrives, sedimentation gives c := obs/dlt = p, and C is unchanged.  
H\_T: e = 0, so the write increment of the training component is zero; under K0 there are no events between windows, and under Kp x1 = 0, so the condition for host sedimentation is not met; at the end of the window Φ = g + p·δ.  
Proof: For H\_L, by 2.2 of *The Reachability Form of Maintenance Law Engineering*—within the window only the act changes Φ, and the read-out is linear in the w1 direction; for H\_T, by the formula of the training component and e = 0. ∎

### 2.3  Continuation Km

Let x denote w1 at the end of a window (w0 = 1/2). A single host beat is h(x) = x + (1/8)·\[x ≥ 1/2\] (membrane drive 1/2 + x ≥ 1 ⟺ x ≥ 1/2); h∘h is executed between two adjacent windows; the entry of the perturbation window is h(h(x)) + δ. H\_T in the teaching period: x := x + η·(g − (1/2 + 2x)); in the withdrawal period nothing is written within the window. H\_L within a window: x := x + (g − Φ\_in)/C, and on triggering C := obs/cmd = p. Both arms start from the same entry; H\_T withdraws teaching after one teaching window, and the perturbation is applied before window 2; H\_L executes two windows, and the perturbation is applied before window 3.  
Window by window: the window-end readings of H\_L are Φ = 5/2, 2, 2, 2, 2, 2 (the reading at the end of W2 is 2, but under Km every later window has a nonzero command, so it does not belong to Ach\_Km); for H\_T the entry of the perturbation window is w1 = h(h(3/4)) + δ = 1 + δ, the window-end reading is Φ = 5/2 + 2δ, and thereafter, while w1 ≥ 1/2, Φ rises by 1/2 per window. Under Km neither arm belongs to Ach\_Km.

## Section 3  Construction of the Distinguishing Observation

Distinguishing observation 𝒪\_a: the reading Φ at the end of the first regulation window after the perturbation.  
Verdict: Φ = g is judged H\_L; Φ ≠ g is judged H\_T.  
Executability: 𝒪\_a reads only the read-out of F and takes its value at the end of the first complete regulation window after the perturbation; the perturbation is applied by the test intervention at the declared phase.  
Undecided: δ = 0 (both arms end the window at g); no regulation window is completed after the perturbation; the two arms do not start from the frozen goal-reaching cross-sections.

## Section 4  Validity of the Distinction

Proposition 1 (First-proof domain)  For K ∈ {K0, Kp} and δ ∈ Δ\_δ, 𝒪\_a = g for H\_L, and 𝒪\_a = g + p·δ ≠ g for H\_T.  
Proof: 2.2 and pδ ≠ 0. ∎  
Case by case: under K0 and Kp each arm is executed for each of the six values of δ; H\_L ends the window at 2 in every case; H\_T ends it at 9/4, 7/4, 5/2, 3/2, 3, 1 (δ = 1/8, −1/8, 1/4, −1/4, 1/2, −1/2); every verdict is correct. When δ = 0 both arms end the window at 2.

Proposition 2 (Individual cases under continuation Km)  With the entry and phases of 2.3, the values of 𝒪\_a for the two arms are 2 and 5/2 + 2δ; they are equal if and only if δ = −1/4. Hence on Km the two arms separate for δ ∈ {±1/8, 1/4, ±1/2}; for δ = −1/4 the +1/4 of host sedimentation cancels the perturbation, and both arms give 2.  
Proof: The recursion of 2.3. ∎  
For δ = −1/2, H\_T has w1 = 1/2 after the perturbation, exactly at the host-sedimentation threshold; the window ends at 3/2, and the reading keeps rising in every later window. The separation on Km consists of individual cases, δ by δ, and does not constitute a discrimination theorem.

## Section 5  Discrimination Theorem

Discrimination Theorem  Under the common host F⁺, the entry (2.3 of *The Construction Form of Field Induction Law Engineering*), the arrival of H\_L's reading-difference observation in window 1, the common goal-reaching criterion Ach\_K (K ∈ {K0, Kp}), and the permitted subsequent history consisting of the withdrawal period and one perturbation δ ∈ Δ\_δ, the observation 𝒪\_a separates Law Engineering substitution H\_L from additional training H\_T: the reading of H\_L at the end of the first window after the perturbation is g, and that of H\_T is g + p·δ.  
Proof: Proposition 1. ∎

5.1  Cost Measures (Listed Separately; Bearing No Part of the Distinction)  
From the entry to the first membership in Ach\_K0: H\_T—1 window, 1 actual write event on w1, 2 teaching-port events of F⁺; H\_L—2 windows, 2 actual write events on w1, 6 input-port events of G, plus 4 teaching-port events of F⁺ (2 per window, with e always 0). The two classes of port events are listed separately; only the values of each arm are reported, and no conclusion is drawn about which is better.

5.2  Mechanism Record (Bearing No Part of the Distinction)  
Actual writes of w1 for δ = 1/4 under K0: for H\_T, one by the training component and one test perturbation; for H\_L, three direct writes by G and one test perturbation. The rule-level P10 of H\_T is 1, with the witness protocol of one teaching window followed by input (1, 1) at two beats (the version of w1 written by the training component → membrane drive → the successor version written by host sedimentation); this is an auxiliary protocol, different from the continuations of the first proof. A carrier intervention (resetting C) changes the act in H\_L (C6 of *The Construction Form of Field Induction Law Engineering*); H\_T has no corresponding carrier. These records only describe the write routes of the two arms; no verdict is drawn from candidate names or event labels.

\- Only the declared candidate pair, the first-proof domain, and the observation 𝒪\_a are signed; for continuation Km, only the recursion and the individual cases, δ by δ, are signed.
