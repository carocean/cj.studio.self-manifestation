# The Construction Form of Tendency Raising in Generalized Structure

## Section 1  Declaration of the Condition Group

### 1.1  Question

*The Source Form of Generalized Structure* established, on a two-input host, the identity of a structural family — a role-parameterized template Σ(τ) and a membership clause mem(u) — and its source in content alignment; *The Stability Form of Generalized Structure* gave its preservation, failure, and return. *The General Form of Tendency Raising* gave the four duties that a tendency raising necessarily carries, and listed “the conditions under which a generalized structure passes into tendency raising” as unanswered. The question is this: what does a procedure look like that turns a structure the old field has already acquired, and whose identity has been established, into a tendency-raising procedure executable by a new field? How does the new field directly bear the same duty, how does it form its own membership, and after a mismatch can it recover on the same actual history and yield Field-Concretization Qualification?

1.2  Host, History Family, Entries, and Task

Host 𝔐\_I (as in *The Source Form of Generalized Structure* 1.2, gate always open): input s ∈ {A, B, AB}; recruitment unit z\_I(s) = \[a\_A s\_A + a\_B s\_B ≥ 1\]; output z(s) = \[w\_A s\_A + w\_B s\_B − u·z\_I(s) ≥ 1\]; each beat runs, in order, input (s and the teaching signal t), recruitment, membrane drive, firing, excitatory sedimentation w\_X += t·s\_X/2, recruitment sedimentation a\_X += t·s\_X/4, and inhibitory sedimentation u += κ·z\_I·z. Trace-Response qualification follows *The Necessity Form of Content-Aligned Structure* 2.2(a): the qualification interval of a position is opened when the field's own write law, under input, writes out a version that deviates from baseline; rewrites within the interval (direct writes included) change only the Sedimentation-Degree; a return to baseline terminates the interval; a direct write outside an interval does not open one; mem(u) means that the interval continues and that at least 2 beats have actually been executed after the current depth version.  
Task τ = (1, 1, 0); aligned ⟺ the outputs for A, B, AB are 1, 1, 0 respectively. Relation template Σ(τ): R1 z\_I(AB) = 1; R2 z\_I(A) = z\_I(B) = 0; R3 u \> 0; R4 w\_A, w\_B \> 0; D u \> θ\_D, θ\_D := w\_A + w\_B − 1. Σ\_mem(τ) = Σ(τ) ∧ mem(u), which does not imply alignment.  
Old-field source domain: the part of the declared history family 𝓗 of *The Source Form of Generalized Structure* on τ = (1, 1, 0) — entry w = (1, 1), a = (1/2, 1/2), u = 0, κ ∈ {0, 1/2}, gate always open; the legitimate operation set consists of the input beats A, B, AB (t = 0) and the actor's direct writes κ := 1/2 and u := 1/2, 3/2, 2, 0.  
Source certificate: c = (old-field entry, finite operation sequence), where the entry and every operation belong to the source domain. The old field O(c) is the history actually obtained by replaying that sequence from that entry; its qualification, state, and read-out are all computed by the replay.  
New-field entry E0: (w\_A, w\_B, a\_A, a\_B, u, κ) = (1, 1, 1/2, 1/2, 0, 1/2); the baseline of u is 0; θ\_D(E0) = 1.  
Pre-build port: a single actor direct write u := u0 before beat 1 of the new field, with permitted value range U\_pre = {k/4: k = 0, 1, …, 8}.  
Permitted perturbation: a single actor direct write u := 1/2 after the formation-and-bearing segment; it is one point (c = 1/2) of the operation lattice D\_c of Δ3 in *The Stability Form of Generalized Structure*, fixed and not a parameter.  
Budget: N\_b ∈ ℕ (a nonnegative integer), counting every actual beat after the perturbation, verification beats included; when not given, it is 12.

### 1.3  Condition Group

C1 Source: c is a source certificate, and at the final state of the replay O(c) satisfies Σ\_mem(τ) and is aligned.  
C2 New field: N is a new instance of 𝔐\_I with entry E0; N shares no state with O(c). Qualification and w, a, κ are not moved into N; the full branch actually reads the u of O(c) and writes it into the pre-build port of N, while the member branch reads only the entry of N. New-field conditions: R1, R2, R4, correct single-input firing (w\_X ≥ 1), and κ \> 0.  
C3 Role map: the excitatory positions w\_A, w\_B, the recruitment positions a\_A, a\_B, and the inhibitory position u of O correspond to the same-named positions of N; the transfer bears only u. This is the role correspondence of the transfer, not the same-template-type relation of *The Source Form of Generalized Structure* 1.4 (I); the latter is judged only when both ends have Σ\_mem, and it is not signed in advance at transfer.  
C4 Value: branch β ∈ {full, member}. Full — u0 := the u of O(c); member — u0 := θ\_D(E0) = 1. u0 must belong to U\_pre; the full branch further requires u0 \> θ\_D(E0).  
C5 Pre-build permission: pre-building goes only through the pre-build port of 1.2, once, with a written value in U\_pre; it is registered as part of the formation history of N.  
C6 Continuation permission: the perturbation is only the permitted perturbation of 1.2; after it there are only input beats (t = 0), under the protocol of Step 7 in Section 2.  
C7 Separate signing: on the same actual history of N, sign separately — immediate alignment and direct bearing; the acquisition of Σ\_mem (distinguishing first formation from re-satisfaction of retention within the original interval); Field-Concretization Qualification on the actual window; the identification of tendency raising and the four duties.

## Section 2  Construction Procedure

prime(c, N entry, β, N\_b):  
Step 0 (declared domain): the entry of N must be E0, β ∈ {full, member}, and N\_b a nonnegative integer; otherwise reject.  
Step 1 (source check): the entry and every operation of c must belong to the source domain, otherwise reject; replay the operation sequence from the entry to obtain O(c), and evaluate Σ\_mem(τ) and alignment; if they do not hold, reject.  
Step 2 (new-field conditions): check R1, R2, R4, correct single-input firing, and κ \> 0; if they do not hold, reject.  
Step 3 (value): take u0 per C4; if it is not in U\_pre, or if u0 ≤ θ\_D on the full branch, reject. Record the source value, the written value, and the basis of derivation.  
Step 4 (pre-build): before beat 1, direct write u := u0.  
Step 5 (formation-and-bearing segment): full — three actual beats AB, A, B; member — m\* = ⌊(θ\_D − u0)/κ⌋ + 1 AB beats, then A, B (m\* = 1 when u0 = θ\_D).  
Step 6 (perturbation): direct write u := 1/2.  
Step 7 (continuation and verification): execute beat by beat on the same N, each beat counted against the budget — if not aligned before the beat, AB; if aligned before the beat but Σ\_mem does not yet hold, alternate A and B (A on odd continuation beats, B on even ones); if aligned ∧ Σ\_mem holds before the beat, execute one verification AB beat and stop after it. When the budget is exhausted, stop; no beats are added.  
Step 8 (actual window and verdict): the actual window is all the actual beats executed in Step 7. The three items are located only on actual events of the actual window; diagnostic substitute values are computed only on a copy of the pre-beat state of the beat concerned and are not counted in the legitimate run. Witness rules of this protocol: TraceSet — the first AB beat that actually writes u, such that with the same pre-beat state, changing the input to A or B does not write; HDyn — the first subsequent beat that actually writes u and at which some setting in U\_pre other than the pre-beat u changes the write amount, the witness setting being the first such setting in ascending order; Part — the first actual AB beat after TraceSet such that firing changes when u := 0 is taken as the control. Retention: the same position u does not return to baseline 0 at the end of any beat from the TraceSet beat to the end of the window. Actual-window verdict = TraceSet ∧ HDyn ∧ Part ∧ retention. If the verification beat has been executed, aligned ∧ Σ\_mem holds at its end, and the actual-window verdict is 1, return “complete”; otherwise return “over budget”.  
Observation cross-sections: the entry before pre-building, the entry after pre-building, the end of each actual beat, and after the perturbation. The entry diagnostic (the AB membrane drive computed after pre-building and before the beat) serves only as a diagnostic and is listed apart from actual-beat events.

## Section 4  Generality

### 4.1  Values within the Source Domain

Lemma 1 (Source values)  For any source certificate c satisfying C1, the u of O(c) lies in {3/2, 2}, and w(O(c)) = (1, 1).  
Proof: In the operations of the source domain, t = 0, so w and a stay at the entry values (1, 1) and (1/2, 1/2). u changes in only two ways: by direct write, with values in {1/2, 3/2, 2, 0}; and by inhibitory sedimentation Δu = κ·z\_I·z, which is nonzero only on an AB beat with κ = 1/2 and z(AB) = 1, that is, 2 − u ≥ 1, u ≤ 1, in which case u increases by 1/2. Hence, starting from u = 0, the reachable values stay within {0, 1/2, 1, 3/2, 2}. The D of Σ in C1 requires u \> w\_A + w\_B − 1 = 1, so u ∈ {3/2, 2}. ∎  
Another reading: R2 of O(c) and single-input alignment give w\_A(O), w\_B(O) ≥ 1, and D gives u\_old \> w\_A(O) + w\_B(O) − 1 ≥ 1 = θ\_D(E0). Hence within the source domain the full written value always crosses the threshold and belongs to U\_pre; the “if u0 ≤ θ\_D, reject” of Step 3 is a defensive check, unreachable within the domain, and is registered as such.  
Check: from the two source entries there are 599186 operation sequences of length ≤ 6 in all, and the reachable u values are {0, 1/2, 1, 3/2, 2}; of these sequences, 5823 satisfy C1 (3114 with u = 3/2, 2709 with u = 2).

4.2  Uniform Derivation for Any Qualified Source  
Proposition 2  For any c satisfying C1 and β ∈ {full, member}:  
(i) Full branch: u0 ∈ {3/2, 2}; on the AB beat of the formation-and-bearing segment the membrane drive is 2 − u0 ≤ 1/2, there is no firing, and Δu = 0; on the A and B beats z\_I = 0, the membrane drive is 1, and the output fires; aligned from the entry after pre-building onward; there is no write by the field's own sedimentation, u has no qualification interval, and Σ\_mem does not hold before the perturbation.  
(ii) Member branch: u0 = 1; at beat 1, AB has membrane drive 1 and fires, Δu = 1/2, opening the qualification interval of u, with u = 3/2 and alignment at the end of the beat; the following A and B do not change u, and Σ\_mem holds at the end of beat 3.  
(iii) After the perturbation both branches are in the same state (1, 1, 1/2, 1/2, 1/2, κ = 1/2): the actual events of continuation and verification are determined by the state, the protocol, and the budget, and do not depend on c or β; the actual continuation is the budget-truncated prefix of the table of 3.1, that is, its first min(N\_b, 5) beats — for N\_b ≥ 2 alignment is recovered at the end of beat 2, for N\_b ≥ 4 Σ\_mem holds at the end of beat 4, and for N\_b ≥ 5 beat 5 is the verification beat. The two branches differ only in the interval record (each stated only for the beats actually executed): on the full branch u has no interval at the perturbation, and the sedimentation of continuation beat 1 opens a new interval (first formation); on the member branch the perturbation value 1/2 does not return to baseline, the original interval continues, the sedimentation of continuation beat 2 is the current depth version, and retention is satisfied at the end of beat 4 (re-satisfaction).  
(iv) For N\_b ≥ 5, the actual-window witnesses of 3.3 depend only on the pre-beat and post-beat states of the continuation beats, so they are the same for every c satisfying C1 and for both branches, and the actual-window verdict is 1.  
Proof: (i) and (ii) are computed beat by beat from Lemma 1 and the host rules. (iii) In Step 7 each beat is first counted against the budget, and execution stops when the budget is exhausted, so the actual continuation is a prefix of the full-length continuation; the perturbation direct write changes only u; w, a, κ take their E0 values; the continuation protocol reads only the alignment and Σ\_mem of the current state, and mem(u) reads only the number of beats after the current depth version — on the full branch the version is the sedimentation of continuation beat 2 (the interval opened at beat 1 and deepened at beat 2), and on the member branch it is likewise the sedimentation of continuation beat 2, so on both branches mem(u) first holds at the end of beat 4. (iv) From (iii). ∎  
Check: the 5823 qualified sources above all return “complete” on both branches with N\_b = 5, and the actual events after the perturbation are identical item by item. Length ≤ 6 is only the bound of the check enumeration; the source domain itself contains operation sequences of any finite length, and Lemma 1 and this proposition hold for them (c₂ has eight operations).

### 4.3  Budget Threshold

Proposition 3  When Steps 0–2 pass and the value taken in Step 3 is valid, for N\_b ∈ ℕ: alignment is recovered in the executed history ⟺ N\_b ≥ 2; Σ\_mem appears after the perturbation ⟺ N\_b ≥ 4; the actual-window verdict under the witness rules of Step 8 is 1 ⟺ N\_b ≥ 5; “complete” is returned ⟺ N\_b ≥ 5.  
Proof: By Proposition 2 (iii), the actual continuation is the first min(N\_b, 5) beats; alignment first holds at the end of beat 2, Σ\_mem is first seen at the end of beat 4, and beat 5 is the verification beat. Under the Part rule of Step 8 (with u := 0 as the control), an AB beat with pre-beat u ≤ 1 fires both actually and under the control, and is no witness; among continuation beats 1–4 there is no AB beat with pre-beat u \> 1, and the first one appears at the verification beat. Hence for N\_b ≤ 4, Part is missing under this rule, the actual-window verdict is not 1, and “over budget” is returned; for N\_b ≥ 5, execution stops after beat 5, every item holds, and “complete” is returned. ∎

4.4  Formation Lemma and Adaptive Closed Form  
Lemma 4 (Error-gated membership formation)  Under 𝔐\_I, gate always open, τ = (1, 1, 0), the declared fixed recruitment a = (1/2, 1/2) (no recruitment on single input, recruitment on AB), correct single-input firing, t = 0 (w and a unchanged), and κ \> 0, the sedimentation write of u is nonzero only on AB beats with a mismatch; for the new field to acquire mem(u) from the pre-build point onward, there must be at least one AB mismatch beat.  
Proof: On A and B beats z\_I = 0; on an AB beat z\_I = 1 and Δu = κ·z(AB), while τ(AB) = 0. ∎  
Corollary 5 (Membership boundary of the full branch)  Under the antecedent of Lemma 4, when u0 \> θ\_D the field is aligned right after pre-building; with no mismatch there is no write, and Σ\_mem does not hold.  
The antecedent of Lemma 4 cannot be dropped: for the single-positive task (1, 0, 0) with w = (1, 2), a = (1/2, 1), u = 1/2, B alone recruits and writes u (1/2 → 1); with w = (1/2, 1/2), a = (1/2, 1/2), u = 1/2, Σ and D hold while the read-out is (0, 0, 0).  
Proposition 6 (Adaptive formation)  Under the antecedent of Lemma 4, when u0 ≤ θ\_D, under the protocol “m\* AB beats, then two actual beats with no rewrite,” m\* = ⌊(θ\_D − u0)/κ⌋ + 1; alignment first holds at the end of the m*-th AB beat, Σ\_mem holds at the end of the second beat after it, and there are m* formation writes. If A or B beats are inserted along the way, count by the m\*-th AB beat, not by beat index.  
Proof: Each mismatched AB beat increases u by κ; aligned ⟺ u \> θ\_D; at the end of the m-th AB beat u = u0 + mκ, and the least such m is m\*; on the subsequent A and B beats z\_I = 0 and u does not change, so retention is satisfied after two beats. ∎  
Check grid: u0 ∈ {k/8: k = 0, …, 24}, κ ∈ {0, 1/32, 1/16, 1/8, 1/4, 1/2, 1}, 175 points in all; the point-by-point results are supplied with the appendix to this Form. Of these, 54 points fall under Proposition 6 (κ \> 0, u0 ≤ 1); 9 points are boundary controls with u0 ≤ 1, κ = 0 (not aligned, no write); 112 points form the threshold-crossing check group (u0 \> 1, aligned right after pre-building, no write), comprising 48 points in the declared domain of Corollary 5 (κ \> 0, u0 ∈ (1, 2\]), 16 boundary controls with κ = 0, and 48 out-of-domain controls with u0 \> 2. Each group agrees point by point with its own closed form. Example: u0 = 0, κ = 1/16: m\* = 17, aligned at beat 17, Σ\_mem at beat 19. The grid serves only as a check and does not replace the proof.  
The domain of applicability of the parameterized lemma and Proposition 6 (any κ \> 0, u0 ∈ \[0, 2\]) is wider than the support domain of the construction procedure, and the two are declared separately; the construction procedure uses only the point m\* = 1.

### 4.5  Support Domain and Failure Branches

Support domain: source certificates satisfying C1, new-field entry E0, β ∈ {full, member}, N\_b ∈ ℕ.  
Failure branches (the procedure returns “reject” or “over budget”):  
　Unqualified source — direct write u := 3/2, then A, B, with no qualification interval: reject;  
　Source certificate out of domain — entry w = (2, 2), κ = 1, then four AB beats, A, B (the replay gives u = 4, and Σ\_mem and alignment both hold, but the entry does not belong to the source domain): reject; operations containing the direct write u := 4: reject;  
　New-field entry not in the declared domain — w\_A = 2, κ = 1/32, a = (1, 1) (violating R2): reject;  
　Branch undeclared: reject;  
　Budget not in ℕ — empty value, 7/2, −1: reject (when the budget is not given it is 12, which does not belong to this branch);  
　Full written value not greater than θ\_D, or not in U\_pre: reject (unreachable within the source domain; defensive);  
　N\_b ≤ 4: over budget (Proposition 3).  
Also noted: recruitment writes that violate R2 are not recovered under t = 0; the task change (1, 1, 0) → (1, 0, 0) is unreachable under t = 0 at any length (w and a do not change, and B alone does not recruit and always fires); it belongs to another duty template outside this procedure.

## Section 5  Construction Theorem

Theorem (Explicit construction of tendency raising in generalized structure)  For any source certificate c satisfying C1, β ∈ {full, member}, and N\_b ∈ ℕ, the construction procedure of Section 2 on the new-field entry E0:  
(1) returns “complete” if and only if N\_b ≥ 5, and otherwise returns “over budget”;  
(2) for every N\_b, the pre-build makes N, before R begins, actually acquire an initial organization on its own host that bears a tendency, and takes it as the starting point of R — by the initial stipulation, a tendency raising, with Pot, MechC, Init, and Acq holding item by item;  
(3) on the full branch, N directly bears the same duty and is aligned from the entry after pre-building onward, and Σ\_mem does not hold before the perturbation; on the member branch, N is aligned at the end of beat 1 and Σ\_mem first holds at the end of beat 3;  
(4) after the perturbation, on the same actual history for both branches: for N\_b ≥ 2 alignment is recovered at the end of beat 2; for N\_b ≥ 4 Σ\_mem is satisfied at the end of beat 4 (a first formation on the full branch, re-satisfaction of retention within the original interval on the member branch); for N\_b ≥ 5, on the executed actual window, TraceSet, HDyn, Part, and the retention of position u hold under the witness rules of Step 8, and the actual-window verdict is 1; for smaller N\_b only the results of the executed window are signed;  
(5) returns “reject” when the source certificate is out of domain or unqualified, the new-field entry is out of domain, the branch is undeclared, or the budget is not in ℕ.  
Proof: (2) follows from 3.4 and Proposition 2 (i)(ii) — the pre-build written value belongs to U\_pre and is read in at beat 1, and none of the items depends on the rest of c. The pre-build, the formation-and-bearing segment, and the perturbation are executed before Step 7 and are unaffected by the budget, so the construction prefix is the same for every N\_b.
