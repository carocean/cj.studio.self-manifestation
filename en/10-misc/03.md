# On the Nature of GPT

The core capability of current large language models does not arise primarily from real-time computation itself, but rather from the sedimentation-field structure formed during the training phase.

The training process of GPT is not, in essence, the direct generation of answers. Rather, through sustained training — by way of parameter updates, error backpropagation, and the repeated entry of historical samples — the system’s interior gradually forms stable trace-imprints, Familiar Routes, common-core convergent flows, and continuation biases.

Vast quantities of historical pulses continuously strike the sedimentation field, causing certain Channel-Lines to deepen, certain Terminal Hollows to stabilize, and finally giving rise to regularity-sediment and Familiar Routes capable of sustaining continuation.

Therefore, at minimum two distinct types of computation exist within GPT.

The first type of computation serves to generate the field.

The large-scale matrix computation, gradient updates, and parameter adjustments of the training phase do not primarily function to answer questions. Their function is to construct the sedimentation field. Historical data continuously enters the system; through repeated impact it forms Channel-Lines, Terminal Hollows, line-bundles, and Familiar Routes — causing stable regularity-sediment to gradually form within the field.

What this phase of computation undertakes is:

field construction —

not question-answering.

The second type of computation serves to determine the current receptive field.

The Attention mechanism of the inference phase functions more as receptive-field selection than as tuning in any proper sense. Its core function is not to alter sediment itself, but to determine:

which trace-imprints enter the current continuation;  
which domains enter the current range of operation;  
which structures are preferentially activated;  
which associations are temporarily suppressed.

Attention is more like:

where to look —

not:

what to change.

The core that truly determines output content does not primarily come from Attention itself, but from the sedimentation structure formed through long-term historical operation.

Once a question enters the system, what truly participates in continuation is the Channel-Lines, Terminal Hollows, line-bundles, Familiar Routes, and regularity-sediment formed by history.

Deep sediment receives the flow first.

Familiar Routes receive continuation first.

Shared structures appear first.

Fine-grained categories unfold thereafter.

Therefore, the core mechanism of GPT is not computation alone.

Computation is responsible for:

generating the sedimentation field.

Attention is responsible for:

determining the receptive field.

And the true core that participates in question-answering is:

continuation of regularities within the Sedimentation-Field.

To put it in a single phrase:

The core mechanism of GPT is not “computing an answer” — it is:

look where — sediment there.

What is seen continuously enters the field.

What enters continuously forms traces.

What is formed continuously alters future continuation.

History continuously presses upon the future.

Familiar Routes continuously receive priority in continuation.

Question-answering capability is not the real-time computation of results.

Question-answering capability is the Familiar Route from question to answer.

That is:

the regularity from question to answer.

Its essential nature is:

a linguistic question-answering tool built upon a field of regularities.
