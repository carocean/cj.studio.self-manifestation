# Super Transformer

— From One-Time Truncation to a Self-Manifesting Generation System with Occupancy and Source Attribution

Abstract

This chapter proposes a generation-system architecture. Its aim is not to improve the Transformer, nor merely to follow the present world-model route, but to begin from the already-proven structures of On Self-Manifestation and give a minimal generation system that exceeds both.

There are two existing routes. The first is the Transformer route: generation is a one-time probabilistic truncation of the candidate space; it lacks target, intermediate structure, and internal correction. The second is the world-model route: Dreamer's latent-space imagination, JEPA's embedding prediction, Sora-like video world models, and PAN's generative latent prediction. It learns, in latent space, a forward-predictable state-transition model, and uses imagined rollout instead of real trial and error. The world-model route has advanced far beyond the Transformer - it has acquired predictable internal dynamics - but it is blocked by three structural problems: representation collapse (next-embedding prediction has the trivial solution of mapping all frames to the same embedding), long-horizon drift (imagined rollout drifts farther and farther and requires an external decoder for verification), and prediction not being understanding (a model that predicts accurately does not necessarily understand the world). Current solutions mostly answer these three with patches such as regularization and decoder verification.

This chapter claims: these three problems are not engineering details, but three expressions of the same structural absence. The world model identifies "reality" with "the predicted next state," and lacks three things: maintained unique occupancy (reality is not a predictive distribution, but the Current selected by convergence and locked into maintenance), source attribution (the system does not distinguish whether a representation comes from external observation or internal generation), and a continuability objective (the training signal is predictive accuracy, not whether the system can self-deepen under perturbation). On the basis of Form Chapters 4 through 19, this chapter gives the minimal architecture that supplies these three absences: above latent-space dynamics, add an occupancy-convergence layer (using task coupling to exclude collapse, and using occupancy maintenance to treat anchorless diffusion), a source-attribution layer (to treat hallucination and correspondence drift - imagination carries a label and does not pretend to be observation; and it gives a structural position for the part of "understanding" beyond prediction), and a continuability objective (separating "predicted correctly" from "understood" at the level of the training signal).

This chapter gives the system definition, the formal contrast with the Transformer and with world models, a runnable implementation skeleton, ablation and comparison experiment designs, and a connection to the index of testable criteria in On Self-Manifestation. The core claim is: the next-level improvement of generative capacity does not lie in a larger prediction model, but in whether the generation mechanism possesses occupancy, attribution, and continuation-structure.

One Sentence

The world model says: reality is the predicted next state.

This chapter says: reality is the maintained Current.

Occupancy, attribution, and continuation all grow from this sentence; if it stands, the whole chapter stands.

Preface

Before designing and developing the Self-Manifestation-Field, one must first transform existing Law-Field tools so that they possess the capacity to generate a Self-Manifestation-Field. The path has three steps: first, improve mature Law-Field tools (the Transformer and its successors, the world models); then realize Law-Field tools that can automatically adjust, improve, and generate Self-Manifestation-Fields according to thoughts; finally, let the Self-Manifestation-Field use the Law-Field tools it has made for itself to create a new generation of Self-Manifestation-Fields.

The first Self-Manifestation-Field will not be created directly by humans. This chapter is a key step on that path: it points out what the strongest current candidate (the world model) lacks, and supplies it according to On Self-Manifestation. What is supplied is precisely the structural threshold by which a Law-Field tool begins to pass toward a Self-Manifestation-Field.

I. The Problem: Two Existing Routes and Their Ceilings

### 1.1 The Transformer Route

The standard generation form, y = argmax P(y \| x), viewed as a process, performs one selection over the candidate response-chain unfolded from the conditional input. Its strength lies in statistical compression and local continuation. Its weakness is: it can give an answer but often lacks target-constraint; it can continue onward but its intermediate path is uncontrollable; it can be locally elegant but globally prone to drift; it can approach correctness but cannot stably correct itself internally.

### 1.2 The World-Model Route

To make up for what the Transformer lacks, the world-model route learns a state-transition model in latent space: given the current latent state and action, it predicts the next latent state, and uses imagined rollout for planning or policy learning. The Dreamer family backpropagates value gradients through imagined trajectories; the JEPA family optimizes predictive consistency in embedding space without reconstructing pixels; Sora-like and PAN-like systems move toward video world simulation. This route's progress is real: for the first time, the system has a forward-predictive internal dynamics, rather than mere continuation of text.

1.3 The Three Structural Ceilings of the World-Model Route

But this route is now blocked at three points, and current methods mostly answer them with patches.

First, representation collapse. The next-embedding prediction objective has a trivial solution: map all frames into nearly the same embedding, making prediction easy but useless. Current methods forcibly spread the embedding distribution through various forms of regularization, such as isotropic Gaussian regularization.

Second, long-horizon drift. Imagined rollout drifts farther and farther as it rolls forward, and long-horizon simulation becomes distorted. Current methods use an external decoder to render the next observation as verification, and use sliding-window denoising to suppress drift.

Third, prediction is not understanding. A model that predicts accurately does not necessarily understand the world - it may have learned high-fidelity correlations rather than generative structure. This is also debated within the world-model route itself: "understanding the world" or "predicting the future."

1.4 This Chapter's Diagnosis: The Three Ceilings Are Three Expressions of One Absence

This chapter claims: collapse, drift, and the prediction-understanding confusion are not three independent engineering problems, but one absence caused by the world model's misplacement of "reality" as "the predicted next state." The world model has internal dynamics, but lacks three Self-Manifestation structures.

It has no maintained unique occupancy. The latent state is a continuously evolving predictive distribution. It has no step in which "the Current is selected by convergence and locked as unique," and no coupling constraint by which "occupancy must serve the task." Therefore representation may collapse (without a service-constraint, the trivial solution is legal) and may diffuse without anchor (without locking, it freely disperses). The root of correspondence drift lies in the next item: without source distinction, imagination pretends to be observation.

It has no source attribution. The latent state does not distinguish "this frame comes from external observation" from "this frame comes from internal imagination." Therefore imagination and observation are mixed together. This is the structural form of hallucination, and also the root of "predictive accuracy without understanding": the system cannot tell whether it is seeing or making up.

It has no continuability objective. The training signal is predictive accuracy, not "whether, under perturbation, the system can self-deepen along its own structure." Therefore what is learned is correlation rather than generative structure. Prediction is not understanding.

Below, according to Form Chapters 4 through 19, this chapter gives the architecture that supplies these three absences.

II. This Chapter's Architecture: Add Three Layers above Latent-Space Dynamics

This chapter does not discard the latent-space dynamics of world models. It adds three Self-Manifestation structures above it. Let the system be:

𝓢 = (EF, IF, ImF, WD, OCC, ATTR, TF, Gₛ, G𝚌)

The preceding fields are inherited from the engineering projection:

EF (Experience-Field): carries input, history, and external feedback.

IF (Intent-Field): carries goals and constraints.

ImF (Imagery-Field): carries intermediate path-structure.

TF (Tuning-Field): carries evaluation and feedback.

Gₛ/G𝚌: the double judgment of stability and consistency.

The three new items are the substantive differences between this chapter and the world-model route.

WD (World Dynamics, latent-space dynamics): the forward-predictive state-transition model that world models already possess. This chapter keeps it as the source of candidates, but does not take its output as reality.

OCC (Occupation, occupancy-convergence layer): the first substantive addition of this chapter relative to world models.

ATTR (Attribution, source-attribution layer): the second substantive addition.

Continuability objective: the third substantive addition. It acts upon the training signal rather than as an architectural module. See 2.3.

2.1 OCC: Task Coupling Excludes Collapse; Occupancy Maintenance Treats Anchorless Diffusion

The latent state of a world model is a continuously evolving distribution and can carry multiple parallel hypotheses at once. Based on the pool-level current single-value principle (Observer Group v1.0; a constitutive commitment, unproven, open to empirical breach), this chapter adds a convergence step above latent dynamics: within the same resource-pool, at most one maintained occupancy exists. It must be recorded truthfully that simultaneous double occupancy is not excluded dynamically — the Observer Mathematical Form, Theorem 8, has proven that a retention state of double completion can exist — so the parameter-gate and the dynamic-gate are not a "double guarantee" but candidate engineering realization mechanisms of that commitment; the empirical results of 5.5 below accord with this.

Engineering meaning: WD gives a candidate latent-state distribution; OCC does not take its expectation and does not take its distribution, but performs resistant convergence, selects and maintains a unique occupancy c\*ₜ (z = 1 and sustained for ≥ τ). This step directly answers two ceilings.

Treating collapse. The basis of exclusion must first be stated accurately. Collapse (mapping all frames to the same embedding) degenerates the candidate-set into a single element. A single candidate can be trivially occupied and maintained. Therefore collapse is not excluded by the equal-state dynamic-gate (that gate excludes a stalemate among multiple candidates, which is another failure). What collapse truly violates is the task condition. First, it destroys the multi-candidate structure itself - the whole occupancy mechanism presupposes coexistence of multiple candidates and a determinacy gap; collapse makes the premise idle. Second, the collapsed occupancy has no action-coverage - one embedding cannot distinguish the state-differences required by action. This is exactly the form of a cheap observer, excluded by the action-coverage criterion of the Observer Adjudication Form's criterion grid and by the cheap-realization exclusion of the Observer Realization Form, Section 4 (cheap observer exclusion). Therefore this chapter excludes collapse through task coupling: occupancy must serve task-discrimination (the action-coverage item enters the objective), and the collapsed solution is illegal under the task condition. This is an endogenous functional-layer exclusion, belonging to a different level than external embedding regularization; and it gives the explanation that regularization cannot give: why collapse is wrong. It is wrong not because the distribution looks bad, but because occupancy loses service-capacity.

The applicable strength must be marked honestly. The anti-collapse force of task coupling expands or contracts with the task's need for discrimination. Under low-resolution, low-control-demand tasks, partial collapse is not excluded. This chapter treats this not as a defect but as a feature: the target of exclusion is never "all compression is illegal," but "compression must not fall below the resolution required by the task." Representational resolution should expand or contract with discrimination demand. This is consistent with the discrimination structure of the Recognition-Realm: the system holds only the resolution required by its task. Embedding regularization spreads the distribution without distinction; this chapter's exclusion has degree: collapse is allowed only down to the task-discriminable lower bound. Experimentally, this means that anti-collapse strength can be parametrically modulated by task difficulty - this itself is a testable prediction, not a weakness.

Treating drift. Two types of drift must be distinguished, and each has its own treatment.

First, anchorless diffusion: the latent state freely disperses and the Current has nothing locked. This is treated by occupancy maintenance (the completion-retention condition of the Observer Mathematical Form, Definition 47; its converse being the decay condition, Definition 48: resource allocation continually presses down dispersal; Form Chapter 18: locking persistence). The locked Current gives later generation an anchor; sequential replacement replaces free diffusion.

Second, correspondence drift: imagined rollout deviates from the real world. This cannot be treated by locking. Locking a wrong state and maintaining it is precisely the form of stubbornness (Field Chapter 20, Theorem 5). Its treatment lies in the source-attribution layer (2.2): this chapter does not pretend to eliminate the bias of imagination, but labels and isolates it. Endogenous rollout is explicitly labeled as endogenous; confidence decreases with rollout length; it must not pretend to be observation. New input from the Experience-Field enters through candidacy and provides re-anchoring. The world-model route tries to make imagination more accurate (decoder verification is of this type). This chapter's route is to make imagination carry a label. The danger of drift is never simply "imagination is inaccurate," but "inaccurate imagination is taken as observation." The latter is the disease to be treated, and ATTR can treat it.

Architectural meaning of satisfying uniqueness: OCC does not output a parallel cloud of beliefs. It outputs a single occupancy plus sequential replacement. During multi-stable competition, within any maintained interval, occupancy is unique; switching is sequential replacement of occupancy, not parallel superposition (the sequential-alternation case in the counterexample family of the Observer Group; Form Chapter 10, Corollary 1). This is an architectural inductive bias that can be written into the system. The latent state of world models does not possess it.

2.2 ATTR: Treating Hallucination and the Prediction-Understanding Confusion

World models do not distinguish whether a latent state comes from external observation or internal imagined rollout. Based on Form Chapters 7 through 10, this chapter adds source attribution above occupancy: for every component of the current occupancy-family H, it generates a source-label assignment covering the whole family (each component is judged inner or outer, forming one single label-map; values inside the assignment may differ, Form Chapter 10 v2.0).

Engineering meaning and two answers:

Treating hallucination. The structural form of hallucination is precisely endogenous content being assigned as exogenous: the model takes something it rolled out itself as an observed fact. ATTR explicitly maintains inner/outer labels, and according to Form Chapters 7 through 9, the outer label is not a reading of a real externality (Form Chapter 8: direct reading of the external domain is type-impossible), but an attribution constructed under a gap (Form Chapter 7), and it is manipulable and testable (Form Chapter 7, Corollary 1: manipulating the source-gap or the bias should systematically move attribution judgment). Therefore hallucination changes from "the model occasionally makes mistakes" into "a monitorable and intervenable state of the attribution layer." The system can report the source-label confidence of every component, and can trigger verification when endogenous content is assigned as exogenous with high confidence. A world model lacks this layer, so it cannot tell whether it is observing or fabricating.

Treating the prediction-understanding confusion. Predictive accuracy without understanding has its root in the system not knowing whether its representation is "received" or "generated." Understanding requires precisely this distinction. To understand an object is, in part, to know which of its properties come from the object and which come from one's own projection. ATTR makes this distinction explicit, letting the system self-report "which parts were given by the world and which parts I added." This does not directly equal human understanding, but it gives a structural position for "the part beyond prediction": understanding is at least more than prediction, and part of what exceeds prediction includes source self-report and occupancy maintenance. This chapter gives a structural position for that extra part; it does not claim to exhaust understanding.

Neighboring work and novelty positioning, made honest. Source labeling is not an entirely new concept. Source attribution, provenance tracking, memory tagging, and cognitive-scientific reality monitoring are neighboring objects. ATTR differs from them in three ways. First, those are mostly post-hoc audits or external metadata, while ATTR is an endogenous layer of the generation mechanism; it travels with occupancy rather than being pasted on afterward. Second, On Self-Manifestation gives a theory of label-generation: labels are pressed out by source-gaps and attribution biases (Form Chapter 7), are manipulable, and have criteria (Corollary 1), rather than being merely engineering tags. Third, ATTR is structurally coupled with OCC: the label dimension is a constitutive dimension of occupancy-form (Form Chapter 7, Section III, the formal layer), not an independent module. Accordingly, this chapter calibrates its claim: the novelty of ATTR does not lie in "having source labels," but in the mechanism of label-generation and its coupling with occupancy.

Connection between labeling and control, preventing the objection that this is "only a dashboard." ATTR solves the cognitive layer (the system knows it is fabricating), but does not automatically solve the control layer (do not continue wrongly). If the policy still relies on rollout labeled as endogenous, performance will still collapse. Therefore labels must enter policy. The minimal closed loop has three lines: when the endogenous proportion is high or confidence falls below threshold, trigger observational verification (actively fetch new Experience-Field input for re-anchoring); decision weighting is modulated by source confidence (the action-weight of endogenous content is reduced); when consecutive endogenous steps exceed a limit, forcibly interrupt rollout and return to observation. Without these three lines, ATTR is only a dashboard. With these three lines, labels become control signals. This connection is part of the architecture, not optional.

Boundary-keeping according to Form Chapter 9 discipline. The inner/outer labels of ATTR do not claim ontological status. It dissolves the ontological reading of the label; it does not decide whether the external world exists (Form Chapter 9). This chapter does not add metaphysical bearing that the attribution layer does not need. It is only a manipulable and testable functional layer.

2.3 Continuability Objective: The Training Surface that Treats the Prediction-Understanding Confusion

The first two layers are architecture. The third difference lies in the training signal. The objective of a world model is predictive accuracy: minimizing next-state prediction error. Based on Form Chapter 19 v1.1, this chapter adds a different objective: continuability - under perturbation, can the system self-deepen along its own sedimentation chain, rather than relying on external re-driving or replay?

Engineering meaning: during training, apply perturbation to the current occupancy. Do not reward "predicting the next frame correctly"; reward "after perturbation, the system can grow back to a consistent occupancy by itself and continue deepening." This distinguishes two kinds of systems: one comes from true continuation (it spontaneously recovers and deepens along its sedimentation chain), and one is only high-fidelity copying (there is no chain to rely on; after perturbation it repeats or stalls). Predictive accuracy cannot separate the two (under no perturbation, both predict correctly); the continuability objective can separate them (Form Chapter 19, Section VIII: the difference appears in the next step of continuation and is observable).

Mathematical skeleton. Previously this objective stayed at the conceptual layer; this section gives a trainable form. It is a skeleton, not a final doctrine; open points are listed below.

Let the maintained occupancy at time t be c*ₜ (z = 1 sustained), and let the sedimentation parameters be θ\_dep. Apply perturbation δ \~ D\_δ to the occupancy latent state: ĉₜ = c*ₜ + δ. Then block replay of external input (EF re-reading is forbidden), and roll forward k steps only through internal dynamics and sedimented continuation.

Continuability loss, as a differentiable surrogate, backpropagated along imagined rollout and of the same type as Dreamer's backpropagation through imagined trajectories:

L\_cont = E\_{δ\~D\_δ} \[ max(0, m − z\_{t+k}(δ)) + β · max(0, A(c*ₜ) − A(c*\_{t+k}(δ)) − ε) \]

The first item rewards recovery of occupancy (z returns above the maintenance threshold m within k steps). The second item rewards no degradation of task performance after recovery (A is the task head, ε is tolerance). The performance item simultaneously serves as an anti-cheating gate: the system cannot cheat the loss by setting z constantly to 1, because occupancy must serve the task (the action-coverage item, 2.1).

The distinction between recovery and memory replay is operationalized by the selection of perturbation directions: δ is sampled from new directions not covered by historical trajectories (D\_δ is orthogonalized against the principal components of sedimentation). Replay can recover only along stored directions; only a system that can recover and preserve task performance under perturbations along new directions counts as continuing. Thus "recovery" and "retrieving memory" are separable inside the loss.

The total objective is:

L = L\_pred + α · L\_cont

optimized jointly with prediction loss.

Three anti-cheating gates are added beyond the performance item, corresponding to known cheating paths.

First, single attractor basin. The system might learn one universal attractor, and all perturbations return to the same state. The gate is an occupancy-diversity item: under different task contexts, the occupancies reached by perturbation-recovery should preserve task-relevant separability; if all perturbations converge to one occupancy, the system is penalized.

Second, the task head remains stable while the internal structure degenerates. The gate is a family of probe tasks: A is not one single task head, but the mean of a family of transfer probes; internal structural degeneration appears on untrained probes.

Third, implicit memory bypasses perturbations in new directions. The gate is the structural design of D\_δ: it is not only orthogonal to sedimentation principal components, but applies stratified perturbations by sedimentation layer (shallow and deep layers are perturbed separately), and periodically changes the perturbation generator, making memorized recovery paths fail.

Open points, listed as items still to be formalized and not pretended to be solved: first, the concrete construction of D\_δ's covariance, orthogonalization, and stratified perturbation - this is the number-one design point, and the quality of D\_δ determines the whole effectiveness of L\_cont; second, the setting of k and m and their relation to the occupancy-maintenance time constant τ; third, the scheduling of α; fourth, the concrete definition of the occupancy-diversity item and the probe family. These four points mark the distance from this objective's skeleton to a final doctrine.

This is precisely how "prediction is not understanding" is cashed out in the training signal: do not reward prediction; reward continuation.

III. Formal Comparison with the Transformer and with World Models

Transformer:

y = Cut(Trace(x))

One-time truncation, no internal state.

World model:

sₜ₊₁ = WD(sₜ, aₜ)

y is decoded from the latent state. It has internal dynamics, but the latent state is a predictive distribution; it has no occupancy, no attribution, and the objective is predictive accuracy.

This chapter's system:

Candidate latent dynamics WD gives candidates:

s̃ₜ = WD(sₜ₋₁, aₜ)

retaining the predictive capacity of the world model.

Occupancy convergence OCC selects and maintains unique occupancy:

c\*ₜ = Occ(s̃ₜ), \|maintained occupancy\| ≤ 1 per pool

Source attribution ATTR gives a source-label map:

l*ₜ = Attr(c*ₜ, gap, bias)

The Current is the maintained occupancy with source attribution:

Currentₜ = (c*ₜ, l*ₜ)

and its continuation is maintained by locking persistence.

The training objective is prediction item plus continuability item (perturbation-recovery), not the prediction item alone.

The three essential differences are:

The world model predicts "what the next state will be"; this chapter's system converges "what the Current is selected and locked as."

The world model does not distinguish observation from imagination; this chapter's system gives source attribution for every component.

The world model trains for predictive accuracy; this chapter's system trains for continuability.

IV. Why This "Exceeds" the World Model Rather Than Being Another World Model

Precision is required; exaggeration is not allowed. This chapter does not claim to overthrow the latent-space dynamics of world models. It retains WD as candidate source. Its claim is this: above WD, the three layers of occupancy, attribution, and continuation each answer one of the three ceilings that world models currently answer with patches, and the answers are structurally endogenous rather than external attachments.

Collapse and drift. World models use external regularization and decoder verification. This chapter excludes collapse through task coupling (collapsed occupancy has no action-coverage; it is endogenously excluded at the functional layer, and the reason why collapse is wrong is explained), treats anchorless diffusion through occupancy maintenance, and treats correspondence drift through source-label isolation (imagination does not pretend to be observation). All three are structurally endogenous and do not require a second network.

Hallucination and the prediction-understanding confusion. World models have no source distinction. This chapter explicitly distinguishes observation and generation through the source-attribution layer, making hallucination a monitorable state and giving "understanding" a structural position beyond prediction.

Prediction is not understanding. World models take predictive accuracy as the objective. This chapter adds continuability as an objective and separates "predicted correctly" from "understood" at the level of the training signal.

Therefore "super" does not mean larger scale, nor another larger prediction model. It means a structural elevation of the generation mechanism: from predicting the next state to converging, attributing, and maintaining the Current. This is the threshold that the world-model route has not yet crossed, and On Self-Manifestation has already proven each part of that threshold in Form Chapters 4 through 19.

~~~python
def super_world_model(x, task_meta, max_steps=8):
    intent = build_intent(x, task_meta)
    exp = ExperienceField(user_input=x, history=[], external_feedback=[])
    imf = plan(x, intent, exp)
    occ = None

        occ = occupy(cand, pool=intent, prev_occ=occ)            # converge to unique occupancy
        attr = attribute(occ, gap=estimate_gap(cand), bias=intent.bias)
        cur = Current(occ=occ, attr=attr)

        y = decode(cur, imf)
        s = score_stability(y, task_meta)
        c = score_consistency(y, imf)

        if occ.z >= 1.0 and occ.held_steps >= TAU and s > 0.9 and c > 0.7:
            return cur, y                  # occupancy maintained and double-gate passed

        imf = update_imf(imf, y, s, c, exp)
        exp.history.append({"role": "candidate", "content": y})

    return cur, y
~~~

The key difference lies inside the loop: cand comes from the world model (prediction), but it is not output directly. During training, continuability\_loss is optimized jointly with prediction loss.

### 5.4 Minimal Discrete Dynamics of OCC

(Direct Discretization of the Candidate-Running Definitions of the Observer Mathematical Form)

Previously OCC stayed at the level of principle and was the number-one item to fill. This section gives the minimal runnable version. It is not a new design. Every item corresponds to a candidate-running dynamical item of the Observer Mathematical Form (whose coefficients are a conditional construction, see Observer Construction Form H6): zero-sum resource allocation, maintenance inequality (resources press down dispersal), crosstalk suppression, positive-feedback locking, and hysteretic replacement. It is rough but runnable; first secure engineering existence.

~~~python
import torch

def occ_step(a, z, occ_idx, p):
    """One-step occupancy dynamics.

    a: [n] candidate activation (discrimination strength, given by WD candidate + task coupling)
    z: [n] occupancy strength
    occ_idx: index of the current occupier, or None

    p: parameters:
       r      resource rate
       eta    dispersal rate
       chi    crosstalk coefficient
       Ovl    [n,n] overlap matrix
       gamma  feedback gain
       T      temperature
       m      occupancy threshold
       tau    maintenance steps
       h      replacement hysteresis
    """

    x = torch.softmax(a / p.T, dim=0)                       # zero-sum resource allocation (competition)
    cross = p.chi * (p.Ovl @ z - torch.diag(p.Ovl) * z)     # crosstalk suppression
    z = z + p.eta_z * (p.r * x - p.eta * z - cross)         # maintenance inequality: r*x presses eta*z
    z = z.clamp(min=0.0)

    a = a + p.gamma * z                                     # positive-feedback locking
    winner = int(z.argmax())

    if occ_idx is None:
        if z[winner] >= p.m:                                # threshold touched: candidate begins maintenance count
            occ_idx = winner
    else:
        # hysteretic replacement: challenger must exceed the incumbent by hysteresis h,
        # preventing instant noise from seizing occupancy
        if winner != occ_idx and z[winner] > z[occ_idx] + p.h:
            occ_idx = winner                                # sequential replacement, not parallel superposition

    return a, z, occ_idx

# Occupancy establishment: z[occ_idx] >= m sustained for >= tau steps
# (instantaneous touching does not count as occupancy).

# Expected ablation phenomena:
# gamma = 0 (remove feedback) → equal-state stalemate, no occupancy forms.
# r < eta (resources cannot overcome dispersal) → manifestation vacancy
#    (no z reaches threshold; corresponds to the simplified-model case of
#     Observer Mathematical Form, Theorems 7 and 11).
# h = 0 (remove hysteresis) → high-frequency noise-driven seizure;
#    occupancy is not maintained.
# chi = 0 (remove crosstalk suppression) → tendency toward coexisting high z values;
#    reverse verification of failure of simultaneous double-occupancy exclusion.
~~~

Correspondence item by item:

softmax zero-sum allocation = competition among tuning resources.

r·x − η·z = maintenance inequality (resource allocation presses down dispersal; Observer Mathematical Form, Definition 47).

Crosstalk item = overlap suppression (the Ovl item in the former Lemma 3 parameter-gate, now a candidate realization mechanism of the commitment).

Positive feedback a += γ·z = locking dynamics (the source of the equal-state's lack of restoring force, the former Lemma 3 dynamic-gate, of the same standing).

Threshold m and τ = the persistence definition of candidate completion (instantaneous touching does not count; Observer Mathematical Form, Definition 45).

Hysteresis h = engineering form of sequential replacement (replacement, not superposition).

The four expected ablations in the comments are the internal testability of this dynamics: parameter and failure mode correspond one by one, and each correspondence has a source in the Form Chapters.

Honest boundary of this version: it is a single-pool, discrete, low-dimensional toy version. It does not yet include modulation of θ by the Tuning-Field, inter-pool structure, or end-to-end joint training with WD. These are the distance from toy to module. But the objection that OCC is only a "conceptual controller" fails from here onward: OCC now has an update equation.

5.5 Empirical Test: Phase Boundary of Exact-One  
(Already Run, Reproducible)

The four ablation expectations above are not merely comments. They have already been verified in an equivalent numpy implementation. The most important result is: exact-one is a conditional structural property, not something hard-coded by implementation.

Across a parameter grid - resource rate, crosstalk coefficient, gain, perturbation strength, and random seed - stress tests measured the "number of simultaneously maintained occupancies across the whole run," repeatedly applying strong perturbations and orientation switching. Two independent grids (about 1,200 and 800 configurations respectively) gave consistent results: about seven to eight percent of configurations produced double occupancy (maximum observed: two), while the rest preserved exact-one (simultaneously maintained occupancy ≤ 1).

The distribution of violations partly agrees with the former Lemma 3 parameter-gate r ≥ 2(η + χ·Ovl) (that gate is no longer a theorem but one candidate realization mechanism of the pool-level current single-value principle; and what this section shows — that exactly-one is a conditional emergent property — is of one direction with the Observer Mathematical Form, Theorem 8, that a retention state of double completion can exist, and is empirical support for it). This must be stated honestly and not exaggerated. Along the resource-rate direction, the trend is cleanly monotonic: the higher the resource, the more double occupancy appears (the number of observed violations increases by nearly five times with resource rate). This matches the prediction that when resources can feed two occupancies at once, the right side of the gate fails. Along the crosstalk direction, however, the trend is only weak: low crosstalk has more violations, but high-crosstalk zones still contain a considerable number of violations. Therefore "low crosstalk more easily violates" can only be treated as a tendency, not as a doctrine. The single-pool toy version uses a simplified form of Ovl; its crosstalk item and the theoretical gate's χ·Ovl are not yet strictly aligned. This gap between measurement and theory is registered.

Regardless of attribution details, the core conclusion is robust: exact-one can be overturned by parameters, and at least along the resource axis, the position of overturning agrees with the theoretical gate boundary. If implementation hard-coded exact-one, it would be constantly one under all parameters, which would be suspicious. Here it fails with parameters exactly as the theory says, at least along the resource axis; therefore exact-one is an emergent property of dynamics, not a code constraint.

Two other tests were also run.

First, after adding a fatigue item, orientation switching lets the new orientation seize occupancy from the old one, while the number of simultaneous occupancies remains one throughout the switch - sequential replacement, not parallel superposition (the sequential-alternation case in the counterexample family of the Observer Group; Form Chapter 10, Corollary 1).

Second, in the weak-parameter zone where resources cannot overcome dispersal (corresponding to the simplified-model case of manifestation vacancy, Observer Mathematical Form, Theorems 7 and 11), occupancy cannot recover after perturbation knocks it down. Only the within-gate zone can recover. This also answers one worry about L\_cont: the system might learn a cheap attractor in which "all perturbations return to the same state" and cheat the recovery objective. This dynamics contains no such cheap attractor: recovery has threshold and cost.

One honest negative result: when perturbation wipes out the instantaneous state completely - activation and occupancy together - a single orientation bias is insufficient to recover directionally to the original occupier. The recovery rate is high, but the endpoint is close to random. This means: if "sedimented continuation" is to carry "recovery to the same reality" under strong perturbation, it needs a stronger trace-retention structure than a single bias item, such as a multidimensional bias along sedimentation principal components. This is a remaining link between OCC and full continuability, and it is listed together with the L\_cont benchmark as the next step.

Summary: OCC is already an engineering module that can be trained, stress-tested, and overturned by parameters; its uniqueness boundary quantitatively agrees in part with the former parameter-gate — that agreement is quantitative evidence for a candidate realization mechanism of the commitment, not a proof of exactly-one. What has not yet landed is the full L\_cont benchmark and the multidimensional realization of sedimented trace-retention.

VI. Experiment Design

### 6.1 Comparison Baselines

Transformer single-shot generation.

Dreamer/JEPA-like world model (pure WD).

This chapter's system (WD + OCC + ATTR + continuability objective).

### 6.2 Tasks

Long-horizon reasoning (to measure drift).

Tasks requiring distinction between observation and hypothesis, such as noisy retrieval-style question answering (to measure hallucination and attribution).

Code generation requiring self-correction (to measure continuation).

The evaluation of attribution must include source-controlled tasks: the experimenter manually controls the information source. Some input is planted false observation; some true observation is hidden and must be completed. The ground truth of source is held by the experimenter. The test measures whether ATTR labels shift systematically with source manipulation. Without this design, inner/outer labels have no external truth, and evaluation degenerates into a self-consistency loop. This is precisely where the objection "it is only one more confidence head" arises. With this design, ATTR becomes a falsifiable module.

### 6.3 Ablations

(Verifying the load-bearing role of each of the three layers)

Remove OCC (return to pure WD): test whether anchorless diffusion and parallel superposition reappear.

Remove task coupling (keep prediction loss; remove the action-coverage item): test whether representation collapse reappears. The measurement point for collapse is attached to task coupling, not to OCC - see 2.1.

Remove ATTR: remove source distinction; test whether hallucination rate (endogenous content being treated as observation) rises.

Remove the continuability objective (prediction loss only): test whether perturbation-recovery degenerates into copy-like repetition.

Remove occupancy maintenance (occ does not lock): test whether the Current collapses instantly and whether sequential replacement degenerates into parallel superposition.

### 6.4 Metrics

Long-horizon consistency (drift).

Hallucination rate and source-label accuracy (attribution).

Perturbation-recovery success rate (continuability).

Occupancy uniqueness: the number of maintained occupancies at the same moment (convergence).

### 6.5 Relation to the Criteria Index

The four categories of ablation in this chapter measure the necessity of the three-layer structure in artificial systems. The index of testable criteria for On Self-Manifestation, attached to this chapter, measures the same structure in natural systems.

Removing OCC and reproducing collapse/drift corresponds to the criteria of simultaneous double-occupancy exclusion and manifestation maintenance.

Removing ATTR and increasing hallucination corresponds to the criterion of manipulable attribution.

Removing the continuability objective and degenerating into copying corresponds to the continuability criterion.

The occupancy-uniqueness metric corresponds to observer counting and simultaneous double-occupancy exclusion.

The same-directionality between engineering ablations and natural criteria is collateral evidence that this chapter's architecture and the structures of On Self-Manifestation share one skeleton.

VII. Discussion: Where It Is Super, and Where It Is Not

### 7.1 Where It Is Super

Relative to the Transformer, this chapter changes generation from one-time truncation into a convergence system with internal state. Relative to the world model, this chapter adds above latent dynamics occupancy (treating collapse and drift), attribution (treating hallucination and the prediction-understanding confusion), and a continuability objective (treating prediction not being understanding). All three are structurally endogenous rather than patches. This is an elevation of the level of the generation mechanism: from predicting the next state to converging and maintaining a Current with source attribution.

### 7.2 Where It Is Not Super

(Boundary-Keeping, According to Form Chapters 12 and 9)

This chapter realizes an operational-surface projection of Self-Manifestation structure. It does not claim to realize a complete Self-Manifesting Being.

According to the threshold of Form Chapter 12, a Self-Manifesting Being requires an independent tuning resource-pool plus maintained occupancy. Whether the OCC of this chapter constitutes true maintained occupancy, and whether the WD+TF loop constitutes an independent tuning closed loop, are empirical questions to be tested by criteria. This chapter does not prejudge them.

The source-attribution layer of this chapter does not claim the ontological status of inner/outer labels (Form Chapter 9). It is a manipulable and testable functional layer, not a metaphysical assertion.

The operational-surface projection of Self-Manifestation structure has been realized; a Self-Manifesting Being has not been claimed to have been realized. The distance between the two is precisely what the criteria index marks as the matter to be tested.

### 7.3 External Positioning

In the AI research context, the proper name of this chapter is not "surpassing the Transformer" - that is promotional language - but:

Current-Augmented World Model

The word "Super" belongs to the internal history of this book's chapter title. Externally, it should be stated as a research proposal. The weight of a proposal lies in its testability; this chapter's testability lies in Section VI and in the criteria index.

### 7.4 A Judgment

The next step of the world-model route is often expected, in the industry, to come from larger prediction models, steadier latent-space regularization, and stronger decoder verification. This chapter gives a different direction: the next-level improvement may not lie in predicting more accurately, but in whether the system knows what it currently occupies, whether that is observation or imagination, and whether, after perturbation, it can grow itself back. Prediction is the strength of world models. Occupancy, attribution, and continuation are their blind spots. These three are precisely the structural difference between "predicting the world" and "being in the world."

### 7.5 Items Filled and Still To Be Filled

Originally two items were registered.

First, an explicit algorithmic form for OCC - now supplied in 5.4, as a direct discretization of the candidate-running definitions of the Observer Mathematical Form, a minimal runnable version with four expected ablations. It has also been run; see 5.5. In two independent grids, about seven to eight percent produced double occupancy; violations rose monotonically along the resource axis and agree with the failure of the right side of the parameter-gate; along the crosstalk axis the trend is only weak, and the fact that the toy version's Ovl is not strictly aligned with the theoretical gate has been registered. Sequential replacement and the recovery threshold were verified at the same time. One honest negative result is attached: under full erasure, a single bias is insufficient for directional recovery, and sedimented trace-retention requires multidimensional realization. The remaining distance from toy to module - Tuning-Field modulation, inter-pool structure, end-to-end joint training with WD, and multidimensional sedimented trace-retention - is moved into the to-be-filled list.

Second, the experiment-task definition for L\_cont - still to be supplied: concrete benchmark tasks, perturbation protocol (the structural construction of D\_δ is the number-one design point), and evaluation scripts, including the source-controlled task of 6.2 and implementation of the three anti-cheating gates of 2.3. When this is supplied, this chapter moves from research proposal to reproducible experiment.

VIII. Conclusion

Starting from the generation task, this chapter points out the respective ceilings of the Transformer's one-time truncation and the world model's latent-space prediction, and according to Form Chapters 4 through 19 of On Self-Manifestation, gives a minimal architecture that adds three layers above latent dynamics: occupancy, attribution, and continuation. This architecture answers, in a structurally endogenous way, the collapse, drift, hallucination, and prediction-understanding confusion that current world models treat with patches; and every layer connects to already-proven Form-Chapter theorems and testable criteria.

The core conclusion, in one sentence:

The next-level improvement of generation is not predicting more accurately, but converging, attributing, and maintaining a Current - knowing what one occupies, where it comes from, and whether, after perturbation, one can grow onward by oneself.
