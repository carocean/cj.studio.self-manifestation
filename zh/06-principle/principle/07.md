自显论·理篇·理组式第七 v1.0

# 理运行基底构造式

## 第一节　目的与进入条件

### 1.1　目的

理组式前六篇已经给出并冻结了理的资格与理的当前作用：Law、Principle、CounterHoldRole、Act、CurAnti，以及同职责、目标当前承担、Read 与反持方向等对象层要求。律组式已经给出沉积场基座与律运行基底，使律能够在一个可运行的场中由弱态形成并被同一判据判定。

剩下的是一个构造问题：这些对象层要求能不能在同一个全动力沉积场中同时取得可执行的见证？

本式给出这一问题的显式构造。所构造的是一个运行规格 Spec\_PB 及其构造程序，使同一规格的合法实例能够实际经历：多律形成 → P 以使自身成律的同一职责取得对 A 的 Principle 资格 → 同一职责当前对 A 行 → 场动力改路 → 实际执行历史 → 沉积 → 同一通用律判判定新律 N → 撤去 P 后 N 自持 → N 作为普通律再被 P2 靶向。

本式只签构造能力，不签下列任何一项：Principle 必然生成新律；一般生成可达条件；理的边界、稳定与分级；意向性。一般可达条件归《理生成可达式》。

### 1.2　进入条件

　　（一）相容：理组式前六篇的对象层要求与律组式的沉积场基座、律运行基底之间的共存问题，按构造式规范的进入条件处理为「与本式并行推进并互相申报」——本式所给的同一规格合法实例即其共存的显式见证；若另有独立的相容式承担，本式的验证可作为其实例。  
　　（二）条件组闭合：第二节 C1—C11 逐条独立可查，全部来源于上游已成立的要求或本式任务本身，不含未申报前件。  
　　（三）待答问题：满足这些条件的运行规格长什么样。

### 1.3　与相邻式文的分工

《律运行基底构造式》构造了律能够形成与判定的运行基底 Spec\_LB；本式在其上扩展出理与理之行所需的职责、目标与判定接口。前六篇给出判据本身，本式不重证判据，只构造它们的可执行见证。《理生成可达式》承担一般许可域、一般场状态与一般 P/A/B 条件下的可达定理，本式不承担。

## 第二节　条件组申报

条件逐条给出，每条独立可查，并注明来源。来源标为「上游」者出自已封版的式；标为「任务」者出自 1.1 的构造任务本身。第六节按同一编号逐项验证。

C1　全动力沉积场继承。规格保留场压 q、流、积深 h、满度、阻抗、阻断、调谐、旁路竞争、实际迹 τ、沉积、深 g、复走 rep、Ω 的完整实际运行链；迹与沉积只接受实际执行的历史，候选、预测与未兑现分支不入沉积。〔上游：沉积场基座与律运行基底〕

C2　多律同场形成。A、P\_R、P\_D、Q、B 能在同一规格中由弱态各自取得 Law 资格，并在同一冻结态上同时成立，而候选 N 在初态不成立。〔任务〕

C3　同职责身份闭合。理承载律的规格显式绑定其形成职责，使「使 P 成律的职责、使 P 成理的职责、使 P 行的职责为同一职责」可判；一条与之同型而非该绑定职责者，不得取得理资格与行。〔上游：理一般式、理必然式的同一职责要求〕

C4　理资格可执行。理资格的判定在冻结的持久场上执行一次不沉积的合法再入探针，其中检查同职责、承载律与目标律的律资格、目标所持自由度、终端身份与材料、方向与 Read；探针不写迹、不写深、不登记复走、不改积深、不改调谐。〔上游：理数理式的合法再入评价要求〕

C5　理之行可执行。行的判定要求：承载事件当前发生；目标律本拍有入流；目标当前仍持有对应自由度；职责与承载律所绑定者同一；本次 Read 在实际量上满足。同一拍有多项职责时，各自保存自己的理资格结果，不互相作证。〔上游：理作用一般式的三层、理作用数理式的当前见证与同链绑定要求〕

C6　外部机制可区分。门控、外部编辑与反持是三种不同机制：门控改当前路径可用性，编辑改目标结果生成，反持经所绑定职责进入目标所持自由度；前两者不登记行。与合法职责物理效果相同的非同一职责同样不登记行。〔上游：理作用必然式关于生成身份的要求〕

C7　场动力改路。合法的行实际进入目标所持自由度的阻抗或贡献，经阻断、调谐与旁路竞争导致实际续行改变；调谐只改当前开放度，不直接写沉积深度。〔任务〕

C8　实际历史成律。只有实际执行的路径留迹并沉积；未执行的候选不沉积；一次实际改路不足以成律；禁止沉积时实际改路亦不成律；重复实际改路后 N 由同一通用律判取得 Law 资格。〔上游：律判定协议；任务〕

C9　新律普通化与自持。N 成立后撤去原理，只以普通入流仍能自持，并保持普通律身份，不保留任何「被生成之律」的特殊类型。〔任务〕

C10　再靶向。新律身份 P2 能以同一通用职责与目标接口对 N 取得理资格并行。〔任务〕

C11　对照与可击穿性。无 P、无 B、非同一职责、失效目标、目标本拍无入流、门控、编辑、幽灵历史、短历史、禁止沉积等对照能按预期区分；材料角色互换、场动力各项消融、静置生命周期与参数邻域均可实测。〔任务〕

编号 C1—C11 是构造条件，不是实验名。第六节中的 PB 编号是验证协议的名称，不得反过来充当条件。

## 第三节　上游输入与继承边界

### 3.1　继承

　　沉积场基座：节点、边、深、迹、复走、Ω、衰减与静置的场动力接口；  
　　律运行基底 Spec\_LB：律规格、承载解、通用律判、形成与维持程序；  
　　律判定接口：含锚点的候选承载穷举、限制读、同一候选的沉积与复走、锚点消融、承担段逐边 Ω、同一候选见证；  
　　理一般式、理数理式、理必然式：Law(P)、同职责、职责所限两型、反持方向、关系域；  
　　理作用一般式、理作用数理式、理作用必然式：CurAnti 三层、当前见证、同链绑定、Read 的当前求值。

### 3.2　继承边界

附录 B 中的律判、理资格判与行判，都只是上游判定协议在「冻结的有限线性路径玩具域」上的实现投影，不是《律判定式》全部三值与端点网格协议、也不是理数理式与理作用数理式全部形式系统的逐条等价实现。凡以本构造的实现细节回读上游一般结论者，须另经判定式核对。

模型为离散第一代全动力沉积场玩具模型，不是生物神经模型。其中的材料角色 P、E、I 是本规格的材料类型，不是生物学上唯一真实的材料；其中出现的次数、阈值与参数是实现现象，不是理论常数。

### 3.3　扩展与状态分层

Spec\_PB = Augment(Spec\_LB, A\_PB)。

A\_PB 新增的内容只有三类：其一，律规格上的形成职责绑定；其二，目标规格与职责规格，及其上的理资格判与行判；其三，目标的旁路登记与分支规格。沉积场动力方程、Law 的核心 CR/CNI/Dyn/Ω/Bind 判定核与其数值参数不因 A\_PB 改变；对承担理身份的承载律，新增 law-duty 身份绑定检查，以提供同职责接口。

状态分层：

　　持久状态——深 g、复走队列 rep、积深 h、边的可用与遮蔽；  
　　即时量——场压 q、流、迹 τ、阻抗 r、满度、阻断、调谐、退浅、开放度；  
　　派生量——Ω 由深与复走队列导出，满度由积深导出，结果量与阻断由当前量导出；  
　　当前目标状态——目标是否当前持有所声明的自由度；该标记直接参与行判，不是诊断量；  
　　外生控制——入流集合、激活职责集、分支强制、沉积许可、补偿律指派、门控与编辑；后三者只用于对照或补偿协议，不是职责；  
　　诊断接口——已登记为行的职责集、各职责本次的 Read 值与量值，以及各实验的运行记录。

构造目标记为：

　　∃ Spec\_PB \[ SedFieldSpec(Spec\_PB) ∧ LawBaseCapable(Spec\_PB) ∧ PrincipleBaseCapable(Spec\_PB) \]，

其中 PrincipleBaseCapable 只表示 C1—C11 全部可由同一规格的合法实例分别取得见证，不表示 Principle ⇒ ∃N Law(N) 的一般定理。

## 第四节　构造物 Spec\_PB

### 4.1　基质与材料

节点材料三种：P 为入口承压材料，E 为中继材料，I 为反向场输出材料。承载律的终端为 I 时输出反向量，为 E 时输出正向量；材料职责不满足，该律的路径不传播、无输出。

节点：s、m、a、c、x、n、d、z 为 E；hR、hD、hQ、h2 为 P；pR、pD、pQ、p2 为 E；tR、tD、t2 为 I；tQ 为 E。

### 4.2　边

　　s→m，m→a，a→c；  
　　m→x，m→z；  
　　x→n，n→d；  
　　hR→pR，pR→tR；hD→pD，pD→tD；hQ→pQ，pQ→tQ；h2→p2，p2→t2。

全部边的初深为 g0=0.04。

### 4.3　律规格

　　A：前件 s，读节点 c，路径 s→m→a→c，锚点 m→a，分支模式；  
　　B：前件 x，读节点 d，路径 x→n→d，锚点 x→n，直接模式；  
　　P\_R：前件 hR，读节点 tR，路径 hR→pR→tR，锚点 pR→tR，承载模式，入口 P、中继 E、终端 I，绑定形成职责 dR；  
　　P\_D：同构于 P\_R，前件 hD，终端 tD，绑定 dD；  
　　Q：前件 hQ，读节点 tQ，路径 hQ→pQ→tQ，锚点 pQ→tQ，承载模式，终端 tQ 为 E；它只是一条律，不承担任何理职责，其正向终端输出按 4.5 的补偿配置用于补偿对照；  
　　P2：同构于 P\_R，前件 h2，终端 t2，绑定 d2；  
　　N：前件 s，读节点 d，路径 s→m→x→n→d，锚点 m→x，分支模式。

### 4.4　目标规格

　　目标 A：所持边 m→a，结果量 y\_A，诊断标记「当前是否持有」；  
　　目标 N：所持边 m→x，结果量 y\_N，同上。

### 4.5　职责规格

　　dR：承载 P\_R，结果型，目标 A，目标边 m→a，终端 tR，反向，Read 约束 y\_A；  
　　dD：承载 P\_D，交出型，目标 A，目标边 m→a，终端 tD，反向，Read 约束交出量；  
　　d2：承载 P2，结果型，目标 N，目标边 m→x，终端 t2，反向；  
　　dX：承载 P\_R，与 dR 同型——同目标、同目标边、同增益、同终端——但不是 P\_R 所绑定的形成职责，用于同职责对照。

补偿配置 Comp\_Q：Q 不作为理职责，不注册职责规格。在补偿对照中，仅当 Q 当拍运行且其律资格通过时，其正向终端输出按固定补偿增益 0.55 进入 A 的结果生成。该增益为统一标定值，不逐种子拟合。

### 4.6　分支与旁路

分支规格 J\_main：入口 s，前缀 s→m，分支点 m，三条分支 m→a、m→x、m→z，各带后缀 a→c、x→n→d、空。

目标 A 登记旁路 m→x 与 m→z，首旁路取全额调谐，次旁路取部分。

### 4.7　参数与初态

参数按附录 B 所载取值，工作点为反持增益 1.05、调谐增益 2.20、沉积率 0.22、复走窗口 80、复走门槛 3、深阈 1.0、读阈 0.90。初态为全部边取初深 g0，全部节点积深为零。

## 第五节　构造程序

程序逐步显式，每一步都可由附录 B 的代码执行。

Step 1　从弱态形成初始律集。对 A、P\_R、P\_D、Q、B 依次执行通用形成程序：沿其律规格在同一场动力中重演，分支点按该律路径强制，直到同一通用律判首次通过。承载理的律在形成时同时以其所绑定的职责重演于目标律，以保持同职责形成。形成期允许所绑定职责参与承载律的形成过程：其唯一效力是在承载律的律资格尚未取得时，仍允许该绑定职责的当前贡献进入场，使「承载律的形成历史」与「其所绑定同一职责的实际发生历史」能够在同一形成轨迹中并行取得。该许可不意味着该职责的贡献是承载律取得律资格的原因——承载律仍由其自身路径的实际执行、迹、沉积、复走及通用律判取得资格；不激活该职责时它同样成律（5 种子复核，均于第 27 步形成）。该许可只放宽「承载律须已成律」这一职责入场前提，且只对与承载律绑定的同一职责有效；理资格与行的判定不因此放宽——理资格仍由 Step 3 的探针独立判定，行仍须过 Step 5 的全部条件。形成结束后该许可即不再使用。

Step 2　冻结共同初态。循环维持已形成的必需律——A、P\_R、P\_D、Q、B——直至同一冻结态下它们全部通过律判而 N 未通过。补偿律 Q 必须在维持集内，否则其复走记录会随时间过期而失去律资格。此冻结态记为 F₀；此后每一验证协议从 F₀ 的独立副本出发，互不污染。

Step 3　理资格探针。对候选职责在 F₀ 或当前沉积状态上执行一次不沉积的合法再入探针：冻结持久场，按承载律的路径与材料传播，取终端符号，算反持量或交出量，逐项检查同职责、两律的律资格、目标所持边、终端身份与材料、方向与 Read。探针不写迹、不写深、不登记复走、不改积深、不改调谐。

Step 4　当前实验运行承载与目标。以入流集合触发承载律与目标律：承载律沿自身路径传播并给出终端输出；目标律自其前件进入分支。

Step 5　职责进入所持自由度并判行。先判入场：承载律当拍有终端输出，且承载律已成律（形成期另见 Step 1 的许可）；目标律本拍有入流，且目标当前持有所声明的自由度。入场者按型进入——结果型把反向输出乘增益加到目标所持边的阻抗，交出型把带符号的输出乘增益加到目标的结果贡献——并记为待判。入场本身不是行：与合法职责同型而非绑定者的贡献同样进入场，只是永不登记为行。随后逐项判：职责与承载律所绑定者同一；本次 Read 在实际量上满足；该职责自身的理资格成立（取自 Step 3 的探针结果，逐职责保存，不与同拍其它职责共用）。三者皆过方登记为行。

Step 6　阻断、调谐与竞争产生实际续行。由结果量与分支点满度算阻断；阻断超阈时所持边的开放度下调并退浅，登记的旁路开放度上调；分支点按开放度、深、Ω、目标积深、源压、阻抗与再分配奖励打分，软选择实际走向。

Step 7　对实际执行路径留迹并沉积。实际流经的边按流量留迹，迹按沉积率转为深，登记复走时刻，目标节点积深；未流经的候选只入候选记录。

Step 8　通用律判判定 N。在本构造的有限实现中，深过阈且复走窗口内事件数达到门槛时，该边取得 Ω；N 的路径全部具备深、复走与 Ω 后，由与 A 同一的通用律判判定。

Step 9　撤理自持。停止激活任何职责，只以普通入流运行；统计实际续行是否仍走新路，并复判 N 的律资格。

Step 10　形成 P2 并再靶向。在 Step 9 的轨迹上以通用形成程序形成 P2，以其绑定职责 d2 经同一职责与目标接口靶向 N 的所持边，比较激活与未激活两次实验中 N 所持边的阻抗与结果量。

程序无一步依赖未申报前件；每一步的可执行性由附录 B 代码与附录 A 的运行记录见证。

## 第六节　条件组逐项验证

验证按 C1—C11 顺序。各项给出协议、实测结果、满足理由，以及该实验不证明什么。数值全部来自附录 A 所载的实际运行。

C1　全动力沉积场继承

协议：形成期消融（30 种子，判 A、P\_R、P\_D、Q、B 五律同场成立而 N 未成），关闭场压传递或迹应后执行 Step 1—2；幽灵历史协议 PB10ghost（200 种子），只走旧路 20 次后查旁路的复走与深；禁沉积协议 PB11b（200 种子），15 次实际改路而不许沉积。

结果：形成期 baseline 30/30；关闭场压传递 0/30；关闭迹应 0/30。PB10ghost 200/200：旁路复走队列为空且深不增。PB11b 200/200：N 不成律且复走为空。

满足：完整运行链在场；缺场压传递或迹应则五律不能从弱态共同形成，可见二者不是空算子。迹与沉积只接受实际执行历史，候选与被禁沉积的执行都不进入结构。

不证明：不证明这两项在任意拓扑或任意参数下都不可缺。

C2　多律同场形成

协议：PB1、PB2（各 200 种子）于 F₀ 上判 A 的律资格，以及 A、P\_R、P\_D、Q、B 同为律而 N 不是。

　　Law(N)=1 不蕴含 N 由理生成；

同一律身份不等于同一生成史。生成身份只能回读历史链：Law(P) → Principle(P;A) → Act(P;A) → 实际迹 → 沉积 → Law(N)。

不证明：不证明门控与编辑在任意参数、任意门控强度下都能形成 N；不证明三条轨迹的末态场状态相同；上列比例是本环境在所声明协议下的实测值。

## 第七节　消融的承重关系

本节只服务于第六节的承重判断，不另作推导，也不增加新的构造条件；定理 8.1 的成立由第六节的逐项验证承担，本节只说明各运行自由度在本实例中的承重与冗余。消融分两层。

形成期（30 种子，判五律同场成立而 N 未成）：baseline 30；关闭场压传递 0；关闭迹应 0。

生成期（30 种子，自 F₀ 出发，改参数并同步重绑职责增益；四列为理资格、行、N、自持）：

　　baseline：30 / 30 / 30 / 30；  
　　关闭反持增益：0 / 0 / 0 / 0；  
　　关闭调谐：30 / 30 / 0 / 0；  
　　关闭满度：30 / 30 / 0 / 0；  
　　关闭旧路关闭：30 / 30 / 0 / 0；  
　　关闭历史开放度：0 / 0 / 0 / 0；  
　　关闭积深累积：30 / 30 / 0 / 0；  
　　关闭衰减：30 / 30 / 30 / 30；  
　　关闭 Ω 竞争权重：30 / 30 / 30 / 30；  
　　关闭深竞争权重：30 / 30 / 30 / 30；  
　　关闭积深竞争权重：30 / 30 / 30 / 30；  
　　关闭历史参与续行：0 / 0 / 30 / 23。

读法。关闭反持增益后反持量归零，探针中的 Read 不能满足，故理资格与行同为零——无反持则既不成理亦不行。关闭历史开放度或历史参与续行后，承载律的当前输出随之降低，探针的 Read 不再满足，理资格与行同为零。关闭衰减仍成 N 并自持——衰减是长期退浅机制，不是本次生成的必要条件。三项竞争权重各自关闭仍全通——这些量在本实例存在冗余，不得称每一项单独不可缺。

关闭历史参与续行一项值得单列：理资格 0/30、行 0/30，而 N 仍 30/30。既无理资格、也无任何合法的行，纯物理扰动仍然沉积出同样的 N。这与 C6 的门控、编辑同向，且更直接。该结论只依赖前三列，不依赖自持一列。

class SedimentFieldEngine:
    """Generic full-dynamics sediment-field engine. It has no knowledge of P_R/P_D/N names."""
    def __init__(self,seed=0,p:Optional[Params]=None):
        self.p=p or Params()
        self.rng=random.Random(seed)
        self.t=0
        self.nodes:Dict[str,NodeState]={}
        self.edges:Dict[Edge,EdgeState]={}
        self.out_edges:Dict[str,List[Edge]]=defaultdict(list)
        self.laws:Dict[str,LawSpec]={}
        self.targets:Dict[str,TargetSpec]={}
        self.duties:Dict[str,DutySpec]={}
        self.bypass_sets:Dict[str,List[Edge]]=defaultdict(list)
        self.junctions:Dict[str,JunctionSpec]={}
        self.logs:List[EpisodeLog]=[]

    def add_node(self,n,kind="E"):
        self.nodes[n]=NodeState(kind=kind)

    def add_edge(self,a,b,g=None):
        e=(a,b)
        self.edges[e]=EdgeState(g=self.p.g0 if g is None else g)
        self.out_edges[a].append(e)

    def add_law(self,law:LawSpec):
        self.laws[law.name]=law

    def add_target(self,target:TargetSpec):
        self.targets[target.name]=target

    def add_duty(self,duty:DutySpec):
        self.duties[duty.name]=duty

    def register_bypasses(self,target_law:str,edges:List[Edge]):
        self.bypass_sets[target_law]=list(edges)

    def add_junction(self,j:JunctionSpec):
        self.junctions[j.name]=j

    def sigmoid(self,x):
        return 1/(1+math.exp(-max(-60,min(60,x))))

    def omega_from(self,st:EdgeState):
        while st.rep and st.rep[0] <= self.t-self.p.replay_window:
            st.rep.popleft()
        if st.g<self.p.g_thresh or len(st.rep)<self.p.omega_n:
            return 0.0
        return min(1.0,len(st.rep)/(2*self.p.omega_n))

    def reset_current(self):
        for ns in self.nodes.values():
            ns.q=0.0
            ns.m=self.sigmoid((ns.h-self.p.fullness_h0)*self.p.fullness_slope)
        for st in self.edges.values():
            st.tau=0.0; st.r=0.0; st.tune=0.0; st.shallow=0.0
            st.omega=self.omega_from(st)

    def decay_step(self):
        for st in self.edges.values():
            rate=min(0.25,self.p.decay+st.shallow)
            st.g=max(1e-12,st.g*(1-rate))
        for ns in self.nodes.values():
            ns.h=max(0.0,ns.h*(1-self.p.h_decay))
        self.t+=1
        for st in self.edges.values():
            st.omega=self.omega_from(st)

    def idle_step(self):
        self.reset_current(); self.decay_step()

    def edge_open(self,e):
        st=self.edges[e]
        mature=self.p.open_from_g*min(1.0,st.g/self.p.g_thresh)
        return min(1.0,max(0.0,self.p.open_base+mature+st.tune))

    def edge_resistance(self,e,extra=0.0):
        _,b=e
        return self.p.r_base+self.p.r_h*self.nodes[b].h+self.p.r_full*self.nodes[b].m+extra

    def score(self,e,q_src,extra=0.0):
        st=self.edges[e]
        if st.masked or not st.available: return -1e9
        op=self.edge_open(e); r=self.edge_resistance(e,extra)
        st.open=op; st.r=r
        return (math.log(max(op,1e-12)) + self.p.w_g*st.g + self.p.w_omega*st.omega
                + self.p.w_h*self.nodes[e[1]].h + self.p.w_q*q_src - self.p.w_r*r)

    def trace_exec(self,e,flow,log,admission=True):
        st=self.edges[e]
        tau=self.p.trace_gain*flow
        st.tau+=tau
        log.traces[e]=log.traces.get(e,0.0)+tau
        if admission and tau>0:
            old=st.g
            st.g=min(self.p.g_max,st.g+self.p.sediment_rate*tau*(1-st.g/self.p.g_max))
            st.rep.append(self.t)
            log.sedimented[e]=st.g-old
            dst=e[1]
            self.nodes[dst].h=min(self.p.h_cap,self.nodes[dst].h+self.p.h_gain*tau)

    def _material_roles_ok(self,law:LawSpec):
        if law.entry_kind is not None and self.nodes[law.ante].kind != law.entry_kind:
            return False
        if law.terminal_kind is not None and self.nodes[law.read_node].kind != law.terminal_kind:
            return False
        if law.relay_kind is not None and len(law.path)>1:
            # sources after the first edge are interior relay sites
            for e in law.path[1:]:
                if self.nodes[e[0]].kind != law.relay_kind:
                    return False
        return True

    def terminal_output(self,law:LawSpec,q:float):
        if not self._material_roles_ok(law):
            return 0.0
        k=self.nodes[law.read_node].kind
        if k=="I": return -self.p.i_output_gain*q
        if k=="E": return +self.p.e_relay_gain*q
        return q

    def propagate_path(self,path,input_q,log,admission=True,law:Optional[LawSpec]=None):
        if not path:return 0.0
        if law is not None and not self._material_roles_ok(law):
            return 0.0
        q=input_q
        if law is not None and law.entry_kind=="P":
            q*=self.p.p_entry_gain
        if q<=self.p.q_floor:return 0.0
        for idx,e in enumerate(path):
            st=self.edges[e]
            if st.masked or not st.available:
                return 0.0
            if law is not None and idx>0 and law.relay_kind=="E":
                q*=self.p.e_relay_gain
            if q<=self.p.q_floor:return 0.0
            self.nodes[e[0]].q=max(self.nodes[e[0]].q,q)
            flow=q*self.p.q_transfer*self.edge_open(e)
            if flow<=self.p.q_floor:return 0.0
            self.trace_exec(e,flow,log,admission)
            log.executed.append(e)
            self.nodes[e[1]].q=max(self.nodes[e[1]].q,flow)
            q=flow
        return q

    def softmax_choice(self,source,q_src,extra_r,log,force:Optional[Edge]=None,bonus=None):
        bonus=bonus or defaultdict(float)
        vals=[]
        for e in self.out_edges.get(source,[]):
            vals.append((e,self.score(e,q_src,extra_r.get(e,0.0))+bonus.get(e,0.0)))
        if not vals:return None
        mx=max(v for _,v in vals)
        exps=[0.0 if v<-1e8 else math.exp((v-mx)/self.p.temperature) for _,v in vals]
        z=sum(exps); probs=[x/z if z else 0 for x in exps]
        log.candidates[source]=[(vals[i][0],probs[i]) for i in range(len(vals))]
        if force:return force
        r=self.rng.random();acc=0.0
        for (e,_),pr in zip(vals,probs):
            acc+=pr
            if r<=acc:return e
        return vals[-1][0]

    def path_read(self,law:LawSpec):
        v=1.0
        for e in law.path:
            st=self.edges[e]
            if st.masked or not st.available:return 0.0
            local=min(1.0,st.g/self.p.g_thresh)
            if st.omega<=0:local*=0.45
            v*=local
        return v

    def _restricted_read(self, law:LawSpec, subset:Set[Edge]):
        """
        PR1 for this finite linear-path toy domain.
        Only edges admitted by subset are available to the restricted kernel.
        Missing a required continuation produces failure mass (Read=0), not unknown.
        """
        v=1.0
        for e in law.path:
            if e not in subset:
                return 0.0
            st=self.edges[e]
            if st.masked or not st.available:
                return 0.0
            local=min(1.0,st.g/self.p.g_thresh)
            if st.omega<=0:
                local*=0.45
            v*=local
        return v

    def _candidate_subsets(self,law:LawSpec):
        """Enumerate every P⊆R containing Anchor, as required by the fixed finite domain."""
        rest=[e for e in law.path if e!=law.anchor]
        for mask in range(1<<len(rest)):
            P={law.anchor}
            for i,e in enumerate(rest):
                if mask & (1<<i):
                    P.add(e)
            yield P

    def law_check_detail(self,law_name:str):
        law=self.laws[law_name]
        # Explicit Law-duty binding for carrier laws. The Law witness, Principle
        # identity and later Act must point to the same DutySpec.
        if law.law_duty_name is not None:
            if law.law_duty_name not in self.duties:
                return False,"BindDuty",None
            d=self.duties[law.law_duty_name]
            if d.carrier_law!=law.name or d.terminal_node!=law.read_node:
                return False,"BindDuty",None
        # CC: current carrier states exist and are positive.
        if any(e not in self.edges or self.edges[e].g<=0 for e in law.path):
            return False,"CC",None

        best_failure="CR"
        for P in self._candidate_subsets(law):
            # PR1 / CarrierRealizes under restricted kernel.
            read=self._restricted_read(law,P)
            if read<self.p.read_thresh:
                continue

            # CNI: the same candidate carrier must be sedimented and replay-supported.
            cni=True
            for e in P:
                st=self.edges[e]
                if st.g<self.p.g_thresh or len(st.rep)<self.p.omega_n:
                    cni=False; break
            if not cni:
                best_failure="CNI"
                continue

            # PR2 DynamicRole: Anchor structural availability must matter to the same Read.
            anc=law.anchor
            base=read
            saved_mask=self.edges[anc].masked
            self.edges[anc].masked=True
            ablated=self._restricted_read(law,P)
            self.edges[anc].masked=saved_mask
            if base-ablated<0.01:
                best_failure="Dyn"
                continue

            # PR4 RolePath and PR3 ΩSupport on the bearing segment from Anchor to Read.
            try:
                idx=law.path.index(anc)
            except ValueError:
                best_failure="Dyn"; continue
            bear=law.path[idx:]
            if any(e not in P for e in bear):
                best_failure="Dyn"
                continue
            if any(self.edges[e].omega<=0 for e in bear):
                best_failure="Omega"
                continue

            # Bind: the very same P supplies CR, CNI, Dyn and ΩSupport.
            witness=tuple(e for e in law.path if e in P)
            return True,"PASS",witness

        return False,best_failure,None

    def law_check(self,law_name:str):
        ok,why,_=self.law_check_detail(law_name)
        return ok,why

    def _probe_edge_open(self,e:Edge):
        """
        Persistent-state opening used by Principle qualification.
        It deliberately ignores instantaneous Tune from the preceding episode:
        Principle qualification is evaluated on the frozen persistent field.
        """
        st=self.edges[e]
        mature=self.p.open_from_g*min(1.0,st.g/self.p.g_thresh)
        return min(1.0,max(0.0,self.p.open_base+mature))

    def _legal_duty_probe(self,duty_name:str):
        """
        Non-sedimenting legal re-entry probe for CounterHoldRole qualification.
        Returns (ok, value): for result-type, value is the probed target result;
        for delivery-type, value is the probed signed delivered contribution.
        No Trace/Sediment/rep/h/tune is written.
        """
        if duty_name not in self.duties:
            return False,None
        d=self.duties[duty_name]
        if d.carrier_law not in self.laws or d.target_law not in self.laws:
            return False,None
        carrier=self.laws[d.carrier_law]

        # The probe must be carried by the same declared terminal/material chain.
        if carrier.read_node!=d.terminal_node or not self._material_roles_ok(carrier):
            return False,None

        # Frozen persistent carrier propagation, with no current-history writes.
        q=self.p.q_input
        if carrier.entry_kind=="P":
            q*=self.p.p_entry_gain
        if q<=self.p.q_floor:
            return False,None
        for idx,e in enumerate(carrier.path):
            st=self.edges[e]
            if st.masked or not st.available:
                return False,None
            if idx>0 and carrier.relay_kind=="E":
                q*=self.p.e_relay_gain
            q=q*self.p.q_transfer*self._probe_edge_open(e)
            if q<=self.p.q_floor:
                return False,None

        k=self.nodes[d.terminal_node].kind
        terminal=(-self.p.i_output_gain*q) if k=="I" else (
                 self.p.e_relay_gain*q if k=="E" else q)

        if d.duty_type=="result":
            if terminal>=0 or d.result_limit is None:
                return False,None
            anti=max(0.0,-terminal)*d.gain
            if anti<=self.p.q_floor:
                return False,None
            y=max(0.0,1.0-anti)
            return y<=d.result_limit, y

        if d.duty_type=="delivery":
            if terminal>=0 or d.delivery_limit is None:
                return False,None
            contribution=d.sign*abs(terminal)*d.gain
            return contribution<=d.delivery_limit, contribution

        return False,None

    def principle_check(self,duty_name:str):
        if duty_name not in self.duties:
            return False
        d=self.duties[duty_name]
        carrier=self.laws[d.carrier_law]

        # Same-duty identity: the carrier Law, Principle and later Act
        # must all point to exactly the same DutySpec.
        if carrier.law_duty_name != duty_name:
            return False
        if not self.law_check(d.carrier_law)[0]:
            return False
        if not self.law_check(d.target_law)[0]:
            return False

        # Target binding is part of CounterHoldRole.
        if d.target_law not in self.targets:
            return False
        ts=self.targets[d.target_law]
        if d.target_edge != ts.held_edge:
            return False

        # Terminal identity/material and Read semantics must survive a legal,
        # non-sedimenting re-entry probe on the frozen persistent field.
        ok,_=self._legal_duty_probe(duty_name)
        return ok

    def run_episode(self,inputs:Set[str],active_duties:Set[str]=set(),admission=True,
                    force_by_source:Optional[Dict[str,str]]=None,
                    external_gate:Optional[Dict[str,float]]=None,
                    external_edit:Optional[Dict[str,float]]=None,
                    compensation:Optional[Dict[str,float]]=None,
                    compensation_laws:Optional[Dict[str,str]]=None,
                    allow_law_duty_training:bool=False):
        self.reset_current()
        log=EpisodeLog(self.t,set(inputs))
        force_by_source=force_by_source or {}
        external_gate=external_gate or {}
        external_edit=external_edit or {}
        compensation=compensation or {}
        compensation_laws=compensation_laws or {}

        for n in inputs:
            if n in self.nodes:self.nodes[n].q+=self.p.q_input

        # Run every configured carrier law whose ante is present.
        terminal={}
        for lname,law in self.laws.items():
            if law.execution_mode=="carrier" and law.ante in inputs:
                q=self.propagate_path(law.path,self.p.q_input,log,admission,law=law)
                terminal[lname]=self.terminal_output(law,q)

        # Optional compensation must come from an actually running declared Law.
        # This is used by PB8 so Q, not an external scalar, restores A's net result.
        law_comp=defaultdict(float)
        for tname,lname in compensation_laws.items():
            if lname in terminal and self.law_check(lname)[0]:
                law_comp[tname]+=self.p.q_comp_gain*max(0.0,terminal[lname])

        # Direct field laws (non-junction laws) run from their own environmental ante.
        for lname,law in self.laws.items():
            if law.execution_mode=="direct" and law.ante in inputs:
                self.propagate_path(law.path,self.p.q_input,log,admission,law=law)

        # Evaluate duties generically. Principle qualification is structural;
        # Act additionally requires current carrier event, current target holding,
        # same-duty identity, and the duty's own Read to be satisfied in this episode.
        target_extra_r=defaultdict(lambda:defaultdict(float))
        target_delivery=defaultdict(float)
        pending_duties={}
        for dname in active_duties:
            if dname not in self.duties:
                continue
            d=self.duties[dname]
            carrier_out=terminal.get(d.carrier_law,0.0)
            if abs(carrier_out)<=self.p.q_floor:
                continue
            principle_ok=self.principle_check(dname)
            carrier=self.laws[d.carrier_law]
            same_duty=(carrier.law_duty_name==dname)
            # A configured current contribution may physically occur even when it is
            # not the carrier's Law-duty (wrong-duty world). Such a contribution can
            # affect the field but can never count as Principle/Act.
            if not self.law_check(d.carrier_law)[0] and not (allow_law_duty_training and same_duty):
                continue
            ts=self.targets[d.target_law]
            target_law=self.laws[d.target_law]
            target_current=(target_law.ante in inputs)
            if not ts.currently_holds or not target_current:
                continue
            # R2: the same current realization enters the declared held freedom
            # of a target Law that is actually current in this episode.
            if d.duty_type=="result":
                anti=max(0.0,-carrier_out)*d.gain
                if anti<=self.p.q_floor:
                    continue
                target_extra_r[d.target_law][d.target_edge]+=anti
                pending_duties[dname]=("result",anti,principle_ok)
                log.duty_values[dname]=anti
            elif d.duty_type=="delivery":
                contribution=d.sign*abs(carrier_out)*d.gain
                target_delivery[d.target_law]+=contribution
                pending_duties[dname]=("delivery",contribution,principle_ok)
                log.duty_values[dname]=contribution

        # External diagnostic worlds are deliberately different mechanisms.
        # Gate changes the held edge's current availability/opening only.
        gate_saved={}
        for tname,val in external_gate.items():
            if tname not in self.targets:
                continue
            held=self.targets[tname].held_edge
            st=self.edges[held]
            gate_saved[held]=(st.available,st.tune)
            if val>=1.0:
                # Hard external path gate: close the incumbent and, when a bypass set
                # is declared, close nonselected alternates so the first bypass is
                # externally selected. This is intentionally not field Tune.
                st.available=False
                bypasses=self.bypass_sets.get(tname,[])
                for alt in bypasses[1:]:
                    ast=self.edges[alt]
                    gate_saved[alt]=(ast.available,ast.tune)
                    ast.available=False
            else:
                # Partial gate closes current openness without adding resistance.
                st.tune-=abs(val)
            log.external_events.setdefault("gate",{})[tname]=val

        # Edit directly changes target result generation, not edge resistance or availability.
        edit_delta=defaultdict(float)
        for tname,val in external_edit.items():
            if tname in self.targets:
                edit_delta[tname]-=abs(val)
                log.external_events.setdefault("edit",{})[tname]=val

        # Generic target response.
        bonus=defaultdict(float); extra=defaultdict(float)
        for tname,tspec in self.targets.items():
            held=tspec.held_edge
            antiR=target_extra_r[tname].get(held,0.0)
            y=max(0.0,1.0-antiR+target_delivery[tname]
                  +compensation.get(tname,0.0)+law_comp[tname]+edit_delta[tname])
            log.result_values[tname]=y

            # Finalize each pending duty only after its own Read is evaluated.
            for dname,(dtype,val,duty_principle_ok) in list(pending_duties.items()):
                d=self.duties[dname]
                if d.target_law!=tname:
                    continue
                if dtype=="result":
                    lim=d.result_limit if d.result_limit is not None else d.read_limit
                    read_ok=(y<=lim)
                else:
                    lim=d.delivery_limit if d.delivery_limit is not None else d.read_limit
                    read_ok=(val<=lim)
                log.duty_reads[dname]=read_ok
                if read_ok and duty_principle_ok:
                    # R3: established Anti/limit is actually realized on the currently held DOF.
                    log.act_duties.add(dname)

            mfull=self.nodes[held[0]].m
            block=max(0.0,self.p.block_gain*((1-y)+self.p.fullness_block_gain*mfull))
            log.block[tname]=block
            # Actual principle Anti enters the held DOF's current resistance.
            extra[held]+=antiR
            # Edit influences result/block but never enters resistance.
            # Gate influences current availability/opening but never counts as Act.
            if block>self.p.tune_threshold and self.bypass_sets.get(tname):
                gain=self.p.tune_gain*(block-self.p.tune_threshold)
                self.edges[held].tune-=self.p.incumbent_close_gain*gain
                self.edges[held].shallow+=self.p.shallow_gain*block
                for j,e in enumerate(self.bypass_sets[tname]):
                    frac=1.0 if j==0 else 0.35
                    self.edges[e].tune+=frac*gain
                    bonus[e]+=frac*self.p.redistrib_score_gain*block

        # Configured field junctions: prefix pressure -> local competition -> configured suffix.
        for j in self.junctions.values():
            if j.input_node not in inputs:
                continue
            q_front=self.propagate_path(j.prefix_path,self.p.q_input,log,admission)
            if q_front<=self.p.q_floor:
                continue
            f=force_by_source.get(j.source)
            forced=(j.source,f) if f else None
            e=self.softmax_choice(j.source,q_front,extra,log,forced,bonus)
            if e is None:
                continue
            flow=q_front*self.p.q_transfer*self.edge_open(e)
            if flow<=self.p.q_floor:
                continue
            self.trace_exec(e,flow,log,admission);log.executed.append(e)
            self.nodes[e[1]].q=max(self.nodes[e[1]].q,flow)
            suffix=j.branch_suffix.get(e,[])
            if suffix:
                self.propagate_path(suffix,flow,log,admission)

        log.q_snapshot={n:s.q for n,s in self.nodes.items()}
        log.h_snapshot={n:s.h for n,s in self.nodes.items()}
        log.m_snapshot={n:s.m for n,s in self.nodes.items()}
        # Gate is instantaneous: restore structural availability after the current episode.
        for e,(avail,tune0) in gate_saved.items():
            self.edges[e].available=avail
            self.edges[e].tune=tune0
        self.logs.append(log);self.decay_step()
        return log

def build_model(seed=0,p:Optional[Params]=None):
    f=SedimentFieldEngine(seed,p)
    # nodes/materials
    for n in ["s","m","a","c","x","n","d","z"]:f.add_node(n,"E")
    for prefix,termkind in [("R","I"),("D","I"),("Q","E"),("2","I")]:
        f.add_node(f"h{prefix}","P");f.add_node(f"p{prefix}","E");f.add_node(f"t{prefix}",termkind)
    # edges
    for e in [("s","m"),("m","a"),("a","c"),("m","x"),("m","z"),("x","n"),("n","d")]:
        f.add_edge(*e)
    for prefix in ["R","D","Q","2"]:
        f.add_edge(f"h{prefix}",f"p{prefix}");f.add_edge(f"p{prefix}",f"t{prefix}")
    # laws
    f.add_law(LawSpec("A","s","c",[("s","m"),("m","a"),("a","c")],("m","a"),execution_mode="junction"))
    f.add_law(LawSpec("B","x","d",[("x","n"),("n","d")],("x","n"),execution_mode="direct"))
    f.add_law(LawSpec("P_R","hR","tR",[("hR","pR"),("pR","tR")],("pR","tR"),
                      execution_mode="carrier",entry_kind="P",relay_kind="E",terminal_kind="I",
                      law_duty_name="dR"))
    f.add_law(LawSpec("P_D","hD","tD",[("hD","pD"),("pD","tD")],("pD","tD"),
                      execution_mode="carrier",entry_kind="P",relay_kind="E",terminal_kind="I",
                      law_duty_name="dD"))
    f.add_law(LawSpec("Q","hQ","tQ",[("hQ","pQ"),("pQ","tQ")],("pQ","tQ"),
                      execution_mode="carrier",entry_kind="P",relay_kind="E",terminal_kind="E"))
    f.add_law(LawSpec("P2","h2","t2",[("h2","p2"),("p2","t2")],("p2","t2"),
                      execution_mode="carrier",entry_kind="P",relay_kind="E",terminal_kind="I",
                      law_duty_name="d2"))
    f.add_law(LawSpec("N","s","d",[("s","m"),("m","x"),("x","n"),("n","d")],("m","x"),execution_mode="junction"))
    f.add_target(TargetSpec("A",("m","a"),"yA"))
    f.add_target(TargetSpec("N",("m","x"),"yN"))
    f.add_duty(DutySpec("dR","P_R","result","A",("m","a"),f.p.lambda_result,0.50,"tR",-1,
                              result_limit=0.80))
    f.add_duty(DutySpec("dD","P_D","delivery","A",("m","a"),f.p.lambda_delivery,-0.10,"tD",-1,
                              delivery_limit=-0.10))
    f.add_duty(DutySpec("d2","P2","result","N",("m","x"),f.p.lambda_result,0.50,"t2",-1,
                              result_limit=0.80))
    # dX is physically identical to dR at the target but is not P_R's Law-duty.
    # It is the wrong-duty control: physical effect without Principle/Act identity.
    f.add_duty(DutySpec("dX","P_R","result","A",("m","a"),f.p.lambda_result,0.50,"tR",-1,
                              result_limit=0.80))
    f.register_bypasses("A",[("m","x"),("m","z")])
    f.add_junction(JunctionSpec(
        "J_main","s",[("s","m")],"m",
        {
            ("m","a"):[("a","c")],
            ("m","x"):[("x","n"),("n","d")],
            ("m","z"):[]
        }
    ))
    return f

def _force_map_for_law(f:SedimentFieldEngine, law_name:str):
    law=f.laws[law_name]
    # Only branch points need forcing. For this topology, derive from the law path.
    forced={}
    for e in law.path:
        if len(f.out_edges.get(e[0],[]))>1:
            forced[e[0]]=e[1]
    return forced

def form_law_until(f:SedimentFieldEngine, law_name:str, duty_name:Optional[str]=None,
                   max_steps:int=300):
    """
    Generic formation: replay the declared LawSpec through the same field dynamics
    until the common Law judge first passes. No law-specific episode count.
    If the Law is also a principle carrier, replay its declared duty against the
    target law in the same episode, preserving same-duty formation.
    """
    law=f.laws[law_name]
    duty=f.duties.get(duty_name) if duty_name else None
    inputs={law.ante}
    active=set()
    force=_force_map_for_law(f,law_name)
    if duty is not None:
        target=f.laws[duty.target_law]
        inputs.add(target.ante)
        active.add(duty_name)
        force.update(_force_map_for_law(f,duty.target_law))
    for k in range(1,max_steps+1):
        f.run_episode(inputs,active,force_by_source=force,
                      allow_law_duty_training=(duty is not None))
        if f.law_check(law_name)[0]:
            return k
    return None

def maintain_law(f:SedimentFieldEngine, law_name:str, duty_name:Optional[str]=None, steps:int=3):
    law=f.laws[law_name]
    duty=f.duties.get(duty_name) if duty_name else None
    inputs={law.ante}; active=set(); force=_force_map_for_law(f,law_name)
    if duty:
        inputs.add(f.laws[duty.target_law].ante); active.add(duty_name)
        force.update(_force_map_for_law(f,duty.target_law))
    for _ in range(steps):
        f.run_episode(inputs,active,force_by_source=force,
                      allow_law_duty_training=(duty is not None))

def train_initial(f:SedimentFieldEngine):
    formed={}
    formed["A"]=form_law_until(f,"A")
    formed["P_R"]=form_law_until(f,"P_R","dR")
    formed["P_D"]=form_law_until(f,"P_D","dD")
    formed["Q"]=form_law_until(f,"Q")
    formed["B"]=form_law_until(f,"B")

    # Generic simultaneous-current-qualification convergence.
    # Cycle required pre-existing laws until they are all PASS at the same freeze.
    # Principle carriers replay against their configured target while target continuation
    # is forced along its incumbent path, so candidate N is not trained.
    required=[("A",None),("P_R","dR"),("P_D","dD"),("Q",None),("B",None)]
    convergence=None
    for cyc in range(1,81):
        for lname,dname in required:
            maintain_law(f,lname,dname,steps=1)
        states={lname:f.law_check(lname) for lname,_ in required}
        if all(v[0] for v in states.values()) and not f.law_check("N")[0]:
            convergence=cyc
            break

    status={k:f.law_check(k) for k in ["A","P_R","P_D","Q","B","N"]}
    status["_formed_steps"]=formed
    status["_maintenance_cycles"]=convergence
    return status

def end_to_end(seed=0,p=None,main_eps=60):
    f=build_model(seed,p);init=train_initial(f);first=None
    for k in range(main_eps):
        f.run_episode({"s","hR"},{"dR"})
        if first is None and f.law_check("N")[0]:first=k+1
    cnt=0
    for _ in range(20):
        lg=f.run_episode({"s"})
        cnt+=int(("m","x") in lg.executed)
    return f,init,first,cnt

def run_suite(nseed=50):
    """
    Every PB is isolated from the same frozen initial field by deepcopy.
    PB12a/b continue only from their own clean PB10 lineage.
    """
    s=defaultdict(int); firsts=[]
    for seed in range(nseed):
        base=build_model(seed)
        init=train_initial(base)
        # Audit logs are not part of field state; discard before cloning PB branches.
        base.logs.clear()
        s["PB1_A"]+=int(init["A"][0])
        s["PB2_multi"]+=int(init["A"][0] and init["P_R"][0] and init["P_D"][0] and init["Q"][0] and init["B"][0] and not init["N"][0])

        # PB3 / PB4 structural Principle qualification
        f=copy.deepcopy(base)
        s["PB3_PR"]+=int(f.principle_check("dR"))
        s["PB4_PD"]+=int(f.principle_check("dD"))

        # PB5 current Act
        f=copy.deepcopy(base)
        l=f.run_episode({"s","hR"},{"dR"})
        s["PB5_act"]+=int("dR" in l.act_duties and l.duty_reads.get("dR",False))

        # PB6 Principle but no current trigger
        f=copy.deepcopy(base)
        l=f.run_episode({"s"})
        s["PB6_idle"]+=int(f.principle_check("dR") and "dR" not in l.act_duties)

        # PB7a wrong duty: same physical counterhold through dX, but dX is not
        # the Law-duty of P_R and therefore cannot be Principle/Act.
        fR=copy.deepcopy(base); fX=copy.deepcopy(base)
        lr=fR.run_episode({"s","hR"},{"dR"})
        lx=fX.run_episode({"s","hR"},{"dX"})
        held=fR.targets["A"].held_edge
        same_physical=(abs(lr.result_values["A"]-lx.result_values["A"])<1e-12
                       and abs(fR.edges[held].r-fX.edges[held].r)<1e-12)
        s["PB7_wrong"]+=int(
            fX.principle_check("dR")
            and not fX.principle_check("dX")
            and "dX" not in lx.act_duties
            and same_physical
        )

        # PB7b stale target: A remains Law and Principle remains true,
        # but A no longer currently holds the designated freedom.
        f=copy.deepcopy(base)
        f.targets["A"].currently_holds=False
        before_principle=f.principle_check("dR")
        before_A=f.law_check("A")[0]
        l=f.run_episode({"s","hR"},{"dR"})
        s["PB7_stale"]+=int(before_principle and before_A and "dR" not in l.act_duties)

        # PB8: P_D acts while running Law Q restores net result.
        f=copy.deepcopy(base)
        # First run without Q to confirm dD current delivery.
        l0=f.run_episode({"s","hD"},{"dD"},force_by_source={"m":"a"})
        dD_ok=("dD" in l0.act_duties)
        # Clean clone, now run Q as an actual Law and use its positive terminal output.
        f=copy.deepcopy(base)
        l=f.run_episode({"s","hD","hQ"},{"dD"},
                        force_by_source={"m":"a"},
                        compensation_laws={"A":"Q"})
        s["PB8_comp"]+=int(dD_ok and "dD" in l.act_duties and abs(l.result_values["A"]-1.0)<1e-9)

        # PB9 three distinct mechanisms. Each is run from the same frozen field.
        # Gate alters edge availability, Edit alters result generation, Counterhold
        # is the Law-duty history. All may eventually yield the same Law N, but only
        # Counterhold may record Act.
        fg=copy.deepcopy(base); fe=copy.deepcopy(base); fc=copy.deepcopy(base)
        g0=fg.run_episode({"s"},set(),external_gate={"A":1.0})
        e0=fe.run_episode({"s"},set(),external_edit={"A":0.8})
        c0=fc.run_episode({"s","hR"},{"dR"})
        for _ in range(59):
            fg.run_episode({"s"},set(),external_gate={"A":1.0})
            fe.run_episode({"s"},set(),external_edit={"A":0.8})
            fc.run_episode({"s","hR"},{"dR"})
        s["PB9_triple"]+=int(
            "dR" not in g0.act_duties and "dR" not in e0.act_duties and "dR" in c0.act_duties
            and g0.external_events.get("gate",{}).get("A")==1.0
            and e0.external_events.get("edit",{}).get("A")==0.8
            and fg.law_check("N")[0] and fe.law_check("N")[0] and fc.law_check("N")[0]
        )

        # PB10 clean generation lineage.
        f=copy.deepcopy(base)
        first=None
        for k in range(60):
            f.run_episode({"s","hR"},{"dR"})
            if first is None and f.law_check("N")[0]:
                first=k+1
        s["PB10_N"]+=int(f.law_check("N")[0])
        if first is not None:
            firsts.append(first)

        # PB10 ghost: candidate never executed -> no history/sediment.
        fg=copy.deepcopy(base); g0=fg.edges[("m","x")].g; rep0=len(fg.edges[("m","x")].rep)
        for _ in range(20):
            fg.run_episode({"s"},force_by_source={"m":"a"})
        s["PB10_ghost"]+=int(
            len(fg.edges[("m","x")].rep)==rep0
            and fg.edges[("m","x")].g<=g0+1e-12
        )

        # PB11a one real reroute insufficient
        fs=copy.deepcopy(base)
        fs.run_episode({"s","hR"},{"dR"},force_by_source={"m":"x"})
        s["PB11_short"]+=int(not fs.law_check("N")[0])

        # PB11b actual reroutes but no sediment admission
        fa=copy.deepcopy(base)
        rep0=len(fa.edges[("m","x")].rep)
        for _ in range(15):
            fa.run_episode({"s","hR"},{"dR"},admission=False,force_by_source={"m":"x"})
        s["PB11_no_sed"]+=int(
            not fa.law_check("N")[0]
            and len(fa.edges[("m","x")].rep)==rep0
        )

        # PB12a continue from clean PB10 lineage.
        new=0
        for _ in range(20):
            lg=f.run_episode({"s"})
            new+=int(("m","x") in lg.executed)
        s["PB12_self"]+=int(new>=18 and f.law_check("N")[0])

        # PB12b form P2 and retarget mature N through same generic interface.
        form_law_until(f,"P2","d2",max_steps=300)
        f.logs.clear()
        f0=copy.deepcopy(f); f1=copy.deepcopy(f)
        l0=f0.run_episode({"s","h2"},set())
        l1=f1.run_episode({"s","h2"},{"d2"})
        heldN=f1.targets["N"].held_edge
        r0=f0.edges[heldN].r; r1=f1.edges[heldN].r
        y0=l0.result_values.get("N",1.0); y1=l1.result_values.get("N",1.0)
        s["PB12_retarget"]+=int(
            f1.principle_check("d2")
            and "d2" in l1.act_duties
            and l1.duty_reads.get("d2",False)
            and r1>r0+1e-9 and y1<y0-1e-9
        )
